Ember · Acervo · the lean version

Acervo, lean

The vision minus everything that needs ongoing writing or a tagging pass: no subjects, no "Quero…", no seasonal feature, no language filter, no essays. What's left is built from three computed fields (reading time, arrival dates, which Bible book a commentary covers), 91 author entries, and two written lists. Rows on the Acervo screen link to their screens.
