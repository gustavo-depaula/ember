Três jeitos de entrar em 710 livros

Today the Books screen is a carousel per author, A to Z. Three huge corpora drown it: 420 works of the Fathers, 160 volumes of Denis the Carthusian, 97 of St. Thomas. That's 677 of 710 books, while the thirty classics people actually open sit on one "Outros autores" shelf at the bottom. A reader comes with one of three questions: where was I, what's good on X, what did this person write. Each prototype answers those differently.

What each needs

  • Subjects (A, C): books carry none today. They'd need a subjects field from a fixed list of about 10, filled by a script with a review. The Fathers can inherit theirs from the work's genre.
  • Eras (B): only 130 books are dated. Authors would get a century, which covers every book at once.
  • Portraits: 13 of the 91 authors already have one in the saints' art. The rest show an initial.
  • Depth (C, "para começar / um pouco por dia / para estudar"): a hand-set label per book.

Shared by all three

  • One search field over title, author and subject.
  • Language first: a Portuguese reader isn't shown 160 Latin volumes unasked.
  • Continue reading, from the progress each book already keeps.
  • The giant corpora count as one door each, not 677 covers.
