Ember · if it were only a library

Ember, the library app

Take away the plan of life, the Mass and the calendar: what remains is 710 books and a reader. A library app (Apple Books, Kindle, Libby) is built on four verbs, one tab each. Every screen below is one of those tabs or a page they open. The tab bars link between the screens.

LendoLer

Open on the book you're in. Your time, your streak, a highlight of yours coming back to you.

EstanteGuardar

What's yours: reading, want to read, finished, your own shelves, and every highlight and note.

AcervoDescobrir

The catalog with an editor's hand: the season, new arrivals, lists, and doors by subject, author, era.

BuscarEncontrar

Titles, authors, and the words inside every book.

Already in Ember, buried in the reader
  • Progress per book: "42% · Capítulo 8 de 31", "~2 h para concluir"
  • Reading stats: streak, total time read, chapters
  • Highlights in five colours, notes, bookmarks
  • Reading palettes (Papel, Sépia, Noite…), search inside the open book, EPUB export
What a library app adds
  • Quero ler and Lidos, plus shelves you make
  • All highlights in one place, and one returned to you each day
  • A book page you can judge before opening: length, edition, first page
  • "Recém-chegados": Frost and Bruyère came in on 23 Oct; Denis arrives tier by tier
  • Search inside every book at once, not only the open one

What this says about Ember

As a library app, the front page is your reading, not the catalog. The catalog is a tab you visit, and the giant corpora become collections inside it. The earlier portals still fit as single features: the page-a-day becomes the resurfaced highlight, the question becomes search inside the text, the masters become author pages.

Bringing it back into Ember

  • Lendo is what the Library tab should open on, and Leitura Espiritual on Hoje links to it.
  • Acervo replaces today's Books list from the Search page.
  • Estante could live under You, next to the plan.
  • Book and author pages stand on their own wherever a book is linked.
