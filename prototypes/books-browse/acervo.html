Acervo, and every screen behind it

The Acervo is the library's front room. It replaces today's Books list and opens onto everything else. Its first screen gathers the season, what's new, lists and doors, plus three ideas kept from earlier rounds: a page to read on the spot, "Quero…" needs, and the books that made saints. The other nine screens are what each row and door opens. Rows on the Acervo screen link to their screens below. At the end there's a recommendation for the data it would need.

Recommendation for the missing data (to discuss, not decided)

The Acervo needs eight things the books don't carry yet. Most can be computed at corpus build. Only three need judgement, and those can be set per collection first, then corrected per book, so the 677 corpus books get sensible values on day one.

commentsA Bíblia comentadaFolder and title patterns (super-iob, enarratio-in-joannem, "Homilies on…"), then reviewedmostly computed
