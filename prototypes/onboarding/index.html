1 · The app todayMain at 4a3d199696 on Android, as a new user finds it, 10 November

  • The starter envelope leads Today, and opening it is lovely, but nothing says what a card is or how to win another.
  • "Build my plan" goes to You (a blank page), then Traditions, then the tradition, then Adopt at the bottom, then a confirm sheet, then back to You. No notification permission is ever asked; no reminder is scheduled.
  • On Today the plan is a small tile saying "0 of 6 · at risk" on day one.
  • "Amen" goes straight back to Today: no acknowledgement, no progress toward a card.
  • Today's holy cards are at the bottom, and "pray the day: morning, day and night" never names the three prayers.
