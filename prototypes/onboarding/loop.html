MY PICKS

Today: T2. The card lives in the plan, beside the prayers that win it, so plan, prayer and reward read as one thing. T1 is the most beautiful but competes with the plan for attention. T3 is the most compact, but it hides the rule behind a tap, and the data says people don't tap into cards.

End of prayer: P1, then P4. The seals make "one left" unmistakable, and the reminder offer sits exactly where it's wanted.

Reminders: B in the morning, C at night. Name the card where it gives a reason, and leave plain reminders plain.

Onboarding: A. Opening an envelope in the first ten seconds is the delight, and it teaches the vocabulary every later screen uses. It also fixes the two-thirds who never open their starter envelope today.
