WHICH FEATURE PAGES TO KEEP

Keep: the Mass of the day, Masses near you, the holy cards. Together they answer what someone opening a Catholic app is looking for: the readings, where to go to Mass, and a reason to come back tomorrow. The Mass page also speaks for the Office, with a closing line: "E a Liturgia das Horas, hora por hora."

Optional: the library. It's the most distinctive thing about Ember, but it rewards someone who already prays, so it fits better as a discovery card in week one. The Liturgy of the Hours page is the most beautiful one; keep it instead of the library if you want the tour to speak to people who already pray the Office.
