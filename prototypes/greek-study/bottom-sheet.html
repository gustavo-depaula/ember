The word on a sheet

The Greek reads as the reader reads today: justified, verse after verse. Every word can be studied, and the verse keeps its own half page.

  • Tap a word: it is marked, and the sheet comes up over the lower half with its lemma, sound, sense, form and lexica. Its other occurrences on the page take a dotted gold underline.
  • Tap another word and the sheet turns to it in place; ‹ › walk the verse word by word. Tap the marked word again, swipe down or ✕ to close.
  • Tap a verse number, or hold any word, for the verse’s half page. Its first kind is Words, the verse word by word; a row opens the study, with the way back to the verse.
  • Draw the sheet up by its bar for a long lexicon entry.
