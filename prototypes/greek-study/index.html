Studying the Greek

Three ways to read John 1:1–14 in the Nestle 1904 and study any word in it: lemma, parsing, Dodson, Abbott-Smith, the Middle Liddell and its occurrences.

❦
i

Bottom sheet

The Greek reads as text; tap a word and a native sheet comes up with its study, while a verse number (or holding a word) still opens the verse’s half page.

 ii

Interlinear toggle

A switch in the reader re-sets the Greek as a flowing interlinear, each word over its gloss (and, if you like, its lemma and parsing); tapping still opens the study.

 iii

Side by side

The Greek with the Douay-Rheims or the Vulgate, in alternating rows or in two columns whose rule you drag; Greek words open the study, the translation opens the verse.
