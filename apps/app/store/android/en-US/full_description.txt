Ember is a companion for the Catholic life of prayer. It meets you where you are, from a first Our Father to the full Divine Office, and walks with you day by day.

It is free and open source, and it works without an account.

A PLAN OF LIFE
Build a daily rule of prayer and keep it. Choose a tradition to begin with (Salesian, Ignatian, Carmelite, the Little Way and others) or shape your own, practice by practice. Set the days and the hour for each, with a reminder if you want one. Today shows what the day asks of you, and a wall of stars shows how faithfully you have kept it.

PRAYERS AND DEVOTIONS
• The Holy Rosary, with the mysteries of the day and sacred art for each
• The Holy Mass, in the Ordinary and the Extraordinary Form, with the day's readings and propers
• The Roman Breviary, hour by hour
• The Angelus, litanies, novenas, consecrations and the essential prayers
• Mental prayer with a candle that keeps watch, and a prayer rope for the Jesus Prayer
• Daily meditations for every day of the year

SCRIPTURE AND THE CATECHISM
Read the Bible in the Douay-Rheims translation, available offline, or in other Catholic translations online. Read the Catechism of the Catholic Church and its Compendium.

A CATHOLIC LIBRARY
The Imitation of Christ, the Church Fathers, St. Thomas Aquinas, St. Alphonsus Liguori, the Catechism of the Council of Trent and many more, set in a reader made for long reading: your choice of typeface, size and margins, paper or night palettes, bookmarks, highlights and notes. Keep any book or prayer on your phone for when there is no connection.

THE SAINTS
Holy cards given through the practice of prayer, each one an introduction to a holy life: who the saint was, what the Church prays on the feast, something to take to heart. Follow the liturgical year, in both calendars, with the saint and the season of each day.

MASS TIMES
Find Mass, confession and adoration at churches near you on a map, save your parishes, and help keep the times right. Coverage begins with São Paulo, Brazil, and is growing.

IN TWO LANGUAGES
English and Brazilian Portuguese throughout, with Latin for the prayers that have it, alone or side by side.

Ember is built in the open. Its prayers and books are public texts in open formats, free for anyone to use and to improve: github.com/gustavo-depaula/ember
