Search, rethought

Today answers "what do I pray now"; You holds my rule and my shelf. Search is the one place that should show the whole corpus (438 prayers, 548 books, 43 collections) and let you find anything in it. Four ways to give it that single job. Each phone scrolls; C's tabs are clickable.
