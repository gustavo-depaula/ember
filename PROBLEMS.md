# Open problems in the corpus

The open problems from the first-wave imports of November 2026, then those from translating church fathers and Aquinas opuscula into pt-BR in September 2026. The en-US OCR slips, the `N. ` list-marker paragraph numbers, and the "Enchirídion" spelling in the Confessions introduction have been fixed. Each book's `pt-BR/translation-journal.md` has the full notes.

Paths are relative to `content/books/`.

## Missing content

- `aquinas-opera-omnia/opuscula/de-articulis-fidei/en-US/g1-pr.md` has headings but no body text. The pt-BR prologue was translated from the Latin alone. `scripts/scrape-aquinas-cc.py` can't re-import it, because the aquinas.cc `getCells` endpoint now returns 404. Import the prologue from another copy of the Collins translation, or update the scraper to the site's current API.

## Source readings left as they are

- `church-fathers/athanasius/incarnation-of-the-word`: the source numbers sections irregularly. For example, §5 jumps from 4 to 8, and §10 restarts at 1. Most section numbers are inline, not at the start of a paragraph. The numbering matches the edition, so it was not changed.
- `church-fathers/justin-martyr/first-apology`, Ch. 55: "state possessions" doesn't make sense, and no reading for it has been found.
- `church-fathers/ambrose/repentance`, Book I ¶61: "the law of his flesh agreed … with the law of his flesh". The second "flesh" is probably "mind". Check the printed NPNF before fixing both languages.
- `church-fathers/john-chrysostom/priesthood`, Book V §4: "puffed up by the promises of these persons" is probably "praises". The pt-BR has "louvores".

## Wrong references in the printed edition

These errors are in the printed edition itself, not the import, so both languages keep them on purpose. They are listed here so that no one fixes only one of the languages.

- Commonitory: 1 Cor 2:9 should be 11:19, Gal 2:9 should be 1:9, 2 Cor 11:12 should be 11:13, and the second 1 Cor 14:33 in §74 should be 14:38.
- First Apology: Ch. 35 names Zephaniah for Zech 9:9, and Ch. 51 names Jeremiah for Dan 7:13.
- De articulis fidei: "Ro. VIII" is cited for Rom 9:5.
- Life of St. Anthony: ¶55 cites Galatians 6:6 for 6:2.
- On the Holy Spirit: several references, including Romans 11:38, Isaiah 60:1, Galatians 6:4 and Ezekiel 23:5. See the book's journal.
- Incarnation: 35.7 reads "Josias of Amos" (historically Amon).
- 1 Clement: Ch. 45 reads "Michael" where the biblical name is Mishael.
- Protoevangelium of James: ¶6 "led her astray" is a known mistranslation in Walker's English. The pt-BR adds a translator's note.

## Unfinished work

These church-fathers books have no pt-BR translation yet: `gregory-nyssa/soul-and-the-resurrection`, `tertullian/apology` and `augustine/spirit-and-the-letter`.

### Fénelon (opera omnia, spiritualité, théologie, littérature)
- Most French text is the archive.org OCR of the 1851 Saint-Sulpice *Œuvres complètes* (tomes 1, 5, 6, 7, 8), cleaned by script (running heads dropped, hyphenation joined, vocabulary-vote spelling fixes; period spelling kept). Tome 6 has pages printed in two columns that the OCR interleaved and garbled, notably the *Prières du matin et du soir*, parts of the *Méditations*, the *Entretiens affectifs* and the whole *Odyssée* (14-18% unknown words against the corpus vocabulary, versus 2-5% elsewhere); the *Dialogues sur l'éloquence* are about 8%. They need a second witness (Versailles edition 1820-30, `oeuvrescompltes00unkngoog`…, or the Wikisource djvu text layer of tome 6).
- Piece titles and heading numerals are mostly lost in the OCR of *Manuel de piété*, *Instructions et avis*, *Lettres spirituelles* and the *Ministère des pasteurs*, so those books are cut into parts of 6,000-9,000 words at paragraph ends (legible headings kept as `##`). `lettres-spirituelles` groups the letters by addressee; the letter numbers are ours, and several letters whose heading the OCR lost sit inside the previous one (the Montberon and "Diverses personnes de piété" files hold many letters each).
- Scripture-reference footnotes of the OCR are turned into `[^N]` only where they stand alone as star-led lines; other editorial footnotes of Gosselin remain in the running text or were dropped with the page foot.
- `sermons-et-entretiens` opens with Gosselin's *Notice sur l'électeur de Cologne* (editor's text, not Fénelon's). `entretiens-ramsay` is Ramsay's redaction "after Fénelon". `vies-des-philosophes` has a doubtful attribution.
- English: Lear's *Letters to Men* (Google scan) keeps stray OCR junk and lost letter headings; the *Letters to Women* is cleaner. Hawkesworth's *Telemachus* (1768) was read with long-s as f and repaired word by word, so residual misreadings remain. *Pious Reflections* (1818) and the CCEL *Existence of God* (Part I only, 1713) are fine apart from scattered OCR errors. Dibdin's *Education of Daughters* (Gutenberg) lacks the *Avis à une dame de qualité*.
- Not imported (left for later): *Réfutation du système de Malebranche*, *De summi Pontificis auctoritate*, *Lettres au P. Lami*, *Lettre à l'évêque d'Arras*, the *Opuscules théologiques* (all tome 2, whose archive.org OCR is not available: use the Wikisource djvu `Livre:Œuvres complètes de Fénelon (1848-1852), tome 2.djvu`); the Latin/French *Opuscules divers* of tome 6 (Fantasque, Médaille, Fabulæ selectæ…), the 22 *Mandements* (class III), and PD English for the *Dialogues on Eloquence* (Stevenson 1722, `dialoguesconcer00unkngoog`, downloaded as `research/sourcing/fenelon/en_eloquence_stevenson.txt`, long-s OCR), the *Dialogues of the Dead* (1770), the *Fables* (Elphinston 1760), the *Lives of the Philosophers* (1825) and Metcalf's *Spiritual Progress* (CCEL, Fénelon part only).

### Fénelon (quietisme, jansenisme, mandements, politique, correspondance)
- Tome 2 of the 1848-52 edition has no archive.org copy and its Wikisource page layer is empty; its text comes from the djvu text layer of the Commons scan (`research/sourcing/fenelon/oc1848_t2.txt`, made with `djvutxt`). Paragraphs are rebuilt from short sentence-final lines, so some paragraph breaks are wrong, and the opening title lines of each piece sit in the first paragraph. Tomes 3-10 are the older archive.org ABBYY OCR (5-9% of words unknown to the corpus vocabulary in the jansenism and quietism volumes): garbled words, merged footnote markers and occasional two-column interleaving remain. A vote against the corpus vocabulary corrected only the obvious cases.
- Pieces were cut at the first-page titles found in the OCR; where the title was lost, the boundary is approximate (chapter `01-premiere-et-seconde-parties` of the Dialogues; the pieces of `jansenisme/memoires-et-ordonnances-1706-1710` chapter 04, which holds the Memoriale, the ordinance for *Vineam Domini* with the bull quoted in capitals, and the later letters to Saint-Pons, Quesnel and the Elector of Cologne in one run; the numbered *Mandements* are labelled generically (carême, prières, jubilé) because the OCR lost their dates). The correspondence books are cut by size (about 9,000 words) inside each section of the edition, not by letter; addressee headings (`## A la même`) survive only where the OCR read them as capitals. Section V of the correspondence (*Lettres spirituelles*) is in the `spiritualite` books; the famous *Lettre à Louis XIV* is printed inside the correspondence where the edition places it, not as a separate book.
- `quietisme/maximes-des-saints` is Cherel's 1911 edition of the 1697 text (Avertissement, Issy articles, the five states of love, the 45 *Vrai/Faux* articles, conclusion) without his preface, introduction, variants, notes and appendices; the article headings are only partly recognised by the OCR (about 30 of 90), so some Vrai/Faux headings are missing, and a few stray variant lines or margin references remain in the running text. The 1697 original on archive.org is too poor (long-s type) to import. Fénelon's *Éclaircissement* (Cherel appendix I) is not imported.
- `quietisme/pieces-de-la-condamnation`: the brief and the Latin of the OC are printed in parallel columns; the Latin and French blocks were separated by a word-frequency test per block, so a few blocks sit in the wrong language folder and the numbered 23 propositions are in French only. The Molinos bull in `issy-et-conferences-de-paris` chapter 02 has the same two-column mixing.
- Latin pieces (`quietisme/ecrits-latins`, `jansenisme/dissertationes-latin`, `mandements/mandatum-de-rituali-edendo`) are uncorrected OCR; the French vocabulary vote was not applied to them. The Latin passages quoted inside French pieces were corrected by the French vote and may carry a few wrong words.
- `politique/essai-sur-le-gouvernement-civil` is Ramsay's redaction "after Fénelon". The *Instruction pastorale* of Noailles (Paris) and Godet des Marais's *Lettre pastorale* (Chartres), the replies of Bissy and Saint-Pons and the letters to Fénelon are printed with Fénelon's own pieces, as in the edition. The editor's *Histoire littéraire*, the *Recueil des principales vertus* and the tables at the end of tome 10 are not imported; the 22 *Mandements* are the 10 French ones the OCR let me cut plus the Latin *Mandatum* (the pieces of the Maximes affair are in `quietisme`).
- No English or Portuguese for any of these (none found); pt-BR comes from the pipeline.

### Bossuet (opera omnia, spiritualité and oratoire)

- All French comes from the Port-Valais (ASB) typing of Lachat's edition, a human typing of an OCR: it has a few isolated typos left in the text (for example "bisons" for "faisons", "delà" for "de la", "suinte" for "sainte", "aines" for "âmes", stray `'-` where a note marker stood, and the odd "parle"/"parla" for "par le"/"par la"). The œ ligature was restored by a dictionary pass (`research/sourcing/_scripts/bossuet/oefix.py`); the ambiguous forms sœur/sur, mœurs/murs, vœu/vu and œil/il are decided by the neighbouring words and the Lachat OCR bigrams, so a rare wrong guess is possible. Latin æ typed as œ in the source was changed to æ.
- Page-bottom notes of the Lachat edition are footnotes; a few have lost their marker in the source and were attached to the last paragraph of their page.
- `bossuet-sermons`, `bossuet-panegyriques` and `bossuet-oraisons-funebres-lachat` use the Lachat text (with its Déforis reconstructions), not the critical *Œuvres oratoires* of Lebarq, Urbain and Levesque (1914-26): of its seven volumes only 1, 2 and 7 (`uvresoratoiresd01boss`, `02boss`, `07boss`) download from archive.org (`03boss` to `06boss` return an error page), and the downloaded ones are in `research/sourcing/bossuet/oo/`. A later pass should re-edit the sermons against it.
- `bossuet-lettres-de-direction` is Lachat's *Lettres de piété et de direction* (vols. XXVII-XXVIII), not the dated Urbain-Levesque *Correspondance*; the *Lettres diverses* (vol. XXVII pages 001-012) and *Maximes et réflexions sur la comédie* are left for the correspondence work.
- Not imported from vol. V: the synodal statutes and ordinances, the Cerfroid and Jouarre documents (pages 027-031), which are administrative acts.
- `bossuet-oraisons-funebres` (Wikisource, Dupuis 1699) keeps the 1699 spellings (`&`, "vanitéz"); two of its date lines were lowercased (for example "Prononcée a saint denis").
- Sermon titles and Lachat headings that stand in capitals were recased by rule; a few proper names may be wrongly cased.
- `bossuet-sermon-on-the-mount-capes` (1900) is an uncorrected archive.org OCR of a Google scan (day headings and page marks partly garbled, some words broken) and holds 37 of the 46 days, so it is a separate book of the work. The 1899 *Devotion to the Blessed Virgin* and the Herder 1919 *Great French Sermons* (funeral orations and sermons in English) are not imported yet.
- `correspondance/` (Lachat XXVI-XXXI, ASB typing) is the Lachat arrangement, not the dated critical Urbain-Levesque *Correspondance* (Hachette 1909-25, 15 vols, archive.org `correspondancede01boss` onward), which is still to do and would supersede the Lachat letters in chronology and dates. Lachat titles carry source typos (for example "19 octobre 1983" for 1658 in the first letter, "1075" for 1675 in a note) and the letters are in Lachat's order, not strictly chronological. The Latin letters of the Castorie and Cibo correspondence and the Italian letter CXLI stay in the French folder. Letters before XL of Lachat XXVI (the Sentences, grammar and Dauphin exercises of the same volume) are not part of these books. Volume XXIX page 001 (cover) and XXXI page 004 were not captured.
- `divers/ordonnances-et-pieces-administratives` leaves out the Latin tract *De doctrina concilii Tridentini* (vol. V p. 032); the Jouarre pieces (Mabillon, Baluze quotations) are in Latin inside the French and their footnotes are as typed.
- `divers/maximes-et-reflexions-sur-la-comedie` is Lachat's text; *L'Église et le théâtre* (Urbain-Levesque, 1930) is not collated.

### Bossuet (doctrine, controverse, quiétisme, écriture, gallicanisme, dauphin)

- French is the Port-Valais typing of Lachat (Wayback mirror) with the same œ repair as the other Bossuet books; Lachat's page-bottom notes are footnotes. A block that the source repeats verbatim (`connaissance-de-dieu` ch. II, sections XIII-XIV) is kept once.
- `ecriture/`: the Apocalypse (*L'Apocalypse*, Lachat II pp. 005-027) is not imported. The mirror holds only the Préface (volume002/005); the commentary pages 006-026 were never captured, so the book would be partial. The Lachat OCR (`research/sourcing/bossuet/lachat_02.txt`, old spelling, interleaved verse text) is the fallback. The Latin commentary on the Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs, Wisdom and Ecclesiasticus is imported; Psalms III, IV, XLV, LVIII, LXXXVI, CX and CXXXIII have no heading in the source and ride with the preceding psalm leaf. In `libri-salomonis/sapientia` the Latin text and the commentary alternate, so some chapters appear twice (the second marked "(commentarius)"). The French *Instruction sur la lecture de l'Écriture sainte* and the Latin *Epistola* (vol. I pp. 003-004) are not imported.
- `controverse/ecrits-sur-le-nouveau-testament`: Lachat III pages 004-005, 007-009 and 011-014 (parts of the Trévoux instructions) were never captured, so the Avertissement sur les Réflexions morales and the two instructions are complete only as far as the pages that exist. `remarques-sur-dupin-et-la-mystique-cite` lacks vol. XX page 016 (Dupin, chapter II).
- `controverse/histoire-des-variations`: the English (Coyne, Dublin 1836) is OCR from archive.org, cleaned by page-head rules; the notes (symbol footnotes) are the noisiest part and a few markers are misplaced. It has no counterpart for the French remarques historiques and the additions to book XIV. Book IX's heading in the source reads "En l'an 1501" for 1561; the title was corrected.
- `doctrine/exposition-de-la-doctrine`: the English (Tracts on Catholic Unity, 1845, a free translation) stops after chapter XX; XXI-XXII are missing. `controverse/conference-with-m-claude-en` (1842) is a separate OCR book, whose sections do not follow the French pages.
- `doctrine/discours-sur-l-histoire-universelle` uses the Lachat typing (with Lachat's variant notes, 154k words), not the proofread Wikisource 1681 text (130k); `doctrine/politique-tiree-de-l-ecriture-sainte` likewise. No English was added to either (Elphinston 1767/1821 and the 1842 Book I are still to do).
- `gallicanisme/`: the Latin *Defensio* and its Appendix come from the Bibliothèque monastique Word-HTML (Lachat XXI-XXII), with page marks of vol. XXI stripped; chapter headings are as in the source, a few chapter titles are the editor's argument line and may be cut. The analytical table that follows the Appendix in the file is left out.
- Not done: `controverse/` English for the Avertissements, Communion, Tradition défendue; the Lachat vol. XXVI-XXX letters and vol. XXVII-XXVIII (left to the correspondence agent); vol. XXXI tables and the *Mémoire* pages 004-030.
