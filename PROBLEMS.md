# Open problems in the corpus

The open problems from the first-wave imports of November 2026, then those from translating church fathers and Aquinas opuscula into pt-BR in September 2026. The en-US OCR slips, the `N. ` list-marker paragraph numbers, and the "Enchirídion" spelling in the Confessions introduction have been fixed. Each book's `pt-BR/translation-journal.md` has the full notes.

Paths are relative to `content/books/`.

## Source readings left as they are

- `church-fathers/athanasius/incarnation-of-the-word`: the source numbers sections irregularly. For example, §5 jumps from 4 to 8, and §10 restarts at 1. Most section numbers are inline, not at the start of a paragraph. The numbering matches the edition, so it was not changed.
- `church-fathers/justin-martyr/first-apology`, Ch. 55: "state possessions" doesn't make sense, and no reading for it has been found.

## Wrong references in the printed edition

These errors are in the printed edition itself, not the import, so both languages keep them on purpose. They are listed here so that no one fixes only one of the languages.

- Commonitory: 1 Cor 2:9 should be 11:19, Gal 2:9 should be 1:9, 2 Cor 11:12 should be 11:13, and the second 1 Cor 14:33 in §74 should be 14:38.
- First Apology: Ch. 35 names Zephaniah for Zech 9:9, and Ch. 51 names Jeremiah for Dan 7:13.
- De articulis fidei: "Ro. VIII" is cited for Rom 9:5.
- Life of St. Anthony: ¶55 cites Galatians 6:6 for 6:2.
- On the Holy Spirit: several references, including Romans 11:38, Isaiah 60:1, Galatians 6:4 and Ezekiel 23:5. See the book's journal.
- Incarnation: 35.7 reads "Josias of Amos" (historically Amon).
- 1 Clement: Ch. 45 reads "Michael" where the biblical name is Mishael.
- Protoevangelium of James: ¶6 "led her astray" is a known mistranslation in Walker's English. The pt-BR adds a translator's note.
- Two wording slips of the printed NPNF are corrected in both languages, with a footnote at each giving the printed reading: Ambrose, *Repentance* I ¶61, "the law of his flesh … with the law of his flesh" now ends "of his mind" (the Latin, through Schulte's German in the BKV, has *Geistes*); Chrysostom, *Priesthood* V §4, "puffed up by the promises" now reads "praises" (as in the 1866 and 1907 translations, and the next sentence).

## Unfinished work

These church-fathers books have no pt-BR translation yet: `gregory-nyssa/soul-and-the-resurrection`, `tertullian/apology` and `augustine/spirit-and-the-letter`.

## First-wave imports (November 2026)

Each book's `book.json` source notes give the details. The scripts that built them are in `research/sourcing/_scripts/` or the author's folder under `research/sourcing/`.

### Rhineland mystics: Ruusbroec, Suso, Tauler

- `ruusbroec-opera-omnia/*/dum/`: David's keyed text from DBNL, not collated against the manuscripts. ### Tanquerey (opera omnia)

- *Précis de théologie ascétique et mystique* (10th ed. 1928) and *The Spiritual Life* (Branderis 1930) are two books, not one pair: the French OCR garbles several chapter headings (CHAPERRE, CHAPILRELIV), so the split follows the chapters found (Part I ch. ### Guéranger (L'Année liturgique, 15 volumes: fr-FR vols 1-15, en-US vols 1-15)

- French vols 1-6 come from domgueranger.net (14th ed., 1900): the site's Latin has no punctuation and carries a few typos (a capital È inside words was repaired); long Latin stanzas are one paragraph, not verse lines; the French translation of a hymn is sometimes one paragraph per line. Title casing of the sanctoral headings is approximate ("Saint André corsini").
- French vols 7-15 and all English volumes come from archive.org OCR. The Latin and vernacular columns of the Mass and Office texts were interleaved line by line; `gq_bi.py` separates them by word language and sets a Latin paragraph followed by its vernacular, so a few words at column edges can sit in the wrong paragraph. Hymn and sequence verse is not recoverable from the OCR (set as paragraphs).
- OCR running heads that are garbled ("LK TEMPS APRÈS LA PENTECOTE. — T. I. 130") leak into some French chapters of vols 7-15; chapter titles of those volumes include OCR noise ("Sainte Rosf;; nu sainte-"). Fromage's sanctoral uses the pre-1911 calendar.
- Day headings are detected automatically. French vols 7 and 11 and the English vols 7, 10-12 have fewer chapters than days (Easter week, the Sundays after Pentecost and the octave of Corpus Christi are merged into the nearest detected chapter); no text was dropped, so the fix is to split those chapters by hand or from a better scan.
- The English volumes are separate books, not an `en-US/` folder of the French ones: the chapters do not follow the French ones one for one. The English vol 2 is the UNC scan `liturgicalyear02gura` (the `V02` upload does not download).
- domgueranger.net posts for the Temps pascal (vol 7, 8877 words of the historique) are not used: vol 7 French comes from the OCR.
- Not done: Guéranger's other works (Institutions liturgiques, Monarchie pontificale, Mémoire sur l'Immaculée Conception, Marie d'Agréda, Naturalisme, Considérations, Jansénisme, Conférences, Explication de la messe, Notions sur la vie religieuse, Louange divine, Médaille de saint Benoît, Sainte Cécile).
