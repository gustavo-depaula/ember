# Open problems in the corpus

The open problems from the first-wave imports of November 2026, then those from translating church fathers and Aquinas opuscula into pt-BR in September 2026. The en-US OCR slips, the `N. ` list-marker paragraph numbers, and the "Enchirídion" spelling in the Confessions introduction have been fixed. Each book's `pt-BR/translation-journal.md` has the full notes.

Paths are relative to `content/books/`.

## Missing content

- `aquinas-opera-omnia/opuscula/de-articulis-fidei/en-US/g1-pr.md` has headings but no body text. The pt-BR prologue was translated from the Latin alone. `scripts/scrape-aquinas-cc.py` can't re-import it, because the aquinas.cc `getCells` endpoint now returns 404. Import the prologue from another copy of the Collins translation, or update the scraper to the site's current API.

## Source readings left as they are

- `church-fathers/athanasius/incarnation-of-the-word`: the source numbers sections irregularly. For example, §5 jumps from 4 to 8, and §10 restarts at 1. Most section numbers are inline, not at the start of a paragraph. The numbering matches the edition, so it was not changed.
- `church-fathers/justin-martyr/first-apology`, Ch. 55: "state possessions" doesn't make sense, and no reading for it has been found.
- `church-fathers/ambrose/repentance`, Book I ¶61: "the law of his flesh agreed … with the law of his flesh". The second "flesh" is probably "mind". Check the printed NPNF before fixing both languages.
- `church-fathers/john-chrysostom/priesthood`, Book V §4: "puffed up by the promises of these persons" is probably "praises". The pt-BR has "louvores".

## Wrong references in the printed edition

These errors are in the printed edition itself, not the import, so both languages keep them on purpose. They are listed here so that no one fixes only one of the languages.

- Commonitory: 1 Cor 2:9 should be 11:19, Gal 2:9 should be 1:9, 2 Cor 11:12 should be 11:13, and the second 1 Cor 14:33 in §74 should be 14:38.
- First Apology: Ch. 35 names Zephaniah for Zech 9:9, and Ch. 51 names Jeremiah for Dan 7:13.
- De articulis fidei: "Ro. VIII" is cited for Rom 9:5.
- Life of St. Anthony: ¶55 cites Galatians 6:6 for 6:2.
- On the Holy Spirit: several references, including Romans 11:38, Isaiah 60:1, Galatians 6:4 and Ezekiel 23:5. See the book's journal.
- Incarnation: 35.7 reads "Josias of Amos" (historically Amon).
- 1 Clement: Ch. 45 reads "Michael" where the biblical name is Mishael.
- Protoevangelium of James: ¶6 "led her astray" is a known mistranslation in Walker's English. The pt-BR adds a translator's note.

## Unfinished work

These church-fathers books have no pt-BR translation yet: `gregory-nyssa/soul-and-the-resurrection`, `tertullian/apology` and `augustine/spirit-and-the-letter`.

- `john-of-the-cross-opera-omnia/{cantico-a,llama-b,avisos,opuscula,epistolario,poesias}`: Spanish only. - `anselm-opera-omnia/{de-veritate,de-libertate-arbitrii,de-casu-diaboli}`: McKeon (1929) doesn't follow Migne's chapters.
- `ignatius-opera-omnia/ejercicios`: the English has no [1]–[370] section numbers, because Mullan's paragraphs don't match the Spanish units.
- `teresa-opera-omnia/opusculos`: no English. Stanbrook's Maxims (1913) and Lewis/Zimmerman's Constitutions and Visitation (1913) exist, but their OCR runs the footnotes into the text and the Maxims lose three numbers; no public-domain English is known for the Desafío, the Vejamen or the Pensamientos.

### Elisabeth of the Trinity (opera omnia)

- `elisabeth-of-the-trinity-opera-omnia/{souvenirs,derniere-retraite,lettres,lettres-stanbrook,poesies,elevation}`: archive.org OCR cleaned by script (`research/sourcing/_scripts/ocr_clean.py`, `elisabeth_*.py`), not proofread. Known leftovers: letter-for-letter OCR errors in the French (`ànie`, `ètrcs`, `l)ras`; accents on `âme` fixed only where the OCR wrote `àme`), page-bottom notes whose text runs onto the next page (the tail then sits as a body paragraph, e.g. `souvenirs/fr-FR/ch-05` note 1), and scripture references in the notes of the English that the OCR garbled (`Ixxxiii`). Notes the script could not match to a marker are attached at the end of the page's last paragraph.
- `derniere-retraite` day 1 (both languages) was reassembled by hand because the Carmel's long editorial note and the text interleave in the OCR; the French word before `où le Maître la fait pénétrer` is garbled in the scan (`roliaile`) and read as `retraite` from the English. In later days some scripture notes are unmatched or misnumbered.
- `souvenirs`: the introduction and the Carmel's letter for the fifth edition exist in French only; the English translator's introduction and the approbations are left out. English chapter 13 and others carry OCR noise at the page joins.
- `lettres`, `lettres-stanbrook`: only the extracts the Souvenirs printed (the Dijon appendix and the Stanbrook appendix group them differently, so they are two books). The complete 342 letters, the 123 poems, the Journal, the Notes intimes, *La grandeur de notre vocation* and *Laisse-toi aimer* exist only in the critical edition (Cerf, De Meester); nothing was found online.
- `poesies`: thirteen poems from the Souvenirs appendix; the stanzas of `Les lois de l'amour` are one OCR line each (verse lines run together).
- `ciel-dans-la-foi`: English only (Stanbrook's "Second Retreat", ten days, from the first scan; the French is not online). The Stanbrook days do not follow De Meester's 44 sections.
- `elevation`: kept as a book (one chapter). It could also become a practice with an inline flow.

### Curé of Ars (opera omnia)

- `cure-of-ars-opera-omnia/sermons`: archive.org OCR of the 1883 four-tome edition, cleaned by script (`research/sourcing/_scripts/vianney_sermons*.py`), not proofread. Known leftovers: the large drop capitals that open each sermon are garbled by the OCR (`RS re n'est plus` for `Ce n'est plus`; stray symbols after the first line); frequent letter errors (`Dicu`, `ct` for `et`, `ànie`); the page-bottom notes are matched to markers only where the OCR kept `(n)`; the epigraph of the Nativity of Our Lady is a garbled Latin line. Titles were composed from the running heads and sub-titles; seventy-seven sermons were found against about eighty-five estimated, so check the tables of contents of the scan if a sermon looks missing (the OCR of the tome II table of contents is incomplete).
- Per the 1883 preface, several sermons are adaptations of earlier preachers (P. Lejeune, Ribadeneyra, Rodriguez). They are kept as the editors published them.
- `cure-of-ars-opera-omnia/esprit` (`vianney-esprit-bowden`): English only. The French original (Monnin, *Esprit du Curé d'Ars*, Douniol 1864; Téqui 1916) was not found as digital text (not on archive.org or Wikisource; Gallica/Google Books not reachable), so the book is Bowden's 1865 translation. Part I (introduction, 20 catechisms) and 16 of the 17 exhortations come from EWTN's clean copy of Bowden; catechism XV, Parts II and III, the preface and exhortation XIV come from the Google-scan OCR, cleaned by script and not proofread (`Cur4` for `Curé`, stray `by Google` lines may remain, unmatched footnotes). The order of Part I follows Bowden's contents.
- `cure-of-ars-opera-omnia/thoughts`: Boston 1896 OCR, script-cleaned, not proofread; it overlaps the *Esprit* (excerpts of the same sayings).
- Not imported for the Curé of Ars: the letters (no collected edition online; scattered in Monnin and Trochu); an open English text of the complete sermons (Wagner 1901 exists only as a restricted scan; Morrissy 1960 is a selection and was not used).
