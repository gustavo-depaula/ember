Then a change came, swift and unexpected. “Mr. Yolland, forgive me, but I must go after him. He means something this time. Kindly order your own carriage. I will send for you both if there is any need.”

Then the door opened once more noiselessly, and the fierce presence was gone.

Dick stood dazed.

“What does it all mean? What’s it about? Father, I don’t understand.”
