Priests had a difficult task, he often told himself; guidance must be so tactfully given in this lawless, materialistic age; there was so much insensibility and carelessness to be overcome. It was better done, he thought, by a few bright and careful words dropped like this, at least in the case of intelligent listeners, than by more definitely authoritative pronouncements.
