But now Christ is risen from the dead, the first-fruits of thefn that sleep:

For by a man came death, and by a man the resurrection of the dead.

And as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all shall be made alive.

But every one in his own order: the first-fruits of Christ, then they that are of Christ, who have believed in His coming.

Afterwards the end, when He shall have delivered up the kingdom to God and the Father, when He shall have brought to nought all principality, and power, and virtue.

For He must reign, Until He hath put all enemies under His feet.

And the enemy death shall be destroyed last....

Now this I say, brethren, that flesh and blood cannot possess the kingdom of God: neither shall corruption possess incorruption.

Behold I tell you a mystery. We shall all indeed rise again: but we shall not all be changed.

In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet: for the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall rise again incorruptible; and we shall be changed.

For this corruptible must put on incorruption; and this mortal must put on immortality.

And when this mortal hath put on immortality, then shall come to pass the saying that is written: Death is swallowed up in victory.

0 death, where is thy victory? 0 death, where is thy sting?

Let us pray.

Incline, O Lord, Thine ear to our prayers in which we Thy suppliants entreat Thy mercy, that Thou mayest set the souls of Thy servants, whom Thou hast commanded to depart from this world, in a place of peace and light, and mayest bid them to the company of Thy saints.

Absolve, we beseech Thee, O Lord, the souls of Thy servants from every chain of their sins; that, when they rise again in the glory of the resurrection, they may live amongst Thy saints and elect.

Remember not, 0 Lord, the sins of their youth and their ignorances, but according to Thy great mercy be mindful of their souls in the brightness of their glory. May the heavens open before them, and the angels rejoice at their coming. Into Thy kingdom, Lord, receive Thy servants. May Michael, Archangel of God, welcome them, who is chief of the heavenly host. May the angels of God come to meet them and lead them into Jerusalem, the celestial city. May blessed Peter the apostle receive them, to whom have been given the keys of the heavenly kingdom. May Saint Paul the apostle help them, who was worthy to be made a vessel of election. May Saint John, chosen apostle of God, intercede for them, to whom were revealed the secrets of heaven. May all the holy apostles pray for them, to whom was given by the Lord the power to bind and to loose. May all the saints and elect of God intercede for them, who for the name of Christ have borne torments in this world: that, released from the bonds of the flesh, they may be worthy to attain to the glory of the heavenly kingdom, by the aid of our Lord Jesus Christ, who with Thee and the Holy Ghost liveth and reigneth, world without end.
