There were some more burnings. It was pleasant to awake in the quiet academic room, to hear the sounds of the College servants about their business in the court, and the chiming of the grave bells that called the students once more to mass; to see the familiar dress in the quadrangle and streets, to dine with classic ceremonial in halls that somehow retained an air which the Court had lost, to watch the sizars, the Fellows, the pensioners, each with his assigned part — all this pleased him, in spite of his emancipation from it.
