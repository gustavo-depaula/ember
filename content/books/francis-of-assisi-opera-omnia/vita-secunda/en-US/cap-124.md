# Chapter CV. How he resigned his companions

**144.** Another time he resigned all his companions to his vicar, saying: "I will not seem to be singular by reason of this privilege of freedom: but let brethren accompany me from ' place ' to ' place ' just as the Lord shall inspire them". And he added: "I have seen before now a blind man with a little dog only to show him the way". This then was his glory, to put aside the appearance of any singularity or ostentation, that the power of Christ might dwell in him.
