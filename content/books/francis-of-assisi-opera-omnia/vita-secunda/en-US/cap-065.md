# Chapter XLVI. How he kissed the shoulder of a brother whom he met carrying alms

**76.** Another time, at Portiuncula, a brother re-

-t\. turning with alms from Assisi began when he was now near the "place "to break forth into song, and to praise the Lord with a loud voice. On hearing this the Saint suddenly jumped up, ran out, kissed the brother's shoulder, and said, taking the wallet on his own shoulder, "Blessed be my brother who goes readily, seeks humbly, and returns rejoicing".
