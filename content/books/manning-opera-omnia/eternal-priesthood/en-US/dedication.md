# Dedication

TO THE PRIESTS OF THE DIOCESE OF WESTMINSTER, IN MEMORY OF MANY HAPPY YEARS OF UNITED LABOUR IN THE SERVICE OF OUR DIVINE MASTER, AS A PLEDGE ALSO OF LOVE MORE LASTING THAN LIFE, This Book IS INSCRIBED.

ORATIO S. GREGORII MAGNI ANGLORUM APOSTOLI.

Deus qui nos pastores in populo vocare voluisti præsta quæsumus, ut hoc quod humano ore dicimur, in tuis oculis esse valeamus, per Dominum nostrum Jesum Christum, qui vivit et regnat in unitate Spiritus Sancti Deus, per omnia sæcula sæculorum. Amen.
