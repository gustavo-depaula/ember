# An Exposition of Certain Questions Not Handled in This Book

I. Of Hallowed and Sanctified Creatures, vsed in the Church. II. Of Pilgrimage vnto holy places. III. Of Indulgences, or Pardons. IIII. Of Seruice and Praier in the Vulgar tongue. Euery one diuided into certaine Articles, in which are also expounded diuerse other Controuersies, belonging to the better vnderstanding of the principall Question.
