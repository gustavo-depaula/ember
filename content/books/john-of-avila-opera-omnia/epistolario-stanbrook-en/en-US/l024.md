# Letter 24. To a young lady about to consecrate herself by vow to our Lord

It was the custom in Spain at the time this letter was written , as we often read in St. Teresa's works , for young girls to consecrate themselves to our Lord by a vow of virginity and to live henceforth a life of penance in the worlds like the consecrated virgins of the early Church. The letter is probably addressed to Doha Sancha Carillo , a court beauty , who on the eve of a brilliant marriage made her confession to B. John of Avila , and at once decided to renounce the world. She made a vow of chastity and lived a most austere life in a hermitage adjoining her father's house at Quadalcazar. At the age of twenty-four , Andalusia being threatened with famine for want of water , Doha Sancha offered herself as a victim to appease Divine Justice that the country might be spared : God accepted the sacrifice , a time of plenty followed and the virgin was afflicted with the terrible disease of which she died on August iyh 1 537. It was for Doha Sancha that B. John of Avila wrote his celebrated treatise called “ Audi Filia." Her life has been written by Father Martin de Roa , S. J.
