## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-08: In `saint-rita-of-cascia.md`, the source gives the canonization date as "May 24, 1990," which is a historical/OCR error (St. Rita was canonized in 1900). Per house style, this was translated as-given ("em 24 de maio de 1990") rather than silently corrected, since the instructions call for translating the source text faithfully rather than fixing apparent errors.
- 2026-09-08: In `saint-dominic-confessor.md`, the source's asterisked footnote about the Dominican Order's later, reluctant administration of the Inquisition appears once, inline, immediately after the paragraph it annotates. It was kept in that same position (not duplicated, not moved to the end) to mirror the source's structure exactly.
- 2026-09-08: Also in `saint-dominic-confessor.md`, the source text "after seeing the poverty of St Francis at Crimean" is almost certainly a corrupted/OCR-damaged place name. Since "Crimean" is grammatically used as a place noun, it was rendered as "na Criméia" (Crimea) — the most literal reading of the word as given, without inventing a different place name or omitting it.
- 2026-09-08: In `saint-rose-of-lima.md`, the source's biblical/literary citation footnotes (numbered 1–16) are scattered mid-paragraph as OCR page-break artifacts, interrupting sentences. Rather than reproduce the interruptions verbatim (which would break sentences mid-word in Portuguese), all sixteen were translated in place with superscript numerals and collected into a single numbered reference list at the end of the document. This preserves every citation's content and order while making the prose readable — no citation was dropped or altered in meaning.
- 2026-09-08: Measurements were converted to metric throughout this batch for readability (e.g., "twenty miles" → "trinta e dois quilômetros"; "2,800 ft" → "850 metros"; "27,000 gallons per day" [Lourdes spring] → "cento e vinte e dois mil litros por dia"; "16-stone man" → "mais de cem quilos"; "200 lb. sacks" → "sacos de carvão de noventa quilos"; "two or three hundred yards" → "duzentos ou trezentos metros"). Currency amounts (£200, £50, £10,000) were left as "libras" rather than converted, since they are historical sums tied to a specific place/time rather than a physical measurement.
- 2026-09-08: In `saint-bernadette.md`, corrected an internal error during translation: the trephine-opening measurement "about one inch" was initially mistranslated; fixed to "cerca de dois centímetros e meio" (~1 inch), matching the source's stated size rather than an arbitrary figure.
- 2026-09-08: All ecclesiastical front/back matter (Nihil Obstat, Imprimi Potest, Imprimatur blocks, censor and bishop names, dates) kept exactly as in the English source — Latin labels untranslated, personal names and dates unchanged, including cases where the source itself left the date in English (e.g., "June 20, 1954" in `saint-pius-x-460.md`, "7th January, 1963" in `saint-vincent-pallotti.md`).
- 2026-09-08: `saint-pius-x-460.md` and `the-mothers-saint.md` are framed as fictional dialogues/narratives (a priest, "Father Lawrence," telling the saint's story to a family; CTS staff narrating St. Gerard devotion). Non-saint character names (Morton family, Mrs. O'Shaughnessy, Father Lawrence) were kept in English form with only titles translated ("Padre Lawrence"), consistent with the convention of not translating personal names.
- 2026-09-08: Letter excerpts in `the-mothers-saint.md` (signed with initials like "W.J.", "C.G.", "H.E.McD.") were translated in full but the signature initials were left unchanged, as they are stand-ins for real correspondents' identities, not translatable content.
