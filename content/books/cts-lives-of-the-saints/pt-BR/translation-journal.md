## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-08: Book has no author-added footnotes; source is OCR'd from CTS devotional pamphlets and marked "not yet proofed against the printed originals" — translate the text as given, without attempting to correct apparent OCR artifacts (stray missing quotation marks, etc.) beyond what's needed for a coherent Portuguese sentence.
- 2026-09-08: Each chapter is a self-contained biography/devotional essay (no shared narrative across chapters), so chapters were translated in parallel batches rather than strictly sequentially. Terms above were pre-seeded to keep saints' names and standard Catholic vocabulary consistent across batches.
- 2026-09-08: Ecclesiastical approbation blocks (Nihil Obstat / Imprimi Potest / Imprimatur, censor and bishop names, dates) are kept as in the original (Latin formula labels untranslated; personal/place names unchanged) — these are historical archival records, not text to adapt.
