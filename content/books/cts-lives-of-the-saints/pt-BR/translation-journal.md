## Translation Decisions

- Source has no footnotes (author or editor) — none to drop.
- Titles like "S.J.", "C.SS.R.", "C.P.", "O.P." after saints' names are kept as-is (standard religious-order post-nominals, understood in Portuguese too).
- File/chapter titles translated to natural Portuguese devotional-book style headings; original English kept as the `en-US` TOC title, `pt-BR` TOC title added in `book.json`.
