## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-03: Book has 75 independent short biographies (CTS devotional pamphlets), each self-contained rather than continuous narrative — chapter-to-chapter continuity matters less than in a single-author treatise, but the tone (warm, devotional, mid-20th-century English Catholic pamphlet register) and recurring formulas (Nihil obstat / Imprimatur footers, honorifics) should stay consistent throughout.
- Each chapter's `Nihil obstat:` / `Imprimatur:` footer block (censor name, bishop name, diocese, date) is administrative apparatus, not text to be prayed or read devotionally — keep censor/bishop proper names and Latin titles unchanged; only translate a plain-English title if the original uses English instead of Latin (e.g. "Diocesan Censor" → "Censor Diocesano", "Auxiliary Bishop" → "Bispo Auxiliar").
- Saints' names rendered in their standard Portuguese devotional form (São Francisco de Sales, Santa Teresa de Ávila, Santo Inácio de Loyola, São Tomás Morus/Tomás More — see per-batch notes for any names without an established Portuguese form, kept close to the original in that case).
