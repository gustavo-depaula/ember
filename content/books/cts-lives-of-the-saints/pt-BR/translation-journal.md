## Translation Decisions

- Saints' names rendered in standard Portuguese forms per chapter (e.g., St. Francis de Sales → São Francisco de Sales; St. Ignatius of Loyola → Santo Inácio de Loyola; St. Teresa of Avila → Santa Teresa de Ávila). Batches should record any non-obvious choice here.
- Editor/publisher footnotes (if any) dropped per default policy; author asides/parentheticals kept.
- Book titles referenced in-text kept in *italics*, translated where a standard Portuguese title exists, otherwise transliterated with translator's note only if needed for comprehension.
