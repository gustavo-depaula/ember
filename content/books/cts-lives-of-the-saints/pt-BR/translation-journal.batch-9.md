## Translation Decisions

- **saint-peter-aloysius-mary-chanel.md**: Translated "Mr. Pitchard/Mr. Pritchard" consistently as "Sr. Pritchard" (source has a typo "Pitchard" once; normalized to the correct historical spelling used elsewhere in the same source paragraph). The closing hymn ("To Mary's son who died...") was translated as free verse, prioritizing meaning and register over rhyme/meter, per the "faithful but accessible" guideline — noted as it is a devotional hymn, not doctrinal prose.
- **saint-peter-aloysius-mary-chanel.md**: Kept "papista(s)" as the direct rendering of "papist(s)" since it is reported speech characterizing period Protestant prejudice, not the author's own voice — judged not to need modernization/softening as it is clearly framed as a slur being overcome through kindness in the narrative itself.
- **saint-rose-of-lima.md**: Rendered "Isabel" as the baptismal name kept in Spanish/Portuguese form (no change needed, already a Portuguese name); poem stanzas translated as free verse.
- **saint-louise-de-marillac.md**: "Confraternity of Charity" → "Confraria da Caridade"; "Ladies of Charity" → "Senhoras da Caridade"; "Daughters of Charity" → "Filhas da Caridade" (all established, current names of these real institutions in Portuguese).
- **saint-louise-de-marillac.md**: Kept English currency/units as in source ("libras", "florins" where applicable) — no conversion attempted, per instruction to preserve factual content as-is.
- **saint-wenceslas.md**: This chapter is heavy with Czech proper names, historians, and place names. Given the source's own spelling of Czech names is inconsistent/anglicized (e.g. "Nikolsky", "Podlaha", "Balbin"), restored standard Czech diacritics for surnames of scholars and historical figures where well established (Pekař, Stříž, Balbín, Škréta, Myslbek, etc.) to aid a modern Portuguese reader, while leaving the two lyrical/hymn insertions ("Good King Wenceslas" carol and the "Svatý Václave" canticle) as free-verse translations preserving sense and religious register rather than the English carol's meter/rhyme.
- **saint-wenceslas.md**: "Hilberstadt Cathedral" in the source is almost certainly a misspelling of "Hildesheim Cathedral" (given the bishop's name, "Hildibald/Hildeward" is historically the Bishop of Hildesheim, not "Hilberstadt," which does not otherwise appear as a place). Rendered as "Catedral de Hildesheim" — a judgment call correcting an evident source typo rather than perpetuating it into a nonexistent place name; flagging here per the "note any such change" instruction even though this is a factual/spelling correction rather than a modernization.
- **saint-dominic-confessor.md**: "La Penne d'Ajen" rendered as "La Pena d'Agenais," matching the real Occitan/French place (Lapenne-d'Agenais region) — likely OCR/spelling variant in source; place names were otherwise left as in the source (Béziers, Carcassonne, Fanjeaux, Pamiers, Montreal, Prouille, etc.), consistent with "place names generally kept in original form."
- **the-martyrdom-of-saint-perpetua...md**: "Timinianus (or Firminianus)" and "Geta"/"Caracalla" historical references kept as in source without correction (these are the author's own textual variants, not modernizations). Endnotes 1–2 (Mabillon and Victor citations) were kept as the author's own source citations rather than dropped as later-editor apparatus, since they are integrated into the narrative voice ("Some with Mabillon... think...") rather than appended scholarly annotations.
- General: No offensive/outdated terms requiring modernization were encountered in this batch beyond the "papista" note above.
