## Translation Decisions

- "Mahométans"-type ethnonyms: encountered "Mohammedans" in saint-john-of-god.md ("to convert the Mohammedans there") — rendered as "muçulmanos" per the project's modernization policy.
- saint-pius-x.md: the source gives "the Eucharistic Congress... in the month of August, 1879" for an event in Cardinal Sarto's life as Patriarch of Venice — historically this should be 1897 (Sarto did not become Patriarch until 1893). Preserved the source's date (1879) as given rather than silently correcting a historical/typographical error, per the instruction to translate faithfully rather than fact-check the source.
- saint-pius-x.md: preserved the author's own footnote about the papal-election veto (marked with `*` in the source, referring to the Austrian Emperor's veto of Cardinal Rampolla) using markdown footnote syntax `[^1]`, since this is the author's aside, not a later editor's scholarly annotation.
- saint-pius-x.md and the-life-of-saint-anthony.md: kept the closing Nihil Obstat / Imprimi Potest / Imprimatur blocks (with censor and archbishop names, dates) essentially untranslated, as these are official Latin ecclesiastical formulas that appear as-is in Portuguese Catholic publishing as well — not treated as droppable "editor footnotes" since they are part of the original published work's front/back matter, not later scholarly apparatus.
- saint-louis-ix-king-of-france-confessor.md: the source contains a clearly later-editor's footnote (marked `*`, mid-paragraph due to OCR reflow) discussing the post-Revolution history and current whereabouts of relics of the Crown of Thorns (British Museum, "the Abbé Cotterel," etc.). This is scholarly apparatus added by a later editor of Butler's Lives of the Saints, not the hagiographer's own text — dropped per the default policy, and the two sentences that had carried the asterisk marker were translated cleanly without it.
- the-life-of-saint-anthony.md: cleaned up numerous OCR-introduced mid-word space artifacts and stray glued words in the source (e.g. "ha nd", "conside rable", "friut") when translating — translated the intended meaning rather than reproducing OCR noise.
- the-life-of-saint-anthony.md: the hymn "Peace does he give..." was unnumbered as stanza 1 in the source (with "2." and "3." following) — numbered it "1." in the translation for internal consistency, since stanzas 2 and 3 were already numbered.
- saint-francis-of-assisi.md and saint-bernard-abbot-of-clairvaux.md and saint-mary-magdalen.md: preserved the source's typographic convention of opening certain transitional paragraphs with the first word(s) fully capitalized (representing an illuminated/drop-capital opening in the original print edition) by capitalizing the analogous opening word(s) in the Portuguese (e.g. "FRANCISCO nasceu...", "DE VOLTA à sua cidade natal...", "SÃO BERNARDO foi...").
- Historical name transliterations updated to standard modern forms where the source's are outdated/garbled: "Josbert de la Ferté," "Gaudri" (Fr. "Gaudry"), "Guilherme de Champeaux," "Godofredo" (Geoffrey of Chartres/Auxerre), "Malik as-Salih" and "Baybars" as noted above.
