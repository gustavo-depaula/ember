# Of his deth

IN ye yere of our redempcion.M.CCCC.iCiiij. whan he had fulfylled ye.xxxij. yere of his age & […] at florence he was sodenly taken with a feruent axes which so ferforth crepte in to ye interior[…] pert[…]s of his body y• hit dyspysed all medycynes & ouercame all remedy and cōpelled him within thre dayes to […]atisfye nature and repaye her ye lyfe whiche he receyued of her.
