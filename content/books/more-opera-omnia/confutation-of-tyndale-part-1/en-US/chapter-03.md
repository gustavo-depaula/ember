# Tyndall

THe grace of our lorde, the lyght of his spyryt, to se and to iudg[…] trew repentaunce toward goddes law, a fast fayth in the mercyfull ꝓmises that are in our sauyour Cryste, feruent loue toward thy neybour after then sample of Cryste and hys sayntes be with the (O reder) and with all that loue the trouthe, and l[…]nge for the redempcion of goddys elect. Amen.
