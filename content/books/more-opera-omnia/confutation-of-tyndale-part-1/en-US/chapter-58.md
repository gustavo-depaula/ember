# Tyndale

And yf he be commaunded to absteyne from wyne, that wyll he obserue vnt[…] the deth to / as charterhouse munkes had leuer dye then eate fleshe. And as for the sobernes and chastysynge of the members wyll he not loke for / but wyll powre in ale and beare of the strongeste wythout measure, and heate them with spyces and so forth.
