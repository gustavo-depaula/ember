# Tyndale

Wherfore beloued reader in as mych as the holy gooste rebuketh the worlde for lacke of iudgement / and in as mych also as theyr ignoraunce is wythoute excuse / before whose faces inough is set to iudge by, yf they wolde open theyr yies to se, and not captyuate theyr vnderstandynge to beleue lyes / and in as mych […]s the spyrytuall iudgeth all thynge euen the very botome of goddes secretes, that is to say the causes of the thynges whych god commaundeth: how mych more ought we to iudge our holy fathers secretes, and not to be as an oxe or an asse wythout vnderstandynge.
