# More

Iudge good crysten reader whyther yt be possyble that he be any better then a beste / oute of whose brutyshe bestely mouth, cōmeth such a fylthye fome of blasphemyes agaynst crystes holy ceremonyes and blessed sacramentes, sent in to his chyrche out of his owne blessed bloody syde. And for bycause ye fynde this felowe so frantike and so false in the ray lynge and iestynge agaynste the sacramentes of Cryste: ye may well iudge that who so can delyte or be cōtent with his blasphemouse rybauldy, hath great cause in hym self to fere that his crysten fayth begynneth to fayle and faynte.
