# Tyndale

Wherfore yt is tyme to a wake and se euery man wyth his owne eyes and to iudge, yf we wyll not be iudged of Cryste when he cometh to iudge. And remember that he whych is warned hath none excuse, yf he take no hede. Here wyth fare well in the lorde Jesu Cryste, whose spyryte be thy gyde and doctryne thy lygth to iudge wyth all. Amen.
