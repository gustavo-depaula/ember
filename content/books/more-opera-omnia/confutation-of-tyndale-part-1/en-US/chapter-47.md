# Tyndale

The sacrament of Crystes body after this wyse preche they: [^1] Thou muste byleue that it is no more brede but the very body of Cryste, flesshe, blood, and bone, euen as he went here on erthe, saue his cote. For that is here yet I wote not in how many places.
