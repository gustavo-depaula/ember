# More

Here ye se that the sacrament of penauns he setteth at lesse then nowght / for he sayth it is but a thynge forged and contryued to deceyue vs with. But euery good crysten man knoweth that such folke as he is, that agaynst the sacramēt of penauns contryue and forge suche false heresyes / sore deceyue them selfe, and all them whom the deuyll blyndeth to byleue them. Now whē he hath spokē his pleasure of the thynge: ye shall here what he sayth of the partes.

Of confessyon.
