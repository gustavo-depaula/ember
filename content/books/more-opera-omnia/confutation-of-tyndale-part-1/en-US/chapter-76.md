# Tyndale

Iudge whyther yt be possible that any good sholde come oute of theyr domme ceremonyes and sacramentes in to thy soule. Iudge theyr penounce, pysgrymages, pardones, purgatorye, praynge to postes, domme blessynges, domme absolucyons, theyr domme paterynge and halowynge, theyr domme straunge holy gestures, wyth all theyr domme disgysynges, theyr satisfaccyons and iustefyenge. And because thou syndest them false in so many thynges / truste them in nothynge, but i[…]dge them in all thinges.
