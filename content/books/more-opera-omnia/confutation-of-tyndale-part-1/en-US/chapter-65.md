# More

Thys is another ferdell full of lyes, and that he woteth well inough. For yf the preste lefte of hys stole / folke wolde then saye he dyd lewdely as they myght well saye in dede. But Tyndale knoweth full well that folke do not reken the sacramēt hurted therby. For he knoweth well that chyldren be somtyme crystened of the mydwyfes hande / and ye people knoweth well that she vseth no stole. And thus euery body well perceyueth how shameles Tyndale is in these lyes.
