# Tyndale

And in ceremonyes and sacramentes there be c[…]pt yuateth his wyt and vnderstandyng[…] to obey holy chyrch, wythout askynge what they meane or desyrynge to knowe / but onely careth for the kepynge, and loketh euer wyth a payre of narow eyes and wyth all his spestacles vppon theym leste ought be lefte oute.
