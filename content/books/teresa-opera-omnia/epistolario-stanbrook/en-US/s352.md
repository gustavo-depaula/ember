# Letter (number unidentified). To Dona Juana Dantisco, Mother of Father Gracian, Palencia, towards the beginning of 1581

*News about the two angels, her daughters at Valladolid.*

... IT received a letter yesterday from Valladolid. Our sister Maria de San José is very well, contented and happy. They give me news about my Isabel de Jesus which makes me thank our Lord, as you should, for you have two angels there continually praying for you...

CCCLVIII
