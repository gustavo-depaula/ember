# Letter (number unidentified). To An Unknown Person, Malagon, towards the end of 1579

*Concernmg the Reform.*

. . . Your Honour need not have paid so much attention to my words, for I know little about lawsuits and wish that all may be at peace. But I believe that if this suit is permitted, it will provoke further animosity, and it is enough that the Count de Tendilla should be of the same opinion. . . .
