# Letter (number unidentified). To the Father Master Domingo Bañez, Valladolid, Salamanca, December 3, 1573

*Remember me most kindly to Señora Doña Maria' when you see her, for it is a long time since I wrote to her. It is a great mercy that her health should be better in such bitterly cold weather. I believe that to-day is December 3.*

Your Reverence's daughter and servant,

Teresa de Jesus.
