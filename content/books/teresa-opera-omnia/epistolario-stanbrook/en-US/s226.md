# Letter (number unidentified). To Mother Ana de San Alberto Prioress of Caravaca, Avila, April 30, 1578

*Permission to profess three novices.*

IN virtue of the authority deputed to me by the Father Visitor Provincial, the Maestro Jeronimo Gracian de la Madre de Dios, I grant permission to the Mother Prioress of Caravaca, Ana de San Alberto, to admit to their profession Sisters Florencia de los Angeles, Ines de San Alberto, and Francisca de la Madre de Dios, and I give leave to the sisters to pronounce their vows. God grant it may be for His honour and glory, and may He make them worthy daughters of the Virgin, our Lady and our Patroness! Amen.

Written at St. Joseph's, Avila, April 30, 1578. Teresa de Jesus, Carmelite.
