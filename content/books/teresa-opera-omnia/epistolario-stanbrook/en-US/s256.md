*COMMUNITY AT VEAS*

My daughters, imitate me with perfect frankness, for I assure you that you can look on him as standing in my place; you will be greatly pleased at the result for he is extremely spiritual, experienced, and learned. Those who were guided by him here miss him greatly. You should thank God for having brought him to your neighbourhood.
