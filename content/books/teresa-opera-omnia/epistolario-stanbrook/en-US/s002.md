# Letter (number unidentified). To Señor Venegrillo, Avila Date uncertain

*Receipt of corn and its payment.*

SENOR Venegrillo. . . amos Garcia has brought the ten bushels of wheat. Will you kindly pay him for it as I have not the money. Señor Martin de Guzman will be obliged by your doing so and will repay you, which is the manner in which we usually settle matters of business. Given on August 12.

Doña Teresa de Ahumada. Be good enough to send me some small pigeons.
