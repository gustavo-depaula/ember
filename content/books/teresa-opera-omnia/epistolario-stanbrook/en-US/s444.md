# Letter (number unidentified). To Senor Alonso Venigrilla, Convent of the Incarnation, Avila, January 10, 1546

*Begs him to take care of the doves in her dove-cote.*

SENOR Alonso Venigrilla. Be kind enough to feed the doves and take good care of them during these cold months, as the cote’ is well filled, so that we may profit by it this year. Will you ask Senior Martin de Guzman for the tares and all that is required, which he will be very willing to give ou.
