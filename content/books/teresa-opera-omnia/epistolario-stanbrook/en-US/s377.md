# Letter (number unidentified). To Father Gracian, Valladolid, Palencia, May 29, 1591

*That the nuns were to enter their convent next day,*

JESUS!

May the grace of the Holy Spirit be with your Paternity, my Father.

| AM tired and it is late at night, so I will only say that the Bishop called here yesterday, and to-day has arranged that the procession shall be made to-morrow, which is no small] concession. It is to take place in the afternoon with all possible solemnity. We shall go from here to San Lazaro. The canons will not celebrate the Octave there, but will only take the Blessed Sacrament to our begs Father Gracian to take care of his health and moderate his penances. (B. Z.)
