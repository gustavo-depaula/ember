# Letter 48. To Don Pedro de la Vanda, Salamanca, October 6, 1573

*Further difficulties now that the community is installed in his house.*

JESUS!

ALL that you noted in your memorandum is being done. Every one tells me that I am not under any such obligations until the faculties are received; however the faól of having entered the house is a strong reason for complying with your requests — God grant I may content you!

May our Lord grant you peace so that you may serve Him better and mav He ever have vou in His keeping!

To-day is October 6.
