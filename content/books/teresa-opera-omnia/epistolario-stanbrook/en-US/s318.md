# Letter (number unidentified). To Mother Mary of S. Joseph, "See letter of Feb. 9, 1580

Our Mother and I arrived here on the eve of Palm Sunday. Mother Brianda was so ill from excessive hemorrhage that they wished to give her Extreme Undétion. She is better now, though she still spits blood from time to time and is never without fever. She is able to get up occasionally. Fancy what it would have been had she gone to Malagon! It would have been fatal both to her and the house, or at least a heavy burden, considering their great need of money.

Our Mother has withdrawn two more nuns from there, and God grant that may suthce. Will your Reverence pray for that and for me (as I am in great want of it), also for the election of the General, that the choice may render God great
