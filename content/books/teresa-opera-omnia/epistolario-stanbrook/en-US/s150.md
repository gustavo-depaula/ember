# Letter 141. To Father Gracian, Near Seville, Toledo, end of November or early in December, 1576

*Fray Antonio s jealousy.*

... I am glad you are not with Fray Antonio. They say he is annoyed at finding that I write often to you and rarely to him. O Jesus! What a great thing it is when one soul understands another! This Father finds fault and loses the peace. . .
