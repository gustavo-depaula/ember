# Letter (number unidentified). FRAGMENT OF A LETTER TO DON ROQUE DE HUERTA, Avila, August, 14-15, 1578

*FRAGMENT OF A LETTER TO DON ROQUE DE HUERTA*

Cautio7i to he shown ahout entrusting the affairs of the Reform entirely to the Nuncio.

... I believe that you are to speak to-day to the king who arrived at the Escorial yesterday. Let the greatest care be taken that what is entrusted to the Nuncio is made sure of, for I see that many things are treated more as a matter of custom than as a right. This is what must be insisted on as regards the province. . .

. . . brother. I beg your Honour to dired: that it should be given into his own hands. . .
