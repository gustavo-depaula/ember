# Letter 88. To Father Gracian in Castile, Seville, December, 1575

*Postcript to the preceding letter.*

... In my opinion it would be better to choose any religious from the community as prioress than to into relations with St. Teresa. He had the gift of discernment of spirits. The Saint addressed Relations vii and viii to him and always spoke of him with the highest respect. He died in 1587. {CEuz'res I i, 276).
