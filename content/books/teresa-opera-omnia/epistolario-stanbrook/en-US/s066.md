# Letter 63. To Don Teutonic de Braganza, Salamanca, Segovia, September 15, 1574

*Project of founding a priory of Discalced friars at Salamanca. Various items.*

JESUS!

May the grace of the Holy Spirit ever be with your Lordship!

I WAS very glad to hear that your health is good: God grant it may continue to improve! May He reward me now for all the prayers addressed to Him and may He make me as well as you are, for I need it greatly, considering the long journeys in prospedt for me.
