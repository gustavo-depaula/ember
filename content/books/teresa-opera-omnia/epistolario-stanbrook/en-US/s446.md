# Letter 75. To Dona Ines Nieto, Seville, June 19, 1575

*Thanks for thé gift of a statue. JESUS! May the grace of the Holy Spirit ever be with your Honour.*

Will you tell Sefior Albornoz that, being so far off, I do not know whether he has taken any further steps in the matter about which you wrote to me at Valladolid? I am well, glory be to God, and am prospering in the place to which obedience has brought me. I heartily wish you good health and progress in the right path in which you have begun to serve our Lord. God grant you may advance far, and may He remove you from the turmoil of the court,‘ though it does no harm to one who truly loves Him.

Written on June rg, in the house of the glorious San José, Seville.

Your Honour’s unworthy servant, Teresa de Jesus, Carmelite.
