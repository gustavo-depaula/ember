# Letter 315. To Dona Isabel Osorio, Madrid, Toledo, April 8, 1580

*Project of the foundation at Madrid. Inés de la*

Encarnacion.

JESUS! May the grace of the Holy Spirit be with you, my Sefiora.

| REACHED Toledo on the eve of Palm Sunday, _ and in spite of the journey of thirty leagues, I was not tired but felt better than usual. My health has been bad since I came here, but I believe it will improve. I was very glad when they told me here that you

The unworthy servant of your Honour, Teresa de Jesus.

Father Prior’ keeps in good health; I gave him your message. You owe much to him. I beg you to obtain an answer to the enclosed letter and to forward it to me safely, as it is important.

To-day is April 8.
