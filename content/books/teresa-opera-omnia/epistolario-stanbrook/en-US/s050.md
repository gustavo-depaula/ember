# Letter (number unidentified). To Father Martin Guttierez, Rector of the Society at Salamanca', Salamanca ? 1573

*Mortification and obedience.*

. . . An exceedingly holy woman has appeared in our days, very much given to corporal penance . . . I envy her all her virtues but one thing I do not envy — her refusal to modify her penances although her contessor told her that they were excessive. . .
