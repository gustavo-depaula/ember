# Letter (number unidentified). To Father Gracian, Date uncertain. 1577 

*Prophecy of the triumph of the Reform over its enemies.*

.... I saw a violent tempest of trials. As the children of Israel were persecuted by the Egyptians, so shall we be persecuted, but God will enable us to pass through the sea dry-shod, and our opponents will be swallowed up by its waves. . . .
