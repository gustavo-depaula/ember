# Course 4, Chapter 34: Christianity visible. — Last fifteen days of Lent

**1.** To what are the last fifteen days of Lent consecrated?

*They are consecrated to honor the passion of our Lord; for this reason the church in the Gospel of each day rehearses the benefits and the most striking miracles of the Saviour, and the injustice of the Jews who sought to put him to death.*

**2.** What more does the church do?

*On the Friday before the passion she celebrates the feast of the seven dolors of the Blessed Virgin, whose heart was pierced with sorrow on seeing the treatment which her divine son received.*

**4.** How was the holy week formerly spent?

*Formerly holy week and Easter week were a continual feast. The tribunals of justice were closed, business suspended and prisoners set at liberty, debtors had their debts paid for them, enemies were reconciled, each one endeavored to renew in himself the true spirit of his duties and of the gospel.*

**5.** How should we spend it V
