**1.** Did David remain always faithful to the Lord?

*David did not remain always faithful to the Lord — he committed two great sins.*

**2.** Did David remain long estranged from God?

*David remained in his sins, estranged from God, about one year, so great is the darkness which sin spreads over even the most holy souls. God, however, had pity on him, and sent the prophet Nathan to open his eyes to the miserable state in which he was living. David acknowledged his fault, and bewailed it the rest of his davs.*

**3.** Did the Lord pardon David?

*The Lord pardoned David; but he sent him great afflictions in order to satisfy his justice. Absalom, his son, revolted against him. David was obliged to rty, and to leave Jerusalem weeping and on foot. Absalom was killed in battle, and was bitterly bewailed by David, who returned to Jerusalem. David afterwards lived many years and died full of days and merit.*
