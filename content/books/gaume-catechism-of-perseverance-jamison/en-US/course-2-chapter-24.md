# Course 2, Chapter 24: Of union with our Lord, the new Adam, by charity. Hope and Charity

**1.** Is it sufficient to unite ourselves to our Lord b} faith, in order to be saved?

*No, it is not sufficient to unite ourselves to our Lord by faith, in order to be saved; for by faith alone our union with the new Adam is not complete.*

**2.** What is the second means by which we are to unite ourselves to our Lord?

*The second means is charity; that is, we must unite our will to the will of our Lord,, by obeying all his commandments; but in order to unite faith with charity, we must make use of hope.*

**3.** What is hope?

*Hope is a gift of God; a supernatural virtue by which wn expect, with confidence, all that God has promised us.*

**4.** What is the foundation of our hope?

*The foundation of our hope, is: 1st, the power, fidelity, and goodness of God; 2d, the infinite merits of our Lord.*

**5.** What are the objects of hope?

*The objects of our hope, are: first, God himself, since he has promised to give us himself for all eternity; secondly, all those spiritual and temporal means necessary to gain heaven; in a word, the grace of God in this world and his glory in the next, are the objects of Christian hope.*

**6.** What are the sins opposed to hope?

*The sins opposed to hope are presumption and despair. We sin by presumption, when we flatter ourselves that we can reach heaven without using the necessary means; for instance, without observing faithfully all the commandments of God and the church.*

**7.** How do we sin by despair?

*We sin by despair when we think our faults too great for pardon; our passions too strong to be overcome; and lastly, when we suffer ourselves to indulge in too great anxiety for the necessaries of life.*

**8.** What is charity?

*Charity is a gift of God; a supernatural virtue by which we love God above all things, because he is infinitely amiable; and our neighbor as ourselves, for the love of God. It is by charity that our heart is united to that of the new Adam, and is freed from concupiscence.*

**9.** What are the three virtues, faith, hope and charity, called?

*They are called the three theological virtues; because they have God for their object. They are opposed to the three great passions of our heart; faith*

**10.** What is the principal object of charity?

*The first and principal object of charity is, God; the second, is the love of our neighbor. In presenting to our heart this two-fold nourishment, our Lord elevates and disengages it from those gross affections with which it was filled after the fall of the first Adam.*

**11.** How must we love God?

*We must love God above all things; since he i» infinitely amiable, it is just that we love him above all things, and refer all our affections to him.*

**12.** What are the motives for which we must love God?

*The motives for loving God are: 1st, his infinite perfections; 2d, his benefits; 3d, his promises; 4th, his commands.*

**13.** What should be the qualities of our love for God?

*We should love God as he loves us; with a generous, constant, and holy or supernatural love.*
