**4.** What is the history of St. Cyr and St. Julitta?

*One of the first victims of the persecution of Diocletian, was St. Julitta with her son St. Cyr. St. Julitta was of the city of Iconium, and of the royal race. She fled to the city of Tarsus in Cilicia, with two servants and her son St. Cyr, at that time about three years of age.*

**5.** What happened to her?

*The governor, named Alexander, caused her to be apprehended and beaten severely with the sinews of an ox; at the same time he took her son in his arms and tried to caress him, but the young martyr scratched, his face with his little hands, and as often as St. Julitta said, " I am a Christian," he replied, I am a Christian.*

**6.** What did the judge do?

*The barbarous judge threw down from the tribunal the innocent victim, whose head was broken by th« fall, and who died bathed in his own blood. St. Julitta thanked God for the victory granted her son, and was herself beheaded.*
