# Course 4, Chapter 1: Christianity visible. — Necessity and advantages of exterior worship

**1.** What is worship?

*Worship is the aggregate testimony of the respect, submission, love and confidence which we havo towards God.*

**2.** How many kinds of worship are there? '

*There are two kinds of worship; interior worship, which consists in the sentiments of faith, hope, adoration, and love, which we owe to God; and exterior worship, which is the manifestation of these sentiments?*

**3.** What are ceremonies?

*Ceremonies are exterior and mysterious actions, established to accompany exterior worship, and to render it more august, expressive and sublime.*

**4.** What is a rite?

*A rite is a ceremony performed according to the order prescribed by the church. Hence we say Roman rite or Parisian rite, to designate the ceremonies as performed at Rome or at Paris.*

**5.** What is the liturgy?

*The liturgy is the aggregate ceremonial employed in the divine service. The word liturgy sig nifies the action by excellence; because the divine service is the most noble action we can perform, since it unites or connects us with God.*

**6.** Why is exterior worship necessary?

*Exterior worship is absolutely necessary: 1st, because man owes to God the homage of both soul and body; the soul honors God by interior worship, and the body by exterior worship; 2d, because man, not*

*being a pure spirit, needs the aid of sensible things, by which to raise himself up to spiritual things.*

**7.** What is the first advantage of exterior worship?

*The first advantage of exterior worship is to remind man, unceasingly, of those truths which it most behooves him to know, love and practise.*

**8.** How do you explain this?

*Under the patriarchs, exterior worship reminded man of the creation of the world, the unity of God, his providence, and a future life. Under the law of Moses, man was reminded that God was not only the master of all nature, but the arbiter of nations; and that he rewards or punishes infallibly, according to our works.*

**9.** Of what truths are we reminded by exterior worship under the gospel?

*Under the gospel, exterior worship reminds us of all the great truths revealed to the patriarchs and to Moses; it reminds us of all the mysteries of our Lord, and of all the duties which we are bound to fulfil to* wards God, our neighbor, and ourselves.*
