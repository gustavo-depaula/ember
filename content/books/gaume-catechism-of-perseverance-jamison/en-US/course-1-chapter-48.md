**3.** What is the history of Aman?

*Aman was the chief favorite of Assuerus, king of Persia. His pride was such that he wished every one to bend the knee in adoration to him whenever he passed. Mardochai, a Jew by birth, refused compliance, because his conscience forbade him to render to man a homage due to God alone. Aman resolved to avenge himself and to destroy the whole Jewish people.*

**4.** Who saved them?

*Esther, the wife of Assuerus, saved them. Shewas the niece of Mardochai; but Aman was not aware of this. She invited the king to come with Aman to a banquet which she had prepared. At the close of the banquet, the king demanded of the queen what it was she desired of him. Esther asked of him to come again the next day with Aman to her banquet.*
