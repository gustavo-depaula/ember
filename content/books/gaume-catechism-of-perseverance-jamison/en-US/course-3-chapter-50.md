A. Impiety made conquests, especially in France. To compensate the church, missionaries converted a great many persons in China; among whom was a branch of the imperial family, which, during the persecution, displayed the courage of the first Christians.

**6.** What were the other conquests of the faith?

*The other conquests of the faith were the conversion and civilization of many savage nations in America, and particularly of the Illinois.*

**7.** What was the character of the savages before their conversion?

*The character of these savages before their conversion was of the most revolting barbarity. They ate their prisoners, whom they roasted at a slow fire, after having pulled off their nails and cut off their fingers and ears. After their conversion they became gentle, hospitable and very pious.*
