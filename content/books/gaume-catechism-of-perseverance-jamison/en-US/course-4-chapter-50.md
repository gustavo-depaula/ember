**2.** What feast has the church established to honor the holy angels?

*She has established two in particular; the feast of St. Michael, and that of the Guardian Angels.*

**3.** . On what occasion was the feast of St. Michael established V

*St. Michael, chief of the heavenly host, appeared on Mount Gargan in Italy, in 493; and on this occasion a special feast was established to honor him and all the jjood angels.*

**4.** . What veneration or honor do we render to the angels?

*The honor we render them is an inferior honor, which refers to God. We honor them as the ministers of God, our intercessors, and our friends.*

**5.** What other feast has the church established in honor of the holy angels V

*It is the feast of the Guardian Angels; it was established in the seventeenth century.*

**6.** With what sentiments ought this feast to inspire us?

*With great gratitude to God and a high esteem for our soul, which will make us respect ourselves, respect others, and never scandalize them.*

**7.** What do we owe to our Angel Guardian?

*Three things: 1st, respect on account of his presence; *2d, love, on account of his goodness to us; 8d, confidence, on account of his powerful protection.*
