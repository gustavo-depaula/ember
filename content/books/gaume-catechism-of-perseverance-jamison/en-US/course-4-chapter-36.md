# Course 4, Chapter 36: Christianity visible. — Good Friday

**1.** What is Good Friday?

*Good Friday is the day on which the church honors the death of our Lord Jesus Christ.*

**2.** How was Good Friday formerly celebrated?

*Formerly it was celebrated by spending the whole night in the church in prayer, and every person fasting, except children under seven years of a<;e.*

**3.** Of how many parts is the office of Good Friday composed?

*The office of Good Friday is composed of three parts.*

**4.** What is the first?

*The first part contains two lessons, one from*

*Exodus, in which Moses describes the ceremony of the*

*Paschal Lamb, the figure of our Lord; the other from*

*Isaias, where this prophet shows that Jesus Christ is*

*the true Paschal Lamb, and foretells his sufferings. These lessons are followed by the reading of the passion according to St John.*

**5.** What is the second?

*The second part are the solemn prayers which the church says for the whole world, even for her great est enemies. Before each prayer a genuflection is made, except before the prayer made for the Jews.*

**6.** What is the third part?

*The third part is the solemn veneration of the cross, which represents to us the Saviour ascending Mount Calvary.*

*We ought to repair to the church about three o'clock, because our Lord died at that hour, and whilst there we would do well to meditate on the seven words spoken by him on the cross.*
