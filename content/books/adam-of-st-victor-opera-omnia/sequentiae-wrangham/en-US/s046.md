# Sequence 46. Annunciation of the Blessed Virgin

March 25TH.  
THE bridesman thus salutes a maid on earth,  
And tells the order of a wondrous birth :  
" Lo ! " saith he, " thou, blessed one !

" Shalt conceive and bear a son,  
" Nor shalt know thy virgin zone  
" Hath been broken ! "  
Forewarned by the grace of God  
Now, but doubtful of the mode,  
She, since naught she understood,  
Asks a token.

Fear thou not, O blessed Mary !  
Show thy faith, for faith can carry  
Thee through this effectually.

Care and doubt, O Mary ! still thou,  
Since a mother by God's will thou, 1 5  
With no man allied, shalt be.

In a virgin's womb, her Son,  
Thus the Word incarnate lay ;  
But the nature of the one  
Took the other's not away.

O thing most strange and blest !  
Love most wonderful indeed !

The Godhead, close compressed,  
Lieth in a manger-bed !

O Child exceeding wise !  
O Word that whines and cries !

O majestic lowliness !  
Both help us and direct.

And, as the Word, protect,  
Like us in our fleshly dress !

Mary, who thus God conceivest.  
And the sinner's hopes revivest !

After God to hope thou givest  
Strength and confidence well-tried.

Jesu, mighty and endearing !  
Jesu ! at our head appearing,  
Grant we may Thy lot be sharing  
By Thy side.

Through Thy mother glorified ! Amen.
