# Sequence 91. Of the Blessed Virgin

At Easter-tide.  
REFA CE. Let all Christians sound the praise  
of the blessed Virgin Mary.

1. Of a son poor Eve deprived — man, but Mary One  
conceived, — Who redemption purchased for  
all sinners.

Death and life together met, — make a music  
wondrous sweet ; — the Son that Mary bore  
is King e'ermore.

2. " Mary ! now be telling, — O Maid in love excel-  
ling ! — how thou a mother didst become, —  
though made by Him sprung from thine  
own womb ? "  
"Angel-proof was given, — sent down to me  
from heaven, — from my womb my hope  
proceeded — but incredulous Jews never  
heeded."

Rather mighty Gabriel's word alone should we  
believe in, — than Jewish legions, base and  
deceiving.

Christ, we know, proceeded from the Virgin Mary  
truly; — O Son and King! have mercy on  
us throughly ! Amen.
