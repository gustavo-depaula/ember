# Sequence 95. Of the Blessed Virgin

THIS bright day, so celebrated,  
Is the fame perpetuated  
Of the mother of our God :  
Of the ever-virgin Mary  
Praise, laudation, let us carry  
In our song this day abroad.

All mankind ! towards her turning  
Night and morning, pray with yearning  
That she will protect our race :  
Sing, with all your hearts' might sing ye,  
As your lips' word-offering bring ye,  
" Hail to thee, full filled with grace ! "  
" Hail to thee, O Queen of heaven !

Ne'er in human wedlock given ;  
Nursing-mother, like to none !

Daughter ! though with child to no man,  
Thou hast wondrously, as woman,  
Borne thy Father as thy Son.

*' Garden fair, where south wind bloweth !  
Portal, closed, through which none goeth  
Ere or since ; path man ne'er trod !

Soil bedewed with dew from heaven !  
Gideon's fleece, to which was given  
Moisture from the rain of God !

" Hail, the firmament's bright glory !  
On our dark minds, we implore thee,  
From above thy rays pour down :  
Calm the sea, thou Star of ocean !

Lest amidst the storm's commotion  
And wild adverse winds we drown ! "  
" Amen," say ye every one !
