# Sequence 80. St., Denis

October qth.  
GREECE ! rejoice thou in thy son !

Let all France's pride be shown  
In St. Denis, her own sire !

And let Paris too, become  
Famed through his blest martyrdom,  
Show a joy yet more entire !

Specially with heart and voice  
Doth the happy Church rejoice  
Over all her martyr-bands ;  
Those whose patronage hath made  
Far and wide the country glad,  
And through whom the kingdom stands.

Near this father placed, a band  
Of illustrious warriors stand,  
Worthy of all memory ;  
But, O royal Church ! of all  
Dost thou constantly recall  
This one more especially.

By the Sovereign Pope to France  
Sent forth, he no fear displays  
At the reckless violence  
Of its unbelieving race  
The apostle set o'er Gaul  
To Lutetia came, a town  
Which the crafty foe of all  
Held in thraldom, as his own.

Here 'mid errors' loathsome mass  
Lay heaped all impurity,  
Here a wretched populace  
Gloried in idolatry.

Mercury's image they adored,  
A profane and lying god ;  
But St. Denis' faith assured  
Soon the devil's work downtrod.

He, while building there God's temple,  
Taught by word and by example.

Bright with miracles' bright fame.  
Truth is heeded,  
Errors ceded ;  
As faith groweth,  
Glory gloweth  
Round so great a bishop's name.

When the fierce Domitian heareth,  
He beside himself appeareth,  
And Sisinnius sendeth down,  
Who to torture forthwith leadeth  
This good shepherd, who souls feedeth,  
For his faith, life, signs, well known.

Scourges, chains, incarceration.  
Feels the old man in his passion ;  
O'er iron bed and fierce flames' heat  
And rack his victory is complete.

Fierce wild beasts by prayer he tameth,  
Bears his cross, slacks fire that flameth ;  
After the nails and gibbet-tree  
Conducted to a jail is he.

As the old man celebrateth  
Mass, and near a vast crowd waiteth,  
Christ His presence indicateth  
With the heavenly host around.

In his close incarceration  
Christ still gives him consolation,  
And life's bread for sustentation.

With great glory to be crowned.  
Forth to fight the martyr goeth,  
Heedless what the headsman doeth ;  
Till the lictor  
Strikes, and victor  
Makes his victim, as he bled.

Soon the trunk resuscitated,  
Bearing off its head truncated,  
Hither, with that burden weighted,  
Came, by angel-cohorts led.

Let this martyrdom so bright  
Fill our hearts with pure delight ! Amen,
