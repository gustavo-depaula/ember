# Sequence 99. Of the Holy Apostles

LIKE the setting sun in heaven,  
These, most glorious in life's even,  
Introduce the Sun's new birth,  
Yea, the true Sun's rise and setting ;  
Of whose fate, their deaths relating,  
Tell thus all the ends of earth.

Patras by geas' orders,  
And by Nero's Rome's own borders,  
Andrew, Paul and Peter slay :  
In Judaea Herod's action,  
And the Pharisaic faction.

Take the Jameses' lives away.  
John, returning, when commanded,  
Back from Ephesus, ascended  
To Christ's banquet in the skies :  
Matthew Moorish butchers martyr,  
India's hordes Nathanael slaughter,  
Whilst in Scythia Philip dies.

hS de ss, apostolis.  
Thomam Indi, Judam Persae  
Jude and Simon Persia, Thomas  
India murders ; thus, ta'en from us,  
All pierce heaven a different way ;  
To the heaven of heavens thus soar they,  
Where sweet songs to Christ outpour they.

And for life for us men pray. Amen.
