# Sequence 58. St. Victor

In Easter-Tide.  
Preface. Let all Christians sound the praise of the  
blessed martyr Victor !

MAXIMIAN the cruel hath— inflicted death  
upon him, — and thus blessings, will he,  
nill he, won him.

Life and death together fought — in a strife with  
wonder fraught : — Christ's own martyr  
slain — lives to reign.

2. " Say, in thine agony — what, O champion !  
didst thou see ? " "I saw the banner of  
Christ reigning, — His glory too,who soothes  
complaining."

Tortures' sore distresses — with signs are his wit-  
nesses : at whose petitions dead bodies —  
come to life, and — healed are infirmities.

3. Far rather should we believe Christ's Church  
alone — than a race perverse, who have  
evil done.

Thus we know that thou dost reign now ; place  
for us to gain now by thee, great Victor,  
deign thou ! Amen,
