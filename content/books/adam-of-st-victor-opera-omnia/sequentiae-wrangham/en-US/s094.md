# Sequence 94. Of the Blessed Virgin

WASHED with shower  
Through the power  
Of the Christian font displayed,  
All earth raises  
High thy praises,  
Mary, loving mother-maid !

Praises ring we,  
Praises sing we,  
Mother ! in glad song to thee  
Who suppliest  
God most Highest  
From thy breast in infancy.

O God's maiden  
Mother, aiding  
Sinners pardon to obtain,  
Wrath disarming.

True love warming,  
Pour out grace on us again !

Foemen scare thou,  
And those spare thou,  
Who, as servants serving thee.

Have confessed thee,  
Ever blest, the  
Mother of our God to be.

Nurse of heaven !  
Help be given  
By thee to that Christian race,  
Praying, praising.

Ever gazing  
Upward to behold thy face.

So adore Him,  
And implore Him,  
That His flock, not left to die,  
With no other,  
But His mother,  
As its guide, may live on high.

" Son of Heaven !  
To me given  
As mine only Son ! " thus say,  
" Whom I call so,  
Though God also !

Look upon me as I pray !  
" Think Thou wherefore,  
Whom to care for,  
Thou didst fill a virgin's womb !

The one glorious  
And notorious  
Cause was saving man from doom !

" For this reason  
At this season  
Hear and grant Thy people's prayer :  
,'Twas this guiding  
Hope, abiding,  
Made Thy youth my nursing care ;  
" Whilst imploring  
And adoring.

Covering up that God from cold,  
Whom I tended,  
And, tears ended,  
Soothed, when hid in human mould ! "  
O I pray that. Mother ! for love of thee  
He may hear thy prayer for this family ! Amen.
