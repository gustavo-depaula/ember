# Letter CCXXV. TO THE BISHOP OF SOISSONS. Bernard urges him to promote peace

*(A.D. 1143.)*

We have worked hard, but it is a question whether we have made much progress. We have sown much, but reaped little. We want, I must tell you, your help and presence. You will hear from our common friend, the Abbot of S. Denys, why we did not seek your help before in our great strait. But now I appeal to your holy watchfulness to dissemble no more, but to labour for the things which make for peace according to the wisdom given you by God. For you ought not to need entreaty to take such action, since it is evident that by it your ministry is not only greatly honoured, but also that if you neglect it, it is greatly disgraced. I hope to see you at the festival an nounced to be held at S. Denys.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Was this an assembly cf the nobles of the kingdom, or the Dedication Festival which is kept by S. Denys in February on S. Matthias' Day?

[^2]: Joscelyn.
