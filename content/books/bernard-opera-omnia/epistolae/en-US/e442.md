# Letter CDXLII. To a Certain Abbot of the Benedictine Order

*Letter CDXLI in Eales's numbering.*

*He sends to him two young men not sufficiently vigorous for the Cistercian Order, but who. would perhaps be serviceable to him.*

That he may strive after peace and good will. These two young men who have a good will, but whose bodily powers are far from being sufficient to our Order, I have thought well to send on to you: because, if they continue to be such as we have found them during their probation, I have great hope that they will be of use to you. But receive them and put them yourself under probation, so as to ascertain the truth of what I say; nor be in any haste to allow them to make profession, until you have recognized that they will be of service.
