# Letter CCCLXXVIII. To the Same

*(A.D. 1149.)*

*He asks assistance in grain for the Religious of the Diocese of Bourgcs.*[^1]

To my very dear lord St'GER, by the grace of God the venerable Abbot of S. Denys, Brother BERNARD, Abbot of Clairvaux,- wishes health and sends the assurance of his prayers.

We have in the Archdiocese of Bourges some brethren who are in want of bread; they are those from Maison Dieu, and I hear that the crops of corn of the lord King are very abundant, and, because abundant, they are not of great value. Therefore, I entreat you that out of those crops you would order such a supply to be bestowed upon those brethren as you shall see tit. For the King, whenever he was in that district, was accustomed to make some gift to them.

[^1]: Formerly No. 360; the former No. 378 is now No. 347.
