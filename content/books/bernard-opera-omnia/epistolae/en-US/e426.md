# Letter CDXXVI. To a Certain Friend

*Letter CDXXV in Eales's numbering.*

*He commends to him the bearer of this letter.*

I commend to your charity the man who bears this, who is very dear to me on account of his love for religion; he is in need of your interest with the Count. Let him have the benefit, I entreat, in favour of his suit, which he will explain to you, of all my humble interest with you, and of all the interest which your Reverence has with the Count.
