# Letter XLI. To the Same

*(In the same year.)*

*He recommends to him an aged religious.*

I fear that you are troubled by my frequent scribblings, but the law of Christ and the necessity of friends drives me to this opportunity. I entreat you not to send away empty this aged man whom I have recommended to you. He is[^1] aged as you see, and of a good and religious house, as I know. Besides this, I would ask you to be so good as to give him a letter to the King, your uncle,[^2] whom he is going to seek. I would wish that all the servants of God might become, if it were possible, your debtors, so that they may receive you one day into the everlasting habitations in return for the mammon of iniquity which you share with them. Farewell.

[^1]: Casamentum. That is, a property dependent from the house (Casa) of the Lord.

[^2]: Henry I., King of England, uncle of Theobald (Emaldus Vit. Bernard B. ii. sub finem, and Robert du Mont under A.D. 1151), by Adela, daughter of William the Conqueror, sister of Henry I., and mother of Theobald.
