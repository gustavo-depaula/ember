# Letter CDXLV. To a Certain Abbot

*Letter CDXLIV in Eales's numbering.*

*Bernard recommends to him L ., a returning fugitive, whom he begs may be kindly received.*

I entreat you by the mercy of God, and by our mutual affection, to receive kindly Brother L., who is repentant of his error, who promises amendment and is desirous to return; and that in his reception and reconciliation he may be gently and clemently treated, as is usual with other fugitives: and permit him to think that my entreaties and interest with you have done something to better his position.
