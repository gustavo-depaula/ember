# Letter CDXXXV. To the Same

*Letter CDXXXIV in Eales's numbering.*

*He recommends to him the bearer of the letter.*

Few are found faithful, now that charity is growing cold: and since every thing that is dear[^1] and costly is precious, the love and fidelity of the Lord N., who has conducted most faithfully your business among us, ought to be bound to you by the strongest bonds of favour and kindness. Let it be a reason in his favour also, that I love him as a man learned, faithful, and spiritually minded.

[^1]: Carum; there is a play upon the words cams and car it very usual with S. Bernard. (E).
