# Letter CDXXXIII. To the Same, on the Same Subject

*Letter CDXXXII in Eales's numbering.*

In the matter of the Bishop of Troyes and his clergy, I entreat you to be very cautious, that both the Bishop may receive the honour to which he has a right, and the clergy may not be deprived of due justice. Let peace be renewed between them, and a curb imposed upon the ill-disposed, so that for the future they may not be able to rise up against their already aged Pastor.
