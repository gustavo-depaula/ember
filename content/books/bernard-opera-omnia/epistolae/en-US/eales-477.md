# Letter CDLXXVII. To the Count of Blois

To his beloved son in Christ, the Count of Blois, B[ernard], Abbot of Clairvaux, safety and peace.

I commend to your Highness this man, the Presbyter Eberhard, as a person most faithful to me; and to whom you may entrust without hesitation, as to myself, any communication you may wish to make to me: and likewise receive what he may say to you on my behalf.
