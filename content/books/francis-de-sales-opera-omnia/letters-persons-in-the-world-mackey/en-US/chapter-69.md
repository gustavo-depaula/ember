# Letter I. To a Friend

Way to live in peace. If you wish nothing to cross your life, desire not reputation or the glory of the world.

Attach yourself not to human consolations and friendships.

Love not your life, and despise all that may be painful to your natural inclinations.

Support generously the pains of the body and the most violent maladies, with acquiescence in the will of God.

Trouble not yourself about human judgments.

Keep silence about all things, and you shall have interior peace; because, for me and for you there is no other secret to acquire this peace save to suffer, a la rigueur, the judgments of men.
