### INARRATIO
IN LIBRUM PRIMUM ESDRÆ

IN LIBRUM PREDIGIT ALIUS

ENARRATID.



L EX veritatis fuit in ore ejus. Malach. II, 6. Verbum hoc Malachiæ, ad litteram scriptum de Levi filio Jacob patriarchæ, convenienter accipitur de Esdra sacerdote ex semine Levi nato, qui idoneus factus imitator præfati Levi patris sui, in pace et æquitate ambulavit cum Deo, et multos avertit ab impietate. Fuitque lex Moysis, quæ est lex summæ et incommutabilis veritatis, in corde, ore ac opere Esdræ : in corde utique, per assiduam memoriam et amorem, interiorisque hominis directionem secundum legis tenorem, secundum quam cogitationes et affectiones suas direxit; in ore, per aliorum informationem; atque in opere, per sanctam conversationem. Denique ipse Esdras scriba velox legem recupervit, et populum Israel gubernavit : qui populus, defunctis Moyse ac Josue, fuit usque ad tempus Saul gubernatus per judices; deinde usque ad Babylonicam captivitatem, per reges; post modicum vero a captivitate eadem, per sacerdotes, inter quos erant Esdras et Nehemias.

I Esdr. VII, 1-5. Malach. II, 6.

I Esdr. VII, 6. Act. XIII, 20-22.

II Esdr. XII, 26.

The following are the results of the experiments in the laboratory of the University of California, on the effect of the temperature on the growth of the tobacco plant. The experiments were made in the laboratory of the University of California, on the effect of the temperature on the growth of the tobacco plant. The results are as follows:

The growth of the tobacco plant was affected by the temperature. The growth was best at 20 degrees Celsius. The growth was less at 10 degrees Celsius and less at 30 degrees Celsius.
