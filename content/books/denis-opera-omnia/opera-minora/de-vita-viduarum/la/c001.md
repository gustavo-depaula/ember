# Initium

In the book of the history of the English language, the first volume, by Mr. William Stedman, is now in its second edition, and it is being revised. The second volume, by the same author, is also in its second edition. The third volume, by Mr. Joseph Stedman, is about to be published. In the same volume is contained the first volume of the dictionary of the English language, edited by Mr. Joseph Stedman.
