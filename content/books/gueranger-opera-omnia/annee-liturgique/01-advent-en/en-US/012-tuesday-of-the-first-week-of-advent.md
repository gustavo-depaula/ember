And in the last days the mountain of the house of the Lord shall be prepared on the top of mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills : and all nations shall flow unto it. And many people shall go, and say : Come and let us go up to the mountain of the Lora, and to the house of the God of Jacob: and he will teach us his ways : and we will walk in his paths, for the law shall come forth from Sion, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.

How the Church loves to hear and say these grand words of the Prophet: Come let U8 go up to the Mountain of the Lord ! She repeats them in the Lauds of every Feria in Advent ; and her children bless the Lord, who, that we might have no difficulty in finding him, has made himself like to a high mountain; high, indeed, yet can we all ascend it. It is thus, Jesus, that thou callest us all, and that thou approachest towards all, and the greatness and sublimity of thy mysteries are put within the reach of our littleness. We desire to join, without delay, that happy multitude of people which is journeying on towards thee; we are already with them; we are resolved to fix our tent under thy shadow, O Mountain ever blessed ! There shelter us, and let us be out of reach of the noise of the world beneath us. Suffer us to go so far up, that we may lose all sight of that same world's vanities. May we never forget those paths which lead even to the blissful summit, where the mountain, the figure, disappears, and the soul finds herself face to face with Him, whose vision eternally keeps the Angels in rapture, and whose delight is to be with the children of men 71
