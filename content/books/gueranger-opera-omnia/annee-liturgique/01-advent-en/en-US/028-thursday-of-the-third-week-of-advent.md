Happy he whose eyes shall thus contemplate the new-born King in the sweet majesty of his love and his humility ! He shall be so taken with this his beauty, that the earth, with all its magnificence, shall appear as nothing in his eyes. The only thing he will care to look upon, will be upon Him that shall be laid in a manger, and wrapped in swaddling clothes. But, that we may have this happiness of closely contemplating the divine King who is coming to us, that we may merit to enter his court, we must do as the Prophet bids us: we mmtwalk injustice, and speak truth. A PROSE FOR THE TIME OF ADVENT.

{Composed in the Wth century \ and taken from the ancient Roman- French Missals.)

Let us sing together to our God, who created all things ;

By whom all ages were made ;

The firmament, which shines with much light, and the countless stars ;

The sun, the ornament of the world ; the moon, which is the night's beauty ; and all shining things :

The sea, the land, the hills, the plains, and the deep rivers ;

The wide space of the air, through which float birds, and winds, and rain ;

All these obey thee alone, O God, as their Maker and King.

Jubilemus omnes una, Deo nostro qui creavit omnia ;
