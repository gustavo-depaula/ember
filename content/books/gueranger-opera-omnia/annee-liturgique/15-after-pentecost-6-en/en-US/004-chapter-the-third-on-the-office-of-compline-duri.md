And the Seraphim cried one to another, and said : Holy, holy, holy, the Lord God of hosts, all the earth is full of his glory. 2 The train and fringes of God's vesture are the eleot, who are the adornment of the Word, the splendour of the Father. For since the Word has ^espoused our human nature, that nature is his glory, as he is the glory of God. The Bride herself is clothed with the justifications of the Saints ; and when this glittering robe is perfected, the signal will be given for the end of time. This feast announces the ever-growing nearness of the eternal nuptials ; for on it we annually celebrate the progress of the Bride's preparations.

Blessed are they that are called to the marriagesupper of the Lamb ! z Blessed are we all, who have received in Baptism the nuptial robe of holy charity, which entitles us to a seat at the heavenly banquet !
