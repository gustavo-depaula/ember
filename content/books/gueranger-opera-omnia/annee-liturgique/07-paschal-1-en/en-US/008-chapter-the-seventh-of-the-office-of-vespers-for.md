The fifth Psalm, In exitu, speaks of the ancient Pasch, (the exodus from Egypt,) and the prodigies that accompanied and followed it; of the Red Sea, the figure of Baptism ; of the water which issued from the rock in the desert ; and of the abolition of idol-worship. Our Christian Pasch and Pentecost are the fulfilment of all these figures ; they bring a blessing upon all, Jews or Gentiles, who love or fear Christ. In consequence of our sins, we were condemned to go down into hell, where we should never have heard the glad hymns of praise sung to our God in the heavenly Jerusalem : but the Resurrection of Christ has restored us to life, and we sing, to His and.
