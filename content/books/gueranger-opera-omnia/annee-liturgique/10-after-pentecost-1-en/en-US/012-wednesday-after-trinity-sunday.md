It does not admit transferred Feasts : and as to the semi-double Feasts, which may occur on the Calendar, during these eight days, they are merely oommemorated Double Feasts are kept ; hut, no matter what may he the solemnity, a commemoration of Corpus Christi is made on each, at Mass, Lauds, and Vespers.
