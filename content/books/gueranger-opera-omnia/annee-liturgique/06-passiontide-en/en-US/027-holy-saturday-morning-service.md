In the centre of the earth there are four immense regions, into which no one living can ever enter: it it is only by divine revelation that we know of their existenoe. The farthest from us is the hell of the damned, the frightful abode where satan and his angels and the reprobate are suffering eternal torments. The opinion whioh has met most favour from the Church is that these souls suffer no torment ; and that, although they can never enjoy the beatifio vision, yet are they enjoying a natural happiness, and one that is proportionate to their desires.
