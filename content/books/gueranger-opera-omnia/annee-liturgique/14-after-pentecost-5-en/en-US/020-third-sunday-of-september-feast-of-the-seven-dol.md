Neither the Immaculate Conception nor the Assumption will give us a higher idea of Mary's exaltation than the title of oo-redemptress. Her dolours were not necessary for the redemption of the world, but in the oounsels of God they were inseparable from it. They belong to the integrity of the divine plan.
