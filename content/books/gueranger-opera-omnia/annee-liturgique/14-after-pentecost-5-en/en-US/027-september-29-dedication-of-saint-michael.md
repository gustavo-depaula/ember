Although the term synaxk is usually applied only to religious assemblies here on earth, we are informed that in this instanoe it also signifies the gathering of the faithful angels at the cry of their chief, and their union eternally sealed by their victory. 3

Who, then, are these heavenly Powers, whose mysterious combat heads the first page of history ? Their existence is attested by the traditions of all nations as well as by the authority of holy Scripture.
