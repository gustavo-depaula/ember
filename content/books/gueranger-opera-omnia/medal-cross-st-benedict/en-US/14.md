# Chapter XIV. Rite to be used in blessing the medal of St. Benedict

We have seen in the Brief of Pope Benedict XIV. the formula of exorcisms and prayers to be used by the priest, empowered to bless the medals for the indulgences which are granted to them, and which we have just enumerated. This formula was presented to the Holy See by Benno Lobl, Abbot of St. Margaret’s of Prague ; and the sacred Congregation of Indulgences, after having made some few changes in it, approved of it by its Decree dated December 23, 1741. We have thought it would be useful to give this blessing, according to the copy printed at Mount Cassino in the year 1844.
