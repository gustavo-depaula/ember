# Letter 1825. To the same

In your absence, dearest daughter, I have found much relief in speaking of my interior state to Sister de Chastellux. She is a sincere and upright soul, has great illumination, and is filled with the spirit of God. Our sisters of Vannes and those of Semur, knowing that she was deposed, have both elected her, but she can go to only one of these houses, and I beg of the Bishop of Autun to give her his obedience for the house of Semur.
