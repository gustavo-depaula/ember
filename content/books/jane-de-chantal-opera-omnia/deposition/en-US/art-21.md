# Twenty-first point. His behaviour towards the bishop of geneva, whose coadjutor he was

Her answer on the twenty-first point ivas :

Many credible persons have told me that our Blessed Prelate took no steps to obtain his Bulls from the Bishop's palace nor to bring about his consecration during the lifetime of the late Monseigneur de Granier. That good Bishop, however, desired greatly that he should do so, and wished him to accept a part of his revenue, but this our Blessed Founder steadily refused, and left the whole matter to Divine Providence without interfering in it at all. This is true, &c.
