# Lesson 9: On the descent of the Holy Ghost, on the New Law, and the Sign of the Cross

**1.** Ox what day, and after what manner, did the Holy Ghost descend on the apostles?

*On Whitsunday the Holy Ghost descended in tlvt form of tongues of fire, and sat upon every one of them. Acts, ii.*

**2.** What does the Scripture say of those who received the Holy Ghost?

As They were all filled with the Holy Ghost; and they began to speak in divers tongues, the wonderful works of God. Acts, ii.

**3.** Why did Christ send the Holy Ghost?

*To sanctify his church, to comfort his apostles, and to enable them to preach his gospel, or the new law.*

**4.** What do you mean by the new law?

*The law which Christ established on earth.*

**5.** Which was the old law?

*The law given to the Jews.*

**6.** How do you call the followers of the new law?

*Christians.*

**7.** How are we known to be Christians?

*By being baptized, by professing the doctrine of Christ, and by the sign of the cross.*

**8.** How is the sign of the cross made?

*By putting the right hand to the forehead, then under the breast, then to the left and right shoulders; saying, \n the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen*

**9.** Why do you make the sign of the cross?

*.To beg that Jesus Christ, by his cross and passion, may bless and protect me.*

**10.** Should we frequently make the sign of the cross?

*Yes; particularly in all temptations and dangers, and before and after prayer; and always with great attention and devotion.*

**11.** What does the sign of the cross signify?

*It signifies and brings to our minds the principal mysteries of religion.*

**12.** What mysteries of religion does the sign of the cross recall to our minds?

*The blessed Trinity, and the incarnation and death of our Saviour.*

**13.** How does the sign of the cross remind us of the blessed Trinity?

*Because in making the sign of the cross, we invoke the three divine persons; saying, In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and cf the Holy Ghost.*

**14.** How does the sign of the cross 1 ring to our minds the incarnation and death of our Sa\iour?

*Because as he suffered death in human flesh on a cross, the sign of the cross must naturally remind all true Christians of his incarnation and death.*

**15.** Where are true Christians to be found?

*In the true church.*
