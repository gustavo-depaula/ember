# Lesson 5: On our First Parents, fyc

**1.** How came we to be in the power of the devil f

*By the disobedience of our first parents, in eating the forbidden fruit. Gen. ii. and iii.*

**2.** Who were our first parents?

*Adam and Eve, the first man and woman.*

**3.** Why did God command our first parents not to oat the forbidden fruit?

*To make them sensible of his dominion over them, and of their dependence on him, and to try their obedience.*

**4.** Who tempted our first parents to eat the forbidden fruit I

*The Devil, envying their happy state. Gen. iii.*

**5.** Whom do you mean by the Devil?

*One of the rebellious or fallen angels, whom God cast out of heaven.*

**6.** What do you mean by Angels?

*Pure spirits, that is, without a body, created to adore and enjoy God in heaven.*

**7.** Were the Angels created for any other purpose?

*Yes; to assist before the throne of God, and to minister unto him; and they have been often sent as messengers from God to man: and are also appointed our guardians. Apoc. vii. 9. Heb. i. 7. Matt. iv. 6. xviii. 10.*

**8.** Why were any Angels cast out of heaven?

*Because through pride they rebelled against God. Isaias, xiv.*

**9.** Did God punish in any other way the Angels who rebelled?

*Yes; he condemned them to Hell, a place of eternal torments.*

**10.** Why did God make Hell?

*To punish the Devils or bad Angels.*

**11.** Are any other condemned to Hell, beside the Devils or bad Angels?

*Yes; all who die enemies to God; that is, all who die in the state of mortal sin.*

**12.** Can any one come out of Hell?

*No; out of Hell there is no redemption.*

**13.** How did God reward the Angels who remained faithful?

A He confirmed them for ever in glory.
