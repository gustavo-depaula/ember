# Lesson 23: On the Sacraments, and on Baptism

**1.** By what other means, besides prayer, can we obtain the grace of God?

*By the Sacraments, the most powerful of all means,*

**2.** What is a sacrament ]

*A visible, that is, an outward sign or action, instituted by Christ, to give grace.*

**3.** Whence have the Sacraments the power of giving grace?

*From the merits of Christ, which they apply to our souls. Rom. vi. 3. v. 9.*

**4.** Why are so many ceremonies used in the administration of the Sacraments %

*To excite devotion, and reverence to them; and to signify and explain their effects.*

**5.** How many Sacraments are there?

*Seven; Baptism, Confirmation, Eucharist, Peaance, Extreme Unction, Holy Order, and Matrimony. Council of Trent, SS. 7. c. 1.*

**6.** What is Baptism?

*A Sacrament, which cleanses us from original sin, makes us Christians and children of God; and heirs to the kingdom of heaven.*

**7.** Does Baptism also remit the actual sins, commitr ted before it?

*Yes; and all the punishment due to them.*

**8.** Is Baptism necessary to salvation?

*Yes; without it one cannot enter into the kingdom of God. John, iii. 5.*

**9.** Who are appointed by Christ to give Baptism 1

*The pastors of his church; but in case of necessity, any lay man or woman can give it.*

**10.** How is Baptism given?

*By pouring water on the head of the person to be baptized; saying at the same time, I baptize thee, in thi name of the Fatherland of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Matt, xxviii. 19.*

**11.** What did we promise in Baptism?

*To renounce the devil, with all his works and pompa,*
