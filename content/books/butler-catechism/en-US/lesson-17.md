**11.** What is commanded by the third commandment?

*To sanctify the Sunday.*

**12.** Which is the chief duty by which we are commanded to sanctify the Sunday?

*Assisting at the holy sacrifice of the Mass.*

**13.** What other religious exercises are recommended to sanctify the Sunday?

*Attending vespers, reading moral and pious books, and going to communion.*

*The works of mercy, spiritual and corporal; and particularly to instruct the ignorant in the way of salvation, by word and example. Daniel, xii. 3.*

**15.** What is forbidden by the third commandment? A. All unnecessary servile work; and whatever may hinder the due observance of the Lord's day, or tend to profane it.
