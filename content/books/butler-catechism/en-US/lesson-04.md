# Lesson 4: On the Trinity, and Incarnation

**1.** How many persons are there in God? A. Three divine persons, really distinct and equal in all things. 1 St. John, v. 7.

**2.** How do you call the three divine persons? A. The Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost

**3.** Is the Father God?

*Yes; the Father is God. and the first person o£ the blessed Trinity.*

**4.** Is the Son God?

*Yes; the Son is God, and the second person of the blessed Trinity. •*

**5.** Is the Holy Ghost God?

*Yes; the Holy Ghost is God, and the third person of the blessed Trinity.*

**6.** What means the blessed Trinity?

*One God in three divine persons. SL John, v. 7.*

**7.** Are the three divine persons, three Gods?

*No; they are one only God, having but one and the same divine nature; and they are from eternity, i Q. Is any one of the three divine persons more powerful or more wise than the other?*

*No; as the three divine persons are all but one and the same God, they must be alike in all divine per.fections; therefore one cannot be more powerful or more wise than the other.*

**8.** Did one of the three divine persons become man?

*Yes; God the Son, the second divine person, became man. St. John, i. 14.*

**9.** How did God the Son become man?

*He was conceived by the Holy Ghost, and was born of the Virgin Mary. Ap. Cr.*

**10.** What do you mean by saying, that the Son of God was conceived by the Holy Ghost?

*I mean that he assumed human nature, that is, a body and soul like ours, by the power or operation of the Holy Ghost.*

**11.** Where did God the Son take a body and soullike ours?

*In the chaste womb of the Virgin Mary, and lie was born man of her.*

**12.** How do you call God the Son, made man?

*Jesus Christ.*

**13.** What fs the meaning of these words, Jesus C krist?

*Jesus signifies Saviour, and Christ signifies the Anointed; and St. Paul says that, in the name of Jesus every knee should bow. Phil. ii. 10.*

**14.** Did Jesus Christ remain God, when he became man?

*Yes; he was always God.*

**15.** Was Jesus Christ always man?

*Only from the time of his conception, or incarnation.*

**16.** What means the incarnation?

*That God the Son, the second person of the blessed Trinity, was made man. St. John, i. 14.,*

**17.** What do you believe Jesus Christ to be?

*True God and true man.*

**18.** Why did Christ become man?

*To redeem and save us.*

**19.** How did Christ redeem and save us?

*By his sufferings and death on the cross.*

**20.** Was it by his passion and death Christ also satisfied the justice of God for our sins?

*Yes; and delivered us from hell, and from the power of the devil. Col. ii. 14.*
