**1.** What means hallowed be thy name?

*By this we beg, that God's name may be praised and glorified by all his creatures.*

**2.** What means, thy kingdom come?

*By this we beg, that God may reign in our hearts by his grace, in this life; and that we may reign for ever with him, in the next.*

**3.** What means, thy will be done?

*By this we beg, that God would enable us, by hb grace, to do his will in all things on earth, as the angels and saints do it in heaven.*

**4.** What means, give us this day our daily bread?

*By this we beg for all necessaries, for our souls and bodies.*

*No; God will show no mercy to us, unless we forgive from our hearts, our enemies, and all those who have offended or injured us. Matt xviii. 35. vi. 15.*

**7.** What means, lead us not into temptation?

*By this we beg, that God would strengthen us against all temptations. Bom. ix. 14.*

**8.** What means, deliver us from evil?

*B) this we beg, that God would deliver us, in body and soul, from all evil, particularly that of sin.*

**9.** Who made the Hail Mary ]

*The Angel Gabriel and saint Elizabeth made the first part of it; and the church made the last. Luke, i. 28.*

**10.** Is it lawful to honour the Virgin Mary?

*Yes; whereas God himself so much honored her;*

*and the Scripture says, All nations shall call her blessed Luke i. 48.*

**11.** What honour do we give our blessed Lady?

*We honour her more than all the other saints, because she is the mother of God — but we never give her divine or supreme honour, which is due to God alone.*

**12.** Why do Catholics so often repeat the Hail Mary and Holy Mary %

*To honour the mystery of the Incarnation, which that prayer expresses; and to show their great respect and devotion to the mother of God, and their special confidence in her assistance, particularly at the hour of death.*

**13.** And why do you always say the Hail Mary after the Lord's Prayer 1

*That, by her intercession, we may more easily obtain what we ask for in the Lord's Prayer.*
