# Lesson 26: On Communion and Penance

**1.** What do you mean by going to communion?

*Receiving the blessed Eucharist.*

**2.** Is it advisable to go often to communion?

*It is; as nothing can conduce more to a holy life. He that eateth this bread, saijs Christ, shall live for ever. John, vi. 59.*

**3.** How must we be prepared for communion?

*We must be in the state of grace; penetrated M§th a lively faith, animated with a firm hope, and inflamed with an ardent charity. 1 Cor. xi, 28.,*

**4.** What means to be in the state of grace?

*To be free, at least, from the guilt of mortal sin.*

**5.** How are we to be penetrated with a lively faith?

*By firmly believing, that the Blessed Eucharist is Jesus Christ himself, true God and true man, his very flesh and blood, with his soul and divinity.*

**6.** How are we to be animated with a firm hope?

*By having great confidence in the goodness of Christ, who gives himself to us without reserve, in that banquet of love.*

**7.** And how are we to be inflamed with ardent charity?

*By returning love for love to Christ, and by devoting ourselves in earnest to his service, all the days of our lives.*

**8.** Is any thing else required before communion?

A ^s; to be fasting from midnight; and we should

appear very modest and humble, and clean in dreas showing in our whole exterior the greatest devotion and reverence to so holy a sacrament.

**9.** What should we do after communion?

*We should spend some time in meditation and prayer; and particularly in acts of thanksgiving.*

**10.** Is it a great sin to receive it unworthily?

*Yes; Whosoever receives unworthily, shall be guilty of the body and of the blood of the Lord; and eats judyment, (that is, damnation,) to himself not discerning ilie body of the Lord. 1 Cor. xi. 27. 29.*

**11.** What do you mean by receiving unworthily?

*To receive the blessed Eucharist in the state of mortal sin.*

**12.** What should "a person do, if he be in mortal sin, before communion?

*He must obtain pardon in the sacrament of Penance.*

**13.** What is Penance?

*A sacrament, by which the sins are forgiven, which are committed after baptism.*

**14.** By whose power are sins forgiven?

*By the power of God, which Christ left to the pastors of his church.*

**15.** When did Christ leave to the pastors of his church the power of forgiving sins?

*Chiefly when he said to his apostles, Receive ye the Holy Ghost: whose sins you shall forgive, they are forgiven them: and whose sins you shall retain, they are retained. John, xx. 22, 23. '*

**16.** What must we do, to obtain pardon of our sins in the sacrament of penance?

*We must make a good confession.*
