# Lesson 6: On original Sin, <$fc

**1.** How did God punish the disobedience oi oui first parents?

*They were stript of original justice and innocence* driven out of paradise; and condemned to death with their posterity.*

**2.** Did God inflict any other punishments on our first parents?

*Yes; he deprived them of all right to Heaven, and of several other blessings intended for them.*

**3.** What were the chief blessings intended for our first parents?

*A constant state of happiness, if they remained faithful to God.*

**4.** Were we condemned to the same punishments with our first parents?

*Yes; we were all made partakers of their sin and punishments: as we would be all sharers in their innocence and happiness, if they had been obedient to God. Rom. v. 12.*

**5.** How do you call the sin of our first parents 1

*Original sin.*

**6.** What is original sin?

*The sin we inherit from our first parents; and in which we were conceived and born children of wrath. Ephes. ii. 3.*

*Q» Why is it called original sin?*

*A, Because it is transmitted to us from our first parents, and we came into the world infected with it; and because it is the origin and source of avary evil and misery to us. Rom. v. 12.*

**7.** What other particular effects follow from the sin of our first parents?

*It darkened our understanding; weakened our will; and left in us a strong inclination to evil.*

**8.** What is the reason that darkness in our understanding, weakness in our will, and a propensity to evil, still remain, with many other temporal punishments, after original sin is forgiven I

*To serve as an occasion of merit to us; by resisting our corrupt inclinations, and by bearing patiently the suffering of this life.*
