# Lesson 21: On Prayer

**1.** What is prayer?

*An elevation of the soul to God, to adore him, to bless his holy name, to praise his goodness, and to return him thanks for his benefits.*

**2.** Is prayer anything else?

*It is an humble petition to God for all necessaries, for soul and body.*
