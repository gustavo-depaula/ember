Q. Is it for this reason that we read in the holy Scripture, that God is wonderful and ad mirable in his Saints ?*

A. Yes; because by his pow er he elevates their weakness to the practice of sublime vir tues, enlightens their ignorance, and makes his grandeur shine forth in their baseness.

A. Yes; our Lord himself, when upon earth, wished that his Father should be honored in him; he did not wish to re-
