# 48. Mary, our Mother, Reigns on High

O vision bright!  
The land of light  
Beams goldenly beyond the sky!  
'Mid heavenly fires,  
'Bove angel-choirs,  
Mary, our Mother, reigns on high.

O vision bright!  
The Father's might  
All round His daughter's throne doth lie;  
Where, in the balm  
Of endless calm,  
Mary, our Mother, reigns on high.
