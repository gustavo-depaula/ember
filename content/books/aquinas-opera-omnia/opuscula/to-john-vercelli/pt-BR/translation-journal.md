# Translation Journal — Letter on the Manner of Studying (to Brother John) (pt-BR)

Source: la (Epistola ad Ioannem Vercellensem de modo studendi)
Target: pt-BR

## Key Terms

| Latin | Portuguese | Notes |
|-------|------------|-------|
| thesaurus scientiae | tesouro da ciência | |
| locutorium | locutório | monastic parlor/visiting room |
| cellam vinariam | cela do vinho | Echoes Song of Songs 2:4 ("introduxit me in cellam vinariam" — "levou-me à cela do vinho" / "casa do vinho" in some vernacular Vulgate-based Bibles); kept the wordplay with "cela" (monk's cell) → "cela do vinho" to preserve Aquinas's pun |
| armariolo mentis | pequeno cofre da mente | *armariolum* = a small cabinet/strongbox; "cofre" carries the sense of safekeeping a treasure, matching *thesaurus scientiae* in the opening line |
| vinea Domini Sabaoth | vinha do Senhor dos Exércitos | "Sabaoth" (Heb. hosts/armies) rendered as the standard Portuguese liturgical equivalent "Senhor dos Exércitos" rather than transliterated |
| discursus | correrias | "running about" — restlessness/wandering outside the cell, not "discourse" |

## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-04: Translated directly from the Latin (`la/ch001.md`), the canonical source per `book.json`. The existing `en-US/ch001.md` (a standard public-domain translation) was consulted only to cross-check register and idiomatic choices (e.g. "wine-cellar," "Lord of Hosts"), not as the source text.
- 2026-09-04: No footnotes in the Latin source — none added. The single paragraph break in each of the three sections mirrors the Latin exactly (opening address, the sixteen precepts, closing exhortation).
- 2026-09-04: "Tardiloquum" rendered as "tardio em falar" (slow of speech) rather than a single adjective, since Portuguese has no equivalent one-word compound.
