# Translation Journal — On the Articles of Faith and the Sacraments of the Church (en-US)

Source: `la/` (Leonine / Marietti text, via aquinas.cc)
Target: en-US

Part 2 (`g2-*`) is J. B. Collins's translation (*Catechetical Instructions of St. Thomas*, 1939), mirrored from aquinas.cc. Collins did not translate the prologue or Part 1. `g1-pr.md` (prologue and Part 1, the articles of faith and the errors against them) is a new translation from the Latin, made for this corpus in November 2026.

## Translation Decisions

- Scripture is rendered from the Latin wording, in the register of the Douay version, with references in the form the Collins chapters use (`Rom 3:24`). "I Petri ult.", "Luce ult." and the like are given as "the last chapter of 1 Peter / Luke"; citations the text gives only as "in the Psalm" or "Isaiah" stay so.
- "Ro. VIII" for Rom 9:5 (Part 2, Art. 1) is the edition's error and is kept as "Rom 8", as in the pt-BR (see PROBLEMS.md, *Wrong references in the printed edition*).
- Heresy names in their usual English forms: Cataphrygians, Tatianists, Euchites (*Eutychiani*, Augustine's *Euchitae*), Passalorynchites (*Passalonitae*, the Greek gloss on *passalos* and *rhynchos* kept), Tessaradecatitae / Quartodecimans, Aerians, Seleucians, Monothelites.
- *sicut Arabs asserit* (Art. 6, quoting *De ecclesiasticis dogmatibus*): "as the Arabian asserts", literally; the source does not say who is meant.
- The doubled emphasis markup of the source (`*peccatorum* *remissionem*`) is mirrored.
