## Translation Decisions

- Conventions from the sibling Gregory pt-BR works (`not-three-gods`, `baptism-of-christ`, `pilgrimages`): author without "São", straight double quotes (single quotes for the source's nested quotes), divine pronouns lowercase (ele, aquele que), reverential capitalization following the source. Inline Scripture references stay where the source puts them, with Portuguese book names (1 Coríntios, 2 Coríntios, Sabedoria, 1 Samuel).
- Scripture quotations translated from the source's wording, not a standard Bible (e.g. Ps 98:5 as "depois de exaltar o Senhor nosso Deus, mesmo então mal adorais o escabelo debaixo dos seus pés"; Wis 16:14 "um espírito que sai e não volta mais").
- Address: `tu` for the generic "you" in the argument ("Se começas a contar", "podes honrar a Divindade"); `vós` where the source addresses the opponents as a group ("A vossa súplica…").
- English `:—` and `—` openers are rendered with Portuguese punctuation, not transplanted.
- Structure: title heading and 27 paragraphs, unchanged. No footnotes in the source; no translator notes added.

## Source slips (mirrored, not corrected)

- ¶15 (the long creation paragraph) cites "He works all things in all, as He wills 1 Corinthians 13:6": the verse is 1 Corinthians 12:6/11; kept as cited.
- ¶18 "It not a symbol of the Kingship?": a dropped "Is"; rendered as the question "Não é o símbolo da Realeza?". Not edited in en-US.
- ¶10 "what reasons can justify them in taking away the Father when once they have granted Him?": the sense points to the Spirit's equality with the Father; rendered literally ("retirar o Pai, uma vez que o concederam").
- Stray spaces before punctuation ("Scripture ,", "a bucket ,", "glory ,") are not transplanted.

¶N counts body paragraphs after the title heading.

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness paragraph by paragraph (27 body paragraphs aligned one to one), Scripture names and chapter:verse numbers, plus the mechanical audit and `book.json`. Verdict: 1 defect, in this journal; the chapter text is complete and the references match.

Fixed:
- Translation Decisions, address line: quoted form "como podes honrar" does not occur in the chapter → "podes honrar a Divindade" (¶11 "De que maneira, então, podes honrar a Divindade?"). A false quote in the journal.

Considered and rejected:
- ¶10 "The Deity … does not grow in honour; He is evermore the same" → "A Divindade … não cresce em honra; ele é sempre o mesmo". The masculine pronoun follows the source's "He" (God), per the lowercase divine-pronoun convention; not a concord error.
- ¶17 "we do not understand how we can count those who make it among the number of Christians at all" → "… entre o número dos cristãos", without "de modo algum", and no comma before "que não entendemos". The intensifier carries no proposition and the clause parses; style, not omission.
- ¶21 the source's single question ending "… 'It is the Spirit that gives life;' but for the completion of this grace …" is split into a question and a new sentence ("Mas, para a completude…"). Sentence boundary only; no content moved or lost.
- ¶16 "after the unprofessional way usual with us" → "à maneira simples e não profissional". "simples" glosses "unprofessional" (Gregory's self-deprecating "lay" manner); no new claim.
- ¶21 "in which the brute creation has no part" → "da qual a criação irracional não tem parte". "ter parte de" is an accepted regência beside "ter parte em".

### Round 2

Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read of all 27 paragraphs against en-US, plus the mechanical audit and `book.json`. Verdict: 1 defect, fixed; no mistranslation found.

Fixed:
- ¶13 "com Aquele que mostrou tal magnificência de poder" → "com aquele que mostrou…". The only mid-sentence capitalized divine pronoun in the chapter; the file's convention (Translation Decisions) is lowercase.

Considered and rejected:
- ¶18 "belief in the Lordship of the Son arises in those who entertain it, by means of the Holy Ghost" → "surge nos que a nutrem por meio do Espírito Santo". The source's comma is dropped, so "por meio do Espírito Santo" can attach to "nutrem" or to "surge"; both readings say the belief comes through the Spirit.
- ¶26 "when coming to meet his brother and to appease his wrath" → "quando foi ao encontro de seu irmão para aplacar a sua ira". Coordination rendered as purpose; the meeting's aim is the appeasing, no claim added.
- ¶6 "Certainly, if there is … anything that falls short …, they do well" → "se houvesse … fariam bem". Counterfactual mood for a hypothesis the author rejects; same proposition.
- ¶24 "I have both glorified, and will glorify again" → "Já glorifiquei e glorificarei de novo". The "both … and" correlative carries no content.
- ¶19 "Kingship is most assuredly shown in the rule over subjects" → "A realeza mostra-se…". Generic sense; the source's capital is sentence-initial, and the Key Terms row ("Realeza") covers the title sense.

### Round 3

Focus: source-blind cold read of all 27 paragraphs, each mark then verified against en-US; then a mechanical sweep of spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation, punctuation and quote pairing; plus the mechanical audit and `book.json`. Verdict: 1 defect, fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶11 "se toda a humanidade viva elevasse … que acréscimo será esta oferta" → "seria". Broken tense correlation (imperfect subjunctive with future indicative); the source's "were to … will" has no grammatical Portuguese equivalent, and the conditional keeps the sense.

Considered and rejected:
- ¶19 "everything among its kind" → "cada coisa entre os da sua espécie". "os" reads as generic "os [seres]", following "homens" and "anjos"; not a broken reference.
- ¶9 "If you begin to count behind the ages" → "contar para trás dos séculos". "para trás de" means "behind"; literal and parseable. Style.
- ¶25 "how will you worship Him only" → "como o adorarás somente a ele". The pleonastic object is grammatical and carries "only".
- ¶25 'worship God and serve Him only' in single quotes. Mirrors the source; quote style is not normalized (`.claude/rules/books.md`).
- ¶6 "As if any one after describing some one as a man" → "É como se alguém, depois de descrever alguém como homem". Mirrors the source's repetition.

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__gregory-nyssa__holy-spirit/review-3.md` (outside the repo).

### Round 4

Focus: function words (prepositions, articles, demonstratives, connectors, possessive `seu/sua`), the heading, and every place where pt-BR must commit and the source is open (pronoun antecedents, gender, tu/vós), plus the mechanical audit and `book.json`. Verdict: 2 defects, fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶2 "eles o tomam num sentido próprio" → "num sentido todo seu". Source "a sense of their own"; "sentido próprio" is the fixed term for "literal sense", so the Portuguese said they take it literally.
- ¶17 "o vosso herege não é homem vivo" → "o herege não é homem vivo". Source "your heretic" is the English generic "your"; the paragraph has no vós addressee, so "vosso" invented one.

Considered and rejected:
- ¶25 the address shifts from vós ("We would say to them, Your supplication …" → "A vossa súplica … vos aproximais") to tu ("But you, persuaded as you are …" → "Mas tu, persuadido como estás"). "But you" opens a new apostrophe after the idol example, and its addressee is a single objector: ¶26 quotes him in the first person singular ("When I think of the Father" → "Quando penso no Pai"). tu holds through ¶26.
- ¶21 "He alone, received by faith, does not give life" → "ele sozinho". "ele" is the Spirit: the next clause, "but belief in our Lord must precede", rules out the Lord.
- ¶26 "as long as they knew him not" → "enquanto não o reconheceram". "enquanto não" + perfect reads "until they recognized him"; same span.
- ¶3 "the conception of Him that possesses them" → "a concepção que deles se apoderou a seu respeito". The context of ¶2 makes the Spirit the referent of "seu"; style.

### Round 5

Focus: per-paragraph term-frequency diff against Key Terms in both directions, every falsifiable journal claim, and proper names against the corpus, plus the mechanical audit and `book.json`. Verdict: clean; nothing changed.

Considered and rejected:
- ¶27 "to feel pity and favour … to wake that pity" → "sentir piedade e favor … despertar essa piedade", although Key Terms gives "piedade" for "devotion". Portuguese "piedade" covers both senses; this passage is about petitioners moving rulers to compassion, and no reader takes it for devotion. The Key Terms row is right for every "devotion/devout" (¶2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 16, 22).
- ¶25 "the Holy Spirit is the Giver", ¶27 "ask of the Giver" → "Doador", beside the Key Terms row "Bestower → Doador". Same referent (the Spirit) and near-synonymous source words; no ambiguity.
- ¶27 "our attendance upon a Higher Power" → "o nosso serviço a um Poder mais alto", beside "service → serviço". "attendance upon" means service here; the closing "nor to attend upon the Potentate" → "nem servir ao Potentado" belongs to the same idea.
- ¶17 "'bones,' he says" (Ecclesiastes) → "'ossos', diz ela" (a palavra do Eclesiastes). The pronoun follows the clause's subject "a palavra"; the quote is still attributed to Ecclesiastes.
