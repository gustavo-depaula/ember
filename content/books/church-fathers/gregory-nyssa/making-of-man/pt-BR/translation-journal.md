## Translation Decisions

- Conventions from the sibling Gregory pt-BR works (`virginity`, `holy-spirit`, `not-three-gods`): author without "São", straight double quotes with single quotes for nested quotes, divine pronouns lowercase (ele, aquele que), reverential capitalization following the source.
- Scripture references keep the source's numbers with Portuguese book names (Gênesis, Deuteronômio, Provérbios, Amós, Mateus, Lucas, Atos, Romanos, Coríntios, Colossenses, Tessalonicenses, Timóteo, Hebreus, 1 João). A reference inside the closing quote moves after it (`beginning Genesis 1:1”` → `"no princípio" Gênesis 1:1`). The reference standing between sentences in ch. 25.3 ("…never bare. Luke 23:27-29 Where then…") stays in its slot. The edition's stray spaces where New Advent dropped a reference ("as Solomon says ,", "heaven and earth ,") are dropped; no reference is supplied.
- Scripture quotations translated from the source's wording, not from a Portuguese Bible (e.g. Heb 11:13 "embraced them" → "os abraçaram"; "You shall eat all kinds of flesh even as the green herb").
- The edition's "the apostle in what he says to the Ephesians" (ch. 8.5, for 1 Thessalonians 5:23) is mirrored, not corrected.
- Address: Peter in the Introduction and the generic "you" of the treatise → `tu` ("à Tua Reverência", "expor-te", "dirás", "vês"); plural addressees in quotations → `vós`.
- The NPNF "Note" (editor's summary, part of the body text, not a footnote) is kept and translated as "## Nota", following the precedent of `ambrose/concerning-virgins` (introductions incorporated in the body are preserved). The chapter headings, also the editor's, are kept with their italics; ch. 17's heading is roman in the source and stays roman.
- Section numbers written as bold `**N.**` per `.claude/rules/books.md` (the en-US file uses bare `N. ` list markers). Ch. 23's first paragraph is unnumbered in the source and stays unnumbered.
- English `:—` and `—` openers rendered with Portuguese punctuation, not transplanted.
- ch. 13.8 "a smouldering activity" → "uma atividade que arde sem chama", keeping the fire-under-chaff image of the same sentence.
- ch. 17.4 "the failure of their will" → "a falha da vontade dos homens": in pt-BR "deles" would read as the angels, named just before.
- ch. 29.11 "a bush … or some plant of the class of shrubs" → "uma moita … ou alguma planta da classe dos arbustos", to avoid "arbusto … arbustos".
- Structure: title, 2 front-matter headings, 30 chapter headings and 221 paragraphs, one to one and line for line with the source. No footnotes in the source; no translator notes added.

- Rejected — Note [3], source “*potentially* in the Divine will, *actually* at the moment”, target `*potencialmente* na vontade divina, *atualmente* no momento`.
