## Translation Decisions

- Conventions from `gregory-nyssa/pilgrimages` and `infants-early-deaths`: author without "São", straight double quotes, divine pronouns lowercase. Scripture references keep the source's numbers with Portuguese book names (Isaías, Mateus, João, Romanos, Números), as in `ambrose/repentance`.
- Inline references sit where the source puts them, after the closing quote and with the source's stray space before punctuation dropped (`dwell Isaiah 49:20 .”` → `habite." Isaías 49:20`). References the source strips (e.g. "the Galatians )", "the stream ,") leave no trace; the stray space is not transplanted.
- Address: `vós` for the congregation; `tu` for the single objector ("thou who contendest…", "Show me…") and for the hypothetical captive in the ransom illustration. "Add to this also the history" → "Acrescentai" (congregation). The closing prayer addresses God as lowercase `tu`, matching the lowercase divine pronouns.
- Scripture quotations translated from the source's wording, not from a Portuguese Bible (e.g. "My soul is thirsty for the mighty" → "tem sede do poderoso"; Isaiah 35:2 "excellency of Carmel" → "excelência do Carmelo", because Gregory argues from the phrase).
- "this syllable 'how'" → "esta sílaba 'como'" (literal, though Portuguese "como" has two syllables; the point survives).
- Structure: title heading, the subtitle line and ten paragraphs, unchanged. No footnotes in the source; no translator notes added.

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness, paragraph by paragraph (title, subtitle, 10 paragraphs aligned one to one; quotations, parentheticals and references), plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: complete; no dropped or added content; all seven references match. Four defects fixed:

- ¶3: "arrastou seu séquito de serpente" → "arrastou sua cauda de serpente". The source has "drew along his serpent train, a disguise worthy of his own intent". "Train" here is the serpent's trailing body, which the devil puts on as a disguise. "Séquito" means a retinue, and a retinue cannot be a disguise.
- ¶3: "torna-se o modelo e a figura de todos nós" → "o tipo e a figura". The source has "the type and figure of us all". The Key Terms table gives type → tipo, and "modelo" means an exemplar.
- ¶6: "em figura de todos aqueles homens ímpios" → "como tipo de". The source has "for a type of". Key Terms: type → tipo.
- ¶10: "somos feitos participar do canto dos anjos" → "somos feitos partícipes do canto dos anjos". Causative "fazer" plus an infinitive does not take the passive in standard Portuguese.

Rejected:
- ¶1 "Pois delas umas estão em seus trabalhos" ("For of them some are at their labours within"): the feminine refers back to "abelhas", the nearest antecedent. That reading is defensible, because the sentence continues the bee simile.
- ¶3 "os Nomes das Três Pessoas Sagradas em quem cremos" ("on Whom we believed"): "cremos" is also the preterite form in Portuguese, so the tense is not lost.
- ¶8 "orai pelos que vos caluniam" ("pray for them that despitefully use you"): this is the standard Portuguese rendering of this saying. It is a style choice, not a mistranslation.

### Round 2

Focus: a clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read of all 10 paragraphs (negation, subject and object, tense and mood, qualifiers, quotation wording), plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: clean. No defects found and nothing changed.

Rejected:
- ¶7 John 4:14 "shall never thirst" → "nunca mais terá sede": this is ordinary emphatic Portuguese for "never" and keeps the source's meaning. Gregory does not argue from the word, so this is not a pull toward a Bible rendering that changes the sense.
- ¶3 "the Maker of the Creation" → "o Artífice da criação" (elsewhere "our Maker" → "nosso Criador"): both words are accurate. "Maker" is not a Key Term.
- ¶1 Isaiah 49:20 "give place that I may dwell" → "dá-me lugar para que eu habite": "me" only makes explicit the beneficiary already named in the verse ("too strait for me").

### Round 3

Focus: a source-blind cold read of the pt-BR, with each marked sentence then checked against the source, followed by a mechanical sweep of spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation, punctuation and quote pairing. The mechanical audit was also run. Verdict: clean. No defects found and nothing changed.

Rejected:
- ¶2 "remission of what is to be accounted for" → "remissão daquilo de que se havia de prestar contas": "havia de" names the account that would otherwise have been owed. The sense is kept.
- ¶7 "select every passage in detail, and set them forth" → "selecionar cada passagem … e expô-las": the plural follows the sense, as "them" does in the source.
- ¶10 "offering the worship of their praise to God" → "o culto do seu louvor": "seu" can refer to the angels or to God. Either way the praise is offered to God, so the meaning does not change.
