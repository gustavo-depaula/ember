## Translation Decisions

- Conventions from the sibling Gregory pt-BR works (`not-three-gods`, `holy-spirit`): author without "São", straight double quotes, divine pronouns lowercase (ele, aquele que), reverential capitalization following the source. Inline Scripture references stay where the source puts them, with Portuguese book names (Provérbios, Isaías, Hebreus, 1 Coríntios).
- Scripture quotations translated from the source's wording, not a standard Bible (Prov 8 "O Senhor me criou como princípio dos seus caminhos, para as suas obras"; Am 4:13 "Aquele que firma o trovão e cria o espírito"). Jn 1:1 "with God" → "junto de Deus", since the argument reads it as the Word's coextension with God's whole being.
- Last paragraph, "another Spirit" → "outro espírito" (lowercase): Gregory's point is that this is not the Holy Spirit but the spirit the believer becomes ("passam de carne a ser espírito"); a capital would contradict the argument.
- English `:—` and `—` openers in the Spirit paragraph are rendered with Portuguese punctuation, not transplanted.
- Structure: title heading, the "A Simplício." line and 4 paragraphs, unchanged. No footnotes in the source; no translator notes added.

## Source slips (mirrored, not corrected)

- ¶1 cites "Proverbs 8:28" twice for "The Lord created me"; the verse is Proverbs 8:22. Kept as cited.
- Stray spaces before punctuation ("was not ,", "as God ,", "Proverbs 8:28 .", "begotten ;") are not transplanted.

¶N counts body paragraphs after the "A Simplício." line.
