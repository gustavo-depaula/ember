# Translation Journal — On the Creation of the World (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (Robert Ernest Wallis, ANF Vol. 7, via New Advent). No Latin directory exists, so the English is the only source.
Target: pt-BR

Sibling precedent: no other Victorinus work has pt-BR. Conventions taken from `ambrose/repentance`, `athanasius/*`, `hippolytus/*`:
- Author without honorific when en-US has none ("Hipólito", "Atanásio") → "Vitorino".
- Scripture references keep the source's numbers and inline position, with Portuguese book names ("Gênesis 1:16-17", "1 Macabeus 2:31-41").
- Citations are mirrored even when wrong.
- Quotes are curly “ ”.
- `vós` for the author's plural address to readers.

## Decisions

- Editor footnotes: none in the source.
- The source's ellipsis ("consecrated it . . . with a blessing") marks a lacuna and is kept.
- "in the sixth Psalm for the eighth day" (the psalm title *pro octava*) → "no sexto Salmo, para o oitavo dia", without quotes, as in the source.
- The Annunciation catalogue keeps the source's order inside each clause: the event of Christ first, then the day of creation it matches.
- A stray space before the period ("quaternion of soldiers .") is not transplanted.

## Source slips (mirrored, not corrected)

- "Exodus 22:9, 12" — Moses with raised hands is Exodus 17:9, 12.
- "Proverbs 11:1" — the seven pillars of wisdom are Proverbs 9:1.
- Isaiah 11:2-3 is quoted with "the spirit of wisdom and of piety" where the list later reads "knowledge"; translated literally.
- "1 Maccabbees" (misspelled) in en-US is left untouched; pt-BR reads "1 Macabeus".
