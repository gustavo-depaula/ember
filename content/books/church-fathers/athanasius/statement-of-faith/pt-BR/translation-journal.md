## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-02: Line 5 of the en-US source (`k27--`) is a stray scraping/anchor artifact from the New Advent HTML import — not part of Athanasius's text. Dropped from the pt-BR translation rather than transliterated; it carries no meaning in either language.
- 2026-09-02: "Offspring" (paragraphs 2–4) is rendered as the noun "Gerado" rather than a more literal "Prole" or "Descendência." The passage's whole argument turns on the Nicene *genitum non factum* distinction — begotten, not made — so "Gerado" (paired with "criatura") preserves that opposition for a Portuguese reader in a way a biological-sounding "Prole" would not.
- 2026-09-02: Scripture references kept in their English book-name form (e.g. "(John 5:23)", "(Hebrews 4:12)") per the corpus convention — references are preserved as-is, not localized to Portuguese Bible abbreviations.
- 2026-09-02: The bracketed Greek gloss `(ἄνθρωπον)` in paragraph 1 is preserved as in the source, following the translated word "humanidade."
- 2026-09-02: "undefiled Virgin Mary" rendered "Virgem Maria imaculada" — the standard Portuguese devotional register, not a literal "não contaminada."
