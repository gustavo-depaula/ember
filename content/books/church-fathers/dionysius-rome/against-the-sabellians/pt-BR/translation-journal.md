## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-02: Scripture references kept in their English book-name form without parentheses (e.g. `Proverbs 8:25`, `John 10:30`, `John 14:10`), immediately following the quotation — matching the exact placement and formatting used in the en-US source rather than adding parentheses, since this source's own convention differs from other church-fathers texts already in the corpus.
- 2026-09-02: "hypostases" kept in italics inline after "substâncias," matching the source's own inline gloss.
- 2026-09-02: "Oh reckless and rash men!" rendered as "Ó homens temerários e precipitados!" — an exclamatory address, kept in the same rhetorical register as the original's direct apostrophe to the opposing teachers.
- 2026-09-02: "with hands" (i.e., manufactured, as an idol is made by hand) rendered literally as "com as mãos" — preserves the original's polemical image of crude manufacture applied (absurdly) to the Lord.
- 2026-09-02 (review fix): "convict"/"convicted" (paragraph 2, archaic legal sense of "prove wrong, expose as being in error") had been rendered as "convencer"/"convencidos" (to persuade), which inverted the meaning — the source says these men are refuted, not that they become persuaded. Corrected to "refutar" and "refutados por pensar" respectively. Caught by independent review pass.
- 2026-09-02 (review fix): Paragraph 2's three consecutive references to "the word 'created'" (analyzing the term from the Proverbs 8:22 quotation "The Lord created me...") had inconsistent forms in the first draft — "criar" (infinitive) for the first mention, "criado" (participle) for the next two. Since the quotation itself uses "criou" (the finite verb, "O Senhor criou-me"), all three references to the word under discussion were corrected to "criou," matching the exact word form the argument is analyzing.
