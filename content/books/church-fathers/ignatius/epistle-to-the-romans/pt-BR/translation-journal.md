## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-02: This is the second Ignatian epistle translated into pt-BR (after `epistle-to-polycarp`, #351/#355). Reused that glossary's register and its choice of "Carta a/aos ___" for the title.
- 2026-09-02 — **address form**: unlike the Polycarp letter (addressed to one bishop, with a mid-text shift to the congregation), this epistle is addressed throughout to a single collective addressee — "the Church which... presides in the place of the region of the Romans." Rendered with plural **"vós"** consistently for the whole letter, no singular "tu" anywhere — there is no personal addressee to shift to/from as there was with Polycarp.
- 2026-09-02: Two Scripture allusions in the source carry no chapter/verse citation ("For the things which are seen are temporal..." in Ch. 3, echoing 2 Corinthians 4:18; "For what shall a man be profited..." in Ch. 6, echoing Matthew 16:26). Left uncited in the translation, matching the source exactly — only quotations the English edition itself cites (1 Corinthians 4:4, 1 Corinthians 15:8-9) were given references, localized to standard Portuguese Catholic usage (1 Coríntios 4:4; 1 Coríntios 15:8-9).
- 2026-09-02: No editor/scholarly footnotes present in the source — a single continuous letter with a greeting and ten chapter headings, all original to this edition and translated as part of the text. No translator notes were needed.
- 2026-09-02: "man-pleaser" (Ch. 2) rendered as a paraphrase, "quem agrada aos homens," rather than coining a single Portuguese noun — matches the parallel construction with "pleasing God" ("quem agrada a Deus") in the same sentence.
- 2026-09-02: "mo impeçais" (Ch. 4, "unless you hinder me") uses the clitic contraction *mo* (me + o) to keep the sentence compact, consistent with the formal register elsewhere in the letter.
