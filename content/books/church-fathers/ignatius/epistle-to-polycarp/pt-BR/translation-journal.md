# Translation Journal — Epistle to Polycarp (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (trans. Alexander Roberts and James Donaldson, Ante-Nicene Fathers, Vol. 1; via New Advent)
Target: pt-BR

## Key Terms

| English | Portuguese | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Epistle | Carta | "Carta a Policarpo" chosen over the more literal "Epístola" — matches the modern, natural register used for this short personal letter (as opposed to a formal treatise) |
| Theophorus | Teóforo | proper epithet of Ignatius ("God-bearer"), kept as a name, not translated |
| Church of the Smyrnæans | Igreja de Esmirna | rendered by city name rather than a demonym ("esmirniota"), which is not standard usage in Portuguese |
| bishop | bispo | |
| presbyters / deacons | presbíteros / diáconos | |
| athlete [of God/Christian life] | atleta [de Deus / na vida cristã] | Ignatius's athletic metaphor kept literal throughout (2 uses) |
| panoply | panóplia | kept as a direct cognate — a recognized (if elevated) Portuguese word for "complete armor," matching the military/armor imagery of the passage (batismo/armadura, fé/elmo, amor/lança) |
| the will [of the emperor] | a vontade [do imperador] | editorial bracket from the source kept as-is |
| arms / helmet / spear / panoply | armas / elmo / lança / panóplia | Chapter 6's armory list — "arms" is *armas* (weapons), not *armadura* (armor), which would collide with *panóplia completa* closing the same list |

## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-02: This is the second pt-BR translation of a Church Father in the corpus (after Ambrose's *On the Mysteries*) and the first from Ignatius of Antioch. No prior Ignatius glossary existed; terms chosen fresh, consistent in register with `ambrose/mysteries/pt-BR` and `augustine-confessions/pt-BR`.
- 2026-09-02 — **tu/vós address**: the English source uses "you" throughout without distinguishing singular/plural, but the letter itself shifts audience mid-text — Ignatius addresses Polycarp personally through most of the letter, then turns to address the whole Smyrnaean congregation directly in places. Rendered accordingly: **singular "tu"** for the Greeting and Chapters 1–5 and 7 (personal address to Polycarp), **plural "vós"** for Chapter 6 ("Give heed to the bishop, that God also may give heed to *you*" — addressed to the flock, since it would not make sense for the bishop to be told to heed himself; likewise "Labour together with one another" is a collective exhortation), and mixed in Chapter 8 (Polycarp is asked personally to write to neighboring churches, then "that *you* may be glorified" shifts to the Smyrnaean church collectively, since it is their work of relaying messages that would be remembered). This follows the traditional reading of the letter's structure (cf. Lightfoot's *Apostolic Fathers*, which marks the same shifts using "thou" vs. "ye").
- 2026-09-02: "Troas" (the region/city of embarkation) rendered "Trôade," the standard Portuguese historical name for the Troad, rather than left as "Troas."
- 2026-09-02: "at the public expense" (referring to funds used to free slaves) rendered "à custa da comunidade" — "público" in Portuguese would misleadingly suggest state/government funds; the source means the common fund of the church community.
- 2026-09-02: No editor/scholarly footnotes present in the source — it is a single continuous letter with chapter headings only (the headings themselves, e.g. "Exhortations," are original to this edition and were translated as part of the text). No translator notes were needed.
- 2026-09-02 — **review pass corrections** (6 defects found comparing against the en-US source):
  - Ch. 4: "of frequent occurrence" had been rendered *mais numerosas* (larger/more numerous), which describes size rather than frequency → *mais frequentes*.
  - Ch. 6: "as your arms" had been rendered *armadura* (armor), duplicating the *panóplia completa* that closes the same list → *armas*.
  - Ch. 8: "by whom continue in the unity" had been rendered *permaneceis* (present indicative — a flat statement of fact). The source is a petition, and the Greek *διαμένοιτε* is optative → *permaneçais*, which continues the *Rogo pela vossa felicidade…* wish.
  - Ch. 7: "most blessed in God" had dropped the superlative → *muito bem-aventurado em Deus* (plain form preferred over the ecclesiastical *beatíssimo*, to match this translation's modern register).
  - Ch. 5: "discourse in public regarding them" had been rendered *contra elas* (against them), narrowing the source; Greek *περὶ τούτων* is "concerning these" → *a respeito delas*. The connective *ou antes* is kept as a good rendering of *μᾶλλον δέ*.
  - Ch. 5: "after their own lust" had dropped the possessive → *segundo a própria concupiscência*.
- 2026-09-02: All Scripture references preserved with the same book/chapter/verse numbers as the English source, book names localized to standard Portuguese Catholic usage (1 Thessalonians 5:17 → 1 Tessalonicenses 5:17; Matthew 10:16 → Mateus 10:16; 1 Timothy 1:3 / 6:3 → 1 Timóteo 1:3 / 6:3; Ephesians 5:25 → Efésios 5:25; 1 Corinthians 10:31 → 1 Coríntios 10:31).
