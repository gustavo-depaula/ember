# Translation Journal — Epistle to the Ephesians (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (trans. Alexander Roberts and James Donaldson, Ante-Nicene Fathers, Vol. 1; via New Advent)
Target: pt-BR

## Key Terms

| English | Portuguese | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Epistle | Carta | consistent with `ignatius/epistle-to-polycarp/pt-BR` and `ignatius/epistle-to-the-romans/pt-BR` |
| Theophorus | Teóforo | proper epithet of Ignatius ("God-bearer"), kept as a name, not translated — matches prior Ignatius translations |
| bishop / presbytery / deacon | bispo / presbitério / diácono | |
| Onesimus | Onésimo | the bishop of Ephesus addressed in this letter; standard Portuguese Bible form of the name (cf. Philemon) |
| Burrhus, Crocus, Euplus | Burrhus, Crocus, Euplus | minor companions named once each; left in their English/Latin source forms rather than invented Portuguese equivalents, since no established Portuguese rendering exists and "Burrhus" in particular has no safe phonetic Portuguese equivalent (a literal "Burro" reads as the common word for "donkey") |
| Fronto | Frontão | follows the standard Portuguese historical rendering of the Latin name (cf. Marcus Cornelius Fronto → Marco Cornélio Frontão) |
| God-bearers, temple-bearers, Christ-bearers, bearers of holiness | portadores de Deus, portadores do templo, portadores de Cristo, portadores da santidade | Ignatius's compound epithets (Ch. 9) rendered as a parallel series rather than as single compound words, which don't exist in Portuguese |
| stumbling-block | escândalo | standard Portuguese Pauline usage (1 Coríntios 1:18) over a more literal "pedra de tropeço" |

## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-02: Third pt-BR translation from Ignatius of Antioch (after *Epistle to Polycarp* and *Epistle to the Romans*). Register and diction kept consistent with those two: dignified, slightly formal modern Portuguese, matching the corpus's other Church Fathers translations.
- 2026-09-02 — **address form**: unlike the *Epistle to Polycarp* (addressed to one bishop, mixing "tu"/"vós"), this letter addresses the whole Ephesian congregation throughout ("your bishop," "your whole multitude," "brethren"). Rendered with **plural "vós"** address throughout, with no singular shifts.
- 2026-09-02: No editor/scholarly footnotes present in the source — chapter headings are original to this edition (Roberts-Donaldson) and were translated as part of the text. No translator notes were needed.
- 2026-09-02: All Scripture references preserved with the same book/chapter/verse numbers as the English source, book names localized to standard Portuguese Catholic usage (Ephesians → Efésios, Matthew → Mateus, 1/2 Corinthians → 1/2 Coríntios, 1 Peter → 1 Pedro, John → João, Colossians → Colossenses, 1 Timothy → 1 Timóteo, 1 John → 1 João, James → Tiago).
- 2026-09-02: Bracketed editorial insertions from the source (e.g. "[of Christ]", "[manifested]", "[you hastened to see me]") preserved as brackets in the translation, following the convention of the prior Ignatius translations.
