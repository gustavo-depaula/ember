## Decisions

- Chrysostom addresses Basil as tu throughout, even where en-US mixes "thou" and "you". God is Vós in the Book III §15 doxology. Paul's quoted address to his hearers stays vós.
- Speaker labels are "Basílio:" and "Crisóstomo:".
- Paragraph numbers are `**N.**`. Book IV opens with an unnumbered section and its numbering starts at 2, as in en-US.
- Scripture references stay inline after the quotation, with Portuguese book names (Sirach → Eclesiástico). Quotations follow the Stephens English, not a standard Portuguese Bible (for example, 2 Cor 11:6 "rude na palavra, mas não na ciência", Ezek 33:6 "requererei da mão da sentinela").
- "glorious company of the Apostles" (a Te Deum echo) → "glorioso coro dos Apóstolos", after the pt-BR Te Deum.
- Book III §11 "lovers of the human person" → "os que se enamoram de uma pessoa". The image is lovers pining for an absent beloved.
- Book V §4 "puffed up by the promises of these persons nor cast down at their censure": rendered "louvores" (praises). The contrast with censure and the whole argument are about praise (Greek ἐπαίνοις), so "promises" is taken as a slip for "praises".
- Book VI §9 "when you all revered and admired us" → "nos reverenciáveis e admiráveis" (vós imperfect, for a standing state).

## Source corrections

Fixed in en-US as OCR or import slips:

- Book III §11: "Matthew 5:1" → "Matthew 5:11-12" (the reference was cut off). pt-BR matches.
- Book IV §2: "want only and inconsiderately" → "wantonly and inconsiderately".
- Book VI: "For my ease does not now call for wailing" → "my case". Basil answers "But not my case".

Left as they are:

- Book III §17: "by converting every one's opinion" reads like a slip. Rendered by sense: "tendo de conciliar a opinião de todos".
- Book III: "indited" (= indicted) → "acusado".
- Book IV §8: the citation "1 Timothy 4:13" comes before its quotation. Placement kept.
- Book VI: "and other passions have their proper nutriment?" has a question mark on a statement. Rendered as a statement.
