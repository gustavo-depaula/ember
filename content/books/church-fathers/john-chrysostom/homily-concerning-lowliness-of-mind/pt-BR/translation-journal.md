## Decisions

- Address: the congregation is vós ("amados", "vossa caridade", "Vedes", "ouvi"). Where Chrysostom turns to a single hearer (§2 "thou have put it", §5 "do not tell me", §11–12 "Are you in a calm?", "But you are unworthy", "seek thou"), the address is tu, with "Vês?" in those stretches. The imagined dialogue with Paul (§7, §8) is tu. Christ's reply to the disciples in §12 ("You hear a voice") is vós.
- Pronouns for God and Christ are capitalized (Ele, Si, O, Se), as in *Carta a uma Jovem Viúva*.
- Paragraph numbers are `**N.**`; en-US writes them as `N. ` list markers. The unnumbered paragraph after §5 stays unnumbered.
- Scripture references stay inline where the edition puts them, with Portuguese book names. Quotations follow the Blackburn English, not a Portuguese Bible; §3 and §10 argue from the wording of Phil 1:17–18.
- Wrong citations carried by the edition are mirrored: Philippians 5:17 and 5:18 (the verses are 1:17, 1:18; Philippians has four chapters), Matthew 5:23 and 5:24 (15:23, 15:24).
- Quotes are curly “ ”, as in the sibling Chrysostom works. book.json `name` and toc title use curly quotes in pt-BR.
- §9 "For it is one thing to state the pretext of their preaching itself was not sound" lacks its second member in the edition; rendered as given ("Pois uma coisa é afirmar que o pretexto da sua própria pregação não era são"), without supplying the missing clause.
- The editorial introduction speaks of "the words of St. Paul cited in the title", but the title here is the short one; translated as is.
- Editorial introduction translated. There are no footnotes.

## Source corrections

Fixed in en-US as import artifacts:

- §9 and §10: stray `k28--` prefix removed before "Philippians 5:17" and "Philippians 5:18" (New Advent scraping residue, as in *Concerning Virgins* and *Statement of Faith*). The reference numbers are kept.

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness, paragraph by paragraph (plus mechanical audit). Verdict: 2 defects, fixed. Paragraphs, quotations, parentheticals and all 25 Scripture references align one to one with en-US.

Fixed:
- §9 "not purely, not soundly": "não puramente, não sinceramente" → "não puramente, não de modo são". "sinceramente" is not "soundly", and the passage argues about soundness (são) as distinct from sincerity.
- §11 "without reference to circumstances and at hap-hazard": "sem atender às circunstâncias e ao acaso" → "sem atender às circunstâncias e a esmo". The coordination read as "without attending to circumstances and to chance".

Rejected:
- §2 "pride… surpassed even an incorporeal power" → "venceu": the sense is that pride overthrew the devil; "venceu" is defensible.
- §3 2 Cor 11:3 "fear lest at some time" → "temo que, de algum modo": renders the quotation's "lest"; defensible.
- §6 "having counselled ill for his life" → "tendo arriscado a sua vida": correct sense of the idiom.
- §10 "under pretence of being of the same mind" → "sob pretexto": the pretence→fingimento row covers Phil 1:18 only; here the word means pretext.

### Round 2

Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read (plus mechanical audit). Verdict: 1 defect, fixed.

Fixed:
- §11 "even if bereft of advocacy": "ainda que privado de defensor" → "ainda que privado de defesa". The Key Terms row gives advocacy → defesa, advocate → defensor; §13 keeps the same pair ("a strong advocacy" → "uma forte defesa").

Rejected:

- §5 "he set down the Master's death" → "pôs diante deles a morte do Senhor": "diante deles" makes the object of "set down" explicit; no meaning added.
- §9 "the one preaching out of sincerity, and the others…" → "uma pregando…, e os outros": mirrors the source's mixed "one … others".

### Round 3

Focus: source-blind cold read, then spelling-only sweep (plus mechanical audit). Verdict: 3 defects, fixed.

Fixed:
- §1 "For the going home from prayer": "Pois voltar para casa da oração" → "Pois voltar da oração para casa". The old order read as "return to the house of prayer".
- §4 "his love for and remembrance of the disciples": "o seu amor e a sua lembrança pelos discípulos" → "o seu amor pelos discípulos e a sua lembrança deles". "lembrança" governs *de*, not *por*.
- §12 "For that it both is possible…": "Pois que é possível" → "Pois, que é possível". Without the comma "Pois que" reads as the causal conjunction and the sentence breaks at "estes três pontos".

Rejected:
- §1 "he had rooted over his soul" → "tinha enraizado sobre a sua alma": mirrors the edition.
- §11 "nor of that much canvassing" → "nem daquele muito solicitar": substantivized infinitive, sense kept.
- §12 "when they were entreating him, he repelled" → "repeliu" with no object: the source has none either.
- §13 "we shall both rebut our own crimes" → "repeliremos": "rebut" here is to beat back; defensible.
- §4 "the nations from all quarters referring to his wisdom everything" → "referindo as nações… à sua sabedoria tudo": postposed subject, "tudo" is the object; resolvable.

### Round 4

Focus: function words, headings and forced commitments (demonstratives, possessives, antecedents, tu/vós trace), plus mechanical audit. Verdict: 2 defects, fixed.

Fixed:
- §1 "For the going home from prayer, whence he ought to have derived gain": "Pois voltar da oração para casa, de onde deveria ter tirado proveito" → "Pois voltar para casa vindo da oração, da qual deveria ter tirado proveito". After the round-3 reorder, "de onde" followed "casa" and read as "home, from which he should have profited"; "whence" is the prayer.
- §12 "to display to all her spiritual wisdom": "a sua sabedoria espiritual" → "a sabedoria espiritual dela". With Christ as subject, "sua" reads as His wisdom.

Rejected:
- §4 "reproving his wickedness" → "reprovava a sua maldade" (subject Paulo): the preceding list of Nero's crimes fixes the referent, as in the source.
- §8 "they saw his body indeed bound" → "viam o seu corpo" (subject the disciples): only Paul was bound; context resolves.
- §8 "not owing to their proper nature" → "natureza própria delas": "their" is plural, so hands, the only plural noun in reach.
- §5 "for his sake the Christ died" → "por sua causa": the stretch is tu, so "sua" cannot be "your".
