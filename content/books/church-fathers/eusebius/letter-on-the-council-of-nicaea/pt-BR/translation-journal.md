# Translation Journal — Letter on the Council of Nicaea (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (John Henry Newman and Archibald Robertson, NPNF Second Series Vol. 4, via New Advent). The book has no Greek directory.
Target: pt-BR

Sibling precedent: no other Eusebius work has pt-BR. Conventions come from `athanasius/statement-of-faith` and `athanasius/incarnation-of-the-word` (same NPNF volume), `ambrose/repentance`, and `councils/constantinople-i` (creed wording). These conventions are adopted:
- Author without honorific, like "Atanásio" and "Sulpício Severo (c. 363-c. 420)": "Eusébio de Cesareia (c. 265-c. 340)".
- Scripture references with full Portuguese book names, the source's numbers ("Mateus 28:19"). Quotations are translated from the source's wording.
- Curly quotes “ ”, with ‘ ’ for nested quotes.
- Reverential pronouns for the Son are lowercase (ele, dele), as in the Athanasius pt-BR works.

## Translation Decisions

- **Homoousios.** The source writes "One in essence". The pt-BR uses "consubstancial", the standard Portuguese term for the Nicene word, also used in the creed of `councils/constantinople-i`. It is also a single word, as ¶4 says ("the single word"). The link to "essência" in ¶5–¶7 stays clear because the creed's "da essência do Pai" stands beside it.
- **¶10** "generated in energy, He was in virtue with the Father" renders ἐνεργείᾳ / δυνάμει. A literal "em energia / em virtude" would mislead a Portuguese reader, so the pt-BR uses the standard pair "em ato / em potência".
- **Creed anathema.** "Once He was not" → "Houve um tempo em que ele não era"; "Before His generation He was not" → "Antes de ser gerado, ele não era". The same wording is repeated where ¶8 and ¶9 quote these phrases.
- **Punctuation.** The source's English `:—` and the stray spaces left by removed footnote anchors ("this :”—", "essence ,'", "came to be ,", "Matthew 28:19 .") are not carried over. The Scripture reference in ¶3 is given in parentheses after the quotation.
- **Paragraph numbers** are bold (`**1.**`) in pt-BR, following `.claude/rules/books.md`. The en-US file keeps its `N. ` markers, which run 1–11 without gaps. It was not edited, since this is a formatting matter and not an OCR slip.
- The unnumbered paragraph after ¶3 ("believing each of these…") begins in lowercase in both files, because it continues the creed's sentence.
- No editor footnotes in the source. No translator notes added.

### Round 3

- **Foco:** leitura fria integral de pt-BR, seguida de confronto com en-US e varredura de ortografia, diacríticos, crase, hifenização e pontuação.
- **Veredito:** CLEAN; nenhum defeito objetivo confirmado; capítulo e Key Terms inalterados.
- **Rejeitados na leitura fria:**
  - ¶6 [14] “essência demasiado elevada […] ; e que os Oráculos divinos ensinam ter sido gerada”: fonte “an essence which is too high […] ; and which the Divine oracles teach to have been generated”. O adjetivo “elevada” e a relativa “que […]” qualificam a mesma “essência”; a coordenação de qualificações com formas diferentes é admissível. O ponto e vírgula é pesado, mas não torna o antecedente irresolúvel; “gerada” mantém a concordância correta já examinada na rodada 2.
  - ¶7 [15] “examinando-o”: fonte “on examination”. O objeto pode ser recuperado como o ponto doutrinal em exame, explicado pela oração seguinte sobre a consubstancialidade; o sujeito implícito são os mesmos examinadores que relatam a discussão. Construção pouco explícita, sem erro objetivo ou mudança de conteúdo.
  - ¶11 [19] “recebemos […] o que […] nos pareceram coincidir”: fonte “received […] what […] they appeared to us to coincide”. O sujeito elíptico de “pareceram” retoma “afirmações”, equivalente a “they”/“statements” da fonte, e não o neutro “o que”. Não há concordância quebrada.
