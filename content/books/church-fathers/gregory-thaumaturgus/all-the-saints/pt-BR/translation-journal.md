# Translation Journal — On All the Saints (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (trans. S.D.F. Salmond, Ante-Nicene Fathers, Vol. 6; via New Advent)
Target: pt-BR

## Key Terms

| English | Portuguese | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| On All the Saints | Sobre Todos os Santos | matches the "On X" → "Sobre X" pattern established in `ambrose/mysteries` ("On the Mysteries" → "Sobre os Mistérios") |
| Hades | Hades | kept as a proper name, standard usage in Portuguese patristic/theological writing |
| the Father [requests me] | o Pai | ambiguous in the source — could be God the Father or the presiding bishop/church father who invited Gregory to preach. Kept literally capitalized, matching the source's ambiguity rather than resolving it |

## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-02: First pt-BR translation of this short homily by Gregory Thaumaturgus. No prior glossary existed for this author; terms chosen fresh, consistent in register with other translated Church Fathers (e.g. `ignatius/epistle-to-polycarp/pt-BR`).
- 2026-09-02 — **address to the Lord**: the opening line ("Grant your blessing, Lord") is a direct petition to God, so it uses the elevated **"vós"** register ("Concedei a vossa bênção, Senhor"), matching the convention established in `augustine-confessions/pt-BR` for addressing God. The body of the homily addresses the congregation in the first person ("I," "our") rather than "you," so no second-person address choice was needed there.
- 2026-09-02: The whole homily is a single unbroken paragraph in the source (no chapter subdivisions, headings, or numbered sections beyond the title) — preserved as a single paragraph in the translation, matching the source's structure exactly.
- 2026-09-02: The Scripture quotation ("O death, where is your sting! O grave, where is your victory?") is rendered "Ó morte, onde está o teu aguilhão! Ó sepulcro, onde está a tua vitória?" — mirroring the source's word order and pairing (death/sting, grave/victory) rather than a specific published Portuguese Bible translation, since no existing pt-BR rendering of 1 Corinthians 15:55 was found elsewhere in the corpus to match against.
- 2026-09-02: No editor or scholarly footnotes present in the source (New Advent's transcription carries none for this chapter) — no translator notes were needed.
- 2026-09-02: Scripture reference localized to standard Portuguese book naming: 1 Corinthians 15:55 → 1 Coríntios 15:55.
- 2026-09-02 — **review pass correction**: the quotation's first clause had been normalized to a question mark ("...o teu aguilhão?"), but the en-US source uses an exclamation mark for that clause ("O death, where is your sting!") and a question mark for the second ("O grave, where is your victory?"). Fixed to match the source's punctuation exactly: "...o teu aguilhão! ... a tua vitória?"
- 2026-09-02 — **review pass correction (post-merge)**: in the sword-forging passage, "sharpened it by sufferings without passion in them" had been rendered "afiou-a por sofrimentos que **nela** não deixaram paixão". Every other verb in that clause chain (`deu-lhe`, `mergulhou-a`, `afiou-a`, `a fez brilhar`) takes *a espada* as its object, so the clitic `nela` pulled the antecedent onto the sword — asserting that the sufferings left no passion *in the sword*, rather than that the sufferings were themselves free of passion. That inverts the theological point (the impassibility of Christ's sufferings). Fixed to "afiou-a por **sofrimentos isentos de paixão**", which keeps the property on the sufferings and drops the interpolated verb "deixaram" (which has no counterpart in the source).
