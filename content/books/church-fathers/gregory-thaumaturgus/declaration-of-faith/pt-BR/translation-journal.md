## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-01: "estranged" (of the Trinity's unity) rendered as "apartada" rather than a literal cognate ("estranhada"), since "apartada" reads more naturally alongside "dividida" in Portuguese theological prose while preserving the sense of non-separation.
- 2026-09-01: "superinduced" (an archaic term meaning "added on afterward") rendered as "acrescentado posteriormente" for clarity to a modern reader, matching the guideline to modernize only where the original would otherwise confuse.
- 2026-09-01: Author's own emphasis (italicized clauses in the source: *who is His*, *Begotten*, *Perfect*, *or Leader*, *abides*) preserved as italics on the corresponding Portuguese words/phrases.
- No footnotes present in the source (single short chapter, no editorial apparatus) — none added.
