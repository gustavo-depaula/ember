# Translation Journal — The Banquet of the Ten Virgins (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (William R. Clark, Ante-Nicene Fathers Vol. 6, via New Advent). No Greek or Latin text is in the corpus; en-US is the only source-language directory.
Target: pt-BR

Conventions follow the sibling `methodius/oration-concerning-simeon-and-anna` (and through it `ambrose/repentance`, `athanasius/*`):
- Author "Metódio"; book name "O Banquete das Dez Virgens"; toc "Introdução", "Discurso I"–"Discurso XI".
- Scripture references keep the source's numbers and inline placement, with Portuguese book names ("Eclesiástico 6:36", "Cântico dos Cânticos 4:9-12", "1 Tessalonicenses 4:16-17"). Citations are mirrored even when wrong.
- Curly quotes “ ”, nested ‘ ’. Reverential pronouns lowercase; capitals only where the source capitalizes (Verbo, Esposo, Esposa, Sabedoria, EU SOU).
- Scripture is rendered from the source's wording, not from a Portuguese Bible.
- No editor footnotes exist in the source; one translator note added (ch009).

## Translation Decisions

- Address: `tu` for one interlocutor, Arete, Christ, the Church/Queen and God; `vós` for the virgins as a group. The women speakers use feminine forms when they speak of themselves ("estou persuadida", "sou examinada"), and "my worthy friend" addressed to a woman is "minha digna amiga" (ch003 §3–4).
- Speaker labels: the source's "Euboulios ." (space before the period) is written "Eubúlio." The en-US file is left as it is.
- Headings: the source's "Introduction.— Plan…" becomes "Introdução — Plano…", without the English ".—". Sub-chapter headings keep the "Capítulo N." form.
- Hymn strophes (ch012) keep their inline numbers after "Tecla." as in the source. They are not at line start, so they cannot become list markers.
- ch009 §1: the pun "virginity is divine by the change of one letter" (παρθενία/παρθεία) is untranslatable. A translator footnote explains it; the text itself is not changed.
- ch009 §12: the Homer/Hesiod Chimaera verses keep the source's line breaks across paragraphs exactly.
- ch009 §14 "burying the truth in fairies and fictions": rendered literally ("fadas e ficções"). "Fables" may be the intended word, but the edition is not available to check.
- ch012 §3 Euboulios addresses Gregorion as "O wisest lady" ("ó sapientíssima senhora"). This is the source's only gender marker for Gregorion, so every open form addressed to or spoken by Gregorion is feminine.
- ch006 §7: the source opens a parenthesis "(the city in heaven…" and never closes it. The pt-BR closes it after the Hebrews quotation.
- Small punctuation slips are normalized in pt-BR only: missing spaces ("lifeless.And", "men.If", "righteousness.I take"), stray periods mid-sentence ("as by a woman. so as", "laying it down. and", "blind. as not", "be gone. who"), "' Wisdom 3:16" spacing, "the Lord.;", "wine“They".
