# Translation Journal — Nine Homilies of Hexaemeron (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (Blomfield Jackson translation, NPNF Second Series Vol. 8, via New Advent)
Target: pt-BR

Sibling precedent: `basil/de-spiritu-sancto/pt-BR` (same translator, same NPNF volume). Its conventions are kept: author "Basílio Magno", `Word` (the Son) → **Verbo**, `Only begotten` → **Unigênito**, `Godhead` → **Divindade**, `true religion` → **verdadeira religião**, `œconomy` → **economia**, curly quotes “ ”, Scripture book names in full Portuguese, inline references kept bare where the source puts them, pronouns for God lowercase (ele, dele) with titles capitalized.

## Translation Decisions

- 2026-10-10: Nine files, one per homily. Each pt-BR file has the same heading, the same non-empty blocks in the same order and the same numbered sections as its en-US source (18/23/19/16/19/27/18/22/18 blocks); the inline Scripture references match one for one, verified by script.
- 2026-10-10: Section numbers written as bold `**N.**` per `.claude/rules/books.md`. The en-US files still use bare `N. ` list markers; converting them is a source-side change left for a later pass.
- 2026-10-10: No footnotes exist in the source (New Advent's NPNF notes were not imported), so there was nothing to drop. No translator notes added.
- 2026-10-10: Address. Basil speaks to a congregation; the English "you" is rendered by its referent: "vós" when he addresses the assembly (openings, closings, "my hearers"), "tu" when he turns to a single hearer or an imagined opponent ("O man!", the astrologer, the reader of Hom. V–IX moral asides). Where one English paragraph moves between the two, the pt-BR follows the move.
- 2026-10-10: Scripture is translated from the source's wording, not from a Portuguese Bible: e.g. Gen 1:2 "invisível e inacabada" (LXX, as Basil argues from it); Gen 1:5 "E houve tarde e manhã: o primeiro dia" / "um dia" (Hom. II §8 turns on "one day" vs "the first day"); Gen 1:6 "Haja um firmamento". Unreferenced quotations stay unreferenced.
- 2026-10-10: Citation errors of the edition are mirrored, not corrected: Hom. IV §3 "Ecclesiastes 1:6-7" (the verse is 1:7); Hom. V §5 "Matthew 4:26-28" (the parable is Mark 4:26-28). Sirach is "Eclesiástico" as in the other church-fathers pt-BR books.
- 2026-10-10: Headings mirror the source's bold/italic mix, including Hom. VIII's "The creation of fowl and water animals" (the homily is in fact on land animals and birds; the heading is the edition's and is kept).
- 2026-10-10: Hom. III §8 "The nature of right belongs to another" is a garbled NPNF sentence (Basil: the name "heaven" properly belongs to the first heaven). Rendered by its sense: "O nome, por direito, pertence a outro". en-US left as is.
- 2026-10-10: Hom. I §2 "and that was all was given up to chance" is read as "that all was given up to chance" ("tudo estava entregue ao acaso"). en-US left as is, since it may be the edition's own slip.
- 2026-10-10: "fiat" (Hom. IV) kept as the source writes it, roman.

## Source edits (en-US, OCR-class)

- ch001 §6 "it, ascending into the past" → "if, ascending into the past".
- ch002 §3 "the activity or our intelligence" → "the activity of our intelligence".
- ch005 §2 "The need, for example, after its annual growth" → "The reed, …" (the sentence answers the list beginning with "the reed").
- ch006 §3 "we find anew proof" → "we find a new proof".
- ch008 §7 Greek "ὁ ρᾶν" (split by a space) → "ὁρᾶν".
- ch009 §1 "has the forth of a winnowing basket" → "has the form of a winnowing basket".

## Review log

### Round 1

- 2026-10-10. Independent review of all nine homilies against the canonical en-US source. Focus: completeness paragraph by paragraph, quotations, parentheticals, Greek/Latin, and Portuguese Scripture references. Verdict: **ISSUES 3**, all fixed.
- ch006 [16], §7: “foi querida por ele” → “foi determinada para ele”. Source: “it was willed for him”; the fatalist argument assigns the deed to an imposed destiny, not to the offender’s will. The former rendering reversed this relation.
- ch006 [17], §8, and Key Terms: “ascios” → “áscios”. Source: “askii” (shadowless). Restored the required accent in the Portuguese term; see [Priberam, áscio](https://dicionario.priberam.org/%C3%A1scio). One spelling defect propagated to the glossary, counted once.
- ch009 [16], §6, João 10:30: “Eu e o Pai somos um” → “Eu e meu Pai somos um”. Source: “I and my Father are one”. Restored the source’s possessive “my”, omitted in the quotation; its reference remains unchanged.
- Rejected emphasis flags throughout: source section openings “1.”, “2.”, etc. correspond to “**1.**”, “**2.**”, etc.; this is required by `.claude/rules/books.md`, not added content. ch005 [12] source “call mischos” → “chamam *mischos*”: italics identify the retained foreign technical term, as recorded in Key Terms; no lost or added wording.
- Rejected ch008 [14] repeated-word flag: source “call these days halcyon days” → “chamam a estes dias dias de alcião”. The first “dias” identifies the days being named; the second belongs to the name. Both are grammatical and meaningful.
- Rejected Greek Key Terms flag, ch008 [16]: source “οὐρανός … ὁρᾶν” → “οὐρανός … ὁρᾶν”. Both Greek terms are retained; “kept in Greek” is a journal instruction, not a literal target expression. The acute-accent encoding difference does not change the word.
- Rejected apparent citation errors: ch004 [6], source “Ecclesiastes 1:6-7” → “Eclesiastes 1:6-7”; ch005 [10], source “Matthew 4:26-28” → “Mateus 4:26-28”. They reproduce the canonical edition as required; the earlier journal decision stands.
- Rejected ch002 [22], source “Why does Scripture say ‘one day the first day’?” → “Por que diz a Escritura ‘um dia, o primeiro dia’?”: the awkward juxtaposition belongs to the source; both expressions are retained and the following sentences supply the contrast.
- Rejected ch001 [5] and ch003 [15] source-garble concerns: “and that was all was given up to chance” → “e que tudo estava entregue ao acaso”; “The nature of right belongs to another” → “O nome, por direito, pertence a outro”. The existing journal records these readings, and the immediate argument supports them; no new source repair was made.
- Rejected ch008 [2] heading concern: source “The creation of fowl and water animals” → “A criação das aves e dos animais aquáticos”. The title follows the edition even though the opening discusses terrestrial animals.
- Rejected ch008 [18], source “How does the grasshopper modulate its song?” → “Como modula a cigarra o seu canto?”. The English edition itself connects this insect with *tettix* and *cicadae* in note 1679; “cigarra” is defensible in this historical usage, not an objective substitution. See [NPNF VIII, Homily VIII, note 1679](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf208.viii.ix.html).
- Rejected ch005 [5], source “seed for future trees” → “semente para as plantas futuras”: the referent is the reed and its root shoots; “plantas” retains that referent without changing the propagation described. Rejected ch009 [5], “fix your conversation in heaven” → “fixa no céu a tua conversação”: the older sense of manner of living is available in both languages. Rejected ch009 [17], “you refuse it” → “recusa-la”: this is correctly formed from “recusas” + “a”, not a misspelled infinitive.

### Round 2

- 2026-10-10. Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read of all nine homilies against en-US. Verdict: **ISSUES 2**, both fixed.
- ch006 [9], §3: “quando está na sua perfeição, faz empalidecer as estrelas e ilumina a terra …, reina sobre a noite” → “quando, na sua perfeição, faz empalidecer as estrelas e ilumina a terra …, ela reina sobre a noite”. Source: “when at its perfection it makes the stars to turn pale and lightens up the earth …, it reigns over the night”. The former text closed the when-clause too early and left a comma splice of main verbs.
- ch007 [17], §5: “rémora” → “rêmora”. Source: “the remora”. Brazilian spelling (VOLP).
- Rejected ch006 [13] “regista” (source “records”) and ch002 [7] “contributo” (source “contribution”): both are valid pt-BR dictionary forms.
- Rejected ch002 [18] “mais agradável do que o qual … não pode sequer imaginar outro” (source “than which man's mind cannot even imagine a pleasanter one”): “do que o qual” is established Portuguese (cf. Anselm's formula), not broken grammar.
- Rejected ch003 [9] “corpo firme” for source “strong body”: one Greek term behind “strong”/“firm”; keeps the firmament word family (Key Terms).
- Rejected ch009 [8] “olharem, do fundo dos seus estábulos, para as portas” (source “look to the end of their stables towards the doors”): both readings have the oxen inside looking toward the doors.
- Rejected ch002 [4] “a ela, mãe universal” (source “her, their universal Mother”): the apposition keeps the earth as mother of what it brings forth.

### Round 3

- 2026-10-10. Focus: source-blind cold read of all nine pt-BR homilies, then verification against en-US and spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation and punctuation sweep. Verdict: **CLEAN**. No objective defects confirmed; no chapter, source, metadata or Key Terms changes.
- Rejected ch001 [6] “a elas se dedicam” (source “those who pursue them”): recoverable reference to the enumerated disciplines; not a lost or unresolvable antecedent.
- Rejected ch002 [11] “Mas, se tal for o caso” after the victory of good (source “If good is the stronger […] But if that be the case”); ch003 [6] “diferem das gotas” (source “differ from the drops”); ch003 [13] “consumo do calor” (source “consumption of heat”): the surprising logic/wording is already in the edition. No basis to insert a new hypothesis, a negation or a different claim.
- Rejected ch005 [3] “deu-lhe fecundidade” (source “it gave fertility”): singular resumes the command/law after the semicolon. Rejected ch005 [13] “Jovens e vigorosas, a sua casca…” (source “Young and vigorous, their bark…”): the plants, named immediately before, remain the recoverable referent of the detached phrase and “envelhecem”. Neither requires an objective grammatical repair.
- Rejected ch006 [10] “ele não pode certamente ser retido” (source “it cannot certainly be retained”): “ele” resumes the condensed air; the unusual physical claim is source wording.
- Rejected crase search candidates ch003 [14] “à singular virtude” (source “to the singular virtue”) and ch006 [17] “à maior parte” (source “to the greater part”): adjectives modify feminine nouns; neither is crase before an infinitive.
- Rejected ch007 [3] plants “vivem” yet “nem têm vida” (source “be said to live […] nor have they life”): the source distinguishes nutritive growth from animal life and feeling. Rejected ch008 [9] “massa inferior” (source “lower mass”): changing to “superior” would alter the edition.
- Rejected ch008 [17] “o único das aves” (source “the only one of the birds”): masculine resumes “um animal, o morcego”; the partitive group and referent are recoverable. Recasting as “a única ave” or “o único entre as aves” is not an objectively necessary correction; the historical classification itself follows the source.
- Rejected ch009 [9] “que aprendemos nos livros” (source “that we have learned useful knowledge in books”): affirmative in both; adding “não” would alter the source despite making a possible reading of the argument smoother.
- Rejected driver flags again: section numbers source “1.” etc. → “**1.**” etc. follow the books rule; ch005 [12] “call mischos” → “chamam *mischos*” marks the retained foreign term. ch008 [14] “call these days halcyon days” → “chamam a estes dias dias de alcião” has two distinct syntactic uses of “dias”. ch008 [16] “οὐρανός / ὁρᾶν” → “οὐρανός / ὁρᾶν” retains both terms; Key Terms “kept in Greek” is an instruction, not target wording.

### Round 4

- 2026-10-10. Focus: function words, headings, and places where Portuguese must commit (gender, antecedent, tu/vós). Verdict: **CLEAN**. No chapter, metadata or Key Terms changes; journal claims re-checked against the files.
- Rejected ch002 [9] “toda espécie de objeto visível e os transmite” (source “all kinds of visible object, and transmits them”): plural by sense, mirrors “them”.
