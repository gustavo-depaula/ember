# Translation Journal — De Spiritu Sancto (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (Blomfield Jackson translation, NPNF Second Series Vol. 8, via New Advent)
Target: pt-BR

## Key Terms

Conventions carried over from `athanasius/incarnation-of-the-word/pt-BR` and `gregory-thaumaturgus/*/pt-BR`: `Word` (the Son) → **Verbo**, `Only-begotten` → **Unigênito**, `Godhead` → **Divindade**, `Paraclete` → **Paráclito**, Scripture book names localized to full Portuguese names, quoted Scripture translated from the source's own wording, pronouns for God lowercase (ele, dele, lhe) with titles capitalized.

### Prepositions (the book's argument)

The treatise turns on the doxologies "with the Son together with the Holy Ghost" and "through the Son in the Holy Ghost", and on the heretics' allotment of "of whom / through whom / in whom". Each English preposition has one fixed Portuguese rendering so that every gloss finds its word in the quote:

| English | Portuguese | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| with (μετά) | com | "with the Son", "with Him", "*with* whom" → "com o Filho", "com ele", "*com* quem" |
| together with (σύν) | juntamente com | §3 doxology; also "quickened together with", "glorified together" → "vivificou juntamente com", "glorificados juntamente" |
| through (διά) | por meio de | "through whom" → "por meio de quem"; "through the Son" → "por meio do Filho" |
| by | por (pelo/pela) | where the English says "by", including "by (διά)" and "by (ἐν)" glosses (§§10–11) and "by the Holy Ghost" (1 Cor 12:3). Kept distinct from "through" because §47 argues "it is not said through the Spirit, but by the Spirit" → "não se diz 'por meio do Espírito', mas 'pelo Espírito'" |
| in (ἐν) | em (no/na) | "in whom" → "em quem"; "in the Holy Ghost" → "no Espírito Santo"; "in [or by]" → "em [ou por]" |
| of whom / of which (ἐξ) | de quem / do qual | "of Him" → "dele" |
| from (whom) | a partir de / da parte de | §12, where "from" is contrasted with "through": "made from a woman" → "feito a partir de uma mulher"; "from God" → "da parte de Deus". Chosen so "from" does not collapse into "de" (= "of") |
| to Him (εἰς) | para ele | Romans 11:36 "of him and through him and to him" → "dele, e por meio dele, e para ele" |
| according to which | segundo o qual | §5 |
| on account of which | por causa do qual | §5 |
| through or by means of which | por meio do qual ou mediante o qual | §5 |
| and (καί) | e | doxology "to the Father and to the Son *and* to the Holy Ghost" → "ao Pai, e ao Filho, *e* ao Espírito Santo" |

## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-27: Single file (`ch001.md`), 30 chapters, 79 numbered sections. Every heading and paragraph has a pt-BR counterpart in the same order (170 non-empty blocks each); inline Scripture references match the source one for one, verified by script.
- 2026-09-27: Chapter headings are the NPNF editor's summaries. They are kept and translated, as in the Athanasius book, because they are the only navigation the source has. Their bold/italic mix is mirrored.
- 2026-09-27: Section numbers written as bold `**N.**` per `.claude/rules/books.md` (the en-US file still uses bare `N. ` list markers; converting it is a source-side change for a later pass).
- 2026-09-27: No footnotes exist in the source file (New Advent's NPNF notes were not imported). One stray editor cross-reference inside the Chapter 25 heading ("cf. note on p. 3", pointing to a note that is not in the corpus) was dropped; the Greek *ἐν* beside it was kept.
- 2026-09-27: NPNF's inline A.V. glosses (e.g. "through [by, A.V.] him", "(A.V., 'of' ...)") are kept, with the English word quoted, since they compare the translation with an English Bible: "por meio [A.V.: by] dele". "[lxx., known]" → "[LXX: conheceu]".
- 2026-09-27: Inline Scripture references are kept bare, where the source places them, as in the sibling Church Fathers books. Book names localized; chapter:verse unchanged, including the edition's oddities (Romanos 11:38 in §7, Isaías 60:1 in §28, 2 Coríntios 1:12 in §46, 1 João 1:20 in §48, Lucas 1:11 for Gabriel in §38, Gálatas 6:4 in §61, Ezequiel 23:5 in §78). Wisdom → Sabedoria, Haggai → Ageu, Hosea → Oseias, Ecclesiastes → Eclesiastes.
- 2026-09-27: Import slips translated as the intended text, without altering en-US: "k28--1 John 16:15" → "João 16:15" (§9); Greek glosses split by a space ("ἐ κ", "ἐ ν", "ἀ νάστασις") written closed; "pastare" → "pastagem"; "in terchanged" → "intercambia"; "com mon" → "comum"; Chapter 16 heading "separable" → "inseparável" (the body, §37, states the thesis as "inseparable", and the heading's point is the same); §32 "they did 'bear about in the body the dying of Jesus'" → "não 'trouxeram...'" (the sentence is a run of negations and the missing "not" is required by the argument); §49 "disability to fall away" → "incapacidade de se afastarem"; §79 "reeking not" (recking) → "sem fazer caso".
- 2026-09-27: §28 "the God who gave, the Son who received, and the Spirit who is, the unction" rendered literally ("o Deus que deu, o Filho que recebeu e o Espírito que é a unção"), not smoothed to the anointing formula.
- 2026-09-27: "the word of truth" / "the Word witnesses" (§49) referring to Scripture → lowercase "a palavra"; "Word" as the Son → "Verbo". §38 Psalm 33:6 "by the word of the Lord were the heavens made" keeps lowercase "palavra" because Basil then glosses it; his gloss ("The Word then is not...") uses "Verbo".
- 2026-09-27: Amphilochius is addressed in the second person singular (tu/te/teu) throughout, including §§20, 42 where Basil turns to an imagined opponent in the singular. Plural opponents ("you who are lovers of Christ", §16; "cease to wage your ... war", §60) keep "vós".
- 2026-09-27: §48 "another comforter" and §49 "the Comforter" → "Consolador", kept apart from "Paráclito", since the source uses both words.
- 2026-09-27 (review round 1): Fixed six preposition-mapping/completeness slips against the fixed table: §17 "pass through from the Father to us" had dropped "through" (added "por meio dele"); §19 "works not by bodily agency" had "by" rendered in the "through" slot ("por meio de agência corporal" → "por agência corporal"); §70 "you who have heard from God" had an added "da boca de" not in the source (→ "ouviste de Deus"); §73 "through the power of the invocations" had "through" rendered as "pelo" (→ "por meio do poder"); §74 "in all that he through grace accomplished" had "through" rendered as "pela" inconsistently with the same paragraph's "through knowledge" → "por meio do conhecimento" (→ "por meio da graça"); §75 "the knowledge of God by such an initiation" had "by" rendered in the "through" slot (→ "por tal iniciação"). §62 "and again the Spirit speaks in him" had "and again" (continuative, mirroring "Paul in Christ / Christ in Paul") rendered as "por outro lado" (adversative, not in the source) → "e também".
- 2026-09-27 (review round 1): Re-verified the two departures from source wording flagged for scrutiny. Ch.16 heading "separable" → "inseparável": confirmed as a source-side slip, not kept — §37 immediately restates "the point raised from the outset" as "inseparable and wholly incapable of being parted," and §§38-40 argue inseparability in creation, human dispensation, and judgment (the heading's own three-part list) with no refutation-of-opponents framing (contrast Ch.17's heading, which explicitly names an opponent's claim). Kept as "inseparável." §32 "not... bear about in the body the dying of Jesus": confirmed the restored "not" — the clause is Paul's own words (2 Cor 4:10, elsewhere an affirmative statement about Christian life), embedded here in a run of four parallel negations about those merely typologically baptized "unto Moses," three of which are negated in the source; an unnegated clause in the middle of that list is grammatically and argumentatively incoherent. Kept as "não 'trouxeram...'."
- 2026-09-27 (review round 2): Full preposition-mapping sweep against the fixed table (mechanical, every with/together with/through/by/in/of/from occurrence where Father/Son/Spirit is the object), a source-blind cold read of the pt-BR text alone, and a function-word pass (articles, este/aquele, conjunctions, "sua", negation scope, mood). Fixed six defects: §9 Galatians 6:8 "sows *to* the spirit" had been rendered "no espírito" (in) instead of "para o espírito" (to). §12 Basil's own paraphrase "it is proper to a woman to be made *of* the man, and to a man to be made *through* the woman" had both collapsed onto the fixed "from" rendering ("a partir do homem" / "a partir da mulher"), erasing the of/through contrast the paragraph exists to point out; restored to "do homem" / "por meio da mulher" (the actual Bible quote right after, which does say "from [A.V. of]", correctly keeps "a partir do/da"). §52 "are for making a slave of the Holy Spirit" → "são por fazer" is a false-friend calque ("estar por + infinitivo" reads as "yet to be done" in Portuguese, i.e. the opposite sense); fixed to "estão a favor de fazer". §56 "rank Him with the creature" had dropped "junto" from the fixed rendering ("colocar junto com", per §24's own worked example) → fixed to "coloque junto com a criatura". §79 "o ardente fervor **da** teu 'amor não fingido'" → "**do** teu" (plain gender-agreement slip; "fervor" is masculine).
- 2026-09-27 (review round 3): §52 "Let those men answer us who are for making a slave of the Holy Spirit" — round 2's fix to "estão a favor de fazer do Espírito Santo um escravo" was itself unidiomatic Portuguese ("estar a favor de fazer" reads stiffly). Fixed to "defendem fazer do Espírito Santo um escravo", checked against the full English sentence.
- 2026-09-27 (review round 3): Full Scripture-quotation sweep for Almeida-drift across every direct quotation in §1-79 (compared clause by clause against the English, per the "Translate the source's wording, not the verse you remember" rule), a negation-scope sweep (every not/nor/neither/no one/none/never/nothing) over §37-79, and a referent sweep (seu/sua/dele/ele pronouns against the English's He/His/Him/It antecedent, Father vs. Son vs. Spirit) over §37-79. No new defects found beyond the §52 fix above; §54 (see corrected entry below) was re-verified and confirmed not a defect. Quotations checked included: §1 Luke 11:10, §2 Matt 5:18, §6 1 Cor 8:6/11:12, Exod 25:31, 1 Cor 15:47, §7 1 Cor 8:6, Rom 11:36/38, Isa 40:12-13, Ps 145:15-16, §9 Gal 6:8, 1 John 3:24, Matt 1:20, John 3:6, §10 1 Cor 1:9, Gal 4:7, Rom 6:4, Isa 29:15, 1 Cor 2:10, §11 2 Tim 1:14, 1 Cor 12:8, Eph 3:9, 2 Thess 1:1, Rom 1:10, Rom 2:17, §12 Gal 4:4, 1 Cor 11:12, §21 Eph 1:22, John 1:16, §24 Matt 28:19, §26 1 Thess 1:9, §27 Prov 23:29, §28 Acts 10:38, Isa 60:1 (=61:1, source's own mislabeling, mirrored), §30 Deut 4:26/32:1, Isa 1:2, §38 John 1:1, Ps 33:6, Heb 1:7/Ps 104:4, Isa 6:3, Dan 7:10, §40 Matt 25:21, 1 Cor 15:41-42, §46 Mal 1:6 (both clauses), Job 33:4, §49 Ps 104:30, §52 Ps 139:7 (see below), Hag 2:4-5, §53 John 17:25, 14:19, 14:17, 15:3, Isa 42:5, §56 Rom 8:11, John 10:27-28, Rom 8:10, John 6:63, §57 Rom 8:2, Acts 1:8, Rom 8:32, Gal 4:6, §64 Gen 28:16, §65 Deut 12:13-14, §66 Amos 5:13, Eccl 9:17, Ezek 23:5 (source's own mislabeling, mirrored), Matt 24:12, Rom 14:1, §67 Job 8:9, 1 Cor 11:2, 2 Thess 2:15, Deut 19:15, §69 Col 2:13, Col 3:3-4, Rom 8:17, 1 Thess 4:17, §70 Rom 8:17, Heb 10:29, §78 Ezek 23:5, §79 1 Cor 13:5, Matt 7:6. In every case where the pt-BR wording could resemble the traditional Almeida Bible text, at least one word or clause diverges from Almeida in a way that instead matches the English exactly (tense, "of" vs "with", presence/absence of a clause, word choice), confirming the rendering was built from the English and not pulled toward the memorized Portuguese verse.
- 2026-09-27 (review round 2): Close calls checked against source and the fixed-preposition table, and NOT changed (recorded so later rounds don't re-flag them): §15 "está unido a ele" and §24 "uniu...ao Pai" render English "with" via the verb "unir a" (Portuguese grammar requires "unir a", not "unir com") — a valence necessity, not a slot violation. §47 "the way...lies from One Spirit through the One Son to the One Father" → "vai do Espírito, por meio do Filho, ao Pai" — this is a directional path metaphor ("de...a", as in "vou de casa ao trabalho"), a different construction from the of/from ambiguity the "from" rule targets (§12); the two other true agent/source "from" instances in the same range (§49, §52: "from the Lord the Spirit" → "da parte do Senhor") do use the fixed mapping. §38/§46 "proceeds from the Father" → "procede do Pai" kept as the standard Portuguese Nicene-Creed wording, not changed to "a partir do Pai". §48 "fulfilment of nature" → "complemento da natureza" is a looser lexical choice than "cumprimento" but not inaccurate; left as a style nuance. §54 Psalm 139:7 "whither shall I go from your spirit? ... flee from your presence?" → "para onde irei do teu espírito? Ou para onde fugirei da tua presença?": checked clause by clause against the English (not against memory of Almeida, per the "Translate the source's wording" rule). Both clauses map word for word onto the English ("go"→"irei", "from"→"do/da", "presence"→"presença"); the second clause's "presença" is NOT Almeida's traditional wording for this verse (Almeida has "face"), which shows the rendering was built from the English, not pulled toward the familiar Bible verse — the overlap with Almeida on clause 1 is coincidental, not evidence of drift. Kept unchanged.
