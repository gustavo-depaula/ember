# Translation Journal — Epistle of Barnabas (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (Roberts-Donaldson translation, revised by Kevin Knight for New Advent)
Target: pt-BR

## Key Terms

| English | Portuguese | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| the way of light / the way of darkness | o caminho da luz / o caminho das trevas | Chs. 18–20, the Two Ways section |
| covenant / testament | aliança / testamento | Both English words render Barnabas's alternating "covenant"/"testament"; kept the same distinction in Portuguese (aliança for covenant, testamento for testament) rather than collapsing to one term |
| the Black One | o Negro | Ch. 4 — an epithet for Satan in the source; kept literal rather than substituting a more familiar Portuguese devil-epithet, since the source itself is using an unusual title |
| circumcision (spiritual) | circuncisão | Ch. 9, the Ι-Η-Τ numerological exegesis (318 = 18 + 300) — kept the Greek letters Ι, Η, Τ inline as the source does, since the argument depends on the Greek numeral values |
| Sabbath | Sábado | Capitalized throughout, matching the source's capitalization pattern |
| the two ways (doctrine of) | os dois caminhos (de doutrina) | Standard rendering also used in the Didache tradition |

## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-07: Single continuous work, 21 chapters in one file (`ch001.md`), each chapter a single paragraph in the source — preserved that one-paragraph-per-chapter structure exactly, no splitting.
- 2026-09-07: Scripture book names localized to standard Portuguese Catholic usage throughout (Isaiah → Isaías, Jeremiah → Jeremias, Genesis → Gênesis, Exodus → Êxodo, Deuteronomy → Deuteronômio, Leviticus → Levítico, Numbers → Números, Zechariah → Zacarias, Zephaniah → Sofonias, Ezekiel → Ezequiel, Daniel → Daniel, Haggai → Ageu, Ephesians → Efésios, Romans → Romanos, Colossians → Colossenses, Hebrews → Hebreus, 1 Timothy → 1 Timóteo, 2 Corinthians → 2 Coríntios, John → João, Acts → Atos, James → Tiago) — matching the convention already established in the Ignatius/Polycarp pt-BR translations in this book series.
- 2026-09-07: No editor/scholarly footnotes present in the source to drop — this is a straight translated text with no footnote apparatus, only inline Scripture citations following certain quotations (not all — many quotations in the source, especially in Ch. 6–16, are given without any citation; left uncited to match).
- 2026-09-07: Ch. 9's Greek-letter numerology (Ι = 10, Η = 8, Τ = 300, spelling ΙΗΤ as an abbreviation of Jesus + the cross) was left with the Greek letters exactly as in the source, since translating "Ten by Ι, and Eight by Η" any other way would break the numerical argument the chapter is making.
- 2026-09-07: Ch. 10's Latin/Greek-fish and animal-symbolism section ("lamprey," "polypus," "cuttlefish," "hyena," "weasel") translated with standard Portuguese zoological terms (lampreia, polvo, sépia, hiena, doninha) rather than more archaic alternatives, for clarity.
- 2026-09-07: Ch. 7's shrub name "*Rachia*" kept in italics and untranslated (transliterated proper name in the source itself, of uncertain identity — commentators disagree on the plant).
- 2026-09-07: Modernization check: no ethnic/religious slurs or outdated terms requiring the "modernize and note" treatment appeared in this text (unlike Montfort's "mahométans"). Ch. 9's mention of "Syrian and Arab" circumcision customs was left as a neutral historical/ethnographic reference, matching the source's own neutral tone.
- 2026-09-07 — **review round 1, four fixes applied**: five parallel agents audited all 21 chapters. Fixed: Ch. 4 "spiritually-minded" was over-translated as "espiritualmente sábios" (wise, not minded) — corrected to "mentalidade espiritual". Ch. 6 "the formation of Adam was from the face of the earth" had drifted to "do pó da terra" (dust) instead of "da face da terra" (face/surface) — corrected. Ch. 12 "the Son of man, but the Son of God" had asymmetric capitalization ("filho do homem" vs "Filho de Deus") for two grammatically parallel titles — capitalized both ("Filho do Homem"). Ch. 19 one clause ("if you are partakers...") had slipped from the second-person-singular "tu" register used throughout the Two Ways section into "vós" ("se sois" instead of "se és") — corrected to match.
- 2026-09-07 — **two candidates examined and dropped as non-defects**: Ch. 13's rendering of Genesis 25:23 ("um povo será mais forte que o outro, e o maior servirá ao menor") was flagged as departing from Barnabas's specific English wording ("shall surpass"/"the elder"), but this phrasing is the standard Portuguese Almeida Bible rendering of the verse ("um povo será mais forte do que o outro povo, e o maior servirá ao menor") — using the receptor language's standard vernacular Bible text for an Old Testament quotation is the same choice made elsewhere in this book series and is preferred over a fresh literal rendering of the source's own paraphrase. Ch. 16's "meted out heaven with a span, and the earth with his palm" → "com o palmo... e a terra com a mão" loses the span/palm unit-of-measure pairing but was judged too minor to touch (no dropped content, no changed meaning). A future pass should not re-litigate either.
- 2026-09-07 — **review round 2, three fixes applied**: three fresh independent agents re-audited all 21 chapters (a different partition than round 1, to avoid blind spots). Fixed: Ch. 2's quoted oracle mixed a *você*-register imperative ("pise") into a sentence otherwise consistently *vós* ("vindes", "tragais") — corrected to "piseis". Ch. 3's "You shall..." list mixed *você*-style subjunctive imperatives ("reparta", "vista", "recolha") into a sentence otherwise consistently *tu* ("teu", "vires", "desprezes", "te afastes") — corrected to the *tu* imperatives "reparte", "veste", "recolhe". Ch. 5's Isaiah 53:7 quote ("brought as a sheep to the slaughter... as a lamb which is dumb before its shearer") had collapsed both distinct animal terms to "cordeiro" — differentiated to "cordeiro ao matadouro" / "ovelha muda... tosquia", matching the standard Portuguese (Almeida) rendering of the verse.
- 2026-09-07 — **Ch. 12 capitalization re-examined, round-1 fix kept**: a round-2 agent re-flagged "Filho do Homem" (capitalized in round 1) arguing the English's asymmetric capitalization ("the Son of **man**, but the Son of **God**") is a deliberate rhetorical deflation that capitalizing both erases. On inspection this is a false lead: "the Son of man" with lowercase "man" is simply the standard *English* Bible capitalization convention for this specific Danielic/Gospel title (KJV and its tradition always render it this way), not a deliberate contrast — the title itself is fully dignified. Standard Portuguese Bible convention (Almeida and others) always capitalizes both words of the equivalent title, "Filho do Homem". The round-1 fix is therefore correct and was kept; do not re-flip this in a future pass.
- 2026-09-07 — **review round 3, one fix applied**: two fresh independent agents (a third partition) re-audited all 21 chapters. Fixed: Ch. 13's Genesis 25:23 quote rendered "the elder shall serve the younger" as "o maior servirá ao menor" ("bigger/smaller"), inconsistent with the *same* birth-order concept appearing twice more later in the same chapter (around Genesis 48:19), correctly rendered "mais velho"/"mais novo". Changed the 25:23 rendering to "o mais velho servirá ao mais novo" for internal consistency — this supersedes the round-1 journal note that treated "maior/menor" as matching standard Almeida phrasing; internal consistency within one chapter's argument takes priority over matching a different verse's standard translation when the two collide.
