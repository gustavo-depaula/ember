## Translation Decisions

- Paragraph numbers written as bold `**N.**` per `.claude/rules/books.md`. The en-US still uses `N. ` list markers; left as is (source edited only for OCR slips).
- Address follows the source: the Jew is addressed in tu; where the source shifts to plural "you" in ¶5–6 ("from you", "you have been darkened", "you wander"), the pt-BR shifts to vós, and returns to tu where the source returns to the singular ("how do you indulge", "your back").
- Scripture (Ps 69/LXX 68, Wis 2 and 5, Ps 2:5) rendered from the source's wording, not from a Portuguese Bible, because Hippolytus glosses word by word ("there is no substance, that is, help"; "Then I restored that which I took not away").
- "the sixty-ninth Psalm" → "salmo sessenta e nove", the source's numbering.
- ANF parenthetical glosses and supplied words ("(and)", "(strain)", "(or, from my hoping)", "(of temptations)", "(that is, Hades)") kept in parentheses in the same places.
- Reverential capitals where the source capitalizes, including in quotations; lowercase kept where the source has lowercase (Wis 2: "he calls himself", "his life"), mirroring the edition.
- Final line "The conclusion is missing." is an editorial notice that is part of the source file's structure; rendered "Falta a conclusão." to keep the paragraph count aligned.
- No editor footnotes in the source; none added.

## Source slips

Fixed in en-US (OCR-class):
- ¶3, ¶6 (twice), ¶7 `;”that` → `;” that` (missing space).
- ¶6 "(and ) thus" → "(and) thus".
- ¶9 "solomon speaks" → "Solomon speaks".
- ¶10 "His end to he without honour" → "to be"; "bath not shined" → "hath not shined".

Mirrored, not corrected (edition readings):
- ¶8 attributes "You bartered the Christ like a slave…" to Job; the words are not in Job. Kept as the edition has it.

## Review log

### Round 1

- Focus: completeness paragraph by paragraph against canonical en-US, including quotations, parentheticals and Scripture references. Verdict: CLEAN; no objective defects confirmed and no text or Key Terms changes.
- Rejected: the ten emphasis flags in ch001 [2]–[11], ¶1–10. Source number markers `1.` through `10.` correspond to pt-BR `**1.**` through `**10.**`; `.claude/rules/books.md` requires inert bold authorial numbers. No prose emphasis was added.
- Rejected: ch001 [4], ¶3, source `I endured the death which was not mine by sinning` / pt-BR `suportei a morte que não era minha por ter pecado`. The negative relative clause can deny a death owed to personal sin, as in the source; the following `for I did not sin` / `porque eu não pequei` confirms that reading. Clarifying the scope would be stylistic, not correction of an objective contradiction.
- Rejected: ch001 [9], ¶8, source `you went to Him like a robber in the garden` / pt-BR `foste a Ele como a um ladrão no jardim`. The English comparison can describe approaching Him as one would a robber; it does not unambiguously identify the approaching subject as the robber. The Portuguese makes a defensible attachment explicit.
