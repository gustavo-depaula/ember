## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-07: Rendered the closing scriptural allusion (1 Cor. 2:9, "eye has not seen...") using the wording already established in this corpus (`morrow-my-catholic-faith/pt-BR/lesson-083.md`): "olho não viu, nem ouvido ouviu, nem entrou no coração do homem, o que Deus preparou para aqueles que O amam" — for consistency across books rather than a fresh literal rendering.
- 2026-09-07: "distributing according to each one's deeds the temporary punishments for (different) characters" restructured slightly for natural Portuguese word order ("distribuindo os castigos temporários segundo as obras e a índole de cada um") — same meaning, avoids an awkward literal calque of "characters."
- 2026-09-07: "hymned by the angels" rendered as "louvados com hinos pelos anjos" rather than a coined verb, since Portuguese has no natural verb form of "hinar."
- No editor footnotes present in the source (single continuous patristic fragment); nothing to drop.
- No Latin phrases in the source to preserve inline.
- 2026-09-07 (round-1 review): Capitalized "teu" → "Teu" in "Justo é o Teu julgamento" for reverential-pronoun consistency with "O" in "aqueles que O amam" later in the same paragraph, matching the en-US source's capitalization of both "Your" and "Him."
- 2026-09-07 (round-2 review): Fixed a meaning-reversing agent/patient swap in paragraph 3 — "Pois já não são vistos pelos justos" (wrongly said the unrighteous are no longer seen by the righteous) corrected to "Pois os justos já não são vistos por eles" (the righteous are no longer seen by the unrighteous), matching "For neither are the righteous seen by them any longer."
- 2026-09-07 (round-2 review): Capitalized "deus" → "Deus" in paragraph 1 ("que honraram como Deus as obras vãs...") for consistency with all 10 other references to God in the file.
- 2026-09-07: Rounds 3 and 4 of review found no further issues (clean). Review closed after 4 rounds, 2 of them consecutive clean.
- 2026-09-07 (post-merge review): Fixed a null-subject antecedent inversion in paragraph 3. "Pois os justos já não são vistos por eles, nem são dignos de lembrança" left "os justos" as the implicit subject of the second clause, asserting that *the righteous* are unworthy of remembrance. The en-US "nor are they worthy of remembrance" refers to the unrighteous — the next sentence's contrastive "But the righteous will remember only the righteous deeds" only works if what precedes says the wicked are not remembered. Made the subject explicit: "nem os injustos são dignos de lembrança." Portuguese cannot carry the English pronoun's ambiguity here, so the antecedent has to be named.
- 2026-09-07 (post-merge review): The antecedent above is a deliberate, contested call — a later review round argued for the opposite reading (that "they" continues the grammatical subject "the righteous"). Recorded here so it is not re-litigated blindly. Three reasons the unrighteous is the right antecedent:
  1. **Theology.** "Worthy of remembrance" is a judgment of desert. Saying the righteous are *not* worthy of remembrance contradicts Ps 111:6 ("the just shall be in everlasting remembrance"); saying it of the wicked is the standard patristic trope from Prov 10:7 ("the name of the wicked shall rot") and Ps 9:6.
  2. **The contrastive "But".** "But the righteous will remember only the righteous deeds by which they reached the heavenly kingdom" only forms a contrast if the preceding clause says the wicked go unremembered.
  3. **Whiston's independent rendering** of the same Greek (the text also circulated as Ps.-Josephus, *Discourse to the Greeks concerning Hades*) reads "nor are they **thought** worthy of remembrance" — an explicit judgment passive, which is coherent of the wicked and incoherent of the just.
  Grammatical parallelism does favour the other reading in English, which is why the ambiguity survives in ANF; it cannot survive in Portuguese, so the antecedent is named.
- 2026-09-07 (post-merge review): Capitalized "lhe" → "Lhe" in "Pois o Pai Lhe confiou todo o julgamento" (en-US "the Father has committed all judgment to Him"). The dative clitic was the one divine pronoun in the file still lowercase; every other one — Ele, Aquele, Teu, "que O amam", "A Ele seja a glória" — was already capitalized, including by the round-1 "teu" → "Teu" fix. Oblique clitics are easy to miss when sweeping for reverential capitalization; relative pronouns ("a quem", "do qual") stay lowercase by the same convention.
- 2026-09-07 (second post-merge review): Restored a dropped divine possessive in paragraph 3. "estando presentes a essa sentença judicial" rendered en-US "And being present at **His** judicial decision" with a demonstrative, so the attribution of the decision to the Judge was only inferable from the preceding sentence rather than asserted. Corrected to "estando presentes à Sua sentença judicial." Note *why* three earlier reverential-capitalization sweeps missed it: the file's divine-pronoun inventory realizes nominative (Ele, Aquele), dative clitic (Lhe), accusative clitic ("que O amam"), prepositional (A Ele) and possessive-of-address (Teu) — but the third-person possessive was **absent, not miscased**, and a form that was never rendered cannot be caught by checking casing. en-US has exactly two divine possessives: "Your judgment" (rendered, Teu) and "His judicial decision" (unrendered until now).
- 2026-09-07 (second post-merge review): Considered and rejected — "estes últimos", "por isso" in "ainda que se corrompa, não é por isso destruído" (the concessive still asserts the body is not destroyed; propositions match "it is not at least destroyed"), and the bracket scope of "(como se já) sentissem" vs "(as if they were) already feeling" (parenthesis placement only, no proposition differs).
- 2026-09-07 (second post-merge review): Further considered-and-rejected findings, recorded so later rounds do not re-tread them. None is a defect; in each the proposition is unchanged. (1) "ídolos por eles mesmos fabricados" for "idols fashioned (by themselves)" — content rendered, only ANF's supplement-marking parentheses dropped, same class as "(como se já)". (2) "E ali habitam os justos desde o princípio" for "And there the righteous from the beginning dwell" — pt reads "desde o princípio" adverbially with the verb rather than attributively with the subject; the en word order is itself ambiguous and the two readings are truth-conditionally equivalent here (unlike the "worthy of remembrance" case, where they were theologically opposite). Whiston is likewise adverbial: "in which the just have dwelt from the beginning of the world." (3) "é algo que não se pode dizer de Deus" for "is a word which cannot be said of God" — "word" flattened to "algo". (4) "para ser novamente plasmado" for "to be moulded again anew" — collapses ANF's redundant "again anew" to one adverb. (5) "os conduzem" for "send them along". (6) "por Deus no tempo, segundo a opinião de Platão" vs "by God, according to the opinion of Plato, in time" — the Plato attribution is parenthetical over the whole clause in both, so its scope over "in time" is unchanged.
- 2026-09-07 (second post-merge review): Review closed after 4 further rounds, the last 2 consecutive clean. Reverential capitalization is now complete across all pronoun forms the file realizes (Ele, Aquele, Lhe, O, A Ele, Teu, Sua), with relatives (a quem, do qual) correctly lowercase. book.json metadata verified: name/toc title/H1 agree in both languages, "Hipólito" matches the corpus pattern for father-name localization, and the source URL does not collide with the other Hippolytus books.
- 2026-09-07 (second post-merge review): The en-US source file was cross-checked against the published New Advent ANF text and matches it, **including its oddities** — do not "correct" these locally. "drag them on to the confines **or** hell" is a typo in ANF/New Advent itself for "confines **of** hell"; the pt-BR already renders the intended sense ("os confins do inferno"), which is correct. Likewise "although it lacks no water" (litotes, cf. Whiston "though it will not be void of moisture"), the redundant "no burning sun", and the hyphenations "un-fading"/"un-quenchable" are all genuine ANF readings. book.json cites New Advent's *modernized* ANF ("Righteous is Your judgment"), so a future diff against CCEL's 1886 printing will show systematic Thy/Your-class differences that are editorial, not errors.
- 2026-09-07 (post-merge review): Considered and rejected a reported defect in "não sem que primeiro tenha morrido, se dissolvido e se misturado com a terra" — a shared auxiliary elided across a coordinated participle chain carrying a clitic is ordinary Portuguese (cf. "tinha caído e se machucado"). The reflexive also renders the en-US passives ("been dissolved", "mingled with earth") idiomatically. Left as written.
