## Translation Decisions

- Paragraph numbers are bold `**N.**` per `.claude/rules/books.md`; the en-US still uses `N. ` markers and was left as is.
- Address: vós for the congregation (¶2, 4, 6, 8, 10 "Vedes, amados"); vós in John's words to the crowd (¶3); John addresses Christ as Tu/Te/Ti/Teu, capitalized throughout (the source has two stray lowercase "you" in ¶4); Christ addresses John as tu. ¶9 "sent because of you, that He may show you" → tu, leading into ¶10 "O man" (singular).
- Divine first person (Christ in ¶5, the Father's voice, the Lord in Isa 1) stays lowercase (eu, me, meu), as in the source. Third-person pronouns for God and Christ are capitalized where the source capitalizes them; inside Scripture quotations lowercase source pronouns stay lowercase (¶9 "se movia").
- ¶9 "show you to be the Son of God" → "que tu és filho de Deus": the referent is the baptized person, not Christ (¶10 "a son of God"), so the capital S was not carried over.
- Scripture is rendered from the source's wording, not a Portuguese Bible: Ps 148:4 "e a água que está acima dos céus"; Ps 29:3 with ANF's parenthetical "(está)"; Isa 1:16–19 "argumentemos juntos" for "let us reason together". ANF parentheses — "(regarding You)", "(came)", "(spirit)", "(who is)", "(to sum up …)" — are kept in place.
- No inline Scripture references and no footnotes in the source; none added.

## Source edits

Fixed in en-US (OCR-class):
- ¶2 (Ps 114:5) "that you reddest" → "that you fled" (scan corruption of ANF "fleddest").
- ¶3 "apply the law" → "I apply the law"; "teach as a slave" → "I teach as a slave" (dropped "I" breaking the parallel series).
- ¶5 "righteousness:”I am" → "righteousness:” I am" (missing space).
- ¶8 "regeneration of the layer" → "laver"; ¶10 "the layer of regeneration" → "laver".

  - [11] ¶10, “E como?, diz alguém”: “And how? Says one.” A vírgula delimita a oração de elocução após a pergunta; a combinação com o ponto de interrogação é defensável, não defeito objetivo.

### Round 4

- Rejeitados:

  - [6] ¶5 “Then at length John suffers Him” → “Então, enfim, João consente”: “suffer” = permitir; “consentir” intransitivo carrega o mesmo objeto implícito, nada se perde.

  - [10] ¶9 “the power of the Highest shall overshadow you” → “te cobrirá com a sua sombra”: “sua” remete ao Altíssimo/poder; locução usual para “overshadow”, sem acréscimo de sentido.
