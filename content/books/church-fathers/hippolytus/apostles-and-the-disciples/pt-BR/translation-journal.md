## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-03: This work is almost entirely a list of proper names (apostles, disciples) and place names (sees/bishoprics). Names are rendered in their standard modern Portuguese liturgical form (Tiago, Filipe, Bartolomeu, Estêvão, etc.) rather than left in English/Latin spelling, matching how these figures are named elsewhere in the pt-BR corpus (e.g. `montfort-true-devotion` pt-BR, saints' lives books).
- Place names are given their standard Portuguese exonym where one is well established (Roma, Éfeso, Antioquia, Alexandria, Cartago, Damasco, Jerusalém, Atenas, etc.) and transliterated phonetically where no standard Portuguese form is attested (e.g. Betania, Calamene, Bibloupolis → Bíblopolis, Panellas → Panelas). No place names were "corrected" against modern historical scholarship — the source's own geography (including its odd repeats, e.g. two men both "bishop of Colophonia") is preserved as-is; this is a translation, not a critical edition.
- Editorial insertions the English translator (MacMahon) italicized to smooth the Greek's telegraphic style (*a town*, *preached*, *called*) are kept in Portuguese with the same italic convention (*uma cidade*, *pregou*, *chamado*), since they mark the same kind of supplied word.
- No footnotes in the source; none added.
- 2026-09-03, review pass 1 (bilingual fidelity read): first draft had drifted from the "transliterate, don't correct" rule in four places — `Allanum` had lost a letter (`Alanum`), `Carchedon in Gaul` (v18) and `Carthage` (v19) had both been rendered `Cartago`, erasing a distinction the source itself keeps between two spellings, and `Odyssus` (v21) / `Olympus` (v42) had been swapped for their scholarly/biblical name forms (`Odessos`, `Olimpas`) instead of transliterated as spelled. Fixed to `Allanum`, `Carquedão` (v18, distinct from `Cartago` in v19), `Odisso`, `Olimpo`.
- 2026-09-03, review passes 2–3 (mechanical structural count + monolingual pt-BR-only read): both reported clean — counts matched exactly (81/81 numbered items, 3/3 headings, 8/8 italic spans, 86/86 paragraphs), diacritics and grammar read naturally throughout.
- 2026-09-03, review pass 4 (deliberate sense-level re-read, prompted by the `On the Trinity` journal lesson that mechanical/naturalness-only clean verdicts miss fluent-but-wrong sentences): found two false-friend/idiom defects the earlier passes missed. v13 rendered "the assumption of Christ" as *a assunção de Cristo* — a literal cognate, but in Portuguese theological usage *Assunção* is reserved for Mary's bodily Assumption; Christ being taken up to heaven is *Ascensão*. Fixed to *a Ascensão de Cristo*. The "these two" paragraph rendered "they were honoured to preach" as *foram honrados a pregar* — "honrado a + infinitive" isn't idiomatic Portuguese (a calque of the English construction); fixed to *tiveram a honra de pregar*.
