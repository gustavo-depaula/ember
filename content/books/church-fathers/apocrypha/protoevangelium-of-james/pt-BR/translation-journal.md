## Translation Decisions

- **Address.** Human singular addressees use *tu*, plural *vós*, following the sibling Church Fathers pt-BR (e.g. `tertullian/martyrdom-of-perpetua-and-felicity`). Prayers to God (Anna ¶2–3, the priests ¶6, Salome ¶20) use the reverent *Vós* with 2nd-person-plural verbs, per the `augustine/creed` and `augustine-confessions` convention. The source's archaic "hast/wast" mixed with "you" is flattened to consistent *tu*.
- **Divine pronouns.** Capitalized where the source capitalizes them (Ele, O, dEle, Vós); lowercase where it does not ("his star", "worship him" in the Magi's and Herod's words, ¶21).
- **Paragraph numbers** written as inert bold (`**1.**`) rather than the en-US file's `1. ` list markers, per `.claude/rules/books.md`. Numbering and paragraph breaks unchanged, including the unnumbered paragraphs after ¶18, ¶19 and the closing colophon.
- **Footnotes.** The source carries no footnotes. The ANF/New Advent inline Scripture references (e.g. "Tobit 2:10", "Luke 1:34, 44") are editorial apparatus but sit inline in the text; kept in place with full pt-BR book names and the source's colon format ("Tobias 2:10", "Lucas 1:34, 44"), as the sibling Perpetua translation does. The references are mirrored as printed, even where they point loosely (¶12 "Luke 1:34, 44").
- **Scripture echoes** (Ave ¶11, Isabel's greeting ¶12, Matthew 1:20–21 ¶14, the Magi ¶21) rendered from the source's wording, not harmonized to a Portuguese Bible: e.g. ¶11 "Eis a serva do Senhor diante da sua face", "Senhor de todas as coisas" (Lord of all).
- **¶6 "they led her astray".** Walker's English here is a known mistranslation (the Greek means the girls amused/entertained her). The source wording is kept ("elas a desencaminhavam") and a single *Nota do tradutor* explains it, since otherwise a reader would take the sentence as a slur on Mary's purity.
- **¶13 "Who is it that has hunted me down?"** → "Quem foi que me armou esta cilada?", the idiomatic Portuguese for being caught by a trap/pursuer.
- **¶24 "fretwork of the temple"** → "os lavores do templo" (carved panelling).
- **Punctuation.** The source's spaced em-dash asides ("strange things— because") use a spaced em dash in Portuguese.
- 2026-09-27: The en-US OCR slips recorded in this journal were corrected in the en-US source. The pt-BR text already rendered the corrected reading.
