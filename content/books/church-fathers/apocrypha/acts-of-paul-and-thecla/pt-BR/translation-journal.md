## Translation Decisions

- **Address.** Human singular addressees use *tu*, plural *vós*. Prayers to God and Christ use reverent *Vós* with 2nd-person-plural verbs, as in the sibling apocrypha, including where the source mixes "hast" and "You". The heavenly voice to Thecla uses *tu*.
- **Divine pronouns** capitalized where the source capitalizes them.
- **Direct speech** follows a colon without quotation marks, as in the source and the sibling apocrypha.
- **Inline Scripture references** (New Advent apparatus) kept in place with pt-BR book names and the source's colon format (`Mateus 5:8`, `1 Coríntios 6:18-19`).
- **Beatitudes** rendered from the source's wording with "Bem-aventurados", not harmonized to a Portuguese Bible.
- **Italic supplied words** (*named*, *to change their opinions*, *wretch*, *the lioness*, *in*, *the God*) kept in italics on their Portuguese counterparts.
- **Source oddities kept literal:** Paul's prayer "let not the fire touch Thecla" though she has already been saved; "praying six days"; "she stood beside… and she, standing behind him" in the same sentence; "a bear… tore her" rendered with *ursa*.
- **Editorial line** before the alternative ending ("Instead of the last two sections, the ms. which Dr. Grabe used…") is part of the source text and is translated. Its `:—` becomes a colon.
- **"Tryphæna… died beside the Abaci ."** The stray space before the period is dropped in pt-BR.
- **Footnotes.** The source carries none. No translator notes added.
- **Source edits.** None.

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness, paragraph by paragraph, plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 33↔33 paragraphs, nothing dropped or added; all six Scripture references match; two defects fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶16 (Paul to Thecla): "e que não a resistas" → "e que não lhe resistas". *Resistir* governs *a*.
- ¶24 (governor to Alexander, "Do what you will"): "Fazei o que quiserdes" → "Faze o que quiseres". A single addressee, so *tu* per the Address rule.

Rejected:
- ¶21 "Take off all of us, O proconsul!" → "Leva-nos a todas": "take off" is literal "take away (to death)"; *levar* carries the same sense. Not a defect.
- ¶27 "found Paul speaking the word of God" → "pregando a palavra de Deus": standard rendering of "speaking the word" in this context; no change in meaning.
- ¶32 "And it guided her" → "E a nuvem a guiou": names the antecedent of "it" (the cloud of light), no added content.
- ¶33 "And thus is her consummation" → "assim foi a sua consumação": the Greek has no verb; tense is a free choice.
- ¶17 "pediu ao governador que, disse ela, eu permaneça pura": present subjunctive follows the source's slide into direct speech ("said she, I may remain").

### Round 2

Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read, plus the mechanical audit and a Key Terms frequency check. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- ¶9 "the resurrection … has taken place" → "já aconteceu": *já* makes the present perfect's completed sense explicit; the meaning is unchanged.
- ¶27 "thinking that some new trial was coming upon her" → "lhe sobreviera": the Greek is a perfect (ἐφέστηκεν, "has come upon"); both tenses say the same thing in context.
- ¶32 "for there shall be for you an everlasting house" → "pois ali haverá": the Greek has locative ἐκεῖ, and the next clause "there you shall obtain shelter" supports it.
- ¶32 "if she ask anything of her, she hears her" → "se ela pede alguma coisa à deusa, esta a ouve": the Portuguese must commit to referents. Thecla asks and Artemis hears, as in the Greek.
- ¶23 "the women seeing" → "vendo-o": adds only an object pronoun, no content.

### Round 3

Focus: source-blind cold read of the pt-BR, with each mark then checked against en-US; a sweep of spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation and punctuation; the mechanical audit. Verdict: chapter text clean; one journal defect fixed.

Paragraphs are counted with the title as ¶1.

Fixed:
- Round 2 log: its locations were file line numbers (¶17, ¶53, ¶63, ¶45) while Round 1 counts paragraphs, so "¶17" named two passages and ¶53/¶63 did not exist. Relabelled ¶9, ¶27, ¶32, ¶23.

Rejected:
- ¶19 "loving her as much as her daughter Falconilla" → "amando-a tanto quanto à sua filha": a direct object with a preposition in a comparison (cf. *estimo-o como a um irmão*). It marks the daughter as the one loved, not the one loving. Not a crase error.
- ¶25 "whoever shall not believe in Him" → "n'Ele": the usual reverential contraction. It occurs once, so the file is consistent.
- ¶27 "and hears everything from her", ¶28 "she goes into … and fell": the switches into the historical present are the source's.
- ¶27 "prayed over Tryphæna" → "oraram por Trifena": the Greek is ὑπέρ, "on behalf of".
- ¶32 "let me not insult my virginity" → "não me deixeis ultrajar a minha virgindade": odd, but it is the source's wording.

### Round 4

Focus: function words (prepositions, articles, demonstratives, connectors, possessive *seu/sua*, *consigo*), headings, and places where the Portuguese must commit where the source is open, including a turn-by-turn tu/vós trace; plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- ¶4 "the word of the Father shall become to them" → "para elas": *elas* = the virgins, the subject of the beatitude, rather than the grammatical head *corpos*. Defensible.
- ¶13 "Burn the wicked *wretch*" → "Queimai": the addressee is unstated, and Theocleia speaks before the council the governor convened, with the crowd present. Plural fits.
- ¶13 "sought for by her friends" → "pelos seus": "her own people" covers her friends and household; no change in meaning.
- ¶11 "stayed his intention" → "a sua intenção", ¶27 "astonished at seeing her, and the crowd with her" → "ao vê-la, e a multidão com ela": the ambiguity is the source's and is kept.
- ¶15/¶21 "tomb" → "sepulcro", ¶29 "tomb of her master Paul" → "túmulo": "tomb" has no Key Terms row, and these are different places (a roadside tomb, Paul's grave in Rome). A style choice.
