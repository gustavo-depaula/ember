# Translation Journal — On Admonition and Repentance (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (A. Edward Johnston, NPNF Second Series Vol. 13, via New Advent). The Syriac original is not in the corpus.
Target: pt-BR

Sibling precedent: when this book was translated no other Ephraim work had pt-BR; `ephraim/sinful-woman` pt-BR was translated afterwards and takes its conventions from this book. Conventions follow `ambrose/repentance` pt-BR:
- Author "Santo Efrém (306-373)", the form used in the corpus's other pt-BR books (Liguori, Montfort's *Tratado da Verdadeira Devoção*; *Intimidade Divina* has "São Efrém").
- Title "Sobre a Admoestação e a Penitência": repentance → penitência, as in Ambrose's *Sobre a Penitência*.
- Curly quotes “ ”; reverential pronouns lowercase (ele, tu, tua graça); capitals only on nouns the source capitalizes (Nome, Ressurreição, Vontade, Redentor).
- Paragraph numbers as bold `**N.**`. The en-US file still uses `N. ` list markers; it was not converted, since this run limits source edits to OCR slips. The numbering is sequential (1–21), so it renders correctly.

## Translation Decisions

- **Scripture** ¶4 (Deut 6:5) and ¶8 (Prov 5:15, 17) are rendered from the source's wording in italics, not from a Portuguese Bible: ¶4 keeps "de todo o teu coração e a tua alma, e com a tua força". The source gives no references.
- **¶4** "you (who art) dust ... rage fills you" → "a ti, (que és) pó, ... a ira te enche": the pt-BR adds "a ti" so the dangling subject reads as Portuguese.
- **¶4** "though he be not neighbour to your sins" → "ainda que não tenha parte nos teus pecados" (the sense of "neighbour to"), avoiding a literal "próximo" clash with the term table.
- **¶11** "receive not others in your house, and drive not out the beggar" is translated literally; the source's "wearisome.— To them" dash is not transplanted.
- **¶14** "continue in contemplations such as these continue" repeats "continue" in the source. Mirrored as "persevera em contemplações como estas, persevera".
- **¶21** "Who has granted to You, Son of man": the capital "You" is a half-modernized "thee", not a divine pronoun (the parallel next sentence has "you, thing of dust"). Rendered "te ... filho do homem", lowercase.
- Bracketed and parenthetical editorial insertions of the English translator ("[seed]", "(the sinner)", "(of the flesh)") are kept in place, as in the source.

## Source edits (en-US)

- ¶21 "Let this be (the measure of your need, that which the day gives you" had an unclosed parenthesis; closed after "need". pt-BR mirrors the fix.

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness, paragraph by paragraph (¶1–21 + closing aligned one to one; quotations, parentheticals, brackets, Scripture). Verdict: 2 defects, both fixed. No paragraph or sentence dropped, added, merged or split; the source carries no chapter:verse references; Bible names (Jó, Abraão, José, Ananias, Daniel, Israel, Egito) are the Portuguese forms.

Fixed:
- ¶13 "with sorrow and with offerings": "com tristeza" → "com pesar". Key Terms map sorrow → pesar and reserve tristeza for sadness; the ¶13 row reference was added to the table.
- Journal header: claimed *Intimidade Divina* uses "Santo Efrém"; it has "São Efrém" / "S. Efrém". Claim corrected.

Rejected:
- ¶6 "for his sickness, there is no healing" → "não há saúde": saúde in the sense of cure/recovery is attested Portuguese and pairs with the parallel "não há cura"; not a mistranslation.
- ¶4 "And if he (the other) is not to encounter you in fury" → "E, para que ele (o outro) não te encontre em fúria": the English "if he is not to" states a purpose condition; the final clause is a faithful reading.
- ¶21 "the yoke of indigence that is honourable" → "o jugo da indigência, que é honroso": the English antecedent is open; agreeing with jugo is a defensible commitment.
- Closing "But as for us" → "Quanto a nós": the contrast is carried by "quanto a"; no content lost.

### Round 2

Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read (negation, subject/object, mood, antecedents, false friends, quotation wording), plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 1 defect, fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶7 "your tongue has made a harp for the music of the devil": "a tua língua fez-se harpa" → "a tua língua fez uma harpa". "Fez-se" made the tongue the harp; in the source the tongue makes one.

Rejected:
- ¶3 "If you should insult the King's image, you shall pay" → "Se insultasses ..., pagarias": the English "should" is hypothetical; the conditional mood carries it.
- ¶1 "Not of compulsion" → "Não é de constrangimento": constrangimento in the sense of coercion is current Portuguese (cf. "constrangimento ilegal"); not a false friend here.
- ¶5 "if it should go to Sheol with you" → "se ela descer contigo": "ela" reads naturally as ira, the nearest antecedent, matching the source's "it" (wrath).
- ¶9 "the lust of raiment" → "o desejo das vestes": the Key Terms row covers plural "lusts" (concupiscências); a singular desire for clothing is not that term.
- ¶15 "not even though you be rich" → "nem mesmo que sejas rico": concessive "nem que" + subjunctive is grammatical; style only.
- ¶20 "a stranger, from his place" → "estrangeiro, longe do seu lugar": "longe" makes explicit the sense of "from"; nothing added to the meaning.

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__ephraim__admonition-and-repentance/review-2.md` (outside the repo).

### Round 3

Focus: source-blind cold read of the pt-BR, each mark then checked against the source; spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation, punctuation and quotation-mark sweep; mechanical audit. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- ¶2 "Foste batizado da água": mirrors the source's "baptised from the water".
- ¶4 "Mesmo aquele que está ao teu lado, ..., a mancha da iniquidade o atinge": the broken construction is the source's ("Even he that stands on your side, ... the taint of iniquity reaches unto him").
- ¶6 "Odeia a zombaria ... e a alegria que é (causa de) purificação": the source also tells the reader to hate "mirth which is (the cause of) cleansing"; mirrored, not corrected.
- ¶10 "Guarda o teu coração ..., para que não peque": the subject is coração ("that it sin not"); singular is right.
- ¶21 "para que ele te conceda ... que ouçamos": the source shifts from "you" to "we" in the same way ("grant you ... that we may hear").

### Round 4

Focus: function words (prepositions, articles, demonstratives, connectors, seu/sua), the heading, and places where Portuguese must commit (gender, antecedent, tu/vós addressee trace), plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 1 defect, fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶18 "that you may be a freeman in very truth": "para que sejas livre em toda a verdade" → "para que sejas verdadeiramente livre". "In very truth" is the intensive "truly"; "em toda a verdade" added "all" and read as "free in all truth".

Rejected:
- ¶19 "if so be that freedom is willing" → "contanto que a liberdade o queira": "o" names what the will consents to (the chastisement); no new content.
- ¶18 "how many commandments … how many laws …; which … you can learn all" → "podes aprender todas": agreement with the nearest noun (leis) is grammatical Portuguese.
- ¶4 "because it is holden with lusts" → "porque está preso": the masculine commits "it" to corpo, the natural reading (the body, not rage, is held by lusts).
- ¶19 "hurtful things become profitable" → "tornar-se-ão proveitosas": the future follows "He will watchfully guard"; consequence, not a tense error.

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__ephraim__admonition-and-repentance/review-4.md` (outside the repo).

### Round 5

Focus: per-paragraph term-frequency diff against Key Terms in both directions, every falsifiable journal claim grepped against the files, proper names against standard Portuguese forms and sibling pt-BR works; mechanical audit. Verdict: 1 defect, fixed (journal only; chapter text unchanged).

Fixed:
- Journal header: "no other Ephraim work has pt-BR" was false; `ephraim/sinful-woman/pt-BR/` exists and cites this book as its convention source. Reworded.

Rejected:
- ¶9 "for she is like one" → "pois ela é semelhante a ele": adjectival "semelhante a" (like), not the noun semelhante that the table reserves for "fellow"; no clash.
- ¶6, ¶15, ¶18, ¶21 "desejo" (verb "desejares"; "wish", "desire", "desires"): not the source's "lust(s)"; the table's concupiscências row is unaffected.

- Author form "Santo Efrém": Liguori, Morrow, Butler and pictorial-lives pt-BR use "Santo Efrém"; Montfort has "santo Efrém" in running text; *Intimidade Divina* has "São/S. Efrém", as the header says.

Rejected:
