# Translation Journal — On the Sinful Woman (pt-BR)

Source: en-US (A. Edward Johnston, NPNF Second Series Vol. 13, via New Advent). The Syriac original is not in the corpus.
Target: pt-BR

Conventions follow the sibling `ephraim/admonition-and-repentance` pt-BR:
- Author "Santo Efrém (306-373)".
- Curly quotes “ ”, with ‘ ’ for the quotation inside Jesus' speech (¶15); reverential pronouns lowercase (ele, tu, tua misericórdia); capitals only on nouns the source capitalizes (Homem, Esposo, Mar da Graça, Águia celeste, Médico, Um).
- Paragraph numbers as bold `**N.**`. The en-US file still uses `N. ` list markers; not converted (source edits limited to OCR slips). Numbering is sequential (1–15), so it renders correctly.
- Every speaker addresses a single listener with tu (woman ↔ perfumer, Satan, Simon, Jesus); the opening "beloved" is the plural vós ("Ouvi e consolai-vos, amados").

## Translation Decisions

- **Scripture** is embedded without references (Lk 7:36–50, the miracles in ¶11, Mt 25:34 in the closing). Rendered from the source's wording, not a Portuguese Bible: the closing keeps "Vinde, entrai ... herdai o reino preparado para todos os que fizerem a minha vontade e guardarem todos os meus mandamentos".
- **¶3** "the tunic the garment of reconciliation" lacks a joining word in the source; mirrored as "a túnica, a veste da reconciliação".
- **¶8** "binds up wounds" → "pensa as feridas" (pensar = to dress a wound).
- **¶9** Satan's soliloquy opens without quotation marks in the source while the speeches he rehearses carry them; mirrored.
- **¶14** "I will propose the matter in the feast" is translated as the source has it ("Proporei"), though the next sentences show Simon was the one expected to propose it.
- **¶15** "to him who is even as you" → "junto daquele que é tal como tu".
- Parenthetical and bracketed insertions of the English translator ("(with herself)", "(even)", "(to mark)", "[and soul]", "[watering]", "[receive him]") are kept in place. The source's `:—` before speeches is not transplanted; a colon is used.
- The "* * *" separator between ¶14 and ¶15 and the unnumbered closing doxology are kept.

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness paragraph by paragraph; Scripture names and numbers. Verdict: 2 defects, fixed. All paragraphs, parentheticals and brackets are present; the text has no Scripture references.

Fixed:
- ¶11 "que lhe tocou a orla do manto" → "que lhe agarrou a orla do manto". Source: "who laid hold of the hem of His garment"; "tocou" pulled the line toward the Gospel wording.
- ¶13 "Põe sobre mim a canga do teu jugo" → "Põe sobre mim o ombro do teu jugo". Source: "Lay upon me the shoulder of Your yoke"; "shoulder" was dropped.

Rejected:
- ¶6 "deste seu feito ele temia" (source: "at this doing of hers he was afraid"). "Temer de" is archaic but grammatical, and it follows the source's construction.
- ¶12 "e não tiver conversado com uma meretriz" (source: "and holds not converse with an harlot"). The future perfect follows "If he shall go forth" and keeps the meaning.
- ¶15 "Chamei a este homem justo" (source: "I have called this man righteous"). Keeps the source's ambiguity between "invited" and "named".

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__ephraim__sinful-woman/review-1.md` (outside the repo).

### Round 2

Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read; mechanical audit; Key Terms frequency parity; journal claims. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- ¶1 "como se havia recolhido num só lugar" (source: "how it had forced itself into one place"). Same sense: the Sea of Grace confined to one house.
- ¶8 "cega entre muitos que cegara" (source: "blind among many whom I blinded"). The pluperfect marks the earlier action; the meaning does not change.
- ¶10 "Como entrarei na presença de Jesus, se para ele as coisas secretas são manifestas?" (source: "for to Him the secret things are manifest"). Causal "se" in a rhetorical question; it does not read as a condition.
- ¶11 "Devolveu-lho" (source: "He gave him to her"). The son is given back to the widow; "devolver" is accurate in context.
- ¶15 "não te desagrade que enxugue este santo corpo" (source: "let it not grieve You that it should wipe"). Same meaning in a petition.

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__ephraim__sinful-woman/review-2.md` (outside the repo).

### Round 3

Focus: source-blind cold read of the pt-BR, then a spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation, punctuation and quote-pairing sweep; mechanical audit. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- ¶6 "Pegou-se então a ela" (source: "He clave then to her"). "Pegar-se a" means to cling to.
- ¶7 "Vamos, ó mulher" (source: "Go to, O woman"). "Go to" is an archaic exhortative interjection, not a verb of motion.
- ¶10 "E no seu coração o porei" (source: "And into his heart I will put it"). The pronoun is as open as the source's "it".
- ¶14 "Se tiver fome e fome de pão" (source: "If he be hungry and hunger for bread"). Ellipsis of "tiver", parallel to "se tiver sede, e sede de água".
- ¶14 "receba o que precisa" (source: "let him receive what he needs"). "Precisar" with a direct object is attested; the regency is not broken.
