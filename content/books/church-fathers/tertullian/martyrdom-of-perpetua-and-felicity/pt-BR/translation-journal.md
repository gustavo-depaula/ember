## Translation Decisions

- **Footnotes.** The source file carries no footnotes; nothing to drop. The single inline Scripture reference ("John 16:24") is kept inline in Portuguese book form ("João 16:24"), matching how the sibling Church Fathers pt-BR (Polycarp, Scillitan acts) render New Advent's inline references.
- **Chapter "Argument" headings** are editorial summaries of the ANF edition, but they are part of the chapter headings the reader navigates by; translated, not dropped. Sentence case in Portuguese (the English is title case).
- **Address.** *Tu* for every singular addressee (father ↔ daughter, Saturus ↔ Perpetua, the tribune, Pudens), *vós* for plural, following the Scillitan acts ("Persistes em ser cristão?"). Optatus and Aspasius: "Não és tu o nosso pai, e tu o nosso presbítero, para vos lançardes…" — singular copulas, plural infinitive, as the English addresses each and then both.
- **Scripture in the Preface** (Joel 2 / Acts 2) is rendered from the source's wording, including the shift "their sons and their daughters … your young men … your old men"; not harmonized to any Portuguese Bible.
- **Quotation marks.** The New Advent text misplaces opening quotes at the start of Perpetua's narrative ("“While” says she, we were…") and of Saturus's vision ("“We had suffered,” says he, and we had…"), yet closes both quotes later. The opening quote after the speech tag was restored ("“Enquanto”, diz ela, “estávamos…"), since otherwise the closing mark has no partner. Inner quotes use ‘ ’.
- **Source-wording over Latin sense**, deliberately: "town-hall" → "paço da cidade" (not *fórum*), "dinner" → "à mesa" (not *almoço*), "objectionable to the most noble Cæsar" → "odiosos ao nobilíssimo César", "yielded our minds" → "entregamos os nossos ânimos", "made peace" → "fizemos a paz". The English is the only source; its choices are followed even where the Latin behind it is known to differ.
- **Saturus to the crowd** ("Tomorrow is not enough for you, for you to behold…") kept as a statement, as in the source, though the Latin is a question.
- **"the child no long desired the breast"** — source typo for "no longer"; translated as intended ("não desejou mais o peito").
- **"while he has yet in the prison"** — source typo for "was"; translated as intended.
- **"came near to place"** — missing article in source; "chegamos perto de um lugar".
- **"Possibly such a woman…"** → "Talvez tal mulher…", keeping the source's hedge.
- **Paragraph numbers** written as inert bold (`**1.**`) rather than the en-US file's `1. ` list markers, per `.claude/rules/books.md` (`marked` renumbers list markers). Numbers and breaks are unchanged.
- **Punctuation.** English `:—` before Saturus's vision and `Argument.—` in the chapter 1 heading not transplanted; plain colon / period used.
