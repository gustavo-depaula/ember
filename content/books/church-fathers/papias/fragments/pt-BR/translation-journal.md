## Translation Decisions

- The source has no footnotes; none added.
- Bracketed passages (Eusebius, Irenaeus and the editor's connecting words) are part of the source text and are translated inside the same brackets. Fragment IV opens with an unmatched closing bracket ("said]:"); mirrored as is ("diziam]:"), since it may reflect the printed edition.
- Inline Scripture references are kept in the source's position, with Portuguese book names: "Isaiah 11:6 ff." → "Isaías 11:6 ss."
- Scripture quotations (John 14:2, 1 Corinthians 15:25-28, Isaiah 11:6) are translated from the source's English, not taken from a Portuguese Bible.
- Fragment IV, Judas's question ends with a period in the source ("by the Lord.'"); pt-BR uses a question mark, since the clause is a question introduced by "asking". The doubled period in "ff.." is rendered once.
- Fragment X's numbering "(1.)" … "(4.)" and the cross-references "(2)", "(3)" are kept as plain parenthesised text.

## Source edits (en-US)

- Fragment IV: "And the wolf shall lie, down with the lamb" → "lie down" (stray comma).

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness, paragraph by paragraph; Scripture names and references. Verdict: one defect, fixed.

Fixed:
- X: "e João a chama de Cléofas" → "e João diz que ela é de Cléofas". Source: "whom John names of Cleophas" (John 19:25, "Mary of Clopas"). "Chamar de X" means "call her X", so the pt-BR named her Cleophas instead of "of Cleophas".

Rejected:
- I, Irenaeus quotation, "Now testimony is borne…" → "Disso dá testemunho…" with no "Ora". The "Now" is a weak connective. The IV repetition of the same quotation lacks it, and both pt-BR renderings match. Not an omission of content.
- V, "But that there is this distinction …; for the first will be taken up" → "Mas há esta distinção …: os primeiros serão elevados". The colon carries the explanatory "for", and the indirect "that" is resolved into direct statement. The meaning is unchanged.
- V, "a share is given to all by the Father, according as each one is … worthy" → "a cada um é dada pelo Pai uma parte". The distributive "a cada um" fits the following "each one", and nothing is lost.
- VI, "swallowed a deadly poison" → "bebeu um veneno mortal". "Bebeu" is the normal Portuguese verb for taking a poison. Not a mistranslation.

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__papias__fragments/review-1.md` (outside the repo).

### Round 2

Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read (negation, subject/object, tense, false friends, Scripture wording), plus the mechanical audit and the journal's precedent claims. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- IV, "They shall see who shall come to them" → "Verão os que chegarem a eles". The masculine "eles" does not agree with the nearest feminine noun ("tais produções"), but it points to "aqueles tempos" (opening sentence) and "os tempos" in the next sentence, which glosses the reply. The source's "them" is open, and "those times" fits the passage. Defensible.
- VI, "And the presbyter said this" → "E o presbítero dizia isto". The imperfect gives repeated speech, which the English simple past allows. Not a tense error.
- VI, "some strange parables and instructions of the Saviour" → "algumas parábolas e instruções estranhas do Salvador". A feminine plural adjective placed after two feminine nouns can modify both. The scope is the same as in the source.

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__papias__fragments/review-2.md` (outside the repo).

### Round 3

Focus: source-blind cold read of the pt-BR, each mark then verified against the source; spelling-only sweep (diacritics, crase, hyphenation, punctuation, quote pairing); mechanical audit. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- I, "coisas que dizem Aristião e o presbítero João" reads loosely after the colon. Source: "which things Aristion and the presbyter John, the disciples of the Lord, say." The pt-BR mirrors the source's loose relative; meaning is intact.
- IX has no main verb ("Tomando ocasião de Papias … que entenderam a obra dos seis dias…"). Source: "Taking occasion from Papias … who understood the work of the six days…" The fragment is the source's own (an extract cut from its context); completing it would add content.
- IV, the dangling "não crendo e perguntando: ‘…?’, o Senhor disse" is the source's own anacoluthon ("not believing, and asking, … the Lord said").

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__papias__fragments/review-3.md` (outside the repo).
