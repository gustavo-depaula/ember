## Translation Decisions

- 2026-10-09 — **address form**: a homily to the congregation, so "you" → plural **vós**. Isaiah 54:1 in Ch. 2 addresses the barren woman → singular **tu**; the unbelievers' cry to Jesus in Ch. 17 ("You were") → **tu**; Isaiah 58:9 in Ch. 15 ("Whilst you are still speaking") → singular, as the verse addresses one suppliant.
- 2026-10-09 — **Scripture and agrapha** follow Keith's English clause by clause, not a Portuguese Bible: e.g. Ch. 4 "he that works righteousness", Ch. 5 the Peter–lambs dialogue, Ch. 12 "When two shall be one…", Ch. 13 "No thank have ye" → "Não tendes mérito algum" (χάρις in Lk 6:32 sense), Ch. 16 "better is fasting than prayer".
- 2026-10-09 — **Scripture references** stay inline mid-sentence, in the source's position, with Portuguese book names (Mateus, Isaías, Lucas, 1 Coríntios, Jeremias, Gênesis, Efésios, 1 Pedro, 1 Timóteo, Tiago), as in `first-epistle/pt-BR`.
- 2026-10-09 — **edition oddities mirrored, not corrected** (they are New Advent's/ANF's readings, not OCR slips): "1 Peter 4:4" for "charity covers a multitude of sins" (actually 4:8); "James 5:19-25" (James 5 has 20 verses); "Isaiah 66:18" placed inside the unbelievers' quotation in Ch. 17; "1 Corinthians 7:29" after the Ch. 12 saying; "Matthew 18:11" mid-sentence in Ch. 2. No en-US source edits were made.
- 2026-10-09 — **brackets**: every editorial bracket in the English is kept, in the same place.
- 2026-10-09 — **footnotes**: the source has none; nothing dropped, no translator notes added.
- 2026-10-09 — **chapter headings**: the ANF summaries are translated in sentence case without a trailing period, as the source has none. Ch. 18 "The Author Sinful, Yet Pursuing" → "O autor, pecador, mas perseverante na busca" (pursuing righteousness, per the chapter body).
- 2026-10-09 — **Ch. 17 "engage in withdrawing from idols"**: the English leaves the object implicit (the Greek means drawing others away from idols); "nos ocupamos em afastar dos ídolos" keeps it implicit too, with no added bracket.
- 2026-10-09 — **Ch. 20 "heavily weighed the fetter"**: obscure in the English; rendered literally, "carregou pesadamente os grilhões".

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness, paragraph by paragraph (21/21 paragraphs, 22/22 Scripture citations, 14/14 brackets), plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 1 defect, fixed.

Fixed:
- Ch. 15, "For this recompense we are able to render to the God who created us, if he who speaks and hears both speak and hear with faith and love": "Pois esta é a retribuição que podemos dar ao Deus que nos criou: que quem fala e quem ouve falem e ouçam com fé e amor." → "Pois esta retribuição podemos dar ao Deus que nos criou, se quem fala e quem ouve falarem e ouvirem com fé e amor." The old rendering turned the condition ("if") into a definition, so "esta" pointed forward to speaking and hearing. In the source it points back to turning a soul back to salvation.

Rejected:
- Ch. 17 "that no one of us may perish amiss" → "pereça miseravelmente": the English "amiss" is vague (perish by the way, perish wrongly). "Miseravelmente" interprets it but does not contradict it, so it is a style choice, not a defect.
- Ch. 12 "no thought concerning her as female… him as male" → "como mulher… como homem": the gloss concerns brother and sister, so the person-nouns say what the source says. The saying itself keeps "masculino/feminino".
- Ch. 17 "the elders who show us plainly" → "que nos anunciavam claramente": the unbelievers speak at the judgment about what the elders did in the past. The imperfect fits that context.

### Round 2

Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read of the whole text against `en-US/`, plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- Ch. 2 "But when He said" → "E quando disse": the "But" introduces the next clause of the verse, not a contrast. "E" carries the same sequence.
- Ch. 12 "When two shall be one" → "Quando os dois forem um": the article refers to the pair already under discussion. The meaning is unchanged.
- Ch. 15 "I think not that I counted trivial counsel" → "Não penso que tenha dado um conselho trivial": the Greek verb means "made" (ἐποιησάμην). The sense is unchanged.
- Ch. 16 "found complete in these" → "perfeito": "perfeito" in the sense of whole or complete covers "complete".

Evidence: `ember-translation-evidence/church-fathers__clement-rome__second-epistle/review-2.md` (outside the repo).

### Round 3

Focus: source-blind cold read of the pt-BR, each mark then verified against `en-US/`; then a mechanical spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation, punctuation and quote-pairing sweep, plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- Ch. 3 "Visto, pois, que… como mostraremos… senão não negando aquele…?": the English has the same broken construction ("Since, then, … whereby shall we show…, but by not denying Him…?"). Restructuring it would smooth the source.
- Ch. 13 "não só não amamos os que odeiam, mas nem sequer amamos os que amam": the English also leaves out the object ("those who hate… those who love"). Supplying "nos" would add words.
- Ch. 11 "Insensatos! comparai-vos" (lowercase after "!") and Ch. 14 "fêmea;” Gênesis 1:27; … a Igreja,)": both follow the edition's punctuation ("You fools! compare"; "female;” … the church,)").
- Ch. 6 "servir a Deus e a Mamon" for "serve both God and mammon": the coordination carries "both". This is a style choice.
