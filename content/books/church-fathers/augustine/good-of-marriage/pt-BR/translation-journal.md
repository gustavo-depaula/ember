## Translation Decisions

- 2026-10-02: The two introductory paragraphs (Benedictine editors' preface, signed "*Bened. Ed.*") are in the source body, not footnotes, so they were translated, as `augustine/enchiridion/pt-BR` did with its editorial introduction. Signature rendered "*Ed. Beneditina*". The source has no footnotes; nothing dropped.
- 2026-10-02: Paragraph numbers written `**N.**` per `.claude/rules/books.md`; the en-US file uses `N.` list markers. Numbering is gapless 1–35.
- 2026-10-02: God. Pronouns lowercase ("ele", "dele"), as the sibling Augustine works. The one verse addressed to God (¶2 Psalm) uses the reverent "Vós". Paul's "If you shall have taken a wife" uses "tu"; Scripture addressed to groups uses "vós". Rhetorical "you" (¶29 "if you compare") → "tu".
- 2026-10-02: Scripture follows the source wording clause by clause; no chapter-and-verse references exist in the source and none were added. Examples: ¶2 "Vós me multiplicareis na minha alma pela virtude"; ¶8 "a ciência será destruída"; ¶10 "a mulher que não é casada e a virgem é diferente" (the source's singular verb mirrored); ¶14 1 Pt 3 "sem discurso", "chamando-lhe Senhor" (source capital); ¶32 1 Cor 7:10–11 kept as the source's infinitive-turned-jussive.
- 2026-10-02: ¶13/¶14: the 1 Cor 7:34 clause is "pensa nas coisas do Senhor" (thinks of); ¶10 renders the same verse's "is anxious about" as "cuida das coisas do Senhor", keeping the source's two verbs apart.
- 2026-10-02: ¶18 analogies "what food is …, this intercourse is …" rendered "o que o alimento é …, tal é a relação …", avoiding "isto é", which reads as "that is" in Portuguese.
- 2026-10-02: ¶5 "who shall say that she sins not, when he is aware" — the source's "he" left unresolved ("quando sabe").
- 2026-10-02: ¶35 "actual chastity" → "castidade efetiva" (in deed, not "atual").
- 2026-10-02: Quotes curly, matching the en-US file and the sibling Augustine translations. The source's unquoted speech (¶10 objectors, ¶26 "He has a devil", ¶27 the questioner) reproduced unquoted.

## Review log

### Round 1

Focus: completeness paragraph by paragraph (38 blocks aligned one to one; quotations, parentheticals, Latin and references checked), Bible names and references, plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 2 defects, fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶3 "marido e mulher se unem" → "marido e esposa se unem" ("husband and wife cleave"). Contradicted the husband/wife → marido/esposa Key Term, which the same paragraph and the other seven "husband and/or wife" follow.
- ¶32 "Pois que o matrimônio se dá …, o Apóstolo é testemunha" → "Portanto, que o matrimônio …". Source "Therefore"; "Pois que" reads as causal "since" and changes the sentence's logic.

Rejected:
- ¶3 "parental affection" → "afeto paterno": "paterno" covers parental affection in Portuguese; not a mistranslation.
- ¶17 "I would not hastily pronounce" → "não ousaria pronunciar precipitadamente": renders the hedge (*non temere dixerim*), adds no claim.
- ¶6 "evil habits impel them" → "maus costumes": morals (*mores*), not the *habitus* of ¶25–¶32 that the habit → hábito row governs.
- ¶22 "only pledge of offspring" → "único penhor da sua descendência": glosses *pignus*, not the threefold good *proles*; the offspring → prole row does not apply.
- ¶35 "tanto mais humilha-te": enclisis with affirmative imperative is standard.

### Round 2

Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read of all 38 blocks (negation, subject/object, tense/mood, connectors, qualifiers, Scripture wording), Key Terms frequency check against the source, journal claims re-grepped, plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 2 defects, fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶6 "mas, para satisfazer a concupiscência, ainda que com o marido ou a esposa, … tem culpa venial" → "porém com o marido ou a esposa". Source "but yet with husband or wife" (*sed tamen cum coniuge*) is adversative: being with the spouse is what keeps the fault venial. Concessive "ainda que" (even though) turned the mitigating clause into an obstacle.
- ¶32 "submeter-se … a um só Esposo, Cristo" → "a um só Marido, Cristo". Source "one Husband Christ" echoes ¶21 "made subject unto one husband, Christ" (rendered "um só marido, Cristo"); "Esposo" broke the echo and the husband → marido Key Term. Capital kept from the source. Key Terms row updated.

Rejected:
- ¶20 "the woman could bear" → "pudesse conceber": the clause is about the wife's fertility against the husband's sterility; "conceber" carries that sense.
- ¶21 "that then each may have praise from God" → "tenha o louvor de Deus": "de Deus" reads as source ("from God") in context; a genitive the reader resolves, not a mistranslation.
- ¶13 "sanctified in a brother" → "no irmão": the article changes no sense.
- ¶10 "charity out of a pure heart" → "caridade nascida de um coração puro": "nascida de" renders "out of" (origin); not the familiar Bible wording.
- ¶27 "even he, who could, put it not forth, but yet possessed it" → "não a manifestava, mas … a possuía": the pronoun is as open in the source as in pt-BR (seed / continence).
- ¶14 1 Pt 3:1 "wives obeying their own husbands" → "as esposas sejam obedientes": the participle is the passage's command; the jussive renders it.

### Round 3

Focus: source-blind cold read of the pt-BR, each mark then verified against the source; then a mechanical spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation, punctuation and quote-pairing sweep, plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 2 defects, fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶15 "quando, por dominar o desejo, se tiverem unido" → "quando, sob o domínio do desejo, se tiverem unido". Source "when through desire reigning they shall have been joined"; "por dominar o desejo" reads as the couple mastering desire, the opposite.
- ¶22 "a não ser que baixou a mão" → "senão que baixou a mão". Source "save that he laid down his hand"; "a não ser que" is conditional and requires the subjunctive, so it cannot carry this factual exception.

Rejected (the pt-BR mirrors the source; the oddity is the source's):
- ¶6 "ou ele busca do matrimônio, ou ela do marido" ("either he seeks of marriage, or she of her husband").
- ¶8 "a vaidade é o pior … a caridade é o melhor": the masculine agrees with "males"/"bens".
- ¶15 "declarar-nos que nunca teve relação": singular as "declaring to us that he has never had".
- ¶26 the verbless "O que o Senhor ali acrescentou, …" and "Os quais veem": fragments in the source.
- ¶27 "não tendo outra coisa que ele, mas fazendo outra coisa" ("not having other than he, but doing other").
- ¶35 "excetuados certos, aqueles santos pais e mães" ("except certain, those holy fathers and mothers").

### Round 4

Focus: function words (prepositions, articles, demonstratives, connectors, seu/sua, consigo), the heading and signature, every place the pt-BR commits where the source is open (referent gender, pronoun antecedents), and a turn-by-turn tu/vós trace, plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 2 defects, fixed.

Fixed:
- ¶4 "ela deixou de ser guardada consigo" → "com ele mesmo". Source "it has failed to be kept with himself". The subject of the clause is "ela" (a fé), so reflexive "consigo" binds to the faith; the referent is the complaining partner.
- ¶25 "de modo a não ficarem presas por eles" → "de modo a, por ele, não ficarem presas por eles". Source "so as, by it, not to be bound by them, and so as by it, to have power": the first "by it" (the habit) was dropped.

Rejected:
- ¶25 "From this habit Paul also said" → "Deste hábito disse também Paulo": "de" carries origin, and Paul's words state the habit itself, so even the "about" reading says nothing false.
- ¶26 "no one doubts of such as out of the Gospel are believers" → "ninguém duvida dos que são crentes": the partitive is as exposed in the source ("doubts of such as"); the topicalized "que" clause already fills the object.
- ¶8 "the faithful are married, that they may be chastely joined unto husbands" → "os fiéis se casam … aos maridos": masculine plural is the unmarked generic, as "the faithful" is in English.
- ¶15 "other things from which the Apostle spares" → "de que o Apóstolo nos poupa": "poupar" needs an object; 1 Cor 7:28 spares the married readers.
- ¶33 "the divine sentence … convicts them" → "os condena": a sentence condemns; the clause pairs it with the reading that sets them forth "to condemn".
- ¶3 "a certain gravity of glowing pleasure" → "gravidade ao ardente prazer": the gravity tempers the pleasure, as the preceding "tempered by parental affection".

### Round 5

Focus: terms, journal and names. A per-paragraph term-frequency diff against Key Terms in both directions; every journal claim grepped against the files; proper names and work titles checked against the standard Portuguese form and against sibling pt-BR works. Plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: 3 defects, fixed. The intercourse and habit/work/deed rows were updated.

Fixed:
- ¶5 "to marry as his compeer": "para desposá-la como sua igual" → "para se casar com ela como sua igual". It broke the marry → casar-se row, which every other instance follows.
- Intro (Gen. ad litt. quote) "that there be no lying with other man or woman": "que não haja união" → "que não haja relação". The intercourse row keeps "união" for "union" only, and "lying with" (*concumbatur*) means intercourse.
- ¶4 "if also the deed be done against it, it is the worse done": "se também se age contra ela, pior se age" → "se também o ato se faz contra ela, pior se faz". It broke the deed → ato row.

Rejected:
- ¶3 "or had given birth to none" → "não tivessem gerado nenhum": the subject is the elderly couple, and "gerar" covers both parents. The begetting row still holds.
- Intro title *Deserts and Remission of Sins* → *Sobre os méritos e a remissão dos pecados*: a work title (*De peccatorum meritis*). Title naming governs it, not the desert → merecimento row.
- ¶14 "the Apostle Peter" → "o apóstolo Pedro": an appositive common noun, lowercase by Portuguese convention. Capital "Apóstolo" is reserved for Paul by antonomasia.
- ¶26 "men lustful and unclean" → "homens luxuriosos": an adjective. The lust → concupiscência row governs the noun.
- ¶2 "to speak even to unbelievers themselves" → "incrédulos": not a spouse. The unbelieving → descrente row is scoped to the spouse.
- ¶22 "chastity and faithfulness" → "fidelidade": matches the intro's rendering. The faith row's note cites only the intro, which is incomplete but not false.

### Round 6

Focus: completeness paragraph by paragraph (all 38 blocks read in full against the source, with per-block counts of quotes, parentheses and question marks), every numeral and Bible name, plus the mechanical audit. Verdict: clean, no changes.

Rejected:
- ¶16 "neither thus is that union to be preferred to the marriage even of those women" → "nem assim … ao matrimônio, nem mesmo daquelas mulheres": under the negation "nem mesmo" means "not even (to that) of those women", the source's sense.
- ¶34 "so far as the order of nature, so far as the use of custom, so far as the decrees of laws prescribe" → one "tanto quanto prescrevem" over three subjects: all three members present; only the anaphora is folded.

- Isaac → "Isaac", not "Isaque": the Catholic pt-BR Bible form, already in the Names list.
