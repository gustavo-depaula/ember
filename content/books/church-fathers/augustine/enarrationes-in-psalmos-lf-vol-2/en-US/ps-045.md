These daughters of Tyre, who worship with gifts, are the rich among the people, whom that " friend of the Bridegroom" addresses: Charge them that are rich in this world that they be not high-minded, nor trust in uncertain riches, but in the Living God, Who giveth us richly all things to enjoy, that they be rich in good works; ready to distribute, willing to communicate.
