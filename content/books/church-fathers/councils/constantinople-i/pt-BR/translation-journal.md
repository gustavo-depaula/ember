## Translation Decisions

- 2026-10-11: Single chapter; no footnotes in the source, so none dropped or added.
- 2026-10-11: The Creed's markers `(*I*)` and `(*II*)` point to NPNF notes not present in the import. Kept verbatim so both languages stay aligned.
- 2026-10-11: Bracketed editorial insertions ([we believe], [and], [Bishops], [of their errors], [the partisans of]) and the bracketed italic gloss in Canon 1 kept, translated.
- 2026-10-11: The editor's parenthetical note under Canon 4 is a paragraph of the imported text, so it is translated, not dropped. "*Vide*." becomes "*Vide* a Introdução" without the stray period.
- 2026-10-11: Canon 5's "anathematize every heresy" follows "upon their giving", so it is rendered as a parallel finite verb ("apresentam … e anatematizam"). The source's `— … :—` punctuation becomes dashes.
- 2026-10-11: Heading of the Canons section keeps the source's final period.

## Review log

### Round 1

- Focus: paragraph-by-paragraph completeness against the canonical en-US source, including quotations, parentheticals, embedded phrases and Scripture references.
- Verdict: CLEAN. No objective defects found; no chapter, metadata or Key Terms changes.
- Rejected — ch001 [17], Canon 4: source “(Probably adopted at a Council held in Constantinople the next year, 382. *Vide*. Introduction on the number of the Canons.)” → “(Provavelmente adotado num Concílio realizado em Constantinopla no ano seguinte, 382. *Vide* a Introdução sobre o número dos Cânones.)”. This is a paragraph of the imported source, not a footnote; retaining it preserves completeness. Removing the isolated period after *Vide* does not omit content.
- Rejected — ch001 [20], Canon 5: source “upon their giving a written renunciation [of their errors] and anathematize every heresy” → “quando apresentam uma renúncia por escrito [de seus erros] e anatematizam toda heresia”. The English coordination is grammatically irregular, but reading both actions as requirements for those received is supported by the surrounding admission conditions. The Portuguese preserves both actions and the bracketed qualification; no objective mistranslation is established.

### Round 2

- Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read against en-US.

### Round 3

- Focus: full source-blind pt-BR read, then verification against canonical en-US and a spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation and punctuation sweep.
- Verdict: CLEAN. No objective defects; no chapter, metadata, Key Terms or prior journal claims changed.
- Rejected — ch001 [5], letter heading: source “the Same Holy Synod […] the Canons Enacted by Them” → “Mesmo Santo Sínodo […] os Cânones por Eles Promulgados”. The plural refers by sense to the Synod's members, explicitly identified as Bishops in [6]; the collective antecedent is resolvable.

The anticipatory objects and their pronominal resumptions are grammatical; the enumerated groups remain identifiable across the parenthetical dash clause. Its closing dash followed by a comma is also defensible.
