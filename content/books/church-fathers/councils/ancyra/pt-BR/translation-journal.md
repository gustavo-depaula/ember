## Translation Decisions

- 2026-10-11: Single chapter; no footnotes in the source, so none dropped or added. The commentary paragraphs (Zonaras, Hefele, Van Espen, Percival's textual notes) are paragraphs of the imported text and are translated, as the sibling councils did.
- 2026-10-11: Bracketed editorial insertions ([what has been granted], [offered to idols], [of penance], …) kept and translated.
- 2026-10-11: Latin quotations (Isidore, Ferrandus, *Codex Canonum*, *Vide*, *sede vacante*) and all Greek words kept verbatim; the English gloss inside the italic *aliquid agere, i.e., …* is translated.
- 2026-10-11: The source mixes straight and curly quotes (Van Espen, Hefele under Canon 15) and closes Harnack's inner quotation with a straight apostrophe; the pt-BR uses curly quotes throughout.
- 2026-10-11: Canon 13 note: Routh's "but still less" / "but certainly not" → *mas menos ainda* / *mas certamente não*; the italic *chorepiscopi* of the commentary → *corepíscopos*, keeping the italics.
- 2026-10-11: Canon 14 note: "the last clause" → "a última oração" (grammatical clause), as in `neocaesarea`.
- 2026-10-11: Canon 25 "betrothed a maiden … married his betrothed" → "se havia desposado com uma donzela … casou-se com sua noiva".

## Review log

### Round 1

- Focus: paragraph-by-paragraph completeness against the canonical en-US source, including quotations, parentheticals, Latin/Greek and references.
- Verdict: CLEAN; no objective defects confirmed; no chapter or Key Terms changes.
- Rejected mechanical flag, introduction [2]: source “as has been shown by the Ballerini” → “como mostraram os Ballerini”. The table's “unchanged” describes preservation of surnames, not a literal Portuguese rendering; “Ballerini” is preserved.
- Rejected mechanical flag, Canons 5, 6, 8 and 9 [18, 20, 26, 28]: source “communicate without oblation” → “estejam em comunhão sem oblação” in all four places. This is the required inflection of the glossary's infinitive “estar em comunhão sem oblação”, not a missing or inconsistent term.

### Round 2

- Focus: clause-by-clause bilingual fidelity read against the en-US source.
- Verdict: CLEAN. No objective defects, and no chapter or Key Terms changes.
- Rejected, Canon 2 [10]: source "nor making proclamations" → "nem fazer as proclamações". The article names the liturgical proclamations and adds no content.
- Rejected, Canon 3 [12]: source "laymen who have fallen under the same compulsion" → "caíram sob a mesma coação". "Cair sob" means "be subjected to", and the canon is about the innocent, so the text does not say "lapsed".
- Rejected, Canon 9 [28]: source "guilty of their being forced" → "culpados de terem sido eles forçados". "Eles" refers to the brethren, as "their" does in the source.
- Rejected, Canon 14 note [47]: source "Love feasts" → "aos ágapes". The masculine "ágape" is attested, and the form matches Key Terms.
- Rejected, Canon 15 [50]: source "Church property shall be reclaimed" → "seja reclamada". "Reclamar" covers "claim back".
- Rejected, Canon 25 [72]: source "betrothed a maiden" → "se havia desposado com". "Desposar-se" covers betrothal (see Translation Decisions), and "sua noiva" settles the sense.
- Mechanical flags ("Ballerini → unchanged", "estar em comunhão sem oblação"): rejected for the reasons given in round 1.

### Round 3

- Focus: source-blind Portuguese cold read, then source verification and whole-text spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation, punctuation and quotation-pairing sweep.
- Verdict: CLEAN; no objective defects confirmed; no chapter or Key Terms changes.
- Rejected, Canon 14 [45]: “a provem … podem abster-se”, source “shall taste of it, and afterwards, if they shall so please, may abstain”. The final clause can independently state permission with its subject understood; changing to “possam” would regularize subordination, not repair an indisputable error. The obligation/permission distinction is preserved.
- Rejected, Canon 15 [50–51]: “render-lhes um valor maior”, source “yield them the greater value”. The implicit purchasers are clarified by the next paragraph (“If the purchaser of ecclesiastical properties…” / “Se o comprador de propriedades eclesiásticas…”); the compressed reference is inherited and resolvable.
- Rejected mechanical flags: introduction [2], “by the Ballerini” → “os Ballerini”, preserves the surname as instructed by “unchanged”; Canons 5, 6, 8 and 9 [18, 20, 26, 28], “communicate without oblation” → “estejam em comunhão sem oblação”, correctly inflects the glossary's infinitive. The round 1 reasons remain valid.
