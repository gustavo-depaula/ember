# Book I

Sibling precedent: only `ambrose/mysteries` has pt-BR. Its author is "Santo Ambrósio (340-397)". Conventions taken from it:
- Scripture references keep the source's numbers with Portuguese book names ("Lucas 15:5", "1 Coríntios 5:7", "Baruc 5:1", "Apocalipse 2:14-16").
- Citations are mirrored even when wrong.
- Quotes are curly “ ”.
- `vós` for plural address.
- "a fonte" for the baptismal font.

### Decisions

- **¶4** "that heavenly Hall which received the world's Redeemer in her virgin womb" (Latin *aula caelestis*, Mary) → "aquele Paço celeste que recebeu o Redentor do mundo em seu seio virginal". "Aula" means classroom in Portuguese; "Paço" keeps the image of a palace hall.
- **¶39** The source runs John's objection and Jesus's reply together in one quotation. The pt-BR puts a dash between the two speakers and adds no words.
- **¶43** The source has a stray period ("affliction. and clothe"). The pt-BR uses a normal comma.
- **Chapter 2 summary** The source splits one italic span in two. The pt-BR uses a single span.
- **Scripture** Quotations follow the source's wording, not a Portuguese Bible. For example, Rom 8:3 is "em semelhança da carne do pecado", keeping "semelhança" throughout because ¶12 argues from the word "likeness".

### Source slips (mirrored, not corrected)

- The chapter 9 summary cites "1 Samuel 3:25". The verse is 1 Samuel 2:25.
- ¶54 cites "Ezekiel 23:11". The verse is Ezekiel 33:11.
- ¶24 "Matthew 15:8" follows Ambrose's own paraphrase, not the Isaiah quotation it parallels.
- ¶61 "if the law of his flesh agreed and was consonant with the law of his flesh": the second "flesh" is probably "mind". It is translated literally. Check the printed NPNF before fixing en-US.

### Register

- tu for the individual sinner/reader; vós when Ambrose addresses the Novatians as a group (¶20-27, ¶59) and in ¶28/¶40 "do you at least repent, who … have fallen" (plural), while the Isaiah quotation inside stays singular (tu).
- Reverential pronouns all lowercase (tu/teu, ele, aquele que), aligned with amb-a ch001; capitals only on nouns the source capitalizes (Nome, Sangue, Corpo, Médico). Curly quotes “ ” as in ch001. luxury → luxo (never luxúria), as in ch001.

### Decisions

- Scripture rendered from the source wording, not from Figueiredo/Almeida. Book names localized; every chapter:verse mirrored (verified: identical citation sequence).
- ¶32 Greek (Iliad 6.408-410): the OCR corruption was later corrected in both files (see slips below).
- ¶96 "It is I." / "But I am not the former I." → "Sou eu." / "Mas eu não sou o eu de antes."
- ¶28 and ¶40 are near-duplicates in the source (¶40 adds "your" and "that"); both translated as they stand, with the small differences preserved.
