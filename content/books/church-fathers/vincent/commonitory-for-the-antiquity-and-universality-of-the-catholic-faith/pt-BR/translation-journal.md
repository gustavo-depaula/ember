## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-27: No NPNF editor footnotes survive in the en-US import; nothing to drop. No translator notes added.
- 2026-09-27: Scripture references kept in the source's bare, parenthesis-free placement right after the quotation, with Portuguese book names (`Deuteronômio 32:7`, `1 Timóteo 6:20`), matching the corpus convention logged in `dionysius-rome/against-the-sabellians`. Chapter-heading references in Roman-numeral style (`Gal. i. 8`, `1 Tim. vi. 20`) normalized to the same form (`Gálatas 1:8`, `1 Timóteo 6:20`).
- 2026-09-27: Wrong references carried by the source edition are mirrored, not corrected, so the language files agree: `1 Corinthians 2:9` for "There must needs be heresies" (really 11:19), `Galatians 2:9` at the end of §63 (really 1:9), `2 Corinthians 11:12` for "false apostles" (really 11:13), and the second `1 Corinthians 14:33` in §74 (really 14:38).
- 2026-09-27: Scripture quotations rendered from Heurtley's wording clause by clause, not pulled toward a Portuguese Bible (e.g. Sirach 8:14 "Não entres em litígio com um Juiz"; Proverbs 9:18 "os filhos da terra perecem em sua casa", because Vincent then asks who these "sons of earth" are).
- 2026-09-27: The canon in §6 keeps the source's three bare adverbials "em toda parte, sempre, por todos" (no conjunction), since this is the formula readers cite. The three notes are always "universalidade, antiguidade, consenso".
- 2026-09-27: "Trial" (God permitting error to test the faithful, Deut 13:3) is consistently "prova" / "pôr à prova", so the recurring Deuteronomy refrain and Vincent's commentary share one word.
- 2026-09-27: Stephen's rule "Let there be no innovation — nothing but what has been handed down" rendered "Nada se inove — nada senão o que foi transmitido", echoing the familiar *nihil innovetur nisi quod traditum est*.
- 2026-09-27: The bracketed editorial note on the lost second book (before ch. 29) is kept, translated, because it explains a real gap in the work rather than glossing the text.
- 2026-09-27: The source typo "enlarged n itself" (§54) translated as "se amplie em si mesma"; the en-US file is left untouched.
