Consider, thirdly, that what is most to be admired and loved in this heavenly sentence, is, that it brings our souls to an eternal union with God himself, our only true and sovereign Good. This cumc, this sweet word of salvation, draws us to our God, unites us to him, receives us into the very bosom of his goodness, and in a manner transforms us into him. And nothing less could ever truly satisfy our souls. O happy blessing indeed, to be thus blessed of the Father by an eternal union with himself! Conclude to spare no pains to secure to thyself this happy sentence, by a diligence in all good works, more especially works of mercy and charity, which, by the Gospel, are particularly entitled to the sentence of the elect.
