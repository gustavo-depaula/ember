# July 26 — On St, Ann

Consider, first, that what gives us the highest idea of the supereminent sanctity of St. Ann, and of her blessed consort, St. Joachim, is that they were chosen by the decrees of Heaven, to be parents of that immaculate Virgin, who was to bring forth the Saviour of the world, and to be the Mother of G<. O what graces did they not receive, to qualify them to furnish this virgin Spouse to the Spirit of God, and this virgin Mother to the Son of God! What a saintlike, what a heavenly education, did they not give to their blessed child! How perfectly did they make good thereby that sentence of their divine grandson, that the tree is to he known by the fruits! What reverence, then, what esteem, what affection, what devotion, do we not owe to this blessed couple! All the true children of Christ have ever loved his Mother, and considered her as their Mother. How, then, can we do otherwise than love also these two great saints, to whom we stand indebted for such a mother?

Now this kingdom of God in our souls is a treasure indeed, of infinite value, which enriches us by the possession of God himself, here by grace, and hereafter in glory.
