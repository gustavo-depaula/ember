# Sermon 230: In the Days of Easter

**1.** As we have sung to our Lord, so let us, with his help, also act. For every day has been made by the Lord; yet it is not without reason that of one day in particular it is written: *This is the day which the Lord has made*[^1]. We read that when God established heaven and earth, he said: *Let there be light; and there was light. And God called the light day, and the darkness night*[^2]. But there is another day, sure for us and especially to be commended, of which the Apostle says: *Let us walk becomingly, as in the day*[^3]. This common, everyday day is completed by the rising and setting of the sun. There is another day, in which the word of God shines in the hearts of the faithful and drives out the darkness, not of the eyes, but of evil ways. Let us therefore acknowledge that day, and rejoice in it. Let us hear the Apostle saying: *For we are sons of light and sons of the day; we are not of the night or of the darkness*[^4]. *Let us walk becomingly, as in the day: not in reveling and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and jealousy; but put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh in its desires*[^5]. If you do this, you sing with your whole heart: *This is the day which the Lord has made*[^6]. For what you sing, you yourselves are, if you live well. How many during these days get drunk? How many during these days, and it is too little that they get drunk, on top of that quarrel shamefully and cruelly? Such people do not sing: *This is the day which the Lord has made*. The Lord answers them: You are darkness; I did not make you. If you wish to be the day which the Lord has made, live well; and you will have the light of truth, which will never set in your hearts.

[^1]: Ps 118:24 (117:24)
[^2]: Gen 1:3-5
[^3]: Rom 13:13
[^4]: 1 Thess 5:5
[^5]: Rom 13:13-14
[^6]: Ps 118:24 (117:24)
