# Sermon 223/I: On the Paschal Vigil

**1.** So great and so holy a solemnity admonishes us, most beloved, to exhort you to watch and pray. For our faith does battle against the night of this age, lest our inner eyes fall asleep into the night of the heart. That we may not fall into this evil, let us pray with that voice with which we read, and say to the Lord our God: *Enlighten my eyes, lest ever I sleep into death, lest at any time my enemy say: I have prevailed against him*[^1]. This is that enemy who roused the mad Jews, as his own vessels and weapons, against our Lord Jesus Christ; but he did not prevail against him. The fleshly enemies indeed seemed to themselves to have prevailed; but in them the spiritual foe was overcome. For by the victim of clean flesh the unclean spirit was conquered; and by that very thing by which he inflamed men to do openly what they willed, he secretly suffered what he did not will. For by killing Christ he shed the blood by which those whom he killed might come back to life. But neither does he possess him among the dead. For he grieved that those were joined to the rising one, whom he wished to join to the dying one. His death we celebrate in this life, whose life after this death we hope for. Therefore by our humility let us recall to memory the humility of our Lord Jesus Christ. Let us humbly watch, humbly pray, with the purest faith, the firmest hope, the most fervent love, considering what kind of day our brightness will have, if our humility turns night into day. So may God, who said that light should shine out of darkness, shine in our hearts[^2], that we may do within something like what we have done in this house of prayer with the lamps lit. Let us adorn the true dwelling place of God, our conscience, with the lamps of righteousness. But not we, but the grace of God with us, of which we have the promise, the Prophet saying: *He will bring forth your righteousness like the light*[^3]. And so, watching, we will not fear the nocturnal terror and the business that walks about in the darkness[^4]; and, when the beasts of the forest have passed by, seeking food for themselves from God[^5], he will hand us over to be the food of no wretched one, who for us handed over his Only-begotten. Turning to the Lord.

[^1]: Ps 13:3-4 (12:4-5)
[^2]: See 2 Cor 4:6
[^3]: See Ps 37:6 (36:6)
[^4]: See Ps 91:5-6 (90:5-6)
[^5]: See Ps 104:20-21 (103:20-21)
