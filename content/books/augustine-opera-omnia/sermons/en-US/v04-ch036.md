# Sermon 120

**1.** The beginning of John's Gospel: *In the beginning was the Word* [^1]. So he began, this he saw, and transcending the whole creation, the mountains, the airs, the heavens, the stars, the Seats, Dominations, Principalities, Powers, all the Angels, all the Archangels, transcending all things, in the beginning he saw the Word, and drank. Above all creation he saw, from the breast of the Lord he drank. For he is John the holy evangelist, whom Jesus especially loved; so that he reclined upon his breast [^2]. There was this secret, that from there might be drunk what was belched forth in the Gospel. Happy those who hear and understand. Of the following happiness are those who, even if they do not understand, believe. For how great a thing it is to see the Word of God, who can explain in human words?

**2.** Lift up your hearts, my brothers; as much as you can, lift them up; whatever occurs to you through the imagination of any body, spit it out. If the Word of God occurs to you, just as you think of the light of this sun—however much you spread it, however much you extend it, you set no bounds of that light in thought—to the Word of God it is nothing. Whatever such the soul thinks, is less in part than in the whole. Think the Word everywhere whole. Understand what I say; as much as I can, I confine myself through my straits for your sake. Understand what I say. Behold, this light from heaven, which is called by the name of sun, when it has come forth, it illumines the lands, unfolds the day, makes shapes, and distinguishes colors. A great good, a great gift of God to all mortals; let his works magnify him. If the sun is so beautiful, what is more beautiful than the maker of the sun? And yet see, brothers; behold, it pours its rays through the whole earth; it penetrates open things, closed things resist; it sends its light through windows, but does it through the wall too? To the Word of God all things lie open, from the Word of God nothing lies hidden. See another difference, how far the creature is from the Creator, especially the bodily. When the sun is in the East, it is not in the West. Its light indeed, poured out from that great body, reaches even into the West; but it itself is not there. When it begins to set, then it will be there. When it rises, it is in the East; when it sets, it is in the West. By these two works of his he gave names to places. Because it is in the East when it rises in the East, he made the East to be called; because it is in the West when it sets in the West, he made the West to be called. By night it appears nowhere. Is the Word of God so? When it is in the East, is it not in the West, or when it is in the West, is it not in the East? Or does it at some time forsake the lands, and go under the lands, or behind the lands? Everywhere it is whole. Who can explain this in words? Who sees this? By what proof shall I prove to you what I say? I, a man, speak; to men I speak; weak, I speak to weaker; and yet, my brothers, I dare to say, that what I tell you, whether through a mirror, or in a riddle, I somehow see, I somehow understand, even in my heart, the word about this matter. But it seeks to go out to you, and finds no worthy vehicle. The vehicle of the word is the sound of the voice. What I say within myself, I seek to tell you, and words fail. For I want to speak of the Word of God. By how great a Word, by what kind of Word? *All things were made through him* [^3]. See the works, and be in awe of the Worker. *All things were made through him*.

**3.** Return with me, human weakness, return then. Let us comprehend human things themselves, if we can. We who speak are men too, and we speak to men, and we put forth the sound of the voice. To the ears of men we carry the sound of our voice, and through the sound of our voice and the understanding we somehow set it through the ear in the heart. From here, then, what we can, as we can, let us speak, this let us comprehend. But if we are not able to comprehend even this, what are we for that? Behold, you hear me: I make a word. If anyone goes out from here and is asked outside what is being done here, he answers: The Bishop is making a word. I make a word about the Word. But what kind of word, about what kind of Word? A mortal word, about the immortal Word; a changeable word, about the unchangeable Word; a transitory word, about the eternal Word. Yet attend to my word. For I had told you: The Word of God is everywhere whole. Behold, I make a word for you; it reaches all what I say. That what I say might reach all, did you divide what I say? If I fed you, so that I wanted to fill not your mind, but your belly, and set before you loaves with which you might be filled; would you not divide my loaves among yourselves? Could my loaves reach each one of you? If they reached one, the rest would have nothing. Behold, I speak, and you all have it. It is too little that you all have it; you all have the whole. The whole reaches all, the whole reaches each. O wonders of my word! What, then, is the Word of God? Hear another thing. I said: What I said came forth to you and did not depart from me. It reached you, nor was it separated from me. Before I said it, I had it, and you did not have it; I said it, and you began to have it, and I lost nothing. O miracle of my word! What, then, is the Word of God? From small things conjecture great. Consider earthly things, praise heavenly things. I am a creature, you are creatures; and so great miracles come to be from my word in my heart, in my mouth, in my voice, in your ears, in your hearts. What is the Creator? O Lord, hear us. Make us, because you made us. Make us good, because you made us men, enlightened. These newly whitened, enlightened, hear through me your word. For, enlightened by your grace, they stand before you. This is the day that the Lord made [^4]. But let them labor for this, let them pray for this, that when these days have passed, they may not become darkness, who have been made the light of the miracles and benefits of God.

[^1]: See Jn 1:1
[^2]: See Jn 13:23, 25
[^3]: See Jn 1:3
[^4]: See Ps 118:24 (117)
