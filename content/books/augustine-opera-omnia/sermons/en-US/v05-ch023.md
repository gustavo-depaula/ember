# Sermon 163/A

**1.** That we dare to speak to you, faith brings about, by which we believe that you pray for us. For I know that we are in your hearts to die together and to live together[^1]; to this hope the affection of your love nourishes us. And so, what the Lord shall grant, I beseech you to deign to hear gladly.

**2.** We heard the apostle Paul, when his epistle was read, admonishing by apostolic authority and saying, *Walk by the Spirit*, he says, *and do not accomplish the desires of the flesh; for the flesh covets against the spirit, but the spirit covets against the flesh; for these are opposed to one another, that you may not do what you wish*[^2]. He set before our eyes a certain warfare, in which the flesh and the spirit so contend that we do not do what we wish; and since the concupiscence of the flesh must be subjected to the command of God, he gave a discourse to those set in the contest, and said, *Walk by the Spirit, and do not accomplish the desires of the flesh*. Fight, he says, bravely, and conquer—not a foreign nature rebelling against you, but the concupiscence that reigns in your members. *I see*, says the Apostle, *another law in my members*[^3]. It assails, assail back; it rebels, restrain it; do not yield your members, and it does not kill your soul. *Let not sin reign*, he says, *in your mortal body, nor present your members as arms of iniquity to sin*[^4]. Deny concupiscence its arms, and your victory prevails. Fight, labor; no athlete is crowned without sweat. You are set in the stadium and in the contest, in the struggle; against your spirit the concupiscence of the flesh fights. *The flesh covets against the spirit, and the spirit covets against the flesh*. The flesh suggests lust, let the spirit command chastity; the flesh inflames to anger, let the spirit command mercy. Set in this contest, when you do not give to the rebelling concupiscence your members, which were arms of iniquity to sin, they become arms of righteousness to God[^5].

**3.** Therefore, dearly beloved, *walk by the Spirit, and do not accomplish the desires of the flesh*[^6]. Set in this fight, do not presume on the strength of your free choice; for the adversary conquers you. Implore the help of the grace of God, and then you conquer the concupiscence of the flesh rebelling against you. But perhaps you are already conquered, and you will say perhaps: I am already conquered, concupiscence already holds arms against me, sin already reigns in my mortal body to obey its desires. Cry out, and say with Paul, *Wretched man that I am, who will free me from the body of this death? I delight in the law of God according to the inner man; but I see another law in my members, fighting against the law of my mind, and leading me captive in the law of sin which is in my members*[^7]. So great a teacher of the field is led captive; what do I do, captive and weak? Implore, then, the help of grace; it will be answered to you, and it will be said, *The grace of God through Jesus Christ our Lord*. *Who will free me*, he says, *from the body of this death*? O Manichean, he said *from the body of this death*, not from the captivity of that nation. *The grace*, he says, *of God through Jesus Christ our Lord*. O Pelagian, *through Jesus Christ*, he says, *our Lord*, not through our free choice.

**4.** And so, dearly beloved, *if we live by the spirit, let us also follow the spirit. Let us not become desirous of vainglory*[^8]. You heard the apostolic reading: *Walk by the Spirit, and do not accomplish the desires of the flesh; for the flesh covets against the spirit, but the spirit against the flesh: but these are opposed to one another, that you may not do what you wish. And so, if you are led by the Spirit, you are no longer under the law. The works of the flesh*, he says, *are manifest, which are fornications, uncleanness, debauchery, the service of idols, sorceries, enmities, contentions, jealousies, animosities, dissensions, heresies, envies, drunkennesses, carousings, and the like*, he says; *of which I foretell you, as I have foretold, that those who do such things will not possess the kingdom of God*[^9]. Kill in yourselves the concupiscence of the flesh with the help of God's grace: despise the works of the flesh, love the fruit of the spirit. *For the fruit of the spirit is joy, peace, long-suffering, kindness, goodness, faith, gentleness, continence*[^10]. *Do these things*, and be in these, *and the God of peace will be with you*[^11].
