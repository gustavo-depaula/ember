# Sermon 3: On Hagar and Ishmael

[A Fragment]

**1.** The Old Testament pertains properly to the Jews. For fleshly benefits were promised, because spiritual ones were not grasped. That earthly kingdom, and an earthly life, was utterly cast down and handed over to subjection to enemies. They hoped for everything from the Lord in a fleshly way, and for the sake of these things they served. Let Christians be questioned, if only there are no such people now. Such people pertain to the Old Testament. For I am not asking about the name but about the life. Of such people are both heresy and schism. Hagar fled from the face of Sarah, and Sarah afflicted her. What is to be wondered at? She afflicted her bodily. If the party of Donatus suffered any affliction, on account of its pride it suffered, as the slave woman Hagar from Sarah. Let Hagar hear the voice of the angel, *Return to your mistress*[^1]. *But as then he who was born according to the flesh persecuted him who was born according to the spirit, so also now*[^2]. We do not find that Isaac was bodily persecuted by Ishmael, but only that the Apostle spoke of persecution. Note there where he said, *But as then he who was born according to the flesh persecuted him who was born according to the spirit, so also now. But what does the Scripture say? Cast out the slave woman and her son: for the son of the slave woman shall not be heir with the son of the free woman*[^3]. Therefore, where Scripture says that, there let us seek. What does it say in Genesis? *And it happened, when Ishmael and Isaac were playing, that she saw them playing*[^4]. Who persecuted? Whom did he persecute? Sarah saw them playing, and said, *Cast out the slave woman and her son*[^5]. Why? Because she saw them playing. But Paul calls that play persecution, because that playing was a mockery. If a mockery, it is a seduction and a deception. Every game of children is an image of greater business: and when an older child plays with a younger, as if to seduce him, knowing that he has other concerns he is pursuing, he pretends certain things to the child, that is, to the weaker one, playing with him. Ishmael was older, and strengthened in malice, but playing with the child Isaac, he was seducing Isaac, and was committing certain frauds in playing with the weaker one. The mother noticed that that play was persecution. So understanding, Sarah the mother said that that play meant, *Cast out the slave woman and her son: for the son of the slave woman shall not be heir with my son Isaac*[^6]. And the Church says, "Cast out the heresies and their children": for the heretics shall not be heirs with the Catholics. But why shall they not be heirs? Were they not born of the seed of Abraham? And how do they have the baptism of the Church? They have baptism: the seed of Abraham would make them heirs, were it not that pride excludes them from the inheritance. By the same word you are born, by the same sacrament; but you do not come to the same inheritance of eternal life unless you return to the Catholic Church. Of the seed of Abraham you are born, but a son of the slave woman, outside, because of pride.

[^1]: Gen 16:9
[^2]: Gal 4:29
[^3]: Gal 4:29-30
[^4]: Gen 21:9
[^5]: Gen 21:10
[^6]: Gen 21:10
