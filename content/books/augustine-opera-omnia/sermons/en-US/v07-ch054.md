# Sermon 267: On the Day of Pentecost

**1.** The solemnity of today's day makes a remembrance of the great Lord God and of the great grace which was poured over us. For a solemnity is therefore celebrated, lest what was once done be wiped from the memory. For a solemnity takes its name from what is customary in the year: as the perennity of a river is so called, because it does not dry up in summer, but flows through the whole year: therefore perennial, that is, through the year; so too a solemnity, what is customarily celebrated in the year. We celebrate today the coming of the Holy Spirit. For the Lord sent from heaven the Holy Spirit, whom on earth he promised. And because he had so promised that he would send him from heaven: *He cannot come*, he says, *unless I go away; but when I have gone away, I will send him to you*[^1]; he suffered, died, rose, ascended: it remained that he fulfill what he promised. His disciples awaiting this, souls, as it is written, one hundred twenty, the number of the apostles multiplied tenfold; for he chose twelve, and into one hundred twenty he sent the Spirit: awaiting this promise, then, they were in one house, they prayed: because they desired now by faith itself, what by prayer itself, by spiritual desire itself; they were new wineskins, the new wine was awaited from heaven, and it came. For now that great cluster had been trodden and glorified. For we read in the Gospel: *For the Spirit had not yet been given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified*[^2].

**2.** Now what answered, you heard, a great miracle. All who were present had learned one tongue. The Holy Spirit came; they were filled, they began to speak in the various tongues of all nations, which they had not known nor learned: but he who had come taught them; he entered, they were filled, he poured forth. And then this was the sign, whoever received the Holy Spirit, suddenly filled with the Spirit spoke in the tongues of all; not those one hundred twenty alone. The Letters themselves teach us, that afterward men believed, were baptized, received the Holy Spirit, spoke in the tongues of all nations. Those who were present were terrified, some marveling, some mocking: so that they said, *These are drunk, they are full of new wine*[^3]. They mocked, and said something true. For they were filled, the wineskins, with new wine. You heard when the Gospel was read: *No one puts new wine into old wineskins*[^4]: the carnal man does not hold spiritual things. Carnality is oldness, grace is newness. However much a man has been renewed for the better, so much the more he holds, what savors of the true. The new wine bubbled, and as the new wine bubbled the tongues of the nations flowed forth.

**3.** Is the Holy Spirit not given now, brothers? Whoever thinks this is not worthy to receive him. He is given now too. Why, then, does no one speak in the tongues of all nations, as he spoke who was then filled with the Holy Spirit? Why? Because what that signified, is fulfilled. What is that? When we celebrated the Fortieth, recall, because we commended to you that the Lord Jesus Christ commended his Church and ascended. The disciples asked, When will the end of the age be? And he: *It is not yours to know the times or the moments, which the Father has set in his own power*[^5]. He was still promising what today he fulfilled: *You will receive the power of the Holy Spirit coming upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth*[^6]. The Church then was in one house, it received the Holy Spirit: it was in a few men, it was in the tongues of the whole world. See what it was then prefiguring. For that that small Church spoke in the tongues of all nations, what is it, except that this great Church from the rising of the sun to its setting speaks in the tongues of all nations? Now is fulfilled what was then promised. We have heard, we see. *Hear, daughter, and see*: it was said to the queen herself: *Hear, daughter, and see*[^7]; hear the promise, see the fulfillment. Your God did not deceive you, your bridegroom did not deceive you, he who endowed you with his own blood did not deceive you: he did not deceive you who from foul made you beautiful, from unclean made you a virgin. You were promised to yourself: but promised in a few, fulfilled in many.

**4.** Let no one, then, say: I received the Holy Spirit; why do I not speak in the tongues of all nations? If you wish to have the Holy Spirit, attend, my brothers: our spirit, by which every man lives, is called the soul; our spirit, by which each single man lives, is called the soul: and you see what the soul does in the body. It enlivens all the members; through the eyes it sees, through the ears it hears, through the nostrils it smells, through the tongue it speaks, through the hands it works, through the feet it walks: it is present to all the members at once, that they may live; it gives life to all, offices to each. The eye does not hear, the ear does not see, the tongue does not see, nor does the ear and the eye speak; but yet it lives: the ear lives, the tongue lives; the offices are diverse, the life common. So is the Church of God: in some saints it works miracles, in other saints it speaks the truth, in other saints it keeps virginity, in other saints it keeps conjugal chastity, in others this, in others that: each does his own work, but they live alike. But what the soul is to the body of a man, this the Holy Spirit is to the body of Christ, which is the Church: the Holy Spirit does in the whole Church what the soul does in all the members of one body. But see what you should beware, see what you should observe, see what you should fear. It happens that in the human body, rather from the body, some member is cut off, a hand, a finger, a foot; does the soul follow what is cut off? When it was in the body, it lived; cut off, it loses life. So too a Christian man is Catholic, while he lives in the body; cut off, he becomes a heretic, the spirit does not follow the amputated member. If, then, you wish to live by the Holy Spirit, hold love, love the truth, desire unity, that you may come to eternity. Amen.

[^1]: John 16:7
[^2]: John 7:39
[^3]: Acts 2:13
[^4]: Matt 9:17
[^5]: Acts 1:7
[^6]: Acts 1:8
[^7]: Ps 45:10 (44:11)
