# Sermon 229/T: From the Sermons of the Octave of Easter

**1.** The Evangelists are the luminaries, the Scripture of God the firmament. But the Gospel, which has its testimony from the Law and the Prophets, is the luminaries in the firmament of heaven. But the little one in the Church cannot yet take solid food, but is content with milk, which signifies the lesser luminary and the stars. And therefore: *The greater luminary for the rule of the day, the lesser luminary for the rule of the night*[^1]. As long as men are carnal, and cannot think upon that wisdom through which all things were made, they are in the night: but he did not abandon the night; he furnished faith. For God did not abandon the night; he gave it his luminaries. The day seeks the sun, the sun is enough for it; the moon and the stars are given to the night, they illumine it; and when the moon does not shine upon the earth, that light which is in the air is from the stars. Hence it comes that we go outside at night and see the trees; and though we do not distinguish them, yet we somehow see by the light of the stars; and therefore when the clouds are thick, the light of the stars too is taken away, and a man is then under the open sky as though indoors in a bedroom. Therefore the night too has a certain light of its own. Let us see what the Apostle says about those gifts, those spiritual gifts: *To each is given the manifestation of the Spirit for profit.* How is it given? *To one indeed is given through the Spirit the word of wisdom, to another the word of knowledge*[^2]. He who understands the difference between the word of wisdom and the word of knowledge, has understood the difference between the greater luminary and the lesser luminary. Let us hear the word of Wisdom: *In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God; this was in the beginning*[^3], and so on. Those who comprehend it, it is day, the sun shines for them; but unless first from the night, they have not come to the light: *For darkness was over the abyss, and God said: Let there be light*[^4]. Let no one say that he began from the light. For we are all justified out of sinners. Let those, then, who can, take what I have said about the Gospel. But they say to me: We have not understood what it is: *In the beginning was the Word.* If, then, it is still night for you, attend that the Word took flesh, and made for you as it were a night-light: *For the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us*[^5]. And in the bush God speaks through an angel to Moses. Hear where the word of Wisdom is. What are you called? said Moses. How do I announce you to the people? If it be said: Who sent you? what shall I say? Hear the word of Wisdom: *You shall say to the children of Israel: "Who Is" sent me to you*[^6]. Who can grasp: *Who Is*? Other things truly are not. Who truly is? He who does not pass away, who always remains unchangeable. But this is the food of the grown; this is the sun of the day. And as though Moses said to him: I am still night, illumine the night, let me say something the night can grasp, let me say something the little ones can hold: *I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob*[^7]. Therefore: *I am who I am* for the rule of the day, *I am the God of Abraham* for the rule of the night. Faith dispensed in time shines for the night, wisdom always remaining shines for the day. He nourishes in the night, that we may go forth into the day. The stars, then, are the other gifts. For when he had said: *To one is given the word of wisdom,* as the sun for the rule of the day; *to another is given the word of knowledge,* as the moon for the rule of the night; he gives also the stars: *To another the gift of healings, to another prophecy*[^8], and so on.

[^1]: Gen 1:16
[^2]: 1 Cor 12:7-8
[^3]: John 1:1-2
[^4]: Gen 1:2-3
[^5]: John 1:14
[^6]: Exod 3:14
[^7]: Exod 3:15
[^8]: 1 Cor 12:8-10
