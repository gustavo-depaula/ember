# Sermon 360: At the Vigils of Maximianus, About a Certain Donatist Who Returned to the Church

**1.** Thanks be to God, brothers, congratulate your brother, who was dead and has come back to life, was lost and is found[^1]. Thanks to the patience and mercy of the Lord our God: to his patience, because he bore with the one delaying; to his mercy, because he deigned to receive the one returning. This is the vineyard where I did not labor, and I had spent my strength on another's. O vineyard beloved of my Lord, not only did my labor profit you nothing, but I even served against you for your enemy. I scattered with great sweat, when I was not gathering for you. Thanks to your planter, with whom the reward of the workers does not fail even of those called at the last hour[^2]. I arrive late, but I do not despair of the denarius. I who was before a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious against you: but I obtained mercy, because I did it in ignorance[^3]. For I held the words of my parents, not of the Patriarchs, not of the Prophets, not of the Apostles; but of the parents of my flesh. I did not yield to flesh and blood; but I yielded, overcome by the truth, and rested, restored to unity. Was I not reading the same Scriptures which now too I read? But even that Teacher of the Gentiles, the vessel of election, from Saul Paul, from one exalted the least, from a robber a shepherd, from a wolf a ram, was a Hebrew of Hebrews, according to the law a Pharisee[^4], instructed in the law at the feet of the teacher Gamaliel[^5]: and yet Christ, whom he read of in the Prophets, he neither knew to sit in heaven, nor permitted to be worshiped on earth. The faith of his passion and resurrection he sang with his mouth not knowing it, and, raging in error, laid it waste. According to the Prophets, in whom he had been born and grown up, Christ, now rising from the dead, was sitting in heaven; and that one was still blinded in the lie of his parents, that his disciples had stolen him from the tomb. So I too was struck about on every side concerning the catholic Church spread throughout the whole world by the voices of the divine Scriptures; and the false charges of traitors, leveled by my parents, made me deaf to them. I do not compare myself to Paul by merits, but by sins. Even if I did not merit to be so good, yet I was not so evil without the medicine of correction. Neither did he recognize the bridegroom in the Books which he read, nor I the bride. He who revealed to him concerning the glorification of Christ what is written: *Be exalted above the heavens, O God*; he himself revealed to me concerning the outpouring of the Church what follows: *Above all the earth be your glory*[^6]. Both testimonies are open to those who see, but closed to the blind. To him the Baptism of Christ opened his eyes, to me the peace of Christ. Him the washing of holy water made new: but love covered the multitude of my sins[^7].

[^1]: See Luke 15:32
[^2]: See Matt 20:9
[^3]: See 1 Tim 1:13
[^4]: See Phil 3:5
[^5]: See Acts 22:3
[^6]: Ps 57:5 (56:6)
[^7]: See 1 Pet 4:8
