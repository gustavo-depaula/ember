# Sermon 335/L: A Sermon on One Righteous Man

**1.** We celebrate today the memory of a righteous man. So this sermon is owed to Your Charity by our ministry, that we say something from that which, with the Lord's consent, we have sung with heart and mouth: *The righteous man will be in everlasting remembrance, he will not fear the evil report*[^1]. For this psalm was read that it might remind us for whose good this solemnity is held. For it is not because the holy Church celebrates by an anniversary return the last days of the righteous and holy who go out of this world, that thereby honor is increased for those righteous, but an example of imitation is set before us.

**2.** For if we despise the memory of the righteous, we do not love the righteous, and we reckon them, the dead, among the dead, which far be it from us, that we should be like those Sadducees of whom, just now when the Gospel was read, we heard that, not believing the resurrection of the dead, and supposing that there is nothing of life after this life, they set before the Lord a question about seven men, who had one woman, succeeding one another in marriage to her. For they troubled the Jews with this question, but they will not trouble Christians, because they did not convict Christ, but rather were convicted by Christ. Who, then, are troubled by such a question? Those who think that the dead rise again for this, that they may live in that world likewise as they live in this world. Therefore they said: All the brothers died, and all had had one woman, afterward she too died: *in the resurrection whose wife of them will she be?*[^2] The Jews were troubled. Why were they troubled? Because they hoped for a carnal life after death. The Jews were troubled, but he whom the Jews killed was not troubled, and therefore Christ resolved this question that Christians might not be troubled in it. So let us hear our Master answering. For he says: *You err, not knowing the Scriptures nor the power of God. For in the resurrection they neither marry nor take wives, but will be like the angels in heaven*[^3]. Why then do you wait to learn whose wife she will be, where no one will have a wife? A wife is necessary in human affairs, that there may be born those who will live, to succeed those who will die. But there what need is there of a spouse, where there is no need of offspring? For what need is there that children be born, where parents do not die, where all have one Father whom they cannot carry to the grave? Because, then, in the resurrection this is promised us, that we will be like the angels in heaven, in such everlasting remembrance the righteous man will be and he will not fear the evil report. But in what everlasting remembrance he will be is plain, but from what evil report he will not fear is hidden. So it behooves that through us you be reminded of what you know, not hearing what you do not know. Now I recall it and you all will recognize from what evil report the righteous man will not fear.

**3.** *The righteous man will be in everlasting remembrance,* in which everlasting remembrance now the soul of the righteous lives in rest with the saints, removed and segregated from all the punishments and darkness of the impious; but at the end of the world, the body received, no longer mortal but immortal, no longer animal but spiritual, which *is sown in dishonor and will rise in glory*[^4], he will be made equal to the angels of God and *the righteous man will be in that everlasting remembrance.* But from what evil report will he not fear? Hear this too, and so act that you may not fear the evil report. For our Lord Jesus Christ says, who cannot be deceived, who deceives no one: *When the Son of man comes in his glory,* he says, *all the nations will be gathered before him, and he separates them as a shepherd the sheep from the goats: the sheep he sets at the right, the goats at the left*[^5]. He recalls their good works, he rebukes their evil ones. Not to make it long, *he will say to those who are at his right: Come, blessed of my Father, receive the kingdom which has been prepared for you from the origin of the world*[^6]. *In this everlasting remembrance the righteous man will be.* What will he say to those at the left? *Go into the eternal fire, which has been prepared for the devil and his angels*[^7]. *From this evil report* the righteous man *will not fear.* Let us choose, then, while we live, where we are to be after death. For according to the merits of each the righteous judge is to give rewards. Let us watch, therefore, as much as by his gift we can, that we may have as our advocate that same Son of God, who is the judge of all.

[^1]: Ps 112:6-7 (111:7)
[^2]: Matt 22:28
[^3]: Matt 22:29-30
[^4]: 1 Cor 15:43
[^5]: Matt 25:31-33
[^6]: Matt 25:34
[^7]: Matt 25:41
