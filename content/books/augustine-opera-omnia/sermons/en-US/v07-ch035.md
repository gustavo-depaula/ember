# Sermon 260: Given on the Same Day in the Leontian Church, on the Instructions for the Baptized

**1.** Lest we cause delay, having much to do, a brief but weighty sermon must be rendered to those reborn in Baptism, who today are to be mingled with the people. You who have been baptized, and today complete the sacred sign of your octave, briefly receive and understand that the figure of the circumcision of the flesh has been transferred to the circumcision of the heart. On the eighth day they are circumcised in the flesh according to the old Law: and this because of the Lord Christ, who, after the seventh day of the Sabbaths, rose on the eighth, the Lord's day. With knives of stone it was commanded that they be circumcised: the Rock was Christ. You are called infants, since you have been reborn, and have entered upon a new life, and have been born again to eternal life, if you do not suffocate by living badly what has been reborn in you. You are to be rendered to the peoples, you are to be mingled with the company of the faithful: beware lest you imitate bad faithful, or rather false faithful; as it were faithful by confessing, but unfaithful by living badly. See, because I testify to you before God and his angels: keep chastity, whether conjugal, or of complete continence. Let each render what he has vowed. You who do not have wives, it is allowed you to take wives, but ones whose husbands are not living. Women who do not have husbands, it is allowed them to marry, but those men whose wives are not living. You who have wives, do nothing bad apart from your wives. Render what you demand. Faithfulness is owed to you, faithfulness you owe. The husband owes faithfulness to the wife, the wife to the husband; both to God. Whoever have vowed continence, render what you have vowed: because it would not be demanded, if you had not vowed it. What could be allowed, is not allowed: not because marriage is condemned, but because he who looks back is condemned. Beware of frauds in your business. Beware of lies and perjuries. Beware of wordiness and self-indulgence. Whatever you do not want done to you, do not do to others, both men and God. Why should I burden you? *Do these things, and the God of peace will be with you*[^1].

[^1]: Phil 4:9
