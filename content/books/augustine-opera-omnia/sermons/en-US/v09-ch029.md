# Sermon 319/A: On Stephen the Martyr

**1.** Surely our care is your building up, and our joy is your salvation: salvation both temporal and everlasting. *For we have the promise,* as the Apostle said, *of the present life and of that to come*[^1]. But present salvation God gives both to men and to beasts, God gives it both to the good and to the bad; this temporal salvation the martyrs despised for the sake of eternal salvation. You rejoice without doubt over ours; and we in Christ rejoice over your salvation. But strive that on that day too we may rejoice with you; because what the Apostle said for his dispensation, so great and ample that we scarcely follow its traces, this is what he was saying to those whom he was building up, when he said: *You are my joy and my crown*[^2]. Whoever, then, profit in Christ from our labors, are our joy and crown: because it is ours to dispense faithfully, not our own money, but the Lord's; it is yours to receive it with great care and anxiety. For I can be a giver, not an exactor; and a giver, not as of my own property, but of God's; whence I too live: because we all belong to one great house, we all have one householder. He has a large storeroom, from which both we and you can live. Only let us ask him to wipe away loathing; let him first give hunger, and so reach out the bread. From where I live, from there I speak; from where I am fed, this I minister. For with you I am poor; the riches are common, our Lord. The life of us all is happy and eternal. So: *He who glories, let him glory in the Lord*[^3].

[^1]: 1 Tim 4:8
[^2]: Phil 4:1
[^3]: 1 Cor 1:31
