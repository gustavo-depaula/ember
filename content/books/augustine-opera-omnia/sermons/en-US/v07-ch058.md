# Sermon 271: On the Day of Pentecost

**1.** A welcome day has dawned for us, brothers, on which the holy Church gleams in the gaze of the faithful, glows in their hearts. For we celebrate this day, on which the Lord Jesus Christ, after the resurrection, glorified by the ascension, sent the Holy Spirit. For so it is written in the Gospel, when he said: *If anyone thirsts, let him come to me, and drink; he who believes in me, rivers of living water will flow from his belly*: the Evangelist goes on to expound, and says: *But this he said about the Spirit, whom those were going to receive who had believed in him. For the Spirit had not yet been given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified*[^1]. It remained, then, that Jesus being made glorious, when he rose from the dead and ascended to the heavens, the Holy Spirit should now be given, sent by him by whom he was promised: as indeed it was done. For the Lord, having lived with his disciples for forty days after the resurrection, ascended into heaven, and on the fiftieth day, which we celebrate today, sent the Holy Spirit, as it is written: that *there came suddenly a sound from heaven, as of a violent blast being borne along; and there appeared to them divided tongues, as of fire, which settled upon each: and they began to speak in all tongues, as the Spirit gave them to utter*[^2]. That blast cleansed hearts from carnal chaff; that fire consumed the hay of the old desire; those tongues, with which they spoke, filled by the Holy Spirit, prefigured the future Church through the tongues of all nations. For as after the flood the proud impiety of men built a high tower against the Lord, when the human race deserved to be divided through diverse tongues, so that each nation spoke in its own tongue, lest it be understood by others: so the humble piety of the faithful conferred the diversity of those tongues on the unity of the Church; that what discord had scattered, love might gather, and the scattered members of the human race, as of one body, might be reduced, fitted together to one head, Christ, and be welded by the fire of love into the unity of the holy body. From this gift of the Holy Spirit, then, those are utterly alien who hate the grace of peace, who do not keep the fellowship of unity. For although they too gather solemnly today, although they hear these readings by which the Holy Spirit was promised and sent: they hear them unto judgment, not unto reward. For what does it profit them to receive with their ears what they spit out from their hearts; and to celebrate his day, whose light they hate? But you, my brothers, members of the body of Christ, shoots of unity, sons of peace, keep this day joyful, celebrate it secure. For there is fulfilled in you what was prefigured in those days when the Holy Spirit came. Because as then he who received the Holy Spirit, even one man, spoke in all tongues: so now through all nations the very unity speaks in all tongues, established in which you have the Holy Spirit; you who dissent by no schism from the Church of Christ speaking in all tongues.

[^1]: John 7:37-39
[^2]: Acts 2:2-4
