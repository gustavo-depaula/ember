# Sermon 260/A: In the Octave of Easter - to the Infants

**1.** My sermon is to you, infants just now born, little ones in Christ, new offspring of the Church, grace of the Father, fruitfulness of the mother, devout shoot, new swarm, flower of our honor and fruit of our labor, my joy and crown, all of you who stand in the Lord. I address you with the Apostle's words: *See, the night has gone before, and the day has drawn near; cast off the works of darkness, and put on the armor of light; walk becomingly as in the day: not in reveling and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and jealousy; but put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh in its desires*[^1], so that by your life too you may put on him whom you have put on in the sacred sign. *For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ. There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is neither male nor female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus*[^2]. This the very force of the sacred sign holds: for it is the sacred sign of the new life, which in this time begins from the forgiveness of all past sins, but will be perfected in the resurrection of the dead. *For you were buried with Christ through baptism into death, that, as Christ rose from the dead, so you too may walk in newness of life*[^3]. But you now walk by faith, as long as you are pilgrims away from the Lord in this mortal body; but a sure way for you to him to whom you tend has been made Christ Jesus according to man, which he condescended to become for us. For he kept much sweetness for those who fear him, to open and perfect it for those who hope in him, when what we have now received in hope we have received also in reality. *For we are sons of God, and it has not yet appeared what we will be. We know that, when he appears, we will be like him, because we will see him as he is*[^4]. This he himself promised in the Gospel: *He who loves me*, he says, *keeps my commandments. And he who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him, and will show myself to him*[^5]. Surely those to whom he spoke saw him, but in the form of a servant, in which the Father is greater, not in the form of God, in which he is equal to the Father. This he showed to those who feared, that he kept for those who hoped; in this he appeared to those on pilgrimage, to that he called them to dwell together; this he spread out for them as they walked, that he promised to those arriving.

**2.** *Having these promises, then, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all defilement of flesh and spirit, perfecting sanctification in the fear of God*[^6]. *I beseech you to walk worthily of the calling by which you were called, with all humility of mind and gentleness, bearing with one another in love, striving to keep the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace*[^7]. For what kind of thing is it, from which we have received such a pledge? But there are some who have put on Christ only by the sacred sign, in which they are naked as to faith or conduct. For many heretics too have the very sacred sign of baptism, but not the very fruit of salvation, nor the bond of peace, having, as the Apostle says, *a form of piety, but denying its power*[^8], whether marked by deserters, or themselves also deserters, bearing the mark of the good king in damnable flesh; who say to us: If we are not faithful, why do you not give us baptism? But if we are already faithful, why do you seek us? As if they do not read that Simon Magus both received baptism, and yet heard from Peter: *You have no part nor lot in this faith*[^9]. See, because it can happen that someone may have the baptism of Christ, and not have the faith or love of Christ; may have the sacred sign of holiness, and not be counted in the lot of the saints. Nor does it matter, as far as the sacred sign alone is concerned, if someone receives the baptism of Christ where there is not the unity of Christ; for even one baptized in the Church, if he becomes a deserter of the Church, will lack the holiness of life, will not lack the seal of the sacred sign. For surely it is shown that one going out could not lose what is not restored to one returning; as a deserter of the army lacks the lawful fellowship, does not lack the royal stamp. And if he himself marks another with the same mark, he will not make him a partner of life, but a companion of punishment; but if both that one returns to the lawful and ordered army, and this one comes, the royal severity being appeased, both the one who departed is forgiven what he departed, and the one who came is received: in both the fault is corrected, to both the punishment is remitted, to both peace is given, in neither is what was marked repeated.

**3.** Let them not, then, now say to us: What will you give us, if we already have baptism? For they so do not know what they say that they will not even read what the holy Scripture testifies, that in the Church itself within, that is, in the communion of the members of Christ, many in Samaria were baptized and did not receive the Holy Spirit, but were in baptism alone, until the apostles came to them from Jerusalem; but on the contrary that Cornelius, and those who were with him, merited to receive the Holy Spirit, before they received the sacred sign of baptism. So God taught that the sign of salvation is one thing, salvation itself another: that the form of piety is one thing, the power of piety another. What will you give us, they say, if we already have baptism? O sacrilegious vanity, so to think that the Church of Christ, which they do not have, is nothing, that they suppose they receive nothing, if they are associated to its communion! Let the prophet Amos say to them: *Woe to those who count Zion as nothing!*[^10] What am I going to receive, he says, if I already have baptism? You are going to receive the Church, which you do not have; you are going to receive unity, which you do not have; you are going to receive peace, which you do not have. Or if these seem nothing to you, fight, deserter, against your emperor who says: *He who does not gather with me, scatters*[^11]. Fight against his Apostle, rather even there against him who spoke through him, who says: *Bearing with one another in love, striving to keep the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace*[^12]. Count what he said: bearing with, love, unity of spirit, peace. The Spirit is there the worker of all things, who was named, whom you do not have. Surely you did not bear with, you who departed from the Church? Whom did you love, when you deserted the members of Christ? What unity is yours in sacrilegious cutting-off? what peace in wicked dissension? Far be it from us that these things be nothing, but you yourself are nothing without these. If you scorn to receive these in the Church, you can indeed have baptism, but you have it for a greater punishment, whatever you have without these: for the baptism of Christ, which with these would be a support of your salvation, without these is a witness of your iniquity.

**4.** But you, holy sons, Catholic members, have received baptism not another, but for another end; for you received it not for punishment, but for life; not for ruin, but for salvation; not for condemnation, but for honor. For at the same time you received also *the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace*[^13]; if indeed you too, which I wish, which I hope, which I exhort and beg, both guard whole what you have received, and by making progress come to greater things. Today is the eighth day of your birth; today is completed in you the seal of faith, which among the ancient fathers was done in the circumcision of the flesh on the eighth day of carnal birth; for there was figured the stripping off of mortality in that human member through which man, who is to die, is born. Hence the Lord himself too, stripping himself by rising from the mortality of the flesh, and raising not indeed another, but yet a body no longer to die, marked the Lord's day in his resurrection, which is the third after the day of his passion, but in the number of days after the Sabbath the eighth, and the same the first. Hence you too, not yet in reality, but now in sure hope, since you both have the sacred sign of this thing, and have received the pledge of the Spirit, *if you have risen with Christ, set your minds on the things that are above, where Christ is, seated at the right hand of God; seek the things that are above, not the things that are on earth. For you have died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God. When Christ who is your life appears, then you also will appear with him in glory*[^14].
