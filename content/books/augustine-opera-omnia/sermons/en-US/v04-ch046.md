# Sermon 129

**1.** Let Your Charity attend to the gospel reading, which has freshly sounded in our ears, while we say a few things which the Lord grants. The Lord Jesus was speaking to the Jews, and saying to them: *Search the Scriptures, in which you think you have eternal life; they themselves bear witness about me*. Then a little after: *I*, he says, *came in the name of my Father, and you did not receive me; if another comes in his own name, him you will receive*. Then a little after: *How can you believe me, awaiting glory from one another, and not seeking the glory which is from God alone*? At last he says: *I do not accuse you before the Father; there is one who accuses you, Moses, in whom you hope. For if you believed Moses, you would perhaps believe me too; for he wrote about me. But since you do not believe his words, how can you believe me* [^1]? To these things set before us divinely, from the mouth of the reader, but by the ministry of the Savior, hear a few things, not to be numbered, but to be weighed.

**2.** For it is easy to understand all these things about the Jews. But we must beware, lest, while we attend too much to them, we take our eyes from ourselves. For the Lord was speaking to his disciples; and surely what he spoke to them, he spoke also to us who come after. For it pertains not to them alone, what he says: *Behold, I am with you even to the consummation of the age* [^2]; but to all Christians to come afterward too, and to those who will come forth even to the end of the age. Speaking to them, then, he says: *Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees* [^3]. Then they thought the Lord had said this because they had not brought loaves; they did not understand that: *Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees*, was said as this: Beware of the doctrine of the Pharisees. What was the doctrine of the Pharisees, except what you just now heard? *Seeking glory from one another, awaiting glory from one another, and not seeking the glory which is from God alone*. Of these the apostle Paul says thus: *I bear them witness, that they have zeal for God, but not according to knowledge. Zeal*, he says, *for God they have*; I know, I am aware, I was among them, I was such. *They have zeal for God, but not according to knowledge?* What is this, Apostle, *not according to knowledge?* Expound to us what knowledge you commend, which you grieve is not in them, and want to be in us. Following, he added, and opened what he had set down closed. What is: *They have zeal for God, but not according to knowledge? For being ignorant of the righteousness of God, and wanting to establish their own, they were not subject to the righteousness of God* [^4]. To be ignorant of the righteousness of God and to want to establish one's own, this is to await glory from one another and not to seek the glory which is from God alone, this is the leaven of the Pharisees. From this the Lord bids us beware. If the servants bid it, and the Lord bids it, let us beware; lest we hear: *Why do you say to me, Lord, Lord, and do not do what I say?*

**3.** Let us leave the Jews, then, for a little, to whom the Lord was then speaking. They are outside, they do not want to hear us. They hate the Gospel itself, they procured false witnesses against the Lord, to condemn him living; other witnesses they bought with money against him dead. When we say to them: Believe in Jesus, they answer us: Are we to believe in a dead man? But when we add: But he rose again, they answer: Far be it; his disciples stole him from the tomb. The Jews, buyers of falsehood, love falsehood, and despise the truth of the Lord Redeemer. What you speak, O Jew, your parents bought with money; and this remained in you, what they bought. Attend rather to him who bought you, not to the one who bought a lie for you.  
But these, as we said, let us leave; let us attend to these brothers of ours, with whom we are dealing. For Christ is head and body. The head is in heaven, the body is on earth; the head is the Lord, the body is his Church. But you remember it was said: *The two will be in one flesh. This sacrament is great*, says the Apostle, *but I say in Christ and in the Church* [^5]. If, then, the two are in one flesh, the two are in one voice. Our head, the Lord Christ, spoke to the Jews the things we heard, when the Gospel was read, the head to his enemies; let the body too, that is the Church, speak to its enemies. You know to whom it speaks. What has it to speak? I did not say it of my own, that the voice may be one; because the flesh is one, the voice is one. This, then, let us say to them: I speak with the voice of the Church. O brothers, scattered sons, straying sheep, cut-off branches, why do you calumniate me? Why do you not recognize me? *Search the Scriptures, in which you think you have eternal life; they themselves bear witness about me*; our head says to the Jews, what the body says to you: *You will seek me, and not find me* [^6]. Why? Because you do not search the Scriptures, which bear witness about me.

**4.** Testimony for the head: *To Abraham the promises were made and to his seed. It does not say: And to seeds, as in many; but as in one: And to your seed, which is Christ* [^7]. Testimony for the body to Abraham, which the Apostle recalled. *To Abraham the promises were made. I live, says the Lord; by myself I swear, that because you obeyed my voice, and did not spare your beloved son for my sake, blessing I will bless you, and fulfilling I will fulfill your seed like the stars of heaven, and like the sand of the sea; and in your seed all the nations of the earth will be blessed* [^8]. You have testimony for the head, you have for the body. Hear another, brief, and comprehended in almost one sentence, for the head and for the body. Of the resurrection of Christ the Psalm spoke: *Be exalted above the heavens, O God*. At once for the body: *And above all the earth your glory* [^9]. Hear testimony for the head: *They pierced my hands and my feet, they numbered all my bones; they themselves considered and gazed on me, they divided my garments among themselves, and over my garment they cast a lot* [^10]. Hear at once for the body, after a few words: *All the ends of the earth will be reminded, and will turn to the Lord; and all the peoples of the nations will adore in his sight; for the kingdom is the Lord's, and he will rule the nations* [^11]. Hear for the head: *And he like a bridegroom coming forth from his chamber*. And in that same Psalm hear for the body: *Their sound went out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world* [^12].

**5.** These things to the Jews, and to these our own. Why? Because these Scriptures of the Old Testament both the Jews accept, and these our own accept. But Christ himself, whom they do not accept, let us see whether these accept. Let him too say, let him say both for himself who is the head, and for his body which is the Church, because in us too the head speaks for the body. Hear for the head: He rose from the dead, found the disciples hesitating, doubting, for joy not believing; he opened their understanding, that they might understand the Scriptures, and said to them: *That so it is written, and so it must be that Christ should suffer, and rise from the dead on the third day*. You have for the head; let him say also for the body: *And that repentance and remission of sins be preached in his name through all nations, beginning from Jerusalem* [^13]. Let the Church, then, say to its enemies, let it say. It says it plainly, it is not silent: but let them hear. Brothers, you have heard the testimonies, now recognize me. *Search the Scriptures, in which you hope to have eternal life; they themselves bear witness about me*. What I have said is not of my own, but of my Lord's, and yet you still turn away, you still shuffle. *How can you believe me, awaiting glory from one another, and not seeking the glory which is from God alone*? Because, being ignorant of the righteousness of God, you have zeal for God, but not according to knowledge. For being ignorant of the righteousness of God, and wanting to establish your own, you are not subject to the righteousness of God. What else is it, to be ignorant of the righteousness of God, and to want to establish one's own, but to say: I sanctify, I justify; what I give, this is holy? Leave to God what is God's: acknowledge, man, what is man's. You are ignorant of the righteousness of God, and want to establish your own. You want to justify me; it suffices you that you be justified with me.

**6.** It was said of the Antichrist, and all so understand what the Lord says: *I came in the name of my Father, and you did not receive me; if another comes in his own name, him you will receive* [^14]. But let us hear John too: *You have heard that the Antichrist comes, and now many antichrists have come to be* [^15]. But why do we take fright at the Antichrist, except because he is going to honor his own name, and despise the name of the Lord? What else does he do who says: I justify? It is answered him: I came to Christ, not with my feet, but with my heart I came; where I heard the Gospel, there I believed, there I was baptized; because I believed in Christ, I believed in God. And he: You are not clean. Why? Because I was not there. Say why I am not cleansed, a man who was baptized in Jerusalem, a man who was baptized, for example, among the Ephesians, to whom you read the Letter given, and whose peace you spurn? Behold, the Apostle wrote to the Ephesians: the Church was founded, it remains until now; and more richly it remains, more manifold it remains, it holds what it received from the Apostle: *If anyone announces to you other than what you received, let him be anathema* [^16]. What, then? What do you say to me? I am not clean? Baptized there, am I not clean? Indeed you are not. Why? Because I was not there. But he who is everywhere was there. He who is everywhere was there, in whose name I believed. You, coming from I know not where, rather not coming, but wanting me to come to you, set here, say to me: You are not rightly baptized, because I was not there. See who was there. What was said to John? *Upon whom you see the Spirit descending like a dove, this is he who baptizes* [^17]. Him you have seeking you; rather, because you envied him to me, baptized, you have lost him.

**7.** Understand, then, my brothers, our voice and theirs, and see what you choose. We say this: Are we holy, God knows; are we wicked, this too he knows the more; do not set your hope in us, of whatever sort we are. If we are good, do what is written: *Be imitators of me, as I am of Christ* [^18]. But if we are evil, not even so are you deserted, not even so are you left without counsel; hear the one saying: *What they say, do; but what they do, do not do* [^19]. But those on the contrary: Unless we are good, you have perished. Behold, there is another who will come in his own name. Will my life, then, depend on you, and my salvation be bound to you? Have I so forgotten my foundation? Was not the rock Christ [^20]? Does not he who builds upon the rock—the wind, the rain, the rivers do not throw him down [^21]? Come, then, with me, if you will, upon the rock, and do not want to be a rock for me.  
Let the Church, then, say also that last thing: *If you believed Moses, you would believe me too; for he wrote about me* [^22]; because I am his body, of whom he wrote. And of the Church Moses wrote. For I spoke Moses's words: *In your seed all the nations will be blessed*. Moses wrote this in the first book. If you believed Moses, you would believe Christ too. Because you despise the words of Moses, it is necessary that you despise the words of Christ. *They have there*, he says, *Moses and the Prophets, let them hear them. No, father Abraham; but if someone comes from the dead, him they will hear*. And he: *If they do not hear Moses and the Prophets, neither, if someone rises from the dead, will they believe* [^23]. This was said of the Jews; was it then not said of heretics?

**8.** He had risen from the dead who said: *It behooved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead on the third day*. This I believe. I believe, he says. Do you believe? Why do you not believe what follows? Because you believe: *It behooved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead on the third day*; this was said of the head; believe also what follows about the Church: *That repentance and remission of sins be preached in his name through all nations* [^24]. Why do you believe about the head, and not believe about the body? What has the Church done to you, that you want, as it were, to behead it? You want to take away the head of the Church, and believe in the head, leave the body, as a lifeless body. Without cause you flatter the head, as a devout servant. He who wants to behead, tries to kill both head and body. They blush to deny Christ, and do not blush to deny the words of Christ. Christ neither we have seen with the eyes, nor you. The Jews saw him, and killed him. We have not seen him, and we believe; his words are with us. Compare yourselves to the Jews; they despised him hanging on the wood, you despise him sitting in heaven; at their prompting the title of Christ stood, while you stand the Baptism of Christ is blotted out. But what remains, brothers, except that we pray even for the proud, that we pray even for the lifted up, who so exalt themselves? Let us say for them to God: *Let them know that to you is the name Lord*; and not men, but *you alone are the Most High over all the earth* [^25]. Turned toward the Lord . . .
