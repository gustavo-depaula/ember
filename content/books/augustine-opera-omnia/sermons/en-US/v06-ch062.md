# Sermon 224: On the Day of Easter

**1.** On this present day, those baptized in Christ and reborn are to be mingled with the whole people of God, the solemnity of the sacraments being celebrated. Let us address them, and you in them and them in you. Behold, you have been made members of Christ. If you should consider what you have been made, *all* your *bones will say: Lord, who is like you?*[^1] For this condescension cannot be worthily thought. Does not all speech and sense fail that to you has come gratuitous grace, with no preceding merits? And therefore it is grace, because it was given freely. What grace? That you may be members of Christ the Son of God, that you too may be brothers of the Only-begotten. If he is the Only-begotten, whence are you brothers, except that he is the Only-begotten by nature, you brothers by grace? Because, then, you are members of Christ, I admonish you. Let those hear me with whom you are to be mingled. Today I fear for you, not so much from pagans, not so much from Jews, not so much from heretics, as from evil catholics. Choose for yourselves among the people of God those whom you may imitate. For if you wish to imitate the crowd, you will not be among the few walking the narrow way[^2]. Abstain from robberies, from frauds, from perjuries. Let the gulf of drunkenness be driven from you. Fear fornications as you fear death — death not that which looses the soul from the body, but where the soul will always burn with the body.

**2.** I know that the devil plays his parts, and does not cease to speak in the hearts of those whom, by seducing, he draws to his side. I know that to fornicators and adulterers, who are not content with their spouses, the devil says in the heart: "The sins of the flesh are not great." Against this, against the devil's whispering, we ought to have Christ's incantation. Because the enemy deceives Christians through the enticement of the flesh, when he makes light for them what is grave, but by lying, not by truly making it so. What does it profit that Satan makes light what Christ shows to be grave? What does it profit that a great weight of unhappiness is weighed light in the balance of falsehood? What does it profit that the devil tells you light what God tells you grave? Will you not find grave what God told you, and the promiser, the enemy, be far from you? Does the devil do something new? to say to faithful Christians: "It is nothing that you do: you sin in your flesh. For you do not sin in the spirit. It is easily blotted out, pardon is easily given." What great thing does he do? That is the artifice which he spoke in paradise: "You will not die the death." God had said: *On the day you eat, you will die the death*[^3]. The enemy came, and said: "What, because God said to you: *You will die the death? You will not die the death, but when you eat, your eyes will be opened, and you will be like gods*"[^4]. The threatening of God was set aside, the promise of the devil was heard. The threatening of God was found true, and the deception of the devil false. But what did it profit, I beg you, what did it profit that the woman said: *The serpent led me on*, and the man himself: *The woman whom you gave me as companion gave me, and I ate*[^5]? Did the excuse avail, and did not the damnation follow?

**3.** Therefore I say to you, my brothers, my children. You who have wives, know nothing else. You who have not, and wish to marry, keep yourselves intact for them, as you wish them to come intact to you. You who have promised continence, do not look back[^6]. Behold, I have told you. I have freed myself. The Lord set me as a dispenser, not an exactor. And yet where we can, where it is granted, where it is permitted, where we know, we rebuke, we chide, we anathematize, we excommunicate. And yet we do not correct. Because *neither he who plants is anything, nor he who waters, but God who gives the growth*[^7]. And now, because I speak to you, because I terrify you, because I admonish you, it is needful that God hear me, and do something in silence in your hearts. Briefly I say it, and this I commend to you. I both terrify the faithful, and build you up. You are members of Christ; do not — hear me, hear the Apostle: *Taking, then, the members of Christ, shall I make them the members of a harlot?*[^8] Someone or other has to say: But she is not a harlot, she is my concubine. Do you have a wife, you who say this? I have. Then she, whether you will or not, is a harlot. Go, and say that the bishop did you an injury, if you have a wife, and another sleeps with you. Whoever she is, she is a harlot. But perhaps she keeps faith with you, and knows none but you alone, and does not arrange to know another. Since, then, she is chaste, why do you fornicate? If she has one, why do you have two? It is not lawful, it is not lawful, it is not lawful. They go into hell. At least from this let me be free. At least from this let it be allowed me to say what is true. Let those who are such correct themselves while they live. Suddenly death may come, and there is no one to be corrected. When the last hour may come is unknown. When it is said "tomorrow, tomorrow," it becomes a crow: it goes, and does not return. But hear me, you baptized; hear me, you infants; hear me, you reborn through Christ. I beseech you by the altar to which you have come, by the sacraments you have received, by the name which is invoked over you, by the future judgment of the living and the dead, I beseech, I adjure and I bind you by the name of Christ; do not imitate them, except those whom you have known to be such faithful ones. Amen.

[^2]: See Matt 7:13-14
[^3]: See Gen 2:17
[^4]: Gen 3:4-5
[^5]: Gen 3:12-13
[^6]: See Luke 9:62
[^7]: 1 Cor 3:7
[^8]: 1 Cor 6:15
