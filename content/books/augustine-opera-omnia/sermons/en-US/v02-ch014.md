# Sermon 28/A

*God alone is truthful, but every man a liar, as it is written*[^1]. Therefore, if God is truthful and alone truthful, but every man a liar, whence will a man be truthful, unless he draws near to the one who is not a liar? Finally, it is said to men: *You were once darkness*[^2]; see, *every man a liar*. But to God it is said: *With you is the fountain of life, in your light we shall see light*[^3]. And because God alone is truthful, *for God is light, and in him there is no darkness at all*[^4]; men are darkness, God is light; man is a liar, God is truthful. When will a man be truthful? *Draw near to him and be enlightened*[^5]. This, then, Scripture wished to show: that every man, absolutely every man, as far as pertains to the man himself, is a liar. For he is a liar only from what is his own, nor has he anything of his own except that he is a liar—not because he cannot be truthful, but because he will not be truthful from what is his own. Therefore, that he may be truthful: *I believed, and for that reason I have spoken*[^6]. Take away from him *I believed: every man a liar*. For when he has withdrawn from the truth of God, he will remain in his own lie, since *he who speaks a lie speaks from what is his own*[^7]. Say, then: *What shall I render to the Lord for all that he has rendered to me?*[^8] For *in my dread I said*, and I said truly: *every man a liar*[^9]. But he rendered to me, not punishment for my lie, but good for evil, and by justifying the impious he made a truthful man out of a liar.

[^1]: Rom 3:4
[^2]: Eph 5:8
[^3]: Ps 36:9 (35:10)
[^4]: 1 John 1:5
[^5]: Ps 34:5 (33:6)
[^6]: Ps 116:10 (115:1); 2 Cor 4:13
[^7]: John 8:44
[^8]: Ps 116:12 (115:3)
[^9]: Ps 116:11 (115:2)
