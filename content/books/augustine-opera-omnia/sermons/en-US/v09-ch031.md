# Sermon 321: The Monday of Easter Week

We said indeed yesterday, as Your Charity remembers: The booklet of this man is his appearance. Yet because he has made known to us some things which you ought to know, for the greater admiration and glory of our Lord, concerning the memorials of his saints, of whom it was said: *Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints*[^1]; it is fitting also to give a booklet, which has all the things we have learned from his mouth. But if the Lord wills, today it will be prepared, and recited to you tomorrow.

[^1]: Ps 116:15 (115:15)
