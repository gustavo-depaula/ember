# Sermon 204/A: (éTaix 4)

**1.** As the heavens declared the glory of God, a star led the Magi to adore Christ. These Magi were the first-fruits of the nations. They exhort you to do what they themselves did. Today Christ received gifts; put your hands into your bags, bring forth thence what may be pleasing to Christ. For he willed to be in want in his poor, and there was no place for him in the inn, for whom the whole world does not suffice, and he was set, as you know, in a manger. He was sought to be adored, he lay hidden when indicated. The Jews are questioned where Christ is born; they say: *in Bethlehem*[^1]. They show whither one should go and do not go. Milestones show the way, they are fixed in their place. A star appears as it were the tongue of the heavens, it leads the Magi, it shows the place. Infancy is not despised, even the little one is adored, because he is understood to be great. The Word of God received gifts, the infant was still silent and yet was in the doctrine of the angels. He was still silent, concerning whom the Gospel was to be fulfilled.

**2.** For the heavens too were afterward to narrate his glory; the heavens are the apostles, flashing with miracles, thundering with precepts. For of these it was said: *Into all the earth their sound has gone out, and to the ends of the world their words*[^2]. When could the words be hidden from us which came even to the ends of the earth? They came to us and found and changed us. The weakness of Christ is our firmness, the infancy of Christ is our eloquence, the want of Christ is our abundance, because afterward too the death of Christ was made our life. The bread from heaven lay in the manger. Beasts of the Lord, if you are hungry, approach, carry him in your hearts, and you are his beasts. He himself is your rider, he himself your food. You know that he sat on a donkey and led that very donkey into Jerusalem. Carry him, he knows whither to lead you. Walk securely, you will be in no error under so great a rider; there is a way that leads to the heavenly Jerusalem. It is faith; walk in it, that you may carry Christ, and may be able to arrive at eternal beatitude.

[^1]: Matt 2:5
[^2]: Ps 19:4 (18:5)
