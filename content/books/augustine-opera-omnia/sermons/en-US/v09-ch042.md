# Sermon 332: On the Feast of the Martyrs

**1.** When we offer honor to the martyrs, we honor the friends of Christ. Do you ask what cause made them friends of Christ? Christ himself shows it: for he says: *This is my commandment, that you love one another*[^1]. Those love one another who watch actors together, those love one another who get drunk together in taverns, those love one another who are joined by a bad conscience. Since, then, Christ had said: *This is my commandment, that you love one another,* he ought to have distinguished the love. He surely did: hear. For when he had said: *This is my commandment, that you love one another;* he soon added *as I have loved you*[^2]. So love one another, for the sake of the kingdom of God, for the sake of eternal life. Love together, but love me. You would love one another, if together you loved an actor: rather love one another, by loving together him who cannot displease, the Savior.

**2.** The Lord drew nearer, and taught further: as though we said to him, And how did you love us, that we too may know how we ought to love ourselves? Hear: *Greater love than this no one has, than that he lay down his soul for his friends*[^3]. So love one another, that each one lay down his soul for the rest. For this the martyrs did, which John the Evangelist too says in his Epistle: *As Christ laid down his soul for us, so we too ought to lay down our souls for the brothers*[^4]. You approach the table of a powerful one: you who are faithful know to what table you approach: recall the Scripture saying: *When you approach the table of a powerful one, know that you ought to prepare such things*[^5]. To what table of a powerful one do you approach? To one who offers you himself, not a table seasoned by the art of cooks: Christ offers you his table, namely himself. Approach this table, and be filled. Be poor, and you will be filled. *The poor will eat, and be filled*[^6]. *Know that you ought to prepare such things.* That you may understand, attend to John the expositor. For perhaps you did not know what is: *When you approach the table of a powerful one, know that you ought to prepare such things.* Hear the expositor: *As Christ laid down his soul for us, so we too ought* to prepare such things. What is, to prepare such things? *To lay down our souls for the brothers*[^7].

**3.** That you might be filled, you approached poor: from where will you prepare such things? Ask from him who invited you, that you may have from where to feed him. Unless he himself gives to you, you will have nothing. But have you already some love? Do not impute even this to yourself: *For what do you have that you have not received?*[^8] Have you already some love? Ask that he increase it, ask that he perfect it, until you come to that table, than which there is no greater in this life. *Greater love than this no one has, than that he lay down his soul for his friends*[^9]. You approached poor, you withdraw rich: indeed you do not withdraw, but by remaining you will be rich. From him the martyrs received what they suffered for him: believe it; from him they received it. The householder gave them from where they might feed him. Him we have, from him let us ask. And if we are less worthy to receive, through his friends, who fed him from his own gift, let us ask. Let them pray for us, that he may give to us too. And that we may have more, we receive from heaven. Hear John his forerunner: *A man cannot receive anything, unless it has been given him from heaven*[^10]. So both what we have, we have received from heaven; and that we may have more, we receive from heaven.

**4.** It is the city which descends from heaven: let us be such that we may enter into it. For you heard what sort enter, what sort do not enter. Do not be such as you heard would not enter: especially fornicators. For when Scripture recalled those who will not enter, it there named murderers too: you were not frightened. It named fornicators: I heard that you beat your breasts. I heard, I heard, I saw: and what I did not see in your beds, I saw in the sound, I saw in your breasts, when you beat your breasts. Cast out from there the sin: for to beat the breast, and to do these same things, is nothing else than to pave over sins. My brothers, my sons, be chaste, love chastity, embrace chastity, love purity: because God the author of purity, in his temple, which you are, seeks it; far from the temple he drives out the unclean. Let your wives suffice you, because you want to suffice your wives. You do not want anything to be done by her apart from you: do not do anything apart from her. You are the lord, she the handmaid: God made both. *Sarah,* says Scripture, *obeyed Abraham, calling him lord*[^11]. It is true; the bishop has subscribed to these tablets: your wives are your handmaids, you are the lords of your wives. But when it comes to that business by which the sex is distinguished, and each sex mingles with itself: *The wife does not have power over her own body, but the husband*[^12]. You rejoiced, you raised yourself up, you boasted. Well did the Apostle say, excellently did the Chosen Vessel say: *The wife does not have power over her own body, but the husband.* Because I am the lord. You praised: hear what follows, hear what you do not want, I ask that you want it. What is this? Hear: *Likewise the husband too;* that lord; *likewise the husband too does not have power over his own body, but the wife*[^13]. This hear gladly. The vice is taken from you, not the lordship: your adulteries are forbidden, women are not set over you. You are a man (vir), show it: for "man" is from "virtue," or "virtue" from "man." Have you, then, virtue? Conquer lust. *The head of the woman,* he says, *is the man*[^14]. If you are the head, lead, and let her follow: but see where you lead. You are the head, lead where she may follow: but do not go where you do not want her to follow. Lest you fall into the precipice, see that you walk on the straight path. So prepare yourselves to enter to that new bride, to that beautiful one, adorned by her husband, not with necklaces, but with virtues. For if chaste, and holy, and good you enter, you too will be members of that new bride, the blessed and glorious heavenly Jerusalem.
