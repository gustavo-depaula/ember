# Sermon 268: On the Day of Pentecost

**1.** Because of the coming of the Holy Spirit today is a solemn day for us, the fiftieth from the Lord's resurrection, seven weeks multiplied. But counting seven weeks, you will find forty-nine: one is added, that unity may be commended to us. What did the very coming of the Holy Spirit do? From what did it teach its presence? from what did it show it? They all spoke in the tongues of all nations. But they were in one place, one hundred twenty: by ten the number twelve of the apostles, consecrated by a mystery, was multiplied tenfold. What then, did the individuals on whom the Holy Spirit came speak, each in single tongues of all the nations, that one in another tongue, and this one in another, and as it were divide among themselves the tongues of all nations? Not so: but each man, one man, spoke in the tongues of all nations. One man spoke in the tongues of all nations: the unity of the Church in the tongues of all nations. See, here too the unity of the Catholic Church, spread through the whole world, is commended.

**2.** He, then, who has the Holy Spirit is in the Church, which speaks in the tongues of all. Whoever is outside this Church does not have the Holy Spirit. For the Holy Spirit condescended to show himself in the tongues of all nations for this reason, that he may understand himself to have the Holy Spirit who is contained in the unity of the Church, which speaks in all tongues. *One body*, the apostle Paul says: *One body, and one Spirit*[^1]. Attend to our members. The body is composed of many members, and one spirit gives life to all the members. See, by the human spirit, by which I myself am a man, I gather all the members: I command the members to be moved, I direct the eyes to seeing, the ears to hearing, the tongue to speaking, the hands to working, the feet to walking. The offices of the members are divided, but one spirit holds all things together. Many things are commanded, many things are done: one commands, one is served. What our spirit is, that is, our soul, to our members; this the Holy Spirit is to the members of Christ, to the body of Christ, which is the Church. Therefore the Apostle, when he had named one body, lest we understand a dead body: *One*, he says, *body.* But I ask you, does this body live? It lives. From what? From one spirit. *And one Spirit.* Attend, then, brothers, in our body, and grieve for those who are cut off from the Church. In our members, as long as we live, when we are healthy, all the members fulfill their offices. If one member somewhere is in pain, all the members suffer with it. Yet because it is in the body, it can be in pain, it cannot expire. For what is it "to expire," except to lose the spirit? But now if a member is cut off from the body, does the spirit follow it? And yet it is recognized what the member is; it is a finger, it is a hand, it is an arm, it is an ear: apart from the body it has form, but does not have life. So too a man separated from the Church. You ask of him the sacred sign, you find it: you ask for Baptism, you find it: you ask for the creed, you find it. It is form: unless within you are given life by the spirit, in vain do you glory outwardly in the form.

**3.** Beloved, God greatly commends unity. Let this very thing move you, that in the beginning of created things, when God established all things, he made the stars in heaven, but on earth the plants and trees, and said: *Let the earth bring forth*, and the trees were brought forth, and all green things: he said: *Let the waters bring forth swimming and flying things*, and so it was done: *Let the earth bring forth the living soul of all cattle and beasts*, and so it was done[^2]. Surely God did not make the other birds from one bird? surely not all the fish from one fish? all the horses from one horse? all the beasts from one beast? Did not the earth bring forth many at once, and fill many things with manifold offspring? It came to the making of man, and one was made, from one the human race. Nor did he wish to make two separately, male and female: but one, and from one, one. Why so? Why is the human race begun from one, except because unity is commended to the human race? And the Lord Christ from one, unity, is a virgin; he holds virginity, keeps incorruption.

**4.** The Lord himself commends the unity of the Church to the apostles: he shows himself, they think they see a spirit: they are terrified, they are strengthened, it is said to them: *Why are you troubled, and do thoughts arise in your heart? See my hands: handle and see, that a spirit does not have bones and flesh, as you see me to have*[^3]. See, while they were still troubled for joy, he takes food, not from need, but from power; he takes it before them: he commends, against the impious, the truth of the body, he commends the unity of the Church. For what does he say? *Are not these the things that I spoke to you, while I was still with you, that all things must be fulfilled which are written in the law of Moses, and the Prophets, and the Psalms about me? Then he opened their minds*, the Gospel says, *that they might understand the Scriptures. And he said to them, That so it is written, and so it was fitting for Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead on the third day.* See our head; see the head, where are the members? See the bridegroom, where is the bride? Read the marriage records: hear the bridegroom. Do you seek the bride? Hear from him: no one takes away his own from him, no one substitutes another's: hear from him. Where do you seek Christ? In the fables of men, or in the truth of the Gospels? He suffered, he rose on the third day, he showed himself to his disciples. Now we have him: where do we seek her? Let us ask of him: *It was fitting for Christ to suffer and to rise from the dead on the third day.* See, it is now done, it is now seen. Tell, O Lord; tell, Lord, lest we err: *And that repentance and the forgiveness of sins be preached in his name among all nations, beginning from Jerusalem*[^4]. He began from Jerusalem, and reached us. And he is there, and here. For not, that he might come to us, did he depart from there: he grew, he did not migrate. This he commended immediately after his resurrection. He spent forty days with them: about to ascend into heaven, again he commended the Church. The bridegroom, about to set out, commended his bride to his friends: not that any of them should love her; but himself as the bridegroom, them as the friends of the bridegroom, none of them as the bridegroom. This the friends of the bridegroom are zealous for, and they do not allow her to be corrupted by lascivious love. They hate it when she is so loved. Attend to the zealous friend of the bridegroom: when he saw the bride in a way fornicating through the friends of the bridegroom, he said: *I hear that there are schisms among you, and in part I believe it.* *It has been reported to me about you, brothers, by those of Chloe, that there are contentions among you, and each of you says: I indeed am of Paul; but I of Apollos; but I of Cephas; but I of Christ. Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you, or were you baptized in the name of Paul?*[^5] O the friend! He repels from himself the love of another's bride. He does not want to be loved in place of the bride, that he may be able to reign with the bridegroom. The Church, then, was commended: and when he ascended into heaven, he said thus to those who asked about the end of the age: *Tell us, when will these things be, and when the time of your coming?* And he: *It is not yours to know the times, which the Father has set in his own power.* Hear what you should know from the master, disciple: *But you will receive the power of the Holy Spirit coming upon you*[^6]. And it was done: on the fortieth day he ascended into heaven, and see, today, the Holy Spirit coming, all who were present are filled, they speak in the tongues of all nations. Likewise that very unity is commended through the tongues of all nations. It is commended by the rising Lord, commended by the ascending Christ; confirmed by the Holy Spirit coming today.

[^1]: Eph 4:4
[^2]: See Gen 1:11-24
[^3]: Luke 24:38-39
[^4]: Luke 24:44-47
[^5]: 1 Cor 1:11-13; 11:18
[^6]: Acts 1:7-8
