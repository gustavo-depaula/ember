# Sermon 146

**1.** In today's reading your Charity has noticed that it was said to Peter by the Lord, by way of question, *Do you love me?* To which he answered, *You know, Lord, that I love you*. This a second time, this a third time; and at each word of the one answering the Lord said, *Feed my lambs*. Christ commended to Peter his lambs to be fed, who fed Peter too. For what could Peter bestow on the Lord, especially now bearing an immortal body and about to ascend into heaven? As if he were saying to him, *Do you love me?* In this show that you love me: *Feed my sheep*[^1]. So, brothers, hear with obedience that you are the sheep of Christ; because we too hear with fear, *Feed my sheep*. If we feed with fear and fear for the sheep, how ought the sheep themselves to fear for themselves? Let the care, then, belong to us, the obedience to you; to us pastoral vigilance, to you the humility of the flock. And yet we too, who seem to you to speak from a higher place, are with fear beneath your feet; since we know how perilous an account is rendered for this, as it were, lofty seat. Therefore, dearly beloved, catholic offspring, members of Christ, consider what a head you have. Children of God, consider what a Father you have found. Christians, consider what an inheritance is promised you. Not such as on earth cannot be possessed by children unless their parents have died. For no one on earth possesses his father's inheritance unless the father is dead. We shall possess, while our Father lives, what he will give; because our Father cannot die. I add more, I say more, and I say what is true; the Father himself will be our inheritance.

**2.** Live in a fitting manner, especially you, candidates of Christ, recently baptized, just now regenerated, as I admonished you before, and now I say, and I declare my solicitude; because the present Gospel reading has filled me with greater fear; watch yourselves, do not imitate bad Christians. Do not say: I will do this, because many of the faithful do this. This is not to prepare a defense for the soul, but to seek companions for hell. Grow in this field of the Lord; in it you will find good people, who will please you too, if you yourselves are good. For are you our private property? Heretics and schismatics made themselves private flocks out of the Lord's stolen goods, and wished to feed flocks not of Christ but their own against Christ. Indeed, in their very plunderings they placed his title, so that their plunder might as it were be defended through the title of the powerful one. What does Christ do when such people are converted, who outside the Church received the title of his Baptism? He casts out the plunderer, he does not lay aside the title, and he possesses the house; because he finds his own title there. What need is there for him to change his name? Surely they attend to what the Lord says to Peter, *Feed my lambs, feed my sheep*[^2]? Surely he did not say to him: Feed your lambs; or, Feed your sheep? But to those shut out, what did he say in the *Song of Songs* to the Church? Speaking, the bridegroom to the bride, he says, *Unless you know yourself, beautiful among women, go out*[^3]. As if saying: I do not cast you out, you go out, unless you know yourself, beautiful among women, unless you know yourself in the mirror of divine Scripture; unless you attend, O beautiful woman, to the mirror that does not deceive you with false splendor; unless you know that it was said of you, *Over all the earth is your glory*[^4]; that it was said of you, *I will give you the nations as your inheritance, and the ends of the earth as your possession*[^5]; and innumerable other testimonies which commend the catholic Church. Unless, then, you know yourself, you have no part, you cannot make yourself an heir. So, *go out in the footprints of the flocks*, not in the fellowship of the flock: *and feed your kids*[^6], not as it was said to Peter, *my sheep*. To Peter it is said, *my sheep*; to schismatics it is said, *your kids*. Here *sheep*, there *kids*; here *my*, there *your*. Recall the right hand and the left hand of our judge; recall where the kids will stand, and where the sheep[^7]; and it will appear to you where is the right hand, where the left, the white and the black, the bright and the dark, the beautiful and the deformed, the one to receive the kingdom and the one to find everlasting punishment.

[^1]: See John 21:15-17
[^2]: See John 21:15-17
[^3]: Song 1:8
[^4]: Ps 57:5,11 (56:6,12)
[^5]: Ps 2:8
[^6]: Song 1:8
[^7]: See Matt 25:32-33
