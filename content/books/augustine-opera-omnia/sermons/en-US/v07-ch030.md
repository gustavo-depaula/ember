# Sermon 255/A: In the Days of Easter, on the Alleluia

**1.** It is fitting for us to say as many praises as we can to our Creator. For when, most beloved brothers, we praise the Lord, we confer something on ourselves, while we stretch ourselves out into his love. For we have sung: *Alleluia. Alleluia* is a new song.

**2.** A new man sings a new song. We have sung: you too have sung, infants, who have recently been made new by him; and we have sung with you, because we have been redeemed at the same price. What fraternal love requires, let me admonish you—not only you, but I admonish also those who hear, as brothers and sons; brothers, because one mother Church bore us; sons, because through the Gospel I begot you. Live well, most beloved sons, that you may be able to have good causes from so great a sacred sign received. Let vices be corrected, let conduct be set in order, let virtues be taken up; let there be in each of you piety, holiness, chastity, humility, sobriety; that, offering such fruits to God, he may delight in you, and you in him. Let us too rejoice over the advance of your hope, seeing in you the fruits of the reward of our hope. Love the Lord, because he loves you; frequent this mother, who bore you. See what this mother has conferred on you, that she might join the creature to the Creator, make servants sons of God, make the chattels of the devil brothers of Christ. To these so great benefits of hers you will not be ungrateful, if you offer her the worthy homage of your presence. Nor can anyone have God the Father favorable, who has despised the Church his mother. This holy and spiritual mother, then, daily prepares for you spiritual food, by which she may refresh not your bodies but your souls. She bestows on you the heavenly bread, she offers you the cup of salvation; she does not want any of her sons to labor under such hunger. Act for yourselves, most beloved, do not abandon so great a mother; that you may be filled with the abundance of her house, and that she may give you to drink of the torrent of her delights, and may assign to God the Father worthy sons, whom, to be devoutly nourished, she may lead, safe and free, to eternal life.
