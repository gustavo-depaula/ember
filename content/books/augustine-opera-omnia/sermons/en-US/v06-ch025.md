# Sermon 204/A: A Sermon on the Epiphany

**1.** The heavens declaring the glory of God, the star led the Magi to adore Christ[^1]. These Magi were the firstfruits of the Gentiles. They exhort you to do what they themselves did. Today Christ received gifts[^2]; put your hands into your bundles, bring forth from there what may be pleasing to Christ. For he willed to be in want in his poor, and there was no room for him in the lodging[^3], for whom the whole world does not suffice, and he was laid, as you know, in a manger. He was sought, to be adored; he lay hidden, when pointed out. The Jews are asked where Christ is born[^4], they say: *In Bethlehem*[^5]. They show where one should go, and do not go. The milestones show the way, are fixed in place. The star appears as the tongue of the heavens, leads the Magi, shows the place[^6]. The infancy is not despised, he is adored even when small, because he is understood to be great. The Word of God received gifts, the infant was still silent, and yet he was in the teaching of the angels. He was still silent, about whom the Gospel was to be fulfilled.

**2.** For the heavens too were afterward to declare his glory[^7]; the heavens are the apostles, flashing with miracles, thundering with precepts. For of these it is said: *Their sound went out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world*[^8]. How could the words be hidden from us which came even to the ends of the earth? They came to us and found and transformed us. The weakness of Christ is our strength, the infancy of Christ is our eloquence, the need of Christ is our abundance, because afterward too the death of Christ became our life. The bread from heaven lay in a manger. If you, the Lord's beasts of burden, are hungry, draw near, carry him in your hearts, and you are his beasts of burden. He himself is your rider, he himself your food. You know that he sat on an ass and led that very ass into Jerusalem[^9]. Carry him, he knows where to lead you. Walk securely, you will be in no error under so great a rider; there is a way that leads to the heavenly Jerusalem. It is faith; walk in it, that you may carry Christ, and may be able to come to eternal blessedness.

[^1]: See Ps 19:1 (18:2); Matt 2:2,9-11
[^2]: See Matt 2:11
[^3]: See Luke 2:7
[^4]: See Matt 2:4
[^5]: Matt 2:5
[^6]: See Matt 2:9
[^7]: See Ps 19:1 (18:2)
[^8]: Ps 19:4 (18:5)
[^9]: See Matt 21:7
