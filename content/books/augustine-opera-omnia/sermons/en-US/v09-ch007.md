# Sermon 307: On the Beheading of Blessed John the Baptist

**1. 1.** When the holy Gospel was being read, a cruel spectacle was set before our eyes: the head of holy John on a platter, a deadly dish of cruelty, on account of hatred of the truth. The girl dances, and the mother rages: and amid the delights and wantonness of the banqueters a rash oath is sworn, and what is sworn is impiously fulfilled. It happened to John as he himself had foretold. For he had said of the Lord Jesus Christ: *He must increase, but I must decrease*[^1]. The one was diminished in his head, the other increased on the cross. The truth bore hatred. It could not be borne with an even mind that the holy man of God admonished them: he who surely sought the salvation of those whom he so admonished. They answered him evil for good. For what would he say, except that with which he was full? And what would they answer, except that with which they were full? He sowed wheat, but found thorns. He said to the king: *It is not lawful for you to have your brother's wife*[^2]. For lust conquered the king: he kept with him his brother's forbidden wife. Yet he so pleased the king that he did not rage. He honored the one from whom he heard the truth. But the detestable woman conceived a hatred, which someday, given the time, she would bring forth. When she was in labor, she bore a daughter, a dancing daughter. And that king who held John a holy man, who feared him on account of the Lord, even though he did not obey him, after John's head on a platter was asked of him, was grieved. But on account of the oath and on account of those reclining at table, he sent the executioner, and fulfilled what he had sworn.

**2. 2.** This passage admonishes us, dearly beloved, that for the sake of your life and conduct we should treat something for you concerning oaths. False swearing is no light sin: indeed, so great a sin is it to swear falsely, that on account of the guilt of false swearing the Lord forbade all swearing. For he says: *It was said, You shall not swear falsely, but you shall perform your oaths to the Lord: but I say to you, do not swear at all, neither by heaven, for it is God's throne; nor by the earth, for it is the footstool of his feet; nor by any other oath whatever; nor shall you swear by your head, because you cannot make one hair white or black. But let there be in your mouth, Yes, yes; No, no. Anything more than this is from evil*[^3].

**2. 3.** But we find in the holy Scriptures that the Lord swore, when Abraham obeyed him even to the immolation of his beloved son. The angel spoke to him from heaven, saying: *By myself I swear, says the Lord, that because you have obeyed my voice, and have not spared your beloved son for my sake, blessing I will bless you, and I will fill up your seed, like the stars of heaven, and like the sand of the sea; and in your seed all nations will be blessed*[^4]. That you see Christians filling the whole world, the truthful oath of God brings to pass. And likewise in the Psalms it was prophesied of the Lord Jesus Christ: *The Lord has sworn, and he will not repent: You are a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek*[^5]. Those who know the Scriptures know what Melchizedek, the priest of God Most High, brought forth when he blessed Abraham. We need not recall this, on account of the catechumens. The faithful, however, recognize how it was prophesied beforehand, what we now see fulfilled. And from where is this? Because the Lord has sworn. And *the Lord has sworn, and he will not repent*[^6]: not as Herod repented because he had sworn.

**3. 4.** Since, then, the Lord has sworn, why did the Lord Christ forbid his own to swear? I will say why. It is not a sin to swear truly. But because it is a great sin to swear falsely, far from the sin of swearing falsely is he who does not swear at all; he draws near to false swearing who swears even truly. The Lord, then, who forbade swearing, did not want you to walk on the bank of the river, lest your foot slip in the narrow place, and you fall. But the Lord swore, you say. He swears secure who does not know how to lie. Let it not move you that the Lord swore; for perhaps no one ought to swear except God. For when you swear, what do you do? You call God as witness. You call him, he calls himself. But you, a man, because in many things you are deceived, for the most part you call truth as witness for your falsehood. Sometimes even unwillingly a man swears falsely, when he thinks what he swears to be true. This is indeed not so great a sin as that of one who knows it to be false, and yet swears. How much better, and altogether far removed from this grave sin, is he who hears the Lord Christ, and does not swear?

**4. 5.** I know this is hard for your habit: but it was hard for our habit too. By fearing God we took swearing from our mouth. See, we live among you: who has ever heard us swearing? Had I not been accustomed to swear daily? But when I read, and feared, I struggled against my habit, and in the very struggle I called upon the Lord as helper. The Lord gave me the help of not swearing. Nothing is easier for me than not to swear. I have admonished Your Charity of this, lest you say, Who can do it? Oh, if only God were feared! oh, if only perjurers trembled! the tongue is bridled, the truth is kept, swearing is taken away.

[^1]: John 3:30
[^2]: Mark 6:18
[^3]: Matt 5:33-37
[^4]: Gen 22:16-18
[^5]: Ps 110:4 (109:4)
[^6]: Ps 110:4 (109:4)
