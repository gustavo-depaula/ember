# Sermon 229/C: On Holy Easter

**1.** While we celebrate the Paschal days, we whose *Passover, Christ, has been sacrificed*[^1], the Jews, still enemies of so clear a manifestation, perform certain nocturnal figures and, when the day has declined, still dream. For they too say that they celebrate the Passover, and, while they pursue perverse shadows of the truth, they are blinded by the night of error. Each year, by the rite of an ancient solemnity, they slay a lamb, and what that lamb signified they do not recognize, even though Christ was slain by their own forefathers. While they read that it was said, and do not understand that it was foretold, they hear when the sayings are recited, and do not see when the things foretold are fulfilled. *They have the Law and the Prophets*[^2], and what the Law figured concerning the Passover, they are unwilling to recognize through the Prophets. By the Law's command the people were fed by the slaying of a lamb; by the Prophet's foretelling Christ *was led like a lamb to the slaughter*[^3]. What the earlier Israelites, freed from Egypt, figured by their festival, this the later ones, taken captive by the devil, did in their iniquity. They were even keeping the Passover itself when Christ was killed by them; impiety was at variance with the truth, and the solemnity was in agreement with the truth; then the lamb was slaughtered for their food, when by their tongues and teeth Christ was being killed. What they signified by their custom, they fulfilled by their crime. Hence Christ himself, figured in the beast, expressed in the man, slew them as they feasted, and feeds us by being slain. And still their children, belching up with undigested vanity the old leaven of their fathers, boast of unleavened bread; nor do they understand that by that food kneaded without the leaven of the old, the new life is signified, which, prefigured in the type, is revealed in Christ.

**2.** Let us, then, whose *Passover, Christ, has been sacrificed,* celebrate the festal day, as the Apostle says, *not with the old leaven and that of wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth*[^4]; so that what the lawful antiquity announced as future, the Christian festival may show to be fulfilled, and thus we may see that they remained in the shadows, and rejoice that we have clung to the light.

[^1]: 1 Cor 5:7
[^2]: See Luke 16:29
[^3]: Isa 53:7
[^4]: 1 Cor 5:7-8
