# Sermon 63: On the Words of the Gospel of Matthew 8:23-27: "and When He Had Gone Up Into the Boat," and What Follows

**1.** On the most recent reading of the holy Gospel, the Lord granting, I address you, and in him I exhort you, that against the tempests and waves of this world faith may not sleep in your hearts. For indeed the Lord Christ did not have death in his power, while not having sleep in his power; and perhaps sleep pressed the Almighty as he sailed, against his will. If you believe this, he sleeps in you: but if Christ is awake in you, your faith is awake. The Apostle says: *That Christ dwell through faith in your hearts*[^1]. So the sleep of Christ too is the sign of a mystery. Those sailing are souls passing through the world on wood. That boat too figured the Church. For individuals too are temples of God, and each one sails in his own heart: nor does he make shipwreck, if he thinks good things.

**2.** You heard a reproach, it is the wind: you grew angry, it is the wave. So when the wind blows, when the wave rises, the boat is endangered, your heart is endangered, your heart is tossed. Hearing the reproach, you desire to be avenged: and behold, you are avenged, and, rejoicing in another's harm, you have made shipwreck. And why is this? Because Christ sleeps in you. What is, Christ sleeps in you? You have forgotten Christ. Rouse Christ, then, remember Christ, let Christ wake in you: consider him. What did you wish? To be avenged. It slipped your mind that he himself, when he was crucified, said: *Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do*[^2]? He who slept in your heart was unwilling to be avenged. Rouse him, recall him. The memory of him is his word: the memory of him is his command. And you will say within yourself, if Christ wakes in you: What kind of man am I, who wish to be avenged? Who am I, who hurl threats against a man? Perhaps I die before I am avenged. And when, panting, inflamed with anger, and thirsting for revenge, I go out of the body, he does not receive me who was unwilling to be avenged: he does not receive me who said: *Give, and it will be given you; forgive, and it will be forgiven you*[^3]. So I will restrain myself from my anger, and return to the quiet of my heart. Christ commanded the sea, and there was a calm[^4].

**3.** But what I said about anger, hold this as a rule in all your temptations. A temptation is born, it is the wind: you are disturbed, it is the wave. Rouse Christ, let him speak with you. *Who is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him?*[^5] Who is this, whom the sea obeys? *The sea is his, and he made it*[^6]. *All things were made through him*[^7]. Rather imitate the winds and the sea: obey the Creator. At the command of Christ the sea hears, and are you deaf? The sea hears, and the wind ceases, and do you puff? What? I say, I do, I devise: what is this but to puff, and under the word of Christ to be unwilling to cease? Let not the wave conquer you in the disturbance of your heart. But yet, because we are men, if the wind drives, if it moves the affection of our soul, let us not despair: let us rouse Christ, that we may sail in calm, and come to our fatherland. Turned to the Lord, etc.

[^1]: Eph 3:17
[^2]: Luke 23:34
[^3]: Luke 6:37-38
[^4]: See Matt 8:26
[^5]: Matt 8:27
[^6]: Ps 95:5 (94:5)
[^7]: John 1:3
