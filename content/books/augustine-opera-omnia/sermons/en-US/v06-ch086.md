# Sermon 229/R: From the Sermons of the Octave of Easter

**1.** By the name of light, in an allegory, we understand all the just and faithful to be called, as the Apostle says: *You were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord*[^1]. But this light was made. *But there was the true light, which enlightens every man coming into this world*[^2], which was not made, but born of God. That light was not made, and through the unmade light this light was made. So too the firmament: as light was made by the shining God, so the firmament was made by the firm God. And we find that the firmament in the Church ought to be understood as the authority of the divine Scriptures; and therefore first the light, afterward the firmament; because Scripture was made through just men, and unless they were first justified, so as to be light, Scripture could not be propagated, so as to become a firmament between the waters and the waters[^3], a midpoint between the lower peoples of men and the higher peoples of the angels. Why? Because the angels do not desire to make progress from the Scriptures, therefore they are not under the firmament, under the authority of the Scriptures; for they contemplate the appearance of the divinity and of wisdom. But we are rightly under the firmament, we to whom the will of God is opened through the authority of the Scriptures.

[^1]: Eph 5:8
[^2]: John 1:9
[^3]: See Gen 1:6
