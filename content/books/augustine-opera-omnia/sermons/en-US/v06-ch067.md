# Sermon 228/A: From a Sermon on the Resurrection of the Lord

**1.** *For in that he died, he died to sin once; but in that he lives, he lives to God. So you too reckon yourselves to be dead indeed to sin, but living to God in Christ Jesus*[^1]. This is the sacrament, in which those who are baptized experience the setting of the old life, and begin the entrance of the new. Whence the same one says: *We were buried, then, with him through baptism into death; that as Christ rose from the dead, so we too may walk in newness of life*[^2]. Through this sacrament let us recognize that we are both dead to sin with Christ, and living to righteousness in Christ. In the cross is the pain of those who confess; in the burial the rest of those who are absolved; in the resurrection the life of the just.

[^1]: Rom 6:10-11
[^2]: Rom 6:4
