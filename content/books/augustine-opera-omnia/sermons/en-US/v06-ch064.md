# Sermon 226: On the Day of Easter

**1.** So you have heard the Lord Christ proclaimed, that *in the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God*[^1]. For he himself is the Lord Christ, who, if he had not humbled himself, but had willed always to remain so, man would have perished. We recognize the Word, God with God, we recognize the only-begotten Son equal to the Father, we recognize light from light, day from day. He is the day who made the day: by day not made, but begotten. If, then, day from day, not made, but begotten; what is the day which the Lord has made? Why day? Because it is light. *And God called the light day*[^2]. Let us seek what day the Lord made, that we may exult and rejoice in it[^3]. In the first founding of the world it is read that *darkness was upon the deep, and the Spirit of God was borne above the water. And God said, Let there be light; and light was made. And God divided between the light and the darkness; and the light he called day, and the darkness he called night*[^4]. Behold the day which the Lord made. But is it surely that one in which we ought to exult and rejoice? There is another day which the Lord made, which we ought rather to recognize, and in it rejoice and exult. Since it was said to the faithful believing in Christ: *You are the light of the world*[^5]. If light, surely day: because he called the light day. Here too, then, yesterday the Spirit of God was borne above the water, and darkness was upon the deep, when these infants still bore their sins. When, therefore, their sins were forgiven them through the Spirit of God, then God said: *Let there be light; and light was made.* Behold *the day which the Lord has made, let us exult and rejoice in it*[^6]. Let us address this day with apostolic words. O day which the Lord made, you were once darkness: but now light in the Lord. *You were*, he says, *once darkness.* Were you, or not? Recall your deeds, if you were not. Look at your consciences, which you have renounced. Because, then, *you were once darkness, but now light*, not in yourselves, but *in the Lord; walk as children of light*[^7]. Let these few things be enough for you, since we are both after labor, and today we must dispute to the infants concerning the sacraments of the altar.

[^1]: John 1:1
[^2]: Gen 1:5
[^3]: See Ps 118:24 (117:24)
[^4]: Gen 1:2-5
[^5]: Matt 5:14
[^6]: Ps 118:24 (117:24)
[^7]: Eph 5:8
