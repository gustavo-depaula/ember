# Sermon 218/A: From a Sermon on the Passion of the Lord

In the Collection of Bede and Florus

**1.** *For if, when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of his Son; much more, being reconciled, shall we be saved in his life*[^1]. Therefore Christ died for the ungodly. And Christ is God. How will the righteous, found in the life of God, not reign, when, lest he perish, the ungodly was sought by the death of God? For in the life of God we shall be saved, because in our own life we had perished. But when we hear both the life of God and the death of God, let us distinguish what is from where. For he brought us life, but he received death from us: and yet not for his own desert, but for us.

**2.** [3]. Therefore Christ is the king of the Jews, under whose gentle yoke even the Gentiles have been sent for salvation; because to them it was granted by a greater mercy. This the Apostle himself shows more openly, where he says: *For I say that Christ was a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises of the fathers; but that the Gentiles should glorify God for his mercy*[^2]. For it was not right to take the bread of the children and to cast it to the dogs[^3], unless the dogs, humbled to gather the crumbs which they saw fall from the table of their masters, exalted through that very humility and made men, might deserve to draw near to that very table.

**3.** [5]. For it is not, as some think, that the old man is the body, and the new man the soul; but the body is the outer man; the soul, the inner. In the inner man this old age and newness are carried on. For when the Apostle said: *Strip off the old man, and put on the new*; he was not commanding that the body be laid aside, but that the life be changed for the better. This, finally, he consequently taught; for, wishing to explain what he had said: *Wherefore*, he says, *putting away lying, let each one speak the truth with his neighbor*[^4].

[^1]: Rom 5:10
[^2]: Rom 15:8-9
[^3]: See Matt 15:26
[^4]: Eph 4:22-25
