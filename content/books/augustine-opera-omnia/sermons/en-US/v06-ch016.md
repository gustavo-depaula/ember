# Sermon 198: On the Kalends of January, Against the Pagans

**1.** We admonish your Charity, brothers, since we see that you have, as it were, assembled solemnly today, and gathered more frequently than usual on this day; that you may remember what you just now sang, lest the tongue be clamoring while the heart is mute; but that what you sounded with your voice to one another's ears, you may cry with feeling to the ears of God. For this you were singing: *Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the Gentiles, that we may confess your holy name*[^1]. And now, if the solemnity of the Gentiles, which takes place on this day in worldly and fleshly joy, in the din of the most empty and most base songs, in banquets and shameful dancing, in the celebration of that false festival — if the things that the Gentiles do do not delight you, you will be gathered from among the Gentiles.

**2.** You certainly sang, and the sound of the divine song is still fresh in your ears: *Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the Gentiles*. Who can be gathered from among the Gentiles, except when he is made safe? Those, then, who mingle with the Gentiles are not safe; but those are saved who are gathered from among the Gentiles, by the salvation of faith, the salvation of hope, the salvation of most sincere love, spiritual salvation, the salvation of the promises of God. He, then, who believes, hopes, and loves, is not at once to be called safe. For it matters what he believes, what he hopes, and what he loves. For no one lives in any sort of life without these three affections of the soul: believing, hoping, loving. If you do not believe what the Gentiles believe, do not hope what the Gentiles hope, do not love what the Gentiles love; you are gathered from among the Gentiles, set apart, that is, separated from the Gentiles. Nor let the bodily commingling frighten you in so great a separation of mind. For what is so separated as that they should believe demons to be gods, while you believe the one and true God? that they should hope for the empty things of the world, while you hope for eternal life with Christ? that they should love the world, while you love the maker of the world? He, then, who believes one thing, hopes another, loves another, let him prove it by his life, show it by his deeds. Are you going to keep the celebration of New Year's gifts, like a pagan, to play at dice, and to make yourself drunk: how do you believe one thing, hope another, love another? How do you sing with free brow: *Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the Gentiles?* For you are set apart from the Gentiles, mingled in body with the Gentiles, by an unlike life. And how great that separation is, see, if only you do it, if only you prove it. For now our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, who for our sake was made man, gave a price for us. So he gave his own price: he gave it for this, that he might redeem, that he might gather us from among the Gentiles. But if you mingle with the Gentiles, you are unwilling to follow him who redeemed you: and you mingle with the Gentiles in life, in deeds, in heart, by believing such things, by hoping for such things, by loving such things: you are ungrateful to your Redeemer, and do not acknowledge your price, the blood of the spotless Lamb. That you may, then, follow your Redeemer, who redeemed you with his blood, do not mingle with the Gentiles in the likeness of manners and deeds. They give New Year's gifts, you give alms. They are called away by songs of luxuries, you call yourselves away by the words of the Scriptures: they run to the theater, you to the church: they get drunk, you fast. If today you cannot fast, at least dine with sobriety. If you do this, you have sung well: *Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the Gentiles*.

**3.** And so many will struggle today in their heart with the word they have heard. For we said, Do not give New Year's gifts, give to the poor. It is too little to give only as much, give even more. You do not want more? then give as much. But you say to me: When I give New Year's gifts, I too receive for myself. What then, when you give to a poor man, do you receive nothing? Surely you do not believe this, which the Gentiles believe; surely you do not hope this, which the Gentiles hope. Behold, if you say that you receive nothing when you give to a poor man, you have become a part of the Gentiles: in vain you have sung: *Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the Gentiles*. Do not forget that precept, where it is said: *He who gives to the poor will never want*[^2]. Have you now forgotten what the Lord is going to say to those who have given to the poor: *Come, you blessed of my Father, receive the kingdom*[^3]? and what will be said to those who have not given: *Send them into eternal fire*[^4]? Now those who have heard willingly what he said stand surely with those who have not heard willingly. I now speak to true Christians. If you believe one thing, hope another, love another; live otherwise, and prove your differing faith, hope, and love by differing manners. Hear the Apostle warning: *Do not*, he says, *be yoked together with unbelievers. For what fellowship has righteousness with iniquity? or what communion has light with darkness? What part has a believer with an unbeliever? or what agreement has the temple of the Lord with idols?*[^5] And elsewhere he says: *For the things that the Gentiles sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons, and not to God. I do not want you*, he says, *to become partners with demons*[^6]. Therefore their manners delight their gods. But he who said: *I do not want you to become partners with demons*, willed that we should be separated, in life and manners, from those who serve demons. For those demons take delight in songs of vanity, take delight in trifling spectacle, and in the various base things of the theaters, the madness of the circus, the cruelty of the amphitheater, the heated contests of those who, for pestilent men, take up quarrels and disputes even to enmities, for a mime, for an actor, for a pantomime, for a charioteer, for a hunter. Doing these things, they place, as it were, incense to demons out of their hearts. For the seducing spirits rejoice in the seduced; and they are fed on the evil manners and the base and infamous life of those whom they have seduced and deceived. *But you*, as the Apostle says, *have not so learned Christ: if indeed you have heard him, and have been taught in him*[^7]. *Do not, then, become partakers with them. For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord; walk as children of light*[^8]: that we too, who preach the word of the Lord to you, may be able to rejoice with you and over you in that perpetual light.

[^1]: Ps 106:47 (105:47)
[^2]: Prov 28:27
[^3]: Matt 25:34
[^4]: Matt 25:41
[^5]: 2 Cor 6:14-16
[^6]: 1 Cor 10:20
[^7]: Eph 4:20-21
[^8]: Eph 5:7-8
