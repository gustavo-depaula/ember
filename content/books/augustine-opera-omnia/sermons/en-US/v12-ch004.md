# Sermon 112B

We see that the Lord's Apostle Paul said without any ambiguity: *Do you not know that we shall judge angels?*[^1] What angels did he mean except the evil ones and their prince himself? Will the Apostle himself, then, sit on the twelve thrones of which the Lord says: *You will sit on twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel*?[^2] Yet he was not among the twelve apostles. For we know who the twelve first chosen by the Lord were, which one fell from their number, and who was appointed in his place. Twelve were chosen; Judas the traitor fell; blessed Matthias was appointed in his place. If each is to sit on one throne, the number is already filled. Where will the Apostle Paul sit? Or did he perhaps claim a throne not due to him when he said: *Do you not know that we shall judge angels*? The twelve thrones, then, are all the thrones on which both the apostles and the other saints will sit with the Lord to judge.

[^1]: 1 Cor 6:3.
[^2]: Matt 19:28.
