# Sermon 257: In the Days of Easter, a Discourse on the Lord's Day Which He Gave at the Memorial of the Saints

**1.** What in the Hebrew tongue is called *Alleluia*, in Latin is: *Praise the Lord.* Let us, then, praise the Lord our God, not only with the voice but also with the heart, since he who praises with the heart praises with the voice of the inner man. The voice toward men is a sound, the voice toward God is an affection.

**2.** A certain man said *in his ecstasy*, as you have read, as you have heard: *Every man is a liar*[^1]. From this came the one who did not believe, unless he had touched the Lord's body. It seemed to him a lie that his fellow disciples were speaking the truth. And it was too little for him to hear the truth, he wanted also to touch. But the Lord showed this to him in those things that follow in the Gospel, to be recited later. But whoever hears: *Every man is a liar* ought not to fix himself in the lie and refuse to rise from it, as if reasoning out within himself an empty reason, like a lying man, and saying to himself: When will I not be a man? But if, as long as I am here a man, I will be a liar; I would rather be a liar than that the Scripture should lie which says: *Every man is a liar.* For if I am truthful, the Scripture will be a liar, but because the Scripture cannot be a liar, I will be a liar. Chattering these things there, he seems to himself to rest secure as it were in a harbor of lying, where he makes shipwreck. You desired to rest well as it were in this harbor; hear the reef on which you run: *You will destroy all who speak a lie*[^2]. And this is the Scripture of God which says: *You will destroy all who speak a lie.* Therefore he is going to destroy all, because *every man is a liar.* But what was said to us? of what were we admonished? That of our own we are liars. But if we wish to be truthful, let us flee to the Lord. From him we are truthful, from our own we are liars.

**3.** Hear this by one example, brief and great; brief because it is quickly said, great because it is weighed wisely. Peter alone shows this thing that I want said. When he said to the Lord Christ: *You are the Christ, the Son of the living God*, what did he answer? *Blessed are you, Simon Bar-Jona, for flesh and blood did not reveal it to you, but my Father who is in heaven. You spoke the truth, but not your own.* From where, then? *My Father who is in heaven revealed it to you.* From where blessed, because from what is of God; not wretched, except from what is your own. After this, where the Lord says to him: *Blessed are you, because flesh and blood did not reveal it to you, but my Father who is in heaven*, after these words the Lord Christ began to foretell his passion and death. There at once Peter: *Far be it from you, Lord!* *Every man is a liar.* See, a little before Peter truthful, see, now Peter a liar. But from where Peter truthful? *Flesh and blood did not reveal it to you, but my Father who is in heaven.* From where Peter a liar? *Get behind me, Satan, for you do not savor the things that are of God, but the things that are of men*[^3]. *Every man is a liar.* Therefore it was said to us: *Every man is a liar*, that we may flee ourselves and run to God who alone is truthful.

[^1]: Ps 116:11 (115:11)
[^2]: Ps 5:6 (5:7)
[^3]: Matt 16:16-23
