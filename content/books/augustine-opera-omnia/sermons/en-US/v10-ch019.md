# Sermon 350/B

**1.** *The rich man*, it says, *and the poor have met one another on the way; but the maker of both is the Lord*[^1]. As it is written, brothers, the rich man and the poor have met one another on the way. On what way, except this life? Come, rich man, you can be relieved of your burdens by giving to the poor what you have acquired by your labors. Give something to him who has not, because you too do not have something. For do you have eternal life? Give, then, from what you have, that you may receive what you do not have. Let the beggar knock at your door, and you knock at the door of your Lord. God does with his beggar what you do with yours. Give, then, and it will be given to you; but if you are unwilling, you must see to it. For the poor man cries out and says to you: . . . are you to be consigned to fire and devouring flames? To these words of the poor man, I know not whether the pride of the rich man will be able to answer. *To everyone*, says the Lord, *who asks you, give*[^2]. If to everyone, how much more to the needy and the wretched, whose leanness and pallor beg, whose tongue is silent, whose squalor and groaning ask for alms. Hear me, then, O rich man, and may my counsel please you. Redeem your sins with almsgiving. Do not brood over gold; naked you came forth from your mother's womb, naked you will return to the earth[^3]. And if naked you will return to the earth, for whom do you gather upon the earth? I believe that, if you could carry something with you, you would have devoured living men. Behold, you will go forth naked; why do you not bestow money, whether well or ill gathered? Promise where you wonder, make investments of most wonderful things, that you may come to the kingdom of heaven. For if you gave some man ten coins, for which he would afterward repay three hundred, how you would rejoice, with what gladness of mind you would exult. If, then, you rejoice in interest, lend to your God. Bestow on your Lord from his own, for with multiplied interest he will repay you. Do you wish to know how manifoldly he will repay you? For a morsel, for a coin, for a tunic you receive eternal life, the kingdom of heaven, blessedness without end. Weigh against your morsel eternal life, everlasting riches. There is no comparison. For give the earth, and you receive him who made heaven and earth; for he himself is our reward, without whom the rich man begs and with whom the poor man is most abundantly rich. For what does the rich man have, if he does not have God? What does the poor man not have, if he has God? Therefore, brothers, as a watchman among the people saying these things and admonishing you, I free myself, I unburden myself, I fulfill my office. There is one who will require and examine your work. Behold, you have groaned. Therefore you are now ready to do almsgiving. Thanks be to God. The Lord, who gave you understanding, is able to grant the fruit of almsgiving.

[^1]: Prov 22:2
[^2]: Luke 6:30
[^3]: See Job 1:21
