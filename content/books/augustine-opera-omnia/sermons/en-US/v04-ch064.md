# Sermon 140a

**1.** He received from us what is cheap, he gave us what is great; he received our evil, he gave us his good; he received death, he gave us life; he received here insults, he gave us honor; he received here the cross, he gave us rest. How great are the evils he received here! how great the goods he gave us! Our Lord Jesus Christ, then, was made the Son of man; yet God was made man, not God changed into man, but remaining in himself perfect God, and changed in nothing for the worse; taking on a man to be changed in himself for the better, not the worse in him. And so on.
