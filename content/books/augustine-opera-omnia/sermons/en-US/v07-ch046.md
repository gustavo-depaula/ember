# Sermon 265: On the Ascension of the Lord

**1. 1.** By the solemnity of this day we admonish those who know, we instruct those who are negligent. Today we solemnly celebrate the Lord's ascent into heaven. For our Lord and Savior, his body laid aside and his body received, after he rose from the dead, showed himself living to the disciples, whom they had despaired of as dying. After he gave himself back to be beheld by the eyes, to be handled by the hands, building up faith by showing the truth; since it was too little for human frailty and weak trembling to show so great a miracle on one day, and then to withdraw it; he lived with them on earth, as we have heard, when the book of the Acts of the Apostles was read, he lived with them on earth forty days, going in and out, eating and drinking; that he might show the truth, not that he had any need. On that very fortieth day, then, which we celebrate today, while they saw, and by seeing escorted him, he ascended into heaven.

**1. 2.** Then, after they marveled at what they saw ascending, yet rejoiced that it went up; for the going-before of the head is the hope of the members; they heard also the angelic voice: *Men of Galilee, why do you stand looking into heaven? This Jesus will so come, as you saw him going into heaven*[^1]. What does it mean, *he will so come?* He will come in that form: that it may be fulfilled which is written: *They will see him whom they pierced*[^2]. *He will so come.* He will come to men, he will come a man; but he will come God-man. He will come true man and God, to make men gods. The judge of heaven ascended, the herald of heaven sounded. Let us have a good cause, that we may not fear the judgment to come. For he ascended: those saw it who announced it to us. Those who did not see, believed: others, not believing, mocked. *For faith is not of all*[^3]. And because *faith is not of all, and the Lord knows who are his*[^4], why do we dispute that God ascended into heaven? Let us rather marvel that God descended into the underworld. Let us marvel at the death of Christ, but let us rather praise the resurrection than marvel at it. Our perdition is our sin: the blood of Christ is our price. The resurrection of Christ is our hope: the coming of Christ is our reality. He, then, who is at the right hand of the Father, is to be awaited until he comes. Let our thirsting soul say to him: When will he come? and: *My soul thirsted for the living God*[^5]. When will he come? He will come: but when will he come? You desire that he come: would that he find you ready.

**2. 3.** Nevertheless, lest we think that we alone have this desire about our Lord, that we say, When will he come? his disciples too had this desire. If I could tell you, gaping, waiting, suspended, desiring to know, when our Lord God comes, if I could tell you, what kind of person would I seem to you? But if you do not hope to be able to hear this from me; and if you do hope it, you are foolish: surely if you had the Lord Jesus Christ himself present in the body, living, speaking, before your eyes and before your hands, I know that out of this desire you would question him, and would say to him: Lord, when are you going to come? The disciples themselves questioned the Lord Jesus Christ present. You cannot question what they questioned, hear what they heard. For they existed then, we did not yet exist: yet if we believe them, they questioned for us too, and heard for us too. The disciples of Christ, then, about to escort Christ ascending with their gaze, questioned him and said to him: *Lord, will you present yourself at this time?* To whom did they say it? Whom they saw present. *Will you present yourself at this time?* And what is that? Did they not see him present? did they not hear him present? did they not even touch him present? What is this: *Will you present yourself at this time?* except that they knew that the future judgment would be by the presence of Christ, that he might be seen by his own and by strangers? For when he rose, he was seen only by his own. This, then, they knew, and held by faith, that there would be a time when, having been judged, he would judge, when, having been rejected, he would approve and reject; when, conspicuous to both kinds of men, he would set some on the right, others on the left, about to say what both would hear, to offer what not both would receive, to threaten what not both would dread. They knew it would be: but when, they asked. *Will you present yourself at this time?* Not surely to us; for we even now see you: but you will present yourself also to those who did not believe in you. *If you will present yourself at this time, tell us, and when the kingdom of Israel?* This they asked: *If you will present yourself at this time, and when the kingdom of Israel?* What kingdom? Of which we say: *Your kingdom come*[^6]. What kingdom? Which those set on the right are going to hear of: *Come, blessed of my Father, receive the kingdom which was prepared for you from the foundation of the world*[^7]. When he is going to say also to those on the left: *Go into the eternal fire, which was prepared for the devil and his angels*[^8]. A terrible voice, a fearful voice: but: *The just will be in eternal memory: he will not fear an evil report*[^9]. To these this, to those that: in both truthful, because in both just.

**3. 4.** But what they asked, if they heard it, let us hear: if they did not hear it, let us hold what they heard; and what is to come, let us not fear. *Lord, will you present yourself at this time?* And we, supposing ourselves to see the Lord present, established in the body, let us say to him: *Lord, will you present yourself at this time, and when the kingdom of Israel?* When the kingdom of your own, when the kingdom of the humble, how long the arrogance of the proud? Surely this is what you were asking, this is what you desired to hear. Let us see what he answered. Let not the lambs disdain to hear what the rams heard. Let us hear what the Lord himself said. To whom? To Peter, John, Andrew, James, the rest, so great and such, so worthy: but whom he found unworthy, and made worthy. What did he answer them when they said: *Will you present yourself at this time, and when the kingdom of Israel? It is not yours to know the times, which the Father has set in his own power*[^10]. What is this? It is said to Peter: *It is not yours*: and do you say, It is mine? *It is not yours to know the times, which the Father has set in his own power.* What you believe, you believe well: because it is to come. When it is to come, what is it to you? When it comes, prepare yourself. *It is not yours to know the times, which the Father has set in his own power.* Let curiosity withdraw, let piety succeed. What is it to you when he comes? Live as though he were going to come today; and you will not fear, when he comes.

**4. 5.** But see the order and discipline of the good master, the singular master, the only master. He did not say what they asked, and he said what they did not ask. For he knew that what they asked did not profit them to know: but what he knew profited them, that he said, even when they did not ask. *It is not yours to know*, he says, *the times.* What good are the times to you? This is being done, that you may escape the times, and you seek the times. *It is not yours to know the times, which the Father has set in his own power.* And as if it were said to him, And what is ours? Now let us hear what especially pertains to us, now let us hear. What ought not to be said was asked: but what ought to be heard was said. *It is not yours to know the times, which the Father has set in his own power.* But what is yours to know?

**5. 6.** *But you will receive the power of the Holy Spirit coming upon you, and you will be my witnesses.* Where? *In Jerusalem.* It was fitting that we hear this: for by these words the Church is preached, the Church is commended, unity is announced, division is accused. To the apostles it was said: *And you will be my witnesses.* It is said to the faithful, it is said to the vessels of God, it is said to the vessels of mercy: *You will be my witnesses.* Where? *In Jerusalem*, where I was killed: *and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth*[^11]. See what you hear, see what you hold. Be the bride, and securely await the bridegroom. The bride is the Church. Where was she foretold as future, whom those witnesses would announce; where was she foretold as future? For many are going to say, See, here he is. I would hear, if another did not also say, See, here he is. What do you say? See, here he is. I was already going: but another recalls me with a like voice, See, here he is. You from one side, See, here he is: another from the other side, See, here he is. Let us question the Lord, let us appeal to the Lord. Let the parts be silent, let us hear the whole. One says from one corner, See, here he is: another from another corner, No, but see, here he is. You, Lord, say: you claim her whom you redeemed, show her whom you loved. We have been invited to your wedding, show your bride, lest by quarreling we trouble your vows. He says it plainly, he shows it plainly: he does not desert those who inquire, he does not love those who quarrel. He says to his disciples, and he says to those who do not ask; because to those who contend he speaks against them. And therefore perhaps this was not yet asked by the apostles, because the flock of Christ was not yet divided by robbers. We, having experienced the pains of division, eagerly seek the binding of unity. The apostles ask the time of the judgment, and the Lord answers the place of the Church. He did not answer what they asked, but he foresaw our pains. *You will be*, he says, *my witnesses in Jerusalem.* It is too little. You did not give so great a price only for this, to buy this alone. *In Jerusalem.* Say more: *And to the ends of the earth.* You came to the ends: why do you not end contentions? Let no one now say to me, See, here he is: No, but see, here. Let human presumption be silent, let the divine preaching be heard, let the true promise be held: *In Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.* These things being said, *a cloud received him*[^12]. Now there was no need that anything be added, lest he be thought of from elsewhere.

**6. 7.** Brothers, the last words of a parent going to the tomb are wont to be heard as a great thing, and are the last words of the Lord ascending into heaven despised? Let us consider that our Lord wrote a testament, and in his testament set down his last words. For he foresaw the future quarrels of bad sons, he foresaw men trying to make parties for themselves from another's possession. For why should they not divide what they did not buy? Why should they not cut up that for which they did not give a price? But he willed that the tunic woven from above, nowhere stitched, not be divided: it came by lot. In that garment unity was commended, in that garment love was preached, it is the very one woven from above. Cupidity is from the earth, love from above. Act, brothers: the Lord wrote a testament, he set down his last words. See, I beseech you, and let it move you as it moves us too, let it move you, if it can be.

**7. 8.** There are two glorifications according to the form of the man he took up: one, by which he rose from the dead on the third day; the other, by which he ascended into heaven before the eyes of his disciples. These two glorifications of his, now done, are commended. There remains one, and that too in the sight of men, when he will present himself for judgment. Of the Holy Spirit this had been said through the Evangelist John: *But the Spirit had not yet been given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified*[^13]. *But the Spirit had not yet been given*: why had it not yet been given? *Because Jesus had not yet been glorified.* It was awaited, then, that when Jesus was glorified the Spirit would be given. And deservedly, twice glorified, by rising and by ascending, he twice gave the Spirit. He gave one, and one gave, he gave to unity, and yet he gave twice. First, after he rose, he said to his disciples: *Receive the Holy Spirit.* And he breathed into their faces[^14]. You have it once. Then he promises that he will still send the Holy Spirit, and says: *You will receive the power of the Holy Spirit coming upon you*[^15]: and in another place: *Sit in the city: for I will fulfill the promise of the Father, which you heard*, he says, *from my mouth*[^16]. After he ascended, ten days being spent there, he sent the Holy Spirit: that is the coming solemnity of Pentecost.

**8. 9.** See, my brothers. Let someone ask me, Why did he give the Holy Spirit twice? Many have said many things, and sought as men: and they said something that is not against the faith; one this, another that, both of which did not exceed the rule of truth. If I should say that I know why he gave it twice, I would lie to you. I do not know. He who says that he knows what he does not know is rash: he who denies that he knows what he knows is ungrateful. So I confess to you, why the Lord gave the Holy Spirit twice, I still seek: I desire to come to something more certain. Let the Lord help me, with you praying, that what he condescends to grant may not be kept silent from you too. Therefore I do not know. Yet what I think, not yet knowing, not yet holding for certain, as I hold most certain that he gave it; what, then, I think, I will not be silent about. If this is it, let the Lord confirm it: if it is something else that appears truer, let the Lord grant it. I think, then, but I think, that the Holy Spirit was given twice for this reason, that the two precepts of love might be commended. For there are two precepts, and love is one: *You shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart, and with your whole soul*: and: *You shall love your neighbor as yourself. On these two precepts hang the whole Law and the Prophets*[^17]. One love, and two precepts: one Spirit, and two givings. For one was not given first, and another given afterward: because it is not another love that loves the neighbor than that which loves God. It is not, then, another love. By the love by which we love the neighbor, by that very love we love God too. But because God is one thing, the neighbor another; they are loved by one love, yet those who are loved are not one: because, then, the love of God, to be commended first, is great, the second the love of neighbor; but one begins from the second, that one may come to the first: *For if you do not love the brother whom you see; God whom you do not see, how can you love?*[^18] Therefore perhaps, forming us to the love of neighbor, conspicuous on earth and near to the near, he gave the Holy Spirit by breathing into their faces; and from this love especially which is in heaven, from heaven he sent the Holy Spirit. Receive the Holy Spirit on earth, and you love the brother: receive him from heaven, and you love God. Because even what you received on earth is from heaven. On earth Christ gave it, but what he gave is from heaven. For he gave it who came down from heaven. Here he found one to whom to give, but from there he brought what he would give.

**9. 10.** What then, brothers? Or perhaps I should recall this too, how love pertains to the Holy Spirit? Hear Paul: *And not only so*, he says, *but we also glory in tribulations: knowing that tribulation works patience, patience trial, but trial hope; and hope does not confound: because the love of God has been poured out in our hearts.* From where has the love of God been poured out in our hearts? From where? What did you give yourself? What did you presume on as if from your own; *For what do you have that you have not received?*[^19] From where, then, except what follows: *Through the Holy Spirit who has been given to us?*[^20]

**9. 11.** This love is not held except in the unity of the Church. The dividers do not have it: as the apostle Jude says: *These are they who set themselves apart, animal men, not having the Spirit.* *Who set themselves apart*: why do they set themselves apart? Because *animal men, not having the Spirit*[^21]. Therefore they flow away, because they do not have the binding of love. Of that very love is full that hen, grown weak for her chicks, humbling her voice with the chicks, stretching out her wings: *How often*, he says, *did I wish to gather your sons?*[^22] To gather, not to divide. Because *I have*, he says, *other sheep, which are not of this fold: I must bring them too, that there may be one flock and one shepherd*[^23]. Deservedly he did not heed the brother appealing against his brother, and saying: *Lord, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me. Lord*, he says, *tell my brother.* What? *To divide the inheritance with me.* And the Lord: *Tell, O man.* For why do you want to divide, except because you are a man? *For when one says, I am of Paul; but another, I am of Apollos: are you not men?*[^24] *Tell, O man, who appointed me a divider of the inheritance among you?* I came to gather, not to divide. *Therefore*, he says, *I tell you, beware of all cupidity*[^25]. For cupidity desires to divide, as love to gather. But what is it: *Beware of all cupidity*, except, Be filled with love? We, having love according to our capacity, appeal to the Lord against the brother, as that man too against his brother: but not with this voice, not with this petition. For he says: *Lord, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me.* We say: Lord, tell my brother to hold the inheritance with me.

**10. 12.** See, then, brothers, what you should most love, what you should firmly hold. The Lord, glorified by rising, commends the Church: to be glorified by ascending, commends the Church: sending the Holy Spirit from the heavens, commends the Church. For rising, what does he say to his disciples? *These things I was telling you, while I was still with you, that all things must be fulfilled which are written in the Law, and the Prophets, and the Psalms about me. And then he opened their minds, that they might understand the Scriptures, and said to them, That so it is written, and so it was fitting for Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead on the third day.* Where is the commendation of the Church? *And that repentance and the forgiveness of sins be preached in his name.* And this where? *Among all nations, beginning from Jerusalem*[^26]. This glorified by the resurrection. What, to be glorified by the ascension? What you heard: *You will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth*[^27]. What by the very coming of the Holy Spirit? The Holy Spirit came: those whom he first filled spoke in the tongues of all nations. Each man speaking in all tongues, what else did he signify, except unity in all tongues? Holding this, established in this, strengthened in this, fixed in this by unshaken love, let us as children praise the Lord, and say alleluia. But in one part? And from where? and how far? *From the rising of the sun to its setting, praise the name of the Lord*[^28].
