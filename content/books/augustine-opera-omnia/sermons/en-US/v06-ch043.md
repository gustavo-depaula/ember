# Sermon 218/B: On the Passion of the Lord

**1.** Today we solemnly celebrate the great and ineffable sacrament of the Lord's passion; which indeed on other days too departs neither from the altar at which we stand, nor from our mouth and forehead; that what is constantly recalled even to the senses of the body may always be held in the heart. Yet this yearly solemnity much more moves the mind to the remembrance of so great a thing; so that what, many years ago, in one place, was committed by the impiety of the Jews and seen by their cruel eyes, is now, in the gaze of faith, as though done today, beheld throughout the whole world. If they then willingly saw what they cruelly did, how much more willingly should we, with memory recalling, bring back to our minds what we piously believe? If they with pleasure beheld their own iniquity, how much more should we with joy remember our own salvation? For in that one deed their present crimes were marked, but also our future ones blotted out. Finally, where we detest the crimes they committed, there we rejoice in our own forgiven. They were the perpetrators of impiety, we the celebrators of the solemnity; they were gathered raging, we obeying; they lost, we found; they sold, we redeemed; they watched insulting, we venerating adore. Therefore Christ crucified is to the unbelieving a scandal and folly, but to us the Power of God and the Wisdom of God: for this is that weak thing of God, which is stronger than men, and the foolish thing of God, which is wiser than men[^1]. The events that followed taught these things more clearly. For what did the rage of the enemies then aim at, except to take away from the earth his memory? But he, crucified by one people, has been set in the hearts of so many peoples; and he who was then slain by one nation is now adored by all nations. And yet not only then, but even now the blind read and the deaf sing what was foretold by the prophetic voice so long before as future: *They dug my hands and my feet, they counted all my bones. But they themselves looked upon me; they divided my garments among themselves, and over my garment they cast lots*[^2]. As these things were foretold in the Psalm, so in the Gospel they are read fulfilled; but then by the hands of the Jews was done what struck their ears in vain; and the prophesied passion of the Lord, the less it was understood by them, the more effectively it was fulfilled; but now they both read it foretold, and recognize it fulfilled. And still they choose to deny Christ, because they can no longer kill him.

**2.** But worse than the Jews are the heretics, for those deny the Christ whom they do not behold; these attack his Church, which they see. And the heretics rage more wretchedly not only than these Jews who now deny Christ, but even than those who killed him; for those did not blot out the title of the one hanging on the wood, these blow away the baptism of the one sitting in heaven. From this present Psalm, then, we answer both adversaries: both the deniers of the head, and the deniers of the body. For the head is Christ, the body the Church. Against the Jews we read: *They dug my hands and my feet, they counted all my bones*[^3], and the rest that follows. Against the heretics we read: *All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the Lord, and all the families of the nations will adore in his sight; because the kingdom is the Lord's, and he will rule the nations*[^4]. But let us hold what that garment woven from above signified[^5], which those who killed Christ did not divide, but to which those came by lot who were able to come. The sacraments of Christ, then, can be divided by many heretics: no one of the faithful tears or divides the love of Christ; but those who belong to the portion of the lot of the saints in light[^6], they hold it as their own, because they love unity spiritually. Wherefore, dearest ones, let us celebrate this day with yearly devotion; but let us glory in the cross of Christ[^7], not once a year, but with continual holiness.

[^1]: See 1 Cor 1:23-25
[^2]: Ps 22:16-18 (21:17-19)
[^3]: Ps 22:16-17 (21:17-18)
[^4]: Ps 22:27-28 (21:28-29)
[^5]: See John 19:23
[^6]: See Col 1:12
[^7]: See Gal 6:14
