# Sermon 251: In the Days of Easter

**1. 1.** Our Liberator's fishing is our liberation. We notice in the holy Gospel two fishings of the Lord, that is, when at his word the nets were cast: one before, when he chose the disciples; and this other, when he rose from the dead. That fishing signified the Church such as it is now: but this one after the Lord's resurrection signified the Church such as it will be at the end of the age. Finally, in that earlier fishing, he ordered the nets to be cast, and did not say on which side; but commanded only that they be cast. The disciples cast them: it was not said, On the right; it was not said, On the left. For the fish signified men: if, then, it were said, On the right, only the good would be: if it were said, On the left, only the bad. But because both good and bad were going to be mixed in the Church, the nets were cast without distinction; that fish might be caught signifying the mixture of good and bad. Then this too is written there, that they caught so many fish that the two boats, filled, sank, that is, were pressed to sinking. For those two boats were not sunk, but endangered. From what endangered? From the multitude of fish. It was signified by this, that through the multitude that the Church was going to gather, discipline was going to be endangered. And this is added in that fishing, and is narrated, that the nets too were broken by the multitude of fish. The broken nets, what did they signify, except the schisms to come? These three things, then, were signified in that fishing: the mixture of good and bad, the pressure of the crowds, the separations of heretics. The mixture of good and bad; because the nets were cast neither on the right nor on the left: the pressure of the crowds; because so much was caught that the boats were pressed: the separations of heretics; because so great had been the multitude that the nets were broken.

**2. 2.** Look now at this fishing, which was recited today. For it was made after the Lord's resurrection, to signify that the Church will be such after our resurrection. *Cast*, he says, *the net on the right side*[^1]. The number of those who will stand on the right is therefore set apart. For you remember that the Lord said he would come with his angels, and that all nations would be gathered before him; and he will divide them, as a shepherd divides the sheep from the goats: he will set the sheep on the right, the goats on the left. To the sheep he is going to say: *Come, receive the kingdom*: to the goats he is going to say: *Go into the eternal fire*[^2]. *Cast on the right.* As though he said: Now I have risen, I want to signify the Church, which will be in the resurrection of the dead. *Cast on the right.* The nets were cast on the right: and they could not lift them for the multitude of fish. And there too multitude was mentioned: but here a fixed number, and multitude, and greatness: but there the number was not given. For now, before the resurrection comes, and the good are separated from the bad, that is fulfilled which the prophet says: *I have announced and spoken.* What does it mean: *I have announced and spoken?* I cast the nets. And what? *They are multiplied beyond number*[^3]. There is a number, there are those beyond the number. The number pertains to the saints, who will reign with Christ. Beyond the number they can now enter the Church, they cannot enter the kingdom of heaven.

**3. 2.** Therefore I admonish you to snatch yourselves out of the present evil age. Therefore I admonish you, that you who wish to live not imitate bad Christians. Do not say: Why? he is unfaithful, and gets drunk? Why? he is unfaithful, and has concubines? Why? he is unfaithful, and commits frauds daily? Why? he is unfaithful, and consults astrologers? For now, you who wish to be grains, then you will be found in the heap of grain. But you who wish to be chaff, you will be found in a great pile, but to be burned with a great fire.

**3. 3.** What then? *They drew*, he says, *the nets to the shore.* Peter drew the nets to the shore: you heard it just now when the Gospel was read. Where you hear shore, understand the end of the sea: where you hear the end of the sea, understand the end of the age. In that fishing the nets were not drawn to the shore: but the very fish that were caught were poured into the boats. But here they drew to the shore. Hope for the end of the age. The end will come; to the good of those on the right, to the bad of those on the left. And how many fish? *They drew*, he says, *the nets holding one hundred fifty-three fish.* And the Evangelist added a necessary thing: *And though they were so many*, that is, so great, *the net was not torn*[^4]. They will be great, but there will be no heresies: and they will the more have no heresies because they will be great. Who are the great? Read the words of the Lord himself in the Gospel, and you will find the great. For he says in a certain place: *I have not come to abolish the Law and the Prophets, but to fulfill them*[^5].

**4. 3.** *For truly I tell you, that whoever loosens one of these least commandments, and teaches*: loosens and teaches thus; *loosens* by living badly, *and teaches* by teaching good things: *he will be called least in the kingdom of heaven.* But in what kingdom of heaven? In the Church that now is; because it too is called the kingdom of heaven. For if this Church too, which gathers good and bad, were not called the kingdom of heaven, the Lord himself, speaking in a parable, would not say: *The kingdom of heaven is like a net cast into the sea, which gathers all kinds of fish.* But see what follows? *The kingdom of heaven is like a net cast into the sea.* The net is the seine: *it gathers all kinds of fish.* But what? They draw it to the shore. This the Lord says in the parable. And when they have drawn it to the shore, they sit, *and gather the good into vessels, but the bad they throw away.* And he expounded what he proposed. For what did he say? *So it will be at the consummation of the age.* Have you understood the shore? *The angels will come*, he says, *and gather the bad from the midst of the just, and cast them into the furnace of burning fire: there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth*[^6]. Yet the Church was called the kingdom of heaven. And since indeed the sea has fish swimming together, both good and bad, in this kingdom of heaven, that is, in the Church of this time, he is called least who teaches good things, and does bad things: because there he too is. For he is not absent from there: there he is in the kingdom of heaven, that is, in the Church, such as it is at this time. He teaches good things, does bad things: he is necessary, he is a hireling. *Truly I tell you*, he says, *they have received their reward*[^7]. He profits something. For if those who teach good things and live badly profited nothing, the Lord himself would not say to his people: *The scribes and Pharisees sit on the chair of Moses: what they say, do; but what they do, do not do.* Why? *For they say, and do not do*[^8].

**5. 4.** Let your Charity attend, then: for I want to expound who are the great fish. *He who loosens*, he says, *one of these least commandments, will be called least in the kingdom of heaven.* He will be there, but least. *But he who does, and so teaches, will be called great in the kingdom of heaven*[^9]. See, those are the great fish, caught on the right. *He who does, and so teaches*: does good things, teaches good things; does not contradict himself when speaking by living badly, when he has a good tongue as a witness of a bad life. *He, then, who does, and so teaches, will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.* And it follows: *For I tell you, that unless your righteousness abounds more than that of the scribes and Pharisees, you will not enter the kingdom of heaven*[^10]. Now how do you understand the kingdom of heaven? That from which it is said to those on the right: *Come, blessed of my Father, receive the kingdom*[^11]. *Unless your righteousness abounds more than that of the scribes and Pharisees.* What does it mean, *more than that of the scribes and Pharisees?* Look at those scribes and Pharisees, who sit on the chair of Moses, of whom it was said: *What they say, do; what they do, do not do: for they say, and do not do.* Therefore the righteousness of the Pharisees is to say and not to do. Let your righteousness abound more than that of the scribes and Pharisees, that you may both say good things, and live well.

**6. 5.** Now then, about the number of one hundred fifty-three fish, what need is there to go over the same things again? You know them. From seventeen the growing number is born. Begin from one, arrive at seventeen, so that you add them all: that is, add one to two, and they become three; add three, and they become six; add four, and they become ten. So add them all up to seventeen, and you arrive at one hundred fifty-three. Therefore our whole attention need only be on what seventeen means: for there is the foundation of one hundred fifty-three. What does seventeen mean? In the law recognize ten. Ten precepts were given first: the Decalogue is called what was written on the tablets by the finger of God. In ten recognize the Law, in seven recognize the Holy Spirit. For the Holy Spirit is commended by the number seven. Therefore sanctification is not named in the Law except on the seventh day. God made light: it was not said, He sanctified it. He made the firmament: it was not said, He sanctified the firmament. He separated the sea from the land, he commanded the earth to bring forth: it was not said, He sanctified. He made the moon and the stars: it was not said, He sanctified. He commanded the animals to come forth from the waters, swimming and flying: it was not said, He sanctified. He commanded the four-footed animals and all creeping things to come forth from the earth: it was not said, He sanctified. He made man himself: it was not said, He sanctified.

**7. 5.** The seventh day was reached, where he rested; and that he sanctified. By his rest God sanctified our rest. There, then, will be our full sanctification, where we will rest with him without end. For why would God rest? For he was not wearied by working. You, if you make something by a word, are not wearied; if you command, and at once it is done, you stand, you remain whole. He said a few words, from which he made everything, and was he suddenly wearied?

**7. 6.** Therefore recognize the law in ten; recognize the Holy Spirit in seven. Let the Spirit be joined to the Law. Because if you receive the Law, and the help of the Spirit is lacking to you, you do not fulfill what you read, you do not fulfill what is commanded you: but a man under the Law is moreover held a transgressor. Let the Spirit be added, let him help; and what is commanded is done. If the Spirit is lacking, the letter kills you. Why does the letter kill you? Because it will make you a transgressor. Nor can you excuse yourself from ignorance, because you have received the law. Now you have learned what you should do, ignorance does not excuse you, the Spirit does not help you: therefore you have perished. But what does the apostle Paul say: *The letter kills, but the Spirit gives life*[^12]? How does the Spirit give life? Because he causes the letter to be fulfilled, lest it kill. These are the saints, who do the law of God by the gift of God. The law can command, it cannot help. The Spirit the helper is added; and the command of God is done with joy, with delight. For many do it out of fear. But those who do the law fearing punishment would prefer that there be nothing to fear. But those who do the Law loving righteousness rejoice even there, because they do not have it as an enemy.

**8. 7.** Therefore the Lord says: *Agree with your adversary quickly, while you are with him on the road*[^13]. Who is your adversary? The word of the Law. What is the road? This life. How is it your adversary? It says: *You shall not commit adultery*: and you want to commit adultery. It says: *You shall not covet your neighbor's goods*: and you want to seize others' goods. It says: *Honor father and mother*: and you are insolent toward your parents. The Law says: *Do not bear false witness*: you do not depart from lying. When you see that that word commands one thing, and you do another, it is your adversary. You have a bad adversary, let him not enter with you into the council chamber: settle while you are with him on the road. God is present to make you agree. How does God make you agree? By forgiving sins, and by inspiring righteousness, so that good works may be done. When, then, you have agreed with your adversary, that is, with the Decalogue of the Law, through the Holy Spirit, you will belong to seventeen. When you have belonged to seventeen, thereafter the number will grow to one hundred fifty-three. You will be on the right, to be crowned: lest you remain on the left, to be condemned.
