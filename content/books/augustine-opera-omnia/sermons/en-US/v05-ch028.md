# Sermon 166

**1.** That this sentence which the Apostle said, *Putting away lying, speak the truth*[^1], is not contrary to that sentence which was said in the Psalm, *Every man is a liar*[^2], if the Lord gives understanding, we will briefly expound. What, then, is, *Putting away lying, speak the truth*; and, *Every man is a liar*? Does God, through the Apostle, command impossible things? No. What, then, does he command? I dare to say it—but receive it as said without offense, because I say it about myself too: God commands this, that we be not men. For if I said, God commands you not to be men, you would perhaps take it harshly; and so I joined myself in, lest anyone be angry.

**2.** For I say more to your Holiness: we find the Apostle as it were objecting it to men as a charge, that they are men; for rebuking them he said this. Just as we, angry, say to someone: You are a beast; so he, rebuking them under the lash of the Lord's discipline, objected to men that they were men. What did he wish them to become, to whom it was a charge that they were men? *For since there is among you*, he says, *jealousy and contention, are you not carnal, and walk according to man? For when one says: I indeed am of Paul; but another, I of Apollos; are you not men*[^3]? Reproaching and rebuking, he says, *Are you not men*? What, then, did he wish them to become, but what is said in the Psalm, *I said, you are gods, and sons of the Most High*[^4]? This indeed God said: for to this he calls. But what does he add? *But you will die like men, and fall like one of the princes*[^5]. And there the reproach is objected, when it is said, *But you will die like men*. For Adam is a man, and not a son of man; but Christ is the Son of man, and God. To lying belongs the old man—that is, Adam; to truth the new man, the Son of man, that is, Christ God. If you put away lying, strip off Adam; if you speak the truth, put on Christ; and the things that are now set down in the Scriptures will not be contrary to you. Because the Apostle too, admonishing that the old man be stripped off and the new put on, says, *Putting away lying, speak the truth*. And the Psalm admonished and lamented those who, unwilling to strip off Adam and put on Christ, desired to be not new men, but only men, to whom it is said, *Are you not men*? and on whom falls what was said, *Every man is a liar*.

**3.** If you wish to be a man, you will be a liar. Do not wish to be a man, and you will not be a liar. Put on Christ, and you will be truthful; so that the things you speak may be not your own as if your own, and established by you, but of the truth that illuminates and enlightens you. For if you are stripped of light, you will remain in your darkness, and you will be able only to speak lies. For the Lord himself says, *He who speaks a lie speaks of his own*[^6]; because *every man is a liar*. He who speaks the truth, then, speaks not of his own, but of God's. Not indeed in such a way that we say he speaks things foreign to himself; for they become his own, when he loves what he receives, and gives thanks to the one who gave. For if the illumination of truth be taken from a man, he will remain as it were naked of the garment of light, and will be able only to speak lies. For this will remain in him, what is written in the Psalm, *Every man is a liar*.

**4.** There is, then, no reason for anyone to slander, and say to me: I will lie, because I am a man. For I too will say most confidently: Do not be a man, that you may not lie. Then, he says, I will not be a man? Surely not. For that you may not be a man, you have been called to this by him who became a man for your sake. Do not be indignant. For it is not said to you that you may not be a man so as to be a beast, but that you may be among those to whom he gave the power to become sons of God[^7]. For God wishes to make you a god; but not by nature, as is the one whom he begot; but by his gift and by adoption. For just as he, through humanity, was made a partaker of your mortality; so he, through exaltation, makes you a partaker of his immortality. Give thanks, then, and embrace what has been given, that you may deserve to enjoy fully that to which you have been called. Do not be Adam, and you will not be a man. If not a man, surely not a liar; because *every man is a liar*. And when you have begun not to lie, do not ascribe it to yourself and be exalted, as if it were your own; lest by the wind of pride, like a lamp kindled from elsewhere, you be extinguished, and remain again in your lie. Do not, then, lie, brothers. For you were once old men; you have come to the grace of God, you have been made new men. Lying belongs to Adam, truth to Christ. *Putting away*, then, *lying, speak the truth*[^8], so that this mortal flesh too, which you still have from Adam, with the newness of the spirit going before, may itself deserve renewal and change at the time of its resurrection; and so the whole man, deified, may cleave to the perpetual and unchangeable truth.

[^2]: Ps 116:11 (115:11)
[^3]: 1 Cor 3:3-4
[^4]: Ps 82:6 (81:6)
[^5]: Ps 82:7 (81:7)
