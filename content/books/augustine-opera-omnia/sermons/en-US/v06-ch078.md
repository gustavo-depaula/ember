# Sermon 229/I: A Tractate on the Wednesday of Easter

**1.** The Gospel readings on the resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ are recited solemnly in order. Today we have heard that the Lord Christ showed his disciples the true flesh in which he had suffered and risen again. They heard him speaking, they saw him present, and yet they even touched him as he said to them: *Touch, handle, and see that a spirit does not have flesh and bones, as you see that I have*[^1]. For when they had seen him, they doubted, and thought they were seeing a spirit, not a body. Whoever, then, still supposes that the Lord's resurrection was not bodily but only spiritual, may God forgive him, for he forgave even his own Apostles; but let them not hold to the error, and let them change their thinking, since those also heard, and changed it. And how great was his gracious favor, that, while he showed them himself present in body, he yet confirmed them from the truth of holy Scripture! *These are,* he says, *the words that I spoke to you while I was still with you*[^2]. Why? Was he not then with them, and speaking with them? What, then, does it mean, *while I was still with you*? Without doubt, while I was still mortal with you, as you are. *These are the words that I spoke to you, that all things must be fulfilled which are written in the law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms concerning me. Then he opened their understanding, that they might understand the Scriptures*[^3]. He it is who to this day opens to us the writings of life, who died for us.

**2.** But let us see what he said to them: *Thus,* he says, *it behooved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead on the third day, and that penance and the forgiveness of sins should be preached in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem*[^4]. Behold, the disciples both saw Christ after the resurrection, and heard from his own mouth that the holy Scriptures had foretold this would be. We did not see Christ present in the flesh; but the Scriptures, by which they too were confirmed, we hear daily when they are read. And what did he say of the Scriptures? *That penance and the forgiveness of sins should be preached in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem.* This the disciples did not see; they saw Christ speaking of the future Church. And so, as Christ spoke, they believed what they did not see. They saw the Head, the body they did not yet see; we see the body, of the Head we believe. There are two, the bridegroom and the bride, the Head and the body, Christ and the Church. To the disciples he showed himself, and promised the Church; to us he shows the Church, and bids us believe concerning himself. The Apostles saw one thing, another thing they did not see; and we see one thing, another thing we do not see. As they, from the Head present, believed concerning the body, so let us, from the body present, believe concerning the Head. Or are we going to deny it? But the very Truth, crying out, will not allow us to deny. For we see the Church of Christ, from the rising of the sun to its setting, praising the name of the Lord[^5]. *Beginning,* he says, *from Jerusalem.* So it was done; for he said to them: *Sit in the city, until you are clothed with power from on high*[^6], for I send to you the promise of my Father.

**3.** And so, while they sat in the city, on the day of Pentecost the Holy Spirit came, filled the disciples, and they spoke in the tongues of all nations[^7]. One man spoke in the tongues of all nations, because the unity of the Church was to be in all nations. All were pricked to the heart at this miracle[^8]. For the Jews who heard were struck with amazement and believed; for they saw so great a miracle done by Peter, in the name of Christ whom they had killed with their own hands, and, pricked to the heart, they did penance in a moment. To them all their sins were forgiven, even to this, that the very deed by which Christ was killed was forgiven. The Jews themselves were converted, I say; and those who before the cross wagged their unbelieving head[^9], by believing deserved to have that very Head, Christ; so that thenceforth their Head should not be wagged, since he sits at the right hand of the Father, and forever will not be moved[^10]. He will not return to miseries, will not return to the cross, will not return to death, but abides in that true beatitude. Therefore those who killed Christ heard and grieved; and the blood which in their rage they poured out, and died, by believing they drank, and lived. The faithful know what I have said, and these neophytes, for they have now drunk. Those, then, who have not drunk, let them hasten and drink, that their heart may be opened: for now it is closed. And so, as it is written in the book whose name is the *Acts of the Apostles,* which is read during these days: *They were pricked to the heart, and many thousands of men drew near to Christ*[^11].

**4.** Then, because of the necessity of ministry, seven deacons were ordained, among whom Stephen, full of the Holy Spirit, stood out. When they had been ordained, the spirit of holy Stephen did not restrain itself from preaching the truth; it was fervent, it gave off sparks, it set things ablaze; until the Jews, filled with zeal for the hardness of their heart, cast upon him a hard stoning, and made for us the martyr who marked out the path for all martyrs. And so, Stephen being killed, the Church which was born in Jerusalem suffered persecution. According to the Lord's promise, then, the foundations of the Church were beginning to be raised, beginning from Jerusalem: and persecution arose, and the brothers were scattered; because from one heap of fire the burning logs, scattered over the earth, set alight whatever they came to. Thus Judea was filled with the Gospel, Samaria was filled; and there was a going forth to the nations, and it reached the ends of the world; not by migrating from its root, but by growing throughout the world we behold the Gospel filling it. Behold, we discern, behold, we hold fast the faith spread through all nations, *beginning from Jerusalem.* Let him deny it who dares; my words do not strike his ears, but the very truth strikes the eyes of those who deny, and shuts their mouth; and what is written is fulfilled: *For the mouth of those who speak iniquities is stopped*[^12].
