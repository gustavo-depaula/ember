# Sermon 147

**1.** You remember that the apostle Peter, first of all the Apostles, was troubled in the Lord's passion. Troubled by himself, but renewed by Christ. For he was first a bold presumer, and afterward became a timid denier. He had promised that he would die for the Lord, when first the Lord was to die for him. So when he said, *I will be with you even to death*; and, *I will lay down my life for you*; the Lord answered him, *Will you lay down your life for me? Amen I say to you, before the cock crows, you will deny me three times*[^1]. The hour came; and because Christ was God but Peter a man, the Scripture was fulfilled, *I said in my alarm: Every man is a liar*[^2]. And the Apostle says, *Since God is truthful, but every man a liar*[^3]. Christ truthful, Peter a liar.

**2.** What now? The Lord questions him, as you heard when the Gospel was read, and says to him, *Simon, son of John, do you love me more than these?* He answered and said, *Yes, Lord, you know that I love you*. And again the Lord asked this, and a third time he asked this. And to the one answering with love he commended the flock. For at each one the Lord Jesus said to Peter, who said, *I love you: Feed my lambs*[^4], feed my little sheep. In the one Peter was figured the unity of all pastors, but of the good ones, who know how to feed the sheep of Christ for Christ, not for themselves. Was Peter now a liar, or was he answering falsely that he loved the Lord? He was answering truthfully; for he was answering what he saw in his own heart. But when he had said, *I will lay down my life for you*[^5], he wished to presume on his future powers. But every man, what he is at the time when he speaks, perhaps he knows; what he will be tomorrow, who knows? So Peter was turning his eyes back to his own heart, when he was questioned by the Lord, and trusting he answered what he saw there: *Yes, Lord, you know that I love you*. What I say to you, you know; what I see here in my heart, you also see. Yet he did not dare to say what the Lord had asked. For the Lord had not simply said, *Do you love me?* but had added, *Do you love me more than these?* that is: Do you love me more than these do? He was speaking of the other disciples; he could only say, *I love you*; he did not dare to say, More than these. He did not wish to be a liar again. It was enough for him to bear witness to his own heart; he ought not to be a judge of another's heart.

**3.** So was Peter truthful, or was Christ truthful in Peter? When the Lord Jesus Christ willed, he forsook Peter, and Peter was found a man; but when it pleased the Lord Jesus Christ, he filled Peter, and Peter was found truthful. The Rock had made Peter truthful; for the Rock was Christ[^6]. And what did he announce to him, when a third time he answered that he loved Christ, and a third time the Lord commended his little sheep to Peter? He foretold to him his passion. *When you were*, he says, *younger, you girded yourself, and went where you wished; but when you have grown old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will gird you, and carry you where you do not wish*. The Evangelist explained to us what Christ meant. *This he said*, he says, *signifying by what death he would glorify God*[^7]; that is, because he was to be crucified for Christ; for this is, *You will stretch out your hands*. Where is that denier? Then after these things the Lord Christ says, *Follow me*[^8]. Not as before, when he called the disciples. For then too he said, *Follow me*; but then to doctrine, now to the crown. Surely, when he denied Christ, he feared to be killed? He feared to suffer what Christ suffered. But now he ought not to fear. For he saw living in the flesh him whom he had seen hanging on the wood. By rising, Christ took away the fear of death; and because he had taken away the fear of death, with reason he asked about Peter's love. Fear had denied three times, love confessed three times. A trinity of denial, the desertion of truth; a trinity of confession, the witness of love.

[^1]: See John 13:37-38
[^2]: Ps 116:11 (115:11)
[^3]: Rom 3:4
[^4]: See John 21:15-17
[^5]: John 13:37
[^6]: See 1 Cor 10:4
[^7]: John 21:18-19
[^8]: John 21:19
