# Sermon 196/A

**1.** Dearest brothers, the invisible sublimity of our Lord Jesus Christ was made a visible humility. His sublimity has no day, his weakness took on a day. Where there is humility, there is weakness. But the weakness of God is strength to the humble. Out of his sublimity he made the world, out of his humility he conquered the world. Unless Christ deigned to be humble, no believer would today be signed with the sign of Christ. You heard what the Apostle said of him, that though he was true God *he did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped*. How was that a thing to be grasped which he was by nature? But what did he do? *He emptied himself, taking the form of a servant*[^1]. And this is what we celebrate today. Many despised the humble Christ and did not come to the height of Christ. But those who adored the humble one found the exalted one. So on the eighth day, after that custom, the Lord was circumcised, and there was offered for him the sacrifice that Moses commanded[^2]. The Jews hear and say: Christ is ours. But if you recognized him, why did you kill him? A great dispensation, dearest ones! About to take away circumcision, he received circumcision. He received the shadow, about to give the light. He received the figure, about to fulfill the truth. For circumcision itself, which was instituted so that a little infant should be circumcised on the eighth day, signified Christ. But you say to me: How are we circumcised in heart by the resurrection of Christ? This, then, was prefigured in the body which he was going to do in the heart. For the apostle Paul says of the Lord Christ, who *was handed over for our trespasses and rose for our justification*[^3]. Circumcision is justification. What is circumcision? To set aside the affection of the flesh, to be circumcised from the world, to serve God and have truth in the heart. For what is added to a man whose little fold of flesh is removed? But it is a sign. It is a sign where the truth is in Christ. How in Christ? I have already said. By his justification we are justified. Therefore by his justification we are circumcised in heart. Look to the eighth day, the Lord's Day. On the sixth day of the week Christ died, on the seventh he was buried, on the other day he rose. See, then, that he rose after the sabbath, that is, on the first day of the week. So if any Jew has heard that Christ was circumcised, let him come and be circumcised by Christ. But he says to me: I do not depart from my law. Would Christ himself have refused to receive circumcision? Would he have condemned what he commanded to be observed? Where would be the fact that he himself gave the law? Or do you think that Christ began to be then, when he was born of Mary? Christ was before his mother, for he created her for himself as a mother. He was before Abraham. I say little to you: He was before Adam. And this too I say little: He was before heaven and earth, for *all things were made through him*[^4]. Do you, then, think that anyone gave the law through Moses but the only-begotten Word of God of the Father? I ask you, dearest ones, what greatness did the old man Simeon see in the little one? What he saw the mother carried. What he understood ruled the world.

**2.** Tell me what Elisha did when he raised the son of his hostess? In that figure the law was given. What happened? It was announced to him: the infant is dead. He gave his servant his staff and said: *Go and lay it upon the dead one*. The servant took his staff, laid it upon the dead one, and he did not rise. Elisha himself came, conformed himself to the dead one, and raised the dead one[^5]. Attend, brothers, attend. The staff through the servant, that is, the law through Moses. The staff was laid upon the dead one, and he did not rise. The Jews could receive the law, but they could not live in it. Let them not, then, despise the humility of Christ; let them not despise him set in weakness. Behold, he is believed and conceived, clothed with flesh, born of a woman, wrapped in swaddling clothes, laid in a manger. Do not despise him while he does such things. He conforms himself to the dead one. See at what you laugh. If you are converted, from that you will live. The humility of Christ conformed itself to us, as to dead ones to be made alive. Through this we have risen meanwhile in faith, and one day truly in the flesh. But, he says, I hold to what God handed to Moses. Hear what God says through the Prophet. What does God speak to Jeremiah? *Behold, the days are coming, says the Lord, and I will establish over the house of Jacob a new covenant*[^6]. Let go the old, take up the new, and you see that you ought to let go circumcision and the fleshly unleavened bread and the fleshly sabbath and the fleshly sacrifices. Hear how the New Covenant is promised: *Behold, the days are coming, says the Lord, and I will establish for them a new covenant, not according to the covenant that I gave to their fathers when I led them out of the land of Egypt*[^7], when the commanded law was given, when the people was led through the desert. Not according to that covenant will I give the new one. Do not, then, wear the old tunic. It crucified Christ. Your father crucified, you hate. He with the hand, you with the heart, both have committed the crime. Let your father, then, displease you, and hear what your Lord did.

**3.** For the solemnity and brevity of the day, a few words will be enough for you. For these are the shortest days in the year, on which Christ was born, but on which the days begin to grow. Let Christ, then, grow in your hearts. Advance and believe, that you may come to eternal life.

[^1]: Phil 2:6-7
[^2]: See Luke 2:21-24
[^3]: Rom 4:25
[^4]: John 1:3
[^5]: See 2 Kgs 4:18-37
[^6]: Jer 31:31
[^7]: Jer 31:31-32
