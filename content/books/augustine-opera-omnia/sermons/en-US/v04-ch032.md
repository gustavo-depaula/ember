# Sermon 116

**1.** The Lord appeared after the resurrection to his disciples, as you have heard, and greeted them, saying: *Peace to you* [^1]. This is peace, and the salutation of salvation: for the salutation itself received its name from salvation. But what is better, than that salvation itself should greet a man? For our salvation is Christ. For he himself is our salvation, who was wounded for us, and fastened with nails to the wood; and, taken down from the wood, laid in the tomb. But from the tomb he rose, the wounds healed, the scars preserved. For he judged it expedient for his disciples that his scars be preserved, from which the wounds of the heart might be healed. What wounds? The wounds of unbelief. For he appeared to their eyes displaying true flesh: and they thought they saw a spirit. This is no light wound of the heart. Indeed, they made a malignant heresy who remained in that wound. But do we not think the disciples were wounded, because they were quickly healed? Let Your Charity consider, if they had remained in that wound, so as to think that the buried body had not risen, but a spirit in the image of a body had deceived human eyes: if they had remained in that faith, rather in that faithlessness, not their wounds, but their death would have to be lamented.

**2.** But what does the Lord Jesus say? *Why are you troubled, and thoughts rise in your heart* [^2]? If thoughts rise in your heart, the thoughts come from the earth. It is good for a man, not that a thought should rise in his heart, but that his heart itself should rise on high: where the Apostle wanted to set the hearts of believers, to whom he said: *If you have risen with Christ, relish the things that are above, where Christ is, sitting at the right hand of God; seek the things that are above, not the things on earth. For you are dead, and your life is hidden with Christ in God: when Christ your life appears, then you too will appear with him in glory* [^3]. In what glory? Of the resurrection. In what glory? Hear the Apostle saying of this body: *It is sown in dishonor, it will rise in glory* [^4]. This glory the Apostles refused to give to their Master, their Christ, their Lord: they did not believe that he could raise up his body from the tomb; they thought him a spirit, and saw flesh, and did not trust even their own eyes. And we believe them announcing it and not displaying it. Behold, they did not believe Christ himself displaying himself. An evil wound: let the medicines of the scars come forth. *Why are you troubled, and thoughts rise in your heart? See my hands and my feet* [^5], where I was fastened with nails. *Touch and see*. But you see, and do not see. *Touch and see*. What? *That a Spirit does not have bones and flesh, as you see me have* [^6]. *Saying this* (so it was recited) *he showed them his hands and feet* [^7].

**3.** *And while they still wavered and marveled for joy*. Now there was joy, and still the wavering remained [^8]. For an incredible thing had been done, but yet done. Is it now incredible, that the flesh of the Lord rose from the tomb? The whole world has believed this: he who did not believe remained unclean. Then, however, it was incredible: and it was persuaded not only to the eyes, but also to the hands, so that through the sense of the body faith might descend into the heart, and faith descending into the heart might be able to be preached through the world, to those not seeing or touching, and yet believing without doubt. *Have you here*, he says, *anything to eat* [^9]? How much does the good builder add to the building of faith? He was not hungry, and sought to eat. And he ate by power, not by necessity. Let the disciples, then, acknowledge the true body, which the world acknowledged at their preaching.

**4.** If perhaps there are some heretics, who still have in their heart that Christ displayed himself to the eyes, and Christ's flesh was not true; let them now lay that aside, let the Gospel persuade them. We rebuke them, because they relish this: he will condemn them, if they persevere in relishing this. Who are you, who do not believe that the body laid in the tomb could rise? If you are a Manichee? who do not even believe him crucified, because you do not even believe him born; you preach that he displayed all things false. He displayed false things, and you tell the truth? You do not lie with the mouth; but did he lie with the body? Behold, you judge that what was not appeared to the eyes; that it was a spirit, not flesh. Hear him: he loves you, lest he condemn you. Hear him saying: behold, he says to you, unhappy one; he speaks to you: Why are you troubled, and thoughts rise in your heart? *See*, he says, *my hands and my feet. Touch and see, because a spirit does not have bones and flesh, as you see me have*. The Truth said these things, and deceived? It was a body, it was flesh; what had been buried appeared. Let doubt perish, let worthy praise follow.  
So he showed himself to the disciples. What is, himself? The head of his Church. The Church to come throughout the world was foreseen by him, was not yet seen by the disciples. He showed the head, he promised the body. For what did he go on to add? *These are the words that I spoke to you, while I was still with you* [^10]. What is this, *while I was still with you?* Was he not then with them, when he was speaking with them? What is, *while I was still with you?* With you mortal, which I now am not. I was with you, when I was about to die. What is, *with you?* With those about to die, myself about to die. Now no longer with you: because with those about to die never again about to die. This, then, I was saying to you. What?

**5.** *That all things must be fulfilled, which are written in the Law, and the Prophets, and the Psalms about me* [^11]. I told you, that all things must be fulfilled. *Then he opened their understanding*. Come, then, Lord, make keys, open, that we may understand. Behold, you say everything, and you are not believed. You are thought a spirit; you are touched, struck, and still those who touch you waver. You admonish from the Scriptures, and still they do not understand. The hearts are shut, open, and enter. He did it: *Then he opened their understanding* [^12]. Open, Lord, and to the one doubting about Christ open the heart. Open the understanding of the one who believes Christ to have been a phantasm. *Then he opened their understanding, that they might understand the Scriptures*.  
*And he said to them*. What? *That so it must be. That so it is written, and so it must be*. What? *That Christ should suffer, and rise from the dead on the third day* [^13]. They saw this; they saw him suffering, they saw him hanging, they saw him present after the resurrection, living. What, then, did they not see? The body, that is, the Church. They saw him, they did not see her. They saw the bridegroom, the bride still lay hidden. Let him promise her too. *So it is written, and so it must be that Christ should suffer, and rise from the dead on the third day*. This is the bridegroom.

**6.** What of the bride? *And that repentance and remission of sins be preached in his name through all nations, beginning from Jerusalem* [^14]. This the disciples did not yet see: the Church through all nations, beginning from Jerusalem, they did not yet see. They saw the head: and from the head they believed the body. By that which they saw, they believed what they did not see. We too are like them: we see something which they did not see; and we do not see something which they saw. What do we see, which they did not see? The Church through all nations. What do we not see, which they saw? Christ set in the flesh. As they saw him, and believed of the body: so we see the body, let us believe of the head. Let our things seen help one another. Christ seen helped them, that they might believe the Church to come: the Church seen helps us, that we may believe Christ to have risen. Their faith was fulfilled, ours too is fulfilled: theirs was fulfilled about the head, ours is fulfilled about the body. The whole Christ became known both to them and to us: but the whole was not seen by them, nor was the whole seen by us. By them the head was seen, the body believed: by us the body is seen, the head believed. Yet to no one is Christ lacking: in all he is full, and still the body remains to him. They believed, through them many of Jerusalem believed; Judea believed, Samaria believed. Let the members come, let the building come to the foundation. *For no one can lay a foundation*, the Apostle says, *other than the one that is laid, which is Christ Jesus* [^15]. Let the Jews rage, let them be filled with zeal: let Stephen be stoned, let Saul keep the garments of those stoning, Saul the future apostle Paul. Let Stephen be killed, let the Jerusalem Church be disturbed [^16]: let the burning timbers depart from there, let them come and kindle. For the timbers in a way were burning in the Jerusalem Church with the Holy Spirit, when they had toward God one soul, and one heart [^17]. With Stephen stoned that heap suffered persecution: the timbers were scattered, and the world was kindled.

**7.** In short, following these things, that raging Saul received letters from the chief priests, and began to go raging, breathing slaughter, thirsting for blood, that from wherever he could, whomever he could he might drag bound, snatch to punishment, sate himself with shed blood [^18]. But where is God, where is Christ, where the crowner of Stephen? Where, except in heaven? Let him see Saul too, let him mock the raging one, let him cry from heaven: *Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me* [^19]? I am in heaven, you on earth; and yet you persecute me. You do not touch the head, but you trample my members. But what do you do? what do you accomplish? *It is hard for you to kick against the goad* [^20]. However much you kick, you torment yourself. Lay aside, then, the fury, take health. Lay aside evil counsel, seek good help. By that voice he was laid low. Who was laid low? The persecutor. Behold, he was conquered by one voice. Why were you going? why were you raging? Those you sought, now you follow: those you persecuted, now for them you suffer persecution. The persecutor who was laid low rises a preacher. He heard the Lord's voice. He was blinded, but in body, that he might be enlightened in heart. Led to Ananias, catechized about many things, baptized, he came forth an apostle [^21]. Speak, preach: preach Christ, disseminate, O good ram, lately a wolf. See him, attend to him, who was raging: *But far be it from me to glory, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world* [^22]. Pour out the Gospel: what you conceived in the heart, disseminate by mouth. Let the nations hear, let the nations believe: let the nations sprout, let the bride for the Lord be born, purpled, from the blood of the martyrs. And from her how many came? how many members clung to the head, and cling now, and believe? And these were baptized, and others will be baptized, and after us others will come. Then, I say, at the end of the age the stones will come to the foundation, living stones, holy stones, that at the end the whole building may be built up by that Church; rather, by this very Church, which now sings a new song, while the house is being built. For so the Psalm itself has it: *When the house was being built after the captivity*. And what? *Sing to the Lord a new song; sing to the Lord, all the earth* [^23]. How great a house! But when does it sing the new song? When it is being built. When is it dedicated? At the end of the age. Its foundation is already dedicated: because he ascended into heaven, and dies not. When we too have risen, to die no more, then we shall be dedicated.
