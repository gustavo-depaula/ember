# Sermon 326: On the Feast of the Martyrs

**1.** The solemnity of the most blessed Martyrs has made this a more joyful day for us. We rejoice, because the Martyrs passed from the land of labor to the region of rest: but this they merited not by dancing, but by praying; not by drinking, but by fasting; not by brawling, but by enduring. Their parents were saddened, I believe, when they went off to their passion: but they rejoiced and said: *I rejoiced in the things that were said to me, We will go into the house of the Lord*[^1]. Do not, parents, do not weep over our joys. If you do not want those whom you reared to go into Gehenna; you ought to imitate, not hinder. They knew where they were going, and the unbelieving parents wept without cause. But then, loving their fleshly children, they mourned: afterward, believing in God, they said: *You have turned my mourning into joy for me, you have torn my sackcloth, and girded me with gladness*[^2]. Would, brothers, that the sackcloth of penitence be torn in us, and the price of pardon be poured out. The Martyrs laid down here all the burdens of the gains of this world, they left them here, and the road which leads to life, unburdened like good soldiers, they ran; as it is written: *As having nothing, and possessing all things*[^3]. And truly on earth they had nothing, but in heaven they possessed perpetual happiness. To heaven they devoutly hastened, and ran the way of life securely; and still set far off, they stretched out their hands to the palm. Run, saints; so run that you may lay hold. *The kingdom of heaven suffers violence, and those who do violence seize it*[^4]. It is not narrow: whoever wants to be happy, let him hasten to the kingdom of heaven. To no one is it closed, except to him who has shut himself out. Christ is ready to receive his confessors. He himself says from above: I watch you, the contending I will help, the conquering I will crown.

**2.** Holding this promise, the Martyrs counted the terrors and threats of the persecutor as nothing. For when the persecutor said: "Sacrifice to the idols," they answered: "We do not do it, because we have an eternal God in heaven, to whom we always sacrifice; for we do not offer sacrifice to demons." And the judge: "Why then do you act against the sacred decree?" They answered: "Because the heavenly Master says to us in the Gospel: *Whoever leaves father and mother, and wife, and children, and all that he possesses, for my name's sake, will receive a hundredfold, and will possess eternal life*[^5]." And the judge: "So you will not obey the commands of the Emperors?" And they answered: "We will not." And he: "What authority then can you have, when you see that you are subject to punishment?" And the Martyrs said: "We bear the authority of the eternal King, therefore we do not care for the authority of mortal man." Then, sent into prisons, they were loaded with chains. How much it was said by the impious: *Where is their God?*[^6] Let their God come, in whom they believed, and free them from the prisons, snatch them from the sword, snatch them from the beasts. All these things they were saying, but those set upon the rock they did not throw down. They raged, but those did not fear. They knew where they were leaving them, and where they were hastening. The Martyrs the confessors are crowned, and the judges the deserters remained. So God wants to test each Christian, that, tested, he may want to crown him with the Martyrs.

[^1]: Ps 122:1 (121:1)
[^2]: Ps 30:11 (29:12)
[^3]: 2 Cor 6:10
[^4]: Matt 11:12
[^5]: See Matt 19:29
[^6]: Ps 42:3 (41:4)
