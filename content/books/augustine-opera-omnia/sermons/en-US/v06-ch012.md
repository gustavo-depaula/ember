# Sermon 195: On the Birthday of the Lord

**1.** The Son of God and likewise the son of man, our Lord Jesus Christ, born of the Father without a mother, created every day; born of the mother without a father, he consecrated this day; invisible by his divine nativity, visible by his human, wonderful in both. Accordingly, of which one the prophet rather said what he foretold of him, *His generation, who shall declare?*[^1], it is difficult to judge: whether of that one in which, never not born, he has the Father coeternal, or of this one in which, born at some time, he had already made the mother in whom he would be made: whether of that one in which he was always born who always was; for who shall declare how light was born of light, and both are one light? how God was born of God, and the number of gods did not grow? how, as of something completed, it is said that he was born, when in that nativity time neither passed by, by which it should be past; nor went before, by which it should be future; nor was present, as if it were still coming to be and were not complete? Who, then, shall declare this generation, when that which is to be declared remains above time, but the speech of the one declaring passes away in time? And this generation too, from a virgin, who shall declare, whose conception in the flesh was not made fleshly, whose rising from the flesh brought abundance to her who nursed, and did not take away integrity from her who bore? Whichever of them, then, or both, *his generation, who shall declare?*

**2.** This is the Lord our God, this is the mediator of God and men, the man, our Savior[^2], who, born of the Father, created even his mother; created of the mother, glorified even the Father: without a female childbearing the only Son to the Father, without a male embrace the only son to the mother. This is the one *beautiful in form beyond the sons of men*[^3], the son of holy Mary, the bridegroom of the holy Church, whom he rendered like to his own mother: for he both made her our mother, and keeps her a virgin for himself. For to her the Apostle says: *I have betrothed you to one husband, to present you as a chaste virgin to Christ*[^4]. Of her he says again that she is our mother, not a slave woman but free, *whose deserted children are more than those of her who has a husband*[^5]. There is therefore in the Church, as in Mary, perpetual integrity and uncorrupted fruitfulness. For what Mary merited in the flesh, the Church preserved in the mind: except that Mary bore one, the Church bears many, to be gathered into one through the One.

**3.** This, then, is the day on which he came into the world, through whom the world was made; on which he was made present in the flesh, never absent in power: for *he was in this world, and he came to his own*[^6]. He was in the world, but the world did not know him: for *the light shone in the darkness, and the darkness did not comprehend it*[^7]. He came, then, in the flesh, to cleanse the vices of the flesh. He came in healing earth, from which he might cure our inner eyes, which our outer earth had blinded: so that, with them healed, we who were before darkness might be *made light in the Lord*[^8]; and the light might no longer shine in the darkness, present to the absent, but appear certain to those who see. For this the bridegroom came forth from his chamber, and *rejoiced like a giant to run his course*[^9]. Beautiful as a bridegroom, strong as a giant, lovable and terrible, severe and serene, fair to the good, harsh to the evil, remaining in the bosom of the Father, he filled the womb of his mother. In which chamber, that is, the womb of the Virgin, the divine nature joined to itself the human: where *the Word was made flesh* for our sake, that, coming forth from the mother, it might *dwell among us*[^10]; that, going ahead to the Father, he might prepare for us where we may dwell[^11]. This day, then, let us joyfully celebrate solemnly; and let us faithfully long for the eternal day, through him who was born for us, eternal in time.

[^2]: See 1 Tim 2:5
[^3]: Ps 45:2 (44:3)
[^4]: 2 Cor 11:2
[^5]: Gal 4:26-27
[^6]: John 1:10-11
[^7]: John 1:5
[^8]: Eph 5:8
[^9]: Ps 19:5 (18:6)
[^10]: John 1:14
[^11]: See John 14:2-3
