# Sermon 390: On Giving Alms

**1.** We wish, as much as the Lord grants, to exhort your love to give alms, which are wont in holy faithful men to be the companions of fasts, so that what is taken from the one who has may be added to the one who has not: so that, while you defraud your soul for your own gain, you may withdraw from the flesh what you may establish in heaven. For you have there your storehouse, you have there your guardian. For where men may most safely place what they love on earth, they hire for themselves the most fortified places, and as much as they can they take pains to keep it for themselves, where thieves cannot approach. And when can this, with them wishing it, with them keeping it, happen on earth? Perhaps the very guardian will be a thief. The Lord Jesus Christ, attending to this, what men want, what they attempt, when they keep their things on earth, gave counsel. Keep in heaven, commit it to me. He, then, who commanded you to give, did not want you to lose, but to move. Let your thing, which is to follow, go before you. What you do not send ahead there, where you will not long be, where after you someone may possess what you kept, you do not know. Lift from here, then, what you love, lest by loving it here you cleave to it, and by cleaving you lose it and perish. Your Lord himself is your guardian and your things'. If your familiar friend should perhaps give counsel to you, ignorant, about preserving grain, that you should lift it from the lower parts to the upper, where you would keep it better; would you not take his counsel? Your Lord, then, gives it to you: he wants neither you to be corrupted, nor your things. Put it here, if you do not want to lose it. Do you want to know what is being done? I know that no one can give better counsel about this building than the one who made it. You say: Where shall I store it up? He answers: In heaven: for thus he says: *Store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where the thief does not dig through, nor the moth corrupt: for where your treasure will be, there will be also your heart*[^1]. Take the counsel, since the option of so great a storehouse is offered to you, for which no contract has been drawn up that you should possess it forever.

**2.** You will perhaps ask how you may lift your thing up there. Do not fret, and seek by thinking for ladders or some contrivances. But as is wont to be done by citizens set abroad, make a transfer. Many indeed do this: when they find suitable persons, they give without delay. Your Lord Christ did this: rich above, here poor. He hungers here: he asks a transfer of you, he will restore an equal sum. Why, then, do you hesitate, why do you delay to give? Or is he not suitable to repay? Give to the poor, you will not lose, do not fear: you give to him himself, when you give to one of his least. Hear the Gospel. When those set on the right were terrified, certain needs being enumerated, saying: *When did we see you in these things?* the Lord answers: *When you did it for one of these least of mine, you did it for me*[^2]. I, he says, received it, when the poor man received it: in him I hungered, in him I was satisfied. Give securely: the Lord receives, the Lord asks. You would not have what to give him, unless you first received it from him. If you lent to men, you would burden them: there is no such one here who is burdened by a loan. If you wish to be a lender, be one with me, says God to you; give to me: I restore with interest. Now then lift yourself up; widen your greed. For one solidus perhaps you are going to receive, not ten, not a hundred, not a thousand, not earth, but heaven. If you gave a pound of copper and received silver, or a pound of silver and received gold, you would rejoice in your good fortune. What you give will truly be changed; not into gold, not silver, but it will become for you eternal life. It will be changed, because you will be changed. He who gave will become an angel. What he gave will become an angelic throne. There is no remedy that frees from death except alms. It is difficult for any man to lead this life without sins. Give out, then, my brothers, distribute your goods: make for yourselves purses that do not grow old, a treasure abiding in heaven. Hear the Psalm: *Although a man walks in an image, yet he is disturbed in vain; he stores up treasure, and does not know for whom he gathers it*[^3]. *Give, and it will be given to you*[^4].

[^1]: Matt 6:20-21
[^2]: Matt 25:39-40
[^3]: Ps 39:6 (38:7)
[^4]: Luke 6:38
