# Sermon 320: On Stephen the Martyr, Delivered on the Day of Easter Itself

We are accustomed to hear booklets concerning the miracles of God through the prayers of the most blessed martyr Stephen. The booklet of this man is his appearance; in place of writing his story is given to be known, in place of paper his face is shown. You who know what, in sorrow, you were accustomed to see in him, read now in gladness what you see: that the Lord our God may be more abundantly honored, and that what is written in the booklet may be written into your memory. Pardon me, that I do not render a long sermon: for you know my weariness. That yesterday, fasting, I could do so much and not fail, and that today too I may speak with you, the prayers of holy Stephen have granted. Turned to the Lord, etc.
