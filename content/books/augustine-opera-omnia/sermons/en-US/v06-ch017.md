# Sermon 198/A: On the Kalends of January, Against the Pagans

**1.** The apostle Peter says to the Church, which is the body of Christ[^1]: *A holy people, a royal priesthood*[^2]. For then one priest was anointed, now all Christians are anointed. The king was anointed, and the priest was anointed; the rest were not anointed. The Lord bore both persons, not in figure, but now in truth, both of king and of priest. For this reason the Apostle says of the Lord himself: *Who was made for him of the seed of David according to the flesh*[^3]. Behold, then, the royal line. So Christ is said to be of the seed of David on account of Mary, because she was *of the seed of David*. But what line is in Mary? It is written in the Gospel that the priest Zechariah had as wife Elizabeth *of the daughters of Aaron*[^4]. Therefore of the priestly line, since that whole tribe is Levi. But it is said in the Gospel to the virgin Mary by the angel: *Your kinswoman Elizabeth*[^5]. If, then, Elizabeth, one of the daughters of Aaron the priest, was kin to Mary, there is no doubt that the virgin Mary belonged not only to royal but also to priestly blood. Wherefore there is in the Lord, according to the man whom he took up, both persons, the royal and the priestly. So, then, two were anointed in figure, the king and the priest, which was fulfilled in that Head of ours, of whom it is manifest that we, the Church, are the whole body. Hence rightly are we called a royal and priestly line.

**2.** In those old sacraments our one Lord Jesus Christ was prefigured. One priest entered into the holy of holies, but all the people stood outside. Are you now, while the bishops stand at the altar, outside, and not within? Then one priest entered into the holy of holies once a year. "Year" signifies the whole of time. Once, then, in the whole of time, our one priest, the Lord Jesus Christ, rising from the dead, entered into the holy of holies, not figured but true, beyond the veils of the heavens, offering himself for us[^6]. He entered, and is there. But the people still stand outside with us; we have not yet risen to go to meet Christ and to remain forever within with him, when it is said to the good servant: *Enter into the joy of your Lord*[^7]. This, then, was prefigured then by the one priest alone entering into the holy of holies, and the people standing outside, which is now fulfilled by our Lord Jesus Christ alone entering above the secret places of the heavens, and the people still groaning outside, made safe by hope, awaiting the redemption of its body, which will be in the resurrection of the dead[^8].

**3.** For he himself, the high priest, offers our prayers, who offered himself a holocaust for us. He himself is the one who leads us through, interposing himself, not to shut us out, but to direct; not to separate, but to reconcile; not to hinder, but to break the hindrances. He himself is the one pontiff and the one priest, who was prefigured in the ancient priests of God. Therefore a priest was sought without blemish of body[^9], because he alone lived without blemish of sin even in mortal flesh. For what was prefigured in their body was signified as future in his life.

[^2]: 1 Pet 2:9
[^3]: Rom 1:3
[^4]: Luke 1:5
[^5]: Luke 1:36

[^7]: Matt 25:21
[^8]: See Rom 8:23-24
