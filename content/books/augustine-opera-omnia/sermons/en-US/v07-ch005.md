# Sermon 234: In the Days of Easter

**1.** The resurrection of the Lord according to all four Evangelists is read during these days. For it is necessary that all be read, because the individual ones did not say everything; but what one left out, another said: and in a way they all made room for one another, so that all are necessary. The Evangelist Mark, whose Gospel was recited yesterday, said briefly what Luke pursued more fully, about two certain disciples, who were not indeed of the number of the twelve, but were nevertheless disciples to whom, as they journeyed, the Lord appeared, and walked with them. For Mark only said that he appeared to two as they journeyed: but this Evangelist Luke said both what he said to them, and what he answered them, and how far he walked with them, and how they recognized him in the breaking of the bread; all these things he said, as we have heard.

**2.** What then, brothers, what do we discuss here? We are built up to believe that the Lord Christ has risen. We already believed when we heard the Gospel, and we entered this church today as believers: and yet somehow it is heard with joy, because the memory is renewed. How do you want our heart to be gladdened, when we seem to be better than those who walked on the road, and to whom the Lord appeared? For we believe what they did not yet believe. They had lost hope, and we do not doubt where they doubted. When the Lord was crucified, they had lost hope: this appeared in their words, when he said to them: *What are these words that you have between you, and why are you sad?* And they: *Are you the only stranger in Jerusalem, and do you not know what has happened there?* And he answered: *What things?* Knowing everything about himself, he asked: because he desired to be in them. *What things?* he said. And they: *Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, who was a prophet, great in words and deeds.* See, we are better. They called Christ a prophet: we have known him as the Lord of the prophets: *He was*, they say, *a great prophet in deeds and words. And how the chief priests crucified him: and behold, it is the third day since all these things were done. But we were hoping*[^1]. You were hoping: do you now not hope? Is this all your discipleship? On the cross a robber surpassed you. You forgot him who was teaching: he recognized the one with whom he was hanging. *We were hoping.* What were you hoping? *That he was the one to redeem Israel*[^2]. What you were hoping, and lost when he was crucified, this the crucified robber recognized. For he said to the Lord: *Lord, remember me when you come into your kingdom*[^3]. See that he was the one to redeem Israel. That cross was a school. There the Master taught the robber. The wood of one hanging became the chair of one teaching. But he who gave himself back to you, let him recall hope to you. And so it was done. Remember, however, beloved, how the Lord Jesus willed to be recognized in the breaking of the bread by those whose eyes were held so that they should not recognize him. The faithful know what I mean: they know Christ in the breaking of the bread. For not all bread, but that which receives the blessing of Christ, becomes the body of Christ. There they recognized him, they exulted, they hastened to the others: now knowing, they found them, telling what they had seen, they added to the Gospel. These things were said, were done, were written: they have come to us.

**3.** Let us believe in Christ crucified; but in him who rose on the third day. This is the faith that distinguishes us from them, distinguishes us from pagans, distinguishes us from Jews; the faith by which we believe that Christ rose from the dead. The Apostle says to Timothy: *Remember that Jesus Christ rose from the dead, of the seed of David, according to my gospel*[^4]. And again the same Apostle: *For if you believe in your heart*, he says, *that Jesus is the Lord, and confess with your mouth that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved*[^5]. This is the salvation about which I discoursed yesterday. *He who believes and is baptized will be saved*[^6]. I know that you believe: you will be saved. Hold in your heart, profess with your mouth that Christ rose from the dead. But have the faith of Christians, not of demons. For see, I distinguish these things for you: what is mine, I distinguish; according to the grace of God that has been given to me, I distinguish for you. When I have distinguished, choose, love. See, I have said: This faith, by which we believe that Christ Jesus rose from the dead, distinguishes us from pagans. Ask a pagan whether Christ was crucified: he cries, Very much so. Whether he rose: he denies it. Ask a Jew whether Christ was crucified: he confesses the crime of his parents; he confesses the crime, in which he too has a share. For he drinks what his parents pledged him: *His blood be upon us, and upon our children*[^7]. But ask him whether he rose from the dead: he will deny it, he will mock, he will accuse. We are distinguished. For we believe that Christ rose from the dead, of the seed of David according to the flesh. Did the demons not know this, or did the demons not believe these things, which they even saw? Even before he had risen, they cried out and said: *We know who you are, the Son of God*[^8]. We distinguished ourselves from pagans by believing that Christ rose: if we can do anything, let us distinguish ourselves from the demons. What is it, I beg you, what is it that the demons said? *We know who you are, the Son of God.* And they hear: *Be silent*[^9]. Did they not say this very thing that Peter also said, when he asked them and said: *Who do men say that I am?* And when they had answered the opinions of strangers, he added and questioned, saying: *But who do you say that I am?* Peter answered: *You are the Christ, the Son of the living God*[^10]. This the demons, this Peter: this the evil spirits, this the Apostle. And the demons hear: *Be silent*: Peter hears: *Blessed are you*[^11]. What distinguished them, let it distinguish us too. From what did the demons cry this out? From fear. From what did Peter? From love. Choose, love. This is the faith that distinguishes Christians from demons; not just any faith. For the apostle James says: *You believe*—the Epistle of the apostle James has this: *You believe that God is one: you do well. The demons also believe, and tremble*[^12]. He himself said this, who wrote in the same Epistle: *If anyone has faith, but does not have works, can faith save him?*[^13] And the apostle Paul, distinguishing, says: *Neither circumcision is of any avail, nor uncircumcision; but faith which works through love*[^14]. We have discerned, we have distinguished: rather, we have found it distinguished, we have read it distinguished, we have learned that it is distinguished. As we are distinguished by faith, so let us be distinguished by conduct too, so let us be distinguished by works too, set ablaze with the love which the demons did not have. With which fire those two on the road also burned. For when Christ was recognized and was departing from them, they said within themselves: *Was not our heart burning within us on the road, while he was opening the Scriptures to us?*[^15] Burn, lest you burn with the fire with which the demons are to burn. Burn with the fire of love, that you may distinguish yourselves from the demons. This burning carries you upward, lifts you up, raises you to heaven. Whatever troubles you have suffered on earth, however much the enemy presses the Christian heart down low; the burning of love seeks the heights. Take a likeness. If you hold a burning torch, hold it upright, head upward; the tress of flame rises to heaven: lower the torch, the flame goes to heaven: turn the torch head downward, surely you do not set the flame down on the earth too? Wherever the burning thing turns, the flame knows no other way, it seeks heaven. Fervent in spirit, be set ablaze with the fire of love: make yourselves fervent with the praises of God, and with the best conduct. One is warm, another cold: let the warm kindle the cold; and let him who burns too little wish for increase, pray for help. The Lord is ready to give: let us with hearts spread open wish to receive. Turning to the Lord, etc.
