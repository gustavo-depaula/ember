# Sermon 222: On the Paschal Vigil

**1.** Although the very solemnity of this holy night exhorts you, most beloved, to watch and pray; yet our address too is solemnly owed you, that against the contrary and envious powers and rulers of darkness, as against nocturnal beasts, the pastoral voice too may rouse the Lord's flock. *For we have not a wrestling against flesh and blood*, as the Apostle says; that is, against men weak in mortal body: *but against the principalities and powers, and rulers of this world, of this darkness, against the spiritual hosts of wickedness in the heavenly places*[^1]. Nor for that reason, surely, should you think the devil and his angels, whom the Apostle signifies by these words, to be the rulers of this world, of which it is written: *And the world was made through him*[^2]. For when he himself had said they are *rulers of the world*, lest anyone should understand the world which in very many places of the Scriptures is named by the name of heaven and earth, he at once added, as if expounding, *of this darkness*, that is, of the unbelieving. On account of which he now says to the faithful: *You were once darkness, but now light in the Lord*[^3]. These are, then, spiritual hosts of wickedness in the heavenly places; not where the arranged stars shine, and the holy angels abide; but in the murky habitation of this lowest air, where also the mist is gathered into a ball (and yet it is written: *Who covers the heaven with clouds*[^4]). Where also birds fly: and yet they are called *the birds of heaven*. In these heavenly places, then, not in that higher tranquillity of the heavenly ones, dwell those most wicked spirits, against whom a spiritual wrestling is declared to us: that, the evil angels conquered, we may enjoy that reward by which we may be joined to the good angels in incorrupt eternity. Whence in another place the same Apostle, when he signified the dark princedom of the devil: *According to the spirit*, he says, *of this world, according to the prince of the power of this air, who now works in the children of unbelief*[^5]. What, then, is the *spirit of this world*, this are the *rulers of the world*. And as there he expounded what he meant by the world, *in the children of unbelief*: so here too, when he added, *of this darkness*. And what there he says, *the prince of the power of the air*, this in this place, *in the heavenly places*. Thanks, then, to the Lord our God, who *rescued us from the power of darkness, and transferred us into the kingdom of the Son of his love*[^6]. Distinguished, then, from that darkness by the gospel light, and redeemed from those powers by the precious blood, *watch and pray, lest you enter into temptation*[^7]. For whoever of you have faith which works through love[^8], the prince of this world has been cast outside from your hearts[^9]: but outside, *like a roaring lion he goes about seeking whom he may devour*[^10]. *Do not, then, give place to the devil*[^11], wishing to penetrate from whatever side: but he who cast him out by suffering for you, let him dwell within in you against him. When he had dominion over you, *you were once darkness; but now light in the Lord: walk as children of light*[^12]. Against the darkness and its rulers watch in the mother, the light, and pray to the Father of lights from the bosom of the mother, the light.
