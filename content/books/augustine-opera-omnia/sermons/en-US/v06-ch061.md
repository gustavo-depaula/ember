# Sermon 223/K: On the Paschal Vigil

**1.** The blessed apostle Paul: *You are all*, he says, *sons of light and sons of the day: we are not of the night nor of darkness. So let us not sleep, as also the rest, but let us watch and be sober. For those who sleep, sleep by night, and those who get drunk, are drunk by night. But we who are of the day, let us be sober*[^1]. What kind of night is that, brothers, in which the Truth does not wish us to be, in which he says those are who sleep? And what kind of sleep is that, from which he distinguishes us sons of light and of the day, and which sleep he addresses us not to sleep? Without doubt that is not the night whose beginning is the setting of the sun, but the fall of man; nor for which the reddening of dawn makes an end, but the renewal of the soul. In this night even the evil who watch sleep; in this night even the good who sleep are not. The common night, by coming, ends the day; that damnable night took us away from that day which made this day. This sleep, which we now by watching resist, with the senses inculpably lulled, receives death; that sleep, by which the unbelieving sleep in the heart, presses the inner eyes into death. Against this let us hear: *Watch and pray*[^2]; against that let us say: *Enlighten my eyes, lest ever I sleep into death*[^3]. Solemnly, then, against the sleep of the body, having these lamps on this night, let us watch; but against that sleep of the heart, as in the night in this age, we ourselves ought to be lamps[^4].

[^1]: 1 Thess 5:5-8
[^2]: See Matt 26:41
[^3]: Ps 13:3 (12:4)
[^4]: See Matt 5:14-15
