# Sermon 223/J: On the Paschal Vigil

**1.** The blessed Apostle, when he admonished us about praying, at the same time admonished us also about watching: *Persist*, he says, *in prayer, watching in it*[^1]. Unclean love, brothers, compels those whom it possesses to watch. The unchaste man watches, to corrupt; the sorcerer, to harm; the drunkard, to drink; the robber, to kill; the lecher, to squander; the covetous, to gather; the thief, to take away; the plunderer, to seize. How much more, then, ought love to watch in the holy and innocent, if iniquity wrings vigils from the shameful and criminal? But those are pressed by a deep sleep of the heart, so that they are compelled to watch in the flesh. Against this sleep it is cried: *Rise, you who sleep, and arise from the dead, and Christ will enlighten you*[^2]. But in us this voice has broken the sleep of this age, if we have heard and risen from the dead, of whom it was said: *Let the dead bury their dead*[^3]. Let us meanwhile render the vigil of the flesh to the solemnity; but let us keep the perpetual vigil of the heart, with Christ enlightening. Let us watch in the flesh, as much as we can, that we may pray; and let us pray, that we may be able to come where even in the flesh we may watch without end. For the angels do not sleep, whose equality is promised to us in the day of resurrection, if now love watches in us.

[^1]: Col 4:2
[^2]: Eph 5:14
[^3]: Matt 8:22
