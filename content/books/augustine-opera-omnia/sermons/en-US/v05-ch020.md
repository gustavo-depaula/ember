# Sermon 162/B

**1.** We have heard the Apostle saying to us, *We perform an embassy for Christ, exhorting you to be reconciled to God*[^1]. He would not exhort us to be reconciled, unless we had been enemies. Therefore the whole world was an enemy to the Savior, a friend to the one who took it captive; that is, an enemy to God and a friend to the devil. And the whole human race, like that woman, was bent down to the earth. Now one who understands these enemies cries out against them and says to God, *They have bent down my soul*[^2]. The devil and his angels bent down the souls of men to the earth—that is, that, prone to the things that are temporal and earthly, they should not seek the things above. For surely the Lord says this of that woman, whom Satan had bound, behold, for eighteen years; and it was now fitting that she be loosed from her bond on the sabbath day. But they slandered him who raised her up. Who, but the "bent ones"? Since even the very things which God had commanded they regarded, not understanding, with an earthly heart. For they celebrated the sacrament of Baptism carnally, they did not see it spiritually.

[^1]: 2 Cor 5:20
[^2]: Ps 57:6 (56:7)
