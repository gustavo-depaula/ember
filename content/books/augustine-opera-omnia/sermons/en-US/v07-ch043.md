# Sermon 263: On the Fortieth Day, the Ascension of the Lord

**1.** The glorification of our Lord Jesus Christ was completed by rising and ascending. His resurrection we celebrated on the Lord's day of Easter: his ascension we celebrate today. Both are festal days for us. For he rose, to show us the example of resurrection; and he ascended, to protect us from above. We have, then, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ first hanging on the wood, now sitting in heaven. He gave our price, when he hung on the wood; he gathered what he bought, when he sits in heaven. For when he has gathered all whom he surely gathered through the times, at the end of time he will come, and, as it is written: *God will come manifest*[^1]; not as he first came hidden, but as it was said: *manifest.* For it was fitting that he come hidden, that he might be judged; but he *will come manifest*, that he may judge. For if he first came *manifest*, who would dare to judge the manifest one? Since indeed the apostle Paul says: *For if they had known, they would never have crucified the Lord of glory*[^2]. But if he were not killed, death would not die: the devil was conquered by his own trophy. For the devil exulted, when by seducing the first man he cast him down into death. By seducing he killed the first man: by killing the last, he lost the first from his snares.

**2.** The true victory of our Lord Jesus Christ, then, was made when he rose and ascended into heaven; and what you heard, when the Apocalypse was read, was fulfilled: *The lion of the tribe of Judah has conquered*[^3]. He himself is called a lion, he himself is called a lamb; a lion because of strength, a lamb because of innocence; a lion because unconquered, a lamb because gentle. And this lamb, slain, by his own death conquered the lion who seeks whom to devour. For the devil is called a lion by ferocity, not by power. For the apostle Peter says: *We ought to watch against temptations, because your adversary the devil goes about seeking whom to devour.* But how he goes about, he said: *Like a roaring lion he goes about, seeking whom to devour*[^4]. Who would not run into the teeth of this lion, unless the lion of the tribe of Judah had conquered? Against the lion a lion, against the wolf a lamb. The devil exulted when Christ died, and in the very death of Christ the devil was conquered; as it were in a mousetrap he took the bait. He rejoiced at the death, as one set over death; at what he rejoiced over, from there a trap was set for him. The mousetrap of the devil was the cross of the Lord; the bait by which he was caught was the death of the Lord. See, our Lord Jesus Christ has risen. Where is the death which hung on the wood? Where is the insult of the Jews? where is the pride and arrogance of those who shook their heads before the cross and said: *If he is the Son of God, let him come down from the cross*[^5]? See, he did more than they demanded by insulting; for it is more to rise from the tomb than to come down from the wood.

**3.** But now how great is the glory, that he ascended into heaven, that he sits at the right hand of the Father? But this we do not see with our eyes, because we neither saw him hanging on the wood, nor beheld him rising from the tomb. All this we hold by faith, we behold with the eyes of the heart. We were praised, because we did not see and yet believed. For the Jews too saw Christ. It is no great thing to see Christ with the eyes of the flesh, but it is great to believe in Christ with the eyes of the heart. If Christ were now presented to us, and stood before us, and were silent, from where would we know who he was? And then, being silent, how would he profit us? Is it not better that he speak absent in the Gospel, than be silent present? And yet he is not absent, if he is held in the heart. Believe in him, and you see him: he is not before your eyes, and he possesses your heart. For if he were absent from us, what we just now heard would be a lie: *behold, I am with you until the consummation of the age*[^6].

[^1]: Ps 50:3 (49:3)
[^2]: 1 Cor 2:8
[^3]: Rev 5:5
[^4]: 1 Pet 5:8
[^5]: See Matt 27:40
[^6]: Matt 28:20
