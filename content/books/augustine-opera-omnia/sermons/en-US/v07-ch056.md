# Sermon 269: On the Day of Pentecost

**1.** We celebrate the coming of the Holy Spirit by an anniversary festival. To this a solemn gathering, a solemn reading, a solemn sermon is owed. Those two have been paid, because you both have come in great numbers, and have heard as it was read. Let us render the third too: let the homage of our tongue not be lacking to him who both gave all tongues to the unlearned, and subjected the tongues of the learned among all nations, and gathered the diverse tongues of the nations to the unity of faith. *For there came suddenly from heaven a sound, as of a violent blast being borne along: and there appeared to them divided tongues, as of fire, which also settled upon each of them; and they began to speak in tongues, as the Spirit gave them to utter*[^1]. For that blast did not puff up, but gave life: that fire did not burn, but kindled. There was fulfilled in them what had been prophesied so long before: *There are no speeches nor words, whose voices are not heard*[^2]: that then, distributed to preach the Gospel, they might do what follows: *Their sound has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world*[^3]. For what else did the Holy Spirit foretell in the tongues of all nations, which he gave to those who had learned only the one tongue of their own nation (which he willed to be then the indication of his presence), except that all nations would believe the Gospel; so that first each of the faithful, but afterward the very unity of the Church, would speak in all tongues? What do those say to these things, who refuse to be incorporated and joined to the Christian fellowship, which bears fruit and grows in all nations? Can they deny that even now the Holy Spirit comes into Christians? Why, then, does no one now, neither among us nor among them, speak in the tongues of all nations (which then was the indication of his coming), except because now is fulfilled what was then signified? For then even one faithful man spoke in all tongues: and now the unity of the faithful speaks in all tongues. And so even now all tongues are ours, since we are members of the body in which they are.

**2.** And not without reason is it rightly understood that, although we confess that they themselves have the Baptism of Christ, heretics or schismatics do not receive the Holy Spirit, except while they cling to the framework of unity through the fellowship of love. For then the tongues of the nations will be theirs too: because where those are, there they too will be, namely in the same body of Christ growing everywhere, keeping the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace. Whom this bond does not bind is a slave. *For we did not receive*, as the Apostle says, *the spirit of slavery again in fear; but we received the Spirit of the adoption of sons, in whom we cry, Abba, Father*[^4]. Accordingly we truly understand that the Holy Spirit demonstrated his presence at that time in the tongues of all nations for this reason, that even in this time, in which he does not so demonstrate himself, whoever is separated from the unity of all nations is not understood to have him, although he is steeped in the sacred sign of Baptism. And lest it be thought to follow that whoever has the Baptism of the Trinity also has the Holy Spirit; therefore even within the unity itself so great a distinction was made, that we find some, baptized, afterward merited the Holy Spirit, when the apostles came to them in Samaria, in whose absence they had been baptized: but others, which is a singular example that occurs, received him before Baptism; as, when Peter spoke, it was granted by the heavenly power to Cornelius and those who were with him, which no man can contradict. Upon others he came soon after they were baptized; as upon that eunuch, to whom Philip had preached Christ from the prophet Isaiah. Upon others through the laying on of the hands of the apostles, as on very many. Upon others, with no one laying on hands, but with all praying; as on that very day which we solemnly celebrate today, when there were in one chamber one hundred twenty souls with the apostles. Upon some, with no one laying on hands, nor anyone praying, but with all hearing the word of God; as upon those whom I mentioned a little before, Cornelius and his household. Why, then, now thus, but now thus, except that nothing here be ascribed to human pride, but the whole to divine grace and power? And so this distinction between the receiving of Baptism, and the receiving of the Holy Spirit, sufficiently instructs us, lest we think those at once to have the Holy Spirit whom we do not deny to have Baptism. How much more those, whom not only no love of Christian unity has armed! *For the love of God has been poured out in our hearts*; not surely through ourselves, but, as it follows, *through the Holy Spirit who has been given to us*[^5]. For which reason, as then the tongues of all nations indicated that the Holy Spirit was present in one man; so now the love of the unity of all nations indicates him.

**3.** *But the animal man*, the Apostle's words are, *does not perceive the things that are of the Spirit of God*[^6]. And surely he rebukes as animal those to whom he says: *Each of you says: I indeed am of Paul, but I of Apollos, but I of Cephas, but I of Christ. Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? or were you baptized in the name of Paul?*[^7] For as the spiritual rejoice in unity, so the animal are eager for dissensions. Of these the apostle Jude too, writing most plainly: *These are they*, he says, *who set themselves apart, animal men, not having the Spirit*[^8]. What more evident? what more express? Let the foolish, then, cease to flatter themselves, and to say to us: What are we going to receive, when we come to you, since you already confess that we have the Baptism of Christ? We answer them: You have the Baptism of Christ; come that you may have also the Spirit of Christ. Fear what is written: *But whoever does not have the Spirit of Christ, he is not his*[^9]. You have put on Christ by the form of the sacred sign; put him on by the imitation of his example. *Since Christ suffered for us, leaving us an example, that we should follow his footsteps*[^10]. Do not be *having a form of piety, but denying its power*[^11]. But what greater power of piety is there than the love of unity? It is said in the Psalms: *I have seen the end of all completion: your commandment is exceedingly wide*[^12]. What commandment, except that of which it was said: *A new commandment I give you, that you love one another*[^13]. Why wide, except because *the love of God has been poured out in our hearts?* Why is it the end of all completion, except because *the fullness of the law is love*; and *all the law is summed up in this saying, in that it is written, You shall love your neighbor as yourself?*[^14] But you so love your neighbors as yourselves, that, when you are unwilling that some evil, which has neither been seen nor proved in you, be believed of you, you yet believe of the whole world that which you have neither seen nor received as proved.

**4.** You seem to yourselves to say: *The Lord Jesus.* And perhaps, not understanding, you attend to that which the Apostle says: *No one says: The Lord Jesus, except in the Holy Spirit*[^15]. But this which he says, *says*, is set down with a mark, and in a certain proper way. *For no one says: The Lord Jesus, except in the Holy Spirit*, but if he *says* it by deeds, not by words alone. For they can say: *The Lord Jesus*, even those of whom he says: *What they say, do; but what they do, do not do*[^16]. All the heresies, which surely you too disapprove, say: *The Lord Jesus.* Nor surely is he going to separate from the kingdom of heaven those whom he finds in the Holy Spirit; and yet he says: *Not everyone who says to me, Lord, Lord, will enter the kingdom of heaven*[^17]. But *No one says: The Lord Jesus, except in the Holy Spirit!* no one plainly, but in that way in which it was said, that is, by deeds. Hence he went on to add: *But he who does the will of my Father, who is in heaven, he will enter the kingdom of heaven*[^18]. For the same Apostle says of certain ones: *They confess that they know God, but by deeds they deny him*[^19]. As he is denied by deeds, so he is said by deeds. In this manner of speaking *no one says: The Lord Jesus, except in the Holy Spirit.* If, then, you do not come to unity, setting yourselves apart, you will be animal, not having the Spirit. But if you come deceitfully: *The Holy Spirit of discipline will flee deceit*[^20]. Then, therefore, know that you have the Holy Spirit, when through sincere love you have consented that your mind cling to unity. Let us answer these things to those who say, What are we going to receive! and let us, brothers, offer them ourselves as an example of good works, neither being proud because we stand, nor despairing of those who lie fallen.
