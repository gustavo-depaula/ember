# Sermon 49/A

**1** *But when the fullness of time came, God sent his Son, made of a woman, made under the Law*[^1]. But "made of a woman" gives offense, because we confess he was born of a Virgin. We confess "made" only of the man; but God is always making, he cannot be made so as to be. God cannot be made; but he is made that he may be something to someone, as it is said: *Lord, you have become a refuge for us*[^2]; and: *The Lord has become my helper*[^3]. How many things he has become, who was never made? But the Lord Christ was made man, that he might be—that he who was always Creator might be a creature. For, remaining God, he was made man, that he might become what he was not, not that he might lose what he was. This, then, that was made of the Virgin—why "of a woman"? Because among the Hebrews, where is the first tongue of Scripture, "woman" is a name signifying sex, not lost integrity. Read: when the woman was formed, *God drew out*, it says, *one of his ribs*—that is, the man's—*and built it into a woman*[^4]. So Mary was a woman; so it was said to her, before she gave birth, before she conceived: *Blessed are you among women*[^5].

[^1]: Gal 4:4
[^2]: Ps 90:1 (89:1)
[^3]: Ps 30:10 (29:11)
[^4]: Gen 2:21-22
[^5]: Luke 1:28
