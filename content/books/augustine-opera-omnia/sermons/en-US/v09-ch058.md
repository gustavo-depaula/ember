# Sermon 335/M: A Sermon on One Righteous Man

**1.** We celebrate today the day of a dead righteous man, and he is not here. But if you want to know where he is, ask the reading which you first heard: *The righteous man, if he is overtaken by death, will be in refreshment*[^1]. For what refreshment is there in this world, in which all things are full of tribulations, and when the tribulations spare us, are full of trials? For this world, whether it threatens or flatters, is to be feared. But let God be feared, and the world rather despised, so that it is the more to be guarded against. If, then, we want to be in refreshment, when death overtakes us, let us be righteous.

**2.** Perhaps someone says: It is too much for me. For let us attend to what is written: *The righteous man, if he is overtaken by death, will be in refreshment,* and when we ask who is righteous, the Psalm terrifies us: *Every man is a liar*[^2]. Let us hear the book of Wisdom: *The righteous man, if he is overtaken by death, will be in refreshment;* let us hear the Psalm: *I said in the excess of my mind: every man is a liar.* Surely holy Domitian was not a man? But if he was a man, *and every man is a liar,* how was he righteous, if he was a liar? or how is he in refreshment, if he was not righteous?

**3.** Let us attend, then, dearly beloved, we have, full of witnesses for this thought, no little refreshment in that we hear the Apostle saying: *For to this end Christ died, that he might be Lord both of the living and of the dead;* and: *For whether we live or die, we are the Lord's*[^3]. He is the righteous one, he will not lose his own: we are certain that Christ was not a liar. Christ a man, and not a liar. How then *every man a liar?* Because Christ was so a man, that he was not only a man. He, unless he were both God and man, would be a liar. So great and so good a one died for us, and do we fear death? He went before and rose again: we are his members and we follow the head.

**4.** For the Psalm too consoles us: *What shall I render to the Lord for all the things he has rendered to me?*[^4] He who said: *I said in the excess of my mind: every man is a liar,* a lying man gives thanks to the Lord, and does not find what to render to him for so great benefits, which he received from him. For what did we receive from him, when we ask what to render and do not find it? We did not exist and he created us; and the beasts did not exist and he created the beasts; more, that he made us to his own image: we had perished and he sought us; he gave us the death of his Son as a pledge, and promised us the life of his Son; from all our sins in the saving baptism he cleansed us, and because by living here we afterward contracted what he might forgive, that we might say: *And forgive us our sins*[^5], he taught us; he cleansed the whole in the baptized, he cleansed the whole in the reborn living one; he does not leave us to die; he does not permit us to die; I mean in sin, which death is malignant and eternal; he admonishes the one called, instructs, helps, forgives, frees. *What shall I render to the Lord for all the things he has rendered to me?* What do you say, man? What do you want to render? Whatever you find, you received from him.

**5.** So we will come, we will come, if we live by faith, for then we will be righteous because *the righteous lives by faith*[^6], so we will come to that reward which will be equal for all. The boys believe, they came at the first hour; the adolescents believe, they came at the third hour; the young men believe, they came at the sixth hour; the more grave believe, they came at the ninth hour; the decrepit believe, they came at the last hour. They came at different hours and received one reward. So the last were made first and the first last. What is, the last first and the first last? Because what the first received, this the last also received. What is that denarius? Eternal life. Why is it equal for all? Because there no one lives more, no one less. To this let us tend, let us trust in the Lord, let us escape the way of trial, let us have the life of happiness.

[^1]: Wis 4:7
[^2]: Ps 116:11 (115:2)
[^3]: Rom 14:9, 8
[^4]: Ps 116:12 (115:12)
[^5]: Luke 11:4
[^6]: Rom 1:17
