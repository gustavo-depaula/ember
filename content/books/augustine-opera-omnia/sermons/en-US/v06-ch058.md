# Sermon 223/H: On the Paschal Vigil

**1.** We celebrate the solemnities of the Lord's humility, who humbled himself, *made obedient even unto death, even the death of the cross*[^1]. Whence we too, on this holy night, by fasting, watching, and praying, humble our souls; nor does this fervor here disagree with that humility. For what else is the grain of mustard than the fervor of humility? By this grain mountains were *transferred into the heart of the sea*[^2], that is, the great preachers of the Gospel, the holy apostles, were transferred from Judea to the Gentiles, and held the very heart of the world, that is, the thoughts of the age, the mountains of which it was said: *Your righteousness is like the mountains of God*[^3]; the mountains of which it was said: *You enlighten wondrously from the eternal mountains*[^4]. For those mountains, enlightened, fervent in their summits, transferred both themselves and that light which enlightens every man[^5], as the mountain of mountains, the king of kings, and the holy of holies, into the heart of the sea, that is, into the faith of the Gentiles; so that in them might be fulfilled what the Prophet had foretold: *In the last times the mountain of the Lord will be manifest, prepared on the summit of the mountains*[^6]; and what he himself had said: *If you have faith like a grain of mustard, you will say to this mountain, Be taken up and cast into the sea, and it will be done*[^7]. These mountains consecrated for us this night, on which the buried Lord rose, so that the buried grain of mustard, by humbling itself, might not appear, and, by sprouting and growing, spreading its branches everywhere, might surpass all things, and invite the lifted up in heart to take refuge and rest, as birds. Let this mountain dwell also in your heart; for it will not suffer narrowness, where love makes for it the widest room. Turning to the Lord.

[^1]: Phil 2:8
[^2]: See Matt 17:20; Mark 11:23
[^3]: Ps 36:6 (35:7)
[^4]: Ps 76:4 (75:5)
[^5]: See John 1:9
[^6]: Isa 2:2
[^7]: See Matt 17:20
