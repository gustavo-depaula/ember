# Sermon 335/J: On the Birthday of the Martyrs

**1.** The holy martyrs, witnesses of Christ, fought against sin even to blood, because he himself was in them, through whom they conquered. And now, those who fight against sins recognize the fight and desire the crown. For when the enemies of the name of Christ persecuted the martyrs, those fought within themselves, and conquered without. But they did not conquer without, unless they first conquered within. Behold I tell you how the enemy came to the martyr. And he seized him, and bound him, and led him to the judge. The judge said to him: Deny Christ if you want to live. But he did not deny, because he wanted to live. How then did he first fight within? Because the sweetness of this life said: Deny. He did not listen, and confessed Christ. Conquering within the sweetness of life, he conquered without the persecutor.

**2.** What is it, then, that I said? Now too those fight who fight against sin, and those who recognize the fight desire the crown. For this I said a while ago, and I owe you to expound what it is. You heard when the Apostle was read. I recall the very words. *For if you live according to the flesh,* he says, *you will die. But if by the spirit you mortify the deeds of the flesh, you will live*[^1]. This is the Christian fight: to mortify by the spirit the deeds of the flesh. A deed of the flesh is to covet another man's wife. The very being delighted and coveting is already a delight of the flesh. He does not yet commit adultery, but already desire titillates him. But conscience will conquer, if patience is held. Who is there who does not suffer such a war? All suffer it but not all conquer. But as not all conquer, so neither are all conquered. But there are those who do not even fight. For when desire has risen in his heart, in that very hour he consents to it. But he does not do it for this reason, because he does not find a place. He who does not do it for the reason that he does not find a place, meanwhile has had no place on earth, and has lost his place in heaven.

**3.** Desire rose, and you rose. But you cannot come to another man's wife. Already you walk bound, because where you consented, there you remained captive. But he who does not consent is not conquered by desire. It fights by that delight. Do you fight by contradiction. It will say: Let us do it, that we may live with delight. Answer you: Let us not do it, that we may live without end. So long do you fight with it, until it too begins not to rise. Or if at some time it has risen for a little, it quickly blushes and perishes. What I said of the love of another man's wife, this I say of the love of drunkenness, of the love of money, this I say of the love of pride and all the rest where there are bad loves and bad conduct.

**4.** But he who contradicts those bad loves, becomes a Christian by good conduct. And he fights daily in conscience, that from him who sees it he may ask the crown, when he has conquered. But would he conquer, if he himself fought? Leave him there alone, and he is conquered. So when you do not consent to the desires of the flesh presuming on yourself, you are alone, and because you presume on yourself, you alone act. But when, reckoning nothing to your own strength, you hand yourself wholly to God, *it is God who* for you *works both to will and to perfect according to* your *good will*[^2]. And therefore he said: *If by the spirit you mortify the deeds of the flesh, you will live.* Let human frailty claim nothing here for itself, let it reckon nothing to its own efforts and nothing to its own strength, because if it imputes it to itself, it makes a place for pride, and pride makes a place for ruin. But he who reckons to God the whole of what he has accomplished, makes a place for the Holy Spirit. And therefore the Apostle says: *For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God*[^3]. If, then, we are sons of God, the Spirit of God leads us, the Spirit of God rules us. Whatever evil we do is ours. But whatever good we do is God's, *who works in us both to will and to perfect according to* our *good will*[^4].

[^1]: Rom 8:13
[^2]: Phil 2:13
[^3]: Rom 8:14
[^4]: Phil 2:13
