# Sermon 79: Again on the Words of the Gospel of Matthew 17:1-8: Where Jesus Showed Himself on the Mountain to Three Disciples

**1.** The great vision on the mountain, where the Lord Jesus showed himself to three disciples, Peter, James, and John, we heard when the holy Gospel was read. *His face shone like the sun*[^1]; this signifies the brightness of the Gospel. *His garments became white like snow*[^2]: this signifies the cleansing of the Church, to whom it was said through the prophet: *And if your sins are like scarlet, I will make them white as snow*[^3]. Elijah and Moses were conversing with him: because the grace of the Gospel has testimony from the Law and the Prophets. In Moses the Law, in Elijah the Prophets: to speak briefly. For there are benefits of God through the holy Martyr, which will be read. Let us hear. It pleased Peter that three tabernacles be made, one for Moses, one for Elijah, and one for Christ. The solitude of the mountain delighted him: he was suffering weariness from the tumult of human affairs. But why did he seek three tabernacles, except because he did not yet know the unity of the Law, prophecy, and the Gospel? Finally, he was corrected by the cloud. *As he was saying these things*, it says, *behold a bright cloud overshadowed them*[^4]. Behold the cloud made one tabernacle: why did you seek three? *And a voice from the cloud: This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear him*[^5]. Elijah speaks; but *hear him.* Moses speaks: but *hear him.* The Prophets speak, the Law speaks: but *hear him*, the voice of the Law, and the tongue of the Prophets. He himself sounded in them, he himself appeared in himself when he deigned. *Hear him:* let us hear him. When the Gospel was speaking, think that it was a cloud: thence the voice sounded to us. Let us hear him: what he says, let us do; what he promised, let us hope.

[^1]: Matt 17:2
[^2]: Matt 17:2
[^3]: Isa 1:18
[^4]: Matt 17:5
[^5]: Matt 17:5
