# Sermon 176/A

**1.** *A bishop, then, must be irreproachable*[^1]. Who denies this? But since a bishop must be irreproachable, does it become a Christian to be reproachable? "Bishop" is a Greek name, but in Latin it can be called "overseer" or "visitor." We are bishops, but with you we are Christians. We are properly called from the visitation, in common all from the anointing. If the anointing is common, the struggle too is common. But why do we visit, if there is nothing good that we may see in you?

[^1]: 1 Tim 3:2
