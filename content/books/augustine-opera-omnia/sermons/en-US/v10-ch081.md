# Sermon 394: On the Birthday of Saints Perpetua and Felicity

**1.** Two gems shone forth today in the Church, and one brightness: because Perpetua and Felicity are one festival; nor can there be any doubt about felicity, which possesses perpetual dignity. The keeping of the prison joined them, grace joined them too: because there is in them no discord. Together they sing in the prison, together they come to meet Christ in the air; together they fight against the heifer, together they will enter the everlasting homeland; together they bore their martyrdom; the one was nursing, the other was giving birth. Perpetua said, as she handed over her infant and removed the suckling: *Who shall separate us from the love of Christ?*[^1] Felicity gave forth groans of childbirth, and afterward hastened undaunted after her companions; and freed by groaning, what did she say to Christ? *You have broken my bonds; to you I will offer a sacrifice of praise*[^2]. And blessed David, to console her groaning, said: *May the Lord give you according to your heart, and confirm all your counsel*[^3]. O frailty! The darkness was fleeing, and the human condition was not passing away. But he who conquered death both freed her from the peril of childbirth, and lifted Perpetua from the weight of milk. For when they were climbing the steps of that ladder, and treading on the treacherous necks of the dragon, they came to the garden of the heavenly meadows, and found there the good Shepherd laying down his soul for his sheep, and seeking the juice of milk from his flocks. For there sat, she says, a shepherd young and old, green in age, gray in head, who knows no old age. A youthful countenance shone in him, because he is the same, and his years will not fail. He was growing gray in head, because the just Lord loved justice, recognized equity in the martyrs. Around him the sheep lay bowed down; he himself milked them with his shepherd's finger, in whom he found an abundance of milk and a conscience fruitful in piety. He was milking with his fingers and addressing them with fatherly comforts, the heavenly promises being prepared, saying: *Come, blessed of my Father, receive the kingdom which has been prepared for you from the foundation of the world*[^4]. And he showed them the troughs of milk foaming with a pure heart through shining almsgiving, and he says: *I was hungry, and you gave me to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me drink*[^5]. Perpetua received from the sweet Shepherd new milk, before she poured out her precious blood. They answered: Amen, and began to ask for his mercy. They prayed in the prison, now secure about the Shepherd. Lord, they say, let our confession not be barren, that we too may deserve to be joined to your precious flocks, and not be separated from your martyrs. There is set before them in a vision a wrestling-place, in the amphitheater a showy arena. There is present that Egyptian who was in heaven the splendid Lucifer: he, about to fight, rolls in the dust, and Perpetua, about to triumph through the Lord the Savior, joined her hands in a cross, having before her the Lord's young man as her defender. She receives the triumph of victory, and obtains the branch from the crown. Let us too offer him our gifts: others offered them, in their time, visits to the prison; let us offer them the vow of this festival, that we may deserve the kingdom with all the saints.

[^1]: Rom 8:35
[^2]: Ps 116:16-17 (115:16-17)
[^3]: Ps 20:4 (19:5)
[^4]: Matt 25:34
[^5]: Matt 25:35
