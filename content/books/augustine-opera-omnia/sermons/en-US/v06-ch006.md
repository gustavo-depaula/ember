# Sermon 189: On the Birthday of the Lord

**1.** For us this day was sanctified by the day that made every day; of which the Psalm sings: *Sing to the Lord a new song, sing to the Lord, all the earth. Sing to the Lord and bless his name, proclaim his salvation, day from day*[^1]. Who is this day from day, but the Son from the Father, light from light? But that day which begot the day that was to be born of the Virgin on this day; that day, then, has no rising, has no setting. I call God the Father day. For Jesus would not be day from day, unless the Father too were day. What is day but light? Not of the eyes of flesh, not light common to men and beasts, but the light that shines for the angels, the light by seeing which hearts are purified. For this night in which we now live passes away, in which the lamps of the Scriptures are lit for us; and there will come that of which another Psalm sings: *In the morning I will stand before you, and I will contemplate you*[^2].

**2.** That day, then, the Word of God, the day that shines for the angels, the day that shines in that homeland from which we are pilgrims, clothed itself in flesh, and was born of the Virgin Mary. He was wondrously born. What is more wonderful than a virgin's childbearing? She conceives, and is a virgin; she bears, and is a virgin. For he was created of her whom he created; and he brought her fruitfulness, he did not corrupt her integrity. Whence was Mary? From Adam. Whence Adam? From the earth. If Adam from the earth, and Mary from Adam, then Mary too is earth. If Mary is earth, let us recognize what we sing: *truth has sprung up from the earth*. What benefit has he bestowed on us? *Truth has sprung up from the earth, and righteousness has looked down from heaven*[^3]. For the Jews, as the Apostle says, *being ignorant of the righteousness of God and wishing to establish their own, did not submit to the righteousness of God*[^4]. Whence can a man be righteous? From himself? What poor man gives himself bread? Who that is naked is clothed, unless he has received a garment? We had no righteousness; here there were only sins. Whence righteousness? What righteousness is there without faith? For *the righteous lives by faith*[^5]. He who calls himself righteous without faith lies. How does he not lie, in whom there is no faith? If he wishes to speak the truth, let him turn himself to the truth. But it was far off. *Truth has sprung up from the earth*. You were sleeping; it came to you: you were snoring; it woke you: it made a way for you through itself, lest it lose you. Therefore, because *truth has sprung up from the earth*, our Lord Jesus Christ was born of the Virgin; *righteousness has looked down from heaven*, that men might have a righteousness not their own, but God's.

**3.** What great condescension! What indignation went before it! What indignation went before? We were mortal, weighed down by sins, bearing our punishments. Every man, when he is born, begins from misery. Do not look for someone prophesying; question the newborn, and see him weeping. When, therefore, there was upon the earth this great indignation of God, what condescension was suddenly made? *Truth has sprung up from the earth*. He created all things, was created among all things; he made the day, came into the day; he was before times, marked out the times. The Lord Christ is forever, without beginning, with the Father: and yet ask today what he is. It is a birthday. Of whom? Of the Lord. Does he have a birthday? He does. *In the beginning the Word, God with God*[^6], does he have a birthday? He does. Unless he had a human generation, we would not come to the divine regeneration: he was born, that we might be reborn. Let no one doubt that he is reborn; Christ was born: he was begotten, not to be regenerated. For who needed regeneration, but the one whose generation is condemned? Let his mercy, then, come to be in our hearts. His mother bore him in the womb; let us bear him in the heart: the Virgin was made pregnant by the incarnation of Christ; let our breasts be made pregnant by faith in Christ: she bore the Savior; let us bring forth praise. Let us not be barren: let our souls be fruitful for God.

**4.** The generation of Christ from the Father is without a mother; the generation of Christ from the mother is without a father: both generations are wonderful. The first generation is eternal, the second temporal. When was he born of the Father? What does "when" mean? Do you ask "when" there, there where you will not find time? Do not ask "when" there. Ask "when" here: when of the mother you ask well. When of the Father you do not ask well: he was born, and he has no time; he was born eternal of the eternal, coeternal. Why do you marvel? He is God. Let there be a consideration of his divinity, and the cause of wonder perishes. And when we say he was born of a Virgin, a great thing, you marvel. He is God, do not marvel; let wonder pass, let praise draw near. Let faith be present: believe that it happened. If you do not believe, that has still happened, but you remain an unbeliever. He deigned to become man: what more do you seek? Is it too little for you that God was humbled for your sake? He who was God was made man. The lodging was cramped: he was wrapped in swaddling clothes, laid in a manger: you heard it when the Gospel was read. Who would not marvel? He who filled the world found no room in the lodging; laid in a manger, he became our food. Let two animals draw near to the manger, two peoples: for *the ox knows its owner, and the ass its master's manger*[^7]. Attend to the manger: do not be ashamed to be the Lord's beast of burden. You will carry Christ, you will not go astray walking along the way: the Way sits upon you. Do you remember that little ass led to the Lord?[^8] Let no one be ashamed, we are it. Let the Lord sit upon us, and call us where he wills. We are his beast of burden, we go to Jerusalem. With him sitting upon us we are not pressed down, but lifted up; with him leading we do not go astray: to him we go, through him we go, we do not perish.
