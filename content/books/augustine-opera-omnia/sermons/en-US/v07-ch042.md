# Sermon 262: Given on the Day of the Lord's Ascension in the Leontian Basilica.

**1. 1.** The Lord Jesus, only-begotten of the Father and coeternal with his begetter, equally invisible, equally unchangeable, equally almighty, equally God; for our sake, as you know, and have received, and hold, became man, the human form being assumed, the divine not lost: powerful while hidden, weak while manifest; as you know, he was born, that we might be reborn; he died, lest we should die forever. He at once, that is, on the third day, rose: to us he promised the resurrection of the flesh at the end. He offered himself to be seen by the eyes of the disciples, and to be handled by their hands; persuading them of what he had become, not taking away what he always was. He lived with them for forty days, as you have heard, going in and out, eating and drinking; now not from need, but the whole from power: and manifesting to them the truth of the flesh, on the cross weakness, from the tomb immortality.

**2. 2.** Today, then, we celebrate the day of his ascension. But another local solemnity meets this Church. Today is the deposition of the holy Leontius, founder of this basilica. But let the star deign to be obscured by the sun. Let us, then, rather speak of the Lord, what we had begun. A good servant rejoices when the Lord is praised.

**3. 3.** Today, then, that is, the fortieth day after his resurrection, the Lord ascended into heaven. We did not see it: but let us believe. Those who saw it preached it, and filled the world. You know who saw it, and who told us: of whom it was foretold: *There are no speeches nor words, whose voices are not heard. Their sound has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world*[^1]. So they came to us too, and roused us from sleep. See, today's day is celebrated throughout the whole world.

**4. 4.** Recall the Psalm. To whom was it said: *Be exalted above the heavens, O God?* To whom was it said? Surely it would not be said to God the Father: *Be exalted*, who was never humbled? Be you exalted: you who were enclosed in your mother's womb; you who were made in her whom you made; you who lay in the manger; you who sucked the breasts like a little one at the vein of the flesh; you who, bearing the world, were borne by your mother: you whom old Simeon recognized as a little one, and praised as great; you whom the widow Anna saw sucking, and knew to be almighty: you who hungered for our sake, thirsted for our sake, were wearied on the road for our sake (surely the bread does not hunger, or the fount thirst, or the way grow weary?); you who endured all these things for our sake: you who slept, and yet do not slumber, keeping Israel: finally, you whom Judas sold, whom the Jews bought, and did not possess: you seized, bound, scourged, crowned with thorns, hung on the wood, struck with a lance, you dead, you buried: *Be exalted above the heavens, O God*[^2].

**5. 4.** *Be exalted*, he says, *be exalted above the heavens*, because you are *God.* Sit in heaven, you who hung on the wood. You are awaited to come as judge, who were awaited and judged. Who would believe these things, unless he were doing it who raises the needy from the earth, and exalts the poor from the dung? He himself raises his needy flesh, and places it with the princes of his people, with whom he is going to judge the living and the dead. With them he placed this needy flesh, to whom he says: *You will sit upon twelve seats, judging the twelve tribes of Israel*[^3].

**6. 5.** *Be exalted*, then, *above the heavens, O God.* It is already done, it is already fulfilled. But we say, How was it foretold as future: *Be exalted above the heavens, O God*; we did not see it, but we believe: see, before our eyes is what follows: *Be exalted above the heavens, O God, and your glory over all the earth*[^4]. Let him not believe that, who does not see this. For what does it mean: *And your glory over all the earth?* except, over all the earth your Church, over all the earth your matron, over all the earth your bride, your beloved, your dove, your spouse. She is your glory: *A man indeed*, says the Apostle, *ought not to veil his head, since he is the image and glory of God: but the woman is the glory of the man*[^5]. If the woman is the glory of the man, the Church is the glory of Christ.

[^1]: Ps 19:3-4 (18:4-5)
[^2]: Ps 57:5 (56:6)
[^3]: Matt 19:28
[^4]: Ps 57:5 (56:6)
[^5]: 1 Cor 11:7
