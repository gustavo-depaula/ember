# Sermon 260/E: A Discourse on the Lord's Day of the Octave of Holy Easter

**1.** When the blessed apostles were doing wonders and healings in the name of Christ, the Jews, filled with zeal, forbade them to speak in the name of him who had chosen them, and had granted them to be such. But they answered, saying: *If it is just before God to obey you rather than God, judge this; for we cannot but speak the things that we have seen and heard*[^1]. They professed themselves witnesses of Christ; but those who are called "witnesses" in Latin are called "martyrs" in Greek. Saying, then, We cannot but speak the things that we have seen and heard, they despised the prohibition of men, that they might have the blessing of God. They saw and heard: what about us? We too ought to preach. But we did not see. Surely let us preach what we have heard; because the Apostle says: *So then faith is from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ*[^2]. Judea saw the Lord Christ in the flesh, the gentiles did not see him; and yet, not seeing, they heard and believed, seeing, they despised and killed. We, then, were going to hear, not to see; but vision is kept for us, that what we now believe we may see at the end of the age. The Lord himself, then, foresaw us, when he said in prophecy: *A people whom I did not know served me*[^3]; the one I knew resisted me, the one I did not know served me. But from where did a people serve you whom you did not know, that is, to whom you did not exhibit the presence of your flesh, to whose eyes you did not show your scars after the resurrection, from where did it serve you? Follow on, and you hear: *In the hearing of the ear it obeyed me*[^4].

**2.** Say, then, you too: We cannot but speak what we have heard and preach the Lord Christ. Let each preach where he can, and he is a martyr. But sometimes a man does not suffer persecution, and fears shame. It happens to him, for example, to dine among pagans, and he blushes to be called a Christian. If he is terrified of a dinner-companion, how can he despise a persecutor? Preach Christ, then, where you can, to whom you can, how you can. Faith is demanded of you, not eloquence: let faith speak of you, and Christ speaks. For if faith is in you, Christ dwells in you. You heard the Psalm: *I believed, therefore I have spoken*[^5]. He could not believe, and be silent. He is ungrateful to the one who fills him, who does not pour out; whatever each one has been filled with, he ought to pour out. For such a fount is born in him that knows how to pour out, does not know how to dry up: *There will become in him a fountain of water springing up to eternal life*[^6]. For you preach securely, because you do not lie about the fount of truth: you have received what you belch out on your tongue. For if you wish to speak of your own, you will be liars; this the same Psalm says: *I said in my ecstasy: Every man is a liar*[^7]. What does it mean: *Every man is a liar?* Every Adam is a liar. Strip off Adam, and put on Christ, and you will not be a liar. Let this suffice your Charity, because there are many things to be done.

[^1]: Acts 4:19-20
[^2]: Rom 10:17
[^3]: Ps 18:43 (17:45)
[^4]: Ps 18:44 (17:45)
[^5]: Ps 116:10 (115:10)
[^6]: John 4:14
[^7]: Ps 116:11 (115:11)
