# Sermon 395: On the Ascension of the Lord

**1.** Today we celebrate the Ascension of the Lord into heaven. Let us not hear "Lift up your heart" in vain, but let us ascend with him with our whole heart, the Apostle teaching and saying: *If you have risen with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is, sitting at the right hand of God; savor the things that are above, not the things that are upon the earth*[^1]. Let the necessity of action be on earth; let the will of ascending be in heaven. Hope here, the reality there. For the time will come when the reality is there. But when the reality is there, hope is neither here nor there: not because hope is empty, but because it is finished when the reality has come. Finally, hear what the Apostle said about hope. *By hope*, he says, *we have been saved. But hope that is seen is not hope: for what a man sees, why does he hope for it? But if we hope for what we do not see, we wait for it through patience*[^2]. Attend to human affairs themselves, and consider that if someone hopes to marry a wife, then he does not yet have her. For if he has her, what does he hope for? He marries, then, the wife whom he hoped for; and now he will not hope. Happily, then, hope is finished, when the reality has come. A pilgrim hopes that he will come to his homeland: as long as he is not there, he hopes; when he has come, he no longer hopes. For the reality has succeeded hope. Happily hope is finished, when what was hoped for is held. Now, then, dearest ones, that which you heard, that we should have our heart on high, is brought about by the heart itself, that we may think of that future life. Let us live well here, that we may live there.

**2.** For behold how great the condescension of our Lord: he who made us descended to us; because we had fallen from him. And that he might come to us, he himself did not fall, but descended to us. If, then, he descended to us, he lifted us up. Already in his body he, our head, has lifted us up: where he is, the members too will follow. For where the head went before, the members will follow. He is the head, we are the members. He is in heaven, we on earth. Is he as it were far from us? Far from it. If you ask of distances, he is far: ask of love, he is with us. For if he were not with us, he would not say in the Gospel: *Behold, I am with you until the consummation of the age*[^3]. If he is not with us, we lie when we say to you: The Lord be with you. He would not have cried out from heaven, when Saul was persecuting—not himself, but his saints, his servants, and, that I may say it more intimately, his members: *Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?*[^4] Behold, I am here in heaven, you on earth, and among the persecutors. Why me? Because my members: through which members I am there. For if the sole of the foot is trodden on, does the tongue not cry out? He, then, through whom heaven and earth were made, for the sake of him whom he made from the earth, descended to the earth, and from here lifted up the earth into heaven. What, then, went before in him, let us hope for in the end. He will render to us what he promised: we are secure, he made out a bond, he wrote the Gospel; he will render to us. What he spent on us is more. For do we suppose that he will not render his life, who for us has already paid out his death? The lowliness of his passion, the injuries, the insults, all the indignities on earth he took up for us: will he not give us the kingdom, happiness, immortality, eternity? He bore our evils; will he not give us his goods? In this hope, because the promiser is truthful, let us walk secure: but let us so live that with a good face we may say to him: We have done what you commanded, render what you promised.

[^1]: Col 3:1-2
[^2]: Rom 8:24-25
[^3]: Matt 28:20
[^4]: Acts 9:4
