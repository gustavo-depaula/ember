# Sermon 335/F: On the Martyrs

**1.** But what shall we say of the martyrs? How great a security was given them, that they did not care even about the dividing of their members? Does he take care about the number of the hairs and not take care in the resurrection about the wholeness of the members? So confess Christ. Choose for yourself one of two. Do you want Christ to confess you before his Father? Yes, you say. So do you too confess Christ before men.

**2.** For the holy martyrs did not presume on themselves but sought consolation from Christ. Therefore they conquered. Hear the voice of those not presuming on themselves. It is the voice of the holy martyrs: *Unless the Lord had been in us, let Israel now say, unless the Lord had been in us, when men rose up against us, perhaps they would have swallowed us alive*[^1]. The martyrs say: *Unless the Lord had been in us,* unless he himself had helped, unless he himself had strengthened the heart in faith, unless he had given patience, unless he had supplied strength to those contending, *perhaps they would have swallowed us alive.* What is: *they would have swallowed us alive?* Thus, as we know Christ, as we know that he suffered and rose again, as we know that he is to come to the judgment of the living and the dead, unless he had helped, they would have swallowed us. He said "alive," that is, knowing. It is another thing to be ignorant of Christ. Therefore he is swallowed because he thinks there is no savior. Therefore he is swallowed but dead he is swallowed. For he who knows Christ the savior and denies him, is swallowed alive. He knows, so that, knowing, alive and aware he perishes. He denies and as though he does not perish. But rather then he perishes. Behold they denied Christ, they were released from the prisons, from the chains. Tell me: Did they perish or did they not perish? I ask the faith of the heart, not the eyes of the flesh. You see that it is not to perish to perish, and to perish not to perish. So it was said: *He who finds his soul will lose it*[^2]. By losing he does not lose, by not losing he rather will lose. Why do you deny Christ? O you who now rejoice loosed from chains. You answer: "I feared for my soul," you say. "I was to be killed. Therefore I denied. I took counsel for my soul lest I lose it." Rather you lost it, you were swallowed alive. If you had feared, you would not have lost it. Fool! you feared for your body not for your soul. Have you not heard him who said: *Do not fear those who kill the body, but can do nothing to the soul?*[^3] Nor did you take counsel for the very body, because on the day of judgment the souls will burn with the body. You feared for the body lest the persecutor kill you. Behold he does not kill you because you denied Christ. You removed the persecutor by denying Christ. Remove the fever if you can. Behold it is coming, behold it will increase, behold it will cast you out of the body. Where will you go who have gone out of the body? But what I had begun to say, you did not take counsel even for the very body. For while your soul is being consumed and seething in the torments of fire your body rots, will be reduced to ash. And were the martyrs, you say, not men? Their bodies too are turned into ash. And when the voice comes from heaven, you will both rise again. The martyr comes forth, you come forth too. But see who where, where he where you. You have indeed a confused resurrection, but *those who did well to the resurrection of life, but those who did evil to the resurrection of judgment*[^4]. Of what judgment? Of what punishment? Hear Isaiah the prophet: *Their worm will not die and their fire will not be quenched*[^5]. If you do not amend while you live, behold where you rise again, to the burning, to the flames where *the fire will not be quenched* and the worms will not die. What is it that you feared? For what did you fear? For the body. Behold there clings to the body the Gehenna of fire and the worm. You see that when you feared you did not take counsel even for it. Do not fear and do fear. Do not fear lest here your soul be cast out by the persecutor. Do not fear lest your body be killed. You did not fear for the soul and you took counsel for the body. Both of soul and of body God will be your upholder, both the restorer of your soul and the restorer of your body.

[^1]: Ps 124:1-3 (123:1-3)
[^2]: Matt 10:39
[^3]: Matt 10:28
[^4]: John 5:29
[^5]: Isa 66:24
