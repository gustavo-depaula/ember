# Revisions II

## Chapter 44 (71): To Orosius the Presbyter, a Refutation of the Priscillianists and Origenists, in One Book

Meanwhile, with all the brevity and clarity I could manage, I replied to an inquiry from a certain Spanish presbyter named Orosius concerning the Priscillianists and concerning certain opinions of Origen that the catholic faith rejects. The title of this little work is: *To Orosius, a Refutation of the Priscillianists and Origenists.* For his inquiry itself has been attached at the head of my reply.

This book begins thus: *To answer you when you ask, my most beloved son Orosius.*
