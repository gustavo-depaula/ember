# Revisions II

## Chapter 58 (85): Answer to an Enemy of the Law and the Prophets, in Two Books

Meanwhile a certain book by some heretic—whether a Marcionist or one of those whose error supposes that this world was not made by God, and that the God of the Law given through Moses and of the Prophets belonging to that same Law is not the true God but a most wicked demon—was being read aloud in the seaside square at Carthage, where many were gathering and listening most attentively. Some brothers, deeply earnest Christians, brought it to me to be refuted and sent it without any delay, urgently asking that I too not put off a reply. I refuted him in two books, which I therefore entitled *Answer to an Enemy of the Law and the Prophets,* because the very manuscript that was sent had no author's name.

This work begins thus: *In the book you sent, most beloved brothers.*
