# Revisions II

## Chapter 52 (79): Answer to an Arian Sermon, in One Book

During this time there came into my hands a certain sermon of the Arians, without the name of its author. To this, at the request and insistence of the man who had sent it to me, I replied with all the brevity and speed I could, attaching that same sermon at the head of my response and applying numbers to its individual sections, so that by looking at them one might easily notice what I answered at each place.

This book, after their sermon, which has been attached at the head, begins as follows: *To their preceding argument I reply with this argument.*
