# Revisions II

## Chapter 67 (94): To the same persons, one book on rebuke and grace

Again I wrote to the same persons another book, which I entitled *Rebuke and Grace*, when it was reported to me that someone there had said that no one should be rebuked if he does not keep God's commandments, but that one should only pray on his behalf that he may keep them.

This book begins thus: *Having read your letter, Valentinus, most beloved brother.*
