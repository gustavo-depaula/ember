## Translation Decisions

- 2026-10-10: Address. Augustine writes to Valentine and his monks in the plural ("vós"). The objector he answers ("O man", "whoever you are") is addressed with "tu". Words addressed to God (Lord's Prayer, psalm verses in ch. 24, Christ to the Father in ch. 21, Peter to Christ in ch. 24) take the reverent "Vós", as in `enchiridion`. Pronouns for God lowercase, as in the Augustine siblings.
- 2026-10-10: Scripture is translated from the source's wording, not a Portuguese Bible (e.g. ch. 2 "esta é a mudança da destra do Altíssimo"; ch. 30 Lc 1:35 "o ente santo"; ch. 46 2 Cor 5:20 "desempenhamos uma embaixada"). Lord's Prayer: "Santificado seja o vosso nome", "Livrai-nos do mal", "Perdoai-nos as nossas dívidas" (as in `enchiridion`).
- 2026-10-10: Scripture references kept inline and unpunctuated as in the source, with pt-BR book names (Coríntios, Tessalonicenses, Apocalipse, Crônicas, Macabeus, Sabedoria, Eclesiastes…). Wrong references the edition carries are mirrored, not fixed: ch. 9 "2 Coríntios 4:7" (1 Cor 4:7) and "Romanos 3:23" (Rom 5:12); ch. 10 "João 8:24" (8:44); ch. 17 "Provérbios 8:35" (the same quote is cited as Pr 16:1 in ch. 6); ch. 20 "Romanos 8:29" after 1 Jo 2:19; ch. 21 "Mateus 20:16" for Jo 6:39; ch. 9 "Eclesiastes 7:30" (Vulgate numbering).
- 2026-10-10: Chapter headings: "Capítulo N [roman.] — título" in sentence case without the final period, as in `grace-and-free-will`. The source's "[VI]" without period and the inline "[XVI.]" in ch. 48 are kept. Bracketed editorial glosses in ch. 35 ([Adão], [não], [os santos]) are kept.
- 2026-10-10: The source carries no footnotes; nothing dropped. Curly quotes, with ‘ ’ for quotes inside quotes.
- 2026-10-10: Two words the edition lost in transcription were not restored, and the pt-BR follows what the source prints: ch. 2 "the true and prophetical and and catholic faith" → "a fé verdadeira, profética e católica" (the Latin adds *apostolicam*); ch. 21 "according to the and true judgment" → "segundo o verdadeiro juízo" (the Latin has *iustum et verum*).
- 2026-10-10: ch. 31 "whereby it is affected that" read as "effected" ("pela qual se faz que").

### Round 3

Focus: independent source-blind pt-BR cold read, then verification against the canonical en-US and a spelling, diacritics, crase, hyphenation and punctuation sweep. Verdict: 4 defects, fixed.

Fixed:
- ch. 11 heading [27]: “e nele morreram, devem” → “e nele morreram devem”; the comma separated the complete subject from its predicate (EN “They Who Have Not Received … and Have Died Therein, Must Righteously Be Condemned”).
- ch. 15 [36]: “Por amor de ti” → “Por amor de vós” (EN “Because for your sake”); the embedded psalm addresses God and must follow the journal’s Vós decision.
- ch. 38 [82]: “para que fosse movida … não desfalecesse, nem fosse vencida” → “para que seja movida … não desfaleça, nem seja vencida”; these clauses depend on present “se presta auxílio”, not on the preceding hypothetical condition (EN “Therefore aid is brought … so that it might be … influenced … might not fail, nor be overcome”).
- ch. 42 [90]: “mas são só por algum tempo” → “mas só por algum tempo permanecem nela”; “ser” lacked a recoverable predicative in Portuguese. EN “they receive the grace of God, but they are only for a season, and do not persevere” refers to their temporary continuance in the grace received, not their temporary existence.

Rejected:
- ch. 8 [22] “o homem aproveita da correção quando se compadece e ajuda aquele que faz aproveitar”: EN “a man then profits by rebuke when He pities and aids who makes … to profit”. “Aproveitar” has the sense of gaining benefit; the postposed subject of “se compadece e ajuda” is “aquele”, referring to God. Unusual order, not an unresolvable subject or reversed agency.
- ch. 15 [36] “Com razão segue-se a palavra ao reino dos eleitos”: EN “Rightly follows the word to the kingdom of the elect”. The difficult connection is in the canonical source; the quotation follows the preceding discussion of the elect’s kingdom. No unsupported rewriting.
- ch. 24 [54] “aprendeu não só acerca da vida eterna, mas também acerca da conduta piedosa e da perseverança nesta vida, como aquilo em que se deve manter a esperança”: EN “learned not only of eternal life, but, moreover, of a pious conversation and perseverance in this life, as that in which hope should be maintained”. The awkward “como aquilo” reflects the source’s own connection; replacing its referent with an explicit “Deus” would impose a clarification absent from the canonical English.
- ch. 49 [104] “o amor nem peca e cobre a multidão dos pecados”: EN “love both does not sin, and does cover the multitude of sins”. Here “nem” can stand alone with the force of “nem sequer”; “e cobre” is affirmative, as in the source. No need to impose a second “nem” (which would reverse the meaning) or normalize to “não”.

Fixed:

### Round 13

Focus: source-blind pt-BR cold read, source verification against canonical en-US, then spelling, diacritics, crase, post-1990 hyphenation and punctuation. Verdict: clean; no defects or chapter changes.

Rejected:
- ch. 10 [26]: “Toda a dádiva ótima e todo o dom perfeito vem do alto” (SRC “Every best gift and every perfect gift is from above”). the singular is defensible with synonymous coordinated heads (dádiva/dom), also presented distributively by toda/todo. The source likewise describes every gift; changing vem to vêm would be an optional grammatical alternative, not correction of an objective defect.
- ch. 32 [70]: “No tempo presente, porém, àqueles a quem falta tal auxílio, é pena do pecado” (SRC “At the present time, however, to those to whom such assistance is wanting, it is the penalty of sin”). the understood subject of é is the lack of assistance just mentioned. The ellipsis is recoverable, and the source has the same anaphoric it; no missing assertion or unresolvable subject.
- ch. 37 [80]: “Mas de onde não se gloriaria a carne na sua presença, senão acerca dos seus méritos?” (SRC “But whence should flesh not glory in His presence, save concerning its merits?”). de onde asks for the ground of boasting, specified by acerca dos seus méritos. The unusual negative rhetorical question follows the source; removing não would change its wording without a demonstrated translation defect.
- ch. 40 [86]: “o número dos santos […] será conduzido para lá na sua integridade, e lá será enfim conservado […] bem-aventuradíssimo” (SRC “the number of the saints […] shall be guided there in its completeness, and there shall be at length without end preserved in its fullest completeness, most blessed”). the singular predicates, including bem-aventuradíssimo, agree with número. The following eles refers semantically to santos. Neither requires a plural correction.
- ch. 46 [98]: “cada um começa então a ser filho da paz quando obedece e crê neste evangelho e, justificado pela fé, começou a ter paz para com Deus” (SRC “every one then begins to be a son of peace who obeys and believes this gospel, and who, being justified by faith, has begun to have peace towards God”). começou expresses the onset already attained, alongside present obedience and belief. The source itself combines begins/obeys/believes with has begun; automatic leveling to começa would erase that aspectual distinction.
