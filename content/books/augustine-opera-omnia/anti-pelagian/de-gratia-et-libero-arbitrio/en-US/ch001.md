## Revisions II: Chapter 66 (93)

### To Valentinus and, with him, the monks, on grace and free choice, one book

On account of those who, when God's grace is defended[^1], suppose that free choice is being denied, and so themselves defend free choice in such a way as to deny God's grace[^2], asserting that it is given according to our merits, I wrote a book whose title is *On Grace and Free Choice.* I wrote it for those monks of Hadrumetum, in whose monastery a dispute over this matter had begun, so that some of them were compelled to consult me.

This book begins thus: *On account of those who so preach human free choice.*

[^1]: Letters 214.1; 215.1.
[^2]: *Grace and Free Choice* 5.10–11.
