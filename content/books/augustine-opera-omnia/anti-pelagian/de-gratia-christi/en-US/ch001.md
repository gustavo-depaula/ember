# Revisions II

## Chapter 50 (77): Against Pelagius and Caelestius, on the grace of Christ and on original sin, to Albina, Pinianus, and Melania, two books

After the Pelagian heresy, along with its authors, had been refuted and condemned by the bishops of the Roman Church, first by Innocent and then by Zosimus[^1], with the assistance of the letters of the African councils, I wrote two books against them, one *The Grace of Christ,* the other *Original Sin.*

This work begins thus: *How greatly we rejoice over your welfare, both bodily and above all spiritual.*

[^1]: Opus imp. c. Iul. 1, 16; 4, 3.
