# Tractate 92

## *But when the Paraclete has come, he will bear witness*[^1].

## The Holy Spirit, by bearing witness and making the witnesses most strong, took away fear from the friends of Christ, and turned the hatred of his enemies into love.

**1.** The Lord Jesus, in the discourse which he held to his disciples after supper, near to his passion — as one about to go and to leave them by his bodily presence, but to be with all his own until the consummation of the world by his spiritual presence — exhorted them to endure the persecutions of the impious, whom he named by the name of the world: out of which world, however, he said that he had chosen even the disciples themselves, that they might know they were by the grace of God what they are, but by their own vices had been what they were. Then he expressly pointed to the persecutors, both his own and theirs, the Jews, so that it might fully appear that they too were included under the appellation of the damnable world, which persecutes the saints. And when he said of them that they did not know him by whom he was sent, and yet hated both the Son and the Father — that is, both him who was sent and him by whom he was sent — about all of which we have already discoursed in other sermons; he came to where he says: *That the word may be fulfilled which is written in their Law: They hated me without cause*[^2].

Then, as if consequently, he added that on which we have now undertaken to discuss: *But when the Paraclete has come, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth who proceeds from the Father, he will bear witness concerning me; and you also will bear witness, because you are with me from the beginning*[^3]. How does this bear on what he had said, *But now they have both seen and hated both me and my Father: but that the word may be fulfilled which is written in their Law: They hated me without cause*? Is it because the Paraclete, when he came, the Spirit of truth, convicted by a more manifest testimony those who had seen and hated? Nay rather, he also, by his manifestation, converted some even of those who had seen and still hated, to the faith which works through love. That we may so understand this, let us recall that it was so done. For the Holy Spirit came on the day of Pentecost upon a hundred and twenty men gathered together, among whom were also all the Apostles, who, filled with him, when they spoke with the tongues of all nations, many of those who had hated, stupefied by so great a miracle (since they saw, while Peter spoke, so great and divine a testimony borne concerning Christ, that he who, slain by them, was reckoned among the dead, was proved to have risen and to be alive), were pricked in heart and converted; and they received the pardon of so much blood so impiously and monstrously shed, redeemed by the very blood that they had shed. For the blood of Christ was so shed for the remission of all sins that it could blot out even that very sin by which it was shed. Looking upon this, then, the Lord was saying: *They hated me without cause: but when the Paraclete has come, he will bear witness concerning me*: as if he said: They hated me, and slew me, seeing; but such a testimony will the Paraclete bear concerning me that he will make them believe in me without seeing.

## The Spirit strengthened and enlarged a love still weak and narrow.

**2.** *And you also,* he says, *will bear witness, because you are with me from the beginning*[^4]. The Holy Spirit will bear witness, and you also will bear witness. For because you are with me from the beginning, you can preach what you know: but that you do not do it now, the fullness of that Spirit is not yet present with you. *He, then, will bear witness concerning me, and you will bear witness*: for the love of God, poured out in your hearts through the Holy Spirit who will be given you, will give you confidence to bear witness.

And this, certainly, was still lacking to Peter, when, terrified by the question of a servant woman, he could not bear true witness, but, against his own promise, was driven by great fear to deny three times. But *this fear is not in love, but perfect love casts out fear*[^5]. Finally, before the Lord's passion, his servile fear was questioned by a woman of servitude; but after the Lord's resurrection, his liberal love by the very Prince of liberty: and therefore there he was troubled, here made tranquil; there he denied the one he had loved, here he loved the one he had denied. But even then this very love had still been weak and narrow, until the Holy Spirit should strengthen and enlarge it. After he had been infused into him by the abundance of a more bountiful grace, he so kindled to the bearing of witness concerning Christ that breast once cold, and so unsealed those lips, before trembling, which had suppressed the truth, that when all upon whom the Holy Spirit had come spoke with the tongues of all nations, while the crowds of the Jews stood around, he alone, more promptly than the rest, flashed forth to bear witness concerning Christ, and confounded his slayers concerning his resurrection. If anyone delights to behold so sweetly so holy a spectacle, let him read the Acts of the Apostles: there let him marvel at blessed Peter preaching, whom he had grieved over as denying; there let him see that tongue, transferred from diffidence to confidence, and from servitude to liberty, convert so many tongues of enemies to the confession of Christ — that tongue which, not being able to bear one tongue, had been turned to denial. What more? So great a brightness of grace, so great a fullness of the Holy Spirit appeared in him, such weights of most precious truth proceeded from the mouth of the preacher, that he made adversaries of a huge multitude, the Jews who slew Christ, ready to die for him, by whom he had feared to be slain together with him. This the Holy Spirit did, sent then, before promised. These great and wondrous benefits of his the Lord foresaw, when he said: *And they have both seen and hated both me and my Father: that the word may be fulfilled which is written in their Law, They hated me without cause. But when the Paraclete has come, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth who proceeds from the Father, he will bear witness concerning me; and you also will bear witness*[^6]. For he, bearing witness and making the witnesses most strong, took away fear from the friends of Christ, and turned the hatred of his enemies into love.
