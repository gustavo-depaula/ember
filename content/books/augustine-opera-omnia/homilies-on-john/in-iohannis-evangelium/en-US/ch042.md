# Tractate 42

## *I came forth from God and have come. Nor did I come of myself, but he sent me*[^1].

The Word of the Father came to us, because *the Word was made flesh, that he might dwell among us*[^2]. His coming is his humanity; his abiding is his divinity; his divinity is whither we go, his humanity is the way by which we go. Unless he became for us the way by which we go, we would never arrive at him who abides.  
The discourse of God: then one grasps it, when one is grasped.

**1.** Our Lord, even in the form of a slave, was not a slave, but even in the form of a slave was the Lord (for that form of flesh was servile, but, although it was *the likeness of the flesh of sin*[^3], it was not the flesh of sin); he promised freedom to those who believed in him. But the Jews, as though priding themselves on their own freedom, disdained to become free, though they were slaves of sin. They said they were free for this reason, that they were the seed of Abraham. What, then, the Lord answered them to this, as today's reading was being recited, we have heard.

*I know,* he says, *that you are the children of Abraham: but you seek to kill me, because my word finds no place in you*[^4]. I recognize you, he says: you are the children of Abraham, but you seek to kill me. I recognize the origin of the flesh, not the faith of the heart. You are the children of Abraham, but according to the flesh. Therefore, he says, you seek to kill me: for my word finds no place in you. If my word were grasped, it would grasp; if you were grasped, you would be enclosed within the nets of faith like fish. What is it, then, *it finds no place in you*? It does not find a place in your heart, because it is not received by your heart. For the word of God is, and ought to be to the faithful, like a hook to a fish: it grasps then, when it is grasped. Nor is any injury done to those who are caught: for they are caught unto salvation, not unto destruction. Hence the Lord says to his disciples: *Come after me, and I will make you fishers of men*[^5]. So these men were not such: and yet they were the children of Abraham; children of a man of God, themselves wicked men. For they drew the kind of the flesh, but they had become degenerate, by not imitating the faith of him whose children they were.

**2.** You have surely heard the Lord saying: *I know that you are the children of Abraham*; hear what he says afterward: *I speak what I have seen with my Father; and you do what you have seen with your father*[^6]. He had already said: I know that you are the children of Abraham. But what do they do? What he said to them: *You seek to kill me.* This they never saw with Abraham. But the Lord wishes God the Father to be understood when he says: *What I have seen with my Father, I speak.* I have seen truth, I speak truth, because I am Truth. For if the Lord speaks truth which he has seen with the Father, he sees himself, he speaks himself: because he himself is the Truth of the Father, which he has seen with the Father; for he himself is the Word, the Word that was with God. These men, then, the evil that they do, which the Lord rebukes and reproves—where did they see it? With their father. When we have heard, in what follows, more plainly said who their father is, then we shall understand what things they have seen with such a father: for as yet he does not name their father. A little above he mentioned Abraham, but by origin of the flesh, not by likeness of life: he is going to name another father of theirs, who neither begot them nor created them that they might be men; but yet they were his children insofar as they were evil, not insofar as they were men; in that they imitated, not in that they were created.

**3.** They answered and said to him: *Our father is Abraham*[^7]: as though to say: What are you going to say against Abraham? Or: If you can do anything, dare to reprove Abraham. Not that the Lord did not dare to reprove Abraham; but Abraham was such that he would not be reproved by the Lord, but rather praised: yet these men seemed to provoke him, that he might say something evil about Abraham, and there might be an occasion for doing what they were thinking. *Our father is Abraham.*

**4.** Let us hear how the Lord answered them, praising Abraham to their condemnation: *Jesus says to them: If you are the children of Abraham, do the works of Abraham. But now you seek to kill me, a man who has spoken to you the truth, which I heard from God: this Abraham did not do*[^8]. Behold, that one praised, these condemned. Abraham was not a murderer. I do not say, he says: I am the Lord of Abraham; though if I were to say it, I would say truly. For he said in another place: *Before Abraham, I am*[^9]: then they wished to stone him: he did not say this. For the present, what you see, what you look upon, what alone you think me to be, I am a man: a man who tells you what he heard from God—why do you wish to kill him, except because you are not the children of Abraham? And yet above he says: I know that you are the children of Abraham. He does not deny their origin, but condemns their deeds: their flesh was from him, but their life was not.

**5.** But we, beloved, do we at all come from the lineage of Abraham, or was Abraham in any way our father according to the flesh? The flesh of the Jews draws its origin from his flesh, not the flesh of Christians: we come from other nations; and yet, by imitating, we have been made children of Abraham. Hear the Apostle: *To Abraham the promises were made, and to his seed. He does not say: And to his seeds, as of many; but as of one: And to your seed, which is Christ. But if you are Christ's, then you are the seed of Abraham, heirs according to the promise*[^10]. We, then, have been made the seed of Abraham by the grace of God. God did not make us co-heirs from the flesh of Abraham. He disinherited those, he adopted these: and from that olive tree, whose root is in the Patriarchs, he cut off the proud natural branches, and grafted in the lowly wild olive[^11].

Therefore, when the Jews came to John to be baptized, he burst out against them and said to them: *Brood of vipers.* For they boasted especially of the loftiness of their origin: but he called them a brood of vipers; not even of men, but of vipers. He saw the form of men, but recognized the poison. Yet they had come to be changed, since indeed they were to be baptized: and he said to them: *Brood of vipers, who showed you to flee from the wrath to come? Bring forth, then, fruits worthy of repentance. And do not say within yourselves: We have Abraham for our father: for God is able from these stones to raise up children to Abraham*[^12]. If you do not bring forth fruits worthy of repentance, do not flatter yourselves about that stock: God is able both to condemn you and not to defraud Abraham of children. For he has whence to raise up children of Abraham; they will become children who have imitated the faith: *God is able from these stones to raise up children to Abraham.* We are they: in our forebears we were stones, when in place of God we worshiped stones; from such stones God made a family for Abraham.

## An empty and vain boasting.

**6.** Why, then, does empty and vain boasting exalt itself? Let the children of Abraham now cease to boast: they have heard what they ought to hear: If you are the children of Abraham, prove it by deeds, not by words. You seek to kill me, a man: for the present I do not say the Son of God, I do not say God, I do not say the Word, because the Word does not die; I say what you see, because you can both kill what you see, and offend whom you do not see. This, then, Abraham did not do. *You do the works of your father*[^13]. And still he does not say who this father of theirs is.

**7.** Now what did they answer. For they began somehow to recognize that the Lord was speaking not of the generation of the flesh, but of the conduct of life. And because it is the custom of the Scriptures, which they read, to call it fornication spiritually when the soul is subjected, like a prostitute, to many and false gods, to this they answered: *So they said to him: We were not born of fornication; we have one father, God*[^14]. Now Abraham had become cheap to them. For they had been repelled, as they ought to have been repelled by a truthful mouth; because Abraham was such that his deeds they did not imitate, yet of his lineage they boasted. And they changed their answer; I believe, saying within themselves: As often as we name Abraham, he will say to us: Why do you not imitate him of whose lineage you boast? We cannot imitate so great a man, holy, just, innocent: let us say God is our father, let us see what he will say to us.

**8.** Did falsehood altogether find what to say, and would truth not find what to answer? Let us hear what they say, let us hear what they hear: *We have one father,* they say, *God. Jesus therefore said to them: If God were your father, you would surely love me: for I came forth from God and have come. For I did not come of myself, but he sent me*[^15]. You say God is your father; recognize me at least as your brother. Nevertheless, he lifted up the heart of those who understand, and touched on that which he is wont to say: I did not come of myself, he sent me, I came forth from God and have come. Remember what we are wont to say: He came from him; and from whom he came, with him he came. The mission of Christ, then, is the incarnation. But that the Word came forth from God is an eternal procession: it does not have time, the one through whom time was made. Let no one say in his heart: Before the Word was, how was God? Never say: Before the Word of God was. Never was God without the Word; because the Word is abiding, not passing; God, not a sound; through whom heaven and earth were made, not something that has gone by together with the things that were made upon the earth.

Therefore he came forth from him as God, as equal, as the only Son, as the Word of the Father: and he came to us, because *the Word was made flesh, that he might dwell among us*[^16]. His coming is his humanity; his abiding is his divinity; his divinity is whither we go, his humanity is the way by which we go. Unless he became for us the way by which we go, we would never arrive at him who abides.

**9.** *Why,* he says, *do you not recognize my speech? Because you cannot hear my word*[^17]. For this reason they could not recognize it, because they could not hear. But whence could they not hear, except because they were unwilling to be corrected by believing? And this, whence? *You are from your father the devil*[^18]. How long do you keep mentioning a father? How long do you keep changing fathers, now Abraham, now God? Hear from the Son of God whose children you are: *You are from your father the devil.*

## Here the heresy of the Manichees is to be guarded against.

**10.** Here now the heresy of the Manichees is to be guarded against, which says that there is a certain nature of evil and a certain race of darkness with its princes, which dared to fight against God; and that God, lest the adverse race subdue his kingdom, sent against it, as it were his own inward parts, princes from his own light; and that this race was subdued, whence the devil draws his origin. From this, they say, our flesh draws its origin; and according to this they think it was said by the Lord, *You are from your father the devil*, because those men were, as it were, by nature evil, drawing their origin from the contrary race of darkness. So they err, so they are blinded, so they make themselves a race of darkness, by believing what is false against him by whom they were created. For every nature is good; but the nature of man has been corrupted by an evil will.

What God made cannot be evil, if man himself is not evil to himself: but plainly the Creator is the Creator; the creature is the creature; the creature cannot be made equal to the Creator. Distinguish him who made from that which he made. The bench cannot be made equal to the carpenter, the column cannot be made equal to the builder; and yet the carpenter, if he made the bench, did not himself create the wood. But our Lord God, because he is almighty, also made by his Word what he made: all the things he made, he did not have material from which to make them, and yet he made them. For they were made because he willed, they were made because he spoke: but the things made cannot be compared to the maker. You ask what you may compare: recognize the only Son.

How, then, are the Jews children of the devil? By imitating, not by being born. Hear the custom of holy Scripture. The prophet says to the Jews themselves: *Your father is the Amorite, and your mother a Hittite*[^19]. The Amorites were a certain race, from which the Jews did not draw their origin: the Hittites too had their own race, wholly foreign to the lineage of the Jews. But because the Amorites and the Hittites were impious, and the Jews imitated their impieties, they found for themselves parents—not those from whom they were born, but those whose ways, by following, they would be equally condemned. But perhaps you ask, whence the devil himself? From there, surely, whence the other angels too. But the other angels stood firm in their obedience: he, by disobeying and being proud, fell as an angel, and became the devil.

**11.** But now hear what the Lord says: *You,* he says, *are from your father the devil, and you wish to do the desires of your father*[^20]. Behold whence you are his children: because you desire such things, not because you were born of him. What are his desires? *He was a murderer from the beginning.* Behold what it is to wish to do the desires of your father: *You seek to kill me, a man who tells you the truth.*

And he envied man, and killed man. For the devil, when he envied man, clothed in the serpent spoke to the woman, and through the woman poisoned the man as well. They died by hearing the devil[^21], whom they would not have heard, had they been willing to hear the Lord: for man, placed between him who created and him who fell, ought to have obeyed the Creator, not the deceiver. So *he was a murderer from the beginning*[^22].

See the kind of murder, brothers. The devil is called a murderer, not armed with a sword, not girt with iron; he came to man, sowed an evil word, and killed. Do not, then, think you are not a murderer when you persuade your brother to evil: if you persuade your brother to evil, you kill. And that you may know that you kill, hear the Psalm: *The sons of men, their teeth are weapons and arrows, and their tongue a sharp sword*[^23]. You, then, wish to do the desires of your father: therefore you rage against the flesh, because you cannot rage against the mind.

He was a murderer from the beginning: surely in the first man. He was a murderer from that point at which murder could be done: murder could be done from that point at which man was made. For man could not be killed unless he first became man. So he was a murderer from the beginning. And whence a murderer? *And he did not stand in the truth.* Therefore he was in the truth, but by not standing he fell. And why did he not stand in the truth? *Because the truth is not in him.* Not as the truth is in Christ, so that Christ himself is the truth. If, then, this one had stood in the truth, he would have stood in Christ; but he did not stand in the truth, because the truth is not in him.

**12.** *When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own; because he is a liar, and the father of it*[^24]. What is this? You have heard the words of the Gospel, you received them attentively: behold, I repeat them, that you may recognize what you require. The Lord was saying of the devil what ought to be said of the devil by the Lord. *He was a murderer from the beginning*—it is true; for he killed the first man: *and he did not stand in the truth*; because he fell from the truth. *When he speaks a lie*—surely the devil himself—*he speaks from his own; because he is a liar, and the father of it.*

In these words some have thought the devil to have a father, and have asked who the devil's father was. Here indeed the detestable error of the Manichees found yet another way to deceive the unskilled. For they are wont to say: Suppose the devil was an angel, and fell; from him sin began, as you say: who was his father? We, on the contrary: For who of us ever said that the devil has a father? And they, in turn: The Lord says it; the Gospel says it, speaking of the devil, it says: *He was a murderer from the beginning, and he did not stand in the truth; because the truth is not in him: when he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own; because he is a liar, and the father of it*[^25].

## Embrace the truth, that you may receive freedom.

**13.** Hear, understand; I do not send you far away, understand within the very words. The Lord called the devil the father of the lie. What is this? Hear what it is, run over the very words again, and understand. For not everyone who lies is the father of his own lie. For if you received a lie from another, and spoke it, you indeed have lied by uttering the lie: but you are not the father of that lie, because you received the lie from another. But the devil was a liar from himself; he himself begot his own lie, he heard it from no one. As God the Father begot the Son, the truth; so the fallen devil begot, as it were as a son, the lie.

Having heard these things, now run over and recall the Lord's words: O Catholic mind, mark what you have heard, attend to what he says. *He*—who? The devil—*was a murderer from the beginning.*

We acknowledge it, he killed Adam. *And he did not stand in the truth.* We acknowledge it, because he fell from the truth. *Because the truth is not in him.* It is true; by withdrawing from the truth he does not have the truth. *When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own.* He does not receive from elsewhere what he speaks. *When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own; because he is a liar, and the father of it.* Both he is a liar, and he is the father of the lie. For you perhaps are a liar, because you speak a lie: but you are not its father. For if what you say you received from the devil, and believed the devil, you are a liar, you are not the father of the lie: but he, because he did not receive the lie from elsewhere, by which lie, as by poison, the serpent killed man, is the father of the lie; just as God is the Father of the truth. Withdraw from the father of the lie, run to the Father of the truth; embrace the truth, that you may receive freedom.

**14.** Those Jews, then, saw with their father what they were speaking: what, except the lie? But the Lord saw with his Father what he was speaking: what, except himself? what, except the Word of the Father? what, except the eternal truth of the Father, coeternal with the Father? He, then, *was a murderer from the beginning, and he did not stand in the truth, because the truth is not in him: when he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own; because he is a liar.* And not only is he a liar, but he is also its father: that is, of the lie itself which he speaks, he is the father, because he himself begot his own lie. *But I, because I tell the truth, you do not believe me. Which of you convicts me of sin?*[^26]—as I convict both you and your father? *If I tell the truth, why do you not believe me?* Except because you are children of the devil?

**15.** *He who is of God hears the words of God: therefore you do not hear, because you are not of God*[^27]. Again, do not attend to nature, but to vice. So these men are of God, and are not of God: by nature of God, by vice not of God. I beseech you, attend; in the Gospel you have whereby you may be healed against the poisonous and abominable errors of the heretics. For of these words too the Manichees are wont to say: Behold, there are two natures, one good and the other evil; the Lord says it: what does the Lord say? *Therefore you do not hear, because you are not of God.* The Lord says this. What then, he says, do you say to this?

Hear what I will say. They are both of God and not of God; by nature they are of God, by vice they are not of God: for the good nature, which is of God, sinned by will, by believing what the devil persuaded, and was corrupted; therefore it seeks a physician, because it is not whole. Behold what I say.

But it seems impossible to you that they should be of God, and not of God: hear that it is not impossible. They are of God, and are not of God, in the same way that they both are children of Abraham, and are not children of Abraham. Here you have it; there is nothing you can say. Hear the Lord himself; he himself said to them: *I know that you are the children of Abraham.* Would the Lord lie? Far be it. Therefore what the Lord said is true? It is true. It is true, then, that they were children of Abraham. It is true. Hear him denying it. He who said: *You are the children of Abraham*, himself denied them to be children of Abraham: *If you are the children of Abraham, do the deeds of Abraham. But now you seek to kill me, a man who tells you the truth, which I heard from God: this Abraham did not do. You do the works of your father*—that is, of the devil.

How, then, were they both children of Abraham, and not children of Abraham? He shows both in them: they were children of Abraham, because of the origin of the flesh; and they were not children of Abraham, because of the vice of diabolical persuasion. So attend likewise to our Lord and God: they were both of him, and not of him. How were they of him? Because he himself created the man from whom they were born. How were they of him? Because he himself is the founder of nature, he himself is the creator of flesh and soul. How, then, were they not of him? Because they had made themselves vicious by themselves. They were not of him, because by imitating the devil they had become children of the devil.

**16.** The Lord God, then, came to sinful man. You have heard two names, both man and sinner. That he is a man is of God: that he is a sinner is not of God. Let the vice be separated from the nature: let the nature be recognized, that the Creator may be praised; let the vice be recognized, on account of which the physician may be called upon. So when the Lord says: *He who is of God hears the words of God: therefore you do not hear, because you are not of God*, he did not distinguish the merits of natures, nor did he find, apart from their own soul and flesh, some nature in men that was not corrupted by sin: but because he had foreknown who were going to believe, them he called of God, because by the adoption of regeneration they would be reborn of God. To these belongs: *He who is of God hears the words of God.* But what follows: *Therefore you do not hear, because you are not of God*; was said to those who were not only corrupt with sin (for this evil was common to all), but were also foreknown not to be going to believe with that faith by which alone they could be set free from the bond of sins. Therefore he foreknew that those to whom he was saying such things would remain in that whereby they were of the devil, that is, would die in their sins and impiety, in which they were like him; and would not come to the regeneration in which they would be children of God, that is, born of God, by whom they had been created men. According to this predestination the Lord spoke: not that he found any of men who, either according to regeneration, was already of God, or, according to nature, was already not of God.
