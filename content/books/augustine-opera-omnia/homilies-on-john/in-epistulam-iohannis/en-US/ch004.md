# Homily 3: Children, it is the last hour

## (1 John 2:18-27).

> *Nourished by Christ by cleaving to his body with fervent love and by casting away the promises of the world. Christ is the inner teacher and the spiritual anointing.*

## Our milk is Christ humble; our bread is Christ equal to the Father.

**1.** *Children, it is the last hour*[^1]*.* In this reading he addresses children; that they may hasten to grow, because it is the last hour. The age of the body is not within our will. So no one grows according to the flesh when he wishes; just as no one is born when he wishes: but where birth is within our will, growth too is within our will. No one is born of water and the Spirit except willingly. Therefore, if he wills, he grows; if he wills, he diminishes. What is to grow? To advance. What is to diminish? To fall back.

Whoever knows that he has been born, let him hear that he is a child and an infant; let him eagerly seek the breasts of his mother, and he grows quickly. Now the mother is the Church; and her two breasts are the two Testaments of the divine Scriptures.

From here let the milk of all the sacraments be sucked, sacraments wrought temporally for our eternal salvation, so that, nourished and strengthened, one may come to the eating of solid food, which is: *In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God*[^2]. Our milk is Christ humble; our food is that same Christ equal to the Father. He nourishes you with milk, that he may feed you with bread: for to touch Jesus spiritually with the heart is to know that he is equal to the Father.

## He touches Christ from a clean heart who understands him to be coequal with the Father.

**2.** For this reason he also forbade Mary to touch him, and said to her: *Do not touch me; for I have not yet ascended to the Father*[^3]*.* What is this? He offered himself to the disciples to be touched, and avoided the touch of Mary? Is he not the same who said to the doubting disciple: *Put in your fingers, and touch the scars*[^4]? Had he then already ascended to the Father?

Why, then, does he forbid Mary, and say: *Do not touch me; for I have not yet ascended to the Father*? Or are we to say this, that he did not fear to be touched by men, and feared to be touched by women? His touch cleanses all flesh. Did he fear to be handled by those to whom he first willed to be made manifest?

Was not his resurrection announced to men through women, that by the opposite art the serpent might be conquered? For because he announced death to the first man through a woman, life too was announced to men through a woman. Why, then, did he not wish to be touched, except because he willed that touch to be understood as spiritual?

The spiritual touch is from a clean heart. He touches Christ from a clean heart who understands him to be coequal with the Father. But he who does not yet understand the divinity of Christ comes as far as the flesh, he does not come as far as the divinity. But what great thing is it to reach as far as the persecutors who crucified him reached? This is the great thing, to understand the Word, God with God in the beginning, through whom all things were made: such as he wished himself to be known, when he said to Philip: *So long a time I am with you, and you have not known me, Philip? He who sees me sees also the Father*[^5].

## Let us not be slothful in advancing, because it is the last hour.

**3.** But lest anyone be slothful in advancing, let him hear: *Children, it is the last hour.* Advance, run, grow, it is the last hour. This last hour is long; yet it is the last. For he put hour for the last time; because in the last times our Lord Jesus Christ will come. But some will say: How is it the last time? how is it the last hour? surely the Antichrist will come first, and then the day of judgment will come. John saw these thoughts: lest, as it were, they become secure, and therefore think it is not the last hour, because the Antichrist was going to come, he said to them: *And as you have heard that the Antichrist is coming, now many antichrists have arisen*[^6]*.* Could it have many antichrists, except in the last hour?

## The concord of the members has no antichrist.

**4.** Whom did he call antichrists? He goes on, and explains: *Whence we know that it is the last hour.* Whence? Because *many antichrists have arisen. They went out from us*[^7]: see the antichrists. *They went out from us*: therefore we mourn the loss. Hear the consolation: *But they were not of us.* All heretics, all schismatics went out from us, that is, they go out from the Church; but they would not have gone out, if they had been of us. Before they went out, then, they were not of us. If, before they went out, they were not of us, many are within, who have not gone out, but are nevertheless antichrists. We dare to say this: why, except that each one, while he is within, may not be an antichrist? For he is going to describe and designate the antichrists; and we shall see them now.

And each one ought to question his own conscience, whether he is an antichrist. For in Latin "Antichrist" is one contrary to Christ. Not as some understand the Antichrist to be so called because he is going to come before Christ, that is, that after him Christ will come: it is not so said, nor so written; but Antichrist, that is, contrary to Christ. Now who is contrary to Christ, you perceive at this point, as he himself explains, and you understand that none can go out except antichrists; but that those who are not contrary to Christ can in no way go out. For he who is not contrary to Christ cleaves in his body, and is reckoned a member.

Members are never contrary to one another. The wholeness of the body consists of all its members. And what does the Apostle say about the concord of the members? *If one member suffers, all the members suffer with it; and if one member is glorified, all the members rejoice with it*[^8]. If, then, in the glorification of a member the other members rejoice with it, and in suffering all the members suffer; the concord of the members has no antichrist.

And there are those who are within, in the body of our Lord Jesus Christ in such a way—since his body is still being healed, and there will be no perfect health except in the resurrection of the dead—they are in the body of Christ in the way that bad humors are. When they are vomited out, then the body is relieved: so too the wicked, when they go out, then the Church is relieved. And the body says, when it vomits them out and casts them off: These humors went out from me, but they were not of me. What is, they were not of me? They were not cut off from my flesh, but they were pressing on my breast while they were within.

## Temptation proves who are antichrists.

**5.** *They went out from us; but,* do not be sad, *they were not of us.* Whence do you prove it? *For if they had been of us, they would surely have remained with us*[^9]*.* From this, then, let your Charity see that many who are not of us receive the sacraments with us, receive Baptism with us, receive with us what the faithful know they receive, the Blessing, the Eucharist, and whatever there is in the holy sacraments; they receive with us the communion of the altar itself, and they are not of us. Temptation proves that they are not of us. When temptation comes to them, as on the occasion of a wind, they fly out; because they were not grains. But all then will fly, which must often be said, when the Lord's threshing-floor begins to be winnowed on the day of judgment. *They went out from us, but they were not of us: for if they had been of us, they would surely have remained with us.*

For you wish to know, beloved, how most certainly this is said, so that those who perhaps went out and return may not be antichrists, may not be contrary to Christ? Those who are not antichrists, it cannot be that they remain outside. But by his own will each one is either an antichrist, or is in Christ. Either we are among the members, or among the bad humors.

He who changes himself for the better is a member in the body: but he who remains in malice is a bad humor; and when he goes out, those who were being pressed will be relieved. *They went out from us, but they were not of us: for if they had been of us, they would surely have remained with us: but that they might be made manifest, that not all are of us*[^10]*.* Therefore he added, *that they might be made manifest,* because even when they are within, they are not of us; yet they are not manifest, but by going out they are made manifest.

*And you have an anointing from the Holy One, that you yourselves may be manifest to yourselves*[^11]*.* The spiritual anointing is the Holy Spirit himself, whose sacrament is in the visible anointing. He says that all who have this anointing of Christ recognize the wicked and the good; and that there is no need that they be taught, because the anointing itself teaches them.

## He who does not stumble at the humble will not dread the exalted.

**6.** *I write to you, not because you do not know the truth; but because you know it, and because no lie is from the truth*[^12]*.* Behold, we have been admonished how we may recognize the Antichrist. What is Christ? Truth. He himself said: *I am the truth*[^13]. But *no lie is from the truth*: therefore all who lie are not yet of Christ. He did not say that one lie is from the truth, and another lie not from the truth. Attend to the sentence; do not flatter yourselves, do not fawn upon yourselves, do not deceive yourselves, do not mock yourselves: *No lie is from the truth.* Let us see, then, how the antichrists lie, since there is not one kind of lie.

*Who is a liar, but he who denies that Jesus is the Christ*?[^14] "Jesus" has one meaning, "Christ" has another: although our Savior is one Jesus Christ, yet "Jesus" is his proper name. As Moses was called by his proper name, as Elijah, as Abraham; so our Lord Jesus has, as it were, a proper name: but "Christ" is the name of a sacrament. As if one should say prophet, as if one should say priest; so "Christ" commends one anointed, in whom there should be the redemption of the whole people of Israel.

This Christ was hoped to come by the people of the Jews; and because he came humble, he was not recognized; because the stone was small, they stumbled against him, and were broken. But the stone grew, and became a great mountain[^15];

and what does the Scripture say? *Whoever stumbles against this stone shall be shattered; and on whomever this stone shall fall, it will crush him*[^16]. The words must be distinguished: he said the one who stumbles is shattered; but the one on whom it falls is crushed.

First, because he came humble, men stumbled against him: because he is going to come exalted to judgment, the one on whom he falls, he will crush. But he who is to come will not crush the one whom he did not shatter when he came. He who does not stumble at the humble will not dread the exalted. You have heard it briefly, brethren: he who does not stumble at the humble will not dread the exalted. For to all the wicked Christ is a stone of stumbling; whatever Christ says is bitter to them.

## The Donatists do not hold the inheritance, because they are not in communion with the whole world.

**7.** For listen, and see. All, surely, who go out of the Church, and are cut off from the unity of the Church, are antichrists; let no one doubt it; for he himself designated it: *They went out from us, but they were not of us: for if they had been of us, they would surely have remained with us.* Whoever, then, do not remain with us, but go out from us, it is manifest that they are antichrists. And how are they proved antichrists? By the lie. And *who is a liar, but he who denies that Jesus is the Christ?* Let us question the heretics: which heretic do you find who denies that Jesus is the Christ? Let your Charity see a great sacrament. Attend to what the Lord God has inspired in us, and what I wish to suggest to you.

Behold, they went out from us, and became Donatists: we ask them whether Jesus is the Christ: at once they confess that Jesus is the Christ. If, then, he is the antichrist who denies that Jesus is the Christ, neither can they call us antichrists, nor we them; because both we and they confess it. If, then, neither do they call us so, nor we them; then neither did they go out from us, nor we from them. If, then, we did not go out from them, we are in unity: if we are in unity, what are two altars doing in this city? what are divided houses, divided marriages, doing? what is a common bed, and a divided Christ, doing? He admonishes us, he wishes us to confess what is true. Either they went out from us, or we from them. But God forbid that we went out from them: for we have the testament of the Lord's inheritance, we read it aloud, and there we find ourselves: *I will give you the nations as your inheritance, and the ends of the earth as your possession*[^17].

We hold the inheritance of Christ: they do not hold it; they are not in communion with the whole world, they are not in communion with the universal body redeemed by the blood of the Lord. We have the Lord himself rising from the dead, who offered himself to be touched by the hands of his doubting disciples. And while they still doubted, he said to them: *Christ had to suffer, and to rise again on the third day, and repentance and the remission of sins to be preached in his name.* Where? in what place? to whom? *Among all nations, beginning from Jerusalem*[^18]. We are secure concerning the unity of the inheritance. Whoever is not in communion with this inheritance has gone out.

## Antichrists profess Christ with the mouth and dissent from Christ in their conduct.

**8.** But let us not be saddened: *They went out from us, but they were not of us: for if they had been of us, they would surely have remained with us.* If, then, they went out from us, they are antichrists; if they are antichrists, they are liars; if they are liars, they deny that Jesus is the Christ. Again we return to the difficulty of the question. Question them one by one, they confess that Jesus is the Christ. A narrow understanding constrains us in this Epistle. You surely see the question; this question troubles both us and them, if it is not understood. Either we are antichrists, or they are antichrists: they call us antichrists, and say that we went out from them; we likewise say it of them: but this Epistle designated the antichrists. Whoever denies that Jesus is the Christ, he is the antichrist. Now, then, let us seek who denies it; and let us not attend to the tongue, but to the deeds.

For if all are questioned, all with one mouth confess that Jesus is the Christ. Let the tongue be silent a little, question the life.

If we find this, if the Scripture itself tells us that denial is made not only with the tongue, but also with deeds; surely we find many antichrists who profess Christ with the mouth, and in their conduct dissent from Christ. Where do we find this in Scripture? Hear the apostle Paul: when he was speaking of such men, he said: *For they confess that they know God, but by their deeds they deny him*[^19].

We find these too to be antichrists: whoever by his deeds denies Christ is an antichrist. I do not hear what he sounds forth, but I see how he lives. The works speak, and do we ask for words? For what wicked man does not wish to speak well? But what does the Lord say to such men? *Hypocrites, how can you speak good things, when you are evil*?[^20] You bring your voices to my ears; I look into your thoughts: I see there an evil will, and you show false fruits. I know whence to gather what; I do not gather figs from thistles, I do not gather grapes from thorns. For each tree is known by its fruit[^21]. More of a liar is the antichrist who professes with the mouth that Jesus is the Christ, and by his deeds denies it. He is a liar for this reason, because he speaks one thing and does another.

## Men perverse with a crooked will accuse the maker.

**9.** Now then, brethren, if deeds are to be questioned, not only do we find that many antichrists have gone out; but many not yet manifest, who have by no means gone out. For however many perjurers the Church has, defrauders, sorcerers, consulters of soothsayers, adulterers, drunkards, usurers, slave-dealers, and all that we cannot enumerate; they are contrary to the doctrine of Christ, contrary to the word of God:

but the word of God is Christ: whatever is contrary to the Word of God is in the Antichrist. For the Antichrist is contrary to Christ. And do you wish to know how openly these men resist Christ? Sometimes it happens that they do something evil, and begin to be rebuked; because they do not dare to blaspheme Christ, they blaspheme his ministers, by whom they are rebuked: but if you show them that you speak Christ's words, not your own; they try as much as they can to convict you of speaking your own words, not the words of Christ:

but if it has become manifest that you speak the words of Christ, they go even against Christ, they begin to reproach Christ: How, they say, and why did he make us such? Do not men convicted of their deeds say this daily? Men perverse with a crooked will accuse the maker. The maker cries out to them from heaven (for he himself made us, who remade us): What did I make you? I made a man, not avarice; I made a man, not robbery; I made a man, not adultery. You have heard that my works praise me.

From the mouth of the three boys it was the very hymn that defended them from the fires[^22]. The works of the Lord praise the Lord; heaven praises, earth, sea; all things that are in heaven praise; the angels praise, the stars praise, the lights praise; whatever swims, whatever flies, whatever walks, whatever creeps, all these praise the Lord.

Have you heard that avarice praises the Lord? have you heard that drunkenness praises the Lord; that luxury praises him, that frivolity praises him? Whatever you do not hear there giving praise to the Lord, the Lord did not make.

Correct what you have made, that what God made in you may be saved. But if you will not, and you love and embrace your sins; you are contrary to Christ. Whether you are within, whether you are without, you are an antichrist: whether you are within, whether you are without, you are chaff. But why are you not without? Because you have not found the occasion of a wind.

## Let each one, if he is a lover of the world, be changed: let him become a lover of Christ.

**10.** Now these things are manifest, brethren. Lest anyone say: I do not worship Christ, but I worship God his Father. *Everyone who denies the Son has neither the Son nor the Father: and he who confesses the Son has both the Son and the Father*[^23]*.* He addresses you grains; and let those who were chaff hear, and become grains.

Let each one, considering his own conscience, if he is a lover of the world, be changed; let him become a lover of Christ, lest he be an antichrist. If anyone tells him that he is an antichrist, he is angry, he thinks an injury has been done him;

perhaps he threatens a charge in writing, if he hears from one quarreling with him that he is an antichrist.

Christ says to him: Be patient; if you have heard what is false, rejoice with me, because I too hear false things from antichrists: but if you have heard what is true, confront your conscience; and if you fear to hear it, fear still more to be it.

## If you were failing amid your labors, take heart at the promised reward.

**11.** *Therefore let what you have heard from the beginning abide in you. But if what you have heard from the beginning abides in you, you too will abide in the Son and in the Father. This is the promise which he himself has promised us*[^24]*.* For perhaps you might seek a reward, and say: Behold, I keep in me what I heard from the beginning, I obey it; perils, labors, temptations I endure for the sake of this perseverance:

with what fruit? with what reward? What will he give me afterward, since in this world I see myself laboring amid temptations? I see that there is no rest here; mortality itself weighs down the soul, and the body that is corrupted presses it toward lower things: but I endure all things, that what I heard from the beginning may abide in me; and I shall say to my God: *For the words of your lips I have kept hard ways*[^25]. For what reward, then?

Listen, and do not fail. If you were failing amid your labors, take heart at the promised reward. Who is there that works in a vineyard, and lets slip from his heart what he is going to receive? Make him forget his reward, and his hands fail. The memory of the promised reward makes him persevere in the work: and a man promised it who can deceive you.

How much stronger ought you to be in the field of God, when Truth has promised, who can neither be supplanted, nor die, nor deceive him to whom the promise has been made? And what is promised? Let us see what he promised. Is it gold, which men here love much, or silver? Or possessions, on which men pour out gold, although they love gold much? Or pleasant estates, spacious houses, many slaves, numerous animals?

This is not the sort of reward to which he exhorts us, that we may hold out in labor. What is this reward called? Eternal life. You have heard, and rejoicing you have cried out: love what you have heard, and you will be freed from your labors into the rest of eternal life. Behold what God promises, eternal life. Behold what God threatens, eternal fire. What to those set on his right hand? *Come, blessed of my Father, receive the kingdom which has been prepared for you from the foundation of the world.* To those on the left, what? *Go into eternal fire, which has been prepared for the devil and his angels*[^26]. If you do not yet love that, at least fear this.

## Shudder at what the Almighty threatens, love what he promises, and the whole world grows worthless.

**12.** Remember, then, my brethren, that Christ promised us eternal life: *This is,* he says, *the promise which he himself has promised us, eternal life. These things I have written to you concerning those who seduce you*[^27]*.* Let no one seduce you to death; desire the promise of eternal life. What can the world promise? Whatever it pleases let it promise, perhaps it promises to one who will die tomorrow. And with what face will you go forth to him who abides forever? But a powerful man threatens me, that I may do something evil. What does he threaten? Prisons, chains, fires, torments, beasts: surely not eternal fire? Shudder at what the Almighty threatens, love what the Almighty promises; and the whole world grows worthless, whether promising or terrifying.

*These things I have written to you concerning those who seduce you; that you may know that you have an anointing, and that the anointing which we have received from him may abide in us*[^28]*.* The sacrament of the anointing is the invisible power itself, the invisible anointing, the Holy Spirit;

the invisible anointing is that love which, in whomever it is, will be to him like a root, and, though the sun burns, it cannot wither. Everything that is rooted is nourished by the heat of the sun, it does not wither.

## Let the anointing of Christ be in your heart, lest your heart be in a wilderness, thirsting.

**13.** *And you have no need that anyone teach you, because his anointing teaches you about all things*[^29]*.* What, then, are we doing, brethren, in teaching you? If his anointing teaches you about all things, it seems we labor without cause. And why do we cry out so much? Let us leave you to his anointing, and let his anointing teach you.

But now I raise a question for myself, and I raise it to the Apostle himself: let him deign to hear a little one questioning him; I say to John himself: Did those to whom you were speaking have the anointing? You said: *Because his anointing teaches you about all things.* Why did you make such an Epistle? What were you teaching them? what were you instructing? what were you building up?

Now here see a great sacrament, brethren: the sound of our words strikes the ears, the master is within. Do not think that anyone learns anything from a man. We can admonish by the noise of our voice; if there is not one within who teaches, our noise is in vain. So, brethren, do you wish to know it? Did you not all hear this sermon? How many are going to leave here untaught? As far as I am concerned, I have spoken to all; but those to whom that anointing does not speak within, whom the Holy Spirit does not teach within, return untaught.

The teachings from without are certain helps and admonitions. He who teaches hearts has his chair in heaven. Therefore he himself says in the Gospel: *Do not call anyone teacher on earth: one is your teacher, Christ*[^30]. Let him, then, speak to you within, when no man is there; for even if someone is at your side, no one is in your heart. And let there not be no one in your heart:

let Christ be in your heart; let his anointing be in your heart, lest your heart be in a wilderness, thirsting, and not having springs by which it may be watered. The inner master, then, is the one who teaches, Christ teaches, his inspiration teaches. Where his inspiration and his anointing are not, the words make noise outside in vain.

These words are, brethren, which we speak from without, as the farmer is to the tree: he works from without, applies water and the diligence of cultivation: whatever he applies from without, does he form the fruits? does he clothe the bareness of the wood with the shade of leaves? does he do any such thing within? But who does this? hear the farmer the Apostle, and see what we are, and hear the inner master: *I planted, Apollos watered; but God gave the increase: neither is he who plants anything, nor he who waters; but God who gives the increase*[^31]. This, then, we say to you: whether we plant, or water by speaking, we are not anything; but he who gives the increase, God, that is, his anointing which teaches you about all things.
