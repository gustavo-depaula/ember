# Exposition of Psalm 22

**1.** [v 1.] *A psalm of David himself.* The Church speaks to Christ: *The Lord feeds me, and nothing will be lacking to me*, the Lord Jesus Christ is my shepherd, and nothing will be lacking to me.

**2.** [v 2.] *In a place of pasture, there he has set me*: in a place of pasture, leading me on to the beginning of faith, there he has set me to be nourished. *Beside the water of refreshment he has reared me*: beside the water of baptism, by which those are refreshed who had lost their wholeness and strength, he has reared me.

**3.** [v 3.] *He has converted my soul. He has led me on the paths of righteousness, for his name's sake.* He has led me on the narrow ways, which few walk, of his righteousness; not on account of my merit, but on account of his name.

**4.** [v 4.] *For even if I walk in the midst of the shadow of death*: for even if I walk in the midst of this life, which is the shadow of death. *I will not fear evils, because you are with me*: I will not fear evils, because you dwell in my heart through faith; and now you are with me, that after the shadow of death I too may be with you. *Your rod and your staff, they have consoled me*: your discipline, as a rod for the flock of sheep, and as a staff now for the more grown-up sons and those growing from the animal life to the spiritual, they have not afflicted me, but rather have consoled me; because you are mindful of me.

**5.** [v 5.] *You have prepared a table in my sight, against those who trouble me*: but after the rod, by which, a little one and animal in the flock, I was instructed in the pastures, after that rod, when I began to be under the staff, you have prepared a table in my sight, so that I am no longer fed with milk as a little one, but, grown up, take food, strengthened against those who trouble me. *You have anointed my head with oil*: you have gladdened my mind with spiritual gladness. *And your inebriating cup, how excellent it is!* and your cup, bestowing forgetfulness of the former vain delights, how excellent it is!

**6.** [v 6.] *And your mercy will follow me all the days of my life*: that is, as long as I live in this mortal life, not yours, but mine. *That I may dwell in the house of the Lord for length of days*: but it will follow me not only here, but also that I may dwell in the house of the Lord forever.
