# Exposition of Psalm 29

**1.** [v. 1.] *To the end, a psalm of the song of the dedication of the house, of David himself*[^1]. To the end, a psalm of the joy of resurrection and of the change and renewal into the immortal state of the body—not only of the Lord, but of the whole Church as well. For in the preceding psalm the tabernacle was completed, where we dwell in the time of war; but now the house is dedicated, which will remain in everlasting peace.

**2.** [v. 2.] So then the whole Christ speaks: *I will exalt you, O Lord, for you have upheld me*[^2]: I will praise your sublimity, O Lord, for you have upheld me. *And you have not let my enemies rejoice over me*[^3]: nor have you let those who so often tried to crush me with various persecutions throughout the world rejoice over me.

**3.** [v. 3.] *O Lord my God, I cried to you, and you healed me*[^4]: O Lord my God, I cried to you, and I no longer bear a body wounded and sick with mortality.

**4.** [v. 4.] *O Lord, you have brought back my soul from the realm of the dead; you have saved me from those who go down into the pit*[^5]: you have saved me from the condition of deep blindness and from the lowest mire of corruptible flesh.

**5.** [v. 5.] *Sing to the Lord, you his holy ones*[^6]: exult. The prophet, seeing these things to come, says also: *Sing to the Lord, you his holy ones. And give thanks to the memorial of his holiness*[^7]: and give thanks to him because he has not forgotten the holiness by which he sanctified you, though this whole intervening time seemed long to your longing.

**6.** [v. 6.] *For there is wrath in his indignation*[^8]: for he avenged in you the first sin, which you paid for by death. *And life in his will*[^9]: and eternal life, to which you could return by no powers of your own, he gave because he willed it. *In the evening weeping will linger*[^10]: it began in the evening, when the light of wisdom withdrew from sinning man, when he was condemned to death; from that very evening weeping will have its delay, as long as the people of God awaits the day of the Lord amid labors and temptations. *And in the morning, rejoicing*[^11]: until the morning, in which the rejoicing of resurrection is to come, which blossomed early in the morning resurrection of the Lord.

**7.** [v. 7.] *But I said in my abundance: I shall not be moved forever*[^12]: but I, that people who was speaking from the beginning, said in my abundance, now suffering no want: *I shall not be moved forever*.

**8.** [v. 8.] *O Lord, in your will you furnished strength to my beauty*[^13]: but this abundance, O Lord, I learned to be not from myself, but from your having furnished strength to my beauty in your will: *But you turned your face away from me, and I was troubled*; in that you once turned your face away from me as I sinned, and I was troubled, the illumination of the knowledge of you withdrawing from me.

**9.** [v. 9.] *To you, O Lord, I will cry, and to my God I will make supplication*[^14]: recalling that time of my trouble and misery, and as though set within it, I hear the voice of your firstborn, my head, who was to die for me, saying: *To you, O Lord, I will cry, and to my God I will make supplication*.

**10.** [v. 10.] *What profit is there in my blood, while I go down into corruption?*[^15] What profit is there in the shedding of my blood, while I go down into corruption? *Shall the dust confess to you?*[^16] For if I do not rise again at once, and my body has been corrupted, *shall the dust confess to you*—that is, the crowd of the ungodly, whom by my resurrection I shall justify? *Or shall it proclaim your truth?*[^17] Or shall it proclaim your truth for the salvation of the rest?

**11.** [v. 11.] *The Lord has heard, and has had mercy on me; the Lord has become my helper*[^18]: nor did he give his holy one to see corruption.

**12.** [v. 12.] *You have turned my mourning into joy for me*[^19]: having attained him as the firstborn from the dead, the Church now, at the dedication of your house, says: *You have turned my mourning into joy for me. You have torn off my sackcloth, and girded me with gladness*[^20]: you have torn off the covering of my sins, the sadness of my mortality, and girded me with the first robe, with immortal gladness.

**13.** [v. 13.] *That my glory may sing to you, and I be not pierced with sorrow*[^21]: that now not my lowliness but my glory may no longer mourn but sing to you, since you have now exalted me out of lowliness; and that I be not pierced with sorrow by the conscience of sin, by the fear of death, by the fear of judgment. *O Lord my God, I will confess to you forever*[^22]: and this is my glory, O Lord my God, that I confess to you forever that nothing is mine from myself, but all good things are from you, who are God all in all.
