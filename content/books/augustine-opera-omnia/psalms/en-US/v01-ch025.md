# Exposition of Psalm 23

**1.** [v 1.] *A psalm of David himself, the first of the week.* A psalm of David himself, of the glorification and resurrection of the Lord, which took place in the morning of the first of the week, the day which is now called the Lord's day.

**2.** [v 2.] *The earth is the Lord's and its fullness, the world and all who dwell in it*: when the glorified Lord is proclaimed unto the faith of all nations, and the whole world becomes his Church. *He himself founded it upon the seas*: he himself most firmly established it upon all the waves of this world, that they might be overcome by it, and not harm it. *And upon the rivers he prepared it*: rivers flow into the sea, and covetous men slip into the world; these too the Church overcomes, which, having conquered worldly desires through the grace of God, is by love prepared to receive immortality.

**3.** [v 3.] *Who will ascend the mountain of the Lord?* who will ascend to the most lofty righteousness of the Lord? *Or who will stand in his holy place?* or who will remain in that to which he ascends, the place founded upon the seas and prepared upon the rivers?

**4.** [v 4.] *The innocent of hands and clean of heart*: who, then, will ascend there, and remain there, but the innocent in works, and clean in thoughts? *Who has not received his soul in vain*: who has not reckoned his soul among things that do not remain, but, perceiving it to be immortal, has desired a stable and unchangeable eternity. *And has not sworn deceitfully to his neighbor*: and therefore, without deceit, as eternal things are simple and not deceiving, so he has shown himself to his neighbor.

**5.** [v 5.] *He will receive a blessing from the Lord, and mercy from God his savior.*

**6.** [v 6.] *This is the generation of those who seek the Lord*: for so are they born who seek him. *Of those who seek the face of the God of Jacob*: but they seek the face of God, who gave the primacy to the one born later.

**7.** [v 7.] *Lift up the gates, you princes*: whoever of you seek principality among men, take away, lest they hinder, the entrances of desire and fear which you have set up. *And be lifted up, eternal gates*: and be lifted up, entrances of eternal life, of renunciation of the world and conversion to God. *And the king of glory will enter in*: and the king will enter in, in whom we may glory without pride; who, the gates of mortality being overcome, and the heavenly ones opened to him, fulfilled what he says: *Rejoice, because I have overcome the world*[^1].

**8.** [v 8.] *Who is this king of glory?* mortal nature is afraid, marveling, and asks: *Who is this king of glory? The Lord strong and mighty*: whom you thought weak and oppressed. *The Lord mighty in war*: handle the scars, and you will perceive them restored, and human weakness restored to immortality. There was paid what was owed to the earth, where it warred with death, the glorification of the Lord.

**9.** [v 9.] *Lift up the gates, you princes*: hence now let the way be made into heaven. Let the prophetic trumpet cry out again: Lift up the gates, you heavenly princes too, which you have in the souls of men who adore the host of heaven. *And be lifted up, eternal gates*: and be lifted up, gates of eternal righteousness, of love and chastity, through which the soul loves the one true God, and does not commit fornication under the many who are called gods. *And the king of glory will enter in*: and the king of glory will enter in, to intercede for us at the right hand of the Father.

**10.** [v 10.] *Who is this king of glory?* why do you too, prince of the power of this air, marvel, and ask: *Who is this king of glory? The Lord of powers, he is the king of glory*: and now, his body made alive, he passes above you, he who was tempted; he stretches above all the angels, he who was tempted by the apostate angel. Let none of you cast yourselves in the way and block our path, that you may be worshiped by us as God: neither principality, nor angel, nor power separates us from the love of Christ. It is good to hope in the Lord, rather than to hope in a prince: that he who glories may glory in the Lord. These powers indeed exist in the ordering of this world, but *the Lord of powers, he is the king of glory.*

[^1]: John 16:33
