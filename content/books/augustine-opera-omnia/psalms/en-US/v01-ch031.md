# Exposition of Psalm 27

**1.** [vv. 1–2] *Of David himself.* It is the voice of the Mediator himself, mighty in hand in the struggle of his Passion. What he seems to wish upon his enemies is not a malicious wish, but an announcement of their punishment: just as in the Gospel, when he speaks of the cities in which he had worked miracles and which had not believed in him, he does not maliciously wish what he says upon them, but foretells what awaits them.

**2.** *To you, O Lord, I have cried; my God, be not silent toward me*[^1]: to you, O Lord, I have cried; my God, do not separate the unity of your Word from what I am as man. *Lest you be silent toward me, and I become like those who go down into the pit*[^2]: for from the fact that the eternity of your Word never ceases to unite itself to me, it comes about that I am not such a man as the rest are, who are born into the deep misery of this world, where, as though you were silent, your Word is not known. *Hear the voice of my supplication, while I pray to you; while I lift up my hands to your holy temple*[^3]: while I am crucified, for the salvation of those who by believing become your holy temple.

**3.** [v. 3.] *Do not drag away my soul together with sinners, and do not destroy me with those who work iniquity: with those who speak peace with their neighbor*[^4]. With those who say to me, *We know that you have come from God as a teacher*[^5]. *But evil in their hearts*[^6]: but they speak evil things in their hearts.

**4.** [v. 4.] *Give to them according to their works*[^7]: give to them according to their works, for this is just. *And according to the malice of their devices*[^8]: for those who devise evil cannot find what is good. *According to the works of their hands give to them*[^9]: although what they did may avail for the salvation of others, give to them nonetheless according to the works of their own wills. *Render to them their recompense*[^10]: because for the truth they were hearing they chose to give back deceit, let their own deceit deceive them.

**5.** [v. 5.] *Because they have not understood the works of the Lord*[^11]: and from what is it plain that this befell them? From this, namely, *that they did not understand the works of the Lord*: this very thing was surely already the recompense, that they did not recognize as God the one whom they tempted as a man with malevolent mind—the one whom the Father sent incarnate, and with what counsel. *And the works of his hands*[^12]: nor were they moved by those very visible works that were set out before their eyes. *You will destroy them, and not build them up*[^13]: let them do me no harm, and let them have no power even as they try once more to raise engines of war against my Church.

**6.** *Blessed be the Lord, for he has heard the voice of my supplication*[^14].

**7.** [v. 7.] *The Lord is my helper and my protector*[^15]: the Lord helping one who suffers such things, and protecting with immortality one who rises again. *In him my heart has hoped, and I have been helped. And my flesh has flourished again*[^16]: that is, and my flesh has risen again. *And of my own will I will confess to him*[^17]: whence now, the dread of death being done away, those who believe in me will confess to him together with the Law, not by the necessity of fear under the Law but by free will; and because I am in them, I myself will confess.

**8.** [v. 8.] *The Lord is the strength of his people*[^18]: not that people who, ignorant of the righteousness of God, wished to establish their own. For it did not think itself strong by its own power, because the Lord is the strength of his people as it fights against the devil in the difficulties of this life. *And he is the protector of the saving acts of his anointed*[^19]: so that, once it has been made safe through his anointed, after its valor in war he may at the last protect it with the immortality of peace.

**9.** [v. 9.] *Save your people, and bless your inheritance*[^20]. I intercede, therefore, after my flesh has flourished again, because you have said, *Ask of me, and I will give you the nations for your inheritance*[^21]: *Save your people, and bless your inheritance*, for all that is mine is yours. *And rule them, and raise them up forever*[^22]: rule them in this temporal life, and from here lift them up into eternity.
