# Exposition of Psalm 33 (32)

**1.** [v. 1] *Exult, you righteous, in the Lord*: exult, you righteous, not in yourselves, for this is not safe; but in the Lord. *Praise befits the upright*: those praise the Lord who submit themselves to the Lord; otherwise they are twisted and crooked.

**2.** [v. 2] *Confess to the Lord with the harp*: confess to the Lord, presenting to him your bodies as a living sacrifice. *Sing to him with the ten-stringed psaltery*: let your members serve the love of God and of neighbor, in which the three commandments and the seven are kept.

**3.** [v. 3] *Sing to him a new song*: sing to him the song of the grace of faith. *Sing to him well, with jubilation*: sing to him well, in gladness.

**4.** [v. 4] *For the word of the Lord is upright*: for the word of the Lord is upright, to make you what of yourselves you cannot be. *And all his works are done in faith*: lest anyone suppose that he has come to faith by the merits of his works, since in faith itself are all the works that God loves.

**5.** [v. 5] *He loves mercy and judgment*: for he loves mercy, which now he grants beforehand; and judgment, by which he exacts what he has granted beforehand. *The earth is full of the mercy of the Lord*: throughout the world sins are forgiven to men in the mercy of the Lord.

**6.** [v. 6] *By the word of the Lord the heavens were made firm*: for not of themselves, but by the word of the Lord, were the righteous made firm. *And all their power by the Spirit of his mouth*: and all their faith by his Holy Spirit.

**7.** [v. 7] *Gathering the waters of the sea as in a wineskin*: he gathers the peoples of the world into the confession of sin put to death, lest through pride they flow freely away. *Setting the depths in storehouses*: and in them he keeps his hidden things for riches.

**8.** [v. 8] *Let all the earth fear the Lord*: let every sinner fear, that he may cease from sinning. *And let them be moved by him*: let them be moved not by the terrors of men or of any creature, but by him, *all who inhabit the world.*

**9.** [v. 9] *For he spoke, and they were made*: for it was not some other who made the things they should fear; but he spoke, and they were made. *He commanded, and they were created*: he commanded by his Word, and they were created.

**10.** [v. 10] *The Lord scatters the counsels of the nations*: of those who seek not his kingdom, but their own kingdoms. *And he reproves the thoughts of the peoples*: of those who desire earthly blessedness. *And he reproves the counsels of princes*: of those who seek to lord it over such peoples.

**11.** [v. 11] *But the counsel of the Lord remains forever*: the counsel of the Lord, by which he makes none blessed except the one subject to him, remains forever. *The thoughts of his heart from generation to generation*: the thoughts of his wisdom are not changeable, but remain from age to age.

**12.** [v. 12] *Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord*: one nation is blessed, belonging to the heavenly city, which has chosen the Lord, and none but him, as its God. *The people whom the Lord has chosen for his inheritance*: and this people was chosen not of itself, but by the gift of God, so that, possessing it, he should not let it remain uncultivated and wretched.

**13.** [v. 13] *From heaven the Lord looked down, he saw all the sons of men*: from the righteous soul the Lord mercifully saw all who wish to be reborn into newness of life.

**14.** [v. 14] *From his prepared dwelling place*: from the dwelling place of the human nature he assumed, which he prepared for himself. *He looked upon all who inhabit the earth*: he mercifully saw all who inhabit the flesh, that he might preside over them in governing them.

**15.** [v. 15] *Who fashioned their hearts one by one*: who spiritually bestows on their hearts their own proper gifts, so that not the whole body is an eye, nor the whole hearing; but one is incorporated into Christ in one way, another in another. *Who understands all their works*: with him all their works are understood.

**16.** [v. 16] *A king is not saved by the abundance of his strength*: he is not saved who governs his own flesh, if he presumes much upon his own strength. *Nor will a giant be saved by the abundance of his strength*: nor will anyone who wars against the habit of his own desire, or against the devil and his angels, be saved, if he entrusts himself much to his own might.

**17.** [v. 17] *A horse is a liar for safety*: he is deceived, whoever supposes either that he obtains a salvation received through men among men, or that he is defended from ruin by the force of his own spirit. *And in the abundance of its strength it will not be saved.*

**18.** [v. 18] *Behold, the eyes of the Lord are upon those who fear him*: for if you seek salvation, behold, the love of the Lord is upon those who fear him. *Upon those who hope in his mercy*: and who hope not in their own strength, but in his mercy.

**19.** [v. 19] *To rescue their souls from death, and to feed them in famine*: to give them the nourishment of the word and of everlasting truth, which they had lost while they presumed upon their own powers, and therefore do not have even those powers in the famine of righteousness.

**20.** [v. 20] *Our soul will be patient for the Lord*: but, that it may afterward be fattened with incorruptible food, meanwhile, while it is here, our soul will be patient for the Lord. *For he is our helper and protector*: he is our helper, while we strive toward him; and our protector, while we resist the adversary.

**21.** [v. 21] *For in him our heart will rejoice*: for not in ourselves, where without him there is great want; but in him our heart will rejoice. *And in his holy name we have hoped*: and therefore we have hoped that we shall come to God, because, while we were absent, he sent us his name through faith.

**22.** [v. 22] *Let your mercy, Lord, be upon us, as we have hoped in you*: let your mercy, Lord, be upon us; for hope does not disappoint, because we have hoped in you.
