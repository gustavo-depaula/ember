# Exposition of Psalm 15

**1.** Our King speaks in this psalm from the person of the manhood he took up, of whom the royal title at the time of the passion stood out inscribed.

**2.** [vv 1.2.] And he says these things. *Preserve me, Lord, for in you I have hoped: I said to the Lord, You are my God, for you have no need of my goods*: for you do not await being made blessed by my goods.

**3.** [v 3.] *To the saints who are in his land*: to the saints who have placed their hope in the land of the living, citizens of the heavenly Jerusalem, whose spiritual conduct is fixed by the anchor of hope in that fatherland, which is rightly called the land of God; although they still dwell in these lands in the flesh. *He has made wonderful all my wishes among them.* Among these saints, then, he made wonderful all my wishes in their advancement, by which they perceived what profited them both in the humanity of my divinity, that I should die, and in the divinity of my humanity, that I should rise again.

**4.** [v 4.] *Their infirmities have been multiplied*: not unto destruction, but that they might desire the physician. *Afterward they made haste*: and so, after the infirmities were multiplied, they made haste, that they might be healed. *I will not gather their assemblies of bloods*: for their assemblies will not be carnal, nor, being appeased by the blood of beasts, will I gather them. *Nor will I be mindful of their names through my lips*: but by a spiritual change they will forget what they were; nor will they any longer be called by me either sinners, or enemies, or men, but righteous and my brothers and sons of God through my peace.

**5.** [v 5.] *The Lord is the portion of my inheritance and of my cup.* For they will possess with me the inheritance, the Lord himself. Let others choose for themselves portions to enjoy, earthly and temporal: the portion of the saints is the eternal Lord. Let others drink deadly pleasures: the portion of my cup is the Lord. In saying *my*, I join the Church; because where the head is, there is also the body: for into the inheritance I will gather their assemblies, and through the drunkenness of the cup I will forget their old names. *You are he who will restore to me my inheritance*: that the brightness in which I was with you before the world was made may be known also to these whom I set free: for you will not restore to me what I did not lose, but you will restore to those who lost the knowledge of that brightness; in whom, because I am, *you will restore to me.*

**6.** [v 6.] *The lines have fallen for me in glorious places*: the boundaries of my possession in your brightness have fallen as it were by lot, as God is the possession of the priests and Levites. *For my inheritance is glorious to me*: for my inheritance is glorious, not to all, but to those who see: in whom, because I am, *it is to me.*

**7.** [v 7.] *I will bless the Lord, who has granted me understanding*: by which this inheritance can be seen and possessed. *Moreover even till night my reins have chastened me*: but beyond understanding, even unto death my lower part, the assumption of flesh, instructed me; that I might experience the darkness of mortality, which that understanding does not have.

**8.** [v 8.] *I beheld the Lord in my sight always*: but coming into the things that pass away, I did not take my eye from him who always remains, foreseeing this, that I might return to him after the temporal things were accomplished. *Because he is at my right hand, that I be not moved*: because he favors me, that I may remain steadfastly in him.

**9.** [v 9.] *Because of this my heart has been gladdened, and my tongue has exulted*: because of this there is both gladness in my thoughts, and exultation in my words. *Moreover even my flesh will rest in hope*: moreover even my flesh will not fail unto destruction, but in the hope of resurrection will fall asleep.

**10.** [v 10.] *Because you will not abandon my soul in hell*: because neither will you give my soul to be possessed by the lower regions. *Nor will you give your holy one to see corruption*: nor will you allow the sanctified body, through which others too are to be sanctified, to be corrupted. *You have made known to me the ways of life*: you have made known through me the ways of humility, that men might return to life, from where through pride they had fallen; in whom, because I am, *you have made known to me. You will fill me with gladness with your countenance*: you will fill them with gladness, that they may seek nothing further, when they have seen you face to face; in whom, because I am, *you will fill me. Delight at your right hand unto the end*: there is delight in your favor and in your propitiation on the journey of this life, leading even unto the end of the glory of beholding you.
