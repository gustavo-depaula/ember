# Exposition of Psalm 17

**1.** [v 1.] To the end, for the servant of the Lord, for David himself, that is, for Christ strong of hand according to the man. The words of this canticle which he spoke to the Lord in the day on which the Lord rescued him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul, and he said. In the day on which the Lord rescued him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul, that is, of the king of the Jews, whom they had asked for themselves. For as David is said to be interpreted "Strong of hand," so Saul "Asking": and it is known how that people asked for and received a king for themselves, not according to God's will, but according to their own.

**2.** [v 2.] Here, then, Christ and the Church speak, that is, the whole Christ, head and body: *I will love you, Lord, my strength*: I will love you, Lord, through whom I am strong.

**3.** [v 3.] *Lord, my firmament, and my refuge, and my deliverer*: Lord, who have made me firm, because I fled to you for refuge; and I fled for refuge because you set me free. *My God, my helper, and I will hope in him*: my God, who first gave me the help of your calling, that I might be able to hope in you. *My protector and the horn of my salvation, and my redeemer*: my protector, because I did not presume upon myself, as it were raising against you the horn of pride; but I found you yourself the horn, that is, the firm height of salvation; and that I might find it, you redeemed me.

**4.** [v 4.] *Praising, I will call upon the Lord, and I will be saved from my enemies*: seeking not my own glory, but the Lord's, I will call upon him, and there will be nothing whence the errors of impiety can harm me.

**5.** [v 5.] *The pains of death surrounded me*: that is, of the flesh. *And the torrents of iniquity troubled me*: the unrighteous crowds, stirred up for a time, like rain-swollen rivers soon to cease, acted to trouble me.

**6.** [v 6.] *The pains of hell surrounded me*: in those who surrounded me to destroy me, there were the pains of envy, which work death and lead to the hell of sin. *The snares of death forestalled me*: they forestalled me, that they might wish to harm me first, which would afterward be repaid to them. But such men capture unto perdition those whom they have ill persuaded by a boasting of righteousness; of which, not in deed, but in name, they glory against the gentiles.

**7.** [v 7.] *And in my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried out to my God. And he heard my voice from his holy temple*: he heard my voice from my heart, in which he dwells. *And my cry in his sight*: and my cry, which I have not in the ears of men, but before him within, *will enter into his ears.*

**8.** [v 8.] *And the earth was moved and trembled*: so, the son of man being glorified, sinners were moved and trembled. *And the foundations of the mountains were troubled*: and the hope of the proud, which was in this world, was troubled. *And they were moved, because God was angry with them*: so that, namely, the hope of temporal goods might no longer have a firm footing in the hearts of men.

**9.** [v 9.] *Smoke went up in his anger*: the tearful supplication of the penitent went up, when they had learned what God threatens to the impious. *And fire blazes forth from his face*: and the burning of love, after penance, is kindled from the knowledge of him. *Coals were set on fire by him*: those who were already dead, deserted by the fire of good desire and the light of righteousness, and had remained cold and dark, again kindled and illumined, came back to life.

**10.** [v 10.] *And he inclined the heaven, and came down*: and he humbled the righteous one, that he might come down to the weakness of men. *And darkness under his feet.* And the impious who savor earthly things, by the darkness of their malice did not know him; for the earth is under his feet, as it were the footstool of his feet.

**11.** [v 11.] *And he ascended above the Cherubim, and flew.* And he was exalted above the fullness of knowledge, so that no one might reach him, except through love: for the fullness of the law is love. And quickly he showed himself to be incomprehensible to his lovers, lest they suppose him to be comprehended by bodily imaginations. *He flew above the wings of the winds.* But that swiftness by which he showed himself to be incomprehensible is above the powers of souls, by which, as by wings, they lift themselves from earthly fears into the airs of liberty.

**12.** [v 12.] *And he made darkness his hiding place*: and he made the obscurity of the sacraments, and the hidden hope in the heart of believers, where he himself might lie hidden, not deserting them; in this darkness too, where through faith we still walk, not by sight, as long as we hope for what we do not see and wait for it with patience. *His tabernacle is round about him*: yet those who believe him, turned toward him, surround him; because he is in their midst, since he favors all equally, in whom, as in a tabernacle, he dwells in this time. *Dark water in the clouds of the air*: nor for this reason let anyone suppose that he is already in that light which is to come when from faith we shall come to sight, if he understands the Scriptures rightly; for in the Prophets and in all the preachers of the divine word the teaching is obscure.

**13.** [v 13.] *Before the brightness in his sight*: in comparison with the brightness which is in the sight of his manifestation. *His clouds passed over*: the preachers of his word are no longer contained within the borders of Judea, but have passed over to the Gentiles. *Hail and coals of fire.* Figurative rebukes, by which, as by hail, hard hearts are beaten: but if cultivated and gentle earth, that is, a pious soul, receives them, the hardness of the hail is dissolved into water, that is, the terror of the lightning-flashed and as it were frozen rebuke into a satisfying teaching: but hearts kindled by the fire of love come back to life. All these in his clouds passed over to the Gentiles.

**14.** [v 14.] *And the Lord thundered from heaven*: and the Lord sounded with Gospel confidence from the heart of the righteous. *And the Most High gave his voice*: that we might have it, and in the depth of human affairs might hear heavenly things.

**15.** [v 15.] *And he sent out his arrows, and scattered them*: and he sent out the Evangelists, flying over straight ways on the wings of the virtues, not by their own strength, but by his by whom they were sent: and he scattered those to whom they were sent, so that to some of them they were the odor of life unto life, to others the odor of death unto death. *And he multiplied lightnings, and troubled them*: and he multiplied miracles, and troubled them.

**16.** [v 16.] *And the fountains of waters appeared*: and there appeared those who had become, in the preachers, fountains of water welling up to eternal life. *And the foundations of the world were revealed*: and the Prophets were revealed, who were not understood, upon whom the world, believing the Lord, might be built. *At your rebuke, Lord*: of one crying, *The kingdom of God has drawn near over you*[^1]. *At the inspiration of the spirit of your anger*: of one saying, *Unless you do penance, you will all likewise perish*[^2].

**17.** [v 17.] *He sent from on high, and received me*: by calling from the Gentiles into the inheritance the glorious Church, having no spot or wrinkle. *He took me up from the multitude of waters*: he took me up from the multitude of peoples.

**18.** [v 18.] *He rescued me from my strongest enemies*: he rescued me from my enemies, who prevailed to afflict and pervert this temporal life of mine. *And from those who hated me, because they were strengthened over me*: as long as I am under them, ignorant of God.

**19.** [v 19.] *They forestalled me in the day of my affliction*: they harmed me first, in the time when I bear a mortal and toilsome body. *And the Lord became my firmament*: and since, by the bitterness of miseries, the firmament of earthly pleasure was troubled and uprooted, the Lord became my firmament.

**20.** [v 20.] *And he led me out into a broad place*: and because I suffered carnal straits, he led me out into the spiritual breadth of faith. *He rescued me, because he willed me*: before I willed him, he rescued me from my most powerful enemies, who envied me now willing him, and from those who hate me, because I will him.

**21.** [v 21.] *And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness*: and the Lord will repay me according to the righteousness of good will, who first offered mercy, before I had good will. *And according to the purity of my hands he will repay me*: and according to the purity of my deeds he will repay me, who granted me to do good, by leading me out into the breadth of faith.

**22.** [v 22.] *Because I have kept the ways of the Lord*: so that the breadth of good works, which are through faith, and the longsuffering of persevering may follow.

**23.** [v 23.] *Nor have I dealt impiously toward my God. Because all his judgments are in my sight. Because all his judgments*: that is, both the rewards of the righteous, and the punishments of the impious, and the scourges of those to be corrected, and the temptations of those to be proved, I consider with persevering contemplation. *And his acts of righteousness I have not put away from me*: which those do who fail under the burden of them, and return to their own vomit.

**24.** *And I will be immaculate with him, and I will guard myself from my iniquity.*

**25.** [v 25.] *And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness*: and so not only because of the breadth of faith, which works through love, but also because of the length of perseverance, the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness. *And according to the purity of my hands in the sight of his eyes*: not what men see, but in the sight of his eyes. For the things that are seen are temporal; but the things that are not seen are eternal: to which pertains the height of hope.

**26.** [v 26.] *With the holy you will be holy*: there is also a hidden depth, by which you are understood to be holy with the holy, because you sanctify. *And with the innocent man you will be innocent*: because you harm no one, but each one is bound by the cords of his own sins.

**27.** [v 27.] *And with the chosen you will be chosen*: and by him whom you choose, you are chosen. *And with the perverse you will be perverse*: and with the perverse you will seem perverse: for they say, *The way of the Lord is not right*[^3]: and their own way is not right.

**28.** [v 28.] *Because you will save the humble people.* But this seems perverse to the perverse, that you will save those who confess their sins. *And you will humble the eyes of the proud*: but those who are ignorant of God's righteousness, and wish to establish their own, you will humble.

**29.** [v 29.] *Because you will light my lamp, Lord*: because our light is not from ourselves; but you will light my lamp, Lord. *My God, you will enlighten my darkness*: for we, by our sins, are darkness; but, my God, you will enlighten my darkness.

**30.** [v 30.] *Because by you I will be rescued from temptation*: because not by myself, but by you I will be rescued from temptation. *And in my God I will pass over the wall*: and not in myself, but in my God I will pass over the wall, which sins have raised between men and the heavenly Jerusalem.

**31.** [v 31.] *My God, his way is immaculate*: my God does not come into men, unless they have cleansed the way of faith by which he may come to them; because his way is immaculate. *The words of the Lord tried by fire*: the words of the Lord are proved by the fire of tribulation. *He is the protector of all who hope in him*: and all who hope not in themselves, but in him, are not consumed by that same tribulation; for hope follows faith.

**32.** [v 32.] *For who is God, besides the Lord?* whom we serve. *And who is God besides our God?* and who is God besides the Lord, whom, after good servitude, we shall possess as sons, as a hoped-for inheritance?

**33.** [v 33.] *God who has girded me with strength*: God who has girded me that I may be strong, lest the loose folds of desire hinder my works and my steps. *And has set my way immaculate*: and has set immaculate the way of love, by which I may come to him, as the way of faith is immaculate, by which he came to me.

**34.** [v 34.] *Who has perfected my feet like a stag's*: who has perfected my love, for the crossing of the thorny and shadowy entanglements of this world. *And he will set me upon high places*: and he will fix my intention upon the heavenly dwelling, that I may be filled unto all the fullness of God.

**35.** [v 35.] *Who teaches my hands for battle*: who teaches me to work for the overcoming of the enemies, who try to shut off from us the heavenly kingdom. *And you have made my arms like a bow of bronze*: and you have made tireless the intention of my good works.

**36.** [v 36.] *And you have given me the protection of my salvation, and your right hand has taken me up*: and the favor of your grace has taken me up. *And your discipline has directed me unto the end*: and your correction, not allowing me to stray, has directed me, so that whatever I do, I may refer it to that end by which one cleaves to you. *And your discipline itself will teach me*: and that same correction of yours will teach me to arrive where it has directed.

**37.** [v 37.] *You have enlarged my steps beneath me*: nor will carnal straits hinder them: since you have made wide my love, which works cheerfully, even from those mortal things and members that are beneath me. *And my footprints have not been weakened*: and there have not been weakened either my journeys, or the marks which I have imprinted for the imitation of those who follow.

**38.** [v 38.] *I will pursue my enemies, and seize them*: I will pursue my carnal affections, and not be seized by them; but I will seize them, that they may be consumed. *And I will not turn back until they fail*: and from this intention I will not turn back to rest, until those fail who clamor against me.

**39.** [v 39.] *I will shatter them, and they will not be able to stand*: nor will they endure against me. *They will fall beneath my feet*: they being cast down, I will set before me the loves by which I walk forever.

**40.** [v 40.] *And you have girded me with strength for war*: and you have bound up the flowing desires of my flesh with strength, lest I be hindered in such a battle. *You have tripped up beneath me those rising against me*: you have made those to be deceived who were pursuing me, so that those became beneath me who desired to be over me.

**41.** [v 41.] *And you have given me the back of my enemies.* And you have turned my enemies, and made them to be a back to me, that is, that they should follow me. *And those who hated me you have destroyed.* But others of them, who persisted in hatred, you have destroyed.

**42.** [v 42.] *They cried out, and there was none to save*: for who would save those whom you would not save? *To the Lord, and he did not hear them*: nor to anyone, but to the Lord they cried; nor did he judge worthy of being heard those who did not withdraw from their malice.

**43.** [v 43.] *And I will crush them small as the dust before the face of the wind*: and I will crush them small; for they are dry, not receiving the rain of God's mercy: so that, lifted up and puffed up with pride, they may be snatched away from firm and unshaken hope, and as it were from the solidity and stability of the earth. *I will wipe them out like the mud of the streets*: through the broad ways of perdition which many walk, luxuriating and slippery, I will wipe them out.

**44.** [v 44.] *You will rescue me from the contradictions of the people*: you will rescue me from the contradictions of those who said: *If we let him go, the whole world will go after him*[^4].

**45.** [v 45.] *You will set me as the head of the nations. A people whom I did not know has served me*: the people of the nations whom I did not visit by bodily presence has served me. *At the hearing of the ear it has obeyed me*: nor did it see me with its eyes; but receiving my preachers, at the hearing of the ear it has obeyed me.

**46.** [v 46.] *Alien sons have lied to me*: sons not to be called mine, but rather alien, to whom it is rightly said, *You are of your father the devil*[^5], have lied to me. *Alien sons have grown old*: alien sons, to whom, that they might be renewed, I brought the New Testament, have remained in the old man. *And they have limped from their own paths*: and as though lame in one foot, because, holding the Old, they spurned the New Testament, they were made lame, even in that same old Law following their own traditions rather than God's; for they made false charges about unwashed hands, because such were the paths which they themselves had made and worn by custom, straying from the ways of God's precepts.

**47.** [v 47.] *The Lord lives, and blessed be my God*: but to savor according to the flesh is death; for the Lord lives, and blessed be my God. *And let the God of my salvation be exalted*: and let me not, by earthly custom, think of the God of my salvation; nor of that salvation itself as earthly, but let me hope of him on high.

**48.** [v 48.] *God who gives me vengeance, and subjects peoples under me*: God who avenges me, by subjecting peoples under me. *My deliverer from my angry enemies*: from the Jews crying, *Crucify, crucify*[^6].

**49.** [v 49.] *From those rising against me you will exalt me*: from the Jews rising against me as I suffered, you will exalt me as I rise again. *From the unjust man you will rescue me*: from their unjust kingdom you will rescue me.

**50.** [v 50.] *Therefore I will confess to you among the nations, Lord*: therefore the nations will confess to you through me, Lord. *And I will sing to your name*: and you will become more widely known by my good works.

**51.** [v 51.] *Magnifying the saving acts of his own king*: God who magnifies, that he may make wonderful the saving acts which his Son gives to believers. *And showing mercy to his Christ*: God who shows mercy to his Christ. *To David and his seed forever*: to that deliverer himself, strong of hand, who overcame this world, and to those whom, believing the Gospel, he begot forever. Whatever has been said in this psalm which cannot properly fit the Lord himself, that is, the head of the Church, is to be referred to the Church. For the whole Christ speaks here, in whom are all his members.

[^1]: Matt 3:2
[^2]: Luke 13:3
[^3]: Ezek 18:25
[^4]: John 11:48; 12:19
[^5]: John 8:44
[^6]: Luke 23:21
