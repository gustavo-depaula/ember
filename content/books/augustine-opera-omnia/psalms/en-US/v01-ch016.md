# Exposition of Psalm 16

**1.** [vv 1.2.] This is to be attributed to the person of the Lord, with the Church joined, which is his body.

**2.** *Hear, God, my righteousness, attend to my supplication. Give ear to my prayer, not in deceitful lips*: not coming to you in deceitful lips. *Let my judgment come forth from your countenance*: from the illumination of your knowledge, a true judge; or surely, not in deceitful lips let my judgment come forth from your countenance, that is, that I may not, in judging, bring forth anything other than what I understand in you. *Let my eyes see equity*: the eyes of the heart, surely.

**3.** [v 3.] *You have proved my heart and visited it by night*: because my very heart was proved by the visitation of tribulation. *You have tried me by fire, and iniquity has not been found in me*: not only night, because it is wont to disturb, but also fire, because it burns, is the name to be given to that tribulation, by which, tried, I was found righteous.

**4.** [v 4.] *That my mouth may not speak the works of men*: that nothing else may come forth from my mouth than what pertains to your glory and praise; not to the works of men, which they do apart from your will. *Because of the words of your lips*: because of the words of your peace, or of your Prophets. *I have kept hard ways*: I have kept the toilsome ways of human mortality and of the passion.

**5.** [v 5.] *To perfect my steps in your paths*: that the love of the Church might be perfected in the narrow ways by which one comes to your rest. *That my footprints may not be moved*: that the marks of my journey may not be moved, which, as footprints, are imprinted on the sacraments and the apostolic Scriptures, which those who wish to follow me may behold and observe; or surely, that I too may remain steadfastly in eternity, after I have completed the hard ways, and in the straits of your paths have perfected my steps.

**6.** [v 6.] *I have cried out, because you have heard me, God*: I have directed prayers to you with a free and strong intention; because, that I might be able to have this, you heard me praying more weakly. *Incline your ear to me, and hear my words*: let your hearing not desert my humility.

**7.** [v 7.] *Make your mercies wonderful*: let your mercies not become cheap, lest they be less loved.

**8.** [v 8.] *You who save those who hope in you, from those who resist your right hand*: from those who resist the favor by which you favor me. *Guard me, Lord, as the pupil of the eye*: which appears very small and slight; yet through it is directed the keenness of the light by which light and darkness are distinguished, as through the humanity of Christ the divinity, distinguishing between the righteous and sinners. *Under the covering of your wings protect me*: under the fortification of your love and mercy protect me *from the face of the impious who have afflicted me.*

**9.** [vv 9.10.] *My enemies have surrounded my soul, they have shut up their own fat*: they have been covered over with their fat gladness, after their desire was sated with crime. *Their mouth has spoken pride*: and therefore their mouth has spoken pride, by saying: *Hail, king of the Jews*[^1], and other such things.

**10.** [v 11.] *Casting me out, now they have surrounded me*: casting me out beyond the city, now they have surrounded me on the cross. *They have set their eyes to turn down to the earth*: they have set the intention of their heart to turn down to these earthly things; thinking that he who was being killed suffered a great evil, and they who were killing none.

**11.** [v 12.] *They received me like a lion ready for the prey*: they received me like that adversary going about, seeking whom he may devour. *And like a lion's cub dwelling in hidden places.* And like his cub, the people to whom it was said: *You are of your father the devil*[^2], thinking of the snares by which they might encircle and destroy the righteous one.

**12.** [v 13.] *Rise up, Lord, forestall them, and overthrow them*: rise up, Lord, whom they think to sleep and not to care for the iniquities of men; let them first be blinded by their own malice, that vengeance may forestall their deed, and so overthrow them.

**13.** [v 14.] *Rescue my soul from the impious*: rescue my soul by raising me from the death which was inflicted on me by the impious. *Your broadsword from the enemies of your hand*: for my soul is your broadsword, which your hand took up, that is, your eternal power, that through it you might war down the kingdoms of iniquity and divide the righteous from the impious; this, then, *rescue from the enemies of your hand*, that is, of your power, that is, from my enemies. *Lord, destroying them from the earth, divide them in their life*: Lord, destroying them from the earth which they inhabit, divide them throughout the world in this life, which alone they think their life who despair of the eternal. *And of your hidden things their belly is filled*: but not only this visible punishment will follow them; but also of the sins which, as darkness, are hidden from the light of your truth, their memory is filled, so that they forget God. *They are sated with swine's food*: they are sated with uncleanness, trampling the pearls of the words of God. *And they have left their leavings to their little ones*: crying, *This sin be upon us and upon our children*[^3].

**14.** [v 15.] *But I in your righteousness will appear in your sight*: but I, who did not appear to those who, with a soiled and dark heart, cannot see the light of wisdom, in your righteousness will appear in your sight. *I will be satisfied when your glory is made manifest*: and when they are sated with their own uncleanness, so that they cannot understand me, I will be satisfied when your glory is made manifest, in those who understand me. Indeed in that verse where it was said *They are sated with swine's food*, some copies have *They are sated with sons.* For from the ambiguous Greek a double interpretation arises. And by sons we understand works; and as good works are good sons, so evil works are evil sons.

[^1]: Matt 27:29
[^2]: John 8:44
[^3]: Matt 27:25
