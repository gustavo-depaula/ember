# Exposition of Psalm 20

**1.** [v 1.] *To the end, a psalm of David himself.* The title is known; it is sung of Christ.

**2.** [v 2.] *Lord, in your power the king will rejoice*: Lord, in your power, by which the Word was made flesh, the man Christ Jesus will rejoice. *And in your salvation he will exult vehemently*: and in this, by which you give life to all things, he will exult vehemently.

**3.** [v 3.] *You have given him the desire of his soul*: he desired to eat the Passover, and to lay down his soul when he willed, and again to take it up when he willed; and you gave it him. *And of the will of his lips you have not deprived him. My peace*, he says, *I leave you*[^1]: and it was done.

**4.** [v 4.] *Because you have gone before him with the blessings of sweetness*: because he had first drunk in the blessing of your sweetness, the gall of our sins did not harm him. *You have set on his head a crown of precious stone*: at the beginning of his discourse the precious stones, his disciples, drawing near, surrounded him, from whom the beginning of his proclamation would be made.

**5.** [v 5.] *He asked life, and you gave it him*: he asked the resurrection, saying, *Father, glorify your Son*[^2]; and you gave it him. *Length of days unto the age of the age*: long times of this world, which the Church would have, and thereafter eternity unto the age of the age.

**6.** [v 6.] *Great is his glory in your salvation*: great indeed is his glory in the salvation by which you raised him up. *Glory and great splendor you will lay upon him*: but still you will add glory and great splendor to him, when you will set him in heaven at your right hand.

**7.** [v 7.] *Because you will give him a blessing unto the age of the age*: this is the blessing which you will give him unto the age of the age: *You will gladden him with joy with your countenance*: according to the man you will gladden him with your countenance, which he lifted up to you.

**8.** [v 8.] *Because the king hopes in the Lord*: because the king is not proud, but, humble of heart, hopes in the Lord. *And in the mercy of the Most High he will not be moved*: and in the mercy of the Most High his obedience even unto the death of the cross will not trouble his humility.

**9.** [v 9.] *May your hand be found by all your enemies*: may your power, O king, be found, when you come to judge, by all your enemies, who did not understand it in your humility. *May your right hand find all who hate you*: may the brightness by which you reign at the right hand of the Father, find, on the day of judgment, all who hate you, to be punished; because now they themselves did not find it.

**10.** [v 10.] *You will set them like an oven of fire*: you will make them burning within, by the conscience of their own impiety. *In the time of your countenance*: in the time of your manifestation. *The Lord in his anger will trouble them, and fire will devour them*: then, troubled by the vengeance of the Lord, after the accusation of conscience, they will be given to the eternal fire to be devoured.

**11.** [v 11.] *Their fruit you will destroy from the earth*: their fruit, because it is earthly, you will destroy from the earth. *And their seed from among the sons of men*: and their works, or whomever they seduced, you will not reckon among the sons of men, whom you have called into the everlasting inheritance.

**12.** [v 12.] *Because they have turned evils upon you*: but this punishment will be repaid to them, because the evils which they thought were threatening them, you reigning, they twisted into your being killed. *They thought a counsel, which they could not establish*: they thought a counsel, saying, *It is expedient that one die for all*[^3]; which they could not establish, not knowing what they had said.

**13.** [v 13.] *Because you will set them as a back*: because you will order them among those things which, set behind and despised, you turn away from. *In your leavings you will prepare their face*: and in those things which you leave behind, that is, in the desires of an earthly kingdom, you will prepare for yourself, for the passion, their shamelessness.

**14.** [v 14.] *Be exalted, Lord, in your power*: whom they did not know in your humility, be exalted, Lord, in your power, which they thought weakness. *We will sing and make melody to your mighty deeds*: with heart and work we will celebrate and make known your wondrous deeds.

[^1]: John 14:27
[^2]: John 17:1
[^3]: John 11:50
