# Exposition of Psalm 116

**1-2.** *Praise the Lord, all you nations, praise him, all you peoples.* These are the courts of the house of the Lord, that all his people, this the true Jerusalem. Let those rather hear who were unwilling to be the children of this city, when they cut themselves off from the communion of all the nations. *For his mercy is confirmed over us, and the truth of the Lord abides forever.* These are those two, mercy and truth, which in the hundred and thirteenth psalm I admonished should be committed to memory. But *his mercy is confirmed over us,* when the raging mouths of the enemy nations yielded to his name, through which we were freed: *and the truth of the Lord abides forever,* whether in the things he promised to the just, or in the things he threatened to the impious.
