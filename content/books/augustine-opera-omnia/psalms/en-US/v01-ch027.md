# Exposition of Psalm 25

**1.** [v 1.] *Of David himself.* It can be attributed to David himself, not to the mediator, the man Christ Jesus, but to the whole Church now perfectly established in Christ.

**2.** *Judge me, Lord, because I have walked in my innocence;* judge me, Lord, because, after the mercy which you bestowed on me beforehand, I have some merit of innocence, whose way I have kept. *And, hoping in the Lord, I will not be moved*: yet not even thus in myself, but, hoping in the Lord, I will remain in him.

**3.** [vv 2.3.] *Prove me, Lord, and test me*: yet, lest anything of my hidden things lie hidden from me, prove me, Lord, and test me; making me known, not to you from whom nothing is hidden, but to myself and to men. *Burn my reins and my heart*: apply a medicinal purgative, as it were a fire, to my delights and my thoughts. *Because your mercy is before my eyes*: because, that I may not be consumed by that fire, not my merits, but your mercy, by which you brought me to such a life, is before my eyes. *And I have been well-pleased in your truth*: and because my own lie displeased me, but your truth pleased me, I too was pleasing with it and in it.

**4.** [v 4.] *I have not sat with the council of vanity*: I have not chosen to set my heart with those who try to provide how, which cannot be, they may be blessed by the full enjoyment of passing things. *And with those who do iniquities I will not enter in*: and because this very thing is the cause of all iniquities, therefore with those who do iniquities I will not have a hidden conscience.

**5.** [v 5.] *I have hated the congregation of the malicious*: but that one may come to that council of vanity, congregations of the malicious are formed, which I have hated. *And with the impious I will not sit*: and therefore with such a council, with the impious I will not sit, that is, I will not enter into an agreement. *And with the impious I will not sit.*

**6.** [v 6.] *I will wash my hands among the innocent*: I will make my works clean among the innocent; I will wash among the innocent my hands, with which I will embrace your sublime things. *And I will go around your altar, Lord.*

**7.** [v 7.] *That I may hear the voice of your praise*: that I may learn how to praise you. *And tell of all your wondrous works*: and when I have learned, set forth all your wondrous works.

**8.** [v 8.] *Lord, I have loved the beauty of your house*: of your Church. *And the place of the dwelling of your glory*: where, dwelling, you are glorified.

**9.** [v 9.] *Do not destroy my soul with the impious*: do not, then, destroy together with those who hate you my soul, which has loved the beauty of your house. *And with men of blood my life*: and with those who hate their neighbor. For by two precepts your house is adorned.

**10.** [v 10.] *In whose hands are iniquities*: do not, then, destroy me with the impious and with men of blood, whose works are unjust. *Their right hand is filled with bribes*: and what was given them for obtaining eternal salvation, they have turned to the receiving of the bribes of this world, esteeming piety to be gain.

**11.** [v 11.] *But I have walked in my innocence: redeem me, and have mercy on me*: let so great a price as the blood of my Lord avail me unto the perfection of liberation; and in the dangers of this life, let your mercy not desert me.

**12.** [v 12.] *My foot has stood in uprightness*: my love has not withdrawn from your righteousness. *In the churches I will bless you, Lord*: I will not hide your blessing from those whom you have called, Lord; since to the love of you I add the love of neighbor.
