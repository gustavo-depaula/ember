# Exposition of Psalm 134

**1.** [v. 1.] *Behold now, bless the Lord, all you servants of the Lord, you who stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God*[^1]. Why did he add, *in the courts*? The courts are understood as the more spacious areas of the house. He who stands in the courts is not straitened, is not pressed; in a certain way he is widened out. Remain in the breadth, and you can love your enemy; because you do not love those things in which you suffer straits from an enemy. How will you be understood to stand in the courts? Stand in love, and you stand in the courts. In love there is breadth, in hatred there is straitness. Hear the apostle: *Wrath and indignation, tribulation and anguish upon every soul of man who works evil*[^2]. But of the breadth of love, what does he say? Because *the love of God has been poured out in our hearts through the Holy Spirit, who has been given to us*[^3]. Where you hear a pouring out, understand breadth; where you hear breadth, understand the courts of the Lord; and you will have the true blessing of the Lord, when you do not curse your enemies. For the Spirit addresses those who suffer tribulation, that they may glory in tribulations; and says to them: *Behold now, bless the Lord, all you servants of the Lord.* What is: *Behold now*? In this time. For after the tribulations are over, it is plain that we will have leisure for the blessing of the Lord, as it was said: *Blessed are those who dwell in your house; forever and ever they will praise you*[^4]. Those who will then bless without failing, here begin to bless the Lord; here in tribulations, in temptations, in troubles, amid the adversities of the world, amid the snares of the enemy, amid the deceits and assaults of the devil. This is: *Behold now, bless the Lord, all you servants of the Lord, you who stand in the house of the Lord.* What is, you who stand? You who persevere. Because it was said of a certain one who was an archangel: *And he did not stand in the truth*[^5]: and it was said of the friend of the bridegroom: *But the friend of the bridegroom stands, and hears him, and rejoices with joy on account of the voice of the bridegroom*[^6].

**2.** [v. 2.] So: *You who stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God, in the nights lift up your hands to the holy place, and bless the Lord*[^7]. It is easy to bless in the days. What is, in the days? In prosperous things. For night is a sad thing; day is a glad thing. When it goes well with you, you bless the Lord. When you long for a son and he is born, you bless the Lord. Your wife has been freed from the danger of childbirth; you bless the Lord. Your son was sick, he is freed; you bless the Lord. Your son was sick; perhaps you sought an astrologer, a fortune-teller: perhaps not from your tongue, but from your conduct went out a curse against the Lord; it went out from your conduct and your life. Do not boast that with your tongue you bless, if with your life you curse. How, you say, do I curse with my life? Because your life is attended to, and it is said: Behold a Christian, behold what kind Christians are. Christ is blasphemed on account of you. Behold, since your life curses, what does it profit that your tongue blesses? So bless the Lord. When? *In the nights.* When did Job bless? When it was a sad night. All that he possessed was taken away; the sons were taken away for whom they were kept: how sad a night! But let us see whether he does not bless in the night: *The Lord gave, the Lord has taken away; as it pleased the Lord, so it has been done: blessed be the name of the Lord*[^8]. How dreadful a night! Struck with a sore from head to foot, rotting he was melting in himself. Then Eve dared to tempt him: *Say something against your God, and die*. Hear him blessing in the nights: *You have spoken*, he says, *like one of the foolish women. If we have received good things from the hand of the Lord, shall we not endure evil things?*[^9] Behold what is: *In the nights lift up your hands to the holy place, and bless the Lord.* What did Job say? *You have spoken like one of the foolish women.* Rotting Adam repelled Eve, as it were saying: Let it suffice that on account of you I was made mortal. You prevailed in paradise, but you are conquered on the dunghill. Great grace of God! But whence is this, except because the dew of Hermon had rained upon that soul, and the Lord had given sweetness, that our earth might give its fruit[^10]? *In the nights lift up your hands to the holy place, and bless the Lord.*

**3.** [v. 3.] *May the Lord bless you out of Zion, he who made heaven and earth*[^11]. He exhorts many to bless, and he himself blesses one, because out of many he made one; *because it is good and pleasant for brothers to dwell as one*[^12]. Plural number, *brothers*; but singular, *to dwell as one*: therefore: *May the Lord bless you out of Zion, he who made heaven and earth.* Let none of you say: It has not reached me. Whom do you suppose he meant when he said: *May the Lord bless you out of Zion*? He blessed one: be one, and the blessing reaches you.
