# Exposition of Psalm 28

**1.** [vv. 1-2.] *A psalm of David himself, for the completion of the tabernacle*[^1]. A psalm for the Mediator himself with a strong hand, of the perfecting of the Church in this world, where the warfare against the devil is waged in time.

**2.** The prophet speaks: *Bring to the Lord, you sons of God, bring to the Lord the offspring of rams*[^2]: bring to the Lord your very selves, whom the apostles, the leaders of the flocks, begot through the Gospel. *Bring to the Lord glory and honor*[^3]: through your works let the Lord be glorified and honored. *Bring to the Lord glory to his name*[^4]: let it be made gloriously known throughout the world. *Worship the Lord in his holy court*[^5]: worship the Lord in your enlarged and sanctified heart; for you are his royal and holy dwelling place.

**3.** [v. 3.] *The voice of the Lord is upon the waters*[^6]: the voice of Christ is upon the peoples. *The God of majesty has thundered*[^7]: the God of majesty, from the cloud of the flesh, has fearfully preached repentance. *The Lord is upon many waters*[^8]: the Lord Jesus himself, after he had sent forth his voice upon the peoples and struck them with terror, turned them to himself and dwelt in them.

**4.** [v. 4.] *The voice of the Lord in power*[^9]: the voice of the Lord now within them, making them powerful. *The voice of the Lord in magnificence*[^10]: the voice of the Lord doing great things in them.

**5.** [v. 5.] *The voice of the Lord shattering the cedars*[^11]: the voice of the Lord humbling the proud by the contrition of the heart. *The Lord will shatter the cedars of Lebanon*[^12]: the Lord will shatter through repentance those puffed up with the splendor of earthly nobility, when, to confound them, he has chosen the lowly things of this world, in which he may show forth his divinity.

**6.** [v. 6.] *And he will break them in pieces like the calf of Lebanon*[^13]. And, their proud loftiness being cut away, he will bring them down to the imitation of his own humility—he who, like a calf, was led to the sacrifice through the very nobility of this world: *For the kings of the earth stood up, and the rulers gathered together as one against the Lord and against his anointed*[^14]. *And the beloved is like the offspring of unicorns*[^15]: for he too, the beloved and only Son to the Father, emptied himself of his nobility and became man, like a son of the Jews who were ignorant of the righteousness of God and proudly boasting their own righteousness as if it were singular.

**7.** [v. 7.] *The voice of the Lord cutting through the flame of fire*[^16]. The voice of the Lord passing without any harm to himself through the most violent blaze of those persecuting him, or dividing the raging wrath of his persecutors, so that some said, Could this perhaps be the Christ?, and others, No, but he leads the people astray; and so cutting through their insane uproar, so as to carry some over into love of him and to leave others in their malice.

**8.** [v. 8.] *The voice of the Lord shaking the wilderness*[^17]: the voice of the Lord shaking to faith the gentiles, once without hope and without God in this world, where no prophet, no preacher of the word of God, as though no man at all, had dwelt. *And the Lord will shake the desert of Kadesh*[^18]: and then the Lord will cause to be celebrated the holy word of his Scriptures, which had been forsaken by the Jews who did not understand it.

**9.** [v. 9.] *The voice of the Lord perfecting the harts*[^19]: for the voice of the Lord first perfects those who overcome and repel venomous tongues. *And it will reveal the woods*[^20]: and then it will reveal to them the shadowy depths of the divine books and the dark coverts of the mysteries, where they may feed in freedom. *And in his temple everyone speaks of glory*[^21]: and in his Church everyone reborn into eternal hope praises God, each for his own gift, which he has received from the Holy Spirit.

**10.** [v. 10.] *The Lord inhabits the flood*[^22]: the Lord, then, first inhabits the flood of this world in his holy ones, kept safe in the Church as in an ark. *And the Lord will sit as king forever*[^23]: and then he will sit reigning in them forever.

**11.** [v. 11.] *The Lord will give strength to his people*[^24]: for the Lord will give strength to his people as it does battle against the storms and whirlwinds of this world, since he did not promise them peace in this world. *The Lord will bless his people with peace*[^25]: and the same Lord will bless his people, granting them peace in himself; for he says, *My peace I give to you, my peace I leave with you*[^26].
