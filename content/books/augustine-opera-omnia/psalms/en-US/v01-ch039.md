# Exposition of Psalm 31

**1.** [v. 1.] *Of David himself, of understanding*[^1]—by which it is understood that a man is freed not by the merits of works, but by the grace of God, when he confesses his sins.

**2.** [v. 2.] *Blessed are those whose iniquities are forgiven, and whose sins are covered*[^2]: and whose sins are brought into forgetfulness. *Blessed is the man to whom the Lord has not imputed sin, nor is there deceit in his mouth*[^3]: nor does he have in his mouth a display of righteousness, while his conscience is full of sins.

**3.** [v. 3.] *Because I kept silent, my bones have grown old*[^4]: because I did not bring forth confession with my mouth unto salvation, all my firmness grew old in weakness. *From crying out all the day long*[^5]: when I was ungodly and a blasphemer, crying out against God, as though defending and excusing my sins.

**4.** [v. 4.] *Because day and night your hand was heavy upon me*[^6]: because by the continual affliction of your scourges: *I was turned to misery, while the thorn was driven in*. I was made wretched by recognizing my misery, pierced by an evil conscience.

**5.** [v. 5.] *I acknowledged my sin, and I did not cover my unrighteousness*[^7]: that is, I did not hide my unrighteousness. *I said: I will declare against myself my unrighteousness to the Lord*. I said: I will declare—not against God, as in the cry of impiety when I kept silent, but against myself my unrighteousness to the Lord. *And you forgave the impiety of my heart*: hearing the voice of confession in the heart, before it was brought forth by the voice.

**6.** [v. 6.] *For this every holy one will pray to you in an opportune time*[^8]: for this impiety of the heart every holy one will pray to you. For they will be holy not by their own merits, but by the opportuneness of the time, that is, at the coming of him who redeemed us from our sins. *Yet in the flood of many waters, they will not draw near to him*: yet let no one suppose that, when the end has suddenly come, as in the days of Noah, there remains a place for confession, by which one may draw near to God.

**7.** [v. 7.] *You are my refuge from the pressure that has surrounded me*[^9]: you are my refuge from the pressure of sins, which has surrounded my heart. *My exultation, redeem me from those who surround me*: in you is my joy; redeem me from that sadness which my sins make for me.

**8.** [v. 8.] The answer of God: *I will give you understanding, and I will set you in this way in which you will walk*[^10]. I will give you understanding after the confession, so that you may not depart from the way in which you will enter, lest you wish to be in your own power. *I will fix my eyes upon you*: thus I will make my love firm upon you.

**9.** [v. 9.] *Do not be like the horse and the mule, which have no understanding*[^11]. For this reason they wish to rule themselves. And the voice of the Prophet: *With bit and bridle bind fast their jaws*. Do therefore to them, O God, what is done to the horse and the mule, that by punishments you may compel them to bear your rule, *who do not draw near to you*.

**10.** [v. 10.] *Many are the scourges of the sinner*[^12]: he is much scourged who, not confessing his sins to God, wishes to be his own ruler. *But mercy will surround the one who hopes in the Lord*: but he who hopes in the Lord, and submits himself to him to be ruled, mercy will surround him.

**11.** [v. 11.] *Be glad in the Lord, and exult, you righteous ones*[^13]: be glad, and exult, you righteous ones, not in yourselves, but in the Lord. *And glory, all you upright of heart*: and glory in him, all you who have understood that it is right to be subject to him, that you may be preferred to the rest.
