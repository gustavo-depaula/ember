# Exposition of Psalm 26

**1.** [v 1.] *Of David himself, before he was anointed. The recruit of Christ speaks, when he comes to the faith. The Lord is my illumination and my salvation, whom shall I fear? The Lord will give me both the knowledge of himself and salvation, who will take me from him? The Lord is the protector of my life, of whom shall I be afraid? The Lord will repel all the assaults and snares of my enemy, of none shall I be afraid.*

**2.** [v 2.] *While the harmful draw near over me, to eat my flesh*: while the harmful draw near to know me, and to insult, that they may set themselves before me as I change for the better; that they may consume with their slanderous teeth not me, but rather my carnal desires. *Those who trouble me, my enemies*: not only those who trouble me, reproving with a friendly mind, and wishing to call me back from my resolve, but also my enemies. *They themselves were weakened, and fell*: while, then, they do this in zeal for defending their own opinion, they were made weak for believing better things; and they began to hate the word of salvation, through which I do what displeases them.

**3.** [v 3.] *If camps stand against me, my heart will not fear*: but if a conspiring multitude of those who contradict stand against me, my heart will not fear, so as to desert to their side. *If war rise up against me, in this I will hope*: if the persecution of this world rise up against me, in this petition, which I am thinking of, I will fix my hope.

**4.** [v 4.] *One thing I have asked of the Lord, this I will seek*: for one petition I have asked of the Lord, this I will seek. *That I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life*: that, as long as I am in this life, no adversities may shut me out of the number of those who hold the unity and truth of the Lord's faith throughout the world. *That I may contemplate the delight of the Lord*: with this end, namely, that to me, persevering in the faith, there may appear that delightful form, which I may contemplate face to face. *And that I may be protected, his temple*: and, death being swallowed up in victory, that I may put on immortality, made his temple.

**5.** [v 5.] *Because he has hidden me in his tabernacle in the day of my evils*: because he has hidden me in the dispensation of his incarnate Word in the time of the temptations to which my mortal life is subject. *He has protected me in the hidden place of his tabernacle*: he has protected me, believing in the heart unto righteousness.

**6.** [v 6.] *On a rock he has exalted me*: and that it might also be made manifest unto salvation, that which I believed, he made my confession stand out in his firmness: *And now behold he has exalted my head above my enemies*: what does he reserve for me at the end, when even now, while the body is dead because of sin, I perceive, behold, my mind serving the law of God, and not being led captive under the rebelling law of sin? *I have gone around, and have sacrificed in his tabernacle a victim of jubilation*: I have considered the world believing in Christ; and in that God was humbled for us in time, rejoicing I have praised him: for with such a victim he is delighted. *I will sing and make melody to the Lord*: with heart and work I will rejoice in the Lord.

**7.** [v 7.] *Hear, Lord, my voice, with which I have cried to you*: hear, Lord, the inner voice, which with strong intention I have directed into your ears. *Have mercy on me, and hear me*: have mercy on me, and hear me in it.

**8.** [v 8.] *To you my heart has said: I have sought your face.* For I have not displayed myself to men; but in secret where you alone hear, my heart has said to you: I have sought not from you some reward outside you, but your face. *Your face, Lord, I will seek*: to this search I will perseveringly press on; for I will seek not something cheap, but your face, Lord, that I may love you freely, because I find nothing more precious.

**9.** [v 9.] *Do not turn away your face from me*: that I may find what I seek. *Do not turn aside in anger from your servant*: lest, seeking you, I run into something else. For what is heavier than this punishment for one loving and seeking the truth of your face? *Be my helper*: when shall I find this, unless you help? *Do not forsake me, nor despise me, O God my savior*: nor despise that a mortal dares to seek out the eternal; for as God you heal the wound of my sin.

**10.** [v 10.] *Because my father and my mother have forsaken me*: because the kingdom of this world and the city of this world, from which I was born temporally and mortally, have forsaken me seeking you, and despising the things they promised, since they could not give what I seek. *But the Lord has taken me up*: but the Lord, who himself can give me himself, has taken me up.

**11.** [v 11.] *Establish a law for me, Lord, in your way*: to me, then, tending toward you, and from fear beginning so great a profession of arriving at wisdom, establish a law for me, Lord, in your way, lest, as I stray, your discipline desert me. *And direct me on the straight path because of my enemies*: and direct me in the uprightness of its straits; for it is not enough to begin, since the enemies, until one arrives, do not rest.

**12.** [v 12.] *Do not hand me over to the souls of those who trouble me*: do not allow those who trouble me to be sated with my evils. *Because unjust witnesses have risen up against me*: because there have risen up against me those saying false things about me, to remove and call me back from you, as though I seek glory from men. *And iniquity has lied to itself.* And so iniquity was delighted with its own lie. For it did not move me, to whom from this a greater reward is promised in heaven.

**13.** [v 13.] *I believe I shall see the good things of the Lord, in the land of the living*: and since my Lord suffered these things first; if I too despise the tongues of the dying (for the mouth that lies kills the soul), *I believe I shall see the good things of the Lord in the land of the living,* where there is no place for falsehood.

**14.** [v 14.] *Wait for the Lord, act manfully: and let your heart be strengthened, and wait for the Lord.* But when will this be? It is hard for the mortal, it is slow for the lover: but hear the not-deceiving voice of him who says: *Wait for the Lord.* Bear the burning of the reins manfully, and the burning of the heart bravely: do not think it denied you, what you do not yet receive. Lest you fail through despair, see that it was said: *Wait for the Lord.*
