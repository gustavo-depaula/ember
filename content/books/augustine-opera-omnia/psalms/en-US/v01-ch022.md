# Exposition of Psalm 21

**1.** [v 1.] *To the end, for the morning reception, a psalm of David.* To the end, for his resurrection the Lord Jesus Christ himself speaks. For his resurrection was in the morning of the first day of the week, by which he was received into eternal life, over which death will no longer have dominion. But these things are said from the person of the crucified one: for from the head of this psalm are the words which he himself cried out when he hung on the cross, keeping also the person of the old man, whose mortality he bore. For our old man was nailed to the cross with him.

**2.** [v 2.] *God, my God, look upon me, why have you forsaken me, far from my salvation?* made far from my salvation; since salvation is far from sinners. *The words of my transgressions*: for these are the words not of righteousness, but of my transgressions. For the old man, nailed to the cross, speaks; not even knowing the cause why God has forsaken him: or surely, *The words of my transgressions are far from my salvation.*

**3.** [v 3.] *My God, I will cry to you by day, and you will not hear*: my God, I will cry to you in the prosperous things of this life, that they may not be changed; and you will not hear, because with the words of my transgressions I will cry to you. *And by night, and not unto folly for me*: and in the adverse things of this life too I will cry that they may be made prosperous, and likewise you will not hear. Nor do you do this unto folly for me, but rather that I may be wise as to what you wish me to cry; not with the words of transgressions out of desire for temporal life, but with the words of conversion to you unto eternal life.

**4.** [v 4.] *But you dwell in the holy place, the praise of Israel*: but you dwell in the holy place, and therefore you will not hear the unclean words of transgressions. The praise of him who sees you, not of him who sought his own praise in tasting the forbidden food, so that, with the bodily eyes opened, he tried to hide himself from your sight.

**5.** [v 5.] *In you our fathers hoped*: namely all the righteous, not seeking their own praise, but yours; *they hoped, and you rescued them.*

**6.** [v 6.] *To you they cried, and were saved*: to you they cried, not with the words of transgressions from which salvation is far, and therefore they were saved. *In you they hoped, and were not confounded*: in you they hoped, nor did hope deceive them; for they did not place it in themselves.

**7.** [v 7.] *But I am a worm, and not a man*: but I now, speaking not from the person of Adam, but I properly, Jesus Christ, was born in the flesh without seed, that I might be among men beyond men; that even thus human pride might deign to imitate my humility. *The reproach of men and the outcast of the people*: in which I was made the reproach of men, so that as an abusive curse it was said; *May you be his disciple*[^1], and the people despised me.

**8.** [v 8.] *All who beheld me mocked me*: all who beheld me derided me. *And they spoke with their lips, and shook the head*: and they spoke not in the heart, but with the lips.

**9.** [v 9.] For derisively they shook the head, saying: *He hoped in the Lord, let him rescue him; let him save him, since he wants him.* These were words, but they were said with the lips.

**10.** [v 10.] *Because you are he who drew me out of the womb*: because you are he who drew me out, not only of that virginal womb, for this law of being born is for all men, that they be drawn out of the womb, but also out of the womb of the Jewish nation; in whose darkness whoever places salvation in the carnal observance of the sabbath and of circumcision and other such things is covered, and is not yet born into the light of Christ. *My hope from my mother's breasts*: my hope is God, not from when I began to be suckled at the breasts of the Virgin, for surely even before; but from the breasts of the Synagogue, as I said of the womb, you drew me out, lest I should suck the carnal custom.

**11.** [v 11.] *In you I was confirmed from the womb*: this is the womb of the Synagogue, which did not bear me, but cast me out: but I did not fall, because you held me. *From the womb of my mother you are my God. From the womb of my mother*: the womb of my mother did not bring it about that, as a little one, I forgot you.

**12.** [v 12.] *You are my God, do not depart from me: because tribulation is near.* You, then, are my God, do not depart from me: because tribulation is beside me; for it is in my body. *Because there is none who helps*: for who helps, if you do not help?

**13.** [v 13.] *Many calves have surrounded me*: the multitude of the luxuriating people has surrounded me. *Fat bulls have beset me*: and its chief men, glad at my oppression, have beset me.

**14.** [v 14.] *They opened their mouth against me*: they opened their mouth against me, not from your Scriptures, but from their own desires. *Like a lion seizing and roaring*: like a lion whose prey it is, that, seized, I was led away; and its roaring, *Crucify, crucify!*[^2]

**15.** [v 15.] *Like water I was poured out, and all my bones were scattered*: like water I was poured out, where my persecutors slipped; and through fear the firm parts of my body, that is, of the Church, my disciples, were scattered from me. *My heart was made like wax melting, in the midst of my belly*: my wisdom, which in the holy books was written of me, as though hard and closed, was not understood; but after the fire of my passion drew near, as though melted, it was made manifest, and was received into the memory of my Church.

**16.** [v 16.] *My strength has dried up like a potsherd*: my strength has dried up in the passion; not like hay, but like a potsherd, which by fire is made firmer. *And my tongue has clung to my jaws*: and they have kept with themselves my precepts, those through whom I was to speak. *And you have brought me down into the dust of death*: and into the impious, destined to death, whom, like dust, the wind casts from the face of the earth, you have brought me down.

**17.** [v 17.] *Because many dogs have surrounded me*: because many, barking not for truth, but for custom, have surrounded me. *The council of the malicious has surrounded me. They have pierced my hands and feet*: they have pierced with nails my hands and feet.

**18.** [v 18.] *They have numbered all my bones*: they have numbered all my bones, stretched out on the wood of the cross. *But they themselves considered and gazed at me*: but they themselves, that is, unchanged, considered and gazed at me.

**19.** [v 19.] *They divided my garments among themselves, and over my vesture they cast lots.*

**20.** [v 20.] *But you, Lord, do not put your help far from me*: but you, Lord, not at the end of the world, like the rest; but at once raise me up. *Look to my defense*: look, lest they harm me in anything.

**21.** [v 21.] *Rescue my soul from the broadsword*: rescue my soul from the tongue of dissension. *And my only one from the hand of the dog*: and my Church from the power of the people barking out of custom.

**22.** [v 22.] *Save me from the mouth of the lion*: save me from the mouth of the worldly kingdom. *And my humility from the horns of the unicorns*: and from the heights of the proud, raising themselves singularly and not bearing partners, save my humility.

**23.** [v 23.] *I will tell of your name to my brothers*: I will tell of your name to the humble, and to those loving one another as they were loved by me, my brothers. *In the midst of the Church I will sing of you*: in the midst of the Church, rejoicing, I will proclaim you.

**24.** [v 24.] *You who fear the Lord, praise him*: you who fear the Lord, do not seek your own praise, but praise him. *All the seed of Jacob, magnify him*: all the seed of him whom the elder will serve, magnify him.

**25.** [v 25.] *Let all the seed of Israel fear him*: let all who are born to the new life, and restored to the vision of God, fear him. *Because he has not spurned nor despised the prayer of the poor*: because he has not spurned the prayer, not of him who, crying to God with the words of transgressions, was unwilling for the vain life to pass away, but the prayer of the poor man who is not swollen amid the passing pomps. *Nor has he turned away his face from me*: as from him who said, I will cry to you, and you will not hear. *And when I cried to him, he heard me.*

**26.** [v 26.] *With you is my praise*: for I do not seek my own praise, because you are my praise, who dwell in the holy place, and now hear me, holy, as I pray, the praise of Israel. *In the great Church I will confess to you*: in the Church of the whole world I will confess to you. *My vows I will render before those who fear him*: the sacraments of my body and blood I will render before those who fear him.

**27.** [v 27.] *The poor will eat, and be satisfied*: the humble and the despisers of the world will eat, and will imitate; for so they will neither covet the abundance of this world, nor fear want. *And those who seek him will praise the Lord*: for the praise of the Lord is the eructation of that satiety. *Their hearts will live unto the age of the age*: for that food is of the heart.

**28.** [v 28.] *All the ends of the earth will be reminded, and will be converted to the Lord*: they will be reminded; for God had slipped from the Gentiles, born mortally and tending toward outer things; and then all the ends of the earth will be converted to the Lord. *And all the families of the nations will adore in his sight*: and all the families of the nations will adore in their consciences.

**29.** [v 29.] *Because the kingdom is the Lord's, and he himself will rule the nations*: because the kingdom is the Lord's, not of proud men; and he himself will rule the nations.

**30.** [v 30.] *All the rich of the earth have eaten and adored*: even the rich of the earth have eaten the body of the humility of their Lord, nor have they, like the poor, been satisfied unto imitation, but yet they have adored. *In his sight all who go down into the earth will fall prostrate*: for he alone sees how all fall prostrate who, deserting the heavenly conduct, choose to appear blessed on earth to men who do not see their ruin.

**31.** [v 31.] *And my soul will live for him*: and my soul, which in the contempt of this world as it were seems to men to die, will live not for itself, but for him. *And my seed will serve him*: and my works, or those believing in him through me, will serve him.

**32.** [v 32.] *A generation to come will be proclaimed to the Lord*: the generation of the New Testament will be proclaimed unto the honor of the Lord. *And they will proclaim his righteousness*: and the Evangelists will proclaim his righteousness. *To a people that will be born, whom the Lord has made*: to a people that will be born by faith to the Lord.

[^1]: John 9:28
[^2]: Luke 23:21
