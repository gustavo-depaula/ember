# Exposition of Psalm 19

**1.** [v 1.] This title is known, nor does Christ speak, but the Prophet speaks to Christ, singing, in the figure of wishing, the things that are to come.

**2.** [v 2.] *May the Lord hear you in the day of tribulation*: may the Lord hear you in the day in which you said, *Father, glorify your Son*[^1]. *May the name of the God of Jacob protect you*: for to you belongs the people younger in birth, because the elder will serve the younger.

**3.** [v 3.] *May he send you help from the holy place, and from Zion may he watch over you*, making for you a sanctified body, the Church, kept safe from the watchtower, which awaits when you come from the wedding.

**4.** [v 4.] *May he be mindful of all your sacrifice*: may he make us mindful of all your injuries and insults, which you bore for us. *And may your whole burnt offering be made fat*: and may he turn the cross, by which you were wholly offered to God, into the gladness of the resurrection.

**5.** [v 5.] *May the Lord grant you according to your heart*: may the Lord grant you, not according to the heart of those who thought they could blot you out by persecuting; but according to your heart, by which you knew what profit your passion had. *And may he fulfill all your counsel*: and may he fulfill all your counsel, not only that by which you laid down your soul for your friends, that the grain, being put to death, might rise again more abundantly; but also that by which blindness came in part upon Israel, that the fullness of the Gentiles might enter in, and so all Israel might be saved.

**6.** [v 6.] *We will exult in your salvation*: we will exult that death will harm you nothing; for so you will show that it can harm us nothing either. *And in the name of the Lord our God we will be magnified*: and the confession of your name will not only not destroy us, but will even magnify us.

**7.** [v 7.] *May the Lord fulfill all your petitions*: may the Lord fulfill not only the petitions which you had on earth, but also those by which in heaven you intercede for us. *Now I have known that the Lord has saved his Christ*; now it has been shown to me by prophecy, that the Lord will raise up his Christ. *He will hear him from his holy heaven*: he will hear him, not from the earth only, where he asked to be glorified; but also from heaven, where now, interceding for us at the right hand of the Father, he has poured out from there the Holy Spirit upon those who believe in him. *In mighty deeds is the salvation of his right hand*: our mighty deeds are the salvation of his favor, when even out of tribulation he gives help, so that when we are weak, then we are mighty. For vain is the salvation of men, which is not of his right hand, but of his left: for by this all are lifted up into great pride, who, while sinning, have been saved in a temporal way.

**8.** [v 8.] *These in chariots and these in horses*: these are drawn by the rolling succession of temporal goods, and these are preferred in proud honors, and exult in them. *But we in the name of the Lord our God will exult*: but we, fixing our hope in eternal things, and not seeking our own glory, will exult in the name of the Lord our God.

**9.** [v 9.] *They themselves are bound, and have fallen*: and therefore they themselves, bound by the desire of temporal things, fearing to spare the Lord, lest they lose their place to the Romans, and rushing upon the stone of offense and the rock of scandal, have fallen from the heavenly hope: to whom blindness came in part upon Israel, being ignorant of God's righteousness, and wishing to establish their own. *But we have risen, and are set upright*: but we, that the people of the Gentiles might enter, sons of Abraham roused from the stones, who did not pursue righteousness, have laid hold of it, and have risen; and not by our own strength, but justified through faith, are set upright.

**10.** [v 10.] *Lord, save the king*: that he himself who showed us by his passion an example of fighting, may also offer our sacrifices, a priest roused from the dead, and set in heaven. *And hear us in the day in which we will call upon you*: he now offering for us, hear us in the day in which we will call upon you.

[^1]: John 17:1
