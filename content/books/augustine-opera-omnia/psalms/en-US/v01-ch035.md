# Exposition of Psalm 30

**1.** [v. 1.] *To the end, a psalm of David himself, of ecstasy*[^1]. To the end, a psalm of David himself, of the Mediator with a strong hand in persecutions. For the ecstasy (*ecstasis*) that is added to the title signifies a going-out of the mind, which happens either through dread or through some revelation. But in this psalm dread chiefly appears—that of the people of God troubled by the persecution of all the nations and by the failing of faith throughout the world. But first the Mediator himself speaks; then the people redeemed by his blood gives thanks; at the last the troubled one speaks at length, which belongs to the ecstasy: and the person of the Prophet himself is interposed twice, near the end and at the end.

**2.** [v. 2.] *In you, O Lord, I have hoped; let me not be confounded forever*[^2]: in you, O Lord, I have hoped; let me never be confounded, while I am insulted as a man like the rest. *In your righteousness rescue me, and deliver me*[^3]: and in your righteousness rescue me from the pit of death, and deliver me from the number of them.

**3.** [v. 3.] *Incline your ear to me*[^4]: hear me in my lowliness, being near to me. *Make haste to deliver me*[^5]: do not defer to the end of the world, as you do for all who believe in me, my separation from sinners. *Be to me a protecting God*[^6]: be a protecting God to me. *And a house of refuge, that you may save me*[^7]: and, as a house, to which I may flee and be made safe.

**4.** [v. 4.] *For you are my strength and my refuge*[^8]: for you are my strength to endure my persecutors, and my refuge to which I may withdraw. *And for your name's sake you will be my guide, and you will nourish me*[^9]: and so that through me you may be made known to all the nations, in all things I will follow your will, and, the saints being gradually gathered to me, you will fill up my body, and my perfect stature.

**5.** [v. 5.] *You will bring me out of this snare, which they have hidden for me*[^10]: you teach me concerning these ambushes which they have hidden for me. *For you are my protector*.

**6.** [v. 6.] *Into your hands I commend my spirit*[^11]: into your power I commend my spirit, soon to take it back. *You have redeemed me, O Lord, God of truth*. Let the people too say, redeemed by the passion of its Lord and glad at the glorifying of its head: *You have redeemed me, O Lord, God of truth*.

**7.** [v. 7.] *You have hated those who keep watch over vanity to no purpose*[^12]: you have hated those who keep watch over the false happiness of the world. *But I have hoped in the Lord*.

**8.** [v. 8.] *I will exult, and be glad in your mercy*[^13]: which does not deceive me. *For you have looked upon my lowliness*[^14]: by which you subjected me to vanity in hope. *You have saved my soul from its necessities*[^15]: you have saved my soul from the necessities of fear, that it may serve you with free love.

**9.** [v. 9.] *And you have not shut me up in the hands of the enemy*[^16]: and you have not shut me up so that I should have no way to breathe again into freedom, and should be given over into the everlasting power of the devil, who ensnares with the desire of this life and terrifies with death. *You have set my feet in a spacious place*[^17]: my love, recognizing the resurrection of my Lord and my own promised to me, abiding, ranges abroad in the breadth of freedom, led out from the straits of fear.

**10.** [v. 10.] *Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am in tribulation*[^18]. But what is this unhoped-for cruelty of my persecutors, striking great dread into me? *Have mercy on me, O Lord*. For I am no longer terrified of death, but of tortures and torments. *My eye is troubled in anger*[^19]: I had my eye on you, that you should not forsake me; you were angry, and troubled it. *My soul and my belly*[^20]: in the same anger my soul was troubled, and the memory by which I held what God had endured for me, and what my God had promised me.

**11.** [v. 11.] *For my life has failed in sorrow*[^21]: for my life is to confess you, but it has failed in sorrow, when the enemy said, Let them be tortured until they deny. *And my years in groanings*[^22]: the times I spend in this world are not taken from me by death, but remain, and are in groanings. *My vigor has been weakened in want*[^23]: I lack the health of this body, and I am not spared the tortures; I lack the dissolution of the body, and death is withheld: and in this want my confidence has been weakened. *And my bones have been troubled*[^24]: and my firmness has been troubled.

**12.** [v. 12.] *Above all my enemies I have become a reproach*[^25]. My enemies are all the wicked; and yet for their crimes they too are tortured until they confess: I have therefore surpassed their reproach, whose confession death does not follow, but torture pursues. *And to my neighbors exceedingly*[^26]: this seemed too much to those who were already drawing near to know you and to hold the faith I hold. *And a fear to my acquaintances*[^27]: and into my very acquaintances I struck fear by the example of my horrible tribulation. *Those who saw me fled outside from me*[^28]: because they did not understand my inner and invisible hope, they fled from me into outward and visible things.

**13.** [v. 13.] *I am forgotten, as one dead, from the heart*[^29]: and they forgot me, as though I were dead from their heart. *I have become like a broken vessel*[^30]: I seemed to myself to have perished from the uses of the Lord, living in this world and gaining no one, since all feared to be gathered to me.

**14.** [v. 14.] *For I have heard the reproach of many who dwell round about*[^31]: for I have heard many reproaching me on the pilgrimage of this earth beside me, following the round of the seasons and refusing to return with me into the eternal homeland. *While they gathered together against me, they took counsel to take away my soul*[^32]: so that my soul, which could easily by death go out of their power, should consent to them, they devised a plan whereby they would not even let me die.

**15.** [v. 15.] *But I have hoped in you, O Lord; I said: You are my God*[^33]: for you are not changed, so as not to save the one whom you amend.

**16.** [v. 16.] *In your hands are my lots*[^34]: in your power are my lots. For I see no merit by which, out of the whole impiety of the human race, you chose me above all for salvation: and if with you there is a righteous and hidden order of my election, I, from whom this lies hidden, have come by lot to the tunic of my Lord. *Rescue me from the hands of my enemies, and from those who persecute me*.

**17.** [v. 17.] *Make your face shine upon your servant*[^35]: make known to men who do not think I belong to you, that your face is bent upon me, and that I serve you. *Save me in your mercy*.

**18.** [v. 18.] *O Lord, let me not be confounded, for I have called upon you*[^36]: O Lord, let me not blush before those who insult me, for the reason that I have called upon you. *Let the ungodly be ashamed, and be brought down to hell*[^37]: rather let those be ashamed who call upon stones, and let them be joined to the shades.

**19.** [v. 19.] *Let the deceitful lips be made dumb*[^38]: making known to the peoples your mysteries in me, make dumbstruck the lips of those who invent falsehoods about me. *Which speak iniquity against the righteous one, in pride and contempt*[^39]: which speak iniquity against Christ, being proud and contemptuous, as of a crucified man.

**20.** [vv. 20-21.] *How great is the multitude of your sweetness, O Lord*[^40]: here the Prophet cries out, seeing these things and marveling in how many ways your sweetness is abundant, O Lord. *Which you have hidden for those who fear you*[^41]: even those whom you amend you love greatly; but lest in dissolute security they act more carelessly, you hide from them the sweetness of your love—from those for whom it is useful to fear you. *You have perfected it for those who hope in you*[^42]: but you have perfected this sweetness for those who hope in you; for you do not withdraw from them what they perseveringly await to the end. *In the sight of the sons of men*[^43]: for it is not hidden from the sons of men, now living not according to Adam but according to the Son of man: *You will hide them in the hiddenness of your countenance*—what perpetual seat you keep in the hiddenness of your knowledge for those who hope in you. *From the troubling of men*[^44]: so that they may now suffer no human troubling.

**21.** *You will protect them in your tabernacle, from the contradiction of tongues*[^45]: but here in the meantime, while slanderous tongues clamor against them, saying, Who has known this, or who has come from there?, you will protect them in the tabernacle of faith in those things which the Lord did and endured for us in time.

**22.** [v. 22.] *Blessed be the Lord, for he has wondrously shown his mercy in the city round about*[^46]: blessed be the Lord, for after the amending of the most bitter persecutions he made his mercy wonderful to all throughout the world, in the circuit of human society.

**23.** [v. 23.] *I said in my ecstasy*[^47]: whence that people, speaking again, says: I said in my dread, when the nations were savagely raging against me: *I am cast away from the sight of your eyes*[^48]: for if you were looking upon me, you would not let me suffer these things. *Therefore you have heard, O Lord, the voice of my prayer, when I cried to you*[^49]: therefore, setting a limit to the amending, and showing that I belong to your care, you have heard, O Lord, the voice of my prayer, when out of tribulation I strained it exceedingly.

**24.** [v. 24.] *Love the Lord, all you his holy ones*[^50]: again the Prophet, seeing these things, exhorts, and says: *Love the Lord, all you his holy ones: for the Lord will require truth*. For if the righteous man will scarcely be saved, where will the sinner and the ungodly appear? *And he will repay those who abundantly work pride*[^51]: and he will repay those who do not turn back even when overcome, because they are exceedingly proud.

**25.** [v. 25.] *Be strong, and let your heart take courage*[^52]: working good without failing, that in due season you may reap. *All you who hope in the Lord*[^53]: that is, you who rightly fear or worship him, hope in the Lord.
