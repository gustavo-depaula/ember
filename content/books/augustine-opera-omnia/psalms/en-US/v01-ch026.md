# Exposition of Psalm 24

**1.** [v 1.] *To the end, a psalm of David himself.* Christ speaks, but in the person of the Church: for the things said pertain rather to the Christian people converted to God.

**2.** [v 2.] *To you, Lord, I have lifted up my soul*: with spiritual desire, the soul which was trodden down on earth by carnal desires. *My God, in you I trust, let me not be ashamed*: my God, from that which I trusted in myself, I was brought even to this weakness of the flesh; and I who, having deserted God, wished to be like God, fearing death from the tiniest little beast, was ashamed, mocked for my pride; now, then, I trust in you, let me not be ashamed.

**3.** [v 3.] *Nor let my enemies mock me*: nor let those mock me who, lying in wait with serpentine and hidden suggestions, and suggesting "Well done, well done," cast me down to these things. *For all who await you will not be confounded.*

**4.** [v 4.] *Let those be confounded who do vain things unjustly*: let those be confounded who act unjustly, to acquire the things that pass away. *Make your ways known to me, Lord, and teach me your paths*: which are not broad, nor lead the multitude to destruction; but teach me your paths, narrow and known to few.

**5.** [v 5.] *Direct me in your truth*: as I flee from errors. *And teach me*: for through myself I have known nothing but a lie. *Because you are God my savior, and I have waited for you all the day long*: for, dismissed by you from paradise, and wandering into a far country, I cannot return through myself, unless you meet me as I err; for my return has, through the whole stretch of worldly time, waited for your mercy.

**6.** [v 6.] *Remember your acts of compassion, Lord*: remember the works of your mercy, Lord, because men think you as it were forgetful. *And that your mercies are from of old*: and remember this, that your mercies are from of old. For you were never without them, you who even subjected the sinning man to vanity, yet in hope, and did not desert him with so many and so great consolations of your creation.

**7.** [v 7.] *The transgressions of my youth and of my ignorance do not remember*: the transgressions of my presumptuous boldness and of my ignorance do not reserve for vengeance, but as it were let them slip from you. *According to your mercy be mindful of me, O God*: be mindful indeed of me, not according to the anger of which I am worthy, but according to your mercy which is worthy of you. *On account of your goodness, Lord*: not on account of my merit, but on account of your goodness, Lord.

**8.** [v 8.] *The Lord is sweet and upright*: the Lord is sweet, since he has so had compassion even on sinners and the impious that he forgave all former things; but the Lord is also upright, who, after the mercy of calling and pardon, which has grace without merits, will require merits worthy of the last judgment. *On account of this he will establish a law for those who transgress on the way*: because he bestowed mercy beforehand, that he might bring them onto the way.

**9.** [v 9.] *He will direct the meek in judgment*: he will direct the meek, and will not trouble in judgment those who follow his will, and do not, by resisting him, prefer their own. *He will teach the gentle his ways*: he will teach his ways, not those who wish to run ahead, as though they could better rule themselves; but those who do not lift up the neck, nor kick back, when the easy yoke and light burden is laid upon them.

**10.** [v 10.] *All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth.* But what ways will he teach them, but the mercy by which he is appeasable, and the truth by which he is incorrupt? Of which the one he offered by forgiving sins, the other by judging merits. And therefore all the ways of the Lord are the two comings of the Son of God, one of him having mercy, the other of him judging. He, then, comes to him, holding his ways, who, seeing himself set free by no merits of his own, lays aside pride, and thereafter, having experienced the clemency of the one coming to his aid, guards against the severity of the one examining. *To those who seek his testament and his testimonies*: for they understand the Lord merciful at the first coming, and a judge at the second, the meek and gentle who seek his testament, by which with his blood he redeemed us into a new life; and in the Prophets and Evangelists his testimonies.

**11.** [v 11.] *On account of your name, Lord, you will be propitious to my sin; for it is much*: you have not only forgiven my sins, which I committed before I believed; but to my sin too, which is much, because even on the way there is no lack of offense, by the sacrifice of a contrite spirit you will be propitious.

**12.** [v 12.] *Who is the man who fears the Lord?* whence he begins to come to wisdom. *He will establish a law for him in the way which he chose*: he will establish a law for him in the way which, being free, he laid hold of, lest he now sin with impunity.

**13.** [v 13.] *His soul will dwell among good things, and his seed will possess the earth as an inheritance*: and his work will possess as a solid inheritance the restored body.

**14.** [v 14.] *The Lord is the firmament of those who fear him*: fear seems to belong to the weak, but the Lord is the firmament of those who fear him. And the name of the Lord, which has been glorified throughout the world, makes firm those who fear him. *And his testament, that it may be made manifest to them*: and he brings it about that his testament is made manifest to them, because the inheritance of Christ is the nations, and the ends of the earth.

**15.** [v 15.] *My eyes are always toward the Lord; because he will pluck my feet out of the snare*: nor let me fear earthly dangers, while I do not gaze upon the earth; since he whom I gaze upon will pluck my feet out of the snare.

**16.** [v 16.] *Look upon me, and have mercy on me; because I am alone and poor*: because I am the only people, keeping the humility of your one Church, which no schisms or heresies hold.

**17.** [v 17.] *The tribulations of my heart have been multiplied*: the tribulations of my heart, iniquity abounding and love growing cold, have been multiplied. *Lead me out of my necessities*: since it is necessary for me to endure these things, that, persevering to the end, I may be saved, lead me out of my necessities.

**18.** [v 18.] *See my humility and my labor*: see my humility, by which I never tear myself away from unity by a boasting of righteousness; and my labor, by which I bear with the undisciplined mixed in with me. *And forgive all my sins*: and, propitiated by these sacrifices, forgive my sins, not only those of my youth and ignorance before I believed, but also these which, now living by faith, I commit through weakness or the fog of this life.

**19.** [v 19.] *Look upon my enemies, because they are multiplied*: for they are not lacking not only outside, but even within the very communion of the Church. *And with an unjust hatred they have hated me*: and they have hated me, who love them.

**20.** [v 20.] *Guard my soul, and rescue me*: guard my soul, lest I turn aside to the imitation of them; and rescue me from the perplexity by which they are mixed with me. *Let me not be confounded, because I have hoped in you*: let me not be confounded if perhaps they rise up against me; because I have hoped not in myself, but in you.

**21.** [v 21.] *The innocent and upright have clung to me, because I have waited for you, Lord*: the innocent and upright are not mixed only by bodily presence, like the evil, but by the consent of the heart cling to me in that very innocence and uprightness; because I did not fail, so as to imitate the evil; but I waited for you, awaiting the winnowing of your last harvest.

**22.** [v 22.] *Redeem, O God, Israel from all his tribulations*: redeem, O God, your people, whom you have prepared for the vision of you, from his tribulations, not only those which he endures outside, but also those which he endures within.
