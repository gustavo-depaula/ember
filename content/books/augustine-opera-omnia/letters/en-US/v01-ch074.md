# Letter 74

To the most blessed and deservedly venerable lord, brother and fellow priest Praesidius, Augustine sends greeting in the Lord

**1.** As I asked your Sincerity in person, so now too I admonish, that you not be reluctant to send my letter to the holy brother and our fellow presbyter Jerome. But that your Charity may know how you too ought to write to him on my behalf, I have sent copies of the letters, both of mine to him, and of his to me, on reading which, by your holy prudence, you may easily see both my measure, which I thought should be kept, and his motion, which I not in vain feared. Or if I have written anything which I ought not, or in a way I ought not; send a discourse, not to him about me, but rather to myself, with brotherly love, by which, corrected, I may ask him to pardon, if I shall myself have recognized my fault.
