# Letter 269

TO NOBILIUS, A MOST BLESSED AND VENERABLE BROTHER AND FELLOW PRIEST, AUGUSTINE.

**1.** So great is the solemnity to which the affection of your Fraternity invites me, that the will would drag my poor body to you, did not weakness hold it back. I could come if it were not winter; I could despise the winter, if I were young: for either the fervor of age would bear the rigor of the season, or the fervor of summer would temper the cold of age. Now, in winter, I cannot endure so long a journey, with the chilly oldness which I carry with me, most blessed lord, holy and venerable brother and fellow priest. I render the greeting due to your merits: but my welfare I commend to your prayers, asking myself too of the Lord that the prosperity of peace may attend the dedication of so great a building.
