# Letter 17\*

TO THE EXCELLENT LORD, DESERVEDLY DISTINGUISHED AND MOST BELOVED SON BONIFACE, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD

**1.** An opportunity has been given me to greet your Charity—which I know is most welcome to you—since there had put in at our shore men who had to hurry on to you. Considering, then, in them both the mercy that one man owes another and your love, we took them in, half shipwrecked as they were, and supported their destitution as far as we could. For, gravely tossed and endangered by the great violence of sky and sea, they had barely escaped death and had lost whatever they were able to keep.

**2.** And so, excellent and deservedly distinguished and most beloved son, by God's favor we announce our good health to your Sincerity, hearing all good things about you and ever longing to hear them, since your most excellent reputation is the glory of him in whom you have placed your hope, whom you fear, worship, and love, and whose eternal peace you seek even amid the works of war itself, so long as you keep faith and love justice in all things.
