# Letter 5

Nebridius to Augustine

**1.** *Is it so, my Augustine? You devote your courage and endurance to the affairs of your fellow citizens, and still that longed-for rest is not given back to you? I ask you, what men interrupt you who are so good? I suppose those who do not know what you love, what you desire. Have you no friend who would tell them of your loves? Not Romanianus, not Lucinianus? Let them at least listen to me. I will cry out, I will testify that you love God, that you long to serve him and to cling to him. I would like to call you to my country estate and have you find rest there. For I shall not fear to be called a seducer of you by your fellow citizens, whom you love too much, and by whom you are too much loved.*
