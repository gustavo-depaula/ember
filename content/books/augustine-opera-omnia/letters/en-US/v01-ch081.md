# Letter 81

TO THE TRULY HOLY AND MOST BLESSED LORD POPE AUGUSTINE, JEROME SENDS GREETING IN CHRIST.

**1.** *When I was anxiously asking our holy brother Firmus how you were, I was glad to hear that you are safe. And again, when I was, I will not say hoping for, but demanding your letters, he said that he had set out from Africa without your knowledge. And so I render to you through him the offices of greeting, who embraces you with a singular love; and at the same time I beseech you to pardon my modesty, in that I could not refuse one who long enjoined me to write back. Nor did I answer you, but cause answered cause. And if it is a fault to have answered, much greater is it to have provoked. But let complaints of this sort be done away; let there be between us pure brotherhood; and henceforth let us send to each other writings not of questions, but of love. The holy brothers* *who serve the Lord with us greet you abundantly. I beg that you greet, with my homage, the saints who with you draw the light yoke of Christ, especially the holy and worthy-to-be-received Pope Alypius. May Christ our God almighty keep you safe and mindful of me, truly holy and most blessed lord Pope. If you have read the book of Explanations on Jonah, I think you will not take up the ridiculous question of the gourd. But if a friend who first attacked me with the sword has been repelled with the pen, let it be the part of your humanity and justice to rebuke the accuser, not the one who answers. In the field of the Scriptures, if it please you, let us sport without mutual hurt to one another.*
