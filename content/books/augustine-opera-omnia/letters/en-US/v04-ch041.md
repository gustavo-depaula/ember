# Letter 249

TO RESTITUTUS, A MOST LONGED-FOR LORD, AND, WITH HONORABLE SINCERITY, A MOST DEAR BROTHER AND FELLOW DEACON, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** The ardors indicating the devout flame of your heart, the most faithful sharer of them, as you know, brother Deogratias, indicated to me. Read, therefore, Tychonius, whom you know well—not indeed about to approve everything; for what is to be guarded against in him, you know well. Yet this question—how, in the Church of God, if perhaps we are unable to correct or extinguish certain perverse or even wicked things, they are to be tolerated with the bond of unity preserved—he seems to me to have treated and resolved vigorously. Although, the intention in his writings being only corrected, we ought to run back to the very fountains of the divine Scriptures, that there we may see how few testimonies of sentences, or examples of deeds, he set down about this matter, and how no one could set down all, except one who wished to transfer almost all the pages of the holy Books into his own writings: so nearly is there none that does not admonish us that, within, in the very fellowship of the Sacraments with which we are imbued for eternal life, we ought to be peaceable with those who hate peace, until, groaning, our far pilgrimage passes[^1]; and in the strength of Jerusalem our eternal mother we may fully enjoy a most secure peace, and in her towers an abundance of true brothers[^2], whose fewness we now groan over amid many false ones. But what is the strength of that city, but its God our God? You see, therefore, in whom alone peace comes to be, both for individual men who wage war with themselves without him, even with no scandal arising from without; and for all together, who, although in this life they love one another, and are held by the bonds of faithful friendship, yet are not coupled, neither by the presence of the body nor by consent of mind, supremely and perfectly. May your heart be strengthened in the Lord, mindful of us.

[^1]: See Ps 120:6-7 (119:6-7)
[^2]: See Ps 122:7 (121:7)
