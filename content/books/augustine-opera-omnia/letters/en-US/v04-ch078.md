# Letter 14\*

TO DOROTHEUS, A DISTINGUISHED AND DESERVEDLY EMINENT AND HONORABLE LORD AND SON, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** I know how much you love Christ, and, because your whole household is his family—as the Apostle says, *a domestic Church*[^1]—and how much you wish the possession of Christ to bear fruit and grow in your possessions, is known to all of us who know you, distinguished and deservedly eminent and honorable lord and son.

**2.** I had something to complain of about a man of yours, whose name and sacrilegious crime I did not dare to intimate in this letter, lest perhaps you be more gravely angered and avenge it more atrociously than it is fitting and right to apply vengeance to an episcopal complaint. And so, to the brothers from whom Your Excellence receives this letter, I have made plain through a memorandum what that was, and asked that they first hold the promise of your most faithful religion that you will not avenge it further than what is comprehended in that same memorandum, and, when they hold it, show it to you to read. But if you are unwilling to promise, let them indicate nothing; but through Christ I beseech that you rather promise, so that we too may be able securely to bring to you whatever the necessity of our care demands, that it may be amended by you, if it cannot be by us.

[^1]: See Rom 16:5
