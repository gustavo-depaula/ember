# Letter 184

Innocent, to the bishops Aurelius and Augustine.

**1.** The return of our most acceptable fellow presbyter Germanus ought not to be empty of our office. For to greet most dear friends through dear ones seems to us in a way natural and consequent. We desire, then, most lovingly, that your brotherhood rejoice in the Lord, and we pray that you repay equal vows to God for us, because, as you well know, we accomplish more by common and mutual prayers than by single or private ones.
