# Letter 252

TO FELIX, A MOST BELOVED AND DESERVEDLY HONORABLE LORD AND BROTHER TO BE PREFERRED, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** Your Reverence knows very well how much care the Church or the bishops owe to protecting all men, but most of all orphans. Hence, having received your letter, and a copy of the letter of the distinguished man, our brother, I neither could nor ought to entrust the girl indiscriminately to anyone: especially because he commended her to the Church, most beloved and deservedly honorable lord, and brother to be preferred. Accordingly, I await her coming, so that, if anything ought to be done, I may deliberate by her presence, and do what the Lord inspires ought to be done.
