# Letter 202

TO THE TRULY HOLY LORDS, AND TO BE VENERATED WITH ALL AFFECTION AND RIGHT, THE BISHOPS ALYPIUS AND AUGUSTINE, FROM JEROME, GREETING IN CHRIST.

**1.** The holy presbyter Innocent, who is the bearer of this discourse, last year, as though he were by no means going to return to Africa, did not take up my writings to your Dignity. Yet we give thanks to God that it so came about that you overcame our silence by your letters. For to me every occasion is most welcome through which I write to your Reverence; calling God to witness that, if it could be, I would, taking the wings of a dove, entwine myself in your embraces—always indeed for the merit of your virtues, but now especially because, with you as fellow workers and authors, the Celestian heresy has been throttled: which has so infected the hearts of many that, although they feel themselves overcome and condemned, they nevertheless do not lay aside the venoms of their minds; and, what alone they can do, they hate us, through whom they think they have lost the liberty of teaching the heresy.

**2.** But as for what you ask, whether I have written back against the books of Annianus, the pseudo-deacon of Celeda, who is most copiously fed, that he may supply the frivolous words of another's blasphemy: know that I received those very books, sent in pamphlets by the holy brother Eusebius the presbyter, not long ago; and that since then, either by reason of oncoming illnesses, or by the falling-asleep of your holy and venerable daughter Eustochium, I have so grieved that I thought them almost to be despised. For he sticks in the same mud, and, except for tinkling and begged-for words, says nothing else. Yet we have accomplished much, in that, while he tries to answer my letter, he betrayed himself more openly, and laid open his blasphemies to all. For whatever he denies he said in that wretched synod of Diospolis, he professes in this work; nor is it a great thing to answer most inept dirges. But if the Lord grants life and we have a supply of secretaries, we will answer in a few late-night labors—not to convince a heresy that is dead, but to refute its inexperience and blasphemy by our discourses: and your Holiness would do this better, lest we be compelled, against a heretic, to praise our own. The common holy children, Albina, Pinianus, and Melania, greet you very much. These little letters I have handed over from holy Bethlehem to be carried by the holy presbyter Innocent. Your granddaughter Paula prays pitiably that you be mindful of her, and greets you much. May the clemency of our Lord Jesus Christ keep you unharmed and mindful of me, truly holy lords and fathers to be venerated with the affection of all.
