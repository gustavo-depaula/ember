# Letter 215A

TO THE MOST BELOVED LORD AND BROTHER VALENTINUS, TO BE EMBRACED IN THE HEART OF CHRIST, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** I give great thanks indeed to your Charity, that you sent me brother Florus when I desired him, and fuller thanks to our God, because I found him such as I had hoped. But although he seems to have returned to you rather late, he was nonetheless with me less than I would have wished. For while he was present, an infirmity of body, of such a kind and lasting so many days, kept me, so that I could not be with him, most beloved lord and brother, to be embraced in the heart of Christ. Hence I ask again that you deign to fulfill now not my desire only but that of us both, and to send him again, that he may be with us for some time. For I think this will not be unfruitful either for him or for us, and that his more abundant instruction in whatever he may, with the Lord's help, be able to be for the Lord through us, will profit the brothers also. May you ever please God.
