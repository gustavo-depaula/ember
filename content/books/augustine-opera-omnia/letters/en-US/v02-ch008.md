# Letter 107

To the most blessed and venerable and much-longed-for father Augustine, Maximus and Theodore send greetings in the Lord

**1.** At the command of Your Holiness we went to bishop Macrobius; and when we brought him the letter of Your Beatitude, he at first refused to have it read to him. Then, at length moved by our suggestion, he wished it to be recited to him; and when it had been read, he said: "I cannot but receive those who come to me, and give them the faith they have asked for." But when he was asked by us what he would say about Primian's deed, he said that he, recently ordained, could not be the judge of his own father, but that he remained in what he had received from his predecessors. This we had to make known to Your Holiness by this letter. May the Lord guard Your Beatitude for us, lord father.
