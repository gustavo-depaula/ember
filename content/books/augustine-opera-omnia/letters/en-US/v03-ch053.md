# Letter 203

TO LARGUS, DISTINGUISHED AND MOST EXCELLENT LORD, AND MOST LONGED-FOR SON, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETING IN THE LORD.

**1.** I have received the letter of your Eximiousness, in which you asked me to write to you. Which indeed you would not desire, unless you held also this—what you supposed I could write—agreeable and pleasant. And that is, that the vanities of this age, if you desired them untried, you should despise once tried. For in them the sweetness is deceitful, the labor fruitless, the fear perpetual, the height perilous. The beginning is without foresight, and the end with repentance. So it is with all things which, in this hardship of mortality, are sought more covetously than prudently. But another is the hope of the pious, another the fruit of their labor, another the reward of their perils. For in this world it is impossible not to fear, not to grieve, not to labor, not to be in peril: but it makes the greatest difference for what cause, with what expectation, with what end each one suffers these things. I indeed, when I behold the lovers of this age, do not know when wisdom can be opportune for healing their minds: for when they have things as it were prosperous, with haughtiness they reject wholesome warnings, and reckon them as an old wives' refrain; but when they are in adversity, they think rather of escaping that by which they are presently distressed, than of taking that by which they may be cured, and by which they may arrive where they can be distressed no longer. Sometimes, however, certain ones apply and bring the ears of the heart to the truth—more rarely amid prosperity, more often amid adversity; but yet they are few, for so they were foretold; among whom I desire you to be, because I truly love you, distinguished and most excellent lord and most longed-for son. Let this admonition be my return of greeting to you; because, although I do not wish you henceforth to suffer such things as you have endured, I nevertheless grieve more that you have endured these very things without any change of life for the better.
