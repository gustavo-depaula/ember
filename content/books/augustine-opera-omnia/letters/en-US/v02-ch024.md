# Letter 123

**1.** Many limp on both feet, and are not bowed even when their necks are broken, holding the affection of their former error, though they do not have the same liberty of preaching it. The holy brothers who are with our littleness, and especially your holy and venerable daughters, supplicantly greet you. Your brothers, my lord Alypius and my lord Evodius, I beg your crown to greet in my name. Jerusalem, captured, is held by Nebuchadnezzar, nor will it hear the counsels of Jeremiah; nay rather it desires Egypt, that it may die in Tahpanhes, and there perish in everlasting servitude.
