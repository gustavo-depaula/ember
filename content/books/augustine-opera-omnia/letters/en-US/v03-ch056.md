# Letter 206

TO VALERIUS, DESERVEDLY ILLUSTRIOUS AND MOST EXCELLENT LORD, AND MOST DEAR SON IN CHRIST, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETING IN THE LORD.

**1.** As often as men ask of me that I commend them to your benignity and faith, if I do not do it, I do not seem to myself to judge rightly of your mercy toward those in need of help, or of your benevolence toward us. And so I do it, and especially the ministers of Christ, who bear the care of the Church, of which we rejoice that you are a coheir and son, I do not hesitate to commend to your Excellency, deservedly illustrious and most excellent lord, and most dear son in Christ. And so, since our holy brother and fellow bishop Felix asked this of me, I surely ought not to refuse. I commend to you, then, a bishop of Christ in need of the help of an exalted man: do, then, what you can; since the Lord has granted you to be able to do very much, whose gains we know you to be most eager for.
