# Letter 21\*

TO THE MOST BELOVED LORDS, HIS SONS THE PEOPLE OF SUPPA, WHOSE LETTER I HAVE RECEIVED, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD

**1.** Since I know that, by God's favor, you have the venerable brother and my fellow bishop, the bishop Honoratus, you ought not to have sent a letter to me apart from his counsel and command; but because you have asked for this, in which he, knowing ecclesiastical discipline, could not consent to you, you therefore wished to write to me apart from his authority. Let this very brief reply, then, suffice for your Love. The man you ask for is not such that, through him, as you have written, the moderation of our discipline should come to you; the rest of what I thought about him I judged should rather be written to my holy brother, your bishop. But if you wish to have him as a fellow citizen, I do not forbid it—indeed I even commend it—provided you know that he cannot be a deacon.
