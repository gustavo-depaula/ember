# Letter 23\*

TO THE LORD, DESERVEDLY MOST BELOVED AND TO BE PROCLAIMED AND HONORED IN CHRIST, THE BROTHER RENATUS, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD

**1.** An excellent opportunity has presented itself for me to send to your Holiness, through him, the book I had promised, through whom you had sent to me the books of our son Victor to which I was to reply, [...] O lord, deservedly most beloved and to be proclaimed and honored in Christ, brother. I ask, moreover, that after the departure from there of the servant of God Marcellinus, our son, you not find it burdensome to instruct us about what has followed concerning them, the sooner as I have learned that the bearers will return. We are in doubt, however, about the letter of the brothers that he brought to us, because we did not recognize the subscription of the holy primate, and in that gathering of fifteen bishops we have heard that he was not present, although neither do we hold it certain whether the others were there and themselves wrote back. Hence we ask that you make us most certain by some indications about which there can be no doubt, my lord.

**2.** The bishop Honorius is with us, promising that, for the peace of the Church and ecclesiastical discipline, he will do nothing at all other than what we wish. May the Lord grant that he speak the truth. For just as we cannot take the evils said about him in such a way as to think that he is already to be condemned—since this too does not belong to the ecclesiastical form—so neither do we easily believe him, unless he confirm his words by deeds [...].
