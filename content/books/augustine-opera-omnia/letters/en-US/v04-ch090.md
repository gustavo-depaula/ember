# Letter 25\*

TO THE MOST BELOVED AND MOST SINCERE LORDS, THE BROTHERS AND FELLOW PRIESTS DEOGRATIAS AND THEODORUS, AND THE DEACONS TITIANUS, QUINTIANUS, AND QUODVULTDEUS, AND THE MOST BELOVED [\*\*\*], AND BROTHER COMES, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD

**1.** We announce that, by God's favor, we have arrived safely home and have celebrated the solemnity of the most blessed martyr with the people of God, who were murmuring much about my absence—which we trust is most welcome to you. I commend, moreover, the bearer, our brother the priest Mascelio, whom I believe your Holiness remembers, who also asks you, brother Comes, through me, that you have provided to him without difficulty the lodging he requests.
