# Letter 171

To the most blessed lord, and venerably most dear brother and fellow bishop Peregrinus, from Alypius and Augustine, greetings in the Lord.

**1.** We have sent a letter to our honorable brother Maximus, believing that he would receive it gratefully: whether, however, we have accomplished anything, deign to write back at the next occasion you can find. Let him know, indeed, that we are accustomed to write lengthy letters to our most intimate friends, not only laymen, but even bishops, in the way this is written, to them—so that they are both quickly written, and the paper is more conveniently held when they are read—lest perhaps, not knowing this custom of ours, he should think an injury has been done him.
