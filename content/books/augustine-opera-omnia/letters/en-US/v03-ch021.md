# Letter 174

To the most blessed lord, and to be revered with most sincere love, holy brother and fellow priest, pope Aurelius, from Augustine, greetings in the Lord.

**1.** The books on the Trinity, who is the supreme and true God, I began as a young man and published as an old man. For I had set aside this work after I learned that they had been snatched or stolen from me before I had finished them and revised them, so as to polish them as my plan had been. For I had decided to publish them, not one by one, but all together, for this reason: since the consequent ones are knit to the preceding by an advancing inquiry. Since, then, through those men—because before I wished, they could reach some of them—my plan could not be fulfilled, I had left the dictation interrupted, thinking to complain of this very thing in some of my writings, so that those who could might know that those same books had not been published by me, but taken away before they seemed to me worthy of my publication. But, compelled by the most vehement demand of many brothers, and especially by your command, I took care, with the Lord's help, to finish so laborious a work; and, emended not as I wished but as I could, lest they should differ very much from those which, stolen, had already gone out into the hands of men, I have sent them to your veneration through our son, the fellow deacon Cresimus, and have permitted them to be heard, copied, and read by anyone. In which, if my plan could have been kept, they would surely, even though having the same opinions, be much more disentangled and plainer, as far as the difficulty of explaining such great matters and our capacity would allow. But there are some who have the first four, or rather even five, without prefaces, and the twelfth without a not small final part: but if this edition can become known to them, they will, if they wish and are able, emend all. I ask, indeed, that you order this letter to be placed before—separately, indeed, but yet at the head of those same books. Farewell. Pray for me.
