# Letter 50

To the leaders and chief men or elders of the colony of Sufes, Augustine the bishop

**1.** The most notorious crime of your monstrousness, and your unexpected cruelty, shakes the earth, and strikes the heaven, so that in your streets and shrines blood gleams, and homicide resounds. Among you the Roman laws are buried, the terror of upright judgments is trodden underfoot. Of the emperors, surely, there is no veneration nor fear. Among you the innocent blood of sixty brothers in number has been shed, and if anyone killed more, he was discharged with praises, and held the chief place in your senate-house. Come, now let us come to the principal cause. If you say it is your Hercules, well then, we will restore him: there are mines at hand, and stones are not lacking; there are added also kinds of marble, a supply of craftsmen is at hand. Besides, your god, while he is diligently sculpted, is turned on the lathe and adorned. We add also the red ochre which paints the redness, by which your sacred vows can resound. For if you say it is your Hercules, individual coins being collected, we buy you a god from your own craftsman. Restore, then, the souls which your savage hand has wrenched away, and, just as your Hercules is restored by us, so also let the souls of so many be restored by you.
