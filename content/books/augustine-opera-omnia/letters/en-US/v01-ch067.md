# Letter 67

To the most dear and most longed-for and, in Christ, to be honored brother and fellow presbyter Jerome, Augustine sends greeting in the Lord

**1. 1.** I have heard that my letter has come into your hands; but, that I have not yet deserved a reply, I would by no means impute to your love: there was without doubt some impediment. Whence I recognize that the Lord is rather to be beseeched by me, that he give to your will the means of sending what you have written back: for the means of writing back he has already given, since, when you wish, you will most easily be able.

**2. 2.** This too, surely brought to me—whether indeed I should believe it I doubted; but whether I should write something about it too to you, I ought not to doubt: but it was told me that this was briefly suggested to your Charity by I know not what brothers, that I had written a book against you, and sent it to Rome. Know that this is false; I call our God to witness, that I have not done this. But if perhaps some things are found in some writings of mine, in which I am found to have felt something otherwise than you, I think you ought to recognize—not said against you, but what seemed to me written by me; or, if it cannot be recognized, to believe it. So indeed would I say this, that I am not only most ready, if anything in my writings has moved you, fraternally to accept what you feel against it, about to rejoice either in my correction or in your very benevolence; but I even demand and entreat this of you.

**2. 3.** Oh, if it had been permitted, even if not dwelling together, at least to enjoy you near in the Lord for frequent and sweet conversation! But because this was not given, I ask that you study to preserve this very thing, that in the Lord we are together as much as we can, and to increase and perfect it, and not to spurn replies, however rare. Greet, with my service, the holy brother Paulinianus, and all the brothers who rejoice with you and over you in the Lord. Mindful of us, may you be heard by the Lord in every holy desire of yours, most dear and most longed-for lord, and, in Christ, to be honored brother.
