# Letter 256

TO CHRISTINUS, A DESERVEDLY PRAISEWORTHY AND SINCERELY MOST DEAR AND MOST LONGED-FOR LORD AND BROTHER, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** That you desire my letter, your letter announced to me. But brother James came as a richer witness of that desire of yours toward us, because he spoke to me more sweet things about you, and things experienced in himself, than that little sheet could. Hence I congratulate your kindness, and give thanks to the Lord our God, whose gifts these are, for your Christian heart, deservedly praiseworthy and sincerely most dear and most longed-for lord and brother. But as for your asking that I seek you by letters; I seek you by affection, which surpasses all letters: and where I seek you, in a way you well understand, I know. But as far as my being read is concerned, I rather fear that my loquacity be reprehended in your hands, than that eloquence be required. This one thing I would say briefly, which, if you ruminate on it with long thought, you will perceive what it savors of: that, on the way of God, easier and fruitful things are fled by slothful dread, while, on the worldly way, harder and barren things are borne with toilsome labor. May you, safe and sound, flourish and make progress in Christ, deservedly praiseworthy and sincerely most dear and most longed-for lord and brother.
