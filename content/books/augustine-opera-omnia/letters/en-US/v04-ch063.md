# Letter 270

**1.** *When at an earlier time I had traveled to the city of Leges, I was exceedingly saddened that I could by no means find you whole there. For I found you in half, and, so to speak, a part of your soul, the most dear Severus, over whom I rejoiced in part. For I would rejoice perfectly, if I found you whole: hence over the part in which I had found you, I was glad; and on account of your part which I by no means beheld, I was saddened in every way. Accordingly I said to my soul:* "Why are you sad, and why do you disturb me? Hope in God[^1], *and he will make present the friend you love." Hence I trust in the Lord and hope that he will make me glad over the sight of you. Oh, if love could be perceived by the eyes! surely you would see how great our love for you is. For either, equaling your love, it would bring you great joy; or, if it were greater, it would bring you great zeal for imitation of your love. Therefore, because I love you in the Lord, love one who loves you. And exhort the others, by ecclesiastical authority, to love along with you. For as to what you ask in your letter, that I pray for you; this I would rightly do, if I myself were free from sins, so that it were permitted to pray for others. And therefore I admonish you to send forth assiduous prayers of your mind for me to the Lord: and, mindful of your profession, to set before your eyes that day, on which* the just man will not fear from an evil report[^2]; *and the just man will therefore not fear, because he will not hear:* Go into the eternal fire[^3], *but:* Come, blessed of my Father, receive the kingdom[^4]. *To which may he lead us who lives and reigns forever and ever. Amen.*

[^1]: Ps 42:5 (41:6)
[^2]: See Ps 112:7 (111:7)
[^3]: Matt 25:41
[^4]: See Matt 25:34
