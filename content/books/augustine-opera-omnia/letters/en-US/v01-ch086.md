# Letter 86

TO THE DISTINGUISHED LORD, AND IN THE LOVE OF CHRIST TRULY AND DESERVEDLY HONORABLE AND ESTEEMED SON CAECILIANUS, BISHOP AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETING IN THE LORD.

**1.** The brightness of your administration and the fame of your virtues, the praiseworthy diligence too of your Christian piety and faithful sincerity—which you rejoice were granted you as divine gifts by him from whom, as he promises, you hope for greater—have roused me to share, in this letter-address, the heat of my affairs with your Excellency. For as much as we rejoice that you have, with marvelous efficacy, provided for Catholic unity throughout the other lands of Africa, so much do we grieve that the region of Hippo Regius and the parts neighboring it, bordering Numidia, have not yet deserved to be helped by the protective force of your edict, distinguished lord, and in the love of Christ truly and deservedly honorable and esteemed son. Lest this be ascribed rather to my negligence—I who bear the episcopal burden at Hippo—I thought it should not be kept silent from your Magnificence. How much heretical audacity even presumes in the field of Hippo, if you should deign to hear from my brothers and colleagues, who can relate these things to your Sublimity, or from the priest whom I have sent with my letter, then, with the Lord our God helping, you will doubtless provide that the swelling of sacrilegious vanity be healed by terrifying it, rather than cut away by avenging it.
