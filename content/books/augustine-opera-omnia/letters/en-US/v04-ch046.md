# Letter 253

TO BENENATUS, A MOST BLESSED AND VENERABLE AND DESIRABLE LORD AND BROTHER, AND TO THE BROTHERS WHO ARE WITH YOU, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** Through the one by whom I greet Your Holiness, we rejoice over his faith and good zeal for the Church. He wished, moreover, to come to your Kindness with my letter, most beloved and venerable lord and brother. And since I have heard that you are thinking of settling that business—if it is true (which I wonder at, if it is true)—you know how you ought, with episcopal fatherly care, to provide for the Catholic Church; so that you do it not with just anyone, if indeed, as I said, what I have heard is true, but rather with a Catholic household, of which the Church can have not only no adversity, but even faithful help.
