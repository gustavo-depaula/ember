# Letter 250A

[TO CLASSICIANUS]

**1.** I, on account of those who, for the sin of one soul, bind the whole household of it, that is, very many souls, with anathema—especially lest anyone there depart from the body without baptism; and whether those too are not to be expelled from the church who flee there to break faith with their sureties—desire, with the Lord helping, both to deal with it in our council, and, if it is needful, to write to the Apostolic See; so that in these cases what we ought to follow may be established and confirmed by the concordant authority of all. This indeed I would not rashly say, that if any of the faithful has been anathematized unjustly, it will rather harm him who does it than him who suffers this injury. For the Holy Spirit dwelling in the saints, through whom each one is bound or loosed, inflicts an undeserved punishment on no one: for through him love is poured out in our hearts[^1], which *does not act wrongly*[^2].

[^1]: See Rom 5:5
[^2]: See 1 Cor 13:4-5
