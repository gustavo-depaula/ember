# Letter 116

To the most excellent lord, deservedly distinguished and honorably most dear son Generosus, Augustine sends greetings in the Lord.

**1.** Although the praise and proclamation of your administration and your illustrious fame greatly delighted me, for the love which we owe to your merits and benevolence, never until now, in asking some benefit, has my intercession been burdensome to Your Eminence, most beloved lord and venerable son. But now, what has been done in the city in which I serve the Church of God—when Your Eminence has learned from the letter which I gave to my venerable brother and fellow bishop Fortunatus by what necessity I was compelled to thrust my petition upon your occupations—your benignity will perceive. And surely you will do what befits not only an upright but also a Christian judge, with that mind toward us which surely ought to have been presumed in the name of Christ.
