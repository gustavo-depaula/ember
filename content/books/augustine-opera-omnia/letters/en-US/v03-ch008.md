# Letter 163

Bishop Evodius, to bishop Augustine.

**1.** Some time ago I sent questions to your holiness: one about reason and God, I think through Jobinus, who serves the handmaids of God; another about the body of the Savior, of which it is estimated that it sees the substance of God. A third I now state: the rational soul which the Savior assumed along with the body—whether it is among one of those opinions which are set forth when the origin of the soul is inquired into, if it can be supported there by any truth; or whether, although it is rational, it is nevertheless not of those species which are spoken of concerning the soul of animals, but is held in another. A fourth I ask: who are those spirits of whom Peter, in his letter, sets down a testimony about the Lord, saying: *Put to death in the flesh, made alive in the spirit, in which also, coming, he preached to those spirits who were in prison*[^1], and the rest; inserting this, that they were in hell, and that Christ, descending, evangelized all, and freed all from the darkness and the punishments through grace, so that from the time of the Lord's resurrection, judgment is awaited, hell being emptied. What, then, seems to your holiness in this matter, I desire to know.

[^1]: 1 Pet 3:18-19
