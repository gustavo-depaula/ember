# Letter 257

TO ORONTIUS, A DISTINGUISHED AND DESERVEDLY HONORABLE LORD AND SON TO BE RECEIVED, AUGUSTINE.

**1.** I give thanks that you deigned even to anticipate the coming of Your Excellence by a letter, and sent conversation before sight, so that we might enjoy your address before your countenance, and, somehow foretasting by the epistolary solace the acquaintance long desired, await the more avidly and ardently what we should the more pleasantly and gratefully receive, distinguished and deservedly honorable lord and son to be received. I therefore render to your merits and anticipating services the owed duty of a return greeting, rejoicing that your safety is announced, and wishing it continual. And as for what you added, demanding back, by the right of a goodwill bestowed in advance, a reply of my slightness, saying, "If indeed we can merit this from so great a holiness"; I by no means dare to despair that the very fountain of that same holiness—by drinking from which, according to our small measure, we are something—is not only to be praised, but also to be shared with us, and will please your prudence; so that God, incomparably and unchangeably good, who through his power is the founder of your so good mind, may also through grace be its restorer. May almighty God protect you safe and happier, distinguished and deservedly honorable lord and son to be received.
