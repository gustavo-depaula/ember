# Letter 146

TO MY MOST BELOVED AND MOST LONGED-FOR BROTHER PELAGIUS, FROM AUGUSTINE, GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** I give you very great thanks that you deigned to gladden me with your letter and to assure me of your well-being. May the Lord repay you with goods by which you may always be good, and may you live forever with him who is eternal, my most beloved and most longed-for brother. As for me, although I do not recognize in myself the praises of me that the letter of your Kindness contains, still I cannot be ungrateful toward your goodwill toward my littleness; at the same time admonishing you rather to pray for me, that I may become from the Lord such as you already think me to be. And in another hand: Mindful of us, may you, safe and sound, be pleasing to the Lord, my most beloved and most longed-for brother.
