# Letter 150

TO THE MOST WORTHY OF HONOR AMONG LADIES, THE DESERVEDLY ILLUSTRIOUS AND MOST EXCELLENT DAUGHTERS PROBA AND JULIANA, FROM AUGUSTINE, GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** You have filled our heart with joy, the more pleasant the dearer, the more welcome the swifter. For the virginal sanctity of your stock, since wherever you have become known—and through this, everywhere—a most renowned report proclaims, you have outrun its swiftest flight with the more faithful and more certain messenger of a letter, and have made us exult over so excellent a good made known, before we could doubt over a thing only heard. Who could unfold in words, who could attend with worthy proclamation, how incomparably more gloriously and more fruitfully Christ has from your blood women who are virgins, than the world has men who are consuls? For if it is a great and illustrious thing to mark the volumes of the ages with the dignity of one's name, how much greater and more illustrious it is to transcend them by the integrity of heart and body! Let a girl, then, noble by birth, more noble by sanctity, rejoice more that through divine partnership she is about to attain a special sublimity in heaven, than if through human marriage she were about to propagate sublime offspring. For more nobly has the Anician posterity chosen to bless so illustrious a family by not knowing marriage, than to multiply it by bearing, and already in the flesh to imitate the life of the angels, than from the flesh still to augment the number of mortals. This is the richer and more fruitful happiness: not to grow heavy with the womb, but to grow great with the mind; not to flow with milk in the breast, but to glow white in the heart; not to bring forth earth from the entrails, but heaven by prayers. Daughters most worthy of honor among ladies, enjoy fully in her what was lacking to you; may she persevere to the end, cleaving to the marriage that has no end. Let many handmaids imitate her, their lady; the lowborn, the noble; the frailly exalted, the more loftily humble; let virgins who wish for themselves the renown of the Anicii choose sanctity. For that, with however great desire, when will they attain it? But this, if they have fully desired it, they will soon have. May the right hand of the Most High protect you safe and happier, daughters most worthy of honor among ladies and most excellent. The pledges of your Holiness, especially her who is herself preeminent in sanctity, we greet with the office due to your merits, in the love of the Lord. We have most gratefully received the gift sent on the occasion of the veiling.
