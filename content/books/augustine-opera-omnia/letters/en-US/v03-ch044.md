# Letter 195

*Written perhaps in the year 418.*

TO AUGUSTINE, HOLY AND MOST BLESSED LORD AND POPE, FROM JEROME.

**1.** At all times indeed I have venerated your Blessedness with the honor that befits it, and have loved the Lord and Savior dwelling in you: but now, if it can be, we add something to the heap and fill up what is already full, so that we do not allow even a single hour to pass without mention of your name—you who have stood firm against the winds blowing against you by the ardor of your faith, and have preferred, as far as it lay in you, to be saved alone out of Sodom rather than to remain with those who perish. Your prudence knows what I mean. Hail to your virtue! You are celebrated throughout the world; the Catholics venerate and look up to you as the founder anew of the ancient faith; and—what is a sign of greater glory—all the heretics detest you: and they persecute me with an equal hatred, so that those whom they cannot kill with swords they kill by their wish. May the clemency of Christ the Lord keep you unharmed and mindful of us, venerable lord and most blessed pope.
