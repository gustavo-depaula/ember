# Letter 212

TO THE MOST BLESSED LORD AND DESERVEDLY VENERABLE BROTHER AND FELLOW BISHOP QUINTILIAN, AUGUSTINE SENDS GREETINGS IN THE LORD.

**1.** I commend to Your Reverence, in the love of Christ, the honorable servants of God and precious members of Christ: Galla, a widow of holy purpose, and her daughter Simpliciola, a consecrated virgin, subject to her mother in age, set above her in holiness—whom we have nourished with the word of the Lord as we could. By this letter I hand them over, as it were by my own hand, to be consoled and helped in all things that their advantage or necessity requires—which, without doubt, Your Holiness would do even without my commendation. For if we, for the sake of the heavenly Jerusalem, of which we are all citizens and in which they have desired to have a place of more excellent holiness, owe them not only a citizen's but also a brotherly affection, how much more do you, among whom is even their earthly homeland, in which those ladies despise their worldly nobility for the love of Christ? I ask too that you deign to receive through them the duty of my greeting with the same love with which I have sent it, and to remember us in your prayers. They carry with them, indeed, relics of the most blessed and most glorious martyr Stephen, which Your Holiness is not unaware—as we have also done—how fittingly you ought to honor.
