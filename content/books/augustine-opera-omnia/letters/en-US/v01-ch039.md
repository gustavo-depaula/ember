# Letter 39

To the truly holy and most blessed pope Augustine, Jerome, greeting in Christ

**1. 1.** *Last year, through our brother Asterius the subdeacon, I had sent a letter to your Dignity, rendering a prompt duty of greeting; which I think was delivered to you. Now too, through my holy brother Praesidius the deacon, I beseech you, first, that you be mindful of me: then, that you hold the bearer of this letter commended, and know him to be most genuinely mine, and that, in whatever necessity may demand, you cherish and sustain him; not that he is in need of anything (by Christ's granting), but because he most avidly seeks the friendships of good men, and thinks that in joining these he has obtained the greatest benefit. But why he has sailed to the West, you will be able to learn from his own telling.*

**2. 2.** *We, settled in the monastery, are shaken by various waves on this side and that, and endure the troubles of pilgrimage. But we believe in him who said:* Be confident; I have overcome the world[^1]*, that, by his granting and protection, we may obtain victory against the enemy, the devil. I beseech you to greet, with my service, the holy and venerable brother our pope Alypius. The holy brothers, who hasten with us to serve the Lord in the monastery, greet you very much. May Christ our God, the almighty, keep you unharmed and mindful of me, truly holy and to-be-received pope.*

[^1]: John 16:33
