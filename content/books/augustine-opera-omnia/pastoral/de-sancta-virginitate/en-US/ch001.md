# Revisions II

## Chapter 23 (48): Holy Virginity, one book

After I wrote *The Excellence of Marriage*, it was expected that I would write *Holy Virginity*; and I did not delay, but showed in a single volume, as best I could, both how great a gift of God it is and with how great a humility it must be guarded.

This book begins thus: *We recently published the book The Excellence of Marriage.*
