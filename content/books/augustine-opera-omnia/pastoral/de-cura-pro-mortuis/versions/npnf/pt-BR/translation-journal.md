## Translation Decisions

- 2026-09-27: Address. Augustine writes to Paulinus in the singular (Latin *Sanctitati tuae*; the English mixes "you" with "do thou" in ¶3). Rendered with "tu" throughout, keeping the honorific nouns "tua Santidade" and "tua caridade" with 2nd-person-singular verbs, as `augustine/catechising-of-the-uninstructed/pt-BR` addresses Deogratias with "tu". ¶19 "among you" (the people of Nola) → "entre vós", with the verb still in "tu" ("amas").
- 2026-09-27: God. Pronouns lowercase ("ele", "dele"), divine nouns capitalized as in the source, per `augustine/creed/pt-BR`. Scripture addressed to God uses the reverent "Vós" (¶4 Ps 79 "vossos servos", ¶16 Is 63:16 "Vós sois nosso Pai"), per the creed journal's convention. God or men addressing a single person in Scripture use "tu" (¶9 1 Kgs 13, ¶16 2 Kgs 22, ¶17 Lk 16:25, ¶21 Sir 3:21–22).
- 2026-09-27: Scripture follows the source wording clause by clause. The source gives no chapter-and-verse references, so none were added. Specific cases: ¶2 2 Cor 5:10 "por meio do corpo" kept in all three occurrences because Augustine argues from it; ¶16 Is 63:16 "Abraão não nos conheceu" matches its repetition in ¶17; ¶16 2 Kgs 22 "eu te acrescentarei a teus pais, e serás acrescentado" keeps the source's "add"; ¶20 1 Cor 12 "discurso de sabedoria… interpretação de discursos" keeps one noun for "discourse", and the lowercase "spirit" of the source's last clause.
- 2026-09-27: Verse. Virgil (*Aen.* VI, ¶3) and Lucan (¶4) rendered from the English verse into unrhymed Portuguese lines, same line count and line breaks.
- 2026-09-27: Footnotes. The en-US file carries no editor footnotes; nothing dropped. Editorial parenthesis "(the tomb of)" in ¶21 kept as "(ao túmulo)".
- 2026-09-27: Paragraph numbers written `**N.**` per `.claude/rules/books.md`. The en-US file uses `N.` list markers; the numbering is gapless 1–23.
- 2026-09-27: ¶9 "checked temporally even unto death" → "reprimido temporalmente", kept apart from "punished" (castigado) and from the Apostle's "chastened" (corrigidos), since the source uses three words.
- 2026-09-27: ¶13 the debt documents: "acknowledgment" → "reconhecimento de dívida", "counter acknowledgment" → "recibo de quitação", "note of hand" → "título de dívida". The literal "contrarreconhecimento" is not Portuguese usage.
- 2026-09-27: ¶20 "beside their Memorials" is the older English sense of "apart from" (Latin *praeter*); rendered "fora de suas Memórias". "junto a" would reverse the meaning.
- 2026-09-27: The source's unquoted speech ("Let some one go, said he", ¶15) and the half-quoted "But" (say I) in ¶4 are reproduced as in the source.

## Review Round 1 (2026-09-27)

Fixed:
- ¶6 "claríssimamente" → "clarissimamente": -mente adverbs drop the base adjective's written accent (facilmente, dificilmente), so the accented superlative "claríssima" loses its accent before "-mente".
- ¶23 closing sentence: "se ele... não me tivesse deixado esquecê-lo, certamente não teria surgido... a resposta" inverted the source's double negative. EN "had not he... not suffered me to forget it" resolves (double negation) to "if he had NOT prevented me from forgetting it" — i.e., the counterfactual is Candidianus *failing* to remind Augustine, which the following clause ("no reply would have come") correctly punishes. The pt-BR had one "não" too many, making the protasis "he DID prevent forgetting" (the actual, positive case) lead to "no reply" — backwards, and contradicting the surrounding praise of Candidianus's insistence as the reason the reply exists. Removed the extra "não".
