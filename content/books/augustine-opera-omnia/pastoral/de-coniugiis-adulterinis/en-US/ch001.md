# Revisions II

## Chapter 57 (84): To Pollentius, on Adulterous Marriages, Two Books

I wrote two books, *Adulterous Marriages*, to the extent that I could in accord with the Scriptures, wishing to resolve a most difficult question. Whether I have done so with full clarity I do not know; rather, I am aware that I have not reached perfection in this matter, although I have opened up many of its recesses, as anyone who reads intelligently will be able to judge.

The first book of this work begins thus: *The first question is, most beloved brother Pollentius.*

And the second thus: *To the things you had written to me.*
