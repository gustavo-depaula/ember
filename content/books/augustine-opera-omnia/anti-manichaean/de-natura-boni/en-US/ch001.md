# Revisions II

## Chapter 9 (36): One Book on the Nature of the Good Against the Manichees

The book *On the Nature of the Good* is against the Manichees, where it is shown that God is an unchangeable nature and the highest good, and that from him come all other natures, whether spiritual or corporeal, and that all of them, insofar as they are natures, are good; and what evil is and from where it comes, and how many good things the Manichees place in the nature of evil and how many evils in the nature of the good, natures that their error has invented.

This book begins thus: *The highest good, than which there is none higher, is God.*
