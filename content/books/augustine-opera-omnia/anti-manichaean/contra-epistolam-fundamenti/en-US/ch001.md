# Revisions II

## Chapter 2 (29): Answer to the Letter of Mani Known as The Foundation, One Book

The book *Answer to the Letter of Mani Known as The Foundation* refutes only its opening statements; but in its other parts notes were attached wherever it seemed fitting, by which the whole letter is overturned and by which I would be reminded, should the leisure ever come to write against the whole of it.

This book begins as follows: *The one true God almighty*.
