# How Aaron is said to have scattered the people when they themselves asked for it.

**146.** (32, 25) *And when Moses had seen the people, that they were scattered—for Aaron had scattered them—so that they became a joy to their adversaries.* It is to be noted how that whole evil which the people did is attributed to Aaron himself, because he consented to them in doing what they had wickedly asked. For it is rather said, *Aaron scattered them,* since he yielded to them, than that they scattered themselves who demanded so great an evil.
