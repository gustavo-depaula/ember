# What the Lord says to Moses.

**9.** (4:12) As to what the Lord says to Moses, *but now go, and I will open your mouth, and instruct you what you are to speak*, here it sufficiently appears that not only the instruction of the mouth, but even its very opening, pertains to the will and grace of God. For he did not say, "you open your mouth, and I will instruct you," but he himself promised both: *I will open, and I will instruct*. But elsewhere he says in the *Psalm*: *open wide your mouth, and I will fill it*[^1]. Where he signifies in man the will to receive what God gives to the willing: so that to the beginning of the will pertains *open wide your mouth*; but to the grace of God, *and I will fill it*. But here, *and I will open your mouth, and instruct you*.

[^1]: Ps 81:10 (80:11).
