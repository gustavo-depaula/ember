# The words that Moses said to his father-in-law.

**67.** (18, 15-16) Moses said to his father-in-law: *because the people come to me to seek judgment from God; when a dispute arises among them, and they come to me, I judge each one, and I instruct them in the precepts of God and his Law.* It can be asked how Moses said these things, when no law of God had yet been written: unless because the law of God is eternal, which all pious minds consult, so that what they find in it they may either do, or command, or forbid, according as it, by its unchangeable truth, has prescribed. For surely we are not to believe that Moses, although God spoke with him, used in each particular case to consult God, if anything arose in the disputes of so great a multitude, which kept him at this business of judging from morning until evening? And yet, unless he consulted the Lord presiding over his own mind, and wisely attended to his eternal Law, he could not find what was most just to judge between the disputants.
