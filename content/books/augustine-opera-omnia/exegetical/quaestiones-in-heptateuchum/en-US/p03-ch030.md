# How Pharaoh's heart was made heavy by contrary causes.

**30.** (9:7) *But Pharaoh, seeing that none of the flocks of the children of Israel had died, his heart was made heavy*. How was this making-heavy of Pharaoh's heart brought about by contrary causes? For if the flocks of the Israelites too had died, then there would seem to be a fitting cause by which his heart was made heavy to despise God, as if the magicians too had made the flocks of the Israelites die: but now, from that whereby he ought to have been moved to fear or to believe—seeing no beast dead of the flocks of the Hebrews—from this his heart was made heavy; that is, that making-heavy advanced even to this point.
