# On the words: You shall not boil a lamb in its mother's milk. (2)

**164.** (34, 26) *You shall not boil a lamb in its mother's milk.* Behold, he said again what I do not know how it can be understood. Yet it is a great prophecy concerning Christ, even if it can be done literally; how much more if it cannot? For in the discourses of God not all things are to be recalled to the proper sense of deeds, just as neither is that about the rock, and its cleft, and the placing of the hand[^1]. But plainly this is to be required of the trustworthiness of the narrator, that what he says was done was truly done, and what he says was said was truly said. Which is required also of the narrators of the Gospel: for when they narrate that Christ said certain things which he said in parables; yet that he said these is not a parable, but a historical narration.

[^1]: See Ex 33:22.
