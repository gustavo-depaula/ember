# The kind of wood of the rod.

**57.** (15, 25) *And the Lord showed him a piece of wood, and he cast it into the water, and the water became sweet.* Was it a kind of wood having this power, or could God, who did such great wonders, do it with any wood whatever? Yet this seems to be signified by what is said, *he showed him,* as though the wood were already such that this could be done by it: unless perhaps it was a place where wood was not to be found at all, so that this very thing belonged to divine aid—that the Lord showed him wood where there was none, and by the wood made the waters sweet, prefiguring the glory and grace of the Cross[^1]; but even in such a nature of the wood, who is to be praised except the Creator and the one who showed it?

[^1]: See Sir 38:5.
