# On the cloud which prevented Moses.

**176.** (40, 34-35) A very wonderful thing is to be noted, that when the cloud descended and filled the tabernacle—which, however, is called the glory of the Lord—Moses could not enter the tabernacle, he who on Mount Sinai, when he first received the Law, *entered into the cloud where God was*[^1]. Without doubt, then, he figured one person then, another now: and then those who are made partakers of the inmost truth of God; but now the Jews, to whom the glory of the Lord, which is in the tabernacle—which is the grace of Christ—is set as it were a cloud against them, since they do not understand it, and therefore do not enter into the tabernacle of the testimony. And this is to be believed to have been done once, as soon as the tabernacle was set up, for the sake of this signification, or of some other. For the cloud was not always so over the tabernacle that Moses could not enter there; since the cloud was not removed except when this sign was given them of breaking up, that is, of moving the camp from the place where they were; and of going where the cloud led by day, the flame by night[^2]. Which two by turns also remained over the tabernacle, where they had pitched camp, the cloud by day, the flame by night.

[^1]: See Ex 19:20; 20:21.
[^2]: See Ex 13:21 and 40:36–38.
