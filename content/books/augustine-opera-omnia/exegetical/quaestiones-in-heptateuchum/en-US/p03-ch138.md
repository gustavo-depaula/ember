# How Bezalel the architect is said to be filled with the spirit.

**138.** (31, 2-3) What does it mean that, when he ordered Bezalel to be employed for the works of making the tabernacle, he said he had filled *him with the divine spirit of wisdom, and understanding, and knowledge in every work, to devise and to do architectural work,* etc.? Are these works too, which seem to pertain to craftsmanship, to be attributed to the gift of the Holy Spirit? Or is this too said significatively, so that those things pertain to the divine spirit of wisdom, and understanding, and knowledge which are signified by these things? Yet here too, although this man is said to be filled with the divine spirit of wisdom, and understanding, and knowledge, the Holy Spirit is not yet read of.
