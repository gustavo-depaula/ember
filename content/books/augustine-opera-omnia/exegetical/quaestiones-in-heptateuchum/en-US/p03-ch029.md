# The origin of vices is from the will of man.

**29.** (8:32) When the locust had been taken away, it was said of Pharaoh: *and Pharaoh made his heart heavy at this time too, and was unwilling to let the people go*. Surely now it was not said, "Pharaoh's heart was made heavy"; but, *Pharaoh made his heart heavy*. So indeed in all the plagues. For the origin of vices is from the will of man: but the hearts of men are moved by causes—some hearts thus, others thus, often even by causes not different in a different way, according to their own qualities, which come from their wills.
