# Whether it should be said as the Latins have it or as the Greek has it.

**27.** (8:25) Where the Latins have, *going, sacrifice to the Lord your God in the land*, the Greek has, *coming, sacrifice to the Lord your God in the land*. For he did not want them to go where they said, but wished them to sacrifice there in Egypt. This is shown by the words of Moses which follow[^1], where he says it cannot be done on account of the abominations of the Egyptians.

[^1]: See Exod 8:26 (22).
