# How Scripture speaks of Moses when he was sent to the people.

**17.** (7:1) It is to be noted that, when he was sent to the people, it was not said to him, "behold, I have made you a god to the people, and your brother will be your prophet"; but, *your brother*, he says, *will speak for you to the people*. It was also said: *he will be your mouth, and you to him as those things which are toward God*[^1]: it was not said, "you a god to him." But Moses is said to be given as a god to Pharaoh, and by analogy Aaron the prophet of Moses, but toward Pharaoh. Here it is suggested to us that the prophets of God speak those things which they hear from him, and that a prophet of God is nothing else than an announcer of the words of God to men, who either cannot or do not deserve to hear God.

[^1]: Exod 4:16.
