# On the distinction between the altar of incense and the tabernacle of the testimony.

**137.** (30, 36) *And you shall grind some of them fine, and you shall place it over against the testimonies, whence I will make myself known to you from there. The incense shall be a Holy of Holies to you.* Behold, again this incense, because it was placed within on the altar of incense which was within, is called a holy of holies: and the tabernacle of the testimony is properly called that very inner part where the ark was, with a distinction surely applied when he says: *whence I will make myself known to you from there.* For so he had said first of the propitiatory, which is surely within, that is, within the veil, over the ark.
