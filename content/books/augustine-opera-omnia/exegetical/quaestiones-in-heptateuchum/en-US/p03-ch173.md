# The altar of holocausts sanctified by anointing.

**173.** (40, 9-10) Above, when God first spoke about anointing the tabernacle, he said that by that same anointing all those things were sanctified, and became a Holy of Holies[^1]. But the altar of holocausts, sanctified by the same anointing, he had said became a Holy of the holy[^2]: and this seemed to be the difference, that he had called none of them a Holy of Holies, except what was separated from the holy place by a veil alone, that is, where the Ark of the Testament and the altar of incense were. But now, when he repeated the same things, he said of the anointed tabernacle and the things that were in it, that they were sanctified by the same anointing, and became holy: but that altar of holocausts, of which he had before said it became a Holy of the holy, he now said became, by the same anointing, a Holy of Holies.

Whence it is given to understand that what had been called a Holy of the holy is worth just as much as what is called a Holy of Holies: and by this, that all those anointed things too, that is, the whole tabernacle, and whatever was in it, which he had before called a Holy of Holies, are worth just as much as what he now calls holy: and that each of them is called, after this anointing, not only a Holy of the holy, but also a Holy of Holies, like the altar of holocausts; so that now there is no difference, as far as this naming is concerned, between those things which had been within the veil, that is, where the Ark of the Testimony was, and the rest outside, except that those within were so called Holy of Holies or Holy of Holies that even before the anointing they were so called; but the rest were sanctified by the anointing, that they might receive this name. What these things signify is to be discussed at leisure.

[^1]: See Ex 30:26–38 and q. 2.135.
[^2]: See Ex 29:37.
