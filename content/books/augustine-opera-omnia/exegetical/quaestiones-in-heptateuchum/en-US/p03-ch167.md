# The Lord acts when he helps those who act.

**167.** (35, 1) Moses, to the children of Israel, after he descended from the mountain, having the other tablets of the Law, with a veil placed about himself on account of the glory of his face, which the children of Israel could not behold: *these,* he says, *are the words which the Lord said to do them*: it is ambiguously put, whether the Lord himself is *to do them,* or they: but surely it is manifest that they are; for he commanded what was to be done. But perhaps it is so put that it may be taken from both; because the Lord too acts, when he helps those who act, according to that saying of the Apostle: *work out your own salvation in fear and trembling; for it is God who works in you both to will and to work, for his good pleasure*[^1].

[^1]: Phil 2:12–13.
