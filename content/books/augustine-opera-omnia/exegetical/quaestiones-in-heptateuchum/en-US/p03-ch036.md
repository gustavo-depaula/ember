# Whether God has any need of anyone's malice.

**36.** (10, 1) *The Lord said to Moses*: '*go in to Pharaoh, for I have hardened his heart and the heart of his servants, so that these signs of mine may come upon them in due order*.' Thus he says: *for I have hardened his heart so that these signs of mine may come upon them in due order*, as though God had need of anyone's malice. But it must be understood as if he had said: "for I have been patient with him and his servants, that I might not take them away, so that my signs may come upon them in due order." For because by God's patience an evil mind was made more obstinate, therefore in place of "I have been patient with him" it is said, *I have hardened his heart.*
