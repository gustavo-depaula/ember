# Moses stretches out his hand a third time.

**38.** (10, 21. 12) A third time it is said to Moses: *stretch out your hand toward heaven,* that the plague of darkness might also come to pass. But it was never said to his brother Aaron that he should stretch out his hand toward heaven. So when it was said to Moses: *stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt, and let the locust come up over the land,* this, I believe, signified that the one who can do more can also do less; but it does not follow at once that the one to whom lesser things are granted can do greater things.
