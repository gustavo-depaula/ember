# In the time of sowing there is harvest.

**160.** (34, 21) When he had prescribed concerning the Sabbath, what does it mean that he added: *in sowing and harvest you shall rest?* For he seems to say: "in the time of sowing and harvest." Did he perhaps prescribe that the rest of the Sabbath be so observed that not even those times have an excuse which are very necessary to farmers for sustenance and life? It was ordered, therefore, that even in the time of sowing and harvest, when the work presses hard, rest be taken on the Sabbath: and thus, through these times which demand the most work, it was signified that at all times one ought to cease for the Sabbath.
