# What is to be thought of the Lord's command.

**6.** (3:22) That the Lord commanded the Hebrews through Moses to take from the Egyptians vessels of gold and silver and clothing, and added, *and you will despoil them*: the judgment of this command cannot be unjust. For it is the command of God, about which one was not to judge, but to obey. For he knows how justly he commanded; but it belongs to the servant to do obediently what he commanded.
