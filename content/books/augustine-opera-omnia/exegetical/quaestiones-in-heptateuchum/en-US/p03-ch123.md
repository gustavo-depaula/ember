# How the priesthood of the sons of Aaron is called everlasting.

**124.** (29, 9) *And the priesthood shall be theirs for me forever.* How he says *forever,* concerning these significative things, we have often said above. For this priesthood was surely changed, so that the other might be forever according to the order of Melchizedek, not according to the order of Aaron. For there are both an oath, and no repentance of God by which a change might be signified. For *the Lord swore, and will not repent*: '*you are,*' he says, '*a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek*[^1].' But of the order of Aaron it was indeed said *forever,* either on account of the time, not prescribed, for which it should be observed, or because it signified eternal things: yet nowhere was it said of the priesthood of Aaron that the Lord swore and will not repent. And therefore it was said in that priesthood according to the order of Melchizedek: *he will not repent,* that it might be signified that of the priesthood of Aaron he did repent, that is, he changed it.

[^1]: Ps 110:4 (109).
