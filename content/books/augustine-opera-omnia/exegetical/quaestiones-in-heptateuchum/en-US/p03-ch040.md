# Whether God had any need of Pharaoh's disobedience for multiplying his signs.

**40.** (11, 9) *And the Lord said to Moses*: '*Pharaoh will not heed you, so that I may multiply my signs and wonders in the land of Egypt*': as though there were need of his disobedience for those signs to be multiplied, which were profitably done to strike fear into the people of God and, by that very distinction, to form them in piety. But this was the work of God, who made good use of the malice of that heart; not the work of Pharaoh, who made evil abuse of God's patience.
