# How, after so great a mourning, the Egyptians lent the Hebrews vessels of gold and silver.

**45.** (12, 35-36) *And the children of Israel did as Moses had commanded them, and they asked of the Egyptians vessels of gold and silver and clothing; and the Lord gave his people favor in the sight of the Egyptians, and they lent to them, and they despoiled the Egyptians.* This had already been done before the deaths of the Egyptian firstborn; but now it is repeated by way of recapitulation. For it was narrated when it was done. For how could it now come about that, amid such great mourning over the deaths of their own, they should lend these things to the children of Israel? Unless perhaps someone should say that the Egyptians who dwelt with the Hebrews in the land of Goshen were not struck by this plague either.
