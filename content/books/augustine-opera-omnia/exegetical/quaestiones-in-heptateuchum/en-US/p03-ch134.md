# On the numbering of the children of Israel.

**134.** (30, 12) What does it mean that he says: *if you take the reckoning of the children of Israel in their visitation;* unless because he orders them at some time to be visited and reckoned, that is, numbered? Which in David is to be understood as having been punished for this reason, because God had not ordered it[^1].

[^1]: See 2 Sam 24.
