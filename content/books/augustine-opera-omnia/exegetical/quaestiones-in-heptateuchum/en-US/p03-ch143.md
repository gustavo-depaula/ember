# What is understood by "malice."

**143.** (32, 14) *And the Lord was made propitious concerning the malice which he said he would do to his people.* Here he wished "malice" to be understood as punishment, as it is: *their departure was reckoned a malice*[^1]. According to this, good and evil are said to be from God[^2], not according to the malice by which men are evil. For God is not evil; but he brings evils upon the evil, because he is just.

[^1]: Wis 3:2.
[^2]: See Sir 11:14.
