# Why it was said above that their hands were to be consummated.

**126.** (29, 10) *And you shall bring the calf to the door of the tabernacle of the testimony, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the calf in the sight of the Lord.* Behold whence it was said above that their hands were to be consummated, that is, their power perfected, so that they too might sanctify, which is now done, when they lay their hands upon the calf to be immolated.
