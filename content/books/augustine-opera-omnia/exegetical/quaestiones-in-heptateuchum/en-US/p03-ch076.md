# All images are forbidden.

**76.** (20, 23) *You shall not make for yourselves gods of silver, and gods of gold you shall not make for yourselves.* What was inculcated in the first precept is repeated; and by gods of silver and gold all images are surely understood, as also in that *Psalm*: *the idols of the nations are silver and gold*[^1].

[^1]: Ps 115:4 (113); 135:15 (134).
