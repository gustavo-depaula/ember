# What "everyone bringing a deduction" is.

**168.** (35, 24) *Everyone bringing a deduction, they brought silver and bronze as a deduction to the Lord*: as though he said: "everyone who brought, brought this and that, mentioning among the rest both silver and bronze." The Latins rendered *deduction* what the Greek has as *ἀφαίρεμα.* But it was called *deduction,* from the fact that he who brought to the Lord deducted from himself.
