# On what Moses says to the Lord.

**14.** (5:22-23) The words which Moses says to the Lord, *why have you afflicted this people? And why have you sent me? For since I went in to Pharaoh to speak in your name against this people, you have not delivered your people*, are not words of obstinacy or indignation, but of inquiry and prayer: which appears from what the Lord answered him. For he did not rebuke his unbelief, but disclosed what he was going to do.
