# On the origin of Moses.

**15.** (6:14-28) There is no doubt that it is a place of sacrament, that Scripture, wishing to show the origin of Moses—since his action now required it—began the lineage from the firstborn of Jacob, that is, Reuben, thence to Simeon, thence to Levi; further it did not go, since Moses was from Levi. But those are mentioned who had already been mentioned among those seventy-five with whom Israel entered Egypt: for God willed that not the first nor the second, but the third tribe, that is, the Levitical, should be priestly.
