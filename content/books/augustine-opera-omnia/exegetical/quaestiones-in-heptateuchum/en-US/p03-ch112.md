# What is the difference between the holy place and the holy of holies.

**112.** (26, 33) *And the veil shall divide for you between the midst of the holy place, and the midst of the Holy of Holies*: that is, that between the holy place and the Holy of Holies there should be this veil, of which he now speaks, stretched on four columns. Of this difference between the holy place and the Holy of Holies the *Letter to the Hebrews* speaks[^1]; because where the Ark of the testimony is, there is the Holy of Holies, that is, within, beyond the veil: but outside, the table and the lampstand, and the other things which he said a little before how they were to be made, were called holy, and not Holy of Holies.

And outside is signified the Old Testament, but within the New, since both are in the reading of the Old Testament, both expressed in works and figured by signification. And by this, in the holy places there is a figure of a figure, because it is a figure of the Old Testament: but in the Holy of Holies there is a figure of the truth itself, because it is a figure of the New Testament. For the whole Old Testament, in these things and celebrations which are prescribed to be observed in this way, is a figure.

[^1]: See Heb 9:1-12.
