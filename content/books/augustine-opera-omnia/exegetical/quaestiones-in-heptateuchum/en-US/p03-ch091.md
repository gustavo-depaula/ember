# Of whom it was said: Behold, I send my angel.

**91.** (23, 20-21) *Behold, I send my angel before your face, to keep you on the way, to bring you into the land which I have prepared for you. Attend to yourself, and hear him, lest you not believe him; for he will withhold nothing from you, for my name is upon him.* This is surely understood of him whose name was changed, that he should be called Jesus: for he it was who brought the people into the land of promise.
