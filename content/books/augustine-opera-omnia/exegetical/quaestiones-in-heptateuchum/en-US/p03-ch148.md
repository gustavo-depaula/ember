# It is asked why no punishment proceeded against Aaron.

**148.** (32, 35) It is rightly asked why, since Aaron was said above to have scattered the people, no punishment proceeded against him—neither when Moses ordered everyone to be killed who, as the Levites went armed to the gate and back, met them[^1], nor when afterward there came about what Scripture says: *and the Lord struck the people on account of the making of the calf, which Aaron made*: especially because here too this same thing was inculcated by repetition. For it was not said: *and the Lord struck the people on account of the making of the calf,* which they made, but, *which Aaron made*: and yet Aaron was not struck; indeed, even that which God prescribed concerning his priesthood before his sin was fulfilled. But he ordered both him and his sons to be washed[^2]; and so they were ordained in the priesthood.

Thus he knows whom he spares unto a change for the better; and whom he spares for a time, although he has foreknown that he will not be changed for the better; and whom he does not spare so that he may be changed for the better; and whom he does not spare, so that he does not even await his change: and all this comes back to that which the Apostle says, exclaiming: *how inscrutable are his judgments, and how unsearchable his ways*[^3].

[^1]: See Ex 32:26–28.
[^2]: See Ex 40:12.
[^3]: Rom 11:33.
