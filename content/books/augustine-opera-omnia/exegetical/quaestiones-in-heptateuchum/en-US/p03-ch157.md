# What it means that it is said to Moses: Attend to yourself.

**157.** (34, 12) What does it mean that it is said to Moses: *attend to yourself, lest perhaps he make a covenant with those who dwell upon the land*? For the Greek does not have, "lest perhaps you make"; but, *lest perhaps he make.* Did he perhaps wish to say this to him of the people, of which he himself was the leader? But he himself did not bring the people into that land, where he forbids a covenant to be made with those who dwelt in it. So it is a strange kind of expression, and as yet untried or unnoticed: if indeed it is an expression, and not some meaning.
