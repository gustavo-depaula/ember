# How Moses excused himself, that he was of a thin voice.

**16.** (6:30) That Moses says, *behold, I am of a thin voice, and how will Pharaoh hear me?*, seems to excuse himself for the thinness of his voice not only on account of the greatness of the people, but even on account of one man. It would be strange if he was of so thin a voice that he could not be heard even by one man: or perhaps royal pride did not allow them to speak from nearby? But it is said to him: *behold, I have made you a god to Pharaoh, and Aaron your brother will be your prophet*[^1].

[^1]: Exod 7:1.
