# Pharaoh's heart hardened even after the removal of the locusts.

**37.** (10, 19-20) *And not one locust was left in all the land of Egypt; and the Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart.* Scripture certainly recalled a benefit of God, that he took away the locusts; and what follows says that the Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart—by that very benefit, indeed, and by his patience, through which that obstinacy came about while Pharaoh was being spared. So too all the evil hearts of men grow hard by misusing the patience of God.
