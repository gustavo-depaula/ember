# It is asked what blood Scripture means as being by the door.

**46.** (12, 22) What does it mean when it says: *and you shall take a bunch of hyssop, and dipping it in the blood that is by the door, you shall smear the lintel and both doorposts?* For it is asked what blood it means as being by the door, since it surely wants the blood of that lamb to be understood, by whose immolation the Passover is made. Or does it command consequently—though it left this unsaid—that the same lamb be slain by the door? Or, what is more credible, did it say *of the blood that is by the door* because the one who is about to smear the lintel and doorposts will surely set the very vessel in which he caught the blood by the door, so as to have it at hand when he dips?
