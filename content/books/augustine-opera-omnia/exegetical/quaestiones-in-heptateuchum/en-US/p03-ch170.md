# How many are signified to have been gifted with skill in building.

**170.** (36, 2-3) *And all who would willingly go to the works, to consummate them, and they received from Moses all the deductions.* Moses brought to their notice only what works the Lord had prescribed to be made, namely the tabernacle with all that was to be in it, and the priestly vestments[^1]. But he mentioned certain ones, to whom he said the Spirit was divinely given, by which they could effect those things: and yet many are understood to have come willingly to those same works, to whom it was neither commanded, nor were their names mentioned as spoken by the Lord to Moses. Not, therefore, did those alone have this gift divinely, who are mentioned by name, but perhaps these chiefly and more excellently. But in all these is to be praised the mind not dragged to the work servilely, but liberally and willingly devoted.

[^1]: See Ex 35:10–19.
