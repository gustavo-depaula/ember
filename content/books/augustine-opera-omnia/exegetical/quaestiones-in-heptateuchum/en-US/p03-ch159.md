# On the words: You shall not appear before me empty.

**159.** (34, 20) What does he say: *you shall not appear before me empty?* As the surrounding things of which he speaks indicate, *before me*: God said "in his tabernacle": and this is: *you shall not appear before him empty,* "you shall never enter without some gift." Which, understood spiritually, is a great mystery. But these things were said of the shadows of significations.
