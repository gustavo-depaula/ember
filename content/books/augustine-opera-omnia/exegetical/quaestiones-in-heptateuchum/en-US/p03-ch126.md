# What is called the odor of God.

**127.** (29, 18) That a sacrifice of animal victims is often called in the Holy Scriptures *an odor of sweetness to the Lord* does not mean that God is delighted by the odor of that smoke; but only that what is spiritually signified by these things delights God when it is spiritually offered, since the very odor of God is understood spiritually. For he does not draw in odor by bodily nostrils as we do. As, then, he smells, so these things signify.
