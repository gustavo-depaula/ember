# What is to be understood concerning the wasps.

**93.** (23, 28) *And I will send wasps before you, and they shall drive out the Amorites, the Hivites, the Canaanites, and the Hittites from before you.* It is asked what is to be understood concerning these wasps. For God both promises this, and the book of Wisdom says it was fulfilled, where it says: *and he sent wasps as forerunners of his army*[^1].

But we do not read that it was done, neither in the times of Moses, nor under Joshua son of Nun, nor under the judges, nor under the kings. And by this, these wasps are perhaps to be understood as the stings of fear, by which the aforementioned nations were driven, so that they yielded to the children of Israel. For God is speaking, in whose discourse, if something is said figuratively which was not fulfilled according to the proper sense, it does not impede the trustworthiness of the history, in which the truth of the narration is perceived. Just as neither is the narration of the evangelists impeded as to its proper sense, if something is said by Christ figuratively.

[^1]: Wis 12:8.
