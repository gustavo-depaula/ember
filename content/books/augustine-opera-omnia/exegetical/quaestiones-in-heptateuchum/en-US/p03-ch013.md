# That God commanded that he would cast the people out of Egypt.

**13.** (5:1-3) It is asked how it is said to the people that God commanded that he would cast them out of Egypt into the land of Canaan; but to Pharaoh it is said that they wished to go out a three days' journey into the desert to sacrifice to their God by his command. But it is to be understood that, although God knew what he was going to do, since he foreknew that Pharaoh would not consent to letting the people go, that was said first which would also be done first, if he should let them go. For that all things might be done as the subsequent Scripture testifies, the obstinacy of Pharaoh and of his people deserved it. For God does not command falsely what he knows the one commanded will not do, so that a just judgment may follow.
