# How fear is signified to pertain to the Old Testament.

**73.** (20, 19) *Speak you to us, and let not God speak to us, lest we die.* It is greatly and solidly signified that fear rather pertains to the Old Testament, as love to the New: although in the Old the New lies hidden, and in the New the Old lies open. But how it is granted to such a people to see the voice of God, if this is to be taken as "to understand," when they fear that God should speak to them lest they die, is not sufficiently clear.
