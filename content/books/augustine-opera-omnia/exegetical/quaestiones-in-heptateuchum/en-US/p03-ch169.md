# A discourse to Moses is made concerning Bezalel.

**169.** (35, 30-32) When Moses narrated, what God said to him concerning Bezalel was recalled in the same words and as many, that he filled him *with the divine spirit of wisdom, and understanding, and knowledge* for doing the works of the tabernacle, which pertain to the arts of craftsmen: whence we have already said what seems to us[^1]. But I thought this same thing was now to be recalled, because it was not in vain repeated in altogether the same words by which it had before been intimated to Moses by the Lord. Surely, by a new usage, the art of architecture is here ascribed, of craftsmen working in gold and silver and any metal, when that is accustomed to be called "architectural" which pertains to the building of edifices.

[^1]: See q. 2.138.
