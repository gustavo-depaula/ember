# Moses so loved the people that for them he poured out those words to God.

**147.** (32, 31-32) When Moses says to God: *I beseech you, this people has sinned a great sin, and they have made for themselves gods of gold; and now, if indeed you forgive them their sin, forgive it; but if not, blot me out of your book which you have written*: he said this securely, so that the reasoning might be concluded from what follows; that is, that since God would not blot Moses out of his book, he would forgive the people that sin. Nevertheless it is to be noted how great an evil Moses perceived in that sin, in that he believed it had to be expiated by so great a slaughter—he who loved them so that for them he poured out those words to God.
