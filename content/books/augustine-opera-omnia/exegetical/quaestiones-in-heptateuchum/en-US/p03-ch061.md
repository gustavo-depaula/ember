# It is doubted where Aaron was to place the vessel before God.

**61.** (16, 33-34) *And Moses said to Aaron*: '*take one golden vessel, and put into it a full omer of manna, and lay it up before God, to be kept for your generations, as the Lord commanded.*' It can be asked where Aaron was to place it before God, when there was as yet no image, nor had the ark of the covenant yet been instituted. Or did he perhaps say *you shall lay it up* in the future tense, so that it might be understood that it could be laid up before God then, when the ark would exist? Or rather, was *before God* said of what is done by the very devotion of offering, in whatever place it was placed? For where is God not? But what he adds: *and Aaron laid it up before the testimony to be kept,* rather supports the former sense. For in this way Scripture spoke by prolepsis of what was done afterward, when the tabernacle of the testimony came to exist.
