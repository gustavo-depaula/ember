# What is the reason that the tabernacle is ordered to be made of ten curtains.

**107.** (26, 1-2) He orders the tabernacle to be made of ten curtains, since the decalogue of the Law has ten. The curtains signify breadth, on account of ease. For *love is the fullness of the Law*[^1]: and the precepts are easy only to love: whence this very enlargement is commended, when it is said: *you have enlarged my steps under me, and my footsteps have not been weakened*[^2]. But since this enlargement comes about through the grace of God—for the love of God is poured out in our hearts, not through ourselves, but *through the Holy Spirit who has been given to us*[^3]—therefore the number itself is here also mystically commended, which pertains also to the Holy Spirit, through whom the Law can be fulfilled. For it is said that the curtain ought to have a length of twenty-eight cubits. And this number, because it is to be divided by seven, it signified when it says the breadth of the curtain is four cubits. For four times seven make twenty-eight. And this number too is perfect; for, like the number six, it is composed of its parts. But what he says so often, *you shall make on them cherubim by the weaver's work,* what else does it commend in all these things but the multitude of knowledge, which is the interpretation of *cherubim*?

[^1]: Rom 13:10.
[^2]: Ps 18:36 (17).
[^3]: Rom 5:5.
