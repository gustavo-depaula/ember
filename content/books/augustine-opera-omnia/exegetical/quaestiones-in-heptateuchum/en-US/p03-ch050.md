# In the fifth generation the children of Israel went up from the land of Egypt.

**50.** (13, 18) *And in the fifth generation the children of Israel went up from the land of Egypt.* Does he mean a generation to be reckoned as a hundred years, and therefore "in the fifth generation" because it was after four hundred and thirty years? Or is this rather to be understood according to the generations of men, from Jacob himself who entered Egypt down to Moses who went out with the people? For Jacob is found first, Levi second, Kohath third, Amram fourth, Moses fifth. These the Latin translator calls *generations,* which the Greeks call *γενεάς,* and which in the Gospel are called *generations*[^1], and are numbered only by the successions of men, not by the number of years.

[^1]: See Matt 1:17.
