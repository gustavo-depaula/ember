# What the people did with the calf.

**142.** (32, 8) The Lord, indicating to Moses what the people did with the calf, that is, with the idol which they had made from their gold, says that they said: *these are your gods, O Israel, who led you out of the land of Egypt.* Which they are not read to have said, but God showed that their mind was this. For they bore in their heart the meaning of these words, which could not be hidden from God.
