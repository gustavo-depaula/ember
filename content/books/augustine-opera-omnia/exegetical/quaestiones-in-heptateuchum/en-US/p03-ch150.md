# How the Lord signifies that there is, as it were, more mercy in the angel who is to lead the people than in himself.

**150.** (33, 1-3) Where God says to Moses: *go, go up from here, you and your people, whom you led out of the land of Egypt, into the land which I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying*: '*to your seed I will give it';* immediately, as though he were still speaking to Moses himself, by a hidden turn, which in Greek is so called, he now speaks to the people themselves, saying: *and I will send my angel before you at the same time, and he will drive out the Canaanite, and the Amorite, and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Girgashite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite; and he will bring you into a land flowing with milk and honey. For I will not go up with you, since you are a people of a stiff neck, lest I destroy you on the way.*

A great and wonderful depth of mystery, as though the angel could have greater mercy than the Lord, who would spare a people of a stiff neck; whom God, if he were with them, would himself not spare: and yet even through his angel, himself in a manner absent from them—he who can be absent nowhere—he says he fulfills what he swore to their fathers; as though here too showing that he does this because he promised it to those just fathers, not because these men were worthy. What, then, did he signify, unless perhaps that he is not with them because they are of a stiff neck; because only humility and piety can receive him as propitious and salutary? But for God to be with men of a stiff neck is nothing other than to be present in punishing and chastising: whence, when in this way he is not present to the evil, he does it by sparing; to which pertains that which is said: *turn away your face from my sins*[^1]: because if he turns toward, he overturns. *For as wax melts before the face of the fire, so the sinners perish before the face of God*[^2].

[^1]: Ps 51 (50):9.
[^2]: Ps 68 (67):2.
