# Linen breeches are prescribed to cover the priests' shame.

**122.** (28, 42) *And you shall make for them linen breeches to cover the shame of their body; they shall be from the loins to the thighs.* Since the garment covers the whole body so fully, what does it mean that he says: *you shall make linen breeches to cover the shame of their body,* as though it could appear when so great a garment was applied over it? Unless because he wished there to be in this a sign of chastity or continence: which is therefore signified by a covering, that it may be understood as not had from oneself, but given.
