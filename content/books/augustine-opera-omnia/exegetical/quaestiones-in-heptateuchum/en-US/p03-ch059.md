# On what Moses and Aaron said to the murmuring people.

**59.** (16, 8) Moses and Aaron say to the people, among other things: *because the Lord has heard your murmuring, which you murmur against us. But what are we? For your murmuring is not against us, but against God.* They did not from this mean that they were as powerful as God: for they said, *what are we?* so that the people might know they had murmured against him who had sent these men and who was working through them.

Nor is that statement of the same kind where Peter says to Ananias: *have you dared to lie to the Holy Spirit? you have not lied to men, but to God*[^1]. For he does not say: "have you dared to lie to me? you have not lied to me, but to God"—which, had he said it, would have been similar. Nor did he say it thus: "have you dared to lie to the Holy Spirit? you have not lied to the Holy Spirit, but to God"—for speaking so, he would deny that the Holy Spirit is God. But now, when he had said: *have you dared to lie to the Holy Spirit?* while Ananias thought he had lied to men, Peter showed that the Holy Spirit is God by adding: *you have not lied to men, but to God.*

[^1]: Acts 5:3-4.
