# The sign commanded concerning the Passover.

**48.** (13, 9) What does it mean when he says, in commanding concerning the Passover: *it shall be for you as a sign upon your hand?* Is it to be understood as "upon your works"—that is, that you ought to set it before your works? For the Passover, on account of the slaying of the sheep, pertains to faith in Christ and to the blood by which we have been redeemed. And this faith is to be set before works, so that it is in a manner "upon the hand," against those who gloried in the works of the Law: of which matter the Apostle speaks, and treats much; he who would have faith so put before works that good works depend upon it and are preceded by it, not that faith itself should seem to be rendered as a reward, as it were, for the merits of good works[^1]. For faith pertains to grace: *but if by grace, then it is no longer of works; otherwise grace is no longer grace*[^2].

[^1]: See Gal 3; Heb 11.
[^2]: Rom 11:6.
