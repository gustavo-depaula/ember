# Concerning the Hebrew slave it is prescribed that he serve six years and be sent away free without payment.

**77.** (21, 2) As for what is prescribed concerning the Hebrew slave—that he serve six years and be sent away free without payment—lest Christian slaves should demand this from their masters, apostolic authority commands slaves to be subject to their masters, lest the name of God and the teaching be blasphemed[^1]. For that this was prescribed as a mystery is sufficiently clear from this, that God commanded the ear of one who had refused that freedom to be pierced with an awl against the doorpost[^2].

[^1]: See Eph 6:5; 1 Tim 6:1.
[^2]: See Exod 21:6.
