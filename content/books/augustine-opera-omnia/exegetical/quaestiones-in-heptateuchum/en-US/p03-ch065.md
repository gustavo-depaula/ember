# How it is called the rod of God which was earlier called the rod of Moses and Aaron.

**65.** (17, 9) *And behold, I stand upon the top of the hill, with the rod of God in my hand,* says Moses to Joshua son of Nun, when he commanded that battle be given against Amalek. Now, then, it is called the rod of God, which was first called the rod of Aaron, afterward the rod of Moses: just as the *spirit of Elijah* is called what is the Spirit of God[^1], of whom Elijah was made a partaker; so could that rod be called. It is also called the righteousness of God, which is ours but given by God: of which the Apostle, speaking, reproves the Jews, saying: *being ignorant of the righteousness of God, and wishing to establish their own righteousness*[^2], that is, as though prepared by themselves for themselves; against such he says: *for what do you have that you did not receive?*[^3].

[^1]: See Luke 1:17.
[^2]: Rom 10:3.
[^3]: 1 Cor 4:7.
