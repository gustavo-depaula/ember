# How it is commanded that the hands of Aaron and his sons be filled.

**121.** (28, 41) When he was prescribing concerning Aaron and the sons of Aaron, speaking to Moses, how they were to be clothed and anointed, what does it mean that he says: *and you shall fill their hands, that they may perform the priestly office for me?* Is it perhaps with the gifts that are to be offered to God?
