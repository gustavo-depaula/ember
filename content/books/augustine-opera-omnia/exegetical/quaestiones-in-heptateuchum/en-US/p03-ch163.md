# On the words: the sacrifice shall not sleep until morning...

**163.** (34, 25) What does it mean that he says: *and the sacrifice of the solemnity of the Passover shall not sleep until morning;* unless what he plainly prescribed above, that nothing of the flesh of the animal which is sacrificed be left until morning? But the expression made the obscurity: for he said *shall sleep,* for "shall remain."
