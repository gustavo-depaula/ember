# "Earth" put for "water."

**54.** (15, 12) *You stretched out your right hand, the earth swallowed them up.* It is no wonder that *earth* is put for *water.* For that whole outermost or lowest part of the world is reckoned under the name of earth; according to what is often said: *God who made heaven and earth*[^1]: and in the distribution of that Psalm, after the heavenly things are mentioned, it says: *praise the Lord from the earth*[^2], and goes on to recount in praise the things that pertain also to the waters.

[^1]: Ps 146:6 (145).
[^2]: Ps 148:7.
