# What the work of a perfumer is.

**136.** (30, 34) When he prescribed with what aromatic spices the *thymiama,* that is, incense, is to be made, and says, in the manner of a perfumer, *compounded, the work of a perfumer*: we ought not for that reason to think that an ointment is being made, that is, something with which to anoint, but, as was said, *thymiama,* or *incense,* which is to be placed on that altar of incense, where it was not lawful to sacrifice, and which was within in the Holy of Holies.
