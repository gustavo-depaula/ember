# How the Spirit of God is now mentioned for the fifth time.

**55.** (15, 10. 8) *You sent your Spirit, and the sea covered them.* See, now for the fifth time the Spirit of God is mentioned—if we count in this number also what was said: *this is the finger of God*[^1]. First, where it is written: *the Spirit of God was borne over the waters*[^2]; second, where it is said: *my Spirit shall not abide in these men, for they are flesh*[^3]; third, where Pharaoh says to Joseph: *for the Spirit of God is in you*[^4]; fourth, where the magicians of the Egyptians say: *this is the finger of God*; fifth, in this song: *you sent your Spirit, and the sea covered them*[^5]*.*

But let us bear in mind that the Spirit of God is mentioned not only with respect to benefits, but also with respect to vengeance. For what else did he say above: *by the Spirit of your wrath the water was divided?* This Spirit of God, therefore, was toward the Egyptians the Spirit of his wrath, to whom the dividing of the waters did harm, so that as they entered they could be overwhelmed by the returning waters; but to the children of Israel, whom the dividing of the water benefited, that was not the Spirit of God's wrath.

From this it is signified that, on account of different operations and effects, the Spirit of God is named differently, though it is one and the same Spirit—who is also received as the Holy Spirit in the unity of the Trinity. Accordingly, I do not think any other than the same is signified where the Apostle says: *for you did not receive the spirit of slavery again unto fear; but you received the Spirit of adoption, in whom we cry: Abba, Father*[^6]: because by the same Spirit of God, that is, the finger of God, by which the Law was written on tablets of stone[^7], fear was struck into those who did not yet understand grace, so that they might be convicted of their own weakness and sins by the Law, and the Law might become for them a tutor, by whom they might be led to the grace that is in faith in Jesus Christ[^8].

But concerning this Spirit of adoption and grace—that is, concerning this work of the Spirit of God by which grace and regeneration unto eternal life are imparted—it is said: *but the Spirit gives life;* when above it had been said, *the letter kills*[^9], that is, the written Law, merely commanding, without the help of grace.
