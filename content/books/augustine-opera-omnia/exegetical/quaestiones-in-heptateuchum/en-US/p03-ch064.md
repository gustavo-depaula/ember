# It is Aaron, not Moses, who is read to have struck the river with the rod.

**64.** (17, 5) *And the Lord said to Moses*: '*go before the people; take with you some of the elders of the people; and take in your hand the rod with which you struck the river.*' It is Aaron, not Moses, who is read to have struck the river with the rod[^1]. For Moses with the same rod divided the sea, not the river[^2]: what then does this mean: *take the rod with which you struck the river?* Or did he perhaps call the sea a river? If so, an example of this manner of speaking must be sought. Or was what Aaron did rather attributed to Moses, because through Moses God commanded what Aaron should do; and in Moses was the authority, but in Aaron the ministry? Since indeed in his very first words God said this to Moses about his brother: *he shall be to you as a mouth to the people, and you to him as the things that pertain to God*[^3]?

[^1]: See Exod 7:19.
[^2]: See Exod 14:21.
[^3]: Exod 4:16.
