# On the fact that the Lord called him from the bush.

**3.** (3:4) *The Lord called him from the bush*. The Lord in an angel? Or the Lord, that angel who was called *the angel of great counsel*[^1], and is understood to be Christ? For above he said: *the angel of the Lord appeared to him in a flame of fire from the bush*[^2].

[^1]: Isa 9:6.
[^2]: Exod 3:2.
