# On the little shields in the priestly vestment.

**115.** (28, 14. 16. 9-10) What does he call *little shields* in the priestly vestment? Are they the small disks, which are called in Latin from the shield (*scutum*), since the Greeks too call a shield *aspis*? Or does he say *little shields* (*aspidiscae*), from the asp serpent, on account of their being carefully fastened together, just as *murenae* too are so named?

*The length a span, and the breadth a span*: some Latin translators rendered, "the measure of an extended palm from the end of the thumb to the end of the little finger."

Likewise, as for what is said: *you shall take two onyx stones, and engrave on them the names of the children of Israel; six names on one stone, and the six remaining on the other, according to the order of their birth*[^1]*,* is it to be understood that the stones are to bear the names of the children of Israel according to their births, that is, according to the order in which they were born?

[^1]: Exod 28:9-10.
