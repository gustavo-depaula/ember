# This testing is a proving, not a seduction into sin.

**58.** (16, 4) *And the Lord said to Moses*: '*behold, I will rain down bread for you from heaven; and the people shall go out and gather a day's portion each day, that I may test them, whether they will walk in my law or not.*' This testing is a proving, not a seduction into sin; nor is it a proving in order that God may know, but in order to show men to themselves, so that they may become more humble in asking for help and in acknowledging the grace of God.
