# On the fact that he ordered all things to be anointed with the ointment of chrism.

**135.** (30, 26-33) It is also to be observed and noted how he ordered all things to be anointed with the ointment of chrism, namely the tabernacle and the things that were in it, and then *they shall be a Holy of Holies.* All things, namely, when they have been anointed, shall be a Holy of Holies. What, then, will now be the difference between those inner things, which are covered by the veil, and the rest, if all things, when they have been anointed, shall be a Holy of Holies, is to be inquired into more carefully: yet we believed these things were to be noted.

Where let us also remember that, just as of that altar of sacrifices, which after the anointing he wished to be called *a Holy of the holy,* it was immediately said: *everyone who touches it shall be sanctified*[^1]: so of all the things afterward which, anointed with that ointment, were called *a Holy of Holies,* the same statement followed, so that it was said: *everyone who touches them shall be sanctified.*

Which can be understood in two ways; either he shall be sanctified by touching, or he shall be sanctified so that it may be lawful for him to touch: if indeed it was not lawful for the people to touch the tabernacle, when they brought victims, or whatever was brought by them and offered to God. For consequently he admonishes that what he says to Moses is to be said not to the priests alone nor to the Levites alone: *and you shall speak to the children of Israel, saying*: surely the children of Israel were that whole people; and he orders it to be said to them: *the oil of the anointing-balm, this shall be holy to you throughout your generations; it shall not be smeared upon the flesh of a man, and according to this composition you shall not make for yourselves the like. It is holy, and it shall be a sanctification to you. Whoever makes the like, and whoever gives of it to a foreign nation, shall perish from his people.*

He orders, therefore, not the priests alone, but the whole people of Israel, not to make such ointment for human uses. For this is what he says, *it shall not be smeared upon the flesh of a man.* He forbids, then, that the like be made for their own uses, and threatens destruction if anyone makes the like, that is, prepares an ointment like it for his own uses, or gives of it to anyone of a foreign nation. And by this, what he says: *it shall be a sanctification to you,* since he orders this to be said to the whole people of Israel, I do not see what I should understand, except that it was lawful for them, when each came with his own gifts, to touch the tabernacle; and by touching they were sanctified on account of that oil with which all things were anointed; and hence it was said: *everyone who touches shall be sanctified*: yet not in the way the priests were, who were also anointed from it so that they might perform the priestly office.

[^1]: Exod 29:37.
