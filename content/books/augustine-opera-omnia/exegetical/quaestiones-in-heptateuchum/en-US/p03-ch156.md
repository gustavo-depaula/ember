# On what God says to Moses.

**156.** (34, 10) God, to Moses, who was again about to write on two tablets of stone on the mountain, says among other things: *in the sight of all your people I will do glorious things.* He does not yet deign to say: "in the sight of all my people." Or did he say *your people* in the way it would be said to any man of that same people, that is: "the people you are from": as we say "your city," not the one you rule, or which you founded, but the one whose citizen you are? For thus too a little after he says: *all the people, among whom you are*: in another way, what is said but, "your people"? But that he did not say "in whom you are," it is the custom of the language.
