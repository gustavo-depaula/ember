# That God (for he is spirit) is contained in no place.

**101.** (24, 9) *And Moses went up, and Aaron, and Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel; and they saw the place where the God of Israel had stood.* It is agreed among those who understand rightly that God is contained in no place, nor sets his members in any posture of the body, as is the case with our body—to sit, to lie, to stand, and whatever is of this kind. For these belong only to bodies, but God is spirit[^1]. So when he shows himself in a bodily appearance or in signs bodily expressed, it is not his substance that appears, by which he himself is what he is, but the assuming of visible forms is subject to his omnipotence.

[^1]: See John 4:24.
