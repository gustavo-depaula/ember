# How Aaron calls that rod his own there.

**20.** (7:10) This too is to be noted: that when that sign was done before Pharaoh, it is written, *and Aaron cast down his rod*: whereas perhaps, if it had said "he cast down the rod," there would be no question; but that it added *his own*, when Moses gave it, was perhaps not said in vain. Or was that rod common to both, so that it might be said to be either one's, and be said truly?
