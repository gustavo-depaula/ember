# What he means by what he prescribed concerning observing the Sabbath.

**139.** (31, 16-17) What does he mean, when prescribing concerning observing the Sabbath, by saying: *an everlasting covenant in me and the children of Israel;* he does not say, "between me and the children of Israel"? Is it because the Sabbath signifies rest, and there is no rest for us except in him? For surely by *the children of Israel* he means his whole people, that is, the seed of Abraham: and there is an Israel according to the flesh, and according to the spirit. For if "Israel" were not to be said except from the lineage of the flesh, the Apostle would not say: *see Israel according to the flesh*[^1]: where surely he signifies that there is an Israel according to the spirit, *he who is a Jew in secret, and by the circumcision of the heart*[^2].

So, then, it is perhaps better distinguished thus: *an everlasting covenant in me;* so that then there is another sense, *and to the children of Israel it is an everlasting sign,* that is, a sign of an eternal thing: as the rock was Christ, because *the rock* signified *Christ*[^3]. It is not, then, to be joined thus: *an everlasting covenant in me and the children of Israel,* as though this covenant were in God and the children of Israel; but *an everlasting covenant in me,* because in him eternal rest was promised; *and to the children of Israel it is an everlasting sign,* because the children of Israel received a sign to be observed, by which eternal rest is signified to the true Israelites, that is, to the children of the promise[^4], who will see God face to face as he is[^5].

[^1]: 1 Cor 10:18.
[^2]: See Rom 2:29.
[^3]: 1 Cor 10:4.
[^4]: See Gal 4:28.
[^5]: See 1 John 3:2.
