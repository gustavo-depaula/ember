# How Moses is read to have first sacrificed to God after the exodus from Egypt.

**99.** (24, 6-7) *And Moses, taking half the blood, poured it into a bowl, and the remaining part of the blood he poured out at the altar; and taking the book of the Testament, he read it in the ears of the people.* It is to be noted that Scripture now plainly says Moses sacrificed for the first time, from the time the people was led out of Egypt. For first it had been said of Jethro his father-in-law, though with some ambiguity, that he sacrificed to God[^1]. And it is to be observed that the book of the Testament is read with the blood of the victim, in which book we ought to take those ordinances of justice to be written. For that the decalogue of the Law was written on tablets of stone is afterward manifest.

[^1]: See Exod 18:12.
