# On the words: No one shall covet anything of your land.

**161.** (34, 24) What he says: *no one shall covet anything of your land, when you go up to appear in the sight of your God three times a year,* he wishes to be understood thus, that each one might go up secure, and not be anxious about his land, God promising protection that no one would covet anything from it, on account of him who went up, lest he fear his own absence from it. And here he sufficiently shows what he said above: *you shall not appear in the sight of the Lord your God empty*[^1], because he said it of that place where God was to have a tabernacle or Temple.

[^1]: Ex 34:20.
