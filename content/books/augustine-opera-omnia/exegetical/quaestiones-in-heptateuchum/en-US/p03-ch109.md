# On the two brackets.

**109.** (26, 17) *You shall make two little brackets for each board, standing over against each other*: that is, one on this side and one on that, on the sides of the board. He says *little brackets,* which we commonly call *brackets,* such as are in the columns of wine cellars, on which rest the timbers that bear the casks. The likeness of the word is drawn from the elbows (*cubiti*), where the arms are bent, on which those who recline lean, who in Greek are called *ἀγκῶνες.*
