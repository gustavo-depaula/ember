# The Lord indicates that what remained over was to be burned with fire.

**41.** (12, 10. 46) *But whatever remains of it until morning you shall burn with fire.* It can be asked how anything would remain over, since they had been forewarned that, if a household did not have a number sufficient for consuming the animal, the neighbors should be taken in[^1]. But it is understood, from the saying *you shall not break a bone of it,* that bones at any rate were to remain, to be burned with fire.

[^1]: See Exod 12:4.
