# Who the elect are.

**102.** (24, 10-11) *And of the elect of Israel not one was discordant; and they appeared in the place of God, and ate, and drank.* Who would doubt that those whom he expressed by name, and the seventy elders, are now called *the elect of Israel?*—who without doubt bore the person of those who are elect among the people of God. For *not all have faith*[^1]: and *the Lord knows those who are his.* But in a great house there are some vessels for honor, others for dishonor[^2]. Since, then, *those whom he foreknew, he also predestined; and those whom he predestined, them he also called; and those whom he called, them he also justified; and those whom he justified, them he also glorified*[^3]: surely *of the elect of Israel not one was discordant.*

And they are signified by the number four in Moses and Aaron and Nadab and Abihu, on account of the four Gospels, and the promise of the whole world, which is divided into four parts: and the seventy of the elders of Israel, by the number seven multiplied tenfold, which pertains to signifying the Holy Spirit. But by the sapphire is signified the heavenly life, especially because it was said, *like the appearance of the firmament.* And who does not know that the firmament is called heaven? And the form of a tile in the same sapphire figures the squareness itself, or stability, or the mystery of that same number four.

But that they eat and drink in the place of God signifies the sweetness and fullness in that kingdom of eternity. For *blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled*[^4]. Whence the Lord too says that many will come (whom indeed, but the elect, the foreknown, the predestined, the called, the justified, the glorified?), and will recline *with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven*[^5]. For in another place too he himself promises this to his faithful, that *he will make them recline, and will pass by and minister to them*[^6].

[^1]: 2 Thess 3:2.
[^2]: See 2 Tim 2:20.
[^3]: Rom 8:29-30.
[^4]: Matt 5:6.
[^5]: Matt 8:11.
[^6]: Luke 12:37.
