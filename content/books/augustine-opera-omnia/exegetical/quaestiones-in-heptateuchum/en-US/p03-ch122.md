# What is signified by the headdresses.

**123.** (29, 8-9) When he was speaking of the sons of Aaron: *and you shall clothe them,* he says, *with tunics, and gird them with girdles, and put headdresses about them.* What he calls a *headdress* or *headdresses,* since it was not translated, nor is it now in use, is unknown: yet I think it is not a covering of the head, as some have thought. For he would not say, *you shall put them about them,* unless it were something of such a kind as would be of use not to the head, but to the body.
