# How Moses is signified to have fasted forty days.

**165.** (34, 28) *And Moses was there in the sight of the Lord forty days and forty nights; he ate no bread, and drank no water.* What he had also said before, when he received the tablets which he broke, this he now repeats in this way; not by recapitulating what was done, but commending that it was done again. For we have already said what the repetition of the Law signifies[^1]. But what he says: *he ate no bread, and drank no water,* is understood as "he fasted," the whole from a part; that is, with Scripture signifying by the name of bread all food, and by the name of water all drink.

[^1]: See q. 2.144.
