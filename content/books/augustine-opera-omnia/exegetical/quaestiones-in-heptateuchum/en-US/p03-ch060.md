# The manna is signified by the name of bread.

**60.** (16, 12) God commands the people through Moses: *in the evening you shall eat flesh, and in the morning you shall be filled with bread.* See, "bread" is not named for every kind of food. For under that name flesh too would be included, since it also is food; yet he does not now call "bread" those things which are made from grain, for these we are accustomed properly to call "bread": but he calls the manna by the name of *bread.* Yet it is not without meaning that he says he will give flesh in the evening and bread in the morning. For something like this was signified also in Elijah, when the raven brought him food[^1]. Or perhaps by flesh in the evening and bread in the morning is signified he *who was delivered up for our offenses, and rose again for our justification*[^2]? For in the evening, having died in weakness, he was buried[^3]; but in the morning he appeared to the disciples[^4], having risen in power.

[^1]: See 1 Kgs 17:6.
[^2]: Rom 4:25.
[^3]: See Matt 27:57 ff.
[^4]: See Mark 16:9.
