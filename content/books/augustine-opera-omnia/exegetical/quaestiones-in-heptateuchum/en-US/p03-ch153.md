# That Moses himself first said this to God.

**153.** (33, 12. 17) Is it to be noted that Moses himself had first said to God: *you said to me*: '*I know you above all';* which God is read to have said to him after Moses himself said this to God, but is not read to have said before; so that we may understand that not all the things God spoke with him are written? But it must be inquired into more carefully in the earlier parts of Scripture whether it is truly so.
