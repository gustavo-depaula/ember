# That God justly wills the whole, no one doubts.

**8.** (4:11) *Who made the mute and the hearing, the seeing and the blind? Is it not I, the Lord God?* There are those who slander God, or rather the Scripture of the Old Testament, because God said that he himself made the blind and the mute. What, then, do they say of the Lord Christ, openly saying in the Gospel: *I came that those who do not see may see, and that those who see may become blind*[^1]? But who, except a fool, would believe that anything could happen to a man according to bodily defects, which God did not will? But that he justly wills the whole, no one doubts.

[^1]: John 9:39.
