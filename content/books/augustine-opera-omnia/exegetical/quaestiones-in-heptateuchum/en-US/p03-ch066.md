# Where they are said to have eaten bread before God or in the presence of God.

**66.** (18, 12) *And Aaron came, and all the elders of Israel, to eat bread with the father-in-law of Moses before God,* or, as other manuscripts have it, *in the presence of God;* which in Greek is written *enantion tou theou.* It is asked where "before God," when there was as yet no tabernacle, nor ark of the covenant, which were instituted afterward. For here we cannot take it as said of the future, as it was said of the manna that was placed in the golden vessel. Therefore we ought to take *before God* as meaning what was done in honor of God: for where is God not?
