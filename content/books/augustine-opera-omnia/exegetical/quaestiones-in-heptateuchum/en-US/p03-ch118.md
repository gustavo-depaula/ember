# The sound of Aaron entering and going out of the holy place.

**119.** (28, 35) *And it shall be, when Aaron begins to perform the priestly office, his sound shall be heard as he enters the holy place in the sight of the Lord and as he goes out, that he may not die*: he said that the sound of one entering and going out is heard from the little bells, and he set such weight of observance upon it that he said, *lest he die.*

He wished, therefore, that certain testimonies be signified in the priestly vestment—by which the Church is surely signified—through these little bells, so that the conduct of the priest may be known: as the Apostle says: *showing yourself in all things an example of good works*[^1]; or that other: *the things which you have heard from me through many witnesses, these same things entrust to faithful men, who will be fit to teach others also*[^2]. Or something else? Yet it is something great, whatever it is. But *to one entering and going out,* for "of one entering and going out"; it is a manner of speech. *Voice* for "sound"; for of little bells it is rather a sound than a voice.

[^1]: Titus 2:7.
[^2]: 2 Tim 2:2.
