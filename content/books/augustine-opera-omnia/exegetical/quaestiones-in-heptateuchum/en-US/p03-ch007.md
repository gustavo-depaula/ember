# On what Moses said to the Lord.

**7.** (4:10) As to what Moses said to the Lord, *I beg you, Lord, I am not eloquent, neither before yesterday, nor before the day before, nor since you began to speak to your servant*, it is understood that he could believe himself to be made suddenly eloquent by the will of God, when he says, *nor since you began to speak to your servant*; as if showing that it could have come about that one who had not been eloquent before yesterday and the day before might suddenly become so, from the time when the Lord began to speak with him.
