# Whence the enchanters are said to have brought forth frogs.

**23.** (8:7) *But the enchanters of the Egyptians did likewise by their sorceries; and they brought forth frogs upon the land of Egypt*. It is asked whence, if it had already been done everywhere. But a similar question is whence they turned water into blood too, if all the water of Egypt had already been turned into blood. Accordingly it is to be understood that the region where the children of Israel dwelt was not struck with such plagues: and from there the enchanters could either draw water to turn into blood, or bring forth some frogs, merely to demonstrate their magic power. Although they could also do it after those things had been suppressed: but Scripture, narrating quickly, joined together what could even afterward be done.
