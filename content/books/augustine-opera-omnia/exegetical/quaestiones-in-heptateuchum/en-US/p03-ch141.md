# On the fact that he orders Aaron to take off the earrings.

**141.** (32, 2) That Aaron orders the earrings to be taken off *from the ears of the wives and daughters,* from which he might make gods for them, is not absurdly understood to mean that he wished to prescribe difficult things, that in this way he might recall them from that intention: yet that very thing was made difficult—that there should be gold for making the idol—I have thought worthy of note on account of those who are saddened, if anything of the kind is divinely ordered to be done, or to be borne with equanimity, for the sake of eternal life.
