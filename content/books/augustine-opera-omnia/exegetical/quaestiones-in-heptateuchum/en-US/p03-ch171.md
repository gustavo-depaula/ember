# How the justice of the architects is to be marked.

**171.** (36, 4-5) It is to be noted that those who are called *wise, makers of the holy works,* were such also in morals, that when they themselves received all that the people offered, judging it necessary for completing all those things, they saw that more was offered than was needed, and told Moses; and he, through a herald, forbade the people to offer further. But they could, if they wished, have taken away much; but modesty forbade, or religion terrified them.
