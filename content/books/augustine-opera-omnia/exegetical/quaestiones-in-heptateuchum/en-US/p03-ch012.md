# Moses placed his wife and infants on the vehicles.

**12.** (4:20) As to what was said above, that Moses placed his wife and his infants on the vehicles, to proceed with them into Egypt, but afterward Jethro his father-in-law met him with them, after he had led the people out of Egypt[^1], it can be asked how both are true. But it is to be understood that, after that killing of Moses or of the infant which was to be made by the angel, she returned with the little ones. For some have thought that the angel terrified them on this account, lest the female sex accompany the ministry which Moses, divinely imposed, was carrying out, to its hindrance.

[^1]: See Exod 18:1-5.
