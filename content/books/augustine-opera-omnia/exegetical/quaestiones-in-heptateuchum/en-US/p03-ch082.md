# How what is said about the ox being struck is to be taken.

**82.** (21, 35) *But if someone's ox gores his neighbor's ox with its horn, and it dies, they shall sell the live ox, and divide its price, and they shall divide the ox that died.* Is this rule of justice to be observed in the ox alone, and not concerning every animal in such a case? Accordingly this too is to be understood as the whole from a part: but this cannot be done with the flesh of the slain animal, which is not eaten.
