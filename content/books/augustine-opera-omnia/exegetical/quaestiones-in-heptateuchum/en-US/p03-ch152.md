# What it means that God says to Moses.

**152.** (33, 17) What does it mean that God says to Moses: *since I know you above all*? Does God know some things more, and some things less? Or is it according to what is said to certain ones in the Gospel: *I do not know you*[^1]? For according to this knowledge, by which God is said to know what pleases him, and not to know what displeases him—not because he is ignorant of these, but because he does not approve, just as an art is rightly said not to know faults, though it knows faults—God knew Moses above all, because Moses pleased God above all.

[^1]: Matt 25:12.
