# Why Aaron is bidden to take the rod.

**19.** (7:9) *If Pharaoh speaks to you, saying: 'Give us a sign or a portent'; you will say to Aaron your brother: 'Take the rod, and cast it down before Pharaoh and before his servants; and it will become a serpent.'* Here surely there was no need of the ministry of the voice, for which Aaron seemed to have been given as it were of necessity on account of the thinness of Moses' voice; but the rod was to be cast down so that it should become a serpent: why, then, did Moses himself not do this, except because this mediation of Aaron between Moses and Pharaoh bears the figure of some great matter?
