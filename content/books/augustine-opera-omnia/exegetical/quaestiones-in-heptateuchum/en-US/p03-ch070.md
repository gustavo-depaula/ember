# How it is signified that the Law, written by the finger of God, was given on the fiftieth day from Israel's exodus from Egypt.

**70.** (19, 1-3. 10-11) *And in the third month of the exodus of the children of Israel from the land of Egypt, on this day they came into the wilderness of Sinai; and they set out from Rephidim, and came into the wilderness of Sinai, and Israel encamped there over against the mountain, and Moses went up into the mountain of God, and the Lord called him from the mountain, saying*: '*these things you shall say to the house of Jacob, and you shall announce to the children of Israel,* and so forth.' Then a little after: *go down, and testify to the people, and purify them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments, and let them be ready for the third day. For on the third day the Lord will come down upon Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people.*

On this day the Law is found to have been given, which was written on tablets of stone by the finger of God, as what follows shows[^1]. And this third day appears to be of the third month from Israel's exodus from Egypt. From the day, then, on which they kept the Passover—that is, sacrificed and ate the lamb, which was the fourteenth of the first month[^2]—until this day on which the Law is given, fifty days are counted: namely seventeen of the first month, those remaining from the fourteenth itself; then all thirty of the second month, which make forty-seven; and the third day of the third month, which is the fiftieth from the solemnity of the slain lamb. And by this, in that shadow of the future, according to the feast day of the immolated lamb, just as the Law was given on the fiftieth day, which was written by the finger of God: so in the very truth of the New Testament, from the festival of the immaculate Lamb Christ Jesus, fifty days are counted, until the Holy Spirit was given from on high[^3]. And that the finger of God is the Holy Spirit, we have already said above, with the Gospel as witness[^4].

[^1]: See Exod 31:18.
[^2]: See Exod 12:6.
[^3]: See Acts 2:2-4.
[^4]: See q. 2, 25.
