# How the incense of continuation is to be understood.

**133.** (30, 8. 10) *He shall burn upon it the incense of continuation in the sight of the Lord throughout their generations.* He says *incense of continuation,* which would be done continuously, that is, omitted on no day. When he was prescribing concerning the altar of incense—that is, on which only incense was placed, not holocaust, not sacrifice, not libation—he had foretold that this very incense ought to be placed daily: but now he says: *and Aaron shall make propitiation* or *shall entreat upon its horns once a year with the blood of the purification of offenses, of propitiation. Of propitiation,* from that which is propitiation, which in Greek is called *exilasmos.*

Whence it is also to be understood that this, which he orders to be done once a year for propitiating God upon the horns of the altar of incense—that is, that with the blood of the purification of offenses, namely of the victims which are offered for offenses, the horns of the altar of incense be touched once a year—does not pertain to that placing of incense which he had ordered to be done daily. For that was done with aromatic spices, not with blood; and daily, not once a year.

We are not, therefore, to understand that the priest was accustomed to enter the holy of holies once a year, but once a year with blood: and daily indeed he was accustomed to enter without blood, for the sake of placing incense; but with blood once a year: especially because it continues and says: *once a year he shall purify it; it is a Holy of Holies to the Lord.* Not, therefore, once a year will he place there the incense, which was ordered to be done daily; but once a year he will purify it, which was prescribed to be done with blood. And after this he adds: *it is a Holy of Holies to the Lord*: and by this, if the Holy of Holies was not outside but within the veil; surely that altar too, of which we now treat, which he ordered to be placed over against the veil, he ordered to be within.
