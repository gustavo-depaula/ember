# Whether the place was called Marah, or "Bitterness," by the people of Israel.

**56.** (15, 23-24) *And they came to Marah, and they could not drink of Marah; for it was bitter.* If the name of that place was called "bitterness" for this reason, that they could not drink the water there because it was bitter (for Marah means "Bitterness"), how did they come to Marah, unless because Scripture called by that name the place to which they came, by which it was already called when these things were being written? For these things were surely written later than they happened.
