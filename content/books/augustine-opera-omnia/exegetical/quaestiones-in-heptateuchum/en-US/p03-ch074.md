# How God tests.

**74.** (20, 20) *And Moses said to them*: '*be steadfast; for God has come to you for this, to test you, that the fear of him may be in you, lest you sin.*' So they had to be restrained from sins, namely by fearing lest they suffer sensible punishments; because they could not yet love righteousness: and in this was for them a testing from the Lord, by which they were proved, so that it might appear what kind of people they were; not that they might become known to God, from whom they were not hidden, of whatever kind they were, but that they might be known among themselves and to themselves. Yet by these terrors the difference of the Old Testament is greatly commended, which is also stated most plainly in the *Letter to the Hebrews*[^1].

[^1]: See Heb 12:24-28.
