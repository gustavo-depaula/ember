# How only two tablets of stone are given to Moses.

**140.** (31, 18) *And he gave to Moses, as soon as he ceased to speak to him on Mount Sinai, two tablets of the testimony, tablets of stone written by the finger of God.* Although God spoke so many things, yet two tablets of stone are given to Moses, which are called *tablets of the testimony,* to be in the Ark. Surely all the other things which God prescribed are understood to depend on those ten precepts, which were written on the two tablets, if they are carefully inquired into and well understood: just as these ten precepts themselves in turn depend on those two, namely the love of God and of neighbor, on which *the whole Law depends, and the Prophets*[^1].

[^1]: Matt 22:40.
