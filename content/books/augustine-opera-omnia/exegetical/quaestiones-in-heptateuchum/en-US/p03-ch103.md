# Joshua son of Nun, not mentioned before, appears with Moses.

**103.** (24, 13) What does it mean that Joshua son of Nun, not mentioned among those four, suddenly appears with Moses, and goes up with him into the mountain to receive the tablets of the Law; and suddenly the same Joshua is again hidden, that is, passed over in silence; and Moses received the Law on two tablets, and again Joshua appears with him? Or does it perhaps signify the New Testament by the name of Joshua, and that it is hidden in the Law, and at times appears to those who understand? But that he is now called Joshua, when Scripture testifies in the book of *Numbers* that he received that name when they were already near to entering the land of promise[^1], Scripture by prolepsis, that is, by anticipation, anticipates what was done afterward. For all these things were written after they were done; and by this, when what is now mentioned was done, he was not yet called Joshua, but when it was written he was already so called.

[^1]: See Num 13:17.
