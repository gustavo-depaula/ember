# On the fact that Moses, angry, broke the tablets of the testimony.

**144.** (32, 19-20) Moses, angry, indeed seems to have dashed down and broken the tablets of the testimony written by the finger of God; yet by a great mystery the repetition of the Testament was figured, since the Old was to be abolished, and the New established. It is to be noted, surely, with how great a supplication he labored to God for the people, he who showed himself so severe in punishing them. But what we think the calf—which, cast molten into the fire, he ground to powder and scattered into water, which he gave the people to drink—signifies, we have already written elsewhere, in the work against Faustus the Manichee.
