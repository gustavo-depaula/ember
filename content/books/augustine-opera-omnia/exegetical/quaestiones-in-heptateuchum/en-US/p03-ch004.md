# How Israel is said to be led out into a good land.

**4.** (3:8) *To lead them out of that land into a good and ample land, into a land flowing with milk and honey*: are we to take "a land flowing with milk and honey" spiritually, because according to its proper sense that land which was given to the people of Israel was not this? Or is it a manner of speech, by which this is referred to the praise of its richness and sweetness?
