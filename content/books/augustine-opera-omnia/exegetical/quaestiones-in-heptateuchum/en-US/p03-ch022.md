# Whether Pharaoh's heart is therefore said to have been hardened.

**22.** (7:22) *But the enchanters of the Egyptians did likewise by their sorceries; and Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not heed them, as the Lord had said*. When these things are said, it seems that Pharaoh's heart was therefore hardened because the enchanters of the Egyptians also did similar things: but what follows will teach how great that hardening was, even when the enchanters failed.
