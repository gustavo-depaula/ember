# What is to be thought of the separation of the land in which the Israelites dwelt and that in which the Egyptians dwelt.

**26.** (8:21-23) *Behold, I send upon you, and upon your servants, and upon your people, and upon your houses the dog-fly, and the houses of the Egyptians will be filled with the dog-fly; that you may know that I am the Lord God of all the earth: and I will set a separation between my people and your people*. What Scripture here disclosed, lest it say it everywhere, we ought to understand to have been done in both the later and the earlier signs: that the land in which the people of God dwelt was vexed by no such plagues.

And it was opportune that this should be set down openly here, whence there now begin the signs which the magicians did not even attempt to do the like: for without doubt, because the gnats had been everywhere in Pharaoh's kingdom, but had not been in the land of Goshen, there the magicians attempted to do likewise, and were by no means able. Up to where, then, they failed, nothing was said about the separation of that land; but from the time when those things began to be done, where they would no longer even dare to attempt to do the like.
