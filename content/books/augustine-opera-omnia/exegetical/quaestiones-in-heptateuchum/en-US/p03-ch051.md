# How the words are true, since the Israelites later saw Egyptians.

**51.** (14, 13) And Moses said: *be confident, and stand firm, and see the salvation that is from the Lord, which he will work for you today. For as you have seen the Egyptians today, you shall see them no more for ever.* How are these words to be taken, since the Israelites later saw Egyptians? Is it because those who then saw them did not see them again—since both those who were pursuing died, and all of these too, each on the day of his own death? For their descendants saw the descendants of the others. Or is it to be understood, "you shall not see them as today"—not as today, pursuing and hostile and coming after you in so great a column—so that there is no question at all, not even about the "for ever" which he set here; because, although both will see each other at the time of the resurrection, they will surely not see each other as today?
