# The purification and sanctification of the altar for seven days.

**130.** (29, 37) What does it mean that he says the altar, purified and sanctified for seven days, *shall be a Holy of the holy?* He does not indeed call the altar a Holy of Holies, like that which is separated by a veil, where the ark of the testimony is: nevertheless this altar too, placed outside the veil, he says becomes a Holy of the holy rather through the sanctification of seven days than through the anointing. And he adds: *everyone who touches the altar shall be sanctified.*
