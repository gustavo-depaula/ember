# Pharaoh does not yet fear the Lord.

**35.** (9, 27. 30) When, at the crash of the heavens, which was violent with hail, Pharaoh was terrified and begged Moses to pray for him, confessing his own iniquity and that of his people, Moses said to him: *I know that neither you nor your servants yet fear the Lord.* What kind of fear was Pharaoh seeking, in whom this fear was not yet the fear of the Lord? It is easy to fear punishment, but this is not to fear God—not, that is, with that fear of piety which Jacob recalls when he says: *if the God of my father Abraham and the fear of Isaac had not been with me, you would now have sent me away empty*[^1].

[^1]: Gen 31:42.
