# On what Aaron says to Moses.

**145.** (32, 24) *And they gave it to me, and I cast it into the fire, and this calf came out.* Did he speak in shorthand, not saying that he himself formed it, so that a molten calf came out? Or, for the sake of excuse, did he lie out of fear, as though he had thrown the gold into the fire to perish, and, without his bringing it about, the form of a calf came out? That he did not say this with such a mind is not to be believed, both because what was in the man with whom God spoke could not be hidden from Moses, and because he did not rebuke his brother for lying.
