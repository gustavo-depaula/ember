# How the rings are to be taken.

**106.** (25, 27) What he says: *the rings shall be on the sheaths of the carrying-poles for lifting the table,* is to be understood thus, that the rings would be as it were sheaths of the carrying-poles; that is, into which the carrying-poles are inserted as into sheaths. For *they shall be on the sheaths* is so said as if it were said: "they shall be for sheaths."
