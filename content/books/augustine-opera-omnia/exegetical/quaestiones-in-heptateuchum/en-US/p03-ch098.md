# How it is said: And they sacrificed a victim of salvation.

**98.** (24, 5) *And they sacrificed a victim of salvation to God.* He did not say "a salutary victim," but *a victim of salvation;* which the Greek has as *σωτηρίου.* Whence in the *Psalm* it says: *I will take the cup of salvation*[^1]: he did not say "the salutary cup." Where it is to be seen whether perhaps he is signified, of whom Simeon said: *for my eyes have seen your salvation*[^2]. For the *Psalm* too commends him where it is read: *declare well day after day his salvation*[^3]. For what else is it, if we attend more carefully, that he says *day from day,* but "light from light," that is, "God from God," which is the only-begotten Son?

[^1]: Ps 116:13 (115).
[^2]: Luke 2:30.
[^3]: Ps 96:2 (95).
