# On what the magicians said to Pharaoh: This is the finger of God.

**25.** (8:19) As to what the magicians said to Pharaoh, *this is the finger of God*, since they could not bring forth the gnats; they perceived assuredly, since they knew the power of their nefarious arts, that their attempts had been frustrated not by such arts—as though Moses were more powerful in them—so that they could not bring forth gnats, but by *the finger of God*, who surely was working through Moses. But the *finger of God*, as the Gospel most manifestly says, is understood as the Holy Spirit. For when one evangelist so narrates the words of the Lord that he says, *if I cast out demons by the finger of God*[^1]; another evangelist, narrating the same thing, wished to explain what the finger of God is, and says, *if I cast out demons by the Spirit of God*[^2].

And so, although the magicians, in whose power Pharaoh had trusted, confessed that the finger of God was in Moses, in whom they were overcome and their sorceries frustrated; yet Pharaoh's heart was now hardened with an altogether marvelous hardness. But why the magicians failed in that third plague (for the plagues began from the time when water was turned into blood), it is difficult both to perceive and to explain. For they could have failed also in the first sign, where the rod was turned into a serpent; and in the first plague, where water was changed into blood; and in the second, about the frogs, if the finger of God, that is, the Spirit of God, had so willed. For who, however demented, would say that the finger of God could hinder the attempts of the magicians in this sign, and was unable in the earlier ones?

Altogether, then, there is a certain cause why they were permitted to do those things up to this point. For perhaps the Trinity is commended, and—what is true—the highest philosophers of the gentiles, as far as can be traced in their writings, philosophized without the Holy Spirit, although they were not silent about the Father and the Son; which Didymus too mentions in his book which he wrote on the Holy Spirit.

[^1]: Luke 11:20.
[^2]: Matt 12:28.
