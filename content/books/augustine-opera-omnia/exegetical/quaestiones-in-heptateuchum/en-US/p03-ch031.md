# On what God says to Aaron and Moses.

**31.** (9:8-9) What does it mean that God says to Aaron and Moses: *take for yourselves handfuls of ashes from the furnace, and let Moses scatter it toward heaven in the sight of Pharaoh and in the sight of his servants, and let it become dust over all the land of Egypt*?

For the earlier signs were done by the rod, which not Moses but Aaron either extended over the water or struck the earth with: but now, two signs about the dog-fly and the deaths of the flocks having been interposed, where neither Aaron nor Moses worked anything with his hand, it is said that Moses should scatter the ashes from the furnace toward heaven, and both are bidden to take it, but he to scatter it not toward the earth, but toward heaven: as though Aaron, who had been given for the people, ought to strike the earth, or extend his hand toward the earth or toward the water; but Moses, of whom it was said, *he will be to you as those things which are toward God*[^1], is bidden to scatter the ashes toward heaven. What of those two earlier signs, where neither Moses nor Aaron works anything with the hand? What does this diversity mean? For it is surely not nothing.

[^1]: Exod 4:16.
