# Revisions II

## Chapter 24 (51): The Literal Meaning of Genesis, Twelve Books

**1.** During the same period I wrote the twelve books *On Genesis*, from its opening down to the point where Adam was sent out of paradise and *a flaming sword was set to guard the way to the tree of life*[^1]. Now when eleven books had been completed up to this point, I added a twelfth, in which paradise is discussed more carefully.

The title of these books is *The Literal Meaning of Genesis,* that is, not according to allegorical significations[^2], but according to the proper character of the events as they actually happened. In this work more things have been asked than found, and of those that were found fewer have been firmly established, while the rest have been so put forward as though they still needed to be inquired into.

These books I in fact began later, but finished before *On the Trinity.* So I have now reviewed them in the order in which I began them.

**2.** In the fifth book, and wherever in these books I put *concerning the seed to which the promise was made, that it was ordained by angels in the hand of a Mediator*[^3]*,* the Apostle does not have it so, as I afterward saw from the more reliable manuscripts, especially the Greek ones. For it was said of the Law that it was ordained as though it were said of the seed, which many Latin manuscripts have through the error of the translator.

In the sixth book, where I said that *Adam lost by sin the image of God according to which he was made*[^4]*,* this is not to be taken as though none of it remained in him, but that it was so disfigured as to need to be reformed.

In the twelfth book, concerning the underworld, I seem to myself to have shown rather that it is beneath the earth than to have given a reason why it is believed or said to be beneath the earth*,* as though it were not so[^5].

This work begins thus: *All Divine Scripture is twofold.*

[^1]: Gen 3:24
[^2]: See Revisions 1.10.18
[^3]: The Literal Meaning of Genesis 5.19.38; 9.16; see Gal 3:19
[^4]: The Literal Meaning of Genesis 6.27.28
[^5]: The Literal Meaning of Genesis 12.33
