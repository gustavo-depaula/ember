# Revisions II

## Chapter 65 (92): Eight Questions of Dulcitius, in one book

The book I have entitled *Eight Questions of Dulcitius* would not need to be mentioned in this work among my books, since it is put together out of things I had written earlier in other places, were it not that some discussion of my own is found inserted in it, and that to one of those questions I gave an answer not from any other small work of mine but from whatever I was then able to think of.

This book begins as follows: *As it seems to me, dearly beloved son Dulcitius*.
