# From the Book of Hosea

**156. 156.** [Hos 4]. Hear the word of the Lord, you children of Israel, for the Lord has a judgment with the inhabitants of the land. For there is no truth, and no mercy, and no knowledge of God in the land. Cursing, and lying, and killing, and theft, and adultery have overflowed, and blood has touched blood [4:1-2].  
*And after twelve verses:* Because you have rejected knowledge, I will reject you, that you shall not exercise the priesthood for me [4:6].  
*And after fifteen verses:* Because they have forsaken the Lord in not keeping faith. Fornication, and wine, and drunkenness take away the heart [4:10-11].  
*And after sixteen verses:* Because they consorted with harlots, and sacrificed with the effeminate. And the people that does not understand shall be beaten. If you commit fornication, O Israel, at least let Judah not transgress. And do not enter into Gilgal; and do not go up to Bethaven; nor swear: The Lord lives [4:14-15].

**157. 157.** [Hos 5]. *And after eighteen verses:* They will not set their thoughts to return to the Lord their God; for the spirit of fornication is in their midst, and they have not known the Lord [5:4].

**158. 158.** [Hos 6]. *And after sixty-one verses:* Your judgments shall go forth like the light. For I desired mercy and not sacrifice, and the knowledge of God more than holocausts [6:5-6].

**159. 159.** [Hos 10]. *And after a little:* Sow for yourselves in justice, and reap in the mouth of mercy: break up for yourselves fallow ground. But it is the time to seek the Lord, when he comes who has taught you justice [10:12].

**160. 160.** [Hos 12]. *And after seventy-eight verses:* And you shall turn to the Lord your God: keep mercy and judgment; and hope in the Lord your God always [12:6].

**161. 161.** [Hos 13]. *And after fifty-one verses:* But I am the Lord your God who brought you out of the land of Egypt; and you shall know no lord but me, and there is no savior besides me [13:4].

**162. 162.** [Hos 14]. *And after thirty-nine verses:* Return, O Israel, to the Lord your God; for you have fallen down in your iniquity. Take with you words, and turn to the Lord; say to him: Take away all iniquity, and receive the good, and we will render the calves of our lips [14:2-3].
