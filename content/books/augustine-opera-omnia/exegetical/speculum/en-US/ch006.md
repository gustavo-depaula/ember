# From Joshua

**35. 35.** [Josh 22]. Return, and go into your tents and into the land of your possession, which Moses the servant of the Lord delivered to you beyond the Jordan: yet so that you carefully keep, and fulfill in deed, the commandment and the law which Moses the servant of the Lord enjoined you; that you love the Lord your God, and walk in all his ways, and keep his commandments, and cleave to him, and serve him with all your heart and with all your soul [22:4-5].

**36. 36.** [Josh 23]. *And in another place:* Lest, after you have come in, *he says,* among the nations that shall be among you, you swear by the name of their gods, and serve them, and adore them: but cleave to the Lord your God, as you have done unto this day [23:7-8].

*But from the book of Psalms many things are to be set down, although the same things are often repeated: yet I will keep measure, as far as I can, lest this work, which is above all to be held in memory, run on to too great a length.*
