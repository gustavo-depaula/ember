# From the Book of Isaiah

**185. 185.** [Isa 1]. Wash yourselves, be clean, take away the evil of your thoughts from my eyes. Cease to act perversely, learn to do good, seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge for the orphan, defend the widow; and come and reprove me, says the Lord [1:16-18].  
*And after eight verses:* Your silver has turned to dross; your wine is mixed with water. Your princes are companions of thieves: they all love gifts, they follow after rewards, they do not judge for the orphan, and the cause of the widow does not come before them [1:22-23].

**186. 186.** [Isa 2]. *And a little later:* Come, O house of Jacob, let us walk in the light of the Lord. For you have cast off your people, the house of Jacob; because they are filled as in times past, and have had soothsayers like the Philistines, and have cleaved to strange children [2:5-6].  
*And after three verses:* Their land is filled with idols; they have adored the work of their own hands, which their fingers have made [2:8].  
*And after five verses:* Because the day of the Lord of hosts shall be upon everyone who is proud and lofty, and upon everyone who is arrogant, and he shall be humbled [2:12].

**187. 187.** [Isa 3]. *And a little later:* My people, their exactors have despoiled them, and women have ruled over them. My people, those who call you blessed, they themselves deceive you, and lay waste the way of your steps [3:12].  
*And after four verses:* For you have eaten up my vineyard, the spoil of the poor is in your house. Why do you grind down my people, and crush the faces of the poor, says the Lord of hosts? And the Lord said: Because the daughters of Zion are haughty, and have walked with stretched-out necks, and went with glances of their eyes, and clapped their hands, and walked, and stepped daintily with their feet [3:14-16].

**188. 188.** [Isa 5]. *And a little later:* Woe to you who join house to house, and couple field to field even to the end of the place [so as to take away from your neighbor: *Symmachus and Theodotion rendered it* until there is no room left, *or* until there is no place]: will you dwell alone in the midst of the earth? These things are in my ears, says the Lord of hosts [5:8-9].  
*And after three verses:* Woe to you who rise up early to pursue drunkenness, and to drink till evening, that you may be inflamed with wine. The harp, and the lyre, and the timbrel, and the pipe, and wine are in your feasts; and you do not regard the work of the Lord, nor consider the works of his hands [5:11-12].  
*And after seventeen verses:* Woe to you who call evil good, and good evil; who put darkness for light, and light for darkness; who put bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter. Woe to you who are wise in your own eyes, and prudent in your own sight. Woe to you who are mighty to drink wine, and strong men to mix drunkenness. You who justify the wicked for rewards, and take away the justice of the just from him [5:20-23].

**189. 189.** [Isa 10]. *And somewhat later:* Woe to those who make unjust laws, and writing have written injustice, to oppress the poor in judgment, and to do violence to the cause of the humble of my people; that widows might be their prey, and that they might plunder the orphans [10:1-2].

**190. 190.** [Isa 11]. *And after a little:* The spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and fortitude, the spirit of knowledge and godliness, and the spirit of the fear of the Lord shall fill him. He shall not judge according to the sight of the eyes, nor reprove according to the hearing of the ears: but he shall judge the poor in justice, and shall reprove in equity for the meek of the earth; and he shall strike the earth with the rod of his mouth, and with the breath of his lips he shall slay the wicked. And justice shall be the girdle of his loins, and faith the girdle of his reins [11:2-5].

**191. 191.** [Isa 12]. *And after sixty verses:* Behold, God is my savior, I will act confidently, and will not fear. For the Lord God is my strength and my praise, and he has become my salvation. You shall draw waters with joy out of the savior's fountains: and you shall say in that day: Give praise to the Lord, and call upon his name, make his works known among the peoples, remember that his name is high. Sing to the Lord, for he has done great things: declare this in all the earth. Rejoice and praise, O dwelling of Zion; for the Holy One of Israel is great in your midst [12:2-6].

**192. 192.** [Isa 13]. *And after forty verses:* And I will cause the pride of the unbelievers to cease, and I will humble the arrogance of the strong [13:11].

**193. 193.** [Isa 17]. *Likewise after a little:* The Lord God of Israel says: In that day a man shall bow down to his maker, and his eyes shall look to the Holy One of Israel; and he shall not bow down to the altars which his hands have made, and which his fingers have wrought; he shall not look to the groves and the shrines [17:6-8].

**194. 194.** [Isa 22]. *And after a little:* The Lord God of hosts shall call, *he says,* in that day to weeping and to mourning, and to baldness, and to girding with sackcloth. And behold joy and gladness, the slaying of calves, and the killing of rams, the eating of flesh, and the drinking of wine. Let us eat and drink; for tomorrow we shall die. And the voice of the Lord of hosts was revealed in my ears: Surely this iniquity shall not be forgiven you till you die, says the Lord God of hosts [22:12-14].

**195. 195.** [Isa 26]. *Likewise after a little:* Open the gates, and let the just nation enter, the nation that keeps the truth. The old error is gone; you will keep peace; peace, because we have hoped in you. You hope in the Lord forever and ever, in the Lord God mighty forever [26:2-4].  
*And after six verses:* In the path of your judgments, O Lord, we have waited for you; your name and your remembrance are the desire of the soul. My soul has desired you in the night; yes, and with my spirit in my inmost being I will watch for you in the morning. When you shall have done your judgments in the earth, the inhabitants of the earth shall learn justice. Let us have pity on the wicked man, and he will not learn to do justice. In the land of the saints he has done wicked things, and he shall not see the glory of God [26:8-10].

**196. 196.** [Isa 29]. *And after a little:* He who prevailed has failed, the scorner is consumed, and all those are cut off who watched for iniquity, who made men to sin by a word [29:20-21].

**197. 197.** [Isa 30]. *And after ten verses:* Woe to you, deserting children, says the Lord, that you would take counsel, and not from me; and would weave a web, and not by my spirit; that you might add sin upon sin [30:1].  
*And a little later:* Thus says the Lord God, the Holy One of Israel: If you return and rest, you shall be saved. In silence and in hope shall be your strength; and you would not [30:15].  
*And after twenty verses:* You shall defile the plates of your graven images of silver, and the garment of your molten thing of gold, and shall scatter them like the uncleanness of a menstruous woman [30:22].

**198. 198.** [Isa 31]. *And after a little:* Turn, as you had deeply revolted, O children of Israel. For in that day a man shall cast away the idols of his silver, and the idols of his gold, which your hands have made for you unto sin [31:6-7].

**199. 199.** [Isa 33]. *Likewise after a little:* And there shall be faith in your times: wisdom and knowledge are the riches of salvation; the fear of the Lord is itself his treasure [33:6].  
*And a little later:* Which of you can dwell with devouring fire? which of you shall dwell with everlasting burnings? He who walks in justice, and speaks truth; who casts away avarice gotten by extortion, and shakes his hands free of every gift; who stops his ears that he may not hear of blood, and shuts his eyes that he may see no evil. He shall dwell on high; the strongholds of the rocks shall be his height. Bread is given him, and his waters are sure [33:14-16].

**200. 200.** [Isa 44]. *And somewhat later*: Remember these things, O Jacob and Israel, for you are my servant: I have formed you, you are my servant, O Israel, do not forget me. I have blotted out your iniquities as a cloud, and your sins as a mist; return to me, for I have redeemed you [44:21-22].

**201. 201.** [Isa 46]. *Likewise after a little:* Remember this, and be ashamed; return, you transgressors, to the heart. Remember the former age; for I am God, and there is no God beside, nor is there any like me [46:8-9].

**202. 202.** [Isa 50]. *And somewhat later:* Who has walked in darkness, and has no light? Let him hope in the name of the Lord, and lean upon his God [50:10].

**203. 203.** [Isa 51]. *Likewise after a little:* Hear me, you who know the just one, O people; my law is in their heart. Do not fear the reproach of men, and do not fear their blasphemies. For the worm shall eat them up as a garment; and the moth shall devour them as wool: but my salvation shall be forever, and my justice from generation to generation [51:7-8].

**204. 204.** [Isa 55]. *And after a little:* Seek the Lord while he may be found, call upon him while he is near. Let the wicked man forsake his way, and the unjust man his thoughts, and return to the Lord; and he will have mercy on him, and to our God, for he is bountiful to forgive [55:6-7].

**205. 205.** [Isa 56]. *And after twenty verses:* Thus says the Lord: Keep judgment, and do justice, for my salvation is near to come, and my justice to be revealed. Blessed is the man who does this, and the son of man who lays hold of it; who keeps the Sabbath so as not to profane it, who keeps his hands from doing any evil [56:1-2].

**206. 206.** [Isa 57]. *And after not much:* You who take comfort in gods under every leafy tree, sacrificing little children in the torrents, under the overhanging rocks. In the portions of the torrent is your portion. This is your lot; and to them you have poured out a libation, you have offered sacrifice. Shall I not be angry over these things? [57:5-6].

**207. 207.** [Isa 58]. *Likewise after a little:* For they seek me from day to day, and wish to know my ways, as a nation that has done justice, and that has not forsaken the judgment of its God. They ask of me the judgments of justice, they wish to draw near to God. Why have we fasted, and you have not regarded? we have humbled our souls, and you have not taken notice? Behold, in the day of your fast your own will is found, and you exact all your debtors. Behold, you fast for strife and contention, and strike wickedly with the fist. Do not fast as you have done until this day, that your cry may be heard on high. Is this such a fast as I have chosen, for a man to afflict his soul for a day? to twist his head about like a ring, and to spread sackcloth and ashes? Will you call this a fast, and a day acceptable to the Lord? Is not this rather the fast that I have chosen? Loose the bands of wickedness, undo the bundles that oppress. Let those who are broken go free; and break asunder every burden. Break your bread to the hungry, and bring the needy and the wanderers into your house: when you see one naked, clothe him, and do not despise your own flesh [58:2-7].  
*And after four verses:* If you take away the chain out of your midst, and cease to stretch out the finger, and to speak what does not profit: when you pour out your soul to the hungry; and satisfy the afflicted soul; your light shall rise in darkness, and your darkness shall be as the noonday; and the Lord will give you rest forever [58:9-11].

**208. 208.** [Isa 65]. *And after many things:* A people who provoke me to anger before my face continually; who sacrifice in gardens, and offer sacrifice upon bricks; who dwell in the tombs, and sleep in the shrines of idols [65:3-4].  
*And after two verses:* Who say: Depart from me, do not come near me, for you are unclean. These shall be smoke in my fury, a fire burning all the day. Behold, it is written before me: I will not be silent, but will render and repay into their bosom your iniquities, and the iniquities of your fathers together, says the Lord. Those who sacrificed upon the mountains and upon the hills reproached me, and I will measure back their former work into their bosom [65:5-7].  
*And after ten verses:* And you, *he says,* who have forsaken the Lord, who have forgotten my holy mountain, who set a table for Fortune, and pour out libations upon it: I will number you for the sword, and you shall all fall in the slaughter; because I called, and you did not answer, I spoke, and you did not listen: and you did evil in my eyes, and chose the things that I did not will [65:11-12].

**209. 209.** [Isa 66]. *And a little later:* Thus says the Lord: Heaven is my throne, and the earth my footstool. What is this house that you will build for me? and what is this place of my rest? My hand made all these things, and all these things were made, says the Lord. But to whom shall I look, except to the poor little one, and the contrite of spirit, and him who trembles at my words? [66:1-2].  
*And after seven verses:* I will choose their mockeries, and will bring upon them the things they feared: because I called, and there was none who answered; I spoke, and they did not hear; and they did evil in my eyes, and chose the things I did not will. Hear the word of the Lord, you who tremble at his word. Your brothers who hate you, and cast you out for my name's sake, have said: Let the Lord be glorified, and we shall see in your joy; but they themselves shall be confounded [66:4-5].
