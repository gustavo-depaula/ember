# From the Song of Songs

I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, by the roes and the harts of the fields, that you stir not up, nor make the beloved to awake, until she herself wills[^1]. I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, by the powers and forces of the field, if you stir up love until she wills[^2].  
*For the Church—in which indeed we are—exhorts her daughters with these words, that is, exhorts herself, established as she is in many members. She is the most fruitful field of God, whose powers and forces are great, to which the martyrs have attained by loving Christ. For how far does he wish the love of his beloved to be stirred up, for the present in this life,* *except as far as he himself taught by his word, and exhorted by his own example, saying:* Greater love has no one than this, that he lay down his life for his friends[^3]: *and what he said, doing it? Therefore, lest this should seem to pertain to him alone, John says in his Epistle:* As Christ laid down his life for us, so we also ought to lay down our lives for the brethren[^4]: *this, then, is* until she wills.

[^1]: Song 2:7; 3:5
[^2]: Song 8:4
[^3]: John 15:13
[^4]: 1 John 3:16
