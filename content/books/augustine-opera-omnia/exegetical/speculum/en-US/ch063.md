# On His Third Letter

**425. 425.** [3 Jn]. *Beloved, it is a loyal thing you do when you render any service to the brethren, especially to strangers, who have testified to your love before the church. You will do well to send them on their journey as befits God's service. For they have set out for the sake of his name, taking nothing from the Gentiles. So we ought to support such people, that we may be fellow workers in the truth*[^1].  
*And a little later:* *Beloved, do not imitate evil but imitate good. He who does good is of God; he who does evil has not seen God*[^2].

[^1]: 3 Jn 5-8
[^2]: 3 Jn 11
