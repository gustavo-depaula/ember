# From the Book of Micah

**169. 169.** [Mic 2]. Woe to you who plot what is useless, and work evil on your beds. In the morning light they do it; because their hand is against God. And they coveted fields, and took them by violence, and seized houses; and they oppressed a man and his house, a man and his inheritance [2:1-2].

**170. 170.** [Mic 3]. *And somewhat later*: Hear this, you princes of the house of Jacob, and judges of the house of Israel, who abhor judgment, and pervert all that is right. You who build up Zion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity. Her princes judged for bribes; and her priests taught for hire; and her prophets divined for money, and they leaned upon the Lord, saying: Is not the Lord in our midst? No evils shall come upon us [3:9-11].

**171. 171.** [Mic 6]. *Likewise somewhat later*: I will show you, O man, what is good, and what the Lord requires of you: namely, to do judgment, and to love mercy, and to walk solicitously with your God [6:8].  
*And after three verses:* There is still a fire in the house of the wicked, treasures of iniquity, and a scant measure full of wrath. Shall I justify the wicked balance, and the deceitful weights of the bag? By which her rich men were filled with iniquity, and those who dwelt in her spoke lies, and their tongue was fraudulent in their mouth [6:10-12].

**172. 172.** [Mic 7]. *And after twelve verses:* Woe is me, for I have become like one who gathers the clusters of the vintage in autumn. There is no cluster to eat; my soul longed for the early figs. The holy man has perished from the earth, and there is none upright among men: all lie in wait for blood; each one hunts his brother to death: the evil of their hands they call good. The prince demands, and the judge is for a reward, and the great man has spoken the desire of his soul [7:1-3].
