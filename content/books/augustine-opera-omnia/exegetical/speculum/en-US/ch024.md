# From the Book of Amos

**165. 165.** [Amos 2]. Thus says the Lord: For three crimes of Judah, and for four, I will not turn him away; because they have cast off the law of the Lord, and have not kept his commandments. For their idols deceived them, after which their fathers had gone [2:4].  
*And after two verses:* Thus says the Lord: For three crimes of Israel, and for four, I will not turn him away; for that they have sold the just man for silver, and the poor man for a pair of shoes. They crush the heads of the poor upon the dust of the earth, and turn aside the way of the humble. And the son and his father have gone in to the same girl, to violate my holy name [2:6-7].  
*And after one verse:* and they drank, *he says,* the wine of the condemned in the house of their God [2:8].

**166. 166.** [Amos 5]. *And somewhat later:* For thus says the Lord to the house of Israel: Seek me, and you shall live [5:4].  
*And after three verses:* Seek the Lord, and live [5:6].  
*And after eight verses:* They hated him who reproved in the gate, and they abhorred him who spoke perfectly. Therefore, because you plundered the poor, and took choice spoil from him [5:10-11].  
*And after three verses:* For I have known your many crimes, and your grievous sins. You are enemies of the just, taking a bribe, and oppressing the poor in the gate [5:12].  
*And after two verses:* Seek good and not evil, that you may live; and the Lord God of hosts shall be with you, as you have said. Hate evil, and love good, and establish judgment in the gate; if perhaps the Lord God of hosts may have mercy on the remnant of Joseph [5:14-15].

**167. 167.** [Amos 6]. *And after fifty verses*: You who sleep on beds of ivory, and are wanton on your couches. You who eat the lamb of the flock, and the calf out of the midst of the herd. You who sing to the sound of the psaltery: like David they thought they had instruments of music; drinking wine in bowls, and anointed with the best ointment, and they suffered nothing over the affliction of Joseph [6:4-6].

**168. 168.** [Amos 8]. *And after a little:* Hear this, you who crush the poor, and make the needy of the land to fail, saying: When will the month pass, that we may sell our wares; and the Sabbath, that we may open the grain: to lessen the measure, and increase the shekel, and substitute deceitful balances; that we may possess the needy for silver, and the poor for a pair of shoes, and sell the refuse of the grain [8:4-6].
