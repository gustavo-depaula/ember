# On the Letter of the Apostle Jude

**426. 426.** [Jude]. *For certain men have crept in unnoticed, who long ago were designated for this condemnation, ungodly persons who pervert the grace of our God into licentiousness and deny our only Master and Lord Jesus Christ*[^1].  
*And a little after:* *These are blemishes on your love feasts, as they feast with you without fear, feeding themselves*[^2].  
*And in another place:* *These are grumblers, malcontents, following their own passions; and their mouth speaks blasphemy, flattering people to gain advantage. But you, beloved, must remember the words spoken beforehand by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ, who said to you that in the last time there will come scoffers, following their own ungodly passions. It is these who set up divisions, worldly people, devoid of the Spirit. But you, beloved, build yourselves up on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Spirit; keep yourselves in the love of God, awaiting the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life. And convince some, who doubt; save others by snatching them out of the fire; on others have mercy with fear, hating even the garment spotted by the flesh*[^3].

[^1]: Jude 4
[^2]: Jude 12
[^3]: Jude 16-23
