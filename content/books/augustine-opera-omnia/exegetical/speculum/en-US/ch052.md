# From the Letter to the Colossians

**385. 385.** [Col 3]. Therefore, if you have risen together with Christ, seek what is above, where Christ sits at God's right hand. Set your minds on what is above, not on what is on earth. For you have died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God. When Christ, your life, appears, you too will appear with him in glory. Therefore, put to death your members that are on earth: fornication, impurity, lust, evil desire, and greed, which is service of idols. Because of these things God's wrath comes upon the children of unbelief. In these things you too once walked, when you lived in them. But now you too must put away everything: anger, indignation, malice, and blasphemy. Do not send shameful speech from your mouth. Do not lie to one another, stripping off the old man with his deeds and putting on the new, who is renewed in the knowledge of God according to the image of the one who created him. There is neither gentile nor Jew, circumcision nor uncircumcision, barbarian nor Scythian, slave nor free, but Christ is everything and in everyone. Therefore, as God's chosen, holy and beloved, put on heartfelt mercy, kindness, humility, moderation, and patience, bearing with one another and forgiving one another if anyone has a complaint against another. Just as the Lord forgave you, so you too should do. Above all these things, have love, which is the bond of perfection. Let Christ's peace exult in your hearts, to which you were also called in one body, and be grateful. Let Christ's word dwell abundantly in you, teaching and admonishing one another in all wisdom with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to God. Whatever you do in word or deed, do everything in the Lord Jesus's name, giving thanks to God the Father through him. Wives, be subject to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord. Husbands, love your wives and do not be bitter toward them. Children, obey your parents in everything, for this is pleasing in the Lord. Fathers, do not provoke your children to indignation, lest they become fainthearted. Slaves, obey your masters according to the flesh in everything, not serving only under their eyes as people-pleasers, but in simplicity of heart, fearing the Lord. Whatever you do, work from the soul, as for the Lord rather than human beings, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the recompense of the inheritance. Serve the Lord Christ. For the one who does wrong receives back what he has done unjustly, and with God there is no partiality [3:1–25].

**386. 386.** [Col 4]. Masters, provide your slaves with what is just and equitable, knowing that you too have a Lord in heaven. Persevere in prayer, keeping watch in it with thanksgiving. Pray at the same time for us, so that God may open for us a door of speech for speaking Christ's mystery, for which I am also imprisoned, and so that I may reveal it as I ought to speak. Walk in wisdom toward outsiders, redeeming the time. Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how you ought to answer each person [4:1–6].
