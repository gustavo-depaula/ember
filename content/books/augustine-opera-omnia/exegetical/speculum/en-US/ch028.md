# From the Book of Zechariah

**178. 178.** [Zech 5]. *When he was speaking of the scroll, which he saw in spirit:* And he said to me: This is the curse that goes forth over the face of the whole earth: for every thief, as it is written there, shall be judged, and everyone who swears shall likewise be judged from this. I will bring it forth, says the Lord of hosts, and it shall come to the house of the thief, and to the house of him who swears falsely by my name; and it shall abide in the midst of his house, and shall consume it, with its timbers and its stones [5:3-4].

**179. 179.** [Zech 7]. *And after a little:* And the word of the Lord came to Zechariah, saying: Thus says the Lord of hosts: Judge true judgment, and show mercy and compassion each one to his brother; and do not oppress the widow, the orphan, the stranger, and the poor; and let no man devise evil against his brother in his heart [7:8-10].

**180. 180.** [Zech 8]. *Likewise after a little:* Speak the truth each one to his neighbor: judge truth and the judgment of peace in your gates; and let none of you devise evil against his friend in your hearts, and love no false oath. For all these are things that I hate, says the Lord [8:16-17].
