# From Leviticus

**6. 6.** [Lev 18]. No man, *he says,* shall approach to one near of kin to his own blood, to uncover her nakedness. I am the Lord. The nakedness of your father and the nakedness of your mother you shall not uncover: she is your mother, you shall not uncover her nakedness. The nakedness of your father's wife you shall not uncover; for it is your father's nakedness. The nakedness of your sister by your father or by your mother, whether born at home or abroad, you shall not uncover. The nakedness of your son's daughter, or of your granddaughter by your daughter, you shall not uncover; because it is your own nakedness. The nakedness of the daughter of your father's wife, whom she bore to your father, and who is your sister, you shall not uncover. The nakedness of your father's sister you shall not uncover; because it is your father's flesh. The nakedness of your mother's sister you shall not uncover; because it is your mother's flesh. The nakedness of your father's brother you shall not uncover, nor approach to his wife, who is joined to you by affinity. The nakedness of your daughter-in-law you shall not uncover; because she is your son's wife; nor shall you uncover her shame. The nakedness of your brother's wife you shall not uncover; because it is your brother's nakedness. The nakedness of your wife and her daughter you shall not uncover. You shall not take her son's daughter and her daughter's daughter, to uncover her shame; because they are her flesh, and such intercourse is incest. You shall not take your wife's sister for a rival to her, nor uncover her nakedness while the other is yet living. You shall not approach a woman who suffers her monthly courses, nor uncover her uncleanness. You shall not lie with your neighbor's wife, nor be defiled with the mingling of seed. You shall not give of your seed to be consecrated to the idol Moloch, nor profane the name of your God. I am the Lord. You shall not lie with a male as with a woman; because it is an abomination. You shall not lie with any beast, nor be defiled with it. A woman shall not submit to a beast, nor mingle with it; because it is a wickedness. Do not defile yourselves in any of these things [18:6-24].

**7. 7.** [Lev 19]. *And after a little:* Let everyone fear his mother and his father [19:3].  
*And after one verse:* Do not turn to idols, nor make for yourselves molten gods. I am the Lord your God [19:4].  
*And a little later*: Neither, *he says,* shall you gather the ears of grain that remain, nor collect the clusters and the falling grapes in your vineyard; but you shall leave them to be picked by the poor and the strangers. I am the Lord your God. You shall not steal. You shall not lie. Neither shall anyone deceive his neighbor. You shall not swear falsely in my name, nor profane the name of your God. I am the Lord. Neither shall you slander your neighbor, nor oppress him by force. The work of your hired servant shall not abide with you until the morning. You shall not curse the deaf, nor put a stumbling block before the blind: but you shall fear the Lord your God, because I am the Lord. You shall not do what is unjust, nor judge unjustly. Neither consider the person of the poor, nor honor the countenance of the mighty: judge your neighbor justly. You shall not be an accuser and a whisperer among the people. You shall not stand against the blood of your neighbor. I am the Lord. Do not hate your brother in your heart: but reprove him openly, lest you incur sin on his account. Do not seek revenge, nor be mindful of the wrong done by your fellow citizens. You shall love your friend as yourself. I am the Lord. Keep my laws [19:9-19].  
*And a little later:* You shall not eat with blood. You shall not divine, nor observe dreams. Neither shall you cut your hair round, nor shave your beard. And you shall not cut your flesh for the dead: nor make any figures or marks upon yourselves. I am the Lord. Do not prostitute your daughter, lest the land be defiled and filled with wickedness [19:26-29].  
*And after one verse:* I am the Lord. Do not turn to magicians, nor inquire anything of soothsayers, to be defiled by them. I am the Lord your God. Rise up before the hoary head, and honor the person of the aged, and fear your God. I am the Lord. If a stranger dwell in your land and abide among you, do not reproach him; but let him be among you as a native, and you shall love him as yourselves: for you also were strangers in the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God. Do not anything unjust in judgment, in rule, in weight, in measure. Let the balance be just and the weights equal; the bushel just, and the sextary fair. I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt. Keep all my precepts and all my judgments, and do them. I am the Lord [19:30-37].

**8. 8.** [Lev 20]. And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: These things you shall say to the children of Israel: A man of the children of Israel, and of the strangers who dwell in Israel, if anyone give of his seed to the idol Moloch, let him die the death; the people of the land shall stone him: and I will set my face against him, and will cut him off from the midst of his people, because he has given of his seed to Moloch and defiled my sanctuary and profaned my holy name. But if the people of the land be negligent, and as it were making little of my authority, let go the man who has given of his seed to Moloch, and be unwilling to kill him; I will set my face upon that man and his kindred; and I will cut off both him and all who consented with him, to commit fornication with Moloch, from the midst of their people. The soul that turns aside to magicians and soothsayers, and commits fornication with them; I will set my face against it, and will destroy it from the midst of its people. Be sanctified, and be holy; because I, the Lord your God, am holy. Keep my precepts and do them. I am the Lord who sanctify you. He who curses his father or mother, let him die the death. He who curses his father and mother, let his blood be upon him. If a man commit adultery with the wife of another, and perpetrate adultery with the spouse of his neighbor; let both the adulterer and the adulteress die the death. He who lies with his stepmother and uncovers his father's shame; let them both die the death: their blood be upon them. If anyone lie with his daughter-in-law, let both die; because they have wrought a wickedness: their blood be upon them. He who lies with a male as with a woman, both have wrought an abomination; let them die the death: their blood be upon them. He who takes the mother together with the daughter as wife has wrought a wickedness; he shall be burned alive with them, nor shall so great a wickedness remain in your midst. He who lies with a beast or with cattle, let him die the death: kill the beast also. The woman that submits to any beast shall be slain together with it; their blood be upon them. He who takes his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother, and sees her nakedness, and she look upon her brother's shame, they have wrought a nefarious deed; they shall be slain in the sight of the people, because they have uncovered their own nakedness to one another; and they shall bear their iniquity. He who lies with a woman in her monthly flow, and uncovers her nakedness, and she lay open the fountain of her blood, both shall be destroyed from the midst of their people. The nakedness of your mother's sister and of your father's sister you shall not uncover. He who does this has laid bare the shame of his own flesh; they shall both bear their iniquity. He who lies with the wife of his father's brother or his mother's brother, and uncovers the shame of his kindred; they shall both bear their sin; they shall die without children. He who takes his brother's wife does an unlawful thing: he has uncovered his brother's nakedness; they shall be without children [20:1-21].  
*And in another place:* A man or a woman in whom there is a python spirit or a spirit of divination, let them die the death; they shall stone them: their blood be upon them [20:27].

**9. 9.** [Lev 21]. *And likewise in another place, when he was speaking of the high priest:* He shall take to wife a virgin, *he says;* a widow, or one repudiated, or defiled, or a harlot he shall not take, but a maiden of his own people: lest he mingle the stock of his lineage with the common people of his nation; for I am the Lord who sanctify him [21:13-15].

**10. 10.** [Lev 24]. *And after many things:* The man who curses his God shall bear his sin. And he who blasphemes the name of the Lord, let him die the death: all the multitude shall stone him, whether he be a citizen or a stranger. He who blasphemes the name of the Lord, let him die the death. He who strikes and kills a man, let him die the death. He who strikes an animal shall make it good, that is, life for life. He who inflicts a blemish on any of his fellow citizens, as he has done, so shall it be done to him: he shall restore fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; whatever blemish he inflicted, the like shall he be compelled to suffer. He who strikes a beast shall render another. He who strikes a man shall be punished. Let the judgment be equal among you, whether a stranger or a citizen has sinned; for I am the Lord your God [24:15-22].

**11. 11.** [Lev 26]. *And after a little*: You shall not make for yourselves an idol or a graven thing, nor set up pillars, nor place a remarkable stone in your land, to adore it. For I am the Lord your God [26:1].

*These are from Leviticus. Now let us set down from the book whose name is Numbers the things that have seemed worth recalling.*
