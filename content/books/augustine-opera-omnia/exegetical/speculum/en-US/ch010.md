# **65. 65.** [Ps 92 (91)]. It is good to give praise to the Lord, and to sing to your name, O Most High [92:2 (91:2)].

**66. 66.** [Ps 94 (93)]. Understand, you foolish among the people; and you senseless, at length be wise [94:8 (93:8)].  
*And a little later:* Blessed is the man whom you instruct, O Lord, and teach out of your law. That you may give him rest from the days of affliction, until a pit be dug for the wicked [94:12-13 (93:12-13)].

**67. 67.** [Ps 95 (94)]. Come, let us adore and bow down, let us kneel before the face of the Lord our maker [95:6 (94:6)].  
*And after two verses:* Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts; as in the provocation, as in the day of temptation in the wilderness [95:8-9 (94:8-9)].

**68. 68.** [Ps 96 (95)]. Sing to the Lord a new song, sing to the Lord, all the earth. Sing to the Lord, bless his name: announce his salvation from day to day. Declare his glory among the nations, his wonders among all peoples [96:1-3 (95:1-3)].  
*And after six verses:* Bring to the Lord, you families of peoples, bring to the Lord glory and strength. Bring to the Lord glory to his name; take up offerings, and enter into his courts. Adore the Lord in the beauty of his sanctuary [96:7-9 (95:7-9)].

**69. 69.** [Ps 97 (96)]. You who love the Lord, hate evil [97:10 (96:10)].  
*And after three verses:* Be glad, you just, in the Lord, and give praise to the memory of his holiness [97:12 (96:12)].
