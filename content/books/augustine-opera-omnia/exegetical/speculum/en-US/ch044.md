# From the Letter of the Apostle Paul to the Romans

**332. 332.** [Rom 1]. For I am not ashamed of the Gospel. It is God's power for the salvation of everyone who believes, the Jew first and the Greek. For in it God's righteousness is revealed from faith to faith, as it is written: The righteous man will live by my faith [^1]. For God's wrath is revealed from heaven against all the ungodliness and injustice of those people who hold the truth in injustice. What can be known of God is manifest in them, for God has manifested it in them. For since the creation of the world, his invisible things are perceived and understood through the things that have been made, including his eternal power and divinity, so that they are without excuse. For although they knew God, they did not glorify him as God or give thanks, but became futile in their thoughts, and their foolish heart was darkened. Claiming to be wise, they became fools, and exchanged the glory of the incorruptible God for the likeness of an image of corruptible man, birds, four-footed animals, and serpents [1:16–23].  
Therefore, God handed them over to the desires of their hearts in impurity, to dishonor their bodies among themselves. They exchanged God's truth for a lie, and worshiped and served the creation rather than the Creator, who is blessed forever. Amen. Therefore, God handed them over to shameful passions. For their women exchanged natural use for the use contrary to nature. Likewise the males too, abandoning the natural use of the female, burned in their desires for one another, males working shamefulness with males and receiving in themselves the recompense due for their error. And just as they did not approve of holding God in their knowledge, God handed them over to a reprobate mind to do things that are not fitting. They are filled with every iniquity, malice, fornication, greed, and wickedness; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, and malice; whisperers, slanderers, hateful to God, insulting, proud, arrogant, inventors of evils, disobedient to parents, foolish, disorderly, without affection, without fidelity to agreements, without mercy. Although they knew God's justice, they did not understand that those who do such things deserve death—not only those who do them, but also those who consent to those doing them [1:24–32].

**333. 333.** [Rom 2]. Therefore, you are without excuse, O man, everyone who judges. For in judging another you condemn yourself, because you who judge do the same things. We know that God's judgment falls according to truth upon those who do such things. But do you suppose, O man, you who judge those doing such things and do them yourself, that you will escape God's judgment? Or do you despise the riches of his goodness, patience, and forbearance, unaware that God's kindness leads you to repentance? By your hardness and impenitent heart, you store up wrath for yourself for the day of wrath and the revelation of God's just judgment. He will repay each person according to his works: eternal life to those who, persevering in good work, seek glory, honor, and incorruptibility; but wrath and indignation to those who act from contentiousness and do not obey the truth but put their trust in iniquity. Tribulation and anguish will fall upon every human soul that does evil, the Jew first and the Greek. But glory, honor, and peace will come to everyone who does good, the Jew first and the Greek [2:1–10].  
*And a little later:* For it is not the hearers of the Law who are righteous before God, but the doers of the Law who will be justified [2:13].  
*Again, a little later:* Therefore, you who teach another, do you not teach yourself? You who preach against stealing, do you steal? You who say not to commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you commit sacrilege? You who glory in the Law dishonor God by transgressing the Law. For God's name is blasphemed among the gentiles because of you, as it is written[^2] [2:21–24].

**334. 334.** [Rom 5]. *And somewhat later:* Therefore, justified by faith, let us have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Through him we also have access by faith into this grace in which we stand, and we glory in the hope of the glory of God's children. Not only this, but we also glory in tribulations, knowing that tribulation produces patience, patience tested character, tested character hope, and hope does not put us to shame, because God's love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit who has been given to us [5:1–5].

**335. 335.** [Rom 6]. *And in another place:* Therefore, do not let sin reign in your mortal body, so that you obey its desires. Nor present your members to sin as weapons of iniquity. Instead, present yourselves to God as people alive from the dead, and your members to God as weapons of righteousness [6:12–13].  
*And a little later:* I speak in human terms because of the weakness of your flesh. Just as you presented your members to serve impurity and iniquity, resulting in iniquity, so now present your members to serve righteousness, resulting in sanctification [6:19].

**336. 336.** [Rom 8]. *And in another place:* Therefore, brothers, we are debtors, but not to the flesh, to live according to the flesh. For if you live according to the flesh, you will die; but if by the spirit you put the deeds of the flesh to death, you will live. For all who are led by God's Spirit are God's children [8:12–14].  
*And a little later:* Heirs indeed of God and coheirs with Christ, provided we suffer with him so that we may also be glorified with him [8:17].  
*And a little later:* But if we hope for what we do not see, we await it with patience [8:25].  
*And a few verses later:* We know that all things work together for good for those who love God [8:28].  
*And a little later:* Who will separate us from Christ's love? Tribulation? Anguish? Persecution? Hunger? Nakedness? Danger? The sword? As it is written: Because of you we are put to death all day; we have been reckoned as sheep for slaughter[^3]. Yet in all these things we prevail through the one who loved us. For I am certain that neither death nor life, neither angels nor principalities, neither present nor future things, neither powers nor height nor depth nor any other created thing will be able to separate us from God's love, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord [8:35–39].

**337. 337.** [Rom 10]. *And in another place:* For belief in the heart leads to righteousness, while confession with the mouth leads to salvation. Scripture says: Everyone who believes in him will not be put to shame[^4]. For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek. The same Lord is Lord of all, rich toward everyone who calls upon him. For everyone who calls upon the Lord's name will be saved[^5] [10:10–13].

**338. 338.** [Rom 11]. *And in another place:* But you, stand by faith. Do not think highly of yourself, but fear. For if God did not spare the natural branches, perhaps he will not spare you either. Therefore, see God's goodness and severity: severity toward those who fell, but goodness toward you, if you remain in goodness [11:20–22].

**339. 339.** [Rom 12]. *And a little later:* Therefore, brothers, I appeal to you through God's mercy to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God, your rational service. Do not conform to this age, but be reformed in the renewal of your understanding, so that you may discern God's good, pleasing, and perfect will. For through the grace given to me I tell everyone among you not to think beyond what he ought to think, but to think soberly, each according to the measure of faith God has allotted. For just as we have many members in one body, but the members do not all have the same activity, so we who are many are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another. Having gifts differing according to the grace given to us, let prophecy be according to the measure of faith; ministry in serving; the teacher in teaching; the exhorter in exhorting; the giver in simplicity; the leader in diligence; the one showing mercy in cheerfulness. Let love be without pretense: hate evil, cling to good, cherish brotherly love for one another, anticipate one another in honor. Do not be sluggish in diligence; be fervent in spirit, serving the Lord, rejoicing in hope, patient in tribulation, persevering in prayer, sharing in the saints' needs, pursuing hospitality. Bless your persecutors; bless, and do not curse. Rejoice with those rejoicing; weep with those weeping. Have the same mind toward one another. Do not set your minds on exalted things, but associate with the lowly. Do not consider yourselves wise. Repay no one evil for evil; provide for good things not only before God but also before all people. If possible, so far as it depends on you, have peace with everyone. Do not avenge yourselves, beloved, but give place to wrath. For it is written: Vengeance is mine; I will repay, says the Lord. But if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him drink, for by doing this you will heap burning coals upon his head. Do not be conquered by evil, but conquer evil with good[^6] [12:1–21].

**340. 340.** [Rom 13]. Let every soul be subject to the higher authorities. For there is no authority except from God, and those that are from God have been ordered by him. Thus anyone resisting authority resists God's ordering. Those who resist bring condemnation upon themselves. For rulers are an object of fear not for good work but for evil. Do you want to have no fear of authority? Do good, and you will receive praise from it. For it is God's servant for your good. But if you do evil, be afraid, for it does not bear the sword without reason. It is God's servant, an avenger bringing wrath upon the evildoer. Therefore, you must necessarily be subject, not only because of wrath but also because of conscience. For this reason you pay taxes too, for they are God's servants, attending to this very thing. Pay everyone what is owed: tax to whom tax is owed, toll to whom toll, fear to whom fear, honor to whom honor. Owe no one anything except love for one another. For anyone who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the Law. For: You shall not commit adultery; you shall not kill; you shall not steal; you shall not bear false witness; you shall not covet[^7], and any other commandment, are summed up in this saying: You shall love your neighbor as yourself[^8]. Love of one's neighbor does no evil. Therefore, love is the fullness of the Law. And do this knowing the time, because the hour has already come for us to rise from sleep. For our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed. The night has advanced, and the day has drawn near. Therefore, let us cast off the works of darkness and put on the weapons of light. Let us walk honorably, as in daylight: not in revelries and drunkenness, not in sexual indulgence and shamelessness, not in strife and jealousy. Instead, put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and do not make provision for the flesh in its desires [13:1–14].

**341. 341.** [Rom 14]. Welcome the one weak in faith, without disputing over opinions. One believes he may eat everything; but let the weak one eat vegetables. The one who eats must not despise the one who does not eat, and the one who does not eat must not judge the one who eats, for God has welcomed him. Who are you to judge another's slave? He stands or falls before his own master; and he will stand, for God can make him stand. One distinguishes one day from another; another regards every day alike. Let each be fully persuaded in his own understanding. The one who observes a day observes it for the Lord. The one who eats eats for the Lord, for he gives thanks to God. The one who does not eat refrains for the Lord and gives thanks to God. For none of us lives for himself, and none dies for himself. If we live, we live for the Lord; if we die, we die for the Lord. Therefore, whether we live or die, we belong to the Lord. For this reason Christ died and rose again, to be Lord of both the dead and the living. But you, why do you judge your brother? Or you, why do you despise your brother? For we will all stand before God's judgment seat. For it is written: As I live, says the Lord, every knee will bend to me, and every tongue will confess to God[^9]. Thus each of us will give God an account of himself. Therefore, let us no longer judge one another. Instead, judge this: that you must not place an obstacle or an occasion of sin before a brother. I know and am confident in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean in itself; but if someone considers something unclean, it is unclean for him. For if your brother is saddened because of food, you are no longer walking according to love. Do not destroy by your food someone for whom Christ died. Therefore, do not let our good be blasphemed. For God's kingdom is not food and drink, but righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit. Anyone serving Christ in this pleases God and is approved by people. Therefore, let us pursue the things of peace and the things that build one another up. Do not destroy God's work for the sake of food. Everything is indeed clean, but it is evil for a person to eat in a way that causes stumbling. It is good not to eat meat or drink wine or do anything by which your brother stumbles, is led into sin, or is weakened. Do you have faith within yourself? Have it before God. Blessed is the one who does not judge himself in what he approves. But the one who doubts is condemned if he eats, because it is not from faith. And everything that is not from faith is sin [14:1–23].

**342. 342.** [Rom 15]. We who are stronger ought to bear the weaknesses of the weak and not please ourselves. Each of us should please his neighbor for the good, to build him up. For Christ did not please himself either, but, as it is written: The reproaches of those reproaching you fell upon me[^10]. Everything that was written was written for our instruction, so that through patience and the consolation of the Scriptures we might have hope. May the God of patience and comfort grant you the same mind toward one another according to Jesus Christ, so that with one accord and one mouth you may honor the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. Therefore, welcome one another, just as Christ too welcomed you for God's honor [15:1–7].  
*And somewhat later:* For Macedonia and Achaia have resolved to make a contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem. It pleased them to do so, and they are their debtors. For if the gentiles have come to share in their spiritual things, they ought to serve them in material things as well [15:26–27].  
*And a very few verses later:* Therefore, brothers, I appeal to you through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the Spirit's love to help me by praying to God for me [15:30].

**343. 343.** [Rom 16]. *And a little later:* I ask you, brothers, to watch for those causing divisions and occasions of stumbling contrary to the teaching you learned, and turn away from them. For such people serve not Christ our Lord but their own belly, and by sweet words and blessings they deceive the hearts of the innocent. For your obedience has become known everywhere. Therefore, I rejoice over you, but I want you to be wise in good and innocent in evil [16:17–19].
