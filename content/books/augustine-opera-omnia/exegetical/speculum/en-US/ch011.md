# **70. 70.** [Ps 98 (97)]. Shout with joy to the Lord, all the earth; cry aloud, and rejoice, and sing [98:4 (97:4)].: **71. 71.** [Ps 99 (98)]. Exalt the Lord our God, and adore his footstool, for it is holy [99:5 (98:5)].

**72. 72.** [Ps 100 (99)]. Shout with joy to the Lord, all the earth, serve the Lord with gladness. Come into his presence with praise. Know that the Lord himself is God; he made us, and we are his [100:2-3 (99:2-3)].  
*And after one verse:* Enter his gates with thanksgiving, his courts with praise; give praise to him [100:4 (99:4)].

**73. 73.** [Ps 101 (100)]. I will walk in the simplicity of my heart in the midst of my house. I will not set before my eyes any word of Belial. I have hated those who commit transgressions, nor has it cleaved to me. The perverse heart shall depart from me; I will not know evil. The one who in secret slanders his neighbor, him will I put to death. The proud of eye and haughty of heart, with him I cannot dwell. My eyes are upon the faithful of the earth, that they may dwell with me: he who walks in the way uprightly, he shall minister to me. He who works deceit shall not dwell in the midst of my house: he who speaks a lie shall not please in the sight of my eyes. In the morning I destroyed all the wicked of the earth, that I might cut off from the city of the Lord all who work iniquity [101:2-8 (100:2-8)].

**74. 74.** [Ps 103 (102)]. But the mercy of the Lord is from everlasting and unto everlasting upon those who fear him. And his justice upon the children of their children, to those who keep his covenant, and remember his precepts to do them [103:17-18 (102:17-18)].

**75. 75.** [Ps 105 (104)]. Give praise to the Lord, and call upon his name; make known his deeds among the peoples. Sing to him, and play to him: tell of all his wonders. Glory in his holy name; let the heart of those who seek the Lord be glad. Seek the Lord and his power, seek his face continually. Remember his wonders which he has done, his signs and the judgments of his mouth [105:1-5 (104:1-5)].

**76. 76.** [Ps 108 (107)]. My heart is ready, O God; I will sing and play: yes, my glory too. Arise, psaltery and harp; I will arise in the morning. I will praise you among the peoples, O Lord, I will sing to you among the nations [108:2-4 (107:2-4)].

**77. 77.** [Ps 111 (110)]. I will praise the Lord with my whole heart, in the council of the just and in the congregation [111:1 (110:1)].  
*And in another place:* The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding to all who do it; his praise endures continually [111:10 (110:10)].

**78. 78.** [Ps 112 (111)]. Blessed is the man who fears the Lord; he shall delight exceedingly in his commandments [112:1 (111:1)].  
*And after five verses:* The good man is merciful and lends, he shall order his words in judgment; for he shall not be moved forever. The just man shall be in everlasting remembrance; he shall not fear an evil report. His heart is ready, trusting in the Lord; his heart is firm, he shall not fear until he looks upon his enemies. He has scattered abroad, he has given to the poor: his justice remains forever; his horn shall be exalted in glory. The wicked shall see and be angry, he shall gnash with his teeth, and pine away: the desire of the wicked shall perish [112:5-10 (111:5-10)].

**79. 79.** [Ps 113 (112)]. Praise the Lord, you servants, praise the name of the Lord. Blessed be the name of the Lord, from this time forth and forever. From the rising of the sun unto its setting, the name of the Lord is worthy of praise [113:1-3 (112:1-3)].

**80. 80.** [Ps 115 (113)]. The idols of the Gentiles are silver and gold, the work of men's hands. They have mouths, and shall not speak; they have eyes, and shall not see. They have ears, and shall not hear [115:4-6 (113:12-14)].  
*And a little later:* Let those who make them become like them, and all who trust in them. Israel has trusted in the Lord; he is their helper and protector. The house of Aaron has trusted in the Lord; he is their helper and protector. But those who fear the Lord trust in the Lord; he is their helper and protector [115:8-11 (113:16-19)].

**81. 81.** [Ps 116 (115)]. I have believed, therefore have I spoken; but I have been afflicted exceedingly [116:10 (115:10)].  
*And after a little:* What shall I render to the Lord for all the things that he has rendered to me? I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the Lord. Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints [116:12-15 (115:12-15)].  
*And after three verses:* To you will I offer the sacrifice of praise, and will call upon the name of the Lord. I will pay my vows to the Lord in the sight of all his people; in the courts of the house of the Lord, in the midst of you, O Jerusalem [116:17-19 (115:17-19)].
