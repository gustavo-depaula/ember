# **45. 45.** [Ps 31 (30)]. Take courage, and let your heart be strengthened, all you who wait for the Lord [31:25 (30:25)].

**46. 46.** [Ps 32 (31)]. Do not become like the horse and the mule, in whom there is no understanding. With bit and bridle bind fast the jaws of those who do not draw near to you. Many are the sorrows of the wicked; but mercy shall surround the one who trusts in the Lord. Be glad in the Lord and rejoice, you just; and exult, all you upright of heart [32:9-11 (31:9-11)].

**47. 47.** [Ps 33 (32)]. Praise the Lord, you just; praise befits the upright [33:1 (32:1)].  
*And in another place:* Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, the people whom the Lord has chosen for his inheritance [33:12 (32:12)].

**48. 48.** [Ps 34 (33)]. I will bless the Lord at all times; his praise shall ever be in my mouth. In the Lord shall my soul be praised; let the meek hear and be glad. Magnify the Lord with me, and let us exalt his name together [34:2-4 (33:2-4)].  
*And after a few verses:* Taste and see that the Lord is good; blessed is the man who hopes in him. Fear the Lord, you his saints; for there is no want to those who fear him. The lions have lacked and gone hungry; but to those who seek the Lord no good thing shall be wanting. Come, children, hear me; I will teach you the fear of the Lord. Who is the man who desires life, loving to see good days? Keep your tongue from evil, and let your lips not speak deceit. Turn away from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it [34:9-15 (33:9-15)].  
*And after seven verses:* The Lord is near to the contrite of heart, and will save the humble of spirit [34:19 (33:19)].

**49. 49.** [Ps 37 (36)]. Do not contend with the malignant, nor envy those who work iniquity. For like grass they shall quickly wither away; and like green herbs they shall fade. Hope in the Lord, and do good; sojourn in the land, and feed on faith. And delight in the Lord, and he will give you the petitions of your heart. Commit your way to the Lord, and trust in him, and he will act. And he will bring forth your justice like the light, and your judgment like the noonday. Be silent before the Lord, and wait for him: do not contend against him who prospers in his way, against the man who does what he plots. Cease from anger, and forsake fury; do not contend so as to do evil. For those who do evil shall perish; but those who wait for the Lord, they shall inherit the land. For yet a little while, and the wicked shall be no more; and you will think upon his place, and it shall not stand. But the meek shall inherit the land, and shall delight in abundance of peace [37:1-11 (36:1-11)].  
*And a little later:* Better is a little to the just man, than the many riches of the wicked. For the arms of the wicked shall be broken; but the Lord upholds the just [37:16-17 (36:16-17)].  
*And after a little:* Turn away from evil, and do good [37:27 (36:27)].  
*And elsewhere:* Wait for the Lord, and keep his way; and he will exalt you to possess the land. Keep simplicity, and behold what is right; for there shall be peace at the last for the man [37:34, 37 (36:34, 37)].

**50. 50.** [Ps 40 (39)]. Blessed is the man who has made the Lord his trust, and has not turned aside to the prides and pomps of falsehood [40:5 (39:5)].

**51. 51.** [Ps 44 (43)]. In the Lord will we rejoice all the day, and in your name we will give praise forever [44:9 (43:9)].  
*And a little later:* All these things have come upon us; and we have not forgotten you, nor dealt falsely in your covenant. Our heart has not turned back, nor have our steps strayed from your path. For you have cast us down in a place of dragons, and covered us with the shadow of death. If we have forgotten the name of our God, and stretched out our hands to a strange god; will not God search this out? For he knows the thoughts of the heart. For your sake are we put to death all the day; we are counted as a flock for the slaughter [44:18-22 (43:18-22)].

**52. 52.** [Ps 47 (46)]. Sing to God, sing: sing to our king, sing. For God is king of all the earth, sing wisely [47:7-8 (46:7-8)].

**53. 53.** [Ps 50 (49)]. Offer to God the sacrifice of praise, and pay your vows to the Most High. And call upon me in the day of tribulation: I will deliver you, and you shall glorify me. But to the wicked God has said: Why do you tell of my precepts, and take my covenant in your mouth? You who hate discipline, and have cast my words behind you. If you saw a thief, you ran with him; and your portion was with adulterers. Your mouth has abounded in malice; and your tongue framed deceit. Sitting, you spoke against your brother; and against your mother's son you devised reproach. These things you did, and I was silent: you thought, wickedly, that I would be like yourself; I will reprove you, and set them before your eyes. Understand this, you who forget God; lest at any time he seize you, and there be none to deliver. He who offers the sacrifice of praise glorifies me; and to him who walks in the right way I will show the salvation of God [50:14-23 (49:14-23)].

**54. 54.** [Ps 51 (50)]. Cleanse me from my iniquity, and from my sin. For I acknowledge my iniquity, and my sin is always before me [51:4-5 (50:4-5)].

*And elsewhere:* A sacrifice to God is an afflicted spirit: a contrite and humbled heart, O God, you will not despise [51:19 (50:19)].

**55. 55.** [Ps 52 (51)]. Why do you glory in malice, you who are mighty? The mercy of God endures all the day. Your tongue plots treacheries, like a sharp razor working deceit. You have loved evil more than good, falsehood more than to speak justice. You have loved all words of devouring, O deceitful tongue. But God will destroy you forever; he will terrify you, and pluck you out of your dwelling, and root you out of the land of the living. The just shall see, and shall fear, and shall laugh at him: Behold the man who did not make God his strength: but hoped in the abundance of his riches; he was strengthened in his treacheries. But I, like a flourishing olive tree in the house of God, have hoped in the mercy of God forever and ever. I will praise you forever, because you have acted; and I will wait on your name, for it is good in the sight of your saints [52:3-11 (51:3-11)].

**56. 56.** [Ps 62 (61)]. Hope in him at all times, you peoples; pour out your hearts before him: God is our hope. Yet vain are the sons of Adam, the sons of men are a lie: in deceitful balances they act fraudulently together. Do not trust in extortion, and do not be made vain in robbery. If riches abound, do not set your heart on them [62:9-11 (61:9-11)].
