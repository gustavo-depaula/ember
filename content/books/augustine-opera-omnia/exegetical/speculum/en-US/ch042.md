# From the Gospel According to John

**323. 323.** [John 5]. How can you believe when you receive glory from one another and do not seek the glory that comes from God alone? [5:44].

**324. 324.** [John 8]. *And a little later*: Jesus said: Neither will I condemn you. Go, and do not sin any further [8:11].  
*And a little later:* If you remain in my word, you will truly be my disciples; you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free [8:31–32].  
*And a little later:* Truly, truly I tell you: everyone who commits sin is a slave of sin [8:34].  
*And elsewhere:* Truly, truly I tell you: if anyone keeps my word, he will never see death [8:51].

**325. 325.** [John 12]. *And somewhat later:* Anyone who loves his life will lose it; anyone who hates his life in this world preserves it for eternal life. If anyone serves me, he must follow me, and where I am, my servant too will be. If anyone serves me, my Father will honor him [12:25–26].  
*And in another place:* For they loved human glory more than God's glory [12:43].

**326. 326.** [John 13]. *And elsewhere:* You call me Teacher and Lord, and you speak well, for that is what I am. Therefore, if I, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you too ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, so that you too may act as I have acted toward you [13:13–15].  
*And a little later:* I give you a new commandment: love one another; just as I have loved you, you too must love one another. By this everyone will recognize that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another [13:34–35].

**327. 327.** [John 14]. *And a little later:* If you love me, keep my commandments [14:15].  
*And a little later:* The one who has my commandments and keeps them is the one who loves me. Anyone who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him and reveal myself to him [14:21].  
*And three verses later:* If anyone loves me, he will keep my word; my Father will love him, and we will come to him and make our dwelling with him. Anyone who does not love me does not keep my words [14:23–24].

**328. 328.** [John 15]. *And a little later:* Remain in me, and I in you. Just as a branch cannot bear fruit of itself unless it remains on the vine, neither can you unless you remain in me. I am the vine; you are the branches. The one who remains in me, and I in him, bears much fruit, because without me you can do nothing. If anyone does not remain in me, he will be thrown outside like a branch and wither. They will gather such branches, throw them into the fire, and they burn. If you remain in me and my words remain in you, you will ask whatever you wish, and it will be done for you. In this my Father is glorified: that you bear abundant fruit and become my disciples. Just as the Father has loved me, I too have loved you. Remain in my love. If you keep my precepts, you will remain in my love, just as I have kept my Father's precepts and remain in his love. I have said these things to you so that my joy may be in you and your joy may be fulfilled. This is my precept: love one another just as I have loved you. No one has greater love than this: to lay down his life for his friends [15:4–13].  
*And a little later:* These things I command you: love one another [15:17].

*These are the things from the four Gospels that I thought should be included in this work. From them it can be understood that the three Evangelists—Matthew, Mark, and Luke—have given us more precepts for living because they chiefly followed the part called active. But because John adhered more to the contemplative part, although his Gospel surpasses the others, we have found far fewer precepts of conduct in it.*

*We will now mention what we have found in the book to which they gave the title Acts of the Apostles, though it is very little indeed. That book contains the history of events by which faith is built up, and in it examples rather than precepts have been recorded.*
