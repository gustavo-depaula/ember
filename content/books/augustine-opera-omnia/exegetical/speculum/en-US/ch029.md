# From the Book of Malachi

**181. 181.** [Mal 1]. The son honors the father, and the servant his master: if then I am a father, where is my honor? and if I am a master, where is my fear, says the Lord of hosts? [1:6].

**182. 182.** [Mal 2]. *And a little later:* I have made you contemptible and base to all peoples, as you have not kept my ways, and have respected persons in the law. Is there not one father of us all? did not one God create us? Why then does each one of us despise his brother, violating the covenant of our fathers? Judah has transgressed, and an abomination has been committed in Israel and in Jerusalem; for Judah has profaned the holiness of the Lord, which he loved, and has taken the daughter of a strange god. May the Lord destroy the man who does this, the master and the disciple, out of the tents of Jacob, and the one who offers a gift to the Lord of hosts. And this again you have done: you covered the altar of the Lord with tears, with weeping and bellowing, so that I no longer regard the sacrifice, nor accept anything acceptable from your hand. And you have said: For what cause? Because the Lord has been witness between you and the wife of your youth, whom you have despised: and she is your partner, and the wife of your covenant. Did not one make her, and is she not the residue of his spirit? And what does each one seek, but the seed of God? Keep then your spirit; and do not despise the wife of your youth. When you have hated her, put her away, says the Lord God of Israel: but iniquity shall cover his garment, says the Lord of hosts. Keep your spirit, and do not despise. You have wearied the Lord with your words, and you have said: In what have we wearied him? In this, when you said: Everyone who does evil is good in the sight of the Lord, and such please him; or surely where is the God of judgment? [2:9-17].

**183. 183.** [Mal 3]. *And after fifteen verses:* And I will come near to you, *he says,* in judgment, and I will be a swift witness against sorcerers and adulterers and perjurers, and those who defraud the hired man of his wages, the widows and the orphans, and oppress the stranger, and have not feared me, says the Lord of hosts. For I am the Lord, and I do not change; and you, sons of Jacob, are not consumed. For from the days of your fathers you have departed from my ordinances, and have not kept them. Return to me, and I will return to you, says the Lord of hosts [3:5-7].  
*And after fifteen verses:* Your words have grown strong against me, says the Lord. And you have said: What have we spoken? You have said: It is vain to serve God: and what profit is it that we have kept his precepts, and that we have walked sorrowful before the Lord of hosts? Therefore now we call the arrogant blessed: for those who work iniquity are built up, and have tempted God, and are saved. Then those who feared God spoke, each one with his neighbor. And the Lord gave heed, and heard, and a book of remembrance was written before him for those who fear the Lord, and think upon his name: and they shall be mine, says the Lord of hosts, in the day when I act, for a special possession; and I will spare them, as a man spares his son who serves him. And you shall turn, and shall see the difference between the just and the wicked, and between him who serves God and him who does not serve him [3:13-18].

**184. 184.** [Mal 4]. For behold, the day shall come kindled like a furnace, and all the proud and all who work iniquity shall be stubble; and the coming day shall set them on fire, says the Lord of hosts, and it shall leave them neither root nor branch. But to you who fear my name the sun of justice shall arise, and health in its wings: and you shall go forth, and shall leap like calves of the herd; and you shall tread down the wicked, when they shall be ashes under the sole of your feet, in the day when I act, says the Lord of hosts [4:1-3].
