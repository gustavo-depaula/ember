# From the Psalms

**37. 37.** [Ps 1]. Blessed is the man who has not walked in the counsel of the wicked, and has not stood in the way of sinners, and has not sat in the seat of scoffers. But his will is in the law of the Lord, and on his law he will meditate day and night [1:1-2].

**38. 38.** [Ps 2]. Now therefore, you kings, understand; be instructed, you judges of the earth. Serve the Lord in fear, and rejoice with trembling. Worship purely, lest at any time he be angry, and you perish from the way. When his fury shall blaze up after a little while, blessed are all who hope in him [2:10-13].

**39. 39.** [Ps 4]. Sons of men, how long, my honored ones, will you love vanity to your dishonor, seeking after a lie? [4:3].  
*And a little later:* Be angry, and do not sin: speak in your hearts upon your beds, and be silent. Offer the sacrifice of justice, and trust in the Lord [4:5-6].

**40. 40.** [Ps 5]. For you are not a God who wills iniquity. Neither shall the malignant dwell beside you; the unjust shall not stand in the sight of your eyes. You hate all who work iniquity, you will destroy all who speak a lie. The bloody and deceitful man the Lord will abhor [5:5-7].

**41. 41.** [Ps 15 (14)]. Lord, who shall sojourn in your tent? and who shall dwell on your holy mountain? He who walks without blemish, and works justice, and speaks the truth in his heart: who is not loose with his tongue, nor has done evil to his friend, nor borne reproach against his neighbor. In his eyes the wicked man is despised, but he glorifies those who fear the Lord. He swears to his own hurt, and does not change. He has not given his money at usury, nor accepted gifts against the innocent. He who does these things shall not be moved forever [15:1-5 (14:1-5)].

**42. 42.** [Ps 24 (23)]. Who shall ascend the mountain of the Lord? or who shall stand in his holy place? He who is innocent of hands and clean of heart, who has not lifted up his soul in vain, and has not sworn deceitfully. He shall receive a blessing from the Lord, and justice from God his savior [24:3-5 (23:3-5)].

**43. 43.** [Ps 27 (26)]. The Lord is my light and my salvation, whom shall I fear? The Lord is the strength of my life, of whom shall I be afraid? [27:1 (26:1)].  
*And after four verses:* If camps stand against me, my heart shall not fear. If war rise up against me, in this will I trust. One thing I have asked of the Lord, and this will I seek after: that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life; that I may see the beauty of the Lord, and behold his temple [27:3-4 (26:3-4)].  
*And in another place:* My heart has said to you: I have sought your countenance; your face, O Lord, will I seek [27:8 (26:8)].  
*And afterward:* Wait for the Lord, take courage, and let your heart be strengthened, and bear with the Lord [27:14 (26:14)].

**44. 44.** [Ps 28 (27)]. Do not deliver me up with the wicked, and with those who work iniquity; who speak peace with their friends, while evil is in their hearts [28:3 (27:3)].
