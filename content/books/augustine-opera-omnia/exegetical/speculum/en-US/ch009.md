# **57. 57.** [Ps 64 (63)]. The just man shall rejoice in the Lord, and shall hope in him [64:11 (63:11)].

**58. 58.** [Ps 69 (68)]. For your sake I have borne reproach; confusion has covered my face. I have become a stranger to my brothers, and a foreigner to my mother's sons. For the zeal of your house has consumed me, and the reproaches of those who reproached you have fallen upon me. And I wept and afflicted my soul with fasting; and it became a reproach to me. And I made sackcloth my garment; and I became a byword to them. They who sat in the gate spoke against me, and those who drank wine sang of me [69:8-13 (68:8-13)].  
*And somewhat later:* I will praise the name of God in song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving. And it shall please the Lord better than a young bullock that brings forth horns and hoofs. Let the meek see and be glad. You who seek the Lord, your soul shall live. For the Lord has heard the poor [69:31-34 (68:31-34)].

**59. 59.** [Ps 73 (72)]. For behold, those who remove themselves far from you shall perish; you have destroyed everyone who plays the harlot away from you. But for me it is good to draw near to God: I have put my hope in the Lord God, that I may declare all your announcements [73:27-28 (72:27-28)].

**60. 60.** [Ps 76 (75)]. Make vows, and pay them to the Lord your God; all who are round about him shall offer gifts to the terrible one, who takes away the spirit of princes, who is terrible to the kings of the earth [76:12-13 (75:12-13)].

**61. 61.** [Ps 78 (77)]. Hear, my people, my law; incline your ear to the words of my mouth. I will open my mouth in a parable, I will utter dark sayings of old [78:1-2 (77:1-2)].  
*And in another place:* And they shall tell their children, that they may set their hope in God, and not forget his deeds, and may keep his commandments [78:6-7 (77:6-7)].

**62. 62.** [Ps 81 (80)]. Praise God our strength, shout with joy to the God of Jacob [81:2 (80:2)].  
*And in another place:* Israel, if you will hear me, let there be no strange god in you, and you shall not adore a foreign god. I am the Lord your God [81:9-11 (80:9-11)].

**63. 63.** [Ps 82 (81)]. How long will you judge iniquity, and accept the persons of the wicked? Judge for the poor and the orphan, do justice to the needy and the destitute [82:2-3 (81:2-3)].

**64. 64.** [Ps 84 (83)]. Blessed are those who dwell in your house, they shall still praise you. Blessed is the man whose strength is in you, who has set ascents in his heart: passing through the valley of weeping, they make it a fountain. The teacher too shall be clothed with blessing: they shall go from strength to strength; they shall appear before God in Zion [84:5-8 (83:5-8)].  
*And a little later:* For better is one day in your courts than a thousand. I have chosen to be cast aside in the house of my God, rather than to dwell in the tents of wickedness [84:11 (83:11)].
