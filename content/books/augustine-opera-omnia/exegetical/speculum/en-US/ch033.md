# **223. 223.** [Jer 48]. *And somewhat later:* Cursed be he who does the work of the Lord fraudulently [48:10].

**224. 224.** [Lam 3]. *Likewise after a little:* The Lord is good to those who hope in him, to the soul that seeks him. It is good to wait with silence for the salvation of the Lord. It is good for a man when he has borne the yoke from his youth. He shall sit solitary and keep silence; because he has taken it up upon himself. He shall put his mouth in the dust, if perhaps there be hope. He shall give his cheek to him who strikes him, he shall be filled with reproaches; for the Lord does not cast off forever [3:25-32].
