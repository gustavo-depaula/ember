# From the Letter to the Hebrews

**400. 400.** [Heb 3]. Take care, brothers, lest any of you have an evil heart of unbelief that departs from the living God. Instead, exhort one another each day, as long as it is called today, so that none of you may be hardened by sin's deceit. For we have become sharers in Christ, provided we hold the beginning of his substance firm to the end, while it is said: Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts as in the provocation[^1] [3:12–15].

**401. 401.** [Heb 4]. *And a few verses later:* Therefore, let us fear lest, while the promise of entering his rest remains, any of you be thought to fall short. For the message was announced to us as to them, but the word they heard did not benefit them, not being mingled with faith in what they heard [4:1–2].  
*And in another place:* Therefore, having a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold to our confession [4:14].  
*And four* *verses later:* Therefore, let us approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may obtain mercy and find grace for timely help [4:16].

**402. 402.** [Heb 6]. *And a little later:* For God is not unjust, so as to forget your work and the love you showed in his name, having served the saints and continuing to serve them. We desire each of you to show the same diligence toward the fulfillment of hope until the end, so that you may not become sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and patience will inherit the promises [6:10–12].  
*And seven verses later:* For human beings swear by one greater than themselves, and an oath for confirmation ends every dispute among them [6:16].

**403. 403.** [Heb 10]. *And somewhat later:* Let us hold the confession of our hope without wavering, for the one who promised is faithful. Let us consider one another so as to stir up love and good works, not abandoning our assembly, as some are accustomed to do, but consoling one another, all the more as you see the day approaching. For if we sin willingly after receiving knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins, but a fearful expectation of judgment and the zeal of fire that will consume the adversaries. Anyone making Moses's Law void dies without mercy on the testimony of two or three witnesses [^2]. How much worse a punishment, do you think, is deserved by one who has trampled the Son of God, regarded as defiled the blood of the Covenant in which he was sanctified, and insulted the Spirit of grace? For we know the one who said: Vengeance is mine, and I will repay [^3]; and again: The Lord will judge his people [^4]. It is dreadful to fall into the hands of the living God. Remember the former days in which, after being enlightened, you endured a great contest of sufferings. At one time you were made a spectacle through reproaches and tribulations; at another you became partners of those so treated. For you shared the suffering of prisoners and joyfully accepted the plundering of your goods, knowing that you have a better and lasting possession. Therefore, do not cast away your confidence, which has a great reward. You need patience, so that by doing God's will you may obtain the promise. For in a little, a very little while, the one who is to come will come and will not delay[^5]. But my righteous one will live by faith; if he withdraws, he will not please my soul[^6] [10:23–38].

**404. 404.** [Heb 12]. *And somewhat later:* Therefore, we too, having so great a cloud of witnesses placed about us, should lay aside every weight and the sin surrounding us, and run with patience the contest set before us, looking toward Jesus, the author and perfecter of faith. For the joy set before him he endured the cross, despising the shame, and sits at the right hand of God's throne. Consider the one who endured such opposition from sinners against himself, so that you may not grow weary and lose heart in your souls. For you have not yet resisted to the point of blood in fighting against sin, and you have forgotten the consolation that speaks to you as children, saying: *My son, do not neglect the Lord's discipline or grow weary when reproved by him. For the Lord chastises the one he loves and scourges every son he receives*[^7]. Persevere in discipline; God offers himself to you as to children. For what son is there whom his father does not correct? But if you are outside the discipline in which all have shared, then you are illegitimate children, not sons. Furthermore, we had fathers of our flesh as instructors and respected them. Shall we not much more obey the Father of spirits and live? They instructed us for a few days according to their own will, but he for our benefit, to receive his sanctification. All discipline seems in the present to bring sorrow rather than joy, but afterward it yields the most peaceful fruit of righteousness to those trained by it. Therefore, raise your slack hands and weakened knees, and make straight steps for your feet, so that what is lame may not stray but rather be healed. Pursue peace with everyone and holiness, without which no one will see God. Watch lest anyone fall short of God's grace; lest a root of bitterness spring up and hinder you, and many be defiled through it; lest anyone be a fornicator or profane like Esau, who sold his birthright for one meal[^8] [12:1–16].

**405. 405.** [Heb 13]. *And in another place:* Let brotherly love remain among you, and do not forget hospitality. For through it some have pleased God by receiving angels as guests. Remember prisoners as though imprisoned with them, and those suffering as though you too remained in the body. Let marriage be honorable in all and the marriage bed undefiled, for God will judge fornicators and adulterers. Let your conduct be free of greed, content with what is at hand. For he himself said: *I will not abandon or forsake you*[^9], so that we may confidently say: *The Lord is my helper; I will not fear what a human being may do to me*[^10]. Remember your leaders, who spoke God's word to you; considering the outcome of their conduct, imitate their faith. Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. Do not be carried away by various foreign teachings. For it is best to establish the heart by grace, not by foods, which have not benefited those walking in them. We have an altar from which those serving the tabernacle have no right to eat. For the bodies of the animals whose blood the high priest carries into the sanctuary for sin[^11] are burned outside the camp. Therefore, Jesus too suffered outside the gate in order to sanctify the people through his own blood. Let us therefore go out to him outside the camp, carrying his reproach. For here we have no lasting city, but seek the future one. Through him, then, let us always offer God a sacrifice of praise, that is, the fruit of lips confessing his name. Do not forget beneficence and sharing, for God is pleased by such sacrifices. Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they keep vigilant watch as those who will give an account for your souls. Let them do this with joy rather than groaning, for that would not benefit you. Pray for us. We are confident that we have a good conscience, wishing to conduct ourselves well in everything [13:1–18].
