# From the Letter to Philemon

[Phlm]. For I have had great joy and consolation in your love, because the saints' hearts have been refreshed through you, brother [7].  
*And a little later:* I had wanted to keep him with me so that he might serve me on your behalf in the Gospel's chains; but I wanted to do nothing without your counsel, so that your good deed might be voluntary, not as though compelled [13–14].
