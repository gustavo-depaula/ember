# **211. 211.** [Jer 3]. *And a little later:* Return, you backsliding children, says the Lord, for I am your husband [3:14].

**212. 212.** [Jer 4]. *Likewise a little later:* If you will return, O Israel, return, says the Lord, to me. If you will take away your stumbling blocks from before my face, you shall not be moved. And you shall swear: The Lord lives, in truth, and in judgment, and in justice; and the nations shall bless him, and shall praise him [4:1-2].  
*And a little later:* My foolish people have not known me: they are senseless children, and without heart; they are wise to do evil, but to do good they have not known how [4:22].

**213. 213.** [Jer 5]. *And after thirty verses:* Go about the streets of Jerusalem, and look, and consider, and seek in its broad places, whether you can find a man who does judgment, and seeks faith, and I will be merciful to it. But even if they say: The Lord lives, even this they will swear falsely. O Lord, your eyes look upon faith. You struck them, and they did not grieve; you bruised them, and they refused to receive discipline: they made their faces harder than rock, they refused to return. But I said: Perhaps they are poor and foolish, not knowing the way of the Lord, the judgment of their God. I will go therefore to the great men, and will speak to them; for they have known the way of the Lord, the judgment of their God: and behold, these together have rather broken the yoke, and burst the bonds [5:1-5].  
*And after six verses:* Your children have forsaken me, and swear by those who are not gods. I fed them to the full, and they committed adultery, and rioted in the harlot's house. They became as wanton horses and stallions: each one neighed after his neighbor's wife. Shall I not visit for these things, says the Lord? and shall not my hand take vengeance on such a nation? [5:7-9].  
*And after forty verses:* I who set the sand as a bound for the sea, an everlasting precept which it shall not pass over; and they shall toss themselves, and shall not prevail, and its waves shall swell, and shall not pass over it. But this people has gotten an unbelieving and provoking heart: they have departed and gone away; and they have not said in their heart: Let us fear the Lord our God [5:22-24].  
*And after seven verses:* There are found, *he says,* among my people wicked men, lying in wait like fowlers, setting snares and traps to catch men. As a snare full of birds, so are their houses full of deceit: therefore they are become great and rich; they are grown fat and gross; and have most wickedly transgressed my words. They did not plead the cause of the orphan, and did not judge the judgment of the poor. Shall I not visit for these things, says the Lord? or shall not my soul take vengeance on such a nation? Astonishment and wondrous things are done in the land. The prophets prophesied falsehood, and the priests clapped their hands, and my people loved such things: what then shall be done in the end of it? [5:26-31].

**214. 214.** [Jer 6]. *And after thirty verses:* To whom shall I speak? and whom shall I call to witness, that he may hear? Behold, their ears are uncircumcised, and they cannot hear: behold, the word of the Lord has become a reproach to them, and they will not receive it [6:10].

**215. 215.** [Jer 7]. *And a little later:* Hear the word of the Lord, all you of Judah, who enter in through these gates to adore the Lord. Thus says the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel: Make your ways and your pursuits good, and I will dwell with you in this place. Do not trust in lying words, saying: The temple of the Lord, the temple of the Lord, it is the temple of the Lord. For if you order your ways and your pursuits well, if you do judgment between a man and his neighbor, if you do not oppress the stranger, the orphan, and the widow, nor shed innocent blood in this place, and do not walk after strange gods to your own hurt; I will dwell with you in this place, in the land which I gave to your fathers, from age to age. Behold, you put your trust in lying words, which shall not profit you, to steal, to kill, to commit adultery, to swear falsely, to offer libations to the Baals, and to go after strange gods, whom you do not know. And you came and stood before me in this house, in which my name has been invoked; and you said: We are delivered, because we have done all these abominations. Has then this house, in which my name has been invoked, become a den of robbers in your eyes? I, I myself, I have seen it, says the Lord. Go to my place in Shiloh, where my name dwelt from the beginning, and see what I did to it for the wickedness of my people Israel. And now, because you have done all these works, says the Lord, and I spoke to you, rising up early and speaking, and you did not hear; and I called you, and you did not answer: I will do to this house, in which my name has been invoked, and in which you have confidence, and to the place which I gave to your fathers, as I did to Shiloh; and I will cast you away from my face, as I cast away all your brethren, the whole seed of Ephraim. Therefore, do not pray for this people, nor take up praise and prayer for them, and do not withstand me; for I will not hear you. Do you not see what they do in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem? The children gather wood, and the fathers kindle the fire, and the women sprinkle the fat, to make cakes to the queen of heaven, and to offer libations to strange gods, and to provoke me to anger. Do they provoke me to anger, says the Lord? do they not rather provoke themselves, to the confusion of their own faces? [7:2-19].

**216. 216.** [Jer 8]. *And a little later:* Shall not he who falls rise again? And he who has turned away, shall he not return? Why then has this people in Jerusalem turned away with a contentious turning away? They have laid hold of falsehood, and have refused to return. I attended and listened; no one speaks what is good: there is none who does penance for his sin, saying: What have I done? [8:4-6].  
*And after nine verses:* How do you say: We are wise, and the law of the Lord is with us? Truly the lying pen of the scribes has wrought falsehood. The wise are confounded, dismayed, and taken. For they have cast away the word of the Lord, and there is no wisdom in them [8:8-9].

**217. 217.** [Jer 9]. *And a little later:* Who will set me in the wilderness as a lodging-place for travelers, that I may forsake my people, and depart from them? for they are all adulterers and a company of transgressors; and they have stretched out their tongue like a bow of falsehood, and not of truth: they have grown strong in the land, for they have gone from evil to evil, and they have not known me, says the Lord. Let each one guard himself against his neighbor, and put no trust in any brother of his: for every brother will utterly supplant, and every friend will walk fraudulently; and a man shall mock his brother, and they shall not speak the truth. For they have taught their tongue to speak lies; they have labored to act unjustly. Your dwelling is in the midst of deceit. Through deceit they have refused to know me, says the Lord. Therefore thus says the Lord of hosts: Behold, I will melt and try them. For what else shall I do before the daughter of my people? Their tongue is a wounding arrow, it has spoken deceit in its mouth: with his mouth one speaks peace to his friend, and secretly lays snares for him. Shall I not visit for these things, says the Lord? or shall not my soul take vengeance on such a nation? [9:2-9].  
*And a little later:* Thus says the Lord: Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, and let not the strong man glory in his strength, and let not the rich man glory in his riches; but let him who glories glory in this, to know and understand me, that I am the Lord, who exercise mercy, and judgment, and justice in the earth. For these things please me, says the Lord [9:23-24].

**218. 218.** [Jer 10]. *And after sixteen verses:* Thus says the Lord: Do not learn according to the ways of the nations, and do not fear the signs of heaven which the nations fear; for the laws of the peoples are vain. For the workman's hand has cut a tree out of the forest with the axe: he has adorned it with silver and gold; he has put it together with nails and hammers, that it may not fall apart. They are made in the likeness of a palm tree, and shall not speak: they must be carried, for they cannot walk. Do not fear them, then, for they can neither do evil nor good. There is none like you, O Lord; you are great, and great is your name in might. Who shall not fear you, O King of nations? for honor is yours among all the wise of the nations, and in all their kingdoms there is none like you. They shall all be proved foolish and senseless. The doctrine of their vanity is wood. Beaten silver is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz, the work of the craftsman and of the hand of the coppersmith. Blue and purple are their clothing, all these are the work of craftsmen. But the Lord is the true God, he is the living God, and the everlasting King. At his indignation the earth shall tremble, and the nations shall not endure his threatening. Thus then shall you say to them: The gods who have not made heaven and earth, let them perish from the earth, and from those things that are under heaven [10:2-11].

**219. 219.** [Jer 17]. *And somewhat later:* Thus says the Lord: Cursed be the man who trusts in man, and makes flesh his arm, and whose heart departs from the Lord. For he shall be like the tamarisk in the desert, and shall not see when good comes; but shall dwell in dryness in the desert, in a salt and uninhabitable land. Blessed is the man who trusts in the Lord, and the Lord shall be his confidence. And he shall be like a tree that is transplanted by the waters, that sends its roots toward moisture; and it shall not fear when the heat comes: and its leaf shall be green, and in the time of drought it shall not be anxious, nor shall it ever cease to bear fruit [17:5-8].

**220. 220.** [Jer 22]. *Likewise somewhat later:* Thus says the Lord: Do judgment and justice, and deliver the one oppressed by force from the hand of the calumniator; and do not grieve the stranger, the orphan, and the widow, nor oppress them unjustly; and do not shed innocent blood in this place [22:3].

**221. 221.** [Jer 23]. *And a little later:* Woe to the shepherds who scatter and tear the flock of my pasture, says the Lord. Therefore thus says the Lord God of Israel to the shepherds who feed my people: You have scattered my flock, and have cast them out, and have not visited them: behold, I will visit upon you the malice of your pursuits, says the Lord [23:1-2].  
*Likewise a little later:* Thus says the Lord of hosts: Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you and deceive you. They speak a vision of their own heart, not from the mouth of the Lord. They say to those who blaspheme me: The Lord has spoken: You shall have peace; and to everyone who walks in the depravity of his own heart they have said: No evil shall come upon you. For who has stood in the counsel of the Lord? [23:16-18].  
*And after eleven verses:* I did not send the prophets, yet they ran; I did not speak to them, yet they prophesied. If they had stood in my counsel, and had made my words known to my people, I would surely have turned them away from their evil way, and from their wicked thoughts [23:21-22].  
*And after four verses:* I have heard what the prophets said, prophesying falsehood in my name, and saying: I have dreamed, I have dreamed. How long is this in the heart of the prophets who prophesy falsehood, and who prophesy the seductions of their own heart; who seek to make my people forget my name, through their dreams which each one tells to his neighbor, as their fathers forgot my name for Baal? The prophet who has a dream, let him tell the dream; and he who has my word, let him tell my word truly. What has chaff to do with wheat? says the Lord. Are not my words like fire, says the Lord, and like a hammer that breaks the rock in pieces? Therefore behold, I am against the prophets, says the Lord, who steal my words, each one from his neighbor. Behold, I am against the prophets, says the Lord, who take up their own tongues, and say: The Lord says. Behold, I am against the prophets who dream falsehood, says the Lord, who have told them and seduced my people by their lying and by their wonders, when I had not sent them, nor commanded them; who have profited this people nothing, says the Lord [23:25-32].

**222. 222.** [Jer 25]. *And a little later:* And the Lord sent to you all his servants the Prophets, rising early and sending them; and you did not hear, nor incline your ears to hear, when he said: Return each one from his evil way, and from your wicked thoughts; and you shall dwell in the land which the Lord gave to you and to your fathers, from age to age; and do not go after strange gods, to serve them and adore them, nor provoke me to anger by the works of your hands, and I will not afflict you. And you did not hear me, says the Lord [25:4-7].
