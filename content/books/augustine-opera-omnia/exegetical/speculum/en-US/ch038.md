# *From the book of Tobit we have judged that these things should be set down.*: From the Book of Tobit

**279. 279.** [Tob 4]. You shall honor your mother all the days of her life. For you ought to remember what great dangers she suffered for you in her womb[^1].  
*And after two verses:* All the days of your life keep God in mind, and take heed never to consent to sin, or to neglect the commandments of the Lord your God. Out of your substance give alms, and do not turn away your face from any poor man: for so it will come to pass that the face of the Lord will not be turned from you. Be merciful in whatever way you are able. If you have much, give abundantly; if you have little, take care to share even the little gladly. For you lay up for yourself a good reward against the day of need. For alms deliver from every sin and from death, and will not allow the soul to go into darkness. Alms will be great confidence before the most high God for all who do it. Take heed to yourself, my son, against all unchastity, and never allow yourself to know any crime besides your own wife. Never permit pride to rule in your mind or in your speech; for in it all ruin had its beginning. Whoever has done any work for you, repay him his wages at once; and let the wages of a hired man by no means remain with you. See that you never do to another what you would hate to have done to yourself. Eat your bread with the hungry and the needy, and with your garments cover the naked[^2].  
*And after two verses:* Always seek counsel from a wise man. At all times bless God, and ask him to direct your ways, and that all your counsels may abide in him[^3].  
*And after seven verses:* Do not be afraid, my son: we lead a poor life indeed, but we will have many good things, if we fear God, and depart from every sin, and do what is good[^4].

**280. 280.** [Tob 12]. *And after much else:* Then Raphael said to them secretly: Bless the God of heaven, and acknowledge him before all the living, because he has shown his mercy to you. For it is good to keep close the secret of a king; but it is honorable to reveal and acknowledge the works of God. Prayer is good with fasting, and alms more than to lay up treasures of gold. For alms deliver from death, and it is alms that purge away sins, and will obtain eternal life. But those who commit sin and iniquity are enemies of their own soul[^5].

**281. 281.** [Tob 14]. *And a little later:* Hear, then, my children, your father: Serve the Lord in truth, and seek to do what is pleasing to him; and charge your children to do justice and almsdeeds, to be mindful of God, and to bless him at all times, in truth and with all their strength[^6].

*Now, since not all the canonical books of the Old Testament* *have supplied what was needed for this work of ours, and it was fitting that those which did should supply it not from all their passages but only from those in which we found clearer precepts for good conduct, whether useful things are commanded or their opposites forbidden, let us pass to the sacred writings of the Evangelists and Apostles. The four best-known and most illustrious Gospels hold the first place in the canon of the New Testament. In the first of these, the Gospel according to Matthew, let us examine what is suitable for this work we have undertaken.*
