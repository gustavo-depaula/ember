# From the Book of Zephaniah

**175. 175.** [Zeph 1]. I will destroy, *he says,* out of this place the remnants of Baal, and the names of the temple-keepers with the priests; and those who adore the host of heaven upon the housetops, and adore and swear by the Lord, and swear by Melchom; and those who turn away from following the Lord; and those who have not sought the Lord, nor searched after him. Be silent before the face of the Lord; for the day of the Lord is at hand [1:4-7].  
*And after sixteen verses:* And it shall come to pass at that time, that I will search Jerusalem with lamps, and will visit the men who are settled in their dregs, who say in their hearts: The Lord will do neither good nor evil [1:12].

**176. 176.** [Zeph 2]. *And after eighteen verses*: Come together, be gathered, O nation not worthy to be loved, before the decree bring forth the day as dust that passes, before the wrath of the Lord's fury come upon you, before the day of the Lord's fury come upon you. Seek the Lord, all you meek of the earth, who have wrought his judgment. Seek the just, seek the meek; if perhaps you may be hidden in the day of the Lord's fury [2:1-3].

**177. 177.** [Zeph 3]. *And after a little:* And I will leave in your midst a poor and needy people; and the remnant of Israel shall hope in the name of the Lord: they shall do no iniquity, nor speak a lie, and a deceitful tongue shall not be found in their mouth [3:12-13].
