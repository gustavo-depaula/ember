# From the Second Letter to Timothy

**393. 393.** [2 Tim 1]. For God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power, love, and sobriety. Therefore, do not be ashamed of our Lord's testimony or of me, his prisoner, but share in labor for the Gospel according to God's power [1:7–8].  
*And a very few* *verses later:* Hold to the pattern of sound words you heard from me in the faith and love in Christ Jesus. Guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit dwelling in us [1:13–14].

**394. 394.** [2 Tim 2]. *And a little later:* Therefore, my son, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus. Entrust what you heard from me through many witnesses to faithful people who will be capable of teaching others too. Labor as a good soldier of Christ Jesus. No one serving as God's soldier entangles himself in worldly business, so that he may please the one to whom he has proved himself. Nor is someone contending in a contest crowned unless he has competed lawfully. The laboring farmer ought to be the first to receive the fruits. Understand what I say, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything. Remember that Jesus Christ, of David's seed, rose from the dead according to my Gospel, in which I labor even to imprisonment as an evildoer; but God's word is not bound. Therefore, I endure everything for the chosen, so that they too may obtain the salvation in Christ Jesus with heavenly glory. The saying is faithful: if we have died together, we will also live together; if we endure, we will also reign together; if we deny him, he too will deny us; if we do not believe, he remains faithful, for he cannot deny

himself. Remind them of these things, testifying before God. Do not contend over words, which serves no use except the overthrow of the hearers. Diligently take care to present yourself approved before God, an irreproachable worker, rightly handling the word of truth. Avoid profane and empty talk, for it will advance greatly toward ungodliness, and their speech spreads like a cancer [2:1–17].  
*And six verses later:* The Lord knows those who are his[^1]; and let everyone calling upon the Lord's name depart from iniquity[^2]. In a great house there are vessels not only of gold and silver but also of wood and clay, some for honor and some for dishonor. Therefore, if anyone cleanses himself from these things, he will be a vessel for honor, sanctified, useful to the Lord, and prepared for every good work. Flee youthful desires; pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace with those calling upon the Lord from a pure heart. Avoid foolish and undisciplined questions, knowing that they generate quarrels. The Lord's slave ought not to quarrel but to be gentle toward everyone, apt to teach, patient, and moderate in correcting opponents. Perhaps God may grant them repentance to recognize the truth, and they may regain their senses out of the devil's snares, by whom they are held captive to his will [2:19–26].

**395. 395.** [2 Tim 3]. Know this: in the last days dangerous times will press upon us. People will be lovers of themselves, greedy, arrogant, proud, blasphemous, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, wicked, without affection, without peace, accusatory, uncontrolled, harsh, without kindness, traitorous, reckless, swollen with pride, lovers of pleasures more than of God, having the appearance of piety but denying its power. Avoid these too. For among them are those who penetrate homes and take captive foolish women burdened with sins, led by various desires, always learning and never reaching the knowledge of truth [3:1–7].  
*And eight verses later:* But you have followed my teaching, manner of life, purpose, faith, forbearance, love, patience, persecutions, and sufferings: what happened to me at Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra, what persecutions I endured, and the Lord rescued me from them all. All who want to live piously in Christ Jesus suffer persecutions. But evil people and deceivers will advance toward worse, going astray and leading others into error. You, however, should remain in what you have learned and what has been entrusted to you, knowing from whom you learned it. From infancy you have known the sacred writings, which can instruct you for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus. Every divinely inspired Scripture is useful for teaching, reproving, correcting, and training in righteousness, so that God's man may be perfect, equipped for every good work [3:10–17].

**396. 396.** [2 Tim 4]. I testify before God and Christ Jesus, who will judge the living and dead, and through his coming and kingdom: preach the word, persevere in season and out of season, reprove, entreat, and rebuke with all patience and teaching. For a time will come when they will not endure sound teaching, but according to their desires will heap up teachers for themselves, their ears itching. They will turn their hearing away from truth and turn toward fables. But you, keep watch, labor in everything, do an evangelist's work, and fulfill your ministry. For I am already being poured out, and the time of my release is approaching. I have fought the good contest, finished the course, and kept the faith. For the future there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the just judge, will repay me on that day—not only me, but also those who love his coming [4:1–8].

[^1]: Cf. Num 16:5
[^2]: Cf. Isa 26:13
