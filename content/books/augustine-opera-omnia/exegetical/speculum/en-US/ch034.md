# From the Book of Ezekiel

**225. 225.** [Ezek 3]. And when seven days had passed, the word of the Lord came to me, saying: Son of man, I have made you a watchman to the house of Israel, and you shall hear the word from my mouth, and shall announce it to them from me. If, when I say to the wicked man: You shall die the death, you do not announce it to him, nor speak to him, that he may turn away from his wicked way and live; that wicked man shall die in his iniquity, but I will require his blood at your hand. But if you announce it to the wicked man, and he is not converted from his wickedness and from his wicked way; he indeed shall die in his iniquity, but you have delivered your soul. Moreover, if the just man turns away from his justice and commits iniquity, I will lay a stumbling block before him, and he shall die, because you did not announce it to him. He shall die in his sin, and the justices he had done shall not be remembered; but I will require his blood at your hand. But if you announce to the just man that the just man should not sin, and he does not sin; he shall surely live, because you announced it to him, and you have delivered your soul [3:16-21].

**226. 226.** [Ezek 9]. *And after a little:* And the Lord said to him: Pass through the midst of the city, in the midst of Jerusalem, and mark the letter Tau upon the foreheads of the men who sigh and grieve over all the abominations that are done in its midst. And to the others he said, while I heard: Pass through the city after him, and strike; let not your eye spare, neither have pity: kill utterly the old man, the young man, and the virgin, the little one, and the women. But upon anyone on whom you see the Tau, do not kill: and begin from my sanctuary [9:4-6].
