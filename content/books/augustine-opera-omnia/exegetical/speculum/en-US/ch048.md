# From the Letter to the Ephesians

**373. 373.** [Eph 4]. Therefore, brothers, I, a prisoner in the Lord, appeal to you to walk worthily of the calling with which you were called, with all humility of mind and gentleness, patiently bearing with one another in love, diligently preserving the spirit's unity in the bond of peace [4:1–3].  
*And in another place:* Therefore, I say and testify in the Lord that you must no longer walk as the gentiles walk, in the vanity of their understanding, having their intellect darkened, alienated from God's life through the ignorance within them because of the blindness of their hearts. Despairing, they have handed themselves over to shamelessness, to the practice of every impurity and greed. But you have not learned Christ in this way, if indeed you have heard him and been taught in him, as truth is in Jesus. Put off, with respect to your former conduct, the old man, who is corrupted according to the desires of error. Be renewed in the spirit of your mind, and put on the new man, created according to God in the righteousness and holiness of truth[^1]. Therefore, put away falsehood and let each speak truth with his neighbor, because we are members of one another. Be angry and do not sin[^2]. Do not let the sun set upon your anger. Do not give the devil a place. The one who stole must no longer steal, but rather labor, doing good work with his hands, so that he may have something to share with the needy. Let no evil speech come from your mouth, but only what is good for building up as needed, so that it may give grace to the hearers. Do not sadden God's Holy Spirit, in whom you were sealed on the day of redemption. Let all bitterness, anger, indignation, shouting, and blasphemy be removed from you, together with all malice. Be kind and merciful to one another, forgiving one another as God too forgave you in Christ [4:17–32].

**374. 374.** [Eph 5]. Therefore, be imitators of God as dearest children, and walk in love, just as Christ too loved us and handed himself over for us as an offering and sacrifice to God, a sweet fragrance. But fornication, every impurity, or greed must not even be named among you, as befits saints; nor shamefulness, foolish talk, or inappropriate jesting, but rather thanksgiving. For know and understand this: every fornicator, unclean person, or greedy person—which is service of idols—has no inheritance in Christ's and God's kingdom. Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things God's wrath comes upon the children of disobedience. Therefore, do not become their partners. For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light—for the fruit of light is in all goodness, righteousness, and truth—discerning what is pleasing to God. Do not share in the unfruitful works of darkness; rather, expose them. For it is shameful even to speak of what they do in secret. But everything exposed is made manifest by light, for everything made manifest is light. Therefore, he says: Rise, sleeper, and rise from the dead, and Christ will illuminate you. Therefore, brothers, see how carefully you walk, not as foolish people but as wise, redeeming the time because the days are evil. Consequently, do not become imprudent, but understand what the Lord's will is. Do not become drunk on wine, in which there is extravagance, but be filled with the Holy Spirit, speaking among yourselves in psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing and making music in your hearts to the Lord, always giving thanks for everything to God the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, subject to one another in Christ's fear. Wives should be subject to their own husbands as to the Lord, because the husband is the wife's head, as Christ is the Church's head, himself the Savior of the body. But just as the Church is subject to Christ, so wives should be subject to their own husbands in everything. Husbands, love your wives as Christ too loved the Church and handed himself over for her, to sanctify her, cleansing her by the washing of water in the word, so that he might present to himself a glorious Church, having no spot or wrinkle or anything of that kind, but holy and immaculate. In the same way husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies. The one who loves his wife loves himself. No one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, as Christ too does the Church, because we are members of his body, of his flesh and of his bones. For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and cling to his wife, and the two will be in one flesh[^3]. This is a great sacrament, but I speak with reference to Christ and the Church. Nevertheless, each of you individually should love his wife as himself, and the wife should fear her husband [5:1–33].

**375. 375.** [Eph 6]. Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right. Honor your father and mother—which is the first commandment with a promise—so that it may go well with you and you may live long upon the earth[^4]. And fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but raise them in the Lord's discipline and correction. Slaves, obey your masters according to the flesh with fear and trembling, in simplicity of heart, as you would Christ. Do not serve only under their eyes as people-pleasers, but as Christ's slaves, doing God's will from the soul, serving with good will as for the Lord rather than human beings. Know that whatever good each does he will receive back from the Lord, whether slave or free. And you masters, do the same toward them, abandoning threats, knowing that both their Lord and yours is in heaven and that with him there is no partiality. For the rest, brothers, be strengthened in the Lord and in the might of his power. Put on God's armor, so that you may stand against the devil's ambushes. For our wrestling is not against flesh and blood, but against rulers and authorities, against the world rulers of this darkness, against spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavens. Therefore, take up God's armor, so that you may resist on the evil day and stand complete in everything. Stand, then, with your loins girded in truth and clothed in the breastplate of righteousness[^5], your feet shod in readiness for the Gospel of peace. In everything take up the shield of faith, with which you can extinguish all the evil one's fiery darts. Take the helmet of salvation and the spirit's sword[^6], which is God's word. With every prayer and entreaty, pray at every time in the spirit, keeping watch in him with all perseverance and entreaty for all the saints, and for me, so that speech may be given me when I open my mouth to make known confidently the Gospel's mystery. For its sake I serve as an ambassador in chains, so that in it I may be bold to speak as I ought [6:1–20].

[^1]: Cf. Zech 8:16
[^2]: Cf. Ps 4:4
[^3]: Cf. Gen 2:24
[^4]: Cf. Exod 20:12; Deut 5:16; Sir 3:9
[^5]: Cf. Isa 11:5; 52:7; 40:3
[^6]: Cf. Isa 59:17
