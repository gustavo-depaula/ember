# **82. 82.** [Ps 117 (116)]. Praise the Lord, all you nations; praise him together, all you peoples [117:1 (116:1)].

**83. 83.** [Ps 118 (117)]. Give praise to the Lord, for he is good, for his mercy endures forever. Let Israel now say that his mercy endures forever. Let the house of Aaron now say that his mercy endures forever. Let those who fear the Lord now say that his mercy endures forever. In my tribulation I called upon the Lord, and the Lord heard me and set me at large [118:1-5 (117:1-5)].  
*And after five verses:* It is better to hope in the Lord, than to hope in man. It is better to hope in the Lord, than to hope in princes [118:8-9 (117:8-9)].  
*And after eight verses:* The Lord is my strength and my praise, and he has become my salvation. The voice of praise and salvation is in the tents of the just [118:14-15 (117:14-15)].

**84. 84.** [Ps 119 (118)]. Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord. Blessed are those who keep his testimonies, who seek him with their whole heart [119:1-2 (118:1-2)].  
*And in another place:* And I will walk at large, because I have sought your precepts. And I will speak of your testimonies before kings, and will not be confounded. And I will delight in your commandments, which I have loved. And I will lift up my hands to your commandments, which I have loved; and I will be exercised in your precepts [119:45-48 (118:45-48)].  
*And after four verses:* I have not turned aside from your law. I have remembered your judgments of old, O Lord, and have been comforted. Horror took hold of me because of the wicked, who have forsaken your law. Your precepts were songs to me, in the house of my pilgrimage. In the night I remembered your name, O Lord, and kept your law. This befell me, because I sought your precepts. My portion, O Lord, I said, is to keep your word. I entreated your countenance with my whole heart; have mercy on me according to your word. I considered my ways, and turned my feet to your testimonies. I made haste, and did not neglect to keep your commandments. The cords of the wicked have entangled me; I have not forgotten your law. At midnight I rose to give praise to you, for the justice of your justifications. I am a partaker with all who fear you, and who keep your precepts [119:51-63 (118:51-63)].  
*And after a few verses:* But I with my whole heart kept your precepts. Their heart is grown fat like milk, but I have meditated on your law. It is good for me that I have been afflicted, that I might learn your precepts. The law of your mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver [119:69-72 (118:69-72)].  
*And after a few verses:* For your law is my delight [119:77 (118:77)].  
*And after a little:* But I will be exercised in your precepts [119:78 (118:78)].  
*Likewise a little later*: I have not forgotten your precepts [119:83 (118:83)].  
*And after two verses:* The proud have dug pits for me, which were not according to your law [119:85 (118:85)].  
*And after two verses:* They had almost made an end of me upon the earth; but I did not forsake your precepts [119:87 (118:87)].  
*And after seven verses:* Unless your law had been my delight, I would perhaps have perished in my distress. I will never forget your precepts; for by them you have given me life. I am yours, save me; for I have sought your precepts. The wicked have waited for me, to destroy me; I will consider your testimony. I have seen an end of all perfection; your commandment is exceedingly broad. How I have loved your law! it is my meditation all the day [119:92-97 (118:92-97)].  
*And a little later:* I have restrained my feet from every evil way, that I might keep your words. I have not departed from your judgments, because you have enlightened me. How sweet are your words to my throat! more than honey to my mouth. I considered your precepts; therefore I hated every way of falsehood. Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light to my path [119:101-105 (118:101-105)].  
*And after five verses:* My soul is continually in my hands, and I have not forgotten your law. The wicked have laid a snare for me, but I have not strayed from your precepts. Your testimonies are my inheritance forever; for they are the joy of my heart. I have inclined my heart to do your justices forever, for the reward. I hated the wicked, and loved your law. You are my protection and my shield: in your word I have greatly hoped. Depart from me, you malignant; and I will keep the commandments of my God [119:109-115 (118:109-115)].  
*And after seven verses:* You have counted all the wicked of the earth as dross; therefore I have loved your testimonies. My flesh has shuddered for fear of you, and I have been afraid of your judgments. I have done judgment and justice; do not give me up to those who slander me [119:119-121 (118:119-121)].  
*And after a few verses:* Therefore I have loved your commandments above gold and topaz. Therefore I directed myself to all your precepts; I have hated every way of falsehood. Wonderful are your testimonies; therefore my soul has kept them [119:127-129 (118:127-129)].  
*And after two verses:* I opened my mouth, and drew breath; because I longed for your commandments [119:131 (118:131)].  
*And in another place:* My zeal has consumed me, because my enemies have forgotten your words. Your word is exceedingly tried, and your servant has loved it. I am small and despised; but I have not forgotten your precepts [119:139-141 (118:139-141)].  
*And after three verses:* Your commandments are my will [119:143 (118:143)].  
*And a little later:* See my affliction, and deliver me; for I have not forgotten your law [119:153 (118:153)].  
*And after six verses:* Many are they who persecute me and afflict me; but I have not turned aside from your testimonies. I saw the transgressors, and I pined away; because they kept not your word. See that I have loved your precepts, O Lord; give me life according to your mercy [119:157-159 (118:157-159)].  
*And after four verses:* I rejoice in your words, like one who finds great spoils. I have hated and abhorred falsehood; but your law I have loved. Seven times a day I have given praise to you, for the judgments of your justice. Much peace have they who love your law, and to them there is no stumbling block. I waited for your salvation, O Lord, and did your commandments. My soul has kept your testimonies, and has loved them exceedingly. I have kept your precepts and your testimonies, because all my ways are in your sight [119:162-168 (118:162-168)].  
*And after eight verses:* Let your hand be my helper; for I have chosen your precepts. I have longed for your salvation, O Lord, and your law is my will [119:173-174 (118:173-174)].  
*And after two verses:* I have strayed like a lost sheep; seek your servant, for I have not forgotten your commandments [119:176 (118:176)].
