# From the Book of Jeremiah

**210. 210.** [Jer 2]. Hear the word of the Lord, O house of Jacob, and all you families of the house of Israel. Thus says the Lord: What iniquity have your fathers found in me, that they have gone far from me, and have walked after vanity, and have become vain? [2:4-5].  
*And after ten verses:* You have made my inheritance an abomination. The priests did not say: Where is the Lord? And those who held the law did not know me, and the shepherds transgressed against me, and the prophets prophesied by Baal, and followed idols [2:7-8].  
*And a little later:* Does a virgin forget her ornament, or a bride the band upon her breast? But my people have forgotten me for days without number. Why do you strive to show your way good in seeking love, you who have moreover taught your ways with your wickedness; and on your skirts is found the blood of the souls of the poor and innocent? I did not find them in pits, but in all the things I have mentioned above. And you have said: I am without sin and innocent, and therefore let your fury be turned away from me. Behold, I will contend with you in judgment, because you have said: I have not sinned. How exceedingly vile you have become, repeating your ways! and you shall be confounded by Egypt, as you were confounded by Assyria. For from her also you shall go forth, and your hands shall be upon your head; because the Lord has crushed your confidence [2:32-37].
