# From the Book of Joel

**163. 163.** [Joel 1]. Awake, you drunkards, and weep, and wail, all you who drink wine in sweetness; for it is cut off from your mouth. For a nation has come up upon my land, strong and innumerable [1:5-6].

**164. 164.** [Joel 2]. *And after a little:* Now therefore the Lord says: Turn to me with all your heart, in fasting and in weeping, and in mourning; and rend your hearts, and not your garments: and turn to the Lord your God; for he is gracious and merciful [2:12-13].
