# On the Book Whose Name Is the Revelation of John

**427. 427.** [Rev 2]. *If human beings are understood to be admonished—indeed, because they are—when the angels are admonished, then in all these same precepts we learn that false brethren are to be borne patiently for the sake of God's name, that one must return through repentance to the first good works, that persecutions must be endured for the faith even unto death, and that one must serve in love*[^1].

**428. 428.** [Rev 21]. *Now near the end of the book, when he was speaking of the holy city:* *But nothing unclean shall enter it,* he says, *nor anyone who practices abomination or falsehood*[^2].

**429. 429.** [Rev 22]. *And elsewhere:* *Blessed are those who wash their robes in the blood of the Lamb, that they may have the right to the tree of life and that they may enter the city by the gates. Outside are the dogs and sorcerers and the impure and murderers and idolaters, and everyone who loves and practices falsehood. I Jesus have sent my angel to testify these things to you*[^3].

[^1]: Rev 2:2, 14-16
[^2]: Rev 21:27
[^3]: Rev 22:14-16
