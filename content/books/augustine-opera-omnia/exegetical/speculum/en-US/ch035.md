# 16:49-50].

**229. 229.** [Ezek 18]. *And after a little:* If a man is just, and does judgment and justice; eats not on the mountains, and lifts not up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel; defiles not his neighbor's wife, and approaches not a menstruous woman; grieves not any man, restores the pledge to the debtor, takes nothing away by force; gives his bread to the hungry, and covers the naked with a garment; lends not at usury, and takes no more than he gave; turns his hand away from iniquity, does true judgment between man and man; walks in my precepts, and keeps my judgments, so as to do truth: this man is just; he shall surely live, says the Lord God. But if he begets a son who is a robber, a shedder of blood, and who does one of these things; and who does not indeed do all these things, but eats on the mountains, and defiles his neighbor's wife; grieves the needy and the poor, takes away by violence, restores not the pledge; and lifts up his eyes to the idols, commits abominations; lends at usury, and takes more: shall he live? He shall not live: since he has done all these detestable things, he shall die the death; his blood shall be upon him. But if he begets a son who, seeing all the sins of his father which he did, fears, and does not do the like; eats not on the mountains, and lifts not up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel; defiles not his neighbor's wife; grieves not any man, withholds not the pledge, and takes nothing away by robbery; gives his bread to the hungry, and covers the naked with a garment; turns away his hand from wronging the poor, takes neither usury nor increase; does my judgments, and walks in my precepts: this man shall not die for the iniquity of his father, but shall surely live. His father, because he oppressed and did violence to his brother, and worked evil in the midst of his people; behold, he has died in his iniquity. And you say: Why has the son not borne the iniquity of the father? Plainly because the son has done judgment and justice; he has kept all my precepts and done them: he shall surely live. The soul that sins, it shall die. The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, and the father shall not bear the iniquity of the son. The justice of the just shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him. But if the wicked man does penance for all his sins which he has committed, and keeps all my precepts, and does judgment and justice; he shall surely live, and shall not die. I will not remember all his iniquities which he has done; in his justice which he has wrought he shall live. Is it my will that the wicked should die, says the Lord God, and not that he should be converted from his ways and live? But if the just man turns away from his justice, and commits iniquity according to all the abominations which the wicked man is wont to work; shall he live? All the justices which he had done shall not be remembered. In the transgression by which he has transgressed, and in his sin which he has sinned, in these he shall die. And you have said: The way of the Lord is not equitable. Hear therefore, O house of Israel: Is my way not equitable, and are not your ways rather perverse? For when the just man turns away from his justice and commits iniquity, he shall die for them: in the injustice which he has wrought he shall die. And when the wicked man turns away from his wickedness which he has wrought, and does judgment and justice, he shall give life to his own soul. For he considered, and turned himself away from all his iniquities which he had wrought; he shall surely live, and shall not die. And the children of Israel say: The way of the Lord is not equitable. Are not my ways equitable, O house of Israel, and are not your ways rather perverse? Therefore I will judge each one according to his ways, O house of Israel, says the Lord God. Be converted, and do penance for all your iniquities, and iniquity shall not be your ruin. Cast away from you all your transgressions by which you have transgressed, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit: and why will you die, O house of Israel? For I do not will the death of him who dies, says the Lord God: return, and live [18:5-32].

**230. 230.** [Ezek 22]. *And somewhat later:* Behold, the princes of Israel, each one according to his strength, were in you to shed blood. They treated father and mother with contempt in you; they oppressed the stranger in your midst; they grieved the orphan and the widow with you. You despised my sanctuaries, and profaned my Sabbaths. There were slanderers in you to shed blood; and they ate upon the mountains in you: they wrought wickedness in your midst. They uncovered the father's shame in you, they humbled in you the woman in her uncleanness. And each one wrought an abomination with his neighbor's wife: and the father-in-law wickedly defiled his daughter-in-law; the brother oppressed his sister, his father's daughter, in you. They took bribes in you to shed blood; you took usury and increase, and greedily oppressed your neighbors, and you forgot me, says the Lord God [22:6-12].  
*And after a little:* And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: Son of man, say to her: You are an unclean land, not rained upon in the day of wrath. There is a conspiracy of prophets in her midst. Like a roaring lion seizing the prey, they have devoured souls, they have taken riches and price, they have multiplied her widows in her midst. Her priests have despised my law, and profaned my sanctuaries: they made no distinction between the holy and the profane, and did not discern between the polluted and the clean [22:23-26].  
*And after two verses:* Her princes in her midst are like wolves seizing the prey, to shed blood, and to destroy souls, and greedily to pursue gain. And her prophets daubed them without tempering, seeing vain things, and divining lies, saying: Thus says the Lord God; when the Lord had not spoken. The people of the land devised oppression, and seized by violence, afflicted the needy and the poor, and oppressed the stranger by calumny without judgment. And I sought among them for a man who might set up a hedge, and stand in the breach before me for the land, that I might not destroy it; and I found none. And I poured out my indignation upon them, and consumed them in the fire of my wrath. I have rendered their way upon their own head, says the Lord God [22:27-31].

**231. 231.** [Ezek 33]. *And somewhat later:* And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: Son of man, speak to the children of your people, and say to them: When I bring the sword upon a land, and the people of the land take one man from among their last, and set him over themselves as a watchman; and he sees the sword coming upon the land, and sounds the trumpet, and warns the people: then whoever hears the sound of the trumpet, and does not take warning, and the sword comes and takes him; his blood shall be upon his own head. He heard the sound of the trumpet, and did not take warning; his blood shall be upon him. But if he had warned himself, he would have saved his soul. And if the watchman sees the sword coming, and does not sound the trumpet, and the people do not take warning, and the sword comes and takes a soul from among them: that soul indeed is taken in his iniquity; but his blood I will require at the watchman's hand. And you, son of man, I have made you a watchman to the house of Israel: hearing therefore the word from my mouth, you shall announce it to them from me. When I say to the wicked man: O wicked man, you shall die the death; if you do not speak, that the wicked man may keep himself from his way: that wicked man shall die in his iniquity; but his blood I will require at your hand. But if you announce to the wicked man, that he may be converted from his ways, and he is not converted from his way: he shall die in his iniquity; but you have delivered your soul. You therefore, son of man, say to the house of Israel: Thus you have spoken, saying: Our iniquities and our sins are upon us, and in them we pine away; how then can we live? Say to them: As I live, says the Lord God, I do not will the death of the wicked, but that the wicked man be converted from his way and live: be converted from your wicked ways; and why will you die, O house of Israel? You therefore, son of man, say to the children of your people: The justice of the just man shall not deliver him on whatever day he sins; and the wickedness of the wicked man shall not harm him on whatever day he is converted from his wickedness: and the just man shall not be able to live in his justice on whatever day he sins. Even though I say to the just man that he shall surely live, if, trusting in his justice, he commits iniquity; all his justices shall be forgotten, and in his iniquity which he has wrought, in that he shall die. But if I say to the wicked man: You shall die the death, and he does penance for his sin, and does judgment and justice, and the wicked man restores the pledge, gives back what he had taken by robbery, walks in the commandments of life, and does nothing unjust; he shall surely live, and shall not die. None of his sins which he has sinned shall be imputed to him. He has done judgment and justice, he shall surely live. And the children of your people have said: The way of the Lord is not of equal weight. And it is their own way that is unjust. For when the just man turns away from his justice and commits iniquities, he shall die in them. And when the wicked man turns away from his wickedness, and does judgment and justice, he shall live in them. And you say: The way of the Lord is not right. I will judge each one according to his ways, O house of Israel [33:1-20].  
*And after forty-nine verses:* And you, son of man, the children of your people who talk about you by the walls and in the doorways of the houses, and say one to another, each man to his neighbor, saying: Come, and let us hear what word goes forth from the Lord. And they come to you, as if a crowd were coming in; and they sit before you, my people, and they hear your words, and do not do them: for they turn them into a song of their own mouth, and their heart follows after their own greed. And you are to them like a musical song, which is sung with a sweet and pleasant sound; and they hear your words, and do not do them. And when that which was foretold comes to pass—for behold, it is coming—then they shall know that a prophet has been among them [33:30-33].

**232. 232.** [Ezek 34]. And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: Son of man, prophesy concerning the shepherds of Israel; prophesy, and say to the shepherds: Thus says the Lord God: Woe to the shepherds of Israel who fed themselves: are not the flocks fed by the shepherds? You ate the milk, and clothed yourselves with the wool, and you killed what was fat, but you did not feed my flock. What was weak you did not strengthen; and what was sick you did not heal; what was broken you did not bind up; and what was cast away you did not bring back; what was lost you did not seek: but with harshness and with power you ruled over them. And my sheep were scattered, because there was no shepherd: and they became the food of all the beasts of the field, and were scattered; and my flocks wandered over all the mountains, and over every high hill; and over all the face of the earth my flocks were scattered. And there was none who searched for them: there was none, I say, who searched for them. Therefore, you shepherds, hear the word of the Lord: As I live, says the Lord God, because my flocks have become a prey, and my sheep the food of all the beasts of the field, because there was no shepherd (for my shepherds did not seek my flock; but the shepherds fed themselves, and did not feed my flocks): therefore, you shepherds, hear the word of the Lord. Thus says the Lord God: Behold, I myself am against the shepherds, I will require my flock at their hands, and I will make them cease feeding the flock, nor shall the shepherds feed themselves any more; and I will deliver my flock from their mouth, and it shall be no more food for them [34:1-10].  
*And after thirty-four verses:* But as for you, my flocks, thus says the Lord God: Behold, I judge between cattle and cattle, between the ram and the he-goat. Was it not enough for you to feed on good pasture? Moreover, you trampled with your feet the remainder of your pastures; and when you drank the purest water, you fouled the rest with your feet: and my sheep fed on what your feet had trampled; and what your feet had fouled, this they drank. Therefore thus says the Lord God to them: Behold, I myself judge between the fat cattle and the lean; because you pushed with your sides and shoulders, and butted with your horns all the weak cattle, until they were scattered abroad [34:17-21].

**233. 233.** [Ezek 45]. *And after a little:* Thus says the Lord God: Let it be enough for you, O princes of Israel; leave off iniquity and robberies, and do judgment and justice, and separate your borders from my people, says the Lord God. You shall have a just balance and a just ephah; and blessed is the just man [45:9-10].

*These things we have set down from the books which the Jews also hold as canonical, in which we found some things of theirs that would suit this work. But we must not omit those books which, though it is agreed they were written before the coming of the Savior, the Jews did not receive, yet the Church of that same Savior receives them. Among these are two which by many are attributed to Solomon, on account of a certain likeness, as I think, of style. For that they are not Solomon's, the more learned do not doubt at all. Nor yet is it clear who is the author of the one that is called of Wisdom. But that other, which we call Ecclesiasticus, a certain Jesus wrote, surnamed Sirach, as is agreed among those who have read the whole of that book. From the book of Wisdom, then, these things have seemed to suit this work.*
