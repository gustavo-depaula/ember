# **297. 297.** [Matt 26]. *And somewhat later*: Keep watch and pray, so that you may not enter into temptation [26:41].: From the Gospel According to Mark

**298. 298.** [Mark 7]. Calling the crowd to him again, he told them: Listen to me, all of you, and understand. Nothing outside a person that enters him can defile him; rather, what comes out of a person is what makes him unclean. If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear. When he had entered the house away from the crowd, his disciples questioned him about the parable. He told them: Are you too so lacking in understanding? Do you not understand that anything entering a person from outside cannot make him unclean, because it does not enter his heart but his stomach, and passes out into the latrine, purifying all foods? He went on to say that what comes out of a person is what makes him unclean. For from within, from people's hearts, come evil thoughts; there proceed adulteries, fornications, murders, thefts, greed, wickedness, deceit, shamelessness, an evil eye, blasphemies, pride, and foolishness. All these evils proceed from within and make a person unclean [7:14–23].

**299. 299.** [Mark 8]. *And in another place:* Summoning the crowd together with his disciples, he told them: Anyone who wants to follow me must renounce himself, carry his cross, and follow me. For anyone who wants to preserve his life will lose it; anyone who loses his life on account of me and the Gospel will save it. What advantage will a man have if he gains the entire world and brings loss upon his soul? Or what will a man offer in exchange for his soul? For anyone who is ashamed of me and my words in this adulterous and sinful generation, the Son of man too will put him to shame in his Father's glory with his angels [8:34–38].

**300. 300.** [Mark 9]. *And elsewhere:* Taking a child, he placed him among them, and after embracing him told them: Anyone who welcomes one of these children in my name welcomes me; and anyone who welcomes me welcomes not me but the one who sent me [9:36–37].  
*And a little later:* For anyone who gives you a cup of water to drink in my name because you belong to Christ, I tell you truly, will not lose his reward. And for anyone who leads into sin one of these little ones who believe in me, it would be better if a donkey's millstone were placed around his neck and he were thrown into the sea [9:41–42].  
*And a little later:* Have salt within yourselves, and have peace among yourselves [9:50].

**301. 301.** [Mark 10]. *And a little later:* Pharisees approached and questioned him, testing him: Is a husband permitted to divorce his wife? He answered them: What did Moses command you? They said: Moses allowed a certificate of divorce to be written and her to be dismissed[^1]. Jesus answered them: Because of your hardness of heart he wrote you this commandment; but from creation's beginning God made them male and female. For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and cling to his wife, and the two will be in one flesh. Thus they are no longer two but one flesh[^2]. Therefore, what God has united, let no man separate. In the house his disciples questioned him again about this. He told them: Anyone who divorces his wife and marries another commits adultery against her; and if a wife divorces her husband and marries another, she commits adultery [10:2–12].  
*And four verses later:* Allow the small children to come to me, and do not prevent them, for God's kingdom belongs to such as these. I tell you truly: anyone who does not receive God's kingdom like a small child will not enter it. Embracing them and laying his hands on them, he blessed them. When he had gone out onto the road, someone ran ahead, knelt before him, and asked him: Good teacher, what shall I do to obtain eternal life? Jesus told him: Why do you call me good? No one is good except the one God. Do you know the commandments? Do not commit adultery; do not kill; do not steal; do not bear false witness; do not defraud; honor your father and mother[^3]. He answered him: Teacher, I have observed all these from my youth. Looking at him, Jesus loved him and told him: You lack one thing: go, sell whatever you have, and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; then come and follow me. Saddened by the saying, he went away grieving, for he had many possessions. Looking around, Jesus told his disciples: How difficult it will be for those who have wealth to enter God's kingdom! The disciples were astounded by his words. But Jesus answered them again: Little children, how difficult it is for those who trust in wealth to enter God's kingdom! It is easier for a camel to pass through a needle's eye than for a rich man to enter God's kingdom. They were even more astonished and said to one another: Who, then, can be saved? Looking at them, Jesus said: With human beings it is impossible, but not with God; for with God everything is possible. Peter began saying to him: See, we have left everything and followed you. Jesus replied: I tell you truly: there is no one who has left house, brothers, sisters, mother, father, children, or fields because of me and because of the Gospel who will not receive a hundred times as much now, in this time—houses, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and fields, with persecutions—and eternal life in the age to come. But many who are first will be last, and the last first [10:14–31].  
*And a little later:* You know that those who seem to rule the gentiles lord it over them, and their leaders exercise power over them. But it is not so among you. Anyone who wants to become greater will be your servant, and anyone who wants to be first among you will be everyone's slave. For the Son of man too came to serve, rather than to be served, and to give his life as a ransom for many [10:42–45].

**302. 302.** [Mark 11]. *And somewhat later:* Jesus answered them: Have faith in God. I tell you truly: if anyone tells this mountain, 'Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,' without wavering in his heart, but believes that whatever he says is to happen, it will happen for him. Therefore, I tell you: everything you ask for in prayer, believe that you will receive it, and it will come to you. And when you stand to pray, forgive anything you hold against anyone, so that your Father in heaven may also forgive you your sins. But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father in heaven forgive you your sins [11:22–26].

**303. 303.** [Mark 12]. *Again, somewhat later:* Jesus answered them: Give back, then, to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God [12:17].  
*And in another place:* One of the scribes approached, having heard their discussion and seen that he had answered them well, and asked him which was the first of all the commandments. Jesus answered him: The first of all the commandments is: Listen, Israel: the Lord your God is one God; and: You shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart, your whole soul, your whole mind, and your whole strength. This is the first commandment. The second resembles it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself[^4]. There is no other commandment greater than these. The scribe told him: Well said, teacher. You have truly said that God is one and there is no other besides him, and that loving him with the whole heart, the whole understanding, the whole soul, and the whole strength, and loving one's neighbor as oneself is greater than all burnt offerings and sacrifices. Seeing that he had answered wisely, Jesus told him: You are not far from God's kingdom. And no one dared question him any further [12:28–34].  
*And a little later:* Beware of the scribes, who want to walk about in long robes, to be greeted in the marketplace, to sit in the foremost seats in synagogues, and to have the foremost places at dinners. They devour widows' houses under the pretext of lengthy prayer; they receive a lengthier judgment. Sitting opposite the treasury, Jesus watched how the crowd put money into it; and many rich people put in large sums. But one poor widow came and put in two small coins, which amount to a quadrans. Calling his disciples, he told them: I tell you truly that this poor widow has put in more than all those who put money into the treasury. For they all contributed from their surplus, but she, from her poverty, put in everything she had, her entire livelihood [12:38–44].

**304. 304.** [Mark 13]. *And a little later:* And when they lead you away to hand you over, do not plan beforehand what you will say. Instead, say whatever is given to you in that hour, for it is not you speaking but the Holy Spirit [13:11].  
*And a little later:* And you will be hated by everyone because of my name. But whoever endures to the end will be saved [13:13].  
*And in another place:* Take care; keep watch in prayer, for you do not know when the time is. It is like a man who goes abroad, leaves his house, gives his slaves authority over each task, and commands the doorkeeper to keep watch. Keep watch, then, for you do not know when the master of the house may come: in the evening, at midnight, at cockcrow, or in the morning. Otherwise, coming suddenly, he may find you asleep. What I tell you, I tell everyone: Keep watch [13:33–37].
