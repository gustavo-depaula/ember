# From the Book of Habakkuk

**173. 173.** [Hab 1]. Judgment has been given against me, and contradiction more powerful. Because of this the law is torn apart, and judgment does not reach its end: because the wicked man prevails against the just, therefore perverse judgment goes forth [1:3-4].

**174. 174.** [Hab 2]. *And a little later:* But the just man shall live by his faith. And as wine deceives the one who drinks it, so shall it be with the proud man, and he shall not be honored [2:4-5].  
*And after a little:* What does the graven thing profit, for its maker has carved it a molten thing and a false image; for the maker has hoped in his own creation, to make mute idols? Woe to him who says to wood: Awake; Arise, to the silent stone. Can it teach? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all in its inward parts. But the Lord is in his holy temple: let all the earth keep silence before his face [2:18-20].
