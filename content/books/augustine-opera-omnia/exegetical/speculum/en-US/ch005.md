# *These things we found in Numbers, which we thought worth inspecting. Next we will consider Deuteronomy.*: From Deuteronomy

**14. 14.** [Deut 1]. There shall be no distinction of persons: you shall hear the small as the great, nor shall you accept anyone's person; because it is the judgment of God [1:17].

**15. 15.** [Deut 4]. *And after very many things are interposed, where he repeats the Decalogue:* Keep therefore your souls carefully. You saw no likeness in the day that the Lord spoke to you in Horeb out of the midst of the fire: lest perhaps, being deceived, you make for yourselves a graven likeness, or the image of male or female; the likeness of all the beasts that are upon the earth, or of the birds that fly under heaven; and of the creeping things that move on the earth, or of the fishes that abide in the waters under the earth; lest perhaps, lifting up your eyes to heaven, you see the sun and the moon, and all the stars of heaven, and being deceived by error you adore and worship the things which the Lord your God created for the service of all the nations that are under heaven [4:15-19].  
*And a little later:* Beware, *he says,* lest at any time you forget the covenant of the Lord your God, which he made with you; and make for yourself a graven likeness of those things which the Lord has forbidden to be made: because the Lord your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God [4:23-24].

**16. 16.** [Deut 5]. *And in another place:* You shall not have strange gods in my sight. You shall not make for yourself a graven thing, nor the likeness of any things that are in heaven above, and that are in the earth beneath, and that abide in the waters under the earth. You shall not adore them, nor worship them. For I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation, to those who hate me; and showing mercy unto many thousands to those who love me and keep my precepts. You shall not take the name of the Lord your God in vain: for he shall not be unpunished who takes his name upon a vain thing [5:7-11].  
*And after a little:* Honor, *he says,* your father and your mother, as the Lord your God has commanded you; that you may live a long time, and it may be well with you in the land which the Lord your God is about to give you. You shall not kill, neither shall you commit adultery, and you shall not steal, nor bear false witness against your neighbor. You shall not covet your neighbor's wife, not his house, not his field, not his manservant, not his maidservant, not his ox, not his ass, and all the things that are his [5:16-21].

**17. 17.** [Deut 6]. *And elsewhere in the same book:* You shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart, and with your whole soul, and with your whole strength [6:5].  
*And a little later:* When you have eaten and are filled, beware diligently lest you forget the Lord, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. You shall fear the Lord your God, and shall serve him only, and shall swear by his name. You shall not go after the strange gods of all the nations that are round about you: because the Lord your God is a jealous God in your midst; lest at any time the fury of the Lord your God be angry against you, and take you away from the face of the earth. You shall not tempt the Lord your God [6:12-16].

**18. 18.** [Deut 7]. *Likewise a little later, when he was speaking of the foreigners*: Neither shall you make marriages with them; you shall not give your daughter to his son, nor take his daughter for your son. For she will seduce your son not to follow me, and rather to serve strange gods: and the fury of the Lord will be angry, and will quickly destroy you. But rather you shall do these things to them: overthrow their altars, break their statues, cut down their groves, and burn their graven things with fire [7:2-5].

*And after a little while:* Their graven things you shall burn with fire. You shall not covet the silver and gold of which they are made, nor take any of it for yourself, lest you offend; because it is an abomination to the Lord your God. Neither shall you bring anything of the idol into your house; lest you become an accursed thing, as it also is: you shall detest it as filth, and abhor it as defilement and uncleanness; because it is an accursed thing [7:25-26].

**19. 19.** [Deut 8]. *And in another place:* Observe and beware lest at any time you forget the Lord your God, and neglect his commandments and judgments and ceremonies, which I command you this day: lest, after you have eaten and are filled, have built fair houses and dwelt in them; and have had herds and flocks of sheep, and abundance of silver and gold and of all things, your heart be lifted up, and you remember not the Lord your God [8:11-14].  
*And after a few verses:* At the last, *he says,* he had mercy on you; lest you say in your heart: My strength and the might of my hand have furnished me all these things: but that you may remember the Lord your God, that it was he who gave you strength [8:16-18].

**20. 20.** [Deut 11]. *And after a little:* That you may eat and be filled. Beware lest perhaps your heart be deceived, and you depart from the Lord God, and serve strange gods and adore them; and the Lord, being angry, shut up heaven [11:15-17].

**21. 21.** [Deut 12]. *And after a little:* Overthrow all the places in which the nations whom you shall possess worshiped their gods, upon the high mountains and the hills, and under every leafy tree. Destroy their altars, and break their statues; burn their groves with fire, and grind their idols to pieces: blot out their names from those places [12:2-3].*  
And after certain things are interposed, when he was speaking of the foreigners:* Beware lest you imitate them, after they have been overthrown at your coming in, and seek out their ceremonies, saying: As these nations worshiped their gods, so will I also worship. You shall not do likewise to the Lord your God. For all the abominations which the Lord abhors they have done to their gods, offering their sons and daughters, and burning them with fire. What I command you, this only do to the Lord; neither add anything, nor diminish [12:30-32].

**22. 22.** [Deut 13]. If there rise up in your midst a prophet, or one who says he has seen a dream, and foretell a sign and a wonder; and it come to pass which he has spoken, and he say to you: Let us go and follow strange gods, whom you know not, and let us serve them; do not listen to the words of that prophet or dreamer: because the Lord your God tests you, that it may be made plain whether you love him or not, with your whole heart and your whole soul. Follow the Lord your God, and fear him: keep his commandments, and hear his voice: him shall you serve, and to him shall you cleave. But that prophet or feigner of dreams shall be slain; because he has spoken to turn you away from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of bondage; that he might make you stray from the way which the Lord your God commanded you; and you shall take away the evil from your midst. If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son, or daughter, or the wife in your bosom, or the friend whom you love as your own soul, would persuade you, saying secretly: Let us go and follow strange gods, whom you and your fathers know not, of all the nations round about, that are near or far off, from the beginning to the ends of the earth; do not consent to him, nor listen, neither let your eye spare him to pity and conceal him: but you shall kill him at once; let your hand be first upon him, and after you let all the people lay their hand: he shall be stoned and killed, because he sought to draw you away from the Lord your God [13:1-10].

**23. 23.** [Deut 14]. *And after a little while:* You shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness for the dead: because you are a holy people to the Lord your God [14:1-2].

**24. 24.** [Deut 15]. *And in another place:* If one, *he says,* of your brothers who dwells within the gates of your city, in the land which the Lord your God is about to give you, come to poverty; you shall not harden your heart, nor draw back your hand: but you shall open it to the poor man, and lend him what you perceive him to need. Beware lest perhaps an impious thought creep upon you, and you say in your heart: The seventh year of remission is at hand; and you turn away your eyes from your poor brother, unwilling to lend him what he asks; lest he cry out against you to the Lord, and it be reckoned to you as sin: but you shall give to him; nor shall you do anything craftily in relieving his needs: that the Lord your God may bless you at all times, and in all things to which you put your hand. The poor shall not be wanting in the land of your habitation: therefore I command you to open your hand to your needy and poor brother, who dwell with you in the land [15:7-11].

**25. 25.** [Deut 16]. *And somewhat later*: You shall appoint judges and magistrates in all your gates, which the Lord your God shall give you, throughout your several tribes, that they may judge the people with just judgment: nor shall they turn aside to either part. You shall not accept persons, nor gifts; because gifts blind the eyes of the wise, and change the words of the just. You shall justly pursue what is just, that you may live and possess the land which the Lord your God shall give you [16:18-20].  
*And after a few words:* Neither shall you make and set up for yourself a statue, which the Lord your God hates [16:22].

**26. 26.** [Deut 17]. *Likewise after a few words:* When there shall be found among you, within one of your gates which the Lord your God shall give you, a man or a woman who do evil in the sight of the Lord your God, and transgress his covenant, so as to go and serve strange gods, and adore them, the sun and the moon and all the host of heaven, which I have not commanded; and this be told you, and hearing it you inquire diligently, and find it to be true, and that an abomination has been done in Israel: you shall bring forth the man and the woman who have perpetrated the most wicked thing, to the gates of your city, and they shall be stoned. He who is to be put to death shall perish on the word of two or three witnesses. Let no one be slain on a single witness testifying against him. The hand of the witnesses shall first kill him, and the hand of the rest of the people shall be put forth last; that you may take away the evil from your midst [17:2-7].  
*Likewise after a little:* But he who shall be proud, unwilling to obey the command of the priest who ministers at that time to the Lord your God, and the decree of the judge; that man shall die: and you shall take away the evil from Israel; and all the people hearing it shall fear, that no one thereafter swell with pride [17:12-13].

**27. 27.** [Deut 18]. *And after certain things are interposed, when he was admonishing concerning the foreigners:* Beware, *he says,* lest you wish to imitate the abominations of those nations. Neither let there be found in you one who purifies his son or daughter by leading them through fire, or one who consults soothsayers, and observes dreams and auguries; let there be no wizard, no enchanter, neither consult pythons nor diviners, nor seek the truth from the dead. For the Lord abhors all these things [18:9-12].

**28. 28.** [Deut 19]. *And after a little:* One witness shall not stand against anyone, whatever that sin and crime may be: but in the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word stand. If a lying witness stand against a man, accusing him of transgression; they shall both stand, whose cause it is, before the Lord in the sight of the priests and judges who shall be in those days: and when, searching most diligently, they shall find that the false witness has spoken a lie against his brother; they shall render to him as he thought to render to his brother; and you shall take away the evil from your midst: that the rest, hearing it, may have fear, and by no means dare to do such things. You shall not pity him; but shall exact life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot [19:15-21].

**29. 29.** [Deut 21]. *And elsewhere in the same book:* If a man beget a stubborn and unruly son, who will not hear the command of his father and mother, and being corrected refuses to obey; they shall lay hold of him, and bring him to the elders of that city, and to the gate of judgment, and shall say to them: This son of ours is unruly and stubborn, he has scorned to hear our admonitions, he gives himself to reveling and lust and feasting: the people of the city shall stone him, and he shall die; that you may take away the evil from your midst, and all Israel hearing it may be afraid [21:18-21].

**30. 30.** [Deut 22]. *And after a few words:* You shall not see your brother's ox or his sheep going astray, and pass by; but you shall bring them back to your brother: even if your brother is not near to you, nor do you know him, you shall lead them to your house, and they shall be with you until your brother seek them; and you shall restore them. You shall do likewise with the ass and the garment, and with everything of your brother's that is lost: if you find it, do not neglect it as though it were another's. If you see your brother's ass or ox fallen on the way, you shall not overlook it, but shall lift it up with him. A woman shall not be clothed with man's apparel, nor shall a man use a woman's garment: for he who does these things is abominable before the Lord [22:1-5].*  
Likewise after a few words:* When you build a new house, you shall make a parapet around the roof; lest blood be shed in your house, and you be guilty if another slip and fall headlong [22:8].  
*Likewise a little later:* If a man take a wife, and afterward hate her; and seek occasions to put her away, charging her with a most evil name, and say: I took this woman to wife, and going in to her I did not find her a virgin: her father and mother shall take her, and shall bring with them the tokens of her virginity to the elders of the city who are in the gate; and the father shall say: I gave my daughter to this man to wife, and because he hates her, he charges her with a most evil name, so as to say: I did not find your daughter a virgin: and behold, these are the tokens of my daughter's virginity. They shall spread out the garment before the elders of the city: and the elders of that city shall lay hold of the man, and shall beat him; condemning him besides in a hundred shekels of silver, which he shall give to the girl's father; because he has spread a most evil name upon a virgin of Israel: and he shall have her to wife, and shall not be able to put her away all the days of his life. But if what he charges be true, and virginity be not found in the girl; they shall cast her out of the doors of her father's house, and the men of her city shall stone her, and she shall die; because she has done a wicked thing in Israel, to commit fornication in her father's house: and you shall take away the evil from your midst. If a man lie with another's wife, they shall both die, that is, the adulterer and the adulteress; and you shall take away the evil from Israel. If a man have betrothed a virgin girl, and someone find her in the city, and lie with her; you shall bring forth both to the gate of that city, and they shall be stoned; the girl, because she did not cry out, being in the city; the man, because he humbled his neighbor's wife: and you shall take away the evil from your midst. But if a man find in the field a girl who is betrothed, and laying hold of her lie with her, he alone shall die: the girl shall suffer nothing, nor is she guilty of death: for as a robber rises up against his brother and slays his soul, so also has the girl suffered: she was alone in the field, she cried out, and there was none to free her. If a man find a virgin girl who has no betrothed, and laying hold of her lie with her, and the matter come to judgment; he who lay with her shall give to the girl's father a hundred shekels of silver, and shall have her to wife, because he humbled her: he shall not be able to put her away all the days of his life. A man shall not take his father's wife, nor uncover his father's covering [22:13-30].

**31. 31.** [Deut 23]. *And after a little while:* There shall be no harlot of the daughters of Israel, nor whoremonger of the sons of Israel [23:17].  
*And after a little while:* You shall not lend to your brother at usury money, nor grain, nor any other thing; but to another [23:19-20].  
*And after a few verses:* When you have vowed a vow to the Lord your God, you shall not delay to fulfill it; because the Lord your God will require it, and if you delay, it shall be reckoned to you as sin. If you are unwilling to promise, you shall be without sin: but what has once gone out of your lips you shall observe, and shall do as you promised to the Lord your God, and have spoken with your own will and with your mouth [23:21-23].

**32. 32.** [Deut 24]. *And a little later:* You shall not take in pledge the lower or the upper millstone, because he has pledged his life to you. If a man be found enticing his brother of the children of Israel, and selling him take a price, he shall be put to death: and you shall take away the evil from your midst [24:6-7].  
*And after a few verses:* When you ask back, *he says,* from your neighbor anything that he owes you, you shall not go into his house to take a pledge; but you shall stand outside, and he shall bring out to you what he has. But if he is poor, the pledge shall not lodge with you overnight; but you shall restore it to him at once before sunset, that sleeping in his garment he may bless you, and you may have justice before the Lord your God. You shall not deny the wages of your needy and poor brother, or of the stranger who dwells with you in the land and is within your gates: but the same day you shall pay him the price of his labor, before sunset; because he is poor, and with it he sustains his life: lest he cry out against you to the Lord, and it be reckoned to you as sin. Fathers shall not be put to death for their children, nor children for their fathers; but everyone shall die for his own sin [24:10-16].  
*And after a few verses:* When you reap the harvest in your field, and have forgotten and left a sheaf; you shall not return to take it: but you shall suffer the stranger, the orphan, and the widow to take it away; that the Lord your God may bless you in all the work of your hands. If you gather the fruits of the olives, whatever remains on the trees, you shall not return to gather; but you shall leave it for the stranger, the orphan, and the widow. If you harvest your vineyard, you shall not gather the clusters that remain; but they shall fall to the use of the stranger, the orphan, and the widow. Remember that you also served in Egypt, and therefore I command you to do this thing [24:19-22].

**33. 33.** [Deut 25]. If there be a cause between any, and they appeal to the judges; whom they shall perceive to be just, to him they shall give the palm of justice; whom they shall perceive to be wicked, they shall condemn of wickedness. But if they shall see that he who sinned is worthy of stripes; they shall throw him down, and shall cause him to be beaten before them. According to the measure of the sin shall be the measure of the stripes also: yet so that they exceed not the number of forty, lest your brother, foully torn, perish before your eyes [25:1-3].  
*And after a little while:* If men have a quarrel between them, and one begin to fight against the other, and the wife of the one, wishing to deliver her husband from the hand of the stronger, put forth her hand, and lay hold of his private parts; you shall cut off her hand, nor shall you be bent by any pity toward her. You shall not have in your bag diverse weights, a greater and a less; nor shall there be in your house a greater and a less bushel. You shall have a just and true weight; and the bushel shall be equal and true to you [25:11-15].

**34. 34.** [Deut 27]. *And after very many things:* Cursed be the man who makes a graven and molten thing, an abomination to the Lord, the work of craftsmen's hands, and sets it up in a hidden place: and all the people shall answer and say: Amen. Cursed be he who does not honor his father and mother: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be he who removes his neighbor's landmarks: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be he who makes the blind to wander out of the way: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be he who perverts the judgment of the stranger, the orphan, and the widow: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be everyone who lies with his father's wife, and uncovers the covering of his bed: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be he who lies with any beast: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be he who lies with his sister, the daughter of his father or of his mother: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be he who lies with his mother-in-law: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be he who secretly strikes his neighbor: and all the people shall say: Amen. Cursed be he who accepts gifts to strike the life of innocent blood: and all the people shall say: Amen [27:15-25].

*Thus far we have set down what seemed worth setting down from the books of Moses. But in those that follow and are called: Joshua, Judges, Kings, Chronicles, there is read rather a history of events than precepts of living. Yet these very few things from the book of Joshua I did not think should be passed over in this work.*
