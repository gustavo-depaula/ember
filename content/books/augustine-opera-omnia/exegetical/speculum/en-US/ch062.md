# From the Second Letter of the Same Apostle (2)

**424. 424.** [2 John]. I write to you not as giving a new commandment, but the one we had from the beginning: that we love one another. This is love: that we walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, that you walk in it as you heard from the beginning [5–6].  
*And a few verses later:* Everyone who departs and does not remain in Christ's teaching does not have God. The one who remains in the teaching has both the Son and the Father. If anyone comes to you without bringing this teaching, do not receive him into your house or say a greeting to him. For the one who greets him shares in his evil works [9–11].
