# **149. 149.** [Song 2]. *It is also read in the same Song:* Set love in order within me [2:4].: **150. 150.** [Song 6]. *Christ himself also says there:* You are beautiful, my love, sweet and comely as Jerusalem [6:3].: **151. 151.** [Song 7]. *And elsewhere:* How beautiful you are, and how comely, my dearest, in delights! [7:6].

**152. 152.** [Song 8]. *And in another place:* Set me as a seal upon your heart, as a seal upon your arm: for love is strong as death, jealousy hard as hell [8:6].  
*And after one verse:* Many waters could not extinguish love, nor shall the floods overwhelm it. If a man should give all the substance of his house for love, they shall despise it as nothing [8:7].
