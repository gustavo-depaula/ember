# From the Book of Job

**153. 153.** [Job 24]. Those who knew him do not know his days. Some have removed landmarks, plundered flocks, and carried them off. They have driven away the ass of orphans, and taken the widow's ox for a pledge [24:1-3].  
*And after five verses:* They reap a field not their own; and they gather the vintage of his vineyard whom they have oppressed by force. They send men away naked, taking their garments, who have no covering in the cold [24:6-7].  
*And after three verses:* They have done violence to the orphan in plundering, and have stripped the common poor. From the naked and those who go without clothing and from the hungry they have taken the ears of grain [24:9-10].  
*And after two verses:* Out of the cities they made men groan; and the soul of the wounded cried out; and God does not suffer it to go unavenged. They were rebels against the light: they knew not his ways, nor turned back by his paths [24:12-13].  
*And after twenty-one verses:* Let him be broken like an unfruitful tree. For he fed the barren woman, and her who does not bear; and did no good to the widow. He brought down the strong by his strength [24:20-22].

**154. 154.** [Job 29]. *And somewhat later*: The ear that heard me blessed me; and the eye that saw bore witness to me, because I had delivered the poor man who cried out, and the orphan who had no helper. The blessing of him who was about to perish came upon me, and I comforted the heart of the widow. I clothed myself with justice, and arrayed myself in my judgment as in a robe and a diadem. I was an eye to the blind, and a foot to the lame. I was a father to the poor; and the cause I did not know, I diligently searched out. I broke the jaws of the wicked man, and from his teeth I took away the prey [29:11-17].

**155. 155.** [Job 31]. *And somewhat later:* If I have walked in vanity, and my foot has hastened in deceit; let him weigh me in a just balance, and let God know my simplicity. If my step has turned aside from the way; and if my eye has followed my heart, and a blemish has cleaved to my hands [31:5-7].  
*And after two verses:* If my heart has been deceived over a woman; and if I have lain in wait at my friend's door [31:9].  
*And after two verses:* For this is a wickedness, and a most grievous iniquity. It is a fire devouring even unto destruction, and rooting out all that springs up. If I have despised to submit to judgment with my manservant and my maidservant, when they had a dispute against me [31:11-13].  
*And after six verses:* If I have denied the poor what they wished, and made the eyes of the widow wait. If I have eaten my morsel alone, and the orphan has not eaten of it. For from my infancy mercy grew up with me, and it came forth with me from my mother's womb. If I have despised one perishing because he had no garment, and the poor man without covering. If his sides have not blessed me, and he was warmed with the fleeces of my sheep. If I have lifted up my hand against the orphan, even when I saw myself superior in the gates [31:16-21].  
*And after five verses:* If I have thought gold my strength, and said to fine gold: My confidence. If I have rejoiced over my many riches, and because my hand had gotten very much [31:24-25].  
*And after six verses:* If I have rejoiced at the ruin of him who hated me, and exulted that evil had found him [31:29].  
*And after four verses:* The stranger did not stay outside; my door was open to the traveler. If I have hidden my sin as a man, and concealed my iniquity in my bosom. If I was afraid at a very great multitude, and the contempt of my kindred terrified me; and did I not rather keep silence, nor go out of doors. Who will grant me a hearer, that the Almighty may hear my desire, and that he who judges may himself write the book; that I may carry it on my shoulder, and put it about me like a crown? At every step of mine I will declare it, and offer it as to a prince. If my land has cried out against me, and its furrows weep with it. If I have eaten its fruits without money, and afflicted the soul of the farmers [31:32-39].
