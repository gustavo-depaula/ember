# From the Second Letter of the Same Apostle

**411. 411.** [2 Pet 1]. Through him he gave us the greatest and most precious promises, so that through these you may become sharers in the divine nature, fleeing the corruption of the desire present in the world. Bring every care to bear and supply virtue in your faith, knowledge in virtue, abstinence in knowledge, patience in abstinence, piety in patience, brotherly love in piety, and charity in brotherly love. For if these things are present and abundant in you, they will establish you neither empty nor fruitless in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ. Anyone lacking them is blind, feeling his way with his hand, having forgotten the purification of his old offenses. Therefore, brothers, strive all the more to make your calling and election certain through good works. For by doing these things you will never sin [1:4–10].

**412. 412.** [2 Pet 2]. *And a little later:* Just so, there will be lying teachers among you who introduce destructive sects and deny the Lord who bought them, bringing swift destruction upon themselves. Many will follow their extravagances, and through them the way of truth will be blasphemed. In greed they will trade upon you with fabricated words. Their judgment has not ceased for a long time, and their destruction does not slumber [2:1–3].  
*And in another place:* The Lord knows how to rescue the pious from temptation and reserve the unjust for torment on the day of judgment, especially those who walk after the flesh in the desire for impurity and despise dominion. Bold and self-pleasing, they do not fear to introduce sects, blaspheming. Whereas angels, although greater in strength and power, do not bring an execrable judgment against themselves, these people, like irrational beasts born for capture and destruction, blaspheme what they do not know and will also perish in their corruption, receiving the reward of injustice. They consider daytime delights a pleasure; defilements and stains, overflowing with delights, they revel with you in their feasts. Their eyes are full of adultery and ceaseless sin; they entice unstable souls, having a heart trained in greed, children of a curse. Abandoning the straight way, they have gone astray [2:9–15].  
*And a few verses later:* For speaking the pride of vanity, they entice through fleshly desires of extravagance those barely escaping from people living in error, promising them freedom while themselves being slaves of corruption. For a person is a slave of whatever has overcome him. If, after fleeing the world's defilements through knowledge of our Lord and savior Jesus Christ, they are again entangled and overcome by them, their latter condition becomes worse than their former. For it was better for them not to know the way of righteousness than after knowing it to turn back from the holy commandment handed on to them. For what is said in the true proverb has happened to them: *A dog returned to its own vomit*; and: *A washed sow in the wallowing place of mud*[^1] [2:18–22].

**413. 413.** [2 Pet 3]. *And somewhat later:* Since all these things are to be dissolved, what sort of people ought you to be in holy conduct and acts of piety, awaiting and hastening toward the coming of God's day? [3:11–12].  
*And a little later:* Beloved, awaiting these things, strive to be found spotless and inviolate in peace, and regard our Lord's forbearance as salvation [3:14–15].  
*And in another place:* Therefore, brothers, knowing beforehand, take care lest, carried away by the error of the foolish, you fall from your own firmness. Grow instead in grace and in the knowledge of our Lord and savior Jesus Christ. To him be glory both now and unto the day of eternity. Amen [3:17–18].

[^1]: Prov 26:11
