# From Numbers

**12. 12.** [Num 27]. When a man dies without a son, his inheritance shall pass to his daughter. If he has no daughter, his brothers shall be his successors. But if he has no brothers either, you shall give the inheritance to his father's brothers. And if he has no uncles either, the inheritance shall be given to those who are next of kin to him: and this shall be holy to the children of Israel by a perpetual law, as the Lord commanded Moses [27:8-11].

**13. 13.** [Num 35]. *And after many things.* No one shall be condemned on the testimony of a single witness. You shall not accept a price from one who is guilty of blood [35:30-31].
