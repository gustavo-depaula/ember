# On the Place of God

**20.** God is not somewhere. For what is somewhere is contained by place; what is contained by place is a body; but God is not a body. He is not, therefore, somewhere. And yet, because he exists and is not in a place, all things are in him rather than he somewhere—and yet not in him in such a way that he is a place. For place is in the space that is occupied by the length, breadth, and height of a body; and God is no such thing. All things, therefore, are in him, and he is not a place.

Yet the temple of God is, by a misuse of the word, called the place of God—not because he is contained by it, but because he is present to it. And this is understood as nothing better than a clean soul.
