# On Evil

**6**. Everything that is is either corporeal or incorporeal; the corporeal is contained by a sensible form, the incorporeal by an intelligible form. Everything that is, therefore, is not without some form. But where there is some form, there is necessarily some measure; and measure is something good. The highest evil, then, has no measure; for it lacks all good. It does not, therefore, exist, because it is contained by no form; and this whole name of "evil" was found from the privation of form.
