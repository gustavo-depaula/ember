# Why Did the Children of Israel Offer Visibly — the Victims of Animals?

**49.** Because there are also spiritual sacred rites, whose images the carnal people had to celebrate, so that there might be a prefiguration of the new people by the servitude of the old. The difference of these two peoples may be observed even in each one of us, since each one, from his mother's womb, must of necessity act the old man, until he comes to the age of youth, where it is now no longer necessary to be wise in a carnal way, but he can be converted by will to spiritual things and be regenerated within.

What, therefore, happens in one rightly educated man by the order of nature and by instruction, this it is most beautiful that, in proportion, should be done and carried out in the whole human race through divine providence.
