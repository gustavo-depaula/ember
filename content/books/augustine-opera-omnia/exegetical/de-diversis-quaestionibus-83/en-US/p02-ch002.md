# On Free Choice

**2**. Everything that is made cannot be equal to that by which it is made. Otherwise justice, which ought to render to each its own, would necessarily be taken away from things.

When God made man, then, although he made him very good, he nevertheless did not make what he himself was. But a man who is good by will is better than one who is good by necessity. A free will, therefore, had to be given to man.
