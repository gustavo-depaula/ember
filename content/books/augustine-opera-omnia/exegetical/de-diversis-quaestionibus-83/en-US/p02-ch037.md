# On Him Who Is Ever Born

**37.** Better is he who is ever born than he who is ever being born, because he who is ever being born is not yet born, and is never born nor will have been born, if he is ever being born.

For to be born is one thing, to have been born another. And through this, never a son, if never born. But the Son, because born, is also ever the Son; therefore ever born.
