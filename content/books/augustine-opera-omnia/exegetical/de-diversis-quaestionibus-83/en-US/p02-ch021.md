# Whether God Is Not the Author of Evil

**21.** Whoever is the author of all the things that are, and to whose goodness it belongs only that everything which exists should exist, can in no way have not-being pertain to him. But everything that fails, fails from being and tends toward not-being. To exist and to fail in nothing is good, and to fail is evil.

But he to whom not-being does not pertain is not the cause of failing—that is, of tending toward not-being—because, so to speak, he is the cause of being. He is, therefore, the cause of good only, and for that reason he is himself the highest good. Wherefore he who is the author of all the things that are is not the author of evil, because insofar as they are, to that extent they are good.
