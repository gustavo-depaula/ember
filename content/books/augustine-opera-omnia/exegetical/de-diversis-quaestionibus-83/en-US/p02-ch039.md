# On Nourishment

**39.** What is it that receives a thing which it changes, as a living being receives food? What is it that is received and changed, as that same food? What is it that is received and not changed, as light by the eyes and sound by the ears? But these the soul receives through the body.

But what is it that the soul receives through itself and changes into itself, as another soul, which by receiving into the friendship of itself it makes like itself?

And what is it that it receives through itself and does not change, as truth? Wherefore it must be known both what was said to Peter, *kill and eat*[^1]; and what is said in the Gospel, *and the life was the light of men*[^2].

[^1]: Acts 10:13.
[^2]: John 1:4.
