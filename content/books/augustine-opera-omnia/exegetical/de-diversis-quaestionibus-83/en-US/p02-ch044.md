# Why Did the Lord Jesus Christ Come So Long Afterward, — and Not at the Beginning of Man's Sin?

**44.** Because every beautiful thing is from the highest beauty, which is God, but temporal beauty is carried out by things departing and succeeding. Now each several age, from infancy to old age, has its own comeliness in each several man. As, therefore, one who would wish only the age of youth to exist in a man subject to time is absurd—for he would begrudge the other beauties which in the other ages carry out their turns and order—so one who longs for a single age in the whole human race itself is absurd. For the race too, as one man, passes through its ages; nor was it fitting that a Teacher come from God, by imitation of whom one might be formed in the best morals, except at the time of youth.

To this bears what the Apostle says, that under the Law the little ones were guarded as under a guardian[^1], until he should come for whom it was being kept, who had been promised through the prophets.

For it is one thing that divine providence does, as it were privately, with individuals, another that it provides, as it were publicly, for the whole race. For even whatever individuals have arrived at sure wisdom have been enlightened only by that same truth, at the opportune time of their several ages. And it was from this truth that, in order that the people might become wise, a man was taken on at the opportune age of the human race itself.

[^1]: See Gal 3:23-24.
