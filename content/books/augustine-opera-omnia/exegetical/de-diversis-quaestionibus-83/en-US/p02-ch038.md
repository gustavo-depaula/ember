# On the Conformation of the Soul

**38.** Since nature is one thing, instruction another, and practice another, and these are understood in one soul with no diversity of substance;

likewise, since natural talent is one thing, virtue another, tranquillity another, similarly of one and the same substance; and since the soul is of a different substance than God, although made by him, while God himself is that most sacred Trinity, known to many by word but to few in reality—most diligently must that be investigated which the Lord Jesus says: *no one comes to me unless the Father has drawn him*[^1]; and, *no one comes to the Father except through me*[^2]; and, *he will lead you into all truth*[^3].

[^1]: John 6:44.
[^2]: John 14:6.
[^3]: John 16:13.
