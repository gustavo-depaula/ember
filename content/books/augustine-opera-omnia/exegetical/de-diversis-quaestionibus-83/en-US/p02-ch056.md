# On the Forty-six Years — of the Building of the Temple

**56.** Six, nine, twelve, eighteen—these come to forty-five in one. Add, then, the one itself, and they come to forty-six; this multiplied by six comes to two hundred seventy-six. Now human conception is said to proceed and be completed in such a way that in the first six days it has, as it were, the likeness of milk; in the following nine days it is turned into blood; then in twelve days it is made solid; in the remaining eighteen it is formed up to the perfect lineaments of all the members; and from then, for the remaining time up to the time of birth, it grows in size.

Forty-five days, then, with one added, which signifies the sum—because six and nine and twelve and eighteen brought into one make forty-five—with one added, then, as was said, make forty-six. And when these have been multiplied by that same number six, which holds the head of this arrangement, they make two hundred seventy-six, that is, nine months and six days, which are reckoned from the eighth day before the Kalends of April, on which day the Lord is believed to have been conceived, because on the same day he also suffered, up to the eighth day before the Kalends of January, on which day he was born. Not absurdly, then, is the Temple said to have been built in forty-six years, which signified his body[^1], so that as many years as there were in the building of the Temple, so many days there were in the perfecting of the Lord's body.

[^1]: See John 2:20-21.
