# Since the Nature of Souls Is One, Whence — Come the Diverse Wills of Human Beings?

**40.** From diverse things seen comes a diverse appetite of souls; from a diverse appetite, a diverse success in attaining; from a diverse success, a diverse habit; from a diverse habit, a diverse will.

But the order of things makes the diverse things seen—hidden, indeed, yet sure under divine providence. It is not, therefore, to be thought, on this account, that the natures of souls are diverse because their wills are diverse, since even the will of one soul is varied according to the diversity of times; seeing that at one time it desires to be rich, at another time, riches being despised, it longs to be wise. And in the very appetite for temporal things, at one time trade pleases one man, at another time soldiering.
