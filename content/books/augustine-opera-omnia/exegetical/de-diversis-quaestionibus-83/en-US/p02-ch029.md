# Whether There Is Anything Above or Below in the Universe

**29.** *Mind the things that are above*[^1]. We are commanded to mind the things that are above—namely, spiritual things, which are to be understood as being above not by the places and parts of this world but by the merit of their own excellence—lest we fix our mind in a part of this world, of which as a whole we ought to divest ourselves. Above and below, however, exist in its parts.

For the universe itself does not have an above and below; for it is corporeal, since everything visible is corporeal, but in the whole body there is nothing above and below. For although motion that is called straight—that is, that is not circular—seems to take place in six directions: forward and backward, to the right and to the left, upward and downward, there is no reason at all why the whole body should have no before and behind and no right and left, but should have an above and below. But in considering this, people are deceived because it is difficult to withstand the senses and habit. For the turning of the body that occurs if someone wishes to be moved head downward is not as easy for us as is the turning from right to left or from front to back. Wherefore, words being set aside, one must busy oneself with the matter inwardly in the mind, so as to be able to discern this.

[^1]: Col 3:2.
