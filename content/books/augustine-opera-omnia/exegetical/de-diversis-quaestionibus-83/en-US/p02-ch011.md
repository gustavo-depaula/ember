# Why Christ Was Born of a Woman

**11.** When God sets free, he does not set free some part, but sets free the whole that happens to be in danger. The Wisdom and Power of God, therefore, who is called the only-begotten Son, by taking on a man indicated the liberation of man.

But the liberation of the human being had to appear in both sexes. Therefore, because it was fitting to take on a man, which is the more honorable sex, it followed that the liberation of the female sex should appear from this, that that man was born of a woman.
