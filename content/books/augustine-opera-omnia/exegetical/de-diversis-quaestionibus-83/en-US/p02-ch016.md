# On the Son of God

**16.** God is the cause of all things that are; but what is the cause of all things is also the cause of his own Wisdom; nor was God ever without Wisdom. He is, therefore, the everlasting cause of his everlasting wisdom; nor is he prior in time to his wisdom. Then, if it belongs to God to be everlastingly a Father, and he was never at any time not a Father, he was never without the Son.
