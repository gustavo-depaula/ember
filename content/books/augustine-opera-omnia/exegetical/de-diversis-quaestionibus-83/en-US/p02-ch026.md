# On the Difference of Sins

**26.** Some sins are of weakness, others of inexperience, others of malice. Weakness is contrary to virtue, inexperience is contrary to wisdom, malice is contrary to goodness. Whoever, therefore, knows what the virtue and wisdom of God is[^1] can judge which sins are venial. And whoever knows what the goodness of God is can judge for which sins a fixed punishment is owed, both here and in the world to come. When these things have been well treated, it can be judged with probability who are not to be compelled to a mournful and tearful penance, although they confess their sins, and for whom no salvation at all is to be hoped, unless they have offered to God the sacrifice of a contrite spirit through penance.

[^1]: See 1 Cor 1:24.
