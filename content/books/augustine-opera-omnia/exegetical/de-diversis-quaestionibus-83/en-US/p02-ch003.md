# Whether, with God as Author, a Human Being Is Made Worse

**3**. With no wise man as its author does a human being become worse. For this is no small fault; indeed it is so great that it cannot fall upon any wise man whatever. But God is more excellent than every wise man. Much less, therefore, does a human being become worse with God as author; for the will of God is far more excellent than the will of a wise man. And when he is said to be the author, it is said that he wills it. There is, then, a defect of the will whereby a human being is made worse. And if this defect is far removed from the will of God, as reason teaches, where it lies must be sought.
