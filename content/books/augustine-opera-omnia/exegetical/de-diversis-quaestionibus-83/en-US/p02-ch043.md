# Why Did the Son of God Appear in a Man — and the Holy Spirit in a Dove?

**43.** Because the one came to show men an example of living, the other appeared to signify the gift itself by which, through living well, one arrives there. But both were done visibly for the sake of the carnal, who are to be carried over, by the steps of the sacraments, from the things that are seen with bodily eyes to the things that are understood by the mind. For words too sound and pass away; yet the things signified by words, when something divine and eternal is set forth in speaking, do not likewise pass away.
