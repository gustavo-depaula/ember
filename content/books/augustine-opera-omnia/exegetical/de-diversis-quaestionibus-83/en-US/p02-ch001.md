# Whether the Soul Is from Itself

**1**. Everything true is true by truth; and every soul is a soul by that whereby it is a true soul. Every soul, therefore, has it from truth that it is a soul at all.

But the soul is one thing, truth another. For truth never undergoes falsity, whereas the soul is often deceived. Therefore, since the soul is by truth, it is not from itself. But truth is God: God, therefore, the soul has as the author of its being a soul.
