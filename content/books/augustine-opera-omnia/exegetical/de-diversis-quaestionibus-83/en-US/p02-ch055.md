# On What Is Written, — There Are Sixty Queens and Eighty Concubines — and Young Maidens without Number

**55.** The number ten can signify the knowledge of the universe. If this is referred to inner and intelligible things, which are signified by the number six, it becomes, as it were, ten times six, which is sixty; if to earthly and corruptible things, which can be signified by the number eight, it becomes ten times eight, which is eighty. The queens, then, are souls reigning over intelligible and spiritual things;

the concubines are those who receive the wage of earthly things, of whom it is said, *they have received their wage*[^1]; the *young maidens without number* are those whose knowledge is not fixed and who can be endangered by diverse doctrines, so that "number," as was said, signifies the sure and undoubted confirmation of knowledge.

[^1]: Matt 6:2.
