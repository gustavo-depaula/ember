# Whether We Can Ever See Our Own Thoughts

**47.** It is usually asked how, after the resurrection and the change of the body which is promised to the saints, we can see our own thoughts. A conjecture, then, must be drawn from that part of our body which has more light, since it must be believed that the angelic bodies, such as we hope to have, are most luminous and ethereal. If, then, many motions of our mind are now recognized in the eyes, it is probable that no motion of the mind will lie hidden when the whole body has become ethereal, in comparison with which these eyes are flesh.
