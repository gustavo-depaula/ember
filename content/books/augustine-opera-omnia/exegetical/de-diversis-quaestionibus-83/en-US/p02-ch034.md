# Whether Nothing Other Than to Be Free from Fear Is to Be Loved

**34.** If it is a vice not to fear, it is not to be loved. But no one most happy fears, and no one most happy is in a vice. Not to fear, then, is not a vice.

But boldness is a vice. It is not, then, the case that whoever does not fear is bold, although everyone who is bold does not fear. Likewise, every corpse does not fear.

Wherefore, since not to fear is common to the most happy, to the bold, and to the corpse—but the most happy one has it through tranquillity of mind, the bold through rashness, the corpse because it lacks all sensation—not to fear is neither to be left unloved, since we wish to be happy, nor to be loved alone, since we do not wish to be bold or lifeless.
