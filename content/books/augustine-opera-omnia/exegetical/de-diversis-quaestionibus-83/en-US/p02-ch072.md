# On Eternal Times

**72.** It can be asked how it was said by the apostle Paul: *before eternal times*[^1]. For if they are times, how are they eternal? Or if they are eternal, how are they times? Unless perhaps he wished "before all times" to be understood, because if he had said *before times* and had not added *eternal*, it could be taken as "before certain times," which would have other times before them. But he preferred to say *eternal* rather than "all," perhaps for this reason: that time did not begin from time. Or did he by *eternal times* signify an age, between which and time there is this difference: that the former is stable, but time is changeable?

[^1]: Tit 1:2.
