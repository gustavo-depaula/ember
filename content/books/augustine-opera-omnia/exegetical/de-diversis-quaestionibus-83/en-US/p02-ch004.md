# What the Cause Is That a Human Being Is Made Worse

**4**. The cause that a human being is made worse is either in himself, or in some other, or in nothing. If in nothing, there is no cause. Or if "in nothing" is taken in the sense that man was made from nothing, or from those things that were made from nothing, then again the cause will be in himself, since the nothing is, as it were, his matter.

If in some other, it must be asked whether it is in God, or in some other man, or in that which is neither God nor man. But not in God; for God is the cause of good things.

If, then, in a man, it is either by force or by persuasion. But by force in no way, lest he be stronger than God. For God made man so very good that, if he willed to remain very good, he would be hindered by nothing standing in his way.

But if we grant that a man is corrupted by the persuasion of another man, it will again have to be asked by whom the persuader himself was corrupted; for one who persuades to such things cannot fail to be depraved.

There remains I know not what, which is neither God nor man. But this too, whatever it is, either brought force to bear or persuaded. As to force, the same answer is given as above. As to persuasion, whatever it is, since persuasion does not compel the unwilling, the cause of his corruption returns to the will of the man himself, whether he was corrupted with someone persuading or with no one persuading.
