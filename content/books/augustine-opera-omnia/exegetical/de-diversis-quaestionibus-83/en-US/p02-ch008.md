# Whether the Soul Is Moved of Itself

**8.** That the soul is moved of itself, he perceives who perceives that there is a will in him. For if we will, no other wills on our behalf.

And this motion of the soul is spontaneous; for this was granted to it by God. Yet this motion is not from place to place, as that of a body is; for to be moved with respect to place is proper to a body.

And though the soul, by the will—that is, by that motion which is not local—nevertheless moves its own body with respect to place, it is not on that account shown that the soul itself is also moved with respect to place. Just as we see something moved by a hinge through a great extent of space, and yet the hinge itself is not moved with respect to place.
