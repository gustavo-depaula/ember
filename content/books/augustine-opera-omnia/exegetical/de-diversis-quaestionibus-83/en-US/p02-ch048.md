# On Things to Be Believed

**48.** There are three kinds of believable things. Some are always believed and never understood, such as is all history, running through temporal and human deeds;

others are understood as soon as they are believed, such as are all human reasonings, whether about numbers or about any disciplines;

a third, which are first believed and afterward understood, such as are those things about divine matters which cannot be understood except by those who are clean of heart, which comes about when the precepts received concerning living well are kept.
