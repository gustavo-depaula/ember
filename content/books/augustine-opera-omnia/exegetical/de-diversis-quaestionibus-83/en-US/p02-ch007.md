# What in a Living Being Is Properly Called Soul

**7.** "Soul" is sometimes spoken of so as to be understood together with the mind, as when we say that man consists of soul and body; sometimes so as to be spoken of apart from the mind. But when it is spoken of apart from the mind, it is understood from those operations which we have in common with the beasts. For the beasts lack reason, which is proper to the mind.
