# On the Word

***63.** In the beginning was the Word*[^1]*.* What in Greek is called logos means in Latin both "reason" and "word." But in this passage we do better to interpret it as "word," so that not only the relation to the Father is signified, but also the operative power directed to those things that were made through the Word. Reason, however, even if nothing is made through it, is rightly called reason.

[^1]: John 1:1.
