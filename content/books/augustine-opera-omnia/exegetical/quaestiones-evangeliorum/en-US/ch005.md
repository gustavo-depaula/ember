# Titles of the Questions of Book Two

**1.** That Zechariah hears from the angel: *your prayer has been heard.*

**2.** That he teaches the crowds from the boat.

**3.** That he says to the cleansed leper: *go, show yourself to the priest.*

**4.** On the paralytic who was let down to him through the roof.

**5.** How Joseph could have had two fathers.

**6.** On the seventy-seven generations.

**7.** On the one who had a withered right hand.

**8.** On his saying: *good measure, pressed down and shaken together and running over, they will give into your bosom.*

**9.** On his saying: *can a blind man lead a blind man?*

**10.** On the one who dug deep and laid the foundation upon the rock.

**11.** On the children sitting in the marketplace and calling to one another.

**12.** On his saying: *no one lights a lamp and covers it with a vessel or puts it under a bed.*

**13.** On the one in whom there was a legion of demons.

**14.** On the seventy-two disciples.

**15.** On his saying: *if the light that is in you is darkness, how great is that darkness?*

**16.** On his saying: *now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and the dish.*

**17.** On the finger of God.

**18.** On the fasting of the bridegroom's sons.

**19.** On the one who, going down from Jerusalem to Jericho, fell among robbers.

**20.** That Martha received him into her house, where Mary sat at his feet.

**21.** On the one who at midnight asks three loaves from a friend.

**22.** On the bread and the fish and the egg.

**23.** On his saying: *you have taken away the key of knowledge.*

**24.** On his saying: *the soul is more than food.*

**25.** On his saying: *let your loins be girded and your lamps burning.*

**26.** On the measure of wheat that the faithful steward gives to the household.

**27.** On his saying: *when you see a cloud rising in the west.*

**28.** On his saying about the stature of the body, to which they cannot add anything.

**29.** On his saying: *do not be lifted up on high.*

**30.** On those invited to the supper.

**31.** On the cost of building a tower and the king who has twenty thousand.

**32.** On the salt that has lost its savor and the lost sheep.

**33.** On the two sons, of whom the younger set out into a far country.

**34.** On his saying: *make for yourselves friends from the mammon of iniquity.*

**35.** On his saying: *if you have not been faithful in what is another's.*

**36.** On his saying: *no one can serve two masters.*

**37.** On his saying: *the kingdom of heaven suffers violence.*

**38.** On the rich man at whose gate Lazarus lay covered with sores.

**39.** That the disciples said: *Lord, increase our faith.*

**40.** On the ten lepers.

**41.** On the one who is on the roof and his goods are in the house.

**42.** On the one who is in the field, that he not turn back.

**43.** On Lot's wife.

**44.** On the two in a bed and the two women grinding and the two in a field.

**45.** On the unjust judge whom the widow kept entreating.

**46.** On the nobleman who went into a far country to receive a kingdom for himself.

**47.** On the camel passing through the eye of a needle.

**48.** On the blind man given sight as he drew near to Jericho.

**49.** On the life of the saints when they have risen.

**50.** On his saying: *pray that you do not enter into temptation.*

**51.** On what is written of the Lord: *he made as though to go farther.*
