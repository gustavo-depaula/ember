# Prologue

This work was not written in such a way as if I had undertaken to expound the Gospel in order, but according to the choice and occasion of the one consulting me, with whom it was being read, whenever something seemed obscure to him. And for that reason many things, and perhaps more obscure ones, have been passed over, because the one who inquired about what he did not yet know already knew them, and did not wish his haste to be held back over those things which he had already received in such a way that, by the very constancy of hearing and discussing them, he had committed them firmly and stably to memory.

Some things here are also found expounded not in the same order in which they are narrated in the Gospel, since certain things, deferred in haste, were taken up again when time was given and were written in the place that followed, left vacant in the order of the matters already expounded. After I discovered this, lest anyone, perhaps seeking to read something in this work that had stirred him in the Gospel and prompted him to inquire, should be put off by weariness at the disordered arrangement (since indeed I came to know that things dictated piecemeal as they could be had been gathered and woven into one), I arranged, by prefixing titles in numerical order, that each one might easily find what he needed.
