# Titles of the Questions of Book One

- **1.** On his saying: *no one knows the Son except the Father.*

- **2.** That the Lord's disciples began to pluck ears of grain and eat them.

- **3.** On the smoking flax.

- **4.** On the blind and mute man.

- **5.** On his saying: *and if I cast out demons by Beelzebul.*

- **6.** On his saying: *brood of vipers.*

- **7.** On his saying: *for as Jonah was in the belly of the whale three days and three nights.*

- **8.** On his saying: *when the unclean spirit has gone out of a man.*

- **9.** On the hundredfold, sixtyfold, and thirtyfold fruit.

- **10.** On his saying: *gather the weeds first.*

- **11.** On the grain of mustard seed, which becomes greater than all the herbs.

- **12.** On the leaven that the woman took and hid in three measures of flour.

- **13.** On the treasure hidden in a field.

- **14.** That the Jews said: *where did this man get this wisdom and these mighty works?*

- **15.** That he walked on the sea toward the disciples.

- **16.** What this means: *whatever gift comes from me will profit you.*

- **17.** On his saying: *every plant that my Father has not planted will be rooted up.*

- **18.** On the centurion's servant and the daughter of the Canaanite woman.

- **19.** What the mute and the blind and the deaf and the lame who were brought to the Lord to be healed signify.

- **20.** On his saying: *when it is evening, you say: 'it will be fair weather, for the sky is red.'*

- **21.** On his saying: *Elijah indeed is to come and will restore all things.*

- **22.** On the one who often fell into the fire and sometimes into the water.

- **23.** On his saying: *then the sons are free,* when the tribute was being demanded.

- **24.** On his saying: *but whoever causes one of these little ones to stumble.*

- **25.** That a debtor of ten thousand talents was brought to him.

- **26.** On the rich man who does not enter the kingdom of God.

- **27.** That he tells two of his disciples apart that he is to suffer.

**28.** That as he was going out from Jericho he gave sight to two blind men.

**29.** That he said to the disciples: *you will say to this mountain: 'be taken up and cast into the sea.'*

**30.** On his saying: *and whoever falls on this stone will be broken to pieces.*

**31.** On the man, a king, who made a marriage feast for his son.

**32.** On the seven brothers who had one wife.

**33.** What this means, that he said: *the whole Law hangs on these two commandments.*

**34.** On his saying: *for which is greater, the gold or the temple that sanctifies the gold?*

**35.** On his saying: *straining out a gnat, but swallowing a camel.*

**36.** On his saying: *how often have I wished to gather your children as a hen.*

**37.** On his saying: *pray that your flight may not be in winter or on a sabbath.*

**38.** On his saying: *for as lightning comes from the east and shines as far as the west.*

**39.** On his saying: *from the fig tree learn its lesson.*

**40.** On his saying of Judas: *it would have been better for him not to have been born.*

**41.** That he was sold for thirty pieces of silver.

**42.** On his saying: *where the body is, there the eagles will be gathered.*

**43.** On his saying: *I will not drink from this fruit of the vine from now on.*

**44.** That they spat in his face and struck him with their fists.

**45.** On Peter's threefold denial.

**46.** That he followed at a distance the Lord going to his passion.

**47.** That the Lord prayed three times before he was handed over.
