# Where he reproached the cities for not doing penance, which Luke too says; where it must be asked how Matthew agrees with him in the order itself.

**32. 79.** Matthew goes on and says: *then he began to reproach the cities in which most of his powers had been done, because they had not done penance*, and the rest[^1], up to where he says, *it shall be more tolerable for the land of Sodom in the day of judgment than for you*[^2]. This too Luke mentions, joining these things from his own mouth to a certain continued discourse of the Lord[^3].

Whence it rather seems that he mentions those things in the order in which they were said by the Lord, but that Matthew kept the order of his own recollection. Or, if what the same Matthew says, *then he began to reproach the cities*, is thought to be taken so that he is believed to have wished to express the very point of time in that word *then*, and not rather the time itself, somewhat more broadly, in which these many things were done and said—whoever believes this, let him also believe that this was said twice.

For since even in one evangelist certain things are found which the Lord said twice—as in the same Luke about not carrying a bag on the way, it is found said by the Lord in two other places similarly[^4]—what wonder if something else too, said twice, is told singly by single evangelists in the same order in which it was said, and therefore a different order appears in the single ones, because it was said both then when the one and then when the other mentions it?

[^1]: Mt 11:20.
[^2]: Mt 11:24.
[^3]: See Lk 10:12–15.
[^4]: See Lk 9:3; 10:4.
