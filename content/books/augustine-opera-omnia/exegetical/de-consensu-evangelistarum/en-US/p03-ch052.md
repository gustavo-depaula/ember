# On the leaven of the Pharisees, how it agrees with Mark, whether in substance or in order.

**52.** 107. Matthew continues: *and leaving them he went away. And when his disciples had come across the strait, they had forgotten to bring bread. And he said to them: 'Watch and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees*[^1],' and so on down to where he says: *then they understood that he had not said to beware of the leaven of bread, but of the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees*[^2]. These same things Mark too sets out, and in the same order[^3].

[^1]: Mt 16:6.
[^2]: Mt 16:12.
[^3]: Mt 16:13–14.
