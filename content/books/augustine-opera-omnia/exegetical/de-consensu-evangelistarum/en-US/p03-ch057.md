# On the man who brought him his son, whom the disciples had not been able to heal.

**58.** 116. Matthew continues and says: *and when he had come to the crowd, a man came up to him, falling on his knees before him and saying: 'Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is a lunatic and suffers terribly*[^1],' and so on down to where he says: *but this kind is not cast out except by prayer and fasting*[^2]. This Mark and Luke too record, and in the same order, without any question of contradiction[^3].

[^1]: Mt 17:14.
[^2]: Mt 17:8.
[^3]: Mk 9:16–18; Lk 9:38–43.
