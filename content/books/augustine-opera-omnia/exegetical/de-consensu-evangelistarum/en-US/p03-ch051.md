# That Matthew says he came from there into the region of Magadan, how this agrees with Mark; and how, when they sought a sign, he answered again about Jonah.

**51.** 106. Matthew continues and says: *and having dismissed the crowd, he got into the boat and came to the region of Magadan*[^1], and so on down to where he says: *an evil and adulterous generation seeks a sign, and no sign shall be given to it except the sign of Jonah*[^2]. This same Matthew has already said this elsewhere too[^3]. Hence again and again it must be kept in mind that the Lord often said the same things, so that what cannot be resolved when a contradiction exists may be understood to have been said twice. Mark too, keeping this same order, after that miracle of the seven loaves, subjoins the very thing Matthew does, except that Matthew did not say *Dalmanutha*, which is read in some manuscripts, but *Magadan*[^4]. But there is no doubt that the same place is meant under either name. For most manuscripts of Mark too have only *Magadan*. Nor let this be a stumbling block, that Mark does not say it was answered to those seeking a sign from heaven, the same thing that Matthew says about Jonah, but says the Lord answered: *no sign shall be given to it.* For we must understand the sort they were seeking, that is, from heaven; but he omitted to mention about Jonah, which Matthew recorded.

[^1]: Mt 15:39.
[^2]: Mt 16:4.
[^3]: See Mt 12:39.
[^4]: See Mk 8:10–12.
