# Where, when he had spoken with the disciples about his passion, they were grieved.

**59.** 117. Matthew continues, saying: *and as they were dwelling in Galilee, Jesus said to them: 'The Son of man is to be handed over into the hands of men, and they will kill him, and on the third day he will rise.' And they were greatly grieved*[^1]. This Mark and Luke record in the same order[^2].

[^1]: Mt 17:21–22.
[^2]: See Mk 9:23–31; Lk 9:44–45.
