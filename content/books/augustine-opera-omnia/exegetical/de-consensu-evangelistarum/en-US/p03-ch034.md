# Where the disciples, plucking the ears of grain, ate them: how Matthew, Mark, and Luke agree among themselves in the order of narrating.

**34. 81.** Matthew goes on and says: *at that time Jesus went on the Sabbath through the standing grain, but his disciples, being hungry, began to pluck the ears and to eat*[^1], and the rest, up to where he says, *for the Son of man is Lord even of the Sabbath*[^2]. This too is mentioned without any question of contradiction both by Mark and by Luke[^3], but they do not say *at that time*. Whence perhaps Matthew rather kept here the order of the thing done, but those two the order of their own recollection—unless what was said, *that time*, is taken more broadly, that is, the time in which these many and various things were done.

[^1]: Mt 12:1.
[^2]: Mt 12:8.
[^3]: See Mk 2:23–28; Lk 6:1–5.
