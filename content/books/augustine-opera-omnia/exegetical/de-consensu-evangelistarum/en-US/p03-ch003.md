# How Christ is the son of David, when he was not born of the intercourse of Joseph the son of David.

**2. 4.** And for this reason, even if someone could demonstrate that Mary drew no origin of blood relationship from David, it would be enough, according to this reasoning, to take Christ as the son of David[^1], by which reasoning Joseph too was rightly called his father: how much more, since, when the apostle Paul evidently says that Christ is of the seed of David according to the flesh[^2], we surely ought not to doubt that Mary herself too drew some blood relationship from the stock of David! And since the priestly lineage of this woman too is not passed over—Luke intimating that Elizabeth was her kinswoman[^3], whom he says to be of the daughters of Aaron[^4]—it must be most firmly held that the flesh of Christ was propagated from both lineages, namely of kings and of priests, in which persons, among that people of the Hebrews, the mystical anointing too was figured, that is, *chrism*, whence the name of Christ shines forth, foretold so long beforehand even by this most evident signification.

[^1]: See Mt 1:1-20
[^2]: See Rom 1:3
[^3]: See Lk 1:36
[^4]: See Lk 1:5
