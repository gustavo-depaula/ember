# On the time of his withdrawal into Galilee.

**18. 42.** This too must be inquired into: how John the Evangelist says that, before John the Baptist was sent into prison, Jesus went into Galilee[^1] (for after he mentioned that in Cana of Galilee he made wine from water and went down to Capernaum with his mother and disciples, and they stayed there not many days, he says that he then went up to Jerusalem on account of the Pasch, and after these things came into the land of Judea, he and his disciples, and there tarried with them and was baptizing[^2], where he goes on and says: *now John too was baptizing in Aenon near Salim, because there were many waters there, and people came and were baptized; for John had not yet been sent into prison*[^3]), whereas Matthew says: *but when he had heard that John had been handed over, he withdrew into Galilee*[^4]. Similarly Mark too: *now after John had been handed over*, he says, *Jesus came into Galilee*[^5]. Luke too says nothing indeed about John being handed over, yet he too, after the baptism and temptation of Christ, says that he went into Galilee, just as those two. For he weaves his narrative thus: *and when all the temptation was finished, the devil departed from him for a time. And Jesus went forth in the power of the Spirit into Galilee, and a report went out about him through the whole region*[^6]. Whence it is understood that these three evangelists did not narrate things contrary to John the Evangelist, but passed over the Lord's first coming into Galilee after he was baptized, when he there turned water into wine; for John had not yet then been handed over; but that they connected to their narratives that coming of his into Galilee which was made after John was handed over. About this return of his into Galilee, John the Evangelist too speaks thus: *when, therefore, Jesus knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus makes more disciples and baptizes than John (although Jesus did not baptize, but his disciples), he left Judea and went again into Galilee*[^7]. Then, therefore, we understand that John had already been handed over, but that the Jews had heard that he made more disciples and baptized than John had made and baptized.

[^1]: See Jn 1:43
[^2]: See Jn 2:1-13; 3:22
[^3]: Jn 3:23-24
[^4]: Mt 4:12
[^5]: Mk 1:14
[^6]: Lk 4:13-14
[^7]: Jn 4:1-3
