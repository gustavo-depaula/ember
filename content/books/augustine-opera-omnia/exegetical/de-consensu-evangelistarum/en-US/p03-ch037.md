# On the mute and blind man who had a demon: how Matthew and Luke agree.

**37. 84.** Matthew, then, goes on and says: *then there was brought to him one having a demon, blind and mute, and he cured him, so that he spoke and saw*[^1]. This Luke mentions not in this order, but after many such things, and he says only mute[^2], not also blind. But because he is silent about something he is not to be thought to say something different from the other; for he too weaves together those following things which Matthew does.

[^1]: Mt 12:22.
[^2]: See Lk 11:14–15.
