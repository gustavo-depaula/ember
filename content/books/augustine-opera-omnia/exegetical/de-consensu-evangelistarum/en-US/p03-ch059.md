# Where he paid the tribute from the mouth of a fish, which Matthew alone tells.

**60.** 118. Matthew continues: *and when they had come to Capernaum, those who collected the two-drachma tax came to Peter and said to him: 'Does your teacher not pay the two-drachma tax?' He said: 'Yes*[^1],' and so on down to where he says: *you will find a stater; take it and give it to them for me and for yourself*[^2]. This he alone records, and once it is interposed he follows the same order in which Mark and Luke too walk along with him alike.

[^1]: Mt 17:23–24.
[^2]: Mt 17:26.
