# How it was for this reason that Joseph went into Galilee with the infant Christ, because he feared Archelaus reigning in place of his father in Jerusalem.

**9. 22.** Or perhaps this too troubles someone: how Matthew says that on this account the parents of the boy Jesus went into Galilee, because out of fear of Archelaus they were unwilling to go into Judea[^1], when they seem rather to have gone into Galilee for this reason, that their city was Nazareth of Galilee, as Luke did not pass over in silence[^2]? But it must be understood that, where the angel in a dream in Egypt said to Joseph, *Rise and take the boy and his mother and go into the land of Israel*[^3], it was so understood at first by Joseph that he thought he was commanded to proceed into Judea; for that could first be understood as the land of Israel. But further, after he learned that the son of Herod, Archelaus, reigned there, he was unwilling to expose himself to that danger, since "the land of Israel" could also be so understood that Galilee too would be reckoned there, because the people of Israel inhabited it too. And yet this question can be resolved in another way as well. For it could seem to the parents of Christ that, with the boy, about whom they had learned such things through angelic responses, they ought not to dwell except in Jerusalem, where the Temple of the Lord was, and that for this reason, returning from Egypt, they would have gone there and dwelt there, had they not been terrified by the presence of Archelaus; for they were not divinely commanded to dwell there, so that they ought to disregard, concerning Archelaus, what they feared.

[^1]: See Mt 2:22
[^2]: See Lk 2:4
[^3]: Mt 2:13
