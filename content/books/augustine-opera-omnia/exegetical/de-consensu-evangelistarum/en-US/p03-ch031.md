# Where John the Baptist sent his disciples to the Lord from prison: what Matthew and Luke say.

**31. 78.** Matthew goes on and says: *and it came to pass, when Jesus had finished giving commands to his twelve disciples, he went on from there to teach and preach in their cities. Now John, when he had heard in his chains the works of Christ, sending two of his disciples, said to him: 'Are you he who is to come, or do we await another?'*[^1] and the rest, up to where he says, *and wisdom is justified by her children*[^2]. This whole passage about John the Baptist—that he sent to Jesus, and what sort of answer those whom he sent received, and whatever the Lord said about John after their departure—Luke too interposes[^3], not indeed in the same order; but which of them keeps the order of his own recollection here, which the order of the things themselves, does not appear.

[^1]: Mt 11:1–3.
[^2]: Mt 11:19.
[^3]: See Lk 7:18–35.
