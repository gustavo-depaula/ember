# On the two blind men and the mute demon, which Matthew alone tells.

**29. 69.** Matthew goes on and says: *and as Jesus passed by from there, two blind men followed him, crying out and saying: 'Have mercy on us, son of David*[^1],' and the rest, up to where he says, *but the Pharisees said: 'He casts out demons by the prince of demons*[^2].' This about the two blind men and the mute demon Matthew alone sets down.

For those two blind men about whom the others too narrate[^3] are not these; but nevertheless a similar thing was done, so that, if Matthew himself had not also mentioned that deed[^4], it could be thought that this which he now narrates was said also by the other two. Which we ought diligently to commit to memory—that there are certain similar deeds—which is proved when this same evangelist mentions both, so that, if ever we find such things, single, in single evangelists, and in them something contrary which cannot be resolved, it may occur to us that this is not the same deed, but another similar one, or one done in a similar way.

[^1]: Mt 9:27.
[^2]: Mt 9:34.
[^3]: See Mk 10:46–52; Lk 18:35–43.
[^4]: See Mt 20:29–34.
