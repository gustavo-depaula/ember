# When he asked the disciples who men said that he was, whether Matthew, Mark, and Luke are in any way opposed to one another, in substance or in order.

**53.** 108. Matthew continues: *now Jesus came into the district of Caesarea Philippi, and he asked his disciples, saying: 'Who do men say that the Son of man is?' And they said: 'Some say John the Baptist, others Elijah, and others Jeremiah or one of the prophets*[^1],' and so on down to where he says: *and whatever you loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven*[^2]. Mark narrates this in almost the same order, but he has interposed first the matter of the blind man given sight, which he alone records[^3], about the one who said to the Lord: *I see men like trees, walking*[^4]. Luke, however, after that miracle of the five loaves, recalls and inserts this[^5], the order of which recollection, as we have already shown above, in no way conflicts with the order of these others. But this can be a stumbling block: that Luke said the Lord asked his disciples who men said that he was at the time when he was praying alone and they too were present, whereas Mark says they were asked this by him on the way[^6]; yet it is a stumbling block only to one who has never prayed on the way.

**53.** 109. I recall that I have already said this too, lest anyone suppose that Peter received his name here, where he says to him: *you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my Church*[^7]. For he did not receive this name except where John records that it was said to him: *you shall be called Cephas, which is interpreted Peter*[^8]. Hence neither in that place where Mark, naming the twelve disciples one by one, said that James and John were called the sons of thunder[^9], is it to be supposed that Peter received his name, because Mark said there that he imposed on him the name by which he should be called Peter; for he said this by way of recollection, not because it then happened.

[^2]: Mt 16:19.
[^3]: See Mk 8:22–29.
[^4]: Mk 8:24.
[^5]: See Lk 9:18–20.
[^6]: See Mk 8:27.
[^7]: Mt 16:18.
[^8]: Jn 1:42.
[^9]: Mk 3:16–19.
