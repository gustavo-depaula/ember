# Verses on Nabor

Slain in the Donatists’ cruel slaughter,

Nabor’s body lies buried here with devout praise.

Although some time before he had been a Donatist,

once converted, he loved the peace for which he would die.

Clothed in crimson blood for the noblest cause,

he perished not through error, nor killed himself in frenzy:

true martyrdom is proved by true devotion.

Take the first letters: there is the name of honor.
