# Revisions I

## Chapter 20 (19): Psalm against the Party of Donatus

Wishing also to bring the case against the Donatists to the notice of the very humblest of the common people and of those wholly without learning or skill, and to fix it in their memory so far as I could, I composed a psalm for them to sing, set out in Latin letters but only as far as the letter *V*. Such pieces they call "abecedarian" psalms.

I left out the last three letters; in their place I added at the end a kind of epilogue, as though mother Church were addressing them. The refrain too, which is sung in response, and the preface to the case, which was likewise to be sung, fall outside the alphabetical order, for that order begins after the preface. I deliberately chose not to compose it in any sort of meter, lest the requirements of versification should force me to use words less familiar to the common people.

This psalm begins thus: *All you who rejoice in peace, now judge what is true,* which is its refrain.
