# *Blessed are the poor.*

**63.** 141. PETILIAN SAID: *By what means do you fulfill the divine commandments? The Lord Christ says: Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven*[^1]*. By the malice of your persecuting fury you breathe out riches.*

**63.** 142. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Say these things rather to your own Circumcellions.

[^1]: Matt 5:3.
