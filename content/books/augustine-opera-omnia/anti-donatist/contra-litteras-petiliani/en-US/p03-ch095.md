# All the goods of the Donatists belong to the Church.

**59.** 134. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Whatever unity possessed is nothing but ours, who stand fast in unity not according to the slanders of men, but according to the words of Christ, in whom all the nations of the whole earth are blessed; nor do we separate ourselves from the fellowship of the wheat on account of the wicked, whom we cannot separate from the Lord's grain before the winnowing of the judgment; and if you, now cut off, have begun to possess anything, then, because the Lord gave to us what was taken from you, we do not therefore covet what belongs to others, since by the command of him to whom all things belong, they have become ours; and they are justly ours: for you were using them for division, we for unity.

Otherwise, even the first people of God could be charged with coveting another's property, who by divine power were driven from the face of those who were misusing that land; and the Jews themselves, from whom the kingdom was taken away[^1], according to the words of the Lord, and given to a nation that does righteousness, could be charged with coveting another's property, because the Church of Christ possesses the place where the persecutors of Christ once reigned. And after all this, when it has been said to you yourselves, You covet the property of others, because you cast the Maximianists out of the basilicas, you find no answer to give.

**60.** 135. PETILIAN SAID: *By what law, then, do you show yourselves to be Christians, when you do things contrary to the law?*

**60.** 136. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: You want to quarrel, not to debate.

[^1]: Matt 21:43.
