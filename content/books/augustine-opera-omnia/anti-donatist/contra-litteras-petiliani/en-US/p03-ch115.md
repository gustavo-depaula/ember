# The Catholics are not blessed, because they falsify the commandments of the Gospel.

**72.** 161. PETILIAN SAID: *Since, then, by falsifying the commandments you are not blessed, Christ the Lord condemns you by the divine pronouncements: But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you shut the kingdom of heaven before men; for you do not enter, and you do not allow those who would enter to go in. Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you traverse sea and land to make a single proselyte; and when he has been made, you make him a child of hell twice as much as yourselves. Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you tithe mint and dill and cumin, and neglect the weightier matters of the law, justice and mercy and faith.*

*These things you ought to have done, without neglecting the others. Blind guides, straining out a gnat but swallowing a camel. Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you are like whitewashed tombs, which outwardly appear beautiful to men, but within are full of dead men's bones and all filth: so you also outwardly appear righteous to men, but within you are full of hypocrisy and iniquity*[^1].

[^1]: Matt 23:13-15, 23-24, 27-28.
