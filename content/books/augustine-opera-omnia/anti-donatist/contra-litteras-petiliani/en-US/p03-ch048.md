# When did the Catholic Church lose the power to baptize?

**31.** 71. Augustine replied: You teach rather—you, the whole world, throughout which the inheritance of Christ is spread, and that multitude of so many nations, where the apostles founded churches—when it lost the power to baptize. You will never teach it: not only because you slander them, you do not prove them betrayers; but because, even if you proved it, the crime of any wicked men whatever—whether unknown, or deceitful, or to be borne like weeds or chaff—cannot overturn the promises of God, so that all the nations should not be blessed in the seed of Abraham[^1]; from which promises you make yourselves strangers, who are unwilling to have the communion of unity with all the nations.

[^1]: See Gen 22:18
