# Your prayers do you no good.

**53.** 121. Petilian said: *If you make a prayer to the Lord, or pour out a petition, it does you no good at all. For your bloody conscience empties your feeble prayers; because the Lord God hears a pure conscience rather than prayers, the Lord Christ saying: 'Not everyone who says to me, Lord, Lord, shall enter the kingdom of heaven; but he who does the will of my Father who is in heaven'*[^1]. *The will of God is surely good; for therefore in the sacred Prayer we ask thus: 'Your will be done in heaven and on earth'*[^2]; *that, because his will is good, he may bestow on us the things that are good. You, then, do not do the will of God, because you perpetrate evils daily.*

[^1]: Matt 7:21.
[^2]: Matt 6:10.
