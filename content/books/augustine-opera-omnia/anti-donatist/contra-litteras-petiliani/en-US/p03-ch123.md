# The Catholics are the false brothers who were persecuting Paul.

**76.** 169. PETILIAN SAID: *The apostle Paul too, though he suffered immense persecutions from all the nations, endured graver ones from false brothers, as he himself testifies, often afflicted: In dangers from the nations, in dangers from my own people, in dangers from false brothers*[^1]. *He who again says: Be imitators of me, as I am of Christ*[^2]. *Since, then, you assail us as false brothers, we imitate the patience of our master Paul under your dangers.*

[^1]: 2 Cor 11:26.
[^2]: 1 Cor 4:16.
