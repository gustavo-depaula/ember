# Petilian's statement is against Scripture.

**101.** 233. Now the other points above, which we have now set forth to be refuted, are such that anyone baptized is said to return altogether such as the one by whom he is baptized was. But far be it that, deranged as you are in saying these things, those whom you baptize should return as deranged as you. And how prettily it sounded to you, what you said: *You are drenched in fraud, drenched in crime, drenched also in fury!*

But you, unless you were not drenched but filled with fury, would not pour out these things. Is it really so, that—to say nothing of other things—whoever, not avaricious, come to your avaricious colleagues or your presbyters to be baptized, return avaricious; and whoever, sober, run to those whirlpools of wine to be baptized, return drunkards? Holding these views and persuading these things, do you dare even, as though against us, to mention what you set down a little before above: *It is better to hope in the Lord than to hope in man; it is better to hope in the Lord than to hope in princes*[^1].

What else do you teach, I ask, than to hope not in the Lord, but in man, when you say that the baptized becomes such as the baptizer was? And because you assume to yourselves this primacy of baptizing, men should believe you; and those who were going to hope in the Lord, should hope in princes? Rather, let them not hear you, but rather those testimonies which you set down against yourselves; and something even more terrible; because not only: *It is better to hope in the Lord than to hope in man*[^2]; but also: *Cursed is everyone who places his hope in man*[^3].

**102.** 234. PETILIAN SAID: *At least imitate the prophets, who feared that their sacred souls be deceived by a false baptism. For Jeremiah said first that among impious men the water is deceitful. The water, he says, is deceitful, it has no faith*[^4].

[^1]: Ps 118:8-9 (117:8-9).
[^2]: Ps 118:8 (117:8).
[^3]: Jer 17:5.
[^4]: Jer 15:18.
