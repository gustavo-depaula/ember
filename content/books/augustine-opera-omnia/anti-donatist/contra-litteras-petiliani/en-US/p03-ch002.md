# How Petilian begins his letter.

**1.** 2. From the very beginning of his letter Petilian said: *Petilian the bishop, to his most beloved brothers, fellow presbyters and deacons, ministers established with us throughout the diocese in the holy Gospel: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.*

**2.** 3. Augustine replied: I recognize the apostolic salutation: see to what you say, yet note from whom you have learned what you say. So Paul salutes the Romans, so the Corinthians, so the Galatians, so the Ephesians, the Colossians, the Philippians, the Thessalonians. What madness, then, is it to be unwilling to share the salutation of peace with these churches, in whose letters you learned the salutation of peace?
