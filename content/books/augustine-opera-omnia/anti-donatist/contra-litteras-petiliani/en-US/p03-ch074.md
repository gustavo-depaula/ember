# A citation of the first psalm, about the truly blessed man.

**46.** 107. Petilian said: *In the first psalm David separates the blessed and the impious, not, of course, making parts, but separating holiness from all the impious. 'Blessed is the man who has not gone in the counsel of the impious, and has not stood in the way of sinners.'*

*Let him who had erred so as to perish return to the way of righteousness. 'And has not sat in the seat of pestilence.' Since he so admonishes, O wretches, why do you sit? 'But his will is in the law of the Lord, and on the law of the Lord he will meditate day and night. And he shall be like a tree which is planted beside the courses of waters, which will give its fruit in its season. And its leaf shall not fall, and all things whatsoever he shall do shall prosper. Not so the impious, not so; but like the dust which the wind casts from the face of the earth.'*

*It fills the eyes, that it may blind. 'Therefore the impious shall not rise in the judgment, nor sinners in the counsel of the just. For the Lord knows the ways of the just, and the way of the impious shall perish'*[^1].

[^1]: Ps 1.
