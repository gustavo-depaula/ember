# Outside the Church love does not profit.

**77.** 172. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: This is what I said a little before, that you want to cover yourself with the sheep's skin, so that, if it can be done, the sheep may feel you biting before it perceives you coming. Is not this the praise of love, which is wont to refute your slander by the clearest truth? Will these therefore not be our weapons, because you try to seize them first? Indeed these are living darts: from wherever they are thrown, they recognize whom they slay. If they are launched by us, they will lodge in you: if they are launched by you, they are turned back against you.

For with these apostolic words, which commend the eminence of love, we are accustomed to show you how it does not profit men, although there be in them either the sacraments or faith, where love is not; so that when you come to Catholic unity, you may understand what is conferred on you, and how great is what you had less of: for Christian love cannot be kept except in the unity of the Church; and thus you may see that without it you are nothing, even if you hold baptism and faith, and through it could even move mountains. But if this is also your view, let us not, in our own case, detest and blow away either the sacraments of God which we acknowledge, or faith itself; but let us hold love, without which, even with the sacraments and with faith, we are nothing. And we hold love if we embrace unity; and we embrace unity if we do not, by our own words, fabricate it in a part, but, by the words of Christ, recognize it in the whole.
