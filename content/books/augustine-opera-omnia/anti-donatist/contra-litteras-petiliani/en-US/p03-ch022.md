# The Donatists are ravening wolves, who cover themselves with the skins of sheep.

**16.** 37. Augustine replied: If I should ask you by what fruits you know us to be ravening wolves, you are going to say the crimes of others, never even proved in those whose crimes they are said to be. But if you should ask me by what fruits we know that you rather are ravening wolves, I charge the crime of schism, which you will deny, but which I will at once prove: for you are not in communion with all the nations and with those churches founded by apostolic labor. Here you are going to say: I am not in communion with betrayers and murderers.

The seed of Abraham answers you: These are those crimes, either not true, or not mine. But these I set aside for the moment; you, show me the Church. Now that voice will sound to me which the Lord warned must be avoided in false prophets who point to parties and try to estrange men from the whole: *Behold, here is Christ, behold, there he is*[^1].

But do you so suppose that the true sheep of Christ, to whom it was said, *Do not believe*, have no heart—that they would hear the wolf saying, *Behold, here is Christ*; and not hear the shepherd saying, *Among all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem*[^2]?

[^1]: Matt 24:23
[^2]: Luke 24:47
