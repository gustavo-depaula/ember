# The Circumcellions use clubs against the Catholics.

**88.** 195. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Why, then, do you not restrain the arms of the Circumcellions with such a saying? Or do you think you are speaking apart from the Gospel, if you say: Whoever uses a club shall die by a club? Pardon us, then, because our elders could not restrain even those by whom you complain Marculus was hurled down: for it is not written in the Gospel: Whoever hurls someone down shall die by being hurled down. And would that, as those things are either false or past, so the clubs of these men might cease.

Although perhaps you are angry that we withdraw the armament from your legions, if not by laws, at least by words, because we say that they rage with clubs alone. For that was their old soldiery, but now they have advanced too far. For amid wine-soaked feasts, and with women who have no husbands, they have learned, with free license to accompany, to roam, to jest, to drink, to spend the night, not only to turn clubs, but even to brandish iron and to whirl slings.

But why should I not say to them (with what disposition I say it, and with what they receive it, God knows): Madmen, the sword of Peter, although still drawn by a carnal motion of the soul, was nevertheless drawn for the body of Christ against the body of the persecutor; but your arms are divided against Christ: his body, of which he is the head, that is, his Church[^1], is throughout all the nations. He himself said this, and ascended into heaven[^2], where the fury of the Jews could not follow him: and your fury assails his members in the body which, ascending, he commended.

For these members, against you, and resisting you, rage whoever in the Catholic communion, still of small faith, are of that disposition in which Peter then was, when he drew the sword for the name of Christ. But there is a great difference between your persecution and theirs. You are like the servant of the priest of the Jews; because, serving your princes, you arm yourselves against the Catholic Church, that is, against the body of Christ: but these men are such as Peter then was, who fight even bodily for the body of Christ, that is, for the Church. But if it is said to them that they should be at rest, as it was then said to Peter; how much more must it be said to you, that, laying aside your heretical fury, you may belong to those members for which they so contend. But, wounded by such men, you hate even us, and, as though you had lost your right ears, you do not hear Christ sitting at the right hand of the Father.

But to whom shall I speak, or when shall I speak to them, for whom I cannot find an hour, since they belch wine even in the morning, whether already drunk or still drinking? Indeed they even threaten, not only they themselves, but about them their own bishops too, ready to deny that what they have done has anything to do with them. May the Lord grant us the song of ascents, by which we may say: *With those who hated peace I was peaceable; when I spoke to them, they warred against me without cause*[^3]. For this is said by the body of Christ, which throughout the whole earth, by some here, by others elsewhere, and by all manner of heretics wherever they are, is assailed.

[^1]: See Col 1:18.
[^2]: See Acts 1:8-9.
[^3]: Ps 120:6-7 (119:6-7).
