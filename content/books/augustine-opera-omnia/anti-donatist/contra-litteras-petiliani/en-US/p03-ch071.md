# We were baptized in the name of Christ, not in the name of Judas.

**44.** 104. Augustine replied: I too could say: You by your contagion put on Optatus the betrayer, robber, oppressor, separator; but far be it that the desire of repaying a curse should provoke me to any falsehood; for you do not put on that man, nor we this one. And so, if anyone coming to us should answer that he was baptized in the name of Optatus, he will also be baptized in the name of Christ; and as for all those whom, coming from us, you baptized, if they had said that they were baptized in the name of Judas the betrayer, we do not blame what you did; but if they had been baptized in the name of Christ, do you not see with how great an error you suppose that the divine sacraments are varied by the variety of human vices, or polluted by the filth of anyone's life?
