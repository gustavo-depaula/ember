# Paul baptized those who did not have the baptism of Christ.

**37.** 86. Augustine replied: We do not accuse Paul, who gave men the baptism of Christ, because they did not have the baptism of Christ, but the baptism of John, according to their own answer; who, asked in what they had been baptized, answered: In the baptism of John, which does not pertain to the baptism of Christ, nor is any part or degree of it; otherwise either the water of the baptism of Christ was then repeated, or, if the baptism of Christ was then completed by two waters, it is now less complete, because that which was given through John is not given; but to suppose either of these is impious and sacrilegious. Paul, then, gave the baptism of Christ to those who did not have the baptism of Christ, but of John.
