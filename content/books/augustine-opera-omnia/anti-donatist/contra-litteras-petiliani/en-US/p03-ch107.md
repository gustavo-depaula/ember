# What the true and great blindness of the Donatists is.

**68.** 152. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Why do you say this? Is it perhaps that we charge against all the nations things said by men that we do not know, and refuse to understand the plain things that God has foretold about all the nations? This is great blindness of heart; and if you do not even recognize it in yourselves, this is greater blindness of heart.
