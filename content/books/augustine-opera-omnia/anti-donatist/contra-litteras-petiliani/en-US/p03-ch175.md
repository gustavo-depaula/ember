# We are poor in spirit, not rich.

**99.** 227. PETILIAN SAID: *But we, poor in spirit*[^1], *do not fear for riches, but dread riches. We, having nothing, and possessing all things*[^2], *believe the soul to be our wealth, and by our punishments and blood purchase the eternal riches of heaven, since the Lord says, Whoever loses his substance shall receive it a hundredfold.*

[^1]: See Matt 5:3.
[^2]: See 2 Cor 6:10.
