# Augustine: we do not desire the communion of heretics.

**95.** 219. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: If we greatly desired the communion of heretics, we would not wish you to be corrected from heretical error; but since we do this with you, that you may not be heretics, how do we greatly desire the communion of heretics? For dissension and division make you heretics; but peace and unity make Catholics. When, therefore, you come here from there, you cease to be what we hate, and you begin to be what we love.

**96.** 220. PETILIAN SAID: *Choose at last which of two things you will say. If you have innocence, why do you pursue us with the sword? Or if you call us guilty, why do you seek us as innocent?*
