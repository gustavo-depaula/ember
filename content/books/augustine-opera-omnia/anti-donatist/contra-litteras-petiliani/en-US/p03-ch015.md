# Who are the true sons of a betrayer.

**11.** 25. Petilian said: *But some will say: We are not the sons of a betrayer. A man is the son of him whose deeds he follows. For these are the most certain sons, and the ones most like their parents, whom not this flesh and blood, but morals and deeds resembling their parents have begotten.*

**11.** 26. Augustine replied: A little before you said nothing against us; now you have even begun to say something for us too. For this proposition of yours holds you to this: that if you do not today convict us, with whom you are dealing, of being betrayers and murderers, and of whatever else you charge, then whatever such thing you show in those who preceded us in time can in no way harm us. For we cannot be the sons of those whose deeds we do not share.

And see where you have committed yourself: if perhaps you should even convict some man of our own age, living with us, of some such guilt, it in no way prejudges all the nations, which are blessed in the seed of Abraham[^1], from which you, by separating yourself, are found to be sacrilegious. Thus—what cannot be done—unless you know all who are everywhere, and have not only learned the morals and deeds of all but have even demonstrated them to be as evil as you say, you have no reason to charge against the world, which is in the holy ones, some parents or other, to whom you may prove them to be alike.

Nor will it help you at all, even if you could demonstrate that those who are not such men receive the common sacraments together with those who are such. First, because you ought to look to yourselves, with whom you celebrate them, to whom you give them, from whom you receive them, and you do not wish those men to be charged against you. Next, if those are sons of Judas, who was a devil among the apostles, whoever imitate his deeds: why should we not call those sons of the apostles who share with such men not their own deeds, but the sacraments of the Lord, just as the apostles took the Lord's supper together with that betrayer? Therefore you are far unlike them, in that you charge against men who keep unity the very thing which you do by tearing unity apart.

**12.** 27. Petilian said: *About himself the Lord Christ says to the Jews:* *'If I do not do the deeds of my Father, do not believe me'*[^2].

**12.** 28. Augustine replied: I have already answered above: This too is both true, and is for us against you.

**13.** 29. Petilian said: *Thus repeatedly does he rebuke the false-speaking and lying: 'You are sons of the devil, for he was an accuser from the beginning, and did not stand in the truth'*[^3].

[^1]: See Gen 22:18
[^2]: John 10:37
[^3]: John 8:44
