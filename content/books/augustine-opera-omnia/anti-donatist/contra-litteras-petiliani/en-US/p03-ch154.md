# The Circumcellions persecute Christians.

**91.** 201. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Do you, then, when you have received a slap, offer the other cheek? It was not this reputation that your frenzied bands won for you, roaming everywhere throughout all Africa with dreadful disgrace. Would that men might bargain with you, that you might at least, according to the old Law, demand an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth[^1], and not raise clubs for words you have heard.

[^1]: See Deut 19:21.
