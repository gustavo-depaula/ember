# The devil is the master of the betrayer and tempted Christ to throw himself down.

**49.** 114. Augustine replied: I could indeed ask you in what Law the words are written which the devil spoke when he accused the holy man Job before God; if it were my purpose to demonstrate that the Law which you say the devil knows, you yourself do not know; but because the question between us is not from there, I pass this over. But you tried to show that the devil was skilled in the Law, as though we said that all who know the words of the Law are just. What, then, what you wished to mention about the devil helps you, I do not see; unless perhaps it should come to our mind how you imitate the devil himself. For just as he brought forth the words of the Law against the giver of the Law, so you too accuse, from the words of the Law, men whom you do not know, that you may resist the promises of God which are written in that very Law.

Next, I would have you tell me: those confessors of yours, when they throw themselves down, to whom do they conduct their martyrdom? To Christ, who repelled the devil suggesting such things, or rather to the devil himself, who suggested such things to be done to Christ? There are two especially base and common deaths of those who kill themselves, the noose and the headlong leap. You yourself certainly said in the first parts of this letter: *The betrayer perished by a noose, and left a noose to such men;* this does not pertain to us at all. For we do not venerate with the name of martyrs those who have tied a rope to their own neck.

But how much more credibly do we say against you: The master of the betrayer, the devil, wished to persuade Christ to a headlong leap, and was repelled? What, then, must those be called to whom he persuaded this and was heeded? What, except enemies of Christ, but friends of the devil; disciples of the seducer, fellow disciples of the betrayer? For both learned voluntary deaths from one master; he the noose, these the headlong leap.

**50.** 115. Petilian said: *But that we may destroy your several points, if you say that you are priests, thus was it said by the Lord God through the prophet: 'The vengeance of the Lord is upon false priests.'*
