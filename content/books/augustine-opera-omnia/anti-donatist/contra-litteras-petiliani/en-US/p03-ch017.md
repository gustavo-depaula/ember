# Who are the persecutors of the prophets.

**14.** 31. Petilian said: *In a third place likewise he calls the madness of persecutors by this name:* *'Brood of vipers, how will you flee the judgment of hell? Therefore I send to you prophets and wise men and scribes; and some of them you will kill and crucify and scourge in your synagogues, until there come upon you all the righteous blood which you have shed upon the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zechariah son of Barachiah, whom you killed between the temple and the altar'*[^1]. *Are they truly, in a carnal sense, sons of vipers, and not rather serpents in mind, scourging with three-tongued malice and deadly touch and a spirit of venom? Truly they have become vipers, who have vomited deaths by their bites upon innocent peoples.*

[^1]: Matt 23:33-35
