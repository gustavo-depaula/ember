# We are Catholics, because we do not desert unity, but you are heretics.

**36.** 84. Augustine replied: Betrayers indeed you not only do not prove us to be, but not even could your fathers prove our fathers to be; who, if they had been proved such, would certainly not be our fathers, according to your earlier assertion, since we would not have followed their deeds; nor yet, on their account, would we be torn from the fellowship of unity, and from the seed of Abraham in which all the nations are blessed[^1]. Nevertheless, if the water of Christ is one thing and the water of a betrayer another, because Christ was not a betrayer; why should not the water of Christ be one thing and the water of a robber another, because Christ certainly was not a robber either?

Baptize, then, after your robber, and I will baptize after a betrayer neither mine nor yours; or, if the produced papers are to be believed, both mine and yours; but if the communion of the whole world is to be believed rather than the party of Donatus, not mine, but yours. But by a better and sounder judgment, because, according to the apostolic voice, *each one* of us *shall bear his own load*[^2]; neither is that robber yours, as many of you as are not robbers, nor is any betrayer either ours or yours, as many of us as are not betrayers. And therefore we are Catholics, who according to this judgment do not desert unity: but you are heretics, who, on account of the crimes—whether true or false—of certain men, are unwilling to keep love with the seed of Abraham.

[^1]: See Gen 22:18
[^2]: Gal 6:5
