# Faith does not profit him who has no love.

**77.** 171. PETILIAN SAID: *For what kind of faith is in you, which has no love? As Paul himself says: If I speak with the tongues of men, and have the knowledge of angels, but have not love, I am like sounding brass or a clashing cymbal. Or if I have prophecy, and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith, so as to move mountains, but have not love, I am nothing. And if I distribute all my goods to the poor, and hand over my body to be burned, but have not love, it profits me nothing*[^1].

[^1]: 1 Cor 13:1-3.
