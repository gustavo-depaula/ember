# Not Judas, but Christ died for us.

**43.** 102. Augustine replied: Not Judas, but Christ died for us, to whom the Church spread throughout the whole world says: *I will answer a word to those who reproach me, for I have hoped in your words*[^1]. When, therefore, I hear the words of the Lord saying: *You shall be witnesses to me in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and unto the whole earth*[^2]; and, through the prophet: *Their sound has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world*[^3]; no bodily mixture with the wicked disturbs me, if I know how to say: *Receive your servant unto good; let not the proud slander me*[^4]. I do not, then, care about an empty slanderer, because I hold a suitable promiser.

But if you complain about the ecclesiastical goods or places which you used to hold and do not hold, the Jews too can call themselves just and charge us with iniquity, because the place in which they impiously reigned the Christians now possess. What, then, is unworthy, if what the heretics held, the Catholics hold according to the equal will of the Lord? For against all such, that is, against all the impious and unjust, that voice of the Lord avails: *The kingdom of God shall be taken away from you, and shall be given to a nation doing righteousness*[^5]. Or is it written in vain: *The just shall eat the labors of the impious*[^6]? Therefore you ought rather to wonder that you still hold anything, than that you have lost something. But do not wonder at this either: for the whitewashed wall falls little by little.

Yet look to the Maximianists, what places they held; and by whose acting and assaulting they were driven thence. And if to suffer these things is righteousness, and to do them iniquity, dare to speak if you can. First, because you did it, they suffered it. Next, because, according to the rule of this righteousness, you are found the lower. For they were expelled from their ancient places through judges, by Catholic emperors; but you are not even by the commands of those same emperors expelled from the basilicas of unity. Whence, except because you are of less merit, not only than your other colleagues, but even than these very men, whom you certainly condemned as sacrilegious by the mouth of your plenary council?

**44.** 103. Petilian said: *For we, as it is written, by our baptism have put on Christ who was betrayed*[^7], but you by your contagion put on Judas the betrayer.

[^1]: Ps 119:42 (118:42).
[^2]: Acts 1:8.
[^3]: Ps 19:4 (18:5).
[^4]: Ps 119:122 (118:122).
[^5]: Matt 21:43.
[^6]: Ps 105:44 (104:44).
[^7]: See Gal 3:27.
