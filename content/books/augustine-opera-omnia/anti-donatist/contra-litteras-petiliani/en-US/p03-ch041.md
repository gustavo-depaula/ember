# It is the baptism of Christ.

**27.** 63. Augustine replied: You say these things against yourselves, but by your insanity you do not know it. For those reckon two baptisms who consider that the just have one and the wicked another; whereas it is neither these men's nor those men's, but is the one baptism of Christ in both; although they themselves are not one, but that which is one the former have unto salvation, the latter unto ruin.
