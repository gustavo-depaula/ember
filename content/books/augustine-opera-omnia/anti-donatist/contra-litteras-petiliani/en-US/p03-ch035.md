# Petilian: You kill someone and make him be baptized in his own blood.

**23.** 51. Petilian said: *But if you say that we administer baptism twice, nay rather it is you who do so, who kill the baptized: we say this not because you baptize, but because, while you kill each one, you make him be baptized in his own blood. For the baptism of water or of the Spirit has, as it were, been doubled by the blood of the martyr wrung out.*

*So too the Savior himself, baptized first by John, professed himself to be baptized again, not now with water or the Spirit*[^1], but with the baptism of blood, the cross of the passion: as it is written: 'Two disciples, the sons of Zebedee, came to him, saying: Lord, when you come into your kingdom, make us sit, one at your right hand and the other at your left. Jesus answered them: You ask a difficult thing. Can you drink the cup that I am to drink, and be baptized with the baptism with which I am baptized? They said to him: We can. And he said to them: The cup indeed that I am to drink, you can drink; and with the baptism with which I am baptized, you will be baptized'[^2] *and the rest.*

*If these are two baptisms, you praise us by your odious charge, we confess it. For when you kill our bodies, we do not administer baptism twice, but we are baptized with our baptism and with our blood, like Christ. Blush; blush, persecutors; you make martyrs like Christ, whom, after the water of true baptism, the blood, as baptizer, drenches.*

[^1]: See John 3:5
[^2]: Mark 10:35-39
