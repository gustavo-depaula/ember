# There are two ways: the way of life and the way of death.

**45.** 105. Petilian said: *But if these are parts, the names of a fellow part prejudge nothing against us. For there are two ways: one narrow, by which we go; the other is for the impious, by which they will perish*[^1]; and yet the equality of the term is far distinguished, lest the way of righteousness be stained by the fellowship of a name.

[^1]: See Matt 7:13-14.
