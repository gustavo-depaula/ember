# The Catholics preach peace from the Gospel and wage the war of love.

**68.** 154. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: We do not feign peace by crime: rather, we preach the peace that comes from the Gospel; and if you have peace with that Gospel, you will have it with us too. The Lord, rising again and offering himself to the disciples not only to be seen with their eyes but even to be touched with their hands, began from this: *Peace be with you*[^1]*,* he said. And how that peace was to be kept, he disclosed in the words that follow. For there he *opened their minds to understand the Scriptures, and said to them: Thus it is written, and thus it was fitting that Christ should suffer and rise on the third day, and that repentance and forgiveness of sins should be preached in his name to all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem*[^2]. Have peace with these words, and you will not disagree with us.

For if we seek unity by war, no war could be praised more splendidly than ours; since it is written: *You shall love your neighbor as yourself*[^3]. And again it is written: *No one ever hated his own flesh*[^4]. And yet *the flesh desires against the spirit, and the spirit against the flesh*[^5]. But if no one hates his own flesh, and yet desires against his own flesh, see, unity is sought by war, so that the body may be chastised and brought into subjection[^6]. And what the spirit does against the flesh, making war not out of hatred but out of love, this the spiritual do against the carnal, so that what they do toward themselves they may do toward them also, because they love their neighbors as themselves[^7]; but the war of the spiritual is that rebuke in love, and their sword is the word of God. To such a war the apostolic trumpet rouses, sounding with great force: *Preach the word, be urgent in season and out of season; reprove, exhort, rebuke, in all patience and teaching*[^8]. See, we act not with the sword, but with the word.

But you neither answer with truths, nor accuse with anything but falsehoods; you do not correct your own, you charge another's. Christ gives true witness about the whole earth; you give false witness against Christ, against the whole earth. But if we believed you rather than Christ, we would be peacemakers: because we believe Christ rather than you, we feign peace by crime. And while you say and do these things, you dare besides to recall: *Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God*[^9]*.*

[^1]: Luke 24:36.
[^2]: Luke 24:45-47.
[^3]: Mark 12:31; Rom 13:9.
[^4]: See Lev 19:19; Eph 5:29.
[^5]: Gal 5:17.
[^6]: See 1 Cor 9:27.
[^7]: See Mark 12:31.
[^8]: 2 Tim 4:2.
[^9]: Matt 5:9.
