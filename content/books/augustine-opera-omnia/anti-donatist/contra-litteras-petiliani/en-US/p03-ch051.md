# What the tongues at Pentecost signify.

**32.** 74. And yet you know, and recall, that the Holy Spirit so came that those whom he then filled spoke in all tongues: what did that sign and prodigy mean? Why is the Holy Spirit now given in such a way that no one to whom he is given can speak in all tongues, unless because that miracle then portended that all the nations would believe, and that thus the Gospel would be in all tongues? This too was foretold so long before in the Psalm: *There are no speeches nor words whose voices are not heard*[^1].

This was said on account of those who, having received the Holy Spirit, were going to speak in all tongues. But because this very thing signified that the Gospel would be in all the tongues of the nations, and that the body of Christ would resound throughout the whole world in all tongues, he went on to say: *Their sound has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world*[^2]. Hence it comes about that the true Church is hidden from no one. Whence is that which he himself says in the Gospel: *A city set on a mountain cannot be hidden*[^3].

And therefore in the same psalm it is added: *In the sun he has set his tabernacle,* that is, in manifestation: as we read it said in the books of Kingdoms: *What you did in secret, you shall suffer in the sun*[^4]. *And he himself, like a bridegroom coming forth from his chamber: he exulted like a giant to run the way; his going forth is from the height of heaven:* behold, you have the coming of the Lord in the flesh. *And his meeting even to his height:* behold, you have the resurrection and ascension. *Nor is there one who can hide himself from his heat*[^5]: behold, you have the coming of the Holy Spirit, whom he sent in tongues of fire, that he might show the fervor of love, which surely he does not have who does not keep, with the Church which is in all tongues, the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace[^6].

[^1]: Ps 19:3 (18:4)
[^2]: Ps 19:4 (18:5)
[^3]: Matt 5:14
[^4]: 2 Sam 12:12
[^5]: Ps 19:5-6 (18:6-7)
[^6]: See Eph 4:3
