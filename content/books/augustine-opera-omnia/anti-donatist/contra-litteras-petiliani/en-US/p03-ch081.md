# Look for what truth you may say.

**50.** 116. Augustine replied: Look rather for what truth you may say, not for whence you may curse; and for what you may teach, not for what you may charge.

**51.** 117. Petilian said: *If you claim a chair for yourselves, wretches, as we said above; you certainly have that which David the psalmist prophet pronounced the seat of pestilence*[^1]; *for it has justly been left to you, because the holy cannot sit in it.*

[^1]: See Ps 1:1.
