# On the sin against the Holy Spirit.

**62.** 140. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: This too is not written so, and see how much it has deceived you. The Apostle, writing to the Corinthians, says: *Every sin that a man commits is outside the body; but whoever fornicates sins against his own body*[^1]. But this is one thing, and that which the Lord said in the Gospel is another: *Every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven men; but whoever sins against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come*[^2]. But you began a sentence from the Apostle, and ended it as though it were one from the Gospel; which I do not believe you did by fraud, but by error.

For neither bears on the matter, and I do not at all see why you said these things, or in what way you said them; unless perhaps, because you had said above that those who break one of the commandments are condemned by the Lord, you considered how many you have who break, not one, but many commandments; and lest this be charged against you, you wished in passing to introduce a distinction among sins: that it is one thing to break some commandment, for which easy pardon may be obtained, and another to sin against the Holy Spirit, which will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come.

And so, fearing the contagion of sins, you did not want to keep silent about this; and again, fearing the depth of a question beyond your powers, you wished to touch on it hurriedly, in such trepidation that, as those who hurry in some agitation often dress or shoe themselves wrong way round, you did not care to attend either to what bears on what, or where and how it is written. But what that sin is which will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come, you so little understand that, although you believe us to be in it, you nevertheless promise us its remission through your baptism. How can this be, if it is such a sin that it is not forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come?

[^1]: 1 Cor 6:18.
[^2]: Matt 12:31-32.
