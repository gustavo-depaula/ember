# True peace lies in bearing with evils.

**69.** 156. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: If you not only said these things but also heard them, you would bear even known evils for the sake of peace, and would not feign unknown ones for the sake of dissension; or rather, since you later learned for the sake of the unity of Donatus to bear the most notorious and infamous evils of Optatus. What madness is this? Known men are tolerated, lest the divided one be divided again; and unknown men are defamed, lest one remain in that very integrity.

**70.** 157. PETILIAN SAID: *The prophet says to you: Peace, peace, and where is peace?*[^1].

[^1]: Jer 8:11.
