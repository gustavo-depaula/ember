# The party of Macarius has nothing to do with the party of Donatus.

**39.** 92. Petilian said: *But there is no part of darkness with light*[^1], nor part of bitterness with honey: there is no part of life with death, no part of innocence with guilt, no part of water with blood; nor part of dregs with oil, although there be a feculent kinship; but all the reprobate things will flow away. It is the very bilge of vices, as John says: 'They went out from us, but they are not of us; for if they had been of us, they would surely have remained with us'[^2]. *There is no gold with its dross: all precious things are purified. For it is written: 'As the furnace tests gold, so the affliction of tribulation tests the just'*[^3]. *Cruelty is no part of meekness, nor sacrilege of religion, nor can the party of Macarius be in any way ours, because it stains by the likeness of our rite. For a hostile battle line is no part, which fulfills the name of an enemy; which, if it is truly called a part, will incur the fitting sentence of Solomon: 'Let their part perish from the earth'*[^4].

[^1]: See 2 Cor 6:14.
[^2]: 1 John 2:19.
[^3]: Wis 3:6.
[^4]: Prov 2:22.
