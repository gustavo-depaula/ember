# Christ's warning about persecution.

**19.** 42. Petilian said: *The Lord Christ commands us: 'When men persecute you in this city, flee to another: and if they persecute you in it, flee to another. For truly I say to you, you will not finish the cities of Israel until the Son of man comes'*[^1]. *If he forewarns us about Jews and pagans, you who recall yourself a Christian ought not to imitate the dread deeds of the Gentiles. Or do you so serve God that we should be killed by your hands? You err, you err, wretches, if you believe this. For God does not have executioner-priests.*

[^1]: Matt 10:23
