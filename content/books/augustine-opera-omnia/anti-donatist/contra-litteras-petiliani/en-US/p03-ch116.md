# The Pharisees did not administer circumcision twice.

**72.** 162. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Tell me whether you have said anything that could not likewise be said in turn against you by a slanderous and abusive mouth. But from the things I have said above, anyone who wishes will find that these things can be said against you not through empty insults, but through true testimonies.

Nevertheless, since the occasion arises, this must not be passed over: surely the ancient people of God had circumcision in place of baptism. I ask, then, if those Pharisees, against whom such things are said, had made a proselyte, who, if he imitated them, would, as it is said, have been a child of hell twice as much as they; whether, if he were corrected and wished to imitate Simeon, Zechariah, Nathanael, he would be judged as needing to be circumcised again by them.

But if it is absurd to say so, although in manner and in empty-talking speech you compare us to such people, why do you baptize after us? But if you are such people, how much more truly and rightly do we not baptize after you; just as those whom I named ought not to be circumcised after the worst Pharisees? Then, since such men sat in the chair of Moses, to which the Lord preserved honor[^1]; why, on account of those to whom you compare them, whether justly or unjustly, do you blaspheme the apostolic chair?

[^1]: See Matt 23:2-3.
