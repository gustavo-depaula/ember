# The Donatists are the killers of the prophets, because they spurn the prophecies.

**14.** 33. Moreover, if we wished to prove that you are killers of the prophets, we do not go further afield to gather, place by place, the slaughters which your frenzied leaders of the Circumcellions and the very bands of the drunken and the insane not only produced from the beginning of your schism, but do not at all cease to produce. I deal with what is at hand: let the divine oracles be brought forward, which are handled in our hands and in yours; let us hold those to be killers of the prophets whom we find contradictors of the tongues of the prophets. What can be said more quickly? What can be shown more readily? You would act more gently by thrusting a sword through the bowels of the prophets, than in that you try with the tongue to kill the words of the prophets. The prophet says: *All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to the Lord*[^1].

Behold, it is being done; behold, it is being fulfilled. But you not only close your unbelieving ears against what is said; you even thrust forth your frenzied tongues against what is now being done. Abraham heard it said: *In your seed all the nations shall be blessed*[^2]: and *he believed, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness*[^3]. You see the deed done, and you cry out against it; and you do not wish to have reckoned to you as unrighteousness what would rightly be reckoned even if you did not believe it when it was not yet done, but only said.

Nay, you not only do not wish it to be reckoned to you as unrighteousness: but even if you suffer anything for this impiety, you wish it to be reckoned to you as righteousness. Or if these are not persecutions of the prophets, because they are done not with the sword but with the word, what was the reason that it was said by God: *Sons of men, their teeth are weapons and arrows, and their tongue a sharp sword*[^4]?

But when shall I gather from all the prophets all the testimonies of the Church spread throughout the whole world, all of which you try by contradicting to kill and extinguish? But you are bound: because *their sound has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world*[^5]. Nevertheless I will recall that one thing from the mouth of the Lord, who is the witness of witnesses: *It behooved,* he says, *that all things should be fulfilled which were written in the Law and the Prophets and the Psalms about me.* What these are, let him himself indicate: *Then he opened their understanding,* he says, *that they might understand the Scriptures, and said to them: Thus it is written, and thus it behooved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead, and that penance and the remission of sins should be preached in his name among all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem*[^6].

Behold what is written in the Law and the Prophets and the Psalms about the Lord. Behold what the Lord himself has opened up about himself and about the Church, showing himself, promising her. But you, resisting these so manifest testimonies, and trying to corrupt them because you cannot delete them, what would you do if you found the limbs of the prophets, you who so rage against the oracles of the prophets that you do not even hear the Lord, the fulfiller, the revealer, the expounder of the prophets? For as much as you can, you strike him too, when you yourselves do not yield.

[^1]: Ps 22:27 (21:28)
[^2]: Gen 22:18
[^3]: Rom 4:3
[^4]: Ps 57:4 (56:5)
[^5]: Ps 19:4 (18:5)
[^6]: Luke 24:44-47
