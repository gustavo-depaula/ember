# Petilian exhorts the catholics to come to the Church.

**108.** 246. PETILIAN SAID: *Come, then, to the Church, peoples, and flee the traitors, if you do not wish to perish with them. For, that you may easily recognize that, although they themselves are guilty, they judge most favorably of our faith: I baptize their infected ones; they receive mine, which God forbid, as already baptized; which they would by no means do, if they had recognized any faults in our baptism. See, then, what we give, how holy it is, which the sacrilegious enemy fears to destroy.*
