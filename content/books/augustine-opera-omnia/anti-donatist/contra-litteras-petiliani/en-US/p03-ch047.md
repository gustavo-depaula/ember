# Whence comes the power to baptize.

**30.** 70. Petilian said: *For all power is from God*[^1], *not in the man of power*: *as the Lord Jesus Christ answered Pontius Pilate: 'You would have no power over me, unless it were given you from above'*[^2]. *And again, when John says: 'A man can do nothing, unless it be given him from heaven'*[^3]. *Teach, then, betrayer, when you received the power of simulating the mysteries.*

[^1]: Rom 13:1
[^2]: John 19:11
[^3]: John 3:27
