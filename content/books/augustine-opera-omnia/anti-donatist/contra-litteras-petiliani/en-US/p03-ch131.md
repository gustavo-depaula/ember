# Christ persecuted no one.

**80.** 177. PETILIAN SAID: *But I, on the contrary, reply: that Jesus Christ persecuted no one; and when certain sects displeased the apostles, who suggested it to him (for he had come to make faith such that he did not compel men, but rather invited them), those apostles say, Many lay on hands in your name, and are not with us. Jesus said: Let them be; if they are not against you, they are for you*[^1].

[^1]: Luke 9:49-50.
