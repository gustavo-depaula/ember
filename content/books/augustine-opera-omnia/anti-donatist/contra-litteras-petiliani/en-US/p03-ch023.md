# Some accusations against Augustine.

**17.** 38. Petilian said: *Thus, thus, wicked persecutor, with whatever veil of goodness you cover yourself, under whatever name of peace you wage war with kisses, with whatever word of unity you entice the human race; you who hitherto deceive and beguile, you are truly a son of the devil, since by your morals you point to your father.*

**17.** 39. Augustine replied: Reckon these things to have been said by us against you: and that you may know whom they rather fit, recall what I said above.
