# How a small number is to be understood.

**45.** 106. Augustine replied: You feared the multitude of the whole world compared to your multitude, and you wished to betake yourself to the praise of the fewness that walks by the narrow way. Would that you had betaken yourself not to its praise, but to the way itself! you would surely perceive that this same fewness is in the Church of all the nations, but that the just are called few in comparison with the many unjust, just as, in comparison with the chaff, the few grains of a most abundant harvest can be called few, which nevertheless, gathered by themselves into a heap, fill the barn. For just as in the tribulations of lost places, so also in fewness, if you think there is righteousness in these things, the Maximianists themselves will surpass you.
