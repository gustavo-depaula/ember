# On the enemy brothers.

**100.** 229. PETILIAN SAID: *By the fear of God in which we live, we do not fear the punishments and deaths which you inflict with the sword; but this alone, in the end, we flee, that by a most wicked communion you cut the throats of souls, since the Lord himself says: Do not fear those who kill the body, for they cannot kill the soul; but rather fear him who has the power to send body and soul into the hell of fire*[^1].

[^1]: Matt 10:28.
