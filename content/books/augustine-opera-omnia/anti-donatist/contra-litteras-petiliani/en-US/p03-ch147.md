# The example of Peter.

**88.** 194. PETILIAN SAID: *See here for you a most complete proof, that it is not lawful for a Christian to be engaged in the destruction of another. The beginning of this discipline was established for Peter, as it is written: Peter struck the ear of the servant of the chief of the Jews, and cut it off. And Jesus said to him: Peter, put back your sword into its sheath. For whoever uses the sword shall die by the sword*[^1].

[^1]: Matt 26:51-52.
