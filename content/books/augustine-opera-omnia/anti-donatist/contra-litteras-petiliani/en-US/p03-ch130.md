# The Donatists sin against the law.

**79.** 176. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Whoever sins does not sin by the authority of the law, but against the authority of the law. But since you ask what the rationale for persecuting is, I ask you in turn whose voice it is in the Psalm that says: *Him who secretly slandered his neighbor, this man I persecuted*[^1]. Seek, therefore, either the cause or the manner of persecution, and do not, with such ignorance, reproach the persecutors of the wicked in general.

[^1]: Ps 101:5 (100:5).
