# Petilian confesses that even sinners effectively invoke the name of Christ.

**54.** 123. Petilian said: *But if perhaps—which I do not know—you cast out demons, this too will do you no good; because the demons themselves yield neither to your faith nor to your merits, but are cast out in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ.*

**54.** 124. Augustine replied: Thanks be to God, that you have at last confessed that the invoked name of Christ can avail for the salvation of others, even if it be invoked by sinners. Hence, then, understand that, when the name of Christ is invoked, the sins of others do no harm to the salvation of others. But how we invoke the name of Christ, we have no need of your judgment, but of his who is invoked by us. For he alone can know with what heart he is invoked. Yet from his words we are certain that he is wholesomely invoked by all the nations, which are blessed in the seed of Abraham[^1].

[^1]: See Gen 22:18.
