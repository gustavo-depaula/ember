# Augustine replied to this objection.

**8.** 18. Augustine replied: See what difference there is between your slanderous words and our truthful assertions. Attend a little. Behold, you have magnified the crime of betrayal, and in the most odious words, like a copious inventor, you have compared us to the lost Judas.

When I have answered you to this most briefly: I have not done what you say, I have not betrayed, you charge me falsely, you will never prove it—does not all this smoke of huge words suddenly dissolve? Or will you perhaps try to prove it?

This, then, you ought to do first, and then rise up against us, as against the convicted, with whatever heap of invective you wished. Behold one piece of emptiness; hear another.
