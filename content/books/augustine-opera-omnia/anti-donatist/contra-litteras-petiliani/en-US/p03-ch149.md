# To die for Christ profits Christians.

**89.** 196. PETILIAN SAID: *Therefore, I say, he established that death is to be undergone for the faith, rather than to be inflicted on anyone for the sake of communion. For Christianity profits by deaths. For no one most faithful would live, if death were feared by the faithful. For the Lord Christ says: If a grain of wheat falling into the earth does not die, it remains alone; but if it dies, it will bear much fruit*[^1].

[^1]: John 12:24-25.
