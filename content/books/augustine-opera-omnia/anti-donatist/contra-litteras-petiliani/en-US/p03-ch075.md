# Who the blessed man is.

**46.** 108. Augustine replied: Who would not distinguish in the Scriptures these two kinds of men? But you, slanderers, charge the crimes of the chaff even against the wheat; and although you are chaff alone, you boast that you alone are the wheat. But the truthful prophets say that both these kinds are mingled, before the winnowing which will come on the day of judgment, throughout the whole world, that is, throughout the whole field of the Lord.

Nevertheless I advise you to read this first psalm in Greek: so you will not dare to charge against the world, as a crime, the party of Macarius; because perhaps you will understand of which Macarius the party is, in all the holy ones, who throughout all the nations are blessed in the seed of Abraham[^1]. For what is written in Latin, *Blessed is the man;* has in Greek: 'μακάριος ἀνήρ.' But that Macarius who displeases you, if he was evil, is neither in this lot, nor does he harm this lot. But if he was good, let him prove his own work, that he may have glory in himself, and not in another[^2].

[^1]: See Gen 22:18.
[^2]: See Gal 6:4.
