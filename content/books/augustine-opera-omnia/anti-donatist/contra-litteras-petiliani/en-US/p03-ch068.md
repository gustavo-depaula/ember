# The same sacrament can be unto salvation or unto judgment.

**40.** 96. Augustine replied: I recognize the words of the Apostle, but how they help you I do not see at all. For which of us says that there is participation of righteousness with iniquity, even if the just and the unjust, like Judas and Peter, alike share the sacraments? For from one holy thing Judas took for himself judgment, Peter salvation; as you, if you were unlike, took the sacrament with Optatus, and were not a robber like him. Or is robbery not iniquity? Who would be so insane as to say this? What participation, then, had your righteousness with his iniquity, when you approached one altar?

**41.** 97. Petilian said: *And again he taught, lest schisms should arise: 'But I say to you, that each one of you says: I indeed am of Paul; but I of Apollos, but I of Cephas; but I of Christ. Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptized in the name of Paul?'*[^1].

**41.** 98. Augustine replied: Remember, you who read these things: Petilian cites these words from the Apostle. For when would you have believed that he would so speak for us against himself?

**42.** 99. Petilian said: *If Paul said these things to the unformed, to the just; I say these things to you, the unjust: Is Christ divided, that you should cut yourselves off from the Church?*

**42.** 100. Augustine replied: I fear lest anyone in this work of mine think that the scribe erred, and put there, Petilian said; where he ought to have put, Augustine replied. But I see what you did: you wished, as it were, to forestall us, lest we should say these things. But what did you accomplish, except that they are said twice? If, then, it so delights you to hear the things that are against you, that they be said; hear them, I pray, from me, Petilian: Is Christ divided, that you should cut yourselves off from the Church?

[^1]: 1 Cor 1:12-13.
