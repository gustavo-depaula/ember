# Judas was an apostle and was dead.

**8.** 17. Petilian said: *We must act, I say, and speak so as to show how a faithless betrayer is held to be dead in his life. Judas was an apostle when he betrayed Christ; and the same man, having lost the honor of an apostle, died spiritually, destined afterward to die by his own noose, as it is written: 'I repent,' he said, 'that I have betrayed innocent blood; and he went away and hanged himself with a noose'*[^1]. *The betrayer perished by a noose, and left a noose to such men: of whom the Lord Christ cried out to the Father: 'Father, those whom you gave me, I have kept them all; and of them none perished, except the son of perdition, that the Scripture might be fulfilled'*[^2].

*For long ago David had pronounced this sentence against him who would betray Christ to the faithless: 'Let another receive his bishopric; let his wife be a widow, and his children orphans'*[^3]. *See how great is the spirit of the prophets, that it saw all things to come as present, so that a betrayer not to be born for many ages was condemned beforehand. Finally, that the sentence pronounced might be fulfilled, holy Matthias received the bishopric of the apostle who had perished*[^4].

*Let no fool, no faithless man dispute this: Matthias brought back a triumph, not an injury, who brought back from the victory of the Lord Christ the spoil of the betrayer. By this deed, then, why do you claim a bishopric for yourself, heir of a more wicked betrayer?*

*Judas betrayed Christ carnally; you, raging spiritually, betrayed the holy Gospel to the sacrilegious flames. Judas betrayed the lawgiver to the faithless; you, as it were his relics, betrayed the law of God to men to be destroyed: for if you loved the law, you would, like the young Maccabees, be slain for the laws of God (if those whom, by dying for the Lord, he makes immortal can even be called men);*

one of whom, namely of the brothers, answered the sacrilegious tyrant with this voice of faith: 'You indeed, wicked and impious man, destroy us from the present life; but the King of the world, who reigns forever and of whose kingdom there shall be no end, will raise us up, who die for his holy laws, into the way of life eternal'[^5]. *If you burned the testament of a dead man with flames, would you not be punished as a forger? What, then, will become of you, who have burned the most holy law of God the judge? Judas at least repented of his deed in death: you not only do not repent, but, a most wicked betrayer, have shown yourself a persecutor and executioner of us who keep the law.*

[^1]: Matt 27:4-5
[^2]: John 17:12
[^3]: Ps 109:8-9 (108:8-9)
[^4]: See Acts 1:25
[^5]: 2 Macc 7:9
