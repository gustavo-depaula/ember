# A citation of Psalm 23.

**47.** 109. Petilian said: *But the praises of our baptism the same psalmist sang: 'The Lord feeds me, and nothing shall be lacking to me; in a place of pasture there he has set me. By the water of refreshment he has brought me up, he has converted my soul. He has led me upon the paths of righteousness for his name's sake. For even if I walk in the midst of the shadow of death,' if a persecutor, he says, should kill me: 'I will not fear evils; for you are with me, Lord. Your rod and your staff, they have consoled me.' Thence David, armored with the oil of chrism, conquered Goliath. 'You have prepared a table in my sight, against those who afflict me. You have anointed my head with oil, and your inebriating cup, how excellent it is. And your mercy shall follow me all the days of my life, that I may dwell in the house of the Lord, unto length of days'*[^1].

[^1]: Ps 23 (22).
