# Another accusation of Petilian against the Catholics.

**98.** 225. PETILIAN SAID: *You, you, wretches, I call upon, who, terrified by the fear of persecutions, while you seek your riches, not your souls, do not so much love the faithless faith of the traitors as, against the malice of those very men, the patronages which you have procured for yourselves; in no other way than as the shipwrecked in the waves go under waves that are about to fall, and in great peril of life reach for what is feared;*

*or as tyrannical fury, that it may fear no one, wishes to be feared even at its own peril; so, so do you flee to the citadel of malice, about to watch the losses or punishments of the innocent without fear for yourselves.*

*If this is to avoid danger, to flee beneath a ruin; and a damnable faith besides, to keep faith with a robber. Finally, it is the commerce of a madman's gain, to lose your souls, lest you lose your riches. For the Lord Christ says: If you gain the whole world, and lose your soul, what will you give in exchange for your soul?*[^1].

[^1]: Matt 16:26.
