# There is a great difference between those who consent and those who tolerate.

**107.** 245. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: However you may have said them, I do not mind; these things are true, and this is what the catholic Church teaches, that there is a great difference between those who consent, whom these things please, and those who tolerate, whom these things displease. For the former become chaff, while they follow the barrenness of chaff; the latter, since they are wheat, let them wait, that they may be separated, for Christ the winnower[^1].

[^1]: See Matt 13:24-40.
