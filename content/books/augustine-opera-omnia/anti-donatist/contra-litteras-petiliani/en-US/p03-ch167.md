# We neither commit nor command slaughter and deaths.

**93.** 215. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: To this, see what the coheirs of Christ[^1] say throughout the whole earth. We neither commit nor command slaughter and deaths: and you rage much more wickedly against those who do these things, you who commit these things in the minds of men against eternal life.

**94.** 216. PETILIAN SAID: *If you want us as friends, why do you drag us in unwilling? But if you make us out to be enemies, why do you kill your enemies?*

[^1]: See Rom 8:17.
