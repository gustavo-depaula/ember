# The Catholics always act out of love.

**94.** 217. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: We neither drag the unwilling in, nor kill our enemies; but whatever we do with you, although we do it to you while you are unwilling, we do nevertheless out of love, so that, willing, you may correct yourselves, and, corrected, may live.

For no one avoids vices unwilling: and yet a boy, that he may learn this willingly, is beaten unwilling, and this for the most part by one most dear to him. And these things indeed kings would say to you, if they struck; for to this their power is divinely ordained; but since even these do not strike, you cry out.

**95.** 218. PETILIAN SAID: *But what is the rationale, or what inconstancy of vanity is it, that while you call us heretics by a false term, you greatly desire our communion?*
