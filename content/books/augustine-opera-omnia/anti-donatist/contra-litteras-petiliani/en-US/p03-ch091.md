# *You shall not commit adultery.*

**57.** 129. PETILIAN SAID: It is said: *You shall not commit adultery*[^1]. *Each one of you, even if he is chaste in the flesh, is an adulterer in the spirit, because he commits adultery against holiness.*

**57.** 130. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: These things can be said truly against certain people, both ours and yours; and if they displease both of us, they belong neither to us nor to you. But you want what you say against certain people, and do not even prove against those very people, to be taken as if you had proved it, not against some who degenerate from the seed of Abraham, but against all the nations that are blessed in the seed of Abraham[^2].

[^1]: Exod 20:14.
[^2]: See Gen 22:18.
