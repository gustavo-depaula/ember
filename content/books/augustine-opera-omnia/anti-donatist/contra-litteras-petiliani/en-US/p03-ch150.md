# The Donatists persecute the wheat of Christ throughout the whole earth.

**89.** 197. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: I would like to know who first, of your party, hurled himself down. For that grain was indeed very fertile, from which so great a crop of the corpses of those who hurled themselves down sprang up. To be sure, since you remember the words of the Lord, that he said he was a grain about to die and to bear much fruit; why do you envy that very fruit, because it has sprung up most abundantly throughout the whole world; and charge it with the crimes of weeds or chaff, whether what you have heard or what you have fabricated?
