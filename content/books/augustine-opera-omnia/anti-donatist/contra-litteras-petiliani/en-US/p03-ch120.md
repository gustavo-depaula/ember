# The Donatists are wretched heretics.

**74.** 166. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: I could rather say: *O wretched traitors!* if it pleased me, or rather if justice persuaded me, to charge all of you with the deeds of certain of your own. What, then, applies to all of you: O wretched heretics! the rest of your words I might now say; for it is written: *There must be heresies, so that those who are approved may be made manifest among you*[^1]. *It was fitting, then, that Scripture be fulfilled thus; but in you I grieve at this, that you have deserved to fulfill the role of malice.*

[^1]: 1 Cor 11:19.
