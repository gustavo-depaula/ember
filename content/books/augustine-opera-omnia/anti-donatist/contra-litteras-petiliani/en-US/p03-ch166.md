# Correct it, then.

**92.** 213. If I had mentioned these examples about Daniel and the three boys, perhaps you would resist, and cry out that they ought not to be taken from those times and applied to these times of ours. Thanks be to God, that you yourself set them down, for the purpose you wished, indeed, but you see that they have rather availed for the purpose you did not wish.

Is this not your deceit; but is it human error? Would that it were so. Correct it, then; do not fear, you will not be the less for it; rather, it is the mark of greater talent to extinguish the flames of animosity by confessing, than to avoid the clouds of falsehood by understanding.
