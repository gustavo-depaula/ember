# And you, what are you?

**90.** 199. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: At least by the mention of the weeds the wheat too would come to your mind; because both were commanded to grow throughout the field until the harvest[^1]; but you fix the eye of malevolence sharply on the weeds, and, against the sentence of Christ, contend that these alone have grown throughout the whole earth, Africa excepted.

[^1]: See Matt 13:30.
