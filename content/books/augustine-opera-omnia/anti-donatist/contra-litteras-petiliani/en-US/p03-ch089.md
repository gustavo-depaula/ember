# The greatest miracle is love.

**55.** 126. Augustine replied: We recognize the words of the Lord. Hence the Apostle too says: *If I should have faith so as to move mountains, but have not love, I am nothing*[^1]. Here, then, it must be asked who has love; you will find that there are none except those who love unity. For about the casting out of demons, and the power of miracles, since very many do not do such things and yet belong to the kingdom of God, while very many do them and do not belong, neither our people nor yours ought to boast, if any perhaps can do these things; since the Lord did not wish even the apostles, who truly could do these things wholesomely and usefully, to rejoice in such things, when he said to them: *Do not rejoice in this, that the demons are subject to you, but rejoice that your names are written in heaven*[^2]. Therefore these things which you set down from the Gospel, I too could say to you, if I saw you working the powers of signs; and you to me, if you saw me doing such things.

Let us not, then, say to one another the things which can be said from the other side as well; and, putting aside circumlocutions, since we are inquiring where the Church of Christ is, let us hear him who redeemed her with his own blood, saying: *You shall be witnesses to me in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and unto the whole earth*[^3]. Whoever is not in communion with this Church, which is spread throughout the whole earth, you see with whom he is not in communion, if you hear whose these words are. But what is more demented than to be in communion with the sacraments of the Lord, and not to be in communion with the words of the Lord? These men will certainly say: *In your name we ate and drank*; and will hear: *I never knew you*[^4]; who eat and drink his body and blood in the sacrament, and do not recognize his members spread throughout the whole world in the Gospel, and therefore are not counted among them in the judgment.

[^1]: 1 Cor 13:2.
[^2]: Luke 10:20.
[^3]: Acts 1:8.
[^4]: Matt 7:22.
