# Paul, a persecutor, was healed by baptism.

**21.** 47. Petilian said: *Therefore, as we said, the Lord Christ cried out to Paul: 'Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me? It is hard for you to kick against the goad.' But Paul said: 'Who are you, Lord?' And it was answered him: 'I am Christ the Nazarene, whom you persecute.' And he, trembling and astonished, said: 'Lord, what do you wish me to do?' And the Lord to him: 'Rise, and enter the city, and you will be told what you must do.' And, after some words: 'Saul rose from the ground, and when his eyes were opened he saw nothing.' O avenging blindness of frenzy, you cloud over the light of the eyes of the persecutor, destined to depart by baptism alone!*

*Let us see, then, what he did in the city. 'Ananias entered,' it says, 'to Saul, and when he had laid his hands on him, he said: Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus has sent me, who appeared to you on the way by which you were coming, that you may see and be filled with the Holy Spirit. At once there fell from his eyes something like scales, and he received his sight, and rising he was baptized'*[^1]. *Since, therefore, Paul, freed by baptism from the crime of persecuting, received innocent eyes; why are you, a persecutor and betrayer, blind by a false baptism, unwilling to be baptized by those whom you pursue?*

[^1]: Acts 9:4-6, 8, 17-18
