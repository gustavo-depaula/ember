# Christ commanded us love.

**75.** 167. PETILIAN SAID: *But to us the Lord Christ has commanded, against your savagery, a simple patience and innocence. For what does he say? A new commandment I give you, that you love one another, as I have loved you; and: By this all will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another*[^1].

[^1]: John 13:34-35.
