# What Catholic baptism is.

**26.** 60. Petilian said: *For if you mingle false things with true, falsehood often imitates the truth in the very same footprints. Thus, thus the painting of nature simulates a man and expresses the true with colors—false faces of the truth.*

*Thus, thus the brightness of a mirror seizes the face, so as to call back the eyes of the one looking: thus, thus it offers to those who come their own faces, so that the very face of the one coming meets itself in turn: and so great is the power of a clean deception, that the very eyes which see through things recognize themselves, as it were, in another. The image of a shadow too, when it has stood still, doubles the thing, in great part dividing unity by a lie.*

*Is it then so true, because the figure lies? But it is one thing to paint a man, another to beget him. For who fashions false infants for a father longing for sons? Or who would look for true heirs by the lie of a painting? Surely it belongs to a demented mind, having dismissed what is true, to love a painting.*
