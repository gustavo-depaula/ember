# Like Judas the betrayer, you do not have peace and baptism.

**22.** 49. Petilian said: *But indeed you steadily object that Christ said to the apostles: 'He who has once been washed has no need except to wash his feet, but is wholly clean.' If you discuss these words fully, you are bound by what follows. For he spoke thus, saying: 'He who has once been washed has no need except to wash his feet, but is wholly clean. And you are clean, but not all'*[^1]. *But he said this on account of Judas, who was going to betray him. Whoever, then, you became a betrayer, you have lost baptism. Finally, after the betrayer, the apostle of Christ, was himself condemned, he confirmed the eleven thus more fully: 'Now you are clean, on account of the word which I have spoken to you. Abide in me, and I in you'*[^2]. *And again he said to these eleven: 'My peace I give to you, my peace I leave with you'*[^3]. *Since, then, these things were said to the eleven apostles, the betrayer being condemned, as we said, you betrayers likewise do not have peace and baptism.*

[^1]: John 13:10
[^2]: John 15:3-4
[^3]: John 14:27
