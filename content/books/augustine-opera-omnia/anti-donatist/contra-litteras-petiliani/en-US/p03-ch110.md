# Peace must be sought.

**69.** 155. PETILIAN SAID: *Since the apostle Paul says: I beg you, brothers, I a prisoner in the Lord, that you walk worthily of the calling to which you have been called, with all humility and meekness, with patience bearing with one another in love, eager to keep the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace*[^1].

[^1]: Eph 4:1-3.
