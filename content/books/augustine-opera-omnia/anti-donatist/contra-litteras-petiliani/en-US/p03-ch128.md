# Those who have love rejoice with the truth.

**78.** 174. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: How often shall I have to say to you: these things, if you do not prove them, apply to no one; but if you do prove them, they do not apply to us, just as the things your own people commit daily do not apply to you—through the crimes of the frenzied, through the debaucheries of the drunken, through the blindness of those who hurl themselves down, through the tyranny of plunderers? Who would not see that what I cry out is true? But now, if there were love in you, it would rejoice with the truth. For how elegantly it is said, under the sheep's skin: *Love bears all things, endures all things*[^1]! But when it comes to the test, the wolf's teeth cannot be hidden.

For when love—because of what was said: *Bearing with one another in love, eager to keep the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace*[^2]—would compel you, even if you knew of some evils within, not indeed to consent to them, but nevertheless, if you could not prevent them, to bear with them, lest on account of the wicked, who are to be separated by the final winnowing, you now break the bond of peace from the fellowship of the good;

shaken outside by the wind of fickleness, you charge the wheat with the crime of the chaff, and you assert that what you fabricate about the wicked holds, through contagion, even in the good. And though the Lord said: *The field is this world, and the harvest is the end of the age*[^3]*;* and when he had spoken of the grain and the weeds, said: *Let both grow until the harvest*[^4]; you labor with your words to make it believed that the grain has now failed throughout the whole field, and has remained in your scanty little part; while you would have Christ held a liar, but yourself truthful.

And indeed you speak against your own conscience; for no one truly, looking in any way at the Gospel, dares to say in his heart that, throughout so many nations in which with almost one voice *Amen* is answered, and *Alleluia* is sung[^5], there are no Christians. And yet, lest the party of Donatus, which does not communicate with the whole world, seem to be in error, if some angel from heaven, who could behold the whole world, were to tell you that outside your communion there are nowhere any innocent and good people, you would without doubt rejoice over the iniquity of the human race, and would boast that you had spoken the truth before you knew it. How then is there love in you, which *does not rejoice over iniquity*? But do not be deceived: there are throughout the field, that is, throughout the world, the Lord's grain growing until the end of the age. Christ said this: Christ is the truth[^6]. Let there be love in you, and let it rejoice with the truth. Against the Gospel, even if an angel from heaven should preach a gospel, let him be anathema[^7].

[^1]: 1 Cor 13:7.
[^2]: Eph 4:2-3.
[^3]: Matt 13:38-39.
[^4]: Matt 13:30.
[^5]: See Tob 13:22.
[^6]: See John 14:6.
[^7]: See Gal 1:8.
