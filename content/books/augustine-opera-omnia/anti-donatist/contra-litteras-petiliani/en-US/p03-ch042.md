# An image is not the truth.

**28.** 64. Petilian said: *But, to make some comparison, a double sun seems to appear to madmen, although a dark-blue cloud often comes together, and its discolored face, struck by the splendor, while the sun's rays return from it, seems to emit its own rays: thus, thus in the faith of baptism it is one thing to seek images, another to recognize the truth.*
