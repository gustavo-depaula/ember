# What the name 'Catholic' signifies.

**38.** 91. Augustine replied: I indeed have grasped very little of the Greek language, and almost nothing; yet I do not say shamelessly that I know "holon" to mean not "one," but "whole"; and "kath holon," "according to the whole"; whence the Catholic Church received its name, the Lord himself saying: *It is not yours to know the times which the Father has set in his own power, but you shall receive the power of the Holy Spirit coming upon you, and you shall be witnesses to me in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and unto the whole earth*[^1]. Behold whence she is called Catholic.

But you so, with closed eyes, stumble against the mountain, which, from a small stone, according to the prophecy of Daniel, grew and filled the whole earth[^2], that you say to us, who have withdrawn into a part and are not in the whole—we whose communion is spread throughout the whole world. But just as, if you should say to me that I am Petilian, I would not find how to refute you, except either to laugh at you as joking or to grieve over you as raving; this I see I must now do; but because I do not believe you are joking, you see what remains.

[^1]: Acts 1:7-8.
[^2]: See Dan 2:35.
