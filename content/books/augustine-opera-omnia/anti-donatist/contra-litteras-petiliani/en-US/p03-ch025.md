# One must prove, not speak emptily.

**18.** 41. Augustine replied: See how you have set down the testimonies of the Scriptures, or how you have understood them; it is nothing to the matter now at issue. For surely you said all these things to this end, to prove that there are false bishops, just as there are false angels and false apostles.

We too know that there are false angels and false apostles and false bishops, and, as the true apostle says, *false brothers*[^1]; but since these things can be hurled by both sides against each other, one must prove something, not speak emptily. Yet whom the charge of deceit fits, recall what we said above, and you will find—lest we be a weariness to our readers by repeating the same things too often.

And yet what is this to the Church spread throughout the whole world—whether what you could say about her chaff, which is with her throughout the whole world, or what you said about the Manichee, or about the other diabolical sects? For if whatever is also said about the chaff which is still with it does not pertain to the wheat, how much less do these monstrosities, so long ago and so openly separated, pertain to the members of Christ spread throughout the whole world!

[^1]: See 2 Cor 11:26
