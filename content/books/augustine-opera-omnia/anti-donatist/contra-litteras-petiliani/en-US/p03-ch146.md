# The Donatists do not follow the form of Christ, because they persecute.

**87.** 193. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Would that your martyrs followed his form; they would not hurl themselves down; which he, at the devil's suggestion, did not do[^1]. But you, when you persecute even our deceased elders with false witness, where did you receive this form? That you try to stain us with the crimes of unknown men, while you refuse to let the most notorious misdeeds of your own people count against you, where did you receive this form?

But we are far too proud if we grow angry on our own account, when we see you giving false witness against the Lord himself; since he both promised and exhibited his Church throughout all the nations[^2]; and you contradict it. This form you did not receive even from the very Jews who were persecutors: for they persecuted the flesh of him who walked on earth; you, the Gospel of him who sits in heaven. Which Gospel endured the flames of raging kings more mildly than it endures your tongues: for while they were burning it, unity remained; while you speak, it could not remain. Those who desired the words of the Lord to be abolished by burning did not believe that, once read, they could be despised. They, then, would not have exercised their flames against the Gospel, if you had been permitted to use your tongues against the Gospel before them.

In that persecution the Gospel of Christ was sought by some who raged, betrayed by some who feared, burned by some who tore it out, hidden by some who loved it; by none who contradicted it was it assailed; you have reserved for yourselves the more wicked parts of persecution, now that the persecution of the nations is past. Those who persecuted the name of Christ did not believe in Christ; those who are honored for the name of Christ contradict Christ.

[^1]: See Matt 4:6-7.
[^2]: See Luke 24:47.
