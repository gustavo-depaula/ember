# The true death is your separation from the Church.

**23.** 52. Augustine replied: First, we answer briefly, that we do not kill you, but you yourselves kill yourselves with a true death, when you cut yourselves off from the living root of unity. Next, if all who are killed are baptized in their own blood, then all robbers, the wicked, the impious, the criminal, who, condemned, are struck down, must be held martyrs, because they are baptized in their own blood. But if none are baptized in their own blood except those who are killed for the sake of righteousness; because *theirs is the kingdom of heaven*[^1]: see that one must first inquire for what you suffer, and afterward what you suffer. Why, then, are the cheeks puffed out before the deeds are defended? Why does the tongue make a din before the life is proved? If you have made a schism, you are impious: if you are impious, you die as a sacrilegious man, when you are punished for impiety: if you die as a sacrilegious man, how are you baptized in your own blood? Or do you say: *I did not make a schism*? Then let us inquire into this: why do you cry out before you prove it?

[^1]: Matt 5:10
