# Petilian is slanderous, not truthful.

**32.** 73. Augustine replied: You are that slanderous reviler, not a truthful disputant. Will you not at some time cease to say such things, which, if you do not prove them, pertain to no one; but if you prove them, do not at all pertain to the unity of the whole world, which is in the holy ones as in the grain? If it should please us too to repay curses for curses, perhaps we too can revile eloquently. We too can say: *With crackling flames:* but what is said ineptly in no way sounds to me eloquent. We too can say: *Burning with hungry points:* but we do not wish the points of our writings, when they are read by anyone sound, to be judged starved of the sap of seriousness, and the reader himself, while he is fed by no useful sentences, to labor under a superfluous fast.

Behold, I say that your Circumcellions burn not with crackling but with headlong flames of fury. If you reply: What is that to us? why, when you have charged whomever you wished, will you not in turn hear: And we do not know them? If you reply: You do not prove it; why should not the whole world reply to you in turn: Neither do you prove it? Let us make a pact, then, if it please you, that neither you charge against us the wicked you suppose to be ours, nor I against you yours. Thus you will see that, by this pact, so just an agreement being settled and confirmed, you have nothing to charge against the seed of Abraham in all the nations. And yet I plainly find something great to charge against you: Why, then, did you impiously separate yourselves from the seed of Abraham, which is in all the nations? This, certainly, you have no way to defend. For we each clear ourselves of the crimes of others: but this, that you are not in communion with all the nations which are blessed in the seed of Abraham, is a great crime, not of certain of you, but of all.
