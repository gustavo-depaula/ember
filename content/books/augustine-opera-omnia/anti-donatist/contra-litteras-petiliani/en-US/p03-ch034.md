# Even with Judas present, the apostles were not contaminated.

**22.** 50. Augustine replied: If, then, a betrayer has lost baptism, whoever, baptized by you, afterward became a betrayer, if he should wish to return to you, let him be baptized again. But if you do not do this, you yourselves sufficiently indicate that what was said is false: *If, then, you became a betrayer, you have lost baptism.* For if he lost it, let him return and receive it: but if he returns and does not receive it, he had not lost it. Next, if it was said to the apostles, *Now you are clean*[^1]; and, *My peace I give to you*[^2], because the betrayer had already departed thence: then that supper of so great a sacrament, which he gave to all before that man went out, was not clean and peaceable.

But if you dare to say this with closed eyes, what shall we do but rather cry out: O avenging blindness of frenzy of those who wish to *be teachers of the law,* as the Apostle says, *understanding neither what they say nor the things about which they make assertions*[^3]? Yet, unless the blindness of obstinacy hindered, the understanding was not difficult, that you might see that the Lord did not say, with Judas present: You are not yet clean; but: *Now you are clean.*

But he added, *not all;* because there was there one who was not clean: who, however, if by his presence he polluted the rest, it would not be said to them: *Now you are clean:* but it would be said, as I said: You are not yet clean. But after that man departed, he said to them: *Now you are clean;* nor did he add: But not all; because he had now departed, even with whom present, as was said to them, they were clean, but not all, because there was there one unclean man. Therefore by these words the Lord rather declared that, in one assembly of men receiving the same sacraments, the uncleanness of some cannot harm the clean.

Certainly, if you think there are among us men like Judas, say these words to us: *You are clean, but not all.* But you do not say this: rather, on account of certain unclean men, you say: You are all unclean. The Lord did not say this to the disciples with Judas present: and therefore he who says this has not learned what he says from the good Master[^4].

[^1]: John 15:3
[^2]: John 14:27
[^3]: 1 Tim 1:7
[^4]: See Luke 18:18
