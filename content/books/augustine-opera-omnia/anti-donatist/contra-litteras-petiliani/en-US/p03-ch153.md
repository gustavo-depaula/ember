# Christians suffer persecutions as Christ did.

**91.** 200. PETILIAN SAID: *Where is that which the Lord Christ says: If you receive a slap, prepare the other cheek as well*[^1]? *Where is that which he who opened the eyes of the blind man with his most holy spittle suffered—spittle in the face? Where is that which the apostle Paul says: If anyone strikes you in the face? Where is that which he says again: In stripes beyond measure, in deaths often, in prisons more abundantly*[^2]? *He recounts what he endured, not what he did. It was enough for the Christian faith that these things be done to it by the Jews: why, wretches, do you perpetrate these things?*

[^1]: Matt 5:39.
[^2]: 2 Cor 11:20-23.
