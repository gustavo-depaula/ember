# The Lord's law is to be followed: *you shall not kill*.

**56.** 127. PETILIAN SAID: *But even granting that you follow the Lord's law purely, as you yourselves suppose, let us argue lawfully from that most holy law itself. The apostle Paul says: The law is good, if one uses it lawfully*[^1]. *What then does the law say? You shall not kill*[^2]*. What Cain the murderer did once, you have done to your brothers again and again.*

**56.** 128. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: We do not want to be like you, for there is no shortage of things that can be said, as you too say these things, nor of things that can be known, for these things you do not know, nor of things that can be shown, for these things you do not show.

[^1]: 1 Tim 1:8.
[^2]: Exod 20:13.
