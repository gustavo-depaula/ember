# Petilian does not see the whole reality of the world.

**39.** 93. Augustine replied: To hurl these words about and prove nothing, what is it but to rave? You attend to the weeds throughout the world, and do not attend to the wheat; although both were commanded to grow throughout the whole. You attend to the seed of the evil one, which at the time of harvest will be separated[^1]; and you do not attend to the seed of Abraham, in which all the nations are blessed[^2].

As though, indeed, you were already a purified lump, and pure honey, and clarified oil, and pure gold, or the very appearance of a whitewashed wall. For, to be silent about the rest of the vices, do drunkards make a part of the sober, or are the greedy reckoned in the part of the wise? If meek men hold the name of light; where, except in the darkness, will the fury of the Circumcellions be reckoned? Why, then, does baptism given through such men avail among you, and the same baptism of Christ, given through anyone whatever in the whole world, not avail? You see, surely, that you have been separated from the communion of the whole world to this end, that you are not indeed all drunkards, nor all greedy, nor all violent, but all heretics, and through this all impious and all sacrilegious.

[^1]: See Matt 13:24-30, 36, 43.
[^2]: See Gen 22:18.
