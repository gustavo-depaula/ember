# How do those baptize in the name of the Trinity who are in communion with betrayers?

**33.** 77. Petilian said: *But that I may fully discuss the baptism of the Trinity, the Lord Christ said to his apostles: 'Go, baptize the nations in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to keep all things whatsoever I command you'*[^1].

*Whom do you teach, betrayer? whom do you condemn? Whom do you teach, betrayer? whom do you kill? Finally, whom do you teach? or whom have you made a murderer? How, then, do you baptize in the name of the Trinity? You cannot call God Father. For since the Lord Christ said: 'Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God'*[^2]; *you who do not have peace of soul, do not have God for a father.*

*And how do you baptize in the name of the Son, you who betray and do not imitate the Son of God himself by any sufferings or crosses? And how do you baptize in the name of the Holy Spirit; since the Holy Spirit came upon those apostles who had not been betrayers? Since, then, God is not your begetter, neither are you truly born by the water of baptism; not one of you is born at all, nor do you have a father, impious ones, nor a mother. Ought I, then, to baptize you, men of this kind, even if, as the Jews, as it were, baptize the flesh, you should wash yourselves a thousand times?*

[^1]: Matt 28:19-20
[^2]: Matt 5:9
