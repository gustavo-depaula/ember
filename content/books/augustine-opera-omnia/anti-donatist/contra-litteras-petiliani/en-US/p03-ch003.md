# Some accusations of Petilian and Augustine's replies.

**2.** 4. Petilian said: *Those people charge us with administering baptism twice who under the name of baptism have polluted their own souls with a guilty washing—men beside whose foulness all filth is cleaner, whom by a perverse purity it has befallen to be defiled by their own water.*

**2.** 5. Augustine replied: We are neither defiled by our water nor cleansed by yours: but the water of baptism, when it is given to someone in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, is neither ours nor yours, but his of whom it was said to John: *He upon whom you see the Spirit descending like a dove and remaining upon him, this is he who baptizes in the Holy Spirit*[^1].

**3.** 6. Petilian said: *For the conscience of the giver is what is regarded, the conscience that cleanses that of the receiver.*

**3.** 7. Augustine replied: Of the conscience of Christ, then, we are secure. For if you set down any human being, the cleansing of the receiver will be uncertain, because the conscience of the one who cleanses is uncertain.

**4.** 8. Petilian said: *For he who receives faith from one who is faithless receives not faith but guilt.*

**4.** 9. Augustine replied: Christ is not faithless; from him the faithful man receives faith, not guilt. For he believes in him who justifies the ungodly, *that his faith may be reckoned to him as righteousness*[^2].

**5.** 10. Petilian said: *For everything consists of an origin and a root; and if it has not something for a head, it is nothing: nor does anything rightly regenerate unless it is regenerated by good seed.*

[^1]: John 1:33
[^2]: See Rom 4:5
