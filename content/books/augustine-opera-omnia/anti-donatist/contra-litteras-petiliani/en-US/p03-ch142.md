# Augustine: but we are not schismatics.

**85.** 189. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Let your Circumcellions be at rest, and do not frighten us with barbarians. But whether we are schismatics or you, let neither me nor you, but Christ be questioned, that he may point out his Church. Read, then, the Gospel; and it answers you: *In Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the whole earth*[^1]. Whoever, then, is not found in the Church, let him no longer be questioned, but either, corrected, let him be converted, or, rebuked, let him not complain.

[^1]: Acts 1:8.
