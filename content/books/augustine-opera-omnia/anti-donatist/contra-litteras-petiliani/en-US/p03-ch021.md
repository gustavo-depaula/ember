# The Donatists are persecutors of souls, because they divide the Church.

**15.** 35. Augustine replied: Their throat is an open grave, whence they breathe out the deaths of lies. For *the mouth that lies kills the soul*[^1]. But if nothing is truer than what Christ said, that his Church is among all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem[^2]; nothing is more lying than what you say, that it is in the party of Donatus. But the tongues are deceitful of those who, although they know their own deeds, not only say that they are righteous men, but even justifiers of men: which was said of the one: *who justifies the ungodly*[^3]: and that he is *righteous and justifying*[^4]. Now about the venom of asps, and the mouth full of cursing and bitterness, we have said enough.

But feet swift to shed blood you said even the Maximianists have: witness the sentence of your plenary council, so often cited in the proconsular and municipal records. Yet those men, so far as we hear, killed no one bodily. You understood, therefore, that blood is shed even by the spiritual slaughter of souls, by the sword of schism, which you condemned in Maximian. See, then, whether your feet are not swift to shed blood, when you cut men off from the unity of the whole world, if you rightly said this of the Maximianists, because they cut some off from the party of Donatus.

Have we then not known the way of peace[^5], who strive to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace[^6]; and do you know it, who so resist the discourse that Christ had with his disciples after the resurrection—so peaceable that he began from it, saying, *Peace be with you*[^7]—that you are convicted of saying nothing else to him than this: What you said about the unity of all the nations is false, what we say about the crime of all the nations is true? Who would say these things, if there were the fear of God before their eyes? See, therefore, whether by saying these things daily you are not trying to finish off, like a morsel of bread, with your teeth, the people of God spread throughout the whole world.

**16.** 36. Petilian said: *The Lord Christ also warns: 'Beware of false prophets, who come to you in the clothing of sheep, but inwardly are ravening wolves: by their fruits you shall know them'*[^8].

[^2]: See Luke 24:47
[^3]: Rom 4:5
[^4]: Rom 3:26
[^5]: See Ps 14:3 (13:3); Isa 59:7-8
[^6]: See Eph 4:3
[^7]: John 20:19
[^8]: Matt 7:15-16
