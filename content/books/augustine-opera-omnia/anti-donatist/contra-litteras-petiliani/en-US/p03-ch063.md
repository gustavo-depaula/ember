# Heretics are to be sought because they are heretics.

**37.** 89. But now, as for what you think you say so elegantly: *If we do this wrongly, why do you seek us?* do you not at some time recall that none are sought except those who have perished? Or does not to fail to see even this belong to that very perdition? For that sheep too could so absurdly say to the shepherd: If I do wrong in straying from the flock, why do you seek me? not understanding that the very reason why it thinks it ought not to be sought is the one reason why it is sought.

But who seeks you—whether through his Scriptures, or through Catholic and peaceable tongues, or through the scourges of temporal tribulations—except he who dispenses this mercy to you in all things? We seek you, then, that we may find you: for we love you, that you may live, as much as we hate your error, that it may perish which destroys you, so long as it itself does not perish. And would that we might so seek you as even to find you, and joyfully say of each one of you: *He was dead, and has come to life again; he had perished, and is found*[^1].

**38.** 90. Petilian said: *If you say that you hold the Catholic Church, "Catholic" is what in Greek is called "single" or "whole." Behold, you are not in the whole, because you have withdrawn into a part.*

[^1]: Luke 15:32.
