# Petilian: you burden your souls with the crimes of the guilty.

**101.** 231. PETILIAN SAID: *You, then, who by a most false baptism wish to be washed rather than to be born, not only do not lay aside your own faults, but you even burden your souls with the crimes of the guilty. For just as the water of the guilty has been emptied of the Holy Spirit, so it is plainly filled with the crimes of traitors. Whoever, then, wretch, you are baptized by such a one, if you wished to be free of lying, you are drenched in falsehood. If you wished to shut out the faults of the flesh, with the conscience of the guilty added you will receive their guilt too. If you wished to extinguish the flame of avarice, you are drenched in fraud, drenched in crime, drenched also in fury. Finally, if you believe that the faith both of the one receiving and of the one giving is at stake, by him who kills a man you are drenched in a brother's blood. So it comes about that you who had come innocent to baptism, return from baptism a parricide.*
