# Petilian refuses the miracles of the Catholics.

**55.** 125. Petilian said: *Even if you should perform powers and wonders outright, on account of your wickedness not even so does the Lord know you, the Lord himself saying: 'Many will say to me on that day: Lord, Lord, in your name we prophesied, and in your name we cast out demons, and in your name we did many powers. Then I will say to them, I never knew you; depart from me, workers of iniquity'*[^1].

[^1]: Matt 7:22-23.
