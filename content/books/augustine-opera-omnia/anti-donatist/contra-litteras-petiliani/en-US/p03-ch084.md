# The sacrifice becomes for each such as is the one who approaches to offer it.

**52.** 120. Augustine replied: We say that the sacrifice becomes for each such as is the one who approaches to offer it, and such as is the one who approaches to receive it; and that those eat of the sacrifices of such men who approach them being such as those men also are; and so, if an evil man offers to God, and a good man receives from it, it becomes for each such as each one has been; because this too is written: *All things are clean to the clean*[^1].

By this truthful and Catholic judgment, you too are not polluted by the sacrifice of Optatus, if his deeds were displeasing to you. For surely his bread was the bread of mourning, under whose iniquities all Africa mourned; but the bread of mourning, common to all of you, the evil of the schism of you all makes. For according to the sentence of your council, Felicianus of Musti shed human blood.

For you said, when you condemned them: *Their feet are swift to shed blood*[^2]. See, then, what sort of sacrifice he offers whom you have for a priest, since you condemned him as sacrilegious; and if you think this does you no harm, I ask you, what harm will the emptiness of your calumnies do to the world?

[^1]: Tit 1:15.
[^2]: Ps 14:3 (13:3); Rom 3:15.
