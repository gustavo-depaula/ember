# Schismatics are sons of the devil.

**13.** 30. Augustine replied: We are not accustomed to read: *He was an accuser;* but: *He was a murderer*. And we ask whence the devil was a murderer from the beginning; and we find that he killed the first man, not by drawing a sword or by bodily inflicting some violence, but by persuading him to sin and casting him down from the happiness of paradise. What paradise was then, the Church is now. They, therefore, are sons of the devil who kill men by seducing them away from the Church. But as through the words of God we have come to know where paradise was planted, so through the words of Christ we have learned where the Church is: *Among all the nations,* he says, *beginning from Jerusalem*[^1].

Whoever separates a man from this whole to some party, that man is convicted of being a son of the devil and a murderer. But also the word which you yourself set down, to say of the devil, *He was an accuser, and did not stand in the truth,* see whom it fits. For you accuse the whole world with the crimes of others, whom even you yourselves were able rather to accuse than to convict; and you did not stand in the truth of Christ. For he says that the Church is among all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem; but you say it is in the party of Donatus.

[^1]: Luke 24:47
