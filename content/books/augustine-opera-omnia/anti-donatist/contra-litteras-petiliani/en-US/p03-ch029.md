# Augustine: You persecuted the Maximianists.

**20.** 45. Augustine replied: Defend yourselves from the persecution which those who, together with Maximian, separated themselves from you suffered at the hands of your own people; and there you will find our defense. For if you say that you did not do it, we read out the proconsular and municipal records. If you say that you did rightly to them, why are you unwilling to suffer such things yourselves? If you say: But we did not make a schism; then let this be inquired into, and before it is established whether it is so, let no one accuse persecutors.

If you say that schismatics too ought not to suffer persecution: I ask whether they ought not also to be expelled, through ordained powers, from the basilicas in which they lie in wait to seduce the weak? If you say that this too ought not to be: first restore the basilicas to the Maximianists, and then deal with us. If you say it ought to be: now inquire what they ought to suffer from the ordained powers, who by resisting them resist the ordinance of God[^1].

Whence the Apostle plainly says: *For not without cause does he bear the sword; for he is an avenger in wrath upon him who does evil*[^2]. But if, the truth being carefully handled, it should altogether be found that schismatics ought neither to suffer anything through public judgments, nor to be expelled from the places of their ambushes and deceits; and you should say that it displeases you that the Maximianists suffered these things from certain of your people: why do the Lord's grain not cry out, more freely, throughout the whole field of the Lord, that is, throughout the whole world: What the weeds or our chaff commit does not pertain to us, because it displeases us?

If you confess that it suffices for your cleansing, that whatever evil is done by your people displeases you: why, then, did you separate yourselves? For your very defense accuses you on this point. For if you do not separate yourselves from the wicked of the party of Donatus, because each one *bears his own burden*[^3]; why did you separate yourselves from the wicked of the whole world, whom you suppose or pretend to exist? Was it so that you might all alike bear the burden of schism?

[^1]: See Rom 13:2
[^2]: Rom 13:4
[^3]: Gal 6:5
