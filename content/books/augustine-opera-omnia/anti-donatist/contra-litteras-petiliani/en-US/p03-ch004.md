# Christ is the head, origin, and root of the baptized.

**5.** 11. Augustine replied: Why do you wish to set yourself up in place of Christ, under whom you are unwilling to place yourself? He is the origin and root and head of the one being born, of whom we have no fear, as we do of any human being, lest perhaps he be a pretender and utterly evil, and we spring from an utterly evil origin, rise from an utterly evil root, be conformed to an utterly evil head. For what man can be secure about a man, when it is written: *Cursed is everyone who places his hope in man*[^1]? But the seed by which we are regenerated is the word of God, that is, the Gospel. Hence the Apostle says: *For in Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the Gospel*[^2].

Yet he permits even those who do not chastely proclaim the Gospel to proclaim it, and rejoices in this[^3], because, although they proclaimed it unchastely, seeking their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ[^4], nevertheless what they proclaimed was chaste. And the Lord had said about certain such men: *What they say, do; but what they do, do not do: for they say, and do not do*[^5]. If, then, what is chaste is chastely proclaimed, the proclaimer himself, because he is joined to the word, begets the believer along with it: but if he himself is not regenerated, yet what he proclaims is chaste, the believer is born not from the barrenness of the minister but from the fruitfulness of the truth.

[^1]: Jer 17:5
[^2]: 1 Cor 4:15
[^3]: See Phil 1:16, 18
[^4]: See Phil 2:21
[^5]: Matt 23:3
