# Citation of Psalm 141:5.

**104.** 238. PETILIAN SAID: *But the ointment of concord he praises among brothers thus: Behold how good and how pleasant it is for brothers to dwell as one. Like the ointment on the head, which runs down upon the beard, the beard of Aaron, which runs down upon the edge of his garment; like the dew of Hermon, which runs down upon Mount Zion. For there the Lord commanded blessing, and life forever*[^1]. *Thus, he says, is unity anointed, as the priests were anointed.*

[^1]: Ps 133:1-3 (132:1-3).
