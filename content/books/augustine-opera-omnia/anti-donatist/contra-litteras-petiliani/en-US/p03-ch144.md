# The Donatists slay all men by hatred.

**86.** 191. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: If God did not will death to be inflicted on the slayer of his brother, but that he be left as the executioner of his own life; see whether perhaps this is the reason that, since the heart of the king is in the hand of God[^1], whence he himself established many laws to admonish and rebuke you, yet no royal law has commanded you to be killed; perhaps for this reason, that whoever of you persist in the stubborn breath of sacrilegious fury may, with the punishment of Cain the murderer, be tormented by a life that is their own executioner.

For we read that many were mercifully slain by Moses, the servant of God; for in this, that he so entreated the Lord on behalf of their wicked sacrilege as to say: *Lord, if you forgive them their sin, forgive it; but if not, blot me out of your book*[^2], his ineffable love and mercy sufficiently appear. Was he then suddenly made cruel, when, coming down from the mountain, he commanded so many thousands to be killed? Consider, therefore, whether perhaps it is by a greater wrath of God that, through so many laws enacted against you, you have been commanded by no emperor to be killed.

Or do you think you are not to be compared to those fratricides? Hear the Lord saying through the prophet: *From the rising of the sun to its setting my name has been glorified among the nations, and in every place incense will be offered to my name, and a pure offering. Great is my name among the nations, says the Lord almighty*[^3]. You show, by your slanders, that you envy this fraternal sacrifice upon which God looks; and whenever you hear that from the rising of the sun to its setting the name of the Lord is praised[^4]—which is the living sacrifice of which it was said: *Offer to God a sacrifice of praise*[^5]—your face falls as that of the murderer's. But because you cannot slay the whole world, you are held by the very hatred of the matter, since John says: *He who hates his brother is a murderer*[^6]. And would that an innocent brother might rather fall to be slaughtered by the weapons of your Circumcellions than to be rebaptized by your tongue!

[^1]: See Prov 21:1.
[^2]: Exod 32:31-32.
[^3]: Mal 1:11.
[^4]: See Ps 113:3 (112:3).
[^5]: Ps 50:14 (49:14).
[^6]: 1 John 3:15.
