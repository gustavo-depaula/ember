# A text of Scripture about persecutors.

**15.** 34. Petilian said: *About you too David says these things against persecutors:* *'Their throat is an open grave, with their tongues they acted deceitfully, the venom of asps is under their lips. Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness, their feet are swift to shed blood. Ruin and misery are in their ways, and the way of peace they have not known; there is no fear of God before their eyes. Will not all those know who work iniquity, who devour my people like a morsel of bread?'*[^1].

[^1]: Ps 14:3-4 (13:3-4)
