# Christ is not dead, but living.

**7.** 15. Augustine replied: Inquire more carefully how the testimony you cited from Scripture was said, and how it is to be understood. For that all the wicked are wont to be called dead in a mystical sense is manifest: but Christ, whose true baptism it is—which on account of a man's vices you call false—lives, seated at the right hand of the Father, and will not die now even by that weakness of the flesh, and death shall no longer have dominion over him[^1]: and whoever is baptized with his baptism is not baptized by a dead man;

and even if perhaps the ministers, deceitful workers seeking their own interests[^2], not those of Jesus Christ[^3], and not chastely proclaiming the Gospel, and preaching Christ out of contention and envy[^4], are to be called dead on account of their iniquities, nevertheless the sacrament of the living God does not die even in a dead man. For that Simon was dead who was baptized by Philip in Samaria, who wished to buy the gift of God with money[^5]: but the baptism he had was alive, to his punishment.

[^1]: See Rom 6:9
[^2]: See 2 Cor 11:13
[^3]: See Phil 2:21
[^4]: See Phil 1:15-17
[^5]: See Acts 8:13, 18-19
