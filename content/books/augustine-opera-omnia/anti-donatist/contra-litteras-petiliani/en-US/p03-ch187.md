# Not to communicate is the same as not to consent.

**106.** 243. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: You err, as the saying goes, by the whole heaven through pride, while you refuse to communicate with the whole earth through humility. For neither was this said to a layman, and you do not know in what way it was said. When the Apostle was writing to Timothy, he was admonishing this same Timothy; to whom in another place he says: *Do not despise the grace that is in you, which was given to you through the laying on of the presbyter's hands*[^1].

And by many other proofs it is shown that he was not a layman. But what he says: *Do not share in the sins of others;* he wished to be understood of consent and approval. Hence, adding at once how this is done: *Keep yourself,* he says, *chaste*[^2]. For neither did Paul himself share in the sins of others, because he tolerated in bodily unity the false brothers over whom he groans[^3]; nor did his predecessors the apostles share in the theft and crime of Judas, because they shared the most holy supper with him when he was already the betrayer of the Lord, and pointed out by the Lord.

**107.** 244. PETILIAN SAID: *And likewise by this sentence the Apostle himself compares the partners of an evil conscience: And those who do these things, he says, and those who consent to those who do such things, are worthy of death*[^4].

[^1]: 1 Tim 4:14.
[^2]: 1 Tim 5:22.
[^3]: See 2 Cor 11:26.
[^4]: Rom 1:32.
