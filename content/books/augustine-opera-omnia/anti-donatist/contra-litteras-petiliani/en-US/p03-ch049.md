# Baptism is one, but consecrated in three degrees.

**32.** 72. Petilian said: *For although baptism is one, it is nevertheless consecrated in three degrees. John gave water without the name of the Trinity, as he himself professed, saying: 'I baptize you in the water of penance: another will come who is stronger than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to carry; he will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire'*[^1]. *Christ gave the Holy Spirit, as it is written: 'He breathed on their faces, and said: Receive the Holy Spirit'*[^2]. *And the fire itself, the Paraclete, blazing with crackling flames, came upon the apostles.*

*O true divinity, which seemed to kindle, not to burn, as it is written: 'There came suddenly from heaven a sound as if the spirit were borne with great force, and it filled the whole house where the apostles were sitting. And there appeared distributed tongues as of fire, and it settled upon each one of them; and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and began to speak in various tongues, as the Holy Spirit gave them utterance'*[^3]. *But you, persecutor, do not have even the water of penance, you who retain the power not of John who was slain, but of Herod the slayer. You, then, betrayer, do not have the Holy Spirit of Christ: for Christ was handed over to death, Christ did not hand anyone over to death. For you there is a fire, lively in the spirit among the dead, which, burning with hungry points, may have power to breathe upon your limbs throughout the age, not to finish them; as it is written about the punishment of the guilty in hell, 'Their fire shall not be extinguished'*[^4].

[^1]: Matt 3:11
[^2]: John 20:22
[^3]: Acts 2:2-4
[^4]: Isa 66:24
