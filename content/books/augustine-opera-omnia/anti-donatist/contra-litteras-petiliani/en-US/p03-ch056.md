# Petilian contradicts himself.

**34.** 80. Augustine replied: Where, then, is what you said above, that the baptism of John was not one thing and that of Christ another, but one baptism consecrated in three degrees, of which three degrees John gave the water, Christ the Spirit, the Paraclete the fire? Why, then, did the apostles repeat the water in those to whom John had already given the water pertaining to one baptism consecrated in three degrees? You see clearly how necessary it is that each one know what he is saying.

**35.** 81. Petilian said: *For the Holy Spirit cannot be implanted in anyone by the laying on of the bishop's hand, unless the begetting water of a pure conscience has gone before.*
