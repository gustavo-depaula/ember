# Your sacrifice is unclean.

**52.** 119. Petilian said: *If you judge that you offer sacrifices, indeed about you most wicked men God himself says thus: 'The criminal,' he says, 'who sacrifices a calf to me, is as one who would kill a dog; and he who offers fine flour, as one who would pour out swine's blood'*[^1]. *Know your sacrifice, you who have already shed human blood. And again: 'Their sacrifices are like the bread of mourning; everyone who shall eat of it shall be defiled'*[^2].

[^1]: Isa 66:3.
[^2]: Hos 9:4.
