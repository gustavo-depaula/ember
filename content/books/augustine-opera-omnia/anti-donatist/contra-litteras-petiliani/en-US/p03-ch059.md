# Did Paul rebaptize?

**37.** 85. Petilian said: *But that these things done by the apostles may shine before us, we are taught by their Acts, as it is written: 'Now it came to pass, while Apollos was at Corinth, that Paul, having passed through the upper parts, came to Ephesus. And when he had found certain disciples, he said to them: Have you received the Holy Spirit since you believed? But they said: But we have not even heard whether there is a Holy Spirit. And Paul said: In what, then, were you baptized? And they answered, saying: In the baptism of John. But Paul said: John baptized with the baptism of penance, telling the people to believe in him who was to come after him, that is, in Jesus Christ our Lord. On hearing this, they were baptized in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. And when Paul had laid his hands on them, the Holy Spirit came upon them. And they spoke in various tongues, and prophesied. And they were all about twelve men'*[^1]. *If, then, those men were baptized that they might receive the Holy Spirit, why do you not, if you wish to receive the Holy Spirit, after your lies receive the true renewal? But if we do this wrongly, why do you seek us? Or, certainly, if it is a crime, condemn Paul first; who indeed washed what already was, but we baptize in you what is not yet. For you do not, as we have often already said, dip with true baptism, but defame it with the empty name of a false baptism.*

[^1]: Acts 19:2-7
