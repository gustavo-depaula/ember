# The Donatists caused much mourning, shouting "Praises to God."

**65.** 146. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Consider for a moment to how many, and how great a mourning, the *Praises to God* of your armed men have brought. Say again: What does that have to do with us? And I will say again, and you say what you say: What does it have to do with us? What does it have to do with the whole world? What does it have to do with those who praise the name of the Lord from the rising of the sun to its setting[^1]? What does it have to do with the whole earth that sings a new song[^2]? What does it have to do with the seed of Abraham, in which all the nations are blessed[^3]? Therefore the sacrilege of the schism has to do with you precisely because the evil deeds of your own people have nothing to do with you, and from this you understand that the deeds of those on whose account you separated yourselves from there have nothing to do with the whole world either, even if you could prove them.

**66.** 147. PETILIAN SAID: *Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be satisfied*[^4]*. For you this is righteousness, to thirst for our blood.*

**66.** 148. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: What shall I say to you, man, except that you are a slanderer? Indeed the unity of Christ hungers and thirsts for you: would that it might swallow you up; you would not be heretics.

**67.** 149. PETILIAN SAID: *Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy*[^5]*. When could I call you merciful, since you inflict punishments on the righteous? Or your most wicked communion, since you defile souls?*

[^1]: See Ps 113:3 (112:3).
[^2]: See Ps 96:1 (95:1).
[^3]: See Gen 22:18.
[^4]: Matt 5:6.
[^5]: Matt 5:7.
