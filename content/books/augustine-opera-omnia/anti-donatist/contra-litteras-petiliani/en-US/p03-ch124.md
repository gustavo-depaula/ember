# But Paul bore with them.

**76.** 170. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Surely these are the false brothers about whom, in another place, the same apostle complains thus, while proclaiming the genuine sincerity of Timothy: *I have no one,* he says, *of like mind, who is genuinely concerned for you; for all seek their own, not the things of Jesus Christ*[^1]. He was certainly speaking of those who, at the time when he was writing that letter, were with him. For not all who were Christians anywhere sought their own, not the things of Jesus Christ.

About these, then, as I said, he groaned, whom he had with him at the time when he was writing this. For whom else does he touch on, when elsewhere he says: *Fightings without, fears within*[^2]; except those whom he feared the more vehemently, the more inwardly they were? If, then, you wished to imitate Paul, you would be within a bearer-with of false brothers, not without a slanderer of the innocent.

[^1]: Phil 2:20-21.
[^2]: 2 Cor 7:5.
