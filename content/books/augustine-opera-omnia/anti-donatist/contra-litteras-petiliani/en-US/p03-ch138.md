# God left men free to believe or not.

**84.** 185. PETILIAN SAID: *For the Lord Christ says: No one comes to me, unless the Father has drawn him*[^1]. *But why do you not permit each one to follow his own free choice, since the Lord God himself gave free choice to men; yet showing the way of righteousness, lest anyone perhaps perish in ignorance? For he said: I have set before you good and evil, I have set before you fire and water; choose what you will*[^2]*. And by this free choice, wretches, you have chosen for yourselves not water, but rather fire. But, he says, choose the good, that you may live*[^3]. *You who refuse to choose the good have condemned yourselves to be unwilling to live.*

[^1]: John 6:44.
[^2]: Sir 15:16-17.
[^3]: Deut 30:19.
