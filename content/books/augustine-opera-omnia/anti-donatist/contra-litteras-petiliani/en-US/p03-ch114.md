# The Donatists hurl themselves down and are not martyrs.

**71.** 160. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Clearly, if it had not been said: *Blessed are those who suffer persecution for the sake of righteousness*[^1]*;* but had been said: Blessed are those who hurl themselves down, then your martyrs would fill heaven. To be sure, we see many flowers of the earth from their bodies, but, as the saying goes, the flower is ash.

[^1]: Matt 5:10.
