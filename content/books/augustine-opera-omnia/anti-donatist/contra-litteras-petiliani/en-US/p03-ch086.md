# Augustine rebukes the Donatists with moderation.

**53.** 122. Augustine replied: If we too should say all these things against you, will not he who hears us, if he himself is sound, judge us insane wranglers rather than Christian disputants? We do not, then, render curse for curse. For the servant of the Lord ought not to wrangle, but to be gentle toward all, teachable, in modesty rebuking those who hold different views[^1].

If, then, we charge against you those who daily perpetrate evils among you, we wrangle ineptly, accusing some by means of others. But if we admonish you that, just as you do not wish those things to be charged against you, so you should not charge against us the deeds of others, we rebuke you in modesty, in case you should at some time come to your senses.

[^1]: See 2 Tim 2:24-25.
