# How the Donatists act.

**28.** 65. Augustine replied: What are you saying, I ask? When a dark-blue cloud, struck by the sun's rays, reflects them back, do two suns, as it were, appear to madmen, and not to all who look? But when it so appears to madmen, it appears to them alone.

But if it is not troublesome that I admonish you, see rather lest perhaps to say such things and to speak so be the act of a madman. Yet surely you meant to say this, that the just have the truth of baptism, but the unjust an image; if it is so, I dare to say that there was an image in that man of yours, for whom not God, but a certain Companion, was God; but the truth either in you, or in the man who elegantly cast this against him, when he said to him: To whom God is a Companion. And distinguish those whom each of them baptized, and in some approve the true baptism, but from others exclude the image, and bring in the truth.
