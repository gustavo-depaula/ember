# Other words of the Gospel brought forward by Petilian.

**62.** 139. PETILIAN SAID: *And again: Every sin that a man commits is outside the body*[^1]*; but whoever sins against the Holy Spirit, it shall not be forgiven him, either in this age or in the age to come*[^2].

[^1]: 1 Cor 6:18.
[^2]: Matt 12:31-32.
