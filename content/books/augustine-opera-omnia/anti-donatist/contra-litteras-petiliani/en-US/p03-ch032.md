# Not even this accusation is proved.

**21.** 48. Augustine replied: You prove neither a persecutor nor a betrayer, the man whom you wish to baptize again. And even if you prove someone such, nevertheless a persecutor and betrayer is not to be baptized again, if he has already been baptized with the baptism of Christ. For Paul had to be baptized for this reason, that he had never been washed with such a baptism. And so what you wished to interpose about Paul has nothing like the case you are pleading with us.

But if you had not interposed this, you would have found no place for your childish declamation, where you said: *O avenging blindness of frenzy, destined to depart by baptism alone!* For how much more strongly must one cry out against you: *O avenging blindness of frenzy,* which, likened not to Paul but to Simon, does not depart from you even when baptism has been received[^1]? For if persecutors are to be baptized by those whom they persecute, let Primian be baptized by the Maximianists, whom he most insistently persecuted.

[^1]: See Acts 8:9-19
