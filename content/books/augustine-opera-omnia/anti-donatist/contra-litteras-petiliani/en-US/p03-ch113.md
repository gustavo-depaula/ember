# The Catholics are not blessed, but make the martyrs blessed.

**71.** 159. PETILIAN SAID: *Blessed are those who suffer persecution for the sake of righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven*[^1]. *You are not blessed, but you make the martyrs blessed, with whose souls indeed the heavens are filled, and at the memory of whose bodies the earth has blossomed. You, therefore, do not give us worship, but you make those whom we worship.*

[^1]: Matt 5:10.
