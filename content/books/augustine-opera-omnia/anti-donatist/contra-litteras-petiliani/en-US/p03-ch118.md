# The Donatists are truly wolves, not sheep, because they do not hear the voice of the shepherd.

**73.** 164. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: I would like to say this sentence against you, but I would not like to say it in these words: they are too foolish, or rather insane. But what was needed was that you should show, by some sure proofs and not by most empty curses, that we are wolves and you are sheep. For when I too have said: We are the sheep, but you are the wolves; do you therefore think it makes any difference, because you say those things in swollen words? But attend to how I prove what I say. Surely the Lord, as you know whether you will or not, says in the Gospel: *My sheep hear my voice, and follow me*[^1].

There are many voices of the Lord about various matters: but, for example, if someone doubted whether the same Lord rose in the body, and his words were read out, saying: *Handle me, and see, for a spirit does not have flesh and bones, as you see me have*[^2]*;* if, even after hearing these words, he would not consent to believe that his body had been raised up, he would surely not be reckoned among the sheep of the Lord, because he did not follow the voice he had heard; so now too, since there is a question between us where the Church is, when we read out, from that very passage of the Gospel where after the resurrection he offered his body to be handled even by the doubting, the words that follow, where he showed the future breadth of the Church, saying: *Thus it is written, and thus it was fitting that Christ should suffer and rise on the third day, and that repentance and forgiveness of sins should be preached in his name to all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem*[^3]; and you refuse to communicate with all the nations, in which these things have been fulfilled, how are you the sheep of this shepherd, whose voices you not only do not follow when you hear them, but even oppose?

And indeed that you are not sheep, we teach from this; but that you are wolves, hear from what we teach it. For now, since where the Church is appears from his own words, it is plain where the sheepfold of Christ is.

From this sheepfold, then, shown and set forth by the most certain words of the Lord, whoever, I do not say by false charges, but by what is plain, by uncertain charges of men, separate the sheep, and kill them when they have been snatched away and estranged from the life of unity and love, are they not themselves ravening wolves?

But they too praise and proclaim the Lord Christ. These, then, are they of whom he himself says: *Clothed in sheep's skins, but within they are ravening wolves. By their fruits you shall know them*[^4]. The sheep's skin is in the praises of Christ, the wolf's fruit in the slanderous teeth.

[^1]: John 10:27.
[^2]: Luke 24:39.
[^3]: Luke 24:46-47.
[^4]: Matt 7:15-16.
