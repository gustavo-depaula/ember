# The Catholics are ravening wolves.

**73.** 163. PETILIAN SAID: *But these things do not frighten us Christians. For we have beforehand from Christ the Lord a command about the evil you are going to do: I send you, he says, as sheep in the midst of wolves*[^1]. *You fulfill the rage of wolves, you who lay or prepare ambushes for the churches in no other way than wolves gaping at the sheepfolds, who, with ruin and onslaught ever in their breath, with jaws steeped in blood, pant out their breath of fury.*

[^1]: Matt 10:16.
