# A salutary persecution too can be given.

**10.** 23. Petilian said: *Did the apostles persecute anyone, or did Christ betray anyone?*

**10.** 24. Augustine replied: I could indeed say that Satan himself is worse than all evil men, to whom nevertheless the Apostle handed over a man *for the destruction of the flesh*, that *the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus*[^1]. And likewise others, of whom he says: *Whom I have handed over to Satan, that they may learn not to blaspheme*[^2]. And the Lord Christ scourged and drove out of the temple the wicked merchants, where the testimony of Scripture is also joined, which says: *Zeal for your house has consumed me*[^3].

Behold, we find an apostle a betrayer, and Christ a persecutor. I could say these things, and throw you into no small turmoil, so that you would be compelled to seek not the complaints of those who suffer, but the mind of those who act. But do not trouble yourself over this; I do not say these things; but I do say that, if anything has been done to you not rightly, it does not pertain to the seed of Abraham, which is in all the nations—perhaps it was done by the chaff of the Lord's harvest, which is nonetheless in all the nations.

You, then, render the account of your separation. But first consider what sort of men you have, whom you do not wish to be charged against you; and see how unjustly you act, when you charge the deeds of others against us, even if you could prove the things you say. Thus there will be no account of your separation.

[^1]: 1 Cor 5:5
[^2]: 1 Tim 1:20
[^3]: John 2:17
