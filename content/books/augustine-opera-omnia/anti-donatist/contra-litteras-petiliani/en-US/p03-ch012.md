# Petilian does not cite the testimonies of Scripture well.

**8.** 19. You yourself, when you were speaking about the aforesaid condemnation of Judas[^1], said: *See how great is the spirit of the prophets, that it saw all things to come as present, so that a betrayer not to be born for many ages was condemned beforehand,* and you did not see that by the same prophecy, with steadfast and certain and unshaken truth by which it was foretold that one disciple would betray Christ, it was also foretold that the whole world would believe in Christ. Why did you attend, in the prophecy, to the man who betrayed Christ, and did not attend there to the world for which Christ was betrayed? Who betrayed Christ? Judas. To whom did he betray him? To the Jews. What did the Jews do to him? *They pierced,* he says, *my hands and my feet, they numbered all my bones: they indeed looked and stared upon me; they divided my garments among them, and upon my clothing they cast lots*[^2].

How great, then, is that which was bought at so great a price, read in the same Psalm a little later: *All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to the Lord; and all the families of the nations shall worship in his sight: for the kingdom is the Lord's, and he shall have dominion over the nations*[^3]. But who can suffice to recount the innumerable other prophetic proofs about the world that would believe? But you praise the prophecy because you see in it the man who sold Christ; and you do not see in it the possession which Christ, having been sold, bought. Behold another piece of emptiness; hear a third.

[^1]: See Ps 109:8-9 (108:8-9)
[^2]: Ps 22:16-18 (21:17-19)
[^3]: Ps 22:27-28 (21:28-29)
