# It is not enough to know Sacred Scripture.

**49.** 113. Petilian said: *For if, faithless men, you know the Law; without injury to the Law itself I would say this, the devil too knows it. For to the Lord God, in the cause of the just Job, he answered about the Law as though he were just, as it is written: 'The Lord said to the devil: Have you considered my servant Job, that there is none like him on earth, a man without malice, a true worshiper of God, abstaining from all evil, persevering in simplicity; but you have asked to destroy all his substance without cause. And the devil answered and said to the Lord: Skin for skin, and all that a man has he will give for his soul'*[^1].

*Behold, he speaks lawfully who strives against the Law. And again he dared to tempt the Lord Christ with his words, as it is written: 'The devil took Jesus into the holy city, and set him upon the pinnacle of the temple, and said to him: Cast yourself down; for it is written: He has given his angels charge concerning you, that they bear you up in their hands, lest perhaps you dash your foot against a stone. Jesus said: Again it is written: You shall not tempt the Lord your God'*[^2]. *You know the Law, I say, like the devil, who is conquered in his attempts, and blushes in his acts.*

[^1]: Job 2:3-4.
[^2]: Matt 4:5-7.
