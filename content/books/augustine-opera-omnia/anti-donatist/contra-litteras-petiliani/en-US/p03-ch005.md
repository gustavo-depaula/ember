# The baptizer transmits neither sin nor holiness.

**6.** 12. Petilian said: *Since these things are so, brothers, what perversity can there be, that one who is guilty by his own crimes should make another innocent, since the Lord Jesus Christ says: 'A good tree bears good fruit, a bad tree bears bad fruit: do they gather grapes from thorns?'*[^1] *And again: 'Every good man brings forth good things from the treasure of his heart; and every evil man brings forth evil things from the treasure of his heart'*[^2]?

[^1]: Matt 7:16-17
[^2]: Matt 12:35
