# You, says Petilian, have so often killed the living Christ.

**20.** 44. Petilian said: *Again the Lord Christ cries out from heaven to Paul: 'Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me? It is hard for you to kick against the goad'*[^1]. *He was then called Saul, that baptism might afterward compose a name for him. Is it not, then, hard for you so often to persecute Christ in his priests, when the Lord himself cries out: 'Touch not my anointed'*[^2]*? Count up so many funerals of the saints, and you have so often killed the living Christ. Finally, if you are not a sacrilegious man, you cannot be holy, being a murderer.*

[^1]: Acts 9:4-5
[^2]: Ps 105:15 (104:15)
