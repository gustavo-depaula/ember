# This example does not pertain to Catholics at all.

**29.** 67. Augustine replied: What if this private person and forger should give someone an emperor's law? Will not that person, when he has compared it with those who have it, and found that it is the very same, not attend to from whom he received it, but what he received? For a forger, when he gives something out of his own falsehood, it is false: but when something true belonging to another is given by someone, even if it is given by a forger, although that man is not truthful, nevertheless what is given is true.

**30.** 68. Petilian said: *Or if anyone should hold by memory the songs of a priest, is he therefore a priest, because with a sacrilegious mouth he publishes the song of a priest?*
