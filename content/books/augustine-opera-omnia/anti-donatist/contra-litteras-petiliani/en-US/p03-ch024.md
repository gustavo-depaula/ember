# You unlawfully take the name of bishop.

**18.** 40. Petilian said: *Nor is it so great a wonder that you unlawfully take to yourself the name of bishop. This is the true custom of the devil, to deceive only when he claims for himself the title of holiness, as the Apostle proclaims: 'Nor is it a wonder,' he says, 'if Satan himself transforms himself as an angel of light, and his ministers as ministers of righteousness'*[^1].

*Nor is it a wonder, then, that you falsely call yourself a bishop. For those lost angels too, lovers of worldly maidens, who by corrupting the flesh were corrupted, although, stripped of their divine powers, they ceased to be angels, nevertheless retain the name of angels, and always think themselves angels—they who, released from the divine service, passed over, made like, into the army of the devil, while the great God cried out: 'My spirit shall not remain in these men forever, for they are flesh'*[^2].

*To which guilty ones, and to you, the Lord Christ will say, 'Go into the eternal fire, which my Father has prepared for the devil and his angels'*[^3]. *If there were no evil angels, the devil would not have angels; whom the holy Apostle too says are to be condemned, in that judgment of rising again, by holy men: 'Do you not know that we shall judge angels?'*[^4]. *If they were true angels, men would not have judgment over the angels of God.*

*So too the sixty apostles, who, the twelve being left with the Lord Christ, withdrew as apostates from the faith, are to this extent still thought apostles by wretched men, that from these Manichaeus and the rest entangle many souls in many diabolical sects, which they came forth to capture.*

*For surely the lost Manichaeus, if indeed he existed, is to be placed among those sixty, if the name of his apostle is not contained among the twelve; but, Matthias having been substituted into the place of Judas the betrayer, Paul was ordained by the voice of Christ as a thirteenth, who recalls himself to be the last of the apostles, lest anyone be believed to be a later apostle. For he said thus: 'For I am the last of the apostles, who am not worthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the Church of God'*[^5].

*And do not flatter yourselves in this; a Jew had done these things: yet you too work destruction on us like pagans. For you wage war unlawfully, you who are not permitted to fight back. For you desire to live when we are killed, but for us victory is to flee or to be killed.*

[^1]: 2 Cor 11:14-15
[^2]: Gen 6:3
[^3]: Matt 25:41
[^4]: 1 Cor 6:3
[^5]: 1 Cor 15:9
