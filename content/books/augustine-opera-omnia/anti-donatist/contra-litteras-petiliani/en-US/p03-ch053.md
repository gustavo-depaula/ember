# There are not two Holy Spirits.

**32.** 76. Finally, what did it seem good to you, that, when you wished to prove that the Holy Spirit was given by Christ, and had brought forward the testimony from the Gospel that, rising from the dead, he breathed on the face of the disciples, saying: *Receive the Holy Spirit*[^1]; and wished to show that last fire which was named together with baptism in the tongues of fire which were shown when the Paraclete came—for you said: *And the fire itself, the Paraclete, blazing with crackling flames, came upon the apostles*—as though one were the Holy Spirit whom he gave by breathing on the face of the disciples, and another he who came upon the apostles after his ascension. Are there, then, two Holy Spirits? What most demented person would say this? And so Christ himself gave the same Holy Spirit, whether by breathing on the face of the disciples, or by sending him from above on the day of Pentecost with a fixed commendation of the sacrament.

Accordingly, it was not that Christ gave the Holy Spirit and the Paraclete the fire, as though to fulfill what had been said: *In the Holy Spirit and fire*[^2]: but the same Christ himself gave the Holy Spirit, whom, while set on earth, he showed by his breath, but, while set in heaven, by the tongues of fire[^3]. For, that you may know that what was said was not then fulfilled, *He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit*[^4], when he breathed on their face—so that they seemed now to be baptized not in the Spirit, but in fire, when the Paraclete should come—recall the most plain Scripture, and see what he said to them when the Lord himself ascended into heaven: *John indeed baptized with water; but you shall be baptized with the Holy Spirit, whom you will receive not after these many days, at Pentecost*[^5]. What is clearer than this testimony?

But according to your view he ought to have said: John indeed baptized with water; but you were baptized with the Holy Spirit when I breathed on your face, and hereafter you shall be baptized in fire, which you will receive not after these many days; so that, namely, those three degrees might be completed, by which you say the one baptism is consecrated. So it comes about that you still do not know what is meant by what was said: *He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire*[^6], and you rashly wish to teach what you do not know.

[^1]: John 20:22
[^2]: Matt 3:11
[^3]: See Acts 2:3
[^4]: Matt 3:11
[^5]: Acts 1:5
[^6]: Matt 3:11
