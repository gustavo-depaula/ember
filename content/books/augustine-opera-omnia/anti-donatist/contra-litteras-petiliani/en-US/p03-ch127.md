# Love does not persecute.

**78.** 173. PETILIAN SAID: *And again: Love is patient and kind; love is not jealous, does not act perversely, is not puffed up, is not ambitious, does not seek what is its own. But you seek what belongs to others. It is not provoked, it does not think evil, it does not rejoice over iniquity, but rejoices with the truth: it bears all things, endures all things: love never fails*[^1]. *This is to say briefly: Love does not persecute, does not inflame emperors against other souls, does not plunder what belongs to others, does not even kill the men it has despoiled.*

[^1]: 1 Cor 13:4-8.
