# Peace is in the universal Church.

**70.** 158. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: It is you who say this to us, not the prophet. To you, then, we reply: If you ask where peace is, open your eyes, and see of whom it was said: *Putting away wars to the ends of the earth*[^1]. If you ask where peace is, open your eyes to that city which cannot be hidden, set upon a mountain[^2]; open your eyes also to the mountain itself, and let Daniel show it to you growing from a small stone, and filling the whole earth[^3]. But when the prophet says to you: *Peace, peace, and where is peace?*[^4], what will you show? The party of Donatus, unknown to the countless nations to whom Christ is known? It is not the one that cannot be hidden: and why, except that it is not set upon that mountain? *For he himself is our peace, who made the two one*[^5]; not Donatus, who made two out of one.

[^1]: Ps 46:9 (45:10).
[^2]: See Matt 5:14.
[^3]: See Dan 2:34-35.
[^4]: Jer 8:11.
[^5]: Eph 2:14.
