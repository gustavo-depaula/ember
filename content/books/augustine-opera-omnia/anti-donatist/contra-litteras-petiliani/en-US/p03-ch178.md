# Augustine: you slaughter souls.

**100.** 230. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: What you say, you do, not with a visible sword, but with that of which it was said: *The sons of men, their teeth are weapons and arrows, and their tongue a sharp sword*[^1]. For with this sword—accuser and slanderer of a world it does not know—you slaughter the souls of the ignorant.

But if you accuse a most wicked communion, see, by a mouth not mine but your own, whether you ascend, descend, go out, enter, turn this way and that, you are such as Optatus. But if you return to your heart, and find that you are not such—not because he did not share the sacraments with you, but because he displeased you—you will absolve the whole earth of the crimes of others, and find that you are implicated in the crime of schism.

[^1]: Ps 57:4 (56:5).
