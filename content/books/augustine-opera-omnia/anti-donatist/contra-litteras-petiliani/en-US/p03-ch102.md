# The Church is universal.

**64.** 144. AUGUSTINE REPLIED: Again and again hear the Lord saying: *You shall be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the whole earth*[^1]. Why then have they not lost earth and heaven, who, that they may not communicate with the whole earth, despise the words of him who sits in heaven? For about your meekness, let not your words, but the clubs of the Circumcellions be questioned. You will say: What does that have to do with us? As if we said these things for any reason but that you might give this answer. For your schism has to do with you precisely because you refuse to have another's sin have anything to do with you: yet you separated yourselves from us for no other reason than that you charge us with another's crime.

[^1]: Acts 1:8.
