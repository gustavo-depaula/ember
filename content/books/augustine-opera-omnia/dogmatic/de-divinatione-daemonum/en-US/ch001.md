# Revisions II

## Chapter 30 (57): On the Divination of Demons, one book

Around the same time it fell to me, as a result of a certain discussion, that I had to write a little book *On the Divination of Demons*, which is its very title.

Now in a certain place in it, where I said that *demons sometimes learn with complete ease the dispositions of human beings, not only those uttered by the voice but even those conceived in thought, when certain signs are expressed from the mind upon the body*, I stated a most hidden matter with greater boldness than I ought to have. For that these things come to the knowledge of demons has been established even by a number of experiments. But whether certain signs are given off from the body of those who are thinking, signs perceptible to them but hidden from us, or whether they know these things by some other power, and that a spiritual one, can either only with the greatest difficulty be discovered by human beings, or cannot be discovered at all.

This book begins thus: *On a certain day, in the holy days of the Octave.*
