# Revisions II

## Chapter 38 (65): Faith and Works, in One Book

Meanwhile certain writings were sent to me by some lay brothers who were nevertheless devoted to the study of the divine words. These so distinguished Christian faith from good works that they urged that, while one cannot attain to eternal life without that faith, one can attain to it without those works. Replying to them, I wrote a book whose title is *Faith and Works.* In it I argued not only how those who have been reborn by the grace of Christ ought to live, but also what kind of people are to be admitted *to the bath of rebirth.*
