# Revisions II

## Chapter 63 (90): To Laurentius, on Faith, Hope, and Charity, One Book

I also wrote a book *On Faith, Hope, and Charity*, when the man to whom it was written had asked me to have some little work of mine that would never leave his hands, the sort of thing the Greeks call an *enchiridion*. There[^1] I think I gathered together carefully enough how God is to be worshiped, which Divine Scripture defines as the true wisdom of man[^2].

This book begins thus: *It cannot be told, most beloved son Laurentius, how much I delight in your learning.*

[^1]: Enchiridion 2, 1.
[^2]: De Trin. 12, 14, 22; 14, 1, 1; De spir. et litt. 11, 18; Ep. 140, 18, 45; 167, 3, 11.
