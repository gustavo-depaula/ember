You may now, without much difficulty, dispose, according to the order of time, the great events of ancient history, and rank them, so to speak, every one under its own standard.

I have not forgot, in this epitome, that celebrated division, which chronologers make of the duration of the world, into seven ages. The beginning of each age serves us for an epoch; if 1 intermix some others with them, it is, that things may be the more distinct; and that the order of time may unfold itself before you with the less confusion.
