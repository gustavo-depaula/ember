The wonderful effects and invincible strength of Jesus Christ's doctrine . — Matt. vii. 28, 29.

HINK over this teaching of Our Lord’s. It is so beautiful and so solid that it calls forth admiration from the whole world. For who could help admiring its purity, grandeur and practicalness?
