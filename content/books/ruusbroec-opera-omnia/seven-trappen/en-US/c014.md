NEXT follows the seventh step, the noblest and most elevated that it is possible to realize in the life of time or eternity. It is attained when, above all

We shall also contemplate the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, triune in Person, one only God in Nature ; by Whom was created Heaven and earth and all that exists ; Whom we shall love, thank, and praise for ever and ever, for that we are made in His image and likeness is the great happiness of those who are noble and pure. The Divinity does not operate, being simple essence, always in repose. Had we part in this repose with Him, we should, with Him, be merely Repose and lifted even to His altitude, and thus be above all the steps of the heavenly Ladder, with God in His Divinity, one essence of Repose and eternal Beatitude. Between Labour and Repose live Love and Possession. Love will always act, for it is ever lasting operation in God. This sort of happiness belongs to the contemplative life.

Another kind leads to the enjoyment of God those of interior life who are perfected in Charity after the most dear will of God. Unity according to Nature, Trinity according to the quali ties they possess in the fecund Nature of the Divinity. These three qualities constitute three Persons distinct in Name and fact, yet one in Unity of Nature and Operation, each Person having the Divine Nature entire. Each is God, all-powerful by Nature and not in virtue of the personal distinction. The three Persons are thus one Divine in divisible Nature, consequently one only God by Nature, and not three Gods after the distinction of Persons. Three, according to Names and Persons, God remains One by Nature ; Trinity in His fecund Nature according to the qualities of the Persons, but Unity by Nature.

And this one God is for us a Father Who is in Heaven, Almighty Creator of Heaven and earth and all beings. He lives in us and governs us, Unity in Trinity, Trinity in Unity, God all - powerful, in the summit of our created essence. We must seek, find, and possess Him by means of His Grace and the aid of our Lord Jesus Christ, in the Christian Faith, with direct intention and sincere Charity; and by means of our virtuous life and His Grace we live in Him and He in us with all the Just. Thus are we all united in one with Him in love; and the Father and the Son take, embrace, and transform us in the unity of the Spirit. Then are we one in love and enjoy-

This is the highest degree of life and death, of love and possession in eternal Beatitude ; and any who conceives it otherwise errs.
