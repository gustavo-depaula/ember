The Stoics contended that the proposed object of life should be to live agreeably to nature; and that to live according to nature^ is never to act in op* position to the suggestion of reason, which is a universal law, to be observed by all men alike.

They taught that virtue should be followed for its own sake alone, without any expectation of reward; that in itself it was sufficient to render men happy, and that they who possessed it would enjoy perfect contentment, even in the midst of the greatest evils.
