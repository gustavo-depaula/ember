# Thirteenth Day. Of the one thing necessary

T.  Thou art troubled and careful about many things, but one thing is needful.' Luke x. 41. We think we have many important things to do and yet we have but one. If that be performed, all others are included in it. If that miscarry, whatever success others may seem to have, they will all come to nothing. Why should we then divide our heart and our care? O my only concern, thou shalt henceforward have my only attention! In the ray of divine light, I will each moment peaceably perform, according to my abilities, what Providence puts in my way. 1 will be careful for nothing else, because nothing else is my business.
