At most, I should only indulge in such amusement, which is unduly honoured with the name of study, as one might play a game of chess after dinner. The main thing is to nurture the spark of grace in your heart. Put aside whatever can extinguish it; seek all that will fan it. Be vigorous in your efforts. " The kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force."'
