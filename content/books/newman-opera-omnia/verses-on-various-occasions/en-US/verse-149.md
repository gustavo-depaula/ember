# 149. Hymn for Vespers, Saturday

Jam sol recedit igneus.

The red sun is gone,  
Thou Light of the heart,  
Blessed Three, Holy One,  
To Thy servants a sun Everlasting impart.

There were Lauds in the morn,  
Here are Vespers at even;  
Oh, may we adorn  
Thy temple new born With our voices in Heaven.

To the Father be praise,  
And praise to the Son  
And the Spirit always,  
While the infinite days Of eternity run.
