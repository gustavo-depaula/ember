Acrescentar à regra

Three questions, each as phones to compare: where the button sits in the Plan of Life sheet, what the page it opens looks like, and what a tap on a practice does. Drawn on the You tab's sheet, in Tenebrae and pt-BR. Practice names, minutes, suggested hours and tradition counts are the corpus's own; the small icons stand in for the watercolour ones.

1 · O botão

Where the way in sits

Today the sheet offers the string, then Traditions. Nothing in it says a single practice can be added, so the carousel reads as the only door.

2 · A página

What it opens

A pushed screen, since the sheet closes before any navigation already. The old picker was removed on 29 Sep because its rows only opened the practice, where “add” lived a second tap away. In all three below, the row's trailing mark adds in place and the row itself opens the practice to read about it. The marks in A are live: tap them.

3 · O gesto

What a tap on the mark does

Most practices carry no suggested hour (the Rosary, the Angelus, spiritual reading), and every time in the rule needs one. So the tap is either instant or one question.
