# Bl. Columba Marmion OSB (1858–1923) — sourcing

Irish-born abbot of Maredsous (Belgium), beatified 2000. Wrote in French; his books are conferences taken down and edited by Dom Raymond Thibaut OSB of Maredsous, partly in Marmion's lifetime (the trilogy, *Sponsa Verbi*, *Chemin de la Croix*) and partly after his death (compilations from notes and letters). Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/`; only incidental mentions in `catholic-encyclopedia`).

**Copyright frame.** Marmion d. 1923: his own text is public domain in Brazil (life + 70, Lei 9.610 art. 41) and the EU. The classic English translations are credited to "a Nun of Tyburn Convent" (identified in catalogues as Mother M. St. Thomas, Tyburn) or to Dom Francis Izard OSB. Anonymous works run 70 years from publication in Brazil (art. 43) and the EU, so every anonymous Tyburn translation published before 1956 is PD there; US editions before 1931 are PD by date. The NYPL transcription of US renewal records (1950–2001, downloaded to `_raw/cce-renewals/`) contains **no renewal under Marmion, Thibaut, Tyburn or any of these titles**, so US Herder editions of 1931–1963 lapsed if registered at all. The posthumous compilations carry Thibaut's editorial arrangement (Thibaut's death date not confirmed): Marmion's words are PD; the arrangement is tier B at best.

**Access notes.** Gallica and HathiTrust both returned Cloudflare challenges (403) to scripted and WebFetch access during this session, so neither could be searched. French Wikisource and Google Books (full-view filter) have no Marmion text.

## Notes per work

### Le Christ, vie de l'âme / Christ the Life of the Soul
First of the trilogy (conferences, structured as Part I "Fundamental Exposition of the Divine Plan" and Part II "Foundation and Twofold Aspect of the Christian Life", 13 + conferences, each divided into numbered sections with Roman-numbered headings; footnotes are scripture references). English sources, best first:
1. https://archive.org/details/christlifeofsoul0000marm — Sands, London/Edinburgh 1922 (imprimatur Bruges 25 Jan 1922). Full text, clean OCR (sample read: only stray spaces like "F urthermore"); running heads/page numbers to strip. Open.
2. https://archive.org/details/christlifeofsoul0000righ_s2n3 and `christlifeofsoul0000righ` — same 1922 Sands edition, other copies; open djvu.txt. For cross-checking OCR.
3. `christlifeofsoul0000dcol`, `bwb_Y0-EAM-995` — Herder 1925 reprint of the same translation, open.
4. https://stmaryscathedral.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/christlifeofsoul0000marm.pdf — a re-host of item 1's PDF.
- Alan Bancroft's 2005 translation (Zaccheus Press; PDF on isidore.co book 7516) is in print: **C**.
French: **no open digital text found**. The only scan seen is a 1936 Desclée printing, borrow-only. Candidates still to try by hand: Gallica (blocked here), Google Books with a browser, the Maredsous abbey. Without the French, this work can only enter as `en-US` (original missing).

### Le Christ dans ses mystères / Christ in His Mysteries
20 conferences on the liturgical cycle (Christ's mysteries from the bosom of the Father to the Ascension, Pentecost, and Our Lady), with numbered subsections. French, best first:
1. https://archive.org/details/lechristdanssesm00marm — 8th edition, Desclée, Paris 1922 (imprimatur 21 Dec 1921), University of Toronto copy. Clean OCR, accents correct (sample: "être par la grâce ce que Jésus est par nature"). Double spaces between words throughout (Toronto OCR style, easy to normalise).
2. https://archive.org/details/lechristdanssesm0000marm — 19th edition, Maredsous / Desclée 1923. Open. Use to cross-check.
English, best first:
1. https://archive.org/details/christinhismyste0000righ — Tyburn nun, "translated from the French (10th ed.)", Sands 1924 (imprimatur Bruges 29 Dec 1923). Clean OCR.
2. https://archive.org/details/christ-in-his-mysteries-abbot-marmion_202109 — another scan (folkscanomy upload, edition not stated, 53 MB PDF + djvu.txt). Probably the same Sands translation; verify the edition before use.
3. Later Sands 1939 printings (`bwb_C0-AJV-271`, `christinhismyste0000revd`) are lending-only.
- The abridgment *Our Way and Our Life* (Tyburn nun, Sands 1927) is an abridgment; skip.

### Le Christ, idéal du moine / Christ the Ideal of the Monk
Two parts ("Fundamental Notions"; "The Monastic Virtues"), ~18 conferences commenting the Rule of St Benedict, with numbered sections and footnotes (Rule and scripture references).
French: **no open digital text found** (1931 Desclée printing borrow-only, `bwb_T3-BRJ-736`, metadata mislabelled "eng").
English (all the same 1926 Tyburn translation, from the 8th French ed.), best first:
1. https://archive.org/details/christidealofmon0000unse — Sands 1926 (imprimatur Bruges Feb 1926), Marygrove College copy. Good OCR; footnotes interleaved before running heads.
2. https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.174061 — Digital Library of India scan; open djvu.txt (221k). Independent scan for cross-checking.
3. https://archive.org/details/ChristTheIdealOfTheMonk — community upload (214k djvu), same as isidore.co book 3462 ("1926").
4. https://archive.org/details/ChristIdealOfTheMonkMarmionAbbotColumba3462.o — another isidore re-upload.
5. `bwb_Y0-DBZ-077` — Herder 1926, open.
- The isidore "abridged" EPUB (book 6832, 2014) is a modern derivative: skip.

### Sponsa Verbi
7 conferences on the consecrated virgin (I The Call to the Dignity of a Spouse of Christ … VII The Admirable Fecundity of a Spouse of the Word), editor's preface dated Maredsous 15 Oct 1924.
- French: Maredsous 1923 (Collection Pax 13), not found online.
- English, best first: (1) https://piercedhearts.org/consecrated_hearts/Sponsa_Verbi_Marmion_.htm — full HTML transcription of the Izard translation (Sands 1925), complete to "And the Spirit and the Bride say: come."; the page carries no copyright notice, only "This page is the work of the Servants of the Pierced Hearts of Jesus and Mary." The text itself is PD (1925, translator Izard; a US 1925 publication is PD by date). The site 403s plain curl and needs a browser user agent. (2) isidore.co book 8480: EPUB + zip, "1925".

### Le Chemin de la Croix / The Way of the Cross
Short devotional treatise with the 14 stations (French 1923). English: https://archive.org/details/wayofcrossitserr0000rtre — Tyburn nun, Sands 1923 (imprimatur Edinburgh 9 May 1923), ~6.5k words, open, clean.

### Consécration à la Sainte Trinité, letters, biography
Thibaut's biography *Dom Columba Marmion, un maître de la vie spirituelle* (Maredsous 1929; open scan of the 1930 printing: https://archive.org/details/un0000unse, djvu 212k words, good OCR) quotes the 1908 Consecration and many letters and notes in full. It is a mine for the letters, but it is Thibaut's book: tier A only if Thibaut died before 1956 (unverified), else B. *L'Union à Dieu* (1934), the main collection of letters of direction, has no open scan. *The English Letters* (1962, open on archive.org) is the only full text of the letters Marmion wrote in English.

### Posthumous compilations
*Le Christ, idéal du prêtre* (1951) and *Paroles de vie* were assembled by Thibaut from notes and conferences after Marmion's death. Their English versions (Dillon 1952; Mother M. St. Thomas 1936–39) are lending-library scans on archive.org (no open text), not renewed in the US, and in print from Angelico/Cenacle in reprint form (the reprints add nothing we need, but they signal that a publisher sells them). They are tier B and need manual digitisation or a borrowed scan.

If a Brazilian printing exists (e.g. from a Brazilian Benedictine house), it needs a by-hand search in the BN Digital or Domínio Público web interfaces, which block scripts. **For pt-BR, our own pipeline translation from the French (or from the 1922–26 English) is the only route.** The Singeverga versions are European Portuguese and not available anyway.

## Recommendation

- **Layout:** `marmion-opera-omnia/<work>`: `christ-life-of-soul`, `christ-in-his-mysteries`, `christ-ideal-of-monk`, `sponsa-verbi`, `way-of-the-cross`, `consecration-trinity`, `letters` (later `christ-ideal-of-priest`, `words-of-life`). One chapter per conference (the author's own unit), with section headings kept.
- **Import first:** *Le Christ dans ses mystères*. It is the only work with a clean French original (two independent editions) plus a clean PD English (1924), so `fr-FR` and `en-US` both come from tier-A text.
- **Next:** *Christ the Life of the Soul* and *Christ the Ideal of the Monk*, English 1922/1926 (A, several clean copies each). Their French originals are the main gap: before importing, search Gallica and Google Books by hand from a browser (both blocked scripted access here). Failing that, import en-US now and add the French later.
- **Small and clean:** *Sponsa Verbi* (en HTML) and *The Way of the Cross* (en) are ready; their French originals were not found.
- **Cannot be sourced cleanly now:** every French original except *Mystères*; the posthumous compilations (lending scans only, tier B); *Union with God* (lending only). The 2008 *Correspondance* and the Bancroft translation are tier C.
- **Portuguese:** no Portuguese text is online. The 1926 Braga and 1937 Braga translations are probably PD (anonymous, 70 years from publication) but exist only on paper (BNP holds them).
- **Total size (estimated, counted where online):** the full œuvre is about 1.1M words per language. The sourceable-now set (Mystères fr+en; Life of the Soul, Ideal of the Monk, Sponsa Verbi and Way of the Cross en; Consecration fr) is about 175k fr + 575k en.

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/marmion/` (djvu.txt files of every open item above).
