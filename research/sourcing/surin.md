## Bibliography and attributions

The authoritative catalogue is Sommervogel, *Bibliothèque de la Compagnie de Jésus*, t. VII (1896), s.v. Surin (Google Books full view `pCpSikZUL7wC`). Certeau's *Correspondance* (1966) also has an introduction on the works. Notes:
- **Catéchisme spirituel**: first printed anonymously ("J. D. S. F. P."). Put on the Index in its Italian translation (1695). It was revised by later editors: the 1801 Belin edition is "nouvellement revu et corrigé par le P. T. B. F." (Thomas-Bernard Fellon). **Prefer the 17th-century editions** (1669, 1683) as the canonical text, and treat 1801/1882 as normalized-spelling witnesses. 8 parts in 2 tomes, each part in chapters, in question-and-answer form.
- **Fondements**: published under the pseudonym "Jean de Sainte-Foy, prêtre". 3 books of chapters, each chapter tied to a passage of the *Imitation*.
- **Cantiques spirituels de l'amour divin**: many expanded editions (1660, 1664, 1731 …); the number of cantiques varies. Catalan's 1957 edition of the *Poésies* is tier C.
- **Lettres spirituelles**: the 1695–98 editions (and 1721) are selections that the editors normalized. The Cavallera–Michel edition (Toulouse 1926–28, 2 vols) and Certeau's *Correspondance* (1966) restored the texts. Cavallera d. 1962, so tier unverified, and it was not found online. Certeau is tier C.
- **Dialogues spirituels**: posthumous, Nantes/Paris 1704–1709, 3 vols. No digitized copy found.
- **Le Prédicateur de l'amour de Dieu** (1813) and *Questions importantes à la vie spirituelle sur l'amour de Dieu* (Pottier/Marie ed., Téqui 1930): how these two texts relate is **unverified**. The 1813 book is labelled "Ouvrage posthume du Père Surin". A German version, *Ueber die Liebe zu Gott* (1883), is on Google Books (`8Lxl6wModVkC`).
- **Loudun writings** (*Triomphe de l'amour divin*, *Science expérimentale*, *Histoire abrégée*): autobiographical and exorcism memoirs. Their printing history is partial: 1828 Avignon/Paris; modern edition Millon 1990/2016 (tier C).
- *L'Homme de Dieu* (1689) is Boudon's life of Surin, **not** a work by Surin.

### Fondements de la vie spirituelle
1. **Marburg UB facsimile, Luxembourg 1697** (link above). Scan, no text layer verified. Tier A.
2. Paris 1674 (la veuve d'Edme Martin) is catalogued at Dickinson College. No digital copy verified.
3. Raoul Plus's edition (1923) is listed in the Holy Cross catalogue. No online text. A by the pre-1930 rule.
- English: **Pusey 1844**, archive.org `FoundationsOfTheSpiritualLife` (~109k words of OCR, good) and `foundationsofspi00suri`; 2nd ed. Oxford: Parker 1847 `foundationofspir0000fsur`; PDF at https://www.anglicanhistory.org/pusey/surin.pdf. Tier A. **Warning:** "adapted to the use of the English Church". Pusey removed or altered Catholic matter, so it is not a faithful witness. Usable as an en-US text only with an "adapted" note, or not at all.

## Recommendation

- **Layout**: `surin-opera-omnia/` with `catechisme-spirituel/`, `fondements/`, `lettres-spirituelles/`, `cantiques/`, `predicateur-amour-de-dieu/`, `dialogues-spirituels/`, `triomphe-amour-divin/`.
- **Import first**: `catechisme-spirituel` from the 1882 *Œuvres spirituelles* (best OCR), checked against 1669/1683. Then `predicateur-amour-de-dieu` (French text already in hand on archive.org). Both will be fr-FR → pt-BR only, with no PD English, so en-US must come from our own translation or stay empty.
- **Cannot be sourced cleanly yet**: *Cantiques*, *Dialogues spirituels* and the Loudun writings (no digital text found; Gallica needs a manual search). *Fondements* exists only as a scan, with an English that is a Protestant adaptation. *Guide spirituel*, *Correspondance* (critical) and *Poésies* are tier C only.
- **Biggest structural risk**: every Surin source is a page scan with Google/IA OCR; there is no clean transcription anywhere. Budget an OCR-correction pass, and an audit against the scans (`audit-book-vs-pdf`).

**Total size (est.)**: French ~1.0M words for the PD-printable works (Catéchisme ~200k, Fondements ~90k, Cantiques ~50k, Lettres 1721 ~200k, Dialogues ~250k, Prédicateur ~90k, Loudun ~80k). Of that, ~400k is currently reachable as scans with OCR. English (PD) ~200k (Pusey 1844, the Christopher 1892 letters).
