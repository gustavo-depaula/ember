# St Bartolomeu dos Mártires OP (Bartholomew of Braga, 1514–1590): sourcing report

Archbishop of Braga (1559–1582), Father of the Council of Trent, model bishop for St Charles Borromeo; beatified 2001, canonized (equipollent) by Pope Francis in 2019. Bibliography from Barbosa Machado, *Bibliotheca Lusitana* I (1741), pp. 468–471 (archive `bibliothecalusit01barbuoft`), which lists the printed works, the letters, and the manuscripts; the 18th-c. *Opera omnia* (Rome 1734–35, ed. Malachie d'Inguimbert, 2 vols folio) gathered eight works; the modern critical edition is the Movimento Bartolomeano's (Braga/Fátima, 1970s–1990s, incl. *Theologica scripta*, ed. Raul de Almeida Rolo) — tier C, not online (unverified).

Works:
1. ***Stimulus pastorum*** (Latin; patristic sentences on the life of bishops), Rome 1564 (printed by St Charles Borromeo), Lisbon, F. Correia, 1565 (ed. Luis de Granada); Rome 1572/1582, Paris 1583/1586/1644/1667, Rome 1715, 1766…
2. ***Compendium spiritualis doctrinae*** (Latin; on prayer and the spiritual life, much from Dionysius, Bernard, Bonaventure, Gerson), Lisbon, A. Ribeiro, 1582 (ed. Granada); Madrid 1594, Paris 1601, Rome 1603, Cologne 1622, Venice 1711. Portuguese translation by Francisco Osório, Lisboa 1653; Spanish 1601; French (Godeau) 1699.
3. ***Catecismo ou Doutrina Cristã e Práticas Espirituais*** (Portuguese; catechism + sermons for feasts and Sundays to be read in parishes without a preacher), Braga, A. de Mariz, 1564; Coimbra 1574; Lisboa 1585; Braga 1594; Évora 1605, 1617, 1628; Lisboa 1656 (with D. Rodrigo da Cunha's *Vida*). Latin by J. Quétif in *Opera* II (1735).
4. In the Rome *Opera omnia*: *Summa conciliorum*; *Annotationes in Davidicos Psalmos* (and canticles; written at Trent, 1561); *Petitiones* for Trent; *Collecta ex gestis Concilii Tridentini* (1562); *Itinerarium* Braga–Trent–Braga (Latin by Quétif from his Portuguese); letters (Latin versions).
5. Letters (Portuguese, 1561–1570) printed in Luís de Sousa's *Vida* (II.2, 5, 7, 9, 14; III.2, 22, 29; IV.12); letter to Queen Catherine, 7 Jan 1561, in Barbosa's *Memórias de D. Sebastião*.
6. Manuscripts listed by Barbosa (largely unpublished until the Movimento Bartolomeano edition, if at all): *Collationes spirituales* (150), *Annotationes in Jeremiam et alios Prophetas*, *Tractatus de Trinitate*, *De superstitionibus*, *Epitome chronicorum mundi*, *Compendium historiarum ecclesiasticarum*, *Epítome das vidas dos Sumos Pontífices*, *Compêndio geral das histórias de Espanha*, kings of Aragon, Navarre and Portugal, *Tratado de práticas devotas para os prelados quando dão ordens*, *Considerações espirituais para resistir às tentações*, Trent documents.

Spurious: ***Banquete espiritual*** (Lisboa 1751; archive `banqueteespiritu00mr`, catalogued under him) is by a namesake, Fr. Bartolomeu dos Mártires *Eborense*, an 18th-c. Dominican missionary (title page checked).

Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/` checked).

## Notes per work, sources ranked

### Stimulus pastorum
Chapters (*Caput* I, II…) of patristic sentences on the bishop's life and office (preaching, residence, visitation, almsgiving), linked by the author's text, in two parts (the 1766 text has a *Secunda operis pars*), each in chapters. One file per chapter.
1. **Rome 1766 edition** (archive `rrddbartholomaei00mrti`, 58,300 OCR tokens) — A; 18th-c. type, long-ſ OCR (*fitatem, faftum*) but readable. Best available text.
2. **Lisbon 1565 princeps** (BND https://purl.pt/23163, and the Faculdade de Letras copy https://purl.pt/14254; 123 ff.; image-only PDF, Public Domain Mark) — authority for collation.
3. Rome 1572, heirs of G. Accolti (`bub_gb_Lig5by4DcxUC`, Google) — A; OCR poor (0.41 proxy).
English: Prudlo 2022 — C (in print). Portuguese: Movimento Bartolomeano bilingual Latin–Portuguese edition, Braga 1981 — C. French (G. de Mello, *Le devoir des pasteurs*, Paris 1672) not checked.

### Compendium spiritualis doctrinae
Mystical-theology anthology arranged by the author in chapters (prayer, contemplation, the active and contemplative lives, mortification), praised by Granada in the preface. One file per chapter.
1. **Rome, Carlo Vulliet, 1603** (`bub_gb_GIS4gd87RRwC`, 82,000 OCR tokens; same edition scanned as `Scansionemar3191MiscellaneaOpal`) — A; OCR poor-to-fair.
2. **Lisbon 1582 princeps** (BND https://purl.pt/23162; 235 ff.) — A, scan only.
Portuguese translation by Francisco Osório (Lisboa 1653): not found online. English: none.

### Catecismo ou Doutrina Cristã e Práticas Espirituais
The catechism (Creed, Commandments, sacraments, prayers) followed by *práticas* (sermons) for the main feasts and some Sundays, written in plain Portuguese for parish priests. Chapter split: catechism by article; one file per *prática*.
1. **BND Coimbra 1574** (https://purl.pt/23026; [2], 209 ff., 4to) and **Lisboa 1585** (https://purl.pt/23027; 148 ff.) — A, image-only PDFs; the 1564 Braga princeps not found online. Needs OCR.
2. Latin *Catechismus* (Quétif) in *Opera* II, 1735 — A; translation, usable as a cross-check.
Modern Portuguese editions exist (Movimento Bartolomeano; a 2020s edition with introduction by Jorge Alves Barbosa, whose site posts only the introduction PDF) — C/unverified.

### *Opera omnia*, Rome 1734–35
Vol. II found (archive `bub_gb_7l06bDPVb7EC`, 487 images, Google): *Annotationes* on the Psalms and the canticles, *Catechismus*, *Collecta*, *Itinerarium*; 256k OCR tokens, 18th-c. Latin with ſ→f errors but legible. Vol. I (Inguimbert's Latin *Vita*, *Stimulus*, *Compendium*, *Summa conciliorum*, Trent *Petitiones*, letters) was not found on archive.org; Google Books (API quota exhausted during this research) and HathiTrust (refuses scripted requests) still to check by hand.

## Portuguese
Portuguese originals: the *Catecismo* (scans only, BND) and the letters (in Sousa's *Vida*, clean 19th-c. text). The Latin works have no public-domain Portuguese translation online: Osório's 1653 *Compêndio de doutrina espiritual* not located; Movimento Bartolomeano translations (*Estímulo de pastores*, Braga 1981, bilingual; others) — C. No author page on pt.wikisource; nothing on Domínio Público (searched dominiopublico.mec.gov.br).

## Recommendation

Layout: `bartolomeu-dos-martires-opera-omnia/<work>`; `la/` canonical for the Latin works, `pt-PT/` for the *Catecismo* and letters; `pt-BR/` and `en-US/` by pipeline.
- **Import first: `…/stimulus-pastorum`** — his best-known work, one volume, 1766 text (re-OCR or ſ→s pass) collated with the 1565 princeps; ≈ 50k words.
- Then `…/compendium-spiritualis-doctrinae` (1603 + 1582 scans, re-OCR).
- Then `…/catecismo` from OCR of the BND 1574 scan (the Portuguese original), checked against the 1585 and the Latin *Catechismus*.
- `…/annotationes-in-psalmos`, `…/itinerarium`, `…/collecta-concilii` from *Opera* II.
- `…/cartas`: extract from Sousa's *Vida* (Rollandiana) once that is imported.
- Search by hand for *Opera* I (Google Books/HathiTrust) for the *Summa conciliorum* and the Latin letters.

Cannot be sourced cleanly: *Opera* I (not located); the manuscript works (only in tier-C modern editions); any PD English (Prudlo 2022 is C); clean text of anything except the letters (scans and noisy OCR only).

Total size: Latin ≈ 370,000 words (Stimulus 50k, Compendium 65k, *Opera* II ≈ 255k OCR incl. the Latin Catechism; *Opera* I unknown, est. 150–250k more); Portuguese ≈ 110,000 (Catecismo ~100k est., letters ~10k); English PD 0.
