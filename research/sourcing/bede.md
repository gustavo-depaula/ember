# St. Bede the Venerable (c. 673–735): sourcing

**Bibliography backbone:** Bede's own list at the end of the *Historia ecclesiastica* (HE V.24), supplemented by the works written after 731 (Letter to Egbert) and the standard canon (Laistner–King, *Hand-List of Bede Manuscripts* 1943; CPL 1343–1384). Migne PL 90–95 (Giles's 1843–44 text reprinted, with large *dubia et spuria* sections) is what every free Latin transcription follows. la.wikisource carries almost all of PL 90–95 (author page https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Scriptor:Beda_Venerabilis, 118 titles, all dumped and counted: `bede/ws/`, log `_scripts/bede-ws.log`). Most come from the Corpus Corporum TEI of PL.

**Already in the corpus:** nothing. No `bede` directory exists, and `_corpus-books.txt` has no Bede entries. (Aquinas's *Catena Aurea*, which *is* in the corpus, quotes Bede's Mark and Luke commentaries heavily, but only in excerpt.)

Spurious/authentic status below follows the standard canon as I know it, against PL's own "genuina / dubia / subdititia" labels. Per-item scholarly ascriptions are **unverified** unless a source is quoted.

### Lives, letters, minor historical works

- **Historia abbatum.** Latin: Wikisource *Vita V sanctorum abbatum* (4.9k), Plummer 1896, King 1930. English: Giles (Fordham, "given here complete in the translation by J.A. Giles"), King 1930. All A. Don't confuse it with the anonymous *Historia abbatum* (*Vita Ceolfridi*), which Plummer and King also print.
- **Epistola ad Ecgbertum** (734). Latin: Wikisource *Epistolae (Beda)* Ep. II; Plummer; King. English: Giles 1843 *Minor Historical Works* (archive.org `theminorhistoric00bede`, "FROM BEDE TO BISHOP EGBERT"); King 1930. A.
- **Other letters** (*Epistolae (Beda)*, 21.6k): Plegwin (apologetic, on the six ages), Accam ×5 (dedicatory prefaces to the commentaries), Nothelm (30 questions on Kings), Eusebius/Hwætberht (Apocalypse preface), *De mansionibus filiorum Israel*, *De eo quod ait Isaias*, plus Albinus as a separate page. Several are prefaces that belong at the head of the commentary they introduce; import them there and keep the stand-alone letters as a small *Epistolae* book. English: only Albinus and Egbert (Giles); the rest have none PD.
- **Vita Cuthberti (prose).** Latin: Wikisource *Vita prosaica* (17.4k). Colgrave 1940 = C. English: Giles (Fordham, complete, A). **Verse life:** Wikisource 6.9k (Jaager 1935 = C critical); no PD English found.
- **Vita Felicis.** Latin: Wikisource 3.1k. English: Giles *Minor Historical Works*, A. (The same Giles volume also prints a *Life of St. Vedast*, which is Alcuin's, not Bede's.)
- **De locis sanctis.** Geyer, CSEL 39 (1898, A), archive.org `itinerahierosoly00geye`. English: the Palestine Pilgrims' Text Society library (archive.org `libraryofpalesti…`) includes Bede *Concerning the Holy Places* (Macpherson, 1895), **not opened: unverified**.
- Giles's *Biographical Writings and Letters* (1845, archive.org `biographicalwrit00bede`) duplicates the 1843 volume.

### Scriptural commentaries

All Latin texts are on la.wikisource (PL 91–93, via Corpus Corporum, with PL column numbers inline), tier A. Independent witnesses: Giles *Complete Works* vols. 7–12 (1843–44, archive.org `CompleteWorksOfVenerableBedeV07`…`V12`, OCR) and the DCO PL 91–93 scan PDFs (author page https://www.documentacatholicaomnia.eu/20_50_0627-0735-_Beda_Venerabilis,_Sanctus.html, nearly all MLT scans; only the Apocalypse and *Proverbiorum Liber* exist as LT text). Modern critical texts (CCSL 118A–121) = **C**, cite only.

- **English: only the Apocalypse commentary has a PD translation:** Edward Marshall, *The Explanation of the Apocalypse by Venerable Beda* (Oxford: Parker, 1878). Archive.org `explanationapoc00bedegoog`, Google OCR, decent, with a preface and footnotes (about 57k words in the djvu.txt). Downloaded: `bede/ia/`. Tier A. Every other English commentary is a Liverpool *Translated Texts for Historians*, Cistercian or Four Courts volume from 1985–2013 = **C**.
- PD English **fragments** of Mark and Luke exist in Newman's *Catena Aurea* (already in the corpus) and in breviary lessons. These are not usable as books.
- The Song of Songs commentary's Book I (against Julian of Eclanum) and Book VII (a Gregory catena) are part of the work. Keep them.
- *De muliere forti* (Prov 31:10–31) is the third book of the Proverbs commentary circulated separately. Merge it into Proverbs. *Fragmenta in Proverbia* (5.4k) are excerpts, doubtful.

### Homiliae evangelii (2 books, 50 homilies)

- **la.wikisource** *Homiliae (Beda)/1* (52.4k, "HOMILIAE GENUINAE") and */2* (55.9k, "HOMILIAE ARTICOLO GENUINAE"). It follows the **PL 94 order, which is not Bede's** (PL regroups the homilies by feast). **Text quality problem:** the transcription has been contaminated by Italian machine-translation artefacts. Examples: *"pro nostra redenzione"*, *"humanae redenzioneis"*, *"Exordium nostrae redenzioneis"*, *"GIOVANNA. XX"*, *"etc." → "ecc."*, *"OMILIA PRIMA"*. There are about 50 *-zione* tokens per book, and 85 Italian-word hits in Book I. **Do not import it as-is.**
- **Alternatives:** Giles *Complete Works* vol. 5 (1843, `CompleteWorksOfVenerableBedeV05`, Latin homilies, OCR) and the PL 94 scan (DCO *Homiliae*, MLT). Both tier A, but they need OCR work and a reordering to Bede's two books (Hurst's CCSL 122, 1955, order = C, used only as an ordering reference).
- Book III (135k) is spurious.
- English: Martin & Hurst, *Homilies on the Gospels* (Cistercian 1991) = **C**. None PD.

### Scientific, grammatical, poetic

*De temporum ratione* (52.6k; Wikisource *De ratione temporum*), *De temporibus*, *De natura rerum*, *De orthographia*, *De arte metrica*, and *De schematibus et tropis* are all Wikisource/PL, tier A, with no PD English. Of the Wikisource pages: *De temporibus* (5.3k) and *De temporibus (Beda)* (3.6k) are two different PL witnesses, so check which one is the authentic short book; *De orthographia* (the 2-word page) is a redirect to *De orthographia (Beda)* (8.5k). For the hymns, *Hymni XIII* (8.3k) mixes authentic and doubtful pieces. *De die iudicii* (1.1k) is authentic. These suit the hymn/practice layer better than books.

## Recommendation

**Layout:** `bede-opera-omnia/` with groups `historical/` (HE, Historia abbatum, Letter to Egbert, Lives of Cuthbert and Felix, De locis sanctis, Martyrology), `commentaries/` (one book per commentary), `homilies/`, `letters/`, `scientific/` (DTR, De temporibus, DNR), `grammatical/`, and `poems/`. Bede is not a Church Father in the corpus's NPNF sense, and the opera-omnia layout matches `aquinas-opera-omnia` and `denis-opera-omnia`.

**Import first:** the **Historia ecclesiastica** (TLL/Wikisource Latin + Sellar English, both clean, about 78k/110k). Then the small historical set with Giles English: Historia abbatum, Letter to Egbert, prose Life of Cuthbert, Felix. Then **Apocalypse** (Latin + Marshall 1878). Then the remaining commentaries as **Latin + pt-BR only**, since no PD English exists.

**Cannot be sourced cleanly:**
- The Homilies: the Wikisource Latin is corrupted, so they need an OCR route from Giles vol. 5 or PL 94, and there is no PD English.
- PD English for every commentary except the Apocalypse, and for the scientific and grammatical works.
- The Martyrology: Bede's own text is buried in PL's interpolated version. Quentin's reconstruction and Dubois–Renaud (1976) are tier C. Skip it, or defer until a clean base is found.
- Passio Anastasii and the Collectaneum on Paul: no free text.

**Total size:**
- Authentic Latin about **1.07M words**: commentaries about 800k, Homilies about 108k, HE 78k, DTR and other science about 70k, lives, letters and poems about 65k. The interpolated Martyrology would add 75k.
- PD English available: about **0.2M** (HE about 110k, Apocalypse about 50k, Cuthbert/Abbots/Felix/letters about 40k).
- Spurious PL/Wikisource material not to import: about 1.0M words.

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/bede/` (`ws/` Wikisource wikitext for all 118 titles, `tll/`, `en/`, `ia/`). Scripts: `research/sourcing/_scripts/` (`wswork.py`, log `bede-ws.log`).
