# Frei Tomé de Jesus OESA (Tomé de Andrade, 1529–1582): sourcing report

Bibliography from Barbosa Machado, *Bibliotheca Lusitana* III (1752), pp. 756–758 (archive `bibliothecalusit03barbuoft`), and Inocêncio, *Dicionário bibliográfico português* VII (1862), nº 235–236 (archive `17558156178888bsb-10808412`). Son of Fernão Álvares de Andrade; Augustinian at the Graça of Lisbon (prof. 1544), founder-in-intention of the Discalced Augustinians; captured at Alcácer-Quibir (1578), wrote in the dungeons of Meknes and Marrakesh, died a captive in Marrakesh on 17 April 1582.

Works:
1. ***Trabalhos de Jesus***, posthumous: Part I (25 *trabalhos*, from the Incarnation to the night of the arrest), Lisboa, Pedro Craesbeeck, 1602; Part II (25 *trabalhos*, the Passion, plus two closing chapters on the opened side and on Christ's company with the afflicted), Lisboa, Vicente Álvares, 1609. Each *trabalho* is followed by an *exercício* (colloquy). Later editions: 1620 (Part I), 1666 (Domingos Carneiro, both parts), 1733 (Officina Augustiniana, 2 vols, adds the *Vida* by D. Fr. Aleixo de Meneses), 1781/1789 (Régia Officina), 1865 ("5.ª edição", A. J. Fernandes Lopes), 1925 and 1951 (Porto, "6.ª edição"). Translated into Spanish (1624/1631), Italian (1644), Latin (Munich 1676; verse paraphrase by Salvador de Mesquita, Rome 1665), French (Alleaume, 1692), English (1720, 1753…), Dutch (1727), German (1793…).
2. ***Carta à nação portuguesa***, from the captivity of Marrakesh, 8 Nov 1581 (Barbosa) — printed with the *Trabalhos* (already in 1602 per Inocêncio, who owned a copy; 1666; 1865).
3. ***Oratório sacro de solilóquios do amor divino, e várias devoções a Nossa Senhora*** — first printed in a Spanish version by Hernando de Camargo (Madrid, herederos de Madrigal, 1628); in Portuguese inside Joaquim Feio Serpa's *Fiel despertador* (Lisboa Oriental, Officina Augustiniana, 1734) and alone, Lisboa, Imprensa Régia, 1805 (viii-133 pp.).
4. Lost or manuscript (Barbosa): letter from Lisbon, 14 July 1557, on the death of D. João III (autograph owned by Barbosa); *Costumes do noviciado*; *Comédia do grande Padre Santo Agostinho* (played in Marrakesh); the fourth part of Luís de Montoya's *Life of Christ*; most of the *Vida do Ven. P. Luís de Montoya*, absorbed unnamed into Jerónimo Román's Spanish *Vida* (Lisbon 1588).
5. Doubtful: Barbosa lists two Latin works, *Praxis vera fidei qua justus vivit* (Cologne 1629) and *De oratione dominica* (Antwerp 1623). No copy found; the titles and Low-Countries imprints match the circle of the Spanish Discalced Carmelite **Tomás de Jesús (1564–1627)**, and are probably his — treat as doubtful. Spurious: *De contemplatione divina* (Antwerp 1620), credited to "Tomé de Jesus" on Wikipedia mirrors, is the Carmelite's.

Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/` checked).

## Notes per work, sources ranked

### Trabalhos de Jesus (with the Carta)
Structure: Part I — *Prólogo*, 25 *Trabalhos* each with its *Exercício* (some with inserted expositions: the *Nunc dimittis*, the Lord's Prayer, Ps 129…); Part II — 25 *Trabalhos* (XXVI–L, Gethsemane to the death) with *Exercícios*, then cap. I *Do lado que foi aberto ao Senhor*, cap. II *Da companhia que o Senhor faz a todos os atribulados*, an *Oferta humilde* and the *Protestação do autor*. One file per *trabalho* (+ its exercício) gives ≈ 55 files; the Carta and Meneses's *Vida* as front matter (the *Vida* is by Aleixo de Meneses, not Tomé — keep it as an appendix or drop it).

Portuguese, ranked:
1. **Lisboa 1865, "5.ª edição"** (archive `trabalhosdejesus00thom`, Toronto/Robarts, 792 images, both tomes in one file) — A. Good OCR (proxy score 0.93, the highest of the Portuguese texts in this batch); 19th-c. semi-diplomatic spelling: modern type and accents but *Deos, Mãi, pêra, hum, Christo, consummado* kept. Best base for a clean text; one OCR pass and proofreading needed. 329,719 OCR tokens: Vida ≈ 9,600, Trabalhos + Carta ≈ 310,000, index ≈ 5,000.
2. **Princeps 1602 (Part I) / 1609 (Part II)** — the canonical authority; held by the BNP (catalogue nº 7 of the search "trabalhos de jesus"), **not digitized** (absent from the BND, archive.org, Google; Google Books API quota was exhausted during this research, so Google full view is unverified). Inocêncio notes irregular foliation in both.
3. 1666, 1733, 1781, 1789 Lisbon editions — BNP holdings, not found online. The 1733 is the first with the Meneses *Vida*.
4. 1925 and 1951 Porto "6.ª ed." (Lello) — modernized; 1951 carries editorial matter: C (unverified), not online.
No Portuguese text of the *Trabalhos* on pt.wikisource, Gutenberg, BND or Domínio Público (searched: dominiopublico.mec.gov.br answers, the www host returns 403; no record under "Tomé/Thomé de Jesus").

English, ranked (all from the French of Gilles Alleaume, Paris 1692, which regroups the 50 *trabalhos* into "Sufferings of Christ during his hidden life / public life / passion" and prefaces them with "Father Thomas's Spiritual Advice", 5 chapters (probably the author's prologue, reworked; not collated); the *exercícios* become "Contemplations"):
1. **London, Thomas Richardson & Son, 1863**, "from the original Portuguese… revised and corrected" (archive `sufferingsourlo00chrigoog`, Oxford/Google) — A. Complete (Life of the author, Spiritual Advice, Sufferings I–L with all Contemplations; 485 pp.), modern type, clean OCR (0.955). Best English base; strip the Google boilerplate.
2. **London, J. Marmaduke, 1753, 3 vols** (`bim_eighteenth-century_trabalhos-de-jesus-eng_thome-de-jesus_1753_1`, `_2`, `_3`) — A; the translation the 1863 text revises; preface criticizes Welton's 1720 version. Long-ſ OCR (*Chriſt, ſuffer*); vol. 2 text file returned HTTP 500 (scan exists).
3. **Dublin, P. Wogan, 1794, 2 vols** (`…_1794_1`, `…_1794_2`) — A; same translation, "to which is added the third and last part, never before published in English" (i.e. the French third section); long-ſ OCR.
4. `the-sufferings-of-our-lord-jesus-christ` (archive, dated 1873, user upload 2024, Tesseract OCR) — A by date; edition not identified (probably a Richardson/Burns reprint); unverified.
5. R. Welton, *The Sufferings of the Son of God*, 1720 (`bim_eighteenth-century_the-sufferings-of-the-so_thom-de-jesus-frei_1720`) — A, but this item is a 17-page cropped fragment; the 1753 translator calls Welton's version loose. Philadelphia 1841 edition (cited by the New Catholic Encyclopedia) not located; Westminster MD 1961 — C.

### Oratório sacro de solilóquios do amor divino
Short devotional book (soliloquies and Marian devotions). Portuguese text only in the 1734 *Fiel despertador* compilation and the 1805 Imprensa Régia edition; neither found online (BNP, BND, archive.org, Google searched). The 1628 Madrid volume is Camargo's Spanish translation, not the original. Needs a scan request to the BNP.

### Lost, manuscript and doubtful works
Nothing located. The 1557 letter was in Barbosa Machado's own collection (now probably BNP/Ajuda manuscripts; not checked). The Latin *Praxis* and *De oratione dominica*: not found under either Tomé or the Carmelite; listed as doubtful.

## Portuguese
The original is Portuguese; see above. Orthography layers for the canonical choice:
- **Princeps 1602/1609** (Craesbeeck / Vicente Álvares): 16th-c. spelling (*Iesu, trabalhos, polo, pera, hũa*), not online.
- **1865 Lisbon (5.ª ed.)**: the princeps text re-set with mid-19th-c. spelling, online and well OCR'd — **recommended canonical `pt-PT/` text**, flagged as "text of the 1865 edition".
- **1925/1951 Porto**: post-1911 spelling, editorial matter, not online — not usable.

## Recommendation

Layout: `tome-de-jesus-opera-omnia/<work>`, canonical `pt-PT/` (1865 text), `en-US/` from the 1863 Richardson edition, `pt-BR/` as a spelling modernization of the `pt-PT/` text (as proposed for Bernardes).
- **Import first: `…/trabalhos-de-jesus`** — complete, one file, good OCR; English 1863 complete and clean. Map the English "hidden/public/passion" sections back onto the Portuguese Parts I–II (Sufferings I–XXV = Part I, XXVI–L = Part II; the English "Spiritual Advice" should be collated against the Portuguese prologue). Include the Carta à nação portuguesa as the first chapter.
- `…/oratorio-sacro` — later, from a BNP scan (1734 or 1805) and fresh OCR; no English.
- Lost works: none to import. Do not import the doubtful Latin titles under Tomé.

Cannot be sourced cleanly: the princeps text (not digitized); the *Oratório sacro* (no scan online).

Total size: Portuguese ≈ 340,000 words (Trabalhos + Carta ≈ 315k, Oratório ≈ 25k est.); English PD ≈ 245,000 (Trabalhos only).

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/tome-de-jesus/ia/` (counts in `counts.tsv`). Bibliography texts in `research/sourcing/_ptscratch/bib/`.
