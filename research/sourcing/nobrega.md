# Pe. Manuel da Nóbrega SJ (1517–1570): sourcing report

Bibliography. Nóbrega, first superior and first provincial of the Jesuits in Brazil, wrote in **Portuguese**; some letters circulated at once in Spanish, Italian and Latin translations. His surviving writings are (1) about 50–60 letters, 1549–1570 (to Simão Rodrigues, Ignatius of Loyola, Lainez, the provincials Miguel de Torres and Francisco Henriques, King João III, the Cardinal-Infante D. Henrique, Tomé de Sousa, the Jesuits of Coimbra…); (2) *Informação das terras do Brasil* (1549); (3) *Diálogo sobre a conversão do gentio* (1556–57), the first prose dialogue written in Brazil; (4) *Apontamentos de coisas do Brasil* (Bahia, 8 May 1558); (5) the 1567 opinion *Se o pai pode vender a seu filho e se um se pode vender a si mesmo* (answer to Quirício Caxa; also called *Caso de consciência sobre a liberdade dos índios*); (6) the *Tratado contra a antropofagia e contra os cristãos seculares e eclesiásticos que a fomentam e consentem* (1559) — included in Leite's collected editions; no pre-1940 printing located, **unverified**. Letters written "por comissão do padre Manoel da Nóbrega" by companions (Cartas avulsas) are not his.

Editions: Valle Cabral, *Cartas do Brasil do padre Manoel da Nóbrega (1549–1560)*, Rio, Imprensa Nacional, 1886 (Materiaes e achegas II), reissued with Rodolfo Garcia's notes by the Academia Brasileira de Letras, 1931 (Cartas jesuíticas I); Serafim Leite, *Novas cartas jesuíticas (de Nóbrega a Vieira)*, Brasiliana 194, 1940; Leite, *Diálogo sobre a conversão do gentio*, Lisbon 1954; Leite, *Cartas do Brasil e mais escritos do P. Manuel da Nóbrega (opera omnia)*, Coimbra 1955; Leite, *Monumenta Brasiliae* I–III (MHSI, 1956–58) — Leite's editions are **C** for their apparatus (Leite d. 1969). *Obra completa* (PUC-Rio / Loyola, 2017, ed. P. R. Pereira) — C.

Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/` checked for "nobrega": no hit).

## Notes per work, sources ranked

### Cartas do Brasil (1549–1560)
The 1886 edition (Valle Cabral, revised against the Biblioteca Nacional originals and copies, the IHGB's Évora and Torre do Tombo copies; two letters translated from Italian) has 21 numbered pieces: I–III to Simão Rodrigues and the Coimbra fathers from Bahia (1549), *Informação das terras do Brasil* (1549), to the Fathers and Brothers (1551), to the Coimbra Brothers (1551), to King João III (1551, 1552, 1554), to the Provincial of Portugal (1552), to Simão Rodrigues (1552–53), to Ignatius (1555, 1556), the quadrimestre Jan.–Apr. 1557, to the inhabitants of São Vicente (1557), to the Provincial (1557), to the Fathers and Brothers of Portugal (1559), the long letter to Tomé de Sousa (5 July 1559), and to the Cardinal-Infante D. Henrique (1 June 1560). It prints as introduction Antonio Franco's *Vida do P. Manoel da Nóbrega* (≈ 25,000 words of the total). One file per letter.
Original, ranked:
1. **BBM, Valle Cabral 1886** (`bbm/8016`) — A; clean modern text layer (90,534 words). Best base.
2. **archive.org `cartaaaapdrmnelnobrg`** (same 1886 edition, BNDigital scan "bndigital1867", Public Domain Mark) — 84,500 OCR words, fair to poor in places ("Nobresa cscreveu"). Second witness.
3. **ABL 1931, *Cartas do Brasil 1549–1560*** (notes Valle Cabral and Rodolfo Garcia; Garcia d. 1949) — A; BBM item for the 1931 ABL series was found for *Cartas avulsas* (`bbm/4676`); the 1931 Nóbrega volume itself was not located online.
4. **RIHGB t. XLIII p. 1 (1880)**, "Archeologia histórica" — first printing of a group of letters with the Diálogo; also Innocêncio's appendix to Vasconcelos's *Chronica*, 2nd ed., Lisbon 1865 (`chronicadacompan00vasc`, `…01vasc`), and Balthasar da Silva Lisboa's *Annaes do Rio de Janeiro* (1834–35). A; cross-checks.
5. **Spanish 1551 *Copia de unas cartas…*** (BBM `bbm/9049`) — contemporary Spanish translation of the first letters; OCR unusable; scan only.
English: none PD; no English translation of the letters as a body found.

### Novas cartas jesuíticas (1940): the further Nóbrega texts
Part I "Cartas de Nóbrega" (pp. 21–131): 14 letters (to Simão Rodrigues, Quinquagesima 1553; to Luís Gonçalves da Câmara, 15 July and 31 Aug. 1553; to Ignatius, 25 Mar. 1555; to Miguel de Torres, 2 Sept. 1557; to Lainez, 30 July 1559 and 12 June 1561; to Miguel de Torres, 14 Apr. 1561; three to Francisco Henriques, 12 June 1561…), no. 8 *Apontamentos de coisas do Brasil* (1558) and no. 15 *Se o pai pode vender a seu filho…* (1567, Caxa's opinion and Nóbrega's answer).
1. **archive `novascartasjesui00leit`** (Princeton, majorityworldcollection; good OCR) and **`CartasNbregaEVieira`** (second scan, 97,000 words). The 16th-century texts are public domain; Leite's introductions and notes are protected until 2040 (Brazil, 70 years p.m.). The scan is distributed openly on archive.org with no rights statement. **Tier: text A / edition C — unverified**; importing Nóbrega's words without Leite's notes is defensible but is an **open decision**.
2. The *Caso de consciência* exists in the Évora codex CVIII/2-1 (noted by Valle Cabral 1886, p. vi); no other free printing located.
English: none.

### Diálogo sobre a conversão do gentio
Dialogue between the Brothers Gonçalo Álvares (preacher-interpreter) and Mateus Nogueira (the blacksmith), on whether and how the Indians can be converted. Written in Bahia 1556–57; Évora codex. Short: one file.
1. **RIHGB t. XLIII parte 1 (1880), pp. 133–152**, "Dialogo do padre Nobrega sobre a conversão do gentio: interlocutores Gonçalo Alves e Matheus Nogueira" — archive `revistadoinstit74brasgoog` (Google scan; ≈ 6,400 OCR words, fair OCR with typical Google errors). A. Leite (1954) judged it "uma cópia moderna (não perfeita)" of the Évora text (quoted in G. S. C. Almeida, UFRJ monograph, https://pantheon.ufrj.br/bitstream/11422/16710/1/GSCAlmeida.pdf). The other archive item labelled vol. 43 (`revistadoinstit81brasgoog`) is the second part and does not contain it.
2. Leite, Lisbon 1954 (Comissão do IV Centenário de São Paulo), critical text from the Évora codex — C. Hansen 2010 — C.
3. Évora manuscript (BPE) — digitization not located.
English: excerpts in modern anthologies (C). None PD.

### Tratado contra a antropofagia (1559) and minor pieces
Known from Leite's 1955 *opera omnia* and *Monumenta Brasiliae*; no PD printing found in the 1880–1940 editions checked (1886 ed., RIHGB 43, Novas cartas). **Only C sources; unverified.** Needs the Évora/ARSI manuscript or a 19th-century printing not yet identified.

## Portuguese
Portuguese is the original language of all of Nóbrega's works; no translation is needed. Sources above, ranked: BBM 1886 (best text layer) → archive 1886 → RIHGB XLIII (1880) → Novas cartas 1940 (text only) → Vasconcelos/Innocêncio 1865. Modernisation of the 16th-century spelling for pt-BR is the only editorial step. Domínio Público returned HTTP 403 and was not searched; Google Books API returned 429; pt.wikisource has no Nóbrega texts in this pass (rate-limited; the Anchieta author page lists none).
