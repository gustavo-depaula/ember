# St. Gertrude the Great (1256–c. 1302) and St. Mechtilde of Hackeborn (1241–1298): sourcing report

Bibliography basis: the Solesmes edition (ed. L. Paquelin), *Revelationes Gertrudianae ac Mechtildianae*, Poitiers/Paris: H. Oudin, vol. I 1875 (Gertrude: *Legatus divinae pietatis* + *Exercitia spiritualia*) and vol. II 1877 (Mechtilde: *Liber specialis gratiae*, plus the Latin version of Mechthild of Magdeburg's *Lux divinitatis*). The modern critical edition is Sources Chrétiennes (Doyère, Clément, Hourlier, Schmitt, 1967–86): tier C.

Authorship: *Legatus* Book II is written by Gertrude herself. Books I and III–V were compiled by a fellow nun from her accounts. The *Liber specialis gratiae* was written down by Gertrude and another nun, from Mechtilde's revelations. **Spurious or derivative:** *Preces Gertrudianae* (a 17th-century prayer manual drawn from the *Legatus*), the "Gertrudenbuch" prayer books, and the "Prayer of St. Gertrude for the souls in purgatory" (modern).

Neither author is in the corpus. (Mechthild of **Magdeburg**, whose *Fließendes Licht* is in Solesmes vol. II, is a different author and out of scope here.)

## Sources ranked

### Latin
1. **Solesmes 1875/1877.** Vol. II is digitized by BLB Karlsruhe. The two small PDFs I fetched are the title page and a preface, the OCR is very poor, and the full volume is a page-image viewer. I did not locate vol. I online (archive.org has nothing; Google Books and HathiTrust likely have it, unverified). **There is no clean Latin text of either work online except IntraText's *Legatus* I–II.**
2. **IntraText LAT0714** (*Legatus*, "IntraText Edition I", 2007): Books I and II only, 24,467 words, with [p. NN] page refs to Solesmes. Clean HTML. The site is "licensed under a Creative Commons License" (EuloTech), so tier B; the base text is PD.
3. Older Latin editions, scans: Lanspergius (Cologne 1536), Canteleu (Paris 1662, used by Cusack), Mège 1664.
4. German, useful for checking: J. Müller, *Leben und Offenbarungen der heiligen Mechtildis*, Regensburg: Manz 1880 (from Solesmes, [archive](https://archive.org/details/lebenundoffenbar01mech), 145k tokens).

### English
- **Legatus:** Cusack's version is the only PD English, and it is not a straight translation. Its preface says Book I was "omitted, and the substance of it carefully embodied" in a new Life of the saint. Books II–V are translated from Canteleu's 1662 Latin, with "one chapter … omitted, and several passages, which could hardly be rendered in English with propriety", and some Book V chapters folded into the Life. Usable for Books II–V with gaps marked. The modern complete versions are C: Winkworth (Paulist 1993), Barratt (Cistercian 1991–2018).
- **Exercitia:** (1) Pope 1921, translated from the Latin, the better of the two; (2) 1863, translated from Guéranger's French. Lewis & Lewis (Cistercian 1989) is C.
- **Liber specialis gratiae:** only selections are PD (1875, 1922). Middle English *The Booke of Gostlye Grace* (ed. Halligan, PIMS 1979) and Newman's *Book of Special Grace* (Paulist 2017) are C.

## Notes
- *Legatus* structure: Book → chapter (I: 17 chapters; II: prologue + 24; III–V long). Split one chapter per file. Liturgical cross-references abound (Book IV follows the church year), which is a good fit with Ember's calendar.
- *Liber specialis gratiae*: Pars I–VII → chapters. Part I follows the liturgical year too.

## Recommendation
- Layout: `helfta/gertrude-legatus`, `helfta/gertrude-exercitia`, `helfta/mechtilde-liber-specialis-gratiae` (or one author dir each).
- **Import first:** the *Exercitia spiritualia*. It is short and liturgical, has a PD English from the Latin (Pope 1921), and its Latin is ~35k words, though the Latin has to be keyed or OCR'd from a Solesmes 1875 scan. Then *Legatus* (Latin from IntraText for I–II and a Solesmes scan for III–V; English from Cusack, II–V).
- **Not cleanly sourceable now:** any clean Latin beyond *Legatus* I–II, which needs a Solesmes vol. I scan (find on Google Books/HathiTrust) and OCR; and a complete English of the *Liber specialis gratiae* (PD only in selections, so the pipeline would translate).
- **Total size (estimates):** Gertrude ≈ 185k words of Latin, Mechtilde ≈ 170k. **≈ 355k words.** PD English ≈ 340k (selective).
