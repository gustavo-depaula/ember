# Fray Luis de León OSA (1527–1591): sourcing report

Bibliography. Spanish works: *De los nombres de Cristo* (1583, 2 books; 1585, 3 books; the name *Cordero* added in the posthumous 1595 ed.); *La perfecta casada* (1583); *Exposición del libro de Job* (finished 1591, first printed 1779); *Cantar de los cantares* (Spanish translation with commentary, 1561, the cause of his trial; printed 1798); poems — ~23 original odes plus verse translations (Psalms, Job in tercets, the *Song of Songs*, Horace, Virgil's *Eclogues* and *Georgics* I–II, Pindar, Tibullus, Petrarch, Bembo…), first printed by Quevedo, 1631; letters and the writings from his trial (*Proceso*, ed. in *Colección de documentos inéditos para la historia de España* X–XI, 1847). He also edited the first edition of St. Teresa's works (1588) with a famous dedicatory letter, and began a Life of her (unfinished). Latin works: *In Cantica canticorum explanatio* (1580; 3rd, triple ed. 1589), *In Psalmum XXVI explanatio* (1580), *In Abdiam prophetam* and *In Epistolam ad Galatas* (1589), *De utriusque agni typici atque veri immolationis legitimo tempore* (1590), *In Ecclesiasten*, and his Salamanca lectures (*De fide*, *De spe*, *De caritate*, *De incarnatione*, *De praedestinatione*, *De legibus*, *De religione*…), collected in *Mag. Luysii Legionensis Augustiniani… Opera*, ed. Marcelino Gutiérrez OSA et al., 7 vols, Salamanca 1891–1895, and now in the critical *Opera* series of the Escurialenses (tier C).

The standard Spanish collected editions: Antolín Merino OSA, *Obras del M. Fr. Luis de León*, 6 vols, Madrid 1804–1816 (reprinted Madrid 1885, 4/6 vols); BAE 37 (*Escritores del siglo XVI*, t. II, ed. Mayans/Rivadeneyra, 1855); Félix García OSA, *Obras completas castellanas* (BAC, 1944ff.) — C.

Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/` checked).

## Notes per work, sources ranked

### De los nombres de Cristo
Dialogue in three books between Marcelo, Juliano and Sabino on the names given to Christ in Scripture: Book I — *De los nombres en general*, Pimpollo, Faces de Dios, Camino, Pastor, Monte, Padre del siglo futuro; Book II — Brazo de Dios, Rey de Dios, Príncipe de paz, Esposo; Book III — Hijo de Dios, Amado, Jesús, Cordero. Each book has a dedication. Wikisource splits long names into (II)/(III) pages; one file per name (≈ 18 files).
Original, ranked:
1. **es.wikisource**, complete (30 pages, 168,372 words by the search index) — clean, proofread transcription of a public-domain edition (the "Tomo 1–3" layout suggests the Onís *Clásicos Castellanos* edition, 1914–21; not confirmed on the page). CC BY-SA for the transcription, PD text. Best base.
2. **Federico de Onís, Clásicos Castellanos (La Lectura), 1914–1921, 3 vols** — A as US publication pre-1930 (Onís d. 1966: his notes may still be protected in Spain). archive.org `delosnombresdecr01le` (vol. 1, 78,700 OCR words, good OCR, with line numbers every 5 lines and notes). Critical text (1587 ed.); best cross-check.
3. **Madrid, Calleja, 1917, 2 vols** (`delosnombresdecr01leon`, `delosnombresdecr02leon`) and the 17th ed. 1907 (`losnombresdecris00leuoft`) — A; popular texts.
4. **BAE 37 (1855)** (`escritoresdelsi00zrgoog`, 759k OCR words for the whole León volume, two-column) and **Merino 1804–16** — A.
English: no public-domain translation found. Schuster, *The Names of Christ*, Herder 1955 (archive `namesofchrist0000loui`, lending only) — renewal not checked, treat as unverified/C; Durán & Kluback, Paulist Classics of Western Spirituality 1984 — C.

### La perfecta casada
Commentary on Proverbs 31 for María Varela Osorio, ~20 chapters. One file per chapter.
1. **Project Gutenberg #61573**, proofread by PGDP from images of the Biblioteca Digital de Castilla y León (an 1898 edition), original spelling kept, notes moved to the end — A; 46,266 words including the Gutenberg licence. Best base.
2. **es.wikisource** (23 pages, 41,272 words) — A/CC BY-SA; second witness.
3. **Elizabeth Wallace's school edition, University of Chicago Press, 1902** (`laperfectacasad00legoog`) — A; Spanish text with English notes.
4. BAE 37; Merino.
English: no PD translation found (Hubbard 1943 unverified; Mellen 1999 bilingual — C).

### Exposición del libro de Job
42 chapters, each: literal translation from the Hebrew, verse-by-verse exposition, and (Book's end) the tercet paraphrase. The longest Spanish work.
1. **es.wikisource** (87 pages, 230,136 words) — A/CC BY-SA. Base.
2. **Madrid 1779 *princeps*** (`exposiciondellibrode-jobobraposthuma`) — A; 18th-c. print, OCR fair.
3. Merino vols 3–4 (1805), BAE 37 — A.
English: none.

### Cantar de los cantares
Translation from the Hebrew with commentary, 8 chapters + prologue.
1. **es.wikisource** pages "Cantar de los cantares: Prólogo / Capítulo 1–8" (category P1855, i.e. from BAE 37), 42,722 words — A. Base.
2. BAE 37 scan; Merino vol. 5.
English: none.

### Poesías
Short pieces: one file per poem, grouped as original poems / translations of Scripture / classical translations.
1. **es.wikisource**, individual pages (*A Felipe Ruiz*, *A Francisco Salinas*, *Noche serena*, *Profecía del Tajo*, *Vida retirada*, *Al salir de la cárcel*…; *Bucólicas*, *Geórgicas*) — A. Not every poem and translation is on Wikisource; the Psalms translations are missing there.
2. **BAE 37 (1855)** — A; complete *Poesías*.
3. **Mayans, *Obras propias y traducciones de latín, griego y toscano, con la paráfrasis de algunos salmos***, Valencia 1761 / 1785 (`obraspropiasytra00leon`, `obraspropriasit00siscgoog`) — A; complete, 18th-c. print.
4. Merino vol. 6; *Poesías* ed. Ramón Fernández 1808 (`poesiasdelmtrofr10leon`).
English: (1) **Henry Phillips Jr., 1883, privately printed** — A; only the major odes. (2) Thomas Walsh, *Hispanic Anthology*, 1920 (several odes) — A, not checked online. (3) Willis Barnstone, *The Unknown Light*, 1979 — C.

### Latin works
Only early prints online (Salamanca 1580/1582 *In Cantica* and *In Psalmum XXVI*; 1589 *In Abdiam/Galatas*, *In Cantica* triplex not located), Italian BNC and Google scans on archive.org, OCR unreliable. The 1891–95 *Opera* (7 vols) is the needed source: the Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León digital library lists a digitized "Opera… 1892" in 2 vols with a CC BY-NC-ND licence on the scans (text itself PD); the full 7 volumes were not located (HathiTrust/BDH likely; unverified). The modern Escurialenses edition — C.

## Portuguese
No Portuguese translation of any work by Luis de León was found online, in the public domain or otherwise (searched archive.org by title and author, Google/web for "A perfeita casada", "Dos nomes de Cristo", "Os nomes de Cristo"; pt.wikisource searches were blocked by rate-limiting, unverified). pt-BR would be translated from the Spanish directly.
