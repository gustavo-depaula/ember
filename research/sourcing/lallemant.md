Lallemant left no writings of his own. The *Doctrine spirituelle* is his teaching, preserved in his disciples' notes (chiefly Rigoleuc's, also Surin's). Pierre Champion, SJ, arranged it into "seven principles" and published it in 1694 with his life of Lallemant. It is the only work under his name, so this one book is his complete works. Not in the corpus.

## Structure

- Champion's *Avertissement* and *Vie du P. Louis Lallemant* (~10k words).
- *Doctrine spirituelle* in **7 Principles**, each with chapters, many of them in articles (the 1908 OCR has 123 "ARTICLE" heads). In some English editions Principle II is split into sections:
  1. La vue de la fin
  2. L'idée de la perfection
  3. La pureté du cœur (incl. "les principes de la corruption du cœur")
  4. La docilité à la conduite du Saint-Esprit
  5. Le recueillement et la vie intérieure
  6. L'union avec Notre-Seigneur
  7. L'ordre et les degrés de la vie spirituelle
- Some editions (Pottier's critical edition; McDougall's English) add an appendix of shorter *pensées*/maxims. Check each witness for it.
- Chapter split: one file per principle with chapters as sections, or one per chapter. Articles become in-file headings.

## Sources, ranked

### French

1. **archive.org `DoctrineSpirituelle`** (https://archive.org/details/DoctrineSpirituelle). *La Doctrine spirituelle du P. Louis Lallemant … précédée de sa vie*, nouvelle édition, Paris: Librairie Victor Lecoffre, J. Gabalda & Cie, 1908 (a Google scan of a Penn State copy). Tier A. OCR is good, with modern spelling. "Digitized by Google / Original from PENN STATE" lines and running heads must be stripped, and line-end hyphens ("cons¬ cience") joined. **Best source.**
2. **archive.org `doctrinespiritue00lall`**. *Doctrine spirituelle du Père Louis Lallemant … précédée de la vie de l'auteur*, Avignon: Chambeau / Paris: Guillot, 1781 (Burns Library copy). ~113k words of OCR. Tier A. 18th-century orthography with long-s, which the OCR reads as "f" ("paflîon", "réfifte"), so this witness is noisy. Use it for cross-checking.
3. **archive.org `lavieetladoctri00chamgoog`**. *La Vie et la doctrine spirituelle du père L. Lallemant*, Lyon: P. Valfray, 1735 (Google scan, Michigan). ~88k words of OCR. Tier A. The oldest witness found; it has the same long-s problems.
4. The **1694 first edition** (Paris, Estienne Michallet) and Aloys Pottier's critical edition (Paris: Téqui, 1924) were not found online. Pottier d. 1942, so his edition is PD by the death rule. It would be the best text if a scan turns up (unverified). François Courel's edition (DDB/Christus, 1959; archive.org `lavieetladoctrin0000fran`) is tier C.

### English

1. **Burns & Lambert, London 1855**, *The Spiritual Doctrine of Father Louis Lallemant … preceded by some account of his life, translated from the French*, edited (with a preface) by Frederick William Faber; translator anonymous. "This Translation is made from the edition published at Paris by Lecoffre and Co. in the year 1846." Three scans on archive.org: `spiritualdoctri00fabegoog` (Oxford, Google; ~111k words of OCR), `a589552400lalluoft` (Toronto), `SpiritualDoctrineFrLouisLallemant1855`. Tier A. OCR is reasonable. **Best**, because it translates the same Lecoffre text family as French source 1.
2. **D. & J. Sadlier, New York c. 1885**, reprint of the 1855 translation. archive.org `fatherlouislalle00lalluoft` (~109k words). Tier A. A second scan of the same text, good for correcting OCR.
3. **Alan G. McDougall (ed.)**, London: Burns Oates & Washbourne 1928 (imprimatur Westminster, 2 Jan 1928), reprinted Westminster MD: Newman Book Shop 1946. archive.org `spiritualdoctrin00lall` (open; ~108k words). A revision of the old translation, with an introduction that uses Pottier's critical edition. Tier A by the pre-1930 rule for the 1928 text; the 1946 US reprint carries no new matter beyond that (renewal unverified). The English is more modern. Use it as a second witness or to fill gaps.

## Recommendation

- **Layout**: `lallemant-doctrine-spirituelle/`, a single book (this is the complete oeuvre). TOC: `avertissement`, `vie` (Champion), `principe-1` … `principe-7` (or per chapter). fr-FR from the 1908 Gabalda text; en-US from Faber 1855, cross-checked against Sadlier 1885.
- **Import first**: the whole book. It is one medium-sized volume and both witnesses are clean OCR.
- **Caveats**: credit the *Vie* to Champion. Check whether the 1908 edition and the 1855 English both carry the appendix of maxims. Pottier 1924 would be the ideal text if it can be found.

**Total size**: ~100k words French, ~100k English.
