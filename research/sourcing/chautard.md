# Dom Jean-Baptiste Chautard OCSO (1858–1935) — sourcing

Trappist abbot of Sept-Fons from 1899. His one major work is *L'Âme de tout apostolat*. It began as the 1907 pamphlet *L'Apostolat des catéchismes et la vie intérieure* (written for the Sisters of the Sainte-Famille du Sacré-Cœur), was recast and enlarged as *L'Âme de tout apostolat* in 1912 (imprimatur Moulins, 2 Aug 1912), and was revised through many editions (6th ed. and 50,000 copies by 1916; 195,000 by 1937). The other titles are short Cistercian brochures (list from fr.wikipedia "Jean-Baptiste Chautard"): *Saint Bernard et la fondation des Cisterciennes dites trappistines* (1919, later *Les Cisterciennes trappistines*), *L'Esprit de simplicité, caractéristique de Cîteaux* (1928), *L'Âme cistercienne* (Paris lecture of 28 Jan 1931, printed as a brochure), *La Règle de saint Benoît illustrée par saint Bernard* (1934), plus war-time articles signed "Frère Aîné" in *Le Prêtre aux Armées*. Not in the corpus.

**Copyright frame.** Chautard d. 1935, so his French text is PD in Brazil and the EU (life + 70, since 2006). For the English: Girardey's 1918 translation is PD (US 1918). Merton's 1946 translation was **renewed**: NYPL renewal record R580642, 24 Jun 1974, "The Soul of the apostolate. By Jean-Baptist Chautard, translation & introd.: Thomas Merton. © 31Dec46; A543945", claimant the Trustees of the Merton Legacy Trust. It is also sold by TAN, so it is **C**. Gallica and HathiTrust returned Cloudflare 403s throughout this session and could not be searched.

## Notes

### L'Âme de tout apostolat: French sources, ranked
1. **https://archive.org/details/lamedetoutaposto0000unse**: a 1927 printing "Abbaye de Sept-Fons, par Dompierre-sur-Besbre (Allier) / Em. Vitte, Lyon / P. Téqui, Paris". The edition number is illegible in the OCR ("…ème édition", "1?0e mille"). It is the post-1912 enlarged text, carrying the 1912 Moulins imprimatur and letters of approbation (Card. Arcoverde, Bp Penon), and is complete through the table of contents (ends p. 295). It is a Graduate Theological Union copy digitised in 2025, open access. OCR is **fair, not clean**: frequent `à`→`4`, `ô`/`â`→`d`/`a`, `œ`→`ce` ("4 prétre", "lorai­son", "mceurs"), and running heads ("42, L'AME DE TOUT APOSTOLAT") interleaved. It needs a correction pass, ideally against page images or a second witness.
2. No second French witness was found. Library records show a 1941 16th edition (210th thousand, with a Pétain preface: avoid that front matter) at the French defence digital library (https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/impression/document/a2ce5762-dae9-4819-ae24-154c101ddd08, unreachable from here, viewability unknown) and an undated "4e édition entièrement revue et augmentée" (Palacký University catalogue). Gallica should be checked by hand for a second edition to cross-check against.
- Modern annotated French edition (B. Martelet, Saint-Paul) and current reprints: **C**, do not use.
- Structure: Prologue; five *Parties* (1 God wills works and the interior life; 2 Union of the active and interior life; 3 Without the interior life action is dangerous; 4 Fruitfulness of works through the interior life; 5 Principles and counsels for the interior life: liturgical life, custody of the heart, devotion to Our Lady), each in lettered/numbered sections; footnotes are references (Homo apost., encyclicals). The chapter split would be one file per Partie, or per numbered subsection inside the long Parts 4–5.
- Edition drift: Chautard kept enlarging the text up to his death (Merton's 1946 version, translated from a late edition, adds the liturgical-life and Marian sections and the "Ten aids to mental prayer" appendix). The 1927 printing already contains the liturgical-life and custody-of-the-heart sections (seen in its table of contents). Whether the last editions (1930s) add more is **unverified**.

### English sources, ranked
1. **https://archive.org/details/bwb_S0-CDW-403**: *The True Apostolate*, "from the French of Dom J. B. Chautard … by Rev. Ferreol Girardey, C.SS.R.", B. Herder, St. Louis 1918, "Copyright, 1918 by Joseph Gummersbach". Open djvu.txt, good OCR after the front matter. It is PD (US 1918) but **condensed**: the preface says "though somewhat condensed in its English dress". It translates the 1916 6th edition and follows the same Part I–V outline at roughly half the length, so it will not align paragraph-by-paragraph with a 1927 French text.
2. Merton 1946 (Gethsemani; Doubleday Image 1961; TAN today): the complete and best-known English, freely uploaded several times on archive.org (`dom-chautard-the-soul-of-the-apostolate`, 94k words, clean text; `the-soul-of-the-apostolate-chautard-catholic-church-theology`, 97k; `TheSoulOfTheApostolateChautardJeanBaptiste4736`, restricted). These uploads carry no licence but the copyright **was renewed**: tier **C**, unusable. (Its endnote 182 mentions "the previous translator … writing in England", which is Moran.)
3. J. A. Moran's "authorised translation": the 3rd American edition (Mission Press S.V.D., Techny, Illinois, 1941) is on archive.org only as a borrow-only scan (`soulofapostolate0000revj`, Graduate Theological Union copy, djvu.txt returns 401). There is also a Gill, Dublin printing [1957]. The NYPL renewal records show no renewal for it (only Merton's), so the US edition is tier B. The first edition's date and Moran's death year are unknown, so its status outside the US is unverified. It is the best candidate for a complete non-Merton English, but it would need the scan borrowed and OCR'd.

## Portuguese

Searched: archive.org (full-text and metadata), pt.wikisource (no hit), Google Books (daily API quota exhausted, not searched), Domínio Público and Biblioteca Nacional Digital (Cloudflare challenge, not searchable from a script), the BNP catalogue (no Chautard record found through the open-data URN), and web searches covering Permanência, Obras Católicas and Montfort (no hit). The book reached Brazil early: Sept-Fons founded the Trappist house of Tremembé (SP) in 1904, and a Portuguese translation existed in Chautard's lifetime. Its front matter carries a letter from Dom Epaminondas Nunes de Ávila, bishop of Taubaté (1909–1935), praising the book "vertida para a Língua Portuguesa". I did not find a catalogue record for that first Brazilian edition.

Ranked:
1. **https://archive.org/details/jean-baptiste_chautard_a_alma_de_todo_apostolado**: *A Alma de Todo Apostolado*, community upload of 13 Jul 2023 (uploader account "veritatis"; no publisher, translator or rights statement in the metadata or the text). It is Brazilian Portuguese in modern orthography ("fato", "contato", "fenômeno"; no European forms) and born-digital, so the text is clean (~78k words, `chautard/alma-pt-2023_djvu.txt`). It is complete: the approbation letters (including the Brazilian ones), a biographical preface on Chautard, the whole text, and 397 endnotes, many of them modern editorial notes ("Em tradução livre: …", "sem versão portuguesa"). It looks like a recent re-edition of the old Brazilian translation with new notes. Translator and editor are unidentified, so the tier is **unverified**: treat it as C until the edition is identified. It is useful now as a reading reference for our pt-BR.
2. **https://archive.org/details/a-alma-de-todo-apostolado**: community upload (2021, no metadata beyond "Capítulo 1 A vida interior"). It is a **fragment** (~8.7k words, chapter 1 only), in European Portuguese of a modern edition ("fenómeno", "factor", "heróica"). Source edition unknown: **unverified**. Low value.
- No pre-1956 Portuguese printing (Brazilian or European) was found digitised.

## Recommendation

- **Layout:** a single book, `chautard-ame-de-tout-apostolat`, with `fr-FR/` from the 1927 Sept-Fons printing (chapters = Prologue, Parts 1–5, Epilogue, splitting Parts 4–5 at their numbered sections). The brochures are too small and too unavailable to justify a `chautard-opera-omnia`.
- **English:** Girardey 1918 is the only usable English, and it is an abridgment of an earlier edition. Either ship it as `en-US/` with a note that it is condensed and from the 1916 text, or ship French + pt-BR only and leave en-US empty until a PD full translation (Moran's, via the borrow-only 1941 scan; tier B in the US) is found. Do not use Merton.
- **Before import:** find a second French witness, from Gallica (by hand in a browser) or another archive scan, to correct the fair-quality OCR.
- **Cannot be sourced cleanly now:** the 1907 pamphlet and the Cistercian brochures (no digital copies found); Merton (C).
- **Portuguese:** no tier-A Portuguese text exists online. The 2023 Brazilian upload is a clean reference to read beside our own pt-BR translation, but do not import it until its edition and translator are identified.
- **Total size:** ~78k words French (1927 edition) + ~41k English (Girardey, condensed).

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/chautard/` (1927 French djvu.txt, Girardey 1918 djvu.txt, two Merton djvu.txt files kept only for reference and not usable, and the two Portuguese djvu.txt files).
