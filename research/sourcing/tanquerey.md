# Adolphe Tanquerey, PSS (1854–1932): sourcing

**Copyright frame.** Tanquerey died 21 February 1932. His own texts are therefore public domain in Brazil (life + 70, Lei 9.610 art. 41, so since 1 Jan 2003) and in the EU (France adds war extensions, which expired by about 2011 at the latest). In the US anything published in 1930 or earlier is PD by date. His later Desclée (Belgian/French) editions of 1931 onward are foreign works, so URAA restored their US copyright whatever their renewal status, and it runs 95 years from publication. Desclée renewed several titles anyway (see the renewal evidence below). By the brief's definition ("author dead 70+ years") every Tanquerey original is **tier A**. Translations are tiered separately.

**US renewal evidence** (NYPL transcription of the renewal records 1950–2001, searched in a local copy of the NYPL cce-renewals TSVs (1950–2001, minus 1994 and 1997–2000); the matching rows are saved in `research/sourcing/tanquerey/renewals-grep.tsv`; grep on "Tanquerey", "Branderis", titles). Desclée & Cie renewed:
- *Brevior synopsis theologiae moralis et pastoralis*, ed. 8, © 22 Nov 1924, AF26163 → R106721 (1952). Now PD in the US by date anyway.
- *Synopsis theologiae dogmaticae* T. 2, ed. 20 "penitus recognita", © 15 Jul 1926 → R124649 (1954). PD by date.
- *Abrégé de théologie ascétique et mystique* (with J. Gautier), © 20 May 1927 → R146659 (1955). PD by date.
- *Compendio de teología ascética y mística* (Spanish, tr. D. García Hughes), © 16 Aug 1930 → R217079 (1958). PD by date (1930).
- *Brevior synopsis theologiae dogmaticae*, ed. 7 (with J. B. Bord), © 15 Oct 1931 → R241222 (1959). US-protected until 1 Jan 2027.
- *Grundriss der aszetischen und mystischen Theologie* (German, 1931) → R241223.

No renewal turned up for *The Spiritual Life* (Branderis, 1930), *Doctrine and Devotion* (1933), *A Manual of Dogmatic Theology* (1959) or *Les dogmes générateurs de la piété*. The data's gaps are the 1994 and 1997–2000 files; a 1959 work would have renewed in 1987, which is present.

**Corpus check.** There is no Tanquerey in `_corpus-books.txt` or `content/books/`; the only match is a mention in the Catholic Encyclopedia.

**Gallica / HathiTrust:** not searched. Gallica returns a Cloudflare challenge to scripted requests ("Attention Required! | Cloudflare"), and HathiTrust likewise. Gallica's text mode could supply a second OCR of the French, so a manual search there is worthwhile.

## Notes per work, with ranked sources

### Précis de théologie ascétique et mystique (1923–24)
His major work, in two volumes in the 1st edition and one volume from about the 5th. **Structure:** Introduction (nos. 1–47); *Première partie: Les principes* (ch. I–VI: origins of the supernatural life, its nature, perfection, the obligation of perfection, general means); *Seconde partie: Les trois voies* in three *Livres* (voie purgative, illuminative, unitive, the last including the mystical states); *Appendice* on the discernment of spirits, and a bibliography. Paragraphs are numbered continuously (≈1–1600). Footnotes are present (patristic and scholastic references). A chapter split would follow Introduction / Part I chapters / Part II books and chapters, with the paragraph numbers kept as anchors; the English uses the same numbering, so the two align paragraph for paragraph.

French, best first:
1. **archive.org `precisdetheologi0000tanq`**: 10th ed., imprimatur 1928, "TOUS DROITS RÉSERVÉS Copyright 1923 et 1924 by DESCLÉE & Co." Full text with a clean `_djvu.txt` (366k words). The OCR is good: accents are right and line-end hyphens are kept ("immédiate- ment"), so they need joining. Best, because it is complete with clean OCR.
2. **archive.org `precis-de-theologie-ascetique-et-mystique-1924_202305`**: the title page says 9th ed. (the IA description says "3rd edition"), same 1928 imprimatur, 368k words. A different scan of a near-identical text, ideal for cross-checking OCR.
3. Gallica: not checked (Cloudflare). A 1st edition (1923–24) would be the best witness to the text as first published, if Gallica has it.

English, best first:
1. **archive.org `spirituallifetre0000tanq`**: Branderis, "Second and revised edition", Desclée Tournai, imprimatur Baltimore 24 May 1930, xlviii + 750 pp. The page says "All rights reserved", but no renewal was found and the date puts it in the public domain in the US. 377k words. Clean modern OCR from the Graduate Theological Union scan.
2. **archive.org `MN41530ucmf_5`**: the same edition from a University of Chicago microfilm, 388k words. The OCR is noisier (stray marks), but it is an independent scan.
3. **archive.org `spiritual_life_1930-tanquerey`**: the same edition from Google Books, marked with the CC Public Domain Mark; 384k words; noisy front matter.
4. **EWTN library** `https://www.ewtn.com/catholicism/library/spiritual-life-12628` … `-12639` (12 pages): clean HTML and **partial** (~180k words, selected sections). One page carries "Copyright (c) Trinity Communications 1995", the others "© 2025 EWTN Inc". Useful only to spot-check against.
5. isidore.co holds an OCR PDF of the 1930 edition (`The Spiritual Life … 1930_OCR.pdf`); not downloaded.
6. `TanquereySpiritualLife` (archive.org community upload): a 26k-word fragment. Ignore it.

Tier C to avoid: the TAN Books reprint (in print, sold). Branderis's dates are given as 1893–1963 in a bookseller listing (unverified), which would make the translation PD in Brazil only from 2034. The US PD status (published 1930) is what makes it usable; record that in the import metadata.

### Les dogmes générateurs de la piété (1926; 4th ed. 1931)
**Structure:** Introduction, then a *Première série* of chapters: I. Le Verbe incarné source de notre vie surnaturelle; II. Notre incorporation au Christ; III. Notre participation à la vie divine; IV. Marie notre mère et médiatrice; V. …, and further *séries*. 261 pp., footnotes. French: the only open copy is archive.org `les-dogmes-generateurs-de-la-pi-adolphe-tanquerey` (4th ed. 1931, "Tous droits réservés"; 86k words; good OCR). It is a community upload; a second copy, `lesdogmesgenerat0000adta_l6v5`, is borrow-only. English: *Doctrine and Devotion* (Desclée, 1933) is borrow-only on IA, with translators unverified. That English translation cannot be sourced cleanly; our own pipeline could translate from the French.

### Synopsis theologiae dogmaticae (3 tomes)
A Latin seminary manual "ad mentem S. Thomae". **Structure:** T. I *De vera religione, de Ecclesia, de fontibus revelationis* (dogmatica fundamentalis); T. II *De fide, de Deo uno et trino, de Deo creante et elevante, de Verbo incarnato*; T. III *De Deo sanctificante et remuneratore: de gratia, de sacramentis, de novissimis*. Within each tome the order is tract → chapter → article → numbered paragraph, with scholia and footnotes. The editions were constantly revised (18th 1921, 20th "penitus recognita" 1926, up to the 25th in the 1940s), so pick one edition and keep to it.

Ranked:
1. **ed. 18, 1921** (Brigham Young copies): `synopsistheologi01tanq` / `02tanq` / `03tanq`, 278k + 311k + 242k words. "Jus proprietatis auctor sibi vindicat." PD in the US (1921). The OCR is fair to good; ligatures come out wrong (Æ→"^E", "TlIOM/E"), mostly on title pages, and the running text is cleaner.
2. **GTU copies, 1921**: `synopsistheologl0001unse`, `synopsistheologi0002unse`, `synopsistheologi0003unse`. Same edition, newer OCR, good for cross-checking (not downloaded).
3. **Earlier editions** (Internet Archive "synopsis-theologiae-dogmaticae-ed.-N-t.-N" series: ed. 1–15, 1895–1914; Benziger NY ed. 15 T.1 1914 `synopsistheologi00tanq`, 279k words). These are different texts, so they are fallbacks only.

English: Byrnes's 1959 translation of the full *Synopsis* was not found online, so it is unavailable.

### Synopsis theologiae moralis et pastoralis (3 tomes)
**Structure:** T. I *De Poenitentia, Matrimonio et Ordine*; T. II *Theologia moralis fundamentalis: de virtutibus et praeceptis*; T. III *De virtute Justitiae et de variis statuum obligationibus*. Ranked: (1) **ed. 7, Desclée 1922** (PIMS Toronto), `synopsistheolog01tanq` / `02tanq` / `03tanq`, 270k + 278k + 225k words. Fair OCR: ligatures and some letter confusions ("dc" for "de"). (2) T. I in two other open copies, `synopsistheologi0000unse` (1925, 275k) and `depanitentiadema0000unse` (1922, 271k), for cross-checking. There is no English.

### Brevior synopsis theologiae dogmaticae
Tanquerey with E.-M. Quévastre and L. Hébert (later J. B. Bord). Ranked: (1) **ed. 3, 1919**, `breviorsynopsist00tanq`, 223k words, PD in the US; (2) `breviorsynopsist0000adta` (1925, open djvu); (3) ed. 7, 1931, `brevior_synopsis_theologiae_dogmaticae_1931-ad_tanquerey` (234k words, "Copyright 1931 by DESCLÉE", renewed R241222; US-protected until 1 Jan 2027, so prefer 1919/1925); (4) `breviorsynopsist00tanq_202601` (community upload, edition unverified). Co-authors: Quévastre and Hébert's death dates are unverified, so the earliest editions are the safest. English: *A Manual of Dogmatic Theology* (Byrnes, 1959), tier B on paper but borrow-only, so not usable now.

### Brevior synopsis theologiae moralis et pastoralis
The only open copy found is the 1946 ed. 12 "penitus recognita" by F. Cimetier and A. Cancé (`breviorsynopsist0000unse`, 228k words, "Copyright 1924 by DESCLEE"). The revisers' matter is post-1931 and its status is unverified. Prefer the 8th ed. (1924, renewed but now PD by date) if Gallica or another library has a scan; none was found on IA.

### Minor and posthumous works
*Abrégé de théologie ascétique et mystique* (1927, a school abridgement of the Précis with J. Gautier) and *La vie de Jésus dans l'Église* (1933, ed. Cimetier): no online text was found on IA. A title *La divinisation de la souffrance* sometimes attributed to him could not be confirmed in any catalogue; treat it as doubtful.

## Recommendation

- **Layout:** `tanquerey-opera-omnia/<work>`, with `precis-theologie-ascetique` (fr-FR canonical + en-US Branderis), `dogmes-generateurs-piete` (fr-FR only; pt-BR via the pipeline), `synopsis-dogmatica/{fundamentalis,specialis-1,specialis-2}` (la) and `synopsis-moralis/{t1,t2,t3}` (la), plus `brevior-dogmatica` (la).
- **Import first:** the **Précis / The Spiritual Life** pair. Both are tier A with complete open OCR, two independent scans per language for cross-checking, and the same paragraph numbering in both languages. It is the most useful Tanquerey for a prayer app.
- **Second:** *Les dogmes générateurs de la piété* (French only; short, devotional).
- **Then, if the corpus wants manuals:** the Latin *Synopses* (1921/1922 editions). They are large, dry seminary texts with mediocre ligature OCR, and they need a cleanup pass.
- **Not cleanly sourceable:** every English version except *The Spiritual Life* (*Doctrine and Devotion* and the Byrnes manuals are borrow-only); the *Abrégé* and *La vie de Jésus dans l'Église* (no online text); the Brevior moralis in a pre-1931 edition.

**Total size** (OCR counts, which include indices and front matter): originals ≈ 366k (Précis) + 86k (Dogmes) + 830k (Synopsis dogm.) + 773k (Synopsis mor.) + 223k (Brevior dogm.) + 228k (Brevior mor.) ≈ **2.5M words**. Usable English ≈ **377k** (*The Spiritual Life*). The recommended first import is ≈ 366k fr + 377k en.

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/tanquerey/` (djvu texts, EWTN pages).
