# Pe. António Vieira SJ (1608–1697): sourcing report

Here the original language is Portuguese, so the brief's "Portuguese" section is the original-language section. Vieira also wrote in Latin (*Clavis Prophetarum*, poems), and a few Roman pieces first in Italian. Not in the corpus: `_corpus-books.txt` and `content/books/` have no Vieira.

**Bibliography.** Vieira prepared his sermons for print himself. The *editio princeps* is *Sermoens do P. Antonio Vieira*, 15 *partes*, Lisbon 1679–1748:

| Parte | Year | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| I–VII | 1679, 1682, 1683, 1685, 1689, 1690, 1692 | sermons |
| VIII | 1694 | *Xavier dormindo e Xavier acordado* |
| IX–X | 1686, 1688 | *Maria Rosa Mística* I–II, the 30 Rosary sermons |
| XI | 1696 | sermons |
| XII | 1699 | sermons |
| XIII | 1690 | sermons, incl. *Palavra de Deus empenhada e desempenhada* |
| XIV | 1710 | *Sermoens e varios discursos* |
| XV | 1748 | *Sermões varios e tratados ainda não impressos* |

The 1690 date of Parte XIII follows the Brasiliana's record (item 4581) and is not confirmed against the volume.

Other early editions:
- Letters: *Cartas*, 3 vols, 1735–1746.
- Supplements by André de Barros SJ: *Vozes saudosas* (1736) and *Voz sagrada, politica, rhetorica e metrica* (1748), with unpublished sermons, papers and poems.
- *História do Futuro*, *Livro anteprimeiro* only, Lisbon 1718; reprinted 1755.

Collected editions:
1. **Seabra & Antunes, *Obras completas do Padre Antonio Vieira*, Lisbon 1854–58, 26 vols:**
   - *Sermões* 15 vols, arranged by the liturgical year rather than by *parte*
   - *Cartas* 3–4 vols
   - *Obras inéditas* 3 vols, 1856–57
   - *Obras várias* 2 vols, 1856–57
   - *Arte de furtar* (spurious)
   - *História do Futuro*
2. **Lello & Irmão / Chardron, *Obras completas do Padre António Vieira: Sermões*, Porto 1907–09, 15 vols.** Edited with prefaces by Pe. Gonçalo Alves. The prospectus calls it "cuidadosamente revista e impressa sobre a 1.ª edição" (carefully revised and printed from the 1st edition). It follows Seabra's arrangement. The sermon volumes were reprinted by Lello and by Editora das Américas/Anchieta (São Paulo) in the 1940s–50s.
3. **J. Lúcio de Azevedo, *Cartas do Padre António Vieira*, Coimbra, Imprensa da Universidade, 3 vols, 1925–28.** About 718 letters (93 + ~321 + ~304). Azevedo died in 1933, so the edition is PD in Portugal since 2004 and in the US as a pre-1931 publication: **tier A**.
4. **Modern editions, all C:**
   - Hernâni Cidade, *Defesa perante o Tribunal do Santo Ofício*, 2 vols, Bahia 1957. Cidade died in 1975.
   - Sérgio & Cidade, *Obras escolhidas*, 12 vols, Sá da Costa 1951–54.
   - J. van den Besselaar, *História do Futuro*, 1976; M. L. Carvalhão Buescu, 1982.
   - A. Muhana, *Apologia das coisas profetizadas*, 1994.
   - A. do Espírito Santo, *Clavis Prophetarum*, BN Lisboa 2000; S. Peloso, 2009.
   - J. A. Hansen, *Cartas*, 2003.
   - Alcir Pécora, *Sermões*, Hedra, 2000–01.
   - **Franco & Calafate (dirs.), *Obra completa Padre António Vieira*, Círculo de Leitores 2013–14 / Loyola, 30 vols.** It has every newly found letter and paper.

**Orthography, which the corpus has to decide on.** The sources fall into three layers:
- **Princeps, 1679–1748:** original 17th-c. spelling. Long s, *&*, *hum/huma*, *Christo*, *-aõ*.
- **19th-c. normalized:** Seabra 1854–58 and Lello 1907–09. *thesouro, fallar, prégador, ha-de, Christo, sabbado*; ã/õ kept; spelling regularized but not modernized.
- **Modernized:**
  - Azevedo 1925–28 uses the 1911 reform spelling with pre-1945 accents (*êle, cabêça*).
  - BibVirt/Domínio Público texts use Brazilian modern spelling, from Edameris 1965.
  - Obra Completa 2013–14 (C) uses post-1990 spelling.

**Political vs religious.** Each work below is marked [R] religious/spiritual, [P] political/diplomatic/missionary-administrative, or [P/R] prophetic-theological (political-messianic). The marks are information only: everything is mapped for import.

## Notes per work, sources ranked

### Sermões
**Structure.** About 207 pieces. Most are single sermons; they are preceded by a Latin theme (an *Evangelho* verse) and divided into numbered §§. The collection also holds:
- three *exortações*, one *prática espiritual*, one *comento/homilia*;
- the *Xavier dormindo* cycle: 3 *sonhos* + conclusion;
- the *Xavier acordado* cycle: preface + 12 sermons;
- *As cinco pedras da funda de David*, 5 *discursos*;
- the *Discurso apologético* and *Lágrimas de Heráclito*;
- two letters of Fr. General Oliva.

The princeps marginal Scripture references become footnotes in Seabra and Lello. One file per sermon.

### Cartas (Azevedo, 1925–28)
**Structure:** 718 numbered letters (est.), chronological, with Azevedo's notes and an alphabetical index per tomo:
- **t. I:** letters I–XCIII, 1626–1661. Opens with the 1626 *Carta ânua* of the Province of Brazil, then the diplomatic missions (Paris, The Hague: Marquês de Niza ~35, D. João IV) and the Maranhão mission (D. Afonso VI, Pe. André Fernandes, Provincial).
- **t. II:** I–CCCXXI, 1662–1673. The Coimbra Inquisition years and Rome (D. Rodrigo de Meneses ~76, Marquês de Gouveia ~58, D. Teodósio de Melo ~28, Duarte Ribeiro de Macedo ~30, Duque de Cadaval ~20).
- **t. III:** I–CCCIV, 1674–1697. Rome and Bahia (Duarte Ribeiro de Macedo ~140, Diogo Marchão Temudo, Cadaval, Sebastião de Matos e Sousa, Cristóvão de Almada, Roque da Costa Barreto…).

Recipient tallies come from the extracted tables of contents (`antonio-vieira/azevedo_letters.tsv`, 578 of ~718 lines caught by the parser). One file per letter, or one per year, grouped by tomo.

Content is mostly political and diplomatic, with the missions, the New Christians and the Inquisition, but a substantial religious part: the 1626 *Ânua* and the mission reports from Maranhão and Ibiapaba. The **Esperanças de Portugal** letter (1659, to Bishop André Fernandes) is not in Azevedo; it is in *Obras inéditas* t. I.

### Clavis Prophetarum (*De regno Christi in terris consummato*) [R, Latin]
Unfinished, 3 books, in Latin. The autograph was identified in 2022 at the Pontifical Gregorian University (APUG ms 1165/1); a copy is at the Casanatense (ms 706), and copies are in the BNP. Editions: Arnaldo do Espírito Santo (BN Lisboa 2000, Latin + Portuguese) and Peloso 2009 — C. ## Recommendation
Layout: `vieira-opera-omnia/` with the canonical text in Portuguese. Use `pt-PT/` if the corpus keeps 19th-c. spelling as the canonical original, and `la/` for the Latin. Then:
- `…/sermoes/<lello-vol>-<nn>-<slug>`: one book per Lello volume, or one book *Sermões* with ~207 chapters; *Maria Rosa Mística* and *Xavier dormindo/acordado* as their own books.
- `…/cartas/` (3 books by tomo)
- `…/historia-do-futuro`, `…/esperancas-de-portugal`, `…/defesa` (Inéditas I)
- `…/papeis-politicos` (Inéditas II–III, Várias I–II)
- `…/obras-varias-espirituais`
- `…/poesias`
- doubtful items flagged

**Import first:** *Maria Rosa Mística* (30 Rosary sermons, Lello X–XIII). It is the most devotional block, complete in Lello, and has princeps scans (IA + Brasiliana) to collate. The Lent and Holy Week sermons (Lello II–V) follow, then the rest of the sermons. Cartas and political papers come after.

Every sermon volume needs a fresh Portuguese OCR of the Lello scans, checked against Seabra (Brasiliana/Commons) and the princeps for doubtful readings.

**Cannot be sourced cleanly:**
- *Clavis Prophetarum* (manuscript; C editions)
- *História do Futuro* books I–II, *Apologia*, the full *Defesa/Representações* (C editions; manuscripts not located online)
- post-Azevedo letters (Obra Completa, C; Leite 1940 B? unverified)
- any public-domain English translation of any work

**Total size, estimated** (OCR counts include some editorial matter):

| Block | Words |
|---|---|

| Cartas (Azevedo) | ≈ 0.52 M |

Archive.org word counts are `_djvu.txt` whitespace tokens. The OCR score is the share of lowercase tokens occurring at least 3 times in the file, a rough language-neutral proxy.
