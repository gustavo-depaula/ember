# St. John of Ávila (Juan de Ávila, 1499?–1569), Doctor of the Church: sourcing

**Corpus check:** nothing by him is in the corpus. `_corpus-books.txt` has no Ávila entry, and `content/books/` holds no `avila*`/`juan*`/`john-of-avila*` directory (checked 2026-11-01).

**Bibliographic basis.** The corpus list below follows two editions. The first is the critical edition of Luis Sala Balust, *Obras completas del B. Mtro. Juan de Ávila* (BAC, 1952–53, 2 vols). Its tables of contents and its "Bibliografía avilista" (vol. I, pp. 3–30) were read directly from the archive.org OCR. The second is the BAC *nueva edición crítica* by Sala Balust and Francisco Martín Hernández (2000–2003, 4 vols): **I** *Audi, filia*, Pláticas, Tratados; **II** Comentarios bíblicos, Tratados de reforma, Tratados menores, Escritos menores; **III** Sermones; **IV** Epistolario. The 1970–71 BAC edition is the earlier six-volume version of the same work. I could not open the 2000–2003 tables of contents, so the per-volume contents come from secondary citations (es.wikipedia; Comillas and UNAV articles). Work-level sizes for anything not in the 1952–53 volumes are **estimates**.

**When things were first printed** (Sala Balust 1952, I, pp. 3–16):
- Written c. 1530s–1560s.
- *Audi, filia* 1556 (pirated print, put on the Index in 1559); the revised version 1574 (posthumous).
- *Epistolario espiritual* 1578.
- *Dos pláticas a sacerdotes* 1595.
- *Tercera parte de las Obras* 1596 (the Eucharistic and Holy Spirit treatise-sermons, *Tratado del amor de Dios*).
- *Doctrina cristiana* (Messina, 1556).
- First printed only in the 20th century:
  - *Advertencias al Concilio de Toledo* 1941 (ed. R. Sánchez de Lamadrid SJ, *Archivo Teológico Granadino* 4).
  - *Dos Memoriales … para el Concilio de Trento* 1945 (ed. C. M. Abad SJ, *Miscelánea Comillas* 3).
  - *Últimos inéditos extensos* 1950 (Abad, *Misc. Comillas* 13).
  - *Colección de sermones inéditos* 1947 (R. García Villoslada SJ, *Misc. Comillas* 7).
  - The rest appeared in the BAC editions (1952 onward).

  **So no public-domain edition exists for the reform memorials, most of the biblical lectures, the *Tratado sobre el sacerdocio*, or many sermons and letters.**

**Doubtful or spurious attributions.**
- The sonnet *No me mueve, mi Dios, para quererte* is sometimes attributed to him on internal parallels only. First printed 1628. Leave it out.
- The *Libro de la Virgen María* and *Libro del Espíritu Santo* in the old *Obras* are editors' groupings of his sermons, not separate works. Import them as sermons.
- Sala Balust and Martín Hernández class several small pieces, fragments and old-edition letters as dubious. The 2000–2003 apparatus lists them, but I could not read it.
- "Meditación del beneficio de la cruz" (named in the request) does not appear in the 1952 bibliography or in the tables of contents I read. **Unverified.** It may be an anthology title for passages from *Audi, filia* chs. 69–85 (the Passion meditations).

## Notes per work

### *Audi, filia* (1574): the second redaction, the canonical text
- **Structure:** 113 chapters, plus the author's prologue and a short *Breve suma*. This is the natural chapter split. Psalm verse divisions: *Audi* chs. 1–?; *Vide*; *Inclina aurem*; *Obliviscere populum tuum*; *Concupiscet rex decorem tuum* (to ch. 113).
- **Spanish (Apostolado Mariano, archive.org folkscanomy upload).** Clean Tesseract OCR of a modern-spelling reprint; chapter headings are clean ("CAPÍTULO 113"). It is the 2007 Sevilla reprint of the 1941 Apostolado de la Prensa edition and still sold ("Con licencia eclesiástica", ISBN 978-84-7770-675-5), so use it **only for cross-checking**.
- **Spanish, public domain:**
  - The 1574 first edition and the 1604 Sevilla edition are archive.org scans with long-s and abbreviations; the OCR is poor (`efte`, `q̃`).
  - Fernández Montaña 1901 vol. I is the best PD modern-spelling edition. HathiTrust lists it as full view (`pdus`) for all four volumes, but babel.hathitrust.org sits behind a Cloudflare challenge, so I could not script it. Expect page-by-page plaintext in a browser, or a Google Books copy of the same Wisconsin scans (unverified).
  - The 1927 Apostolado de la Prensa one-volume *Obras* (2199 pp.) is also full view on HathiTrust. Because it was published before 1930, it is tier A.
- **English (1620 St Omer):** a complete translation of the 1574 text, all 113 chapters with the chapter table at the end. The translator's marginal glosses (a), (b)… are inlined into the TCP text. EarlyPrint gives normalised spelling ("spirituall" → "spiritual"); the TCP XML gives original spelling. The text is clean and keyed, not OCR. EEBO-TCP Phase I texts are public domain/CC0, and the printed book is from 1620: **tier A**.
  - Fallback: the raw microfilm OCR on archive.org (`bim_…1620`), which is very poor.
  - Matthew's English is free and seventeenth-century in idiom. For en-US it is usable as is, in the way the corpus already uses Douay-era English.
- **Tier C:** Joan Frances Gormley, *Audi, filia – Listen, O Daughter* (Paulist Classics of Western Spirituality, 2006), [archive.org lending copy](https://archive.org/details/audifilialisteno0000john). Unusable.

### *Audi, filia* (1556): the first redaction
- Printed without his consent in Alcalá by Juan de Brocar for the bookseller Luis Gutiérrez, and banned in 1559. The only copies are in Lisbon and Évora.
- **The one digital text is Sala Balust's 1963 Juan Flors edition.**
  - Contents: introduction ≈35k words, then the text (≈94k words with footnotes), then indices.
  - Hosted openly on archive.org by Princeton Theological Seminary (Majority World Collection), with no rights statement.
  - Ávila's text itself is long public domain. In Spain, art. 129 of the copyright law (LPI) protects an editor of public-domain or unpublished works for only 25 years (1963 → 1988). Sala Balust's introduction and notes stay protected (he died in 1969, so until 2039).
  - **Tier: unverified (B at best for the bare text, with the editorial matter stripped). Decide before importing.**
- The USAL thesis ("Estudio y edición crítica de *Audi, filia* (1556)") would be the second witness. Its repository licence is unverified because gredos.usal.es returned 403.

### *Epistolario*
- **Structure:**
  - 1578 *Epistolario espiritual para todos los estados*, 2 parts. BAE 13 groups it into "tratados" by recipient: prelates, priests and preachers; lords; women; …
  - BAC 1952 renumbers the letters 1–252, chronologically where possible: Primera parte (1578 letters) plus later finds.
  - New BAC 2003: further letters (count unverified, c. 260+).
  - Letters are the chapter unit. Keep Sala Balust's numbers as the stable ids, because modern scholarship cites them, and map the 1578 numbering onto them.
- **BAE 13 (1850).**
  - Ochoa's reprint of the 1578 collection, modernised to 1850 spelling.
  - Wikisource has the djvu (670 pp.) with a text layer but no proofread pages ("A transcribir"). I pulled the text layer for pp. 313–480 through the edit-box preload (`_scripts/ws_page.py`) and measured ≈219k words. That count includes running heads.
  - OCR quality: moderate. Two columns, Latin tags, some garbled words ("nolijlere", "cpsé").
  - Best PD base for the 1578 core; it could also be proofread on Wikisource itself.
  - Note: archive.org `epistolarioespa00cabagoog` and `gb2AeYdKhvdqsC` are **tomo II** (BAE 62), which has no Ávila letters.
- **Clásicos Castellanos 11 (1912):** a selection of ~25 letters in 1578 spelling, with V. García de Diego's notes. Toronto scan, good OCR. Published 1912, so tier A, though García de Diego died in 1978 and his notes could be stripped to be safe. Useful as a second PD witness.
- **BAC 1952 vol. I.** Good OCR, critical text, all 252 letters plus the letters first published by Jiménez de la Llave (*Cartas inéditas*, BRAH 1894; Cervantes Virtual has an HTML of that article, Ref=020114, behind Cloudflare). Apparatus: footnotes. Rights: same reasoning as the 1963 *Audi, filia*, so **unverified**. Letters found after 1578 (≈80) exist in no PD edition except the 1894 article and Fernández Montaña 1901 (partial). For those, BAC 1952 is the only practical text.
- **English:**
  - **1631 Rouen, *Certain Selected Spiritual Epistles*:** 57 letters, TCP keyed, clean.
  - **1904 Stanbrook:** 25 letters (incl. the letter to St Teresa, the one to Guzmán & Loarte, and others). Plain modern English, good OCR, three scans on archive.org (Toronto marked NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT).
  - **1632 *The Cure of Discomfort*:** more letters, "particularly for persons in distress". Microfilm OCR only, very poor, and not in TCP.
  - Together the three cover perhaps 70–90 distinct letters, with overlaps (not deduplicated). Map them to the Sala Balust numbers when importing.
  - Excerpts only: *Spanish Mystics* (Kegan Paul, 1886) [`spanishmysticsse00tolluoft`](https://archive.org/details/spanishmysticsse00tolluoft).

### *Sermones* and *Pláticas*
- **BAC 1953 vol. II:**
  - ≈81 sermons in two cycles: temporal (Advent → Pentecost season, then the Santísimo Sacramento sermons ≈ nos. 35–59 and the Espíritu Santo sermons) and santoral (Marian feasts, saints).
  - Then 16 *pláticas* (to priests; to nuns at Montilla, etc.).
  - Each sermon has an editorial summary of headings, which can be dropped.
  - Good OCR.
- **Public-domain witnesses:**
  - The 1596/1603 *Tercera parte* (Sacramento and Espíritu Santo). The 1603 Sevilla print is on archive.org with poor OCR.
  - The 1793 Imprenta Real *Obras* t. III (Google scan, good OCR, 18th-century spelling).
  - The 1865 Barcelona Marian *Libro espiritual* (not found online).
  - Fernández Montaña 1901.
- Sermons first published by García Villoslada (1947) or in BAC have no PD edition.
- **English:** only *The Holy Ghost* (Scepter, 1959; lending-only scan). Tier unverified.

### Treatises, biblical lectures, reform writings
- *Tratado del amor de Dios* is short (≈8k), and good public-domain witnesses exist (1793, 1603).
- *Tratado sobre el sacerdocio*, *Lecciones sobre Gálatas*, the lectures on *1 Juan*, *Memoriales* I–II, *Causas y remedios de las herejías*, *Advertencias al Concilio de Toledo*, *Doctrina cristiana*, and the *Tratados menores* / *Siete nuevos escritos* exist only in 20th-century editions (1941, 1945, 1950, BAC 1970 and 2000–03). None of them is online as open text.
- Strictly, under Spanish LPI art. 129 the 25-year right of whoever first published a public-domain unpublished work has expired for 1941–1971. Ávila's words are therefore free, but they can only be taken from those editions, minus the apparatus.
- The 2000–2003 volumes are in print and sold by BAC: **C**.

## Recommendation

**Layout:** `content/books/john-of-avila-opera-omnia/<work>/`, with `es/` canonical and `en-US/` where a PD translation exists. Suggested work slugs: `audi-filia` (1574), `audi-filia-1556`, `epistolario`, `sermones`, `platicas`, `tratado-del-amor-de-dios`, `escritos-menores`, and later `lecciones-galatas`, `lecciones-1-juan`, `memoriales-trento`, `advertencias-toledo`, `doctrina-cristiana`, `tratado-sacerdocio`.

**Import order:**
1. ***Audi, filia* (1574), es + en-US.**
   - en-US: the TCP A23406 keyed text (clean, complete, tier A).
   - es: Fernández Montaña 1901 vol. I, or the 1927 Apostolado de la Prensa edition, from HathiTrust. Getting the text out needs a browser or a member PDF; if that fails, look for a Google Books copy.
   - Cross-check against the Apostolado Mariano OCR (C, check only) and the 1574 scan.
2. ***Epistolario*, es + en-US.**
   - es: the 1578 core from BAE 13, via the Wikisource text layer (A).
   - Second witness: Clásicos Castellanos 1912 (A).
   - en-US: 1631 TCP (57 letters) and 1904 Stanbrook (25). OCR *The Cure of Discomfort* 1632 again from the images if wanted.
   - The ≈80 letters outside 1578 need BAC 1952 (unverified tier) or Fernández Montaña 1901.
3. ***Tratado del amor de Dios*** (es only, from the 1793 scan): small and quick.
4. ***Sermones* and *Pláticas*:** es only. A PD text exists for perhaps a third (*Tercera parte* 1596/1603/1793, Fernández Montaña 1901); the rest only in BAC 1953. Get a decision on the BAC 1952–53 tier first.
5. **The 20th-century inéditos** (lectures, memoriales, Toledo, sacerdocio, catechism): **cannot be sourced cleanly**. They exist only in copyrighted editions (C) or as print copies of 1941–1950 journals. Skip them for now, or decide on the "bare 16th-century text from a critical edition" question for Spain and the US. That question also governs BAC 1952–53 and Flors 1963.

**Cannot be sourced cleanly at all:** the Paulist 2006 *Audi, filia* (C); the 1556 redaction outside Flors 1963 and the USAL thesis; all English for the sermons, treatises, lectures and reform writings (none PD).

### *Audi, filia* (2nd redaction, 1574)

1. **Fr. Manuel de São José Lisboa, OFM (fl. 1757–1780) (tr.), *Livro espiritual, que trata das más linguagens do mundo, carne, e demonio, e dos remedios contra ellas* — "seu autor na lingua espanhola o R. P. Mestre João de Avila pregador em Andalusia; traductor na lingua portuguesa o P. Fr. Manoel de S. Jozé Lisboa", **manuscript, 1765**.** BNP cod-5278, [1] f., 354 p., [4] f., 30 cm.
   - Digital copy: http://purl.pt/30859 (record https://bndigital.bnportugal.gov.pt/records/item/27193; PDF of 374 images, 36 MB).
   - **Tier A**: the BNP page marks it "PUBLIC DOMAIN … Public Domain Mark", and the translator worked in the 18th century.
   - The title is that of the 1574 Toledo edition, so the **second redaction**, and it looks complete (354 pp.).
   - **Quality: a handwritten fair copy** in a regular, legible 18th-c. hand with marginal summaries (checked pp. 1 and ~200). No text layer, so it would need a vision transcription.
   - European Portuguese of the 1760s (*vos* address, old spelling). The same translator made the manuscript Portuguese *Caminho de perfeição* and Andrade's *Avisos* commentary for Teresa (see `teresa-of-avila.md`).

2. **Cultor de Livros, *Audi, filia: avisos para os que desejam servir a Deus*** (Clássicos de Espiritualidade), ISBN 978-65-86580-23-5, 448 pp. (https://cultordelivros.com.br/produtos/audi-filia/; also in the Paulus store).
