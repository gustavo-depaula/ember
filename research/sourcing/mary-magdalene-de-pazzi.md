# St. Mary Magdalene de' Pazzi, O.Carm. (1566–1607) — sourcing

Her "writings" are her ecstatic utterances, taken down by her fellow nuns of S. Maria degli Angeli in Florence, plus a few letters and teachings. The authoritative canon is the critical edition ***Tutte le opere di Santa Maria Maddalena de' Pazzi dai manoscritti originali***, general ed. Fulvio Nardoni, volumes edited by Carmelite scholars (Claudio M. Catena, Fausto Vallainc and others) (Firenze: Centro Internazionale del Libro, 1960–1966, 7 vols, + appendix). Its contents:

1. **I quaranta giorni** (27 May–15 Aug 1584), the first book of the manuscripts.
2–3. **I colloqui**, part I (Dec 1584–Apr 1585) and part II (Apr–Jun 1585).
4. **Revelatione e intelligentie** (*Otto giorni dello Spirito Santo*, 8–16 June 1585).
5–6. **La Probatione**, part I (1585–1590) and part II (1590–1600): the five years of trial.
7. **Renovatione della Chiesa e opere minori**: 12 letters dictated in ecstasy (1586, to the pope, cardinals, Catherine de' Ricci and others), 27 personal letters, 40 *Ammaestramenti*, 12 *Avvisi*, *Ricordi*, *Ratti e intelligenze*, *Ultime estasi*.
- Appendices: a *Breve ragguaglio* and works not included in *Tutte le opere*.

Earlier, pre-critical sources: Vincenzo Puccini's *Vita* (1609/1611; enlarged ed. 1639, *Vita e ratti* 1716), which embeds large parts of the revelations; the *Opere* (Venice 1712, an anthology from Puccini); the *Lettere* (1736); *Detti e sentenze* (in the 1669 *Ristretto*); and the Latin *Intelligentiarum divinarum* (1666).

Nothing by her is in the corpus.

## Original language: the O.Carm. edition of *Tutte le opere*

Original sources, best first:
1. **The Carmelite Order's website (O.Carm., prepared by Fr. Pedro Bravo, O.Carm., 2001).** One HTML page per volume of *Tutte le opere*, with the printed edition's page numbers marked ("Numerazione delle pagine secondo l'edizione stampata"): `1giorni.htm`, `2colloq.htm`, `3colloq.htm`, `4revela.htm`, `5probat.htm`, `6probat.htm`, `7renova.htm`, `8appen.htm` under `http://www.ocarm.org/smmdep/`. **The live site is gone** (404, since the site moved to /en/), but every page is complete on the Wayback Machine, e.g. https://web.archive.org/web/20051214id_/http://www.ocarm.org/smmdep/2colloq.htm. I downloaded all eight; the Wayback connection drops mid-file, so I had to retry until each file ended in `</html>`. Counts after stripping HTML: 49k, 112k, 112k, 67k, 72k, 70k, 64k and 38k words, 585k in all. That includes page numbers and scribal notes; the Word metadata embedded in the files gives 491k. The text is clean (typed, not OCR) and in 16th-century Florentine spelling, with the scribes' interventions in italics.
   - **Tier B**, my judgement, not confirmed. The underlying text is 16th century. Italian law protects critical and scientific editions of public-domain works for 20 years from publication (L. 633/1941 art. 85-quater), and that term ran out for the 1960–66 volumes by 1986. The religious order itself put the text online with no rights notice I could find (no "copyright" or "diritti" string in the files). The editors' introductions and notes, if present, would carry ordinary copyright, so import the saint's text only.
2. **it.wikisource, *Renovatione della Chiesa***: https://it.wikisource.org/wiki/Renovatione_della_Chiesa. The infotesto credits the edition "Ed. p. Fausto Vallainc – Claudio Maria Catena, in *Tutte le opere* … vol. IV [sic], Firenze: CIL 1966" and gives the source as the ocarm.org page. It has 107 subpages (12 letters in ecstasy, 27 personal letters, 40 Ammaestramenti, 12 Avvisi, Ricordi, 5 Ratti, 2 Ultime estasi). It is marked complete at SAL 75% and licensed CC BY-SA. This is a cleaned, split copy of (1) for vol. VII, and the best ready-made chapter split.
3. **it.wikisource, *Detti e sentenze memorabili***, from the *Ristretto della serafica vita di S. Maria Maddalena de' Pazzi* (French original by Léon de Saint-Jean, Italian by Giuseppe Fozi SJ, Bologna: Recaldini 1669): https://it.wikisource.org/wiki/Detti_e_sentenze_memorabili. Five subpages (general; for those entering religion; the three vows; superiors; virtues). SAL 75%, complete, 4,942 words by my API count. Tier A.
4. Pre-critical witnesses (scans, long s): Puccini, *Vita e ratti* (Firenze 1716, 2 vols): https://archive.org/details/vitaerattidisant01puccuoft and `…02puccuoft`. *Opere di santa Maria Maddalena de' Pazzi* (1712), a thematic anthology from Puccini: https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_XtBPXztdY5cC (246k OCR words, poor). *Lettere* (1736): https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_w8gtpUtVpHYC. Latin *Intelligentiarum divinarum* (1666): `bub_gb_q1sOhA9FabsC`. All tier A, useful for cross-checks only; the critical text supersedes them.

## English

1. **Placido Fabrini, *The Life of St. Mary Magdalen De-Pazzi, Florentine Noble, Sacred Carmelite Virgin*, tr. Rev. Antony Isoleri, Philadelphia 1900**: https://archive.org/details/lifeofstmarymagd00fabr (OCR 311k words, the whole book). Tier A (US 1900). Part I is the Life. **Part II, "The Works of St. Mary Magdalen De-Pazzi"**, is a thematic anthology of her contemplations and teachings, translated from Puccini's arrangement, not following the manuscript books. Then come the "Maxims and exclamations" (the four sections of the *Detti*), her letters, and the letters dictated in ecstasy. The OCR is good, with some ligature errors ("difl5culties"). This is the only PD English of any extent, and it covers the *Renovatione* letters and the *Detti* directly.
2. *The Life of St. Mary Magdalen of Pazzi* (Puccini's *Vita*, in the Oratorian *Lives of the Saints*, London, mid-19th century): I did not locate it on archive.org, so it is unverified. It would add more excerpts in the same pre-critical form.
3. **Tier C**: Armando Maggi, *Maria Maddalena de' Pazzi: Selected Revelations* (Paulist CWS 2000); Gabriel Pausback, O.Carm., translations of parts of *Tutte le opere* (Carmelite Fathers, 1969–75; I did not check their status).

The five big books (Quaranta giorni, Colloqui, Revelatione, Probatione) have **no PD English translation**. pt-BR would come from the Italian.

## Portuguese

No Portuguese translation of her writings found. Leads, best first:
1. A Portuguese version of a Life (Cepari's, through a Flemish adaptation) by Frei Emídio ter Beke, O.Carm., circulating as a Google Doc (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NkiHiTSfwHoQXYK6kZiXmsAYd4cbqMhS/export?format=pdf). It is a biography, not her works. The translator's note says her "comunicações e instruções" were translated separately, but I could not locate that. Unverified, probably B (given freely by a friar), but not her text.
2. The 17th-century Lisbon printings recorded by BN Portugal (*Vida de la bienaventurada Madre Soror Maria Magdalena de Pazzi*, Fr. Luis de la Presentación, Lisboa 1626; another 1642) are **in Castilian**, not Portuguese: https://permalinkbnd.bnportugal.gov.pt/en/records/item/68001-vida-de-la-bienaventurada-madre-soror-maria-magdalena-de-pazzi

- Layout: `mary-magdalene-de-pazzi-opera-omnia/` with `quaranta-giorni/`, `colloqui/` (2 parts), `revelatione-e-intelligentie/`, `probatione/` (2 parts), `renovatione-della-chiesa/` (letters in ecstasy, personal letters, ammaestramenti, avvisi…) and `detti-e-sentenze/`. - **Import first: *Renovatione della Chiesa* with the minor works and the *Detti e sentenze***. The Italian is already split on Wikisource, and the PD English for the letters and maxims is in Fabrini/Isoleri 1900.
- Then the five books from the archived O.Carm. HTML. The Italian text is clean, with no English. Tier B depends on the order's free publication and the expired 20-year critical-edition term; a lawyer would call it B, not A. Before importing, save the Wayback files into `research/sourcing/mary-magdalene-de-pazzi/` (already there in `raw/`), because the source exists only in the Wayback Machine.
- Cannot source cleanly: a PD English of the big books.
