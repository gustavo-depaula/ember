# St. Jane Frances Frémyot de Chantal (1572–1641): sourcing report

**Reference bibliography:** *Sainte Jeanne-Françoise Frémyot de Chantal, sa vie et ses œuvres. Édition authentique publiée par les soins des Religieuses du Premier Monastère de la Visitation Sainte-Marie d'Annecy*, Paris, E. Plon, 1874–1879, 8 volumes (title pages read from the scans):

| Tome | Contents | Date |
|---|---|---|
| I | *Mémoires sur la vie et les vertus de Ste J.-F. de Chantal* by Mère **Françoise-Madeleine de Chaugy**: a biography, **not Chantal's own work** | 1874 |
| II | *Œuvres diverses* I: *Petit livret*; *Questions de sainte de Chantal; Réponses de saint François de Sales*; *Papiers intimes*; **Exhortations / Entretiens** (about 50 conferences to the Annecy sisters: humility, obedience, prayer, tepidity, "Si quelqu'un veut venir après moi"…; *Défis* given to the sisters of Annecy) | 1875 |
| III | *Œuvres diverses* II: *Méditations pour les solitudes [retraites] annuelles* (drawn from Francis de Sales, compiled under her direction by Mère de Chaugy); **Déposition** at the canonization process of Francis de Sales; *Opuscules divers*; fragments of *Paroles, instructions et avis de saint François de Sales à la Sainte*; *Sentences pour tous les jours de l'année*; points of religious life explained by the founder; the questions put to her in writing and her answers on prayer | 1876 |
| IV–VIII | **Lettres**, I–V ("Première édition entièrement conforme aux originaux, enrichie d'environ six cents lettres inédites"). Numbered in Roman numerals up to the high MDCCC (≈1,850+ letters), with summaries and notes | 1877–1879 |

**Other works by her, not in the Plon edition as a block:** the *Réponses de notre très honorée et digne Mère Jeanne-Françoise Fremiot sur les Règles, Constitutions et Coutumier* (Annecy, 1632; 2nd ed. 1665). The Visitation's *Coutumier* and *Directoire* are corporate works shared with Francis de Sales; they belong with his Opuscules (Annecy XXV, see `francis-de-sales.md`).

**Modern critical edition:** *Correspondance*, ed. Sr Marie-Patricia Burns, Cerf / CEFAG, 1986–1996, 6 vols: **tier C**, not usable. It is the fuller and better-dated letter corpus (≈2,600 letters).

**Attribution notes:** the *Exhortations* and *Entretiens* are reportationes by her daughters, like Francis's *Entretiens*. The *Méditations pour les solitudes* are substantially Francis's text, selected by her. Vol. I (Chaugy) is about her, not by her.

## Original-language sources, ranked

**Common:** no clean HTML of Chantal's works exists on the sites I checked. JesusMarie.com has no Chantal page. Fr.wikisource refused API calls from this machine at the time of checking, so Wikisource is **unverified**, but it had no Chantal works when its author index was last seen. Everything below is archive.org OCR.

### Lettres
1. **Plon 1877–79, vols IV–VIII**, Bibliothèque Sainte-Geneviève copy: https://archive.org/details/8DSUP48_4 , `…_5`, `…_6`, `…_7`, `…_8`. Raw words 207k, 217k, 231k, 249k, 235k. The OCR is **good** (e.g. "il faut faire rarement les corrections fortes et sévères, et qu'elles profitent incomparablement davantage…"). Typical errors: `profilent` for `profitent`, `écoule` for `écoute`, `poul- ies` for `pour les`, plus hyphenation. **Spelling is modernized** by the 1870s editors. Structure: letters numbered in Roman numerals in date order, each with addressee, place and a summary heading; footnotes are historical. There is a long errata list at the end of vol. VIII. Tier A. **Best.**
2. Same edition, Toronto scans: `lettresc04chan` (vols 4–5 bound together, 423k), `lettresc06chan` (6–7, 482k), `lettresc08chan` (8, 234k). An independent OCR pass, useful for cross-checking OCR errors. Tier A.
3. Édouard de Barthélemy, *Lettres inédites de la sainte mère J.-F. Frémyot, baronne de Rabutin-Chantal*, Paris, Lecoffre, 1860: `lettresindites00chan` (181k raw; 2 vols in one item?). Overlaps with Plon; occasionally different readings. Tier A.
4. The Guyot 1851 Francis de Sales *Œuvres complètes* "augmentée des lettres de Sainte Chantal" (`oeuvrescompltes0{1,2,3}frangoog`). Older and partial. Tier A.
5. Burns, *Correspondance* (Cerf 1986–96): **C**. Use it at most as a reference for dates.

### Exhortations, Entretiens, Instructions (Plon II)
1. `8DSUP48_2` (Plon 1875; 192k raw). OCR good. The table at the end of the volume lists the numbered entretiens (e.g. "VIII. Sur l'humilité, l'obéissance et l'oraison … XXIII. Sur la manière de tout voir venir de Dieu…"). Tier A.
2. `oeuvresdiverses02chan` (Plon-Nourrit 1909 reissue, vols II–III; 365k). Second witness. Tier A.
3. `dechantalsavieet0000sain` (Plon 1874, a 2nd scan of vol. I or II: 195k; check which). Tier A.

### Méditations pour les solitudes; Déposition; Opuscules (Plon III)
1. `8DSUP48_3` and `8DSUP48_3_`: two scans of the same 1876 volume (174k each). Running heads: *MÉDITATIONS POUR LA SOLITUDE*, *DÉPOSITION POUR LA CANONISATION DE S. FRANÇOIS*. The meditations are in "points" (Premier/Second/Troisième point). The Déposition follows the interrogatories of the process. Tier A.
2. `oeuvresdiverses02chan` (1909 reissue). Tier A.

### Réponses sur les Règles (1632/1665)
1. *Vive Jesus. Responses de nostre tres-honorée et digne Mere Jeanne Françoise Fremiot sur les Regles, Constitutions & Coustumier…*, 2nd ed. 1665: archive `wotb_6744006`. 17th-c. print; the OCR is **poor** (long s read as f, ligatures), so it needs re-OCR or proofreading from the images. Tier A.
2. Later printings (e.g. Paris 1828?) unverified.

## English translations, ranked

### Letters
1. **Sisters of the Visitation, Harrow-on-the-Hill, *Selected Letters of Saint Jane Frances de Chantal***, preface by Cardinal Bourne, London, R. & J. Washbourne, 1918 (imprimatur 1917). Project Gutenberg #50592 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/50592; 68k words with PG boilerplate, DP-proofread, footnotes kept). Also at archive `selectedletterso00chanuoft`. Tier A. **Best, and the cleanest text** of anything in this report.
2. **Sisters of the Visitation, Harrow, *The Spirit of Saint Jane Frances de Chantal as shown by her letters***, Longmans, Green, 1922 ("All rights reserved", but 1922 → A): archive `spiritofsaintjan0000vari` (188k raw incl. index; Kahle/Austin 2022 scan, full text downloadable, OCR good). A second, larger, non-overlapping selection. Tier A.
3. *Francis de Sales, Jane de Chantal: Letters of Spiritual Direction*, Paulist CWS 1988 (Wright & Power): **C**.
- Coverage: the two PD selections hold perhaps 400–500 letters out of ≈1,850. Map them to Plon numbers on import; translate the rest.

### Exhortations / Conferences
- The PD-era English, *Exhortations, Conferences, and Instructions of St Jane Frances Frémyot de Chantal*, a revised translation published by Loyola University Press, Chicago, in 1928 (as I recall), **was not found** on archive.org or by web search. Its existence and date are unverified, and a 1928 US book would also need a renewal check. Otherwise none. **Translate from French.**

### Méditations pour les solitudes
1. *Meditations for Retreats, from the writings of St Francis de Sales*, compiled by St Jane de Chantal, translated by a Visitandine of Baltimore, Benziger, 1900: archive `MeditationsForRetreats` (Google scan; 33k words incl. the Google boilerplate). Tier A.

### Déposition
1. *The Depositions of St Jane Frances de Chantal in the Cause of the Canonisation of St Francis de Sales*, Library of St Francis de Sales vol. VI (with the *Canticle*), Burns & Oates 1908, issued by the Visitation, Harrow: archive `themysticalexpla00franuoft`, `libraryofstfranc06fran` (≈53k words for the Depositions part). Tier A.

## Recommendation

**Layout:** `content/books/chantal-opera-omnia/<work>/`, with one book per work:
- `lettres/`: grouped by Plon volume or by year; ≈1,850 letters is too many for one chapter each.
- `exhortations/`: one chapter per entretien.
- `meditations-solitudes/`, `deposition/`, `opuscules/`, `reponses/`.
- The Déposition could also sit beside `sales-opera-omnia`, since its subject is Francis; keep it under Chantal and cross-link it.

**Import first:**
1. **Letters**: fr-FR from Plon IV–VIII (Ste-Geneviève OCR, checked against the Toronto OCR), en-US from Gutenberg *Selected Letters* (1918) plus *The Spirit… as shown by her letters* (1922) where they overlap.
2. **Déposition**: fr-FR Plon III + en-US Library vol. VI (1908). A small, complete bilingual pair.
3. **Méditations pour les solitudes** + *Meditations for Retreats* (1900).
4. **Exhortations** (French only).
5. **Réponses** (after re-OCR of the 1665 print).

**Cannot be sourced cleanly:**
- No HTML transcription exists anywhere: every French text is OCR from 1870s scans, in modernized spelling.
- The Burns critical *Correspondance* (more letters, better dates) is tier C.
- The *Réponses* exists only as a 1665 print with poor OCR.
- There is no PD English for the Exhortations, the Opuscules or the Réponses.

**Total size (est.):** French by Chantal ≈ **1.4M words**: letters ~1.0M, exhortations ~170k, meditations ~50k, déposition ~50k, opuscules ~60k, réponses ~100k after stripping the table. Chaugy's *Mémoires* (~190k) are excluded. PD English ≈ **0.32M words**: Selected Letters 60k, Spirit 175k, Depositions 53k, Meditations 30k.

Downloads used for counting: `research/sourcing/jane-de-chantal/` (archive djvu texts, Gutenberg #50592, the 1922 *Spirit*, the 1665 *Réponses*).

## Portuguese

Almost nothing of Chantal's exists in Portuguese, and nothing is online. The only Portuguese printing I found is an 18th-century Lisbon translation of the *Méditations pour les solitudes*. It is known from bibliography only.

Where I searched: archive.org (advancedsearch by title, creator and subject with `language:por`, plus a full-text search for "Joanna Francisca" Chantal); pt.wikisource (no author page); the BNP digital library (bndigital.bnportugal.gov.pt / purl.pt: only engravings of Chantal, no texts); Inocêncio's *Diccionario bibliographico portuguez* (tomos I–XXIII, to 1923, grepped in `francis-de-sales/pt/inocencio/`); and the Alexandria Católica blog. Domínio Público returned 403, so it is unverified. One web search for Portuguese editions of her letters found none. The web-search budget ran out before I could check the Brazilian publishers' catalogues (Paulus, Loyola, Vozes, Cultor de Livros, Ecclesiae) one by one, so a modern tier-C edition of her letters is possible but **unverified**.
