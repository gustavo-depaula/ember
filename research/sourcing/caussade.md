## Authorship and bibliography

- **Instructions spirituelles** (Perpignan 1741) is the only book printed in Caussade's lifetime. It has 2 parts: Book I, a preliminary dialogue + 17 dialogues (theory); Book II, 12 dialogues (practice). Editions: 1741 Perpignan; a Toulouse reprint; 1758 Perpignan (Book II only, "mutilated"); 1825 Avignon, Seguin (complete); Bussenot, Reims 1892 and 1895 (Book II, then Book I, with a preface by Ludovic de Besse); Bremond, *Bossuet maître d'oraison*, Bloud & Gay 1931 (follows 1741). Source for this list: Thorold's Translator's Note and de Besse's preface in `onprayer_201911`. BnF (data.bnf.fr) and Wikipedia record a competing attribution to the Jesuit Paul-Gabriel Antoine, under whose name the book appeared; the scholarly consensus still treats it as Caussade's.
- **L'Abandon à la Providence divine**: published 1861 by Henri Ramière, who rearranged it into 2 books (virtue / state of abandonment) and added letters. Michel Olphe-Galliard's critical edition (1966, DDB/Christus, tier C) restored the manuscript order. **Attribution to Caussade is now rejected.** Dominique Salin, *The Way* 46/2 (2007), 21–36 (https://theway.org.uk/Salin.pdf, not reachable from here, quoted on en.wikipedia), says "it now seems almost impossible that the author was in fact the Jesuit Jean-Pierre de Caussade". J. Gagey's 2001 edition credits an anonymous "lady from Lorraine" and accepts only 32 letters as genuine. fr.wikipedia: "L'Abandon … n'est pas directement de sa plume … Ramière fut trompé par des visitandines." Catalogue it under Caussade's name as the tradition does, but flag it as *attributed*.
- **Lettres spirituelles**: Ramière's letters to the Visitandines of Nancy (1730s–1740s) fill most of his edition. The critical edition is Olphe-Galliard, *Lettres spirituelles*, 2 vols, DDB 1962–64 (tier C).
- *Traité sur l'oraison du cœur*: a manuscript first printed by Olphe-Galliard (1981; with the *Instructions*, 1992). Tier C only.

## Sources per work, ranked

### 1. L'Abandon à la Providence divine (treatise)

French:
1. **archive.org `labandonlaprovid0001caus`** (https://archive.org/details/labandonlaprovid0001caus, full text `_djvu.txt`). Ramière, 7th ed., Paris: Librairie catholique de Périsse, 1874, t. I. Tier A. OCR is good: modern type, few errors (sampled: "Il n'y a rien de plus assuré que cet état…" clean). Running heads and page numbers are mixed into the text and must be stripped. Best clean scan of the edition both English translations follow.
2. **archive.org `bwb_KW-744-045`**. Ramière, 11th ed., Paris: Lecoffre 1901, 2 tomes in one scan (~203k words of OCR). Tier A. OCR is worse (old-style ligatures read as "&", e.g. "Evéque pu Puy"). Use it for cross-checking.
3. **archive.org `labandonlaprovid0000caus`**: the same 1874 set, t. II (letters only). See work 2.
4. **livres-mystiques.com** https://livres-mystiques.com/partieTEXTES/Caussade/Abandon.html. HTML, ~40k words, 11 chapters ("Chapitre I – De quelle façon Dieu nous parle…"). Clean HTML text, but in a **different order from Ramière**. Its note calls it "une ancienne version compilée", which looks like the manuscript order of the Olphe-Galliard edition (1966; tier C if so). The edition is unidentified, so the tier is unverified. Useful only for cross-checking wording, not as the canonical source.

English:
1. **E. J. Strickland, 1921** (from Ramière's 10th ed.; Exeter: Catholic Records Press / St Louis: Herder). CCEL plain text https://ccel.org/ccel/d/decaussade/abandonment/cache/abandonment.txt; CCEL marks it "Rights: Public Domain", proofed. Also on archive.org as `abandonmenttodiv00causuoft` and `divineprovidence00causuoft` (scans). Tier A (1921). Best choice: it follows the same Ramière arrangement as French source 1 (Book I, chapters and sections; Book II), so the chapter split lines up. Dom Arnold's preface admits "more regard has been paid to the meaning than to the literal exactness".
2. **Ella McMahon, 1887** (Benziger; "translated from the eighth French edition"). Gutenberg #52057 https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/52057, ~37.6k words. Tier A. Treatise only, divided differently (3 books of chapters, an earlier Ramière arrangement). A good second witness, but it does not align chapter for chapter with Strickland.
3. Algar Thorold, *Self-Abandonment to Divine Providence*, Burns Oates 1933. archive.org `selfabandonmentt0000caus_m1n8` is **lending-restricted**, so no usable text. Tier A by the death rule (Thorold d. 1936); US renewal status unverified. Not needed.
4. Kitty Muggeridge, *The Sacrament of the Present Moment* (1981), and other modern translations: tier C.

### 2. Lettres spirituelles (Ramière's letter collection)

French:
1. **1874 Périsse ed.**: t. I `labandonlaprovid0001caus` from "SECONDE PARTIE – LETTRES" (line ~7296 of the djvu text; letter books I–III, ~49k words) and t. II `labandonlaprovid0000caus` (books IV–VII, ~95k words, plus publisher's adverts at the front to strip). Tier A, good OCR.
2. 1901 Lecoffre 11th ed., `bwb_KW-744-045` (both tomes). Tier A, poorer OCR.

English:
1. **Strickland 1921**, the same CCEL file, "Second Part: Letters on the practice of abandonment", 7 books, 149 letters headed `Letter I.--…` with addressee, place and date (e.g. "To Sister Elizabeth Bourcier de Monthureux … Perpignan, 1732"). ~127k words. Tier A. Matches Ramière's book and letter order.
2. Algar Thorold, *The Spiritual Letters of Father P. J. de Caussade*, Burns Oates 1934, archive.org `bwb_C0-AQU-244`, **lending-restricted**. Translator probably Thorold (unverified). Tier by death rule A; no usable text.

Structure: 7 books by theme (esteem of abandonment; exercise; obstacles; first trials; further trials; continuation; …). Each letter has a number, addressee, summary and date. The chapter split would be one file per letter, or one per book. Gagey's view that only 32 letters are genuine should go in the book's description.

## Recommendation

- **Layout**: `caussade-opera-omnia/` with
  - `abandon/`: the treatise in Ramière's 2-book order (fr-FR 1874 + en-US Strickland 1921). `book.json` should say "attribuée à J.-P. de Caussade".
  - `lettres/`: Ramière's 7 books of letters (fr-FR 1874 t. I–II + en-US Strickland).
  - `instructions-spirituelles/`: Book I–II dialogues. en-US Thorold 1931 is available now. fr-FR needs a scan to be found (Gallica/Google), then an OCR pass.
- **Import first**: `abandon` (the most-read text; both witnesses already downloaded), then `lettres` (the largest block, and both texts are in hand).
- **Order to follow**: Ramière's, because both PD English translations (Strickland, McMahon) and the clean 1874 French follow it. The manuscript order (Olphe-Galliard) is tier C.
- **Cannot be sourced cleanly**: the French *Instructions spirituelles* (no text found yet), and the *Traité sur l'oraison du cœur* (tier C only).

**Total size (est.)**: French ~195k words (treatise 48k + letters 144k) + *Instructions* (est. ~85–95k, not in hand), so ~285k. English ~270k (Strickland 175k + Thorold 95k).

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/caussade/` (djvu texts, the CCEL text, Gutenberg 52057, livres-mystiques HTML).
