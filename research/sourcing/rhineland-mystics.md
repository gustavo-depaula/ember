# Rhineland and Flemish mystics: Bl. Henry Suso, Johannes Tauler, Bl. John Ruusbroec — sourcing report

None of the three is in the corpus. Word counts are archive.org OCR tokens or DBNL PDF text (`wc -w`), including editorial matter.

---

## 1. Bl. Henry Suso (Heinrich Seuse, c. 1295–1366)

Bibliography: K. Bihlmeyer, *Heinrich Seuse: Deutsche Schriften* (Stuttgart: Kohlhammer, 1907). This is still the standard edition and is PD (Bihlmeyer died 1942; published 1907). Its contents: *Exemplar* (*Vita* / *Leben Seuses*, *Büchlein der ewigen Weisheit*, *Büchlein der Wahrheit*, *Briefbüchlein*), *Großes Briefbuch*, *Predigten* (four, partly doubtful), *Minnebüchlein* (doubtful). In Latin: *Horologium Sapientiae*. **Spurious:** *Buch von den neun Felsen* (Rulman Merswin circle); the "Colóquio dos nove rochedos" in Portuguese is that work.

## 2. Johannes Tauler OP (c. 1300–1361)

Bibliography: the ~80 authentic German sermons, ed. F. Vetter, *Die Predigten Taulers* (Deutsche Texte des Mittelalters XI, Berlin: Weidmann 1910). Vetter died in 1924, so it is PD. **Spurious, though long printed under his name:** *Institutiones* / *Medulla animae*, *Meditations on the Life and Passion*, *Buch von geistlicher Armut* (*The Following of Christ*), *Colloquium theologi et mendici*, and the *Meisterbuch* "History and Life" (Merswin circle).

## 3. Bl. John Ruusbroec (Jan van Ruusbroec, 1293–1381)

## Recommendation (all three)
- Layout: `suso-opera-omnia/<work>` (`vita`, `buchlein-der-ewigen-weisheit`, `buchlein-der-wahrheit`, `briefbuchlein`, `grosses-briefbuch`, `predigten`, `horologium-sapientiae`); `tauler-predigten/<sermon-NN>`; `ruusbroec-opera-omnia/<work>`. The original language codes are `gmh` (Middle High German) and `dum` (Middle Dutch). The corpus has no such language dirs yet, so this needs a decision.
- **Import first:** Ruusbroec's *Brulocht*, *Blinkende steen* and *Boecsken* (clean DBNL Middle Dutch plus the 1916 Wynschenk/Underhill English); then Suso's *Büchlein der ewigen Weisheit* and *Vita* (Bihlmeyer OCR + Knox/1866 English + Luís de Sousa Portuguese); then Tauler's sermons with Elliott's English, once a Vetter scan is secured.
- **Cannot be sourced cleanly yet:** Vetter's Tauler (access unverified, so Corin 1924 covers only one manuscript); clean MHG for Suso (OCR needs heavy correction); English for half of Ruusbroec's treatises and for Suso's letters and *Büchlein der Wahrheit*.
- **Total size (originals, estimates from measured volumes):** Suso ≈ 240k words (MHG ≈ 160k + *Horologium* ≈ 80k); Tauler ≈ 300k; Ruusbroec ≈ 500k (David vols 2–6, with ~20 % editorial matter, ≈ 600k tokens). **≈ 1.04M words.** PD English ≈ 990k tokens (Elliott alone 349k).
