# Frei Luís de Sousa OP (Manuel de Sousa Coutinho, c. 1555–1632): sourcing report

Bibliography from Barbosa Machado, *Bibliotheca Lusitana* III (1752), pp. 144–148 (archive `bibliothecalusit03barbuoft`). Knight of Malta, captive in Algiers (where he met Cervantes), married D. Madalena de Vilhena; when her first husband proved alive both entered religion (1613–14), he at the Dominican convent of Benfica. The classic prose stylist of Portuguese; his religious writings are all chronicles and lives built on the papers of Fr. Luís de Cácegas, which he rewrote.

Works (as friar, Portuguese):
1. ***Vida de D. Frei Bertolameu dos Mártires*** (6 books, with the *tresladação* of his body), Viana, Nicolau Carvalho, 1619. Reprints include Lisboa, Typ. Rollandiana, 1818, 1850 and 1853 (2 tomes); Sá da Costa 1946 (2 vols, ed. A. Reis Machado); INCM/Movimento Bartolomeano 1984 (ed. Gladstone Chaves de Melo and Aníbal Pinto de Castro). Cácegas is credited as first author on many title pages.
2. ***História de S. Domingos particular do Reino e Conquistas de Portugal***: Part I, Benfica, Giraldo da Vinha, 1623; Part II, Lisboa, 1662 (Barbosa gives 1626; posthumous, ed. Fr. António da Encarnação, with a *Vida* of the author); Part III, Lisboa, Domingos Carneiro, 1678 (censura by António Vieira). **Part IV (1733) is by Fr. Lucas de Santa Catarina**, not Sousa. Reprints: Lisboa, Galhardo, 1767, 4 vols; Lisboa, Typ. do Panorama, 1866, 6 vols ("3.ª edição": vols 1–2 Part I, 3 Part II, 4 Part III, 5–6 Part IV).
3. ***Anais de el-rei D. João III*** (18 years of the reign; unfinished, his last work), MS, first published by Alexandre Herculano, Lisboa, 1844; ed. M. Rodrigues Lapa, Sá da Costa, 1938 (2 vols).
4. ***Vida do Beato Henrique Suso*** (translated from Surius's Latin), Lisboa, Lourenço de Anvers, 1642; 1672.
5. ***Considerações das lágrimas que a Virgem Nossa Senhora derramou na Sagrada Paixão*** (10 *passos* for 10 Saturdays), Lisboa, 1645; 1711. Items 4–5 and the scattered pieces were collected as *Vida do Beato Henrique Suso… Considerações das lágrimas de N. Senhora, e outras obras em prosa e em verso, que andavam dispersas*, Lisboa, 1764 (archive credits José Caetano de Quadros) and 1836.
6. Latin distichs on the life of St Dominic (17 tile captions, Lisbon convent cloister; printed in Part IV of the *História*, pp. 12–15).
Before religion (as Manuel de Sousa Coutinho): Latin *Carmen heroicum* for Bernardo de Brito (*Monarquia Lusitana* I, 1597); edition of Jaime Falcão's Latin poems (Madrid 1600, his dedication and preface); Latin inscription for Luís de Sotomaior (1602; reprinted in the *Vida* II.17); epigrams (on Camões; on the Cumaean Sibyl, 1588); sonnet for Gallegos's *Gigantomaquia* (1628); *Navigatio antartica* (Latin hexameters, MS, lost).

Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/` checked). Garrett's play *Frei Luís de Sousa* (Gutenberg #17591) is about him, not by him.

## Notes per work, sources ranked

Orthography layers, since the corpus must choose one canonical text:
- **Princeps / 17th-c.** (1619 Viana; 1623, 1662, 1678): Baroque spelling (*Bertolameu, polo, pera, hũa, ſ*), scans with poor OCR (ſ→f).
- **18th-c. reprints** (1764, 1767): same spelling, cleaner type, long ſ.
- **19th-c. Lisbon editions** (Rollandiana 1818/1850/1853, Herculano 1844, Rollandiana/Panorama 1836/1866): old spelling largely kept (*&, polo, pera, cousa, Deos*) in modern type — near-diplomatic and well OCR'd. **Recommended canonical layer.**
- **20th-c.** (Sá da Costa 1938/1946, INCM 1984): modernized with editorial notes — C (unverified), not online.

### Vida de D. Frei Bertolameu dos Mártires
Six books, ≈ 30–40 chapters each (Book I youth and religious life; II Trent; III–IV pastoral government; V renunciation and Viana; VI death, miracles, translation of the body). Includes the archbishop's Portuguese letters (II.2–14, III.29, IV.12) — the principal source for them. One file per chapter.
1. **Rollandiana 1850 (tome I `vidadedfrbertol00luisgoog`, tome II `bub_gb_KWAAAAAAMAAJ`) / 1853 tome II (`vidadedfrbertol01luisgoog`)** — A; Google scans, spelling of the princeps kept (*&, polo, mes|ma*), OCR fair (some line-break hyphen noise). Base text.
2. **Rollandiana 1818** (`vidadedfrbertol00sousgoog`, tome I only, 546 images) — A; earlier setting of the same edition; second witness for tome I.
3. **Princeps Viana 1619** (archive `bub_gb_ejJfgR_PmtQC`, 582 images; BND https://purl.pt/33227, Public Domain Mark) — A; authority for collation; OCR poor (ſ).
4. Sá da Costa 1946 / INCM 1984 — C (unverified), not online.
English: **Lady Herbert, London, T. Baker, new ed. 1890** (first ed. c. 1880, not seen) (`LifeOfDomBartholomewOfTheMartyrs`, Google; `lifeofdombarthol00ladyuoft`, Toronto) — A (Lady Herbert d. 1911). Translated from the French *Vie de Dom Barthélemy des Martyrs* (Port-Royal: Du Fossé/Le Maistre de Sacy, 1663), itself compiled from Sousa, Granada and others: an adaptation in a different order, not a translation of Sousa. Usable as an English companion only; an `en-US/` aligned with the Portuguese chapters would have to be translated.

### História de S. Domingos (Parts I–III)
Chronicle of the Dominican province of Portugal: each part divided into books and chapters (foundations, convents, friars and nuns, saints' lives; Part III carries Vieira's censura). Book counts not tallied here. Mostly lives of friars and nuns, many edifying; the Part I life of St Gonçalo de Amarante and the convent histories are the most read. One file per chapter, by part and book.
1. **1866 Panorama edition, Toronto copies** (`primeiraquartapa01caceuoft` … `06caceuoft`; also Michigan/Google `primeiraquartap0*encagoog`, Robarts `primeiraquartap0*cace`) — A; two-column modern type, old spelling kept, good OCR (proxy 0.83–0.88). Base text. Vols 5–6 are Part IV (Lucas de Santa Catarina): exclude, or import separately as his.
2. **First editions, Getty set** (`primeiraquartapa01cace` = Part I 1623, `02cace` = Part II 1662, `03cace` = Part III 1678, `04cace` = Part IV 1733) — A; authority, long-ſ OCR. BND has the same set: https://purl.pt/43133 (4 vols, 1623–1733).
3. **Lisboa, Galhardo, 1767, 4 vols** (`1767primeiraquar01caceuoft` … `04caceuoft`; also Wellcome `b30412845_0001`…, Europeana `wotb_8901419_*`) — A; second historical witness.
Size: Part I 357k + Part II 240k + Part III 222k OCR tokens (incl. indexes and the 1866 front matter) ≈ 780,000 words.

### Anais de D. João III
Herculano's edition from the rediscovered manuscript, with his *Advertência preliminar*; divided into parts and chapters (≈ 105 *Capítulo* headings in the OCR), covering the first 18 years of the reign. Secular court chronicle — import for completeness only.
1. **Herculano 1844** (`annaesdeelreidom00sous`, Brandeis, OCR 0.87) — A (Herculano d. 1877).
2. Same edition, Oxford/Google (`annaesdeelreido00sousgoog`) — A.
3. BND: MS *Índice dos Anais* (https://purl.pt/48168, 1912–17) — finding aid only. Rodrigues Lapa 1938 (Sá da Costa) — C, not online.

### Vida do B. Henrique Suso; Considerações das lágrimas; obras dispersas
The 1764/1836 volume gathers: the Life of Bl. Henry Suso (translation of Surius's Latin version of the *Exemplar* life — a translation, not Sousa's own composition), the *Considerações das lágrimas de Nossa Senhora* (10 meditations for Saturdays, Sousa's own devotional work), and "other works in prose and verse that were scattered" (letters, Latin poems, the Dominic distichs). One file per piece.
1. **1836** (`vidadobeatohenri00sous`, Robarts) — A; modern type.
2. **1764** (`vidadobeatohenr00sous`, Fisher) — A; long-ſ.
The princeps of each (1642, 1645) not found online.

## Portuguese
All works except the early Latin verse are Portuguese originals (above). No transcribed text on pt.wikisource (no author page), Gutenberg, BND (scans only) or Domínio Público (searched dominiopublico.mec.gov.br — no record; the www host returns 403). The BND PDFs are image-only.

## Recommendation

Layout: `luis-de-sousa-opera-omnia/<work>` with `pt-PT/` canonical (19th-c. Lisbon editions, old spelling kept), `pt-BR/` as a spelling modernization, `en-US/` by pipeline translation.
- **Import first: `…/vida-de-bartolomeu-dos-martires`** — the spiritual masterpiece and the source for St Bartholomew's own letters; Rollandiana 1850 text with the 1619 princeps for collation. Keep Lady Herbert as a reference, not as the `en-US/` text (different structure).
- Then `…/consideracoes-das-lagrimas` and `…/obras-dispersas` (from the 1836 volume; small), `…/vida-do-beato-henrique-suso` (marked as Sousa's translation; Suso's own *Life* may be better imported under Suso).
- Then `…/historia-de-s-domingos` Parts I–III (1866 text; ≈ 780k words, the bulk). Part IV belongs to Lucas de Santa Catarina.
- Last: `…/anais-de-d-joao-iii` (secular chronicle) and the scattered Latin verse.

Cannot be sourced cleanly: any English translation of Sousa's own text (none exists; Herbert is an adaptation); the 20th-c. critical editions (C).

Total size: Portuguese ≈ 1.31 M words (Vida 240k, História I–III 780k, Anais 175k, Suso/Considerações/dispersas 85k, Latin verse ~2k est.); English PD ≈ 175,000 (Herbert's adaptation of the Vida only).

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/luis-de-sousa/ia/` (counts in `counts.tsv`). Bartolomeu dos Mártires's own works: `research/sourcing/bartolomeu-dos-martires.md`.
