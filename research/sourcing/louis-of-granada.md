# Ven. Louis of Granada OP (Luis de Granada, 1504–1588): sourcing report

Bibliography: the critical edition of the **Spanish works** is Justo Cuervo OP, *Obras de Fr. Luis de Granada*, 14 vols, Madrid, Viuda e hija de Gómez Fuentenebro, 1906–1908. Cuervo reprints the first edition of each work (modern spelling, old phonetics kept) and adds inedita (biographies, letters). It **does not include the Latin works** (sermons, rhetoric, *Silva*). The complete modern edition is Álvaro Huerga OP, *Obras completas*, 51 vols, Fundación Universitaria Española / Dominicos de Andalucía, 1994–2008 (Latin with Spanish translation) — tier C. The older collected edition is the BAE (Biblioteca de Autores Españoles vols 6, 8, 11, ed. José Joaquín de Mora, 1848–49), which modernizes and abridges.

Language: Spanish (most works), **Latin** (*Concionum* de tempore and de sanctis, *Ecclesiastica rhetorica*, *Silva locorum*, *Collectanea moralis philosophiae*), **Portuguese** (*Compendio de doctrina christã*, Lisbon 1559). He also translated the *Imitation* (as *Contemptus mundi*, 1536) and Climacus's *Ladder* into Spanish, and Cajetan's *Summula* (*Suma cayetana*, 1557) — translations, not his works.

Nothing by Granada is in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/` checked).

### Guía de pecadores (1567)
Two books: I (motives for virtue, 30 ch.), II (doctrine of virtue, 21 ch. plus treatises). One file per chapter (~50). The 1556–57 "Guía primitiva" is a different, shorter work (Cuervo X) — keep separate.
- Original: (1) **dominicos.org PDF**, transcription of the Madrid 1657 *Doctrina cristiana* collected edition, with page numbers of the source in brackets, glosses in square brackets, near-faithful orthography — B: given away by the Spanish Dominicans, no licence terms seen; 240 pp., 203,300 words counted (includes a methodological introduction and the *Prólogo galeato*). Born-digital, the cleanest. Strip the bracketed glosses/page numbers. (2) **Cuervo I** (from the 1567 first edition) — A; better text, OCR. (3) BAE t.1. (4) 1573 Salamanca print, Google `bub_gb_FVmTo3TITasC`; 1899 Spanish ed. `guiadelospecador00luis`.
- English, ranked: (1) **"Translated from the Spanish by a Dominican Father" (C. H. McKenna OP), 1883** — A (copyright 1883, McKenna; the archive scan is the TAN reprint of that translation; front matter to strip). Complete, readable, the standard. (2) **Philadelphia 1844 (H. McGrath), "translated from the Spanish"** — A, https://archive.org/details/sinnersguideintw00luisuoft (221,600 OCR words; an 18th-c. translation, also London 1760 `sinnersguidefrom00luisiala`; Boston 1886 `sinnersguide00luis_1`; New York 1890 `TheSinnersGuideLuisDeGranada`). Fuller and closer than McKenna, older English; second witness. (3) **Francis Meres, *The Sinners Guyde*, 1598** — A, EEBO-TCP A06447 (CC0 transcription; Oxford Text Archive https://ota.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/repository/xmlui/handle/20.500.12024/A06447). Translated via the Latin/Italian; early-modern spelling. (4) Orby Shipley, *Counsels on Holiness of Life* (Ascetic Library 3), 1869 — A, abridged, https://archive.org/details/AceticLibraryV3CounselsOn.

### Libro de la oración y meditación (1554)
Part I: 14 meditations (two weekly series: mornings/evenings), Part II: on devotion (impediments, aids, temptations, advice), plus the three treatises on prayer, fasting, almsgiving (from 1566). ~25 chapters.
- Original: (1) Cuervo II (1554 text) — A. (2) BAE t.1. (3) 1594 print `bub_gb_67wILbire2IC`.
- English: (1) **Anonymous, *A Treatise on Prayer and Meditation… translated from the Spanish*, Chester 1862** — A; 96,800 OCR words — probably abridged (Cuervo II is ~240k): verify coverage before use. (2) **Richard Hopkins, *Of Prayer and Meditation*, Paris/Rouen 1582** (many reprints 1592–1634, archive `bim_early-english-books-…of-prayer-and-meditation…`) — A; EEBO-TCP A06436 (phase II, now released as CC0); complete translation of Part I with Part II, Elizabethan English. (3) Shipley, *Considerations on Mysteries of the Faith*, 1862 (abridged meditations) — A.

### Memorial de la vida cristiana (1565) and Adiciones (1574)
Memorial: 7 treatises (conversion, confession, communion, Christian life, prayer, love of God…); Adiciones: treatise on the love of God and a Life of Christ in meditations.
- Original: Cuervo III and IV — A; BAE t.2; 1594 prints `bub_gb_Kc1XckiyInAC` (Memorial) and `bub_gb_LyqJEpXgTXUC` / `bub_gb_hMOH5gDx99MC` (Adiciones).
- English: (1) **Dublin 1824, 5th ed. revised by F. J. L'Estrange** — A; 116,300 OCR words, "in four books" — **partial** (the full Memorial is ~240k words). (2) Richard Hopkins, *A Memoriall of a Christian Life*, Rouen 1586 (and 1612) — A; on archive only as EEBO page images (`bim_early-english-books-1475-1640_a-memoriall-of-a-christi_luis-de-granada_1586`); not in the EEBO-TCP list, so no transcription. (3) Jordan Aumann, *Summa of the Christian Life* (3 vols, Herder 1954–58; TAN reprint) — anthology from all works; unverified, treat as C.
- Adiciones: no PD English found.

### Introducción del símbolo de la fe (1583)
Four parts (I creation and the creatures, 38 ch.; II excellences of the faith; III the redemption; IV the mystery of redemption through the light of faith), then the separately written *Compendio* (the so-called Part V, 1585) with the *Breve tratado* on catechizing new converts. His masterpiece and a monument of Spanish prose. One file per chapter (~150).
- Original: (1) Cuervo V–VIII (Part I–IV, from the 1583 Salamanca first edition) and IX (Compendio) — A. (2) **biblioteca-antologica.org PDF, Part I only** (201 pp., 125,700 words counted, born-digital, modernized; source edition not stated, possibly a modern annotated ed.) — B (free online library; underlying edition unverified). (3) BAE t.3 (`obrasdelvpmfrayl113luis`). (4) 1584 Salamanca print `bub_gb_5_QJ3MaTZFcC`.
- English: no complete PD translation found. Shipley 1862 (abridged); Lodge, *The Flowers of Lodowicke of Granado*, 1601 (EEBO-TCP A06430, from the Latin, extracts) — A, fragmentary. This is the main English gap.

### Opúsculos, Manual, Doctrina espiritual, Lives, Letters, Sermón
Cuervo X, XI and XIV collect the minor works. Each is a natural small book; the Lives (Juan de Ávila, Bartolomé de los Mártires) and the ~65 letters in XIV are worth importing as units.
- English: Meres 1598 (*Granados Devotion* = Manual; *Spirituall and Heavenlie Exercises*) and Gibbons 1599 (*A Spiritual Doctrine*), Lodge 1614 (*A Paradise of Prayers*, TCP A06445) — A, all EEBO-TCP early-modern transcriptions; translated from Latin or Italian intermediaries in some cases, so loose.

### Compendio de doctrina cristiana (Portuguese, 1559)
Original Portuguese text not located online (searched archive.org); Cuervo XIII prints his own Spanish translation from the Portuguese (1906, A). If imported, label the Spanish as translation, and look in the Biblioteca Nacional de Portugal (purl.pt) for the 1559 print (not checked).

### Latin works
*Concionum de tempore* (4 tomes), *Conciones de praecipuis sanctorum festis* (2 tomes), *Ecclesiastica rhetorica* (6 books), *Silva locorum communium*, *Collectanea moralis philosophiae* (an anthology of Seneca, Plutarch, Cicero — compilation, exclude), the Latin versions of his Spanish works (*Catechismus in symbolum fidei*, by translators). Only early prints exist online, as Google Books / BNE scans on archive.org with 16th-c. typography (ligatures, long s): OCR unreliable; would need fresh OCR. Huerga's FUE edition is tier C. No English for any of them.

## Portuguese
Granada lived in Lisbon from 1551 to his death, so Portugal holds his first editions; but Portuguese **translations** of his Spanish works were not found online. Searched archive.org (Portuguese titles: *Guia dos peccadores*, *Memorial da vida christã*, *Symbolo da fé*, *Livro da oração e meditação*), the web, and BNP (bndigital.bnportugal.gov.pt) via search; pt.wikisource was rate-limited (unverified).
- **Compendio de doctrina christãa (1559) — written in Portuguese, so this is the original**: Coimbra 1789 edition, digitized by the University of Coimbra: https://dl.uc.pt/handle/10316.2/3119 — A; page scans (OCR not checked). The 1559 Lisbon *princeps* (with the *Treze sermões*) was not located as a scan. Canonical `pt-PT/` text for this work if imported.
- **BNP scans of Lisbon first editions of the Spanish works** (originals, not translations, but extra witnesses): *Segunda parte del libro llamado Guía de pecadores*, Lisbon 1557: https://bndigital.bnportugal.gov.pt/idurl/1/91208; *Manual de diversas oraciones*, Lisbon 1559: https://purl.pt/15055 — A, page images.
- *Vida de D. Fr. Bertolameu dos Martyres*, Portuguese, 1850 ed. (`bub_gb_KWAAAAAAMAAJ`) — A; this is Fr. Luís de Sousa's Life (built on Granada's and Cacegas's material), not Granada's own *Vida*.
- Modern Brazilian translations (e.g. *Guia de pecadores*, *Livro da oração e meditação* from Catholic publishers) — C where they exist; none found freely distributed.

- `granada-opera-omnia/guia-de-pecadores` — **import first**: born-digital Spanish (dominicos.org, checked against Cuervo I) + McKenna 1883 English, cross-checked with the 1844 translation. Clean on both sides.
- `granada-opera-omnia/libro-de-la-oracion` — Cuervo II + Hopkins 1582 (TCP) / Chester 1862.
- `granada-opera-omnia/memorial-de-la-vida-cristiana`, `…/adiciones-al-memorial` — Cuervo III–IV; English partial (1824).
- `granada-opera-omnia/introduccion-del-simbolo` (+ `compendio-del-simbolo`) — Cuervo V–IX; no English: pt-BR from the Spanish directly.
- `granada-opera-omnia/opusculos`, `…/vidas`, `…/cartas`, `…/doctrina-espiritual`, `…/guia-primitiva` — Cuervo X, XI, XIV.
- Granada's translations (Contemptus mundi, Escala espiritual) belong with their authors, not here; optional.
- Latin sermons and rhetoric: last; needs OCR from early prints.

Cannot be sourced cleanly: any English of the *Introducción del símbolo*, the *Adiciones*, the Compendio, the sermons and the rhetoric; clean text of all Latin works (scans of 16th–18th-c. prints only); the Portuguese original of the Compendio.
