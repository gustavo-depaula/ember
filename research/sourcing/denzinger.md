# Denzinger, *Enchiridion symbolorum, definitionum et declarationum de rebus fidei et morum*: sources

A handbook of excerpts: creeds, conciliar definitions, papal and curial doctrinal acts, and condemned propositions, each with a running marginal number. Heinrich Denzinger (1819–1883) compiled it in 1854. Later editors kept it current, and each edition adds documents and renumbers or re-cuts texts. It is not in the corpus. Overlap: the corpus will hold the councils (`councils.md`) and has `papal-magisterium/`, so much of Denzinger's content would exist there as full texts. Denzinger's value is the **selection and the numbering** ("D 1792", "DS 3011"), which the literature cites.

Raw downloads used to measure: `research/sourcing/denzinger/`.

The documents themselves are public domain: magisterial acts and ancient or medieval texts. The copyrightable layer is each edition's selection, headnotes, and apparatus. An edition of 1930 or earlier is tier A as a whole.

## Recommendation

- Denzinger as a book is lower priority than the full councils and papal documents it excerpts. Those can be imported whole (see `councils.md`), and Denzinger would then mostly duplicate them. Its unique content is the condemned-proposition lists (Baius, Jansen, Quesnel, the Lax and Rigorist propositions, Rosmini, Lamentabili, …) and the numbering itself.
- If imported: `denzinger-enchiridion/` with `la/` canonical, from the **1911 11th edition** (tier A, D numbering, ~191k words body), cleaned from the archive.org OCR. Cross-check every text against catho.org/DCO: their old-D numbers line up with Bannwart's for everything up to 1907, which makes the alignment mechanical. Use catho.org's later documents (1908–1958) only if the user accepts that tier (unverified; DS-derived). Otherwise stop at 1907, or append later PD documents from the corpus's own `papal-magisterium/`.
- Chapters: follow Denzinger's own sequence by pontificate/council (the Index chronologicus), about 150 sections. Keep the marginal numbers as paragraph anchors.
- English: none safely usable yet. Deferrari is unverified, so check the renewal first. Without it, generate en/pt-BR from the Latin, reusing existing PD English for the creeds, councils and Trent where they overlap.
- **Cannot be sourced cleanly:** a PD *clean* Latin (only OCR of the 1911 or earlier editions), and a PD English beyond the creeds and councils.
- **Size:** Latin ~191k words (1911 body) or ~240k (catho.org, to 1958). English (Deferrari) estimated ~250k, unverified.
