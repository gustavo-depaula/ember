# St. John Vianney, the Curé d'Ars (1786–1859): sourcing report

What survives "by" Vianney falls into three groups of very different authorship:

1. **Sermons** (*prônes*). His own manuscripts, written mostly in 1818–1827. They were published as *Sermons du vénérable serviteur de Dieu J.-B.-M. Vianney, curé d'Ars* (4 vols, Paris: Victor Lecoffre / Lyon: Ruban, 1883), with the approbation of Cardinal Caverot, Lyon, 20 Aug 1882.
   - About 85 sermons for the Sundays and feasts of the year, Advent to the last Sunday after Pentecost.
   - **Attribution caveat:** the 1883 preface itself says he drew on Scripture, an elementary theology, Ribadeneyra, the Desert Fathers, Rodriguez and **the works of P. Lejeune**. Several sermons are close adaptations of earlier preachers. They are "his" as compositions, not as original thought throughout.
2. **Catechisms, homilies and sayings.** Not written by him: they were taken down by listeners and published by Abbé Alfred Monnin (d. 1886) as ***Esprit du Curé d'Ars*** (Paris: Douniol, 1864).
   - Three parts: the catechisms (*catéchismes*), homilies, and his conversation.
   - Later anthologies (*Pensées*, the *Little Catechism*) are excerpts of this.
3. **Letters.** A few dozen, scattered through the biographies (Monnin, Trochu 1925). No collected edition found online.

Copyright: Vianney died 1859, Monnin 1886. Every text above is in the public domain in the original language.

**In the corpus?** No. He appears only as a biography entry in `cts-lives-of-the-saints` and `catholic-encyclopedia`.

## Notes per work, with ranked sources

### Sermons

Structure: 4 tomes, each with its own table of contents; one sermon per Sunday or feast, titled "IVe Dimanche de l'Avent. — Sur la Satisfaction".
- Tome I: Advent to Good Friday, 19 TOC entries.
- Tome II: 6 TOC entries in the OCR, which looks incomplete; check the scan.
- Tome III: 20 entries. Tome IV: 12 entries.
- The editors added end notes ("Notes", e.g. t. I p. 409).
- Measured OCR words per tome: 129k / 146k / 128k / 88k.

Original:
1. **archive.org/details/sermons-du-venerable-serviteur-de-dieu-jean-baptiste-marie-vianney-4-tomes**: all four tomes in one PDF plus `_djvu.txt` (2.9 MB).
   - The scan comes from the Bibliothèque Saint Libère (liberius.net), stamped "© Bibliothèque Saint Libère 2011. Toute reproduction à but non lucratif est autorisée". That covers only the scan; the 1883 text is PD.
   - OCR is fair to good in the body; tables of contents and running heads are noisy ("IV® DIMANCHE", "ot" for "et").
2. **archive.org/details/sermons-du-venerable-serviteur-de-dieu-jean-baptiste-marie-vianney-cure-d-ars-tome-1-000000934** and `…-tome-3-000000936`: tomes 1 and 3 separately, with **EPUB** files (uploader "oradtrad").
   - Possibly a cleaner, re-OCRed text. Use as the second witness for those tomes.
3. Liberius.net PDFs (the scan source). Not checked: the site search didn't surface the Vianney pages.

English:
1. **Wagner 1901**, *Sermons for the Sundays and Feasts of the Year* (292 + 78 pp.). PD, but:
   - It is only on archive.org as the restricted 1995 Neumann reprint (`sermonsforsunday0000vian`).
   - Per its p. iv, "a few sermons for Sundays and feasts, not found among those of the Curé of Ars, have been added … by other authors". Those must be excluded.
   - Translator not identified. Mediatrix Press and Tumblar House sell modern resets of it.
   - **No open clean text located.**
2. **Una Morrissy, *The Sermons of the Curé of Ars*** (Regnery, Chicago, 1960; Burns & Oates). A 75-piece selection of excerpts. **Tier C** (1960; renewal not verified).
   - Copies circulating freely on archive.org (`cure-of-ars-sermons-of-the-cure-of-ars`, 67k words; `TheSermonsOfStJeanMarieVianney1`–`8`) are this translation. Their opening ("In speaking to you today, my dear brethren, of the dreadful state of the lukewarm soul…") matches Regnery's first title. Do not use them.

### Esprit du Curé d'Ars (catechisms, homilies, conversation)

Structure (Bowden 1865 contents):
- Part I: about 20 catechisms (Salvation, Love of God, Holy Spirit, Word of God, Prayer, Priesthood, Mass, Real Presence, Communion, Sin, Pride, Impurity, Confession, Hope, Suffering, …).
- Part II: homilies on the last things and the capital sins.
- Part III: "The Curé of Ars in his conversation" (faith, hope, charity, zeal, …).
- Plus 17 additional exhortations from Monnin's *Ars*.

Original: **not located as digital text.** It is not on archive.org (only the German *Geist des Pfarrers von Ars*, 1865, `10265184bsb`) and not on Wikisource. Gallica and Google Books could not be queried from here (Cloudflare block, API quota).

There are many French editions (Douniol 1864; Téqui 1916, 28th ed.), so a Gallica scan is very likely. **Next step:** search Gallica by hand for "Esprit du curé d'Ars".

English:
1. **Bowden 1865**: archive.org/details/TheSpiritOfTheCureOfArs (PD mark; Google-scanned, OCR with Google boilerplate, 70k words). Two more scans to cross-check: `spiritcurarsstj00monngoog`, `the_spirit_of_the_cure_of_ars_1865-abbe_monnin`.
2. **EWTN, *Instructions on the Catechism***: the same Bowden translation of Part I (matched the "Catechism on Salvation" opening), clean HTML, 20 chapters. Best clean text for the catechisms.
3. LibriVox audio of *The Spirit of the Curé of Ars* (librivox.org): its text source is the same Bowden.

*The Little Catechism of the Curé of Ars* (TAN 1951/1987) and *The Curé of Ars to His People* (St Meinrad 1951) are restricted lending scans and post-1930: C or unverified.

### Thoughts

*Thoughts of the Curé of Ars* (Boston 1896; editor's preface signed "A. Mennin", i.e. Monnin): 7.7k words, A. A sayings anthology, useful for daily quotes; overlaps the *Esprit*.

## Recommendation

Layout: `content/books/vianney-opera-omnia/` with
- `sermons` (fr-FR from the 1883 4-vol. OCR, one chapter per sermon, in liturgical order), and
- `esprit` (the Monnin compilation: catechisms / homilies / conversation; en from Bowden 1865, with EWTN as the clean Part I text).

Import first: `esprit` in English (Bowden + EWTN), which is ready, PD and well structured. Then the French sermons; their OCR needs a cleanup pass (running heads, ligatures). pt-BR can be translated from the French sermons. For the `esprit` the en is a translation of a French original we don't hold: either find the French on Gallica first, or flag it as an en-source book.

Can't be sourced cleanly:
- An open English text of the complete sermons: Wagner 1901 is PD but only available as a restricted scan. Someone would have to scan a 1901 copy, or translate the sermons ourselves.
- The French *Esprit* (not found).
- The letters.

**Total size:** about **470k words French** (sermons) + about **70k English** (*Esprit*, Bowden), +8k (*Thoughts*). The French *Esprit*, once found, would add about 75k.

Raw downloads are in `research/sourcing/cure-of-ars/raw/`.

### Sermons

1. **Paulus, *Clássicos do Cristianismo* 7/1–7/3: *Sermões*, tr. Tiago José Risi Leme** (vol. 1 also "tradução e apresentação"), São Paulo: Paulus; 1st ed. (hardback "Luxo"), 2nd ed. 2026 (Clássicos de Espiritualidade). Vol. 1 *do Primeiro Domingo do Advento à Sexta-feira Santa* (ISBN 978-85-349-6138-7); vol. 2 *do Domingo de Quasímodo ao XI Domingo de Pentecostes*; vol. 3 *do XII ao XXIII Domingo depois de Pentecostes*. The CIP record names the 1883 Lecoffre/Ruban edition as the original ("Sermons du Vénérable Serviteur de Dieu… Tome Premier"). Sample with CIP: https://deg.paulus.com.br/9788534961387.pdf; series list: https://deg.paulus.com.br/7178.pdf; store pages: https://loja.paulus.com.br/sermoes-1/p, `/sermoes-vol2/p`, `/sermoes-vol3/p`.
   - **Tier C.** The Paulus imprint says "Todos os direitos reservados pela Paulus Editora…".
   - Translated from the French, and the only full Portuguese version found. It seems to follow tomes I–III. Whether it includes tome IV (the 1883 ed. has 4) is unconfirmed: no fourth volume is listed.
   - Use as a terminology reference for our own pt-BR. Not importable.
2. **Gercione Lima (Toronto), *Sermões de São João Maria Vianney, o Cura D'Ars*.** An internet PDF "traduzido do francês", archived as archive.org `sermoes_cura_dars` (OCR ~14k words).
   - A selection of about 5 sermons, retitled: "Eles pertencem ao mundo", "Danças", "Não somos nada por nós mesmos (Sobre as Tentações)", "A Pureza", "Eu venho em nome de Deus (O Purgatório)".
   - **Tier unverified.** It has no licence or rights line; the archive item carries no licence field. It is a private translation circulated freely online since the 2000s, so B at best.
   - Fluent modern Brazilian prose with some free rendering. Small, retitled, and the sermons are not identified by Sunday, so it is of low value even as a reference.
