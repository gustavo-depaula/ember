# Alonso Rodríguez SJ (1538–1616), *Ejercicio de perfección y virtudes cristianas*: sourcing report

**Attribution first.** The *Ejercicio de perfección* is by **Fr. Alonso Rodríguez SJ of Valladolid** (1538–1616; older sources give 1526), novice master and spiritual writer, who is **not canonized**. The saint of the same name is **St. Alphonsus (Alonso) Rodríguez SJ** (1532–1617), the lay brother and porter of Montesión in Majorca, canonized in 1888 (Hopkins's sonnet), whose own writings are a separate, smaller corpus (see the last section). Library catalogues and archive.org metadata mix the two (e.g. "Rodríguez, Alonso, Saint" on Fr. Alonso's books). This report treats the *Ejercicio* as asked, under the slug `alonso-rodriguez`; the saint is noted at the end.

Fr. Alonso's work: *Ejercicio de perfección y virtudes cristianas*, Seville, Matías Clavijo, 1609 (3 parts), one of the most printed books of 17th-c. spirituality and the classic Jesuit novitiate reading. No other substantial work by him is known; the *Tratado de la conformidad con la voluntad de Dios* and the *Tratado de la humildad* that circulate separately are treatises of the *Ejercicio* (Part I tr. 8, Part II tr. 3).

Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/` checked).

## Notes, sources ranked

Structure: 3 Parts × 8 *tratados*, each treatise in numbered chapters (≈ 20–40 per treatise; ≈ 700 chapters in all). Part I: esteem and desire of perfection; purity of intention; union and fraternal charity; prayer; presence of God; examination of conscience; conformity with the will of God… Part II: mortification; modesty and silence; humility; temptations; sadness and joy; the sacraments… Part III (written for Jesuits): the end of the Society, the three vows, rules, obedience, manifestation of conscience. Chapter files per treatise (24 books-within-the-book) or per chapter grouped under treatise folders. Each chapter opens with Latin scriptural/patristic authorities, which the original prints and the modern digital text drops.

## St. Alphonsus Rodríguez (the canonized lay brother)
His writings — the *Memorial* (spiritual autobiography written under obedience), and many short treatises and notes (*Obras espirituales del B. Alonso Rodríguez*, ed. Jaime Nonell SJ, Barcelona 1885–87, 3 vols) — are tier A by date, but no digitized copy was found on archive.org (searched by title and editor); possibly in Google Books / Biblioteca Digital Hispánica (unverified). English: only the *Autobiography* tr. William Yeomans SJ, 1964 (archive `autobiography0000alph`, lending library) — C. A Life by Francis Goldie SJ (1889, `lifeofstalonso00golduoft`, A) quotes his writings extensively. If the coordinator meant the saint, this is the gap to research next.

## Recommendation

Layout: `rodriguez-ejercicio-de-perfeccion/` (single work; `es-ES/` canonical, `en-US/` Rickaby), with chapters grouped by Part and treatise, e.g. `p1-t01-c01.md`. If an author folder is preferred: `alonso-rodriguez/ejercicio-de-perfeccion`.

Import plan: take the canonical Spanish from the 1861/1879 Librería Religiosa scans plus the 1834 edition (full text with the Latin authorities), OCR-cleaned, using the modern digital text as a reading aid to resolve OCR errors (it is the same text minus the Latin). English from Rickaby 1929, cross-checked against the 1861 Duffy translation. First unit worth importing on its own: **Part I, Treatise 8, *De la conformidad con la voluntad de Dios*** (widely read separately; ~35,000 words) — it also has a 1630 English (TCP A10877).

Cannot be sourced cleanly: a clean, provenance-known, complete Spanish text (every complete source is OCR of 19th-c. prints); the 1834 vol. 3 and 1861/1879 vol. 1 scans (not located); the saint's *Obras espirituales* (not located online).

Total size: ≈ 640,000 words Spanish, ≈ 600,000 words English (Rickaby). One of the largest single works in the corpus.
