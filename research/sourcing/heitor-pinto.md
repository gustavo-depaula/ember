# Frei Heitor Pinto OSH (c. 1528 – c. 1584): sourcing report

Bibliography from Barbosa Machado, *Bibliotheca Lusitana* II (1747), pp. 428–430 (archive `bibliothecalusit02barbuoft`), and Inocêncio, *Dicionário bibliográfico* III (1859), pp. 175–176 (archive `bub_gb_OenFHzCeMFUC`). Hieronymite of Belém (prof. 1543), doctor of Siguenza, professor of Scripture at Coimbra, provincial (1571); opposed Philip II and died in exile in Castile (1584 per the common view).

Works:
1. ***Imagem da vida cristã*** ("christam"), dialogues:
   - **Part I** (6 dialogues: *da verdadeira filosofia*, *da religião*, *da justiça*, *da tribulação*, *da vida solitária*, *da lembrança da morte*), Coimbra, João de Barreira, 1563; expanded by the author in later "acrescentadas" impressions (1572 "quarta impressam", 1580, 1585, 1591, 1592…).
   - **Part II** (5 dialogues: *da tranquilidade da vida*, *da discreta ignorância*, *da verdadeira amizade*, *das causas*, *dos verdadeiros e falsos bens*), Lisboa, João de Barreira, 1572; 1575, 1591, 1592, 1593.
   - Both parts together: Lisboa, Miguel Manescal, 1681; **Lisboa, Typ. Rollandiana, 1843, 3 vols** (ed. Rego Abranches, which Inocêncio praises as following the first editions "sem discrepância", since the old reprints all differ); **Lisboa, Sá da Costa, 1940 ("Clássicos"), pref. and notes by Manuel Alves Correia**, 2nd ed. 1950s. The *Vida solitária* dialogue was said (J. A. de Macedo) to translate Petrarch's *De vita solitaria* — Inocêncio leaves it unverified.
   - Translations: Spanish (Madrid 1572, Medina 1573, Salamanca 1576, Zaragoza 1577, Alcalá 1592), French (Guillaume de Cursol, Paris 1580/Lyon 1593), Italian (Fr. Zaccaria da Lisbona, Venice 1594), Latin (Paris 1617, *Opera* tome IV).
2. Latin Scripture commentaries ("each chapter: literal sense, then notes from the Hebrew, Chaldee and Greek"):
   - *In Esaiam prophetam commentaria* — Lyon, Thibaud Payen, 1561; Antwerp 1567; Cologne 1572; Salamanca 1581; Antwerp 1584.
   - *In Ezechielem prophetam commentaria* — Salamanca 1568; Antwerp 1570; Salamanca 1574; Lyon 1581 (×2); Lyon 1584; Cologne 1615.
   - *In divinum vatem Danielem commentarii* — Coimbra, António de Mariz, 1579; Venice 1583; Cologne 1582; Antwerp 1595.
   - *In Danielem, Lamentationes Hieremiae et Nahum commentarii* — Cologne, Birckmann, 1582; Venice, Tramezini heirs, 1583.
   - Collected: **Coimbra, A. de Mariz, 1579** (one vol., folio); **Lyon, 1584, *Opera omnia Latina*, 3 tomes** (Honorat / Payen / S. Michel; tome I Isaiah + Lamentations, II Ezekiel, III Daniel + Nahum); Lyon 1590; **Paris, Michel Sonnius, 1617, 4 tomes** (IV = the Dialogues in Latin).
3. Manuscript/lost: *Commentaria in primos decem Davidis Psalmos* (MS, S. Francisco de Lisboa); commentaries on all the Minor Prophets (only Nahum printed; MSS at N. Sra. do Espinheiro, Évora).

Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt`, `content/books/` checked).

## Notes per work, sources ranked

### Imagem da vida cristã
Eleven Platonic dialogues (Ciceronian Portuguese; interlocutors include a philosopher, a religious, a courtier), each divided into chapters. One file per chapter, grouped by dialogue (`part-1/dialogo-1-…`), or one file per dialogue (≈ 11 files of 25–40k words — too long; prefer chapters).

No transcribed text exists online: not on pt.wikisource (no page for the author), Gutenberg, Domínio Público (searched dominiopublico.mec.gov.br; the www host returns 403), archive.org (no Portuguese edition of the *Imagem* at all) or the BND as text. The BND PDFs are page images without a text layer (checked on a BND PDF of the same series).

Original, ranked:
1. **Rollandiana 1843, 3 vols** — the best text by Inocêncio's account (princeps text, re-set); A. **Not found online** (BNP has two copies; Google Books not checkable here — API quota exhausted; HathiTrust refuses scripted requests). Worth a manual Google Books / HathiTrust check: a digitized copy would be the canonical base.
2. **BND scans of the princeps and author-revised impressions** (table above) — A, Public Domain Mark on each record. Part I 1563 is the first text; the 1572 "4.ª impressão" and 1580/1585 carry the author's additions ("per elle acrescentados") and are the fuller authorial text. Part II: 1572 princeps. Base for fresh OCR if no 19th-c. text is found; 16th-c. type (abbreviations, tildes, long ſ).
3. **Sá da Costa 1940 / 2nd ed.** — modernized spelling with notes by Manuel Alves Correia: editorial layer C (unverified; Alves Correia's death date not confirmed), text PD; not online.
4. Lisboa 1681 (Manescal) — not online.
Cross-check witnesses: Latin translation, *Opera* Paris 1617 tome IV (`bub_gb_da5rSduDBTAC`, `bub_gb_7trCxKXHKhYC`, `bub_gb_e0HHi7PUri8C`, three Google copies, 220–290k OCR tokens each, poor OCR); Italian 1594 (`bub_gb_0-RT6hvXdJUC`, 351k OCR tokens).

Size: estimated from the princeps foliation (Part I 476 ff., Part II 564 ff., small 8vo, ≈ 180–200 words a page) and the 1843 pagination (1,297 pp.): ≈ 370,000 words for both parts.

English: none found (no English title on archive.org; none in the bibliographies).

### Latin commentaries
All four commentaries survive in many 16th-c. printings; the 1584 Lyon *Opera omnia Latina* is the collected authorial text (tome I Isaiah + Threnos; II Ezekiel; III Daniel + Nahum).
1. **archive `bub_gb_qHfFPFKgnd4C`** (Lyon 1584, all 3 tomes in one 1,297-image scan, Google) — A; 1,046,000 OCR tokens. OCR poor (ſ→f, broken word spacing: *cepidi,trepidaucrunt,abbreuiatamanu*); usable only for re-OCR.
2. **Sevilla copy, `ARes14213` (tome I) + `ARes14214` (tomes II–III)** — A; second witness, cleaner scans, similar OCR.
3. Separate editions: Isaiah Antwerp 1584 (`bub_gb_nDdjhJ6tSsMC`), Ezekiel Lyon 1581 (`bub_gb_HdMusjRKNtcC`) and Antwerp 1582 (`bub_gb_2lr41SMzYNQC`), Daniel Coimbra 1579 (`bub_gb_oFy1YSupmWwC`; text file HTTP 500 at download time), Daniel/Lamentations/Nahum Venice 1583 (`bub_gb_WWFfKFplzdIC`); BND Coimbra prints https://purl.pt/14558 (1579, 303 ff.), https://purl.pt/14858 (1579, 36 ff., probably the Nahum/notes section; title truncated in the record) and https://purl.pt/14758 (Coimbra 1582, 279 ff.; contents not confirmed).
4. Paris 1617, 4 tomes (Google copies above; tomes I–III not all identified).
Size: OCR tokens of the 1584 set ≈ 1.05 M; with OCR splitting/joining noise, ≈ 1.0 M words (estimate).
English/Portuguese translations: none.

## Portuguese
The *Imagem* is the Portuguese original (see above). Orthography layers:
- **Princeps / 16th-c. impressions (BND)**: 16th-c. spelling (*christam, composiçam, philosophia, hũa, polo*), scans only.
- **1843 Rollandiana**: princeps text in mid-19th-c. spelling — the ideal canonical text if a scan is found.
- **1940 Sá da Costa**: modernized, editorial notes — not usable.
The Latin commentaries have no Portuguese translation.
