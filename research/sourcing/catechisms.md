## Baltimore Catechism

**Editions.** The Third Plenary Council of Baltimore (1884) ordered the catechism. Its text was approved on 6 April 1885 (Cardinal McCloskey, Archbishop Gibbons: the approbation is in the PG files). It was drafted by Mgr Januarius De Concilio, after Bellarmine and Butler. **No. 2** is the full 1885 text: 37 lessons, 421 questions in the PG file. **No. 1** (33 lessons, ~206 questions) is the abridgment for first communicants (1885/1891). **No. 3** and **No. 4** are Fr Thomas L. Kinkead's: No. 3 is the expanded question-and-answer edition for post-confirmation classes (imprimaturs New York 1885/1901/1921), and No. 4 is his *Explanation* for teachers (imprimatur 5 Sept 1891, re-approved 29 June 1921). PG's numbering is the conventional one (No. 4 = Kinkead's *Explanation*).

**Sources, best first:**
1. **Project Gutenberg #14551, #14552, #14553, #14554** (released 2005, updated 28 Oct 2024; produced by Dennis McCarthy, "substantially based on files generously provided by http://www.catholic.net/ with some missing material transcribed"). Plain text, HTML and EPUB. Clean. The transcriber's notes are in `{T.N.: …}` braces and have to be stripped, as does the PG licence. A 1922 note on the prayers after Mass shows that Nos. 1–2 were transcribed from a 1920s Benziger printing of the 1885 text. Tier **A** (1885/1891 texts; printings of 1921 or earlier). PG metadata lists "Saint Roberto Francesco Romolo Bellarmino" and "J. De Concilio" as co-authors, a cataloguing quirk.
2. **drbo.org** `https://drbo.org/cat/htm/01001.htm` ("Baltimore Catechism 3"): HTML, probably the same catholic.net lineage. Use as a cross-check for No. 3.
3. **archive.org scans** of the 1885 first edition and the Benziger printings (identifiers not pinned down). Distant Reader PDFs (`distantreader.org/stacks/pamphlets/pdf/005195309–11.pdf`) are derived from the PG files. These are the witnesses for checking PG's text against print.
4. **The 1941 revision** ("New Baltimore Catechism", Confraternity of Christian Doctrine; editions by St. Anthony Guild Press, and Benziger's "Official Revised Edition … Father Kinkead Memorial Edition" 1941; No. 3 by Francis J. Connell CSsR, 1943/1949). Tier: **unverified**. A Princeton Theological Seminary record of the 1949 Confraternity No. 3 says "Public domain according to HathiTrust rights database", so HathiTrust found no renewal for that one. The 1941 Nos. 1–2 were not checked. Modern reprints are sold (Catholic Book Publishing, TAN), which does not by itself make the text C.

**Structure.** Lessons → numbered Q&A. Nos. 1 and 2 split naturally by lesson. No. 3 has the same lessons with double-numbered questions (it notes that "question 130 below is question 1 in Baltimore Catechism No. 2"). No. 4 is lesson-by-lesson prose expansion with the Q&A embedded.

**Recommendation.** Import **No. 2** first (the Council's own text, small), then **No. 4** (Kinkead's *Explanation*, the substantive one), then No. 3. Skip No. 1 or include it as the abridgment. Layout: `baltimore-catechism/{no-1,no-2,no-3}` and `kinkead-explanation-baltimore-catechism`, en-US canonical, one chapter per lesson. Leave the 1941 revision until its renewal status is verified.

## Other catechisms

### *Catechismus Romanus* (Catechism of the Council of Trent), Latin
The corpus `trent-catechism` has en-US and pt-BR but not the Latin original. Sources: archive.org scans of the editio princeps (Rome: Paulus Manutius 1566: `bub_gb_lyTbTDLKuJMC`, `bub_gb_vwHHgFT64VQC`, `bub_gb_CHfYRK1oQ8oC`), 1567 Aldine (`ita-bnc-ald-00000512-001`), 1572/1574 (`bub_gb_ojY8AAAAcAAJ`, `bub_gb_7DU8AAAAcAAJ`), and many 18th–19th c. editions. **No clean Latin text found.** DCO carries the Roman Catechism only in EN/FR/IT (`z_1545-1545__SS_Pius_V__Catechismus_Catholicae_Ecclesiae_Tridentinum__…`), and Latin Wikisource searches found nothing. A 19th-c. edition such as Turin/Marietti or Ratisbon (Pustet) would OCR better than the 1566 print. Tier A. **This is the most valuable item here, since it completes an existing book**, but it needs OCR work.

### Deharbe (Joseph Deharbe SJ, 1800–1871)
- German: *Katholischer Katechismus oder Lehrbegriff* (Regensburg 1847), the "Großer", "Mittlerer" and "Kleiner" catechisms. BSB scans on archive.org (`10389286bsb` 1849, `11774646bsb` 1848, `10390346bsb` *Großer katholischer Catechismus* 1857, `katholischerkate02deha` 1871). The Fraktur OCR is unusable (`Natholiſcher Katechismus…`), so the German would need fresh OCR. Tier A.
- English, ranked:
  1. *A Complete Catechism of the Catholic Religion*, trans. John Fander, 6th American ed. "conformed to the Codex Juris Canonici", ed. James J. Fox and Thomas McMillan CSP (New York: Schwartz, Kirwin & Fauss; ©1912, 1919, 1924). archive.org `completecatechis00deharich` (113k words OCR, good). en.wikisource is transcribing it (`A Complete Catechism of the Catholic Religion`, marked `{{incomplete}}`, PD-US). Tier A (1924 or earlier). Best: the most current text and partly proofread.
  2. *A Full Catechism of the Catholic Religion*, trans. Fander (London 1863; imprimatur Wiseman 1862), archive.org `AFullCatechismOfTheCatholic` (110k, Google OCR), and `afullcatechismc00dehagoog` (1876). The original English translation, for cross-checking.
  3. *A Catechism of the Catholic Religion*, ed. Bishop P. N. Lynch (New York: Catholic Publication Society 1878), archive.org `catechismofcatho00deha` (51k). A shorter text (the middle catechism).
  4. *Little Catholic Catechism for Beginners* (1873), `littlecatholicca00deha`.
- Structure: a "Short History of Religion" (sacred history), then Part I Faith (Creed), Part II Commandments, Part III Means of Grace. Q&A numbered within chapters, with "Application" paragraphs.

### St Peter Canisius (1521–1597)
Three catechisms: *Summa doctrinae christianae* (major, 1555; 1566 edition with patristic authorities, later published as *Opus catechisticum*, 1577, with Busaeus's citations, which is much larger), *Catechismus minimus* (1556), and *Parvus catechismus catholicorum* (1558).
- Latin: archive.org scans of the *Summa* (`bub_gb_Uj3tT9cFgAoC` 1565, `bub_gb_czGt9CymA-wC` 1569, `bub_gb_-DorH66zLnUC` 1573, `bub_gb_YiDXX5mUfoAC` 1587, `bub_gb_kzGIOIY_1uYC` 1623, `bub_gb_dAMzzSJPjp0C` 1757 "Cathechismus major"), *Opus catechisticum* (`ARes04109` 1577, `ARes10113`/`bub_gb_oGAY0CnFPrYC` 1585), *Parvus* (1687 EEBO; BSB 1732/1754/1756 Latin–German). DCO has Latin PDFs: `z_1521-1597__Canisius__Summa_Doctrinae_Christianae__LT.pdf`, `…Catechismus_Minor__LT.pdf`, `…Catechismus_Catholicus__LT.pdf`, `…Catechismus_Graeco_Latinus__LT_GR.pdf`. Whether they are scans or text was not checked. Clean Latin: **none found**. Tier A.
- English: **Henry Garnet SJ, *A Summe of Christian Doctrine*** ([London, secret press], 1592–96). EEBO-TCP Phase II **A69066**, `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/textcreationpartnership/A69066/master/A69066.xml`. Its availability statement: "the Text Creation Partnership has waived all copyright … CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication". **143,419 words** (counted, including the "appendix of the fall of man & iustification" and marginal references). Clean keyboarded text, but early-modern spelling (ſ, u/v, "Chriſtian Rel • gion"; `•` marks illegible characters). Tier **A**. The only good English. The 1971 Scolar Press facsimile shows it was a standard recusant text. A modern-spelling normalisation pass would be needed.
- Recommendation: `canisius-summa-doctrinae-christianae` with `la/` from a clean OCR of the 1566 text (or a 19th-c. reprint, which would OCR better; not searched) and `en-US/` from Garnet. Canisius likely deserves his own author file if his other works are wanted.

## Recommendation

1. **Baltimore No. 2 + Kinkead's Explanation (No. 4)**: clean Gutenberg text, tier A, English original, ~163k words for both. Then No. 3 (~75k) and No. 1 (~13k). The easiest import of the whole set.
2. **Deharbe, *Complete Catechism* (1924 American ed.)**: ~110k, A, good OCR, partly proofread on en.wikisource. Add the German later if OCR'd.
3. **Canisius, *Summa doctrinae christianae***: Garnet's English (CC0, 143k, needs spelling normalisation) and Latin from scans.
4. **Latin *Catechismus Romanus*** to complete `trent-catechism`: high value, but scans only, so it needs OCR.
5. **Gaume** (Jamison abridgment ~120k first, the full 4-vol ~1M later) and **Spirago** (~400k): large, OCR cleanup.

Layout: one top-level book per catechism (`baltimore-catechism-no-2`, `kinkead-explanation-baltimore-catechism`, `deharbe-complete-catechism`, `canisius-summa-doctrinae-christianae`, `gaume-catechism-of-perseverance`, `spirago-catechism-explained`), matching how `pius-x-catechism` sits at top level. Chapters follow lessons/articles.

**Cannot be sourced cleanly:** the Latin of the Roman Catechism and of Canisius (scans only), the German of Deharbe and Spirago (Fraktur scans), and the 1941 revised Baltimore (tier unverified).

**Total (English, recommended set):** Baltimore 1–4 ≈ 250k + Deharbe ≈ 110k + Canisius/Garnet ≈ 143k + Gaume abridged ≈ 120k + Spirago ≈ 400k ≈ **1.0M words**, plus the full Gaume (~1M) if wanted. Originals to OCR: Catechismus Romanus est. 150k, Canisius *Summa* est. 70k.
