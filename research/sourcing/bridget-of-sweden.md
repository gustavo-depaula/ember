# St. Bridget (Birgitta) of Sweden (c. 1303–1373): sourcing report

Bibliography basis: the critical Latin edition, *Samlingar utgivna av Svenska Fornskriftsällskapet*, Ser. 2, Latinska skrifter VII (Kungl. Vitterhetsakademien, Stockholm 1956–2002; editors Undhagen, Bergh, Jönsson, Aili, Eklund). It runs to twelve volumes: *Revelaciones* Books I–VIII, *Revelaciones extravagantes*, and *Opera minora* I–III (*Regula Salvatoris*, *Sermo angelicus*, *Quattuor oraciones*). Together that is about 700 revelations and ~1 000 printed pages of text, per the Riksarkivet page captured in 2015 ([Wayback](http://web.archive.org/web/20150911212835/http://www.ra.se/crb)). Her own Old Swedish autograph fragments survive and are on Litteraturbanken ("Heliga Birgittas originaltexter").

**Spurious or late:** the "Fifteen Prayers / Fifteen O's" (*Quindecim orationes*) are late-medieval and not hers. The *Coroa das Dores* and the various "promises" are later devotions.

Nothing by Bridget is in the corpus.

## Sources ranked

### Latin
1. **SSFS critical edition on Litteraturbanken**, all eight books: Book I `lb502476` (291k tokens incl. intro and apparatus), II `lb8208450` (55k), III `lb8208449` (113k), IV `lb502479` (152k), V `lb8208448` (84k), VI `lb502478` (109k), VII `lb8208447` (255k), VIII `lb8588698` (102k). Export pattern: `https://litteraturbanken.se/export/faksimil/<id>.txt` (downloaded to `bridget-of-sweden/crb/`). This is the best text by far, born-digital and not proofread. **But** the rights note in every file says the work may not be redistributed or altered and may not be used commercially: **tier C**. The medieval text itself is PD; the edition, with its apparatus and constituted text, is not ours to redistribute without permission.
2. **CRB PDFs (Riksarkivet 2004–c. 2016)**: the same edition as born-digital PDFs per book plus *Extravagantes* and *Opera minora*. They are now offline, and Wayback has copies (e.g. [Liber Primus](http://web.archive.org/web/20160102193940/http://www.ra.se/Media/pdf-filer/SanctaBirgitta_Reuelacionum_LiberPrimus.pdf)). The terms were not seen: unverified, assume C.
3. **DCO Word file** (3.7 MB, 2006, downloaded to `bridget-of-sweden/dco/`; 367.7k words). It covers Books I–VIII, "Liber X" *Regula Salvatoris*, "Liber XI" *Sermo angelicus* and "Liber XII" *Oraciones*. Medieval orthography (*reuelacionum*) and SSFS-style verse numbers suggest it is derived from CRB, despite the "Ghotan 1492" heading. DCO states "© 2006 Cooperatorum Veritatis Societas quoad hanc editionem iura omnia asservantur": **unverified / C**. It is still useful as a working draft to collate against a PD print.
4. **PD prints**: Ghotan 1492 (the SSFS base text for several books), Koberger 1500 (incunable scan), Durante's annotated editions (Rome 1606 and 1628; Antwerp 1611; Munich 1680). These are scans only (Gothic or early type); no usable OCR was found.

### English
1. **catholic-saints.net / saintbirgitta.com** (ProphecyFilm, the Hietanen brothers), reissued by Jazzybee Verlag (ISBN 9783849622541), with the note "The texts are licenced under the Creative Commons Attribution — Share Alike License". It covers Books 1–8, the extravagantes, *Sermo angelicus* and the four prayers: ~340k tokens, clean modern English. **The translator is not named and the source is unknown.** The phrasing ("like the sun shining through the clearest gem") resembles the Searby/OUP translation, so it may derive from it. Tier **unverified**. If the provenance can be shown, CC BY-SA would be tier B, with share-alike to consider.
2. Searby (tr.) & Morris (notes), *The Revelations of St. Birgitta of Sweden*, OUP 2006–2015: complete and scholarly, **C**.
3. Sadlier 1862 / Neligan 1852, *Revelations… on the Life and Passion of Our Lord and the Life of His Blessed Mother*: a thematic selection, A, 35k, clean.
4. Cumming 1929 (EETS 178): Middle English selections, A (published 1929).
5. Kezel & Morris, *Life and Selected Revelations* (Paulist 1990): C.

## Notes
- Structure: Book → chapter (*capitulum*) → numbered verses. The verse numbers come from SSFS and are absent from old prints, which number only chapters. Book V (*Liber quaestionum*) is "interrogations" plus revelations. Book VII is the pilgrimage book.
- If we import, the Latin needs a PD base we may redistribute. Two options: (a) transcribe Durante 1628 or Ghotan 1492 and correct it against SSFS, reading only; or (b) **ask Vitterhetsakademien/SSFS for permission**. They commissioned the free CRB edition to widen access, so they may agree.

## Portuguese
Searched: archive.org (language: por), pt.wikisource and web search. **No Portuguese translation of the Revelations found**, complete or partial, apart from devotional leaflets (*Coroa das Dores de Maria Santíssima*, archive 2025, translated from Spanish; spurious-tradition material).

## Recommendation
- Layout: `bridget-revelationes/<book-N>` plus `regula-salvatoris`, `sermo-angelicus`, `quattuor-oraciones`, `revelaciones-extravagantes`.
- **Blocked on rights.** Every convenient Latin text (SSFS/Litteraturbanken, CRB, DCO) is restricted or unverified, and the only complete English is of unverified provenance. Before any import: (1) write to SSFS/Vitterhetsakademien about reusing the CRB/SSFS text; (2) try to identify the catholic-saints.net translator. Failing both, a PD Latin base means keying from Durante 1628 scans, which have to be located on Google Books or BSB first. Then the *Sermo angelicus* (short, liturgical, the most useful for a prayer app) and the *Quattuor oraciones* could go first.
- **Total size:** Latin ≈ 370k words (DCO count for the whole corpus). English (unverified) ≈ 340k. PD English selections ≈ 110k.
