# Pe. Manuel Bernardes CO (1644–1710): sourcing report

Bibliography. Oratorian of the Lisbon Congregation (entered 1674), the classic of Portuguese spiritual prose. The authoritative lists are **Diogo Barbosa Machado, *Bibliotheca Lusitana* III (1752), pp. 194–196** (archive `bibliothecalusit03barbuoft`) and **Inocêncio Francisco da Silva, *Diccionario bibliographico portuguez* V (1860), nos. M-193 to M-200, pp. 374–376** (archive `016843-05-completo`, `bub_gb_Bo8A446yn74C`), repeated by Castilho's *Notícia da vida e obras* (*Livraria Clássica* VII, 1847; reprinted Rio, Garnier, 1865, II pp. 271ff.). Both read and checked for this report. The canon they agree on:

Pieces found only in later catalogues, with their status:
- *Meditações sobre os quatro novíssimos do homem* (Lisboa 1744 "with other spiritual works of various authors"; 1798): an extract (from *Pão partido* / the *Tratado breve*), not a separate work.
- *Estímulos do amor divino* ("3.ª impressão", Lisboa, Miguel Rodrigues, 1758; BNP): **not in Barbosa or Inocêncio**. *Luz e calor* Part II is organized in numbered "Estímulos" (OCR of the 1696 ed.: "ESTIMULO I…"), so this is most likely an extract of *Luz e calor*; **unverified, treat as an excerpt**.
- *Discurso sobre a educação* (1908, BND purl.pt/6527), *Dois discursos* (1907), *Histórias várias* (1920), *Exercícios espirituais* ed. selecta (1915, 1923), *Leituras piedosas e prodigiosas* (1962), *Páginas escolhidas* (1941)…: modern **anthologies of extracts**, no new text.
- *Tratado das sagradas imagens* and *Vida de Santo António*: **not attested** in Barbosa Machado, Inocêncio or Castilho — treat as **spurious/unattested**. The "Santo António" item is very likely a confusion with the namesake **Manuel Bernardes Branco (1832–1900)**, author of *O padre S.to Antonio de Lisboa: thaumaturgo…* (1887, archive `opadrestoantoni00brangoog`), whose books flood any author search for "Bernardes".
- **Letters and poems:** no collection of letters or poems is recorded by Barbosa, Inocêncio or Castilho; Barbosa says he wrote only under obedience and never revised. Bernardes's verses (Latin and Portuguese quatrains, translated epigrams) are embedded inside *Nova Floresta* and *Luz e calor*; no separate letters were found. Nothing to import separately; flag as "none known".

**Not in the corpus** (`_corpus-books.txt` and `content/books/` checked: no Bernardes).

**English.** No English translation of any work was found, PD or otherwise (archive.org, Gutenberg, Online Books Page, web searches). English literature only quotes him in passing (Aubrey Bell, *Portuguese Literature*, Oxford 1922; Bouterwek/Ross). A French translation of the *Exercícios* exists (Paris, Périsse, 1863; BNP) — usable only as a witness. **English would have to be produced by our pipeline from the Portuguese.**

## Original text (Portuguese): notes per work, sources ranked

Orthography layers, since the corpus must choose one canonical text:
- **Princeps and 18th-c. reprints (1686–1785)**: Baroque spelling (*hum, naõ, Deos, cousa, mãy, perfeyção, efficaz, rezaõ*), long ſ, ligatures, Latin marginal citations. archive.org OCR reads ſ as *f* throughout (*efta, fua, Chrifto*) and mangles the margins: legible but not importable as is; my token-quality score (share of clean alphabetic tokens) is 0.77–0.86 for these, against 0.92 for the 1865/1920 books. Needs re-OCR (the research/ocr-books pipeline) or a systematic ſ→s pass plus proofreading.
- **19th-c. (Castilho 1845–47/1865; *Luz e calor* 1871; Chardron 1909–11)**: pre-1911 etymological spelling (*auctor, theologia, apophthegmas, Deus, não*) — the princeps text re-spelt, set in modern type; good OCR expected.
- **1911-reform / 20th-c. (Campos/Aillaud 1920; Lello/Chardron 1940s–1970s; Sá da Costa/Clássica 1942; Verbo 1965; Editora das Américas, São Paulo, 1959–60 "*Obras completas*… de acordo com a edição-princeps")**: modernized spelling. Only Campos 1920 (excerpts) is clearly PD; the Lello individual editions (*Luz e calor* 1953, *Pão partido* 1957, *Obras* 1974), the Clássica, Verbo and Américas editions carry editors' notes/prefaces and are **C (unverified, treat as unusable)**.

No 1859–1860s Lisbon edition of any work was found (the 4th impression of *Nova Floresta* is **1759**, Régia Oficina Silviana; BNP); the 19th-c. layer is Castilho's excerpts and *Luz e calor* 1871.

### Nova Floresta (5 tomes)
Commonplace book arranged by alphabetical *Títulos* (tome I: I *Abstinência, jejum* … X *Avareza*; ~10 títulos per tome), each holding numbered apophthegms of saints, philosophers and princes, each followed by Bernardes's *reflexão*, with Latin citations in the margin. Chapter split: one file per *Título* (≈ 50 files), the apophthegms as numbered sections.
1. **Princeps, archive.org `novaflorestaousy01bernuoft` … `05bernuoft`** (Toronto, NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT) — complete 5-tome set of the first edition (I 1706, II 1708, III 1711, IV 1726, V 1728); OCR 170k/137k/183k/181k/181k tokens, ſ→f. Best authority, poor text layer. A.
2. **Porto, Livraria Chardron (Lello & Irmão), 1909–1911, 5 vols, preamble by Sampaio Bruno** — HathiTrust record `001722113`: `mdp.39015062911535` (v.1), `…911451` (v.2), `…911543` (v.3), `…911550` (v.4), `…911568` (v.5), all **Full view**; also record `011262420` (v.1–3, Wisconsin/UC); Google Books `NfoAAAAAMAAJ` (one volume, full view). Complete text in modern type, pre-1911 spelling; Sampaio Bruno d. 1915 → A. **Best base for a clean text**, but HathiTrust's page text and Google's EPUB are behind a Cloudflare/browser check for scripted requests (both refused here); needs a manual PDF download or a browser session.
3. **HathiTrust (UCM copies, Google-digitized), full view**: 1706 `009343245`, 1708 `009294638`, 1726 `009343444`, 1728 `009333539`; Google `lG5iJBbSKcYC` (1706), `72RaAAAAcAAJ` (1728). Second scans of the princeps tomes (III 1711 not among them). A.
4. **BND purl.pt/253** — 1726 reissue of tomes I–III, page images. A.
5. Agostinho de Campos, *Antologia Portuguesa: Bernardes I «Nova Floresta»*, 2nd ed. 1920 (`novaflorestaesti01bernuoft`, OCR 51k, clean, 1911 spelling) — excerpts only; useful for spot-checking. A.

### Paraíso dos contemplativos (translation) — not located online
Bernardes's annotated Portuguese version of Saluzzo's *Paradiso de' contemplativi*. Prints of 1739 and 1761 at the BNP; no digitization found on archive.org, HathiTrust (Online Books Page list), Google (legacy feed search returned 0) or BND. Lowest priority: a translation, and its Italian original would arguably belong to Saluzzo.

### Searches that failed or were blocked
- **dominiopublico.gov.br**: every request (plain curl, with redirects) gets a Cloudflare "Just a moment…" challenge, HTTP 403 — **blocked**, not searched. Web searches surface no Bernardes title there.
- **pt.wikisource**: author page `Autor:Manuel Bernardes` exists with a work list but **no transcribed text** (`{{povoar}}`); no subpages.
- **Project Gutenberg**: nothing by Bernardes.
- **HathiTrust**: catalogue and bibliographic API (`catalog.hathitrust.org/api/volumes/brief/…`) answer; full-text/plain-text pages and catalogue search are behind Cloudflare.
- **Google Books API v1**: daily quota exhausted (HTTP 429/quota error); the legacy feed `www.google.com/books/feeds/volumes?q=…&min-viewability=full` worked and gave the ids above; EPUB/text downloads redirect to a browser page.

## English
None exists in the public domain, and no modern English translation was found either. Bernardes was never translated into English beyond quotations in literary histories. The pipeline would produce `en-US/` from the Portuguese, like the pt-BR translations it makes for other authors.

## Recommendation

Layout: `manuel-bernardes-opera-omnia/<work>`, canonical text in Portuguese. The corpus has one `pt-PT/` precedent (`gaume-resumo-catecismo-perseveranca/pt-PT`). Proposed: `pt-PT/` = the transcribed historical text, `pt-BR/` = the same text in current Brazilian spelling (a modernization pass, not a translation), `en-US/` = pipeline translation.

Works (≈ 50 + 40 + 60 + … files): `nova-floresta` (5 tomes, one file per Título), `luz-e-calor`, `exercicios-espirituais` (with the *Instrução breve da oração mental*), `ultimos-fins-do-homem`, `estimulo-pratico`, `pao-partido-em-pequeninos`, `meditacoes-misterios-da-virgem`, `direcao-nove-dias-exercicios`, `armas-da-castidade`, `sermoes-e-praticas` (2 parts), and, last, `paraiso-dos-contemplativos` (translation; or skip).

Order:
1. **Import first: *Os últimos fins do homem*** or ***Pão partido em pequeninos*** — single volumes on archive.org (1761 / 1726) with second witnesses (1728 / 1704–08) on HathiTrust/Google; *Pão partido* is the smallest (≈ 62k) and the most catechetical, a good pilot for the OCR + orthography pipeline.
2. *Nova Floresta* — his masterpiece and the largest work: OCR the **Chardron 1909–11** volumes (manual PDF download from HathiTrust/Google), collate against the archive.org princeps.
3. *Luz e calor* (1871 ed. as clean base, 1696 as authority), *Exercícios* (1706 as copy-text), *Estímulo prático*, *Vários tratados* I.
4. Scan-only works: *Sermões e práticas* I–II and *Armas da castidade* (HathiTrust/Google PDFs → OCR).

Cannot be sourced cleanly: any English (none exists); a clean text layer for any work (all are scans; only the 1909 Nova Floresta and 1871 Luz e calor are in modern type); *Paraíso dos contemplativos* (no digitization located).

Total size: Portuguese ≈ 2.1 million words (Nova Floresta ≈ 740k, Exercícios ≈ 340k, Sermões ≈ 290k est., Luz e calor ≈ 230k, Últimos fins ≈ 155k, Estímulo prático ≈ 135k, Meditações da Virgem ≈ 85k, Armas ≈ 65k est., Pão partido ≈ 62k, Direção ≈ 44k), plus ≈ 150k for the *Paraíso* translation; English 0.

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/manuel-bernardes/` (`ia/` archive.org `_djvu.txt` of all 20 items, `bib/` Barbosa Machado III and Inocêncio V, `bnp.html` BNP catalogue results, `bin/iacount.py` the counting script).
