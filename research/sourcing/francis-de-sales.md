# St. Francis de Sales (1567–1622): sourcing report

Reference bibliography: the **Annecy edition**, *Œuvres de saint François de Sales, évêque et prince de Genève et docteur de l'Église. Édition complète d'après les autographes et les éditions originales*, edited by the Religious of the Visitation of the First Monastery of Annecy (Dom H. B. Mackey OSB, then Dom J.-J. Navatel and others). Annecy: J. Niérat / J. Abry, 1892–1932: 26 volumes, plus vol. XXVII *Table analytique* (1964). It is the critical text, in Francis's own 17th-century spelling, with variants and notes. Every later French edition derives from it.

| Vol. | Contents | Date |
|---|---|---|
| I | Les Controverses | 1892 |
| II | Défense de l'Estendart de la Sainte Croix | 1892 |
| III | Introduction à la vie dévote | 1893 |
| IV–V | Traitté de l'Amour de Dieu (I–II) | 1894 |
| VI | Les Vrays Entretiens spirituels | 1895 |
| VII–X | Sermons (I–IV) | 1896–1898 |
| XI–XXI | Lettres (I–XI): 1585 → 1622, plus undated letters and a supplement in XXI | 1900–1923 |
| XXII–XXVI | Opuscules (I–V) | 1925–1932 |
| XXVII | Table analytique | 1964 |

The opuscules are arranged in series. XXII: 1st series, *Études et vie intime* (Paris/Padua student notes, the Padua *Exercices spirituels*, the *Règles pour la réception de la sainte Communion*, the act of abandonment of 1586/7, theology notes) and the 2nd series. XXIII: 3rd series, *Controverse* (fragments on predestination, *Briefve Méditation sur le Symbole*, letters to the minister Viret, the inédit fragment of Book IV of the *Estendart*, the *Codex Fabrianus* first title), and the start of the 4th series, *Administration épiscopale*. XXIV: that series continued (ordinances, the diocesan synods, parish and chapel patents, mostly short legal/administrative documents). XXV: 5th series, *Visitation* (Préface, Règle de saint Augustin, Constitutions, *Directoire spirituel*, Coutumier, etc.). XXVI: 6th series, *Ascétisme et mystique* (*Déclaration mystique sur le Cantique des Cantiques*, fragments on the cardinal virtues, on Our Lady, on the sign of the cross, *Conseils aux Supérieurs*, the collections of *Similitudes*, notes for the *Traité*, *Avis*, the *Lettre sur la prédication* to André Frémyot, and others). I read the tables of contents of XXII–XXVI from the PDFs below.

**Spurious or doubtful works:** the Annecy editors treat the *Traité de la Démonomanie* as wrongly attributed (critical note in XXIII, p. 26). *L'Esprit de saint François de Sales* is by J.-P. Camus. The *Pensées consolantes* are Huguet's compilation. *Maximes et conseils*, *Practical Piety*, *The Secret of Sanctity* and *Mystical Flora* are 19th-century anthologies or compilations, not works of Francis. The *Méditations pour les solitudes* were compiled by St Jane de Chantal (see `jane-de-chantal.md`). The *Entretiens* and the *Sermons recueillis* are reportationes: texts taken down by the Visitandines, authentic in substance.

## Original-language sources, ranked per work

### Common to all works: the Annecy volumes

1. **donboscosanto.eu PDFs**, directory listing at https://www.donboscosanto.eu/francesco_di_sales/. All 27 volumes are there, each in two versions: `NN-Oeuvres de Saint Francois de Sales-Tome …pdf` (2022 re-OCR, 16–30 MB) and `…-web.pdf` (2013). They are page images with a text layer (fonts TimesNewRoman/Cambria, FineReader-style). I downloaded tomes XXII–XXVI and ran `pdftotext` on them. The text is **good**: accents and the 17th-century spelling are mostly kept. Typical errors: `qtti` for `qui`, `0 filles` for `O filles`, soft-hyphen splits (`particulière­ment`), marginal scripture refs (`* Vers. 8, 9.`) and running heads interleaved. This is the only online source for XXII–XXVI and the best OCR overall. The site gives no licence or rights statement; the scans are of a PD edition.
   - Page counts / raw words: XXII 431 pp. / 161k; XXIII 456 / 179k; XXIV 576 / 210k; XXV 577 / 213k; XXVI 524 / 199k.
   - Also there: `00-fr_sales-touts_les_index.htm`, an HTML of all the indexes (1.8 MB).
2. **archive.org, University of Ottawa/Toronto scans** of vols I–XXI: `oeuvresdesaintfr{01..20}fran`, with `oeuvresf05fran`, `oeuvresf16fran`, `oeuvresf19fran` and `oeuvresf21fran` for the volumes whose primary item lacks a djvu.txt. There are also duplicate scans (`oeuvresf{10..21}fran`, `oeuvres{15..21}fran`, `oeuvresdesaintf0Nmackgoog`). The djvu OCR is **fair**: title pages are garbled (`ÉVÊaUE`, `l: AINT`) and long-s/old-spelling errors occur, but the body is usable. Raw word counts: I 188k, II 174k, III 219k, IV 155k, V 184k, VI 206k, VII 182k, VIII 172k, IX 184k, X 217k, XI 174k, XII 188k, XIII 171k, XIV 175k, XV 169k, XVI 181k, XVII 179k, XVIII 184k, XIX 191k, XX 171k, XXI 197k. **Vols XXII–XXVII are not on archive.org.** Use these scans as the cross-check witness for donboscosanto.
3. **Gallica (BnF)** holds Annecy volumes and the Vivès edition, but the SRU API returned a Cloudflare block from this machine: **unverified**.

### Secondary French editions (modernized spelling): fallbacks and cross-checks

- **Vivès**, *Œuvres complètes*, Paris, L. Vivès, 8th ed. 1899 (12 vols): archive `oeuvrescomplte{01..12}fran`, `oeuvres{01..11}franuoft`. Pre-Annecy text in modernized spelling; contains works and letters but fewer letters and no critical apparatus. Tier A.
- **Berche et Tralin**, *Œuvres complètes*, 1898 (10 vols): `oeuvrescompletes00{01..10}fran`. t.1–2 sermons, Controverses; t.3 Estendart, Introduction… Tier A.
- **Société générale de librairie catholique**, *Œuvres*, 1880: `oeuvresdestfran{05,10,11}franuoft`. **Guyot**, 1851 (with Chantal's letters): `oeuvrescompltes0{1,2,3}frangoog`. **Chaumont/Palmé**, 1879: `oeuvrespublies01fran`. **Houdaille**, 1836 (1 vol.): `bub_gb_gRO_AJb3vHYC`. All tier A, OCR fair to poor (Google scans).

### Per work

**Les Controverses** (written as tracts for the Chablais, 1595–96; first ed. 1672)
1. Annecy I (donboscosanto `01-…-Les Controverses.pdf`; archive `oeuvresdesaintfr01fran`). Critical text. Structure: Avant-propos, *Première partie: Défense de l'autorité de l'Église* (chap. I *Rayson I: de la Mission*, articles), *Seconde partie: Les règles de la foi*, unfinished *Troisième partie*, then articles. Tier A.
2. JesusMarie, *Lettre ouverte aux Protestants*: http://jesusmarie.free.fr/francois_de_sales_lettre_ouverte_aux_protestants.html (one page, 102k words; plan at `…_plan.html`; zip). It transcribes the Annecy text in its original spelling (`Premierement, Messieurs, vos devanciers…`). The **last pages switch to modernized spelling and stop mid-argument** (purgatory), so completeness is doubtful: diff it against Annecy before relying on it. The site says "édition numérique originale JESUSMARIE.com, Paris, 4 juin 2001… téléchargement gratuit" with no licence → tier B for the digital text.
3. Vivès / Berche & Tralin (modernized spelling) as a third witness. Tier A.

**Défense de l'Estendart de la Sainte Croix** (1600)
1. Annecy II (donboscosanto 02; archive `oeuvresdesaintfr02fran`). Four books (*Livre premier* … *quatrième*) in chapters. The inédit fragment of Book IV is in Opuscules XXIII (no. IX). Tier A.
2. Berche & Tralin t.3, Vivès: modernized spelling. Tier A.
- No clean HTML found.

**Introduction à la vie dévote** (1609; definitive 1619): the corpus has en-US and pt-BR only, so a French `fr-FR/` would complete it.
1. **fr.wikisource**, *Introduction à la vie dévote (Boulenger)*: https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Introduction_à_la_vie_dévote_(Boulenger). Fernand Boulenger's 1909 edition (Paris, Garnier) of the **1619 definitive text**, original spelling, proofread and validated ("4/4", category *Bon pour export*). Clean text with page-level transclusion of the PDF. Boulenger died 1934 (apparatus PD in France since 2005; US PD as 1909) → tier A. **Best source.**
2. Annecy III (donboscosanto 03; `oeuvresdesaintfr03fran`): the critical 1619 text with variants. Use it as the check witness. Tier A.
3. fr.wikisource, *Introduction à la vie dévote (Brignon)*: Brignon's 1695 revision (Curet 1810 printing), validated. Modernized; a different textual state. Tier A.
4. Gutenberg #53540 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/53540): Périsse, Lyon 1832 "édition corrigée", modernized; 98k words with front matter. Clean. Tier A.
5. JesusMarie HTML in 12 parts (`francois_de_sales_introduction_a_la_vie_devote_1ere_partie_1.html` …; ~10k words per page). Tier B.
- Structure: Préface + five parts (I: 24 ch., II: 21, III: 41, IV: 15, V: 18). The corpus en-US uses the same five-part split (`part-i-chapter-i` …), so the French aligns one-to-one.

**Traitté de l'Amour de Dieu** (1616)
1. Annecy IV–V (donboscosanto 04, 05; archive `oeuvresdesaintfr04fran`, `oeuvresf05fran`). Critical text; Préface, 12 books with chapters. Tier A.
2. JesusMarie, http://jesusmarie.free.fr/francois_de_sales_traite_amour_de_dieu.html (one page, 216k words incl. the dedication and glossing footnotes such as `emmi (1)`). Complete: it ends at "FIN DU DOUZIÈME LIVRE ET DE TOUT LE TRAITÉ". **Modernized spelling** (*vous êtes*, *cœur*), probably a 19th-c. edition, source unnamed. Clean HTML. Tier B.
3. Vivès / Berche & Tralin. Tier A.

**Les Vrays Entretiens spirituels** (21 entretiens, ed. 1629 by Chantal)
1. Annecy VI (donboscosanto 06). It gives the "vrays" text of 1629, with the earlier pirated text (Lyon 1628) and fragments in an appendix. Tier A.
2. JesusMarie, http://jesusmarie.free.fr/francois_de_sales_les_entretiens.html (142k words). Modernized spelling with numbered glosses. Source line at the end: "l'abbaye Saint Benoit, Port Valais Suisse, http://www.abbaye-saint-benoit.ch/bibliotheque.htm" (that site timed out from here). Tier B.
3. Vivès. Tier A.

**Sermons** (Annecy VII–X)
1. Annecy VII–X (donboscosanto 07–10; archive `oeuvresdesaintfr07fran`–`10fran`). Vol. VII is 1896, X is 1898. These are the autograph sermons (Latin-French drafts, 1593–1622) and the *sermons recueillis* (sermons taken down by Visitandines). Annecy splits them by type, and within each type by date; one chapter per sermon. My reading is that VII–VIII are the autographs and IX–X the recueillis, but I have not confirmed the split per volume. Tier A.
2. JesusMarie: ~18 single sermons (Notre Père, paille et poutre, Transfiguration, St Blaise, Noël, Sexagésime 1594, 26 July 1618, Good Friday 1620, Annunciation 1621, 2 July 1621, All Saints 1621, Ash Wednesday 1622, 1st Sunday of Lent 1622, 20 Feb 1622) plus *Méditation sur la passion* and others, at `http://jesusmarie.free.fr/francois_de_sales_sermon_*.html`. Fr.wikisource lists the same set as titles without pages. Tier B.
3. Berche & Tralin t.1–2 (Sermons): modernized. Tier A.

**Lettres** (~2,100 letters, Annecy XI–XXI)
1. **JesusMarie, Lettres, 11 tomes**: http://jesusmarie.free.fr/francois_de_sales_lettres_tome_{1..11}.html, zip of all 11 at `francois_de_sales_lettres.zip`. Each tome is headed "Tome XI de l'édition d'Annecy" … "Tome XXI". It is a **transcription of the Annecy text in original spelling** (*vostre lettre du 6 decembre*), with the editors' Avant-propos, the letter numbers (DCCXLII …), headings with addressee and summary, the appendix pieces, the *Glossaire des locutions et des mots surannés*, the index of correspondents and the table. Text-only word counts per tome: 82k, 80k, 74k, 120k, 105k, 94k, 102k, 100k, 110k, 105k, 119k = **1.09M**. Footnotes are mostly dropped (the Annecy raw count is 1.98M). Clean HTML. Tier B (no licence; offered freely). **Best source for the letters**; check spot errors against Annecy.
2. Annecy XI–XXI (archive.org and donboscosanto, see above). Tier A.
3. Vivès t. 9–12 (fewer letters, modernized). Tier A.

**Opuscules** (Annecy XXII–XXVI)
1. donboscosanto PDFs 22–26, the **only full source**. Tiers: XXII (1925), XXIII (1928) and XXIV (1929) are **A**. XXV (1931) and XXVI (1932) contain Francis's own 17th-century text, which is PD, plus anonymous collective editorial apparatus by the Visitation of Annecy: PD in France 70 years after publication (2001/2002); in the US the 95-year term ends on 1 Jan 2027 (XXV) and 1 Jan 2028 (XXVI). Some pieces were first published there as inédits, and their US status follows the same dates. → **A for the author's text; apparatus "unverified" until those dates.** The simplest route is to import the author's text without the editors' notes, or to wait.
2. JesusMarie short pieces (`…avis_spirituels_1.htm`, `_2.html`, `petit_traite_sur_la_sainte_communion`, `bien_faire_sa_confession`, `amourduprochain_1/2`, `meditations_mysteres_joyeux/douloureux`, `traite_de_la_predication`, `conseils_aux_superieurs`, `lettre_a_sainte_jeanne_de_chantal`, `meditation_sur_la_passion`) and a zip of "écrits divers". Tier B; sources not named.
3. Opuscules in Vivès t.? and Berche & Tralin (*Directoire*, *Exercices*, *Avis*). Tier A, modernized.
- Structure: many short items (a few lines to ~40 pages). The major ones: *Déclaration mystique sur le Cantique des Cantiques* (XXVI), *Directoire spirituel* and *Constitutions* of the Visitation (XXV), *Exercices spirituels* of Padua (XXII), *Lettre sur la prédication* (in XXVI; also published separately), the *Similitudes* notebooks (XXVI). A large share of XXIII–XXIV is Latin legal and administrative paperwork (ordinances, patents, synodal statutes, *Codex Fabrianus* notes): import last, or as an appendix.

## Recommendation

**Layout:** `content/books/sales-opera-omnia/<work>/`, mirroring `aquinas-opera-omnia/`:
- `controverses/`, `estendart-de-la-croix/`, `traite-amour-de-dieu/`, `entretiens/`, `sermons/` (one chapter per sermon, grouped autographes/recueillis), `lettres/` (one chapter per Annecy tome or per year; one file per letter would make ~2,100 files, so group by year), `opuscules/` (by Annecy series).
- The existing `sales-introduction-to-the-devout-life` should get a `fr-FR/` (from Wikisource Boulenger) rather than be duplicated. Either keep it in place and link it from the opera-omnia collection, or move it in.

**Import order:**
1. **Introduction: add French** (Wikisource Boulenger; clean, aligned to the existing chapters).
2. **Traité de l'amour de Dieu**: French from Annecy IV–V (donboscosanto OCR, checked against JesusMarie's modernized HTML); English Mackey from CCEL.
3. **Entretiens**: Annecy VI + Gasquet/Mackey 1906.
4. **Controverses**: Annecy I + Mackey 1886.
5. **Letters**: JesusMarie Annecy transcription (1.09M words), with Mackey's two volumes mapped to letter numbers where they overlap. Large job: much of the en-US must come from our own translation, or the book stays fr-FR + pt-BR.
6. **Sermons** and **Estendart**: French only (Annecy OCR); no PD English.
7. **Opuscules**: XXII–XXIV now; XXV–XXVI author text only, or after 2027/2028. The administrative documents go last.

**Cannot be sourced cleanly:**
- The Sermons, the Estendart and most of the opuscules have **no PD English**.
- Opuscules XXII–XXVI exist **only as PDF OCR** (donboscosanto; no archive.org or HTML), so they need a proofreading pass.
- Annecy XXV–XXVI apparatus: US status pending (see above).
- The JesusMarie texts are tier B (no licence stated) and, for the Traité and Entretiens, in modernized spelling. Use them as clean cross-check text against the Annecy OCR, not as the sole witness.

**Total size (est.):** French author text ≈ **2.75M words**: Controverses 110k, Estendart 110k, Introduction 98k, Traité 200k, Entretiens 140k, Sermons 450k, Lettres 1.09M, Opuscules 550k. Annecy raw incl. apparatus ≈ 4.9M. PD English ≈ **0.95M words** (Introduction 84k in corpus, Treatise 240k, Controversy 130k, Conferences 142k, Letters ~270k, Canticle 12k).

Downloads used for counting are in `research/sourcing/francis-de-sales/` (`dl/`: archive djvu texts a01–a21 and donboscosanto PDFs t22–t26; `web/`: JesusMarie pages; `en/`: archive English texts).

## Portuguese

Portuguese has three printed translations: the *Introduction*, the *Traité de l'amour de Dieu* and the Visitation *Constitutions*/*Directoire*. Only the first two have a text online, and only the *Introduction* has a tier-A text online. That text is the 1784 Lisbon version, an 18th-century scan with poor OCR. I found nothing in Portuguese for the *Controverses*, the *Estendart*, the *Entretiens*, the Sermons or the Letters.

Where I searched: archive.org (advancedsearch by title, creator and subject with `language:por`, plus full-text search); pt.wikisource (no author page for Francisco de Sales; search finds only passing mentions); the BNP digital library (bndigital.bnportugal.gov.pt, which serves purl.pt: nothing by Sales); Inocêncio's *Diccionario bibliographico portuguez* (tomos I–XXIII, to 1923, grepped in `francis-de-sales/pt/inocencio/`); and the Alexandria Católica blog. Domínio Público returned **403** to every request, so it is unverified. Google Books (API quota exhausted) and the web-search budget ran out before I could check the minor works or the BN Digital Brasil site. The Montfort site search returned no results (it seems to run on JavaScript). Obras Católicas now redirects to a publisher's shop.

Not works of Francis, so excluded: the *Pensamentos consoladores* (Huguet's compilation, on Alexandria Católica) and Camus's *Espírito e doutrina de São Francisco de Sales* (Porto 1950, also there). The two-volume *Vida de S. Francisco de Sales* (Lisboa, Ameno, 1794) is a biography.

**pt-BR reference:** for the *Introdução*, use **Castro/Vozes** (modern Brazilian, complete, clean A5 text) beside the 1784 Lisbon version, the only tier-A text, read for older wording. The existing `pt-BR/` in `sales-introduction-to-the-devout-life` is our own translation and can be checked against both. For the *Tratado*, use **Apostolado da Imprensa 1950**: complete, it follows the Annecy text, and it is the only full Portuguese version online. It is European Portuguese and tier B at best, so use it as a reference, not for import. The Paulus Risi Leme volume is a C reference for Books I–VI. Every other work must be translated from the French.
