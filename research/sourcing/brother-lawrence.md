## Bibliography

Brother Lawrence wrote little. All of his writings come down in two small books edited after his death by Abbé Joseph de Beaufort, vicar-general to Cardinal de Noailles:
1. *Maximes spirituelles fort utiles aux âmes pieuses pour acquérir la présence de Dieu, recueillies de quelques manuscrits du Frère Laurent de la Résurrection … avec l'abrégé de la vie de l'auteur et quelques lettres* (Paris/Châlons, 1692). Contents: Beaufort's *Éloge* (life), Lawrence's *Maximes* (in sections: Pratiques nécessaires pour acquérir la vie spirituelle; Comment il faut adorer Dieu en esprit et en vérité; De l'union de l'âme avec Dieu; De la présence de Dieu; Moyens pour acquérir la présence de Dieu; Les utilités de la présence de Dieu), and 16 letters.
2. *Les Mœurs et entretiens du frère Laurent de la Résurrection … avec la pratique de l'amour de Dieu* (Châlons, 1694). Contents: Beaufort's *Mœurs* (a portrait) and the 4 *Entretiens* (3 Aug 1666 – 25 Nov 1667), which are Beaufort's notes of his conversations with Lawrence.

The modern critical edition is Conrad De Meester, *Écrits et entretiens sur la pratique de la présence de Dieu* (Cerf, 1991; tier C). It adds a few fragments, among them the "Points" or "Pensées" (cf. Cerf 2026 *Pensées*). Sciurba's English (ICS, 1994) is also tier C. Beaufort's texts are not Lawrence's own writing, but they hold half of what is known of him (all of the *Entretiens* and the life) and every edition prints them, so import them as part of the book and credit them to Beaufort.

## Sources per work, ranked

### French (all four parts)

1. **archive.org `laurent-de-la-resurrection-la-pratique-de-la-presence-de-dieu`** (https://archive.org/details/laurent-de-la-resurrection-la-pratique-de-la-presence-de-dieu). Born-digital PDF with a `.txt`, ~23.9k words. It re-edits (Valdemar Teodoro Éditeur, Niterói, 2026, ed. E. L. de Souza Campos) the **1924 Paris, Librairie Fischbacher / Société chrétienne des Amis** edition. That edition reproduces "intégralement" the two original editions, from BnF copies of the *Mœurs et Entretiens* (Châlons 1693/94) and the *Maximes* (Paris 1692, BnF D 37601). Order: Préambule, Éloge, Mœurs et Entretiens, Entretiens I–IV, Maximes (6 sections), Lettres I–XVI, Table. Text quality is high: a digital transcription, not OCR. One caveat: the 1924 editors "ont dû y faire quelques coupures" in Beaufort's two prefaces (the Éloge and Mœurs). Lawrence's own texts are complete. Tier: the 1924 text is A; the 2026 re-edition carries "Toute copie et diffusion sont autorisées, à condition d'en citer la source", which is B for its added notes. **Best source.**
2. **Gallica / BnF originals**: *Maximes spirituelles* 1692 (BnF D 37601) and *Mœurs et entretiens* 1694. Gallica is blocked from this machine, so whether digitized copies exist is **unverified**. If found, they are scans only. Use them to restore the 1924 cuts in the Éloge/Mœurs and for an orthography check.
3. **livres-mystiques.com** https://livres-mystiques.com/partieTEXTES/Laurent/laurent.html. HTML, ~6k words. Taken from an undated booklet by "Les Veilleurs" (Protestant Third Order). The Entretiens follow the 1694 edition, but **the letters are a re-translation into French from English** (the page says so: "Le premier éditeur … les traduisit d'après une version anglaise"). **Do not use the letters.** The Entretiens can serve as a cross-check.
4. Avoid **archive.org `frere-laurent-de-la-resurrection-la-pratique-de-la-presence-de-dieu`**, an "édition retravaillée … réalisée par intelligence artificielle" (voirjesus.com). It is modernized paraphrase.

### English

1. **Revell, New York [c. 1895]**, *The Practice of the Presence of God, the best rule of a holy life: being conversations and letters of Nicholas Herman of Lorraine*, anonymous translator. archive.org `brotherlawrencep00lawr` (~12k words of OCR; 4 conversations + 15 letters). Also `practiceofpresen00lawriala` (Revell, 63 pp.) and DLI `in.ernet.dli.2015.209844`. Tier A. This is the standard 19th-century translation that most online copies derive from. **Best for the Entretiens + Lettres.**
2. **H. R. Allenson, London 1906**, *The Practice of the Presence of God, being the conversations and letters …*, "new and revised edition", with the letters "arranged in chronological order" and an "additional letter". archive.org `ThePracticeOfThePresenceOfGod` (~15k words). Tier A. Its letter order differs from the French numbering; map each letter by date.
3. **H. R. Allenson, London 1907**, *The Spiritual Maxims of Brother Lawrence, together with The Character by the chronicler of the "Conversations" and Gathered Thoughts*, "newly translated". archive.org `spiritualmaximso01lawr` (~11k words). Tier A. **The only PD English of the *Maximes*.** "The Character" is the *Mœurs*. "Gathered Thoughts" are extracts.
4. Gutenberg #5657 / CCEL: Gutenberg is a "2002 Edition edited by Lightheart, Copyright (C) 2002", a modernized edit, so avoid it as a witness. CCEL https://ccel.org/ccel/lawrence/practice gives no print basis (its plain-text cache returned empty here), so the edition is unverified.
5. 18th-century English (*The Life of Nicolas Herman*, 1727; *The Great Advantages…*, Edinburgh 1741; archive.org `bim_eighteenth-century_*`): tier A, but ECCO OCR is poor. Historical interest only. The 1741 Edinburgh volume is the only English before 1907 that contains the *Maximes*.
6. Donald Attwater (Burns Oates 1926), A by the pre-1930 rule, no open text found. Sciurba (ICS 1994) and modern translations: C.

## Recommendation

- **Layout**: a single book `lawrence-practice-presence-of-god`, since the whole oeuvre is one small volume. Other authors use `*-opera-omnia/` for a complete set; for Lawrence one book is the complete set. Use fr-FR from archive.org item 1, and en-US combined from Revell c. 1895 (Entretiens + Lettres) and Allenson 1907 (Maximes + Mœurs). There is **no PD English of the *Éloge***; pt-BR from the French covers it, and en-US can mark the *Éloge* as untranslated or be translated from the French.
- **Import first**: this whole book. It is small (~24k words) and clean.
- **Caveats**: restore the 1924 cuts in the Éloge/Mœurs from a Gallica scan if one turns up. Map the letter numbering by date across the three English editions.

**Total size**: French ~24k words (Lawrence's own ~16k; Beaufort ~8k). English ~15k.
