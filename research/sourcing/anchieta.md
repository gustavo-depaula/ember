# St. José de Anchieta SJ (1534–1597), Apostle of Brazil: sourcing report

Bibliography. Anchieta wrote in four languages: Latin, Spanish (his mother tongue; born in Tenerife), Portuguese and Old Tupi (*língua brasílica*). The bibliography used here is the one printed in the Academia Brasileira de Letras edition of 1933 (pp. 27–31; extracted from Sommervogel I 310–312, VIII 1631 and Rivière's *Corrections et additions*, with Sotwel's 1676 list of his works), checked against the contents of that edition and of the 1954 critical edition of the poems. Sotwel's list: (1) *Ars grammatica linguae Brasilicae* (printed); (2) *Dictionarium* of the same language (lost / not securely identified); (3) *Doctrina christiana pleniorque catechismus* in Tupi; (4) *Dialogi de religionis mysteriis*; (5) *Institutio ad interrogandos inter confessionem paenitentes*; (6) *Syntagma monitorum ad iuvandos moribundos*; (7) *Cantiones sacrae* in Latin, Portuguese, Spanish and Tupi; (8) *Drama ad extirpanda Brasiliae vitia*; (9) *Poema de B. Virginis vita* (elegiac, "ad bis mille et octoginta disticha"); (10) a *Brasilica Societatis historia et vita clarorum patrum* (survives as the *Fragmentos históricos*). To these: *De gestis Mendi de Saa* (Latin epic, Coimbra 1563, anonymous), the letters, the *Informações*, and two sermons.

The modern complete edition is the Loyola *Obras completas* (Pe. Armando Cardoso SJ et al., São Paulo, 1975– ; ~13 vols: *Poema da Virgem*, *De gestis*, *Lírica portuguesa e tupi*, *Lírica espanhola*, *Teatro*, *Cartas*, *Doutrina cristã* I–II, *Diálogo da fé*, *Arte de gramática*, *Textos históricos*, *Sermões*…) — **tier C** (Cardoso d. 2002; publisher active). Pt.wikisource removed Cardoso's verse translation of the *Poema da Virgem* in 2020 for copyright (notice on `pt.wikisource.org/wiki/Anexo:Poema_da_Virgem`), which confirms the C tier for his translations. Serafim Leite's *Monumenta Brasiliae* I–V (Rome, MHSI, 1956–68; the letters in their original Latin/Spanish/Portuguese) is also C (Leite d. 1969).

Not in the corpus (`_corpus-books.txt` and `content/books/` checked for "anchieta": no hit).

## Notes per work, sources ranked

### Cartas (letters)
The ABL 1933 edition numbers 40 pieces: I–XIX letters 1554–1570 (quadrimestres of 1554 and 1556, letters to Loyola 1554 and 1555, to the General Lainez 1561, 1562, 1563, 1565, to Diogo Mirão 1565, to Borja 1570), XX a letter "toda de palavras da Sagrada Escritura" to a newly ordained priest, XXI–XXVI later letters (Gaspar Schetz, Antwerp, 1578; Capt. Jerónimo Leitão 1579; Antonio Ribeiro 1587; Francisco de Escalante 1587, 1591, s.d.; Capt. Miguel de Azevedo 1592; Aquaviva 1594), then XXVII–XXXVI the *Informações* and XXXVII–XXXVIII the sermons. Two of the longest: the 1560 letter on the natural history of São Vicente (X) and the 1565 letter on the Iperoig mission (XV). The originals are Latin (the early quadrimestres and letters to the General), Spanish (many) and Portuguese; the ABL prints Portuguese throughout, reusing 19th-century translations (Teixeira de Melo, Annaes da BN 1876–77; Cônego Januário da Cunha Barbosa, RIHGB II–III, 1840–41; João Vieira de Almeida, *Cartas inéditas*, São Paulo 1900) and Afrânio Peixoto's own.
Original language, ranked:
1. **Latin 1560 letter: *Epistola quamplurimarum rerum naturalium…*, ed. Diogo de Toledo Lara Ordonhes, Lisbon, Academia Real das Ciências, 1799** — BBM https://digital.bbm.usp.br/handle/bbm/4688 (PDF with text layer, 15,100 words incl. notes; long-s OCR, fair). A. Also in *Collecção de notícias para a história… das nações ultramarinas* I (1812), BBM `bbm/5379`.
2. **Beretario, *Josephi Anchietae… vita*, Lyon/Cologne 1617** — Latin texts of five later letters (to Ribeiro, three to Escalante, extract to Ignatius Tolosa); Spanish version *Vida del Padre Ioseph de Ancheta*, 1618, BBM `bbm/4673`. A, scans; not counted.
3. 16th-century Spanish/Italian prints (*Copia de unas cartas…*, Lisbon/Barcelona 1555–56; *Diversi avisi*, Venice 1559/1562/1565) — A, early prints, not located online except the BBM 1551 Nóbrega volume.
4. *Monumenta Brasiliae* (Leite, 1956–68) — the only complete original-language edition. C.
Portuguese (see also `## Portuguese`): ABL 1933 is the base (A; see tier note there).
English: no translation found, PD or otherwise, of the letters as a corpus (searched archive.org, web). Single letters appear in modern anthologies (C).

### De Beata Virgine Dei Matre Maria (Poema da Virgem)
Latin elegiac distichs on the life of Mary, composed 1563 as a hostage at Iperoig (tradition: written on the sand, memorised). Divided into five books in Cardoso's edition (Vasconcelos prints it continuously with section titles); followed in Vasconcelos by a short Office of the Immaculate Conception in sapphics.
1. **Simão de Vasconcelos, *Vida do venerável padre Joseph de Anchieta*, Lisbon, João da Costa, 1672** ("Poema em louvor da Virgem N. Senhora… R. P. Iosephi de Anchieta carmen de B. Virgine Maria", pp. ≈ 443–592 with running head "Versos do P. Ioseph de Anchieta") — archive `vidadovenerauelp00vasc` (JCB copy), BBM `bbm/5156`. A. 38,700 OCR words for poem + office, but the OCR is poor (long s read as *f*, ligatures, broken lines): usable only as a scan; needs fresh OCR/proofreading.
2. **Vasconcelos, *Chronica da Companhia de Jesu do estado do Brasil*, 1663** (`chronicadacompan02vasc`) announces the poem; the Latin text is in the 1672 *Vida*. The 1865 reprint of the *Chronica* (`chronicadacompan00vasc`, `chronicadacompan01vasc`) does not carry it.
3. *Poema Marianum autore ven. P. Josepho de Anchieta*, Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Vicente Bonnet, 1887 (176 pp.) — A, **not located online** (searched archive.org, ULPGC Memoria Digital de Canarias, web).
4. Pt.wikisource / la.wikisource carry only the excerpt *De compassione et planctu Virginis in morte Filii* (≈ 900 words; https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/De_compassione_et_planctu_Virginis_in_morte_filii, from literaturabrasileira.ufsc.br, 25% proofread).
5. Cardoso, Latin–Portuguese, Rio, Arquivo Nacional, 1940; Loyola *Obras completas* 1 (1980, 1988…) — C.
English: none found.

### De gestis Mendi de Saa
Latin epic (≈ 3,000 hexameters) on Governor Mem de Sá's wars (1557–1560), printed anonymously in Coimbra, 1563 — the first poem printed about the Americas by a resident. Attribution to Anchieta accepted since the 1930s (Cardoso), but contested earlier.
1. **Princeps, Coimbra, João Álvares, 1563** — BNP holds a copy (catalogue record "Excellentissimo, singularisque fidei ac pietatis viro Mendo de Saa…"); a public digitization was **not found** (BNP digital and purl.pt searched by title and by "Mendo de Saa"). A if found.
2. Fundação Biblioteca Nacional facsimile, Rio 1997 (intro P. R. Pereira) — facsimile of a PD print; the volume is C for its apparatus; not online.
3. Cardoso, Latin–Portuguese, Arquivo Nacional 1958; Loyola 1970 — C. La.wikisource once had a page "De Gestis Mendi de Saa" (linked from the pt author page); it no longer exists (API: missing).
English: none. **Gap: no clean, free Latin text located.**

### Poesias and autos (theatre)
The core is Anchieta's own *caderno* (ARSI, Rome, Opp. NN. 24), 86 pieces in the 1954 numbering: Portuguese poems (*Do Santíssimo Sacramento*, *De São Maurício*, *O pelote domingueiro*, *Carta da Companhia de Jesus para o seráfico São Francisco*, *Ao P. Bartolomeu Simões Pereira*…), Spanish poems (*El pelote*, *Jesús y el pecador*, *Del niño Jesús y de su madre*, *Diálogo de Cristo con Pero Dias*, *A Inácio de Azevedo*, *Sobre el ciego amor*, *Na visitação de Santa Isabel*…), Latin lyrics, and Tupi songs (*Cantiga por o sem ventura*, *Dos mistérios do rosário*, *Tupansý Santa Maria*…). The autos mix all four languages: *Na festa de São Lourenço* (1583/87, ≈ 1,500 lines, the longest), *Na vila de Vitória* (*São Maurício*), *Na visitação de Santa Isabel*, *Recebimento que fizeram os índios de Guaraparim ao P. Provincial Marçal Beliarte*, *Na aldeia de Guaraparim*, *Dia da Assunção, quando levaram sua imagem a Reritiba*, *Quando no Espírito Santo se recebeu uma relíquia das Onze Mil Virgens*, *Na festa de Natal*, *Dança que se fez na procissão de São Lourenço*, *Diálogo de Cristo com Pero Dias*, and others. The anonymous *Auto da pregação universal* is attributed (lost).
Original, ranked:
1. **M. de L. de Paula Martins, *Poesias: manuscrito do séc. XVI, em português, castelhano, latim e tupi*, São Paulo, Museu Paulista (Boletim IV), 1954, 905 pp.** — part 1 diplomatic reproduction of the whole *caderno*, part 2 critical text grouped by language, with Portuguese translations of the Spanish, Latin and Tupi. archive.org copies `poesias-pesquisavel-manuscrito-do-sec.-xvi-em-port.-castelhano-latim-e-tupi.-tra` (142,500 OCR words, fair) and `anchieta-poesisas` (135,900). **C**: the BNP's own digitization (purl.pt/6596) is marked "COPYRIGHTED — Obra protegida por Direito de Autor"; Paula Martins was still alive in 1989 (BnF authority record). The archive.org copies are user uploads without rights statement. ### Arte de gramática da língua mais usada na costa do Brasil

1. **pt.wikisource, transcription of the 1595 princeps** (main + *Licença* + chapters I–XVI as subpages), 27,100 words counted (rendered text incl. navigation; ch. VII alone 7,000). A/CC BY-SA. A modernised-spelling version (`Arte de gramática da língua mais usada na costa do Brasil`) covers only ch. I–VI ("incompleto").
2. **Julius Platzmann, facsimile reprint, Leipzig, Teubner, 1874** — archive `artedegrammatica00anch` (LoC, 26,300 OCR words incl. Platzmann's Spanish prolegomena) and `artedegrammatic00anchgoog` (23,600). A. Cross-check.
3. **Princeps 1595**, BBM https://digital.bbm.usp.br/handle/bbm/4674 — A; scan.
4. Imprensa Nacional reprint 1933 (BNP catalogue) — A text; not online. Cardoso's *Leitura da Gramática* (1990, on archive.org as a user upload) — C.
Structure: one file per chapter. Content is linguistic, not devotional: low priority for a prayer app but needed for completeness.

### Tupi catechetical works (Doutrina, Diálogo da fé, confessionário, aids for the dying)
Anchieta's Tupi catechism, dialogues and confession aids circulated in manuscript; his autographs (ARSI) were published only by Cardoso (*Doutrina cristã* I–II, 1977; *Diálogo da fé*, 1988) — C.
1. **Antônio de Araújo SJ, *Catecismo na lingoa brasilica… composto a modo de dialogos por Padres doctos & bons lingoas da Companhia de Iesu*, Lisbon, Pedro Crasbeeck, 1618** — archive `catecismo-na-lingoa-brasilica-1618` (Public Domain Mark), 47,300 OCR words (17th-c. type, Tupi + Portuguese). 4. *Vocabulário na língua brasílica* (1621 ms., traditionally linked to Anchieta, more likely Leonardo do Vale) — Ayrosa 1938 / Drummond 1952–53 (archive `vlb-drumond`) — C, doubtful attribution; out of scope.

## Portuguese
Portuguese is one of Anchieta's own languages, so the Portuguese works (letters written in Portuguese, the *Informações*, *Fragmentos*, sermons, Portuguese poems and the *Arte*) need no translation; the ABL translations cover the Latin and Spanish letters.
- **ABL 1933, *Cartas, informações, fragmentos históricos e sermões*** — BNP https://purl.pt/155 ("cópia pública"), mirrored at archive.org `r-19794-v_0000_grav-569_t24-C-R0150` with the Public Domain Mark; 223,500 OCR words (incl. Capistrano's essay, Peixoto's introduction, bibliography and ≈ 650 endnotes); OCR good (sample of letter XIII clean). Tier A: published 1933 but every contributor died 70+ years ago (Capistrano de Abreu d. 1927, Alcântara Machado d. 1941, Afrânio Peixoto d. 1947), and its translations of Latin/Spanish letters reuse pre-1930 publications (Teixeira de Melo 1877, Cunha Barbosa 1840–41, Vieira de Almeida 1900); BNP treats the digitization as public. ## Recommendation

Layout: `anchieta-opera-omnia/<work>`. Canonical language per work: Portuguese for the Portuguese works, `la/` for the Latin epics, `es-ES/` for the Spanish poems; Tupi needs a language code decision (ISO 639-3 `tpw`, Old Tupi) — **open decision**. Ember's pt-BR slot can carry the 16th-century Portuguese originals with modernised spelling (as was done for other pt originals), or keep a separate `pt-PT` original: **open decision**.
- `…/cartas` — **import first**: ABL 1933 (A, good OCR, complete in Portuguese), one file per letter (≈ 26) with the numbering of the ABL; Peixoto's/Alcântara Machado's notes optional. Where a Latin original is free (1560 *Epistola*, BBM 1799), add `la/` for that letter.
- `…/informacoes` and `…/fragmentos-historicos` — ABL 1933, cross-checked against Capistrano 1886 (BBM).
- `…/sermoes` — ABL 1933 + BBM 1895.
- `…/arte-de-gramatica` — pt.wikisource (1595), checked against Platzmann 1874.
- `…/de-beata-virgine` — Latin from Vasconcelos 1672 needs fresh OCR of the scan (long s; ≈ 150 pages) and proofreading; pt-BR and English by the pipeline (no free translation).
- `…/poesias` and `…/teatro` — **blocked on a rights decision**: the only complete text is Paula Martins 1954 (C). Free partials (Melo Morais Filho, Rev. Exposição 1882, Gutenberg #7384) give perhaps a fifth of the corpus. Options: (a) treat her diplomatic transcription of the 16th-c. manuscript as unprotected and use only that part; (b) request the ARSI manuscript images and transcribe; (c) defer.
- `…/de-gestis-mendi-de-saa` — blocked until the 1563 print is found digitized (BNP/BNE/other); then OCR.
- `…/catecismo-brasilico` — Araújo 1618 as a doubtful/composite Anchieta-school text, last.

Cannot be sourced cleanly: any English translation of any work; a free Latin text of *De gestis*; the complete poems and plays (C only); Anchieta's own Tupi doctrinal autographs (Cardoso, C); free Portuguese translations of the two Latin epics.

Total size (Anchieta's own text, estimates unless marked): letters ≈ 75,000; *Informações* + *Fragmentos* ≈ 48,000; sermons ≈ 10,000; *Arte* ≈ 25,000 (27,100 counted incl. navigation); *De Beata Virgine* ≈ 30,000; *De gestis* ≈ 18,000; poems + autos ≈ 45,000; Tupi catechism (composite) ≈ 47,000 (counted OCR). ≈ 250,000 words without the catechism, ≈ 300,000 with it. Freely sourceable today: ≈ 190,000 (letters, informações, fragmentos, sermons, *Arte*, *De Beata Virgine* as scan). English PD: 0.

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/anchieta/` — `ia/` (archive.org `_djvu.txt`), `bbm/` (BBM PDFs + `pdftotext`), `ws/` (wikitext). Word counts are whitespace tokens of those files.
