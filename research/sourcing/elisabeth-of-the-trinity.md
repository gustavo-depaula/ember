Copyright: she died in 1906, so all her text is in the public domain. The Carmel of Dijon's *Souvenirs* (1909, compiled by Mother Germaine de Saint-Seine) and its PD English translation (1913) carry DR, the elevation, CF (in English), extracts of letters, and some poems. These texts are lightly edited by the Carmel, as with Thérèse's 1898 recension.

**In the corpus?** No. Nothing by or about her in `content/books/`.

## Notes per work, with ranked sources

### *Souvenirs* / *The Praise of Glory*

The *Souvenirs* is the Dijon Carmel's book: a 17-chapter biography quoting her letters, journal and notes at length, plus an appendix of her own texts. It is the only PD vehicle for most of her writing in both languages, and the most practical import, a bilingual pair with aligned chapters.

French:
1. **archive.org/details/soeurlisabethd00li**: *Sœur Élisabeth de la Trinité, religieuse carmélite, 1880–1906 : Souvenirs*, Dijon, Imp. Jobard, 1913 (University of Ottawa copy). `_djvu.txt` is 96.5k words.
   - OCR is fair: correct accents, double spaces between words, running heads ("DERNIÈRE RETRAITE DE LAUDEM GLORIÆ 271") to strip, some garbling in Latin.
   - Appendix: Dernière retraite p. 269, Pieuse élévation p. 305, Lettres p. 307, Poésies p. 335, Lettre de l'abbé D… p. 358.
   - **This printing lacks CF.**
2. No second French witness found. Gallica and HathiTrust were behind Cloudflare challenges from here. Search them by hand for other *Souvenirs* editions (the "sixième édition augmentée" of about 1912 is the source of the English and includes CF).

English:
1. **archive.org/details/thepraiseofglory00elizuoft**: *The Praise of Glory: Reminiscences of Sister Elizabeth of the Trinity*, "authorized translation by the Benedictines of Stanbrook from the sixth and enlarged edition of the French", introduction by Benedict Zimmerman OCD; imprimatur Westminster, 10 Oct 1912. Published 1913 (this scan dated 1914).
   - 99.9k words; OCR fair.
   - Appendix: Last Retreat p. 227, Prayer p. 260, letters to a friend, to M. le Chanoine A., to Madame —, to someone who wished to become a Carmelite, fragments (pp. 261–284), Letter from the abbé D. p. 285, and **"A Second Retreat… Heaven on Earth"** p. 289 (= *Le Ciel dans la foi*).
2. **archive.org/details/praiseofgloryrem00eliziala**: another scan of the same book (95.9k words). Use it to cross-check the OCR.

Later English versions (*Reminiscences*, revised, 1952; Philipon's *Spiritual Writings*, 1962; *The Spiritual Doctrine of Sister Elizabeth*, 1947) are restricted lending scans and post-1930: tier C or unverified. ICS *Complete Works* is C.

### Dernière retraite

Structure: 16 days ("Premier jour" …), each a short meditation. The 1913 French gives it in full as the Carmel printed it. De Meester notes that early editions altered Elisabeth's wording slightly; the OC restores the autograph.

### Ô mon Dieu, Trinité que j'adore

Found in *Souvenirs* 1913 (as "Pieuse élévation"), with its English in *Praise of Glory* p. 260. This short text is the most valuable piece for the app. Recommendation: import it as a **practice** (a prayer with an inline flow) rather than a book chapter, with fr from *Souvenirs* 1913 and en from Stanbrook 1913.

Cross-check the French with the many devotional reproductions online; they follow the autograph.

### Le Ciel dans la foi

English only (Stanbrook 1913, "Heaven on Earth", 4.0k words). The English is divided into "days", which suggests the Carmel's early arrangement rather than De Meester's 44 numbered §§.
- I found no French digital text: not on archive.org, not on Wikisource, and web searches for its opening phrases returned only quotations.
- The text itself is PD. A French source needs a scan of a pre-1930 *Souvenirs* edition that includes it (6th edition or later).

### Lettres, Poèmes, Journal, Notes intimes

There is **no complete PD edition, in any language**. The full corpus (342 letters, 123 poems, the Journal) first appeared in De Meester's 1979–1991 editions (Cerf). The texts are PD, but the only complete transcriptions are in that copyrighted edition, and nothing is online.

What is usable is the selection quoted in *Souvenirs* and *Praise of Glory*. These are often excerpts, not whole letters, so don't present them as "the letters".

## Recommendation

- Layout: `content/books/elisabeth-trinite-souvenirs/` (the Dijon *Souvenirs*: biography chapters I–XVII + appendix). fr from the 1913 OCR, en from Stanbrook 1913/14 (two scans to cross-check), both tier A.
- Add a **practice** for "Ô mon Dieu, Trinité que j'adore".
- Later, an `elisabeth-opera-omnia/` with `derniere-retraite` and `ciel-dans-la-foi` as standalone works, once a French witness of CF is found.
- Import first: the elevation practice, then *Souvenirs*/*Praise of Glory*.

Can't be sourced cleanly:
- *Le Ciel dans la foi* in French.
- The complete letters, poems, Journal and Notes intimes (they exist only in the Cerf/ICS critical editions, tier C as editions).
- The OCR of both 1913 books needs real cleanup: running heads, hyphenation, double spacing.

**Total size:** sourceable now is about **96k words French / 100k English** (the *Souvenirs* pair, of which about 25k is Elisabeth's own text and the rest the Carmel's narrative). The complete works (OC) are about 200k words (estimate), most of them unsourceable in clean PD form.

Raw downloads are in `research/sourcing/elisabeth-of-the-trinity/raw/`.

### Single works and anthologies
