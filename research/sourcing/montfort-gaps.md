# St. Louis-Marie Grignion de Montfort (1673–1716): completeness check

The corpus holds 7 Montfort books, all fr-FR + en-US + pt-BR, with French from livres-mystiques.com:
`montfort-love-wisdom` (L'Amour de la Sagesse éternelle), `montfort-friends-cross` (Lettre circulaire aux Amis de la Croix), `montfort-secret-rosary` (Le Secret admirable du très saint Rosaire), `montfort-secret-mary` (Le Secret de Marie), `montfort-true-devotion` (Traité de la vraie dévotion), `montfort-ardent-prayer` (Prière embrasée), `montfort-missionary-rules` (Règles des prêtres missionnaires de la Compagnie de Marie).

The reference list of his writings is the critical *Œuvres complètes* (Paris: Seuil, 1966), whose order the English *God Alone: The Collected Writings of St. Louis Mary de Montfort* (Montfort Publications, 1987) follows. The Montfort Missionaries' site reproduces that list (https://montfort.org.uk/Writings.php and https://montfort.org.uk/Writings/MontWork.php). **The Seuil French and the God Alone English are both tier C.** montfort.org.uk says: "These texts are copyright © The Montfort Missionaries 1987. They may be used for private study only, and may not be reproduced or published in any way whatever, without permission." EWTN's copies carry "Provided courtesy of the Montfort Fathers © All Rights Reserved."

## Notes

- **Biggest gaps by size**: the *Cantiques* (~150k words, a large share of Montfort's total output) and the *Lettres*. Neither has a clean PD transcription online. The best route for the Cantiques is a scan of Fradet (1929–32) or of an 18th/19th-c. *Cantiques des missions* edition (e.g. Google Books `X41osA3zXdwC`, 1779, unverified as Montfort's). Gallica was blocked by Cloudflare from this machine, and should be searched by hand for "Cantiques des missions" / "Grignion de Montfort".
- **Gap inside an existing book**: `montfort-secret-rosary` lacks the *Méthodes pour réciter le Rosaire*, which Montfort appended to *Le Secret admirable* (§154 announces them). Adding them needs a PD French edition of *Le Secret admirable* that prints the methods (any 1911–1930 Oudin/Mariale edition; not yet located online).
- **The 1870 *Life and Select Writings***, Derby: Richardson, preface by Faber (archive.org `lifeselectwritin0000secu`, open, ~154k words). Tier A. It contains the *Circular Letter to the Friends of the Cross*, *Montfort's Prayer for the Missionaries* (Prière embrasée), "Montfort's Discourse to the Missionaries of the Holy Ghost" (identity unverified; possibly the Letter to the Company of Mary or a sermon), *The Secret of Mary*, the *Tree of Life*, *Prayer to Jesus*, *Prayer to Mary*, *Offering of our actions*, plus letters and hymn stanzas quoted in the Life. It can supply **PD English** for some short pieces where our en-US now comes from elsewhere, and for letter excerpts.
- The corpus's existing French all comes from livres-mystiques.com. That site's Montfort page also links a dead mirror (`www3.sympatico.ca/a.janos/Montfort/`).
- Every minor writing listed (Lettre aux associés, Croix de Poitiers, Règle des Filles de la Sagesse, Maximes de la Sagesse, Montbernage, Règlements, Contrat d'alliance, Prières, Cahier, Sermons, Dispositions) is **tier C in every edition found**. No PD French transcription exists online. The 18th–19th-c. lives (Grandet 1724, Pauvert 1875, Fonteneau 1887, Quérard 1898 *La mission providentielle…*, archive.org `cihm_12319`) quote some of them. Those lives are PD scans and the only legitimate source; the texts would have to be extracted from them piecemeal.

## Recommendation

1. **Fix the existing gap first**: add the *Méthodes pour réciter le Rosaire* to `montfort-secret-rosary` once a PD French edition is found (search Gallica/Google Books for *Le Secret admirable du très saint Rosaire*, 1911 onward). The en-US would need a PD translation or our own.
2. **Lettres**: a new `montfort-letters` book, with French extracted from Pauvert 1875 (archive.org `vieduvnrable00pauv`, open djvu text) and cross-checked with Fonteneau 1887. A letter-by-letter concordance against the Seuil numbering is needed (Seuil itself is C; use it only for numbering/identification, not text). English: our own translation, since God Alone is C.
3. **Cantiques**: medium-term. Start a `montfort-cantiques` book from the 1904 Ottawa selection (`cihm_77872`) plus the Wikisource anthology pieces, and aim to complete it from a Fradet scan.
4. The remaining minor writings are small and **cannot be sourced cleanly** (tier C only) unless a 19th-c. life prints them. Skip them for now.

**Total missing (est.)**: ~250k words, dominated by the Cantiques (~150k). About 45k of that (letters, testament, some cantiques) is reachable now through PD scans. The rest is tier C or not found.

Raw downloads: `research/sourcing/montfort-gaps/`.

### The complete works in Portuguese (covers every missing work)

1. **São Luís Maria Grignion de Montfort, *Obras completas*, tr. Pe. José Raimundo Vidigal, CSsR and Pe. Manuel Ramos Nunes Vieira, SMM; rev. Pe. Amílcar Tavares, SMM; historical introductions by Pe. Battista Cortinovis.** São Paulo: Paulus / Portugal: Edições Monfortinas, 2021 (Clássicos do Cristianismo 10). ISBN 978-65-5562-267-6 (e-ISBN 978-65-5562-393-2), 1594 pp.
   - Store: https://loja.paulus.com.br/obras-completas-vol-10-luxo/p. Sample with CIP and preface: https://deg.paulus.com.br/6535.pdf (saved as `pt/paulus-obras-completas-amostra.pdf`).
   - Publisher's description: "todos os escritos … a totalidade das cartas, dos sermões e dos cânticos do santo". The preface calls it "a edição completa em língua portuguesa", and it follows the order of the Œuvres complètes / God Alone. Its numbering cites cantiques as "Cântico 5, 25". The *Tratado* text "teve também o contributo da tradução da editora Caminhos Romanos, edição 2015".
   - **Tier C**: "© PAULUS – 2021".
   - **The one Portuguese source for the letters, the Cantiques, the Rules (Filhas da Sabedoria, Companhia de Maria), the Rosary methods, the Cahier de notes, the Livro dos sermões, the Testament, the Contrato de aliança, etc.** Reference only. Its structure is the best map for our own pt-BR of these works, including the Cantiques' numbering and verse division.
   - Edições Monfortinas (Fátima) lists the same *Obras Completas* at http://monfortinos.pt/obras/ (no translator or price given; a "Ler" link that could not be resolved). Its http://monfortinos.pt/escritos/ page summarises the works and says 164 cantiques of ~23,000 verses survive. It gives no texts and no rights statement beyond the site credit.

### Méthodes pour réciter le Rosaire (the gap inside `montfort-secret-rosary`)

1. **Luís Boavida Araújo (tr.), *O Segredo Admirável do Santíssimo Rosário*, 1st ed., Alpha-Ômega, 2026** (European Portuguese spelling: "actualmente", "distracções"; Bible quotations from Matos Soares). archive.org `sao-luis-maria-grignion-de-monfort-o-segredo-admiravel-do-santissimo-rosario`, PDF + OCR (~34k words; `pt/segredo.txt`).
   - **It includes the methods**: "50ª Rosa: Métodos santos para rezar o Santo Rosário…", §154 "eis aqui vários métodos…", the "1º Método" with the general offering (§155) and the offering of each decade (§156 ff.), and the "Método mais abreviado" (§160, with the Latin clausulae in the notes).
   - That confirms that the French edition it was made from prints the methods. Its title page "Le Secret Admirable du Très Saint Rosaire pour se convertir et se sauver" is the post-1911 title, so a 20th-c. French edition with the methods exists to look for.
   - **Tier unverified, probably C**: a 2026 first edition, no licence stated, posted by a third party.
   - Reference only. It fixes the paragraph range the corpus is missing (§154–§160+), and is an EP witness for wording.
2. **Paulus, *O Segredo Admirável do Santíssimo Rosário: para se converter e se salvar*** (240 pp., https://loja.paulus.com.br/o-segredo-admiravel-do-santissimo-rosario-para-se-converter-e-se-salvar/p) and the *Obras completas* above. **Tier C.** Translator not confirmed from here. Likely includes the methods (unverified).
3. **"Rosário meditado (São Luís Maria Grignion de Montfort)"**, archive.org `rosario-meditado-sao-luis-maria-g.-de-montfort` (from leiturascatolicas.com; ~2k words).
   - A leaflet of the Montfortian method with one intention per Ave Maria ("Ave-Maria para honrar: …"): one of the Rosary methods.
   - Scan OCR is poor, and no translator or source is given. The archive item carries a Public Domain Mark (set by the uploader), so tier unverified.
   - Reference only.
4. **"Método para rezar com fruto o Santo Rosário, segundo São Luís Maria Grignion de Montfort"** (Legião de São Miguel Arcanjo), archive.org `rosario-sob-sao-luis-de-montford`. A modern devotional adaptation (offering + Credo + per-mystery prayers), not Montfort's text verbatim. Tier unverified. Lowest rank.

**Recommendation.** No Portuguese text for the gaps is importable: everything is tier C or unverified. Our pt-BR must come from the pipeline once a PD French text is sourced. Buy or borrow the Paulus *Obras completas* (2021) as the reference: it is the only Portuguese source for the letters and cantiques, and it gives a ready concordance to the Seuil numbering. It also proves that a French edition titled *…pour se convertir et se sauver* prints the methods; that edition is the PD French to hunt for.
