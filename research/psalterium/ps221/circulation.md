# Canticle 221 (Isaiah 12:1–6, Vulgate) — Brazilian circulation

Checked 2026-09-23 on the web. Nothing here is from memory. Where a source is only secondary, it says so.

## Liturgia das Horas (CNBB) — Thursday Lauds, Week II

Source: Paulus Editora, *Liturgia Diária das Horas*, "Dia 1º – Quinta-feira" (https://www.paulus.com.br/portal/liturgia-diaria-das-horas/dia-1o-quinta-feira-12/). Saved as `consult/ps221/lh-paulus-quinta.html` (gitignored). Paulus prints the CNBB text. The canticle stands between Ps 79(80) and Ps 80(81), which is Thursday Lauds of Week II. A web search showed the same canticle on liturgiadashoras.online for Thursday Lauds in several seasons. That site refused a scripted download (Mod_Security), so it was not saved.

Heading: **Cântico Is 12,1-6 – Exultação do povo redimido.** Antiphon: *Publicai em toda a terra as maravilhas do Senhor!*

Points of contact and departure, in short fragments only (the full text is in the saved page):

| DO | CNBB LH (fragment) |
| --- | --- |
| 12:1 | *Dou-vos graças, ó Senhor* · concessive *porque, estando irritado* · *me consolastes* |
| 12:2–3 | *Eis o Deus, meu Salvador* · *minha força, meu louvor* |
| 12:4 | *Com alegria bebereis* · *manancial da salvação* · *naquele dia* |
| 12:5 | *invocai seu santo nome* · *entre os povos* · *maravilhas* |
| 12:6 | *em toda a terra* |
| 12:7 | *habitantes de Sião* · *em vosso meio* |

The CNBB text is a metrical paraphrase, translated from the Hebrew or the Nova Vulgata rather than from this Latin. It follows the Hebrew and the Nova Vulgata in several places: *manancial da salvação* (Heb. *ha-yeshu'ah*) where Jerome has *Salvatóris*, *maravilhas* for *adinventiónes*, *habitantes* (plural) for *habitátio*, and the concessive *porque, estando irritado* in 12:1. So it shows what Brazilian ears know, but it is no witness of sense.

Secondary source: musicasparamissa.com.br, "Salmo Is 12 – Com alegria bebereis do manancial da salvação (Paulo Neto)", saved as `consult/ps221/musicasparamissa-is12.html` (gitignored). The page labels its text "LETRA: LECIONÁRIO Is 12,2-3.4bcd.5-6 (R.3)" and lists the uses:

A web search found many settings of the same Lectionary text with a second refrain for the **3rd Sunday of Advent, Year C** (Is 12, R. 6): *Exultai cantando alegres, habitantes de Sião …* These were YouTube, cifraclub.com.br and musicasparamissa.com.br listings. They were not opened one by one.

The verse fragments in the search snippets match the LH wording word for word. The Lectionary and the LH share this translation.

## What this means for the translation

Brazilian Catholics know this canticle well. Its two refrains are sung at the Easter Vigil and in Advent, and it is prayed every other Thursday at Lauds. But what they know is a paraphrase from the Hebrew. Three of the places where it departs are exactly the places where this Latin differs from the Hebrew:

- *de fóntibus Salvatóris*, where the CNBB has *manancial da salvação*;
- *adinventiónes ejus*, where the CNBB has *maravilhas*;
- *habitátio Sion*, where the CNBB has *habitantes*.

Rule 1 keeps the Latin in all three. What can be kept from the CNBB where it is also faithful to the Vulgate:

- *Eis o Deus, meu Salvador*, where we follow the Latin's copula instead: *Eis que Deus é o meu Salvador*;
- *naquele dia*;
- *invocai … o seu nome*;
- *em toda a terra*;
- *no meio de ti / em vosso meio … o Santo de Israel*.
