# Ps 144 — circulation checked (2026-09-23)

Fetched, not from memory. Hebrew-based / Nova Vulgata texts: evidence of what Brazilian ears know, never of sense (rule 1).

## The Latin's own liturgical uses (grep of `content/do`, not fetched)

- **Benedictio mensae** (`content/do/horas/Latin/Appendix/Benedictio mensae.txt`, line 34): *V. Oculi omnium, * in te sperant, Domine, et tu das escam illorum in tempore opportuno. Aperis tu manum tuam, et imples omne animal benedictione.* — 144:15–16 as the grace before meals, with the psalter's *illorum*.
- **Mass Graduals** with *illis* for *illórum*: `missa/Latin/Tempora/Pent01-4.txt` (Corpus Christi, lines 34–35), `Quad3-4.txt`, `Pent20-0.txt`: *Oculi ómnium in te sperant, Dómine: et tu das illis escam in témpore opportúno. V. Aperis tu manum tuam: et imples omne ánimal benedictióne.* The Portuguese *lhes dais* serves both (*illórum* = *illis* in sense).

## Brazilian circulation (fetched)

- **Lecionário (CNBB), Ps 144(145), responsorial setting "Saciai vossos filhos, ó Senhor"** — letrasliturgicas.com.br/2015/07/saciai-vossos-filhos-o-senhor-salmo.html (fetched; lyrics attributed to the Lecionário, music Reginaldo Veloso / André Zamur). The stanza for vv. 15–16: *"Todos os olhos, ó Senhor, em vós esperam, / e vós lhes dais no tempo certo o alimento. / Vós abris a vossa mão prodigamente / e saciais todo ser vivo com fartura."* From the Hebrew (*saciais … com fartura* is the Hebrew's רָצוֹן / "satisfy"; the Latin has *imples … benedictióne*). The search engine's summaries also give the refrain *"Vós abris a vossa mão e saciais os vossos filhos"* (18th Sunday) — from search results only, not fetched.
- fiquefirme.com.br, article title (fetched): *"Senhor, os olhos de todos em ti esperam" (Sl 144,15)* — a *tu* wording, source not named.
- **Grace before meals in Brazil**: the two Brazilian/Portuguese prayer pages fetched (diocesedeanapolis.org.br "Orações antes das refeições"; paroquias.org "Oração para as Refeições") give *Abençoai, Senhor, …* prayers and **do not** use 144:15–16. A search-engine summary of gospelmount.org shows *"Os olhos de todos esperam em Ti, e Tu lhes dás o alimento a seu tempo…"* as a mealtime prayer (Protestant site, *tu*, not fetched). So: the verses are known in Brazil mostly through the Sunday lectionary (Hebrew-based), and as a mealtime grace only in some circles — the claim that they are "widely prayed" at meals was **not confirmed** by what was fetched.

## Result for the translation

- *Os olhos de todos esperam em vós, Senhor* is shared by every Brazilian wording seen (order varies: *em vós esperam*, *esperam em ti*); our colon keeps it.
- *Vós abris a vossa mão* = the lectionary's words exactly (Latin *Áperis tu*, the pronoun kept).
- *no tempo oportuno* (the glossary row, 31:6) against the lectionary's *no tempo certo*: kept for the row; the ear of the lectionary is an option.
- *todo ser vivo* = the lectionary's words for *omne ánimal*.
