# Glossary

One rendering per controlled Latin term, so that what repeats in the Latin repeats in the Portuguese. It grows from the work: add an entry when a word turns out to recur or to collide with a neighbour; put your psalm number beside it. **Status:** `decided` (Gustavo) · `settled` (ruled by Claude overnight under Gustavo's delegation, with reasons in `DECISIONS.md` — use it; he reviews) · `working` (use it; nobody has ruled) · `open` (no settled rendering — propose, and make it a decision in your psalm).

Heavy-duty words get a study in `words/` (`python3.13 research/psalterium/words.py <name> '<regex>' --greek … --pt …`).
