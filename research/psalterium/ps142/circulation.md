→ In Brazil the two lines live as **"Não entreis em juízo com o vosso servo"** and **"Ensinai-me a fazer (a) vossa vontade, pois sois o meu Deus"**. The first half of each is the Latin word for word and we have it; the second halves differ (the Hebrew's *ninguém que viva é justo*; *pois*): our *porque vós sois o meu Deus* is the psalter formula (15:1, 30:15, 117:28). Nothing Vulgate-based and official was found.
