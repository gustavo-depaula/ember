# Canticle 226 (Deuteronomy 32:1–43, Vulgate) — liturgical use and Brazilian circulation

Checked 2026-09-23. Nothing here is from memory; each source says how it was seen.

## In the Latin books (DO, grep)

- DO files it as `Psalterium/Psalmorum/Psalm226.txt`. Its first line is the heading *(Canticum Moysis \* Deut 32:1-65)*, which is not a prayed verse, so D7 applies and it is not translated. The 65 ids are line numbers of the canticle. The text is Deut 32:1–43 (map at the head of `consult/parallels/ps226.md`).
- `Psalterium/Psalmi/Psalmi major.txt`:
  - `[Day6 Laudes2]` puts it at Saturday Lauds, fourth place, in the penitential series, with the antiphon ***In servis suis \* miserébitur Dóminus et propítius erit terræ pópuli sui*** (from 32:52 and 32:65). The 1960 rubrics shorten it to `226(1-27)`, which ends at DO 32:27 = Deut 32:18.
  - `[Daya6 Laudes]` uses the antiphon ***Date magnitúdinem \* Deo nostro*** (*magnitúdinem*, the antiphon's word, where the canticle's 32:4 has *magnificéntiam*).
  - `[Monastic Laudes] (feria 7)` uses the same antiphon and splits the canticle in two: `226(1-32)` and `226(33-65)`.
- The wording agrees with the Clementine Vulgate of Deut 32:1–43 (Bolls `VULG`, `consult/ps226/bolls-VULG-5-32.json`).

## Diurnal Monástico 1962 (consult/diurnal-1.md, Saturday Lauds)

- It prints only Deut 32:1–18 (= DO 32:1–32:27), in the *tu* form. It opens with ***Escutai***, as the draft does (decision `audite`). It is quoted in the parallels file only.

## Liturgia das Horas, CNBB

- Source: liturgiadashoras.online, *Laudes de Sábado da 2ª Semana do Tempo Comum*, saved as `consult/ps226/lh-online-laudes-sab-2tc.html` (gitignored). The canticle is Saturday Lauds of Week II.
- Heading: *Cântico Dt 32,1-12 — Os benefícios de Deus ao povo*. Antiphon: ***Vinde todos e dai glória ao nosso Deus!*** The text stops at Deut 32:12 (= DO 32:17) and leaves out the verses of apostasy and judgment.
- It is a metrical paraphrase from the Neo-Vulgate, i.e. from the Hebrew. It shows what Brazilian ears know, not what the text means.
- Fragments only (copyright; the saved page is gitignored): *Ó céus, vinde, escutai*; *ouça a terra*; *Minha doutrina se derrame como chuva*; *como orvalho*; *aguaceiros*; *sobre a relva*; *O nome do Senhor vou invocar*; *Ele é a Rocha: suas obras são perfeitas*; *povo louco, povo estulto e insensato*; *Recorda-te dos dias do passado*; *interroga, e teus avós*; *Quando o Altíssimo*; *os filhos de Adão*; *a parte do Senhor foi o seu povo*; *a porção de sua herança*; *cercou-o*; *como a pupila de seus olhos*; *Como a águia*; *incita os seus filhotes a voar*; *estendeu as suas asas e o tomou*; *O Senhor, somente ele, foi seu guia*; *um outro deus*.

## Lectionary (Brazil)

- **Tuesday, Week 19, Year I** (first reading Dt 31,1-8). The responsorial is ***Dt 32,3-12***, with the refrain ***A porção do Senhor é o seu povo***.
  - Source: Canção Nova, *19ª Semana do Tempo Comum – Terça-feira* (13 Aug 2019), saved as `consult/ps226/cancaonova-19tc-terca.html` (gitignored).
  - The strophes are the LH paraphrase word for word (the same fragments as above).
- **Friday, Week 18, Year II** (first reading Naum). The responsorial is ***Dt 32,35cd-36ab.39abcd.41 (R. 39c)***, with the refrain ***Sou eu que tiro a vida, sou eu quem faz viver!***
  - Source: *Pai Nosso Deus* (painossodeus.wordpress.com, 7 Aug 2026), which prints the CNBB responsorial. Saved as `consult/ps226/painossodeus-2026-08-07.html` (gitignored).

## What Brazilian ears know, and what the draft takes

- ***escutai*** (LH): the draft's *Escutai* for *Audíte* is forced by the homograph ban, and it agrees with the LH and the Diurnal.
- ***Minha doutrina*** (LH) matches *doctrína → doutrina*. The LH verb *se derrame* is the Hebrew's. The draft's *Condense-se* follows the Latin *concréscat* (decision `concrescat`).
- ***aguaceiros … sobre a relva*** (LH) supports *imber → aguaceiro* and *grámina → relva*.
- ***suas obras são perfeitas*** (LH, Lectionary) is the draft's *Perfeitas são as obras de Deus*. *Ele é a Rocha* is the Hebrew's; the Latin has *Dei*.
- ***Recorda-te dos dias*** (LH) vs the draft's *Lembra-te*: *meménto → lembrar-se* is the glossary's.
- ***teus avós*** (LH, as MS1932) for *majóres*. The draft has *antepassados*, with *avós* as an option (decision `majores`).
- ***a parte do Senhor … a porção de sua herança*** (LH) and the Lectionary refrain ***A porção do Senhor é o seu povo***: the Hebrew *heleq*, which the Latin renders *pars*. The draft keeps *parte* (glossary: *pars → parte*, *pórtio → porção*). The refrain Brazilians sing uses *porção*, which is noted here for Gustavo.
- ***cercou-o*** (LH, MS1932) is the Hebrew and Greek sense. The draft keeps the Latin *circumdúxit* (decision `circumduxit`).
- ***como a pupila*** (LH) vs the draft's *como a menina do seu olho* (16:8, the glossary).
- ***incita os seus filhotes a voar*** (LH): *incita* is an option in decision `provocans`. The draft keeps *provoca* (the canticle's repeated verb, MS1932). *filhotes* is shared.
- ***e o tomou*** (LH, MS1932) is an option in decision `assumpsit`. The draft has *o recolheu* (26:10).
- ***O Senhor, somente ele, foi seu guia*** (LH) supports *dux → guia*.
- ***não existe outro Deus além de mim*** (Lectionary) for *præter me*. The draft has *senão eu* (canticles 215, 216), with *além de mim* as an option (decision `praeter`).
- ***dos adversários todos hei de me vingar*** (Lectionary) for *hóstibus meis* in DO 32:61. It supports *adversários* for *hostes* (decision `hostes`).
- ***Se eu afiar a minha espada*** (Lectionary) matches the draft's *Se eu afiar … a minha espada*.
- ***sou eu que firo e eu que torno a curar*** (Lectionary) keeps the repeated *eu*, as the draft does (*ferirei, e eu curarei*).
