# Ps 134 — liturgical use and Brazilian circulation (checked 2026-09-23)

## In the Latin books (grep of `content/do/`)

- Antiphons, `horas/Latin/Psalterium/Psalmi/Psalmi major.txt`:
  - l. 48, section `[Day2 Laudes1]`: *Laudáte * nomen Dómini, qui statis in domo Dómini* — 134:1a + 134:2a joined. So 134:1's first colon and 134:2's first colon must each read alone and join cleanly.
  - l. 55, section `[Day2 Laudes2]`: *Laudáte * Dóminum quia benígnus est, et in servis suis deprecábitur* — adapts 134:3 (*benígnus* for *bonus*) + 134:14b. The second colon of 134:14 is sung alone at the end of an antiphon.
  - l. 251 `[Daya4 Vespera]` and l. 421 `[Monastic Vespera]` (feria 4): *Ómnia * quæcúmque vóluit Dóminus fecit* — 134:6 head. Should read alone: *Tudo o que quis, o Senhor fez*.
  - Which weekday `Day2` is was not checked beyond the section names (other psalms' notes read `[Day1]` as Monday, which would make this Tuesday).
- No Mass proper uses a verse of Ps 134 (grep for the verse heads; only 112:1's *laudáte nomen Dómini* turned up, another psalm).
- The Alleluia title (*Allelúja*, LXX αλληλουια, MS1932 "1 Aleluia.") is not in DO's Latin text (`Psalm134.txt` opens with *Laudáte nomen Dómini*). As in Pss 110–112 and 113 (none of whose prayed texts carry it; D7), it is not reproduced.
