# Ps 130 — liturgical use and Brazilian circulation (checked 2026-09-23)

## In the Latin books (grep of `content/do/horas/Latin`, `content/do/missa/Latin`)

- **Vespers antiphon, Wednesday** (`Psalterium/Psalmi/Psalmi major.txt` l. 82, `[Day3 Vespera]`): *Dómine, * non est exaltátum cor meum.* Another scheme of the same file (l. 246, 416) has *Speret * Israël in Dómino.*
- **Rule of St Benedict, ch. 7 (on humility)**, read at Prime (`Regula/01-25.txt` l. 3): *Omnis qui se exáltat humiliábitur … Quod se cavére Prophéta índicat dicens: Dómine, non est exaltátum cor meum neque eláti sunt óculi mei, neque ambulávi in magnis neque in mirabílibus super me. Sed quid, si non humíliter sentiébam, si exaltávi ánimam meam, sicut ablactátum super matrem suam, ita retríbues in ánimam suam.* — Benedict (1) joins *non est exaltátum* to *qui se exáltat* (the reflexive of pride), and (2) reads 130:2 as a conditional with an apodosis of requital (*retríbues*, "so shalt thou repay"): the self-curse reading.
- No Mass proper uses the psalm (grep found none). Titulus *Canticum graduum* is in the LXX and MS1932 (*Cântico gradual de David*) but not in DO's Latin — D7, as Pss 119–125, 133.
- *ablactáre* elsewhere in DO's Latin only in Isaiah lessons (Adv2-0 Is 11:8; Adv3-1o Is 28:9 *Ablactátos a lacte, avúlsos ab ubéribus*).

## Brazilian circulation (fetched with WebFetch, which summarizes through a model — wordings probable, not guaranteed; the site refused a verbatim copy, citing CNBB copyright)

- **Liturgia das Horas, CNBB** (Hebrew-based; no witness of sense), https://liturgiadashoras.online/salmo-130131/ — title *Confiança filial e repouso em Deus*; epigraph *Aprendei de mim, porque sou manso e humilde de coração (Mt 11,29)*. Fragments: *meu coração não é orgulhoso*, *nem se eleva arrogante o meu olhar*; *criança bem tranqüila, amamentada, no regaço acolhedor de sua mãe*; last line begins *Confia no Senhor, ó Israel*. (That it is sung at Tuesday Vespers with Ps 124 comes from `ps124/circulation.md`, not re-checked here.)
- **Almeida ARC** (Protestant, Hebrew-based), from a search-result summary only: *Senhor, o meu coração não se elevou, nem os meus olhos se levantaram … como uma criança desmamada de sua mãe*.
- → Brazilian ears know the psalm in the Hebrew's picture (a quiet child at rest), which the Latin does not have: the Latin (with the Greek) makes 130:2 a conditional ending in a *retribútio*. Nothing Vulgate-based and Brazilian was found. *criança desmamada* circulates (Almeida).
