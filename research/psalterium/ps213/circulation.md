# Canticle 213 (Judith 16:15–22, Vulgate) — Brazilian circulation

Checked 2026-09-23 on the web; nothing here is from memory. Where a source is only a secondary page, it says so.

## Liturgia das Horas (CNBB) — Wednesday Lauds, Week I

Source: Paulus Editora, *Liturgia Diária das Horas*, "Dia 16 – Quarta-feira" (https://www.paulus.com.br/portal/liturgia-diaria-das-horas/dia-16-quarta-feira/, saved as `consult/ps213/lh-paulus-quarta.html` (gitignored)). Paulus prints the CNBB text. The canticle stands between Ps 35 and Ps 46, which is Wednesday Lauds of Week I. A secondary page (pilulasliturgicas.blogspot.com, 2023/09) gives the same slot.

Heading: **Cântico Jt 16,1-2.13-15 – Deus, Criador do mundo e protetor do seu povo.** Antiphon: *Vós sois grande, Senhor-Adonai, admirável, de força invencível!*

The strophes that match DO's verses (opening words only; the full text is in the saved page under `consult/`, since the CNBB text is copyrighted) (the LH follows the Nova Vulgata, which here follows the Greek, not Jerome's Vulgate):

| DO | CNBB LH |
| --- | --- |
| 16:15 | *Cantemos louvores a Deus, …* |
| 16:16 | *Vós sois grande, Senhor-Adonai, …* |
| 16:17 | *Toda a vossa criatura vos …* |
| 16:18 | *Vosso sopro de vida enviastes, …* |
| 16:19 | *Desde as bases, os montes …* |
| 16:20 | *Mas aqueles que a vós …* |
| 16:21–22 | not in the LH (the canticle stops at 16:15 of the Greek) |

It is a metrical paraphrase from another text, so it is a witness of what Brazilian ears know, not of sense. Points of contact worth keeping in view: *Toda a vossa criatura vos sirva* (the singular *criatura* with *toda a vossa* is the CNBB's own phrase), *os montes se abalam* (the psalter's verb *abalar*), *como cera, derretem-se* (the image), *junto a vós serão grandes em tudo* (*apud te … per ómnia*, close to our *junto de vós … em tudo*), *Adonai* kept.

## Missal / Lectionary

- Secondary (pilulasliturgicas.blogspot.com): Jt 16,13-15 replaces the psalm in the votive Mass of Mary, Help of Christians (Collection of Masses of the BVM, n. 42). Its Portuguese wording was not fetched.
- No other Mass use of Jt 16 was found.
- *omnípotens* → *todo-poderoso* is the Brazilian liturgical rendering: *Creio em Deus Pai todo-poderoso* and the collect opening *Deus eterno e todo-poderoso* in CNBB and diocesan liturgy sheets (CNBB, *Celebrar em família, 4º Domingo da Páscoa*, 2022; Diocese de Colatina sheets 2025–2026, found by web search, not opened one by one).

## What this means for the translation

The Brazilian liturgy prays this canticle from the Greek, in a paraphrase, and leaves out the last two verses. So no fixed Brazilian wording of *this* Latin has to be matched. Where the CNBB's wording is also a faithful rendering of the Vulgate, it is a plus for the ear: *toda a vossa criatura vos sirva*, *abalar*, *derreter-se*, *junto de vós … em tudo*, *todo-poderoso*.
