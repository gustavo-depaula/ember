# Canticle 222 (Isaiah 38:10–20, Vulgate — *Ego dixi: In dimídio diérum meórum*, Canticum Ezechiæ) — liturgical use and Brazilian circulation

Checked 2026-09-23. Nothing here is from memory; each source says how it was seen. Downloaded pages are in `consult/ps222/` (gitignored).

## In the Latin books (DO, grep)

- DO files it as `Psalterium/Psalmorum/Psalm222.txt`. Its first line, *(Canticum Ezechiæ \* Isa 38:10-24)*, is a heading, not a prayed verse; D7 applies and it is not translated (as 233, 211, 213). The range it prints, "38:10-24", is DO's own line ids 38:10–38:24; the text is Isaiah 38:10–20 of the Vulgate (the map is at the head of `consult/parallels/ps222.md`).
- The wording agrees with the Clementine Vulgate (Bolls `VULG`, `consult/ps222/bolls-VULG-23-38.json`), apart from spelling and punctuation.
- `Psalmi/Psalmi major.txt`:
  - `[Day2 Laudes2]` (Tuesday Lauds II, the penitential Lauds of the 1960 psalter): fourth place, antiphon ***Corrípies me, Dómine, \* et vivificábis me*** — drawn from 38:20.
  - `[Daya2 Laudes]` and `[Monastic Laudes] (feria 3)`: antiphon ***Cunctis diébus \* vitæ nostræ salvos nos fac Dómine*** — drawn from 38:24.
  - So both 38:20 and 38:24 must yield a sayable antiphon: *Vós me castigareis, Senhor, e me vivificareis*; *Todos os dias da nossa vida, salvai-nos, Senhor*. The draft's wording allows both.

## Diurnal Monástico 1962 (consult/diurnal-1.md, Tuesday Lauds, ferial canticle)

- Heading *Cântico de Ezequias — Is. 38, 10-20*, subtitle *Angústia numa doença mortal e ação de graças depois de salvo*. Antiphon *Salva-nos, Senhor, † em todos os dias de nossa vida*.
- Its Portuguese is from the Hebrew-family text and says *tu*: *sob as portas da morte*; *qual uma tenda de pastores*; *gemo como a pomba*; *Meus olhos cansaram-se*; *mansão dos mortos*. No witness of sense; diction only.

## Liturgia das Horas (CNBB)

- Source: liturgiadashoras.online, *Laudes de Terça-feira da 2ª Semana do Tempo Comum*, saved as `consult/ps222/lh-online-laudes-ter-2tc.html`, text extract `lh-ter-2tc.txt`. The canticle is Tuesday Lauds, Week II, between Ps 42 and Ps 64.
- Heading: *Cântico Is 38,10-14.17-20 — Angústia de um agonizante e alegria da cura*; epigraph Ap 1,18. Antiphon: ***Salvai-nos, ó Senhor, todos os dias!***
- It is a metrical paraphrase of the Nova Vulgata / Hebrew text, and it omits Isa 38:15–16 (our 38:18b–20). Short fragments worth knowing (what Brazilian ears know, not a witness of sense): *Eu dizia*; *no apogeu de minha vida*; *para a mansão triste dos mortos descerei*; *a terra dos viventes*; *Minha morada foi … arrebatada*; *como a tenda de um pastor*; *Qual tecelão*; *de manhã até à tarde*; *o meu gemido*; *Os meus olhos já se cansam*; *lançastes para trás os meus pecados*; *O pai há de contar para seus filhos*; *Senhor, salvai-me!*; *cantaremos nossos salmos*.

## Lectionary (Mass)

- Source: Canção Nova, *15ª Semana do Tempo Comum | Sexta-feira* (19 Jul 2024), saved as `consult/ps222/cancaonova-15tc-sexta.html`; and the blog *remicon-salmo* (18 Jul 2014, same day in the calendar), `consult/ps222/remicon-is38.html`. The first reading (year II) is Is 38,1-6.21-22.7-8; the responsorial is **Is 38,10.11.12abcd.16**, refrain ***Vós livrastes minha vida do sepulcro, a fim de eu não deixar de existir*** (R. cf. 17b).
- Its strophes for 10–12 are word for word the LH text above (so the Lectionary uses the same translation). The strophe for v. 16 differs between the two sources — not resolved, and nothing here depends on it.

## *ínferi* and the gates (for decision `inferi`)

- The CNBB Bible (Bolls `CNBB`, Mt 16:18, `consult/ps222/bolls-CNBB-40-16.json`) renders the Vulgate's *portæ inferi* (`bolls-VULG-40-16.json`) as ***as forças do Inferno***: even a Hebrew/Greek-based modern Brazilian version says *Inferno* for this genitive, in the one phrase of the NT that has the same two Latin words.
- The psalter's own genitive precedent: 48:16 and 88:49 *de manu ínferi* → *da mão do inferno*; 9:15 *de portis mortis* and 106:18 *ad portas mortis* → *portas da morte*.
- The LH and the Lectionary both say *para a mansão triste dos mortos descerei* (a paraphrase, from the Hebrew *šeʾôl*), and the Brazilian Creed says *desceu à mansão dos mortos* (ps212/circulation.md, Vatican News pt).

## What Brazilian ears know, and what the draft takes

- ***na terra dos viventes*** (LH) — the psalter's *terra vivéntium → terra dos vivos* (26:13, 141:6) is kept; *viventes* is not taken.
- ***como a tenda de um pastor*** (LH) — the draft keeps the Latin's plural: *como uma tenda de pastores* (MS1932's words).
- ***Qual tecelão … cortada*** — the weaving image; the draft keeps *urdir* for *ordíri* (the Latin's word: to lay the warp).
- ***lançastes para trás … os meus pecados*** (LH) — the same verb as the draft's *lançastes*.
- ***Senhor, salvai-me!*** (LH) — the psalter's formula *salvai-me*.
- ***cantaremos nossos salmos*** (LH) — the same words as the draft.
- ***Vós livrastes minha vida do sepulcro*** (Lectionary refrain) — the draft has *libertastes a minha alma* (the *éruere* row without a source; *ánima → alma*).
