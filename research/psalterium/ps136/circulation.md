# Ps 136 — liturgical use and Brazilian circulation (checked 2026-09-23)

## In the Latin books (grep of `content/do/`, Portuguese excluded)

- **Offertory** of the 20th Sunday after Pentecost (`missa/Latin/Tempora/Pent20-0.txt` l. 43–44) and of Thursday in Passion Week (`Quad5-4.txt`, `Quad5-4t.txt` l. 44), headed *Ps 136:1*:
  *Super flúmina Babylónis illic sédimus et flévimus: dum recordarémur tui, Sion.* — note the Missal's *dum … tui, Sion* against the psalter's *cum recordarémur Sion*. The psalm keeps its own wording.
- **Responsories of Easter IV** (`horas/Latin/Tempora/Pasc4-0.txt`):
  - Responsory 1 (l. 33–36): *R. Si oblítus fúero tui, allelúja, obliviscátur mei déxtera mea: * Adhǽreat lingua mea fáucibus meis, si non memínero tui … V. Super flúmina Babylónis …* — the Office's *obliviscátur mei* for the psalm's *oblivióni detur*.
  - Responsory 6 (l. 93–96): *R. Hymnum cantáte nobis, allelúja: * Quómodo cantábimus cánticum Dómini in terra aliéna? … V. Illic interrogavérunt nos, qui captívos duxérunt nos, verba cantiónum.* — so 136:3b's second colon, 136:4 and 136:3a's first half are each sung alone.
- **Antiphon** *Hymnum cantáte nobis * de cánticis Sion* (`Psalterium/Psalmi/Psalmi major.txt` l. 423, [Monastic Vespera] feria 4).
- No titulus in DO's Latin (D7). (MS1932 prints *Salmo de David, para Jeremias* — not reproduced; LXX has τῷ Δαυιδ.)

## Brazilian circulation (fetched)

- **Liturgia das Horas, CNBB translation** (Hebrew-based; not a witness of sense), liturgiadashoras.online/salmo-1361371-6/, as returned by the fetch tool (a summarizing model; wording probable, not guaranteed):
  title *Junto aos rios da Babilônia*; the page is headed *Salmo 136(137),1-6* and prints **verses 1–6 only — 7–9 are not on it** (that the Liturgy of the Hours omits them is what the page shows; the rubric behind it was not fetched).
  "Junto aos rios da Babilônia † nos sentávamos chorando, * com saudades de Sião." · "Nos salgueiros por ali * penduramos nossas harpas." · "Cantai hoje para nós * algum canto de Sião!" · "Como havemos de cantar † os cantares do Senhor * numa terra estrangeira?" · "Se de ti, Jerusalém, † algum dia eu me esquecer, * que resseque a minha mão!" · "Que se cole a minha língua † e se prenda ao céu da boca, * se de ti não me lembrar! – Se não for Jerusalém * minha grande alegria!"
  A web search lists it at Vespers of Tuesday of week IV (the pages of Tuesday of the 4th week of Easter and of the 16th week of Ordinary Time — search titles only).
- **Lectionary (Brazil)**: responsorial psalm of the **4th Sunday of Lent, year B**, and weekdays (Friday of week 12, even years; Wednesday of week 26, odd years) — musicasparamissa.com.br (fetched). Refrain: ***Que se prenda a minha língua ao céu da boca, se de ti, Jerusalém, eu me esquecer!*** (the same words in several sung settings found by search: O Salmista, Flávio Bras Fonseca, letrasliturgicas).
- The Portuguese (Portugal) lectionary (presbiteros.org.br, fetched): *Sobre os rios de Babilónia nos sentámos a chorar, com saudades de Sião* … *Se eu me esquecer de ti, Jerusalém, esquecida fique a minha mão direita. Apegue-se-me a língua ao paladar* — European, not Brazilian; noted only because it circulates online.
- → Brazilian ears know: **"rios da Babilônia"** (with the article), **"salgueiros"**, **"harpas"** (Hebrew *kinnor*; the Latin has *órgana*), **"cantares / cânticos do Senhor"**, **"terra estrangeira"**, **"língua … ao céu da boca"**, **"se de ti, Jerusalém, eu me esquecer"**. The opening *Junto aos rios* (LH, CNBB) against *Sobre os rios* (the Latin's *Super*, MS1932 *Junto dos*, DM1962 *Sôbre*): both circulate.
