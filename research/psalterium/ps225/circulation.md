# Canticle 225 (Habakkuk 3:2–19, Vulgate) — liturgical use and Brazilian circulation

Checked 2026-09-23. Nothing here is from memory; each source says how it was seen.

## In the Latin books (DO, grep)

- DO files it as `Psalterium/Psalmorum/Psalm225.txt`. Its first line is the heading *(Canticum Habacuc \* Hab 3:2-19)*, which is not a prayed verse. D7 applies, so it is not translated. Canticles 233 and 210–216 did the same.
- `Psalterium/Psalmi/Psalmi major.txt`:
  - `[Day5 Laudes2]`: Friday Lauds (the second, penitential scheme), fourth place, antiphon ***Cum irátus fúeris, \* Dómine, misericórdiæ recordáberis***.
  - `[Daya5 Laudes]`: antiphon ***Dómine \* audívi auditiónem tuam et tímui***.
  - `[Monastic Laudes]`, feria 6: antiphon *Dómine, \* audívi audítum tuam, et tímui*.
- The text is Jerome's Vulgate of Habakkuk, from the Hebrew, not a Gallican psalter text. It agrees with the Clementine Vulgate (Bolls `VULG`, book 35, `consult/ps225/bolls-VULG-35-3.json`). DO's lines cross verse boundaries at the inline markers `(5a)`, `(6a)` and `(10a)`, which the Portuguese does not reproduce.

## Diurnal Monástico 1962 (consult/diurnal-1.md, Friday Lauds)

- It prints the canticle as ***Cântico de Habacuc — Hab. 3, 1-19***, the ferial canticle of Friday Lauds. Its antiphon is *Senhor, ouvi o teu oráculo, e temi*.
- Its Portuguese follows the Hebrew, not the Vulgate: *Deus vem de Theman*, *Diante dêle marcha a Peste*, *eu me alegrarei em Deus, meu Salvador*, *semelhantes aos da corça*. It addresses God as *tu*. It is a witness of Brazilian monastic diction, not of the Latin's sense.

## Liturgia das Horas, CNBB

- Source: liturgiadashoras.online, *Laudes de Sexta-feira da 2ª Semana do Tempo Comum*, saved as `consult/ps225/lh-online-laudes-sex-2tc.html` (gitignored). The canticle is Friday Lauds of Week II.
- Heading: *Cântico Hab 3,2-4.13a.15-19 — Deus há de vir para julgar*. Antiphon: ***Ó Senhor, mesmo na cólera, lembrai-vos de ter misericórdia***.
- The text is a metrical paraphrase from the Neo-Vulgate (i.e. the Hebrew). It is a witness of what Brazilian ears know, not of sense:
  - Fragments only (copyright; the saved page is gitignored): *Eu ouvi vossa mensagem, ó Senhor*; *enchi-me de temor*; *Manifestai a vossa obra*; *tornai-a conhecida*; *o Santo, de Farã*; *saem raios de suas mãos*; *para salvar o vosso Ungido*; *no turbilhão das grandes águas*; *e tremeram os meus lábios*; *Ainda que a figueira não floresça*; *nos apriscos*; *o gado nos currais*; *exulto em Deus, meu Salvador*; *ágil como a corça*; *para as alturas me conduz*.
- It leaves out DO 3:5–3:12 and 3:13b–3:14, the verses of wrath.
- The pilulasliturgicas.blogspot survey of Habakkuk (saved as `consult/ps225/pilulas-naum-habacuc.html`, gitignored) also says this canticle is used at Lauds on Good Friday.

## Lectionary (Brazil)

- Per the same pilulasliturgicas survey (16 Nov 2022), Habakkuk is read at Mass only as Hab 1,2-3; 2,2-4 (27th Sunday, Year C) and Hab 1,12–2,4 (a weekday). Hab 3 is not a Mass reading. This rests on that survey; a web search found no Lectionary use of Hab 3 to contradict it.

## What Brazilian ears know, and what the draft takes

- ***Eu ouvi*** (LH): the pronoun is there too. The draft has *eu ouvi* (3:2a) and *Eu ouvi* (3:16a), so the verb is not heard as a *vós* imperative (decision `ouvi`).
- ***vossa mensagem*** (LH), *o teu oráculo* (DM1962), *a tua palavra* (MS1932): not taken. The draft keeps the glossary's *notícia* for *audítio* (decision `auditio`).
- ***tornai-a conhecida*** (LH) is listed as an option for *notum fácies* (decision `notum`); the draft keeps the glossary's *fazer conhecer*, in the Latin's future.
- ***o Santo, de Farã*** (LH): the same words and the same gapped verb as the draft.
- ***raios*** (LH, DM1962, MS1932): not taken. The Latin has *córnua*, and the draft has *chifres* (decision `cornua`).
- ***o vosso Ungido*** (LH): not taken. The Latin has *Christo*, so *o vosso Cristo* (D19).
- ***turbilhão*** (LH, for the waters): the draft has *turbilhão* for *turbo* in 3:14a (the enemies), and *redemoinho* for *gurges* (decision `gurges`).
- ***Ainda que a figueira não floresça*** (LH) turns the Latin's future into a concession; the draft keeps *Pois a figueira não florescerá*.
- ***o gado*** (LH) is listed as an option for *arméntum* (decision `armentum`).
- ***meu Salvador*** (LH, DM1962, MS1932): not taken. The Latin has *Jesu meo*, so *meu Jesus* (decision `jesu`). This is the widest gap from what Brazilian ears know, and it is the Latin's own word.
- ***como a corça*** (LH, DM1962): the draft has *das corças*, as 17:34 (decision `ponet`).
- ***para as alturas me conduz*** (LH): the draft has *sobre as minhas alturas … me guiará* (D22, D42).
