# Ps 119 — liturgical use and Brazilian circulation (checked 2026-09-23)

## In the Latin books (grep of `content/do/`)

- Gradual of the 2nd Sunday after Pentecost (`missa/Latin/Tempora/Pent02-0.txt`) and of Friday in the 2nd week of Lent (`Quad2-5.txt`): *Ad Dóminum, cum tribulárer, clamávi, et exaudívit me. V. Dómine, líbera ánimam meam a lábiis iníquis et a lingua dolósa.* (119:1–2)
- Antiphons: *Clamávi, * et exaudívit me* (`Psalmi major.txt` l. 231; `Psalmi minor.txt` Tertia Feria, over 119–121); *Clamávi * et Dóminus exaudívit me* (`Psalmi major.txt` l. 39).
- Office of the Dead, Vespers: *Heu mihi Dómine, * quia incolátus meus prolongátus est* (`Commune/C9.txt` l. 27).
- Holy Thursday / Holy Saturday Matins: *Cum his, * qui odérunt pacem, eram pacíficus: dum loquébar illis, impugnábant me gratis* (`Tempora/Quad6-4.txt` l. 19, `Quad6-6r.txt` l. 5) — note *dum* for the psalm's *cum*.

## Brazilian circulation (fetched)

- **Liturgia das Horas, CNBB translation** (from the Neo-Vulgate/Hebrew family, not a witness of sense), as printed at liturgiadashoras.online/salmo-119120/ (page states "A tradução da Liturgia das Horas nesta página pertence à © CNBB"): title *Desejo da paz*; lines 1–3 begin
  "Clamei pelo Senhor na minha angústia, * / e ele me escutou, quando eu dizia: / "Senhor, livrai-me desses lábios mentirosos, * / e da língua enganadora libertai-me! / Qual será a tua paga, o teu castigo, *"
  It is in the complementary psalmody, series I (the mid-morning hour), with Pss 120(121) and 121(122) (liturgiadashoras.online/salmodia-complementar/). The antiphon was reported by the fetch tool as "Clamai pelo Senhor, e ele me escutou" — the spelling (*Clamai* / *Clamei*) is not trustworthy from that tool; unverified.
  → Brazilian ears already know **"e ele me escutou"** and **"língua enganadora"** in this psalm.
- **Bíblia Ave Maria** (mundodoscatolicos.com.br/salmos/119/, fetched): v. 1 begins *"Cântico das peregrinações. Na hora da tribulação, clamei ao Senhor e ele me atendeu."*
- Pe. Ney Brasil, Arquidiocese de Florianópolis (arquifln.org.br, fetched): names in circulation for the series — *cantos das subidas*, *cantos dos degraus*, *salmos graduais*, *cantos das romarias*, *cantos de peregrinação*.
- Matos Soares 1932 (parallels file): heading *Cântico gradual*.
- The titulus *Canticum graduum* is not in DO's Latin (collation/report.md: tituli omitted, e.g. 133:1).
