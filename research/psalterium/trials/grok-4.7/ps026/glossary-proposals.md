# Glossary proposals — Ps 26

Rows I would change. Not applied to `glossary.md`.

| Latin | Portuguese | Status | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| consístere (a camp, *castra*) | assentar (*Se contra mim se assentar um acampamento*) | open | Ps 26:3. Was *instalar-se* in the *consístere (a person)* row (D39), which keeps *erguer-se* for a man. *instalar-se* was heard as the vocabulary of offices. *assentar* is the camp pitching, the same image, and the colon ends on *acampamento*, where *castra* stands. *erguer-se* does not fit a camp. *instalar-se* and *postar* stay options. |
| hóstia + genitive of a shout | vítima (*uma vítima de aclamação*) | open | Ps 26:6b, unchanged as the rendering. Both the stylist and the ambiguity reader heard *vítima de aclamação* as ‘a victim of acclaim’. *um sacrifício de aclamação* fixes the mishearing and spends *sacrifícium* (115:17 has both roots), so it stays an option, not the row. |
