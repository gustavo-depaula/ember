# Glossary proposals from Ps 22 (trial, draft 2)

Settled rows followed and not reopened: *dedúcere* (guidance) → guiar (D22); *sémita* → vereda (D15); *convértere* of a soul turned to God → converter (D25, as 18:8); *in conspéctu* → à vista (the stylist’s *diante de mim* refused: that is *ante*); *tribuláre* → atribular; *virga* → vara; *domus* → casa; *calix* → cálice; *præclárus* → excelente; *ómnibus diébus vitæ meæ* copied from 26:4.

## Adjustments to open rows

- *reféctio* → *refrigério*, but *água de refrigério* (genitive of quality), not *água do refrigério*. Listed as unknown; *repouso* and *que restaura* stay options. The hearing was still “refreshment”.
- *in longitúdinem diérum* (22:6b): *por longos dias*, the row’s preposition. Draft 1’s *durante* (Matos Soares 1932) was only holding the syllable count; the stylist found it heavy. The colon is −4. *longos dias* itself stands (20:5, 90:16).
- *ēducáre* → *nutrir*, *báculus* → *cajado*, *impinguáre* → *ungir*, *inebriáre* → *embriagar*, *subséqui* → *seguir*, *régere* → *reger*, *páscua* → *pastagem* (singular): confirmed. *pastor* for *regit* refused. The latinist’s *Ungistes fartamente* refused as an added adverb; *Engordastes* stays the option that keeps the fat.
