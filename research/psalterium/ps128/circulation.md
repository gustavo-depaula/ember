# Ps 128 — liturgical use and Brazilian circulation (checked 2026-09-23)

## In the Latin books (grep of `content/do/`)

- **Tract of Passion Sunday** (`missa/Latin/Tempora/Quad5-0.txt` l. 36–40, headed *Ps 128:1-4*):
  *Sæpe expugnavérunt me a juventúte mea. V. Dicat nunc Israël: sæpe expugnavérunt me a juventúte mea. V. Etenim non potuérunt mihi: supra dorsum meum fabricavérunt peccatóres. V. Prolongavérunt iniquitátes suas: Dóminus justus concídit cervíces peccatórum.*
  Note the Tract's regrouping: *Dicat nunc Israël* introduces the repeated line (so the repeat is what Israel says), *Etenim non potuérunt mihi* is joined to *supra dorsum*, and *iniquitátes suas* is plural (the psalm: *iniquitátem suam*).
- **Vespers antiphon** *Sæpe * expugnavérunt me a juventúte mea* (`Psalterium/Psalmi/Psalmi major.txt` l. 244 [Daya3 Vespera], l. 412 [Monastic Vespera] feria 2). So the first colon of 128:1 is sung alone.
- **St Bernard in the Passion votive office** (`Commune/Votiva/V6.txt` l. 182): *cum supra dorsum ejus fabricárent peccatóres* — 128:3 read of the scourging of Christ. The image kept concrete (a back worked upon) matters for this reading.
- The titulus *Canticum graduum* is not in DO's Latin (D7; not reproduced, as in Pss 119–122, 133).

  "Quanto eu fui perseguido desde jovem" · "Aravam lavradores o meu dorso, rasgando longos sulcos" · "Mas o Senhor, que sempre age com justiça" · "Sejam eles como a erva dos telhados, que bem antes de arrancada" · "Em nome do Senhor vos bendizemos!"
- Where it is prayed: a web search on the title returned pages of the **Hora Média** (Terça, Sexta, Nona) of Thursday after Ash Wednesday, Thursday of the 20th week of Ordinary Time, Thursday of the 4th week of Easter, and Friday 22 December (search summary only). That these are Thursday of week IV of the four-week psalter is my inference from the dates, not read on a page.
- Lectionary: not searched beyond this; no evidence either way that it is a Sunday responsorial psalm.
- → Brazilian ears know **"a erva dos telhados"**, **"antes de arrancada"**, **"o meu dorso"**, and **"vos bendizemos"** (present tense, *bendizer*, not *abençoar*) **"em nome do Senhor"**.
