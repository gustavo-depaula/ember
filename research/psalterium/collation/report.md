## Rule

1. The DO mirror is the working text, verbatim — it is what the Latin column shows.
2. Each psalm gets its Hetzenauer page read before it is translated, and the result is added here. A psalm is "collated" only after that.
3. A wording divergence from the print, if one ever turns up, is not silently fixed: it is recorded here, the translation follows DO (the column must match), and it is reported upstream to Divinum Officium.

## `Psalm94.txt` is not an ordinary Ps 94

It lacks 94:6–7a and has `$ant` slots between verse groups. DO assigns it at Epiphany Matins (`Sancti/01-06.txt:87`: `Veníte adorémus eum: * quia ipse est Dóminus Deus noster.;;94`), where the antiphon — sung in the slots — *is* the missing verse. So the two DO files are the Epiphany form (Gallican wording) and the daily invitatory (`Invitatorium.txt`, the other wording), and neither is a plain Ps 94.

Where the invitatory's wording parts from the Gallican (full table at the end of `diffs.md`):

## Marks

The pilot files carry more than `†` and `*` (census at the end of `diffs.md`):

- `‡` is a **second mediant**, not a second flex. Ember renders DO's default `noflexa=1` (`packages/divinum-officium/src/hours/scripts.ts:110-121`): `‡ … *` becomes `* …` — the asterisk moves to the `‡` and the original `*` disappears — and every `†` is dropped. So `4:5` is prayed in the app as *Irascímini, et nolíte peccáre: \* quæ dícitis in córdibus vestris, in cubílibus vestris compungímini.* The port implements only that default, so how upstream shows `‡` with flexes switched on is not verified here; the source line itself keeps both marks, which reads as flex at `‡`, mediant at `*`. Until that is settled, treat a `‡` verse as having two pointings and make the Portuguese work under both — the app's one first. 13 such verses in `Psalmorum/` (psalms and canticles); 2 in the pilot (`4:5`, `90:15`).
- `(20)`, `(26a)`: inline biblical-verse numbers where a prayed verse crosses a boundary — 74 of them in 37 psalm files. With the 194 `a`/`b` suffixes, this is the full picture of "Breviary verse ≠ Bible verse".
- Invitatory and canticle only: `+` (flex), `=`, `^`, `_`, `/:(genuflectitur):/`, `$ant`/`$ant2`, `&Gloria`. Meaning of `=`, `^`, `_` not yet checked; settle it from the DO engine before the invitatory is translated.
