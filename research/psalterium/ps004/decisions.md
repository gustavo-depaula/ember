## Decided

- **Address: *vós*** — Gustavo, 2026-09-20, for the whole corpus. Consequence here: *exaudire* cannot be *ouvir* at 4:2b (*ouvi* = "I heard"), so the *exaudire* decision below is between *atender* and *escutar* — and the word study ([`../words/exaudire.md`](../words/exaudire.md)) recommends *escutar*, because *atender* is needed for *inténde*, which stands beside *exáudi* in five verses.

## The one that governs the rest: how strange may it sound?

The Latinist critic and both judges found nothing to fix. The stylist marked **8 of 10 verses "not native"** — "várias construções fazem ouvir o trabalho da tradução durante a oração". Nearly every fix it offered buys fluency by leaving the Latin:

| Verse | Draft | Stylist's fix | What the fix costs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4:3 | *até quando de coração pesado?* | *até quando tereis o coração pesado?* | Supplies a verb the Latin does not have |
| 4:4 | *fez maravilhoso o seu santo* | *fez maravilhas pelo seu santo* | Says what the Hebrew says, not *mirificávit sanctum suum* |
| 4:5 | *nos vossos leitos compungi-vos* | *arrependei-vos nos vossos leitos* | Loses the sting of *compungi*; moves the verb off the cadence |
| 4:6 | *Sacrificai um sacrifício* | *Oferecei um sacrifício* | Loses the Latin's own repetition |
| 4:8 | *se multiplicaram* | *eles se multiplicaram* | Small: names a subject the Latin leaves in the participle |
| 4:10 | *a sós na esperança* (draft 1: *singularmente*) | *só vós, Senhor* | Closes an ambiguity the Latin keeps open |

This is the README's first rule against the design doc's first style priority, and the pipeline cannot settle it. Whatever is decided here becomes the brief for every later psalm.

**Leaning (Gustavo, 2026-09-20):** "I really liked the stylist suggestions, they are really great." Not yet a rule. What it needs before it is one: which of the six fixes are taken. Five of them change *how* the Latin is said (a supplied verb, a plainer word, a named subject, a resolved ambiguity, a dropped repetition) and Matos Soares 1932 already does three of those. One changes *what* is said — 4:4 *fez maravilhas pelo seu santo* is the Hebrew's sense, not *mirificávit sanctum suum* — and that one Claude would still refuse under any setting of the dial.

## What the nearest antecedent did

Matos Soares 1932, from the Vulgate (consulted after draft 2 was written, so the agreements are independent; text in the gitignored `consult/ms1932-ps4.json`, from a PDF text layer). He addresses God as *tu*.

| Verse | Matos Soares 1932 | Bears on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4:2a | *na angústia me puseste ao largo* | *dilatásti mihi* — a fourth option, idiomatic, keeps the image of width and needs no supplied object |
| 4:2a–4 | *ouviu-me … ouve … me ouvirá* | *exaudire* → *ouvir* — safe under *tu*; under *vós* the *ouvi* trap stands |
| 4:3 | *até quando sereis de coração pesado?* | Keeps *de coração pesado*, but supplies the verb, as the stylist wanted |
| 4:4 | *fez maravilhoso o seu santo* | Identical to the draft |
| 4:5 | *Irai-vos, e não queirais pecar … compungi-vos no retiro dos vossos leitos* | Keeps *compungi-vos*; takes the *nolíte* calque the draft rejected, which also repairs the colon's length |
| 4:6 | *Oferecei sacrifícios de justiça … Quem nos fará ver os bens* | Drops the repetition (*Oferecei*); keeps *os bens* |
| 4:7 | *Gravada está, Senhor, sobre nós a luz do teu rosto* | Participle first, the Latin's order; *gravada* |
| 4:8 | *Eles estão bem abastecidos e alegres com a abundância…* | Paraphrase — names the subject, leaves the Latin |
| 4:9 | *Mas eu dormirei em paz e descansarei* | Leaves *in idípsum* untranslated |
| 4:10 | *de uma maneira singular me firmaste na esperança* | *singuláriter* adverbial and open; *firmar* for *constitúere* |

## Verse by verse

**4:2a — *dilatásti mihi*.** *me abristes espaço* (draft) · *me destes largueza* · *me alargastes*. The Claude judge counts *espaço* a minor addition: the Latin verb has no object. **Decision:**

**4:2a/2b/4:4 — *exaudire* → *atender*.** Chosen because *ouvi a minha oração* is also "I heard my prayer" under *vós*. This is a glossary entry for the whole psalter (*Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam*). Alternatives: *escutar*; *ouvir* if the address is *tu*. **Decision:**

**4:4 — *mirificávit*.** *fez maravilhoso* (draft; leaves *engrandecer* free for *magnificare*) · *tornou admirável* · *engrandeceu*. Recurs 15:3, 16:7, 30:22. **Decision:**

**4:5 — *compungímini*.** *compungi-vos* (draft) · *arrependei-vos*. Is *compungir-se* a word the person in the pew owns? **Decision:**

**4:6 — *bona*.** *os bens* (draft) can be heard as possessions (back-translator). *o bem* · *coisas boas* · *o que é bom*. **Decision:**

**4:7 — *Signátum est*.** Word: *marcada* (draft) · *assinalada* · *impressa* · *gravada*. Order: draft 2 puts the subject first for the ear; draft 1 kept the Latin's *Marcada está sobre nós a luz…*, which matches the facing column. **Decision:**

**4:8 — who multiplied?** The Latin's masculine plural excludes wheat, wine and oil; *se multiplicaram* does not. Leave it, or *eles se multiplicaram*. **Decision:**

**4:9 — *in idípsum*.** *a um só tempo* (draft 2) · *juntamente* (draft 1; two reviewers: "together with what?") · *nisso mesmo* (the calque; keeps Augustine's reading possible). Also at 33:4, 40:7, 61:10, 73:6, 121:3 — decide whether it must be one rendering everywhere. **Decision:**
