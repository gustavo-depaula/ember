Verse by verse

1

The psalm begins at 15:1 (the titulus Tituli inscríptio, ipsi David is out, D7). DO's 15:1 carries 15:2 after '‡' (second mediant); the inline '(2)' is not reproduced. speráre in → 'esperar em' (glossary), order as 7:2 'em vós esperei'. Deus meus es tu → 'Vós sois o meu Deus', as 117:28a (the same words also 21:11, 30:15, 139:7, 142:9 — grep). bonum (goods) → 'bens'.

3

The verse is kept as loose as the Latin: the dative 'Aos santos' is taken up by 'neles' at the end. The Latin's 'meas' (my wills) against the Greek's αὐτοῦ (his) is followed (rule 1).

4a

multiplicári → multiplicar-se (glossary); infírmitas → enfermidade (glossary row infírmus, which names 102:3 infirmitátes → enfermidades). eórum → 'deles', as open as the Latin (whose infirmities is not said).

4b

memor esse → lembrar-se de (glossary). nómina → nomes. The last colon fronts ‘com os meus lábios’ so that the verse ends on ‘nomes’ (the stylist, rule 4).

5

tu es, qui restítues → 'sois vós que me restituireis': the Latin turns from third person (Dóminus) to second (tu) within the verse, and so does the Portuguese. hereditátem meam mihi → 'me … a minha herança' (both kept). restitúere → restituir (34:17 restítue ánimam meam is the other place, grep).

6

funes → cordas (glossary). étenim → pois (glossary). mihi twice → 'me … para mim'.

7

benedícere + accusative → 'Bendirei o Senhor' (glossary). tríbuere … intelléctum → 'que me deu entendimento' (the glossary row bona tríbuere proposed exactly this for 15:7; intelléctus → entendimento).

8

a dextris est mihi → 'está à minha direita' (as 90:7 'à tua direita'; no subject supplied — the Lord of the first colon is heard). ne commóvear → 'para eu não ser abalado', the build of 118:80 'para eu não ser envergonhado' (commovéri with movéri → ser abalado: one Greek verb, σαλεύω, as 14:5b). Before any critic read it, draft 1 had 'porque ele está à minha direita, para que eu não seja abalado' (+9 syllables on the Latin); shortened by the infinitive and by leaving the subject in the verb, as the Latin does. Draft 2: ‘antevia’ replaced by ‘via’ (the Latinist’s major; the stylist and the blind reader agreed).

9

propter hoc → 'Por isso'; lætári / exsultáre → alegrar-se / exultar (glossary); ínsuper et → 'e ainda' (glossary, which wrote this colon); requiéscere → repousar (14:1); spes → esperança. = Acts 2:26 (fetched).

10

The first colon's 'abandonar' (derelínquere, glossary) leaves 'deixar' free for the second colon's idiom. sanctum tuum → 'o vosso santo', lowercase (divine pronouns and titles lowercase; ὅσιος).

11

Notas fecísti → 'Fizestes-me conhecer' (the formula for 76:15 Notam fecísti, 102:7 Notas fecit — grep). via → caminho. adimplére → encher (16:14b adimplétus est is the other place); 'vós' named so that the colon does not open on a clitic. lætítia → alegria (as 4:7 'destes alegria'). vultus → rosto (glossary). The verse = Acts 2:28 as far as 'vias vitæ' (Acts then reads 'replebis me jucunditate cum facie tua' — another Latin, fetched).
