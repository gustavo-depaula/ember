minor

“e as suas forças” et virtútem ejus

Latin 'virtutem' is singular; the plural 'forças' changes the number (and leans toward an interpretive 'troops'). The singular keeps the Latin's word as it stands.

proposedE sacudiu Faraó e a sua força no Mar Vermelho: * porque é para sempre a sua misericórdia.

❧ taken — the row's singular 'o seu poder' in place of his 'a sua força' (fortitúdo's word)

A faithful, close rendering that keeps the Latin's oddities (e.g. 'cum primogénitis eórum', 'in divisiónes', the accusative kings in 19-20) without smoothing them. The only fault found is a number change in v. 15 ('virtutem' rendered plural). Pointing marks match the Latin in every verse (a single mediant '*' throughout).

4

fails: native

“Aquele que faz grandes maravilhas, só ele”

After 'Dai graças ao Senhor', the relative lines that follow are heard as continuing the dative. Written 'Aquele' makes them a separate subject that never gets a verb. The sound is the same, but on the page it reads like a broken sentence. This affects every 'Aquele que' line (4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 13, 16, 17, 25).

proposedÀquele que faz grandes maravilhas, só ele

❧ taken

8

fails: sayable

“para o domínio do dia”

'domínio do dia' runs three d-onsets together in a row (do-, do, di-). In chant the tongue stumbles right before the mediant.

proposedO sol para o domínio sobre o dia

kept as an option under in potestátem diéi / noctis — adds a preposition for the Latin's genitive; the blind reader heard the sense at once

12

fails: plain, concrete

“braço excelso”

'excelso' is a bookish Latinism and turns the physical image of a raised arm into a word for loftiness. 'Levantado' is plain, keeps the arm lifted high, and still takes a paroxytone stress at the mediant.

proposedCom mão poderosa e braço levantado

❧ taken

23

fails: native

“Porque na nossa humilhação se lembrou de nós: * porque é para sempre”

'Porque' at the start and 'porque' again after the mediant sound like a stutter. The Latin separates them (quia / quoniam), so the Portuguese can separate them too.

proposedPois na nossa humilhação se lembrou de nós

❧ taken

The litany sounds plain, steady and dignified. The refrain 'porque é para sempre a sua misericórdia' is easy to say 27 times, and its paroxytone close sits well on the psalm tone. The short narrative lines keep the Latin's concrete images (feriu, sacudiu, partiu em partes) without sounding translated. The few problems are small ones of grammar and wording, not of rhythm.

best line135:6

12

minor

“braço levantado” brácchio excélso

excelsus is 'lofty, high, exalted' (a state), not the participle of an action; 'levantado' is defensible but drifts slightly toward 'raised up' and toward the Hebrew-English 'outstretched/uplifted' tradition.

proposedCom mão poderosa e braço excelso: * porque é para sempre a sua misericórdia.

kept as an option under brácchio excélso — held: the stylist and the ambiguity reader (who did not know 'excelso') both asked the concrete arm; rule 5 keeps images concrete; the row was built for excélsus of God. 'excelso' is option 3, one click.

23

minor

“na nossa humilhação” in humilitáte nostra

humilitas here is lowliness, low estate; 'humilhação' adds the nuance of being humiliated by an agent. Defensible, but 'baixeza' or 'abatimento' is closer to the state the Latin names.

proposedPois no nosso abatimento se lembrou de nós: * porque é para sempre a sua misericórdia.

kept as an option under in humilitáte nostra — held: the humílitas row (Pss 9, 24, 30, 89, 118) in its eleventh place; the CNBB Bible has the same word here; 'abatimento' (MS1932) is option 3.

A faithful rendering throughout: the Latin oddities are kept (cum primogenitis eorum, divisit in divisiones rendered as partiu em partes, excussit as sacudiu), the dative relative clauses are sensibly supplied with 'Àquele que' from the governing Confitemini, and the refrain is rendered uniformly. The pointing marks (a single mediant in every verse) match the Latin exactly. Only two minor lexical points, neither of which alters the sense.
