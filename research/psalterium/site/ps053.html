Verse by verse

3

The psalm begins at 53:3 (53:1–2 are the title, which the Office does not say). júdica me → 'julgai-me', bare, as in the Latin; Matos Soares 1932 and DM1962 expand it ('julga a minha causa', 'defende a minha causa') — see the decision 'judica', added after the ambiguity reader. 'julgai' is safe under rule 3 (past 'julguei'). 'no vosso nome' kept against the stylist's 'em vosso nome' (decision 'in'). First colon 3 syllables short of the Latin by the checker, with 'salvai-me'; accepted.

4

exáudi → 'escutai' (glossary, working): the formula 'Senhor, escutai a minha oração' with the Latin's vocative, 'Deus'. verba oris mei → 'as palavras da minha boca': the mouth stays (DM1962 'lábios' is the Hebrew-family habit). The second colon is the glossary proposal for áuribus percípere — see the decision.

5

No flex in the Latin, so none here: the first colon is the longest of the psalm (28 syllables in Latin by the checker) and is said on the tenor. Draft 2 takes two of the stylist's three proposals for this verse, both matters of grammar: the bare plurals 'estranhos … fortes' (decision 'alieni') and 'puseram Deus' without 'a' (decision 'deum'). His other two changes in the same line are refused: 'Pois' for 'Porque', because Quóniam is 'Porque' three times in the psalm (53:5, 53:8, 53:9) as in the Latin, and 'Pois' is kept for enim in 53:6; and 'minha alma' without the article, against the glossary (a minha alma). quæsiérunt ánimam meam → 'buscaram a minha alma': ánima mea → a minha alma (glossary), not 'vida' (Matos Soares 1932, pt-PT, DM1962) — see the decision 'animam'; quǽrere → buscar as in Ps 4:3. proposuérunt → 'puseram … diante': the pro- is carried by 'diante'; 'propuseram' would mean 'proposed'. The ambiguity reader asked whether 'estranhos' are unknown people or odd ones, whether 'fortes' are strong in body or in power, and whether both groups or only the second forgot God: each time the Latin is open in the same way (aliéni, fortes, one plural verb), and the likelier hearing was the right one; nothing changed.

6

Ecce enim → 'Pois eis que' (ἰδοὺ γάρ); Matos Soares 1932 has 'Mas eis que', which is not enim. ádjuvat is a present: 'me auxilia'. 'est' kept as 'é'. ánimæ meæ → 'da minha alma'.

7

The Latin has an imperative (Avérte) where the LXX has a future; the Latin is followed. véritas → 'verdade' (glossary, Ps 90:5), not the Hebrew-family 'fidelidade' (DM1962) — see the decision 'veritate'; 'in veritáte tua' → 'na vossa verdade', the same 'in' as in 53:3, and kept before the verb as in the Latin: the verse closes on the imperative. mala → 'os males', plural and concrete; the ambiguity reader heard 'sufferings, dangers' first and 'wicked deeds' second, which is the range of mala. He also asked whether 'os' in 'exterminai-os' are the enemies or the evils: in the Latin illos is masculine and mala neuter, so it is the enemies, and that is what he heard first.

8

sacrificábo tibi → 'sacrificarei a vós': sacrificáre → sacrificar as in Ps 4:6; the stylist's 'oferecerei sacrifício' is an option (decision 'sacrificabo'). The stressed 'a vós' after the verb because 'vos sacrificarei' can be heard as 'I will sacrifice you' (pt-PT's 'Sacrificar-me-ei … a Vós' is a slip of another kind). Draft 2 moves the vocative: 'e darei graças, Senhor, ao vosso nome: porque é bom' (decision 'v8b'), so that the name and not 'Senhor' stands before 'porque é bom'. The verse ends on a colon, as in the Latin, because 53:9 depends on it; 'porque … : / Porque …' repeats as 'quóniam … : / Quóniam …' does (Matos Soares 1932 varies it: 'porque … Porquanto'). Second colon 2 syllables short of the Latin by the checker: nothing is left out. Douay-Rheims reads 'O God' where this Latin has Dómine; the Latin is followed. confitébor → 'darei graças' is the glossary proposal of this psalm (decision 'confitebor').

9

eripuísti is a perfect: 'me arrancastes' (Matos Soares' 'tens-me livrado' is iterative in Brazil). 'de toda tribulação' without the article, which in Brazil is 'every' (ex omni), where 'toda a tribulação' would be 'the whole of the'. tribulátio → tribulação (glossary). First colon 4 short of the Latin, accepted; naming the subject ('vós me arrancastes') would mend the count and is unnecessary. óculus meus → 'o meu olho', singular as in the Latin (Matos Soares 1932 and DM1962 make it plural). Second colon: draft 2 takes the stylist's natural order, with 'olho olhou' side by side — see the decision 'despexit', where draft 1's line is the second option.
