1

fails: no rhyme

“de todo o meu coração: * no conselho dos justos, e na congregação.”

Mediant and final both land on -ção (coração / congregação), an accidental rhyme that chimes loudly under the psalm tone; 'congregação' also sounds bureaucratic or religious-order in Brazilian ears.

proposedEu vos darei graças, Senhor, de todo o meu coração: * no conselho dos justos, e na assembleia.

kept as an option under in consílio justórum, et congregatióne — *assembleia* is *ecclésia*'s (D34); the rhyme is fixed by the 9:2 formula and the row

2

fails: native, plain

“procuradas segundo todas as suas vontades”

'Procuradas' reads as 'searched for / in demand' and the colon doesn't parse on first hearing; 'todas as suas vontades' in the plural sounds foreign. The line sounds like a gloss, not like prayer.

proposedGrandes são as obras do Senhor: * primorosas segundo todas as suas vontades.

kept as an option under exquisíta in omnes voluntátes ejus — takes the adjectival 'exquisite' sense and drops the seeking of *exquisíta* / ἐξεζητημένα; *segundo* is the closing the Latinist refused

3

fails: native

“Confissão e magnificência”

In current Brazilian usage 'confissão' means the sacrament or an admission of guilt; opening the verse with it makes the ear hear 'his work is a confession'. The sense here is praise.

proposedLouvor e magnificência é a sua obra: * e a sua justiça permanece pelos séculos dos séculos.

kept as an option under Conféssio et magnificéntia opus ejus — 95:6 / 103:1b twins rule *confissão*; *louvor* is *laudátio*'s in 110:10b

The psalm sounds sober, even and prayable: the short declarative cola fall naturally and the refrains 'pelos séculos dos séculos' anchor it well. Its weak spots are a few Latinate calques in the opening verses (procuradas, confissão, congregação) that sound translated rather than prayed; from v.4 on it is clean and dignified.

best line110:9b

Verse by verse

1

Soft flag accepted: *coração / congregação* rhyme at mediant and final, which the Latin (*meo / congregatióne*) does not have. Both words are fixed by rulings — the first colon copies 9:2 (D5 formula), *congregação* is the *congregátio* row — and the only escapes are to reorder the Latin's two nouns or to spend *assembleia* (*ecclésia*'s, D34). Left for the stylist's ear.

2

*Grandes são as obras do Senhor*: copula supplied (D2); MS1932 word for word. *voluntátes* plural kept (*vontades*, as 15:3 *todas as minhas vontades*). Draft 2 had *em* (v1 gate); draft 3 returns to *segundo* (v2 gate; decision `exquisita`).

3

The second colon *e a sua justiça permanece pelos séculos dos séculos* is repeated in 110:10b with *o seu louvor*, as the Latin repeats *manet in sǽculum sǽculi*; *pelos séculos dos séculos* (D27) ends on a proparoxytone, as *sǽculi* does.

4

*escam dedit timéntibus se → deu alimento aos que o temem*: *esca* given → *alimento* (the *esca* row; 103:27); MS1932 word for word.

5b

*virtútem óperum suórum annuntiábit pópulo suo* → *anunciará ao seu povo o poder das suas obras*: *annuntiáre → anunciar* (D41), *virtus → poder* (row); the Latin's future kept (the Greek's aorist ἀνήγγειλεν is not followed, rule 1); natural order puts the indirect object first. The verse ends in a colon, as the Latin does, because 110:7 continues it.

7

*Ut det illis → Para lhes dar* (purpose; proclisis after *para*). *véritas, et judícium → verdade e juízo*: the rows (*juízo*, not the Lectionary's *justiça*, which is *justítia*'s, 110:3). Copula supplied.

8

*Fidélia → Fiéis são* (copula supplied, the Latin's order); *confirmáta → confirmados* (the *confirmáre* row: with things as object the cognate is right; the Lectionary has *confirmados* too); *in veritáte et æquitáte → em verdade e equidade*, no articles, as the Latin (*equidade* is the row's word, noted unknown by some readers; kept). The mediant falls on *séculos* (proparoxytone), as the Latin's *sǽculi*: the formula is not bent for the cadence.

9a

*Redemptiónem → redenção* (the *redímere* row: the noun keeps the Christian word), without an article as the Latin; MS1932 *a redenção*. *misit → Enviou*.

9b

*Sanctum, et terríbile nomen ejus → Santo e terrível é o seu nome*: *terríbilis → terrível* (D43; the row names 110:9), the Latin's order, copula supplied; MS1932 word for word. The ambiguity reader heard *terrível* partly as "horrible" and *temor* as being afraid of God — both the rows' known costs (D43; *timor → temor*), kept.
