Verse by verse

1

The soul is a singular addressee: *tu* (*Bendize*). *intra me → dentro de mim*.

3

*sanáre → curar* (row); *infirmitátes → enfermidades* (the infírmus row names this verse). **propitiári: flagged for Gustavo** (see decision).

4

*redímere → resgatar* (row). *coronáre → coroar* (row) with *de*, as 8:6 *de glória e de honra o coroastes*. *miseratiónes → compaixões* (row; side by side with *misericórdia* as 24:6, 50:3).

5

*juvéntus → juventude* (42:4). The verse ends with a colon, as the Latin, running into 102:6.

6

*Fáciens* made a finite verb (grammar); *misericórdias* plural as the Latin (ἐλεημοσύνας; MS1932 *faz misericórdias*).

7

*Notas fecit → Fez conhecer* (the notum fácere row: 15:11, 76:15, 97:2). No *e* between the colons, as the Latin (MS1932 adds one). *Móyses → Moisés* (row). *voluntátes* plural kept (*vontades*, MS1932).

8

144:8 has the same Latin and should copy this verse. *multum miséricors → muito misericordioso*, not 85:15's *de muita misericórdia* (a different Latin, as the multæ misericórdiæ row warns).

9

*irascétur → estará irado*, the copula future of 79:5 / 84:6 (*se irará* is a knot of r's). *comminári → ameaçar*.

10

*fácere + dative → tratar* (the fácere cum row: *Tratai o vosso servo*); MS1932 *Não nos tratou*. *retríbuit → retribuiu* (17:21).

11

*secúndum altitúdinem → segundo a altura* (the altitúdo row names this verse: the concrete word). *a terra* ('from the earth') → *acima da terra*, the height measured upward. *super timéntes se → sobre os que o temem*.

12

*ortus / occidens → oriente / ocidente* (MS1932): *ortus* alone is the rising quarter; 49:1's *Desde o nascer do sol até o poente* is a different phrase (*a solis ortu usque ad occásum*).

13

First colon long (the Latin's is long too).

14

*Recordátus est → Lembrou-se de que*. *pulvis → pó* (row). *efflorébit → florescerá*; the prefix of ἐξανθήσει is not rendered separately (*desabrochar* would lose the root of *flos*).

16

*ámplius non → já não*, as 38:14. *cognóscet → conhecerá* (with an object, the row). *subsístet → subsistirá* (MS1932). No subject supplied for *subsistirá*: the man or the flower, as the Latin.

17

*ab ætérno … usque in ætérnum → desde sempre … para todo o sempre* — D37 for *usque in ætérnum* (the row names 102:17), and *desde sempre* for the first member as 40:14 *a sǽculo* (D41). *autem → Mas*. Copula supplied (*é*).

17b

*justítia → justiça*; *filii filiórum → os filhos dos filhos* (MS1932). The verse ends with a colon, as the Latin.

18

*mémores sunt → se lembram* (memor esse row). *mandáta → mandamentos* (D15). v2: *E se lembram* was heard by the ambiguity reader as 'and if they remember' (conditional *se*); enclisis *E lembram-se* cannot be. *E que se lembram* (a relative continuing 102:17b) is the alternative the Latin's main clause does not ask for.

19

*sedes → trono* (row, which names 102:19); *paráre → preparar* (row). The Latin's order *in cælo parávit* kept with the place before the object.

20

*ángelus → anjo*, lowercase (row); *todos os seus anjos* as 96:7b. The participles addressed to the angels become *que* + second person plural (plural addressee, *vós*). Second colon long, as the Latin's.

21

*minístri → ministros*; bare vocative *seus ministros*.

22

*ómnia ópera ejus → todas as suas obras* (row). *in omni loco → em todo lugar* (every place; *em todo o lugar* is 'in the whole place'). *dominátio → domínio*. The last words repeat 102:1a.
