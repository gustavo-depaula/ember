“escorreram à face do Senhor” fluxerunt a facie Domini

'a facie' is 'from before / at the presence of' (ablative of source/cause); 'à face' reads more like 'in front of, facing'. Defensible, but 'diante da face' is closer. Applies to both occurrences.

proposedOs montes, como cera, escorreram diante da face do Senhor: * diante da face do Senhor, toda a terra.

kept as an option under Montes, sicut cera fluxérunt a fácie Dómini: * a fácie Dómini — 67:3 (the same wax image) has à face de, passed the Latinist twice; +4 syllables per colon

2

minor

“a firmeza do seu trono” corréctio sedis ejus

Correctio (LXX katorthōsis) is the act of setting upright / establishing, not a quality; 'firmeza' turns it into a static attribute. Defensible, but 'estabelecimento' or 'retidão' stays closer to the Latin noun of action.

proposedjustiça e juízo são o estabelecimento do seu trono.

kept as an option under corréctio sedis ejus — estabelecimento is heard as a shop or business; retidão is diréctio's

12

minor

“à memória da sua santidade” memóriæ sanctificatiónis ejus

Latin has sanctificatio, not sanctitas; 'santidade' is the right sense (LXX hagiōsynē) but 'santificação' keeps the Latin word the psalter chose.

proposede dai graças à memória da sua santificação.

kept as an option under memóriæ sanctificatiónis ejus — one Greek word (ἁγιωσύνη) with 29:5's sanctitátis; D15's test; repeated from v1

A faithful rendering: tenses (praecedet/inflammabit future, custodit present vs liberabit future), the elliptical 'a facie Domini omnis terra' and the Septuagintal verse division of 7b/8 are all kept, and the pointing marks match the Latin in every verse. Only two minor lexical points, neither of which changes the sense.

Verse by verse

1

*exsúltet / læténtur → exulte / alegrem-se* (the exsultáre / lætári row).

2

Both copulas supplied (the Latin is verbless in both colons), as 92:2. *in circúitu ejus → ao seu redor* (75:12's form). *sedes → trono* (the row).

3

*Ignis* without article → *Um fogo* (as 38:4 *um fogo se inflamará*); MS1932 *O fogo*. *in circúitu → ao redor* with no object, as 11:9. v2: *abrasará → queimará* (decision `inflammabit`).

4

Copies 76:19 *Os vossos relâmpagos iluminaram o mundo: * a terra se abalou e estremeceu*: *fúlgura* here and *coruscatiónes* there are one Greek word (ἀστραπαί), so one Portuguese word (D15; 17:15 *relâmpagos* for *fúlgura* too). *orbis terræ → o mundo* (D30). *commóta est → se abalou* (76:19, 17:8). Subject named first in the second colon (natural order).

6

Plain; natural order (subject first).

7

*Confundántur → Sejam envergonhados* (D15; 34:4). The first colon runs long for *imagens esculpidas* (see decision). *gloriári in → gloriar-se em*: the row kept the verb for exactly this place (sinners' boasting).

7b

*ángelus → anjo*, lowercase. *Adorai-o* is safe under rule 3 (past *adorei*). Draft 1 had *Adorai-o, vós, todos os seus anjos* (the 29:5 / 30:24 build); the stylist (v1) found the inserted *vós* a stumble that blurs the address in a psalm where God is *vós*. Taken in v2: here the object *-o* is already expressed, so *todos os seus anjos* can only be the vocative — the reason *vós* was supplied at 29:5 and 30:24 (a bare noun that could be heard as the object) does not apply. The line is quoted at Heb 1:6.

8

Second colon identical to 47:12b (*por causa dos vossos juízos, Senhor*); *exsultáre → exultar* as 47:12a. The psalm turns to address the Lord here (*tua*), so *vossos*.

9

*tu Dóminus Altíssimus → vós sois o Senhor altíssimo*: copula supplied; lowercase adjective as 7:18 (*altíssimo Senhor*), after the noun here because the colon's cadence is on *terra*. *super … super → sobre … sobre*, the Latin's repetition kept.

10

*odíte → odiai* (safe: past *odiei*). *Vós que amais* names the plural humans (D1, Ps 10:2 remedy) — the Lord is in the third person here, so no confusion with the address of 96:8–9. *sanctus (ὅσιος) → santo*; *peccátor* singular kept.

11

*justo* singular kept (MS1932 pluralizes). *rectus corde → reto de coração* (the row). Order: *e a alegria para os retos de coração* — the Latin ends on *lætítia*; the natural order closes on the oxytone *coração*.

12

*Lætámini, justi, in Dómino* in the Latin's order with *ó* before the vocative (31:11, 32:1 have *ó justos*). *confitéri → dar graças a* (D5). Second colon = 29:5b.
