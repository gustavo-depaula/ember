Verse by verse

2

*dolósus → enganador* (the dolus row; 51:6 *língua enganadora*).

3

*gratis → sem motivo* (row; 34:7, 68:5). *expugnáre → combater* (34:1). *sermo → palavra* (D15). The Latin's plural *locúti sunt* after a singular *lingua dolósa* kept.

4

*Pro eo ut me dilígerent* → *Em vez de me amarem*: the Latin makes them the subject (the Greek infinitive and the Hebrew are 'in return for my love'; the Latin is followed). *detráhere → difamar* (D39), also 108:20 and 108:29. *ego autem → eu, porém,* (row).

6

*constitúere → estabelecer* (row); *Estabelecei* is safe under rule 3. *a dextris ejus* here and *a dextris páuperis* in 108:31 both say *à … direita*: the psalm's turn (the devil at the wicked man's right, the Lord at the poor man's) is kept.

7

*Cum judicátur* → *Quando for julgado* (the Portuguese future subjunctive for a general 'when'). *fiat in peccátum* → *se torne pecado* (*fíeri* in the sense of becoming: row 544).

9

*Sejam órfãos os seus filhos: * e a sua mulher, viúva* — the Latin's gapped verb kept, marked by a comma. *órphanus / pupíllus → órfão* (row), also 108:12.

10

*eícere → lançar fora* (row, 79:9). *habitátio → habitação* (row; *morada* is habitáculum's, D44).

11

*aliéni → estranhos* (row), no article, as the Latin. *labóres ejus → os seus trabalhos*: the fruit of his toil, as 77:46 (a local departure from the row's *fadiga*, which there was heard as tiredness). v2: subject first (decision fenerator).

13

*delére → apagar* (row); 108:13 and 108:14 have the same form *deleátur* and say *se apague* both times.

14

*in conspéctu Dómini → à vista do Senhor* (the row's proposal, 5:9; no verb of motion here). *In memóriam rédeat → Volte à memória*: *memória* also in 108:15, where the Latin repeats it.

15

*dispéreat → pereça* (decision dispereat). *eórum → deles*, apart from the singular *ejus → seu* of the curses. *fácere misericórdiam → fazer misericórdia* (102:6 *faz misericórdias*). The subject of *Estejam* is unnamed, as in the Latin (the sins of 108:14); the ambiguity reader heard it as people, but naming it would add a word. The Latin's 108:15 includes the Greek and Hebrew v. 16; DO has no 108:16.

17

*inops → carente* (D38), *mendícus → mendigo* (D41), *compúnctus corde → compungido de coração* (the passive participle of the compúngi row, 29:13, 34:16). *mortificáre → dar a morte* (row); *lhe* is supplied, as 36:32.

18

*elongáre → ficar longe* (row). The future tenses of *véniet … elongábitur* kept after the perfects: the Latin's own turn from what he did to what will come. *ela* supplied for the curse and the blessing in turn, so that each second half is not heard as 'he will come'.

18b

*índuit maledictiónem* → *vestiu a maldição* (transitive, as the Latin: he put it on). *vestiméntum → veste* (D43), also 108:19.

19

*Fiat ei → Seja para ele* (the *Fiat* row). *zona → cinto*: Brazilian *cinta* is also a girdle worn under clothes. *præcíngere → cingir* (17:33, 64:7).

20

*apud Dóminum → junto do Senhor* (the apud row, 36:23; the Greek παρὰ κυρίου 'from the Lord' is not followed). *loqui mala advérsus → falar males contra* (as *mala mihi*, row 1007).

21

*Dómine, Dómine* → *Senhor, Senhor*: the doubled cry is the Latin's and the Greek's, so it is kept. The 8:2a row refused a doubling there only because the Latin had *Dómine, Dóminus noster*. *suávis → suave* (row).

22

*egénus → necessitado*, *pauper → pobre* (D38). *conturbáre → perturbar* (row). *intra me → dentro de mim* (38:4).

23

*declináre* (of the shadow) → *declinar*, as 101:12 *Os meus dias declinaram como a sombra*. *locústa → gafanhoto* (77:46, 104:34).

24

*infirmári → enfraquecer* (the infírmus / languére rows). *a jejúnio → pelo jejum*: the Latin's *a* and *propter* differ, and so do *pelo* and *por causa*.

25

*oppróbrium → afronta* (row); *factus sum oppróbrium illis → me tornei uma afronta para eles* (30:12, 78:4). *movérunt cápita sua → moveram as suas cabeças*: the plural of the Latin, where 21:8 has the singular *caput*.

26

The formula *Senhor, meu Deus* (row). *adjuváre → auxiliar* (row), *salvum me fac → salvai-me* (row).

28

*insúrgere in → levantar-se contra* (row). *confúndi → ser envergonhado* (D15); *sejam envergonhados* comes first, where Portuguese puts a jussive. The Latin's second colon is 17 syllables, and the Portuguese is longer because D15's verb is. Accepted.

29

*pudor → desonra*, *confúsio → vergonha*: the pair as 70:13 *sejam cobertos de vergonha e de desonra* (ἐντροπή / αἰσχύνη, the reveréri row). *Induántur … pudóre* → *Vistam-se de desonra*, as 34:26b.

31

*astáre a dextris → pôr-se à direita* (the astáre row's split, 44:10b). *a persequéntibus → dos que a perseguem*: *a* is supplied (the Latin's participle has no object). DRB 'from persecutors'.
