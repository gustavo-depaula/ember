1

The psalm begins at 14:1 (the titulus Psalmus David is out, D7). Two questions, each 'quem' as the Latin's 'quis'. Ps 23:3 (Quis ascéndet in montem Dómini? aut quis stabit in loco sancto ejus?) is the cousin of this verse, not a twin.

2

operári justítiam → 'praticar a justiça' (glossary: operári → praticar; the row fácere judícium names 14:2).

3a

loqui veritátem → ‘falar a verdade’ (véritas → verdade, glossary); in corde suo → ‘no seu coração’, as the Latin. in lingua sua → ‘com a sua língua’: the instrumental in, as linguis suis in 13:3b.

3b

Nec → 'nem', continuing the list of 14:3a. The Greek has two words for 'neighbour' (πλησίον, then ἔγγιστα); the Latin has próximus twice and so does the Portuguese.

4a

autem → 'mas'.

4b

The inline '(5a)' of DO is not reproduced (rule 4). próximus → 'próximo' (not at a cadence here). accépit → 'aceitou', the same verb as 14:3b. innocens → 'inocente' (innocéntia → inocência, glossary). The second colon is long (the Latin's is 27 syllables).

5b

Qui facit hæc → 'Aquele que faz estas coisas' (hæc, neuter plural).
