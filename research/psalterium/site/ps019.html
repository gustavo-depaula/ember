Verse by verse

2

The psalm speaks to the king (a single human addressee → tu, decided); God is spoken of in the third person until 19:10, where he is addressed (vós). protégere → proteger (glossary). 'nomen Dei Jacob' → 'o nome do Deus de Jacó'. The jussives are said with the subject first ('O Senhor te escute'; 'o nome … te proteja').

3

The Latin names no subject for Mittat; the Lord of 19:2 is heard, as in the Latin. 'Envie-te' opens the series of enclitic jussives (19:3–5).

4

memor esse → lembrar-se de (glossary). holocáustum → holocausto (7 lines, grep).

6

7b

8

9
