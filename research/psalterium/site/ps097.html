1b

fails: native, sayable

“A sua direita salvou para ele”

'salvou para ele' sounds like a gloss, and with no object the 'ele' makes the ear look for someone else being saved. Latin 'sibi' is reflexive, and in Portuguese that is 'para si'. That also keeps the colon to one clean breath.

proposedA sua direita salvou para si, * e o seu braço santo.

❧ taken

2

fails: native

“fez conhecer a sua salvação”

'fez conhecer' is a calque of 'notum fecit'. The ordinary, dignified Portuguese idiom is 'deu a conhecer'. It says the same thing and moves better into 'a sua salvação'.

proposedO Senhor deu a conhecer a sua salvação: * à vista das nações revelou a sua justiça.

kept as an option under Notum fecit Dóminus — 76:15 renders the identical Latin Notam fecísti as Fizestes conhecer (and 15:11); rule 6 — changing it here would split one Latin phrase in two

5

fails: sayable

“e voz de trombeta de chifre”

Without an article, 'e voz de' sounds clipped after the fuller 'com trombetas de metal batido', and 'de... de...' piles up at the end. Adding the article 'e a voz' eases the breath at no cost to the sense, and it matches 'com a cítara'.

proposedEntoai salmos ao Senhor com a cítara, com a cítara e a voz do salmo: * com trombetas de metal batido, e a voz da trombeta de chifre.

❧ taken

8

fails: sayable, native

“juntos os montes exultarão”

After the comma, 'juntos' dangles between the two clauses. The reader cannot tell whether it goes with the rivers or the mountains until after the verb. Putting 'juntos' after the verb sets it with the mountains and keeps the Latin 'simul'.

proposedOs rios baterão palmas, os montes exultarão juntos à vista do Senhor: * porque vem julgar a terra.

❧ taken

A bright, plain psalm that chants well. The imperatives in 97:4-6 have real drive, and the closing pair of 97:9 is clean and weighty. The few stumbles come from Latin syntax carried over too literally ('salvou para ele', 'fez conhecer'), and in 97:8 an adverb sits in an ambiguous place.

worst line97:1b

best line97:9

Verse by verse

1

*Cantáte Dómino cánticum novum* → *Cantai ao Senhor um cântico novo*, the formula of 95:1 and 149:1 (95 drafted in parallel with the same words; 32:3 *Cantai-lhe um cântico novo*). *quia mirabília fecit → porque fez maravilhas*, as 77:12, 85:10.

1b

*déxtera → a direita* (row, open; 43:4b *a vossa direita, e o vosso braço*). *brácchium sanctum → o seu braço santo*, adjective after, as *monte santo*. The second colon is elliptic, as the Latin: the arm is a second subject of *salvou*.

2

*Notum fecit → fez conhecer*, as 76:15 *Fizestes conhecer*; subject first, since *Fez conhecer o Senhor* would make the Lord the object. *salutáre → salvação* (D6), three times in the psalm (97:1b's root, 97:2, 97:3b).

3

*Recordátus est → Lembrou-se*, as 77:39. *véritas → verdade* (row).

3b

*salutáre Dei nostri → a salvação do nosso Deus* (D6). Isaiah 52:10 has the same words; not checked here.

4

First colon = 65:1 *Aclamai a Deus, toda a terra* (D43). *psállite → entoai salmos* (D25). The Latin's polysyndeton *cantáte, et exsultáte, et psállite* kept.

5

*Psállite … psalmi*: the Latin's root echo survives in *entoai salmos … voz de salmo*. *cíthara → cítara* (row, open; known to be unknown to blind readers, kept). *cítara* repeated as the Latin repeats it. First colon runs a few syllables over the Latin (the price of *entoar salmos*, D25).

6

DO joins 97:6 and 97:7 into one prayed verse with an inner colon (':'), not a mark; kept as one verse. *orbis terrárum → o mundo* (D30); *et qui hábitant in eo → e os que nele habitam*, as 23:1 (which has *univérsi*, not here).

8

Second colon = 95:12b *porque vem julgar a terra*. *exsultáre → exultar*.

9

As 9:8b and 95:13: *julgará o mundo … com equidade*, *in justítia → com justiça* (9:8b). The first colon is four syllables under the Latin (D30's cost: *orbem terrárum* is one word, *mundo*).
