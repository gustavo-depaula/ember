6b

minor

“Narraram que esconderiam laços” Narravérunt ut abscónderent láqueos

The ut-clause is final/object of the telling ('they talked, discussed how to hide snares'; LXX τοῦ κρύψαι), not reported speech of a future intention. 'narraram que esconderiam' turns it into an indirect statement ('they said that they would hide'), a slight shift of construction and mood.

proposedNarraram como esconderiam laços

kept as an option under Narravérunt ut abscónderent láqueos — the v1 Latinist asked the opposite (*como* → *que*); held at *que*, *como* one touch away

minor

'seu' is not in the Latin; scrutinio is a bare ablative ('in / by the search'). Small addition that narrows the reference.

proposeddesfaleceram os que sondavam, no sondar

Only two minor points: the construction of narraverunt ut in 63:6b and an added possessive in 63:7.

8

minor

“As feridas deles tornaram-se setas de pequeninos” Sagíttæ parvulórum factæ sunt plagæ eórum

Both nouns are nominative and the Latin leaves open which is subject and which predicate (Douay: 'The arrows of children are their wounds'). The translation reverses the order and fixes 'plagæ eorum' as subject, following the LXX article rather than the Latin, which keeps the ambiguity. Keeping the Latin order preserves it.

proposedSetas de pequeninos tornaram-se as feridas deles

kept as an option under Sagíttæ parvulórum factæ sunt plagæ eórum — The stylist named the Latin order the worst line and the blind reader could not say what it meant; the parse is the Greek’s (αἱ πληγαὶ αὐτῶν), which the Latin translates. Draft 1’s order is option 2.

A close, faithful rendering: tenses, persons, numbers and the concrete images (sharpened tongues, bow, snares, searching with a search) are kept, and the Septuagintal oddities of vv. 7-8 are not smoothed away. The only point is v. 8, where the reversed word order resolves a subject/predicate ambiguity the Latin keeps. Pointing marks match the Latin in every verse.

Verse by verse

2

*Exáudi → escutai* (D3); *cum déprecor → quando suplico* (*deprecári → suplicar*, D35), no object supplied, as the Latin. The Introit of 04-25 and the martyrs' Common open with this verse.

3

*Protexísti* with the enclitic at the head of the verse (*Protegestes-me*); *operántium iniquitátem → dos que praticam a iniquidade* (row *operári iniquitátem*). The verse is the Paschaltide martyrs' Introit (*Protexísti me, Deus, a convéntu malignántium*).

4

*exacuérunt → afiaram* (D44: *afiado* for an edge); *ut gládium → como espada* (v2: the stylist asked the article away; the Latin has none), with the object moved before the comparison, Portuguese order. *intendérunt arcum → armaram o arco* copies 10:3 (row). The second colon is long, as the Latin's is. Checks: +3 on the second colon, accepted.

6

Ps 63:5 has no verse of its own in DO; the Latin numbers 63:4 then 63:6. *Sermo → palavra* (D15).

6b

*Quis vidébit eos?* → *Quem os verá?* — *eos* can be the snares or the plotters, as in Portuguese.

7b

*exaltábitur → será exaltado*, the future passive (17:47's *exalte-se* is a jussive). The first colon is +8 syllables in v2 (checks): *cor altum* is three Latin syllables, *um coração profundo* seven, and *se aproximará de* (the Latinist's fix, = 33:6) is longer than *chegará a*; no shorter faithful phrase was found. Accepted; the colon is still one breath.

8

*infirmátæ sunt → se enfraqueceram* (17:37, 26:2b, 30:11b). *Contra eos* kept: their tongues grew weak against themselves. v2's order removes draft 1's *deles … eles* echo at mediant and final.

9

*conturbáti sunt → perturbaram-se* (47:6); *omnis homo → todo homem*, verb first as the Latin and 38:12b. v2 (the stylist): *todos aqueles que os viam* for the stutter *os que os*; *e temeu todo homem* for a fuller close than the clipped *todo homem temeu*.

10

*Et annuntiavérunt → E anunciaram* (D41); the initial *Et* kept. *facta → feitos*; *intellégere → entender*.

11

The versicle and responsory of the martyrs (*Lætábitur justus in Dómino, et sperábit in eo. R. Et laudabúntur omnes recti corde*); each half reads alone. *speráre in → esperar em* (D36); *recti corde → retos de coração* (row). Second colon +4 (checks): *retos de coração* is the settled phrase.

7

The second colon is +3 (checks): the participle *scrutántes* needs *os que sondavam*, and the root is kept a third time; accepted.
