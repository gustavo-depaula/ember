claude-opus-5-5 (fresh context, with latin.json) · 2026-09-22 · critic/v1.latinist.json · read prayed.vos.json

1

minor

“para sempre não serei envergonhado” non confúndar in ætérnum

confúndar is formally both future indicative and present subjunctive; after 'In te speravi' the Breviary tradition reads it as a wish ('let me not be confounded'). Rendering it as a flat future indicative settles the form one way. Defensible, but a wish is closer to the usual reading.

proposedEm vós, Senhor, esperei: que eu não seja envergonhado para sempre: * livrai-me na vossa justiça, e libertai-me.

kept as an option under In te, Dómine, sperávi, non confúndar in ætérnum — identical Latin to 30:2, copied with Ps 30's ruling (D15, D23); the wish is option 3.

10

minor

“os meus inimigos falaram de mim” dixérunt inimíci mei mihi

mihi is a bare dative ('to me' / 'against me'). 'de mim' ('about me') commits to one sense, taken from the Hebrew, where the Latin leaves the dative open.

proposedPorque os meus inimigos falaram contra mim: * e os que guardavam a minha alma tomaram conselho juntos.

kept as an option under Quia dixérunt inimíci mei mihi — 70:11 speaks of the psalmist in the third person ('Deus o abandonou'), so the words are about him, not to him; 'contra' adds a hostility the dative does not say (as 40:6 'disseram mal de mim'). Option 2.

12

minor

“olhai para o meu auxílio” in auxílium meum réspice

'in auxilium' is directional/purposive ('look [down] to help me'), not 'look at my help'. The Portuguese makes 'auxílio' the thing looked at.

proposedmeu Deus, olhai em meu auxílio.

❧ taken — with the stylist and the ambiguity reader; D40's phrase.

16

minor

“não conheço as letras” non cognóvi litteratúram

cognovi is perfect in form. The present 'conheço' is a legitimate reading of the perfect-present sense, but the past 'conheci' keeps the Latin tense.

proposedPorque não conheci as letras, † entrarei nas potências do Senhor: * Senhor, eu me lembrarei só da vossa justiça.

kept as an option under memorábor justítiæ tuæ solíus — his wording leans the same way ('justice only'); neither order in Portuguese keeps the 'of you alone' reading audible without spelling it out (option 3). Option 2 for Gustavo.

22

minor

“vos darei graças com instrumentos de salmo pela vossa verdade” confitébor tibi in vasis psalmi veritátem tuam

veritatem tuam is the direct object of confitebor ('I will confess/praise your truth to you'). It is not a cause ('for your truth'). The 'pela' shifts the grammatical role.

proposedPois também eu vos louvarei com instrumentos de salmo a vossa verdade: * Deus, a vós entoarei salmos com a cítara, Santo de Israel.

not taken — D5 fixes confitéri → dar graças a; 'pela' is the grammar that verb needs (D2), as MS1932.

A faithful, close translation. Every verse keeps the Latin's content, and the Septuagintal readings (litteraturam, potentias Domini, in vasis psalmi, conversus) are preserved rather than smoothed. The issues are all minor: one mood choice (70:1), one dative that is narrowed (70:10), one prepositional idiom (70:12), one tense (70:16), and one direct object recast as a cause (70:22). The pointing marks (†, *) match the Latin in every verse.

claude-opus-5-5 (fresh context, with latin.json) · 2026-09-22 · critic/v1.stylist.json · read prayed.vos.json

1

fails: native

“para sempre não serei envergonhado”

Fronting 'para sempre' before the negation is a Latin word order. The ear first hears 'para sempre' and then gets a 'não' it did not expect, so for a moment it sounds like 'forever I will not be...' and has to reparse. The native order puts the adverb after the verb.

proposedEm vós, Senhor, esperei: não serei envergonhado para sempre: * livrai-me na vossa justiça, e libertai-me.

kept as an option under In te, Dómine, sperávi, non confúndar in ætérnum — Ps 30's ruling (D23: after 'não', 'para sempre' is heard as 'not for ever'); identical Latin, identical Portuguese. Option 5.

11

fails: native

“porque não há quem liberte”

'Libertar' is transitive, so without an object the line stops short and sounds unfinished. Brazilian speech supplies the clitic here without thinking about it. Adding it is a clitic placement, not a change of sense, because the 'eum' of the whole verse is clearly the object.

proposedporque não há quem o liberte.

kept as an option under quia non est qui erípiat — the Latin has no object; 49:22 'não haja quem liberte' copied. Option 2.

12

fails: native, plain

“olhai para o meu auxílio”

'Olhar para o meu auxílio' sounds as if God is asked to look at some help the psalmist already has. 'In auxilium' is purpose: look so as to help. Portuguese has the fixed idiom 'em auxílio de' (as in 'vir em auxílio'), which keeps both the noun and 'olhai'.

proposedmeu Deus, olhai em meu auxílio.

❧ taken — as the Latinist.

13

fails: native

“os que buscam males contra mim”

'Buscar males contra' mixes two constructions: you seek something 'for' someone, and you plot something 'against' them. It comes out stiff at the very end of a long colon. 'Para mim' is ambiguous (it could be read as good for me), but the dative-of-disadvantage clitic is natural and short.

proposedsejam cobertos de vergonha e de desonra os que me buscam males.

not taken — 37:13 has 'procuravam males contra mim' for the same 'mala mihi'; one wording kept across psalms. Noted for the glossary.

17

fails: plain

“pronunciarei as vossas maravilhas”

In current Portuguese 'pronunciar' means to articulate sounds or to deliver a verdict, so 'pronunciar maravilhas' sounds technical. 'Proclamar' is the plain liturgical verb for 'pronuntiare' and stays distinct from 'anunciar' (annuntiare) in 70:15 and 70:18b.

proposede até agora proclamarei as vossas maravilhas.

kept as an option under et usque nunc pronuntiábo mirabília tua — Ps 118:13, 118:172 already say pronunciar; proposed as a new glossary row (open) with proclamar as its alternative, for Gustavo. Option 4.

18

fails: plain

“a velhice e a decrepitude”

'Decrepitude' is clinical and ugly in the mouth: three hard stops in a row, and a word from medical reports. It also turns a prayer about extreme age into an observation about bodily decay. 'Senium' is simply advanced old age.

proposedE até a velhice e a idade extrema: * Deus, não me abandoneis,

❧ taken — also an unknown word to the ambiguity reader.

20

fails: plain, sayable

“de novo me reconduzistes”

'Reconduzir' already contains 'again' ('re-'), so 'de novo me reconduzistes' is doubly redundant. It is also a bureaucratic word, the one used for reappointing an official. It comes at the end of the longest verse in the psalm, where the voice needs a light, plain close.

proposede dos abismos da terra de novo me trouxestes.

❧ taken — íterum carries the return; 'trazer' stays apart from edúcere → tirar.

22

fails: native, sayable

“vos darei graças com instrumentos de salmo pela vossa verdade”

'Instrumentos de salmo' is a calque that makes the listener stop to decode it. The colon also stacks two prepositional phrases before the mark, so it is hard to say in one breath and it ends limply on 'verdade' after 'pela'. Putting the instrument phrase first and using the plain liturgical noun 'salmodia' keeps the image of instruments and lets the colon run.

proposedPois também eu, com instrumentos de salmodia, vos darei graças pela vossa verdade: * Deus, a vós entoarei salmos com a cítara, Santo de Israel.

❧ taken — the order taken; 'salmodia' refused (psalmódia's word; psalmus → salmo).

The psalm mostly prays well: the short petitions (70:2, 70:9, 70:23) are plain and rhythmic, and the vós forms carry weight without stiffness. The weak spots come where Latin order or a Latinate word shows through: the fronted 'para sempre', 'decrepitude', 'reconduzistes', 'pronunciarei' and 'instrumentos de salmo'. These make the text sound translated rather than prayed.
