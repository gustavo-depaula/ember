Verse by verse

1

The psalm begins at 12:1: DO puts the titulus (In finem, Psalmus David) outside the verse, and 12:1 is the first prayed verse (D7). ‘Senhor’ bare (glossary).

2

The Latin’s question mark kept. ‘dor’ (dolor, glossary) is the second object of ‘hei de pôr’, as dolórem of ponam; ‘e’ supplied (decision zeugma).

3

The inline marker (4a) is not reproduced. exáudi me → ‘escutai-me’ (D3; the imperative is safe). Dómine, Deus meus → ‘Senhor, meu Deus’ (glossary formula). The imperative begins with a lower-case letter after the mark, as the Latin’s.

4b

illumináre → iluminar (glossary; ‘Iluminai’ is safe, past ‘iluminei’). nequándo → ‘para que não’ (glossary, 2:12). præválere advérsus → ‘prevalecer contra’ (Matos Soares 1932 ‘Prevaleci contra ele’); the enemy’s words are quoted with a capital, as the Latin. The inline marker (5a) is not reproduced.

5b

tribuláre → atribular (glossary, Ps 3:2 ‘os que me atribulam’). exsultáre → exultar. ego autem → ‘mas eu’ (5:7b, 118:69); speráre in → esperar em (glossary), the perfect kept (‘esperei’, as 7:2 ‘em vós esperei’). The inline marker (6a) is not reproduced. The blind reader listed ‘atribulam’ as unknown (as in Ps 3); kept (glossary).

6b

salutáre → salvação (D6); ‘exultará na vossa salvação’ as Ps 9:16a (the other agent) ‘Exultarei na vossa salvação’. cantáre → cantar (D25 keeps it apart from psállere).
