The audit trail

1draft

Psalm-level draft from the parallels file (Latin, LXX, Hebrew, DRB, MS1932; no Diurnal: a Matins psalm). The Latin's duplicate ids made b-ids: 4b, 5b, 8b, 20b, 38b, 54b. Liturgical uses grepped and every colon said alone checked (Introit verse 77:1–2; Corpus Christi versicle 24b/25a; the six Thursday Matins antiphons; the versicles 77:20 and 77:68). Glossary applied throughout; new points: 77:38 *propítius fiet* → *será clemente com os pecados deles*, taking the Ps 78 agent's ruling (78:9) for the family where the object is a sin; 77:2 D43's *propósitio* clause turned passive; 77:17 appónere → *tornar a* (76:8 row asked); *excitáre* → *despertar* in 77:17 and 77:65 for the echo. Helper: gl.py (glossary rows by Latin head).

2checks

Hard checks pass (ids, pointing). Soft length flags accepted: most are ±3–4 where the Latin's long words (e.g. *cum festinatióne*, 77:33 −5) or the Portuguese article/possessive system shift weight; 77:55 −6 because the Latin's subject *tribus Israël* stands alone in the second colon and is kept there; 77:29 −5 because the Latin's first colon is very long. Neighbouring rhymes accepted: 77:23/24 *céu* (the Latin repeats *cæli*), 77:63/64 *pranteadas / choradas* (the Latin's parallel passives).

minor

“falarei o que foi proposto desde o princípio” loquar propositiónes ab inítio

propositiones is a noun (LXX problemata: riddles, dark sayings, set questions), parallel to parabolis; rendering it as a relative clause 'what was proposed' turns the noun into an event and blurs the parallel.

proposedfalarei proposições desde o princípio

kept as an option under loquar propositiónes ab inítio — D43 settled the clause (48:5); *proposições* was bookish to Ps 48's stylist and unknown to its blind reader. Kept as option 3 for Gustavo.

17

minor

“despertaram a ira do Excelso” in iram excitavérunt Excélsum

Latin object is the Most High, stirred to anger; the Portuguese makes 'ira' the object. Same sense, but it departs from the construction the translation keeps elsewhere (77:40, 77:58 'incitaram-no à ira').

proposedincitaram à ira o Excelso no lugar sem água

❧ taken — Taken as the Latin build with *despertar* (*despertaram o Excelso para a ira*): *incitar à ira* is concitáre's (77:40, 58), and *despertar* keeps the echo with 77:65. His wording is an option.

35

minor

“Deus é o seu auxílio” Deus adjútor est eórum

adjutor is an agent noun (helper), parallel to redemptor; 'auxílio' abstracts the person into the help.

proposedDeus é o seu auxiliador

not taken — Glossary: adjútor → auxílio, the formula *meu auxílio e meu redentor*; *auxiliador* is not in the psalter's Portuguese.

38b

minor

“E muitas vezes desviou a sua ira” Et abundávit ut avérteret iram suam

'muitas vezes' follows the Hebrew/Douay ('many a time') rather than the Latin, which says he abounded (was lavish) so as to turn away his wrath; the image of abundance and the purpose/result clause are lost.

proposedE foi abundante em desviar a sua ira

❧ taken — The Latin's abundance (πληθυνεῖ) restored.

48

minor

“a sua posse ao fogo” possessiónem eórum igni

Portuguese 'posse' normally denotes the act or right of possessing, not the thing possessed (here: livestock/goods, cf. LXX hyparxin).

proposede a sua possessão ao fogo

❧ taken — The fault taken; the fix refused because *possessão* is demonic possession in Brazil: the stylist's *os seus bens*.

57

minor

“assim como os pais deles, converteram-se num arco torto” quemádmodum patres eórum convérsi sunt in arcum pravum

In the Latin, 'patres eorum' is the grammatical subject of 'conversi sunt' inside the quemadmodum clause; the comma detaches it and makes the present generation the subject, resolving an ambiguity the Latin keeps (LXX reads the same way as the Latin).

proposedassim como os pais deles se converteram num arco torto

❧ taken — Comma dropped; the fathers are the subject.

72

minor

“com o entendimento das suas mãos” in intelléctibus mánuum suárum

Latin plural 'intellectibus' (LXX synesesin) rendered singular.

proposede com os entendimentos das suas mãos os guiou

not taken — D28: *os entendimentos das suas mãos* is not Portuguese; the ambiguity reader already found the singular odd. The plural is heard in no Portuguese build.

A faithful, close rendering of the whole psalm that keeps the Septuagintal oddities (unicorns, mosca canina, vento sudoeste, arco torto, the Latin tenses in 77:34 and 77:64); no verse says something the Latin does not say. The findings are all minor: one place follows the Hebrew over the Latin (77:38b 'muitas vezes'), a few nouns abstracted or reshaped (77:2, 77:35, 77:48), one punctuation choice that resolves an ambiguity (77:57), and one number shift (77:72). Pointing marks match the Latin in every verse.

“despertou como quem dorme”

The psalm reads with steady, plain narrative dignity: the long 'E ... e ...' chains carry the Latin's saga rhythm well, and most cola fit one breath with clean cadences. The weak spots are a few calques and stiff supplied phrases ('fizessem conhecer', 'o que atribula', 'o que enviou'), some 'para ... para' stumbles, one dactylic mediant (77:34) and two accidental -ão/-ou rhymes. Fixing those would make it sound written in Portuguese.

5ambiguity

77:38 *será clemente com os pecados deles* heard as 'forgive' — no reading of favouring sin (the point of the propitiári row). Acted on: 77:20 *Porque* (heard *Por que*), 77:31 *gordos*, 77:46 *fadigas* (tiredness), 77:54b *por sorte* (luck), 77:57 conversion, 77:65, 77:70 *das que*. Unknown words: Tânis, odre, Excelso, entravou, atribula, mosca canina, amoreiras, vereda, primícias, Cam, Silo, pranteadas, davam cria — names and glossary words kept.

“falarei o que foi proposto desde o princípio” loquar propositiónes ab inítio

propositiones is a plural noun (things propounded, riddles, dark sayings, parallel to parabolis); the Portuguese turns it into a passive relative clause, which weakens the noun and blurs the riddle sense.

in Israël stands in parallel with in Jacob, which is rendered 'em Jacó'; rendering 'contra' resolves the preposition (Israel is indeclinable, so in + acc. or abl. is left open) and breaks the parallel.
