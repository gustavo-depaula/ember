Verse by verse

1a

exaudíre → escutar (D3), inténdere → atender (D3): the two verbs stand side by side here, as D3 foresaw (16:1).

1b

2

Jussive subjunctives with the verb first ('saia', 'vejam'), as Portuguese says a wish.

3

examináre → examinar, 'no fogo' as 11:7 and 17:31 ('examinadas no fogo'). 'não se encontrou iniquidade em mim': the pronominal passive for 'non est invénta'; no article, as the Latin. Draft 2 moves 'em mim' to the end (draft 1 'não se encontrou em mim iniquidade'): with 16:2's singular 'a equidade' the two verses would end in a rhyme (-idade) the Latin does not have (checks).

4

propter → por causa de (glossary). custodíre vias → 'guardar caminhos', as 17:22 'guardei os caminhos do Senhor'. verba → palavras. ego kept ('eu guardei').

5

perfícere → aperfeiçoar (glossary: tried on the imperative 79:16; 17:34 'Que aperfeiçoou os meus pés'); 'aperfeiçoai' is safe (past 'aperfeiçoei'). gressus → passos (17:37); sémita → vereda (D15).

6

exaudísti / exáudi → escutastes / escutai (D3). Deus bare in the vocative (glossary). inclína aurem tuam mihi → 'inclinai para mim o vosso ouvido' (proposed as a formula: 44:11, 87:3); 'inclinai' is safe (past 'inclinei'). quóniam → porque, the Latin's reading.

7

salvos fácere → salvar (glossary); speráre in → esperar em. 'vós que' names the subject of the relative after the imperative.

8b

sub → sob (glossary); alæ → asas; protégere → proteger ('protegei' ≠ 'protegi'); a fácie → 'da face de' (glossary); afflígere → afligir, perfect kept.

9b

circumdáre → cercar (and again 16:11, as the Latin repeats it). eórum → 'deles'.

14b

15
