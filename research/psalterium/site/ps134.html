minor

“Faz subir as nuvens do extremo da terra” Edúcens nubes ab extrémo terræ

'educere' is 'to lead out, bring forth'. 'Faz subir' follows the LXX 'anagon' (bringing up) rather than the Latin verb. Rendering the participle as a finite present is acceptable.

❧ taken — rule 1; prodúcere moves to 'Tira' so the two verbs stay apart

minor

14

minor

“e se deixará aplacar para com os seus servos” et in servis suis deprecábitur

The passive 'deprecabitur' means 'he will be entreated' or 'will let himself be prayed to'. 'Aplacar' (to appease) moves toward propitiation, which is an interpretation. It is defensible but could be closer.

proposede se deixará rogar para com os seus servos

kept as an option under et in servis suis deprecábitur — 'deixar-se rogar' is heard in Brazil as reluctance ('fazer-se rogar'); 89:13 has the same Greek and 'deixar-se aplacar'

15

minor

“Os ídolos das nações” Simulácra géntium

'simulacra' means images or likenesses. 'Ídolos' is the standard sense but names the category rather than the Latin's concrete word.

proposedOs simulacros das nações

kept as an option under Simulácra géntium — the row (εἴδωλα) and 113:12, identical Latin, refused the same proposal

I found no critical or major faults. The translation follows the Latin closely, keeps the future tenses in vv. 16-17, and keeps the ambiguities in vv. 3 and 12. The only issues are four minor lexical choices. Every verse has the same pointing as the Latin: a single mediant (*), with no flex or second mediant.

5

fails: native

“Porque eu soube que o Senhor é grande”

'eu soube' sounds like receiving news ('I found out'), not the settled knowledge of a confession of faith; the ear hears gossip, not creed.

proposedPorque eu reconheci que o Senhor é grande

❧ taken — taken as 'eu sei' (also the ambiguity reader's complaint); 'reconheci' kept as an option

7b

fails: concrete, plain

“dos seus depósitos”

'depósitos' is warehouse/bank vocabulary; it flattens the Latin image of treasuries (thesauris) into something commercial and prosaic.

proposedFaz sair dos seus tesouros os ventos

kept as an option under de thesáuris suis — the thesáurus row and 32:7; 'tesouros' was heard as riches there

“desde o homem até o animal”

'animal' is generic and slightly clinical; pecus is livestock, and the plainer, more concrete word sits better in the mouth.

proposeddesde o homem até o gado

❧ taken

13

fails: plain

“o vosso memorial”

'memorial' in current Portuguese evokes a monument or a written brief; in prayer it sounds technical.

proposedSenhor, a vossa memória, de geração em geração.

kept as an option under memoriále tuum — = 101:13 (rule 6); the memoriále row

14

fails: sayable, native

“e se deixará aplacar para com os seus servos”

'aplacar para com' is a stumble: the reflexive-causative plus the heavy compound preposition makes the final colon lumpy and legalistic; the voice runs out of breath before the cadence.

proposede com os seus servos se deixará aplacar.

❧ taken — the verb-last order taken; 'com' for 'para com' refused — heard as the agent

17

fails: native

“pois nem há sopro na sua boca”

'nem há' reads as a clipped calque of neque enim; spoken Portuguese wants 'nem sequer' here, which also gives the colon a steadier rhythm.

proposedpois nem sequer há sopro em sua boca.

❧ taken — 'nem sequer' taken; 'em sua' refused (the article before the possessive)

18

fails: sayable, no rhyme

“Façam-se semelhantes a eles aqueles que os fazem”

'Façam-se ... fazem' is a jingle of the same verb at both ends of the colon, and 'a eles aqueles' clusters vowels and pronouns; the line is hard to say in one breath.

proposedTornem-se semelhantes a eles os que os fazem:

kept as an option under Símiles illis fiant qui fáciunt ea — the echo is the Latin's fiant / fáciunt; = 113:16, which refused the same proposal

The psalm sounds plain, sturdy and liturgical: the litany of 'louvai' and 'bendizei ao Senhor' carries well, and the historical middle keeps its concrete verbs. The weak spots are a few prosaic nouns ('depósitos', 'memorial') and two knotted cola (14 and 18) where pronoun and preposition pile-ups break the breath.

worst line134:14

best line134:16

The translation follows the Latin closely throughout: tenses (futures in 16-17, jussive fiant in 18, perfects in 6-12) are preserved, the Septuagintal deprecabitur in 14 is rendered as the Latin reads it (se deixara aplacar) rather than by the Hebrew, and the ambiguity of quoniam suave in 3 is kept. Only permitted liberties appear (copulas in 13 and 15, participle and relative clauses in 7a/7b turned into finite verbs, natural word order); pointing marks match the Latin in every verse.

Verse by verse

1

The Alleluia title (LXX αλληλουια; MS1932 prints 'Aleluia.') is not in DO's Latin, which opens on 'Laudáte nomen Dómini'; as in Pss 110–113 it is not reproduced (D7). 'Louvai, servos, o Senhor': 112:1's build ('Louvai, meninos, o Senhor'), the vocative bare. 'Senhor … Senhor' at mediant and final is the Latin's own echo (Dómini / Dóminum). The first colon is sung alone as an antiphon ([Day2 Laudes1]). The checks flag the rhyme Senhor / Senhor: it is the Latin's Dómini / Dóminum, kept (as 134:19–20 keep Dómino / Dómino, and 134:16 falarão / verão = 113:13, the Latin's loquéntur / vidébunt).

2

Otherwise 133:1b word for word (stare → estar de pé; átrium → átrio). See decision statis. +3 syllables on the first colon (the named 'Vós' and 'de pé'), accepted.

3

quia / quóniam both → porque. 'quóniam suáve' (neuter; ὅτι καλόν): what is sweet may be the name (nomen is neuter) or the singing; 'porque é suave' keeps it open. suávis → suave (row, 99:4b). psállere → entoar salmos (D25).

4

The verb and subject brought forward (natural order, D2); Jacó at the mediant. 'in possessiónem sibi' → 'como sua posse' after 32:12 'quem elégit in hereditátem sibi → que ele escolheu como sua herança' (sibi absorbed into the possessive). heréditas / posséssio → herança / posse (row). περιουσιασμός is a treasured possession; 'posse' is the row's word for the land (2:8).

5

præ ómnibus diis → acima de todos os deuses (95:4 'acima de todos os deuses', 88:28 præ → acima de). The second colon is verbless as the Latin's.

6

Its head copies 113:11's 'tudo o que quis, ele fez' with Dóminus named, as the glossary's 113:11 note asks; the antiphon 'Tudo o que quis, o Senhor fez' ([Daya4 Vespera], [Monastic Vespera] feria 4) reads alone. The first colon is short against the Latin (≈13 vs 20 syllables): Portuguese uses fewer. (checks: −6)

7a

ab extrémo terræ → do extremo da terra (the CNBB LH has the same words, fetched). See decisions educens and fulgura. The ambiguity reader noticed the tense shift (Faz … fez), which is the Latin's (Edúcens … fecit), and still heard what God does; kept.

7b

DO's (8) run in, not reproduced. primogénita → os primogênitos (77:51). See decisions educens (Tira), thesauris, qui, pecus.

9

mittere → enviar (56:4b). signa et prodígia → sinais e prodígios (77:43, 104:27). The vocative 'Egito' without 'ó' (the psalter's rule: 'ó' only where the Latin has 'o').

10

gentes multas → muitas nações (plainer than 96:1's 'numerosas'). reges fortes → reis fortes (fortes row: strong or mighty, open as the Latin; 135:18 has the same words).

12

The Latin repeats 'hereditátem', and the Portuguese repeats 'como herança'. 'pópulo suo' → 'o seu povo', the article before the possessive.

13

The second colon copies 101:13b ('e o vosso memorial, de geração em geração'), as the memoriále row asks; the copula supplied in the first ('é para sempre', D14's build) and understood in the second. in ætérnum → para sempre (D23). The second colon is 7 syllables short of the Latin (checks): 'de geração em geração' is the formula.

14

judicáre → julgar (row). The second colon is +4 (checks): 89:13's phrase costs it.

16

= 113:13 word for word; the Latin's futures kept, as there.

17

The first colon = 113:14a. Ps 113 has two more pairs (nose, hands, feet); Ps 134 has not.

19

benedícere + dative → 'bendizei ao Senhor' (row; 102:20–22, 113:26). Aaron → Aarão.

20

'qui timétis Dóminum' → 'vós que temeis o Senhor' (21:24).

21

See decision sion21.

18

= 113:16 word for word (v2 there); +4 syllables on the first colon, as there.
