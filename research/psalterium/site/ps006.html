Verse by verse

2

The psalm begins at 6:2 (6:1 is the titulus; D7). 6:2 = 37:2 word for word (grep): Ps 37 copies this verse. furor → furor, ira → ira (glossary), in the Latin's order — furor first. The Latin fronts 'in furóre tuo' for emphasis; in Portuguese the emphasis falls at the end of the clause, so 'no vosso furor' closes the colon.

3

'Miserére mei' → 'Tende piedade de mim' (glossary formula). 'Dómine' twice, kept twice. 'ossa mea conturbáta sunt' returns in 30:11.

4

'Et' kept. valde → 'muito'. 'sed tu, Dómine, úsquequo?' → 'mas vós, Senhor, até quando?': the broken question is the Latin's and is not completed (Matos Soares adds dots and a footnote); úsquequo → 'até quando' as Ps 4:3.

5

'salvum me fac' → 'salvai-me' (glossary). propter → 'por causa de', as Ps 5:9 (Matos Soares 1932 'pela'). ánima mea → 'a minha alma' (glossary).

6

'memor esse' + genitive → 'lembrar-se de', as Ps 118:49, 52, 55. autem → 'porém'. The Latin's question mark kept. The blind reader heard 'no inferno' first as the place of eternal punishment and second as the world of the dead (decision inferno) — foreseen, and kept.

7

The flex is kept. Tenses as the Latin: one perfect (Laborávi) and two futures (lavábo, rigábo); the Diurnal, from the Hebrew, has presents. rigáre → regar (103:13); lácrimæ → lágrimas.

8

óculus singular → 'olho' (glossary). inveteráre → envelhecer (31:3 inveteravérunt ossa mea). 'inter omnes inimícos meos' → 'entre todos os meus inimigos'.

9

'omnes, qui operámini' is second person: 'todos vós que praticais' (plural humans are vós). operári iniquitátem → 'praticar a iniquidade' (glossary; as Ps 5:7a). fletus → 'pranto' (Matos Soares 1932, the Diurnal); 'vocem fletus mei' → 'a voz do meu pranto'. Second colon −3.

10

The Latin is a chiasm (Exaudívit Dóminus … Dóminus … suscépit); Portuguese has subject–verb–object twice (D2: order yields), and 'O Senhor … o Senhor' keeps the doubled name. First colon −4: the Latin noun alone has six syllables.

11

conturbéntur is passive: 'fiquem … perturbados' (draft 1 'sejam … perturbados'). Subject first since draft 2 (decision v11order).
