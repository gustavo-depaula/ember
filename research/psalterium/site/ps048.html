12

major

“chamaram as suas terras pelos seus nomes” vocavérunt nómina sua in terris suis

Restructures the Latin: in the Latin, nomina sua is the object and in terris suis is a place ('in/upon their lands'). The Portuguese makes 'terras' the object and adds 'pelos', which is the Douay/Hebrew-derived explanation (they named their estates after themselves). The Latin keeps it open: they called (invoked, proclaimed) their names in their lands.

proposedchamaram os seus nomes nas suas terras

❧ taken

15b

minor

“no inferno, longe da sua glória” in inférno a glória eórum

'longe da' decides what 'a' means (separation, 'far from'); the Latin 'a gloria eorum' is ambiguous (from / away from / on the part of their glory). 'longe' adds a nuance of distance not in the Latin.

proposedno inferno, desde a sua glória

❧ taken

18

minor

“nada levará” non sumet ómnia

The Latin says 'he will not take all things'; 'nada' resolves it to 'nothing' (the Hebraism behind the LXX), which the Latin wording does not itself state.

proposednão levará todas as coisas

not taken — In Portuguese *não levará tudo / todas as coisas* means he takes part of it — a wrong sense, not an open one; the Greek οὐκ … τὰ πάντα is the Hebrew 'not … anything', and both Vulgate-family versions (DRB, MS1932) say nothing. The negation of the whole is grammar (D2); the stylist named the line the psalm's best. The word-for-word form stays option 2.

A faithful, close rendering that keeps the psalm's Septuagintal hard spots as they stand (the dangling 'E o preço do resgate' in v9, 'o homem resgatará' without a question in v8, 'a iniquidade do meu calcanhar', 'acharão prazer na sua boca'). The one real fault is v12b, where the Douay-style explanation 'chamaram as suas terras pelos seus nomes' replaces the Latin's syntax; v15b and v18 are small resolutions of ambiguity. In v19 'te/fizeres' continues the 'tu' of v17 rather than taking 'tibi' as God; the Latin's second singular is consistent with that and I do not flag it. Pointing marks match the Latin in every verse.

“abrirei com o saltério o que proponho” apériam in psaltério propositiónem meam

15b

minor

“desde a glória deles” a glória eórum

'a' + ablative here is separative ('away from / out of their glory'); 'desde' reads as temporal ('since their glory').

3

The vocative of 48:2 runs on. dives et pauper → *o rico e o pobre* (pauper → pobre, D38).

4

5

inclináre aurem → *inclinar o ouvido* (row; auris → ouvido); the Latin's order kept (*à parábola* before the ear), which puts a paroxytone at the mediant. parábola → *parábola* (Christ's word in the Gospel; Mt 13:35 quotes 77:2 *apériam in parábolis os meum*). psaltérium → *saltério* (row; 32:2 *com o saltério*). *saltério* was unknown to the blind reader, as in 32:2; kept (row).

6

in die mala → *no dia mau* (40:2). circumdáre → *cercar* (row). The Latin ends on a colon: 48:7 goes on.

7

gloriári in → *gloriar-se em* (row). multitúdo → *multidão* (row). The verse is a hanging nominative (the Latin's; DRB also leaves it hanging).

8

frater → *irmão* (21:23). *propiciação* was unknown to the blind reader; kept for now (decision `placatio`; *o que o aplaque* is the plain option) — flagged in the glossary row.

11

Verse 10 of the Vulgate is inside DO's 48:9; DO's ids kept. intéritus → *destruição* (row). v2 *ao ver os sábios morrerem* (decision `videns`). simul → *juntos* (row), next to the verb (36:38). períre → *perecer* (row).

11b

relínquere → *deixar*; aliénus → *estranho* (row), without article (the Latin has none). The second colon has no verb in the Latin: *serão* supplied (grammar, D2; DRB 'shall be'). eórum / illórum both → *seus*.

12

tabernáculum → *tenda* (row). *in progénie et progénie* → *de geração em geração* — the formula of *in generatióne et generatiónem* (32:11, 118:90): the Greek is γενεὰν καὶ γενεάν in both, so D15's test lets progénies share *geração*. The first colon verbless, as the Latin, hanging on 48:11b's *serão*.

13

The refrain (= 48:21 but for *Et*). comparáre (passive) → *foi comparado*; *símilis factus est* → *se fez semelhante* (v2, decision `factus`).

14

The Latin is verbless in the first colon; *é* supplied. via → *caminho* (row); scándalum → *tropeço* (row; 48:14 is on its list; unknown to the blind reader, kept — 118:165). v2: *Este caminho deles é tropeço para eles* — draft 1's *Este seu* (to save syllables) was the stylist's worst line (two determiners); the +3/+4 length is accepted. *deles … para eles* keeps *illórum … ipsis*.

15

inférnus → *inferno* (row, open; here the wicked, like 9:18). oves → *ovelhas*. *pósiti sunt* → *foram postos*.

15b

dominári + genitive → *dominar* with an object (21:29). in matutíno → *pela manhã* (29:6b *ad matutínum → pela manhã*). veteráscere → *envelhecer*; auxílium → *auxílio*.

16

verúmtamen → *Todavia* (row). *de manu ínferi* → *da mão do inferno* (the hand kept, as 30:16 *da mão dos meus inimigos*). redímere → *resgatar* (row).

17

*Ne timúeris* to one hearer → *tu* (*Não temas*; as 36:1 *Não tenhas inveja*). homo here is 'a man', any man. *dives factus fúerit* → *se fizer rico* (factus kept as *fazer-se*). multiplicári → *multiplicar-se* (row).

18

*neque descéndet cum eo glória ejus* → *nem descerá com ele a sua glória*, the Latin's order.

19

benedícere (passive) → *será bendita* (row). *in vita ipsíus* → *em vida* (v2, decision `invita`).

20

introíre usque in → *entrar até* (D42's *entrar até*). *usque in ætérnum* → *para todo o sempre* (D37). progénies (plural) → *as gerações*. lumen → *luz* (row). The Latin's order: the negation after the adverb; *para todo o sempre não verá* is not bounded as *até o fim* was at 9:19.

21

= 48:13 without *E* (the Latin has no *Et* here); every other word identical, from the same slots.
