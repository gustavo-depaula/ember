minor

“Erguei vítimas” Tóllite hóstias

'Tollite' here is 'take up / take and bring' (LXX ἄρατε, 'bring an offering'); 'erguei' can do this, but it tends to suggest only lifting the victims into the air, not taking them up to carry into the courts.

proposedTomai vítimas, e entrai nos seus átrios: * adorai o Senhor no seu átrio santo.

❧ taken

The translation follows the Latin closely throughout. It keeps the Septuagintal readings ('demônios', 'corrigiu o mundo, que não será abalado', 'famílias das nações', 'confissão e beleza'), and nothing is added or dropped. The pointing marks match the Latin in every verse. My only note is a minor one on the verb for 'tollite'.

fails: native

“bendizei ao seu nome”

In current Brazilian usage 'bendizer' takes a direct object; 'bendizer a' sounds dated or like a hypercorrection, and the extra 'ao' blurs into the 'ao Senhor' just before it.

proposedCantai ao Senhor, e bendizei o seu nome: * anunciai de dia em dia a sua salvação.

❧ taken

4

fails: no rhyme

“Porque grande é o Senhor, e muito digno de louvor:”

'Senhor' and 'louvor' chime inside one short colon, and 'louvor' falls on the mediant, so the rhyme sounds like a jingle when chanted.

proposedPorque o Senhor é grande, e muito digno de louvor: * é terrível acima de todos os deuses.

kept as an option under Quóniam magnus Dóminus, et laudábilis nimis — the settled 47:2 formula (D43); one Latin, one wording

8

fails: native, sayable

“Erguei vítimas, e entrai”

A bare plural with no article right after the imperative is abrupt. In modern Portuguese 'vítimas' first suggests crime or accident victims, so it needs the article to read as sacrificial offerings. The run 'vítimas, e entrai' also gives a stumbling 'e-e' hiatus.

proposedErguei as vítimas e entrai nos seus átrios: * adorai o Senhor no seu átrio santo.

kept as an option under Tóllite hóstias — the verb changed to Tomai on the Latinist's remark, which removes the 'help the victims' hearing; the article would make them particular victims the Latin does not name

10

fails: concrete, native

“Pois ele corrigiu o mundo”

'Corrigiu' sounds like fixing a mistake or grading homework. The Latin 'corréxit' has the concrete image of setting the world straight or upright, and the ear hears school rather than creation.

proposedPois ele endireitou o mundo, que não será abalado: * julgará os povos com equidade.

❧ taken

12

fails: sayable, plain

“diante da face do Senhor, porque vem:”

The first colon is very long, and the bare 'porque vem' at the mediant sounds clipped in speech because the subject goes missing just where the voice lands.

proposedEntão exultarão todas as árvores das florestas diante da face do Senhor, porque ele vem: * porque vem julgar a terra.

kept as an option under quia venit: * quóniam venit — the Latin's bare repetition across the mediant; colon already long

“árvores das selvas”

In Brazil 'selva' suggests jungle, which is exotic and a little literary. 'Florestas' is the plainer word for 'silvárum'.

proposedtodas as árvores das florestas

kept as an option under ómnia ligna silvárum — D44 settled selva; third psalm with this objection — for the coordinator

This is a clean, faithful, and mostly very sayable version. The imperatives in 1–3 and 7 ring out well, and the Latin's repetitions ('cantai… cantai', 'trazei… trazei', 'porque vem… porque vem') keep their liturgical weight. The weak spots are a few lexical choices that sound off to a modern ear ('corrigiu', a bare 'vítimas', 'bendizer a') and one overloaded colon in v.12.

Verse by verse

1

*cantáre → cantar*, *cánticum novum → cântico novo* as 32:3, 39:4. The vocative *omnis terra → toda a terra* without *ó*, as the Latin.

2

*annuntiáre → anunciar* (D41), *de die in diem → de dia em dia* (row, 60:9), *salutáre → salvação* (D6).

3

The second colon has no verb, as the Latin; *anunciai … entre as nações* as 9:12. *mirabília → maravilhas*.

4

The first colon is the formula of 47:2 (*Grande é o Senhor, e muito digno de louvor*, settled D43), with *Porque* for *Quóniam*.

5

*Dóminus autem → o Senhor, porém,* (26:10's placing of *autem*).

6

*pulchritúdo → beleza* (row decor: kept apart from *esplendor* for exactly this verse and 103:1); *sanctimónia → santidade* (ἁγιωσύνη = 29:5 *sanctitátis*); *magnificéntia → magnificência* (row, open; unknown to two blind readers, kept).

7

Copies the formula of 28:1–2 (*Trazei ao Senhor … glória e honra … trazei ao Senhor glória ao seu nome*) as the glossary row asks.

8

The second colon copies 28:2 (*adorai o Senhor no seu átrio santo*), word for word as the Latin; *átrio* is the Latin's word (unknown to three blind readers, kept by the row). *introíte → entrai* (safe: *entrai* ≠ *entrei*).

12

*quia venit … quóniam venit → porque vem … porque vem*: the Latin varies the conjunction, the Greek repeats ὅτι; the doubled *porque vem* is the psalm's climax and is kept. *exsultáre → exultar*.

13

First colon = 9:8b *julgará o mundo com equidade* (twin, D30). *æquitas → equidade* (row; 66:5 *julgais os povos com equidade* is 95:10's second colon).
