5

minor

“Fui tido entre os que descem à cova” Æstimátus sum cum descendéntibus in lacum

'cum' is 'with', not 'among'; 'tido entre' shifts the preposition slightly. 'Contado com' keeps both 'æstimatus' (reckoned) and 'cum'.

proposedFui contado com os que descem à cova

❧ taken — The verb “contado” is taken. The preposition “com” is refused for “entre” (grammar, D2) and kept as an option.

19

minor

“e os meus conhecidos, por causa da miséria” et notos meos a miséria

'a miseria' is terse and open (LXX ἀπὸ ταλαιπωρίας); 'por causa da' commits to the causal reading. It is the traditional reading (Douay 'because of misery'), so defensible, but a bare 'da miséria' would keep the Latin's openness.

proposede os meus conhecidos, da miséria

kept as an option under et notos meos a miséria — Bare “da” is heard as a genitive. “pela miséria” (the stylist’s) is taken as the more open middle way.

A close, faithful rendering: tenses, persons, voice and the Septuagintal readings (médici suscitábunt, in lacu inferióri, præveniet) are all kept, and the added 'os' in 87:11 only supplies the object Portuguese needs for a transitive 'ressuscitarão'. Pointing marks match the Latin in every verse. Only two minor points of closeness, no majors.

fails: native

“Entre na vossa presença a minha oração”

Opening a colon on bare 'Entre' makes the ear hear the preposition 'entre' (between) until the sentence resolves; the jussive subjunctive needs a signal at the head.

fails: plain

“Fui tido entre os que descem à cova”

'Fui tido' is stiff and bookish; 'contado' is the plainer faithful word for aestimatus and sits better on the tongue.

proposedFui contado entre os que descem à cova: * tornei-me como um homem sem auxílio, livre entre os mortos.

❧ taken

9

fails: native, sayable

“puseram-me por abominação para si”

The trailing 'para si' is a calque of sibi; it dangles after the heavy noun and falls flat at the final. No Brazilian speaker would end the sentence that way.

proposedpara eles me puseram como abominação.

❧ taken

9b

fails: plain, native

“se enfraqueceram de carência”

'Carência' now sounds clinical or psychological ('carência afetiva'); it breaks the austere register. 'Se enfraqueceram de' also runs long before a weak noun.

proposedos meus olhos definharam de penúria.

❧ taken — The noun “penúria” is taken. The verb “definharam” is refused (the Greek is infirmári’s; D15) and kept as an option.

12

fails: sayable

“alguém narrará no sepulcro”

'narrará no' stacks r-a-r-a-n-o sounds, a small stumble in choral recitation; 'narrar' is also the more literary of two faithful verbs.

proposedAcaso alguém contará no sepulcro a vossa misericórdia, * e a vossa verdade na perdição?

kept as an option under Numquid narrábit áliquis in sepúlcro — narráre → narrar is a working row (18:2, 21:23), so the verb is kept one across the psalter.

14

fails: native

“a minha oração se adiantará a vós”

'Adiantar-se a alguém' is unidiomatic here and the pronominal clutter weakens the final; 'preceder' says praeveniet exactly and plainly.

proposede de manhã a minha oração vos precederá.

❧ taken

16

fails: sayable

“e em fadigas”

The hiatus 'e em' blurs into one vowel when said aloud, and the colon lacks a verb, so it sounds truncated. A supplied copula is allowed.

proposedEu sou pobre, e estou em fadigas desde a minha juventude: * exaltado, porém, fui humilhado e perturbado.

❧ taken

18

fails: sayable

“cercaram-me juntos”

Too clipped to carry the final cadence after a long first colon. The masculine 'juntos' also quietly fixes the subject as masculine (terrores), where the Latin simul does not.

proposedcercaram-me ao mesmo tempo.

❧ taken

19

fails: plain

“por causa da miséria”

'Por causa da' is prosaic and heavy at the close of the psalm; the flat stress run weakens the last line.

proposede os meus conhecidos, pela miséria.

❧ taken

The psalm speaks with a plain, grave voice and mostly keeps the Latin's bare lament, and the vós address sits naturally throughout. The weak spots are a few calques ('para si', 'se adiantará a vós') and one word in the wrong register ('carência'). The closing verses lose weight at the final cadence.

5

minor

“Fui contado entre os que descem à cova” Æstimátus sum cum descendéntibus in lacum

'cum' is 'with', not 'among'; 'entre' is defensible but slightly shifts the preposition, and 'entre' then repeats in 'entre os mortos', blurring the Latin's cum / inter distinction.

proposedFui contado com os que descem à cova

kept as an option under Æstimátus sum cum descendéntibus in lacum — The same request as v1. Portuguese says “contado entre” for a company, and a preposition is grammar (D2). “contado com” stays option 2.

18

minor

“cercaram-me ao mesmo tempo” circumdedérunt me simul

'simul' here carries the sense 'together, all at once' (LXX hama, Heb. yachad); 'ao mesmo tempo' is purely temporal and a little narrower.

proposedcercaram-me todos juntos

kept as an option under circumdedérunt me simul — “ao mesmo tempo” is one of the simul row’s options, and the v1 stylist asked for it. “todos juntos” supplies “todos” and makes the subject masculine. Added as an option.

A faithful, close rendering: tenses (imperfect 'non egrediebar' as 'não saía', future 'prævéniet'), moods and persons are kept, and the Septuagintal readings (medici suscitabunt, inter mortuos liber, a miseria) are preserved without smoothing. Pointing marks match the Latin in every verse. Only two minor prepositional/adverbial nuances could be tightened.

Verse by verse

2

Deus salútis meæ → 'Deus da minha salvação' (17:47; D6). in die … nocte → 'de dia … e de noite' (the pair; 21:3's per diem was 'durante o dia'). coram te → 'diante de vós'. Verse 87:2 + 87:3 is the Introit and Offertory of Quad1-6.

4

repléta est → 'está cheia' (the replére row: estar cheio). appropinquáre → 'aproximar-se' (row).

6

vulneráti → 'feridos' (MS1932; L&S 'wounded'; DRB 'slain' — τραυματίαι covers both, and 'feridos' keeps the Latin's word). memor esse → 'lembrar-se de' (row); ámplius → 'já não'. repúlsi sunt de manu tua → 'foram repelidos da vossa mão': the same Greek as 87:15 (ἀπωθέω), so the same verb, repelir (D42's row).

7

lacus → 'cova' (row); inferióri → 'mais baixa' (the inferióra terræ row, 62:10). umbra mortis → 'sombra da morte' (22:4, 43:20).

10

tota die → 'o dia todo' (D24). expándere manus → 'estender as mãos'.

11

numquid → 'Acaso' in all three questions (row; the row names five places in Ps 87, and the Latin has it three times, 87:11–13). mirabília → 'maravilhas' (87:11, 87:13, identical). confitéri → 'dar graças' (D5).

12

narráre → 'narrar' (row). véritas → 'verdade' (row), not the Hebrew family's 'fidelidade'.

13

oblívio → 'esquecimento'. The first colon is long (the Latin's is too).

14

The V. of the Sunday Prime preces reads alone: 'E eu clamei a vós, Senhor.'

16

pauper → 'pobre' (D38). labor → 'fadiga' (row), plural as the Latin. juvéntus → 'juventude' (24:7, 42:4, 70:5).

17

iræ (plural) → 'iras', the Latin's number kept (MS1932). terróres → 'terrores'. conturbáre → 'perturbar' (row) — the same verb as 87:16's conturbátus, as in the Latin.
