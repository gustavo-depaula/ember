Verse by verse

1a

The psalm begins at 13:1a (the titulus In finem, Psalmus David is out, D7). 13:1a = 52:1 word for word. 'Não há Deus' as 9:26a has it ('Não há Deus à vista dele').

1b

facti sunt → 'tornaram-se', and again in 13:3a (the Latin's repetition kept; the glossary's fíeri row keeps 'fazer-se' for Ps 117 and allows 'tornar-se' for a state that comes upon one, 118:83). 'Corrúpti sunt, et abominábiles facti sunt' and 'non est qui fáciat bonum' are 52:2's words too; its middle (in iniquitátibus) and its end (no usque ad unum) differ.

2

13:2 differs from 52:3 in one word only: Dóminus / Deus. intéllegens → 'quem entenda' (intellégere → entender, glossary). fílii hóminum → 'filhos dos homens' (glossary).

3a

13:3a = 52:4 word for word, and = Romans 3:12 (Clementine text fetched to consult/bolls-VULG-45-3.json).

3b

13:3b–3d are not in the Hebrew (WLC, consult/parallels/ps013.md) nor in Ps 52; they are in the Rahlfs Septuagint text on disk (its verse 3) and in the Clementine Vulgate (bolls-VULG-19-13.json, verse 3), and they stand word for word in Romans 3:13–18 (bolls-VULG-45-3.json). How they came into the Greek psalm is not verified here and is not asserted. The first two colons copy Ps 5:11a exactly (glossary formula; dolóse agébant → 'enganavam', the light verb yields as in Ps 5).

3c

Quorum (a relative running on from 13:3b) → ‘A sua boca’, a new sentence: Portuguese does not open a prayed verse with ‘Cuja’; grammar (D2). maledíctio → maldição, amaritúdo → amargura. ‘são’ supplied in the second colon (decision velozes). effúndere → derramar (the glossary reserves ‘derramar’ for it, row eructáre).

3d

via → caminho (both 'in viis' and 'viam pacis'). ante óculos eórum → 'diante dos seus olhos' (as ante óculos tuos in Ps 5). cognovérunt with an object keeps 'conheceram'.

4

operári iniquitátem → 'praticar a iniquidade' (glossary). plebs → povo (glossary, open; its row names 13:7). The colon 'todos os que praticam a iniquidade, * os que devoram o meu povo' is shared with 52:5 word for word.

5

13:5b = 52:6b word for word. invocáre → invocar (glossary). Draft 1’s rhyme Senhor / temor removed by the Latin’s own order (decision invocaverunt).

6

spes → esperança. The Latin's two quóniam kept as two 'porque'.

7

Jacob → Jacó (the Brazilian form). exsultáre / lætári → exultar / alegrar-se (glossary). 13:7a and the last clause (captivitátem plebis suæ, exsultábit Jacob, et lætábitur Israël) are 52:7's words too.
