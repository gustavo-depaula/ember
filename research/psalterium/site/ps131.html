The Latin has a genitive, 'the horn of David'; 'um chifre para Davi' turns it into a dative and adds indefiniteness, following the Hebrew construal rather than the Latin.

A faithful, close rendering with no critical or major faults; pointing marks (one '*' per verse, no flex or second mediant) match the Latin throughout. The residual issues are small: the oath-'si' of vv. 3-4 is resolved into plain negatives, the genitive 'cornu David' becomes a dative, 'pánibus' loses its plural, and 'stetérunt' is rendered as 'se detiveram'.

6

fails: cadence, native

“ouvimos falar dela em Éfrata: *”

The mediant falls on the proparoxytone 'Éfrata', so the tone's accent lands on a dead syllable. Fronting the place name makes the colon end on 'dela'.

proposedEis que em Éfrata ouvimos falar dela: * nós a encontramos nos campos da selva.

❧ taken — *Eis que em Éfrata ouvimos falar dela*.

“campos da selva”

To a Brazilian ear 'selva' means tropical jungle, which clashes with 'campos'. 'Bosque' is the plainer everyday word for a wood.

proposednós a encontramos nos campos do bosque.

not taken — D44 rules *silva → selva* psalter-wide.

11

fails: sayable, native

“Jurou o Senhor a verdade a Davi, e não a frustrará”

Three unstressed 'a's in a row (article, preposition, pronoun: 'a verdade a Davi… não a') blur together when spoken, and the inverted verb-subject order adds stiffness.

proposedO Senhor jurou a Davi a verdade, e não há de frustrá-la: * Do fruto do teu ventre porei sobre o teu trono.

❧ taken — *O Senhor jurou a Davi a verdade, e não há de frustrá-la*.

13

fails: native

“para habitação sua”

Putting the possessive after the noun is bookish. Normal word order keeps the word 'habitação' and ends on a strong final syllable.

proposedescolheu-a para sua habitação.

kept as an option under elégit eam in habitatiónem sibi — *sua habitação* rhymes with the mediant *Sião* (rule 5, accidental rhyme); the postposed possessive is the Latin's order and still sayable.

17

fails: native, plain

“farei brotar um chifre para Davi”

With the dative 'para Davi', a Brazilian ear hears 'pôr chifre em alguém' (to cuckold), which is dangerous in choir. The Latin 'cornu David' is a genitive, 'the horn of David', and the genitive keeps the image while removing the echo.

proposedAli farei brotar o chifre de Davi, * preparei uma lâmpada para o meu Cristo.

❧ taken — Genitive *o chifre de Davi*.

The psalm reads plainly and with dignity. The oath in vv. 3-5 has a good cumulative rhythm ('não… não… nem… nem'), and the promise section has the right weight. The problems are local: a proparoxytone mediant at Éfrata, a pile-up of unstressed 'a's in v. 11, and one serious unwanted echo, 'chifre para Davi', which the literal genitive fixes. 'Exultarão com exultação' clangs a little but the Latin requires it, and it holds.
