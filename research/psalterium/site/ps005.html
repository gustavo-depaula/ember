Verse by verse

2

The psalm begins at 5:2 (5:1 is the titulus; D7). clamor → 'clamor', of the family of clamáre → clamar. Three verbs of attention in two verses, each with its own word: dar ouvidos (áuribus percípere, D4), entender (intellégere), atender (inténdere, D3); escutar (exaudíre) follows in 5:4.

3

inténdere → atender (D3). 'Rex meus et Deus meus' → 'meu Rei e meu Deus', the living vocative order, as 3:7 'meu Deus'; no 'ó' (the Latin has none).

4

The Latin's punctuation is kept: the vocative belongs to the second colon. 'mane … Mane' (5:4, 5:5) → 'de manhã … De manhã', the repetition kept. Future tenses kept throughout 5:4–8 (orábo, exáudies, astábo, vidébo, habitábit, permanébunt, perdes, abominábitur, introíbo, adorábo).

5

'estarei/porei … verei' rhyme inside the colon as astábo … vidébo do in the Latin. 'vós' is said because the Latin says 'tu es'.

6

'Neque … neque' → 'Nem … nem'. juxta te → 'junto de vós'; permanére → permanecer; injústi → 'os injustos' (the Latin's word; the Greek has παράνομοι). Subject first in both colons since draft 2 (decision v6order).

7a

operári iniquitátem → 'praticar a iniquidade' (glossary; the phrase returns in 6:9). 'omnes, qui … omnes, qui' → 'todos os que … todos os que'. loqui mendácium → 'falar mentira', singular and without article as people say it; Matos Soares 1932 'dizem a mentira'.

7b

abominári → abominar (118:163 will want it). The Latin's full stop after the verbless colon is kept. '(8a)' is DO's inline marker and is not reproduced.

8b

introíre → entrar (glossary: íngredi / intráre → entrar — a third Latin verb shares it). 'in timóre tuo' → 'no vosso temor', the objective genitive kept as Douay-Rheims keeps it ('in thy fear'); the blind reader heard reverence first and fear second. Matos Soares paraphrases ('penetrado do teu temor'). Second colon +4 syllables, accepted.

9

justítia → justiça, inimíci → inimigos, via → caminho (glossary). The Latin's pronouns are kept against the Greek and the Hebrew (which have 'before me your way'): 'à vossa vista o meu caminho'.

10

véritas → verdade (glossary). 'eórum … eórum' → 'deles … seu', as Matos Soares 1932: a pronoun, not a figure. vanum → 'vão', of the family of vánitas → vaidade. First colon −3 syllables.

11a

The flex is kept. agébant is an imperfect and stays one ('enganavam'). 5:11a = 13:3b as far as agébant: Ps 13 should copy 'A sua garganta é um sepulcro aberto, † com as suas línguas enganavam'. Romans quotes the line (from memory, unverified).

11b

impíetas → impiedade (ímpius → ímpio). expéllere → expulsar: 'expulsai-os' is safe (past 'expulsei'). 'segundo a multidão … expulsai-os': the Latin's order, the verb at the mediant, oxytone. The blind reader heard 'Caiam dos seus próprios pensamentos' first as 'let their own plans bring them down' and second as 'let them fall away from their thoughts', and said the construction leaves the relation unclear — which is the Latin's own state.

12a

'Et' kept ('E alegrem-se'): the Latin turns from the enemies to the faithful with a bare 'and' (Douay-Rheims makes it 'But'). lætári → alegrar-se, speráre in → esperar em, in ætérnum → para sempre (D23; decision in_aeternum — draft 3 had eternamente), exsultáre → exultar (glossary). 'exultarão para sempre' in natural order (the Latin has the adverb first). The change of mood (læténtur subjunctive, exsultábunt future) is kept.

12b

gloriári in → gloriar-se em (Matos Soares 1932). dilígere → amar. 'tu' is said in the Latin: 'vós'.

13b

'coronásti nos': the perfect kept ('coroastes'); 'nos' as the Latin (the Hebrew has 'him'). scutum → escudo.
