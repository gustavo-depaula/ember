Verse by verse

1

Identical to 117:1 and 117:29 (D5, D14): the formula rows. 105:1 has the same Latin and should read the same.

2

The verse ends in a colon, as the Latin, running into 106:3. congregáre active → congregar (the row: 15:4b active). regio → regiões (χωρῶν). The subject 'ele' is named in the relative (os que ele resgatou) so the Lord is the agent of both verbs.

3

a solis ortu, et occásu → 'Do nascer do sol, e do poente' — the formula row (Desde o nascer do sol até o poente, 49:1b) adapted to this Latin, which has et, not usque ad; the commas are the Latin's. aquilo → norte (D43, and its row names 106:3). mare → mar: the Latin's word; the west or south it stands for is not explained (MS1932 glosses it).

4

in inaquóso → 'no lugar sem água', word for word as 77:40 (the same Latin). The object of the second colon comes after the verb (natural order); the verse ends on 'morada' (paroxytone).

5

Esuriéntes, et sitiéntes: the participles kept as adjectives with no verb, as the Latin, so that 106:10 (Sedéntes …) can open the same way. First colon -3 syllables, accepted. in ipsis → 'neles'.

6

The first colon is one slot for 106:6, 13, 19, 28 (decision tribul); the second varies the verb as the Latin does (arrancou / livrou / livrou / tirou). 'ele' named: the subject changes from them to the Lord.

7

dedúcere → guiar (D22), and the same verb at 106:30 (dedúxit eos in portum). in viam rectam → 'ao caminho reto' (into, motion), ut irent → 'para irem' (the same subject).

9

satiáre → saciar (row), twice as the Latin; inánis → vazio (row names 106:9). The Latin's chiasmus (satiávit … satiávit bonis) yields to natural order in the second colon; the verb repeats.

10

Sedéntes (nominative) and vinctos (accusative) are two participles with no main verb, the Latin's (and the Greek's) hanging description; kept verbless. umbra mortis → sombra da morte (22:4, 43:20). vinctos → 'presos': the vincti row has the noun 'prisioneiros' (68:34), but with 'in mendicitáte et ferro' the participle is needed. mendícitas: decision mendicitate (v2 'pobreza').

11

irritáre → provocar (D27, settled). consílium Altíssimi → 'o conselho do Altíssimo': D33 keeps 'conselho' where a verse can mean both counsel and plan, and here it stands beside what God said. The second colon keeps the Latin's order (object first, verb last): with the verb first the colon would end on 'Altíssimo', a proparoxytone at the final (rule 4). v2: the stylist asked for the verb first; refused, it would end the verse on the proparoxytone 'Altíssimo' (rule 4).

12

labor → fadiga (row), plural 'nas fadigas' as the Latin. humiliáre → humilhar; infirmári → enfraquecer (row, 9:4, 108:24). nec fuit qui adjuváret → 'e não houve quem auxiliasse', no object, as the Latin and 21:12 'não há quem auxilie'.

14

edúcere de → tirar de (row), the same verb as 106:28. víncula → cadeias (row names 106:14). disrúmpere → romper.

16

contérere → quebrar, confríngere → despedaçar, as 45:10 decided where the two stand together (two Greek verbs here too). ǽreus → de bronze.

17

injustítia → injustiça, iníquitas → iniquidade (rows; the Latin has both and so does the Portuguese). enim → 'pois'. v2: the stylist heard 'pois por' as a stumble and the long adverbial before the verb as cadenceless; the verb moved before 'por causa' (order yields to the ear), the colon ending on the paroxytone 'injustiças'.

18

esca → 'comida' (the esca row foresees 'alimento or comida'; the plainer). 'toda comida', no article: every kind of food (omnem escam). abomináre → abominar (row names 106:18). appropinquáre → aproximar-se (row); 'até as portas' without crase, the Brazilian use.

20

The Matins versicle (Psalterium/Special/Matutinum Special) reads 'Misit verbum suum, et sanávit eos' alone: 'Enviou a sua palavra, e os curou' stands alone. sanáre → curar (row names 106:20). verbum → palavra (D15). Second colon -7 syllables (the Latin's interitiónibus is long); accepted.

22

sacrificáre sacrifícium → 'sacrifiquem um sacrifício': the row keeps the verb so that the Latin's figure repeats (4:6). Sacrifícium laudis → sacrifício de louvor (formula row). in exsultatióne → 'com exultação' (41:5b 'com voz de exultação'). The bare jussive 'E sacrifiquem' follows the refrain's Que; the E keeps it from being heard as an imperative to 'vocês'.

23

navis → navio (D43, row names 106:23). in aquis multis → 'em muitas águas' (76:20, 17:17). operátio → 'o seu trabalho' (ἐργασίαν, DRB 'doing business'); the possessive supplied. The verse ends with a comma, as the Latin, running into 106:24.

24

Ipsi → 'Esses' (DRB 'These'), the emphasis kept. profúndum → profundeza (row, βυθός here as at 68:3). mirabília → maravilhas.

26

Present tenses kept (ascéndunt, descéndunt), then the imperfect tabescébat → 'definhava' (row names 106:26; unknown to blind readers twice, kept). abýssus → abismo (D42). in malis → 'nos males'. First colon -5 syllables, accepted.

27

turbári → 'ficaram perturbados' (the build of 76:5, 89:7). devoráre → devorar (not absorbére's engolir). 'toda a sua sabedoria' with the article: the whole of it.

29

silére → 'ficar em silêncio' (row; 106:29–30 named there), kept apart from tacére's calar-se. Subject first (natural order); the colon ends on 'silêncio', paroxytone. 'as suas ondas', ejus left open as in 106:25.

30

lætári → alegrar-se. quia siluérunt → 'porque ficaram em silêncio': the subject is not named in the Latin (the waves of 106:29, most naturally), and not named here. volúntas → vontade; 'da vontade deles' (voluntátis eórum) rather than 'da sua vontade', which could be heard as the Lord's.

32

ecclésia → assembleia (D34), plebs → povo (row), cáthedra → cátedra (v2: a local departure from the row's cadeira, which named 106:32 as its test — the stylist and the ambiguity reader both heard a literal chair; this verse is the Gradual of the Chair of St Peter, in Brazil 'Cátedra de São Pedro'; 1:1 'cadeira da pestilência' is unaffected), senióres → anciãos (senes row). The verse (without Et) and the refrain are the Gradual of St Peter's Chair (missa Sancti 02-22) and of the Common of Popes (C4b, Coronatio); read alone it is 'Exaltem-no na assembleia do povo: e o louvem na cátedra dos anciãos'.

33

éxitus aquárum → 'nascentes de água' (row, which names 106:33, 35). in sitim → 'em sede': the Latin's abstract noun kept (DRB and MS1932 turn it into dry ground).

34

The verse has no verb; it hangs on 106:33's, as in the Latin; v2: capitalised as the Latin (Terram), after the Latinist. fructíferus → frutífero (row). malítia → malícia (row names 106:34). a (cause) → 'pela'. inhabitántium in ea → 'dos que nela habitam'. First colon -3.

35

stagna aquárum → 'lagoas de água' (λίμνας): 'tanques' (MS1932) are tanks in Brazil. The Latin's three 'water' words in one verse are kept.

36

collocáre → colocar (row; 22:1 'ali me colocou'). constitúere → estabelecer (row). 'eles' named for the change of subject. 'estabeleceram' kept (the row) against the stylist's 'fundaram'.

37

ager → campo; víneæ → vinhas.

38

benedícere God → man → bendizer (row; 5:12b, 28:11). juménta → animais (row names 106:38; κτήνη). non minorávit: active, God the subject ('não diminuiu os seus animais'); the plural 'animais' cannot be the subject of 'diminuiu'.

39

pauci facti sunt → 'ficaram poucos'. vexáre → maltratar (row names 106:39). a tribulatióne malórum, et dolóre: dolóre is ablative, coordinated with tribulatióne, not with malórum — so 'pela tribulação dos males, e pela dor', the preposition repeated to keep the parse.

40

effúndere → derramar (D43); contémptio → desprezo (contémptus row). The first colon is reordered so that it does not end on 'príncipes', a proparoxytone at the mediant. erráre → extraviar-se (row, as 106:4; erráre fecit → 'os fez extraviar-se'). in ínvio, et non in via → 'no lugar sem caminho, e não no caminho': the ínvius row's 'sem caminho', built as 106:4's 'no lugar sem água' (in inaquóso), so that the two wildernesses of the psalm sound alike. Second colon +3.

41

pónere sicut → 'pôs … como': kept as the Latin's plain verb (the misreading of 106:33 does not arise with 'como'). oves → ovelhas; família → famílias. Natural order (the Latin ends on famílias).

42

recti → os retos (rectus corde row, without corde). oppiláre → 'tapar' (L&S 'to stop up'); MS1932 'fechará'. 'toda iniquidade' with no article: every iniquity (omnis). os suum → 'a sua boca', its own mouth.

43

The question mark after the first colon and the lower case after it are the Latin's. custodíre → guardar (row), intellégere → entender (row). Quis sápiens et custódiet → 'Quem é sábio, e guardará': the copula supplied, the et kept.
