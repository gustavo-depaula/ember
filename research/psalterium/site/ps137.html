2b

fails: cadence, native

“porque engrandecestes o vosso santo nome acima de tudo”

The colon trails off on the adverbial 'acima de tudo'. The weight should fall on 'nome', where the Latin puts it ('super omne, nomen sanctum tuum'). As it stands the line ends flat and sounds like an afterthought.

proposedporque engrandecestes acima de tudo o vosso santo nome

❧ taken — order only (D2), and it is the Latin's order

3

fails: sayable, native

“Em qualquer dia em que vos invocar”

'Em ... em que' makes the opening clunky. Without a subject, 'vos invocar' can be heard as an infinitive before the ear settles on the future subjunctive, so the reciter hesitates.

proposedEm qualquer dia em que eu vos invocar, escutai-me

kept as an option under In quacúmque die invocávero te — 101:3b and 55:10 have the same Latin and the same words; the formula holds

6

fails: native, plain

“e olha para as coisas humildes: * e conhece de longe as coisas altas”

'as coisas humildes' and 'as coisas altas' sound like literal glosses of a neuter plural. Using 'coisas' twice makes both cola heavy and prosaic. A plain Portuguese neuter keeps the parallel without the padding.

proposedPorque o Senhor é excelso, e olha para o que é humilde: * e conhece de longe o que é alto.

kept as an option under et humília réspicit: * et alta a longe cognóscit — 112:5 formula; 'o que é humilde' was heard as a person there

7

fails: plain

“vós me vivificareis”

'vivificareis' is a five-syllable Latinism and lands heavily on the ‡ pause. It is faithful but bookish. Keep it if the correspondence with 'vivificabis' is wanted. Otherwise a plainer verb says the same thing.

proposedSe eu andar no meio da tribulação, vós me dareis vida

kept as an option under vivificábis me — the vivificáre row is cross-psalm (79:19, 84:7, Ps 118); proposed for a ruling in glossary.md rather than departed from here

8

fails: plain

“não desdenheis as obras das vossas mãos”

'desdenheis' is literary and slightly haughty in register. 'desprezeis' is the plainer word and sits closer to 'despicias'.

proposednão desprezeis as obras das vossas mãos

kept as an option under ópera mánuum tuárum ne despícias — the despícere (God) row is desdenhar (26:9b, 54:2); desprezar is the ἐξουθενόω branch, not this Greek

The psalm reads cleanly and with reverence. It sounds like prayer, and most cola fit in one breath with good paroxytone or oxytone cadences. The weak spots are glossing Latinisms ('as coisas humildes/altas', 'vivificareis', 'desdenheis') and one line (2b) whose word order lets the cadence fall on a trailing adverb instead of on the Name.

best line137:7

1b

minor

“adorarei voltado para o vosso templo santo” adorábo ad templum sanctum tuum

'voltado' adds a participle describing the worshipper's posture; 'ad' alone gives direction and Portuguese can carry it without the added word.

proposedadorarei em direção ao vosso templo santo

kept as an option under adorábo ad templum sanctum tuum — the same Latin as 5:8b, whose gate passed voltado para after em direção ao was heard as a route by the stylist; the doublet keeps one wording, and the open adoráre ad row rules both lines at once

The translation is faithful to the Latin throughout: tenses (perfect 'extendísti', future 'vivificábis', 'multiplicábis'), moods and persons are preserved, and 'super omne, nomen sanctum tuum' follows the Breviary's comma. The only point is a slight addition in 1b; pointing marks match the Latin in every verse.

Verse by verse

1a

= 9:2 = 110:1 first colon, copied (glossary formula row; confitéri → dar graças, D5). No titulus: DO's file has none (D7), though the LXX, MS1932 and the Ave Maria print 'De Davi'.

1b

'psallam tibi' → 'vos entoarei salmos' (D25; 107:4 'e vos entoarei salmos'). 'confitébor nómini tuo' → 'darei graças ao vosso nome' (glossary formula row, 53:8). DO's inline '(2a)' not reproduced. Second colon long (checks), accepted: both halves are formulas already fixed elsewhere.

3

Latin as 101:3b's first colon; 'exáudi me' → 'escutai-me' (D3).

4

'Confiteántur tibi' → 'Que vos deem graças', as 66:4.

6

'excélsus' → 'excelso' (D43; 46:3 'Porque o Senhor é excelso'); listed as unknown by the v1 ambiguity reader, kept (settled). 'réspicit' → 'olha para', as 112:5. The ambiguity reader noted colloquial 'de longe' = 'by far'; the Latin's 'a longe' kept, the context (knows … from afar) governs.

7

'Si ambulávero in médio' → 'Se eu andar no meio', the future subjunctive for the future perfect (22:4 builds the same phrase). 'salvum me fecit' → 'me salvou' (salvum fácere row). 'déxtera' → 'a direita' (row).

8

'Dómine' as a bare vocative, as everywhere ('Senhor', without 'ó').
