Verse by verse

2

The Introit / Gradual verse (Quadp3-3, Quad6-3, grep): *Salvum me fac → Salvai-me* (the row), bare vocative *Deus* (the row). *usque ad → até*.

3

*limus → lodo*, kept apart from 68:15 *lutum → lama* (39:3 *lama da borra*): two Latin words, two Portuguese.

4

*Laborávi clamans → Cansei-me clamando* (the *laboráre* row names this verse). *fauces → garganta* (the row; the plural is the Latin idiom for one throat). *defecérunt óculi mei, dum spero in Deum meum*: the *defícere in* row notes this is another build (*dum*), so *enquanto espero no meu Deus* (*speráre in → esperar em*).

5

= 39:13b's first colon word for word (*Multiplicaram-se mais que os cabelos da minha cabeça*). *gratis → sem motivo* (the row names 68:5).

5b

*confortári → fortalecer-se* (the row). The Latin's order (*qui persecúti sunt me inimíci mei injúste*) is rebuilt as *os meus inimigos, que me perseguiram injustamente* — order, D2.

6

*insipiéntia → insensatez* (the row names 68:6), *delícta → faltas* (the row).

7

*exspectáre → aguardar* (D24; the row names 68:7), *erubéscere → corar* (the row), *confúndi → ser envergonhado* (D15), *Dóminus virtútum → Senhor dos poderes* (D43; for Gustavo's ear, as flagged there).

8

*sustínui oppróbrium → suportei a afronta* (the *sustinére* row names this verse for *suportar*). *confúsio → vergonha* (34:26b, 43:16). *fácies → face* (the row; 43:16 *a vergonha da minha face*). v2: the stylist heard *Porque por* as a stammer; kept, as 43:22b *Porque por vossa causa* (same Latin), for one formula.

9

*extráneus → estranho*: the *aliénus* row has the same word, but *aliénus* does not occur in this psalm and the Greek differs (ἀπηλλοτριωμένος / ἀλλότριος); *peregrínus → peregrino* (the row names 68:9).

10

Jn 2:17 / Rom 15:3; the Holy Thursday Matins antiphon (Quad6-4). *oppróbria exprobrántium tibi → as afrontas dos que vos afrontam*, exactly as the *oppróbrium* row foresaw.

11

*Et opérui in jejúnio ánimam meam*: the Latin's verb is *cover* (the Greek has συνέκαμψα, 'bent'); the Latin is followed. *in jejúnio → com o jejum*, as 34:13b *Humilhava com o jejum a minha alma*.

13

*in porta → à porta* (the city gate, where elders sat). *advérsum me … in me → contra mim … contra mim*: the Greek varies too (κατ᾿ ἐμοῦ / εἰς ἐμέ); *de mim* was weighed for the second and heard as 'about me', which is weaker than the mockery; kept as a repetition the Portuguese makes, not the Latin — flagged here for the readers.

14

= 5:7b's formula *na multidão da vossa misericórdia*; *véritas → verdade* (the row).

15
