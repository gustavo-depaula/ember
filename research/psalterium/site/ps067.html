Psalm-level draft from the parallels (no DM1962 for this psalm). Hard readings: 67:13 (verbless Rex … dilécti; dative + infinitive), 67:14 (cleros, pallor), 67:15–17 (the fat and curdled mountains; suspicámini with object or vocative), 67:19b (non credéntes inhabitáre), 67:24 (ab ipso), 67:31a (ut exclúdant, against the Greek). minor

“e à beleza da casa cabe repartir os despojos” et speciéi domus divídere spólia

'cabe' supplies a full verb (it falls to / it is proper), which interprets the bare dative + infinitive more than a copula would. The Latin leaves the construction elliptical.

proposede à beleza da casa, repartir os despojos

kept as an option under et speciéi domus divídere spólia — the ambiguity reader could not follow even the *cabe* version; the bare form is option 2

15

minor

“ficarão brancos de neve no Selmon” nive dealbabúntur in Selmon

The Latin future passive ('they will be whitened') becomes a resultative 'ficarão brancos', so the voice is lost.

proposedserão embranquecidos de neve no Selmon

not taken — D44 *dealbári → ficar (mais) branco*: the resultative is the ruled form, and *embranquecidos* is heavy

minor

“o Deus da nossa salvação” Deus salutárium nostrórum

'salutarium' is plural ('of our salvations'), and the translation makes it singular, which changes the number.

proposedo Deus das nossas salvações

24

minor

“a língua dos teus cães, dos inimigos, dele mesmo” lingua canum tuórum ex inimícis, ab ipso

'ex inimicis' means 'from/out of the enemies'. Placed in apposition, the bare 'dos inimigos' reads as a possessive, so the dogs seem to be the enemies. The preposition's sense of source is lost.

proposeda língua dos teus cães, a partir dos inimigos, dele mesmo

❧ taken — *de entre os inimigos* in place of his *a partir dos inimigos*

28a

minor

“fora de si” in mentis excéssu

In ordinary Portuguese, 'fora de si' usually means enraged or out of control. The Latin 'excess of mind' is ecstasy or rapture, and the translation also drops the concrete 'mentis'.

proposedem êxtase da mente

❧ taken — *em êxtase*; *da mente* after *êxtase* is redundant in Portuguese

36

minor

“bendito seja Deus” benedíctus Deus

'seja' makes the verbless Latin optative. Elsewhere in this psalm the same formula is left without a verb (67:20 'Bendito o Senhor'), so the mood is resolved here and inconsistently.

proposedbendito Deus

kept as an option under benedíctus Deus — 67:20 has an article (*Bendito o Senhor*) and stands verbless; *bendito Deus* is heard as a noun phrase, "blessed God"

A faithful, close rendering that keeps the Septuagintal oddities of this very obscure psalm (cleros as 'partilhas', mons coagulatus, speciei domus, the gen. patris orphanorum agreeing with ejus) instead of smoothing them toward the Hebrew. No critical or major faults were found, and the pointing marks match the Latin in every verse. The remaining points are minor: voice in 67:15, number in 67:20, the force of 'ex' in 67:24, the connotation of 'fora de si', and the supplied verbs 'cabe' and 'seja'.
