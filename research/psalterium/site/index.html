Psalterium

Psalmi Davidis, lingua Brasiliensi

The psalter of the Roman Breviary — the Gallican Psalter, St Jerome’s — in Brazilian Portuguese, made to be prayed aloud, verse by verse, beside the Latin.

It is drafted by language models, read by other models that do not know how it was made, and decided in the open: every alternative that was weighed, every reader’s remark and what became of it, stays one touch away. Nothing here has yet had a philologist’s sign-off.

Keep the Latin’s words, images, repetitions and ambiguities. Let the ear lead on grammar and word order.
