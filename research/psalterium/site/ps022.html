«Impinguasti» significa tornar gordo; «ungistes» conserva a aplicação do óleo, mas elimina a imagem concreta de engordar presente no latim.

Verse by verse

1

The psalm speaks of God in the third person until 22:4 turns to him (vós). 'nihil mihi déerit' (future) → 'nada me faltará' (Matos Soares 1932; the Hebrew family's present 'nada me falta' is refused). 'ibi' kept as 'ali'. The two halves are said apart as V/R at Sunday Sext and as an antiphon at Thursday Prime and in the Office of the Dead (checked in DO).

3

dedúcere → guiar (D22, 'deduc me'): the perfect 'Guiou-me'. 'propter nomen suum' → 'por causa do seu nome' (glossary propter).

4

'quóniam tu mecum es' → 'porque vós estais comigo' (D1). The familiar 'porque tu estás comigo' is the address the project does not use for God.

4b

virga → vara (glossary). The Latin's hanging nominative kept, resumed by the pronoun.

5

tribuláre → atribular (glossary; the blind reader of Ps 3 did not know it, kept).

6

The verse ends on a colon in the Latin, and so does the Portuguese: 22:6b depends on it.
