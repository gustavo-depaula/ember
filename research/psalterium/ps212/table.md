The Latin text is Jerome's Vulgate of Tobit, translated from an Aramaic text, not the Gallican psalter; the Greek (LXX 13:1–8) is a different text of the same prayer and not a witness of this Latin's sense. The stylist heard a hiatus in 'e exaltai' after the asterisk and proposed 'exaltai também'; refused: 'também' is not in the Latin (the -que is 'e'), and the two e-sounds of 'e exaltai' run together in speech and song without loss. |
