# Ps 212 (Tobit 13, *Magnus es, Dómine, in ætérnum*) — Brazilian circulation

Checked 2026-09-23. The pages are saved in `consult/`, which is gitignored because the texts are copyrighted.

## Liturgia das Horas (CNBB)

- **Where it stands.** The post-conciliar Office prays the canticle as **"Cântico Tb 13,2-8"**, titled *Deus castiga e salva*, at Lauds on Tuesday of week I.
- **Source.** liturgiadashoras.online, Tuesday of the 13th week of Ordinary Time (psalter week I), saved as `consult/lho-terca-13tc-laudes.htm` / `.txt`. The site credits the CNBB text.
- **Epigraph.** 1 Pd 1,3.
- **Antiphon.** *Vossas obras celebrem a Deus e exaltem o Rei sempiterno.*
- **A different text base.** The CNBB text translates the Nova Vulgata / Greek Tobit, not Jerome's Vulgate that DO has:
  - Its verse numbers are the Greek's (2–8).
  - It has lines our Latin lacks, such as *para narrardes sua glória entre os povos* and *fazê-los saber, para sempre*.
  - It lacks lines our Latin has, such as *crendo que … usará convosco da sua misericórdia*, which it replaces with *e sabei que, se ele vos ama, também vos dará seu perdão*.
- **A metrical paraphrase.** The Brazilian text is a free, sung paraphrase. It is not a bound on our wording (D12 applies by analogy). It is useful for one thing: what the Brazilian ear already knows in the lines both texts share.
  - *Vós sois grande, Senhor, para sempre* — our 13:1 has *Grande sois vós, Senhor, para sempre*, and *Vós sois grande* stays as an option.
  - *Porque vós castigais e salvais* — the Greek μαστιγοῖ is rendered with *castigar*. We keep *flagelar* for flagéllas and *castigar* for 13:6's castigávit (see choices 13:2).
  - *fazeis descer aos abismos da terra, e de lá nos trazeis novamente* — *trazer* is used for the return, which supports our *trazeis de volta*. The realm of the dead is paraphrased as *abismos da terra*, which is neither *inferno* nor *infernos*.
  - *de vossa mão nada pode escapar* — *escapar* is used, as in our 13:2.
  - *Nesta terra do meu cativeiro* — the same noun, *cativeiro*.
  - *Convertei-vos, enfim, pecadores* — the same verb and vocative as our 13:9.
  - *Bendizei o Senhor, seus eleitos* — the CNBB has *eleitos*, while the glossary's open row has *escolhidos* (104:6, 104:43). Our text keeps *escolhidos*; the stylist may weigh this.

## Lectionary (Mass)

- **Where it stands.** Tb 13 is sung as a responsorial psalm ("Tb 13,2.3-4.5.8"), with the refrain *Bendito seja Deus que vive eternamente!*.
- **Source.** A musicasparamissa.com.br setting by Paulo Neto, saved as `consult/mpm-salmo-tb13.htm` / `.txt`. Its strophes are word for word the LH text above, so the Brazilian Lectionary has the same translation.
- **Unverified.** A search summary placed it on Wednesday of the 25th week of Ordinary Time in odd years. I have not checked that against a Lectionary page, and nothing here depends on it.

## Apostles' Creed (for *ad ínferos*, *omnípotens*)

- **Text checked.** Vatican News pt, saved as `consult/vaticannews-simbolo-apostolos.htm` / `.txt`. It reads *Creio em Deus, Pai todo-poderoso* and *desceu à mansão dos mortos*.
- **Caveat.** This page is printed in European spelling (*há-de*, *Ámen*).
- **Brazilian wording.** A web search attributes the same two wordings to the CNBB's Credo.
- **What follows.**
  - *todo-poderoso* is the word Brazilian Catholics say for omnípotens.
  - The current Creed says *mansão dos mortos*, not *infernos*. So *aos infernos* in 13:2 is not the Creed's living wording. It is the older traditional term, and I have not verified from a source that Brazil used it in the Creed.
  - This bears on the `inferos` decision and the proposed formula row, which also covers 223 and 234. That is why *à morada dos mortos* is an option.

## DO's own use of the Latin

- **One antiphon.** The canticle's antiphon (`Psalmi major.txt`, lines 47 and 325) is *Exaltáte \* Regem sæculórum in opéribus vestris.* It reorders 13:7b to put the verb first.
- **Effect on 13:7.** Our 13:7b, *e exaltai o Rei dos séculos nas vossas obras*, keeps that order, so the antiphon can be taken from the verse as it stands.
