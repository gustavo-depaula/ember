# Canticle 220 (Benedíctus es, Dómine, Deus patrum nostrórum — Dan 3:52–57) — Brazilian circulation

Checked 2026-09-23 on the web; nothing here is from memory. The pages are saved under `consult/` (gitignored). What Brazilian ears know; never evidence of sense (rule 1).

## Liturgia das Horas (CNBB) — Sunday Lauds

Source: Paulus Editora, *Liturgia Diária das Horas*, "Dia 2 – 22º Domingo do Tempo Comum" (https://www.paulus.com.br/portal/liturgia-diaria-das-horas/dia-2-22o-domingo-do-tempo-comum/, saved as `consult/ps220/lh-paulus-22dtc-dia2.html`). Paulus prints the CNBB text. Heading: **3. Cântico Dn 3,52-57 – Louvor das criaturas ao Senhor**, between Ps 117 and Ps 150 (the Sunday Lauds of that week of the psalter; which week was not checked beyond this page). Short fragments only: the opening *Sede bendito, Senhor Deus de nossos pais!*; the refrain after every line, *A vós louvor, honra e glória eternamente!*; *nome santo e glorioso*; *que sondais as profundezas*; *vos assentais* (the cherubim); *no celeste firmamento*.

It is a metrical paraphrase: the Latin's second cola (*et laudábilis, et gloriósus…*) are replaced by one refrain; *super-* is not rendered; the last line (Vulg 3:57 = DO 3:58) is in it.

## Lectionary (Mass)

Source: Pai Nosso Deus song sheet (`consult/lecionario-dn3-52-painossodeus.html`, fetched earlier by the ps210 agent): **Salmo responsorial – Dn 3,52.53-54.55.56-57 (R. 52b)**, refrain *A vós louvor, honra e glória eternamente!*, strophes word for word the LH text above. A second page (`consult/lecionario-dn3-fiquefirme.html`) gives the same refrain for *Dn 3 – 4ª feira da 5ª Semana da Quaresma*. So the Lectionary uses the LH wording.

## Matos Soares 1932 (Vulgate family; a witness of sense, not of circulation)

`consult/ps220/ms1932-dan3-p766-767.txt` (full text in the gitignored parallels file). Fragments: *Tu és bendito, Senhor Deus de nossos pais*; *bendito é o teu nome santo e glorioso*; *levantado acima de todo o louvor*; *tu que penetras o fundo dos abismos*; *assentado sobre os Querubins*.

## Diurnal Monástico 1962

Does not contain this canticle (grep of `consult/diurnal-1.md`, `diurnal-2.md` for *nossos pais*, *querubins*: no hit in a Daniel canticle). The monastic Sunday Lauds uses the Benedicite (canticle 210).

## What this draft takes and leaves

- Shared with the CNBB where it is also the Latin: *Senhor Deus de nossos pais* (here *Senhor, Deus dos nossos pais*, the article as the psalter has it), *templo santo … vossa glória*, *querubins*, *firmamento*.
- Left: *Sede bendito* (a wish; the Latin's *es* is indicative — option in `benedictus_es`); *nome santo e glorioso* (resolves the genitive — option in `nome53`); *sondais as profundezas* (option in `intu`); the paraphrased refrain.
