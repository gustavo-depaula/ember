# Batch 6 of new cards: faces

Faces, dress and settings for ten saints of the general calendar: John of God, Cyril of Jerusalem, Francis of Paola, Vincent Ferrer, Anselm, Louis de Montfort, Pius V, Bernardine of Siena, Christopher Magallanes and companions, Bede. The card data is in `../batches/batch-6.json`. As in `first-batch.md`, each "Face:" line uses the marks quoted below it, and says so where a mark is an artistic choice. Pictures and texts consulted are in `../consult/batch-6/` (the contact sheets `sheet-sources.jpg` and `sheet-sources-2.jpg` put the pictures side by side; `sheet-existing.jpg` shows the existing friars and bishops they must not repeat).

Excerpts: nine come from the feast's collect in `content/of/formularies/sanctoral/MM-DD.json`, checked verbatim with a script (the collect is the only proper text in these files besides two gospel acclamations). Clauses are kept word for word and turned into a wish; `excerptSource` says what was dropped. Montfort has no formulary in the repo (04-28.json is Peter Chanel's), so his card quotes *True Devotion* in Faber's 1863 translation.

## John of God

Face: a lean, ascetic man of about fifty, olive-skinned, with a long, angular face, a pronounced baldness over the crown, short dark hair at the sides, a thin dark moustache and a short, sparse beard on the chin, and eyes lowered with compassion.

- Romero Dorado and Moreno Arana, *Luisa Roldán: un San Juan de Dios perdido reencontrado*, Norba 42 (2022) (local `john_of_god-roldan-norbaarte.pdf`/`.txt`): the Sanlúcar type is "de cabeza de pronunciada calva y ascético rostro, alargado y anguloso"; it is close to "el Retrato de San Juan de Dios conservado en la Casa de los Pisa, de Granada, obra de Pedro de Raxis el Viejo … se considera que Raxis conoció en vida a San Juan de Dios, por lo que su pintura podría ser un verdadero retrato del santo, tomado del natural". The same article notes "la incipiente barba del santo". https://doi.org/10.17398/2660-714X.42.437
- Pictures checked: the Raxis-type portrait (`john_of_god-de.jpg`): a long, angular face, a balding brow, a strong nose, a thin moustache and a small beard, dark eyes; Alonso Cano's polychrome head (`john_of_god-cano.jpg`): the same balding, long, hollow-cheeked face; Murillo's *St John of God carrying a sick man* (`john_of_god-murillo.jpg`), for the habit.
- Age: 1495–1550; the Granada hospital years are his last decade.
- Attributes: the pomegranate crowned with a cross (Granada, "granada"), and the alms baskets he carried through the city begging for his sick. The sick man on his back is left out: a second figure would crowd the half-length card.
- Background: Granada and the Alhambra, with the Sierra Nevada.

## Cyril of Jerusalem

Face: an old man of about sixty-five with a wide, rectangular face, short iron-grey hair receding at the temples and a thick rounded iron-grey beard.

- Didron, *Manuel d'iconographie chrétienne* (the Painter's Manual; local `../prelates-src/didron-fr.txt`, l. 14297): "Saint Cyrille de Jérusalem : vieillard, barbe arrondie."
- Picture checked: the Russian icon medallion (`cyril_jerusalem-wiki.jpg`): a bald crown, a long grey beard, a pale blue phelonion, a white omophorion with black crosses, a book, blessing.
- Artistic choice: the icon's bald dome and white beard would repeat athanasius and nicholas (both bald with white beards on the existing cards). The card keeps the Manual's "rounded beard" and old age but makes the beard and hair iron-grey and the face wide and square.
- Dress: the icon's pale blue phelonion and white omophorion; teaching, with the book of his *Catecheses*, which he preached in the Holy Sepulchre.
- Background: the Anastasis rotunda and the Mount of Olives.

## Francis of Paola

Face: a very old, wiry hermit of about seventy-five, hooded, with a narrow, weathered face, a long hooked nose, small deep-set eyes, hollow cheeks and a long, thin, wispy grey-white beard parting into two strands.

- Picture checked: Giambattista Tiepolo's altarpiece (`francis_paola-tiepolo.jpg`): a gaunt, hooded old hermit with a long grey beard, a knotted staff, a dark brown habit, kneeling.
- Wikipedia (local `wiki-Francis_of_Paola.txt`): 1416–1507, died at ninety-one; founder of the Minims; the word *Charitas* in a sun is the order's emblem. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_of_Paola
- Age: an artistic choice of about seventy-five, old but not decrepit.
- The CHARITAS sun is shown, as asked, as a plain radiant disc with no lettering.
- Background: Paola and the Strait of Messina, which he crossed on his cloak in the legend; Sicily and Etna beyond.

## Vincent Ferrer

Face: a clean-shaven man of about sixty with a long, full oval face, a long nose with a fleshy tip, large heavy-lidded dark eyes under high arched brows, and greying dark hair in a thick corona.

- Picture checked: Joan de Joanes, *San Vicente Ferrer* (`vincent_ferrer-joanes.jpg`): clean-shaven, a full oval face, a long nose, heavy-lidded eyes, a tonsure, the Dominican white and black, the raised forefinger, a book, and a banderole with Rev 14:7 ("Timete Deum et date illi honorem quia venit hora iudicii eius").
- Life (Wikipedia; no local copy): 1350–1419; he preached the coming Judgment across Europe and called himself the angel of Rev 14:6, hence the wings of his iconography. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Ferrer
- Age: he preached until his death at sixty-nine; the greying hair is an artistic choice that keeps him apart from the young dominic and thomas_aquinas.
- No banderole: the card carries no lettering.
- Background: Valencia, his birthplace, with the cathedral and the Micalet.

## Anselm

Face: a man of about seventy with a long, high-domed head, a very high receding forehead, a strong aquiline nose, deep-set pale eyes, hollow cheeks, thin grey hair long at the nape, and a very short grey beard.

- Picture checked: André Thevet's engraved portrait in *Les vrais pourtraits et vies des hommes illustres* (1584) (`anselm-wiki.jpg`): in profile, a high bald forehead, long grey hair falling behind, a strong aquiline nose, hollow cheek, stubble on jaw and chin. Thevet's portraits are imaginative, but this is the image that fixed his type.
- The Chester Cathedral window (`anselm-chester.jpg`): mitre, pallium, crosier, book; beardless.
- Wikipedia (local `wiki-Anselm_of_Canterbury.txt`): 1033–1109; monk and abbot of Bec, archbishop of Canterbury from 1093; *Proslogion*, "faith seeking understanding". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anselm_of_Canterbury
- Dress: a black Benedictine cowl with the pallium and crosier, bareheaded, to keep him apart from the mitred augustine and ambrose.
- Excerpt: the collect's petition, which is his *fides quaerens intellectum*.

## Louis de Montfort

Face: a tall, robust man of about forty, clean-shaven, ruddy, with a long face, a broad high forehead, an aquiline nose, large lively eyes lowered modestly, a rather long chin, and very short flat chestnut hair.

- Besnard, *Vie*, book VIII, quoted by Henri Daniel, *Saint Louis-Marie Grignion de Montfort* (1967) (local `montfort-daniel.txt`, l. 8421): "Sa taille était au-dessus de la médiocre, sa constitution robuste, un air de grandeur, mais avec cela de la bonté, prévenant, affable, agréable. Il avait les joues assez vermeilles, le visage long, le front large et élevé, les yeux grands et vifs, cependant très modestes, le nez aquilin, sans être trop cavé comme on le représente, le menton un peu long, les cheveux châtains, plats et fort courts et retombant modestement sur le haut de la tête, un peu au-dessus du front."
- Daniel, same work: "le crucifix et la petite statuette de Marie qu'il portait continuellement sur lui."
- Picture checked: the devotional print (`montfort-wiki.jpg`): black cassock with white bands, a long face and aquiline nose, a rosary, a statue of the Virgin and Child.
- Age: 1673–1716; the missions in the west of France are his last decade.
- Background: the Calvary of Pontchâteau, raised by the people at his missions in 1709–10.
- Excerpt: *True Devotion* 49, Faber's translation (local `montfort-faber-bub_gb_OccCAAAAQAAJ.txt`): "It is by Mary that the salvation of the world has begun, and it is by Mary that it must be consummated." The pt-BR line is our rendering. https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_OccCAAAAQAAJ

## Pius V

Face: a thin, ascetic man of about sixty-six with a gaunt, long face, a bald bony forehead, a long aquiline nose, deep-set dark eyes, hollow cheeks and a full white beard trimmed square.

- Picture checked: Bartolomeo Passarotti's portrait (`pius_v-passarotti.jpg`): seated, gaunt, bald under the camauro, a long aquiline nose, deep-set eyes, hollow cheeks, a full white beard; a red mozzetta over white.
- Wikipedia (local `wiki-Pope_Pius_V.txt`): 1504–1572, a Dominican; pope from 1566; he attributed Lepanto (1571) to the Virgin's intercession and instituted the feast of Our Lady of Victory. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Pius_V
- Dress: all in white, as asked, the Dominican white he kept as pope; no red mozzetta. The rosary and the far-off galleys carry Lepanto.
- Pastor's *History of the Popes*, vol. 18 (local `pastor-v18-piusV.txt`) was searched for a written portrait and has none in this volume.

## Bernardine of Siena

Face: a small, gaunt man of about sixty-three, clean-shaven, with a thin pointed face, a bald crown with a thin grey fringe, a long fine nose, heavy-lidded eyes, sunken cheeks, a mouth drawn slightly inward and a prominent upturned chin.

- Wikipedia (local `wiki-Bernardino_of_Siena.txt`): "Bernardino, it seems, has the distinction of being the first saint in Church history for whom we have an indisputably authentic portrait in art. Many of the earliest portraits were presumably based on his death mask"; "Artists … often represented him as small and emaciated, with three mitres at his feet … and holding in his hand the IHS monogram with rays emanating from it." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernardino_of_Siena
- Picture checked: the portrait (`bernardine-wiki.png`): bald with white hair at the sides, heavy-lidded lowered eyes, a long nose, a toothless sunken mouth and a jutting chin, grey habit, a small cross.
- Idealised: the toothless mouth becomes only a slight inward fold of thin lips.
- The tablet is a sun disc without the IHS monogram (no lettering on the cards). The three mitres are left out of the half-length.
- Background: the Piazza del Campo, where he preached in 1425.

## Christopher Magallanes and companions

Face (Christopher): a mestizo man of about fifty-eight, broad and full-faced, clean-shaven, with thick wavy black hair combed up and back, heavy-lidded dark eyes, a broad nose and full lips.

Face (Agustín Caloca): a slim man of about twenty-nine with a narrow oval face, short dark hair parted at the side, straight brows, clean-shaven.

- Pictures checked: the studio photograph of Magallanes as parish priest of Totatiche (`magallanes-photo.jpg`): clean-shaven, a broad, heavy face, thick wavy hair combed up, heavy lids, cassock with Roman collar. Caloca's photograph (`caloca-wiki.jpg`): young, narrow-faced, short parted hair, ears set out, clean-shaven, cassock.
- Magallanes (1869–1927) and Caloca (1898–1927), his assistant, were shot together at Colotlán on 25 May 1927; they lead the 25 companions canonised in 2000. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saints_of_the_Cristero_War
- He died before 1930, but the card still takes formulary text: the collect's "faithful to Christ the King even to the point of martyrdom".
- No violence: the crucifix, rosary, breviary and palm say "martyrs".
- Name: en-US "Sts." for the group; pt-BR follows the calendar's *Cristóvão Magalhães*.

## Bede

Face: a clean-shaven man of about sixty with a broad, flat-planed oval face, a short, slightly upturned nose, pale blue eyes, full cheeks, a faint smile, and a tonsure with a ring of fair hair turned grey.

- No likeness survives. Pictures checked: the twelfth-century manuscript image (`bede-ecodices.jpg`): beardless, tonsured, writing on a tablet; Doyle Penrose, *The Last Chapter* (`bede-doyle-penrose.jpg`): an old hooded monk with a long white beard, dictating.
- Wikipedia (local `wiki-Bede.txt`): 672/3–735, monk of Monkwearmouth–Jarrow from the age of seven; *Ecclesiastical History of the English People*. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bede
- Choice: the beardless manuscript type, because a white-bearded Benedictine would repeat benedict and jerome. The fair colouring is an artistic choice for a Northumbrian.
- Background: Jarrow on the Tyne.

## Pairs that may still look alike

Checked against content/saints and the faces in batches 1–5.

- **Pius V and cletus (batch 3) / charbel / benedict**: gaunt old men with white beards. Pius V's beard is medium and square (Cletus's is long and forked, Charbel's falls to the chest), and he is all in papal white with a zucchetto. **Pius V and pius_x**: both in white zucchetto; Pius X is clean-shaven and broad-faced.
- **Francis of Paola and anthony_abbot / jerome**: old hermits with long beards. Francis is hooded, his beard wispy and parted, with the sun disc.
- **Cyril of Jerusalem and athanasius / nicholas**: Eastern bishops in omophorion with crosses. Cyril has iron-grey, not white, hair and beard, a square face, and a pale blue phelonion. Check this pair on the contact sheet.
- **Anselm and paul_vi / fabian (batch 5)**: long-faced, high-foreheaded old men. Anselm has grey hair long at the nape, a short grey beard and the black cowl with pallium.
- **Vincent Ferrer and dominic / thomas_aquinas**: clean-shaven Dominicans with a corona. Vincent is sixty and greying, and has the wings and raised finger.
- **Bernardine and bonaventure / frei_galvao (batch 5) / fabian**: clean-shaven friars. Bernardine is small and gaunt with a sunken mouth and upturned chin, holding the sun tablet.
- **John of God and paul_vi / cyril_methodius (batch 3)**: long, balding ascetic faces. John has a thin moustache and chin beard, olive skin, a rough habit and the pomegranate.
- **Montfort and vincent_de_paul / alphonsus_liguori**: priests in black cassocks. Montfort is bareheaded, forty, ruddy, with very short chestnut hair and the statuette.
- **Christopher Magallanes and josemaria_escriva (batch 4) / john_xxiii**: broad, clean-shaven priests. Magallanes has a brown complexion and thick wavy black hair, and stands beside the young Caloca.
- **Bede and blase / john_baptist_de_la_salle**: soft, clean-shaven faces. Bede is tonsured in a black cowl at a writing desk.
