# Batch 8 of new cards: faces

Faces, dress and settings for ten saints of August and September: Cajetan, Jane Frances de Chantal, Stephen of Hungary, Louis IX, Peter Claver, Robert Bellarmine, Januarius, Cosmas and Damian, Wenceslaus, and Lawrence Ruiz. The card data is in `../batches/batch-8.json`. As in `first-batch.md`, each "Face:" line keeps only marks with a source below it and says where a mark is an artistic choice. Pictures and texts consulted are in `../consult/batch-8/` (`sheet-sources.jpg` puts the ten Wikipedia lead images side by side; `sheet-existing.jpg` shows the nearest existing cards).

Excerpts: `consult/batch-8/verify.py` checks that each line appears in its cited file after normalising case and punctuation. Cajetan and Lawrence Ruiz have no proper formulary in the repo (`08-07.json` is Sixtus II; there is no Ruiz file), so their lines come from Commons. Januarius and Cosmas and Damian have only a generic collect, so theirs also come from the Common of Martyrs.

## Cajetan

Face: a man of about fifty with a round face, large, beautiful dark eyes, a small, sweet mouth, short dark hair thinning at the crown, and a short, rounded dark beard with grey in it, bending toward the Child.

- No portrait from life. Erasmo Danese's description, as quoted in the iconographic study of the Theatine jubilee: "statura mediocre … viso tondo, occhi belli, bocca piena di dolcezza" (medium height, round face, beautiful eyes, a mouth full of sweetness). https://www.aboutartonline.com/liconografia-di-san-gaetano-thiene-nellarte-convegno-dedicato-ai-500-anni-della-fondazione-dellordine-teatino/
- The same study: artists follow his letter to Laura Mignani about Christmas 1517 at the crib altar of Santa Maria Maggiore. He wears "a simple black cassock cinched at the waist", with his head inclined toward the Child, who "caresses his beard". Solimena's painting (local `wiki-Saint_Cajetan.jpg`, checked): a dark, short beard and receding hair, a surplice over the black cassock.
- Age: 1480–1547. The vision was at 37; the card makes him about fifty, between the vision and his Naples years.
- The wheat: in Brazil, São Caetano is invoked for "pão e trabalho", so a few ears of wheat are added at the edge.
- Background: Santa Maria Maggiore, the place of the vision. Naples (where he is buried) is left to Januarius.
- Excerpt: the communion antiphon of the Common of Holy Men (sanct5), Mt 6:33, which matches the 1970 collect's petition that we "seek unceasingly your Kingdom". The Common has no Portuguese in the repo; the pt-BR line is our rendering.

## Jane Frances de Chantal

Face: a woman of about fifty-five with a broad oval face, full lower cheeks, a long straight nose, wide-set grey-blue eyes, thin lips with a faint smile, and a firm chin, framed by the Visitation veil and guimpe.

- The seventeenth-century portrait (Wikipedia lead, local `wiki-Jane_Frances_de_Chantal.jpg`), which I checked: a broad, smooth oval face, light eyes, a long nose, thin closed lips with a hint of a smile, the black veil over a white band, the wide square white guimpe, and a silver cross with IHS on her breast. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Frances_de_Chantal
- The heart: the Catholic Encyclopedia says that as a widow she branded the name of Jesus over her heart. The card gives her a small red heart with the golden IHS, not a wound. https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08286a.htm
- Age: 1572–1641. She founded the Visitation at Annecy in 1610, at 38. The portrait is of an older woman; about fifty-five splits the difference.
- Background: the Visitation cloister at Annecy with the Semnoz. The lake and the Alps are on francis_de_sales, so the card leaves out the lake.
- Excerpt: the collect of 08-12. The two languages paraphrase differently ("examples of shining light" / "a vossa luz resplandeça em nossas obras"); each line is verbatim in its own language.

## Stephen of Hungary

Face: a king of about sixty with a square, heavy-boned face, a strong straight nose, deep-set grey eyes under heavy brows, a drooping moustache and a long, full, grey-streaked beard cut square, and straight hair to the shoulders under the Holy Crown.

- The coronation mantle of 1031, which he and Gisela gave to Székesfehérvár, shows him with "a circlet crown richly decorated with gemstones … In his right hand, he holds a lance … his left hand holds an orb." https://dailynewshungary.com/1000-year-old-authentic-image-saint-stephen/ ; https://kultura.hu/en/st-stephens-coronation-mantle/ The local copy `wiki-Stephen_I_of_Hungary.jpg` (the mantle detail) is too worn to read the face; it shows the crowned figure and the gold ground.
- Later Hungarian tradition gives him the Holy Crown with its bent cross, the double (apostolic) cross, and a mature, bearded face. The square face, the beard's shape and the age are artistic choices to separate him from the two other rulers in this batch.
- Dress: a red bell-shaped mantle embroidered in gold, recalling the coronation mantle.
- Background: Esztergom, his birthplace and seat, with the basilica above the Danube.
- Excerpt: two verbatim phrases of the 08-16 collect. The English and Portuguese collects word the clause differently ("who fostered her growth while a king on earth" / "um valoroso propagador do Evangelho, quando reinava na terra").

## Louis IX of France

Face: a tall, slender man of about forty with a long, narrow oval face, a fine straight nose, wide-set light-blue eyes, slightly hollow cheeks, clean-shaven, and straight ash-blond hair with the first grey cut level at the jaw.

- Salimbene of Parma saw him at Sens in 1248 and described him as "slender and delicate, tall, … a very pleasing face and an angelic expression" (Wikipedia, which quotes him). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_IX_of_France
- The thirteenth-century miniature (Wikipedia lead, local `wiki-Louis_IX_of_France.jpg`), which I checked: a beardless king with fair hair to the jaw, crowned, in blue and gold, with a sceptre.
- Age: 1214–1270. The card makes him about forty, around the building of the Sainte-Chapelle (consecrated 1248) and the return from the first crusade. The greying and the hollow cheeks mark the adult penitent king and keep him apart from george.png and martin_tours.png, both youthful, beardless and curly or wavy blond.
- Attributes: the Crown of Thorns in its reliquary, which he brought to Paris in 1239; the blue mantle with gold fleurs-de-lis.
- Background: the Sainte-Chapelle and the Île de la Cité.
- Excerpt: the collect of 08-25.

## Peter Claver, with an African man he serves

Face: Claver is a lean, clean-shaven Jesuit of about fifty with a triangular face, a long aquiline nose, deep-set dark eyes and short black receding hair. The man beside him is a young West African of about twenty-five with a smooth oval face, high rounded cheekbones and short curled hair, standing upright.

- The Cartagena portrait type (Wikipedia lead `StPeterClaver.jpg`, checked on the page) and the painting in `wiki-Peter_Claver.jpg`: a gaunt, clean-shaven Jesuit in black with short dark hair, among the Africans in the harbour. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Claver
- Catholic Encyclopedia: he met every slave ship at Cartagena and went down into the holds with medicine, food and water. Most captives came from Angola and the Guinea coast. https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11763b.htm
- The companion: as the brief asks, dignified. He stands and faces Claver as an equal, in clean white linen, with no chains or wounds, receiving the cup of water. His face is built to differ from moses_the_black (full beard, stern), charles_lwanga (long, sculpted face), benedito and martin_de_porres (older lay brothers).
- Age: 1580–1654. He reached Cartagena in 1610 and worked there almost forty years; about fifty is the middle of that.
- Excerpt: the collect of 09-09 ("love our neighbor in deeds and in truth", 1 Jn 3:18). The same collect calls him "a slave of slaves", which gives the patronOf line.

## Robert Bellarmine

Face: a small, lean man of about seventy with a long, rectangular face, a long prominent nose, deep-set heavy-lidded dark eyes, sunken cheeks, a trimmed grey moustache and a short pointed grey goatee, in cardinal's red.

- The portrait at the Plantin-Moretus Museum, Antwerp (Wikipedia lead, local `wiki-Robert_Bellarmine.jpg`), which I checked: a long face, a long straight nose, heavy-lidded dark eyes, grey hair at the temples, a grey moustache and a pointed goatee, a red biretta and mozzetta over a white collar. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Bellarmine
- Thomas Hobbes, who saw him in Rome on All Saints' Day 1614, called him "a little lean old man" (Wikipedia).
- Age: 1542–1621; cardinal from 1599. At 72 in Hobbes's account.
- Attributes: his *Controversies* (a heavy book) and a quill. Background: the Collegio Romano, where he taught, and Sant'Ignazio, where he is buried.
- Excerpt: the collect of 09-17. The pt-BR clause ends with "conservá-la", whose antecedent ("a fé católica") is in the omitted clause; on the card it reads as the faith.

## Januarius

Face: a bishop of about forty with an angular face, prominent cheekbones, a straight Roman nose, dark intense eyes, a strong jaw with a small cleft chin, olive skin, short dark curling hair, clean-shaven.

- No likeness survives. The Neapolitan painting on Wikipedia (local `wiki-Januarius.jpg`), which I checked: a clean-shaven, dark-haired bishop in his prime, in cope and mitre, with the crozier and the ampoules of blood. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Januarius
- The Naples reliquary bust of 1305, given by Charles II of Anjou, is the chief cult image; the New Liturgical Movement's survey of the chapel's busts has photographs. https://www.newliturgicalmovement.org/2024/05/reliquary-busts-of-chapel-of-st.html
- Age: the cleft chin and the angular structure are artistic choices so that he is neither the stock thirties man with curls and a beard nor blase (round, ruddy, clean-shaven).
- Attributes: the two ampoules of blood in a silver reliquary, and Vesuvius (he is invoked against its eruptions). No head or sword; the martyrdom stays out of the picture.
- Excerpt: the communion antiphon of the Common of Martyrs for one martyr (mart6). The 09-19 collect is a generic memorial collect.

## Cosmas and Damian

Face: twin Syrian-Arab physicians of about thirty with olive-brown skin and the same dark almond eyes and aquiline nose. Cosmas has a broader, square-jawed face, heavier brows, short waved black hair and a short, sparse beard. Damian has a narrower, heart-shaped face with a small pointed chin, finer brows, wavy hair to the ears, a faint moustache and a wisp of chin beard.

- Dionysius (Didron 1845, p. 330, local `../consult/prelates-src/didron-fr.txt`): three pairs of Anargyroi. "Ceux de Rome, Côme et Damien : jeunes, barbe en pointe … Ceux d'Arabie : noirs, peu de barbe, la tête enveloppée d'un voile." The Roman feast of 26 September keeps the Arabian twins martyred at Cyrrhus, so the card follows "little beard" and a dark complexion, rendered as olive-brown. The head-veil is left out to match the Latin dress.
- Bourdichon's miniature (local `wiki-Saints_Cosmas_and_Damian.jpg`, checked): two physicians in long robes with fur trim, one holding up a glass urine flask, the other an ointment jar. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saints_Cosmas_and_Damian
- Brazilian devotion (Cosme e Damião are very popular in Brazil) dresses them in red and green with palms and a box of medicine. The card follows it and crosses the colours so they differ.
- Twins yet distinguishable: the shared eyes, nose and skin make them brothers; the face shape, brows, hair length and beard tell them apart. Cosmas looks out, Damian toward his brother.
- Background: the Roman Forum, where Pope Felix IV turned the Temple of Romulus into the basilica of Santi Cosma e Damiano (526–530).
- Excerpt: the second entrance antiphon of the Common of several martyrs (mart4), on the saints who "persevered in loving brotherhood", chosen for the twins.

## Wenceslaus

Face: a duke of about twenty-eight with a broad Slavic face, high flat cheekbones and a short firm chin, a short straight nose, light grey eyes, chestnut hair cut just below the ears, a neat moustache and a short, small forked beard.

- The statue in the St. Wenceslas Chapel of St. Vitus, attributed to Peter Parler (1373; local `wenceslaus-parler.jpg`, re-downloaded because the earlier copy was empty), which I checked: a young man with a moustache and a short forked beard, hair to the jaw, a crowned ducal cap, armour with a tunic, a lance, and a shield with the eagle. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wenzeslaus_by_Peter_Parler.JPG
- The Gumpold manuscript (local `wiki-Wenceslaus_I,_Duke_of_Bohemia.jpg`) shows him bearded and crowned at the foot of Christ. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenceslaus_I,_Duke_of_Bohemia
- Age: born about 907, killed in 935 at Stará Boleslav, under thirty.
- Hair kept short on purpose: long hair with a forked beard would read as Christ.
- Background: Prague Castle and St. Vitus above the Vltava.
- Excerpt: the collect of 09-28.

## Lawrence Ruiz and companions

Face: a Filipino-Chinese man of about forty with golden-tan skin and a round-square face, wide flat cheekbones, a broad low-bridged nose, dark almond eyes with a slight epicanthic fold, short straight black hair, clean-shaven.

- Born in Binondo about 1594 "to a Chinese father and a Filipino mother"; his father taught him Chinese and his mother Tagalog; an *escribano* (calligrapher) trained by the Dominicans, a member of the Rosary confraternity, married with three children; executed at Nishizaka, Nagasaki, in 1637. https://www.ucanews.com/directory/saint/lorenzo-ruiz-filipino-layman-who-became-a-martyr-in-japan/15 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Ruiz
- Attributes (Wikipedia): "rosary in clasped hands, gallows and pit, barong tagalog or camisa de chino and black trousers, cross, palm of martyrdom". The stained glass in the local sheet (`wiki-Lorenzo_Ruiz.jpg`) shows a clean-shaven young man with straight black hair and a rosary; the card makes him his real age, about forty.
- Companions: the card shows him alone, as the Philippine image does; the name carries the companions. The gallows and pit are left out (no gore).
- Background: Binondo church, his home parish, with its octagonal bell tower, rather than Nishizaka, which is already paul_miki's hill.
- Excerpt: the communion antiphon of the Common of several missionary martyrs (mart11). It suits his reply to the judges that he was a Christian.

## Pairs that may still look alike

Checked against `content/saints/` and the face lines of batches 1–5. The batch 6 and 7 JSON files are not in this worktree, so those batches were not compared.

- **Louis and martin_tours / george**: all three are beardless and fair. Louis is about forty, long-faced, straight-haired and greying, in blue and ermine with the Crown of Thorns. Martin is younger with wavy blond hair in armour; George has auburn curls. Check the sheet for a youthful drift.
- **Wenceslaus and george / martin_tours**: all three in armour. Wenceslaus is the only one with a moustache and forked beard, and has the ducal cap and the eagle shield. Keep george.png out of the refs, as the JSON does.
- **Stephen and Wenceslaus / Louis**: three rulers, kept apart by age (sixty, twenty-eight, forty), face shape (square, broad Slavic, long narrow) and beard (long square, short forked, none).
- **Bellarmine and charles_borromeo**: both cardinals in red. Borromeo is clean-shaven with a large hooked nose; Bellarmine is older, with a grey goatee and heavy lids.
- **Peter Claver and francis_xavier / ignatius_loyola / jose_anchieta**: Jesuits in black. Claver is the only clean-shaven gaunt one and shares the frame with the African man.
- **Cajetan and ignatius_loyola / vincent_de_paul**: bearded priests in black. Cajetan is round-faced with the Child; Ignatius is long-faced and bald, Vincent white-bearded.
- **Cajetan and anthony_padua**: both hold the Child. Anthony is a young, clean-shaven Franciscan in brown; Cajetan is bearded in black and surplice.
- **Januarius and blase / ambrose / nicholas**: bishops. Januarius is clean-shaven with an angular face and holds the ampoules; Blase is round and ruddy with candles.
- **Jane Frances and margaret_mary**: both Visitation nuns in the same habit, and margaret_mary is a ref. Jane is older and broader-faced, holds the IHS heart and the Rule (not the Sacred Heart vision), and stands in Annecy. This is the closest pair; watch it.
- **Lawrence Ruiz and paul_miki / andrew_kim / andrew_dung_lac**: East and Southeast Asian martyrs. Ruiz is a layman in a white camisa, round-square and fuller-faced, with a different background.
- **Cosmas and Damian**: the twins themselves may collapse into one face. The JSON asks for the face-shape and beard differences explicitly; regenerate if they come out identical.
