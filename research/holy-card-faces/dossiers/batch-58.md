# Batch 58: the Consecration in four cards

The single Consecration card of batch 47 showed the elevation of the Host. It becomes four cards, in the order of the rite: the Consecration of the Host, the Elevation of the Host, the Consecration of the Chalice, the Elevation of the Chalice. Convention, sources and builder as in `dossiers/batch-56.md` (**RS**, **F**; `consult/batch-56/build.py`).

## What the rubrics give

- **At the words over the Host** the priest stands "cubitis super Altare positis, stans capite inclinato", and says the words "distincte, reverenter et secreto", holding the Host "pollicibus et indicibus" (RS VIII.5). Fortescue: "He now bows over the altar, leaning the forearms on it. Holding the bread before him, he says … the words of consecration" (F l. 4871–4874).
- **At the elevation of the Host** he genuflects, then "elevat in altum Hostiam, et intentis in eam oculis … populo reverenter ostendit adorandam", lays it on the corporal and genuflects again (RS VIII.5–6). "He lifts it straight up before him to such a height that it may be seen from behind, over his head" (F l. 4889–4890).
- **At the words over the Chalice** he holds it "ambabus manibus … sinistra pedem, dextera nodum infra cuppam, cubitis super Altare positis, et capite inclinato" (RS VIII.7), "tenens illum parum elevatum" (DO `Latin/Ordo/Ordo.txt`). "He bends over the altar, leaning the forearms on it. He lifts the chalice a little from the altar" (F l. 4945–4953).
- **At the elevation of the Chalice** he sets it down saying Haec quotiescumque, genuflects, and "accipiens Calicem discoopertum cum Sanguine ambabus manibus, ut prius, elevat eum, et erectum quantum commode potest, ostendit populo adorandum" (RS VIII.7).
- **The server** "manu sinistra elevat fimbrias posteriores Planetae, ne ipsum Celebrantem impediat in elevatione brachiorum; quod et facit in elevatione Calicis; et manu dextera pulsat campanulam ter ad unamquamque elevationem, vel continuate" (RS VIII.6). Only at the elevations: "At each elevation he holds up slightly the end of the chasuble", with the note "Only as the priest elevates, not as he genuflects" (F l. 6384–6390, 6403). So on the two Consecration cards no one touches the chasuble and the bell is at rest.
- **The thurifer**, kneeling at the epistle side, incenses the Host and the Chalice with three double swings at each elevation (RS VIII.8; F l. 9949–9956). **The torches** are held from the Sanctus until after the elevation of the Chalice (RS VIII.8; F l. 9613–9617), so they are on all four cards.

## Decisions

1. **The existing picture is kept as the Elevation of the Host.** It is that moment, rightly drawn. The card and its image are renamed `consecration` → `elevation_host`, and only its illuminated initial changes, C to E (`drafts/` edit, logged). The old Consecration card's words, the new Mass's "Take this, all of you, and eat of it", go with it: all four cards now quote the Divinum Officium Ordo.
2. **The hem of the chasuble stays lifted on the two Elevation cards.** `to-assess.md` records "No hem" for batch 47 and then the later decision that the traditional look overrides it; `dossiers/batch-47.md` ("reverses 'no hem'") drew it from RS VIII.6, and the kept picture has it. The Elevation of the Chalice has to match that picture, and the rubric says "quod et facit in elevatione Calicis".
3. **Two viewpoints, not one.** The Elevations are seen straight on from the foot of the steps, as the kept picture is: that is how the people see them, and the Host and Chalice rise above the priest's head. From that same place the priest's bowed body hides the Host and the Chalice at the words of consecration, and the two Consecration cards would be one picture twice. So the Consecrations are seen from the same foot of the steps a few paces to the right, where his hands show. Altar, vestments, servers, thurifer and torchbearers are the same on all four: the three new cards take the kept picture as a fourth reference.
4. **Excerpts.** The two Consecrations carry the words of consecration, which the Ordo prints in capitals and the cards set in sentence case. The Elevation of the Host carries "My Lord and my God", the words the Ordo's rubric gives the faithful as they look up at the Host (English and Portuguese `Ordo.txt`; it is not in the Latin). The Elevation of the Chalice carries Haec quotiescumque, said as the chalice is set down before it is adored and raised.
5. **`mystery_of_faith` now overlaps.** It shows the elevation of the Chalice from a side aisle, with the faithful in the foreground. It stays, right after the Elevation of the Chalice. See the open question in `to-assess.md`.

## The cards

- **`consecration_host`** — bowed over the altar, forearms on it, the Host between thumbs and forefingers above the corporal; the chalice covered. Excerpt: "For this is My Body."
- **`elevation_host`** — the kept picture: the Host raised above the head, the hem lifted, the bell, the thurible, the torches. Excerpt: "My Lord and my God."
- **`consecration_chalice`** — bowed, the uncovered chalice a little raised in both hands, the Host on the corporal. Excerpt: the words over the chalice, whole.
- **`elevation_chalice`** — as the Elevation of the Host, with the Chalice. Excerpt: "As often as ye do these things, ye shall do them in remembrance of Me."

## Drafts

The first draft of `consecration_chalice` was seen from the left of the altar while `consecration_host` was seen from the right; it was redrawn with the accepted Consecration of the Host as its fourth reference, so the two Consecrations share one view. The other three were accepted as first drawn.
