## The three the user named

- **Carlo Acutis** was canonized on 7 Sep 2025. His memorial is 12 Oct.
- **Pier Giorgio Frassati** was canonized on 7 Sep 2025. His memorial is 4 Jul.
- **Fulton J. Sheen is not a saint.** He was **beatified on 24 Sep 2026** in St. Louis, at the Dome at America's Center. Cardinal Luis Antonio Tagle presided as papal legate and read Leo XIV's apostolic letter. His beatification had been postponed in December 2019. His miracle (the 2010 revival of James Fulton Engstrom) was approved by Francis in 2019. Pope Leo set his feast for **9 December**, the anniversary of his death. EWTN News reports it as "expected to be on the calendar of the Church in the United States", so it is a local observance for a Blessed. He cannot be canonized until a second miracle is approved, and none has been. Sources: Vatican News, 2026-09, "Who was Archbishop Fulton Sheen"; EWTN News live coverage of the beatification.

**Where the memorial dates come from.** Every date in the "Memorial" column is the "Memoria liturgica" field on the saint's own page at causesanti.va. The Dicastery is the body that drafts Roman Martyrology entries. As a rule these follow the dies natalis (death day). Where the Dicastery's date is not the death day, the table says so.

**General Roman Calendar.** None of these saints is on it. The Dicastery for Divine Worship's decree list (`vatican_dicastery-divine-worship_documenti.html`) shows only two inscriptions since 2022: Teresa of Calcutta (decree of 24 Dec 2024) and John Henry Newman (decree of 9 Nov 2025). **Memorial dates I could not confirm: none.** Every person canonized in the window has a "Memoria liturgica" on the Dicastery site. Two points are worth checking before they are relied on:

- **Palazzolo's 22 May.** It is not his death day (15 Jun), and I found no second official source that explains it. English Wikipedia gives 2 Feb, which does not match either date.
- **The Dicastery's dates are not always the ones religious families keep.** A religious family's proper calendar can move a date, and I did not check those calendars. For example, the Carmelite calendar may keep Brandsma on 27 Jul.

The Portuguese forms are the usual Brazilian renderings, and I did not verify them against a CNBB source. Carlo Acutis and Pier Giorgio Frassati are normally left untranslated in Brazil.
