# Recurring figures

Face lines for people who appear on many cards, so every card paints them alike. Research agents read this before writing a scene and add a line when they first describe someone here.

## Our Lady

A young woman of about eighteen with a delicate, softly OVAL face — a smooth high forehead, a fine straight nose with a narrow bridge, almond-shaped brown eyes under gently arched light-brown brows, lids lowered in a recollected gaze, softly rounded cheeks, a small rosy mouth, a small rounded chin; light-brown hair parted in the centre under a white inner veil and a blue mantle drawn over the head (a rose-red or white gown beneath).

Taken from `annunciation`, `nativity_christ` and `epiphany`, which already agree. On scenes of her later life (the Passion, Pentecost, the Assumption) she is the same face matured, about forty-five to fifty, not a different woman. A Marian title that shows its own statue or image (`aparecida`, `fatima`) paints that image instead.

## The Christ Child

A plump, ROUND baby face with full rosy cheeks, a high rounded forehead, a small button nose, large clear brown eyes, a small rosy mouth, soft short golden-brown curls; fair, glowing skin; a plain gold halo.

Taken from `nativity_christ` (the newborn in the manger) and `epiphany` (on Mary's lap in a white tunic, raising a hand). State the age on each card: newborn, forty days, about three, twelve (the Finding in the Temple: the same face lengthened, curls to the neck).

## St. Joseph

A man of about forty with a LONG OVAL face — a straight nose, gentle dark-brown eyes under level brows, a quiet mouth; full dark-brown curly hair to the nape and a short full dark-brown beard; a green tunic and an ochre-brown mantle, often a staff.

Taken from `nativity_christ` and `epiphany`. This is close to the stock thirties bearded man; it stays because both cards already show him so.

## Christ (adult)

A man of about thirty-three with a LONG OVAL face narrowing gently to the chin — a high, smooth forehead, a long straight nose with a fine bridge, calm almond-shaped dark-brown eyes under level brown brows, high, softly modelled cheekbones, a quiet closed mouth; chestnut-brown hair parted in the centre and falling in soft waves past the shoulders; a short, full chestnut beard and moustache, lightly parted at the chin; a plain gold halo (ringed with rays in glory). In glory (the Transfiguration, the Resurrection, the Ascension) a white robe and white mantle; otherwise a rose-red tunic and a blue mantle. After the Resurrection the wounds show only as small, clean marks on the hands and feet.

Taken from `transfiguration`, the one card that shows him grown. Keep him apart from the men who stand near him: St. Joseph has shorter, darker, curly hair to the nape; St. John the Baptist (`john_baptist`) has unkempt curly dark hair, a rougher beard and a camel-hair tunic.

## Sts. Peter and John at scenes with the Apostles

Peter as on `peter`: about sixty-five, a broad, weathered fisherman's face, a strong wide nose, deep-set earnest eyes, a lined forehead; curly grey-white hair balding from the back with a curly tuft at the forehead; a short, square, curly grey-white beard; a blue tunic and golden-ochre mantle. John as on `john_evangelist`: a beardless young man of about twenty-two with a smooth OVAL face, large upturned brown eyes, a straight nose, a small full mouth; wavy golden-brown hair to the jaw; a green tunic and a red-orange mantle.

## God the Father

The Ancient of Days (Daniel 7:9), a venerable man of about seventy with a BROAD, SQUARE-BROWED face — a wide, smooth forehead, a strong straight nose, deep-set, kindly grey-blue eyes under full white brows, broad cheekbones, a calm mouth half hidden by the moustache; snow-white hair falling in soft waves to the shoulders and a long, full, flowing white beard; a triangular gold halo; a white robe and a golden-yellow mantle.

First written for `trinity`. The white-bearded elder the brief otherwise avoids is required here by Daniel's "hair of his head like clean wool". Keep him apart from St. Peter (short, square, curly grey beard, balding from the back, blue and ochre) and from the Son beside him (chestnut hair, long oval face).
