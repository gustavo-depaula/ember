Doubts:
- book/papal-magisterium on john_paul_ii: it is a whole library of popes' documents, so possibly the "general" kind; drop it if that is too loose.
- The Commentaries on His Letters shelf on paul (28 books): in line with timothy_titus and onesimus, which already link commentaries on single letters, but it is long.
- Methodius's Oration Concerning Simeon and Anna (Presentation of the Lord) was left out because its authenticity is doubtful; Venantius's Poem on Easter and the Lactantius Poem on the Passion were left out of the Triduum for the same reason.
- last_gospel on john_evangelist, and miraculous_medal plus immaculate_conception on maximilian_kolbe, rest on well-known but not in-repo facts (the Last Gospel is the Prologue of John; the Militia Immaculatae used the Miraculous Medal).
- Left out holy_face on therese (the Holy Face novena says her family embraced the devotion, but she did not found or spread it), breviary-passion on triduum (a generic votive breviary), and the Carmelite saints' link to mount_carmel.
