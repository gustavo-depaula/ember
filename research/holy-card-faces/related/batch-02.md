## Doubts (left out)

- **Catechism of St. Pius X for the Ember cards.** The three bases cite its Part III, ch. IV, q. 23, but the README excludes a general catechism, so book/pius-x-catechism is not linked. It is the only book on the topic. The coordinator could add it to the four Ember cards.
- **Montfort's Secret of the Rosary on the mysteries.** I linked it to the Sorrowful Mysteries and the Finding in the Temple because para. 62-63 names them, but the book is about the Rosary generally, so it is a thin link. It is omitted from the Luminous Mysteries, which postdate it. The chapter how-to-pray-the-rosary-well fits every mystery equally, so it is omitted.
- **Aspergillum and Baptism.** The basis calls the sprinkling a "memorial of Baptism", but the link to baptism_lord (and the chapter the-baptism-you-already-have) is a theological inference, not data. Only easter_sunday is linked.
- **Agony and the Last Supper.** The agony_garden and institution_eucharist link rests on the Holy Hour manifests and thursday-eucharist, not on a card basis.
- **Roman Missal and the Lectionary books.** GIRM 349 treats them together, but the Missal basis does not mention the other books, so no link.
- **Ciborium and tabernacle**, and **altar_crucifix and crucifixion**: obvious to a Catholic but absent from the repo's data, so omitted.
- **Vestment colours and seasons** (stole violet for Advent and Lent, dalmatic rose on Gaudete): the bases say so, but pairing vestments with season cards seemed the "same colour" kind of link the README rejects.
- **christ_king** as the close of Ordinary Time II: no repo data, so not linked.
- **Gospel of the Day on weekday cards**, and **Sunday devotion** on Sunday cards: these are generic practices matched only on the "daily Mass" and "Sunday" themes. Drop them if too loose.
- **Ave Regina Caelorum** (Feb 3 to Holy Wednesday) is omitted from the Lent cards because it covers more than Lent.
- **Pange Lingua** (sung on Holy Thursday, per its manifest) is omitted from institution_eucharist; it is a Corpus Christi hymn.
- **Nothing for the liturgical-year Sundays** beyond what the cards already link: no Advent wreath, O Antiphons or Easter sequence (Victimae paschali) practice exists in the corpus.
