Doubts (left out, or kept with a caveat)
- eulogius_alexandria: the Register of Letters is a very large book of which the 12 letters to Eulogius are a small part. Kept on the Flavian/Leo-letters precedent; drop it if that precedent is read strictly.
- matthew: whether a Gospel commentary "belongs" to the evangelist's card at all. Kept to match batch 04 (Mark). Four commentaries is generous; the Catena Aurea alone would parallel Mark. The Douay-Rheims Bible is a whole-Bible book and left out.
- holy_name_mary: Ave maris stella is the only practice, kept because the card's own image rests on Mary as stella maris (Jerome, Bernard); the hymn is not about the Name itself.
- catherine_genoa and nicholas_tolentino to all_souls: the card is a feast of the dead rather than their devotion. Kept because the two saints are known for the Holy Souls (her treatise, his patronage); drop catherine_genoa first.
- anthony_claret to immaculate_heart: kept because his congregation is named for it; no text in the corpus says so.
- john_capistrano to bernardine_siena: kept from well-established hagiography only (no Pictorial chapter for Capistrano).
- Early popes (linus, evaristus, mark_pope, callistus) are linked to predecessors or successors only, following simeon_jerusalem and leander in batch 03. Not chained any further than the Cletus chapter supports.
- frumentius: book/athanasius-apologia-ad-constantium holds Constantius's letter about him, but athanasius already lists that book, so only the card link is made.
- bruno: practice/little-office-bvm-carthusian (the Carthusian usage of Our Lady's office) left out, since it is the Order's office and not addressed to or written by him.
- paul_of_the_cross: the Passion devotion is his, but no corpus practice is tied to him (Stations of the Cross, the Passion little offices by Francis and Bonaventure and the Friday collection are general); left out.
- john_xxiii: book/papal-magisterium holds eight of his documents among many popes; left out as for paul_vi in batch 05.
- theban_legion to severinus_agaunum (abbot of the abbey of St. Maurice, per his chapter): not shared as a link; the connection is the shrine, not the martyrs. andre_soveral to inacio_azevedo (Brazilian martyrs): different century and event.
- bartolo_longo: left out book/montfort-secret-rosary (about the Rosary, not him) and the Rosary novenas (they belong to our_lady_rosary).
- hilarion: the card shares its Pictorial Lives chapter with mello (oct-22-mello is Hilarion's text), so mello gets nothing.
- guy_anderlecht: his chapter's reflection is on the tabernacle, but that is the card tabernacle's subject, not his; left out.
- carlo_acutis: no practice or book of his; only the Eucharistic collection.
