Doubts (left out, or kept with a caveat)
- celestine_i: book/councils-ephesus is a whole council, of which Celestine's letters are a part; the link to patrick rests on the "writers of his life say" tradition, which the chapter hedges. Drop either if the rule is read strictly.
- pius_v: practice/breviary-1570 is the app's multi-form Roman Breviary set to the 1570 rubrics, not a prayer of his own. Left out book/trent-catechism (its author line says "promulgated by Pope St. Pius V", but the README excludes general catechisms) and practice/mass-vetus-ordo (the Missal of Pius V is only named in its history).
- colette: clare_assisi is on the reformed-order reading ("the Colettines are the Poor Clares she restored"), like Benedict of Aniane and Benedict; the chapter itself only says "her Order" and the Third Order of Francis.
- abraham_mary to ephrem, richard_chichester to edmund_canterbury, peter_verona to dominic, cunegundes to henry, catharine_sweden to bridget_sweden: each is plain from the chapter but is a friend, master or spouse rather than a founder; the batch-03 review accepted that kind.
- elena_guerra to pentecost: the link is the feast of her novena, not a person; keep or drop with the pentecost card's own curation.
- mama_antula to ignatius_loyola: Ignatius is the author of the Exercises she spread; there is no chapter on her to confirm it, only her card's patronage line ("Pilgrim of the Spiritual Exercises").
- frances_rome: practice/little-office-benedictine-oblates (two versions) fits her card's "Patron of Benedictine oblates", but the office does not mention her, so it is left out.
- herman_joseph: practice/little-office-bvm-norbertine (his Premonstratensian order's Marian office) and a card for Norbert, left out because being of the same order is not enough.
- casimir: "Omni die dic Mariae" (the hymn the chapter says he wrote) is inside little-office-holy-ghost-2 as a stray Marian hymn, not a practice of its own.
- hegesippus: card anicetus (the chapter says he lived at Rome from Anicetus to Eleutherius) and soter to anicetus (his successor); left out as dates, not relationships.
- mary_egypt: card exaltation_cross (her conversion happened at the Exaltation); the card is the feast, not her story, so left out.
- hermenegild, isidore: nothing to add on top of leander's links; fulgentius (card) is the bishop of Ruspe, not Isidore's brother.
- perpetuus to martin_tours (he translated Martin's body and enlarged the church): left out, not a relationship between the saints.
- forty_martyrs_sebaste, bademus, jonas_barachisius, benjamin: Sozomen's and Theodoret's Ecclesiastical Histories cover the Sapor and Persian persecutions, but only as general histories.
- saragossa_martyrs to vincent_saragossa (same governor Dacian, same persecution): left out as co-persecution, not co-martyrdom.
