Doubts (left out unless the coordinator wants them):
- Apocryphal Acts (barnabas, matthias, philip_james) are included but are legends; strike them if apocrypha should not appear. The Matthias one is mostly about Andrew rescuing him.
- book/barnabas-epistle for barnabas: traditionally attributed to him, but pseudonymous.
- Justin: the shelf also holds five works under his name that are spurious or fragmentary (Discourse to the Greeks, Hortatory Address, Sole Government of God, Fragments on the Resurrection, Miscellaneous Fragments). I linked only the authentic works and the Martyrdom.
- pamphilus: book/pamphilus-exposition-on-the-acts-of-the-apostles has a Pamphilus as author, but the work's attribution to the martyr of Caesarea is uncertain, so I left it out.
- pothinus_blandina: book/eusebius-church-history contains the Letter of the Churches of Vienne and Lyons (their martyrdom), but it is a general history, so I left it out.
- cyril_alexandria: card celestine_i (the Pope who charged Cyril against Nestorius) is not one of the listed link types.
- ladislas: card stephen_hungary (he restored the laws St. Stephen established; not a founder or namesake case).
- clotilda and medard: card remigius (baptized Clovis; consecrated Medard). The Pictorial Lives do not name him for Clotilda, and the tie to Medard is weak.
- barnabas: card mark (John Mark, whom Barnabas took to Cyprus), which depends on identifying him with the Evangelist.
- francis_caracciolo: his order's perpetual adoration (collection/eucharistic) is less central than Paschal's devotion, so no link.
- juliana_falconieri: practice/little-office-seven-dolours-of-the-blessed-virgin-mary (the Servite devotion) and card our_lady_sorrows, as devotion of her order rather than hers specifically.
- norbert: practice/little-office-bvm-norbertine (the Premonstratensian use of the BVM office); the order he founded, but not addressed to him.
- paul_vi: book/papal-magisterium and collection/papal-magisterium contain his documents (Humanae Vitae, Populorum Progressio, ...) among those of many popes; neither is about him.
- first_martyrs_rome: book/clement-of-rome 1 Clement mentions the Neronian martyrs in passing; not linked. Card peter (martyred under Nero) is not a listed link type.
- philip_james: card hegesippus (the Pictorial Life cites him for James's traditions).
- bernardine_siena: card vincent_ferrer (his prophecy about Bernardine; Pictorial Life), left out as incidental.
