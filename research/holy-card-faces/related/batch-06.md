Doubts, links left out:
- agapetus and helena: the Pictorial Life of Helena also holds Agapetus's paragraph, so Agapetus's card shows nothing of its own. A link agapetus to helena would surface it, but sharing a feast chapter is not one of the link kinds.
- cajetan: the Pictorial Life says he introduced the Forty Hours' Adoration, but collection/eucharistic lists Forty Hours only as a to-do, so I did not link it.
- compiegne_martyrs and titus_brandsma: collection/carmelite is Teresian spirituality. They are Carmelites, but unlike Simon Stock and Teresa Benedicta, the collection does not name them.
- eugenius_carthage and liberatus: both suffered under the Vandal king Huneric at Carthage, about 483. The Pictorial Lives say so, but the same persecution is not a listed link kind.
- helena: the Life of Constantine (Eusebius) is the early witness for her pilgrimage, but it is about Constantine. Rufinus, Socrates and Sozomen tell the finding of the Cross inside general church histories.
- henry: his vision at St. Mary Major with Laurence and Vincent could link to mary_major and lawrence. It is an incident, not a relationship.
- james_nisibis: Ephrem's Nisibene Hymns are about Nisibis and its bishops, and tradition makes Ephrem his disciple. The Pictorial Life says neither, and the hymns are mostly about the sieges under his successors. A card link to ephrem would be defensible.
- martha_mary_lazarus: Mary of Bethany and Mary Magdalene are traditionally conflated in the West. The Pictorial Life keeps them apart, so I did not link mary_magdalene.
- mary_major: its chapter describes the manger relic, so a link to nativity_christ is possible. The relic is an object in the church, not the mystery on the card.
- radegundes: Vexilla Regis was written by Venantius Fortunatus, her friend, for the relic of the Cross that she obtained for Poitiers. The manifest says only Poitiers, and the link is not among the listed kinds.
- sixtus_ii: Cyprian's Letter 80 (book/cyprian-epistles) reports his martyrdom, and Ambrose's Duties of the Clergy retells the exchange with Lawrence. Both are whole books on other subjects.
- stephen_hungary: possible card links to stephen (the protomartyr whose vision foretold his birth) and assumption (his devotion; he died on the feast), but neither is a listed kind.
- stephen_i: Cyprian opposed him in the rebaptism dispute. A card link to cornelius_cyprian and book/cyprian-epistles would fit, but the Pictorial Life does not name Cyprian.
- zephyrinus: Hippolytus's Refutation (book VII) attacks him and Callistus; Eusebius and Tertullian also mention him. He is the object of the polemic, not its subject.
- dominic: morning-offering-dominican and little-office-bvm-dominican are the Order's prayers, not his. Montfort's Secret of the Rosary retells his Rosary story, but is a book on the Rosary.
- hippolytus works: Against Plato and the Discourse on the Theophany are of doubtful attribution, so I left them out.
