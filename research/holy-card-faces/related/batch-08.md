Doubts (left out, or kept with a caveat)
- gertrude: left out practice/st-gertrude-souls ("Eternal Father, I offer Thee the most precious Blood..."). The app names it hers, but it is a popular attribution not found in her writings; practice/monday-souls is a general Monday devotion that merely includes it. Add it if you accept the attribution.
- francis_xavier: little-office-s-ignatius is addressed to Ignatius and to Xavier jointly. Drop it if you want only offices that are his alone.
- loreto: annunciation rests on the tradition that the Holy House is Mary's house at the Annunciation. Drop it if that is too much tradition.
- charles_borromeo: left out book/trent-catechism (the Roman Catechism he helped bring about, but nothing in the corpus ties it to him) and the card link to aloysius_gonzaga (Pictorial Life: Aloysius made his first Communion at Charles's hands). That relationship is not in the README's list.
- holy_relics: left out chapter/morrow-the-altar and Trent's catechism commandments, which mention relics only in passing. Left out the finding_stephen_relics card, a different feast.
- sylvester: left out book/councils-nicaea-i (the chapter has him confirming Nicaea, but he did not preside) and the card mark_pope (his successor).
- saturninus_toulouse: left out the card fabian (sent him to Gaul, per the chapter) as not on the README's list.
- andrew_avellino and barbara: both are patrons against sudden death in their Pictorial reflections. Left out a card link as not on the README's list.
- leo_great: left out book/councils-chalcedon (the council that adopted his Tome), as batch 03 did for flavian.
- clement_i: the Pictorial Life's "consecrated by Peter" is tradition (Tertullian), not certain; the card link to peter rests on it.
- willibrord to wulfran and malachi_armagh to bernard_clairvaux: the first is only a mention in Wulfran's chapter; the second is well-known hagiography beyond the chapter. Drop if too loose.
- martin_tours: left out book/sulpitius-severus-genuine, which holds the three letters (two about Martin's death and critics); only the first of them is about him.
- gregory_thaumaturgus: left out the card john_evangelist (the chapter says John appeared to him and gave the creed), and collection/origen (his teacher).
- elizabeth_hungary: left out the card francis_assisi (the Third Order she is patron of) as not on the README's list.
- bertille to bathildes and thrasilla_emiliana to gregory_great rest on the chapters of the cards (Chelles; aunts); the Delphinus and Thrasilla chapter, and the Valery and Finian chapter, are shared by date only, so no link was written between those pairs.
- leonard_noblac: Micy is where Leonard withdrew and Mesmin's abbey; left out a mesmin link as only a place in common.
