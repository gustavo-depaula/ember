Doubts (left out, or kept with a caveat)
- timothy_titus and onesimus: the commentaries are on the letters addressed to them, not about the saints. Kept because the letters are the saints' main appearance in Scripture. Drop timothy_titus's books if the rule is read strictly (Onesimus's Philemon commentaries are firmer, the letter is about him).
- flavian: leo-letters is a large book of which Flavian is a part. Left out the Council of Chalcedon (the council that vindicated him) and the card leo_great, which has no chapter link to Flavian.
- Card links I judged beyond the README's list and left out: holy_name_jesus to bernardine_siena (spread the devotion), angela_merici to ursula (named her company after her), john_britto to francis_xavier (his inspiration), benedict_aniane to benedict (namesake and restorer of his Rule), raymund_penyafort to our_lady_mercy (co-founded the Mercedarians), circumcision to mary_mother_of_god (same day, Jan 1), clotilda for genevieve.
- agnes, apollonia: the books are whole treatises or letter collections that contain her account; strictly the book is not only about her.
- holy_name_jesus: left out practice/jesus-prayer (invokes the Name, but nothing in the corpus ties it to the devotion). conversion_paul: left out the Peter and Paul novena, since the paul card already carries it.
- Jerome's Letters (which include those to Marcella) were left off marcella because they are not about her.
