# Batch 01: the Mass cards

Every card got two baseline links: the Mass practice of the card's own form (`practice/mass` for the Ordinary Form cards, whose excerpt comes from content/of; `practice/mass-vetus-ordo` for the Extraordinary Form cards, whose excerpt comes from content/do) and `chapter/what-the-mass-is` (its "The shape" section walks the parts of the Mass). Entries below list only what is added beyond those two.

ablutions: chalice, purificator (cards; the basis names both: wine and water poured over the fingers, wiped with the purificator)
agnus_dei: paten, chalice (the Host is broken over the paten and a piece put in the chalice); the libera_nos link is written on libera_nos
collect: roman_missal (the Missal kept beside the chair, GIRM 118a)
consecration_chalice: chalice; elevation_chalice (the elevation that follows)
consecration_host: consecration_chalice, elevation_host (the pair of words and showing)
dismissal: final_blessing (the dismissal follows the blessing, GIRM 168)
domine_non_sum_dignus: paten (the Host held with the paten beneath)
doxology_amen: paten, chalice (the priest raises both at the doxology)
elevation_chalice: elevation_host (the same elevation), altar_bells, thurible (the server rings and the thurifer incenses at each elevation, Ritus servandus VIII.6, VIII.8)
elevation_host: altar_bells, thurible (same reason)
entrance: practice/sign-of-cross (the excerpt is the Sign of the Cross), book_of_gospels, thurible (both in the procession, GIRM 120)
epiclesis: nothing beyond the baseline (see doubts)
final_blessing: nothing beyond the baseline (linked from dismissal)
first_reading: ambo, lectionary (the reader proclaims from the Lectionary at the ambo), responsorial_psalm (the psalm that answers it)
gloria: nothing beyond the baseline (no Gloria practice exists; practice/pax-in-caelo is a different chant)
gospel: ambo, book_of_gospels, thurible (the priest announces the Gospel at the ambo and incenses the book)
gospel_acclamation: gospel, book_of_gospels, thurible (incense is put in the thurible and the book is taken up), munda_cor (the basis says Munda cor is said during the Alleluia; the EF Low Mass form is that card)
greeting: nothing beyond the baseline
holy_communion: ciborium, paten (GIRM 160: "takes the paten or ciborium"); pray groups Before Communion (aquinas-before-communion, prayer-of-st-ambrose) and After Communion (thanksgiving-mass, aquinas-after-communion, prayer-of-st-bonaventure), each described in its manifest as prayer for before or after Communion; collection/eucharistic
homily: nothing beyond the baseline
incensing_altar: thurible, altar, altar_crucifix (the celebrant incenses the cross, then the altar), dalmatic (the deacon assists at Solemn Mass)
introit: roman_missal, altar (he kisses the altar, then reads the Introit from the Missal at the epistle corner)
kyrie: nothing beyond the baseline (the litanies with Kyrie in them are not this prayer)
last_gospel: nothing beyond the baseline (no practice or book holds the Prologue of John as such)
leonine_prayers: practice/prayer-st-michael (its description says the short form is the one prayed after Low Mass, the Leonine prayer), practice/hail-mary, practice/hail-holy-queen (the Ordo gives three Hail Marys and the Hail Holy Queen), mass-vetus-ordo; card michael_archangel (added in review: the prayer to St Michael is one of the Leonine prayers)
libera_nos: paten, lords_prayer (the embolism follows the Our Father), agnus_dei (the breaking and commingling that end the prayer are on that card)
lords_prayer: practice/our-father
memento_dead: memento_living (the two commemorations of the Canon)
memento_living: nothing beyond the baseline (linked from memento_dead)
munda_cor: roman_missal (the Missal is carried to the gospel corner)
nobis_quoque: nothing beyond the baseline
orate_fratres: prayer_over_offerings (the people's answer leads straight into it; both bases name it)
penitential_act: practice/confiteor (the excerpt is the Confiteor, and the practice's own description says it opens the Penitential Act)
placeat: dismissal (the basis says the dismissal comes before this prayer in the old rite)
prayer_after_communion: nothing beyond the baseline (see doubts)
prayer_over_offerings: pall, corporal (the gifts on the corporal, chalice covered with the pall, GIRM 142)
prayers_foot_altar: penitential_act (the basis refers to it for the Confiteor), practice/confiteor
preface: sanctus (the Preface ends in it)
preparation_of_gifts: corporal, purificator, chalice, cruets, paten, roman_missal (all named in the basis), water_and_wine (the parallel offertory act in the old rite)
profession_of_faith: practice/nicene-creed (the excerpt is order.credo-nicene)
responsorial_psalm: ambo, lectionary
sanctus: nothing beyond the baseline
second_reading: ambo, lectionary
sign_of_peace: nothing beyond the baseline
te_igitur: nothing beyond the baseline
universal_prayer: ambo (the intentions are announced from it)
veni_sanctificator: chalice, pall (he signs the host and chalice, already covered with the pall)
washing_of_hands: lavabo (the basis says that object card shows the cruet, basin and towel)
water_and_wine: cruets, chalice

## Doubts

- epiclesis and veni_sanctificator both call the Holy Spirit down on the offerings, but they are different prayers in different rites (the Canon's epiclesis versus an Offertory prayer), so I did not link them.
- Domine, non sum dignus and Prayer after Communion: no practice is the very prayer. I linked the Aquinas Communion prayers only to holy_communion, not to the priest's own prayers. Also left out spiritual-communion, and the prayers before and after Mass to Our Lady and St. Joseph.
- Leonine prayers: michael_archangel and the Our Lady cards are plausible card links (the prayer is to St. Michael), but they fall outside the stated rules; the statue and window are decoration per the basis.
- Cards whose part exists in both forms (consecration, Our Father, Agnus Dei, Sanctus...) are linked only to the form the card depicts. practice/apostles-creed is a permitted alternative to the Nicene Creed (GIRM 68) but the card shows only the Nicene excerpt.
- Eastern anaphora books (liturgies-liturgy-of-james, etc.) not linked to the epiclesis: not the Roman rite, and no card text ties them.
- The chasuble card is named in some bases (lifted by the server, held by the subdeacon) but I did not link it. The entrance's procession cross is not obviously the altar_crucifix object, so that is left out too.
- Object and vestment cards in other batches may link back to these Mass cards; apply-related drops a link the other card already makes.
- I did not verify that mass-vetus-ordo's flow actually shows the Leonine prayers (they are in the DO Ordo.txt).
